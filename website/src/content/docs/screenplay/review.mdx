# Independent review

The evidence graph proves that every obligation was answered. It cannot prove that an answer is true or that the writing works. Independent review covers that: a reader in a separate context reads each layer as its recipient, records findings, and binds each review to the exact text it read.

## Separate contexts

The production keeps three roles apart:

| Role | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Author | A writer subagent drafts and revises the layer |
| Evidence answerer | A separate subagent writes the answers and returns gaps to the author |
| Independent reviewer | A fresh subagent reads the layer and owns the verdict |

The main agent is the director. It assigns these roles, inspects each handoff against the commission and returns work that falls short, even after it passed validation or review. A fresh context starts with the job and the input paths, without inherited conversation or memory. If isolation is unavailable, the agent reports the review as unperformed.

## Reading as the recipient

The reviewer receives the task, the recipient and the annotation-free body before parents, contracts or earlier verdicts, and never the Evidence answers. It reads every unit and locks its first response and the concrete losses before reading other records.

Each layer is read as the person who receives it:

| Layer | Read as |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Treatment writer and designer |
| Brief | Screenplay writer and designer |
| Treatments | Script writer |
| Scripts | Screenplay writer and performer |
| Screenplay | Audience, then filmmaker |

Only then does the reviewer compare decisions, names, notation, quotations, action, picture and sound with the parents and contracts. A loss the recipient suffers stays a finding even when upstream knowledge explains it, and a defective parent is reported with its earliest owner.

## Review records

Reviews mirror the body. Each authored file has a review file at `docs/review/draft/<layer>/<relative-file>.md`, and each authored H2 has a review H2 there. One verdict file per layer, `docs/review/draft/<layer>/verdict/index.md`, answers the whole-layer duties once and holds the findings and the author's decisions. Review records stay out of Git, under the ignored `docs/review/`.

A review H2 cites its authored H2 and answers the review questions. Its `@evidenceReview` tag carries the fingerprint of the text it read:

```markdown
# The lift

## Rehearsal hall {#s03}
<!--
@evidence rehearsal/030-lift.md#s03 Read the complete section.
@evidence shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading Read the section as its audience before the parents.
@evidenceReview rehearsal/030-lift.md#s03 #<fingerprint> Independently read the section.
-->
The press, the late read and the short landing read on first viewing.
```

## Stale reviews

The fingerprint is a hash of the reviewed unit's content. Annotations such as HTML comments are excluded, so a drafting note leaves the review current. A change to the action, dialogue or record changes the fingerprint, and the check reports `graph-stale-review` for exactly the units that changed. The graph shows which units to read again, instead of a whole new round.

A citation without its fingerprint is not a review. The check reports `graph-missing-review` until the reviewer records one. Review claims are active while the layer's stage is `"evidence"`.

## Findings and decisions

The author records acceptance or refusal of each finding, with reasons. The reviewer withdraws a finding or rebuts the refusal, and the author's decision after a rebuttal stands. A settled finding reopens only with new evidence from the body, a parent or a source.

The author repairs the accepted findings by cause, at their owners, and the answerer brings the affected answers up to date. On the long route a pass runs at most two rounds. A short takes one review, after which its brief and screenplay stages return to disabled, so the repairs that follow owe no story answers.

## Delivery review and the acceptance record

A film or a whole motion hint has its own acceptance, judged on stills in two passes: stills drawn from the film's source, and stills extracted from the delivered file at matching times. Each film keeps one `docs/acceptance/<film>.md` across revisions. It records:

- technical file completion;
- a separate verdict on the depicted practice;
- rejected requirements and any exclusion you adopted;
- every scene and beat seen, with on-model entities, contacts, presences, camera and continuity;
- the measures;
- the views and passes reviewed;
- what you watched or heard, and what nobody did.

Playback and sound are yours to judge, because stills cannot show them. `pnpm run review` records the inspector's playback as a video for you, and [Capture, render and review](/docs/delivery/capture-render-review) describes it.
