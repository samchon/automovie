# Story contracts

Long films and series retain settings → treatments → scripts → final screenplay. Scripts receives content and expression assessment together in construction, with discovery and independent review. Final screenplay retains construction and its separate expression revision, evidence and independent review. A short work may begin directly at final screenplay; it still receives those screenplay responsibilities. Standalone asset work has no narrative population. An explicit user waiver may change the path; document its actual scope without claiming an omitted stage passed.

Each checklist H2 asks its named respondent a question. Principles apply to an authored H2, obligations to one document for a complete declared population, discovery to the inspected scope's discovery result, and review questions to review records. Do not make every scene testify for an entire film or attach a conditional requirement to unrelated hosts. The evidence configuration owns the actual selected populations and relationships; installed Evidence APIs own its mechanics.

Use [common discovery](shared/discovery/common.md), then [settings](shared/discovery/settings.md), [treatments](shared/discovery/treatments.md), [scripts](shared/discovery/scripts.md), [screenplay](shared/discovery/screenplay.md), and [narrative](shared/discovery/narratives.md) for the actual selected populations. Authors create `work/discovery/results.md` under this story contract tree from inspected findings and negatives; the blank scaffold cannot know their results. Create work-specific requirement subtrees only for adopted, observable needs with canonical owners.

Scripts construction and screenplay expression revision use [common](shared/naturalness/common.md) and [actual speech](shared/naturalness/realized.md), plus [scripts](shared/naturalness/scripts.md) or [screenplay](shared/naturalness/screenplay.md). Their full-population comparisons use [common](shared/naturalness/obligations/common.md), [voices](shared/naturalness/obligations/realized.md), and the applicable [script](shared/naturalness/obligations/scripts.md) or [screenplay](shared/naturalness/obligations/screenplay.md) obligations. Fidelity and natural reception remain separate questions.

Use creation to test actual parents through [treatment](shared/upstream/treatments.md), [script](shared/upstream/scripts.md), and [screenplay](shared/upstream/screenplay.md) upstream audits. Repair the earliest semantic owner and affected descendants together. Acknowledgments cannot supply missing content, revive stale observations, or turn a source relationship into proof of an actual rendered experience.
