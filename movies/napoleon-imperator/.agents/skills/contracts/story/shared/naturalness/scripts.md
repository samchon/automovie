# Script expression principles

Each script construction H2 answers for the clarity and rhythm of its executable scene in the same pass as its content. Compare inherited storyline with its actual treatments rather than inventing a frozen scripts revision.

## Do execution blocks carry the scene's pressure naturally? {#execution-rhythm}

Do block length, speaker changes, directions, interruptions, silence, and simultaneous action make the actual action and reaction easy to perform at the required pressure and pace? Inspect a uniform beat mold, gesture on every line, decisive dialogue buried in exposition, summary substituted for exchange, repeated performance labels, or a long explanation that hides where a response happens. Urgency, comedy, weight, and sustained stillness need the arrangement that serves them rather than decorative variation or a prescribed line count.

Keep executable instructions and precise staging, audiovisual state, and viewing positions; removing their vocabulary because it sounds technical would destroy this layer's work. Replace interpretive labels with a performable operation within the inherited scene. Complete content and expression in this construction while preserving inherited facts, knowledge, appearance, event results and reveal order; repair a defective treatment at its actual owner. Classify actual passages and prove any permitted exception under [common expression principles](common.md#reads-as-written).
