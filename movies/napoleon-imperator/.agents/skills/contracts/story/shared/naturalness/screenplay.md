# Final screenplay expression revision principles

Each revised final screenplay H2 answers for the finished image, sound, performance, attention, and temporal experience specified by that unit.

## Do audiovisual voice and rhythm belong to this moment? {#audiovisual-voice}

Do the chosen image and sound, distance and access, duration, interruption, transition, and arrangement of performance carry this scene's pressure within the work's adopted voice? Inspect a habitual opening, explanation, montage pattern, musical emphasis, reaction pause, or closing image imposed on different material; frantic moments buried in duration; weight lost to a uniform short beat; and production reading units whose grouping conceals the movement of attention. The same reading can require long duration, flat delivery, a fixed view, or recurrence when its effect is realized. The sequence must convey the present pressure; listing each image or sentence's function cannot excuse delayed continuous experience. Do not vary forms merely for variety.

## Does the final medium transform the upstream register? {#upstream-transposition}

Does the plan convey inherited content as the audience's image, sound, action, and permitted narration rather than import the author's management language? Inspect copied planning labels, source-status or research commentary inserted into the scene, explanatory category names that nobody in the world would use, repeated character appearance and vocal inventories, redundant acting tails, and a shot list with no finished audiovisual relation. Useful camera, sound, edit, and performance instructions remain necessary production notation; the failure is their substitution for an audience experience, not their mere presence.

Preserve genuine world names, titles and terms, useful time transitions, adopted documentary quotations, captions, and narration whose function cannot be delivered within the scene. Named failures follow the decision, exception and record rules in [common expression principles](common.md#reads-as-written); do not change events or canonical values to escape an awkward register.
