# Narrative requirement discovery

The narrative discovery result answers for the current complete treatment, script, or final screenplay population. It does not require every H2 to answer a conditional work-wide concern.

## Are work-specific narrative needs assigned to honest respondents? {#narrative-requirements}

Find recurring authenticity, representation, agency, causality, form, and audience-experience conditions not already owned by shared, language, work, or settings requirements. Give each finding its observable form, application, audience effect, representative refutation, and smallest honest host population. A repeated subject or conditional risk alone does not justify an all-H2 checklist. Apply the [common discovery boundary](common.md#shared-work-boundary), including existing coverage, inspected negatives, unresolved scope, and the canonical owner for any adopted choice.
