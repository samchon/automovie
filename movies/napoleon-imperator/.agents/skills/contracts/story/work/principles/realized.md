Each authored narrative H2 in treatments, scripts, and screenplay answers these questions. The population is the production's actual narrative units; population-wide distribution belongs to the work obligations. Facts and actual relationship registers remain with settings.

## Does the unit preserve the historian's distance and the participant's limited knowledge? {#historian-and-participant}

When the unit gives retrospective judgment, a future outcome, a modern comparison, or a source qualification, can the recipient distinguish the later historian from a person acting inside 1769–1840? Does the historian's concise overview give way to playable human choices rather than explain away the decisive exchange? Does the participant act on received information and period assumptions, with dispatched orders, observed events, later memoir knowledge, and audience access kept distinct? A unit without narration can answer through its actual information boundary; it need not insert narration to comply.

The desired difference is an informed historian beside fallible contemporaries. A representative failure gives Napoleon knowledge of a report before its arrival, or turns a later witness's explanation into something every soldier directly observed. Repair the earliest information owner; preserve the fixed event while changing access or expression. Basis: adopted instructions `002-viewing-experience#chronicler-and-scene` and `#unequal-knowledge`.

## Does its humor, violence, or loss keep the adopted historical perspective? {#human-cost}

Does any satire target power, propaganda, etiquette, bureaucracy, or the contradiction between a legend and recorded conduct? Does serious harm retain the harmed person's weight rather than make the body the joke? Can the recipient tell when a legend or reconstructed exchange is being represented rather than authenticated? Maintain the scene's intended tone; a tragic unit need not add comedy. A representative failure interrupts an amputation with the narrator's punchline or celebrates victory while its human cost disappears. Repair the target and tonal transition while preserving the event and its required emotional effect. Basis: `002-viewing-experience#satire-and-grief`, `#war-and-society`.
