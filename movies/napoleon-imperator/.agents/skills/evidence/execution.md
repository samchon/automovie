# Trace narrative decisions into production

Use this guide when settings and screenplay decisions become assets, performances, shots and a delivered artifact. The existing [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) keep their paths, H2 questions, creative ownership and closed proof. Execution evidence establishes a different claim: what those decisions actually caused the production to build and deliver. It cannot approve a draft retrospectively or make an unmade output exist.

## Record actual provenance and consumers

Keep each relationship with the source, accepted asset or edit that actually uses it, rather than another unconsumed inventory. Use canonical file/H2 addresses and the exact source revision or content identity actually read. Appearance, dimensions, baseline relations and physical world constraints come from their settings owners; required actions, participants, access and results come from the actual final screenplay and its handoff. Follow links to an adopted reference only within its declared adoption scope. Do not treat an entire retained reference as active canon.

| Actual owner | Establish |
| --- | --- |
| Asset or rig | Used settings facts and screenplay role; fixed source/derivative identity; meaningful dimensions, controls and admitted rig interpretation |
| Motion or performance | Required action/result; actual asset/rig; accepted clip/controller; timing, contacts and constraints with current observations |
| Executable shot | Source screenplay units, invoked assets/performance and camera/sound decisions, with its actual time range and output |
| Edit | Included shots/takes, trims/order/transitions, sound/captions and retained required material |
| Delivered artifact | Exact encoded output and media identities, observed profile, measured/playback observations and reproducible entry |

These relationships are many-to-many. One character can serve many scenes; one unit can need several assets/shots, and one shot can realize several units. Do not demand one asset for every settings H2 or one shot per paragraph. A background fact may need no visible asset; an omission of required action must remain unresolved. Every consumer claiming narrative realization must identify its real parents; standalone technical work uses its actual brief without invented ancestry.

## Separate native coverage from semantic and output proof

Use the installed native Evidence relationship API where the actual respondent and legitimate owner support the edge. Read its current cardinality contract: target coverage without exclusions can establish that all selected targets are cited, but it does not automatically require an arbitrary positive number of parents for every host. Do not impose a false one-parent rule on a genuine many-to-many relationship. Review the actual respondent for unsupported attribution and inspect whether the referenced material is executed, visible or audible.

The installed narrative lifecycle currently owns four authored layers and construction/naturalness passes. Its global engineering claims cannot select protected creative bodies, instructions, frozen/working editions, creative proof or story contracts. A custom claim consuming those inputs requires its truthful explicit narrative layer/pass owner. Do not omit narrative mode, copy protected text, relabel a runtime duty as naturalness, weaken selectors or mutate a factory config to bypass that boundary.

When the appropriate creative pass is open, bind a genuinely owned additional claim through the installed composition API. Completed writing stays completed: do not reopen its reviews or rewrite answers merely to attach later asset/motion proof. For downstream execution outside that native ownership model, keep the actual provenance relationships and scoped observations with current production records, and have [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) compare the complete canonical inputs with the complete actual output. State which relationships received native coverage, which were checked by actual source/runtime observations and independent review, and which remain unverified. Those observations are not a fabricated native execution stage or fully validated narrative completion. If the commission requires a mechanical guarantee the current API cannot express, report that exact missing check and extend the responsible reusable checker with its consumer and meaningful tests instead of declaring it passed.

The installed engine's supported shot compiler can check its declared participants, event predicates, sampled camera conditions and continuity against compiled artifacts. Use its actual result through [staging](../motion/staging.md), and keep that bounded numerical proof distinct from native narrative lifecycle coverage and captured appearance. A sampled frustum check cannot prove pixel occlusion; an engine result cannot establish capture or edit inclusion. [Conditioning handoff](../production/conditioning.md) owns the actual control-channel observations.

## Invalidate the right evidence

A changed canonical value, rig, source take, controller, schedule or edit invalidates its affected production relationships and observations. Repair the actual consumers together and inspect their dependency closure; preserve unaffected work and historical reports at their original inputs. A relocated settings record uses its declared address map, not a guessed new parent. Hashes identify bytes and detect change; they do not establish correct appearance, faithful performance, sound intelligibility, edit inclusion or artistic approval. Closed creative proof follows [upstream repair](../screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes) throughout.
