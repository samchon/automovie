# Scripts

Recreate reviewed treatments as scenes performers and filmmakers can execute. The [script principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/scripts.md) judge the complete execution, staging, and view; [population duties](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/scripts.md) judge partition and handoff.

After [discovery](discovery.md), map treatments to their script consumers and settle part, episode, and meaningful-unit partition before bulk scene writing. Merge thin units and divide heavy ones according to their actual viewing material and release form. Report unresolved parents before fixing that partition.

Use `docs/body/scripts/NNN-part/NNN-episode.md` with one H1 and ordered explicitly anchored H2s. Each part has an `index.md` with its H1 and links to episode files. A continuous film can use one part and a suitable sequence division without pretending it is an episodic release. Hand identities and order to the final screenplay.

Build each scene around what its participants or process do, encounter, and change. Recreate the treatment's pressure through actual exchanges, selecting the material that moves them. Write directions in the order people perform and film them; use block breaks, sustained action, and separated replies to shape the scene's rhythm. The contracts determine which execution decisions must be supplied and when detail matters.

Build the story before inspecting its named failures. Avoiding every suspect form before writing motivation or conflict can drain the scene. Replace an unplayable explanation of intent with performable action, and read long arguments as exchanges with adjacent directions and replies. Give a consequential interruption or comic response its own beat where needed. Block breaks shape reading and performance rhythm without deciding shot size or angle for production.

Before completing each episode or sequence, search and compare preceding units for repeated audible lines and dramatic patterns under [script expression obligations](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/obligations/scripts.md#execution-distribution); a single-unit reading cannot expose cross-unit recurrence.

Inherit world facts and uncertainty through treatments, citing every realized parent. Repair defective parent facts or design yourself under [upstream repair](repair.md#upstream-defects) before writing from them; do not cover them with local expression. Use the [working notation](notation.md). Before evidence, follow the complete script as its first reader, performer and filmmaker. Record ambiguous directions, speech that does not sit in the mouth and places where the scene stops before starting repairs, because editing changes that first response. Read exchanges with adjacent action as if aloud; use the [execution](../contracts/story/shared/principles/scripts.md#executable-scene) questions and the common, speech, and script expression questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) used by independent review. Collect and judge their whole-scope candidates through [repair by cause](repair.md#cause-rounds), then remove dialogue and directions whose loss changes no information, access, or pressure. Preserve motivation-setting speech, promised humor, traps and payoffs by rewriting their function without the failure rather than deleting it.

Complete content and expression together in construction under [workflow](workflow.md), then hand the reviewed body to screenplay. Scripts is a complete scene-execution layer; the next layer owns its final audiovisual realization.
