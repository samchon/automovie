# An Emperor Reported Dead and an Army Going Home

## In the capital false news gains the force of command {#paris}

Factual parents: [retreat and Malet](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#russia-and-coalition), [the delay in news](../settings/110-knowledge/010-information.md#malet-waterloo), [Bourgogne](../settings/050-people/300-bourgogne.md#bourgogne), [access to information](../settings/110-knowledge/010-information.md#access).

Paris, the night of October 22–23, 1812. Reveal to the audience the course of Malet's conspiracy using false information that the Emperor is dead. Without opening a close performance or invented voice for a new individual, convey through the historian and reports how the announcement of death is treated as authority, moves orders, and is suppressed. The result reveals how much rule depends on the Emperor's individual survival.

Napoleon in Russia does not yet know of the event. Do not make him decide to retreat or immediately rage that day using the audience's knowledge of the capital. A fracture remains between having an heir and being certain that the regime will automatically hold for him. Arrival of this information is a separate later event.

Additional factual parent: [additional historical decisions](../settings/100-history/030-decisions-and-results.md#malet-stopped).

Doucet recognizes the document's forgery and Laborde arrests Malet. Troops return and the false orders stop. The audience receives not only the obedience Malet briefly obtained but the process by which it is withdrawn. Maintain the treatment's prohibition on a new close performance by a named person, conveying it at the historian's distant and report level. What the capital stopped reaches Napoleon later.

## What was lost does not return on the road home {#retreat}

-->
For the army withdrawing from Moscow, an order to return does not immediately mean safety. Already depleted horses and transport, food and skill are insufficient, and cold is added. Pressure from fighting and encirclement also narrows the road. Rather than understand a larger map, soldiers choose how to eat that day, walk together, and reach the next halt alive. Bourgogne's view does not exceed his own experience.

Commanders executing French orders and pursuing Russian forces alike are bound by their units' condition and objectives. Preserve the fact that Russians also suffer attrition; do not use cold as a supernatural weapon favoring only one side. The audience's lasting feeling is frustration at the different burdens of a road already chosen, rather than the pleasure of swift punishment for the Emperor's error.

## A late report changes the meaning of return {#arrival}

News of the Malet affair reaches Napoleon within November 6–7. The audience that knows the event in Paris now receives the moment the information gap closes. The need to protect the capital and regime joins the need to bring the army back. How a state that officially followed him moved at news of his death is a different kind of report from a victory bulletin.

That shock is not the single cause of the army's entire retreat and his later departure. Even on a field where the Emperor is still present, soldiers and staff must continue the work of survival. He seeks to return alive, and the army must cross a river. Carry to the Berezina the condition that the two returns cannot have the same speed and safety.
