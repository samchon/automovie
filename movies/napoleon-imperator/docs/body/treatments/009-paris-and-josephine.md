# The Guns That Saved Paris and the Answer That Does Not Arrive

## A government seeking order chooses an officer {#vendemiaire}

Paris, October 5, 1795. In streets where the revolutionary government could fall, Napoleon takes charge of the suppression. Artillery expertise here has a different cost from talent used against a foreign army. Swift violence supporting the regime restores his position; to those struck by it, the Republic's order arrives as shot. Show the efficiency of the gunners' skill without making victims the objects of laughter.

The government gains survival, the officer renewed confidence and the possibility of a next command. Do not give everyone in the crowd a single political motive. The man who had depended on a patron's signature now saves a government that might fall, but his achievement brings army and politics closer together.

Additional factual parent: [the delegated suppression](../settings/100-history/030-decisions-and-results.md#vendemiaire).

The authority entrusting the suppression is the government on the National Convention's side; its target is the royalist uprising. Do not assign one mind to everyone in the streets. The government chooses military violence to survive, and the officer's standing is restored. Do not move the incident to a time after the Directory has already begun.

## Love does not stop marching orders {#letters}

The union with Joséphine brings together love and social access, the life of a woman on her own and a young officer's desire for recognition. Do not fix Joséphine in advance as a wife destined to be discarded. She has a family, a network, and a life of her own to maintain. Napoleon receives command in Italy and leaves for the army after the marriage rather than staying beside her; his passion to be together becomes demands in letters.

Intercut his quick, insistent letters with the distance created by her failure to respond at the same speed. Convey the difference between tu and vous in his letter of March 30, 1796 as the distance he feels, and do not invent the words of a reply from Joséphine that has not survived. The audience awaits both the growing authority of the general in the next campaign and the private assurance that authority cannot obtain.
