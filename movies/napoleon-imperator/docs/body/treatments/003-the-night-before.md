On the night of December 1, letters from France reach the staff at headquarters. They hear a song, drink from a cask through straws, then sleep on straw in their greatcoats. The contrast between headquarters' authority over an army and a night lived with straws in place of cups gives light humor without making exhausted soldiers its target. Rather than invent a particular lover or the contents of a letter, establish that life left far away has reached them on paper.

When cheers and burning straw from soldiers who recognize the Emperor wake the staff, the immediate experience is nearby sound and light. It is an act of confidence in their commander, but a staff officer waking up does not thereby know the whole army's feelings or the enemy's response. Introduce the anecdote through Lejeune's later memoir.

The same night Friant's troops reach Raygern and have farther to march. The people who must perform Davout's task are not everyone on a nominal table of organization. Their need for rest conflicts with the need to hold the south the next day. Leave the audience with both the force of men calling the Emperor's name and the bodies wearing down to answer that name. Do not make the torches cause the Allied plan or calculate loyalty from their number.

Among the Allies, Weyrother reads out the dispositions for attack. An order having been read is different from every commander receiving it and being able to move his troops in time. Establish delayed transmission and the burden of roads shared by several columns before explaining the next day's confusion, so these are not first offered as excuses after defeat. Do not invent an unrecorded speech of opposition or final resolution for Kutuzov.

Just before battle on December 2, Lannes again asks Soult about the unanswered challenge. Soult replies that there are more important matters that day, and Lannes answers with an insult. Preserve the meaning of the three utterances as a reconstruction from a memoir. The quarrel does not close in restored friendship; it stops as the two men part for their respective duties. Fighting may already have begun in the south. The awaited outcome of the challenge is not a sword fight, but two marshals with unresolved feelings having to fight in the same army.
