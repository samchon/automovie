# A Name to Continue

## The river divides the army {#aspern}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/025-the-danube-and-lannes.md#aspern) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/006-name.md#aspern)

Aspern and Essling, May 21–22, 1809. Across the river French troops defend two villages. Archduke Charles looks down at a map with a pale face, then continues the attack. Show the army receiving his orders and the center defended by French troops from different perspectives.

CHARLES: Keep pressure on both villages.

While troops are tied to village defense, the French central attack is prepared. Empty hands return to guns waiting for ammunition. A person running from the river reports the large bridge broken. Napoleon stops speaking and looks at the river.

NAPOLEON: Stop the central attack. Turn the men to hold this side.

Advancing ranks change direction. Before those waiting for a connection to the rear, the broken waterway appears. Next dawn French troops withdraw. Between sounds of men crossing, guns near the two villages sound separately.

## A body that does not recover while they wait {#lannes}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/025-the-danube-and-lannes.md#lannes) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/006-name.md#lannes)

Lannes, wounded May 22. His broad chest heaves and a breath about to speak becomes short. His face contorts as the stretcher comes down. A surgeon prepares hands and instruments. His body is screened by people; while amputation proceeds, stay long on his face and hands holding him.

After treatment. Another day's light enters the same place. A waiting person looks at Lannes, and Lannes moves his eyes to recognize them. His jaw relaxes from bearing pain, then tightens again. The caregiver lifts a hand, then resumes what can be done.

HISTORIAN (voice): Lannes died on May 31.

Napoleon remains beside the dead man. Do not return his face to a scene of reconciliation with Soult in 1805. The insult that had no answer while he lived remains. Where Lannes's deep voice is gone, only moving cloth is heard.

## Authority outside victory {#wagram-and-church}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/025-the-danube-and-lannes.md#wagram) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/025-the-danube-and-lannes.md#church-and-force) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/006-name.md#wagram-and-church)

Night of July 4. The French cross east of Lobau. Follow the connection through darkness, then go to the attack on the 5th. Though it presses, the enemy does not collapse. No victory arrives before daylight ends.

The 6th. Austrian pressure grows. French guns concentrate; between shots see bodies handling them again. Napoleon sends an order toward Davout.

NAPOLEON: Strike the flank. It must work with MacDonald's advance.

Davout's flank attack and MacDonald's advance press the enemy from different directions. The road where Charles is waiting remains empty.

CHARLES: John's reinforcements still haven't come?

MESSENGER: They have not arrived.

Charles turns from the empty road to his own army holding now.

CHARLES: Withdraw the remaining army.

The order travels, and Austrians withdraw preserving their remaining forces.

After victory Napoleon sees returning commanders. Stay briefly among them where Lannes is absent. No one calls his name. Transition to another space of papal authority.

Another space in 1809, proceeding alongside the battle. Open the imperial annexation document before the historian speaks.

HISTORIAN (voice): The Emperor sought control of territory and blockade. The papacy held temporal territory, neutrality, and religious authority.

After annexation, allow time to read the essence of the excommunication document: “Those who violated temporal sovereignty.” Do not turn it into a document explicitly naming Napoleon. The arrest in July comes in a separate image. Detaining and transporting a body does not lead to a scene granting religious consent.

HISTORIAN (voice): Whether there was a direct arrest order from the Emperor remains unresolved.

Show no newly named commander of the arrest or private papal speech in a meeting.

## A husband's dynastic reason {#divorce}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/026-two-marriages.md#divorce) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/006-name.md#divorce)

Late 1809. Napoleon and Joséphine sit together. A husband who speaks quickly chooses his sentence this time.

NAPOLEON: There must be an heir. If this regime is to stand after I am gone, we must decide who succeeds me.

JOSÉPHINE: So I cannot stay beside you.

NAPOLEON: Your livelihood will be secured.

JOSÉPHINE: That is not what I am asking.

He starts to speak again, then stops at her face looking at him. Give time to both sitting in the same space before moving to procedure. Separate the public divorce procedure, later annulment of the church marriage, and move by changes in clothes, light, and place.

Screen text at Eugène's face: “Eugène — Joséphine's son.”

Eugène helps his mother with what she needs. When business in Italy is conveyed to him, he cannot answer immediately and looks at Joséphine.

JOSÉPHINE: You have your own work too.

EUGÈNE: Yes. I will go.

After he leaves Joséphine sits again. Do not present the size of the life left to her as a number of guaranteed funds.

## The new family's present {#marriage-and-heir}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/026-two-marriages.md#remarriage) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/027-heir-and-hunger.md#heir) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/006-name.md#marriage-and-heir)

1810. A distant view of Metternich, responsible for Austrian diplomacy, doing work needed for the marriage. Then move closer to eighteen-year-old Marie Louise in French ceremony.

HISTORIAN (voice): Austria's security and Metternich's diplomacy were at stake in this marriage.

Marie Louise starts to turn following an unfamiliar sequence, sees the person ahead stop, and draws back her foot. Napoleon, walking beside her, waits too.

MARIE LOUISE: Do we stop here a moment?

NAPOLEON: Yes. We stay here for now.

As the procession ahead moves, they move together. Outside ceremony, the husband slows enough for his wife to follow. She reaches his side and both go in the same direction.

After childbirth in 1811. From the nineteen-year-old Marie Louise's resting height, see Napoleon already holding the baby beside her. His hands support the head and body at the lower edge of the view. She stays awake watching them. The child stirs; Napoleon bends close and smiles, keeping his voice low.

NAPOLEON: Rest. I have him.

Marie Louise watches a little longer, then closes her eyes. Keep her face in the foreground as the father's quieter movement and the baby's breathing continue beside her. The distant celebration remains farther away than these sounds. Screen text gives the child the title “King of Rome,” then disappears while the mother rests and the father remains with their child.

## A country demanding more {#grain}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/027-heir-and-hunger.md#grain-and-conscription) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/006-name.md#grain)

At a grain collection point, hands trying to meet a demand reach toward grain to be kept. Someone hesitates between the household table and an army sack.

Two other households before conscription. One raises money for substitute service; in the other, hearing the same words, no money can be put down.

FAMILY MEMBER: We don't have that much.

The person leaving looks at someone nearby and draws their own pack close. Doors of the paying household and departing household close at different speeds.

Move to a space conveying the blockade's burden in Russia. French demands put more pressure on local life and trade.

HISTORIAN (voice): The blockade's cost made Russia's own interests harder to reconcile with France's demands.

News travels by messenger to Napoleon. He hears the reporter's voice in his own space.

MESSENGER: Russia is not following the blockade. It is moving away from the alliance too.

Napoleon reads the document received and opens the map nearby again.

NAPOLEON: We cannot leave it so.

Outside the map, a person to be conscripted moves away from family. The main work ends after that household door closes.

A separate “Author's Commentary” screen.

AUTHOR (voice): Between Lannes's wound and death was time spent treating him and waiting. We did not newly settle disputed matters such as his last words or which leg was amputated. Nor did the divorce and new marriage procedures end in one night. The words and actions of caring for the child are reconstructed. We gave both parents the child of that moment, when they did not yet know the future. Behind the dynasty's name we placed families who actually stayed and left.
