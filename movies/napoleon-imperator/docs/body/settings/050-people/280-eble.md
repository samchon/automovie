## Éblé {#eble}

An engineering commander who makes an actual crossing possible. Bridge achievement depends on tools, time, and sacrifice by people entering water; preserve memoir attribution of famous rescue words and actions.

Basis: relevant statements and work decisions in the [retained individual record](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/070-eble.md#eble).

Performance adoption: The entire appearance and voice paragraph beginning “Work decision (appearance and voice)” in the [relevant H2 of the individual source](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/070-eble.md#eble) is directly adopted. Its body, face, hair, clothes, carried objects, voice, pace, emotional changes, and source or invented status are active performance values. Age follows the birth date and variants in the same H2, subject to these limits: Crossing in part 5. He is fifty-three at the Berezina in 1812. His birthday is December 21, so distinguish his age at the battle from death at the end of that year. Do not adopt the unsupported number nine survivors out of 400.
