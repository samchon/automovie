## Metternich {#metternich}

He holds his country’s security, balance, and mediation as leverage. Words at a private meeting are his later account, not a transcript.

Basis: relevant statements and work decisions in the [retained individual record](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/136-metternich.md#metternich).

Performance adoption: The entire appearance and voice paragraph beginning “Work decision (appearance and voice)” in the [relevant H2 of the individual source](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/136-metternich.md#metternich) is directly adopted. Its body, face, hair, clothes, carried objects, voice, pace, emotional changes, and source or invented status are active performance values. Age follows the birth date and variants in the same H2, subject to these limits: Diplomacy in parts 4 and 6. Distinguish thirty-three in Paris in 1806 and forty in Dresden in 1813. Do not swap the blue diplomatic dress of 1806 and black clothing with one star in 1813.
