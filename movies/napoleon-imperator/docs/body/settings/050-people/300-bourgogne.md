## Bourgogne {#bourgogne}

His scope is war seen by a noncommissioned officer and memory written later. He did not see every front or everyone’s thoughts there; do not combine another memoirist’s experience with his.

Basis: relevant statements and work decisions in the [retained individual record](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/270-bourgogne.md#bourgogne).

Performance adoption: The entire appearance and voice paragraph beginning “Appearance and voice are decisions of the work” in the [relevant H2 of the individual source](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/270-bourgogne.md#bourgogne) is directly adopted. Its body, face, hair, clothes, carried objects, voice, pace, emotional changes, and source or invented status are active performance values. Age follows the birth date and variants in the same H2, subject to these limits: Soldier’s perspective in part 5. He is twenty-six in summer 1812 and twenty-seven after November 12. Distinguish retreat beard and rags from dragging his step when tired after a right-thigh wound in 1809. In 1813 he is a second lieutenant in the 145th Line; do not turn him into a Leipzig prisoner.
