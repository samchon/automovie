## Joséphine {#josephine}

Security of marriage, her children’s places, social influence, taste and debts, love and fear coexist. Her closed-lipped smile is a social buffer; intimacy and letters retain distance. Follow [relationship registers](010-ensemble.md#registers) for limits on letter speech and the unpreserved reply.
