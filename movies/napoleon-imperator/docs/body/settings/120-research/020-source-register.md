## Connecting source values to current owners {#adoption}

The substantive bodies and limitations of 707 retained settings files are preserved as conditional references for this production. The [source-by-source ownership and adoption table](../../../analysis/source-owner-map.md) records the original factual owner, the owner of facts actually expressed in the current body, and the conditional adoption entry in separate columns. A source H2 name linked to some facts in the current body does not mean every attribute in that source is activated or newly verified. Records with an empty active-owner column are not counted as independent factual units of current settings.

This register is an entry for classification and adoption, not the factual owner that decides or changes an individual's voice or troop strength on a day. Correct facts expressed in the current body at the actual responsible section designated in the table. If a later layer needs a conditional record, read the relevant original H2, establish the necessary values, conditions, source status, and unresolved matters in an active H2 of the actual subject, then link the table. Direct adoption by reference is permitted, but a source address or general entry alone cannot replace the facts and limits to be used. The new unit must pass settings evidence and independent review. This is not a procedure for arbitrarily changing retained source facts or forcing unused characters to appear. Explicit restrictions by which current canon narrowly adopts a conflict take precedence in relevant use.

The source's 14 volumes, episodes, character counts, and purchase boundary are replaced by [medium transposition](../../../instructions/001-adaptation.md#medium-transposition). Source treatments, scripts, and prose addresses are historical ownership guidance and do not supply this production's events or dialogue. Exclude HTML comments, evidence answers, “actual answers for the current population,” benchmarks, approval and review links, and completion claims from adopted body text. This production's factual foundations are the new canon and retained factual records, not historical approval language. When relative links point to support outside the source, do not claim the original was newly read.

## Government and society by year {#government-and-society}

Of 63 `030-society` files and nonmilitary state institutions in `060-institutions`, facts on law, taxation, Church, schools, court, family kingdoms, slavery, governments, occupation, science, and communication actually expressed in current [power and cost](../030-society/010-power-and-cost.md) belong to the relevant subject sections. The table links the smallest source H2 actually used, not a file overview. When several current sections use values from one source, distinguish responsible sections and scopes. An active section linked in the table owns the facts it expresses, not the entire original as one active unit. Distinguish a law's date, publication, enactment, and actual implementation; do not make a universal imperial decree apply in occupied lands on the same day. An incorrect character number in an old source link does not become new support for a law or title. Schools, budgets, conscription, and occupation administration after 1815 are not a mandate to reopen the whole history, nor do they erase recorded later conditions.

## People and relationships between two people {#people-and-relationships}

The 306 `050-people` and 21 `070-relationships` files are conditional materials with individual source owners. Actual person and relationship H2s in [character canon](../050-people/010-ensemble.md) own values and limits expressed there. Selected people actively adopt exact original performance passages and yearly restrictions specified by their individual H2s. The character file distinguishes limited facts for eight candidates and reference recorders from performances not yet selected. Find remaining records with empty active owners through this register and the source; do not make this register or a shared character boundary section the active factual owner for that person. The [conditional character records](../050-people/010-ensemble.md#conditional-records) set selection and activation boundaries. Bad assessments, affairs, and diagnoses in memoirs retain that status; people present before 1840 do not know in advance a death date occurring later. Even records not performed retain results and limits affecting later states.

## Units and campaign states by date {#armies-and-battles}

Military records in `060-institutions` for corps, divisions, armies, and fleets, and 132 `100-history` files preserve dated organization, orders, arrivals, losses, terrain, and unresolved matters in individual source H2s. [Life and memory](../100-history/020-life-and-memory.md) and [the 1805 battlefield](../100-history/010-austerlitz.md) own historical and military states actually established in their bodies. Military records without active owners in the table are conditional material not yet authored as independent active units, not strength tables owned by this register.

When selecting a new battle or unit state, read together both sides' corps, divisions, brigades and commanders, order and report routes, arriving advance elements, main bodies, artillery and baggage, wounded, dead, prisoners and stragglers, and denominators for dates and numbers in original H2s. Establish the state actually needed in the subject H2 for that date, campaign, and unit, make the scope and unresolved matters adopted by direct reference findable there, and then link the table's active owner. Do not collect addresses in one general military-state section and claim all military facts received evidence as one unit. Confirm reorganization when the date changes even for a column of the same number or unit of the same name. Do not fill gaps with regiments, brigades, personal routes, or shell counts absent from the record. Conscription and military reforms follow the actual responsible social sections designated in the source-by-source table.

## Actual appearance of places and objects {#places-and-objects}

Buildings, roads, battlefields, camps, uniforms, standards, thrones, ships, and paintings in 35 `080-places` and nine `090-objects` files are conditional material. Facts of appearance and period actually expressed in the current [material world](../020-world/010-material-world.md#appearance) belong to that section. If new values are needed to realize other spaces or objects, establish scope in an independent active H2 for that subject. Do not retroactively use existing buildings or later paintings as completion, color, furniture, or actual attendance for the year concerned. Source-selected appearances such as the identification of the Pozořice building, Victory's dark hull and pale ochre gunport stripe, and a dark night carry forward as choices of the work. Unverified dimensions or seating are unknown; if needed for background production, mark them as choices that do not change historical results.
