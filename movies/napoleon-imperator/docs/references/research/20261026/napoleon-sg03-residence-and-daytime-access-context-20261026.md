This supplements, and does not rewrite, the original SG03 note. It supports the upcoming complete parent-repair review, not a new adopted event or a standalone correction request.

The [Venice State Archives' exhibition entry](https://1600anni.archiviodistatovenezia.it/mostra-documentaria/percorso-mostra/18-mostra/comunita-e-attivita-di-forestieru-a-venezia/101-gli-ebrei-a-venezia-e-l-istituzione-del-ghetto.html) identifies the Senate register, date and folio for the 1516 founding measure. Its transcribed passage and institutional explanation concern compelled residence in the designated quarter; the entry states that the ghetto provisions continued until the Republic ended in 1797. The complete short entry, including its surrounding earlier history and limits, was read. The physical register and its complete original act were not inspected. Earlier clothing distinctions and temporary-stay periods on the page do not become new 1797 costume or enforcement canon.

The already-read [Jewish Community account](https://jvenice.org/en/life-in-the-ghetto/) describes regular commercial and intellectual exchange and doctors working inside and outside the ghetto, with special permission for night outings, before discussing the 1797 gate removal. Consequently, an ordinary daytime walk outside cannot by itself establish a newly gained permission to leave: the earlier sources do not describe a universal ban on daytime exit. A scene can make the actual changed restriction legible without inventing such a ban. A resident's hesitation is possible authored behavior, but its cause cannot be falsely attributed to a researched general daytime prohibition.

The original SG03 option's temporary end of segregation and Austrian-return qualification remain. The exact new 1797 order and execution dates are not needed. The note adds no subplot or numerical requirement.
