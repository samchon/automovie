The initial readings below precede inspection of treatments, creative requirements and contracts.

The work makes command a problem of moving bodies, resources and information. The opening victory does not promise an uninterrupted ascent: the empty place beside a bowl already carries the cost. I can follow the backward jump to childhood, the November 27 return to the opening's eve, and the December 3 continuation. The ending replaces the mobile emperor with a still coffin and differentiated companions' work. Short commands, practical answers and moments of dry soldier humor often play cleanly; I do not require them to be ornamental or long.

Initial concerns to adjudicate after parent comparison: (C1) repeated hand, document, road, waiting and empty-place forms might flatten different pressures across the complete population; (C2) Tilsit repeatedly states Russia's agency rather than gaining new bargaining information; (C3) the legal deliberation, Nile battle and 1814 campaign expand substantially while other important battles remain montages, so distribution and parent authorization need checking; (C4) scripts' occasional broad future summaries may leave actual staging to the next writer; (C5) inherited bodily details, ages, dates, forms of address and report certainty require comparison with settings and actual treatments. These are candidates, not findings. The reception did not establish a factual error by itself.

Protected functions: the soldier joke about cleanliness preserves pride together with irreverence; the duel's absence of reconciliation protects an unfinished relationship; Joséphine's answer about livelihood rejects a practical substitute for intimacy; present tenderness with the baby prevents future tragedy from erasing a lived joy; delayed orders preserve recipients' information limits; Marchand's completion and Bertrand's remaining presence must retain distinct work. Exact supporting quotations appear in the unit rows, with additional tests below.
