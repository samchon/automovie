# Kickboxing

Deliver a complete 180-second direct 3D film for [issue 3040](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/issues/3040), with runnable source, a working browser Viewer, actual playback review and a fully decoded final video. The film covers the beginning of a scheduled three-minute round, its knockout and the aftermath through exactly 180 seconds.

Use the compact [individual settings](docs/body/settings) followed by [one continuous screenplay scene](docs/body/screenplay/kickboxing.md), with meaningful H3 action groups for fifteen-second review intervals. [BoutScript](src/shots/kickboxing/ring-bout/BoutScript.ts) transcribes every screenplay exchange as executable data (contact second, attacker, technique, answer); [FilmPlan](src/shots/kickboxing/ring-bout/FilmPlan.ts) owns the film clock, event vocabulary and camera. Motion is authored as clinical joint channels and world limb goals through the movie's [rig adapter](src/motion/rig/FilmBodyRig.ts), which delegates clinical fitting to Human. Footsteps follow the moving stance with a live rhythm of feints, slips, weaves and guard changes between exchanges. [Prepared targets](src/physics/BoutTargetSource.ts) bind actual provider shapes through shared support and contact fitting, and the shared [motion audit adapter](src/physics/auditBoutMotion.ts) measures foot support, limb goals, speed, interpenetration, canvas penetration and knee lock.

The operational default [authored comparison](src/runtime/FilmRuntime.ts) remains a staged migration under issue 3085. Its existing IK/floor adapter and geometric outcome report do not qualify the prepared-target path or native dynamics. Replace that consumer only after the common kinematic target/cache path passes the original geometry checks and a bounded actual cost check. Kinematic comparison body acting uses explicit geometric impulse estimates; simulated bodies receive no formula pose offset. Defense owns hand protection in either mode. The [response recipe](src/motion/reactions/BoutFormulaResponse.ts) changes only body/head clinical flexion and facial acting, before fitting, and never changes a solver impulse or a displayed-only bone transform.

Both fighters are highly trained adult men, 180 cm and 80 kg. A wears red; B wears cyan and navy. They step into a lead-glove touch, then fight immediately through changing initiative, range and angles. A's left high roundhouse reaches B's right lateral head or jaw at 150.000 seconds. A stops attacking, clears B and reaches the farthest neutral corner through supported steps. Once A is inside and watching ring center, the count opens at 157: One at 158 through Ten at 167. B fails to stand. The referee signals knockout at 167.6; A celebrates from 168.1 through 180 while keeping clear of B.

The visible referee is an adult man with an independent body, voice and support. His start, knockdown, corner, count and result performances must appear and sound in the film. The fall uses connected knee, pelvis, trunk, shoulder and head motion, followed by body-supported recovery attempts. No attack follows the knockdown, and no medical mechanism is asserted.

The film uses only the original Human polygon figure. The owner explicitly excludes photorealistic Human rendering from this campaign. Reusable anatomy, typed rigging, Viewer and Render capability belong to their packages; kickboxing choreography and production-specific tests and recorded comparisons belong to this production.

The polygon Viewer shows actual rig points and parent-child sticks by default. Its visible toggle and `?rig=0` hide the guides without changing pose or timing. Physical attacks bind their weapons to the opponent's actual head, trunk, guard or checking-shin collider through shared Engine tracking. Moving an opponent therefore changes the target through that relationship; coordinate tuning cannot substitute for a missing solver contact.

During every round kick, extend the kicking-side hand forward at the protective head line and keep the opposite hand guarding the head. Maintain that protection through chamber, delivery, recoil and replant on either side. The [kick-guard research decision](docs/research/kick-guard.md) records the coaching sources, meaningful variants and the owner's adopted choice. A downward/backward balancing-arm animation does not satisfy this film's performance.

Develop and review reusable actions before assembling their sequence. Test pure timing, ownership, continuation and interaction logic, inspect actual displayed geometry through public APIs, then review the film in successive fifteen-second intervals. Complete normal-speed playback, slow exchange/KO review, listening and whole-file decoding establish delivery. Source checks, screenshots and authored timings establish their narrower scopes. Everything stored in this repository is English.
