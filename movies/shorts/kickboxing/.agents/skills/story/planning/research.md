# Research

Study everything the film must show truthfully before the screenplay decides it or code programs it: each practice, the conduct of officials and crowds, and how the genre is actually shot and heard. The study answers the [research principles](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/research.md); settings, screenplay beats and every [behavior](../../motion/SKILL.md) cite it.

## Performance study {#performance-study}

Study real practice at the depicted level, such as a sport, fight, dance, craft or procedure, an everyday handling, gait or reach, and the broadcast or film practice of shooting it. An invented action studies its closest real analogue and states where it departs. Record what code and screenplay need without inventing anything: each technique's law (its shape prior in the performer's own body frame, timing windows, reach, contact surfaces and what the rules allow), the outcome table of which intent combinations can yield which outcome classes, the observed patterns of top-level performance such as phrasing, spatial strategy and how officials or bystanders move, and measurable targets a composed scene must meet, such as actions per minute, phrase length, lateral versus linear movement, idle time per presence and shot-change cadence.

## Sources

Use originals: rulebooks, coaching and training texts, measured studies, analyses of real top-level performances and productions, official documentation and footage of qualified practitioners; search results only locate them. Read each passage in context and watch footage at normal speed through its connected phases. Keep each value with its unit, method and conditions, cross-check disputed claims, and record what stays unavailable.

Save permitted originals in `references/`, rights-unconfirmed ones in ignored `references/private/` and footage in ignored `media/acquired/videos/`, then open the saved files. Keep one `docs/research/index.md` naming each original's creator, URL, retrieval date, locator, rights and use, and write each study in `docs/research/<discipline>/<topic>.md`. A URL or an empty index is not a retained source.

## Use

Test the adopted values in the real entity, behavior or sound and watch the result. The study informs the work; [review](../../review/SKILL.md) judges what it produced.
