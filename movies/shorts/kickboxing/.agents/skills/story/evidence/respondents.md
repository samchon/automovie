# Place the answer with its respondent

Construction answers remain detached from creative bodies. Put positive H2 answers in `docs/evidence/construction/units/<layer>/...md` and file answers in `docs/evidence/construction/files/<layer>/...md`. The document's H1 identifies the exact body file or file-and-anchor address, followed by Evidence headings and supporting prose. Read installed `@wrtnlabs/evidence` documentation for native address and annotation syntax.

Detached documents do not support exclusion or exclusion-review rows. Put a permitted exclusion in the actual host unit's HTML comment, or use the designated discovery carrier for a discovery negative. State the inspected scope and exact inapplicability; a population exclusion requires the entire population to be inapplicable.

Script expression answers belong to its detached construction units and population files. Screenplay naturalness answers stay in HTML comments attached to their working unit in `docs/naturalness/screenplay`. Place H2-owned answers directly beneath their H2, before its subordinate H3 headings, and a file answer before the file's first heading. Keep them with that edition when reviewed text is transferred; [rewriting procedure](../naturalness/procedure.md#transfer) owns the comment-free transfer and [upstream repair](../correction/repair.md) its closed-proof boundary.

Review records and their fingerprints follow [review rounds](../../review/rounds.md). A work- or language-specific question is adopted through [adoption](../../evidence/adoption.md) in its family under `contracts/story/{work,language}`; a unit answers for its own behavior and a designated population respondent for a whole-work duty.
