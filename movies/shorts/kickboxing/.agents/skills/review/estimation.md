# Experience estimation

Assess the requested work against its declared audience and promise. Read complete current bodies and requested frozen editions through independent recipient judgments, and return reactions, provisional scores, disagreement, and edition comparisons using the [assessment method](estimation-method.md).

Keep this read-only: do not edit files, investigate external facts, prescribe repairs, or change stages, evidence, or reviews. Gate status, author identity, and generation method do not establish the audience's experience. Do not feed these scores to an approval reviewer or use them to claim a human judgment, an approved pass, or a finished film.

Distinguish a screenplay assessment, a commissioned motion-hint assessment and an observation of a finished film. Judge each against its declared purpose; a hint is not proof of an uncommissioned post-diffusion result. If media is in scope, inspect the actual complete artifact and identify its edition; text alone cannot establish its audiovisual result. Report unavailable judges, unread material, or unobserved media rather than invent a score.
