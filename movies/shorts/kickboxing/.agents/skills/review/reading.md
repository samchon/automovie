# Read before auditing

Use a fresh context of the author's model for the same pass and scope, without the author's creative work memory. Give it the task, declared recipient and annotation-free body before parents, contracts, author evidence or earlier verdicts. If that context is unavailable, report the missing review. Read the complete declared scope and lock the first response, supported effects and concrete losses of understanding, intent, tension, performance or continuity under the [independent-reading questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading). Divide a large scope into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling.

After locking that reading, inventory the actual body's facts, quantities, chronology, names, titles, address, notation, recurring expression, quotations and consequential action, image, sound and continuity values. Read the selected creative contracts, actual parents and adopted requirements, and compare the inventory with them. Before author defenses or earlier verdicts, lock the [independent classification](../screenwriting/inspection.md) in an actually isolated worker with only its job and required input paths, within that guide's authorization boundary. Contract answers and matching quotations cannot replace the body-derived inventory.

For settings and treatments, independently compare externally checkable facts and causal claims with the original source's whole relevant sequence and context. A matching word or number cannot establish an inference or comparison. Scripts and screenplay inherit facts through actual parents. For a defective parent value or design, issue a finding identifying the earliest owner and what must become true there; the construction author repairs that owner under [upstream repair](../screenwriting/repair.md#upstream-defects). An expression pass reports it without changing its frozen content. A local downstream workaround cannot correct what the next unit inherits.

Read settings as the treatment writer and production designer, treatments as the script writer, scripts as the final screenplay writer and performer, and screenplay as filmmaker and audience. Weak final realization is not excused by a less expressive parent. A recipient's concrete loss remains a finding when upstream knowledge merely explains the missing experience. Apply the selected contract's decision and exception rule to named failures and the [retention test](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) to other candidates. Resolve factual objections against the passage, definition and actual source under [rounds](rounds.md); author intent or a function label cannot overturn the locked judgment. Keep comparison alternatives in ignored working notes, not prose for adoption.

For screenplay expression, lock the voice and continuous experience made by actual speech, performance, image, sound and timing before the detailed audit. Judge [audiovisual voice](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice) across the whole unit after local repairs as well as checking named failures. Fidelity, a function list and negative sentence classifications cannot answer that overall question; cite actual expression and context for the audience loss or the legitimate form it realizes. Screenplay reading judges the specified experience, while [video review](video.md) must observe its actual realization.

Review scripts content and expression together: actual exchanges, executable instructions, adjacent action and replies, and inherited limits all belong to construction. Scene time/place/weather, useful character parentheticals, unnamed-person position markers and first-entry appearance are execution notation, not automatic expression failures. Check treatment prescriptions under its [completion question](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md#decided-event) rather than broadening that question from a review guide.

Complete creative correction before the evidence audit under [rounds](rounds.md). During that audit, compare each author assertion with the entire respondent, actual parents and sources; search the whole defined scope for counterexamples to negatives and completeness claims. A truthful answer and a usable scene remain separate achievements.
