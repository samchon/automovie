# Evidence for source, assets and delivery

Use this method for engineering declarations, tests, assets and output reports. Creative construction and expression answers retain their own [narrative evidence method](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md); an asset or host repair needs no fictional body, frozen manuscript or narrative pass.

## Answer the actual respondent

Read the selected question and the complete declaration, asset or output it judges, including actual callers, inputs and consumers needed to test the answer. Use [adoption](adoption.md) to resolve a missing requirement or dishonest respondent before changing a claim. Installed Evidence documentation owns annotation syntax, selectors, addresses and supported respondent forms.

Engineering declarations answer in their doc comments, and exported test functions answer the testing questions. Asset builders can establish construction and controls; scoped observation reports establish what was actually rendered or heard; delivery reports establish the exact assembled artifact and handoff. A source function or its citation cannot answer for unobserved playback. [Evidence configuration](configuration.md) selects those actual respondents while retaining the engineering baseline and each respondent's actual responsibility.

State assumptions, scope, exact inputs, observed behavior and remaining limits with traceable owner addresses or reports. Verify quotations, quantities, timing and identities across the complete respondent; one example cannot prove an entire population or film. Distinguish computation, moving or static observation, listening and unavailable checks. Describe the actual work and repairs rather than declaring their effectiveness; review owns acceptance. Inapplicability needs its concrete reason and inspected scope through a permitted native mechanism; missing output or failure is not inapplicability.

## Correct and verify together

Finish the whole inspection round, group failures by cause, and repair responsible source through [development](../development/SKILL.md), assets through [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md), sound through [audio](../audio/SKILL.md), or assembled media through [finishing](../production/finishing.md). Correct dependent answers and reports in the same repair, keeping historical results attributable to the inputs they observed. Evidence explains corrected work or a remaining limit; it cannot argue an absent implementation into existence.

Run applicable source checks and inspect the actual asset or artifact, then recheck each answer against its complete current respondent. Read unfiltered diagnostics and preserve failure statuses. Hand source and evidence to [review](../review/SKILL.md), and commissioned output to [delivery review](../review/delivery.md). Mechanical coverage proves selection and recorded answers, not their truth or artistic acceptance.
