# Assets

## Source and control

Can the production identify and reproduce the actual asset it uses, with the required usage rights, registration, and controls? Record fixed source identity and acquisition provenance where applicable. Explain how authored parameters, recovered coefficients, imported rigging, or direct placement meet the required variation and motion, including any unsupported behavior. Distinguish source-DCC evaluation from channels and deformation actually retained by the adopted conversion and runtime, with matched source/import observations for required controls. Enough control serves the promised use; unused rig complexity does not establish fitness, and omitted necessary deformation remains a defect.

For generated assets, can the recipient trace the exact submitted request to its settings, screenplay, brief or other actual sources and their revisions, then through input references and attempts to the exact used bytes? Apply [generation records](../production/generation-records.md): identify deliberate production choices, transformations, unknown historical fields, current consumer and retained availability. Git exclusion or a prompt/seed does not establish restoration of the accepted asset.

## Observed fitness

Has the current assembled result been inspected at its intended screen scale, views, and motion states? Link observations to the exact inputs and output, distinguishing geometric or numerical checks from visible quality. Show generating construction when editing authored shape and matched source comparisons when recovering it. Account for joins, contact, deformation, materials, and the production budget where relevant; report missing observations.
