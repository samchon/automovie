# Upstream repair exposed by scripts

Each script H2 answers about its actual treatment parents and, through them, settings causes. Repair follows the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).

## What did scene execution prove missing or unusable upstream? {#script-upstream-repair}

Identify treatment decisions that are missing, contradictory, overdetermined or impossible to execute, including designs that force a defined downstream failure or block an adopted goal or promise. Distinguish inherited constraints and continuity records from prescriptions that consume the scene's realization. Trace a settings cause to its exact owner rather than bypassing the treatment with direct settings evidence. Correct the earliest owner's body locally before writing from the repair, naming the owner, defect and corrected passage; covering it downstream leaves the cause in place. If no repair is needed, identify actual parent decisions executed unchanged and the concrete tests they survived. A generic assurance cannot establish a sound parent, and missing events or facts must be decided at their owner.
