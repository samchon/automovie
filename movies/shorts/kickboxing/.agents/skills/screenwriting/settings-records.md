# Organize settings records

Use this guide to organize subject records or separate an overloaded collection. [Settings](settings.md) owns research and construction; the [settings questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/settings.md) and [population obligations](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/settings.md) judge completeness and truth.

## Domains and subjects

Give each independently used subject one file, with inseparable attributes kept together. Group files into the domains the work actually needs, using [production layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership). A compact short needs no domain tree or outline. A cast list or other finding guide links to canonical records rather than repeats their facts.

A group may share a file while it functions as one subject; give a member its own owner when individual speech, choice, reaction or relationship requires it. Incidental participants need their consequential role and recognizable attributes; central or recurring subjects need the depth their actual use demands.

## Character sections

Give the file one H1 naming its subject and stable, explicitly anchored H2s for independently cited decisions. Write the identity, appearance and voice, drives and limits, relationships, knowledge and consequential state variants the work needs. A short complete profile may need one H2. Attach sources, adopted inventions and uncertainty to the facts they qualify.

A shared relationship has one owner that records both sides, with links from other participants. Distinct personal interpretations may have their own owners. Shared history stays with its world or chronology owner; actual changes, revelations and consequences belong to treatments or the compact short's screenplay.

## Places, buildings, backgrounds and objects

Give independently used places and objects their own records. Establish recognizable form and material, usable spatial organization and routes, meaningful dimensions, light, sound and state variants. A room or outdoor zone can remain part of its containing place or become an independent subject when separate design or reuse warrants it. Shared background grammar can cover incidental surroundings.

Settings supplies world facts to asset, motion and sound authors. Executable source owns generation parameters and rendering controls; link their realization to the actual facts and screenplay use through [execution traceability](../evidence/execution.md). Implementation choices become canon only through an adopted world decision.

## Change existing records safely

Before splitting or moving records, map old file/H2 addresses to their new owners and preserve each decision's meaning, limits and source adoption. Update links, finding guides and actual consumers together under [layout ownership](../production/layout.md#project-ownership-and-customization). Keep address migration distinct from content rewriting so callers still reach the decision they cite.

Preserve revision identity through [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership) and apply the [closed-pass boundary](repair.md#closed-passes). A new path or matching hash does not renew approval.
