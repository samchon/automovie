They step into an actual glove touch and immediately exchange expert combinations, defenses, evasions, counters and varied footwork. The visible referee is an adult man. No downed attack or asserted carotid mechanism.

## Direction {#direction}

Keep one continuous English H2 ring scene, with meaningful H3 action/exchange groups, short present-tense action and separate speaker/dialogue blocks. This heading hierarchy is the adopted repository format; it does not introduce new scenes or time jumps. Change pressure, angle and initiative across the round; do not loop the first minute. Body and glancing contacts interrupt breath and guard without early knockdowns. Body attacks establish the low defensive response; the final left body hook draws the right guard down before the connected left high kick. The consumed [FilmPlan](../../src/film/FilmPlan.ts) supplies precise action placements, camera and event timing; its runtime realizes the decided support, contact and recovery.

## Image and sound {#presentation}

Keep feet, targets and heads readable together on the same screen axis. Show the decisive kick and articulated fall without a cut. Keep the entire count in real time with A in the neutral corner and B down. Start and match-ending bells, English referee words, breaths, mat friction and distinct contacts carry the film. The winner's raised gloves, supported victory steps and excited breath remain visible beside the downed opponent in the wide ending. The referee is a visible participant with readable signals, gaze, expression and support. No replay, time compression or commentary.
