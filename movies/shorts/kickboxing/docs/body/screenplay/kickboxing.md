# Kickboxing

One three-minute round between two champions in an indoor ring at night, filmed as one continuous take: combinations, feints, checks and counters until A's right Brazilian kick drops B at 150 seconds, followed by the real-time ten-count, the knockout and A's celebration to 180 seconds. Each scene is the next fifteen-second phase of the same continuous action; times stated in the action are anchors of the film's clock. Between exchanges neither man stands still: they circle, cut the ring off, feint and probe at kicking distance, and every attack is thrown while closing that distance and ends on an exit or an angle off the line.
