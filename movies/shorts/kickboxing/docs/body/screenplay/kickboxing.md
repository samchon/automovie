# Kickboxing

One three-minute round between two champions in an indoor ring at night, filmed as one continuous take: combinations, feints, checks and counters until A's right Brazilian kick drops B at 150 seconds, followed by the real-time ten-count, the knockout and A's celebration to 180 seconds. Each scene is the next fifteen-second phase of the same continuous action; times stated in the action are anchors of the film's clock.
