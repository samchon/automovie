# Ring

## Shape and dimensions {#shape}

### Canvas and ropes {#canvas}

A fictional indoor ring with a six-metre square fighting area inside four ropes, on a 6.4-metre pale matte canvas. Padded posts stand outside the fighting area at the four corners. A dark apron separates the canvas from the surrounding darkness. A fighter standing at the ropes can touch them with an outstretched glove.

## Zones and landmarks {#zones}

### Centre {#centre}

The middle of the canvas, the ground both fighters want; taking it back is a beat of its own.

### Open ring {#open-ring}

The canvas around the centre, where the fighters circle and trade.

### Rope zone {#rope-zone}

A band along each rope as deep as a fighter's shoulder half-width and glove plus two short steps, measured from the rope line. A fighter inside it starts to feel trapped and circles out toward the side with more room.

### Fighters' corners {#corners}

The red corner belongs to A's team and the cyan corner, diagonally opposite, to B's. Fighters start the bout near centre, A on the red side and B on the cyan side.

### Neutral corners {#neutral-corners}

The two other corners are padded in a light colour. After a knockdown the referee sends the standing fighter to the neutral corner farther from the fallen man.

## Routes, entrances and levels {#routes}

### Inside the ropes {#inside}

Fighters and referee move freely on the one level of the canvas. Nobody enters or leaves the ring during the film.

## Dressing, lights and acoustics {#dressing}

### Light {#light}

Broad neutral overhead light and soft frontal fill separate the gloves, limbs and contact shadows; the audience beyond the apron stays dark.

### Acoustics {#acoustics}

A restrained indoor reverberation: close breaths, glove impacts, foot friction and the referee's voice carry; canvas landings sound duller than glove-on-glove contact.

## Use {#use}

### How the fighters use it {#fighters-use}

The fighters fight for the centre, circle in the open ring, cut each other off toward the ropes and escape along them. The referee works the perimeter across the fighters' line from their centre.

## State changes {#state}

### Blood on the canvas {#blood}

From the finish on, drops of B's blood mark the canvas beside his face and stay there to the end.
