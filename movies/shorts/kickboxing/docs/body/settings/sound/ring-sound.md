# Ring sound

## Ambience palette {#ambience}

### Room {#room}

A low arena crowd murmur under everything, dropping away whenever the referee speaks; the fighters' exhales on their kicks, foot friction on the canvas and the referee's voice carry over it.

### Contacts {#contacts}

Glove taps on gloves, the hard knock of shin on shin, the slap of shin on thigh or ribs, the thud of a sole on skin and duller canvas landings, each heard only when bodies actually meet.

### Bell {#bell}

A clear bell opens the round and rings the match-ending bell after the result.

## Music palette {#music}

### None {#no-music}

The film has no music.
