# The contest

## Rules {#rules}

### Ruleset {#ruleset}

A fictional professional kickboxing contest run under the principles of GLORY's v2.2 rules, as the [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md) records. It claims no accredited event.

### Weight {#weight}

Both fighters weigh 80 kg, inside the ruleset's middleweight range; the film claims no separate 80 kg division.

### Legal actions {#legal-actions}

Punches to the head and body, round kicks to the legs, body and head (including the switch kick and the Brazilian kick), the teep to the stomach and a single knee from the clinch are legal. Elbows, throws, sweeps, linear kicks to the legs and attacks on a downed fighter are illegal. A clinch holds only for one immediate knee and releases at once. A kick is blocked or checked, never caught.

### Round {#round}

The film shows one scheduled three-minute round from the opening bell. The bout can end early only by the referee's decision.

### Knockdown and count {#count}

After a knockdown the standing fighter goes to the farthest neutral corner and faces centre before the count begins; leaving the corner stops it. The referee counts one number per elapsed second and assesses readiness at eight. A fighter who has not regained standing balance and a ready guard at ten loses by knockout, and the match-ending bell follows the result.

## Time, weather and season {#time}

### Night indoors {#night}

The bout takes place at night in an indoor arena; weather and season do not reach the ring.

## Tone {#tone}

### Sober and physical {#sober}

The film plays the fight straight: two equals, real technique, no slow motion, replay, commentary or music. The finish is decisive but the film asserts no injury mechanism and shows no attack after the knockdown.
