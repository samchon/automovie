# Fighter A

## Identity {#identity}

A is a fictional 28-year-old adult man, 180 cm tall and 80 kg. Broad shoulders, a thick athletic trunk, developed arms and thighs, and proportionate hands and feet give him a trained fighter's silhouette. He wears red gloves, red mid-thigh shorts with a pale waistband, a mouthguard and wrist wraps. His chest and feet are bare. Warm medium skin distinguishes him from B even when the camera reverses their screen positions.

## Stance and technique {#stance}

A is a highly trained orthodox kickboxer: left foot forward, chin tucked, gloves beside the cheeks and elbows available to protect the ribs. His knees remain responsive as he loads, steps, evades and strikes. His stance stays wide enough for either leg to move without crossing the feet.

Foot pressure, pelvis and trunk rotation carry his punches. His lead hand can return while the rear hand attacks; each combination continues from its current guard, torso turn and support. For a roundhouse, he unloads the striking leg and turns on the opposite forefoot with the heel free. The knee leads before the lower leg extends into the target corridor, then folds and replants. Either leg can strike from his continuing orthodox stance. A left shin check leaves the right leg supporting him. These principles use the [retained technique research](../../research/technique.md#technique); the screenplay chooses their actual sequence and result.

## Conduct and sound {#limits}

A reads B's guard, changes range and counters during B's recovery. His short effort exhales accompany attacks; his breathing deepens as the round proceeds. A received blow moves his head, neck and trunk as connected parts. Each clean blow sinks into his skin and throws sweat off it; a clean face blow also draws blood from his nose or mouth. Struck skin reddens and stays marked for the rest of the bout. He obeys the referee, stops attacking on a knockdown and remains in the assigned neutral corner until the result. His celebration uses raised gloves, turning, quick supported steps and excited breath while leaving B clear.

## Fighting profile {#profile}

### Repertoire {#repertoire}

A fights as a Dutch-style pressure kicker. He prefers combinations that finish with a kick: jab, cross, hook and a right low kick; jab, cross, a hook to the body and the low kick; or jab, cross and a right kick to the body. He counters from his checking leg, from a slip outside the cross and by pivoting off the line. He keeps one rare weapon in reserve, the right Brazilian kick, thrown from the same chamber as his body kick. He enters the clinch only to throw a single knee and lets go at once.

### Habits and tells {#tells}

When B drags his rear foot and turns his hip as if to kick low, A lifts his lead leg to check without waiting to see the kick. B uses this against him with the low-kick feint. A pump jab at his eyes draws his right glove up to meet it.

After landing a combination, A steps out on an angle, pivoting on his lead foot, rather than backing straight out. When B gives ground straight back, A does not chase him: he cuts across toward the side B is circling to.

### Values and speech {#values}

A is patient and methodical. He builds his patterns on purpose and cashes them in late. He says nothing during the round. Short effort exhales and his breathing are his only sounds until the wordless shout of his celebration.
