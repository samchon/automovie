# Fighter A

## Identity {#identity}

### Who he is {#who}

A is a fictional 28-year-old professional kickboxer fighting for the win in a championship-level bout. The film never names him; the referee calls him by his corner colour, "Red".

## Body and model sheet {#body}

### Build {#build}

A is an adult man 180 cm tall weighing 80 kg, the middleweight range of the [rules](../world/glory-rules.md#weight). Broad shoulders, a thick athletic trunk, developed arms and thighs, and proportionate hands and feet give him a trained fighter's silhouette. The weight is character information, not a measured mesh volume.

### Recognition {#recognition}

He is carried by the original Human polygon figure. Warm medium skin and his red gloves and shorts distinguish him from B from the front, the side and behind, even when the camera reverses their screen positions. His face reads as a simple cartoon face whose eyes and brows show strain.

### Hands {#hands}

His fingers close around the fist with the thumb outside, and the glove follows the same hand and wrist through every rotation.

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### Fight kit {#kit}

Red gloves, red mid-thigh shorts with a pale waistband, a mouthguard and wrist wraps. His chest and feet are bare.

### Marks by state {#marks}

Each clean blow he takes throws sweat off the struck skin and leaves it reddened for the rest of the bout; a clean face blow also draws blood from his nose or mouth. He starts the bout unmarked.

## Carried props {#props}

### None {#no-props}

N/A: a fighter carries nothing into the ring beyond his kit.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Effort {#effort}

Direct, sudden and strong in attack; direct and sustained in footwork. He moves on the balls of his feet with knees responsive, never in a shared idle pose.

### Stance {#stance}

An orthodox kickboxer: left foot forward, chin tucked, gloves beside the cheeks and elbows ready to cover the ribs. The stance stays wide enough for either leg to move without crossing the feet.

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Weapons {#weapons}

A fights as a Dutch-style pressure kicker at championship level. He prefers combinations that finish with a kick: jab, cross, hook and a right low kick; jab, cross, a hook to the body and the low kick; or jab, cross and a right kick to the body. He also throws the double jab, the left uppercut, the straight right to the body, the teep and the clinch knee. He keeps one rare weapon in reserve, the right Brazilian kick, thrown from the same chamber as his body kick.

### Defense {#defense}

He parries, catches, slips outside the cross, rolls under hooks, sways back, blocks with the elbow and checks low kicks with his lead shin, and counters from the check, from the slip and by pivoting off the line.

### Kick guard {#kick-guard}

During every round kick his kicking-side glove reaches forward at his own head line while the other glove guards his cheek, through chamber, delivery, recoil and replant.

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Checks the drag {#checks-drag}

When B drags his rear foot and turns his hip as if to kick low, A lifts his lead leg to check without waiting to see the kick, at once and every time. B uses this against him with the low-kick feint.

### Answers the pump jab {#answers-pump}

A pump jab at his eyes draws his right glove up to meet it, at once and every time.

### Leaves on an angle {#leaves-on-angle}

After landing a combination he steps out on an angle, pivoting on his lead foot, rather than backing straight out. When B gives ground straight back, A does not chase him but cuts across toward the side B is circling to.

## Speech {#speech}

### Silent fighter {#silent}

He says nothing during the round. Short effort exhales accompany his attacks and his breathing deepens as the round proceeds, until the wordless shout of his celebration.

## Values and objectives {#values}

### Patience {#patience}

A is patient and methodical. He builds patterns on purpose and cashes them in late. His super-objective is to win by a clean finish; in this bout his objective is to teach B to fear the body kick and then use that fear.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Opponent and referee {#opponent}

He respects B as an equal and touches gloves before the first exchange. He obeys the referee at once: he stops attacking on a knockdown and stays in the assigned neutral corner until the result.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### Reads the tell {#reads-tell}

From the first landed body kick on, A sees that B drops his left arm whenever A's right knee rises, and he plans the Brazilian kick around it.

## State changes {#state}

### Through the bout {#through-bout}

His breathing deepens and his skin collects marks from each clean blow he takes. After the knockdown he goes to the farthest neutral corner and waits; after the result he celebrates with raised gloves, turning, quick supported steps and excited breath, keeping clear of B.
