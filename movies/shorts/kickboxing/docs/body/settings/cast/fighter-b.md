# Fighter B

## Identity {#identity}

### Who he is {#who}

B is a fictional 30-year-old professional kickboxer, a confident counter-puncher who likes to set the pace. The film never names him; the referee would call him "Blue".

## Body and model sheet {#body}

### Build {#build}

B is an adult man 180 cm tall weighing 80 kg, the same competitive build as A, within the middleweight range of the [rules](../world/glory-rules.md#weight). The weight is character information, not a measured mesh volume.

### Recognition {#recognition}

He is carried by an independent instance of the original Human polygon figure. Dark cool skin, cyan gloves and navy shorts with cyan side stripes identify him from every view. His simple cartoon face shows strain through eyes and brows.

### Hands {#hands}

Closed fingers and an outside thumb stay inside each glove; glove, hand and wrist move together.

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### Fight kit {#kit}

Cyan gloves, navy mid-thigh shorts with cyan side stripes, a mouthguard and wrist wraps, with chest and feet bare.

### Marks by state {#marks}

Each clean blow he takes throws sweat off the struck skin and leaves it reddened for the rest of the bout; a clean face blow also draws blood from his nose or mouth, and blood from the finish marks the canvas beside his face. He starts the bout unmarked.

## Carried props {#props}

### None {#no-props}

N/A: a fighter carries nothing into the ring beyond his kit.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Effort {#effort}

Light, sudden and flexible between exchanges; direct and strong when he commits. He likes rhythm and answers every exchange.

### Stance {#stance}

An orthodox kickboxer, left foot forward, chin tucked, hands near the cheeks and elbows near the ribs. Retreat begins with the rear foot; lateral movement begins with the foot on the side of travel.

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Weapons {#weapons}

B fights at championship level and enjoys long combinations and the low kick. He favours jab, cross and left hook; jab, cross, a hook to the liver and a right low kick behind it; jab, cross, hook and the right high kick; and the switch kick. He sells a low kick by dragging his rear foot and turning his hip, then throws the real kick or a cross. He takes the centre behind a double jab and closes into the clinch for a single knee.

### Defense {#defense}

His right glove parries a jab and returns to the cheek. A high cover closes the threatened head corridor; a body block turns the near elbow and forearm onto the ribs without abandoning the other hand. His slips and shallow rolls combine knees, hips and trunk while his eyes stay on A, and he counters from the resulting angle. A checking left shin returns through a controlled replant.

### Kick guard {#kick-guard}

During every round kick his kicking-side glove reaches forward at his own head line while the other glove guards his cheek, through chamber, delivery, recoil and replant.

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Drops the left arm {#drops-left-arm}

Whenever A's right knee rises, B drops his left elbow to his ribs, expecting the body kick, by the time the knee reaches its chamber and every time. He learns it from the right body kick A lands at the start of the fifth phase and repeats it from then on; before that landing he answers A's kicks by their line. A's body-kick feint and his Brazilian kick both exploit it.

### Chases the pump jab {#chases-pump}

A pump jab makes B's right glove jump toward it, at once and every time.

### Stays on the leg {#stays-on-leg}

After his own low kick is checked, B stays on the kicking leg a beat too long before replanting.

### Circles out {#circles-out}

When he feels the ropes behind him, he circles out toward the open side instead of covering up.

## Speech {#speech}

### Silent fighter {#silent}

He says nothing during the round. His voice is limited to effort exhales, a grunt when his body takes a blow, and breathing that stays audible on the canvas.

## Values and objectives {#values}

### Pace {#pace}

B is confident and rhythmic. His super-objective is to outwork A; in this bout his objective is to take the centre and break A's lead leg with low kicks.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Opponent and referee {#opponent}

He respects A as an equal, touches gloves before the first exchange and obeys the referee.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### Misreads the knee {#misreads}

He believes every rising right knee from A brings the body kick, because the first ones did.

## State changes {#state}

### Through the bout {#through-bout}

His left thigh and left ribs collect marks from A's low and body kicks. After the finish he lies on his right side, breathing, makes failed attempts to rise through his left arm and stays down through the count and the celebration. No injury mechanism is asserted.
