# Referee

## Identity {#identity}

### Who he is {#who}

The referee is a fictional professional official, an adult man running the bout under the [rules](../world/glory-rules.md).

## Body and model sheet {#body}

### Build {#build}

He stands 178 cm tall and weighs 76 kg, with a regular athletic build, carried by his own instance of the original Human polygon figure with a simple cartoon face.

### Recognition {#recognition}

A white long-sleeve shirt, black trousers, black shoes and bare hands distinguish him from both fighters from every view. His head turns show which exchange he is watching.

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### Uniform {#uniform}

White long-sleeve shirt, black trousers and black shoes, unchanged and unmarked through the film.

## Carried props {#props}

### None {#no-props}

N/A: he carries nothing; his hands stay free for signals and breaks.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Effort {#effort}

Direct and sustained: he walks with short supported steps, never bounces and never runs. His stance keeps soft knees and both hands ready in front of his waist.

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Positioning {#positioning}

He follows the [referee mechanics](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#positioning): he walks with the circling fighters and watches both of them side-on across their line, as far from them as the ring allows, never behind a fighter or between a fighter and the ropes, and never between the two. To change sides he goes around.

### Closing in {#closing}

He comes closer when the fighters work along the ropes, when one of them clinches for a knee, and when a stoppage may be near, stepping in at an angle, never from behind.

### Signals {#signals}

"Fight!" with a palm that rises beside his shoulder and sweeps down; "Down!" with a pointing hand; "Neutral corner!" with a pointing arm; the count with his fingers, one per number; a sweep of both hands across his body to end the contest, then an open hand toward the winner.

### Knockdown {#knockdown}

He sends the standing fighter to the farthest neutral corner and keeps him in view while counting over the fallen one; if the standing fighter leaves the corner, he stops the count until he returns. He assesses readiness at eight.

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Watches the hands {#watches-hands}

His gaze follows the hands of the fighter attacking; at a clinch it stays on the clinching gloves until they release.

## Speech {#speech}

### Voice {#voice}

Clear, firm English in a middle register: brief commands and an even count, one number per second. His mouth opens while he speaks and closes between phrases.

## Values and objectives {#values}

### Safety and fairness {#fairness}

His super-objective is a fair, safe contest; his objective in every phase is to see both fighters' hands and feet without blocking the view.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Authority {#authority}

Both fighters obey him at once.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### The rules {#rules}

He knows the [rules](../world/glory-rules.md) and gives no medical diagnosis.

## State changes {#state}

### After the result {#after-result}

After the knockout he lowers into a shallow supported crouch beside B, watching his face, while A celebrates.
