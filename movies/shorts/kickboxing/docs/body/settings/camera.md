# Camera

## Look {#look}

One continuous inside-the-ring take on the original Human polygon figures, with no cuts. The camera behaves like a ringside operator: it keeps a side view of both fighters' profiles from across their line, on the side opposite the referee, so he never blocks an exchange. It moves only when the action motivates the move and follows with an operator's lag. [Ring craft](../../research/ring-craft.md#camera) records the broadcast and fight-film sources.

## Shot sizes {#sizes}

| Size | Used | Framing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wide | While the fighters reset | Both full figures, the referee and canvas around them |
| Medium | While an exchange is live | Both full figures filling the frame |
| Close | On a clean landing | The struck fighter's upper body |

Each size holds for at least 2.4 s. Over the bout, the mean interval between size changes falls between 2 and 8 s.

## Anchors {#anchors}

At 143 s the camera begins widening into the side-on knockout view. It holds that view without a cut through the Brazilian kick, the fall, the neutral corner, the count and A's celebration to 180 s.
