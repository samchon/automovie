## Rules {#rules}

[GLORY's 16 March 2026 v2.2 rules](https://glory.pinkyellow.network/assets/rules/glory-rules-2026-v2.pdf), section 2.01, include 80 kg in middleweight. Sections 5.05.B.2–5 and Appendix 3.D require the opponent in the farthest neutral corner facing center before counting; leaving pauses it. Readiness is assessed at eight. An uninterrupted ten-count ends in KO, followed by the match-ending bell. Section 5.06.E.2 distinguishes that outcome from an immediate-assistance early stoppage. No downed follow-up is permitted.

The film chooses a real ten-second clock interval 157–167, with numbers spoken at 158–167, one per elapsed second. That exact rhythm is an authored implementation of the ten-count, not a quoted timing formula from the rulebook. The count remains uncut; A stays in the corner, B fails to stand, and the official declaration follows Ten. This is a fictional contest, not an accredited GLORY event.

## Technique {#technique}

[IBA Coaches Manual](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/AIBA-Coach-Regulations-Manual_WEB.pdf), printed pages 36–39, 62–66, 130–135 and 187–190: move the direction-side foot first, restore stance, connect blocks/body defenses/counters, and turn around the lead forefoot. Page 133 differentiates short hooks/uppercuts from longer straight combinations and selects accented punches within a series. This is boxing instruction; the film adapts shallow evasions to a kickboxing threat rather than importing deep ducks or clinch rules.

Gavagan and Sayers, [PLoS ONE 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5571909/), doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0182645, Methods, Table 2 and Discussion: eight trained participants per style struck a fixed elevated pad. Pelvis rotation, hip motion, knee extension and movement toward the target support a linked roundhouse. The Muay Thai group's 1.02±0.15 s phase measure is not a fight-wide timing rule or a KO threshold.

Chang et al., [JSSM 2021](https://jssm.org/jssm-20-457.xml-Fulltext), doi:10.52082/jssm.2021.457, Methods and Discussion: twelve taekwondo athletes show height-related support-leg and recovery differences. The film uses active pivot support and connected recovery; this study supplies no universal safe pivot angle for kickboxing.

Martijn de Jong, GLORY's [The Art of Low Kicks](https://glorykickboxing.com/news/the-art-of-low-kicks), 15 January 2014: outside-thigh kicks can follow punch combinations; defenders can withdraw the leg or turn a raised shin toward the incoming leg. The film uses those options, excluding knee-cap and groin contacts. The article's angle cue and injury anecdote are not adopted as universal safety or medical claims.

## Medical uncertainty {#uncertainty}

[CDC's TBI explanation](https://www.cdc.gov/traumatic-brain-injury/about/index.html) describes head impact and rapid head movement as possible concussion causes. It does not diagnose this fictional outcome, establish an impact threshold or prove carotid obstruction. The film shows a lateral jaw impact and loss of support. The exchanges cease, A moves to the neutral corner and the referee counts to ten before declaring the official knockout.

## Screenplay form {#screenplay-form}

Matt Carless's official BBC [Screenplay Format](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/writersroom/scripts/screenplay.pdf), updated 6 February 2004, printed pages 1, 4 and 7: scene heading, short visible-action beats, separate speaker/dialogue blocks, O.S. cues and restrained technical directions. A four-to-five-line paragraph is a reading guide, not a duration calculation. One continuous location retains one master scene heading. Printed page 4 also permits short action-focus subheadings within a complex scene; it does not prescribe Markdown H3. This production adopts H3 action/exchange groups under its H2 scene at the user's request.

The Academy/YMI [screenwriting guide](https://www.oscars.org/sites/oscars/files/complete_screen_writing_activities_guide.pdf), copyright 1998, Activities 1–2, supports visual action and distinguishes submission from shooting-script technical density. Its classroom long-form structure is not a mandatory 180-second structure. No inaccessible feature-film script is claimed as read.

Final Draft's official [screenplay elements guide](https://www.finaldraft.com/learn/screenplay-formatting-elements/) preserves scene/action/character/dialogue blocks and short action paragraphs. It supports readable action grouping, not an industry mandate for Markdown heading levels.
