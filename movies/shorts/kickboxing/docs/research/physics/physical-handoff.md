# Authored motion and physical reactions

This study selects the ownership boundary for the polygon bout. Ordinary movement keeps authored support and placement; selected reacting regions become dynamic, and the finishing fall requires an actual qualifying contact. This matches issue 3083's staging contract. It does not qualify the failed full-body simulation or excuse the independent standing-controller failure. The [reference index](../index.md#physical-handoff-supplement-2026-11-07) records retained originals and their identities.

## What the sources establish

Epic's [Physics-Based Animation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/physics-driven-animation-in-unreal-engine?lang=en-US), sections “Set All Bodies Below Simulate Physics” and “Hit-Based Reaction Overview”, enables a selected bone subtree, varies its influence, then disables simulation after returning to animation. This establishes a normal production technique, rather than a requirement to continuously balance every animated character. The page supplies neither a velocity-transfer algorithm nor a guarantee that its rendered blend equals collision geometry.

The [Rapier JavaScript 0.21 rigid-body guide](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/rigid_bodies/), sections “Rigid-body type”, “Position” and “Velocity”, matches the installed `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat` 0.21.0. Position-based kinematics derive velocity from `setNextKinematicTranslation` and `setNextKinematicRotation`. Direct transform assignment teleports. Kinematics ignore forces and can cross obstacles, so collision-aware target fitting remains necessary. The [setBodyType API](https://rapier.rs/javascript3d/classes/RigidBody.html#setBodyType) promises a status change; it does not specify our required handoff continuity. Preserving the current pose and explicitly transferring authored linear and angular velocity must therefore be tested against the installed implementation.

Epic's historical [UE-72912](https://issues.unrealengine.com/issue/UE-72912) reports rendered animation/physics blending while colliders remained at the fully simulated pose. Its scope is Unreal 4.20/4.21, resolved “Won't Fix” in 2021; it is not evidence of a current Unreal defect. It demonstrates why our own render/collider correspondence must be checked independently.

The [Rapier collision guide](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/advanced_collision_detection/), sections “Collision and contact force events” and “The contact graph”, separates broad-phase candidates, signed surface distance and solved contact impulses. A graph edge alone does not establish touching or momentum transfer. Unity's [Collision.impulse contract](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/script-reference/unityengine/collision/impulse), definition and remarks, describes the sum over a collision pair's contact points; dividing by the fixed step gives average force. These support distinct geometric and solver provenance, with N s retained for impact strength rather than treating a nominal event as a measured force.

## Adopted implementation constraints

- The movie declares acting regions and time windows. Engine owns native body types, joint consistency, interpolation, velocity transfer and checkpoint state; Human owns anatomical support and bracing. Engine imports no Human policy.
- A transition into dynamics preserves the actual pose and inherits the authored trajectory's world linear and angular velocity. An already dynamic segment keeps its solved velocity. A cold interval initialized at 149 s with zero velocity is not a continuation of the film.
- A return to authored control must converge through the same coherent articulated state used for collision and rendering. A display-only crossfade over unrelated dynamic colliders is unsuitable for this film's contact promises. Return duration is an authored control parameter, not proof of momentum conservation.
- Activating both participants' interacting regions is necessary where reciprocal physical reactions are promised. A collision against an authored region remains a declared infinite-authority boundary, not free-body momentum conservation.
- The referee and ordinary support remain authored unless a scene declares a physical reaction. A successful finishing weapon/head solver contact releases the receiver's full body; a miss cannot release it by clock alone.
- The forward guard uses actual opponent collider separation constraints. Kinematic ownership does not itself prevent glove or forearm penetration.
- Physical impact sounds and visible reaction strength require touching, positive solver impulses from the same playback. Comparison effects may use a separately declared formula estimate, retain `geometric` provenance and never populate a solver impulse field.
- A physical footstep requires actual airborne-to-floor arrival, or an explicitly confirmed recovery landing with matching floor contact. Planned swing ends, busy reservations, failed steps and cancelled recoveries do not establish a landing.

## Required checks and present limits

The generic handoff needs native tests for a moving and rotating chain, partial activation, return continuity, missed-contact nonrelease, checkpoint equivalence and collider/render agreement. The film then needs one short contiguous consumer interval containing preparation, contact and recovery, with finite runtime cost, exact seek replay, rig-visible playback, actual audio cues and decoded export. Only that result can qualify extension toward the complete 180 s performance. The v21 actual Human and four-support-rig quiet test passed; the complete core round still retained other failures. No normalized simulated movie interval has passed the film checks. The separate five-frame kinematic archive at 150 s exercised real binary loading, authoritative authored ownership, bounded host controls and the capture seek hook; its geometric contacts retain null impulses.

Its reported timings and random-restart strategy are not adopted here. They do not justify random production motion or assuming that a converged limb goal cleared a receiver.

Every declared receiver shape remains in the clearance solve, together with own-profile self contacts, floor and planted supports. The nominal maximum-reach endpoint does not override these constraints or require a locked elbow. Generic fitting and numerical budgets belong to Engine; anatomical geometry belongs to Human.
