## EXT. SCHOOL SIDE COURTYARD - LATE AFTERNOON {#courtyard-invitation}



### 0–13 seconds

From the open south side, the full-body view holds Carina two steps from the empty gray stone bench.[^opening-space] About fifteen, pale-blond and in her Slytherin uniform, she wears the green-and-silver tie beneath an open black robe, with a charcoal jumper, gray skirt, black tights and flat school shoes. Late-afternoon light catches the white note in her right hand. Close dry taps follow her shoes; beyond the north wall, out of frame, birds give faint irregular calls.

She walks up to the bench, lifts the note to chest height and squares her shoulders toward the empty seat.

CARINA
Potter. On Saturday...

She stops, lowers the paper and retraces the two steps. She turns to face the bench and approaches again.[^rehearsal-turn]

CARINA
Saturday. Three. Don't be late.

The note stays extended toward the empty bench. Her elbow droops. She lets out a small breath and returns to her starting place.

### 13–25 seconds

Her third approach leaves the west gap in view. Harry comes through it: about fifteen, dark hair, round glasses, a red-and-gold Gryffindor tie, charcoal jumper and trousers beneath an open black robe. His footfalls merge under hers as she keeps walking toward the bench, her back to him. Along the clear paving south of the bench, he stops an arm's length behind her.

She holds the note out toward the bench again, this time with her shoulders less square.

CARINA
(quieter)
I just wanted to go with you.

HARRY
All right.

Her right arm freezes in the air. The paper trembles once as her fingers tighten.

### 25–36 seconds

Carina whips the note behind her right hip, turns to face him and stops.[^discovery-turn] The medium full-body two-shot holds their hands and the gap between them.

CARINA
How long have you been there?

Harry looks from her face to the strip of paper beside her hip. He offers his right palm across the gap. His feet stay where they are.

She brings the note forward until its short edge hovers above his palm, then draws it back against her chest before his fingers touch it. His hand remains open in the space she has left.

### 36–48 seconds

She glances at his empty palm and lowers the note toward it again. Her fingers open too soon. The paper slips free, flutters between them and lands with a faint dry tap on the paving. Both look down.

The two-shot stays unbroken as they reach for the note together.[^shared-reach] Their hands descend on opposite sides of the paper between their toes. Their foreheads brush before either hand gets there: light, slow contact and a soft tick. Both stop, then straighten apart. The paper lies untouched between them.

They look at each other. Both begin another bend—then stop after a small forward lean. They straighten together.

Harry lifts both empty hands, palms toward her, and steps half a metre back.[^offered-space]

### 48–60 seconds

Carina bends into the cleared space, picks up the paper by its short edge with her right hand and straightens. Harry lowers his left hand and offers his right palm again. She puts the note into it and keeps hold until his fingers close; only then does she release it.

CARINA
Three o'clock.

She turns west and walks past him toward the opening. He stays where he is, holding the note in his right hand, and touches his forehead with his left.

HARRY
I heard.

The full-body view keeps them together. Harry looks down at the note, his smile growing. Along the clear western route, farther from him, Carina touches her forehead with her empty left hand and smiles toward the gap. Turned away from one another, neither sees the other's smile. Her dry paving taps recede as she leaves the recess. Cut to black. The same faint bird calls from beyond the wall continue briefly through the cut, then end.
