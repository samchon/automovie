# Carina Malfoy

## Identity and performance baseline {#carina}

Carina is an invented female reinterpretation of Draco Malfoy for this fan scene, a Hogwarts student and school-age peer of Harry at about fifteen. She is about 1.66 metres tall, slim and fair-skinned, with a narrow face and straight pale-blond hair ending above her shoulders. Her Slytherin school uniform has a white pointed shirt collar, a green tie crossed by silver stripes, a charcoal V-neck knit jumper and an open black robe with green lining and a small left-chest house badge. A gray pleated skirt ends just above her knees; opaque black tights and flat black loafers complete the silhouette. Coarse polygon shapes may simplify fabric and the badge, while retaining these recognizable garment layers and house colors.

The robe ends at mid-calf with separate front panels; its opening leaves the hands, knees and space between her shoes visible. Sleeves end at the wrists, leaving fingers uncovered. The skirt permits knee flexion and a hip hinge without narrowing her planted stance; neither skirt nor robe sweeps the grounded note. Her empty left hand can balance her, while her right hand carries the [invitation note](note.md#note). Her shoes remain flat and stable on dry paving. She has ordinary human reach, balance and strength; magic does not move her or the note.

She is used to giving brisk instructions when watched, but asking for company exposes her to refusal. She can abandon a rehearsal or leave freely. She does not threaten Harry, claim his agreement before he gives it, or interpret an accidental touch as permission for closer contact. Her voice is youthful, female, middle-to-upper pitched, clear and lightly bright, with contemporary British pronunciation. Rehearsal commands have clipped ends; direct vulnerability lowers her volume and slows the last few words. She retains intelligible consonants rather than shouting or adopting a caricatured aristocratic accent. Her bilateral relationship and knowledge boundary are owned by [Harry's record](harry.md#harry).
