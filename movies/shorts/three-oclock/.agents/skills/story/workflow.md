# Writing workflow

Choose the commissioned route from the request, project context, adopted instructions and current bodies.

## Creative ownership

| Layer | Owns | Hands onward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Settings](layers/settings.md) | World facts, baseline states, characters, relationships, places, objects, knowledge boundaries and sensory conditions | Usable facts with conditions, uncertainty, sources and one owner |
| [Treatments](layers/treatments.md) | Events, causes, choices, consequences, setup and payoff, and information release | A progression that needs no invented decisive event |
| [Scripts](layers/scripts.md) | Partition and fully executed scenes: speech, action, reactions, performance, stage, light, sound and view | Playable scenes for the final screenplay |
| [Final screenplay](layers/screenplay.md) | The audience's image, sound, attention, duration, juxtaposition and transitions | Complete production text the shots execute |

## Route and parents

Commissioned video follows one order: performance study, settings, screenplay, the motion library with its tests, shot staging, then assembly. A later layer can only realize what an earlier one decided, so motion written before the screenplay settles its action is fitted to guesses.

Long work runs settings → treatments → scripts → screenplay construction, then screenplay naturalness; only the final screenplay has a separate expression pass. A short enters the screenplay from its brief (`short`) or after compact settings (`short-with-settings`), keeping expression, evidence and review without empty treatment, script or outline files. Asset-only work has no narrative passes, and only an explicit user waiver omits screenplay naturalness. Choose form and scale through [direction](planning/direction.md); complete work is the default.

## Construction

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, discovered requirements and any completed [performance study](planning/research.md#performance-study) |
| Treatments | Completed settings |
| Scripts | Completed treatments |
| Screenplay, long work | Completed treatments and scripts, including scripts expression |
| Screenplay, direct short | The request, adopted constraints and any completed performance study |
| Screenplay, compact short | Completed compact settings, the brief and the studies the settings cite |

Inherit through actual parents, repairing a missing handoff rather than bypassing it, and run [discovery](planning/discovery.md) before bulk settings.

1. Finish the whole body while its pass is disabled.
2. Complete [inspection and cause repair](correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), enable the stage through [evidence configuration](../evidence/configuration.md), and write [answers](evidence/writing.md).
3. Submit the body, boundaries and population to independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) through creative closure and evidence audit under [review rounds](../review/rounds.md).
4. Repair findings at their owners and complete the closing review.

## Completion

Run `pnpm evidence` and read its whole report; a passing graph shows the recorded relationships, and review judges the creative result. Whole-work completion needs the selected passes, a current closing review, passing unfiltered validation, the compiler and source checks and required tests, and [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) of any commissioned media.

A manuscript with its closing review may be delivered with its revision and named remaining diagnostics, but that is not fully validated completion. A creative defect, missing parent, pending transfer, unfinished review or unverified media blocks its completion, and until review finishes the result is a draft. Changes to completed layers follow [closed passes](correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
