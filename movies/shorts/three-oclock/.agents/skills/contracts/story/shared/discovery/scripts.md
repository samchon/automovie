# Script requirement discovery

The discovery result covers the complete executable scene and partition population under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#shared-work-boundary).

## Are the work's execution and presentation rules discovered? {#script-requirements}

Find uncovered rules for partition, action, audience access, speech and silence, inserted forms, transitions and exits that change actual scene execution and can be refuted in their selected hosts. Inspect positive conventions for entry and exit, action-line placement, block content and withholding, release boundaries, direction register, scene headings, views and sound notation. Distinguish notation from the meaningful view decision itself. Identify the actual form expression revision preserves and final screenplay inherits, with its canonical owner.
