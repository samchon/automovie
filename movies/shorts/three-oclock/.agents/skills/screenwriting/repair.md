# Repair and changed scope

Identify the earliest owner, actual consumers, current pass and closed boundaries before changing the work. Repair construction decisions at their owner and propagate only to consumers that use them. Expression repair changes its working edition against the unchanged frozen original.

For a requested substantive revision, first identify the intended change, achievements the user wants preserved and the smallest useful scope: wording, scene, sequence or whole-work design. Trace its impact through dependent facts, scenes, assets, performance and edit. Use that brief impact plan to protect successful material and check the seams with unchanged work; keep it proportional to the change.

## Upstream defects

A construction author who finds a missing, conflicting or unrealizable parent decision, or a design that prevents the adopted experience, repairs its earliest owner before writing from it. Use the selected [upstream questions](../contracts/story/index.md) to identify the defect and correction, tracing a settings cause through treatments on the long route. A downstream workaround leaves other consumers with the same defect.

During expression work, report an upstream defect instead of changing frozen content. A separate construction repair follows [closed passes](#closed-passes); it does not replace the ongoing expression cycle's original baseline.

## Cause rounds

Finish the whole declared inspection round before edits. [Inspection](inspection.md) owns independent classification and changed-context verification; selected contracts own the judgment. Group all findings by cause and affected consumers, including the whole recurring class rather than a few examples.

Repair the causes together where their action or explanation belongs. Preserve the passage's needed meaning and achieved effect through recomposition when cutting would lose them. After repair, read changed passages together and the complete progression for new uniformity, fidelity losses, broken orientation and seams with unchanged material.

Within the submitted pass, reconcile dependent statements, quotations, inherited values, observations and [answers](evidence/writing.md) in the same repair. Candidate decisions follow the canonical [decision and retention method](evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate). Finish the next whole inspection round before further repairs; author correction closes under [changed-work verification](inspection.md#verify-changed-work), and independent acceptance follows [review rounds](../review/rounds.md).

## Closed passes

Completed layers stay closed when their bodies or upstream dependencies change. Reopen neither their evidence nor their reviews or approvals. Remove answers for deleted H2s and write answers for new H2s only; report other resulting diagnostics without repairing them. Preserve original failed reports and statuses under [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership).

After completed expression transfer, hand off the current repaired body, repair scope and unfiltered report, identifying the transfer's working edition and frozen original. Pending or stale transfer checks remain failures; preserve the repaired body rather than overwrite it with the old working edition. Whole-work review does not renew frozen construction approval against transferred expression. [Completion](workflow.md#completion) owns qualified delivery.

Replacing a unit from scratch removes its obsolete current answers, frozen and working inputs, reviews and replaced descendants. Preserve completed material outside that scope and history in Git. A reused filename grants no prior approval; new construction begins its own completion and expression cycle.

## Scope expansion

Complete work is the default. A first-part pilot needs the user's commission and exact selected part. Settings remains work-wide; treatments, scripts and screenplay cover the declared slice with actual lineage, applicable obligations and current reviews. State what the slice cannot establish about the whole work.

After current pilot passes and slice review, expand through [evidence configuration](../evidence/configuration.md). Reset affected narrative passes together, design the complete treatment sequence including pilot material and reconsider script partition. Establish whole-population evidence and review; pilot approval cannot establish the expanded work. Preserve content outside the authorized rewrite.
