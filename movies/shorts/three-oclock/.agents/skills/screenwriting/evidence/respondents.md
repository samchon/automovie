# Place the answer with its respondent

Construction answers remain detached from creative bodies. Put positive H2 answers in `docs/evidence/construction/units/<layer>/...md` and file answers in `docs/evidence/construction/files/<layer>/...md`. The document's H1 identifies the exact body file or file-and-anchor address, followed by Evidence headings and supporting prose. Read installed `@wrtnlabs/evidence` documentation for native address and annotation syntax.

Detached documents do not support exclusion or exclusion-review rows. Put a permitted exclusion in the actual host unit's HTML comment, or use the designated discovery carrier for a discovery negative. State the inspected scope and exact inapplicability; a population exclusion requires the entire population to be inapplicable.

Script expression answers belong to its detached construction units and population files. Screenplay naturalness answers stay in HTML comments attached to their working unit in `docs/naturalness/screenplay`. Place H2-owned answers directly beneath their H2, before its subordinate H3 headings. File answers attach before its first heading. Keep them with that edition when reviewed text is transferred; [rewriting procedure](../rewriting/procedure.md#transfer) owns the comment-free transfer and [upstream repair](../repair.md) its closed-proof boundary.

Current review records in `docs/review/<draft|final>/<layer>/` mirror every selected file and H2 and include `verdict/index.md` for the complete population. Include native current fingerprint reviews for body-clean units and literal support for findings; identify carried judgments and unperformed checks honestly. These reviewer judgments do not substitute for author evidence. [Review](../../review/rounds.md) owns creative reading, factual objections and the subsequent evidence audit. Temporary blind reads, classifications and comparisons belong in ignored `.cache/`; Git preserves superseded published records.

Work-specific and language-specific questions belong in their matching families under `.agents/skills/contracts/story/{work,language}`. [Contracts](../../evidence/SKILL.md) owns adoption and [evidence configuration](../../evidence/configuration.md) selects their actual populations and owners. Use a unit for its behavior and a designated population respondent for a whole-work duty. Asset and delivery respondents can be builders or scoped observation and handoff reports when they can establish the commissioned result.
