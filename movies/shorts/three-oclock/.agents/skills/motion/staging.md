# Stage the screenplay for the camera

Use this when a screenplay or standalone performance becomes executable shots, or when blocking, cameras or continuity change. [Asset work](../development/assets.md) chooses representations, [frame evaluation](timeline.md) owns time sampling and production owns the final edit. A shot list or an animated object without the required exchange does not realize a scene.

## Decide the observable performance

Read each screenplay unit in context and identify the required action or change, who starts it, the intended reaction and the resulting state, with consequential props, supports, entrances and exits. Silence, stillness or an off-screen event can be the decision. Choose shot boundaries from that progression, not from paragraph count; one unit may need several shots and one shot may realize several units, and each shot function cites the units it executes under the [shots contract](../contracts/development/shots.md).

## Block one coherent space

Set meaningful dimensions, frames, identities, facing, routes, eye lines and object placement from the settings and the adopted asset controls. Program anticipation, action, reaction, pauses and result against the shared clock, through the motion library, [physical feedback](physics.md) and [retargeting](retargeting.md). Each actor and object has one transform owner, so a clip, an attachment and a procedural driver never fight for the same state; a reach or scale mismatch is fixed at its owner, not by changing the outcome.

With the installed shot compiler, read its current contracts for participants, opening and closing states, events and continuity, feed it the prior accepted state, and choose event predicates from the screenplay, not from a failing output. Compilation captures no video.

## Direct the actual view

Choose camera, lens, framing, movement and cuts for what the audience must perceive at each moment, keeping spatial relations, screen direction, eye lines and reveal timing; deliberate discontinuity is valid when it serves the experience. Inspect subjects, contact and reaction at the delivered aspect and scale through the whole action, checking clipping, occlusion and competing detail in the actual camera frames; a subject box inside the frustum does not prove readable pixels.

## Carry state through the edit

Continuing action keeps its state across cuts as the [shots contract](../contracts/development/shots.md#continuing-state) asks. Build and review [contiguous segments](segments.md), carrying each accepted ending into the next and checking both sides of the join, and hand accepted shots to [finishing](../production/finishing.md).
