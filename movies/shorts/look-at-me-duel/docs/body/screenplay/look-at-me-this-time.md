



# Look at Me This Time

Provisional 60-second performance plan for one vertical short. Windows allocate action and breath; recorded speech, motion and the assembled edit must establish final duration. Speaker cues and time windows are production notation, not screen text. This replacement construction consumes the completed seven-record compact settings and the current five-part creative brief; no treatment or script stage is implied.

## EXT. TRAINING COURT - DAY {#duel}








### 0-9 seconds

From the open south approach, a wide view finds CARINA, fifteen, just south-west of the court's centre, facing her schoolmate HARRY, also fifteen, a pace to her north-east. Her pale shoulder-length hair frames a small proud smile. His dark hair is untidy above round glasses. White collars and house-striped ties show between their open charcoal Hogwarts robes: green and silver for Carina, burgundy and gold for Harry. Her skirt, grey socks and black shoes and his charcoal trousers and black shoes leave their steps readable. Each holds a dark wooden wand in the right hand. The view includes the low north boundary and bare brown ground around the open sides; the two students have the court to themselves.

Behind Harry at frame right, a grey cap projects south from the stone upright. Daylight catches a cracked pale mortar strip beneath the cap's rear edge. The floor west of the assembly lies open. Soft wind sits beneath their foot scuffs.

Harry circles toward the support in short, springy steps, watching her wand. Carina turns on the balls of her feet to keep facing him, her free left hand loose beside her.

HARRY
Ready?

Carina plants her leading foot and raises her wand.

CARINA
Are you?

### 9-25 seconds

Harry steps inside her wand's reach. Their wands meet with a wooden click. His right-hand wand turns her tip outward; his left hand closes around her right wrist, clear of the tapered robe cuff. The view moves with them, keeping both sets of feet and the held wrist together in frame.

Carina bends the caught elbow, brings their joined hands above her head and steps under the hold. Her torso turns beneath her raised arm; her wand stays pointed out from the pair. Harry follows the turn for half a step, then opens his fingers before her shoulder pulls against his grip.

She completes the turn on a planted foot and faces him again. Harry takes two quick diagonal steps back toward the support, finishing beneath the projecting cap, just south of the base. His left hand drops free; his wand remains in his right. Carina settles her heels, facing him across about two metres of open court. Her wrist is clear.

A blue-white shot hisses from her wand. Harry sweeps his wand across its path. The streak bends up past his shoulder, cracks against the damaged lower strip and goes out.

### 25-35 seconds

Two small grey chips tap the floor beside the base. Harry raises his wand toward Carina again. Above and behind him, the cap's front edge dips. Its rear lower edge still rests on the ledge, held against the upright by an upper mortar strip.

A thin stream of grit runs from the broken lower strip. Carina's gaze lifts over Harry's shoulder. Her wand drops below his chest.

The upper strip grinds. She drives off her back foot and runs at him. Harry braces, his left hand opening toward her right wrist as it did before.

### 35-48 seconds

Carina's left palm turns his reaching forearm outward. His fingers close farther up her right forearm, below her elbow. She steps across his front and puts her left shoulder against his upper chest; her wand points down and away beside them.

She pushes off the foot nearest the support and steps west. Harry bends his knees and moves with her, his left hand following her arm. The view widens with them without a cut, holding both whole bodies and the cap's path. Their first sideways step carries his feet out of the fall region. On the second, a strip of open floor separates the tipping cap from his leaned torso and trailing wand. Both plant their feet in the west lane, Carina farther west beside him. She brings her right wand arm up, and his left hand rises with her forearm.

The remaining upper strip breaks. The cap slips off its rear ledge, tips down through the free space south of the upright and drops into the place Harry has left. It strikes the floor on a broad face with a heavy crack and stays there. The upright remains standing; the grit stops.

Three small fragments spring west from that impact. A thin pale-blue oval opens just east of the pair, its near edge at Carina's raised wand tip. The three fragments strike the transparent surface with dry ticks, then drop on its stone-facing side. Beyond it, the main block lies on the floor. A low grey puff spreads around the fallen block and thins without covering the two bodies or their joined arms.

The shield fades. Carina lowers her arm, and Harry's hand comes down with it. Their shoulders part as they straighten. Each draws a breath. The camera stays with the two bodies; behind them the fallen block, scattered chips and stopped fragments remain on the floor.

### 48-60 seconds

Harry looks from the fallen block to Carina. His left hand is still around her forearm.

HARRY
Did you just save me?

Carina looks down at his hand. He follows her gaze and releases. Her arm stays where he let go for a moment.

She steps back into the open court, sets her feet and brings her wand up between them. Harry turns fully toward her, his own wand still low.

CARINA
Look at me this time.

He meets her eyes and raises his wand. Hold both figures facing one another, the fallen stone behind Harry. Cut to black on their shared intake of breath.
