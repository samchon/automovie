## Carina {#carina}

She has a slim athletic build, fair skin, a youthful rounded face and straight pale-blond hair reaching her shoulders. Her lively eyes, slightly raised chin and small proud smile give her a cute, self-assured Malfoy manner. She values competence and wants Harry to take her seriously. She competes directly, dislikes being handled as though she has already lost and uses a composed challenge to recover dignity after exposure. She can pivot under a raised wrist hold, redirect an approaching forearm and guide a cooperating schoolmate through a low lateral step; none of these abilities permits carrying Harry or stopping falling masonry. Recognition of that concern embarrasses her; deliberate cruelty or indifference would contradict this threshold.
