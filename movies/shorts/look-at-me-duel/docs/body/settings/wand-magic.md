# Wands and practice magic

## Wands and bounded effects {#wand-magic}

There are exactly two plain dark-brown wooden wands, one belonging to each rival and initially held in the owner's right hand. Each is about thirty centimetres long, tapering from a roughly one-centimetre grip to a half-centimetre tip. Neither has an identifying ornament or an extra attachment. Both remain with their owners throughout the scene; Harry's left hand is free for contact. Wands touch with a light wooden click. Grip, wrist orientation and the direction of both tips must remain visible through any effect.

A practice shot is one brief blue-white impulse from the tip, with a narrow luminous streak that shows its direction and disappears at impact. Firing needs a free wand wrist and a settled aim. One short hiss accompanies travel and ends in the impact crack; the scene has no unseen second shot or continuing beam. A shot can knock an opponent's aim aside and chip weakened masonry. It is not intended to kill, lift a person or push a whole wall over.

A wand crossing the incoming path can deflect the shot along a new visible direction. The redirected impulse retains enough force to remove the support's weakened lower mortar fillet. The cap's weight, not remote spell attraction, then drives its movement. These are invented operating rules for this scene, not claims about franchise canon or an implemented effects engine.

After planting her feet Carina can raise her wand to form one thin, translucent pale-blue shield: a shallow convex oval about 1.5 metres wide and 1.8 metres high, its lower edge near the floor. It stands just east of the close pair, facing the broken support, with its inside surface between their bodies and the arriving fragments. Her raised wand tip anchors its near edge; it remains separate from the fallen main block. Its transparency keeps the raised arm and Harry's following hand legible. Only its brief appearance, three dry fragment ticks and fade are needed; it has no loud sustained hum.

The shield intercepts only the three loose impact fragments, which drop to the floor on its support-facing side. It cannot bear the cap block, arrest its rotation, move its footprint or protect a person underneath it. Harry keeps his own wand and casts no second shield. The main mass is survived by bodily evacuation before the shield appears. The screenplay owns when the oval forms and fades; its final geometry, attachment, sound and motion have not yet been built or tested.
