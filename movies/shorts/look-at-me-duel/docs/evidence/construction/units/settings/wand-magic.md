# docs/body/settings/wand-magic.md#wand-magic

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The body owns exactly two wands, their initial and retained custody, contact sound, one practice shot and deflection, and one fragment-only shield. It supplies operational limits rather than a catalogue of unrelated magic.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Plain dark-brown tapered 30 cm wands keep the held wrist readable. One redirected blue-white impulse can damage the already weak lower fillet; gravity drives the block. The pale-blue oval stays transparent around the following hand and cannot remove the need to escape.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each right-hand wand tapers from about 1 cm grip to 0.5 cm tip; Harry's left hand remains available. Free wrist and settled aim constrain firing. The shield is a shallow convex oval about 1.5 m wide and 1.8 m high, just east of the close pair and facing the support, lower edge near floor and near edge anchored to Carina's raised wand tip. It stops only three impact fragments, which fall on its outside. Its implementation is explicitly untested.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

One magic-and-instrument owner defines custody, sensory grammar and capacities. Support geometry and fragment source remain linked external facts. Planting feet is a prerequisite, while actual shot, deflection, shield appearance, ticks and fade remain screenplay choices.

## @evidence instructions/creative-brief.md#contact-and-danger

Wand grips and directions must remain visible, and a shot needs a released wrist. The main mass requires bodily evacuation before the shield appears; it cannot be stopped by an oval or an unseen second spell. Those limits preserve the repeated physical contact's role.

## @evidence instructions/creative-brief.md#scene-completeness

Both props have quantity, shape, material, colour, scale, ownership and contact sound. Shot and shield have visible forms, attachment and end-state rules; the finite three-fragment load links to its source. No generic effect or missing sound source silently replaces a necessary decision.
