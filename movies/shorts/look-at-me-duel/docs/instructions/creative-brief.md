# Creative brief

## Rivalry becomes protection {#rivalry-and-protection}

The user approved a short action direction within the Carina Malfoy fan-scene project. The delegated version places Carina and Harry in a private practice duel. Harry's grip on Carina's wand wrist prompts her escape; later his attempt to repeat that grip becomes part of her rescue of him. Her competitive poise returns at the end without cancelling her choice to protect him. The romantic recognition remains unspoken. The title, chosen by the producer, is *Look at Me This Time*.

## Contact and danger must be readable {#contact-and-danger}

The user wants a story whose programmed motion makes a useful video-to-video difference. Build one connected physical exchange and one consequential danger: a deflected shot damages an old support and releases a block behind Harry. Carina sees it, abandons her opening and moves both people laterally outside its fall path. A shield catches residual fragments only after they clear the main block. Grip, release, foot support, changed direction and recovery must be visible at coarse polygon detail; spell effects cannot replace them. These are delegated execution requirements, not claims that an engine or generator has already realized them.

## A sparse one-minute film {#format-and-economy}

The user's format is one 60-second short using Human polygon people, sparse background and simple props. Use two performers, two wands, one training court and one damaged support/block assembly. Exclude spectators and extra attackers. Preserve recognizable Hogwarts school uniforms at coarse detail: Slytherin for Carina and Gryffindor for Harry, including house-colored ties, white collars, charcoal knitwear, open dark robes, suitable bottoms and closed shoes. Avoid decorative costume complexity, but do not omit school identity. Compose complete visible and audible action with provisional time windows, conventional English screenplay blocks and purposeful views. The intended format is vertical 9:16. Keep danger, contact and escape within readable shared frames.

## English original fan scene {#language-and-source}

The user requires all production content to be English, including speech, screen text, titles, documents and code. This is a newly invented fan scene, not an adaptation of a quoted novel passage and not a claim about the canon of *The Villainess Malfoy*. Following the user's age correction, Harry and Carina are fifteen-year-old Hogwarts schoolmates. Use their names consistently and retain age-appropriate rivalry with mutual concern. The precise practice court, appearance, magic limits and shared history are production inventions owned by compact settings. Shared generated character references have their own source records; no external literary quotations or music are adopted here.

## Complete the visible and audible scene {#scene-completeness}

The user's later scene-completeness requirement asks every necessary person, group, place, prop, object, effect and environmental source to have findable usable facts and actual scene treatment. Preserve the two-person private practice premise: it requires neither a referee nor an unseen class, but its access and empty occupancy must be clear. Establish that private occupancy in the opening view rather than imply an audience outside the frame. Specify the support's initial bearing, damaged joint, retaining contact and free fall space; keep one main block, bounded residual debris and a fragment-only shield. Give the court, wands and effects enough form and state detail for later staging without adding decoration or pretending geometry, motion, sound or video has been implemented. Preserve the four existing English dialogue lines and recorded candidate WAV bytes. This substantive scene revision replaces affected settings and screenplay units in a new construction cycle; predecessor approvals and source hashes remain historical.
