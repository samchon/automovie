# Common population obligations

Each question compares the complete declared layer population and has exactly one answering body file through its detached construction evidence. Questions may have different respondents; individual H2 answers cannot replace these comparisons.

## Is the complete scope assigned to useful, unique owners? {#scope-and-ownership}

Map every required beginning, ending, interval, person, event, fact, constraint, relationship and result to its smallest current owner. Report omissions, incompatible duplicate owners, independently useful content hidden in unrelated units, and files whose removal changes no fact, progression, relationship, experience or formal effect. Split independently changeable or refutable facts and progressions; keep dependent attributes and inseparable continuous operations together. Remove or reconnect purposeless material. Only an authorized scope decision permits an omission.

Compare central and peripheral development: settings and treatments need sufficient decisions, scripts and screenplay sufficient execution and experience. Identify core omissions and disproportionate expansion. Counts, lengths, parent-child ratios and diffs locate passages to read, not quality verdicts.

## Are layer boundaries and work-wide choices consistent? {#layer-boundaries}

Identify the semantic owners: settings establishes world facts, appearance and baseline constraints; treatments events, event time and place and reveal order; scripts partition, scene execution, staged audiovisual state and meaningful view; final screenplay finished emphasis, image-sound relations, transitions and temporal experience. A direct short owns necessary facts and choices in its actual brief and screenplay, with separately adopted compact settings where present, rather than invented skipped stages.

Give language, audience, variants, registers, code-switching, access rules and expressive form one identifiable instruction or work-contract owner; character voice remains a world property. Preserve declared nonstandard forms and their audience context. Report downstream inventions, duplicated authority and planning language replacing a layer's output, then repair the earliest owner and consumers. Layout and adapter mechanics do not decide these boundaries.

Compare actual color, material, dimension and unit expressions across the population with established values. Where wording would make artists invent incompatible values, define the needed reference or conversion at that value's owner. Preserve those values downstream without requiring appearance inventories everywhere or removing idiomatic perception that changes no fact.
