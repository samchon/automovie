# Assets

Each exported function under `src/assets/` answers these items in its doc comment, beside [common](common.md) and [implementation](implementation.md). Its folder follows the path of the settings record it realizes, and that record links the folder and embeds the subject's canon images.

## Does the asset realize its settings record? {#settings-fidelity}

Does the function build the subject from the dimensions, proportions, appearance and state variants the record at its folder's settings path establishes, deriving placements, weights, texture coordinates and measurements from its generating definition so that changing the detail level moves none of them? A proportion or appearance the record does not establish is decided in the record first.

## Can the scenes drive and rebuild it? {#controls-and-source}

Does the function expose the controls the scenes' motion and variation use, and rebuild the asset from a fixed source with its usage rights? An adopted or converted asset states which channels and deformation the conversion and runtime retain. Rig complexity no scene uses is not required, and an omitted necessary deformation remains a defect.
