# Settings

Establish usable facts that later authors, artists and performers can find without reconstructing the world. Read [instructions](brief.md#requirements), complete [discovery](discovery.md) before bulk writing and apply the settings questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). Use the adopted scope, references and intended realization to choose the needed specificity.

Work backward from anticipated scenes to discover facts, conditions and knowledge boundaries. Keep baseline relationships and historical states distinct from the events that change or reveal them; [treatments](treatments.md) owns those choices on the long route. Resolve open direction and provisional scale through [direction](direction.md).

Write concise narrative attributes: identity, appearance, character, abilities, voice and constraints as the work needs them. Include dimensions, routes, material, light and sound where they affect recognition, action or continuity. [Record organization](settings-records.md) keeps each independently used subject in one file and its inseparable attributes together; executable generation parameters belong to their source consumer.

Check [scene coverage](settings-records.md#scene-coverage) against the commissioned activity before declaring the population complete, so unnamed participants, set elements and sound sources do not disappear between the brief and implementation.

Use the adopted [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership), with one H1 per subject file and stable, explicitly anchored H2s for independently usable facts or constraints. A compact short may use a few flat records without an outline or unused domains. Complete their selected construction questions and review before its screenplay relies on them. A self-contained direct short may hold sufficient facts in its brief instead.

On the long route, maintain the settings outline as a finding guide: domain H2s and file H3s in reading order, explaining what to find and why it belongs there. Each file entry uses its H1 and an explicit anchor formed from the settings-relative path by removing `.md` and replacing `/` with `--`; each settings file cites its entry. Update navigation with the records as part of settings completion. Existing layouts and any adopted migration follow [layout ownership](../production/layout.md#project-ownership-and-customization).

User-fixed interpretations are canon for this production. Research open facts using [production research](../production/research.md) under [usable facts and their limits](../contracts/story/shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts), attaching support and uncertainty to their owner. Report a conflict between an instruction and its source without silently changing canon. Open supplied reference images before deciding appearance; work contracts own adopted style and form.

Complete facts, explanation, organization and document correction in [construction](workflow.md#construction); settings has no separate expression pass. Repair missing facts here and propagate changes through [upstream repair](repair.md).
