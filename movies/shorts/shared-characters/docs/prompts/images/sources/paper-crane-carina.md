# Carina Malfoy

## Adult identity, voice and restraint {#identity}

Carina is an invented 20-year-old magical student in this adult alternate universe, not a claimed canonical biography. She is approximately 1.70 metres tall, slim, with light skin and straight pale-blond hair cut at the shoulder. At the polygon abstraction her pale hair mass, upright posture, muted green torso and dark trousers distinguish her from Harry; use simple body surfaces rather than detailed clothing. She carries no wand or bag. Her practical responsibilities are her own study and possessions; no authority over Harry is implied.

She wants Harry's company but fears an openly offered invitation will expose her to rejection. She masks uncertainty with brisk demands and precise diction. Her English voice is a clear adult mezzo, medium pitch, close and unforced, with quick clipped openings and a slower, quieter register when she stops defending herself. This is a generic performance direction, not an impersonation. She knows what her own note says and why two cups are ready. Harry has never been told she wants a private tea. The reciprocal relationship and her refusal limits are owned by [their relationship](relationship.md#relationship); her ability to send paper is owned by [the paper](paper-crane.md#paper).
