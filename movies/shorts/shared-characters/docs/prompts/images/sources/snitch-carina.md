# Carina Malfoy

## Identity, performance and limits {#identity}

Carina is a 21-year-old woman and an experienced broom rider in this production's invented adult alternate universe. She is 1.72 metres tall, slim and athletic, with pale skin, a narrow angular face, straight platinum-blond hair tied at the nape, and no visible marks. Her simple fitted charcoal flight outfit has a broad muted-green torso panel and dark boots. She wants to beat Harry cleanly and takes his attention seriously enough to tease him by surname. She masks concern with impatience; she can abandon a sporting advantage for immediate physical danger without becoming willing to confess affection. She can retain a supported seated position while another rider loads the empty rear shaft; she cannot pull an adult up by one arm or stop a free-falling person with a rigid wrist catch. [Brooms](brooms.md#flight) owns the load support available to her, and [their relationship](relationship.md#rivals) owns both people's understanding of one another.

Her mid-register English voice is clear, dry and precisely articulated, with a contemporary southern British accent. The racing taunt projects over flight noise and gives "Potter" a small upward inflection; after exertion, her short response is breathier and quieter but remains intelligible. Surname address marks familiar competitive distance rather than authority. Hair silhouette, green panel, head turns, torso orientation and hand contact must distinguish her without relying on eyebrows or lips. She knows the race route and can see Harry when he remains ahead and to her right; she has no awareness of anything behind her without turning.
