# Harry Potter

## Identity, performance and limits {#identity}

Harry is a 21-year-old man and an experienced broom rider in the same invented adult alternate universe. He is 1.78 metres tall, lean, with light skin, a softer oval face, untidy short black hair, round black glasses and a small forehead scar. His fitted charcoal flight outfit has a broad muted-red torso panel and dark boots. These adopted recognition cues require only a readable silhouette and coarse color; the scar may remain unseen at the chosen distance. No wand, school robe or detailed uniform is used.

He wants to win the pursuit through a decisive reach and accepts a little more reach than his seated support safely allows. He can fly confidently, steer with one hand and make a grasping reach with the other, but cannot remain suspended indefinitely from one hand. Losing his seat leaves him dependent on his remaining grip; he cannot recall a departed broom or arrest his fall by wandless magic. His unused arm remains available to reach a rescuer. [Brooms](brooms.md#flight) owns how a riderless broom continues moving, and [their relationship](relationship.md#rivals) owns his interpretation of Carina.

His mid-to-low English voice is conversational and direct, with a contemporary southern British accent. Breath is interrupted by the slip and returns during recovery; the final observation is spoken only after flight is controlled, as a quiet recognition rather than a joke at her expense. Familiar direct address fits their equal standing. He knows where she was during the race but must look toward her to know her current position. Round glasses, dark hair and the red torso panel distinguish him from Carina at action distance.

Age, physique, behavior thresholds, outfit, voice and the alternate-universe situation are author choices for this production. The named familiar identity cues are adopted fan-scene design, without a claim of researched canonical completeness.
