# Harry Potter

## Harry {#harry}

Harry is an adult wizard in his early twenties and Carina's former schoolmate. He is lean, approximately 1.78 metres tall, with medium-light skin, a short untidy dark hair mass and round dark glasses represented as a simple identity cue. A dark burgundy torso over a charcoal body silhouette separates him from Carina at coarse polygon detail. Exact facial likeness and complex clothing are outside the chosen abstraction. His wand starts and remains in his right hand; his left hand is free for contact. These are adopted appearance and staging inventions, not claims about a novel edition.

Harry enjoys earning an advantage in a fair contest. He advances through changes of distance and watches Carina's wand hand, which can leave his rear unattended. He can hold her right wrist with his left hand, release when the grip turns against him, step back into balance and deflect one simple practice shot with his wand. He cannot see behind himself or identify a rescue before its visible circumstances reach him. A sudden approach can elicit the same practiced left-hand reach. Surprise after danger can leave that hand in contact until he notices or Carina draws attention to it; he then releases willingly.

His adult British English voice is medium-low, lightly rough with relaxed consonants and an unforced conversational pace. His questions are direct rather than ceremonial. Exertion changes breath and available volume; it does not change accent or turn him into a narrator. His relationship and refusal boundaries are in [rivalry](rivalry.md#rivalry); usable magic limits are in [wand magic](wand-magic.md#wand-magic).
