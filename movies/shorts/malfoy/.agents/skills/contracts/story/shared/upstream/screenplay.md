# Upstream repair exposed by final screenplay

Each final screenplay H2 answers about its actual script parents and their treatment and settings causes. A short instead identifies its brief, creative constraints and settings as its actual parents; it does not claim nonexistent upstream stages. Repair follows the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).

## What did audiovisual realization prove missing or unusable upstream? {#screenplay-upstream-repair}

Identify the exact earliest owner of a missing, contradictory, overdetermined or unrealizable inherited decision, including a design that forces a defined downstream failure or prevents an adopted goal or promise. Trace script defects to their treatment or settings causes; distinguish legitimate constraints and source-status records from prescriptions that leave no audiovisual realization to create. Correct the earliest owner's body locally before writing from the repair, naming the owner, defect and corrected passage. Covering it with polished expression leaves its cause in place, and direct settings evidence cannot replace an absent script decision.

If no repair is needed, identify concrete actual parent decisions tested unchanged and how they survived realization. A short tests its brief, settings and screenplay-owned decisions without inventing skipped parents. Name future performance or output verification as pending; this writing stage establishes a realizable plan, not an unmade film.
