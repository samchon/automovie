# Review contiguous segments

Watch each finished short segment in the production's moving Viewer before extending it, with its adopted scene, view, clock and sound, so defects do not spread through unwatched motion. [Visual debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md) owns repeatable observation conditions and cost.

Watch the whole segment at normal speed, listening when sound is commissioned, then replay its exchanges, contacts and joins slowly through the host's seek controls. Judge whether it convinces as the depicted practice against the [performance study](../story/planning/research.md#performance-study); numbers, hashes and receipts support that judgment but cannot close it, and stills or sampled poses cannot replace continuous playback. A joint overlay and frame sheets locate defects after watching; `npm run render -- --film <name> --from <s> --to <s> --every 20` writes a segment's stills for that.

Gather the whole round, repair by cause, replay only the [affected interval](../production/stages.md#iterate-on-the-affected-interval), and extend only from an accepted ending, checking the seam with the next segment. Use the live Viewer for routine debugging and encode only to verify the delivery path. Accepted segments do not establish the whole film; [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) watches the assembled artifact.
