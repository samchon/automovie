# Review contiguous segments

Review each finished short segment before extending it, with its adopted scene, view and clock, so defects do not spread through unreviewed motion. [Visual debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md) owns repeatable observation conditions and cost.

Read the whole segment as consecutive frames at the dense-sheet rate, then step through its exchanges, contacts and joins frame by frame with the host's seek controls. Judge its staging, camera and timing and how its accepted cases join; a case that no longer convinces as the depicted practice returns to [case review](SKILL.md). Numbers, hashes and receipts support that judgment but cannot close it, and a few sparse stills or sampled poses cannot replace the consecutive sequence. Locate defects with a joint overlay on the [dense contact sheets](../review/delivery.md#measure-and-compare) of the 10-second review segments it spans.

Each 10-second review segment, taken in film order, is one round: capture its sheets, review them at once, repair its findings by cause, recheck that [affected interval](../production/stages.md#iterate-on-the-affected-interval), and only then move to the next segment, checking the seam between them. Accepted segments do not establish the whole film; [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) reviews the assembled artifact.
