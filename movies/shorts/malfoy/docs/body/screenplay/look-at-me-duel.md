# Look at Me

A private practice duel between Carina Malfoy and Harry Potter on the training court one Saturday late morning, told as one continuous action in four scenes: shots exchanged at long range, a wrist grip Carina escapes, the deflected shot that loosens the old support's cap over Harry and the same grip turned into her rescue of him, and the quiet after it. The film runs 60 seconds; its scenes begin at 0, 15, 27 and 36 seconds, and times stated in the action are anchors of the film's clock.

### Wand up {#s01-b01}

A flagstone court on bare brown earth under bright high cloud, a low grey wall along its far side and nothing else but, at the right of the frame, one old stone support: a square upright with a heavy grey cap jutting out over open air. Under the cap's back edge runs a pale strip of mortar with a dark crack across it.

CARINA MALFOY, fifteen, stands near us at the south-west edge of the court's centre: platinum hair loose over the black robe, green lining and a green-and-silver tie against the charcoal knit, a pale wand in her right hand. HARRY POTTER, fifteen, stands beyond the court's centre, the support rising behind him: round glasses, untidy black hair, scarlet and gold at his collar and robe, a dark wand in his right hand. A long stretch of open stone lies between them.

At 1.000 Carina's chin lifts. Her shoulders square, her right foot plants forward and her wand comes up to the line of his chest.

CARINA
Wand up, Potter.

HARRY
After you.

He sets his right foot forward and brings his wand up level with hers. Both free hands hang loose beside the hips.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#chin-up), [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#lexicon)
- Intent: Carina opens the practice with a challenge; Harry answers with a shrug of a phrase and comes on guard.
- Outcome: Both stand in the on-guard [stance](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#stance) at the far end of [casting distance](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#distance).
- Consequence: Carina's chin is raised and her leading foot planted; neither body has moved off the line between them.
- Space: Carina at the south-west edge of [the centre](../settings/sets/training-court.md#zone-list), Harry at its north-east edge with the support behind him; the court and the earth around it otherwise empty.
- Camera: wide from the court's south-west corner at chest height, Carina nearer and lower at the left, Harry farther and higher at the right, the support and its cracked strip in frame; held.
- Sound: dialogue "Wand up, Potter." from 1.500 and "After you." at 2.800, as text only; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: start of the film; the cap in place, its lower strip cracked.

### Not the wand {#s01-b02}

From 4.100, Harry moves first: his lead foot sidesteps, the rear foot follows, again and again in short springy steps that carry him round her toward the open west side. His eyes stay on her wand hand. Carina turns on the balls of her feet with him, her chest square to his the whole way round.

CARINA
Look at me, Potter. Not the wand.

HARRY
The wand's the dangerous part.

He checks on his lead foot and circles back the way he came. She turns back with him until the support stands behind him again.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#watches-wand-hand), [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#squares-to-face)
- Intent: Harry circles for an angle and back; Carina keeps square to him and needles him about where he looks.
- Outcome: No opening; the circle returns them to their places, the stance width kept by the [footwork](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#footwork).
- Consequence: none on either body.
- Space: Harry circles round her along the centre's north edge and back to its north-east edge; Carina pivots in place at its south-west edge.
- Camera: full two-shot in profile as Harry starts to circle; at 6.000 a medium on Carina for her line; at 8.000 a held full shot of Harry as he circles back; at 10.000 a medium on Carina as she takes aim.
- Sound: dialogue "Look at me, Potter. Not the wand." at 6.200 and "The wand's the dangerous part." at 8.100, as text only; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: as before.

### The first shot {#s01-b03}

At 11.900 Carina's wand tip settles on his wand hand and stays there. A short flick.

CARINA
Flipendo!

At 12.350 a thin blue-white streak leaves her tip and crosses the open stone at his hand. Harry sweeps his wand down across its path and meets it at 12.750. The streak glances away low past his hip and goes out in a small flash on the flagstones beyond him.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#duelling), [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#duelling)
- Intent: Carina tests him with the practice jinx at his wand hand; Harry turns it aside.
- Outcome: Deflected, as the [outcome table](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#outcomes) gives for a watched shot from this far; the jinx strikes sound stone and only flashes ([wand magic](../settings/world/magic.md#practice-only)).
- Consequence: none on either body; the floor unmarked.
- Space: unchanged.
- Camera: full two-shot in profile, held, the streak crossing the frame between them.
- Sound: dialogue "Flipendo!" as text only; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: as before.

### The scarlet answer {#s01-b04}

Harry gives a step back. From 13.500 his wand comes back on her and holds still. He flicks.

HARRY
Expelliarmus!

A scarlet streak flies at her wand hand. Carina's wand is already still on him; at 13.960 she sweeps it up.

CARINA
Protego!

A pale-blue oval of light grows from her wand tip to her full height between them. The scarlet streak meets it and goes out. She holds it a moment, wand raised and feet planted, the corner of her mouth lifting. Then the wand lowers and the oval fades where it stood.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#duelling), [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#duelling)
- Intent: Harry tries to disarm her; Carina raises her shield.
- Outcome: Blocked, as the [outcome table](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#outcomes) gives for a shot against a standing, planted shield; her shield is up in a quarter second ([the shield](../settings/props/shield.md#lifecycle)).
- Consequence: Carina keeps her wand; none on either body.
- Space: unchanged; the shield forms and fades midway between them, at her wand tip.
- Camera: full two-shot in profile, held.
- Sound: dialogue "Expelliarmus!" and "Protego!" as text only; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: as before.

### She comes in {#s02-b01}

From 15 seconds, Carina stops trading shots. She drives off her back foot and comes at him in quick running steps; her wand beats his aside with a wooden click and she carries on inside it, her tip hunting for his chest. Harry's left hand opens toward her and at 16.550 closes round her right wrist from the outside. He turns the wrist out, and her wand tip swings away from him.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#effort), [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#reaches-for-wrist)
- Intent: Carina closes past his wand to win at close range; Harry stops her with his reflex grip.
- Outcome: Held: his grip turns her wand away, as the [grip law](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#grip) describes; she has not yet answered it.
- Consequence: Harry's left hand round her right wrist; her wand pointing out and away from both; their chests inside [grip range](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#distance).
- Space: Carina runs from the centre's south-west edge across it and meets Harry at its north-east edge.
- Camera: a full two-shot in profile for the run, then one medium two-shot in profile from just before the grip until he lets go, both wands, both free hands and the held wrist inside the frame's margins.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the first grip begins.

### The underarm turn {#s02-b02}

At 16.600 Carina bends the caught elbow and lifts their joined hands above her head. She steps in under them and turns, her back passing beneath his raised arm, all the way round, her wand pointing out and away the whole time. Harry's wrist rolls with her; before it can be twisted, at 17.500, his fingers open. She comes round on a planted foot, facing him again, her wrist free.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#duelling), [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#duelling)
- Intent: Carina escapes the grip by the underarm turn; Harry lets go rather than have his wrist forced.
- Outcome: Escaped: the gripper releases, as the [outcome table](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#outcomes) gives for a grip answered by the underarm turn.
- Consequence: Carina's wrist free and unhurt; Harry's left hand open and empty; both facing each other again at wand reach.
- Space: both turn in place on the centre's north-east side.
- Camera: the same medium two-shot, arcing with the turn to keep both faces and the joined hands in frame; the shot ends once he has let go.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the first grip ends; Harry's left hand is free.

- Camera: medium two-shot in profile, Carina at the left, Harry at the right.
- Sound: dialogue "That's new." at 18.700, "Stop grabbing, Potter." at 19.900 and "Stop getting so close." at 21.400, as text only; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.

### Back to range {#s02-b04}

From 23.000 they part. Carina gives ground in short steps, rear foot first, to the centre's south-west side. Harry backs away on a diagonal in short springy steps, his eyes on her wand hand and never once on what is behind him, until the support stands at his back and the cap juts out above and behind his head, its cracked pale strip hanging over him. Carina turns to keep him square.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#watches-wand-hand), [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#squares-to-face)
- Intent: Both reopen casting distance; Harry backs off without looking where he goes.
- Outcome: They stand at the near end of [casting distance](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#distance), still far enough apart for a watched shot to be turned ([deflection](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#deflection)).
- Consequence: none on either body.
- Space: Harry backs out of the centre into [the support zone](../settings/sets/training-court.md#zone-list), under [the cap's front edge](../settings/sets/training-court.md#landmarks), just inside [the danger region](../settings/sets/training-court.md#danger-region) where it comes nearest the centre; Carina gives ground to the centre's south-west side.
- Camera: cuts to a full shot from the court's south-east corner, framed for the whole parting, Carina nearer and lower at the left, Harry farther and higher at the right, the cap's front edge high above him in the tall frame; held.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: Harry stands under the cap from here until he is moved out from under it.

CARINA

### The cap tips {#s03-b02}

The cap's front edge dips. Its back edge still rests on the ledge, the upper mortar still holds it to the upright, and a thin stream of grit runs from the broken joint over Harry's head. Harry, his eyes on her wand hand, brings his wand back on guard.

At 30.230 Carina's wand tip falls below his chest. Her eyes go above him. She drives off her back foot and runs at him.

CARINA
Potter!

- Outcome: The cap tips while the upper web holds ([stages](../../research/physics/falling-masonry.md#stages)); Carina's wand drops one [reaction time](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#reaction) after the tipping shows, and her [run](../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#run-in) begins.

### The same reach {#s03-b03}

### Out from under {#s03-b04}

At 31.370 she pushes off and swings aside toward the open west, her shoulder carrying him past her; he goes with her in two quick steps, knees bent, his hand riding her arm, and plants at her side out from under the cap.

Behind them the upper mortar breaks. The cap slides off its ledge, turns forward through the open air in front of the upright and lands flat on a broad face on the flagstones where Harry stood. The upright stands. The grit stops.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#duelling), [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#duelling)
- Intent: Carina guides Harry two quick steps out from under the cap; he moves with her.
- Outcome: Clear, as the [outcome table](../../research/physics/falling-masonry.md#outcomes) gives when both bodies and both wands are outside the danger region before the cap reaches head height ([clearance](../../research/physics/falling-masonry.md#clearance)); the cap is released and falls ([stages](../../research/physics/falling-masonry.md#stages)).
- Consequence: the cap lies flat where Harry stood, the upright still standing; neither student touched by stone.
- Space: Carina gives one quick step west and Harry two quick steps past her toward the centre's south side, out of the support zone; they end side by side in the centre facing the support; the cap falls inside the danger region.
- Camera: the same shot, arcing from the south-east round to the court's south edge as they step out, holding both whole bodies and the cap's path together as it falls behind them.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.

### The shield {#s03-b05}

- Sound: dialogue "Protego!" as text only; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
