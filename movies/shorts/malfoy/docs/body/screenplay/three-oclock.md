# Three O'Clock

Carina Malfoy rehearses an invitation to an empty stone bench until Harry Potter overhears the third try; a note held out and snatched back, a note let fall, and two heads bending for it at once finally put it in his hand. One late afternoon in the side courtyard, sixty seconds, silent. Times stated in the action are anchors of the film's clock.

### The first try {#s01-b01}

From 0 seconds, low sun lies across a paved recess and turns its north wall a warm beige. Against that wall stands one grey stone bench, empty. Two paces in front of it waits Carina Malfoy, fifteen, her platinum hair loose above the shoulders of an open black robe, a green-and-silver tie at her white collar. Between her right thumb and fingers she pinches a small white note, the writing turned toward herself.

She walks the two paces up to the bench and lifts the note toward the empty seat, at the height of a seated boy's chest.

CARINA
Potter—

That is as far as it gets. The note sinks back against her chest. Still facing the seat, she steps backward the two paces to where she started.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#walk), at her rehearsing pace
- Intent: Carina rehearses inviting Harry by addressing the empty seat, and breaks off after his name.
- Outcome: She arrives at the bench front facing the seat, as the [walking table](../../research/everyday/walking.md#outcomes) gives for a free landmark; the held-out note is an offer with nobody to take it, so it [returns to her chest](../../research/everyday/handover.md#withdrawal).
- Consequence: She stands at the starting place again, facing the bench, the note at her chest.
- Space: Carina walks from the starting place to the bench front and steps backward to the starting place.
- Camera: wide from the east end of the recess, looking west along the bench at standing eye height, holding Carina and the bench: Carina in profile, the bench at the right of the frame, the west wall and its passage gap far behind her at the left.
- Sound: dialogue "Potter—"; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: start of the film; the note in Carina's right hand.

### The second try {#s01-b02}

From 6.3 seconds, she comes forward again, slower, and holds the note lower, nearer her waist.

CARINA
(quieter)
Saturday. Three o'clock. If you're free.
(quieter still)
...Please.

The word stops her as if someone else had said it. The note comes back to her chest. She turns her back on the bench and stands facing the open lawn, and the fingertips of her empty left hand press against her forehead for a long second. Then she walks the two paces back to her place and turns round to face the bench once more.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#forehead-touch), whose [lines go quiet and slow](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#lexicon) when she is exposed
- Intent: Carina tries a softer invitation and hears herself say the word she never says.
- Outcome: The bench stays empty; the second offer to the seat returns to her chest like the first.
- Consequence: Embarrassed and unwatched, she turns away and touches her forehead with her left fingertips for about a second; she ends at the starting place facing the bench.
- Space: Carina walks from the starting place to the bench front, turns to face the open south, walks back to the starting place and turns to the bench.
- Camera: cut in to a full shot from the same east side at standing eye height that holds her and the seat: Carina in profile with the note held toward the empty bench; when she turns away, her face and the hand at her forehead are in profile toward the frame's left. At 14.7 seconds, as she walks back to her place, cut back to the wide from the east end.
- Sound: dialogue "Saturday. Three o'clock. If you're free. ...Please."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note in her right hand; the west gap has been empty so far.

### Harry comes in {#s01-b03}

From 18 seconds, she walks up to the bench a third time. Behind her back, in the gap of the west wall, Harry Potter steps into the recess: fifteen, untidy black hair, round glasses, a scarlet-and-gold tie under an open black robe, his hands in his trouser pockets. He sees her and slows.

The note rises toward the seat; her shoulders stay down. Harry comes along the paving and stops an arm's length behind her left shoulder. At 23.1 seconds:

CARINA
(very quiet, the last words slow)
I just want you to come.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#walk), unhurried with his hands in his pockets; [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#walk), at her rehearsing pace
- Intent: Carina makes her third try at the seat; Harry, crossing the empty recess, stops to listen.
- Outcome: Harry stops at conversational distance from her, as the [walking table](../../research/everyday/walking.md#outcomes) gives for a landmark another person occupies; Carina, facing the bench, has the gap behind her left shoulder and [cannot see him come in](../settings/sets/side-courtyard.md#entrance).
- Consequence: Harry has heard an invitation with no place or time in it; Carina does not know he is there.
- Space: Carina walks from the starting place to the bench front; Harry walks from the west gap along the clear paving to a point behind her left shoulder.
- Camera: the wide from the east end holds: Carina in profile walks up to the bench while Harry comes through the gap in the background at the left and crosses toward her. The audience sees him before she does.
- Sound: dialogue "I just want you to come."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note held out toward the seat; Harry's hands in his pockets.

### Come where {#s02-b01}

From 24.9 seconds:

HARRY
Come where?

Carina's arm stops in the air. A breath later her right hand whips the note behind her right hip, and her left hand goes still against her skirt. She turns round on the spot, two small steps, until she is facing him.

CARINA
Nowhere.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#lexicon), plain and direct; [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#hides-the-personal)
- Intent: Harry asks the question her invitation left open; Carina hides the note and denies there is anywhere.
- Outcome: She realizes she is seen and hides the note behind her right hip, per her habit; she turns to face him.
- Consequence: The note is out of his sight behind her hip; they stand face to face at conversational distance.
- Space: Carina turns in place at the bench front to face Harry on the clear paving.
- Camera: cut to a full two-shot from the south-east at standing eye height, close enough to hold the hiding hand: Harry at the left, Carina at the right with the lit wall behind her; the camera arcs a little as she turns so the two stand in profile across the frame.
- Sound: dialogue "Come where?" and "Nowhere."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note behind Carina's right hip; Harry's hands still in his pockets.

### The first offer {#s02-b02}

From 27.9 seconds, Harry's eyes go to the hand behind her hip. He takes his right hand out of his pocket and holds it out between them, palm up, and leaves it there.

Carina lifts her chin. The note comes out from behind her and travels toward his palm, and stops a handspan short of it. Before anything can touch it, the note snaps back to her chest.

Harry lifts both open hands toward her and steps back half a metre.

HARRY
No hurry.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#gesture), whose open palm is offered and then held still; [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#withdraws-first-offer), whose chin lifts while [her hand reaches out and draws back](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#gesture); [Harry giving room](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#gives-room)
- Intent: Harry offers to take whatever she is hiding; Carina offers the note and cannot let it go.
- Outcome: Withdrawn before contact, as the [handover table](../../research/everyday/handover.md#outcomes) gives for an offer withdrawn before contact: the withdrawal starts at the handspan's stop, before his fingers can touch the note, and she keeps it.
- Consequence: The note is at her chest; Harry, seeing her retract it, lifts both palms and steps half a metre back within his habit's reaction, leaving an empty space of paving between them.
- Space: Harry gives half a metre toward the west gap; Carina stays at the bench front.
- Camera: the full two-shot holds both hands and the note inside the margins from the offer to the withdrawal and widens with him as he steps away.
- Sound: dialogue "No hurry."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note at Carina's chest; Harry's left hand out of its pocket from here on.

### Let fall {#s02-b03}

From 33 seconds, Carina looks at the space he has left her. She steps into it, up to handing distance, and opens her fingers.

The note slips from her hand, rocks down past her knee from side to side and settles flat on the paving between their shoes.

CARINA
You dropped something.

HARRY
Did I.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#wants), who will not ask outright; [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#lexicon), answering with a dry understatement
- Intent: Carina gives the note without handing it, by letting it fall at his feet and calling it his.
- Outcome: Released with no hand closed on it, the note [flutters down and lands flat](../../research/everyday/handover.md#dropped), as [the note's dropped state](../settings/props/invitation-note.md#dropped) describes.
- Consequence: The note lies still on the paving between their shoes; Carina's right hand is empty for the first time.
- Space: Carina steps from the bench front onto the clear paving, back to arm's length from Harry.
- Camera: cut to a lower full two-shot square to their profiles, low enough that the paving between their shoes stays in frame; it tilts with the falling note until the note lies still.
- Sound: dialogue "You dropped something." and "Did I."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note on the paving between them.

### Two heads {#s02-b04}

From 37.4 seconds, he bends for it. So does she. Both plant their feet wider, both bend their knees, both tip forward from the hips, and their heads, travelling out over their toes, meet forehead to forehead with a soft bump before either hand reaches the stone.

They hold there, bent, for an instant. Then both straighten, apart. Harry's open hands come up again and he steps back another half metre, then pushes his glasses up his nose with one finger. Carina's empty hand hangs still at her side.

HARRY
Sorry.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#gives-room), [pushing up his glasses](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#pushes-glasses); [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#effort), bound by embarrassment
- Intent: Harry bends to pick up the note she called his; Carina bends at the same moment to take it back.
- Outcome: Bending toward the same point from arm's length, their heads meet at forehead height before their hands reach the floor, as [the picking-up law](../../research/everyday/handover.md#pick-up) gives for two people that far apart; neither touches the note.
- Consequence: A light bump with no mark. Both rise. Bumped, Harry lifts his palms and steps half a metre back, then pushes his glasses up within a second of the surprise; Carina's flow binds, her free hand still at her side.
- Space: Harry gives a second half metre toward the west gap; Carina stays over the note.
- Camera: the same low full two-shot, unbroken from the start of the bend through the bump to their separation, easing back with his step.
- Sound: dialogue "Sorry."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note untouched on the paving.

### Again {#s02-b05}

From 41.2 seconds, they both look down at the note. They both start to bend for it again, and both stop, leaning a little forward, each watching the other. They straighten together.

Harry's hands lift a third time, and his third half metre back brings him to the edge of the gap in the west wall. He lowers his left hand. His right stays out, palm up, still.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#gives-room), then [his open palm held still](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#gesture); [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#harry), who lowers her guard when he gives her room
- Intent: Each starts to pick the note up for the other; each stops to let the other go first.
- Outcome: Both stop short in a small forward lean and one yields, as the [walking table](../../research/everyday/walking.md#outcomes) gives when two people move into the same gap at once; here the gap is the paving over the note, and Harry is the one who yields.
- Consequence: Carina has stopped short, so Harry gives room once more and is left at the gap's edge, his right palm open toward her: the note is hers to pick up.
- Space: Harry gives a third half metre and stands at the edge of the west gap; Carina stays over the note on the clear paving.
- Camera: the same low full two-shot holds both as they start to bend again and stop, the note on the paving between them; as Harry gives room a third time, cut to a wide from the open south side at standing eye height: both full figures, the note between them and the west gap behind Harry.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note on the paving; Harry's right palm out.

### Given {#s02-b06}

From 45 seconds, Carina bends alone. She pinches the note by its short edge, straightens, and walks the two paces to him. She lays it in his palm and keeps hold of it until his fingers close on it, at 49.7 seconds. Only then, at 50.1, does she let go.

CARINA
(quiet, the last words slow)
Saturday. Three o'clock.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#withdraws-first-offer), whose later offer completes; [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#everyday)
- Intent: Carina picks the note up and gives it to him properly.
- Outcome: She [picks the note up](../../research/everyday/handover.md#pick-up) by an edge; then transferred, as the [handover table](../../research/everyday/handover.md#outcomes) gives when the giver opens after the receiver closes, under [the ownership rule](../../research/everyday/handover.md#ownership).
- Consequence: The note is in Harry's right hand; Carina's hand is empty.
- Space: Carina walks from the clear paving to handing distance in front of Harry at the edge of the west gap.
- Camera: the camera follows her down to the note and up again, then cut to a full two-shot from the south-east that holds both hands and the note from his fingers' first touch to her release.
- Sound: dialogue "Saturday. Three o'clock."; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the note in Harry's right hand.

### This one {#s03-b01}

From 50.7 seconds, Harry looks down at the note in his hand.

HARRY
Courtyard.

This one?

CARINA
This one.

### The seat {#s03-b02}

From 54.5 seconds, as Carina hurries out through the gap and the passage beyond the wall takes her out of sight, Harry walks back across the paving to the bench. He turns and sits down in the middle of it, in the seat she was talking to, and rests the note on his knee. The picture stays on him there until it ends at 60 seconds.

- Cast: [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#carina), who likes her company more than he lets on; [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#walk), hurrying
- Intent: Harry tries the place she has named; Carina leaves.
- Outcome: He walks to the bench centre and sits, as the [walking table](../../research/everyday/walking.md#outcomes) gives for a free landmark and the [sitting table](../../research/everyday/sitting.md#outcomes) gives when the calves reach the seat edge.
- Consequence: Harry sits with his feet flat and the note on his knee; the seat Carina rehearsed to is filled. Carina is gone into the side passage. The film ends at 60 seconds.
- Space: Carina walks from the west gap out of view into the passage; Harry walks from the edge of the west gap along the clear paving to the bench front and sits on the bench at its centre.
- Camera: the two-shot from the south-east holds Carina going out through the gap as Harry walks away from her; at 56.3 seconds, as she goes, cut to a wide from the open south side that holds Harry walking to the bench and sitting down on it, the lit wall behind him, to the end.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley none; ambience none; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: end of the film; the note on Harry's knee; Carina out of view.
