# Snitch Before You

One continuous Saturday practice match over the Hogwarts practice pitch, framed 9:16 and silent, so its dialogue exists only on this page. Two Seekers race for the Snitch out past the east goals; one slips, and the other lets the Snitch go to bring him across onto her own broom. Times stated in the action are anchors of the film's clock. The twelve supporting players carry [authoring IDs](../settings/crowds/practice-teams.md#players) in brackets for staging; nobody shows or speaks them.

### The game {#s01-b01}

A long oval of mown grass under a pale October sky. At the near end three pale-gold posts hold up their hoops; at the far end, three more. Between them, fourteen riders.

A scarlet Quaffle leaves the hand of a Gryffindor Chaser with dark braids [G-C1] and drops into the arms of a slight blonde teammate [G-C2], who takes it against her chest. The third, a wiry girl with a low tail [G-C3], swings wide to open the next lane. Three Slytherin Chasers [S-C1, S-C2, S-C3] turn with them, each a few lengths off one Gryffindor, on the side of their own hoops.

A black Bludger cuts toward the lane. A heavy Slytherin Beater [S-B1] meets it with a swing of his bat and sends it north, out of the way. The red-haired Gryffindor twins [G-B1, G-B2] and the second Slytherin Beater [S-B2] hang back on the other Bludger, bats ready. Before each set of hoops a Keeper [S-K, G-K] patrols, turning with the ball.

High above the game, two riders drift in slow circles, looking for something else. CARINA MALFOY, fifteen, platinum hair tied low at her neck, a green-and-silver tie under her white collar. HARRY POTTER, fifteen, round glasses, black hair standing up at the back, scarlet and gold. She is on his left.

- Cast: [Carina](../settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#flying), [Harry](../settings/cast/harry-potter.md#flying), [the twelve players](../settings/crowds/practice-teams.md#in-play)

- Outcome: the pass is received and S-B1's swing strikes the Bludger away, as the [practice-play outcome table](../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#outcomes) gives.
