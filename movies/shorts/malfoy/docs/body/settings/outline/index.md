# Settings outline

The settings records of the four Malfoy shorts, in reading order: the world's rules first, then the people, the crowds, the props, the places, the camera's look and the sound.

## World

### Broom flight {#world--flight}

How a broom flies under its rider: seat and control, speeds and turns, reach and balance, hanging, riderless brooms and two riders on one broom.

### Hogwarts {#world--hogwarts}

The school's rules as the films use them: uniforms, students' freedoms, adults, wands at school and the school day.

### Wand magic {#world--magic}

Casting, the visible cue, practice-only spells, deflection, the paper charm and what magic does not do.

### Quidditch practice {#world--quidditch}

The game, this practice, conduct and how play stops.

### The Malfoy shorts {#world--work}

The four standalone films: their audience, delivery, what they leave out and where the rules live.

## Cast

### Carina Malfoy {#cast--carina-malfoy}

Her body, wardrobe, movement, repertoire, habits and what she wants.

### Harry Potter {#cast--harry-potter}

His body, wardrobe, movement, repertoire, habits and what he wants.

## Crowds

### The practice teams {#crowds--practice-teams}

The twelve supporting players of the Slytherin and Gryffindor practice sides.

## Props

### The Beaters' bats {#props--beater-bats}

The two teams' bats and how a Beater holds and swings one.

### The two Bludgers {#props--bludgers}

The iron balls the Beaters keep in play.

### The brooms {#props--brooms}

The practice brooms and their states: ridden, carrying a hanging rider, riderless and carrying two.

### The support's cap and its debris {#props--falling-masonry}

The cracked stone cap on the training court's support and everything its failure releases.

### The invitation note {#props--invitation-note}

Carina's folded note and its handling.

### The paper crane {#props--paper-crane}

The charmed paper crane and its stages from folded to open.

### The Quaffle {#props--quaffle}

The Chasers' ball.

### The shield charm's surface {#props--shield}

How a shield charm forms, stands and fades.

### The Golden Snitch {#props--snitch}

The small winged ball the Seekers chase.

### The two tea cups {#props--tea-cups}

The cups on the sill and how they are carried and handed over.

### The two wands {#props--wands}

Carina's and Harry's wands and how each is held.
