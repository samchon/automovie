# The four films

The work as a whole: what it promises, how its four films relate, when they happen and how they feel. Rules of magic, duelling and Quidditch have their own records: [magic](magic.md), [practice duelling](practice-duelling.md) and [Quidditch](quidditch.md).

## Rules {#rules}

### Four separate sixty-second films {#release-units}

The work is four films, each a complete story of 60 seconds, framed vertically at 9:16 (720 × 1280 pixels) and played at 24 frames per second: `look-at-me-duel`, `paper-crane-invitation`, `snitch-before-you` and `three-oclock` ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#four-films)). Each is released and watched on its own; none needs another to be understood, and they may be watched in any order. Sixty seconds is a fixed ceiling and length, not a target to pad: each film holds one physical exchange and its turn.

### Every film starts from the same baseline {#baseline-reset}

Each film begins from the baseline states these settings record — the two leads' relationship ([direction](../../../instructions/direction.md#baseline-relationship)), their bodies, clothes and knowledge, the places and the objects intact — and its events end with it. Nothing said, broken, given or learned in one film exists in another ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#shared-world)). A record that describes a change describes what may happen within one film and how it resets.

### What the work promises {#promise}

- **Audience:** viewers of short vertical fan video who know Harry Potter's school, houses and Quidditch, and need no other knowledge of these films.
- **Central experience:** watching two shy fifteen-year-olds move closer through one physical exchange — a grip, a shared hold, a catch, two people bending at once.
- **Recurring reward:** the moment a touch that began as a pretext, an accident or a rescue becomes an invitation both of them accept.
- **Engine:** Carina attempts something she cannot quite say; Harry reads her late; a contact between their bodies, or between their hands and one object, does what words could not.
- **Variation:** each film changes the arena (a duelling room, a courtyard bench, a Quidditch pitch, a courtyard walk) and the contact (a repeated wrist grip, a two-handed tug on a paper crane, a Snitch given up for Harry's sake, two people bending at the same moment).
- **Title promise:** each title names its film's device — a demand to be looked at, a paper crane, a Snitch given up, an hour named in an invitation.

### The canon frame {#canon-frame}

Hogwarts, its houses, wands, spells, brooms and Quidditch are taken from Harry Potter canon as an adopted choice, not checked against the books ([canon boundary](../../../instructions/commission.md#canon-boundary)). Two departures are deliberate: Carina Malfoy stands in the place canon gives Draco Malfoy, who does not exist in this world ([direction](../../../instructions/direction.md#carina-malfoy-family)); and the dark events of the fifth year — the war, the Ministry, the new Defence teacher — never touch these films ([direction](../../../instructions/direction.md#quiet-autumn-fifth-year)). No published Carina Malfoy story is adapted.

### Who appears {#population}

Carina and Harry appear in all four films and are the only people in `look-at-me-duel`, `paper-crane-invitation` and `three-oclock`, each of which happens in a place that is empty at that hour ([duelling room](../sets/duelling-room.md#empty-room), [courtyard](../sets/clock-tower-courtyard.md#occupancy)). `snitch-before-you` adds Madam Hooch, who referees ([Hooch](../cast/rolanda-hooch.md)), and the twelve other riders of the two teams ([riders](../crowds/quidditch-riders.md)). Nobody else is seen or heard; friends, family and teachers exist offscreen and are not mentioned.

### People are bare bodies in the hint {#bare-bodies}

In the delivered hint every person is a bare human body carrying pose and motion only, and sets and props are coarse shapes that stay identifiable ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#bare-bodies)). Clothing, hair, faces and surface colours recorded in these settings are facts of the world for the reference images diffusion uses later; on the hint, people are told apart by body form, height and carriage, and objects by silhouette and size.

## Time, weather and season {#time-weather-season}

### Mid-October of the fifth year {#october}

All four films happen in the middle of October in the autumn term of the leads' fifth year, when both are fifteen. Canon's calendar places that in 1995; no date or year is ever shown or said. Each film happens on its own day; the days are not ordered.

### Mid-afternoon {#afternoon}

Each film happens between 14:30 and 16:30 by the castle clocks, after the day's last lesson for the two leads; light is warm and low. Hogwarts lies in the Scottish Highlands (canon adopted), taken as about 57° N. Inference for that latitude in mid-October, on British Summer Time: the sun stands about 20° above the horizon toward the south-south-west at 14:30 and sinks to about 10° toward the south-west by 16:30 (each value uncertain by about 3°), so shadows are long — about two and a half to six times the height of what casts them (1/tan 20° ≈ 2.7; 1/tan 10° ≈ 5.7). The [courtyard clock](../sets/clock-tower-courtyard.md#clock-face) shows the hour where a film needs it.

### Cool, dry and still {#weather}

The air is cool (10–14 °C), the sky clear to broken cloud, the ground dry, and wind below about 3 m/s — calm enough that paper flies where it is charmed to and a practice match is flown. Fallen leaves lie in the courtyard's corners. No film has rain, fog or wind strong enough to move people.

## Tone {#tone}

### Light, warm and physical {#register}

The films are light romantic comedy-drama ([direction](../../../instructions/direction.md#tone)): embarrassment, nerve and kindness. Humour comes from bodies — a reach that comes too early, a walk repeated, two people moving at the same moment — not from jokes or insults. Danger lasts a moment and is ended by a hand.

### Where closeness stops {#closeness-limit}

The films end at shared closeness that suits two fifteen-year-olds: a held wrist or hand, shoulders touching on a bench, one riding behind the other with arms around a waist. There is no kiss, no real injury, no blood and no cruelty in any film.
