# Hogwarts

## Rules {#rules}

### The school {#school}

Hogwarts is a boarding school of magic in a castle in the Scottish Highlands, with students from eleven to seventeen in seven years; fifteen-year-olds are in their fifth year. Students belong to four houses; these films use two, Slytherin, whose colours are green and silver, and Gryffindor, whose colours are scarlet and gold. These are Harry Potter canon facts the production adopts ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#source-boundary)).

### Uniform grammar {#uniform-grammar}

Every student in these films wears the same school uniform, distinguished only by house colour, as the commission fixes ([uniforms](../../../instructions/commission.md#uniforms)). Its layers, from the inside out, are the design rules every student follows:

| Layer | Form | House colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shirt | White, long-sleeved, with a pointed collar that shows above the knit | None |
| Tie | Knotted at the collar, hanging to the waist under the knit, with diagonal stripes 2 cm wide | Slytherin dark green with silver stripes; Gryffindor scarlet with gold stripes |
| Knit | Charcoal V-neck jumper, hip-length, sleeves to the wrist; a 1 cm house-colour band edges the V | Green or scarlet band |
| Robe | Black, open down the front, knee-length, with wide sleeves that end at the wrist bone; the lining shows at the open front edges; a small house crest sits on the left chest as a plain coloured shield | Lining and crest green or scarlet |
| Bottoms | Charcoal pleated skirt ending just above the knee with opaque black tights, or straight charcoal trousers | None |
| Shoes | Flat black leather school shoes with laces or a strap | None |

At the coarse detail of these films each layer is one simple shape of its colour: a white collar wedge, a tie strip, a charcoal torso with a V band, two black robe panels and sleeves with a coloured edge, the skirt or trouser legs and black shoe blocks. The robe never covers the hands, the knees as they bend or the feet. A student who will fly folds the robe's lower front panels back and tucks them into the waistband so no cloth reaches the shaft, the thighs or the bristles; a skirted student wears opaque black shorts under the skirt for riding.

### Students' freedoms {#freedoms}

After the day's last lesson and at weekends students may go where they like in the castle and on the grounds, alone or in pairs, until dusk. Two students may practise spells on each other at the outdoor training court without a teacher, using practice spells only ([wand magic](magic.md#practice-only)). The house teams fly practice matches on the practice pitch when their captains have arranged it. These freedoms are the production's choices.

### Adults {#adults}

No teacher, prefect, parent or other adult appears in the films. The places are ones no adult needs at those times: an outdoor practice court nobody has booked, a corridor bay off the main route after lessons, a recess beside a side wing, and a pitch booked by the two teams themselves.

### Wands at school {#wands}

Every student carries a wand. Casting at another student outside agreed practice is against school rules and none of the cast does it.

## Time, weather and season {#time}

### School day {#school-day}

Lessons end in mid-afternoon; the corridor and courtyard films take place in the hour after the last lesson, the duel and the practice match on a Saturday. The world's season and weather defaults are in [the work](work.md#autumn).
