# Broom flight

## Rules {#rules}

### A broom flies under its rider {#broom-magic}

A school broom flies by its own enchantment, steered by the rider astride it; nothing else in these films flies people ([wand magic](magic.md#limits)). The broom itself is described in [brooms](../props/brooms.md).

### Seat and control {#seat}

The rider's pelvis rests on the shaft at the seat point and the closed thighs hold it; both hands hold the shaft ahead and steer. Pulling the nose up climbs and pushing it down dives, within 45° of level; leaning and shifting the seat rolls the broom into a bank; leaning the chest down speeds it up and sitting up slows it ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#seat)).

### Speeds and turns {#speeds}

Riders hover and manoeuvre at up to 3 m/s, cruise in play at 6–10 m/s and sprint at 12–18 m/s, accelerating and braking under 3 m/s². A turn's bank angle φ satisfies tan φ = v² / (g r) and never exceeds 60°; a rider who needs a tighter turn must slow down ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#banking)).

### Reach and balance {#reach}

A seated rider can reach one arm length plus a 0.3 m trunk lean without unloading the seat. Reaching farther slides the pelvis off; past the shaft's side the thighs lose their hold and the rider drops, keeping a hold only with a hand already on the shaft ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#overreach)).

### Falling and hanging {#falling}

A rider who leaves the broom falls under ordinary gravity; no charm cushions the fall in these films, and a fall from play height would cause real harm. A rider hanging by the hands under a moving broom can hold two-handed for 30 s or more and one-handed for about 10 s, and the broom keeps its line while sinking 0.3–0.5 m/s under the unseated load ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#hanging)).

### Riderless brooms {#riderless}

A broom with no rider keeps its heading, slows at about 1 m/s² and sinks gently. It does not follow its rider and does not come when called ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#riderless)).

### Two riders {#tandem}

One broom carries two riders astride, the second behind the first; it dips under the added weight, recovers in a shallow climb over 2–3 s and then answers more slowly, banking no more than 30°. A hanging rider can move onto another broom's empty rear section only when the shafts are parallel, their speeds matched and the section within reach, taking the new shaft before letting go of the old ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#transfer)).

## Time, weather and season {#time}

### Fair weather {#fair}

Brooms fly in the dry, still or lightly breezy weather of every film ([the work](work.md#autumn)); no film needs wind or rain on a broom.
