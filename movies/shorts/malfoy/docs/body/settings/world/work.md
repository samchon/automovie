# The Malfoy shorts

## Work and scope {#scope}

### Four standalone films {#films}

The work is four original English fan shorts about two fifteen-year-old Hogwarts schoolmates, Carina Malfoy of Slytherin and Harry Potter of Gryffindor ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#four-films)). Each film is a complete story of its own: none continues another, and nothing one film changes, such as a fallen stone or an accepted invitation, exists in another. All four share the cast, the school, the props, the magic and the look recorded in these settings.

| Film | Place | Its world |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `look-at-me-duel` | [Training court](../sets/training-court.md) | A private practice duel beside an old damaged stone support |
| `paper-crane-invitation` | [School corridor](../sets/school-corridor.md) | A quiet window bay with a bench, two cups of tea and a charmed paper crane |
| `snitch-before-you` | [Quidditch pitch](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md) | A fourteen-rider practice match of the two house teams |
| `three-oclock` | [Side courtyard](../sets/side-courtyard.md) | An empty recess with a stone bench and a written invitation |

### Audience and promise {#promise}

The audience is general, teenage and adult, and needs no knowledge of the books beyond the idea of a school of magic. The central experience of every film is a rivalry between equals that turns, through what their bodies do, into care that neither says aloud. The recurring rewards are competitive banter, physical near-misses, small objects passing between hands, and a protective or generous choice that costs the chooser something. Each film varies the engine by place and practice: a duel, a tug over paper, a race in the air, a rehearsed invitation.

### Delivery {#delivery}

Each film runs 60 seconds, vertical 9:16 at 720 × 1280 and 24 fps, drawn with Human polygon figures and coarse geometry, and silent ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#format)). The four together are the whole delivery; no series continues them.

### What the films leave out {#exclusions}

No teacher, parent or other adult, no spectator and no unnamed passer-by appears; the places and times are chosen where none would be ([Hogwarts](hogwarts.md#adults)). Nobody is injured, and no spell is cast to hurt. The twelve supporting Quidditch players are the only people besides the two leads ([practice teams](../crowds/practice-teams.md)).

## Rules {#rules}

### Where the rules live {#rule-owners}

The school's rules are in [Hogwarts](hogwarts.md), magic in [wand magic](magic.md), flight in [broom flight](flight.md) and the game in [Quidditch practice](quidditch.md).

## Time, weather and season {#time}

### An autumn term {#autumn}

All four films take place in October of the two students' fifth year, in daylight and dry weather with no rain, snow or strong wind. Each set records its own time of day and light: [training court](../sets/training-court.md#light), [corridor](../sets/school-corridor.md#light), [courtyard](../sets/side-courtyard.md#light) and [pitch](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md#light).

## Tone {#tone}

### Light romantic rivalry {#register}

The films are warm comedies of rivalry with real stakes in two of them. The two leads tease, compete and misjudge each other's intentions; affection stays unspoken in all four, so no film ends in a confession or a kiss. The four premises end on an invitation, a shared seat, a rescue and a shared broom, and none needs more.

### Physical truth {#physical-truth}

Comedy and danger come from bodies and objects obeying their laws: a walk takes its steps, paper creases when two people pull it, a falling stone falls at the speed of gravity and a rider who lets go drops. Magic is plain and visible and never solves what a body must do ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#readable-action)).
