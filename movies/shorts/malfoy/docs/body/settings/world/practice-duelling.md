# Practice duelling

The rules of a private practice duel between two students, as `look-at-me-duel` uses it. The form of a duel is canon used as an adopted choice; the movement of stance, cast, shield, footwork and grip comes from the [practice duelling study](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md). The spells and their looks are in [magic](magic.md#spell-catalogue).

## Rules {#rules}

### Form of a practice duel {#form}

Two students practise without a referee or audience. They take positions facing each other at casting distance, 4–5 m apart, each with the lead foot's heel on a chalk cross ([platform](../sets/duelling-room.md#platform)) and the rear foot 0.45–0.55 m behind it ([stance](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#stance)), raise their wands and bow from the waist (a short nod-bow of about 15–30°) without taking their eyes off each other, take the ready stance, and begin on a spoken count of three. A phrase of two to four actions follows; either may call "Stop" or lower their wand to end it, and they reset to their positions. Between phrases they may walk up to each other to talk or correct each other's form.

### Spells allowed {#spells}

Only the Disarming Charm and the Shield Charm are used in a private practice ([magic](magic.md#spell-catalogue)). Stunning and every other jinx are against the students' own rule for practising unsupervised; neither lead breaks that rule in the film's baseline.

### What a disarm does in practice {#disarm-outcome}

A Disarming Charm that reaches an unshielded wand hand opens the hand, sends the wand toward the caster, who catches it or lets it fall, and jolts the target's arm; the target may be pushed back about half a step (0.2–0.3 m), or up to a full step (0.4–0.5 m) from a strong caster such as Harry ([Harry's duelling](../cast/harry-potter.md#duelling)). A Shield Charm takes 0.3–0.5 s to raise and stops the jet only if it is up before the jet arrives; at 4–5 m the jet arrives 0.16–0.33 s after the cast ends ([spell jet](magic.md#spell-jet)), so a shield must be started as the opponent's cast begins, not when the jet is seen. A jet that misses passes on and fades at the far wall. The outcomes the movement can produce are tabled in the [duelling study](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#outcome-table).

### Watch the person, not the wand {#watch-the-person}

A practised duellist watches the opponent's eyes and shoulders, which begin a cast about 0.2–0.3 s before the wand moves, rather than the wand tip; a student who watches only the wand starts reacting that much later. At practice distance the difference decides a shield: from the wand's first movement, a cast and its jet take 0.4–0.7 s to arrive ([spell jet](magic.md#spell-jet)), while seeing it and raising a shield takes 0.6–1.1 s ([reaction](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#sequences-distance-and-tempo), [shield](#disarm-outcome)); only a duellist who reads the eyes and shoulders, gaining 0.2–0.3 s, can shield in time. This is taught practice in this world and the core of Carina's coaching ([Carina's coaching](../cast/carina-malfoy.md#coaching)).

### The edge {#edge}

The practice in `look-at-me-duel` takes place on the old duelling platform ([duelling room](../sets/duelling-room.md#platform)), whose sides and ends drop 0.9 m to a stone floor with no rail. A duellist standing on a chalk cross has 0.7–0.8 m of platform behind the rear heel, so two or three blind backward steps of 0.3–0.4 m put the rear foot over the end. A fall from it onto stone is a real danger, though the films show no injury ([closeness limit](four-films.md#closeness-limit)). Whoever would stop such a fall must already be within arm's reach and moving before the tip begins ([wrist grip](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#wrist-grip)).

### No score {#no-score}

Practice keeps no score and has no winner. It ends when either asks to stop.

## Time, weather and season {#time-weather-season}

### After the last lesson {#after-lessons}

Students practise duelling privately only after lessons, when an empty classroom can be used; the [four films](four-films.md#afternoon) set that between 14:30 and 16:30. Weather does not reach the indoor room except as the afternoon sun through its windows ([duelling room light](../sets/duelling-room.md#light)).

## Tone {#tone}

### Serious practice between two students {#practice-tone}

The duelling itself is taken seriously — clean form, real spells — but nothing is at stake except pride and skill; it is practice between two people who chose to be alone together, under the films' [tone](four-films.md#register).
