# Quidditch practice

## Rules {#rules}

### The game {#game}

Each team flies seven players: three Chasers who pass the Quaffle and score ten points by throwing it through one of the opposing three hoops, two Beaters who drive the two Bludgers away with bats, a Keeper who guards the hoops and a Seeker who hunts the Golden Snitch, whose catch scores 150 points and ends the match ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#roles)). These are canon rules the production adopts. The balls are [the Quaffle](../props/quaffle.md), [the Bludgers](../props/bludgers.md) and [the Snitch](../props/snitch.md); the place is [the pitch](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md).

### This practice {#practice}

The Slytherin and Gryffindor house teams fly a joint practice match on a Saturday afternoon, full teams of seven against seven, with Carina as Slytherin's Seeker and Harry as Gryffindor's ([practice teams](../crowds/practice-teams.md)). It is self-managed: no referee, coach or spectator attends, the two captains keep an informal score, and a Snitch catch ends the practice as it would a match. The arrangement is the production's invention.

### Conduct {#conduct}

Players do not grab, push or ram one another or another rider's broom, and Beaters strike only at Bludgers, never at a person ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#beaters)). Seekers race for the Snitch side by side and may cut close but never collide.

### Stopping play {#stopping}

Any player stops play by raising an arm straight up. Then everyone holds position and turns toward the trouble, Chasers keep the Quaffle, Beaters keep the Bludgers away from stopped riders because Bludgers do not stop, and nobody flies within 3 m of a rescue already under way ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#stopping)). A rider in danger is helped by whoever is nearest; distant players cannot cross the pitch in time to do it ([broom flight](flight.md#speeds)).

## Time, weather and season {#time}

### Saturday afternoon {#saturday}

The practice runs in the early afternoon of an October Saturday, under a clear sky ([the pitch](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md#light)).
