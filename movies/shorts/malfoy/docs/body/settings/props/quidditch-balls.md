# Quidditch balls

The four balls of the practice match in `snitch-before-you`: the Quaffle, two Bludgers and the Golden Snitch. Canon used as an adopted choice; sizes in metric are the production's conversions and inventions; rules in [Quidditch](../world/quidditch.md#balls-and-score).

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### Quaffle {#quaffle}

A scarlet leather ball about 30 cm across with finger-grip seams; thrown and caught by Chasers.

### Bludgers {#bludgers}

Two identical black iron balls about 25 cm across, slightly smaller than the Quaffle, heavy and hard.

### Golden Snitch {#snitch}

A gold ball the size of a large walnut, about 4 cm across, with two thin, silvery, ribbed wings that spread to about 10 cm and blur when beating. It glints in sunlight, most brightly against the low south-western sun ([practice hour](../world/quidditch.md#practice-hour)).

### On the hint {#on-the-hint}

The Quaffle and Bludgers are spheres of their sizes; the Snitch is a small sphere with two flat wing shapes, and must be shown large in frame or near a hand to be read.

## Handling and use {#handling}

### Quaffle and Bludgers in play {#play}

Chasers catch the Quaffle in both hands or one, tuck it under an arm and throw it overarm. Bludgers fly by themselves at riders at 10–20 m/s and are struck away with Beaters' bats; a Bludger's blow can break a broom's tail or throw a rider ([broken tail](broomsticks.md#broken-tail)).

### The Snitch's flight {#snitch-flight}

Production's invention within canon: the Snitch darts at up to about 20 m/s, stops and hovers, changes direction sharply, and stays mostly 2–30 m above the grass. It can hover still for a second or two, which is when a Seeker can close on it.

### Catching the Snitch {#snitch-catch}

A Seeker catches it in one gloved hand: the open hand is brought to the Snitch's path and closes around it at the contact moment, fingers continuing to close after ([catching](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#catching)). Caught, its wings fold and it stays still in the closed hand.

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Released and in play {#released}

All four balls start the film released and in play, or in their crate at mid-pitch if the screenplay begins with the release. The Snitch is caught at most once; if no Seeker catches it, it flies on, even after Madam Hooch stops play, since it does not answer her whistle, and a catch made while play is stopped still ends the match and wins ([stopping play](../world/quidditch.md#stopping-play)). Nothing carries over.
