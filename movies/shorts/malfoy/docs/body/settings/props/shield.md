# The shield charm's surface

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### The surface {#surface}

A shallow convex oval of translucent pale-blue light, 1.5 m wide and 1.8 m tall, bulging 0.15 m away from the caster at its centre, its lower edge 0.1 m above the ground. Its rim is a brighter blue line; its face lets about 70% of the light through, so bodies, hands and wands behind it stay readable. It has no thickness to see. Its look is the production's invention ([visible cue](../world/magic.md#visible-cue)).

### Placement {#placement}

Its near edge touches the caster's raised wand tip and it faces the direction the wand points, standing between the caster and the threat. It does not move with the caster: it stays where it formed.

## Use {#use}

### What it does {#does}

It stops spells and light thrown or flying objects, such as stone fragments, which strike it and drop to the ground on its far side; it cannot hold up or turn aside a heavy falling mass such as the support's cap ([wand magic](../world/magic.md#practice-only), [falling-masonry study](../../../research/physics/falling-masonry.md#shield)). Fragments striking it make dry ticks the silent films do not play.

## State changes {#state}

### Form, hold and fade {#lifecycle}

It grows outward from the wand tip to full size in 0.25 s for Carina and 0.4 s for Harry after the gesture ([Carina](../cast/carina-malfoy.md#duelling), [Harry](../cast/harry-potter.md#duelling)), holds while the caster keeps the wand raised and the feet planted for at most 3 s, and fades evenly over 0.4 s when the wand lowers or the time runs out. One caster holds one shield at a time.
