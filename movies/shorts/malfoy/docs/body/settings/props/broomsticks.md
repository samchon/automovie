# Broomsticks

The racing brooms ridden in `snitch-before-you`. Broom models are canon used as an adopted choice, their dimensions and footrests the production's invention; how a broom flies is in [magic](../world/magic.md#broom-flight) and how a body rides it in the [riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md).

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### Common build {#common-build}

Production's invention: every racing broom in the film is about 1.9 m long — a straight wooden handle (shaft) of about 1.4 m, about 3.5 cm thick, ending in a bound tail of twigs about 0.5 m long that flares to about 0.3 m across. Positions along the shaft are measured forward from the tail binding:

| Point | Distance ahead of the binding |
| --- | --- |
| Rear stirrups (one each side, a second rider's) | 0.35 m |
| Second rider's seat (hips) | 0.40 m |
| Front stirrups (one each side) | 0.70 m |
| Rider's seat (hips) | 0.75 m |
| Rider's rear hand | 1.00–1.10 m |
| Rider's forward hand | 1.10–1.20 m |
| Handle tip | 1.40 m |

Each footrest is a small metal stirrup hung on a strut about 0.45 m below the shaft and about 0.15 m out to the side, so a rider's soles rest almost under the hips with the knees bent to about 110° and the thighs angled forward and down along either side of the shaft (hip to sole about 0.45 m with thigh and shin of about 0.40 m each). A second rider's hips sit 0.35 m behind the first's; the second rider's thighs and knees close on the outside of the front rider's hips, the way a motorcycle pillion's do, so neither rider's legs cross the other's ([riding astride](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#riding-astride), [riding pillion](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#riding-pillion)).

### Harry's Firebolt {#firebolt}

A streamlined, polished ash handle, dark and glossy, with its registration number in gold near the tip; a tail of smooth birch twigs, tightly bound.

### Carina's Nimbus 2001 {#nimbus-2001}

A sleek, dark mahogany handle with "Nimbus Two Thousand and One" in silver near the tip; a tail of straight black twigs.

### School and team brooms {#school-brooms}

The Slytherin riders fly matching Nimbus 2001s; the Gryffindor riders and Madam Hooch fly older brooms of the same overall size (Cleansweeps and a Nimbus 2000), lighter-coloured wood with plainer tails.

### On the hint {#on-the-hint}

A long thin rod with a fan-shaped tail and four stirrups hanging on short struts below the shaft; brooms differ only by colour, which the hint does not carry, so a broom is identified by its rider.

## Handling and use {#handling}

### Riding astride {#astride}

The rider sits astride the shaft, inner thighs pressing it, soles in the front stirrups, both hands on the handle ahead of the body — the forward hand 0.35–0.45 m ahead of the seat, the other just behind it ([riding astride](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#riding-astride)). Folding forward speeds the broom; leaning turns it; lifting or lowering the handle climbs or dives.

### Mounting {#mounting}

On the ground, a rider steps a leg over the shaft held at hip height and sits as it rises. In the air, a second rider can climb onto a hovering or slowly moving broom from close alongside: hands to the front rider's waist or the shaft, a leg swung over the shaft behind the front rider, then feet to the rear stirrups ([riding pillion](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#riding-pillion)).

### Two riders {#two-riders}

With two aboard, the front rider steers and the passenger holds the front rider's waist, closes the knees on the outside of the front rider's hips, keeps both feet in the rear stirrups, and leans with the front rider; the broom cruises at no more than 10 m/s and needs about twice the room to turn ([broom flight](../world/magic.md#broom-flight), [two to a broom](../world/quidditch.md#two-to-a-broom)).

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Whole at the start {#baseline}

Every broom is whole at the start of the film.

### Broken tail {#broken-tail}

A Bludger striking a broom's tail can snap the binding: twigs burst loose, the broom bucks and spins and sinks in a descending spiral at about 2–5 m/s ([broom flight](../world/magic.md#broom-flight)), and the rider may be thrown off or left hanging from the shaft by the hands. A rider hanging from a sinking broom can hold on for several seconds; one thrown off falls freely. How a rider is saved in either case is in [saving a rider in the air](../world/quidditch.md#air-rescue). A broken broom does not mend within the film, and nothing carries over.
