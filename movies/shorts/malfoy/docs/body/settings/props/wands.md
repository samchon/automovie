# Wands

The two leads' wands, used in `look-at-me-duel` and carried in the other films. Wands as such are canon used as an adopted choice; what a wand does is in [magic](../world/magic.md#wand-in-hand).

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### Harry's wand {#harry-wand}

Holly, 11 inches (28 cm), with a phoenix-feather core (canon). Slim and straight, tapering from about 1.2 cm across at the butt to about 0.5 cm at the tip; pale brown wood with a plain, slightly thicker handle about 9 cm long; no ornament.

### Carina's wand {#carina-wand}

Production's invention: black walnut, 10¼ inches (26 cm), with a unicorn-hair core. Straight and slender, about 1.1 cm at the butt tapering to 0.5 cm; dark, almost black polished wood; a handle about 8 cm long ending in a narrow plain silver ring at its base.

### On the hint {#on-the-hint}

Each wand is a thin straight rod of its length, butt to tip; Harry's is 2 cm longer. They are told apart by length and by whose hand holds them.

## Handling and use {#handling}

### How a wand is held {#grip}

A wand is held by its handle in the casting hand, like a pen held a little farther back: thumb and index finger along the handle, the other fingers wrapped round it, the butt resting against the heel of the palm, the tip extending about 17–19 cm beyond the hand. Both leads cast right-handed. The tip is the effector of every cast: a cast ends with the tip on the line to its target ([cast](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#cast)).

### Carrying {#carrying}

Out of use, a wand is carried in an inner robe pocket (Carina) or in a back trouser pocket or robe pocket (Harry); taking it out and putting it away is a one-handed reach to the pocket.

### When it is disarmed {#disarmed}

Production's invention: a disarmed wand spins out of the hand and flies toward the caster in a lazy arc, end over end, at about 4–6 m/s, rising about 0.5 m and coming down toward the caster's chest, so it takes 0.7–1.2 s to cross a 4–5 m practice distance. That leaves the caster time to see it leave the hand (0.3–0.6 s) and bring the free hand — the left, since the right holds her own wand — to its path; she catches it like any one-handed catch, which can fail ([catching](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#catching)). Uncaught, it lands 0.5–1.5 m in front of her and rolls ([disarm outcome](../world/practice-duelling.md#disarm-outcome)).

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Intact in every film {#intact}

Both wands are whole at the start of every film and no film breaks one. Within the duel a wand can be out of its owner's hand — flown, caught by the other, dropped on the platform or floor — until it is picked up or handed back.
