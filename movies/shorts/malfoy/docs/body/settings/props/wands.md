# The two wands

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### Carina's wand {#carinas-wand}

Ten inches (25.4 cm) of pale grey-brown hawthorn, straight, with a plain grip 7 cm long and 1.4 cm thick that tapers along the shaft to a 0.6 cm tip. No carving, band or ornament. Its wood and length are the production's invention.

### Harry's wand {#harrys-wand}

Eleven inches (27.9 cm) of warm dark brown holly, as canon gives it, with a slightly knotted 7 cm grip 1.5 cm thick tapering to a 0.6 cm tip. The two wands read apart by colour, pale against dark, even when the hands are small in frame.

## Use {#use}

### Holding and casting {#holding}

A wand is held in the right fist with the index finger along the shaft and the tip forward. Casting moves the wrist and forearm through the spell's gesture, a short flick or a swish-and-flick, after a still aim ([wand magic](../world/magic.md#casting)). Two wands that meet make a light wooden click. Out of the hand a wand rides in the inside pocket of the owner's robe on the left chest, the grip just below the collar.

### Deflecting {#deflecting}

A wand swept across an incoming spell turns it aside ([wand magic](../world/magic.md#deflection)); the sweep is the wand's shaft crossing the streak's path, not a spell.

## State changes {#state}

### Disarmed {#disarmed}

A disarming charm sends the wand out of its owner's hand on a 2–4 m arc; it lands on the ground, rolls less than its own length and lies there until picked up ([wand magic](../world/magic.md#practice-only)). A wand never breaks in these films.
