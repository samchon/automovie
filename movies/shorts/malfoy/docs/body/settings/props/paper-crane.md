# Paper crane

Carina's tea invitation to Harry in `paper-crane-invitation`, folded as a crane and charmed to fly to him ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#paper-crane-invitation)). It is the object both of them hold at once.

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### A traditional crane from one square {#shape}

A traditional origami crane folded from one 15 cm square of Carina's cream notepaper ([stationery](../cast/carina-malfoy.md#stationery)): a diamond-shaped body, a pointed neck with a folded-down head on one side and a pointed tail on the other, and two flat triangular wings. Inference from the fold (the crane's long points come from half the square's diagonal, about 10.6 cm): about 10–11 cm from head to tail, a wingspan of about 9–11 cm with the wings spread flat, and about 6–7 cm tall from the wing fold to the body's lower point. These are approximations, uncertain by about 2 cm.

### Paper and ink {#material}

Cream, smooth, fairly stiff writing paper, a little heavier than ordinary notepaper, weighing a few grams. On the inside of the square, before folding, Carina wrote an invitation to tea in green ink, signed "C. M."; the exact words, day, time and place are the screenplay's to give. Folded, a few green strokes of her handwriting show faintly through the paper and at the edges of the wings.

### On the hint {#on-the-hint}

A small pale crane silhouette: a body with two flat wings and two raised points. At the delivered raster it must be held in a close shot to be read.

## Handling and use {#handling}

### Held by an edge {#pinch}

The crane is held in a pinch between thumb pad and index pad at the base of a wing, the base of the neck or tail, or along the body's lower edge — never squeezed through its body. Two people can hold it at once only by different points: one at a wing, one at the other wing or at the tail ([pinch on paper](../../../research/motion/effector-targeting.md#contact-inventory)).

### Flying {#flying}

Charmed, it flies to Harry at about 1–1.5 m/s at head height, its wings flapping two to three times a second, and settles on his hand or shoulder ([paper flight](../world/magic.md#paper-flight)). Until he opens it, it keeps a pull of about 0.2–0.5 N toward him if anyone else holds it.

### Taking it back {#taking-back}

To take it back, Carina must reach it with her hand — a reach to a moving or held target ([outcome table](../../../research/motion/effector-targeting.md#outcome-table)). Cancelling the charm with her wand stops its flight and pull but leaves it wherever it is, so it does not return it to her ([paper flight](../world/magic.md#paper-flight)). If Harry also holds it, the crane stays between their two pinches, at the midpoint, and moves only as their hands move ([contested hold](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#interrupted-and-contested-handovers)); its charm adds a pull of about 0.2–0.5 N toward Harry, enough to draw it his way only if her grip loosens.

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Folded and whole at the start {#baseline}

At the start of the film the crane is folded, whole and unread, either in Carina's hand or already charmed and on its way or on Harry, as the screenplay decides.

### What can happen to it {#possible-changes}

Within the film it can be flying, held by one person, held by both, crumpled (a tight grip flattens a wing first), torn (pulled apart from both wings, it tears first at a wing root), or unfolded and read (which ends the charm and shows the green writing). It never folds itself back. Nothing that happens to it carries into another film.
