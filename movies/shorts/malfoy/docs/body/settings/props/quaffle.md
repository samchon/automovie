# The Quaffle

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### The ball {#ball}

One scarlet leather ball, 30 cm across, with no visible seams or markings at the films' scale. Canon makes it scarlet leather ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#roles)); its size is the production's choice, large enough to read in a wide shot and clearly bigger than a Bludger.

## Use {#use}

### Handling {#handling}

Chasers and Keepers handle it: a rider holds it in one hand against the chest or in both hands, throws it with one arm on a shallow arc 8–15 m to a teammate, and catches it in both hands or against the chest ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#chasers)). A goal is the ball passing through a hoop. Beaters and Seekers never touch it.

## State changes {#state}

### Possession {#possession}

The ball is held, in flight or, if nobody catches it, falling to the lawn, where it lies until a Chaser flies down for it. At a [stop of play](../world/quidditch.md#stopping) the Chaser who holds it keeps it close.
