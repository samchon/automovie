# Folded note

The note Carina means to give Harry in `three-oclock`: an invitation she can offer and withdraw, and, if it falls, something either of them can bend to ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#three-oclock)).

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### A slip folded twice {#shape}

A slip cut from Carina's cream notepaper ([stationery](../cast/carina-malfoy.md#stationery)), about 10 × 7 cm, folded in half and in half again to a small packet about 5 × 3.5 cm, a few millimetres thick.

### What is written {#writing}

One or two lines in her green handwriting inviting Harry to meet her at three o'clock at a place she names, signed "C."; the exact words and the place are the screenplay's to give, but the hour is three o'clock. Folded, the writing is inside and nothing shows.

### On the hint {#on-the-hint}

A small pale flat rectangle; legible on screen only in a close shot or in a hand against a darker background.

## Handling and use {#handling}

### Held, offered, withdrawn {#offering}

Carina holds it in a pinch between her right thumb and index finger. Offering it is the giver's part of a handover: she carries it toward the midpoint between them at about chest height ([handover](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#handover)). Withdrawing it is a handover stopped before Harry's hand touches it: her hand returns along its path to her body or into her pocket ([interrupted handover](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#interrupted-and-contested-handovers)).

### Dropped and picked up {#dropped}

If dropped, it falls flat or on edge within about a second and lies on the flagstones; being so light, it can skid a little but does not blow away in the [still air](../world/four-films.md#weather). Picking it up is a bend to the floor and a pinch from the stone ([bending](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#bending)). Two standing people bending to it from facing positions less than about 0.8 m apart bring their heads together over it; a standing person and a person seated on the bench, 0.6–0.9 m apart, bending at once toward it between them meet at about 0.7–0.9 m above the floor ([seated bend](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#bending)).

### Carried {#carried}

When not in her hand it is in the right pocket of her skirt, reached by her right hand.

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Folded and unread at the start {#baseline}

At the start of the film the note is folded, unread, and with Carina.

### What can happen to it {#possible-changes}

Within the film it can be offered, withdrawn, dropped, picked up by either of them, handed over, unfolded and read. It is not torn. Nothing that happens to it carries into another film.
