# The invitation note

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### The note {#note}

One uncreased rectangle of white paper, 10 × 15 cm, weighing about 2 g. One face carries three short lines in black ink in Carina's upright hand: "Saturday. Three o'clock. Courtyard." The back is blank. The wording is the production's invention; it is too small to read in a full shot, so the films never depend on reading it.

## Use {#use}

### Handling {#handling}

Carina holds it by its lower short edge between right thumb and fingers, the writing toward herself. It can be held out face up toward someone, drawn back to her chest, hidden behind her right hip ([her habit](../cast/carina-malfoy.md#hides-the-personal)), released, and picked up again by a short edge from the ground. It passes from hand to hand under the [ownership rule](../../../research/everyday/handover.md#ownership); an open palm under it receives it without touching the giver's fingers.

## State changes {#state}

### Dropped and lifted {#dropped}

Released with no hand closed on it, it flutters down 1.2–1.8 s per metre and lands flat, drifting no more than 0.3 m sideways, and lies still in the still air until lifted ([handover study](../../../research/everyday/handover.md#dropped)). It does not crease or tear in the courtyard film unless the screenplay pulls it. It has no magic.
