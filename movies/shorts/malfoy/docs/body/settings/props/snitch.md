# The Golden Snitch

## Form, material and markings {#form}

### The ball {#ball}

A bright gold sphere about the size of a walnut, 4 cm across, with two thin silver-white wings 12 cm from tip to tip that beat too fast to see except as a pale blur. It catches the light as a small glint, which is how a rider or the camera finds it at distance. Size follows canon's walnut ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#roles)); the wingspan is the production's choice.

## Use {#use}

### Flight {#flight}

It flies by itself on short darting curves of 1–3 m radius, hovers 0.5–2 s at a time, reaches 18 m/s and never leaves [the pitch's boundary](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md#boundary) ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#seekers)). It cannot strike or pull anyone. It is caught only when a hand closes around it; an open or late hand earns nothing and it flies on ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#outcomes)). There is one Snitch and no replacement.

## State changes {#state}

### Caught {#caught}

Caught, its wings fold flat against the sphere and it stops; the practice ends ([Quidditch practice](../world/quidditch.md#practice)). Uncaught, it keeps flying.
