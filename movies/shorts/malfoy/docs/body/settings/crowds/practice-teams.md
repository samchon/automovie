# The practice teams

## Number and arrangement {#arrangement}

### Twelve supporting players {#twelve}

The joint practice of [Quidditch practice](../world/quidditch.md#practice) flies fourteen riders: the two full house teams of seven. Twelve of them are the anonymous supporting players below; the two Seekers are [Carina](../cast/carina-malfoy.md) for Slytherin and [Harry](../cast/harry-potter.md) for Gryffindor ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#fourteen-riders)). Nobody else is on or around the pitch: no referee, coach, reserve or spectator, because a self-managed practice needs none ([Quidditch practice](../world/quidditch.md#practice)).

### Where they fly {#zones}

Slytherin defends the west hoops and Gryffindor the east hoops of [the pitch](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md#zones). Chasers fly the middle of the pitch at 10–16 m above the lawn; each Keeper patrols the goal front of the team's own hoops at 12–18 m; Beaters fly wherever the two Bludgers are, mostly over the middle third; the Seekers fly above or outside the play ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#law)).

## Behavior {#behavior}

### Throughout play {#in-play}

Every supporting player is always doing their job, never hovering idle for more than 2 s ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#targets)):

- Chasers of the team in possession fly a loose triangle 8–15 m apart and pass every 2–5 s; the three defending Chasers each mark one attacker from 3–6 m on the side of their own hoops; possession changes on an interception, a save or a goal ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#chasers)).
- Keepers patrol their goal front within 10 m either side of the middle hoop, facing the ball, and come out to meet a shot ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#keepers)).
- Beaters shadow the Bludgers from 10–20 m and strike one away whenever it comes within bat reach of themselves or a teammate ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#beaters)).

### What they watch {#attention}

Each player watches the ball of their role: Chasers and Keepers the Quaffle, Beaters the nearer Bludger. None watches the Seekers' race, so a Seeker's trouble far from the play is unseen until a player's turn of the head brings it into view or another player stops play.

### At trouble {#trouble}

A player who sees a rider in danger raises an arm straight up to stop play within 1 s of seeing it, and the [stopping rule](../world/quidditch.md#stopping) follows: everyone holds and turns toward the trouble within 2 s, Chasers keep the Quaffle close, Beaters keep driving the Bludgers away from the stopped riders, the nearest player flies toward the trouble at cruise speed, and anyone arriving while a rescue is under way holds off outside 3 m. A player 100 m or more away needs at least 10 s to arrive at cruise speed and cannot outrun a rider already beside the trouble ([broom flight](../world/flight.md#speeds)).

### Captains {#captains}

S-C1 and G-C1 are the captains. They restart play after a stop with a raised and lowered arm, and they keep the informal score; neither has any authority over the Seekers' race.
