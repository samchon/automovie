# Quidditch riders

The twelve riders who fly with Carina and Harry in the `snitch-before-you` practice match: both teams apart from their Seekers. They are unnamed and act by position; any of them who must act alone in a beat is the role named here (for example "the Slytherin Keeper"). Their riding follows the [riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#riders-in-a-practice-match); the rules they play by are in [Quidditch](../world/quidditch.md).

## Number and arrangement {#arrangement}

### Twelve riders in six roles per team {#roster}

Gryffindor: three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper. Slytherin: three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper. With the two Seekers they make fourteen riders ([teams](../world/quidditch.md#teams)).

### Where they fly {#positions}

- **Chasers** fly the length of the pitch in a loose triangle 5–15 m above the grass, passing the Quaffle, 5–15 m apart from each other.
- **Beaters** stay within about 10 m of their own Chasers, at the same height, bats ready.
- **Keepers** patrol 1–3 m in front of their own three hoops, at hoop height (about 15 m) ([the pitch](../sets/quidditch-pitch.md#hoops)).
- **The Seekers** (Carina and Harry, not part of this group) fly above all of them at 15–30 m.

## Makeup {#makeup}

### Two houses of teenagers {#makeup-houses}

Students aged 13–17, boys and girls on both teams, heights 155–185 cm. Gryffindors wear scarlet team robes, Slytherins dark green ([kits](../cast/carina-malfoy.md#quidditch-kit) follow the leads' pattern without names). Gryffindor riders fly mixed school-grade brooms, Slytherin riders matching Nimbus 2001s ([broomsticks](../props/broomsticks.md#school-brooms)). On the hint they are Human figures of mixed adolescent bodies; their roles and heights in the air tell them apart.

## Action, attention and reactions {#action}

### During play {#during-play}

For their whole presence the Chasers pass, dodge and shoot, a pass every 2–4 s, attacking one end and then the other; the Beaters swing at any Bludger that comes within about 3 m of a teammate, with a short wooden bat about 0.6 m long held in one hand (the other hand on the broom handle); the Keepers shift left and right with the Quaffle and dive at shots. Their attention is on the Quaffle and the Bludgers, not on the Seekers above.

### When a rider is in danger {#danger-reaction}

When a rider falls or is struck, the nearest riders slow and turn their heads toward it within about half a second; at Madam Hooch's long whistle all twelve stop and hover where they are within about 2 s, turning to face the incident ([stopping play](../world/quidditch.md#stopping-play)). A rider can save a falling rider only from within about 3–5 m when the fall begins ([saving a rider in the air](../world/quidditch.md#air-rescue)); a free fall can be saved only if it begins about 25 m or more up. The twelve fly at 5–15 m (the Keepers at hoop height, 15 m), so none of them is within 3–5 m of a Seeker in the upper air at 25–30 m and none can save a fall from there; they watch. A Seeker who comes down to their height, for instance over the hoops, is within their reach, which a screenplay that needs only the Seekers involved avoids.

### After the danger {#after}

They stay hovering, watching, until Madam Hooch signals; they react with relief — straightening, a rider's fist raised — rather than with cheering. Nothing they do changes the outcome between the two Seekers.
