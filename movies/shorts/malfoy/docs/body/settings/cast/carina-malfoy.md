# Carina Malfoy

## Identity {#identity}

### Who she is {#who}

Carina Malfoy is a fifteen-year-old witch in her fifth year at Hogwarts, in Slytherin, and the Seeker of the Slytherin house team ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#cast-and-ages)). She is the user's Carina Malfoy, a girl of the Malfoy family; these films claim nothing about any published story of her. Her family never appears.

### Build and recognition {#recognition}

A slim, athletic build with straight shoulders and a long neck. Fair, pale skin; grey eyes; a narrow face with a pointed chin. Straight platinum-blonde hair falls to just above her shoulders, so from behind the pale hair mass over the black robe identifies her, and from the side its straight fall and her raised chin do. Her face reads as a simple cartoon face whose brows and mouth can show a small proud smile; no beat depends on it.

### Hands {#hands}

She is right-handed: the wand, the crane and the note live in her right hand, and her left hand balances, steadies or touches her forehead.

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### School uniform {#uniform}

The Slytherin uniform of the [uniform grammar](../world/hogwarts.md#uniform-grammar): white pointed collar, dark green tie with silver stripes, charcoal V-neck knit with a green band, open knee-length black robe lined in green with a green crest, charcoal pleated skirt just above the knee, opaque black tights and flat black strap shoes. She wears it in all four films; her hair is loose in the duel, corridor and courtyard films.

### Riding state {#riding-state}

For the practice match the robe's lower front panels are folded back and tucked at her waist, opaque black shorts are under the skirt, and her hair is tied in a low tail at the nape ([uniform grammar](../world/hogwarts.md#uniform-grammar)).

### Marks {#marks}

N/A: she has no visible marks, and none of the films injures her.

## Carried props {#props}

### By film {#props-by-film}

Her wand ([wands](../props/wands.md)) is in her right hand in the duel and in the inside pocket of her robe in the other films. In the corridor film she carries the [paper crane](../props/paper-crane.md) and has brought the [tea cups](../props/tea-cups.md); in the courtyard film she carries the [invitation note](../props/invitation-note.md); in the practice match she rides her own [broom](../props/brooms.md).

## Movement style {#movement}

### Effort {#effort}

Direct by default: she looks at and moves toward one thing at a time. In a duel her efforts are dab and punch, direct and sudden, light in a feint and strong in a commitment. Walking where she is watched, she glides, direct, light and sustained, with a straight back and a slightly raised chin. Embarrassed, her flow binds: elbows come in, steps shorten, and the free hand stops at her side. In the air she punches, direct, sudden and strong, in dives.

### Walk {#walk}

An ordinary walk at the upper end of the ordinary range, 1.3–1.4 m/s, with short decisive steps; when rehearsing or hesitating she drops to 0.9–1.1 m/s ([walking study](../../../research/everyday/walking.md#pace)).

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Duelling {#duelling}

Among the best fifth-year duellists: she holds the on-guard stance with her right foot leading, keeps her distance, casts the practice jinx accurately at 2–4 m, disarms, and raises the fastest shield of her year, formed 0.25 s after her gesture ([wand magic](../world/magic.md#practice-only), [duel study](../../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#casting)). She escapes a grip on her wand wrist by the underarm turn ([duel study](../../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#grip)), can turn an approaching forearm aside with her free palm, and can guide a willing partner two quick sideways steps by her shoulder against his chest. She cannot carry or drag him, and no spell of hers can stop a falling block.

### Flying {#flying}

A strong Seeker who dives steeply and banks to 50° at sprint speed, can fly one-handed for a few seconds, and can hold her broom steady at a matched speed beside another rider while leaning forward to leave her rear shaft clear ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#transfer)). She cannot pull another rider up by an arm.

### Everyday {#everyday}

She folds paper cranes neatly, knows the [paper charm](../world/magic.md#paper-charm), carries two filled cups by their handles without spilling at a slow walk, and sits and stands without using her hands ([handover study](../../../research/everyday/handover.md#cups), [sitting study](../../../research/everyday/sitting.md#law)).

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Chin up {#chin-up}

When she issues a challenge or sees Harry ready to compete, she lifts her chin about 10°, squares her shoulders and plants her leading foot, within 0.3 s, every time.

### Squares to face {#squares-to-face}

When an opponent circles her in a duel she turns on the balls of her feet to keep her chest square to him, never more than 0.3 s behind his movement, every time.

### Hides the personal {#hides-the-personal}

When someone sees her holding something personal she has not decided to give, such as a note or a paper crane, her right hand sweeps it behind her right hip while her free hand goes still at her side, 0.3–0.5 s after she realizes she is seen, every time.

### Withdraws the first offer {#withdraws-first-offer}

The first time in a scene that she holds something personal out to Harry, it stops a handspan short of his hand and comes back to her chest before his fingers touch it; the withdrawal starts when his hand is within 10 cm. Every first offer does this; a later offer in the same scene completes. "Personal" means something she has written or made for him, such as a note or a paper crane; a cup of tea or any other plain hospitality is not personal, and she hands it over on the first offer.

### Wand drops for him {#wand-drops}

When she sees danger threaten Harry her wand tip drops below his chest and her eyes fix on the danger, one reaction time (0.21–0.25 s) after it becomes visible to her, every time ([duel study](../../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#reaction)).

### Touches her forehead {#forehead-touch}

After an embarrassing moment, once nobody is facing her, she touches her forehead with the fingertips of her empty left hand for about a second, 0.5–1.5 s after turning away, every time she has a free hand.

### Chest down to race {#chest-down}

When she races level with a rival on a broom she lowers her chest toward the shaft and edges half a broom ahead within 0.5 s, every time.

## Speech {#speech}

### Lexicon and rhythm {#lexicon}

She calls Harry "Potter". She speaks in short, clipped sentences of two to six words, often imperatives, with a dry edge; she seldom says please or explains herself. When she is vulnerable her lines get quieter and the last words slow down. She never swears and is never cruel.

### Voice {#voice}

N/A: the films are silent ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#silence)); her lines exist only as screenplay text.

## Values and objectives {#values}

### What she wants {#wants}

Her super-objective is Harry's attention and company on her own terms, as his equal, without having to ask for it outright. She fears being seen as soft and being refused, and at first reads his kindness as pity. She copes through competition and composure. Her threshold is firm: immediate physical danger to Harry outweighs winning, pride and embarrassment, and embarrassment never makes her cruel.

### Psychological gesture {#gesture}

The chin lifting while the hand reaches out and draws back.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Harry {#harry}

Schoolmates of equal standing in rival houses, familiar from years of sparring, opposing Seekers and occasional practice-duel partners. She is drawn to him and hides it; she does not know whether he feels the same. She has more to hide than he does, which makes her the one who retreats. A sign that he respects her choice without pressing her, such as stepping back to give her room, lets her drop her guard; mockery would close her. Harry's side is in [his record](harry-potter.md#carina).

### Teammates {#teammates}

She is the Slytherin team's Seeker and flies apart from her teammates' ball play ([practice teams](../crowds/practice-teams.md)); she has no individual relationship with any of them in these films.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### What she knows {#knows}

Fifth-year spellwork, the rules of Quidditch and the places the films use. She knows the training court's support is old but not that its joint is cracked. She cannot see behind herself and knows nothing that happens outside her view until she turns or someone tells her. She does not know Harry's feelings.

## State changes {#state}

### Within and between films {#state-by-film}

Each film starts her from this record. Within a film only her wardrobe state (loose or tied hair, riding state) and what she holds change; she ends no film injured.
