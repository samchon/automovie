# Carina Malfoy

The lead of all four films: a fifteen-year-old Slytherin who has liked Harry Potter for a long time and has never said so. Her place in canon is a [direction choice](../../../instructions/direction.md#carina-malfoy-family); her measured movement comes from the [teen movement](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md), [proxemics and gaze](../../../research/behavior/proxemics-and-gaze.md), [duelling](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md) and [riding](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md) studies.

## Identity {#identity}

### Name, age, house and year {#name-age-house}

Carina Malfoy, born 5 June 1980, fifteen in every film. A fifth-year student in Slytherin house at Hogwarts, in the same year as Harry Potter. Not a prefect.

### Family and name {#family}

The only child of Lucius and Narcissa Malfoy, an old, wealthy pure-blood family that other students associate with arrogance and with dark wizards ([canon frame](../world/four-films.md#canon-frame)). She stands where canon puts Draco Malfoy; Draco does not exist. Her family never appears in the films and is never named on screen except through her surname.

### Seeker for Slytherin {#seeker}

She has flown Seeker for the Slytherin house team since her second year, opposite Harry, Gryffindor's Seeker.

## Body and model sheet {#body}

### Height and build {#height-build}

163 cm tall, inside the interquartile range for fifteen-year-old girls ([stature](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#stature)); slim, light build of about 50 kg. On the hint she is the installed Human figure's adolescent female body at that height, and the figure's own segment lengths govern every reach.

### Reach and proportions {#reach}

Approximate values for planning, from conventional adult segment ratios scaled to her height (an inference; the figure's measured lengths replace them in code): shoulder to fingertip about 71 cm, of which the hand is about 17.5 cm; shoulder width about 35 cm; hip joint about 85 cm above the floor; eyes about 152 cm above the floor standing and about 120 cm seated on a 0.45 m bench. Standing still, she can bring a hand to a point about 0.6–0.65 m from her shoulder without moving her feet, and about 0.9 m by leaning; farther needs a step. Her right hand is dominant.

### Which hand does what {#hands}

She casts, offers the note and takes a wrist with her right hand; she reaches with whichever hand is nearer the target when her right is occupied. Her habits ([hair tuck](#hair-tuck), [thumb in fist](#thumb-in-fist)) use her left hand when it is free and give way when it is holding something: holding always takes priority over a habit.

### How she is recognized {#recognition}

On the bare-body hint she is told from Harry by the female body form, by being 4 cm shorter, and by her carriage: spine straight, chin level, feet close together when standing, elbows near her sides. From the front her narrow stance and level head identify her; in profile her upright back and level chin; from behind her straight back and squared shoulders.

### Appearance for reference images {#appearance}

Straight platinum-blonde hair to the middle of her back, usually loose, with the front strands tucked behind her ears; pale grey eyes; very pale skin; a narrow face with a pointed chin and a fine straight nose; light eyebrows; no freckles, scars or marks. Not modelled on the hint ([bare bodies](../world/four-films.md#bare-bodies)).

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### School uniform {#school-uniform}

In `look-at-me-duel`, `paper-crane-invitation` and `three-oclock`: a black open-fronted school robe with a green-lined hood and the Slytherin crest (a silver serpent on green) on the left breast; white collared shirt; green-and-silver striped tie; grey V-neck jumper with green and silver trim at the neck; grey pleated skirt to the knee; black opaque tights; black low-heeled leather shoes.

### Duelling state of the uniform {#duelling-dress}

For the practice duel she takes off her robe and folds it over a desk, and rolls her shirt sleeves to just below the elbow, so her forearms and wrists are bare.

### Quidditch kit {#quidditch-kit}

In `snitch-before-you`: Slytherin team Quidditch robes — dark green, knee-length, split at the sides for riding, with "MALFOY" and a number 7 in silver on the back; brown leather arm guards from wrist to elbow; thin brown leather gloves; brown leather knee guards; black lace-up boots. Hair in a single low plait.

### Makeup and wounds {#makeup-wounds}

No makeup and no wounds, bruises or bandages at the start of any film.

## Carried props {#carried-props}

### Her wand {#wand}

Her own wand ([wands](../props/wands.md#carina-wand)), kept in an inner robe pocket and, in the duel, held in her right hand. In `snitch-before-you` it is with Madam Hooch ([no wands](../world/quidditch.md#no-wands)).

In `snitch-before-you` she rides her own [Nimbus 2001](../props/broomsticks.md#nimbus-2001).

## Movement style {#movement-style}

### Composed: direct, sustained, light, bound {#composed}

Her baseline when anyone else might see her, and whenever she performs a skill: a glide. Steps of comfortable length at an even pace ([gait](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#gait)), spine upright, head level, hands close to her body; each movement goes straight to its end and stops cleanly.

### Nervous near Harry: indirect, sudden, light, bound {#nervous}

When Harry looks at her or speaks to her, her movement breaks into small, quick, contained adjustments — flicks: a hand to her hair, a hand smoothing her skirt or robe, a half step forward or back, a glance away and back. It starts within about half a second of his look and lasts until she commits to an action. Her walk toward him in this state is hesitant: steps of 0.45–0.55 m at 90–105 steps per minute ([gait](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#gait)).

### Decisive in danger: direct, sudden, strong, free {#decisive}

When someone near her is in danger, her nerves vanish: she reacts in about 0.3–0.4 s, moves straight at the person, and commits her whole body — a long step, a full reach, her weight thrown back to hold. Thrust and punch, not glide. Because she watches people rather than objects, she often sees danger coming before it starts — a partner retreating toward an edge he cannot see, a Bludger on a line toward a rider — and is already moving when it begins; only then is she in time to catch a fall by the wrist, which needs her within arm's reach as the tip begins ([wrist grip](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#wrist-grip), [the edge](../world/practice-duelling.md#edge)).

### Precise contact {#contact-style}

Her touches go exactly to their targets: she brings her hand to a wrist, the edge of a paper or the handle of a broom and closes on it, without fumbling or grabbing at clothing. Every action of hers can be named as a body part brought to a target ([motion instruction](../../../instructions/motion.md#effectors-to-targets)).

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Duelling {#duelling}

Above her year's level, from private tutoring at home. She uses the Disarming and Shield Charms cleanly, casting from wrist and elbow without a wind-up in 0.25–0.35 s, raising a shield in about 0.3 s and starting it as her opponent's eyes and shoulders begin a cast, before the wand moves, so that against Harry's casts (arriving 0.41–0.63 s after his wand first moves) her shield is up after about 0.33–0.45 s, in time against nearly all of them ([watch the person](../world/practice-duelling.md#watch-the-person)), extending her arm before she steps, keeping a narrow quiet stance and recovering to it at once — the skilled markers of the [duelling study](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#level-markers). Her disarm pushes a target back about half a step. She never retreats without a glance behind.

### Coaching {#coaching}

She teaches by touch: she corrects a partner's wand arm with the coaching wrist grip — her hand from outside the forearm, closing just above the wrist, thumb on the inner wrist, turning the wrist to the right angle and letting go after 1–3 s ([wrist grip](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#wrist-grip)). Her one rule, which she repeats: watch the person, not the wand ([watch the person](../world/practice-duelling.md#watch-the-person)) — a rule that improves a partner's chances of shielding in time rather than guaranteeing it.

### Flying and seeking {#flying}

A secure, fast rider: knees on the shaft through every turn, folding to within 0.1–0.2 m of the shaft for speed, hands never leaving the handle except to catch ([riding astride](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#riding-astride)). She cruises at 8–15 m/s and dives at up to 30 m/s like Harry, but pulls out of a dive higher (about 5 m above the grass, where he leaves it to 2–3 m). She catches with the hand open early and closing once, timed to the Snitch's path ([catching](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#catching)), one-handed with her right hand while her left keeps the handle; she is Harry's equal as a Seeker in this world.

### Paper folding and the paper-flight charm {#paper-craft}

She folds a traditional paper crane in about two minutes without instructions and knows the [paper-flight charm](../world/magic.md#paper-flight).

## Habits, tells and mannerisms {#habits}

### Tucks her hair when he looks {#hair-tuck}

Trigger: Harry's eyes rest on her for more than about one second. Response: her left hand rises and tucks the hair at her left temple behind her ear, then drops. Speed: begins within half a second. Reliability: nearly every time in the first look of an encounter, less often once she has committed to speaking.

### Drops her eyes to his hands {#gaze-to-hands}

Trigger: a mutual look with Harry reaches about 1–1.5 s, well short of the 2–4 s ordinary look ([gaze](../../../research/behavior/proxemics-and-gaze.md#gaze)). Response: her eyes drop to his hands, not to the floor. Speed: within a fifth of a second. Reliability: reliable while she is nervous; when she is decisive or has finally spoken, she holds his look for the full ordinary length or longer.

### The half step {#half-step}

Trigger: she is about to say or offer something that matters to her. Response: her weight shifts forward and she takes a half step (about 0.3 m) toward him, closing from acquaintance distance toward 0.5–0.8 m ([distance](../../../research/behavior/proxemics-and-gaze.md#distance)); if he looks up at that moment, she often steps back to where she was. Speed: the step and its retreat take about one second. Reliability: she retreats on most first attempts and goes through on a later one.

### Thumb inside her fist {#thumb-in-fist}

Trigger: waiting for Harry's answer. Response: her left hand closes at her side with the thumb pressed inside the fingers, held until the answer comes. Speed: immediate. Reliability: every time she waits on him; hidden from him, visible to the camera.

### Straightens when watched {#straightens}

Trigger: a third person comes into view or she believes she is watched. Response: her spine straightens, chin lifts, face goes neutral and she returns to her composed style. Speed: within half a second. Reliability: always.

## Speech and voice {#speech}

### Lexicon and register {#lexicon}

Educated British English with a slightly formal vocabulary from her upbringing ("rather", "I suppose", "do"). She calls him "Potter" while composed and slips into "Harry" when she forgets herself — a tell she notices too late. Dry, quick wit; no slang; no swearing.

### Rhythm and tics {#rhythm}

Composed, she speaks in short, even, finished sentences. Nervous, she starts a sentence twice ("I— I only meant—"), stops short of the important word, and fills the gap with an action instead of speech.

### Voice {#voice}

A clear, light voice in the middle-high range, quiet rather than loud, crisp consonants, slightly clipped. The hints are silent ([silence](../sound/palette.md#ambience)); the voice is recorded for reading the screenplays and for any later stage.

## Values, worldview, super-objective and psychological gesture {#inner-life}

### Values {#values}

She values competence, fairness and keeping her word. She resents being judged by her family name, and privately disagrees with her family's contempt for others.

### Worldview {#worldview}

People show who they are by what they do with their hands — what they reach for, what they hold, what they let go — more than by what they say.

### Super-objective {#super-objective}

To be seen by Harry as herself — Carina — not as "a Malfoy".

### Fears {#fears}

Being laughed at; being refused in front of others; showing her feelings and having them treated as a Malfoy trick.

### Psychological gesture {#psychological-gesture}

A hand offered and drawn back: an arm that extends toward someone and returns before it arrives. Whether, in a given moment, her hand goes all the way is what she struggles with.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Harry, from her side {#harry}

At the shared baseline ([direction](../../../instructions/direction.md#baseline-relationship)): she has liked him for more than a year and has told nobody. She shares Potions and other lessons with him, has watched him from the opposite end of the Quidditch pitch, and has spoken with him a few times, briefly and politely. She knows far more about him than he knows about her. The house rivalry and her name make being seen with him a cost among Slytherins, which is why she seeks him out where nobody else is.

### Her house {#slytherin}

Respected by her housemates for her flying; she keeps her private life from them. None of them appears in the films except as unnamed riders.

### Madam Hooch {#hooch}

Madam Hooch trusts her as a reliable, clean flyer; Carina obeys her whistle at once, except where the rule of the air itself asks her to help a rider in danger ([stopping play](../world/quidditch.md#stopping-play)).

### Change conditions {#relationship-change}

She lets herself drop her guard with Harry when he treats her as herself — kindness or attention addressed to her, not to her name — and she risks her composure only when he is in need or when she has nothing left to hide.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### What she knows {#knows}

Harry's habits: that he pushes his glasses up before he speaks, retreats without looking when pressed, and that the Disarming Charm is his best spell ([Harry's habits](harry-potter.md#habits)). Duelling form, Seeker craft, paper folding and the paper-flight charm. The rules of a practice duel and of Quidditch.

### What she does not know {#does-not-know}

Whether Harry likes her, or what he thinks of her beyond politeness. No charm that stops a fall ([limits](../world/magic.md#limits)). What Harry will do in any moment of a film until he does it.

## State changes over story time {#state-changes}

### Starting state in every film {#baseline-state}

Composed, uninjured, in the clothes of that film, her feelings untold. When they talk at the baseline the two keep acquaintance distance, 0.9–1.3 m ([distance](../../../research/behavior/proxemics-and-gaze.md#distance)); where each film first places her relative to Harry is that film's screenplay's choice ([baseline reset](../world/four-films.md#baseline-reset)).

### Within a film {#within-a-film}

Within one film she can move from composed to nervous to decisive and back; she can speak her feelings, take his hand or wrist, sit beside him or carry him on her broom, as that film's screenplay decides. Whatever changes ends with that film.
