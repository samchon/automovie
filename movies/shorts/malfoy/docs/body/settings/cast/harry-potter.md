# Harry Potter

The other lead of all four films: a fifteen-year-old Gryffindor, famous at school, who knows Carina Malfoy by name and reputation and has no idea she likes him. Canon's Harry used as an adopted choice ([canon frame](../world/four-films.md#canon-frame)); his measured movement comes from the [teen movement](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md), [proxemics and gaze](../../../research/behavior/proxemics-and-gaze.md), [duelling](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md) and [riding](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md) studies.

## Identity {#identity}

### Name, age, house and year {#name-age-house}

Harry James Potter, born 31 July 1980, fifteen in every film. A fifth-year student in Gryffindor house, in Carina's year.

### Fame and background {#fame}

Famous throughout the wizarding world since infancy for surviving the curse that killed his parents; raised by his aunt's family. In these films his past is never shown or mentioned; it shows only in his dislike of being stared at and treated as a celebrity.

### Seeker for Gryffindor {#seeker}

Gryffindor's Seeker since his first year, the youngest house player in a century, opposite Carina as Slytherin's Seeker.

## Body and model sheet {#body}

### Height and build {#height-build}

167 cm tall, inside the interquartile range for fifteen-year-old boys ([stature](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#stature)); slight, wiry build of about 55 kg. On the hint he is the installed Human figure's adolescent male body at that height, and the figure's own segment lengths govern every reach.

### Reach and proportions {#reach}

Approximate values for planning, from conventional adult segment ratios scaled to his height with arms a little long for it (an inference; the figure's measured lengths replace them in code): shoulder to fingertip about 75 cm, of which the hand is about 18 cm; shoulder width about 38 cm; hip joint about 88 cm above the floor; eyes about 156 cm above the floor standing and about 122 cm seated on a 0.45 m bench. Standing still, he can bring a hand to a point about 0.65–0.7 m from his shoulder without moving his feet, and about 0.95 m by leaning. His right hand is dominant: he casts, catches and reaches first with it.

### How he is recognized {#recognition}

On the bare-body hint he is told from Carina by the male body form, by being 4 cm taller and by his carriage: shoulders slightly rounded forward, head a little forward of his shoulders, feet wider apart, weight often on one leg. From the front his wider stance; in profile his forward head; from behind his rounded shoulders.

### Appearance for reference images {#appearance}

Untidy jet-black hair that sticks up at the back; bright green eyes; round wire-rimmed glasses; a thin lightning-bolt scar on the right side of his forehead; fair skin. Not modelled on the hint ([bare bodies](../world/four-films.md#bare-bodies)).

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### School uniform {#school-uniform}

In `look-at-me-duel`, `paper-crane-invitation` and `three-oclock`: a black school robe with a scarlet-lined hood and the Gryffindor crest (a gold lion on scarlet); white shirt, collar slightly crooked; scarlet-and-gold striped tie, loosely knotted; grey V-neck jumper with scarlet and gold trim; grey trousers; black shoes, scuffed.

### Duelling state of the uniform {#duelling-dress}

For the practice duel he drops his robe on a desk and pushes his shirt sleeves up his forearms, so his wrists are bare.

### Quidditch kit {#quidditch-kit}

In `snitch-before-you`: Gryffindor team Quidditch robes — scarlet, knee-length, split at the sides, with "POTTER" and a number 7 in gold on the back; brown leather arm guards; thin brown leather gloves; knee guards; black boots. Glasses on.

### Makeup and wounds {#makeup-wounds}

No wounds, bruises or bandages at the start of any film; the scar is his only mark.

## Carried props {#carried-props}

### His wand {#wand}

His own wand ([wands](../props/wands.md#harry-wand)), carried in his back trouser pocket or robe, held in his right hand in the duel; left with Madam Hooch during the match ([no wands](../world/quidditch.md#no-wands)).

### His broom {#broom}

In `snitch-before-you` he rides his own [Firebolt](../props/broomsticks.md#firebolt).

### A book {#book}

In `paper-crane-invitation` and `three-oclock` he carries his Potions textbook — a thick hardback about 24 × 17 × 4 cm in a dark green cloth cover, about 1 kg — and sits reading or half-reading it, holding it open in his left hand or on his knee, a finger keeping his page. When he needs both hands he shuts it on his finger or puts it down beside him on the bench; it can fall from his knee if he moves suddenly.

## Movement style {#movement-style}

### Quick and reactive: direct, sudden, light, free {#reactive}

His baseline when something happens: a dab — he turns his head fast, reaches fast, steps fast, and the movement is loose rather than precise. His comfortable walk is at the upper end of the comfortable range, steps 0.66–0.70 m ([gait](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#gait)).

### At rest: indirect, sustained, light, free {#at-rest}

When nothing demands him: a float — he slouches on a bench with his legs out, shifts his weight from foot to foot, lets his eyes wander, keeps a hand in a pocket.

### In the air: direct, sudden, strong, free {#in-the-air}

On a broom he is bold and fast: dives at up to 30 m/s pulled out 2–3 m above the grass, turns at speed on a radius of 3–5 m, his body folded flat to the shaft (chest within about 0.1 m of it), and he passes within 1–2 m of other riders and Bludgers where others keep 3 m or more.

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Duelling {#duelling}

His Disarming Charm is unusually strong and fast: he casts it in 0.25–0.3 s and it pushes a target back up to a full step (0.4–0.5 m), against half a step for an ordinary caster ([disarm outcome](../world/practice-duelling.md#disarm-outcome)). His reactions are quick, a Seeker's (about 0.35–0.45 s), but his Shield Charm is ordinary: he needs about 0.5 s to raise it, and because he watches the wand rather than the caster's eyes and shoulders he starts it late; against a quick caster at 4–5 m it is late every time unless he reads her eyes and shoulders before her wand moves, which gives him just enough time against all but her quickest casts ([watch the person](../world/practice-duelling.md#watch-the-person)). His form is untidy: he casts with a wide swing from the shoulder, steps before extending his arm, watches his opponent's wand rather than her eyes, and retreats along a line without glancing back — the less polished markers of the [duelling study](../../../research/duel/practice-duelling.md#level-markers).

### Flying and seeking {#flying}

An exceptional natural flyer and Seeker; he flies by instinct and takes risks: he cruises at 8–15 m/s, dives at up to 30 m/s and pulls out of a dive only 2–3 m above the grass, and he will fly between other riders and within 1–2 m of Bludgers to keep his line. He catches one-handed with his right hand ([catching](../../../research/flight/broom-riding-and-snitch-catch.md#catching)).

## Habits, tells and mannerisms {#habits}

### Pushes up his glasses {#glasses-push}

Trigger: he is about to speak after a pause, or he is puzzled. Response: his right index finger pushes his glasses up the bridge of his nose. Speed: about half a second before he speaks. Reliability: most times.

### Rubs the back of his head {#head-rub}

Trigger: embarrassment, or being thanked or praised. Response: his right hand goes to the back of his head and rubs his hair, eyes down and to the side. Speed: within half a second. Reliability: reliable.

### Reads her late {#reads-late}

Trigger: a hint from Carina that she likes him — a look held, a word, an offered object. Response: he takes it at face value first, then understands one to two seconds later; when he understands, he goes still for about half a second, his eyes widen and his hand stops whatever it was doing. Reliability: every time at the baseline.

### Retreats without looking {#retreats-blind}

Trigger: an opponent advancing on him within about 2 m in a duel, or a strong jet pushing him back. Response: he gives ground with backward steps of 0.3–0.4 m along a straight line without glancing behind. Speed: one step per half second. Reliability: reliable in the duel. On the duelling platform two or three such steps from a chalk cross put his rear foot over the end ([the edge](../world/practice-duelling.md#edge)).

### Leans in to listen {#leans-in}

Trigger: someone speaks quietly to him. Response: he leans his head and shoulders toward them, closing the distance by 0.1–0.2 m without noticing. Speed: over about one second, starting as they begin to speak. Reliability: often.

## Speech and voice {#speech}

### Lexicon and register {#lexicon}

Plain, informal British schoolboy English ("yeah", "s'pose", "brilliant", "mental"). He calls her "Malfoy" at the baseline, guarded but not rude, and "Carina" once she has won him over.

### Rhythm and tics {#rhythm}

Short answers and short questions; he answers a feeling with a joke or a deflection, then a pause; deadpan humour.

### Voice {#voice}

A mid-range voice, a little rough, unhurried, slightly lower when embarrassed. The hints are silent ([silence](../sound/palette.md#ambience)); the voice is recorded for reading the screenplays.

## Values, worldview, super-objective and psychological gesture {#inner-life}

### Values {#values}

Loyalty, fairness and courage; he dislikes snobbery and cruelty, and acts at once when someone is in danger.

### Worldview {#worldview}

People are what they do when it counts, not what their names say — though he does not yet apply that to the Malfoy name.

### Super-objective {#super-objective}

To be treated as an ordinary boy rather than as the famous Harry Potter. It mirrors Carina's wish to be seen apart from her name; neither knows the other shares it.

### Psychological gesture {#psychological-gesture}

An open hand held out, palm up — he gives help before he thinks.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Carina, from his side {#carina}

At the shared baseline ([direction](../../../instructions/direction.md#baseline-relationship)): he knows her as a Malfoy, Slytherin's Seeker and the sharpest student in their Potions class; they have spoken a few times, briefly. He is wary of her name, not hostile to her, and has never noticed that she likes him.

### Friends offscreen {#friends}

His closest friends are not in the films and are not mentioned.

### Madam Hooch {#hooch}

His flying teacher; he trusts her and obeys her whistle.

### Change conditions {#relationship-change}

He drops his guard toward Carina when she shows him something her name would not predict — patience, help, a risk taken for him — and from then reads her more quickly.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### What he knows {#knows}

The Disarming and Shield Charms; practice duelling from the school's old Duelling Club; Quidditch and Seeker craft; Carina's name, house and flying.

### What he does not know {#does-not-know}

That Carina likes him; that she has watched him; that his retreating habit is dangerous on a platform. No charm that stops a fall ([limits](../world/magic.md#limits)). He does not know Carina's handwriting or that she writes in green ink; he knows a crane or a note comes from her only if he sees her send or hold it, or reads her signature.

## State changes over story time {#state-changes}

### Starting state in every film {#baseline-state}

Relaxed, uninjured, in that film's clothes, unaware of Carina's feelings. When they talk at the baseline the two keep acquaintance distance, 0.9–1.3 m ([distance](../../../research/behavior/proxemics-and-gaze.md#distance)); where each film first places him relative to her is that film's screenplay's choice ([baseline reset](../world/four-films.md#baseline-reset)).

### Within a film {#within-a-film}

Within one film he can be disarmed, fall or nearly fall, lose his broom, read and understand her invitation, accept it, take her hand or ride behind her, as that film's screenplay decides. Whatever changes ends with that film.
