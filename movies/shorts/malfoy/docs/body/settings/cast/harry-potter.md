# Harry Potter

## Identity {#identity}

### Who he is {#who}

Harry Potter is a fifteen-year-old wizard in his fifth year at Hogwarts, in Gryffindor, and the Seeker of the Gryffindor house team ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#cast-and-ages)). His canon identity (name, house, Seeker, round glasses, untidy black hair, green eyes) is adopted; his history outside these films plays no part in them.

### Build and recognition {#recognition}

Lean and wiry, narrow-hipped, with slightly forward shoulders. Light skin, green eyes, short untidy black hair that sticks up at the back, and round glasses with thin black frames, drawn as two rings and a bridge at the polygon scale; the glasses identify him from the front and side, the dark hair mass from behind. The thin scar on his forehead is too small for these figures and is not drawn. His face reads as a simple cartoon face; no beat depends on it.

### Hands {#hands}

Right-handed: his wand is in his right hand; his left hand is free to catch, grip or reach.

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### School uniform {#uniform}

The Gryffindor uniform of the [uniform grammar](../world/hogwarts.md#uniform-grammar): white pointed collar, scarlet tie with gold stripes, charcoal V-neck knit with a scarlet band, open knee-length black robe lined in scarlet with a scarlet crest, straight charcoal trousers and black lace-up shoes. He wears it in all four films.

### Riding state {#riding-state}

For the practice match the robe's lower front panels are folded back and tucked at his waist ([uniform grammar](../world/hogwarts.md#uniform-grammar)); his glasses stay on.

### Marks {#marks}

N/A: no visible mark is drawn, and none of the films injures him.

## Carried props {#props}

### By film {#props-by-film}

His wand ([wands](../props/wands.md)) is in his right hand in the duel and in his robe's inside pocket in the other films; he rides his own [broom](../props/brooms.md) in the practice match. He brings nothing else; what he holds in the corridor and courtyard films he is given there.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Effort {#effort}

Indirect by default: his attention wanders around the space and comes back. In a duel he flicks, indirect, light and sudden, circling in short springy steps. In everyday moments he floats, indirect, light and sustained, loose in the shoulders with his hands easy. In the air he slashes, indirect, strong and sudden, in daring reaches.

### Walk {#walk}

An ordinary, unhurried walk at 1.2–1.3 m/s with an easy arm swing, hands often in his trouser pockets when he has nothing to carry ([walking study](../../../research/everyday/walking.md#pace)).

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Duelling {#duelling}

A quick and fair duellist: he circles, advances and retreats in short diagonal steps, deflects a watched jinx with a sweep of his wand at casting distance, disarms reliably, and casts the practice jinx at 2–4 m ([duel study](../../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#deflection), [wand magic](../world/magic.md#practice-only)). His shield is slower than Carina's, formed 0.4 s after his gesture. With his free left hand he can catch an opponent's wand wrist from the outside inside grip range, and he lets go when an underarm turn rotates his wrist toward its limit ([duel study](../../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#grip)).

### Flying {#flying}

An exceptional Seeker: fast off the mark, banks to 60° at sprint speed, reaches far for the Snitch, and can hang under his broom two-handed for 30 s or more and one-handed for about 10 s ([broom-riding study](../../../research/flight/broom-riding.md#hanging)). He can take a second broom's rear shaft and climb astride behind its rider when the conditions of a [transfer](../world/flight.md#tandem) hold. He cannot pull himself up one-armed onto a moving shaft.

### Everyday {#everyday}

He catches small thrown or gliding objects one-handed, unfolds paper carefully to avoid tearing it, sits and slides along a bench, and receives a cup by supporting its body before its giver lets go ([handover study](../../../research/everyday/handover.md#ownership)).

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Reaches for the wand wrist {#reaches-for-wrist}

When an opponent comes at him fast inside grip range in a duel, his left hand opens toward the opponent's right wrist within 0.25 s, every time; he repeats the reach even when it failed before.

### Watches the wand hand {#watches-wand-hand}

In a duel his eyes stay on the opponent's wand hand, so he notices nothing behind him or above his own shoulders until someone's reaction shows it. Every duel.

### Gives room {#gives-room}

When someone facing him stops short, retracts something or bumps into him, he lifts both open palms toward them and takes a half-metre step back, within 0.6 s, every time.

### Pushes up his glasses {#pushes-glasses}

After a surprise, a fall or a near miss he pushes his glasses up the bridge of his nose with the index finger of his free hand, within 1 s, once per surprise.

### Reaches past his balance {#overreaches}

When the Snitch is just beyond his fingertips he keeps reaching, past the arm-plus-lean limit of a [seated reach](../world/flight.md#reach), every time; only a hand already on the shaft keeps him with the broom.

### Holds on after danger {#holds-on}

When a shared danger has passed, a hand he used to grip someone stays where it gripped until he notices it or the other person looks at it; then he lets go willingly, within 0.5 s of noticing.

## Speech {#speech}

### Lexicon and rhythm {#lexicon}

He calls her "Malfoy". He speaks plainly in short direct questions and dry understatements, answers a challenge with a shrug of a phrase, and is direct when he means something. He does not lecture or tease cruelly.

### Voice {#voice}

N/A: the films are silent ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#silence)); his lines exist only as screenplay text.

## Values and objectives {#values}

### What he wants {#wants}

He wants a fair contest and the people around him safe, and he likes Carina's company more than he lets on. His super-objective in every film is to meet her as she is, without making her ask for what she wants. He dislikes taking advantage: he will not use something private of hers as leverage or keep something she asks for back. He copes with awkwardness by giving room and making a dry remark.

### Psychological gesture {#gesture}

The open palm offered and then held still.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Carina {#carina}

Schoolmates of equal standing in rival houses, opposing Seekers and occasional practice-duel partners. He enjoys her sharpness and takes her challenges as invitations to compete, not as hostility. He is drawn to her and does not know she feels the same. He reads her retreats as wanting space and gives it, which is what lets her come back. Carina's side is in [her record](carina-malfoy.md#harry).

### Teammates {#teammates}

He is the Gryffindor team's Seeker and flies apart from his teammates' ball play ([practice teams](../crowds/practice-teams.md)); no teammate has an individual relationship with him in these films.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### What he knows {#knows}

Fifth-year spellwork, Quidditch and the places the films use. He cannot see behind himself; in a duel he sees only what is in front of the opponent's wand hand. He does not know Carina's feelings or what she has written until he reads it.

## State changes {#state}

### Within and between films {#state-by-film}

Each film starts him from this record. Within a film only his riding state and what he holds change; he ends no film injured.
