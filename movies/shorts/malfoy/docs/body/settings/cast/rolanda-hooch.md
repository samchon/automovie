# Rolanda Hooch

The referee of the practice match in `snitch-before-you`, and the only adult in the films. Canon's flying teacher used as an adopted choice ([canon frame](../world/four-films.md#canon-frame)). She appears in no other film.

## Identity {#identity}

### Name and role {#name-role}

Madam Rolanda Hooch, Hogwarts flying instructor and Quidditch referee, in her fifties. In `snitch-before-you` she referees the practice match ([practice match](../world/quidditch.md#practice-match)).

## Body and model sheet {#body}

### Height and build {#height-build}

Production's invention: 168 cm, lean and wiry, an adult female body. On the hint she is the installed Human figure's adult female body.

### How she is recognized {#recognition}

On the hint she is the only adult body, and the only rider who hovers upright and nearly still at mid-pitch for long periods. Riding, she sits bolt upright with her elbows out.

### Appearance for reference images {#appearance}

Short, spiky grey hair; sharp yellow eyes like a hawk's; a lined, weathered face.

## Wardrobe, makeup and wounds {#wardrobe}

### Referee's robes {#robes}

Plain black flying robes with long sleeves, a silver whistle on a cord round her neck, brown leather gloves. No makeup, no wounds.

## Carried props {#carried-props}

### Whistle, wand and the players' wands {#carried}

Her silver whistle; her own wand in a sleeve holster; the players' wands in a box left on the grass beside the centre circle ([no wands](../world/quidditch.md#no-wands)). She rides a plain school broom, a Cleansweep ([broomsticks](../props/broomsticks.md#school-brooms)).

## Movement style {#movement-style}

### Sharp and economical: direct, sudden, strong, bound {#officiating}

She moves in short decisive bursts and then stops: a turn of the head, a whistle, a straight flight to where she is needed. Between bursts she hovers upright, still, turning her head to follow the Quaffle.

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Refereeing a match {#refereeing}

To release the balls she stands on the grass beside the open crate at the centre circle: she unbuckles the two Bludgers' straps with both hands and they shoot up and away; she opens the Snitch's small catch, and it hovers a moment at head height and darts off, out of sight within 2–3 s; she takes the Quaffle in both hands, mounts, rises to about 5 m and throws it straight up with both hands, and play begins as it leaves her hands with a short blast. She then hovers at mid-pitch 10–15 m up, turning to follow the play. She blows a short blast to start or restart and a long blast to stop ([stopping play](../world/quidditch.md#stopping-play)). When a rider falls or is in danger she blows the long blast within half a second of seeing it and flies straight toward the rider at up to 20 m/s, wand drawn; because she starts from mid-pitch she arrives after any rider who was already close. She knows a charm that slows a falling body, usable only within about 10 m of her wand, which gives her a reach of about 15–25 m for a free fall and 30–60 m for a rider who first hangs from a broken broom ([limits](../world/magic.md#limits), [who can reach a rider in time](../world/quidditch.md#who-can-reach)).

### Her hands {#hands}

She flies with her left hand on the handle and her right hand free. The silver whistle hangs on its cord; her right hand brings it to her lips and holds it there while she watches a dangerous moment, and lets it drop on its cord to draw her wand from the holster strapped inside her left sleeve. With the wand drawn she steers with her left hand alone and casts with her right. Holding always takes priority: she never whistles and casts at once.

## Habits, tells and mannerisms {#habits}

### Whistle to lips {#whistle-ready}

Trigger: a rider closes on another or a Bludger nears a rider. Response: the whistle comes up to her lips and stays there until the moment passes. Speed: within a second. Reliability: always.

## Speech and voice {#speech}

### Clipped commands {#commands}

She speaks in short commands ("Mount your brooms!", "Hold!") in a carrying, slightly rasping voice. The hints are silent ([silence](../sound/palette.md#ambience)).

## Values, worldview, super-objective and psychological gesture {#inner-life}

### Safety first {#safety-first}

She cares about her riders' safety above the game; her one aim in the film is that nobody is hurt. Psychological gesture: an arm flung out flat, palm down — "hold".

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Her riders {#riders}

She treats every rider alike, house regardless; she trusts Carina and Harry as her best flyers ([Carina](carina-malfoy.md#hooch), [Harry](harry-potter.md#hooch)).

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### What she sees {#sees}

She sees the whole match from mid-pitch but cannot see the Seekers' faces at 15–30 m above and away from her; she does not know Carina's feelings and judges only the riding.

## State changes over story time {#state-changes}

### Starting state {#baseline-state}

At the start of `snitch-before-you` she is in the air at mid-pitch with the match under way, or releasing the balls, as the screenplay decides; she ends the film where the screenplay leaves her. Nothing carries over.
