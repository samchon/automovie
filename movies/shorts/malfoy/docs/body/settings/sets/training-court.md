# The training court

## Shape and dimensions {#shape}

### The floor {#floor}

An outdoor practice court on the Hogwarts grounds: a level, dry floor of rough medium-grey flagstones 8 m east to west and 6 m north to south. Along its north edge runs a weathered grey stone wall 0.45 m high and 0.15 m thick; the south, east and west edges lie open and level with bare brown earth, which runs at least 2 m beyond them before rough grass. Distances below are in metres from the floor's centre, east and north positive. The court and its support are the production's invention.

### The stone support {#support}

In the north-east of the floor stands one old grey stone support, the last remnant of a demolished arcade. Its square base is 1.0 m across and 0.12 m high, spanning 1.2–2.2 m east and 1.8–2.8 m north. On the base's north half rises a plain upright 0.6 m wide and 0.5 m deep (1.4–2.0 m east, 2.3–2.8 m north), 3.0 m tall from the floor, which stands firm throughout every film. From the upright's south face the [cap](../props/falling-masonry.md#cap) projects south over open air, 1.3–2.1 m east and 1.3–2.3 m north, its underside 2.70 m and its top 2.95 m above the floor. Three joints hold the cap, each a broad light-grey mortar strip readable at polygon scale: a lower fillet along the cap's rear underside, already cracked across its exposed south face; the shallow stone bearing ledge that projects 0.10 m from the upright's south face under the cap's rear edge; and an upper mortar web across the cap's rear top. Nobody has inspected these joints.

### The danger region {#danger-region}

The floor the cap could reach if it fell, widened by 0.3 m ([falling-masonry study](../../../research/physics/falling-masonry.md#clearance)): 1.0–2.4 m east and 0.5–2.6 m north. A person whose whole body and wand are west of 0.7 m east is clear of it.

## Zones and landmarks {#zones}

### Zones {#zone-list}

| Zone | Where | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Centre | Within 1.0 m of the floor's centre | The open court where a duel usually stands |
| Support zone | The base and the danger region | Nobody's place; the support stands there |
| West lane | The open floor from the west edge to 0.7 m east, across the court's whole depth | Clear floor away from the support |
| South approach | The open south edge and the earth beyond it | Arriving and leaving |
| North wall | The low wall along the north edge | The court's back; nobody sits or stands on it |

### Landmarks {#landmarks}

The support's base corner nearest the centre (1.2 m east, 1.8 m north); the cap's front edge; the cracked lower fillet; the wall's west end.

## Routes, entrances and levels {#routes}

### One open level {#open-level}

People arrive and leave across the open south, east or west edges; there is no gate or step. Everything happens on the one floor level.

## Dressing, lights and acoustics {#dressing}

### Dressing {#bare}

Nothing else: no bench, target, tool or bag. The earth around the court is bare, and only pale sky and distant grass lie beyond it.

### Light {#light}

Saturday late morning under bright high cloud: soft daylight from the south-west that keeps faces, hands, feet and the support's joint strips readable, with soft shadows. A light breeze moves nothing in the frame.

### Acoustics {#acoustics}

N/A: the films are silent ([sound](../sound/silence.md)).

## Use {#use}

### Practice duels {#practice}

Two students practise duels on the open floor, usually facing each other across the centre at casting distance ([duel study](../../../research/duelling/practice-duel.md#distance)), circling as they please. Nobody uses the support for anything; its base is simply something to step around.

## State changes {#state}

### The cap {#cap-state}

The court changes only if the cap fails, in the order its [record](../props/falling-masonry.md#failure) gives; afterwards the fallen cap, chips and fragments lie on the floor south of the upright for the rest of the film, the upright still standing.
