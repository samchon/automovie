# Charms corridor

A long third-floor corridor outside the Charms classroom, with deep south windows over the grounds; the place of `three-oclock`. Production's invention within canon's Hogwarts. The other three films have their own places: the [duelling room](duelling-room.md), the [Clock Tower courtyard](clock-tower-courtyard.md) and the [Quidditch pitch](quidditch-pitch.md).

## Shape, zones and landmarks {#shape}

### The corridor {#corridor}

A straight stone corridor 30 m long, running west–east, 3.5 m wide between its walls, under a plain stone vault 5 m high; grey flagstone floor, bare grey stone walls. Positions along it are measured from its west end.

### The south windows {#windows}

Five tall, leaded windows in the south wall, centred at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 m. Each is 1.4 m wide and 2.8 m tall, its sill 0.9 m above the floor, set in a stone embrasure 0.6 m deep, so that someone standing at a window rests their forearms on a sill about waist high and has the corridor at their back. The windows look south over the sloping grounds; the Quidditch pitch, its hoops and its stands are visible small in the distance to the south-west.

### The Charms door and the clock {#door-and-clock}

The Charms classroom door, a heavy oak door 1.4 m wide, is in the north wall at 15 m, opposite the middle window. Above it hangs a round wall clock 0.6 m across, its centre 3.2 m above the floor, with a white face and black hands, readable from anywhere in the corridor. It shows the real time of the film ([afternoon](../world/four-films.md#afternoon)). Two other classroom doors stand in the north wall at 5 m and 25 m. A few dim portraits hang between the doors; their subjects doze and play no part.

## Routes, entrances and levels {#routes}

### The two ends {#ends}

The west end opens onto a staircase landing (the way up from the floors below); the east end turns a right-angle corner to the north, beyond which a walker is out of sight after about 2 m. These two ends are the only ways in and out; the classroom doors are shut and the classrooms empty.

### The corridor walk {#corridor-walk}

Walking the corridor from the west landing to the east corner is 30 m: 22–25 s at a comfortable pace (1.2–1.35 m/s) and 32–43 s at a hesitant one (0.7–0.95 m/s) ([gait](../../../research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md#gait)). From the landing to just past the middle window is about 17 m: 13–14 s comfortable, 18–24 s hesitant. Someone standing at the middle window (15 m) with their back to the corridor is passed 1.5–2.5 m behind them by anyone walking down the corridor's middle, and passed at arm's length (about 1 m) by anyone keeping close to the window side.

### Level {#level}

One level: the flagstone floor, flat and dry, with no steps between the two ends.

## Dressing, practical lights and acoustics {#dressing}

### Dressing {#dressing-items}

The portraits; a tall iron candelabrum, unlit, beside each classroom door; a notice board beside the Charms door with a few pinned parchments. Nothing on the floor.

### Light {#light}

The low afternoon sun from the south-south-west to south-west ([afternoon](../world/four-films.md#afternoon)) comes in through the south windows at an angle and lays five bright, slanting panels of warm light across the floor; because the sun is only 10–20° up and the windows' tops 3.7 m high, the light crosses the whole 3.5 m width and climbs the north wall in bright patches that move eastward along it as the afternoon goes on. Between the windows the corridor is in soft shade; a person standing in a window is lit from the front and seen from the corridor against the bright glass.

### Acoustics {#acoustics}

Long, hard and live: footsteps on the flagstones carry the corridor's whole length and echo off the vault. Recorded for the world; the hints are silent ([silence](../sound/palette.md#ambience)).

## Use of the space {#use}

### How the two use it {#window-use}

Harry stands at the middle window (15 m), forearms on the sill, watching the distant pitch, with his back to the corridor ([Harry at the window](../cast/harry-potter.md#window)). Someone coming from the west landing walks toward his back along 15 m of open corridor, can pass behind him to the east corner, turn out of sight and come back, so a walk past him can be repeated in either direction. Turning from the window, he faces the corridor and the Charms door with the clock above it. The floor in front of the window, between the embrasure and the corridor's middle, is where two standing people meet face to face at their ordinary distance ([folded note](../props/folded-note.md#dropped)).

### Occupancy {#occupancy}

The corridor is empty except for the two: lessons on this floor have ended for the day and the classrooms are shut. No one enters during the film.

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Baseline and change {#baseline}

At the start of the film the corridor is as described, its floor clear, with Harry at the window or arriving there, as the screenplay decides. Within the film a note can fall on the flagstones; nothing else changes. Nothing carries over.
