# Quidditch pitch

The Hogwarts Quidditch pitch, where `snitch-before-you` is flown. Its shape and canon dimensions (500 by 180 feet, hoops 50 feet up) are canon used as an adopted choice, converted to metres; the rest is the production's invention. The rules are in [Quidditch](../world/quidditch.md).

## Shape, zones and landmarks {#shape}

### The field {#field}

An oval grass field about 152 m long (east–west) and 55 m wide, close-mown and green, with a white centre circle about 6 m across where the balls are released.

### The hoops {#hoops}

At each end, three gold hoops on tall poles, about 15 m above the grass, set side by side about 5 m apart across the end of the pitch; the middle hoop is the highest. Each hoop is a ring about 2 m across. The Slytherin hoops are at the west end, the Gryffindor hoops at the east end.

### The stands {#stands}

Around the oval, tall wooden stands rise on towers to about 15–20 m, hung with house-coloured cloths: scarlet and gold, green and silver, blue and bronze, yellow and black. For the practice they are empty.

### Zones of the air {#zones}

- **Low play** (5–15 m): Chasers, Beaters and the Quaffle.
- **Hoop zones**: the air within about 10 m of each set of hoops, at hoop height, where the Keepers patrol.
- **High air** (15–30 m): the Seekers' level, above the play.
- **Mid-pitch** (10–15 m above the centre circle): the referee's post.

## Routes, entrances and levels {#routes}

### Ways in {#ways-in}

Players walk out from the changing rooms at the east end and take off from the grass. Nobody leaves or arrives during the film; everyone is in the air or on the grass at the start.

### Levels {#levels}

The grass, and the air above it; the high air is 15–30 m up, so a rider falling from Seeker height falls for about 1.7–2.5 s before reaching the ground under ordinary gravity.

## Dressing, practical lights and acoustics {#dressing}

### Dressing {#dressing-items}

The box of players' wands (a plain wooden box about 40 × 25 × 10 cm) and the open ball crate (a dark oak chest about 90 × 50 × 30 cm with leather straps, holding the Quaffle, the chained Bludgers and the Snitch before release) on the grass beside the centre circle ([no wands](../world/quidditch.md#no-wands)); the empty stands and their hangings stirring slightly; beyond the stands, the castle to the north and the dark edge of the forest to the west.

### Light {#light}

Open sky, clear to broken cloud; the low sun in the south-west ([afternoon](../world/four-films.md#afternoon)) lights the riders from one side and makes riders flying west squint; the stands on the south-west side throw long shadows north-east across the grass.

### Acoustics {#acoustics}

Open air: wind in the stands' cloths, riders' shouts carrying across the field, the referee's whistle. Recorded for the world; the hints are silent ([silence](../sound/palette.md#ambience)).

## Use of the space {#use}

### The match in the space {#match-use}

The Chasers' play runs end to end in the low air; the Seekers circle in the high air, often over mid-pitch and the ends; the referee hovers at mid-pitch. A Seeker chasing the Snitch can cross the whole pitch in a few seconds and dive from the high air to the grass. The riders' positions are in [the riders record](../crowds/quidditch-riders.md#positions).

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Baseline {#baseline}

The pitch is the same at the start and end of the film; a broken broom or loose twigs may fall to the grass within it. Nothing carries over.
