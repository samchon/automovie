# The practice pitch

## Shape and dimensions {#shape}

### The lawn {#lawn}

A flat oval of short green grass 152 m long east to west and 55 m wide, the canonical 500 by 180 feet ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#roles)). Distances below are in metres from the oval's centre, east and north positive, and heights above the grass. The mown lawn runs on 20 m beyond the oval on the north, south and west, and 100 m beyond its east end as an open runoff where nothing stands. There are no stands, nets or buildings on the practice pitch.

### The goals {#goals}

Three goal posts stand at each end on a goal line 66 m from the centre, at 8 m south, on the axis and 8 m north. Each is a straight pale-gold post 0.3 m thick topped by a hoop of 2.4 m inside diameter facing along the pitch; the middle post's hoop is centred 15 m above the grass and the side posts' 13 m. Nothing spans the gaps between posts, so a rider can fly between them below the hoops. Heights, spacing and hoop size are the production's choices; canon's values were not verified ([practice-play study](../../../research/quidditch/practice-play.md#roles)).

### Boundary {#boundary}

The pitch's boundary, which the [Snitch](../props/snitch.md) never leaves, is the mown lawn including the east runoff, up to 60 m above it.

### Landmarks {#landmarks}

The six goal posts and their hoops; the centre of the oval; the east end of the runoff; a dark band of distant forest ringing the grounds about 450 m out, nearest beyond the north side, which gives an unbroken horizon line in every direction.

## Routes, entrances and levels {#routes}

### Air and ground {#levels}

Riders take off from and land on the lawn anywhere; in play they fly between the grass and 60 m. There is nothing to enter through; the pitch is open on every side.

## Dressing, lights and acoustics {#dressing}

### Light {#light}

Early afternoon on an October Saturday under a clear pale-blue sky: the sun stands high in the south and casts each rider's small shadow on the grass, which shows height above the lawn. No cloud, rain or strong wind.

### Acoustics {#acoustics}

N/A: the films are silent ([sound](../sound/silence.md)).

## Use {#use}

### Practice {#practice}

The two house teams fly their [practice](../world/quidditch.md#practice) over the oval, each in its role's zone ([practice teams](../crowds/practice-teams.md#zones)); the Seekers range over the whole boundary, the east runoff included. A rider far out in the east runoff is more than 100 m from the nearest goal front and out of sight of players watching the ball.

## State changes {#state}

### None {#none}

N/A: the pitch, posts and hoops never change.
