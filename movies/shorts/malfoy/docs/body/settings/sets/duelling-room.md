# Duelling room

A disused classroom on the castle's third floor where the school's old duelling platform is stored; the place of `look-at-me-duel`. Production's invention within canon's Hogwarts.

## Shape, zones and landmarks {#shape}

### The room {#room}

A rectangular stone room 10 m long (north–south) and 8 m wide, with a 4.5 m ceiling of dark beams. Grey stone-flag floor, bare stone walls.

### The platform {#platform}

The old Duelling Club platform runs along the room's length: a raised oak stage 7 m long, 1.8 m wide and 0.9 m high, its top of worn, scuffed planks. It has no rail on any side: its long sides and its ends drop straight 0.9 m to the stone floor, about a teenager's hip height. Two chalk crosses on its top, on its centre line, 4.5 m apart and each 1.25 m in from an end, mark the duellists' places ([form](../world/practice-duelling.md#form)); a duellist with the lead heel on a cross has 0.7–0.8 m of platform behind the rear heel ([the edge](../world/practice-duelling.md#edge)). Across the room, from west to east: 2.4 m of open floor between the window wall and the platform, the 1.8 m platform, 2.0 m of open floor, and the stacked desks filling the last 1.8 m against the east wall. Along its length there is 1.5 m of floor beyond each end.

### Landmarks {#landmarks}

The tall west windows; the stack of desks along the east wall; a blackboard on the north wall with old, half-wiped chalk; the door in the south wall. Each end and long side of the platform is named by the wall it faces: the window side (west), the desk side (east), the blackboard end (north) and the door end (south).

## Routes, entrances and levels {#routes}

### Door {#door}

One heavy wooden door in the middle of the south wall, 1.2 m wide, opening inward; the room's only entrance. It is closed during the practice.

### Steps onto the platform {#steps}

A single wooden stair of three steps, each 0.3 m high and 0.3 m deep and 1 m wide, stands against the middle of the platform's east long side, covering 3.0–4.0 m from either end. The ends have no steps, and the platform's sides beside the chalk crosses drop straight to the floor. The platform is reached only by this stair; climbing elsewhere means a scramble.

### Levels {#levels}

Two levels: the floor and the platform top 0.9 m above it. A person standing on the platform has their feet at about the hip height, and their knees at about the chest height, of a person of the same size standing on the floor beside it.

## Dressing, practical lights and acoustics {#dressing}

### Furniture {#furniture}

About twenty old wooden desks and chairs stacked two high against the east wall; one desk (1.2 m × 0.6 m, top 0.75 m high) left free on the open floor near the south-east corner, where the two leave their robes and their school bags — a brown leather satchel each, about 40 × 30 × 12 cm, with a shoulder strap. A dusty teacher's desk in the north-west corner.

### Light {#light}

Three tall, leaded west windows, each 1.2 m wide and 3 m tall with the sill 1.0 m above the floor, centred 2.5 m, 5 m and 7.5 m from the north wall. The low afternoon sun ([afternoon](../world/four-films.md#afternoon)) comes through them from the south-south-west to south-west, so the beams run steeply toward the north-east as long slanting bars of warm light with dust visible in them. Inference from the sun's position: at half past two (sun about 20° up, toward the south-south-west) the bars fall on the west floor and across the platform, stretched toward the north end; as the sun lowers and swings west they lengthen eastward, and by half past four (about 10° up, toward the south-west) they cross the east floor and climb the stacked desks. Between the bars, and in the corners, the room is in soft shade. Iron wall sconces with unlit candles.

### Acoustics {#acoustics}

A hard, live room: stone and wood make footsteps on the platform boom and voices echo slightly. Recorded for the world; the hints are silent ([silence](../sound/palette.md#ambience)).

## Use of the space {#use}

### How the two use it {#practice-use}

The two practise on the platform, standing on the chalk crosses to duel and walking along it to meet and talk. Casting runs along the platform's length, north–south, so the windows' light crosses the line of the duel from the side. A duellist who retreats along the platform toward an end, or sideways toward a long side, reaches its edge within a few steps: from a chalk cross the rear heel has 0.7–0.8 m of platform behind it, and either long side is 0.9 m from the centre line ([the edge](../world/practice-duelling.md#edge)). The floor around the platform is where robes are left, where a disarmed wand can land, and where someone stepping off would fall.

### Empty at this hour {#empty-room}

Nobody else uses the room after lessons; nobody comes in during the film.

## State changes {#state-changes}

### Baseline and change {#baseline}

At the start of the film the room is as described, the platform clear, the desks stacked. Within the film a wand can lie on the floor, chalk can be scuffed, and a robe can slip from the desk; nothing carries over.
