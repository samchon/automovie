# Sound palette

What the films sound like: nothing, in the delivered hints. The commission makes the motion hints silent, keeps dialogue only in the screenplays and produces no audio ([silent hints](../../../instructions/commission.md#silent-hints)). Characters' voices belong to their cast records.

## Ambience {#ambience}

### No ambience is produced {#no-ambience}

No ambience, room tone or sound effect is recorded, generated or mixed for any film; the delivered picture has no audio track. The places' acoustics are recorded in their set records as facts of the world only, and the only words in the films are the dialogue the screenplays hold as text.

## Music {#music}

### No music {#no-music}

No music of any usage — background or source, instrumental or vocal — is chosen, licensed or produced for any film, and no character plays or sings music on screen.
