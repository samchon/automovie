# Silence

## Ambience palette {#ambience}

### No soundtrack {#no-soundtrack}

N/A: the four films are silent motion hints with no soundtrack at all, no ambience, effects, speech or music ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#silence)). The dialogue the screenplays write is never heard; a screenplay's sound records state `none` for every layer except dialogue, which exists as text. Sets and props mention a sound only to say the films do not play it.
