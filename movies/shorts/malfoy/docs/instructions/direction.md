# Direction

Choices the commission leaves open, decided by the lead writer within delegation. Each records its reason; changing one changes its settings consumers through repair.

## Carina takes the Malfoy heir's place {#carina-malfoy-family}

Delegated choice. Carina Malfoy is the only child of Lucius and Narcissa Malfoy and stands where canon puts Draco: a Slytherin in Harry's year and Slytherin's Seeker. Draco does not exist in this world. Reason: the commission names her a Malfoy and a fifteen-year-old schoolmate of Harry; giving her canon's Malfoy place gives her a known family weight, a house, a Quidditch position for `snitch-before-you`, and a reason for Harry's guarded first reading of her, without adapting any published Carina story.

## A quiet autumn of the fifth year {#quiet-autumn-fifth-year}

Delegated choice. The films take place in the autumn term of Harry's fifth year (both are fifteen), but the canon events of that year — the war, the Ministry and the new Defence teacher — are outside these films: never shown, mentioned or felt. Reason: the premises are small, private and warm; a calm school term keeps their stakes on the two people.

## Warm, light and physical tone {#tone}

Delegated choice. The films are light romantic comedy-drama: embarrassment, nerve and kindness, never cruelty or real injury. Danger exists only for a moment and is resolved by a hand. Humour comes from bodies — mistimed reaches, repeated attempts, two people moving at once — rather than from jokes. Reason: each premise turns on a physical exchange, and a silent hint must carry the tone through motion.

## Contacts are seen; the cut is brisk {#seen-contacts-and-pace}

Delegated choice. Every contact a film depends on is seen on screen at the moment it is made — held in an insert or in a shot that frames both the hands and the faces — and is never cut away from or hidden behind a body at that moment. Each film's mean shot lasts 2.5–4.5 s (about 13–24 shots in 60 s), with holds of 5–8 s where a contact or a look must be watched and 1–2 s shots at its climax ([vertical short coverage](../research/cinematography/vertical-short-coverage.md#pace)). Reason: the films are told through contacts, so a contact the audience does not see has not happened for them; and a 60-second vertical film is watched at short-form pace.

## The same starting point in every film {#baseline-relationship}

Delegated choice. Every film begins from one relationship baseline: Carina has liked Harry for some time and has never told him or anyone; Harry knows her by name, house and reputation, has shared lessons and a few short exchanges with her, and has no idea of her feelings. Each film moves them closer in its own way, and the next film starts again from this baseline. Reason: the commission forbids carry-over, so the baseline must be one fixed starting state that every premise can grow from.
