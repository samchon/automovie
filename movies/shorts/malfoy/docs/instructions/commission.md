# Commission facts the settings rely on

The user's requirements that bind every settings record, as [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md) adopts them. Each H2 is one independently changeable requirement with its source.

## Four films, one world {#four-films}

User requirement: four 60-second shorts, `look-at-me-duel`, `paper-crane-invitation`, `snitch-before-you` and `three-oclock`, share one cast, one world, one set of props and one motion library (PROJECT.md, Commission and Films). Each film is its own story; nothing one film changes carries into another.

## Premises {#premises}

User-adopted premises, quoted from PROJECT.md's film table: a private practice duel in which a repeated wrist grip becomes a rescue; Carina tries to take back a paper-crane tea invitation from Harry, and the tug becomes an invitation to share a bench; in a fourteen-rider practice match Carina gives up an almost certain Snitch catch to save Harry, ending in tandem flight; Carina tries to invite Harry out, through a repeated walk, a note offered and withdrawn and two people bending at once. The settings supply the world these premises need and leave every event to the screenplays.

## Cast and ages {#cast-and-ages}

User requirement: Carina Malfoy and Harry Potter are fifteen-year-old Hogwarts schoolmates (PROJECT.md, opening paragraph).

## Uniforms {#uniforms}

User requirement: Carina wears a Slytherin uniform and Harry a Gryffindor uniform, readable as collar, house-striped tie, charcoal knit, open black robe, school bottoms and black shoes, with hands, knees and feet left legible (PROJECT.md, Scope and form).

## Representation and format {#format}

User requirement: Human polygon figures for people, coarse code-authored geometry for sets and props; vertical 9:16 at 720 × 1280 and 24 fps; each film 60 seconds; direct 3D render is the delivered picture (PROJECT.md, Scope and form).

## Silence {#silence}

User requirement: the films are silent motion hints with no speech or music; dialogue lives only in the screenplays (PROJECT.md, Scope and form).

## Language {#language}

User requirement: all production text, titles and source are English (PROJECT.md, Scope and form).

## Source boundary {#source-boundary}

User requirement: the four stories earlier lived as separate productions; their settings, screenplays and source are references only, and every settings record is written anew under the current skills (PROJECT.md, Commission). These are original fan shorts: no published passage is adapted, and no fact here claims to be the canon of a published Carina Malfoy story. Where Harry Potter canon is used (houses, Quidditch, wands, Hogwarts), it is a choice the production adopts, not a claim checked against every book.

## Readable bodily action {#readable-action}

Commission purpose: the films are deterministic staging and motion hints for video diffusion (repository AGENTS.md and PROJECT.md). A grip, a release, a support, a handover or a rescue is shown by bodies, hands and objects; a spell's light or a face never stands in for the bodily action.

## Fourteen riders {#fourteen-riders}

User requirement: the Quidditch film is a fourteen-rider practice match (PROJECT.md, Films), so both full teams fly, twelve anonymous supporting players among them.
