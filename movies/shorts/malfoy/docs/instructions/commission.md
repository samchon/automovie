# Commission

The user's requirements for the four films, as commissioned. Operating scope (output files, exclusions, tools) is recorded in [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md); these are the creative requirements the settings and screenplays must keep.

## Four fan shorts about Carina Malfoy and Harry Potter {#four-films}

Source: user commission. The work is four original English Harry Potter fan shorts about Carina Malfoy and Harry Potter, fifteen-year-old Hogwarts schoolmates. The four film names are fixed as `look-at-me-duel`, `paper-crane-invitation`, `snitch-before-you` and `three-oclock`; each name is also its screenplay file name and its `?film=` value.

## One shared world, with no carry-over between films {#shared-world}

Source: user commission. The four films share one cast, one world, one set of props and one motion library. Nothing one film changes carries into another: each film starts from the same baseline states of people, relationship, places and objects, and its events end with the film.

## look-at-me-duel {#look-at-me-duel}

Source: user commission, premise quoted: "A private practice duel in which a repeated wrist grip becomes a rescue." The duel is private (the two of them only), it is practice rather than a fight, a wrist grip recurs within it, and the same grip finally rescues someone.

## paper-crane-invitation {#paper-crane-invitation}

Source: user commission, premise quoted: "Carina tries to take back a paper-crane tea invitation from Harry, and the tug becomes an invitation to share a bench." The invitation is a folded paper crane inviting Harry to tea; it has reached Harry; Carina tries to retrieve it; both hold it at once; the tug ends with the two sharing a bench.

## snitch-before-you {#snitch-before-you}

Source: user commission, premise quoted: "In a fourteen-rider practice match Carina gives up an almost certain Snitch catch to save Harry, ending in tandem flight." Fourteen riders fly (two full Quidditch teams of seven), the match is practice, Carina is within reach of the Snitch when she turns away from it to save Harry, and the film ends with the two flying together on one broom.

## three-oclock {#three-oclock}

Source: user commission, premise quoted: "Carina tries to invite Harry out: a repeated walk, a note offered and withdrawn, two people bending at once." Carina means to ask Harry out; she walks past or toward him more than once; she holds out a note and pulls it back; at some point both of them bend down at the same moment.

## Sixty-second vertical films {#vertical-sixty-seconds}

Source: user commission. Each film runs 60 seconds, vertical 9:16 at 720 × 1280 pixels and 24 frames per second, with programmed staging, motion and camera for every scene. Composition, framing and the look are designed for that vertical frame.

## Bare bodies and coarse geometry {#bare-bodies}

Source: user commission. People are the installed Human figures as bare bodies carrying pose and motion only; uniforms, hair and faces are not modelled, because diffusion later takes appearance from reference images. Sets and props are coarse code-authored geometry that stays identifiable. The settings still record appearance, so that later reference images and the hint agree.

## Silent hints; dialogue only in the screenplays {#silent-hints}

Source: user commission. The motion hints are silent. Dialogue lives only in the screenplays, and no audio is produced: no voices, sound effects, ambience or music.

## English {#english}

Source: user commission. All production text, titles and source are English, and the characters speak English as British Hogwarts students.

## Canon boundary {#canon-boundary}

Source: user commission. These are original fan shorts. Harry Potter canon used (houses, Quidditch, wands, Hogwarts) is an adopted choice, not a checked claim, and no published Carina Malfoy story is adapted.
