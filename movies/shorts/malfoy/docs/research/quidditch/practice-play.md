# Quidditch practice play

What fourteen riders do during a self-managed Hogwarts practice match: the canonical roles and balls, and the flying patterns that keep every supporting player visibly working while the two Seekers race. Canon supplies the roles, balls and scoring; the real ground sport Quadball supplies the closest observed play patterns; aerial distances and rates are the production's choices. Sources are in the [index](../index.md).

## Law {#law}

### Roles and balls {#roles}

Each team flies seven players: three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper and one Seeker (WIKI-Q, lead). Chasers pass the scarlet leather Quaffle and score ten points by throwing it through one of the opposing three hoops; two iron Bludgers fly at the players and the Beaters drive them away with short wooden clubs; the Seeker hunts the golden Snitch, "the approximate size of a walnut", whose catch ends the match and scores 150 points (WIKI-Q, Game progression). The canonical pitch is an oval 500 feet long and 180 feet wide (QTTA-R, citing *Quidditch Through the Ages*), about 152 m by 55 m. Canon's hoop heights and ball diameters were not verified at a page; the production's sizes are its own and live in [the pitch](../../body/settings/sets/quidditch-pitch.md) and the ball records.

### Observed patterns of the ground analogue {#analogue}

In Quadball, Chasers pass and score while the Keeper holds privileges in a zone around the team's own hoops, Beaters contest the balls among themselves, possession changes quickly after goals, and a team that trails delays the Seeker's catch (WIKI-QB, Positions and Playing). The production keeps those duties and their spatial habits and lifts them into the air.

### Chasers {#chasers}

Three attacking Chasers fly a loose triangle 8–15 m between riders and pass every 2–5 s; a pass travels 8–15 m on a shallow arc in 0.8–1.5 s and is caught in both hands or one hand against the chest. Each defending Chaser marks one attacker from 3–6 m, on the side toward the defenders' hoops. A shot is thrown from 10–20 m out. Production choices for aerial play.

### Keepers {#keepers}

A Keeper patrols in front of the team's own hoops, within 10 m to either side of the middle hoop, 5–15 m out and within 3 m of hoop height, always facing the ball. Production choice following the analogue's keeper zone.

### Beaters and Bludgers {#beaters}

Each Beater stays within 10–20 m of a Bludger and swings when it comes within bat reach, about 1.2 m from the shoulder; a struck Bludger leaves at 10–15 m/s in the direction of the swing and travels 20–40 m before it turns toward another rider. Between strikes a Bludger flies at 8–12 m/s toward the nearest rider in its half of the pitch. A Beater never swings at the Quaffle. Production choices.

### Seekers and the Snitch {#seekers}

Seekers fly apart from the ball play, 10–30 m above or outside it, scanning; on sighting the Snitch they sprint (see [broom riding](../flight/broom-riding.md#speed)). The Snitch darts on short curves of 1–3 m radius, hovers 0.5–2 s at a time, reaches 18 m/s and stays inside the pitch's boundary. Production choices.

### Stopping play {#stopping}

A practice has no referee in these films; the two captains manage it. Any player stops play by raising an arm straight up. At a stop every player holds position and turns toward the trouble; Chasers keep hold of the Quaffle; Beaters keep driving the Bludgers away from stopped riders, because a Bludger does not stop; nobody flies within 3 m of a rescue already under way. Production choices for a self-managed practice.

## Outcome table {#outcomes}

| Intent | Condition | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pass | Receiver's hands meet the ball on its arc | Received |
| Pass | A marker's hand meets it first | Intercepted |
| Shot | Ball passes inside a hoop, Keeper's hands miss it | Goal, ten points |
| Shot | Keeper's hands or body meet it | Saved |
| Beater swing | Bludger inside bat reach | Struck away |
| Beater swing | Bludger outside bat reach | Missed |
| Seeker reach | See the [broom-riding outcomes](../flight/broom-riding.md#outcomes) | Caught, missed or unseated |
| Raised arm | Any | Play stops; Bludgers keep flying |

## Measurable targets {#targets}

- Every supporting player is moving at all times: no rider hovers still for more than 2 s.
- In open play a pass is completed every 2–5 s; attacking Chasers stay 8–15 m apart.
- Keepers stay within their goal-front band; Beaters stay within 20 m of a Bludger.
- Seekers fly at least 10 m from the nearest Chaser except while crossing the play.
- After a stop, every rider faces the trouble within 2 s and none comes within 3 m of a rescue.
