# Research sources

Every study under `docs/research/` cites the sources below by ID. All were read online on 2026-11-09. No original is retained under `references/`: saving a copy of a third-party file needs the user's approval, which this commission has not given, so each row names the online original, the locator that was read and its rights. Two public-domain CDC files were opened from the fetch tool's own cached copies (`CDC-STAT`, `NHANES`). A row marked *summary only* was located but read only through a search engine's or publisher's summary; studies treat its values as routes to evidence with stated uncertainty, never as measurements.

## Unavailable {#unavailable}

- The FAA *Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge*, chapter 5 (https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/07_phak_ch5.pdf), returned HTTP 403. The level-turn relations the flight study uses are derived there from Newtonian mechanics instead.
- Sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit timing: the open studies located (Wairagkar et al. 2021, arXiv 2107.06859; a 2022 PLOS ONE comparison, PMC9578638) were not read past their abstracts, and a PMC table (PMC8276699, Table 3) was blocked by a verification page. The sitting study states its values as production inferences.
- Chan, Parker, Van der Loos and Croft (2012), *Grip forces and load forces in handovers* (ACM/IEEE HRI), and Strabala et al. (2013), *Toward seamless human-robot handovers*, were located but not opened.
- No measured source for the flutter of a falling paper sheet, an enchanted paper glider or aerial ball passing was found; the studies record those values as stylized choices.
