# Research sources

Every original the studies cite, with where its retained copy lives. Copies under `references/` carry an open licence that permits them; copies under the ignored `references/private/` have unconfirmed or restrictive reuse terms and stay local. All were retrieved on 2026-11-09. Studies cite a source by its key.

## Unavailable

- No open source for fifteen-year-old gait was retained: ito-2022 stops at 12 years and an adult study (World Journal of Clinical Cases 2023, 11(29) 7061) refused download. The study translates the 12-year values by leg length and says so.
- No source describing a trapeze or rescue wrist grip was retained; the duel study records the grip as the production's own choice built from hand anatomy.
- Quidditch, wand duelling and spells are canon used as an adopted choice under the [canon boundary](../instructions/commission.md#canon-boundary), not checked claims, so no source is retained for them.
