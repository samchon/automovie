# A projecting stone cap failing and falling

How the damaged support's projecting cap on the [training court](../../body/settings/sets/training-court.md) tips, falls and scatters, and how quickly a person must move to clear it. The physics is ordinary rigid-body fall; the failure stages are the production's choice for a cracked mortar joint, since no measured source for this assembly exists.

## Law {#law}

### Free fall {#fall}

A released body falls with the standard acceleration of gravity, 9.80665 m/s² (the SI conventional value). The time to fall a height h from rest is √(2h/g): 0.45 s for 1.0 m, 0.64 s for 2.0 m, 0.74 s for the 2.7 m from the cap's underside to the floor. Air drag is negligible for a 0.2 m³ block and for stone fragments over these heights.

### Failure stages {#stages}

| Stage | What is seen | Duration | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Struck | A spell or blow breaks the cracked lower mortar fillet; two small chips drop beside the base | Instant; chips fall 0.74 s | Fall law |
| Tipping | The cap's front edge dips while its rear lower edge still rests on the bearing ledge and the upper mortar web still holds; a thin stream of grit runs from the broken joint | 0.8–1.5 s, rotating 5–15° | Production choice: long enough to be noticed after one reaction time |
| Release | The upper web breaks; the cap slips off the ledge | Instant | Production choice |
| Fall | The cap drops through the free air in front of the upright, rotating forward 60–90° | 0.74 s | Fall law; rotation a production choice |
| Impact | It lands on a broad face, stays where it lands and throws three fragments | Instant | Production choice: a broad landing does not roll |
| Settling | A low dust puff spreads and thins | 1.5–2.0 s | Production choice |

### Fragments and dust {#fragments}

The impact throws exactly three angular fragments no larger than 8 cm. They leave at 2–4 m/s, within 40° of the horizontal direction away from the upright, travel at most 2.5 m and stop. Dust rises no higher than 0.8 m within 1 m of the block. All of these are production choices bounding what the scene can show; their actual directions are seeded variation inside these limits.

### Danger region and clearance {#clearance}

The danger region is the floor area under the cap's fall path widened by 0.3 m on every side. A person is clear when the whole body and any held wand lie outside it. Quick sideways steps take 0.35–0.45 s each and cover 0.5–0.7 m (production inference from the fast walking cadence of 133 steps/min in the [walking study](../everyday/walking.md#gait) and a lateral step shorter than a forward one), so two of them move a person 1.0–1.4 m in 0.7–0.9 s. The cap reaches standing head height (1.7 m) 0.45 s after release. A person who starts moving one reaction time (0.21–0.25 s, [duel study](../duelling/practice-duel.md#reaction)) after the tipping becomes visible therefore clears a region they stand at most 1.4 m inside before the cap arrives.

### What a shield can do {#shield}

A shield charm stops light thrown objects and spells; it cannot support or deflect a falling block of this mass (about 500 kg, from the cap's 0.2 m³ at the 2.5 t/m³ typical of limestone, a production inference). The [wand magic](../../body/settings/world/magic.md) rules own that limit.

## Outcome table {#outcomes}

| Person's intent | Condition at the cap's arrival | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moves out | Whole body and wand outside the danger region | Clear |
| Moves out | Any part inside | Struck; not admitted in these films, and the law reports it |
| Shields against the block | Any | Not admitted: a shield cannot hold the cap |
| Shields against fragments | Shield standing between fragments and people | Blocked: fragments drop on the far side |

## Measurable targets {#targets}

- The cap falls the 2.7 m to the floor in 0.74 s ± 1 frame and never passes through the upright.
- The tipping stage lasts 0.8–1.5 s before release.
- Exactly three impact fragments, each stopping within 2.5 m of the block; dust stays below 0.8 m.
- Every person and wand is outside the danger region when the cap reaches head height.
