# Riding a broom

How a student sits, steers, banks and sprints on a broom, how a rider who slips hangs on, and how a hanging rider transfers onto another broom's rear seat into tandem flight. Broom flight is invented. Its closest real analogues are horse riding and equestrian vaulting for the seat and the mount, and aircraft or motorcycle level turns for banking; the production departs from them where stated. Sources are in the [index](../index.md).

## Law {#law}

### Seat {#seat}

The rider sits astride the shaft with the pelvis on it at the seat point, the thighs closed on the wood and the lower legs hanging forward and down. Both hands hold the shaft ahead of the pelvis, the leading hand 0.35–0.50 m ahead of it. The trunk inclines forward 20–45° from upright when cruising and 50–70° when sprinting, chest toward the shaft. Pelvis and thighs carry the weight; hands steer. These are production choices on the riding analogue: a mounted vaulter lands "erect and centred in the seat astride" (WIKI-EV, Competition movements), and sitting a moving mount needs conditioned trunk muscles rather than hands (WIKI-HG, rider's seat).

### Steering {#steering}

Pulling the shaft's nose up climbs, pushing it down dives; climbs and dives stay within 45° of level. Leaning the trunk and shifting the seat to one side rolls the broom into a bank to that side. Leaning the chest down speeds the broom up; sitting up slows it. Production choices.

### Speed {#speed}

| Riding | Speed, m/s | Analogue (WIKI-HG) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hovering and manoeuvring | 0–3 | A horse's walk is about 1.9 m/s |
| Cruising in play | 6–10 | Canter 4.4–7.5 m/s, toward a gallop |
| Seeker sprint | 12–18 | Gallop 11–13 m/s and beyond |

Acceleration and braking stay under 3 m/s². The sprint above a gallop is a stylized departure that keeps a Seeker race faster than the ball play behind it.

### Banking {#banking}

In a level turn without sideslip the broom and rider lean into the turn by the bank angle φ for which tan φ = v² / (g r), at speed v on radius r, and the rider's load factor is 1 / cos φ. This follows from Newton's second law (the horizontal share of the seat's supporting force provides the centripetal force and the vertical share balances gravity); it is the relation pilot training derives (the FAA handbook chapter was unavailable; see the [index](../index.md#unavailable)).

| Speed | Radius 15 m | Radius 25 m | Radius 40 m |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 m/s | 23° | 15° | 9° |
| 12 m/s | 44° | 30° | 20° |
| 16 m/s | 60° (cap) | 46° | 33° |

The production caps banks at 60°, a load factor of 2, and a tighter turn at that speed is not admitted.

### Overreaching {#overreach}

A seated rider can reach forward or sideways about one arm length plus a 0.3 m lean of the trunk without unloading the pelvis (production inference from the cast's arm lengths). Reaching farther slides the pelvis off the seat point; once the pelvis passes the shaft's side the thighs lose their hold and the rider drops, catching the shaft with one or both hands if a hand is on it.

### Hanging {#hanging}

A rider hanging under a moving shaft holds by the hands with arms straight. The healthy-fitness minimum for a fifteen-year-old's flexed-arm hang is 15 s for boys and 8 s for girls (FITG, summary only); a trained Seeker can therefore hang two-handed for 30 s or more and one-handed for up to 10 s (production inference). The hanging body swings below the hands; the free arm cannot reach back up to the shaft against that swing. A broom carrying an unseated rider keeps its line and sinks 0.3–0.5 m/s (production choice).

### Riderless broom {#riderless}

A broom left without a hand or a seat keeps its heading, slows at about 1 m/s² and sinks gently; it neither follows its rider nor returns (production choice).

### Tandem {#tandem}

A broom carries two riders astride, the rear rider's pelvis 0.45–0.55 m behind the front rider's. When the second rider's weight comes on, the broom dips 0.5–1.0 m and recovers in a shallow climb over 2–3 s; with two aboard, pitch answers more slowly and banks stay under 30° (production choices).

### Transfer onto a rear seat {#transfer}

Closest analogue: the vaulting mount, in which a vaulter beside a cantering horse on a 30 m circle springs up, swings one leg high over the back and lands softly, erect and centred astride (WIKI-EV). The production adapts it to a hanging rider and a second broom brought alongside. The conditions are: the shafts parallel within 10°, their speeds matched within 0.5 m/s, and the second broom's empty rear section within 0.5 m of the hanging rider's free hand, at lower-chest height. The sequence and its windows are production choices:

1. The free hand closes on the new shaft's rear section: 0.3–0.5 s.
2. The other hand opens on the old shaft and joins the first on the new one; the weight swings onto the new grip: 0.5–1.0 s.
3. The elbows bend and the lower chest comes over the shaft: 0.8–1.2 s.
4. The near leg swings across behind the front rider and the pelvis settles astride: 0.8–1.2 s.
5. Both riders steady while the broom recovers: 0.5–1.0 s.

The front rider leans forward over her hands through the whole transfer, keeping her knees and back clear of the rear section. The old broom, released, follows the riderless rule and must have open air ahead of it.

## Outcome table {#outcomes}

| Intent | Condition | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach for the Snitch | Hand closes on it within arm length plus 0.3 m | Caught |
| Reach for the Snitch | Snitch moves out of the closing hand | Missed |
| Reach for the Snitch | Reach beyond arm length plus 0.3 m | Unseated; hangs if a hand holds the shaft, falls otherwise |
| Transfer to a rear seat | All three conditions met, new grip before old release | Transferred into tandem |
| Transfer to a rear seat | Any condition missing | Not admitted |
| Release the old shaft first | Any | Falls; not admitted in these films |
| Bank | Required angle over 60° | Not admitted: widen the turn |

## Measurable targets {#targets}

- Riders cruise at 6–10 m/s and sprint at 12–18 m/s; accelerations stay under 3 m/s².
- Every turn's bank matches tan φ = v² / (g r) within 5° and never exceeds 60°.
- A hanging rider hangs one-handed for no more than 10 s.
- During a transfer the two shafts stay parallel within 10°, speeds within 0.5 m/s, and the rider always holds at least one shaft.
- The tandem broom dips 0.5–1.0 m and recovers within 3 s.
