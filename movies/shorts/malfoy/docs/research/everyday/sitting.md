# Sitting down, shifting along and standing up

How a student lowers onto a backless bench, slides along it to make room and rises again, for the corridor and courtyard films. No retained source measures these transitions for teenagers (see [unavailable sources](../index.md#unavailable)); every value below is a production inference or choice and says so.

## Law {#law}

### Stand to sit {#stand-to-sit}

The sitter first stands with the backs of both calves within a few centimetres of the seat's front edge, feet shoulder-width apart and flat. The descent then runs in three connected phases, the order the four-phase stand-to-sit model of the located studies names (neutral, pre-squat, squat, stabilization):

1. Pre-squat: the trunk flexes forward 20–35° at the hips while the knees begin to bend, moving the centre of mass back over the feet.
2. Squat: hips and knees flex together until the buttocks meet the seat; the hands may rest on the thighs or the seat edge.
3. Stabilization: the trunk returns upright and the knees settle near 90° on a seat at knee height.

A whole descent at natural pace takes 1.5–2.5 s, with the squat about half of it. Basis: the located summaries report sit-to-stand under about 2 s in young people and a slow, instructed rise of 3.3 ± 0.3 s; stand-to-sit took about as long as sit-to-stand in the 2022 comparison's abstract. These are routes, not measurements read in full, so the range is wide.

### Sit to stand {#sit-to-stand}

The reverse: the feet draw back under the knees, the trunk flexes forward until the shoulders pass over the knees (momentum transfer), the buttocks lift, and hips and knees extend together. 1.2–2.0 s at natural pace, production inference.

### Shifting along a bench {#shift}

A seated person moves sideways along a backless bench by planting both feet, unloading the seat through the feet and hands, sliding or hopping the pelvis 0.3–0.6 m and settling. One shift takes 0.8–1.2 s and moves the pelvis no more than 0.6 m; a longer move is two shifts. Production choice: the loaded pelvis never leaves the seat by more than 3 cm.

### Seat height {#seat-height}

A seat 0.45 m high suits both cast members: their knee heights (about 0.47–0.50 m, derived from their statures at the usual 0.29 ratio of knee height to stature, a production inference) put the thighs close to level when seated.

## Outcome table {#outcomes}

| Intent combination | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Sit with calves at the seat edge | Seated, feet flat |
| Sit with the seat more than 0.15 m behind the calves | Not admitted: the sitter steps back first |
| Shift toward an occupied place | Stops 0.10–0.15 m short of the other person's hip |
| Two people sit side by side on a 1.6–1.8 m bench | Each occupies 0.45–0.55 m of seat with 0.05–0.20 m between hips |

## Measurable targets {#targets}

- Stand to sit 1.5–2.5 s; sit to stand 1.2–2.0 s; one sideways shift 0.8–1.2 s.
- At seat contact the pelvis is within 3 cm of the seat surface and the feet have not moved.
- Seated, both feet stay planted unless the screenplay moves them.
