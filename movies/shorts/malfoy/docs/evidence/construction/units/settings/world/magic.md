# docs/body/settings/world/magic.md#rules

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet delivers the magic the films show: how a cast is made ("A person without their wand in hand cannot cast"), the complete spell catalogue with effect and look for every spell any film can show, how a jet travels, how charmed paper and brooms fly, and the limits, including why a fall is stopped "by a hand or a broom" away from Madam Hooch.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Every rule serves a premise: the two duelling spells for the duel, *Avis Charta* with its pull and its counter-charm *Finite* for the crane tug, broom speeds and the two-rider rule for the tandem flight, and the Slowing Charm's range for the match rescue. The catalogue is closed ("No other spell is cast in any film") rather than a general inventory of canon magic.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The facts carry values and conditions a later author can be held to: a jet "at about 15–25 m/s, crossing a 4–5 m practice distance in 0.16–0.33 s"; paper flight "(about 1–1.5 m/s) at head height (1.4–1.8 m)" with "a force of about 0.2–0.5 N"; cancelling with "the counter-charm *Finite*" leaves the paper where it is, and "Cancelling does not bring it back to the caster"; brooms that cruise "at 8–15 m/s and dives at up to 30 m/s", two riders cruising "at no more than 10 m/s" yet carried "at the speed it already has while its rider brakes" during a rescue; the Slowing Charm brakes a fall "to a gentle descent of about 2 m/s within about half a second" and works "only on someone she can see within about 10 m of her wand", which with her reaction, cast and flight times gives the stated range of "about 20 m of her wand (for a fall from 15 m) to about 33 m (for a fall from 30 m)". Canon is marked adopted and each invention is labelled as one.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Magic's rules live here; duelling rules and the wands are routed to their own records in the opening paragraph, broom builds to the broom record and the rescue procedure to the Quidditch record it links. The limits state what can and cannot stop a fall without deciding whether anyone falls or is saved.

# docs/body/settings/world/magic.md#tone

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states how magic feels in these films: "magic is schoolwork and ordinary life, not spectacle".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

It names the films' own magic — "nobody marvels at a flying crane or a hovering broom" — and states that "the films never pause to show magic off", which follows from the films' bodies-first tone.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The fact is checkable in a screenplay: a cast is as casual as a thrown ball, no character reacts with wonder, and no beat exists to display magic.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

It links the work's tone owner (`docs/body/settings/world/four-films.md#register`) rather than restating it, and adds only what is specific to magic.
