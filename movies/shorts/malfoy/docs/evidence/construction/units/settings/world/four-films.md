# docs/body/settings/world/four-films.md#rules

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the work-level rules and states each: the release units ("The work is four films, each a complete story of 60 seconds"), the reset rule ("Nothing said, broken, given or learned in one film exists in another"), the work's promise in six named parts, the canon frame with its two departures, the full population of who appears, and how people and objects are identified on a bare-body hint.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The rules come from this commission: the four named films, the reset that the no-carry-over requirement forces, a promise built on "a contact between their bodies, or between their hands and one object", and a population justified by the actual places ("each of which happens in a place that is empty at that hour"). No institution or interest is borrowed from the author's process.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each H3 is one checkable statement with its scope and limits: the raster, frame rate and length; the reset with its stated reading of state-change records ("A record that describes a change describes what may happen within one film and how it resets"); the promise's audience, experience, reward, engine, variation and title promise; canon as "an adopted choice, not checked against the books"; the exact people per film ("Nobody else is seen or heard"); and the identification rule "people are told apart by body form, height and carriage, and objects by silhouette and size". Canon, invention and requirement are distinguished by their links to the commission and direction.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The record opens by routing magic, duelling and Quidditch to their own files, and the population rule links each place's occupancy fact and each participant's record instead of copying them. It fixes baselines and the reset rule but no event: the promise names what kind of exchange each film holds, not which beat happens or in what order.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#four-films

The release-units fact names the four films with their fixed names, and the promise and population facts make the two fifteen-year-old schoolmates the leads of all four.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#shared-world

The baseline-reset fact realizes one shared world without carry-over: every film starts from the recorded baselines and "Nothing said, broken, given or learned in one film exists in another".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#canon-boundary

The canon-frame fact adopts Hogwarts, houses, wands, spells, brooms and Quidditch "as an adopted choice, not checked against the books" and states "No published Carina Malfoy story is adapted."

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#bare-bodies

The bare-bodies fact states that people are bare bodies carrying pose and motion only, that recorded clothing, hair and faces serve later reference images, and how figures and objects are told apart on the hint.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#vertical-sixty-seconds

The release-units fact fixes each film at 60 seconds, 9:16 at 720 × 1280 and 24 frames per second.

# docs/body/settings/world/four-films.md#time-weather-season

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet delivers the films' date, hour and weather: "the middle of October in the autumn term of the leads' fifth year", "between 14:30 and 16:30 by the castle clocks", and "The air is cool (10–14 °C), the sky clear to broken cloud, the ground dry, and wind below about 3 m/s".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The values follow what these films need: the afternoon follows "after the day's last lesson for the two leads", the still air is stated as "calm enough that paper flies where it is charmed to and a practice match is flown", and the sun's height is derived for Hogwarts' adopted latitude so the long shadows the sets record have a cause.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each value is checkable and bounded: the year is never shown; days are "not ordered"; the sun's height is an inference "for that latitude in mid-October, on British Summer Time" with "each value uncertain by about 3°" and its consequence for shadow length, "about two and a half to six times the height of what casts them (1/tan 20° ≈ 2.7; 1/tan 10° ≈ 5.7)"; the weather excludes "rain, fog or wind strong enough to move people". Canon (the Highlands) is marked as adopted and the sun values as inference.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Time and weather are owned once here and linked by the magic, duelling, Quidditch and set records; the courtyard clock is linked rather than redescribed. No event is placed on a day or hour.

## @evidence instructions/direction.md#quiet-autumn-fifth-year

The October fact places all four films in the autumn term of the fifth year, when both leads are fifteen, without showing a date or year, as the quiet-autumn choice requires.

# docs/body/settings/world/four-films.md#tone

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states the films' tone and its limits: "light romantic comedy-drama", humour that "comes from bodies", danger that "lasts a moment and is ended by a hand", and the closeness boundary "There is no kiss, no real injury, no blood and no cruelty in any film."

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The tone is tied to these premises' physical exchanges — "a reach that comes too early, a walk repeated, two people moving at the same moment" — and the closeness limit lists the films' own contacts ("a held wrist or hand, shoulders touching on a bench, one riding behind the other with arms around a waist").

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Both facts are checkable against a screenplay: where humour may come from, how long danger may last, and an explicit list of what never happens, so a later author can be held to the boundary rather than to an adjective.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Tone is owned here and cited by the magic, duelling and Quidditch tone facts; it routes its source to the direction instruction. It sets limits on closeness without choosing which closeness any film reaches.

## @evidence instructions/direction.md#tone

The register fact restates the delegated tone as world fact — embarrassment, nerve and kindness, physical humour, momentary danger ended by a hand — and the closeness-limit fact adds its concrete boundary.
