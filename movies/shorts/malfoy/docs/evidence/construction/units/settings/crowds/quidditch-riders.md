# docs/body/settings/crowds/quidditch-riders.md#arrangement

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the group's number and arrangement: "Gryffindor: three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper. Slytherin: three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper", and where each role flies.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The arrangement is this match's: Chasers "in a loose triangle 5–15 m above the grass", Keepers "1–3 m in front of their own three hoops, at hoop height", and the Seekers placed above them, outside the group.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each role has a position, height and spacing in metres, and the count is checked against the rule: "With the two Seekers they make fourteen riders".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The group shares one file because its members act by position; the opening paragraph says any rider who must act alone is named by role. Rules and hoops are linked to their owners.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#snitch-before-you

It supplies the premise's fourteen riders: twelve team riders here plus the two Seekers.

# docs/body/settings/crowds/quidditch-riders.md#action

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet writes the riders' behaviour for their whole presence: during play, when a rider is in danger, and after.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Their reactions fit the film's rescue: "none of them is within 3–5 m of a Seeker in the upper air at 25–30 m and none can save a fall from there; they watch", with the stated exception that "A Seeker who comes down to their height, for instance over the hoops, is within their reach", and afterward "they react with relief — straightening, a rider's fist raised — rather than with cheering."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each behaviour is timed or measured: "a pass every 2–4 s"; Beaters swing at a Bludger "within about 3 m of a teammate" with "a short wooden bat about 0.6 m long held in one hand"; heads turn "within about half a second"; all stop "within about 2 s" of the whistle; rescue is possible only "from within about 3–5 m when the fall begins" and only if "it begins about 25 m or more up", against the twelve's heights "at 5–15 m (the Keepers at hoop height, 15 m)".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The stopping and rescue rules are owned by the Quidditch record and linked; the group's reactions are baseline conduct, and "Nothing they do changes the outcome between the two Seekers."
