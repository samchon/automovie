# docs/body/settings/sets/duelling-room.md#shape

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the room's shape and size, the platform with its edges and chalk crosses, the room's cross-section from wall to wall, and the landmarks that name each direction.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The platform is built for this duel's danger: "It has no rail on any side: its long sides and its ends drop straight 0.9 m to the stone floor", and "a duellist with the lead heel on a cross has 0.7–0.8 m of platform behind the rear heel".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

All dimensions are human-relative or in metres and add up: "10 m long (north–south) and 8 m wide"; across the room "2.4 m of open floor", "the 1.8 m platform, 2.0 m of open floor, and the stacked desks filling the last 1.8 m"; "1.5 m of floor beyond each end" of the 7 m platform; crosses "4.5 m apart and each 1.25 m in from an end". Landmarks give a shared naming of ends and sides.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The room is one file; the duel's form and edge rule are linked to the duelling record rather than restated. It sets the space only, with no blocking.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#look-at-me-duel

The facet provides the private practice duel's place: a closed room with a duelling platform whose marked positions and unrailed edges make the premise's rescue physically meaningful.

# docs/body/settings/sets/duelling-room.md#routes

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the room's single entrance, the one stair onto the platform and its two levels.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The stair is placed so the duel line's ends and the sides beside its positions stay open: it "stands against the middle of the platform's east long side, covering 3.0–4.0 m from either end. The ends have no steps, and the platform's sides beside the chalk crosses drop straight to the floor."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Steps are "each 0.3 m high and 0.3 m deep and 1 m wide"; the door is "1.2 m wide, opening inward" and "closed during the practice"; the levels are related to bodies: "feet at about the hip height, and their knees at about the chest height, of a person of the same size standing on the floor".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Routes and levels are owned here; whether anyone climbs, steps off or leaves is left to the screenplay.

# docs/body/settings/sets/duelling-room.md#dressing

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the furniture, the leads' bags, the window light and the acoustics.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The dressing serves this film: one free desk where "the two leave their robes and their school bags", and bars of light whose reach changes through the afternoon, from "the west floor and across the platform" at half past two until "they cross the east floor and climb the stacked desks" by half past four; the light follows the sun's position recorded for these films.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Objects carry sizes ("1.2 m × 0.6 m, top 0.75 m high"; satchels "about 40 × 30 × 12 cm"); windows are "1.2 m wide and 3 m tall with the sill 1.0 m above the floor, centred 2.5 m, 5 m and 7.5 m from the north wall"; the beams "run steeply toward the north-east", and their reach at each end of the afternoon is stated and marked as an "Inference from the sun's position"; acoustics are marked "Recorded for the world; the hints are silent".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The sun's height links the four-films record and silence links the sound palette; the room's dressing is owned here only.

# docs/body/settings/sets/duelling-room.md#use

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states how the two use the room and that it is empty: "Nobody else uses the room after lessons; nobody comes in during the film."

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

It relates the room to the duel: "Casting runs along the platform's length, north–south, so the windows' light crosses the line of the duel from the side", and the floor is "where someone stepping off would fall".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Distances to the edges are restated as use: "the rear heel has 0.7–0.8 m of platform behind it, and either long side is 0.9 m from the centre line"; the occupancy is an absolute condition for the film.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The edge rule links the duelling record; the facet describes how the space is used without staging any beat.

# docs/body/settings/sets/duelling-room.md#state-changes

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the room's baseline and the changes a film can make to it, with the reset.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The changes are this duel's: "a wand can lie on the floor, chalk can be scuffed, and a robe can slip from the desk".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The baseline ("the platform clear, the desks stacked") and the possible changes are checkable states, and "nothing carries over".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

It records possible states, not events.
