# docs/body/settings/sound/palette.md#ambience

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the films' ambience and delivers the decision itself: "No ambience, room tone or sound effect is recorded, generated or mixed for any film; the delivered picture has no audio track." It does not defer the choice to a later author.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The decision follows this commission's silent motion hints, not a genre habit: the set acoustics are kept "as facts of the world only", and "the only words in the films are the dialogue the screenplays hold as text".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

One checkable fact: no audio track and no ambience in any film. Its scope (all four films), its limit (set acoustics remain world facts) and its exception (dialogue as screenplay text) are stated, so a screenplay or production author can be held to it.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The sound domain owns the ambience palette; voices are routed to the cast records in the file's opening paragraph, and acoustics to the set records. No event or editing choice is made here.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#silent-hints

The facet realizes the silent-hint requirement for ambience and effects: nothing is recorded or produced, and dialogue exists only as screenplay text.

# docs/body/settings/sound/palette.md#music

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet decides the music palette completely: "No music of any usage — background or source, instrumental or vocal — is chosen, licensed or produced for any film".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

It names every music usage the notation distinguishes (background or source, instrumental or vocal) and adds the on-screen consequence that "no character plays or sings music on screen", which is specific to these silent films.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

One checkable fact covering all films and every usage, with the on-screen consequence stated so a screenplay cannot introduce visible music-making.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Music belongs to the sound domain and is recorded once here; it makes no edit or event decision.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#silent-hints

The facet realizes the silent-hint requirement for music: none is produced.
