# docs/body/settings/world/quidditch.md#rules

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet delivers the practice match's rules: the teams, balls and scoring, the practice form and its endings, the no-wand rule, stopping play and what the balls do then, two riders on a broom, Seeker craft, what counts as an "almost certain" catch, and how a rider in the air can be saved.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The rules serve `snitch-before-you`: "full teams, fourteen riders in the air"; a whistle that stops the Quaffle and Bludgers while "The Snitch does not answer the whistle and flies on"; a Seeker may not grab another rider "except to save them"; the long whistle halts every rider "except a rider already near enough to save the one in danger"; and the rescue rule "A rider can save another only with their broom".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The facts carry values and conditions: Seekers "15–30 m up, circling at 8–15 m/s" and diving "at up to 30 m/s"; an almost certain catch "within about one forearm's length (0.25–0.3 m) of the Snitch's path in the last half-second"; a rescuer "must be within about 3–5 m and dive at once; nobody catches a falling body at arm's length without matching it"; the take must come "within the first 1–1.5 s of the fall", so "a free fall can be saved only if it begins about 25 m or more above the grass", while a rider hanging from a sinking broom "can be taken from any height"; balls stopping "within about a second" of the long blast. Canon is marked adopted and the practice match is marked "Production's invention".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The opening paragraph routes players, balls, brooms and the field to their records, and the rescue rule links the riding study for its law. The rules state what is possible and what ends play without deciding whether the Snitch is caught, who is in danger or who saves whom.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#snitch-before-you

The teams fact gives the fourteen riders of a practice match; the almost-certain-catch fact defines the premise's near catch; the rescue and two-to-a-broom facts make saving Harry and ending in tandem flight possible under stated conditions.
