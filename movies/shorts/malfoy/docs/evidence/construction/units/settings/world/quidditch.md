# docs/body/settings/world/quidditch.md#rules

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives every rule the practice match runs on: the teams, the balls and score, the practice form, the wand rule, stopping play and what a catch during a stoppage is worth, riding two to a broom, Seeker craft, the "almost certain" catch, how a rider is saved in the air, and who can reach a rider in time in each case.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The rules are fitted to this film's choice between a Snitch and Harry's safety: the whistle exempts "a rider already near enough to save the one in danger", and a catch keeps its full price through the danger — "a Snitch caught while play is stopped still counts", so "turning away from it costs the catch at any moment".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The rescue conditions are quantified for both branches: a free fall can be saved by a rider "only if it begins about 25 m or more above the grass", by a rescuer "within about 3–5 m" who matches "within the first 1–1.5 s of the fall"; a hanging rider "can be taken only during the 1–2 s before the spinning shaft shakes them off", by a rescuer "within about 5–10 m when the tail breaks". The who-can-reach table compares the rescuer, Madam Hooch ("Only if within about 25 m of her wand"; "Only if within about 30–60 m of her wand") and the twelve riders for each branch, and states the decision condition: a Seeker hit "more than about 60 m from Madam Hooch's post — over either end of the pitch" can be saved "by nobody but a rider already within those few metres". Inventions are marked: the practice match, the wand rule, the whistle-answering balls and the stoppage catch rule each carry "production's invention"; Seeker speeds, the "almost certain" distance and the catch condition are stated in units.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The rules link the players, balls, brooms, pitch, Hooch's charm and the riding study instead of restating them; the table combines values owned by those records. Which branch happens, where on the pitch and whether the Snitch is caught are left to the screenplay.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#snitch-before-you

The rules realize the premise's world: "full teams, fourteen riders in the air" in a practice match; an "almost certain" catch defined so the near catch is checkable; a catch that still counts during the stoppage, so giving it up has a cost; and rescue rules under which only Carina, near Harry over an end of the pitch, can save him and carry him "two to a broom".

The facet states the match's tone: "competitive — both teams want to win — but sporting: no deliberate fouls, no taunting that matters. A fall stops everyone at once."

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The boundaries are checkable: no deliberate fouls, no meaningful taunting, play stops at a fall.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary
