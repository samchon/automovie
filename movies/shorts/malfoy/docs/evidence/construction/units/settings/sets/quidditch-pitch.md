# docs/body/settings/sets/quidditch-pitch.md#shape

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the field, the hoops, the stands and the zones of the air.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The zones are those this practice match uses — low play at 5–15 m, the hoop zones, the high air at 15–30 m ("the Seekers' level, above the play") and mid-pitch ("the referee's post") — and the stands are stated for this practice: "For the practice they are empty."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Canon dimensions are converted and marked adopted ("about 152 m long (east–west) and 55 m wide"; hoops "about 15 m above the grass", "about 5 m apart", "about 2 m across"); team ends are fixed ("The Slytherin hoops are at the west end, the Gryffindor hoops at the east end").

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The pitch is one file; rules are routed to the Quidditch record. It sets the space without placing any rider.

# docs/body/settings/sets/quidditch-pitch.md#routes

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the ways in and the levels.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The levels give this film's fall time: "a rider falling from Seeker height falls for about 1.7–2.5 s before reaching the ground under ordinary gravity".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Arrivals are closed ("Nobody leaves or arrives during the film") and the fall time follows from the stated heights by free fall.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The fall time is owned here and cited by the magic limits; no fall is decided.

# docs/body/settings/sets/quidditch-pitch.md#dressing

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the dressing on the grass, the surroundings, the light and the acoustics.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The dressing holds this match's own objects: the wand box and the ball crate "beside the centre circle", and the light "makes riders flying west squint".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Objects are sized ("about 40 × 25 × 10 cm", "about 90 × 50 × 30 cm"); the light's direction and shadows are stated ("the stands on the south-west side throw long shadows north-east across the grass"); acoustics are marked as world facts for silent hints.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The sun's height links the four-films record and the no-wands rule links the Quidditch record.

# docs/body/settings/sets/quidditch-pitch.md#use

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states how the match occupies the space: play in the low air, Seekers in the high air, the referee at mid-pitch.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

It gives the Seekers' reach of the space: "A Seeker chasing the Snitch can cross the whole pitch in a few seconds and dive from the high air to the grass."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The use is stated by zone, and riders' positions are linked to their record for exact heights and spacing.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

It routes riders' positions to the crowds record and decides no event.

# docs/body/settings/sets/quidditch-pitch.md#state-changes

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states the pitch's baseline and the only change the film can make.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The change is this film's possible damage: "a broken broom or loose twigs may fall to the grass within it".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The pitch "is the same at the start and end of the film" apart from that change, and "Nothing carries over."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

It records a possibility, not an event.
