# docs/body/settings/cast/harry-potter.md#identity

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet fixes who he is in these films: "Harry James Potter, born 31 July 1980, fifteen in every film. A fifth-year student in Gryffindor house, in Carina's year", his fame and background, and his Seeker place.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Canon fame is reduced to what these films can show: "his past is never shown or mentioned; it shows only in his dislike of being stared at and treated as a celebrity."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each H3 is one checkable fact (date and age, house and year, background limited to what is visible, "Gryffindor's Seeker since his first year … opposite Carina as Slytherin's Seeker"), and the file opens by marking him as "Canon's Harry used as an adopted choice".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Identity facts sit in his one cast file; the canon departure rules are linked to the world record's canon frame, and no backstory event is staged here.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#four-films

It realizes the commission's second lead: Harry Potter, "fifteen in every film", Carina's schoolmate "in Carina's year".

# docs/body/settings/cast/harry-potter.md#body

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#bare-bodies

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Wand and broom form live in the props records and are linked; only the book, which has no other owner, is described here, and only as a baseline object with possible states.

# docs/body/settings/cast/harry-potter.md#movement-style

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives three Laban states — reactive, at rest, in the air — with when each applies.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The states contrast him with Carina: his movement is "loose rather than precise", at rest "he slouches on a bench with his legs out", and in the air "he passes within 1–2 m of other riders and Bludgers where others keep 3 m or more".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each state names its effort qualities and observable body ("he turns his head fast, reaches fast, steps fast"; "his body folded flat to the shaft (chest within about 0.1 m of it)"), and its values are measured: "steps 0.66–0.70 m" on foot; in the air "dives at up to 30 m/s pulled out 2–3 m above the grass" and "turns at speed on a radius of 3–5 m".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Movement style stays in his cast file with gait linked to the movement study; flying speeds are owned by his repertoire facet and the magic record.

## @evidence instructions/motion.md#effectors-to-targets

The states describe how his body travels to its targets — fast, loose reaches and steps, body flat to the shaft — so each action can be authored as an effector brought to a target with his own timing.

# docs/body/settings/cast/harry-potter.md#repertoire

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

His duelling is defined against Carina's: "watches his opponent's wand rather than her eyes, and retreats along a line without glancing back", the faults her coaching addresses.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The skills are measured at the depicted level: a disarm "in 0.25–0.3 s" that "pushes a target back up to a full step (0.4–0.5 m)", reactions "about 0.35–0.45 s", a shield needing "about 0.5 s" that is "late every time unless he reads her eyes and shoulders before her wand moves", flying at "8–15 m/s", dives "up to 30 m/s", pull-out "only 2–3 m above the grass", Bludgers passed "within 1–2 m", a right-hand catch.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Skill facts link to the duelling outcome rules and studies; no duel or match outcome is decided.

# docs/body/settings/cast/harry-potter.md#habits

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The habits drive these premises: "Reads her late" ("he takes it at face value first, then understands one to two seconds later") and "Retreats without looking" on the duelling platform.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each is checkable: glasses push "about half a second before he speaks"; retreat "backward steps of 0.3–0.4 m … one step per half second", and "two or three such steps from a chalk cross put his rear foot over the end"; leaning in closes "0.1–0.2 m … over about one second".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The habits are character facts; the platform geometry is linked to [the edge](../../../../../body/settings/world/practice-duelling.md#edge) rule that owns it, and the habit states a consequence of geometry rather than an event.
