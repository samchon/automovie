# docs/body/settings/props/paper-crane.md#handling

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states how the crane is held, how it flies and what taking it back involves, including the shared hold: "the crane stays between their two pinches, at the midpoint, and moves only as their hands move".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

It resolves this premise's mechanics: cancelling the charm "leaves it wherever it is, so it does not return it to her", and the charm "adds a pull of about 0.2–0.5 N toward Harry, enough to draw it his way only if her grip loosens".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The handling is stated as effector and target: "a pinch between thumb pad and index pad at the base of a wing, the base of the neck or tail, or along the body's lower edge", with the limit "Two people can hold it at once only by different points"; flight values "about 1–1.5 m/s at head height, its wings flapping two to three times a second" are given.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Flight and cancel rules link the magic record, and the reach and contested hold link their research sections. The facet states how a retrieval and a shared hold would work, not whether they happen.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#paper-crane-invitation

The handling facts make the premise's tug realizable: the crane reaches Harry, Carina can reach it only by hand, cancelling does not return it, and both can hold it at different points with the charm pulling toward Harry.

## @evidence instructions/motion.md#effectors-to-targets

Every handling is named as a body part brought to a target: thumb and index pads to a wing base, neck, tail or lower edge; Carina's hand to a moving or held crane; two pinches holding it at their midpoint.
