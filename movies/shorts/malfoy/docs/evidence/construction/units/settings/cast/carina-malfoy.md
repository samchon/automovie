# docs/body/settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#identity

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet fixes who she is: "Carina Malfoy, born 5 June 1980, fifteen in every film. A fifth-year student in Slytherin house", her family ("The only child of Lucius and Narcissa Malfoy") and her team place ("She has flown Seeker for the Slytherin house team since her second year"). Nothing is left for a later author to decide.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The identity follows this work's direction choice: "She stands where canon puts Draco Malfoy; Draco does not exist", and her name is the only trace of the family on screen ("never named on screen except through her surname").

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each H3 is one checkable fact: a birth date and age, a house and year, "Not a prefect", a family with its reputation among students, and a Seeker position held "since her second year, opposite Harry". The canon placement is marked as a departure through the [canon frame](../../../../../body/settings/world/four-films.md#canon-frame) link rather than presented as checked canon.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Identity facts sit in her one cast file, in the cast facet order; the family's wider reputation is linked to the world record's canon frame instead of being restated. No event involving her family is decided here.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#four-films

It realizes the commission's lead: "Carina Malfoy … fifteen in every film", a Hogwarts schoolmate "in the same year as Harry Potter".

## @evidence instructions/direction.md#carina-malfoy-family

It records the delegated choice as fact: "The only child of Lucius and Narcissa Malfoy … She stands where canon puts Draco Malfoy; Draco does not exist", with the Seeker place that follows from it.

# docs/body/settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#body

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet delivers her measurable body and model sheet: "163 cm tall … slim, light build of about 50 kg", planning reach values, hand assignment, recognition on the bare-body hint and appearance for reference images.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The values come from this production's needs: a height chosen inside the age-15 distribution, reach stated for effector targeting ("she can bring a hand to a point about 0.6–0.65 m from her shoulder without moving her feet, and about 0.9 m by leaning; farther needs a step"), and recognition built for bare figures ("told from Harry by the female body form, by being 4 cm shorter, and by her carriage").

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each fact is checkable with units: height and mass, "shoulder to fingertip about 71 cm", "hip joint about 85 cm", eye heights standing and seated, and the hand rule "holding always takes priority over a habit". The reach values state their uncertainty and authority: "an inference; the figure's measured lengths replace them in code". Recognition gives front, profile and back views.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Body, reach, hand use, recognition and appearance are kept together in the body facet in that order; stature research and the bare-body rule are linked, not copied. Appearance is stated as a world fact for later reference images and "Not modelled on the hint", leaving rendering to source.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#bare-bodies

The facet states how she is carried by a bare body ("the installed Human figure's adolescent female body at that height") and how she is still identified without clothes, hair or face ("told from Harry by the female body form, by being 4 cm shorter, and by her carriage"), while recording appearance for diffusion.

# docs/body/settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#movement-style

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives her three movement states with their Laban efforts and triggers — composed, nervous near Harry, decisive in danger — and her contact style.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Each state is tied to this story: nervous "When Harry looks at her or speaks to her", decisive because "she watches people rather than objects" and so sees "a partner retreating toward an edge he cannot see".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The states give measurable responses: onset "within about half a second of his look", a hesitant walk of "steps of 0.45–0.55 m at 90–105 steps per minute", a reaction "in about 0.3–0.4 s", and the rescue limit that she is in time only when "within arm's reach as the tip begins".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Movement style sits in her cast file with gait and grip values linked to the research studies; the conditions for a rescue are stated as a limit, not as an event the screenplay must use.

## @evidence instructions/motion.md#effectors-to-targets

"Precise contact" states her actions as effectors to targets: "she brings her hand to a wrist, the edge of a paper or the handle of a broom and closes on it … Every action of hers can be named as a body part brought to a target".

# docs/body/settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#repertoire

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet covers every practised skill the films can use: duelling, coaching, flying and seeking, paper folding and the paper-flight charm.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Her coaching is this work's wrist grip — "her hand from outside the forearm, closing just above the wrist, thumb on the inner wrist" — and her repeated rule "watch the person, not the wand" gives the duel its title's sense.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The skills carry the studied vocabulary and level: casts "in 0.25–0.35 s", a shield "in about 0.3 s" started "as her opponent's eyes and shoulders begin a cast, before the wand moves", "extending her arm before she steps", a disarm that "pushes a target back about half a step"; flying "cruises at 8–15 m/s and dives at up to 30 m/s", pulling out "about 5 m above the grass"; a one-handed right-hand catch.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Skill facts stay in the repertoire facet with their laws linked to the duelling and riding studies and the world rules; no match or duel outcome is decided.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#look-at-me-duel

Her coaching grip is the premise's repeatable wrist grip: she "corrects a partner's wand arm with the coaching wrist grip", which the duel can repeat and turn into a rescue under the decisive state's limits.

# docs/body/settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#habits

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives five habits, each with trigger, response, speed and reliability, as promised.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The habits belong to her situation with Harry: "her eyes drop to his hands, not to the floor", "she takes a half step (about 0.3 m) toward him … she often steps back", and "her left hand closes at her side with the thumb pressed inside the fingers".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each habit is checkable: the gaze break comes when a look "reaches about 1–1.5 s, well short of the 2–4 s ordinary look" and happens "within a fifth of a second"; in the half step "the step and its retreat take about one second" and "she retreats on most first attempts and goes through on a later one"; straightening is "within half a second" and "always".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Habits are grouped in their own facet and use the distance and gaze values by link to the proxemics study; which hand they use is owned by the body facet's [hands](../../../../../body/settings/cast/carina-malfoy.md#hands) fact.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The history is this pair's: "She shares Potions and other lessons with him, has watched him from the opposite end of the Quidditch pitch", and the asymmetry "She knows far more about him than he knows about her."

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

It states a reason that shapes staging — being seen with him "a cost among Slytherins, which is why she seeks him out where nobody else is" — and a change condition: "kindness or attention addressed to her, not to her name". Her tie to Madam Hooch is a checkable rule with its one exception: she "obeys her whistle at once, except where the rule of the air itself asks her to help a rider in danger".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Her side is here; Harry's side is in his record, and the shared baseline links to the direction instruction. No change is said to happen; only its condition.

## @evidence instructions/direction.md#baseline-relationship

It realizes the baseline from her side: "she has liked him for more than a year and has told nobody … has spoken with him a few times, briefly and politely."
