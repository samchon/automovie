# docs/body/settings/sets/charms-corridor.md#shape

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the corridor's shape, its five south windows with their embrasures, and the Charms door with the clock above it, each with dimensions and position along a stated datum ("Positions along it are measured from its west end").

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The landmarks serve this film's own picture, which differs from the crane film's seated bench: a window where "someone standing at a window rests their forearms on a sill about waist high and has the corridor at their back", and a clock above the Charms door "readable from anywhere in the corridor" that shows the hour the film turns on.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Dimensions are human-relative and checkable: corridor "30 m long, running west–east, 3.5 m wide", vault "5 m high"; windows "centred at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 m", each "1.4 m wide and 2.8 m tall, its sill 0.9 m above the floor", embrasure "0.6 m deep"; door "1.4 m wide" "at 15 m, opposite the middle window"; clock "0.6 m across, its centre 3.2 m above the floor". The view is bounded: the pitch is "visible small in the distance to the south-west".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The corridor is the place of `three-oclock` only, as the opening states, and routes the other films to their own set records; the hour shown links the four-films record. Nothing here places a person.

# docs/body/settings/sets/charms-corridor.md#routes

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the corridor's two ends, the timed walk along it and its single level.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The walk is measured for this film's repeated passes behind a person at the middle window: "From the landing to just past the middle window is about 17 m: 13–14 s comfortable, 18–24 s hesitant", and the east end "turns a right-angle corner to the north, beyond which a walker is out of sight after about 2 m".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The routes are closed ("These two ends are the only ways in and out; the classroom doors are shut") and timed from the gait study ("22–25 s at a comfortable pace (1.2–1.35 m/s) and 32–43 s at a hesitant one (0.7–0.95 m/s)"); passing distances are given ("1.5–2.5 m behind them" down the middle, "about 1 m" along the window side); the floor is "flat and dry, with no steps between the two ends".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Routes and their timings are owned here; how often anyone walks them, and in which direction, is left to the screenplay.

# docs/body/settings/sets/charms-corridor.md#dressing

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives the corridor's dressing, its light through the afternoon and its acoustics.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The light is worked out for these south windows: "five bright, slanting panels of warm light across the floor" that cross the width and "climbs the north wall in bright patches that move eastward along it as the afternoon goes on", so "a person standing in a window is lit from the front and seen from the corridor against the bright glass".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The light claim carries its geometry ("because the sun is only 10–20° up and the windows' tops 3.7 m high, the light crosses the whole 3.5 m width"); the dressing is a closed list ending "Nothing on the floor."; the acoustics follow the space ("footsteps on the flagstones carry the corridor's whole length") and are marked as world facts for silent hints.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The sun's position links the four-films record and silence links the sound palette; only the corridor's own dressing and light are owned here.

# docs/body/settings/sets/charms-corridor.md#use

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states how the two can use the corridor and that it is empty: "The corridor is empty except for the two".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The use is this film's own: Harry "stands at the middle window (15 m), forearms on the sill, watching the distant pitch, with his back to the corridor", so a walker approaches unseen along "15 m of open corridor".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The occupancy is a checkable condition with its reason ("lessons on this floor have ended for the day and the classrooms are shut") and "No one enters during the film"; the meeting place is located ("between the embrasure and the corridor's middle"), and turning from the window gives a stated view ("he faces the corridor and the Charms door with the clock above it").

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

It describes what the space affords — a walk that "can be repeated in either direction", a face-to-face meeting — without choosing the film's events, and links Harry's activity to his cast record.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#three-oclock

The corridor affords each element of the premise: Harry's back to an open corridor lets Carina "pass behind him to the east corner, turn out of sight and come back, so a walk past him can be repeated in either direction"; the floor in front of the window is "where two standing people meet face to face at their ordinary distance", where a note can be offered, withdrawn and, if dropped on the flagstones, bent to by both.
