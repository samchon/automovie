# docs/body/settings/props/folded-note.md#handling

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The handling is fitted to this film's place and people: Carina holds it "between her right thumb and index finger", and a dropped note "lies on the corridor's flagstones", in front of the window where the two meet standing.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each action is defined against its research law: offering is carrying it "toward the midpoint between them at about chest height"; withdrawing is "a handover stopped before Harry's hand touches it"; a drop "falls flat or on edge within about a second" and "does not blow away"; two standing people "less than about 0.8 m apart bring their heads together over it"; it is carried "in the right pocket of her skirt, reached by her right hand".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary
