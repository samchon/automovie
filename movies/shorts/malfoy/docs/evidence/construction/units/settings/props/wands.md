# docs/body/settings/props/wands.md#form

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet gives both wands' form, material and markings and how they appear on the hint: Harry's "Holly, 11 inches (28 cm), with a phoenix-feather core (canon)", Carina's "black walnut, 10¼ inches (26 cm), with a unicorn-hair core", and "Each wand is a thin straight rod of its length".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Carina's wand is an invention made distinct from canon's Malfoy wands, with "a narrow plain silver ring at its base"; the hint fact names how the two are told apart in these films: "by length and by whose hand holds them".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each wand has length, taper ("about 1.2 cm across at the butt to about 0.5 cm at the tip"), colour and handle length; canon and invention are labelled; the hint difference is quantified ("Harry's is 2 cm longer").

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Both wands share one file because they are one prop kind used together; what a wand does is routed to the magic record in the opening paragraph.

# docs/body/settings/props/wands.md#handling

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states how a wand is held, carried and what happens when it is disarmed, ending with the effector rule "The tip is the effector of every cast".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The grip and carrying places are specific to the two leads: "Both leads cast right-handed"; carried "in an inner robe pocket (Carina) or in a back trouser pocket or robe pocket (Harry)".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

The grip is described by contact points ("thumb and index finger along the handle", "the butt resting against the heel of the palm") with "the tip extending about 17–19 cm beyond the hand"; a disarmed wand "flies toward the caster at about the jet's speed, end over end" and is caught or "falls and rolls". A motion author can place the effector and the tip target from this.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

Disarm outcomes link the duelling rule and the cast law links the duelling study; this record owns only the object's handling.

# docs/body/settings/props/wands.md#state-changes

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet states the baseline and possible changes: "Both wands are whole at the start of every film and no film breaks one", and within the duel a wand can be "flown, caught by the other, dropped on the platform or floor".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The possible states are those this duel can produce, ending "until it is picked up or handed back".

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Checkable states and a fixed limit (no film breaks a wand) are stated.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

It records baselines and possible states only; whether a wand is flown or caught is left to the screenplay.
