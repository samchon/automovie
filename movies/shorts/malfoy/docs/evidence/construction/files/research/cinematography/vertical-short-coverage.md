## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The depicted practice is how such a film is shot and cut. "Shot pace" gives the law (faster cutting in contemporary films; pace following structure, with the climax's shorter shots and higher motion; conversations at the film's mean pace; brightening across the climax) and its use (a 2.5–4.5 s mean, 13–24 shots, long holds for watched contacts, 1–2 s climax shots). "The tall frame" gives safe zones converted to 720 × 1280 and the framing grammar for a frame twice as tall as wide (full figures, depth staging for distance, contact two-shots from hands to faces, inserts, seated and riding pairs). "Shooting the practices" connects duel, flight and everyday movement to their own studies' filming sections. "Measurable targets" tabulates mean shot length, shot count, climax shots, eye-line height and the contact zone.
