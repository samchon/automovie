# docs/research/motion/effector-targeting.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The depicted practice is the authoring method itself: every action as a body part brought to a target. The study gives each element a code author needs. Laws: "Laws of a contact" states the shape prior in the performer's body frame (law 1), the four phases approach–contact–hold–release with their windows (law 2), support before free effectors (law 3), reach before step with fixed segment lengths (law 4), track-or-freeze for moving targets (law 5) and the declared bend direction (law 6). Outcome table: "Outcome table" maps intent and geometric condition to contact, step-then-contact, stated miss, shared hold, plant and seated support. Contact surfaces and support: "Contact inventory" names effector, target and support for every contact the four films use (wrist grip, pinch, handover, foot to floor and step, seat to bench and broom, hands to handle and to a waist, catch), with the hand and wrist dimensions that make the grip close. Chaining and spatial strategy: "Chaining and spatial strategy" overlaps release and approach and treats walking as foot targets on a path named by landmarks. Tempo: "Tempo" sets approach windows from the reach study and 4–8-frame blends from kovar-2002. Installed capability and its limits: "Installed capabilities" maps each need to the Human or engine API with its stated limit and the two boundaries (no engine-to-Human pose converter; the facade has no foot-contact solving). Shooting: "How it is shot". Level markers: "Level markers" contrasts non-sliding, arriving, target-driven contact with the beginner's sliding feet and floating hands. Measurable targets: the final table gives drift, penetration, contact distance, hold drift, segment length, blend window and the unreachable rule, each with its method, stated as the production's choice because no source gives contact thresholds.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Each technique claim cites its retained source and locator: juckett-2008 "Math review" for the law-of-cosines solution, the two bend solutions and clamping; aristidou-2011 pp. 245, 247 and 248 (§§4, 4.1, 4.3) for FABRIK's passes, multiple end effectors and constraints; kovar-2002 p. 1 §1, p. 2 §2, pp. 4–7 §§4.2–5 for footplant definition, specification, root and leg adjustment, blend curve, window lengths and the 2.6 % stretch; epic-motion-warping "Animation Montage" and "Details" for warp targets and windows; the installed sources by file path and line range for every API row. Witkin and Popović's paper is recorded as named but not retained, and nothing relies on it. The measurable thresholds are marked as the production's choice, with the reason (kovar-2002 hands contact detection to hand editing, p. 2 §2). Values derived from other studies link to those studies' sections.
