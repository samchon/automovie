# docs/research/movement/teen-bodies-and-everyday-movement.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The study covers the ordinary movements the four films show at the depicted age. Stature gives the two leads' heights against the age-15 distribution. Each movement has its law and values: walking ("Law": heel strike, carry, toe-off, double support, opposing arm swing; "Values": step length, cadence, speed, step and support times) with performed variants (comfortable, hesitant, brisk, stop) as the production's choice; reach to grasp (single-peak velocity, aperture opening then closing, obstacle double peak; movement time, peak velocity and its timing, aperture and its timing); handover (four phases, exchange point midway and lower at distance, elbow-versus-shoulder use, transfer duration) with the interrupted and contested handovers the note and crane need; sitting and standing (forward lean, seat-off, extension; durations); bending to the floor (stoop versus squat, lowering speed, the head-meeting geometry for two people bending at once, and a seated bend from a bench with its meeting height, marked as an inference). The translation from measured groups to fifteen-year-olds is stated wherever a value is inferred. "Conduct, level and filming" gives the level marker (ordinariness, no frozen starts) and the shots that make each movement read; "Measurable targets" tabulates every value with its basis. No official or crowd is present in these movements, which the conduct section states.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Every value names its source and locator and is marked as a reported measurement, an inference or the production's choice: who-2007 rows 180 and 186 months; ito-2022 Table 2 group C and the Introduction; valevicius-2018 Introduction, Abstract and Table 2; kopnarski-2023 abstract and §§3.5, 4.1, 4.3, 4.4 with page numbers; wairagkar-2021 pp. 12 and 14; hwang-2009 Results. Inferences state their translation (leg-length scaling of step length, gram-weight objects at the light end of the transfer report, a lowering distance divided by the reported lift speed). The missing open source for fifteen-year-old gait is recorded as unavailable with a link to the index's "Unavailable" section.
