## @evidence shared/obligations/settings.md#world-and-scope

The facts `#release-units`, `#baseline-reset` and `#promise` declare the audience, central experience, recurring reward, engine, variation and title promise; delivery is four complete 60-second vertical films with no carry-over and free viewing order, a fixed length rather than a padding target. Domains map to owners: world rules (`magic.md`, `practice-duelling.md`, `quidditch.md`), time and environment (`#time-weather-season`), agents (cast and crowd files), relationships (cast `#relationships`), places (set files), objects (prop files), knowledge (cast `#knowledge`), sensory consistency (`cinematography/vertical-look.md`, `sound/palette.md`), research (linked studies). Consequential departures are explicit before descendants rely on them: Carina in Draco's place and the quiet fifth year (`#canon-frame`), the paper-flight charm and broom dimensions marked as inventions. Derived coherence was checked across records: the platform's 7 m length, crosses 1.25 m in and 4.5 m apart match the 4–5 m casting distance, and with the 0.45–0.55 m stance leave 0.7–0.8 m behind the rear heel, so two or three 0.3–0.4 m blind steps reach the edge, while the single stair at the middle of the east side keeps clear of both crosses; the duel's timings (cast plus jet 0.4–0.7 s against seeing and shielding 0.6–1.1 s, with a 0.2–0.3 s gain for reading eyes and shoulders) make a shield possible only for a duellist who reads the person; the room's 10 × 8 m holds the platform with 1.5 m beyond each end and 2.4 + 1.8 + 2.0 + 1.8 m across; free-fall times of 1.7–2.5 s from 15–30 m match the rescue condition (3–5 m, diving at once, match within 1–1.5 s, a start about 25 m up or higher) and Hooch's range limit (about 20–33 m from her wand, from reaction, casting and flight times); broom seats and stirrups hung 0.45 m below the shaft fit two riders' legs on a 1.4 m shaft without crossing; the sun's stated heights give the shadow factors 2.7–5.7 and place the duelling-room beams and the courtyard shade by hour; the clock's sightline from the bench clears the arcade vault; heights and reaches fit the 0.9–1.3 m acquaintance distance and the 0.6–0.9 m seated-bend geometry. Persistent conditions are explained: why danger can only be ended by a hand or a broom (`magic.md#limits`, `quidditch.md#no-wands`), why the places are empty (`duelling-room.md#empty-room`, `clock-tower-courtyard.md#occupancy`), and why Carina's feelings stay untold at the baseline (her fears and her house's gossip, `cast/carina-malfoy.md#fears`, `#harry`). No unresolved value blocks a planned screenplay; the crane's exact size keeps a stated ±2 cm uncertainty that changes no staging.

## @evidence shared/obligations/settings.md#people-and-audiovisual-world

Every participant is recorded for the whole of their presence: the two leads in all films, Madam Hooch for the match (`cast/rolanda-hooch.md#refereeing`), and the twelve riders by position during play, at a rider in danger and after it (`crowds/quidditch-riders.md#action`); `#population` states that nobody else appears in any film. The central agents have situated life (age, house, family, team), drives and fears, abilities with limits and thresholds (cast times, dive pull-out heights, reach lengths, the rescue condition), knowledge and its boundaries (`#knows`, `#does-not-know`), speech and voice, and change conditions (`#relationship-change`). Appearance is given for later reference images and for the bare-body hint separately (`#appearance`, `#recognition`), with height, build, carriage and kit colours, so independent artists reach the same identities. The relationship is defined from both sides with its asymmetry of knowledge and its mirrored super-objectives. Shared material grammar holds across houses (green and silver against scarlet and gold in uniforms, kits and stand hangings; Nimbus 2001s for Slytherin). Each used location has shape, human-relative dimensions, entrances, levels, landmarks, furniture, light from a stated sun position and its shadows, acoustics, occupancy and how people use it; each focused object has size, shape, material, colour, markings, handling and states. Rendering style stays a production choice and shot design is left to the screenplays within the look.
