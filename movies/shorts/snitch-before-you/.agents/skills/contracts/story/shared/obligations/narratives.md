# Narrative population obligations

Each completed treatment, script and final screenplay population answers once per question across all units and boundaries. Major treatment spans follow supplied closing turns; scripts and screenplay use their actual partition.

## Are progression, state, and long pursuits continuous? {#continuity-and-progression}

Classify every unit by its actual event, understanding, relationship, emotion, pressure, rhythm or formal contribution. Identify functionless duplication and independently addressable progressions hidden together, preserving inseparable montage, continuous action, parallel or other owned forms. Connect exits and entries through their causal or declared formal relation; test what movable units' order contributes without imposing linearity on episodic, nonlinear or nonhuman work.

Compare inherited time, place, knowledge, injury, appearance, clothing, possession, resources, status, relationships and promises. Follow recurring agents' drives, beliefs, interpretations, experiences and actions across units. Report unsupported reversals or thresholds crossed without pressure or cost, with the earliest contradictory owner and any contradiction derived across facts. An unchanged or unreached threshold needs its owned scope or purpose.

Where protagonist pursuit holds the work together, identify that desire, its first audience recognition, the actions it drives and how major-unit goals continue or transform it. Ensembles may sustain competing pursuits and other forms another center; local tasks alone cannot establish the whole, and no shared goal need be invented.

## Are promises, rhythm, and variation realized across the whole? {#promises-and-distribution}

Read the opening without prior settings knowledge: does it give a reason to continue and timely understanding of people, relationships, pressure, time and space? Trace actual introductions and returns, the declared engine and rewards, every payoff's setup, risk or cost, and the longest gaps without promised satisfaction. Report spans where the engine fails, rewards are unearned or worn out, or eventual return cannot support delay.

Compare scene, compression, pause, omission, simultaneity, habitual time, flashback, anticipation and retelling against each span's pressure. For departures from ordinary once-told forward sequence, show what removing them loses. Compare openings and endings, speech frequency and length, who explains, evades, interrupts or stays silent, material occupying attention and recurring image-sound functions. Treatments compare assigned exchanges; scripts and screenplay their realization. Uniform speed, mechanical alternation, habitual aphorisms, uniform voices and functionless recurrence can fail despite local passes.

Ground each classification in all actual members, not samples or character summaries; counts locate patterns, reading judges them. An owned uniform form, refrain, silence, narrow world or long frustration must realize its purpose. Central concerns develop through changed events, choices, relationships, places, images, sound and form; motifs acquire force rather than append or repeat an answer.
