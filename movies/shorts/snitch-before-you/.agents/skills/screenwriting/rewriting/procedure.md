After the closing round, transfer every supplied working file to its actual body counterpart with HTML comments removed and every other byte preserved. Supplied navigation indexes participate; absent optional indexes need no copies. Pending initial transfer prevents downstream consumption and completion.

Keep current comparison inputs and decisions under [layout](../../production/layout.md#source-ownership). An unchanged working edition keeps its naturalness review.

Initial transfer requires exact equality with the comment-free reviewed edition. Subsequent semantic body changes follow [closed-pass repair](../repair.md#closed-passes), preserving their correction rather than restoring the old working edition. New construction begins its own cycle, evidence and review.
