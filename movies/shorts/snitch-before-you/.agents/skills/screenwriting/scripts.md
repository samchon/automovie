# Scripts

Realize completed treatments as scenes performers and filmmakers can execute. The [script principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/scripts.md) judge execution, staging and view; [population duties](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/scripts.md) judge partition and handoff.

After [discovery](discovery.md), map treatments to their consumers and settle meaningful scene, sequence and release units before bulk writing. Size them by actual viewing material and the adopted form. Use [production layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership), retaining stable H1/H2 identities and order for final screenplay. A continuous film uses its own sequence divisions.

Build each scene around what its participants or process do, encounter and change. Supply actual dialogue, action, reaction and consequential performance in their playable order, with usable space, light, sound and viewing access. Preserve inherited event chronology while creating the scene's own execution; [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation) distinguishes that inheritance from copied realization.

Use [notation](notation.md) to make the exchange readable. Paragraph breaks follow meaningful performance beats rather than prescribe cuts. Decide the view needed to understand each beat, including a declared fixed view where appropriate. Read replies with adjacent action as if spoken, giving interruptions, responses and sustained action the time their effect requires.

Inherit world facts and uncertainty through actual treatments and cite every realized parent. Repair defective parent facts or design at their earliest owner through [upstream repair](repair.md#upstream-defects), so later scenes inherit the corrected decision.

Before evidence, read the complete script as its first reader, performer and filmmaker. Record the experience before repairs, then use [inspection](inspection.md) and [cause rounds](repair.md#cause-rounds) for the selected expression and inheritance questions. Compare preceding units as well as individual exchanges; their distribution is judged by [script expression obligations](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/obligations/scripts.md#execution-distribution).

Complete content and expression together in [construction](workflow.md#construction). Hand complete playable scenes to screenplay, which owns their final audiovisual realization.
