# Inspect before repairing

Use this procedure for author inspection, evidence correction and independent review. The selected contracts own failure conditions and permitted exceptions; a table records their application rather than creates another checklist.

## Isolated context

Use an available subagent or separate-session tool, within its authorization rules, to launch the author's model with no inherited conversation or creative work memory. Pass only the assigned job and input file paths; the worker reads those files directly. Naming the current context "fresh" does not isolate its memory. If that tool or authorization is unavailable, report the missing task instead of performing it in the current context.

## Independent classification

Give the classifier the exact current body and necessary neighboring context, selected contracts, and actual parents or frozen original needed for that comparison. It handles classification alone, excluding author explanations, evidence, prior verdicts and other judging tasks.

For scripts construction and screenplay construction or expression revision, classify every sentence against every applicable named failure, including speech and the layer-specific questions. For narrative construction, also pair every downstream paragraph with its actual parent passages, or the brief for a direct short, and test [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation); word matching cannot detect a paraphrased sequence. Independently inventory inherited names, forms of address, quantities, dates, times, places, consequential states and certainty and compare their values with the actual parents.

Record the source revision, files and covered ranges. List tested failures once, then use one row per sentence with its exact locator and yes-pattern names, or `-` when all are no. Read connected paragraphs and exchanges for insufficient orientation, realization or voice as well as excess. For an ambiguous named failure, the independent classifier reads the context and decides the actual recipient loss under the selected definition; ambiguity alone is not yes. For each yes, retain the passage and any proven contractual exception. Keep the current raw table in ignored `.cache/`, with its identity and supported findings in the accountable author or review record under [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership).

Lock the complete initial table before author defenses. The author chooses repairs or raises factual objections against a quotation, definition, exception or actual parent or source; the independent classifier resolves them without substituting author intent for the recipient judgment. Search recurring forms and record all discovered members of each cause. An illustrative question permits other supported losses, while a named-failure boundary cannot be enlarged by taste. Send recurring uncovered losses to discovery.

## Verify changed work

Keep the same author through its correction cycle. Compare each changed passage with its previous form, then classify the changed sentences, their neighbors and every statement whose meaning depends on them. Changed parents or requirements invalidate all affected comparisons too. Relocate unchanged rows when line numbers move without calling their carried classification a fresh reading.

Do not reverse an unchanged classification merely because a later reader prefers another interpretation. Reopen it for new body, parent, source or contract evidence; record that evidence. A complete closing review still rereads every selected body and makes its own current unit and population judgments under [review rounds](../review/rounds.md); stable sentence tests do not carry an old unit approval into new text.

The first round classifies the complete scope. Subsequent rounds classify changed sentences or paragraphs and affected neighbors, without rebuilding the whole table or reversing unchanged decisions. Text rewritten to repair a prior yes receives another yes only for a clear applicable failure under its expression or parent-differentiation contract. Retain before/after passages and rechecked rows in the current ignored comparison record, identifying the source in the accountable review.

An author correction cycle is dry only when initial coverage is complete and no unprotected yes remains after the affected rows are rechecked. Check changed passages together for a new common mold, fidelity losses and dependent-answer errors under [cause rounds](repair.md#cause-rounds). A dry classification table is not independent approval or whole-work completion.
