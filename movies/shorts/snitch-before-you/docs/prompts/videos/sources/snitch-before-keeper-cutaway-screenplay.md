
# Snitch Before You

## EXT. QUIDDITCH PRACTICE PITCH AND EASTERN RUNOFF - DAY {#airborne-race}


### Side by side | 0–8 seconds

A gold winged ball drops toward an oval of green lawn. Two sets of three pale hoop goals rise at opposite ends. Between them, a red leather Quaffle leaves broad green Chaser G-C3's hand and lands in tall green G-C1's hands with a soft thud. Green G-C2 opens a lane; three red Chasers turn with the triangle, one marking each green rider.

Farther north, four bat-carrying riders, two green and two red, track two dark balls. G-B1 knocks the nearer Bludger away from the passing lane; its dark twin flies west between G-B2 and R-B2, both bats ready. One Keeper hangs before each goal end. All twelve supporting riders remain seated on their own brooms.

The view drops with the gold glint to two more riders, south of the ball play: CARINA MALFOY, about fifteen, platinum hair tied back above a green-and-silver tie, white collar, grey knit and black Slytherin robe, and HARRY POTTER, the same age, black hair and round glasses above red-and-gold Gryffindor uniform cues. Their lower robe panels are secured behind their waists, clear of the shafts; her pleated skirt divides over opaque riding shorts, his trousered knees hug the wood. They race east, Carina nearest us and Harry level on her right. Wind and vibrating bristles rise over the game sounds.

Carina lowers her chest and edges half a broom ahead. She turns just far enough for him to hear.

CARINA

You lose today, Potter.

Harry leans forward and draws level again. Her face returns to the gold ball.

### The gap | 8–23 seconds

The Snitch bends between the southern and middle eastern goal uprights, below their hoops. Seen from a three-quarter side, the pale poles widen around the approaching pair; grass stays visible beneath their feet.

Harry banks first. Carina rolls after him, bent knees holding her shaft. The nearer upright sweeps behind her. He levels sooner and gains a body length. Beyond the goals, open sky replaces the poles.

The camera travels with Harry and Carina into the clear eastern runoff. The hoops dwindle behind their moving bodies, framing G-C1's Quaffle pass to G-C2 and R-C2 cutting across the goalward lane. The receiver banks away holding the ball close. Farther north, G-B2's short swing sends the western Bludger back north; its knock fades behind their bristles as the other three Beaters turn after both dark balls. The eastern green Keeper watches that play from the field side of the goals.

The Snitch swerves up toward Harry's left hand. He slides that palm forward, reaches, and misses as its wings flick down. Carina follows below his left shoulder. A thin wing buzz crosses the rushing air.

### One hand | 23–35 seconds

The gold ball turns sharply left. Harry stretches farther; his hips slide off before his hand closes. His chest drops below the broom. His right hand catches the wood as his thighs lose contact, leaving him hanging beneath its continuing eastward glide.

His free left arm swings down. He draws it back, but his body swings away before it reaches the shaft. The eastern goals are now more than a hundred metres behind him; none of the other twelve riders has crossed them. The distant Keeper remains turned toward the field.

The Snitch curves back toward Carina. She levels, opens her left hand, and the ball hangs a handspan beyond it. In the same view, lower and ahead on her right, Harry's right fingers creep along his broom's shaft; his boots search empty air. He looks down at the distant grass, then up at her advancing hand and the gold sphere just beyond its fingertips.

Carina turns her head from the Snitch to him. Her left hand closes around her own shaft.

### Make room | 35–43 seconds

She pitches down and accelerates beneath the prize. The Snitch flashes past her retreating shoulder, still unheld, and climbs behind her.

The camera stays with Carina in a continuous three-quarter side view through the transfer and recovery. Harry's broom glides onward above and to his right. She comes from his left, easing her descent until she travels beside him rather than across his fall.

Carina keeps closing the gap. Far behind her moving broom, the green Keeper turns toward Harry's dangling legs and raises a hand; G-C2 turns to it and hugs the Quaffle as five Chasers brake around the receiver. The western Keeper holds the far hoops while four Beaters keep both dark balls north. As the green Keeper launches across the short grass, still far short of them, Carina's seat draws ahead of Harry's chest and her empty rear shaft comes up to his lower ribs.

The view keeps Harry's hanging right arm visible beside Carina's seated silhouette, with a strip of sky between the two brown shafts and the approaching Keeper distant outside both.

Harry looks at the offered shaft. Carina nudges closer and holds the nose steady.

### Across | 43–53 seconds

Harry closes his left hand around her rear shaft. His right hand opens on the old broom. For a beat his weight pulls through the new left grip; then his right hand closes beside it. The old broom slides above and to his right, clear of his head, and continues unheld across empty sky. No other rider reaches it.

Her broom dips. Carina folds forward over her steering hand, thighs firm around the shaft, leaving the rear section clear. Harry draws the wood against his lower ribs and hooks his left forearm over it. His left thigh swings across behind her; his hips follow the roll until he is astride, right leg hanging on the near side. His boot clears the bristles; well behind and to their left, the green Keeper sees Harry's legs settle on either side and yields to an outside trailing line, opposite the loose broom's upper-right glide.

Carina raises the nose into a broad, shallow climb. Harry keeps both hands on the shaft until the rocking settles. The lawn's upward rush slows beneath them. Bristle vibration lowers into a steady hum; both take a full breath. Game impacts no longer reach the foreground.

### What she missed | 53–60 seconds

Harry turns toward the small gold glint retreating above the distant goals. He looks back at Carina's turned shoulder.

HARRY

You almost had it.

Carina glances toward the glint, then faces forward. She shifts her front hand and leaves him a little more room behind her.

CARINA

Then don't fall next time.

Harry eases his shoulders. The view holds close on their two steady silhouettes sharing one broom. The Keeper remains outside the frame; the unattended broom and uncaught Snitch keep moving away. Wind softens. Cut to black.
