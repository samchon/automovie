# docs/body/settings/goals.md#structure

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

Both complete goal ends and the southern under-ring gap are specified without adding stadium detail. The original pursuit obstacle remains available inside a populated Quidditch setting.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Six dull pale rings on separate slender uprights provide a readable goal silhouette and depth reference; no decorative architecture or unsupported animated effect is required.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts

Each ring has6metres clear diameter and centre45metres above grass, with separate uprights aty=-12/0/+12. Ends atx=0/-160 link to arena. These dimensions are invented; no net/crossbar blocks the below-ring gap or turns a Seeker's flight into a score.

## @evidence shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary

The independently used goal objects have one form/clearance owner. Arena owns geography and players own defense; this record does not dictate approach, cut order, shot result or a collapsing obstacle.
