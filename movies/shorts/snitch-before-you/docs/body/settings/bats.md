# Four Beater bats

## Form, ownership and use {#handling}

Each of the four Beaters owns one plain brown wooden bat, for a total of four. Each is about sixty centimetres long, with a narrow grip and a blunt wider striking end. These simple dimensions are invented for coarse Human hand contact; the Beater role follows [official primary research](../../../references/index.md#official-population). There are no straps, moving parts, metal ornaments or additional bats lying on the lawn.

A seated Beater holds the grip in one hand and uses the free hand, pelvis and thighs to maintain broom support. A short visible swing can strike a Bludger clear of the contested northern lane; its completion must not pass through another player. The bat stays with its owner unless screenplay explicitly releases it. This short requires no release, breakage, ground pickup or bat used as a rescue pole. Its brown material and broad end distinguish it from a longer broom shaft and dark bristles.
