# Supporting practice players

## Teams, roles and background activity {#population}

Fourteen student riders form two complete practice teams. Each has three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper and one Seeker; there is one Quaffle, two Bludgers and one Snitch. These role/count facts follow [official primary sources](../../../references/index.md#official-population). The anonymous students, team colors, routes and self-managed training arrangement are invented. Carina is the green Seeker and Harry the red Seeker; their individual records remain authoritative. The twelve supporting players are school students aged fourteen to seventeen; no individual exact age is needed. They are the complete additional human population: no referee, coach, spectators or unlisted rescuer is implied.

Green/silver Slytherin and red/gold Gryffindor ties and robe lining distinguish teams. Robe lower panels are secured at the waist before flight, clear of hands, thighs, shafts and bristles. Nobody carries another ball or hidden rescue device.
