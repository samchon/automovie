# Eastern green Keeper

## Identity, station and recognition limits {#identity}

G-K is an anonymous student green-team Keeper, with a stocky silhouette, close-cropped brown hair, a green-and-silver tie, grey knit layer, white collar and black Slytherin robe over charcoal trousers and black shoes, with lower robe panels secured for riding. This invented supporting design must remain distinct from Carina's pale tied hair and slimmer form. The Keeper rides one ordinary practice broom and has no bat, wand, harness or extra rescue apparatus. No individual name, spoken line, portrait or unrelated biography is needed.

The normal station is just west of the eastern goals, near x=-10, y=+8, at about forty metres, facing west/north toward the Chasers. Keeping an attempted Quaffle shot out matters until a seen danger takes priority. The Keeper can look east by turning the head and shoulders, raise a free hand to signal other players and leave the goal front to offer help. A hanging Seeker far into the eastern runoff is initially outside that forward ball-watching view. Seeing the other players turn is not knowledge of the precise grip state.

The Keeper needs a visible sightline to recognize Harry's unsupported legs and moving broom. Crossing more than a hundred metres and lowering to his flight height takes longer than Carina's few-metre matched approach; ordinary acceleration cannot collapse that separation. Once Harry has a secure new grip, entering the narrow two-broom space would endanger both riders. The Keeper can instead slow behind their outside flank, stay clear of the departing broom and signal the field to hold play. Screenplay determines the actual notice, attempted aid and restraint; this baseline grants no instantaneous awareness or guaranteed rescue.
