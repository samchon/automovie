# Familiar opposing Seekers

## Rivalry and unspoken care {#rivals}

Carina Malfoy and Harry Potter are familiar Hogwarts student sporting rivals of equal standing, serving as the opposing Seekers in this self-managed practice match. Carina belongs to Slytherin and Harry to Gryffindor; they are familiar students of about the same school age. Their teams use green/silver and red/gold uniform cues respectively. Both have raced each other before, but neither is the other's teacher, captain, battle enemy or established romantic partner. The shared sporting history and school practice setting are production inventions; [players](players.md#population) owns the rest of the teams.

Each wants the catch and the right to tease the other about winning. They may interrupt practice for danger without a championship sanction or an official disciplinary consequence. Carina values Harry's competitive attention and conceals tenderness with impatience. Harry understands her taunt as a challenge, not a wish to harm him. Neither knows that the other returns romantic interest; a protective choice can expose a priority without producing a confession or a relationship.

Their familiar appearance and flying competence let each recognize the other at action distance. Neither knows what happens behind their current view or the outcome of a reach in advance. Immediate physical danger can outweigh sporting advantage; restored support makes a guarded exchange possible. [Carina](carina.md#identity) and [Harry](harry.md#identity) own their individual limits, while screenplay owns their actual seeing, choice, cost and recognition. No family dispute, past debt or secret arrangement with a supporting player explains the rescue.
