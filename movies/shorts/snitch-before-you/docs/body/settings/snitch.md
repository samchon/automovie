# The gold Snitch

## Appearance, pursuit and escape {#flight}

The Snitch is a bright gold sphere four centimetres across with two thin pale wings and a total extended span of twelve centimetres. A compact glint and a fine wing buzz distinguish it from either rider. The model may use simple solid shapes; a detailed engraved surface is unnecessary. This appearance and the following behavior are adopted fan-scene inventions for the short, not a researched statement of sporting law.

It flies independently, changes direction in short curves and briefly holds a near-hover before accelerating away. It cannot teleport, strike a rider or physically pull a reaching person off a broom. A rider must securely close a hand around the sphere to catch it. An unclosed reaching hand earns no catch and does not stop its movement. The practice chase has one Snitch and no replacement.

It can be close to Carina's left hand while Harry remains lower and ahead in her right field of view, creating a visible spatial alternative rather than two distant tasks. Leaving it unheld permits escape; no unseen contestant catches it within this scene. Screenplay owns its actual route, near-hover, escape and which person can see each movement.
