# Practice Quaffle

## Appearance, possession and passing {#handling}

The match has one Quaffle: a dull red leather sphere about twenty-five centimetres across, distinguishable from the tiny gold Snitch and dark Bludgers. The role and single-ball count follow [official primary research](../../../references/index.md#official-population); this size, texture and training behavior are adopted inventions. Its whole form can be a coarse sphere without seams or a logo.

Chasers hold, throw and receive it by hand while seated on brooms. A pass follows a visible airborne arc and requires a real receiving hand; no teleport, duplicate ball or automatic catch is available. A reception makes a short soft leather impact. A player can retain it to hold play during a noticed emergency rather than throw it into the rescue lane. Only this ball is aimed through the goal hoops. Screenplay owns the particular passer, receiver, arc, any interrupted attempt and its final possession; the ball is not another character's rescue aid.
