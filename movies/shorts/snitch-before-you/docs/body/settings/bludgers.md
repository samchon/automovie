# Two practice Bludgers

## Recognition, motion and Beater control {#flight}

Two dark iron Bludgers, B-N and B-W, are present, each a matte charcoal sphere about twenty centimetres across. The two-ball count and Beaters' redirection duty follow [official primary research](../../../references/index.md#official-population). The dimensions, subdued finish and selected practice paths are inventions. They have neither wings nor the Snitch's gold glint and cannot be mistaken for the red leather Quaffle at the chosen view.

Each moves continuously through the air and can threaten a rider's path. A visible bat strike redirects it; there is no instant jump, unseen catch or invisible wall. The training Beaters attend to them in the northern field, well away from the southern Seeker corridor. A bat impact makes one brief dry knock; it does not add an explosion or magical effect. The actual opening paths and later protective redirections belong to screenplay. Neither ball causes Harry's overreach or enters the foreground hand/shaft transfer. During a safety interruption the Beaters must still attend to both moving balls rather than leave them unaccounted for.
