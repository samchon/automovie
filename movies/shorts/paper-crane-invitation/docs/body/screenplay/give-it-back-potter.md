## INT. WINDOW CORRIDOR - LATE AFTERNOON {#invitation}

### 00:00–00:09 — Sent

A quiet window bay, open to the corridor in front. Late light crosses its grey stone floor from a plain fixed pane; pale sky rests beyond it. A backless brown wooden bench stands below the window on four square legs. The floor in front and around its right end is clear.

On the sill's exposed right end, two off-white handled cups stand upright, their centres 25 centimetres apart. Amber tea rests below each rim. A 20-centimetre patch of bare sill remains to their right. CARINA, fifteen, pale-blond hair and the collar, green-and-silver tie, knit and open black robe of her Slytherin uniform, stands beside this patch, beyond the bench's right end. She holds a small cream paper crane between her right thumb and forefinger.

Through the open front comes HARRY, fifteen, dark hair, round glasses and a Gryffindor uniform: white collar, red-and-gold tie, charcoal knit, open black robe and dark trousers. His empty hands swing as he approaches the bench's front-right corner. Carina follows him with the crane's beak. Her fingertip pushes it into the air; it glides out past the bench's right end toward him, leaving her open hand behind.

She starts to draw that hand back, then lunges one short step onto the clear floor beyond the seat's end. Her fingers close on air. A light paper flutter crosses the low indoor air tone.

### 00:09–00:19 — Wrong note

Harry stops beside the bench's front-right corner and catches the crane in his left palm. Its flight stops. He unfolds it with his right hand, keeping the sheet below chest height. The view stays on the blank back of the paper; his eyes travel across its hidden writing.

Carina comes around the bench's right end into the clear front route. Her quick footsteps bring her into the same view, right palm out.

CARINA
Potter.

Harry looks up from the open sheet.

HARRY
Is this yours?

CARINA
Wrong note. Give it back.

### 00:19–00:32 — Both this way

HARRY
Of course.

He lowers the sheet toward her offered hand. She arrives fast enough that her fingers brush its loose upper flap. The paper buckles with a small crackle.

HARRY
Wait.

Carina stops her reaching hand. Harry turns the sheet inward across his chest, bringing the folded base toward her instead of the loose flap. At the same time, he sidesteps toward the bench's right end to give her room. Carina turns into that same gap, following the paper. Their shoulders stop short of meeting.

Both look down at the space between their feet. Harry opens his empty right palm toward the clear front floor; Carina takes half a step back into it. He turns until the bench is behind him, then takes a short backward step to its right half. His calves meet the seat's front edge. With both feet planted, he bends his knees and lowers onto the fixed seat. A small wood creak. He brings the paper forward again at waist height, folded base first.

Carina takes the base in her right hand. Harry releases the sheet. Her left hand steadies its open edge. He leaves both hands on his own knees.

### 00:32–00:43 — Already read

Carina draws the paper to herself. Harry stays seated, looking up at her.

HARRY
I read it.

Close on the sheet in her hands. For the first time, its writing faces the audience:

SCREEN TEXT — ON THE PAPER
Would you have tea with me?

Hold long enough to read the single line. Her thumbs meet along an old crease. She folds the writing shut once.

Harry turns at the waist to look back around the bench's right end toward the window. His view reveals the two cups waiting on the sill. He turns back to her folded note.

CARINA
Then forget it.

### 00:43–01:00 — A place beside him

Carina turns toward the window and walks around the bench's right end to the same open sill. She lays the once-folded paper flat on the bare patch to the cups' right, freeing both hands. Behind her, Harry plants his feet, lifts his weight just enough to shift left along the fixed bench, then settles, leaving its right half open. Wood creaks under the shift.

HARRY
Your tea's getting cold.

Carina's hands stop beside the cups. She turns her head toward the open place, then hooks a finger through each handle and lifts one cup in each hand. Both remain upright as she comes back around the same bench end along the clear front floor.

She offers the cup in her left hand to Harry. He lifts his empty right hand under its body and takes its weight. Only then does she let go.

CARINA
That one's yours.

Harry settles his cup near his chest. Carina turns in front of the empty right half and steps back until her calves touch the seat. With both feet planted, she bends her knees and lowers beside him, keeping her own cup upright in her right hand. Her free left hand comes to its side once she is seated.

Stay with both figures and their two cups. Carina draws her feet back beside his; Harry raises his cup, and she raises hers. The folded invitation rests on the ledge behind them. Quiet ceramic movement and the indoor air tone carry to the cut at 01:00.
