# Carina Malfoy

## Student identity, uniform, voice and restraint {#identity}

Carina is an invented fifteen-year-old Hogwarts student in this school-age alternate universe, not a claimed canonical biography. She is approximately 1.70 metres tall, slim, with light skin and straight pale-blond hair cut at the shoulder. Her Hogwarts uniform identifies Slytherin: a white pointed collar, green-and-silver striped tie, charcoal V-neck knit, open black knee-length robe, charcoal knee-length pleated skirt, opaque black tights and flat black school shoes. Simplify these as distinct polygon surfaces while retaining collar, striped tie, knit/robe layering and the skirt silhouette. The robe is open at the front and split at the sides and back below the hips, leaving bent knees and the seat edge clear; its cuffs finish above the hands. Hair and fabric stay behind her working fingers and clear of cups, paper and the bench supports. She carries no wand or bag. Her practical responsibilities are her own study and possessions; no authority over Harry is implied.

She wants Harry's company but fears an openly offered invitation will expose her to rejection. She masks uncertainty with brisk demands and precise diction. Her English voice is a clear youthful medium-pitched voice, medium pitch, close and unforced, with quick clipped openings and a slower, quieter register when she stops defending herself. This is a generic performance direction, not an impersonation. She knows what her own note says and why two cups are ready. Harry has never been told she wants a private tea. The reciprocal relationship and her refusal limits are owned by [their relationship](relationship.md#relationship); her ability to send paper is owned by [the paper](paper-crane.md#paper).
