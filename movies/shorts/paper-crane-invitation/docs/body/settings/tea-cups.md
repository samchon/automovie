# Two tea cups

## Supported cups and safe handoffs {#cups}

Two matching plain off-white ceramic cups have round open rims, short cylindrical bodies about nine centimetres tall and eight centimetres across, and one simple rounded side handle each. The handles project about three centimetres and have an opening large enough for an index-finger grip; holding the body is also allowed. Their flat bases stand independently on the sill. Each contains ordinary warm amber tea about two centimetres below its rim, leaving carrying clearance. The liquid surface stays level at rest and follows a gentle upright carry; abrupt tilting can spill. The simple visible form needs no pattern, lid or transparent glass.

These are invented props with no potion or truth-telling effect. Carina has already brought this pair for a private tea before the film begins; preparation and supply equipment are outside this encounter. Tea is already comfortable to hold and drink; no boiling liquid, aggressive steam plume or scalding peril is part of this scene. Both cups remain upright and supported on the window sill until their intended pickup. They may be placed 0.25 metres apart at the sill's open right end, leaving at least 0.20 metres of clear top beside them for the folded note; the actual opening positions belong to screenplay.

The cups have no saucers, tray, spoons or labels. Each can be carried upright by one hand through its handle or around its body. One supported cup can be lifted and offered to the other person's empty hand; the giver releases only after the receiver supports it. A person holding a cup needs the other hand free to handle paper safely. The window sill in [the corridor](corridor.md#space) is the initial support; the bench is available seating, not an unshown tea-service surface. There is no additional tea service or unseen server. Soft ceramic handling is the audible prop texture; a collision, sip or clink must not be assumed unless screenplay actually calls for it.
