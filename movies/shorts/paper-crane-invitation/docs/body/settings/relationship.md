# Carina and Harry

## Prickly acquaintances with room to choose {#relationship}

This invented school-age relationship is mutual, familiar verbal sparring between peers. Neither owes the other affection or controls the other's study, status, movement or possessions. Harry finds Carina's dry company enjoyable; Carina interprets her own visible interest as a risk to her composure. They have shared public conversation before, but neither has proposed private tea. No debt, coercion, previous romantic commitment or prior rejection drives this scene.

Carina may request the return of her own paper and may step away. Harry respects a serious request and does not deliberately make her chase it. Carina does not seize his body, block his exit or threaten punishment. Their brief path interference can arise from both trying to move at once, but either can stop or yield into the open corridor. A clear offered seat and an unforced acceptance can change the relationship; neither a caught wrist nor an accidentally close stance counts as consent. These are baseline possibilities and limits, not a prescription of the scene's eventual choices.
