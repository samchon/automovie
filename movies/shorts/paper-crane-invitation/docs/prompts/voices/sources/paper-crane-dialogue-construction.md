# Give It Back, Potter

Original adult-AU fan short. English. Vertical 9:16. One continuous corridor scene, planned for 60 seconds. Time spans allocate performance and edit attention; speech recordings and the whole staged performance must establish actual timing. Parents: [brief](../../instructions/brief.md) and the six independently reviewed [compact settings records](../settings/). No treatment or script layer is used.

## INT. WINDOW CORRIDOR - LATE AFTERNOON {#invitation}

### 00:00–00:09 — Sent

Two warm cups stand on a stone window ledge. Beside them, CARINA, pale-blond hair and green torso, holds a small cream paper crane between her right thumb and forefinger. Beyond the backless bench below the window, HARRY, dark hair, round glasses and red torso, crosses the open corridor toward the bench's right end. His hands are empty.

Carina follows him with the crane's beak. Her fingertip pushes it into the air. It glides past the bench toward Harry, leaving her open hand behind.

She starts to draw that hand back, then lunges one step after the crane. Her fingers close on air. A light paper flutter carries across the quiet corridor.

### 00:09–00:19 — Wrong note

Harry catches the crane in his left palm at the bench's front-right corner. Its flight stops. He unfolds it with his right hand, keeping the sheet below chest height. The view stays on the blank back of the paper; his eyes travel across its hidden writing.

Carina comes around the bench's right end into the clear front route. Her quick footsteps bring her into the same view, right palm out.

CARINA
Potter.

Harry looks up from the open sheet.

HARRY
Is this yours?

CARINA
Wrong note. Give it back.

### 00:19–00:32 — Both this way

HARRY
Of course.

He lowers the sheet toward her offered hand. She arrives fast enough that her fingers brush its loose upper flap. The paper buckles with a small crackle.

HARRY
Wait.

Carina stops her reaching hand. Harry turns the sheet inward across his chest, bringing the folded base toward her instead of the loose flap. At the same time, he sidesteps toward the bench's right end to give her room. Carina steps that way too, following the paper. Their shoulders stop short of meeting.

Both look down at the space between their feet. Harry opens his empty right palm toward the clear corridor; Carina takes half a step back. With room behind his knees, he bends and sits on the bench's right half. His feet settle flat. He brings the paper forward again at waist height, folded base first.

Carina takes the base in her right hand. Harry releases the sheet. Her left hand steadies its open edge. He leaves both hands on his own knees.

### 00:32–00:43 — Already read

Carina draws the paper to herself. Harry stays seated, looking up at her.

HARRY
I read it.

Close on the sheet in her hands. For the first time, its writing faces the audience:

SCREEN TEXT — ON THE PAPER
Would you have tea with me?

Hold long enough to read the single line. Her thumbs meet along an old crease. She folds the writing shut once.

Harry's gaze moves past her shoulder. His view reveals the two cups waiting on the window ledge. He looks back to her folded note.

CARINA
Then forget it.

### 00:43–01:00 — A place beside him

Carina turns toward the window and takes the short route around the bench's right end. She lays the folded paper on the ledge beside the cups, freeing both hands. Behind her, wood creaks as Harry shifts left along the bench, leaving its right half open.

HARRY
Your tea's getting cold.

Carina's hands stop beside the cups. She turns her head toward the open place, then lifts one cup in each hand. Both remain upright as she comes back around the same bench end.

She offers the cup in her left hand to Harry. He lifts his empty right hand under its body and takes its weight. Only then does she let go.

CARINA
That one's yours.

Harry settles his cup near his chest. Carina turns, bends her knees and sits on the space to his right, keeping her own cup upright in her right hand. Her free left hand comes to its side once she is seated.

Stay with both figures and their two cups. Carina draws her feet back beside his; Harry raises his cup, and she raises hers. The folded invitation rests on the ledge behind them. Quiet ceramic movement and the indoor air tone carry to the cut at 01:00.
