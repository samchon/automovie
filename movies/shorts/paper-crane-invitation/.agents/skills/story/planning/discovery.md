# Discovery

Before bulk writing, break the adopted instructions, references, research and delegated choices into consequential elements and assign each to its existing owner or a missing one, under the discovery questions in the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md). A genre label or a general reading of the brief does not establish coverage. Discover the chosen form too: what each unit presents or withholds, how it guides its recipient, and the visible difference a named style makes.

Facts and constraints belong in settings, events and release in their layer, and a production-specific judgment in `contracts/story/work`, adopted with its population through [adoption](../../evidence/adoption.md). A requirement that lives only in status notes, selector names or repeated guidance needs a canonical owner.

If no independent requirement remains, write `contracts/story/work/discovery/results.md` with the inspected scope, the risks examined and the owners that cover them. A finding in one area does not establish absence elsewhere, and a blank production never receives a fabricated negative.
