# Final video screenplay

Compose the audience's complete viewing and listening experience, understandable without its parents, under the screenplay questions in the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md). Choose what is seen and heard, how attention moves, and when the audience recognizes, waits and connects, through speech, action, reactions, sound, detail, duration and transitions in [readable notation](notation.md). A fixed view, silence, caption or narration serves the form when its contribution earns the attention.

For long work, extract concise notes of the required identities, decisions, states, chronology, space and audience knowledge from completed treatments and scripts, draft in an [isolated context](../correction/inspection.md#isolated-context) with those notes, the requirements and fixed quotations, then compare against the actual parents. Keep the decided event order while composing the realization afresh under [parent differentiation](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation). A direct short composes events, action and realization from its brief; a compact short also consumes its settings and cites the facts it uses.

Write a specialist performance from its settings vocabulary and [performance study](../planning/research.md#performance-study): chained sequences, changes of tempo, spatial strategy and the continuous response of everyone present, never one exchange shape repeated or a participant left standing still. Every contact the scene shows has a decided consequence for both bodies, its duration and the mark it leaves, so motion and sound realize it rather than invent it. Shot code can carry implementation precision but never an unresolved creative choice; a defective parent is repaired under [upstream repair](../correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).

Before evidence, read the whole progression in viewing order, then inspect and repair under [cause rounds](../correction/repair.md#cause-rounds) and complete the passes through [workflow](../workflow.md).
