# Script population obligations

The completed script population answers once per question for its partition, titles, material allocation and executable handoff.

## Does the chosen partition give the audience meaningful units? {#partition-and-titles}

Place act, part, sequence, episode or season boundaries where the actual form delivers change and an earned reason to continue or stop. Scene quotas or an unfinished exchange cut cannot establish that boundary. After a release interval, action, goals, address, objects or other retrieval cues restore needed state without mandatory recap. Units need not share the same crisis. Where purchase boundaries are declared, inspect whether prior material has delivered the promised experience and reward.

Compare audience-facing titles with actual unit content and prior viewing: the promised event, object, conflict, image or question must gain meaning there. Report absent promises and interchangeable titles, without demanding plot summary or spoilers. Numbers, ambiguous or poetic titles, part-numbered repetition and refrains may work; preserve user-fixed wording. Repair semantic mismatches at scripts, which owns the titles and identities descendants inherit.

## Does every assigned event become a complete executable handoff? {#complete-execution}

Map treatments to implementing scripts and scripts to actual parents, allowing many-to-many relations while exposing missing, orphaned and duplicated progression. Compare assigned execution material with declared scale: later authors must not invent essential progression, repeat or pad to sustain it. Text-length ratios cannot establish sufficiency; repair the boundary, event assignment or delivery promise that causes the gap.

Read every H2 as an independent initial execution script. Can a filmmaker stage essential action, process, exchange, transition, consequence, appearance, blocking, light, in-scene sound, atmosphere, viewing position and meaning-changing delivery without inventing them? Missing decisions and decisions buried under directions both make a handoff unusable. Treatment summary, elapsed-action report, dialogue buried in explanation and results without intervening execution fail even if later specialists could rescue them. Leave composition, lens values and incidental acting detail open only where they replace no script-owned decision; final screenplay realizes this already executable handoff.
