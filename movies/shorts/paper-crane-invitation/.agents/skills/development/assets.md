# Asset work

Build what the commission's scenes need, in the folder [source layout](layout.md) gives, under the [asset contract](../contracts/development/assets.md). [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md) owns how an asset is made, rigged and verified.

## Scope

Read [project context](../production/profile.md) first. A single scene can use a ready asset with a few placements; a recurring character needs editable proportions, expressions and motion. A standalone asset defines its views and controls without a screenplay.

## Coarse first

Build stick figures, block forms or frames first, keeping identity, dimensions, pivots, attachments and controls independent of surface detail, and finish the complete low-cost motion through [motion](../motion/SKILL.md) before any upgrade. Upgrade only what its visible role needs, keeping the controls and the measured budget. A motion hint needs reliable action and staging; photographic detail only when the output contract asks for it. A 2D image can be appearance reference or a conditioning input, never a substitute for coded motion. Use the installed Human package only for what its public surface provides.

## Inspect and hand off

Inspect the asset in its commissioned use, with its shot cameras, neighbors and motion, segment by segment under [segment review](../motion/segments.md). After an upgrade, compare the same segments for changed proportions, pivots, contact, timing and silhouette. Hand the rigs and controls to motion, and link the asset from the settings record it realizes under [settings](../story/layers/settings.md#realized-assets).
