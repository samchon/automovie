The work reads as a tragedy of competent decisions that cease to protect the people who made them possible. Gaesomun learns to wait for the rear, hears household costs, and learns what the murdered king had to decide. Namsaeng carries that practice further, then knowingly uses the same roads and relationships to destroy the country. The closing son remains alive without releasing either man's responsibility. The emotional progression is understandable without consulting a proof record.

Part 1 makes the childhood error executable. In screenplay `001-the-right-to-command/001-people-to-command.md#t004-night-shortcut`, “Only when the person ahead moves aside can those behind see the stones' outlines” gives the older soldier's warning a visible cause. “I crossed it. Close the gaps and follow” is therefore more than youthful impatience. In `#t006-conditional-inheritance`, “Without answering for them, he states his own household's conditions first” protects the separate consent of the three households; this must survive any shortening.

Part 2 makes Gaesomun's understanding costly rather than absolving. In screenplay `002-the-kings-work/001-the-kings-work.md#t008-last-encounter`, “The sword arm moves. After the king's breathing stops, the living Gaesomun lowers the sword” makes the killing his act. In `#t013-eulbo-after-withdrawal`, “Eulbo withdraws, sits and rests; in the distance, voices still call for loads at the less-repaired section” makes the concession happen and preserves what it defers. In scripts at the same anchor, “Though the road home has not opened, he gains time to rest” clearly keeps rest separate from discharge.

Parts 3 and 4 let armies act as connected groups. In screenplay `003-the-roads-that-move/001-moving-roads.md#t020-camp-separation`, “Follow their approach across the intervening ground before returning to the carrier still trying to enter” makes the central force travel before it reaches the western access. In `004-the-victory-that-remains/001-remaining-victory.md#t028-assault-on-camp`, “The next rank enters and takes the pressure. Its advance stops, but it ceases to retreat” gives resistance a change rather than announcing victory. Chano's damaged grip survives success: in `#t029-knife-to-ajin`, “Chano starts to take out the small knife and asks another hand to undo the knot.” Preserve the grip, the carried knife, and Ajin's continuing need for help together.

Part 5 makes Namsaeng's limitation legible before the coup. In screenplay `005-days-to-sow/001-days-to-sow.md#t032-competence-strengthens-opposition`, Namsan says “I am asking for a place in those decisions.” and Namsaeng replies “I need both of you. Do the work I have given you.” This is a concrete refusal to share the broader decision while valuing delegated work. The separate thoughts in `#t034-royal-command-enables-removal` are unusually explicit, but they distinguish the three actors' incompatible purposes. Explicitness alone is not a defect. The separate days in `#t037-reports-close-the-negotiating-path` preserve the audience's knowledge of death before Namsaeng learns it.

Part 6 retains both responsibility and the survivors' work. In screenplay `006-the-son-left-living/001-the-son-left-living.md#t038-namsaeng-chooses-destructive-aid`, “I know the country may fall” prevents an innocent-mistake explanation. In `#t042-remaining-communities-resist-new-demands`, “The official keeps the unfilled portion of the demand open” prevents local negotiation becoming effortless liberation. In `#t043-death-leaves-heonseong-living`, “I knew when I went too. I asked for aid. I have my share” keeps Heonseong an implicated living person. The final “End as he turns toward the door” leaves a next obligation, rather than a posthumous pardon.

One concrete continuity concern requires parent comparison. In screenplay part 1 `#t003-shared-bow`, “At the gyeongdang as the sun falls, the shooting place is empty, but Eulbo is absent” establishes the evening wait. After Eulbo enters and the two speak, “The next child receives the shared bow from Eulbo and faces the target” resumes a populated queue without showing its return or a change of time. I read the later children as unexplained occupants of the emptied shooting place. The strongest initial counterreading is that only the shooting position is temporarily empty, with other children still nearby; the plural queue before the evening transition could support that interpretation if the parent or staging establishes it. This is a concern, not yet a confirmed source finding.

The frequent reports, lists, shares, and delayed work form a persistent administrative rhythm. It can feel dense, especially in the historical bridges, but I did not lose the main causal movement. I have no first-reading basis to demand decorative changes to concise orders, basic actions, required causal sentences, or short completed replies. The simple command “Keep them here!” in screenplay part 4 `#t028-assault-on-camp` has an immediate tactical referent and should not be expanded merely for variety.
