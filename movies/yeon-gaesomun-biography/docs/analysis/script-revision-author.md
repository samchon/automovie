## Scope and method

The first inspection read all six scripts and their six indexes in performance order, all six screenplays and their six indexes in viewing order, and all 43 actual treatments. Every narrative layer has 115 H2s. The author then compared the applicable instructions and actual setting owners, and bounded passages of the Korean source ZIP. The batch below followed that completed inspection, rather than fixing examples while discovery was still running.

After the batch, the author read the complete twelve episode bodies again, all twelve indexes, all 43 treatment parents and all fourteen changed calendar setting records. Truncated displays were recovered for the missing sections. This second full round found the follow-up items below. At root's request the first body snapshot is now held unchanged while independent reading and changed-sentence classification proceed. The hashes below identify that snapshot, rather than an approved or frozen Scripts edition.

Supporting settings were inspected where used, rather than all settings being declared read: Gaesomun, Taejo, Namsaeng, Namgeon, Namsan, Heonchung, Bojang, Sokro, Miran, Seokbu, Nuri, the gyeongdang children and household soldiers; earlier Sui wars and the second restoration; royal/noble authority, martial status and Yeon protection; capital isolation, the Yalu, northern diversion, concentration, post-conquest administration; Yodong, the injured soldier's house and the Henan household. The full Gaesomun record and every external reference were not exhaustively revalidated. The ZIP was consulted in memory for age/appearance, Taejo's designation, the sowing chronology, Seokbu's assigned role and the lunar calendar. The archive and historical HTML answers were preserved and were not treated as current English approval.

This scope supports local Scripts construction repairs and their actual screenplay consumers. No event partition needs redesign. Screenplay changes to rank, temporal state, physical action or missing adopted functions are construction repairs. Local language, speaker clarity and audience attention repairs preserve the corresponding event's meaning. This is not a newly drafted whole screenplay constructed in an isolated context, a completed naturalness transfer, a new historical-evidence campaign, a measured performance or a native evidence pass.
