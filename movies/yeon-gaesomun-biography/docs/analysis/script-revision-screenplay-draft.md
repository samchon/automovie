# Selected screenplay candidates

These five complete H2 candidates are for author comparison and adoption. Dates use lunar months. Speech is rendered in English. No running time has been measured.

## The unheard address {#t001-unheard-address}

Part 1, episode 1. Parents: treatment001/unheard-address; script001/t001-unheard-address.

INT. YEON RESIDENCE, MILITARY OFFICE, PYONGYANG. NIGHT.

CAPTION: Late spring, 661. The night after the council.

Lamplight falls across the Liaodong report. Gaesomun sits alone beyond it. We hear the slight movement of the paper as he brings it nearer the light.

His eyes stop moving across the writing. His mouth remains closed.

GAESOMUN (INWARD VOICE): Your Majesty.

The view stays with the living man at his desk.

HISTORIAN (V.O.): He was addressing Go Geonmu, King Yeongnyu. Gaesomun had killed the king with his own hands.

Gaesomun's gaze has lifted from the report. There is lamplight on his face and darkness beyond the desk.

GAESOMUN (INWARD VOICE): I used to wonder why you hesitated.

He draws breath, as though there were more to say aloud. Only the audience hears the continuation.

GAESOMUN (INWARD VOICE): They are afraid to lose the army. So am I.

He looks down at the report again, but does not resume reading it.

GAESOMUN (INWARD VOICE): You had to answer for all of it. I understand that now.

The paper shifts beneath his hand.

GAESOMUN (INWARD VOICE): I will still take them out to fight.

Hold through the breath after the words. Then his eyes begin moving over the report once more. The paper and his breathing are audible in the room.

CUT TO the same Pyongyang household in 614: Gaesomun as a newborn.

## The camp separates {#t020-camp-separation}

Part 3, episode 1. Parents: treatment020/camp-separation; script003/t020-camp-separation. Spatial owner: setting060-070/yodong.

EXT. NUFANG CAMP, WEST OF YODONGSEONG. DAY.

CAPTION: Late tenth month, 661. Zheng Mingzhen's Nufang camp.

A brief overhead map places Yodongseong to the east of the camp and identifies Sinseong as the source of cooperating Goguryeo forces. The northwestern and southwestern supply roads converge on the camp's western side. Between the southwestern road and the eastern approach, the southern ground remains open. The map gives way to the western access, where incoming cargo has slowed to make room for armed men coming out.

From the opposite side of the camp comes the noise of fighting.

ZHENG: The northern armies have gone. Send the escort to bring in the supplies. Keep the east manned.

The escort presses westward past the cargo. A man bringing a load toward the camp has to wait while soldiers squeeze by him. The last men of the escort are still within calling distance of the camp when the first are already outside it.

CUT EAST. Gaesomun watches Goguryeo troops engage the camp's eastern defenders. The Tang line gives ground, steadies and pushes back. The clash continues while he looks toward the western side.

CUT WEST, BEYOND THE CAMP. Sokro's force moves against the connection behind the departing escort. Tang soldiers turn to meet the threat. Their movement back toward the camp stalls as they have to face the men cutting them off.

SOKRO: Keep them apart!

He directs his men along the separating space. When Tang troops attempt to cross it, they meet resistance; when they draw together to fight, the way back to the camp remains beyond them.

AT THE EASTERN ENGAGEMENT. The escort has cleared the camp. Fighting still holds Zheng's eastern defenders in place.

GAESOMUN: The west entrance. Go.

The central troops start toward the camp side of the western bottleneck. Stay with their advance long enough to see the ground between their waiting position and the access close. Behind and to the east, the first engagement continues.

AT THE WESTERN ACCESS. Goguryeo infantry reaches the men trying to admit the cargo. Tang defenders turn outward to meet them. Those still coming in collide with those trying to get out; loads obstruct the space in which they need to form.

TANG OFFICER: Back to the camp! Form inside!

The order reaches men on both sides of the blockage. They move in opposite directions. A few force their way through, but Goguryeo infantry takes the access before the rest can join them.

BEYOND THE CAMP. An escort soldier tries to wave his companions toward the entrance. Between them and the camp stand Sokro's men; at the entrance itself, another body of Goguryeo infantry is now holding against the defenders. The soldier lowers his arm and turns back into the fight beside him.

INSIDE THE CAMP. Zheng calls for men to move west. The men immediately before him obey. Farther away, soldiers are still facing the eastern attack, and the order is lost among competing calls.

The sound carries us from one resisting group to another. Each is fighting. The spaces between them no longer fill when an officer calls.

At the southern edge, individual Tang soldiers break away from the groups inside. They pass into the open ground between the southwestern transport road and the eastern approach. Others follow separately while fighting continues behind them.

The final wide view keeps that southern opening visible, with the western access held and the camp's defenders broken into disconnected bodies. Fighting and scattered orders continue as individual men get clear to the south.

## Gwidol dies {#t025-guidol-dies}

Part 4, episode 1. Parents: treatment025/guidol-dies; script004/t025-guidol-dies. Continuity continues into t027-river-pressure and t029-knife-to-ajin.

EXT. MOUNTAIN ROAD. DAY.

CAPTION: The Silla transport party, returning after the delivery.

Feet pass close to us on the road. Chano's steps arrive unevenly among them. Beside him, Gwidol can barely lift his frozen feet.

Chano takes more of their load. His injured fingers fail to close securely around it. He changes his grip and gets the weight up, but Gwidol has stopped.

CHANO: Lean on me.

Gwidol leans against him. Chano braces with his own damaged feet. Gwidol tries to step, and the two sag together.

Men behind them wait, then work their way past. Chano lowers Gwidol to the roadside. For a moment the column fills the view beyond Gwidol's shoulder, still moving homeward.

CHANO: Rest a little.

He crouches close enough to hear Gwidol beneath the passing footsteps.

GWIDOL: Mother will be in the field.

Chano waits for another breath.

GWIDOL: If she has to do it all...

Gwidol draws breath again. Chano leans closer. The next words do not come, and Gwidol's breathing stops.

CHANO: Gwidol?

Chano keeps his arm beneath him. Gwidol's weight settles fully against it. The footsteps continue while Chano watches for the movement of his chest.

CHANO: Gwidol.

A companion stops beside them. Chano looks up at him, then toward the retreating backs on the road.

COMPANION: Chano. We have to go.

Chano tries to lift Gwidol higher against him. He gets him only as far as his knees before his feet give under the weight. The companion steadies Chano, and they lower Gwidol together.

CHANO: I can't leave him here.

The companion remains crouched beside him. The gap to the column grows.

Chano eases his arm out from under Gwidol. He takes Gwidol's small working knife. The view comes close to the everyday tool in his damaged hand, then returns to the two men beside the body.

CHANO: His mother. Ajin. I have to take this to her.

He wraps the knife. When he tries to secure the wrapping with cord, his fingers slip off it. The companion takes the cord while Chano holds the wrapped knife against his body.

The companion ties it securely and puts the bound knife into Chano's keeping. Chano looks from the fastening to Gwidol. He rises with the companion's help.

Keep Gwidol in the same view as the two men regain the road. Chano carries the secured knife. The men ahead slow in their walking to negotiate the ground, and Chano labors to close the distance.

We stay beside the body until Chano and his companion have passed out of the near view. Their steps join the sounds of the returning party farther down the road.

## The assault on the camp {#t028-assault-on-camp}

Part 4, episode 1. Parents: treatment028/assault-on-camp; script004/t028-assault-on-camp. Spatial owner: setting060-100/sasu-ground. Calendar owner: setting050-120/sasu-battle.

EXT. SASU CAMP, BOTONG RIVER AREA. MORNING.

CAPTION: Day 18 of the second month, 662. Pang Xiaotai's Sasu camp.

From the riverside rise, the view runs through the camp's land entrance and out onto the open slope. Tang troops hold the entrance. Beyond them, Namgeon's central heavy cavalry has room to form. Inside, the barracks and baggage divide the ground into narrow passages.

A Tang drum sounds. Men between the barracks turn toward it, though their view of the entrance is cut off.

PANG: Hold the entrance. Keep looking for relief.

CUT OUTSIDE. Sokro's light cavalry cuts across the movements of scattered Tang soldiers trying to pass beyond the fighting. A Tang messenger trying to leave the camp turns back from the riders. A call from one separated group receives an answer from another, but the riders force them apart before they can join.

HISTORIAN (V.O.): Ren's camp had fallen. The land rendezvous had been blocked since the previous day.

The drum continues over the return to Pang's camp.

ON THE OPEN SLOPE. Namgeon's horsemen advance against the outer Tang formation. The line before them holds long enough to strike back. A horseman falls; those beside him keep their formation moving against the men still in front.

AT THE ENTRANCE. Local Goguryeo infantry presses into the constrained opening while the heavy cavalry remains outside. Tang defenders fight back within reach of the barracks. The close view follows the infantry into the passage; the impact of the cavalry is still heard behind us.

Farther inside, a Tang junior commander moves until he can see past the end of a barrack. Men are coming back toward him. He calls them together and drives a counterattack toward the entrance.

TANG COMMANDER: Forward! Clear the passage!

The counterattack strikes the Goguryeo infantry. Men at the opening are forced backward. A wounded Goguryeo soldier goes down, and those behind him have to fight across the ground he has lost.

FROM GAESOMUN'S POSITION. Through the entrance, he sees the infantry recoil.

GAESOMUN: More infantry to that opening. Hold it.

The additional men enter on foot. The passage fills with their advance, then stops under the counterattack. Stay close while they take the pressure. One man drops back wounded; another reaches his place. Their forward movement is spent, but they cease to retreat.

CUT OUTSIDE. Tang troops attempt to draw away from Namgeon's cavalry toward the entrance. The mounted formation bears against them again, forcing them to face outward. The sound of the men fighting inside remains behind them.

NAMGEON: Keep them here!

The horsemen sustain the engagement on the open ground. They do not follow their opponents into the inner lanes.

AT THE PASSAGE OPENING. Tang's counterattack pushes once more. Goguryeo infantry holds. The Tang commander calls for support from outside, but the answer comes through the noise of the cavalry fight, with no formation breaking free to reach him.

He falls back with his men. The view follows their withdrawal between the barracks until baggage forces them to narrow again. Those at the rear try to make room for the men still fighting backward toward them.

A drum sounds on the far side of the buildings. The commander turns toward it, then has to turn back to the Goguryeo infantry coming through the passage.

OUTSIDE THE ENTRANCE. A tired Goguryeo infantryman pauses against another man's shoulder before they move forward together. Wounded men come back through the opening. Beyond the cavalry, the horse and baggage personnel maintain the rear connection as men and animals continue moving through it.

Return to the camp's land side in one wide view: the heavy cavalry still engages the outer formation, the infantry holds the passage opening, and the inner defenders withdraw among the baggage. Fighting continues in both sectors, with neither Tang group able to reach the other.

## Competence strengthens opposition {#t032-competence-strengthens-opposition}

Part 5, episode 1. Parents: treatment032/competence-strengthens-opposition; script005/t032-competence-strengthens-opposition.

INT. MILITARY OFFICE, PYONGYANG. DAY.

CAPTION: Postwar reconstruction. During Yeon Gaesomun's lifetime.

Namgeon stands before Namsaeng. Namsan remains beside him. Namsaeng has heard them out; Namgeon waits for an answer rather than leave.

NAMSAENG: You will keep your military command. I need you there.

NAMGEON: Then let me use it.

NAMSAENG: For defense.

NAMGEON: We broke their armies. We have men who know how to fight them now.

NAMSAENG: I am not raising another expedition.

NAMGEON: At Sasu I moved the cavalry clear of the entrance so the infantry could work. I know when to hold men back.

NAMSAENG: I know what you did.

NAMGEON: Enough to keep me doing it here. Never enough to give me more.

Namsaeng looks directly at his brother.

NAMSAENG: You think I would send you out and lose only the men you take? The land still has to feed them.

NAMGEON: I know what your delay at the Yalu gave us. But you were defeated there. Must every army wait because yours lost?

NAMSAENG: They will wait because I have decided to rebuild. That decision stands.

Namgeon turns toward the door. Namsan speaks before he leaves.

NAMSAN: And the court? Will every question between the king and our house have to wait for you too?

NAMSAENG: Bring those questions to me.

NAMSAN: I can settle them with him. You need someone who can hear both sides before a refusal has to become a threat.

NAMSAENG: You can carry the business between us. You will not decide it in my place.

NAMSAN: I am asking for a place in those decisions.

NAMSAENG: And I am telling you what I can entrust to you.

Namsan looks toward Namgeon, who is still at the door.

NAMSAENG: I need both of you. Do the work I have given you.

Namsan bows and follows Namgeon out. Namsaeng remains in the office.

CUT TO:

INT. PALACE, PYONGYANG. A SEPARATE ROYAL AUDIENCE.

Namgeon and Namsan stand before King Bojang. Namsaeng is absent from this audience.

BOJANG: Your brother has kept the army on defense.

NAMGEON: I have asked him for authority to prepare another offensive, Your Majesty. He refuses it.

BOJANG: You commanded the heavy cavalry at Sasu.

NAMGEON: Yes, Your Majesty.

BOJANG: I have not forgotten that service.

Namgeon lifts his eyes to the king.

BOJANG: And you, Namsan? What have you asked for?

NAMSAN: A part in deciding the business between Your Majesty and our family. My brother will let me carry it to him.

BOJANG: You believe you could do more.

NAMSAN: I believe we could agree on more, Your Majesty. Our house need not lose its authority for the court to be heard.

BOJANG: I would rather hear that from you than receive another refusal after it is settled.

The king allows Namsan to answer.

NAMSAN: Then let me come to you while the matter is still open.

BOJANG: Come to me. Both of you. Bring the business that remains unresolved between the court and your family.

NAMSAN: With your permission, I will arrange the next audience before we leave.

BOJANG: You have it.

Namsan bows. Namgeon bows beside him.

At the end of the audience, Namgeon withdraws. Namsan remains long enough to speak to the palace attendant at the entrance.

NAMSAN: His Majesty has allowed us to return. I would arrange our next audience.

The attendant turns with him toward the palace interior to make the arrangement. Through the entrance, Namgeon continues out toward his military duties.
