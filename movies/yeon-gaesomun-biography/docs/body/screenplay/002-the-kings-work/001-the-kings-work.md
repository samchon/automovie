On screen: “After 634.” At the palace, a noble reports on supplies for fortress repairs. When Gaesomun asks for a share to equip the army, Go Geonmu asks where the supplies will go rather than what they are.

**King**　If we call back the people we agreed to return to the fields, who will tend those fields?

**Gaesomun**　Delay our preparations and the troops to defend us will be late, Your Majesty. If they mean to conquer us, further concessions will not stop them.

**King**　First send what is due to the fortresses and what is to return to the fields. Do not divert it back to the army.

The noble answers the king and withdraws. As he crosses the doorway, Gaesomun calls after him.

**Gaesomun**　Wait. List what remains as well and bring it to me. I will see where it is tied up.

Between the king's door and Gaesomun, the noble chooses a direction. His feet turn back toward Gaesomun. Those inside the open palace door see him turn and report. Even houses that waited for requests in his father Taejo's time send people first. Gaesomun handles the military matter and sends them away; the next reporter takes their place. The queue grows. Voices asking whose turn it is reach the king's seat.

## The king's last chance to explain {#t008-last-encounter}

[Event parent](../../../body/treatments/008-king-killed.md#last-encounter) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/002-the-kings-work/001-the-kings-work.md#t008-last-encounter)

A separate room in the palace. The captured Go Geonmu and Gaesomun face each other alone. Soldiers can be heard faintly outside. The king looks at Gaesomun as he enters. There is a seat, but Gaesomun remains standing. Amid the soldiers' voices outside, the king begins.

**Go Geonmu**　You know what my father and brother brought together again: the cooperation of estranged families and the people of the east. The strength to fight Sui came from that too. We won, and they came again; even after another victory, some had no homes to return to.

**Gaesomun**　That is why we must first secure the strength to fight now.

**Go Geonmu**　The households that went to relatives on the Silla frontier are still there. So are those who went to families joined by marriage in Baekje or to trading contacts. Will they abandon their lives again and return just because the country has recovered here? Will you take away the time we need to wait for them?

**Gaesomun**　You should have attacked before they became one. Then you could have bought time.

**Go Geonmu**　Who knew then that Tang would unite so quickly? Knowing the outcome now cannot conjure up hands we did not have then.

Gaesomun looks at the king, then lowers his eyes. Tears gather, but he does not turn away.

**Gaesomun**　You discussed killing me. I do not believe I would live if I stepped aside. I will take the power to defend us.

**Go Geonmu**　Now the day to send soldiers and the day to send them home will be in your hands. A time will come when you cannot defer that to anyone else. Stand in that place. You will see what I was holding together.

Gaesomun approaches. The sound of his sword being drawn is distinct from the sounds outside. Keep both faces and the sword arm in the same continuous sequence. The point of contact and the blade's path remain concealed below the frame; show the king's bodily response to the act. The king watches him with his mouth closed. The sword arm moves. After the king's breathing stops, the living Gaesomun lowers the sword. The sounds outside become clear.

## The drum cannot reach the ships {#t012-naval-camp}

**Zhang Jinshu**　Beat the drums! Those scattered, gather at the center! Once gathered, counterattack this way!

The official responsible for the postponed task reads the changed order and withdraws. On a day before sowing, the aid cargo enters the courtyard of the eastern gyeongdang. Gubin checks the households and hands over grain and tools. While Miran holds a tool and examines its edge, someone from a household that transported early asks Gubin.

**Early transport household member**　Our house went early. Does that count toward the next share we must provide too?

**Gubin**　This turn was brought forward. It does not complete the share to be decided next time.

**Miran**　And the answer delaying this year's transport until once after sowing still stands?

**Gubin**　It does.

Miran takes the grain and tools she received into her home. After the handover, at the central office, a noble objects to Gaesomun about the order of aid.

**Noble**　Soldiers from our house died too. Are you not favoring the Malgal households?

**Gaesomun**　Those households sent people too. Some died. I promised to help the families that lost them. Send it.

Sokro hears the answer and conveys it in the east. After word reaches them that the center's promise was fulfilled ahead of another task a powerful figure on their own side had prioritized, Miran agrees to discuss the next military levy.

**Miran**　I will come to discuss the next turn. Ask Nuri about her share when she is grown. I cannot promise everything she will do then here and now.

Sokro listens. Miran returns to Nuri.

## An order understood matters more than a loud voice {#t014-shared-command}

[Event parent](../../../body/treatments/014-promises-delivered.md#shared-command) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/002-the-kings-work/001-the-kings-work.md#t014-shared-command)

On screen: “646.” Mujin points to the place to stop. The young person's words, spoken in their own language, are heard by the audience in English.

**Mujin**　Stop there, where I pointed. Tell me again what you will pass on to the people behind you.

**Young person**　I will tell them to go ahead and stop.

**Mujin**　How far ahead? Is it the same place I pointed to?

The young person checks the position and relays it to their people.

**Young person**　As far as the place Mujin pointed to. Stop there.

After the lead arrives and stops, the people behind stop at that position too. Mujin watches until the last person stops. As the young person waits for the next words, the historian's voice enters.

**Historian**　That eastern relationship began before Gaesomun. Kings Pyeongwon and Yeongyang expanded places to protect and teach people, and increased routes for buying and selling. Iron tools, salt and grain went in. Returning loads carried hides and forest products. When raids were small, they demanded the stolen goods back. They also punished raids by reducing trade or setting the extent of pursuit. When Doljigye led troops from several groups in a greater threat, the previous generation used force to defeat him.

A central official opens a report Chojibu sent from the northwest. Beside the names of young people who studied at the winter-base gyeongdang, the official separately identifies households able to take the field as cavalry immediately. Moving livestock in spring and military service require the same households' hands.

**Reporter**　Households with arms and horses will go first, and the next households will follow. The others must move the families and livestock of those who leave first together.

**Gaesomun**　Do not halt the whole migration. Continue the mission with that rotation. Include the burden that remains in the next report.

The messenger departs with the answer. Return briefly to hands sowing, hunting and fishing in the eastern village, distinguishing the different seasonal work.

Elsewhere, at the rendezvous for joint military service, Sokro's men enter among troops assigned positions by a central officer. Sokro calls his people and points to where they stand. He explains in their own language; the audience hears it in English.

**Sokro**　Cut that road and return here. The troops waiting behind will set out when they see us. Even if you see cargo, do not miss the time to rejoin.

At captured goods by a fork in the road, Sokro stops a subordinate trying to load cargo.

**Sokro**　Leave it. It is time to go back.

The subordinate puts down the load, turns and rejoins the column. Back at the rendezvous, Sokro approaches the central officer.

**Sokro**　I thought taking the cargo would make us late, so we left it. We blocked the road as assigned.

The central officer joins the returnees to the same formation as his own troops.

**Central officer**　They have joined us. Troops waiting behind, move out!

The formation receiving the order moves. After Sokro's judgment and the result of the rendezvous are reported to Gaesomun, a more distant interception mission is assigned.

**Messenger**　You may change it locally. If you do, the order is to report the reason.

Sokro looks at the next route and divides responsibilities among his people.

## Emptying the fields again {#t015-raids-and-recovery}

[Event parent](../../../body/treatments/015-years-without-rest.md#raids-and-recovery) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/002-the-kings-work/001-the-kings-work.md#t015-raids-and-recovery)

On screen: “647–648.” People again leave fields they had returned to and repaired. With each return, more needs attention. After the report and cheers that the fortress held, a separate report of defeat in the field is read in a quiet room.

**Historian**　Tang sought to keep farming hands confined inside the fortresses. Its policy was to send small armies in rotation. The reports from Seokseong, Jeokriseong, Yeoksan and Bakjakseong each distinguished defeat in the field from the fortress holding.

In an eastern village, Miran examines the seed remaining.

**Miran**　What we had last time will not be enough for the next sowing.

Gaesomun, having heard her words, works through the night. It is quiet outside. Each time he turns a page, footsteps outside the door draw closer. Among the allocations decided under the lamp, some shares are still delayed.

**Gubin**　They ask to be allowed to protect seed for the next planting. If we release aid to those households first, there will not be enough to allocate to warhorses and arms.

**Gaesomun**　Leave the share needed to maintain the army untouched. Bring me the amount of aid we cannot send as a result. I must know which households will wait and until when.

Gaesomun keeps the document before him rather than burying it under others. Under the lamp, he passes the remaining delayed allocations to the next official. The recipient must ask again when they can be released.

## The difference between gains and an expedition {#t015-unequal-fronts}

[Event parent](../../../body/treatments/015-years-without-rest.md#unequal-fronts) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/002-the-kings-work/001-the-kings-work.md#t015-unequal-fronts)

On screen: “654, Sinseong.” A report of defeat by the Khitan ends. Over a hand moving the folded report, an audience-only voice is heard.

**Historian**　What they sought to recover in that direction was more than a name. Livestock, routes for travel and trade, and access to western salt were at stake. The livelihoods of households that shared in that trade's benefits differed from those that did not.

On screen: “655, Gwidansu.” On another day, at another reporting post, another defeat is heard. When that report ends, a southern messenger reports thirty-three newly acquired fortresses.

**Reporter**　Goguryeo, Baekje and the Malgal took these fortresses together from Silla.

**Go Hwi**　After setting aside people to leave in those fortresses, do we have troops for a new expedition? Is there separate grain to feed them?

People to be sent to the secured fortresses are entered on a roll. An official opens that roll again for the person asking for a share for the next attack. Before those who heard the victory cries, names are called one by one for the new fortresses. No formation emerges to undertake the next attack.

While the allocation of people is being watched in the same room, an earlier time in the south opens to the audience alone. Attach the dates “554 → King Mu's reign → 642” one at a time to the corresponding voice; do not leave previous captions stacked.

**Historian**　After King Seong died at Gwansanseong in 554, Baekje rebuilt its military strength under King Mu. King Uija attacked Silla fortresses including Daeyaseong in 642. It was strength Baekje had developed itself before the present alliance.

On screen: “658: two records of the same expedition.” Place “Samguk Sagi: Tang did not win” and “Zizhi Tongjian: Jeokbongjin fell” side by side. Do not move to another report while both lines are read.

“659.” Go Hwi and Gaesomun hear news of On Samun's defeat.

**Historian**　On Samun, a descendant of Ondal, also lost that battle.

**Go Hwi**　We keep losing in the field. We must defend from the fortresses.

**Gaesomun**　We must cut the routes by which they communicate and receive food. We cannot send our people against the same front again.

On screen: “660.” This report concerns the fall of Baekje's royal capital. They hear of the defeat of King Uija, who earlier led the offensive against Daeyaseong. The hand about to fold the southern report stops. News of Buyeo Pung's restoration effort arrives separately afterward.

## A successor who asks what he does not know {#t015-successor-learns}

[Event parent](../../../body/treatments/015-years-without-rest.md#successor-learns) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/002-the-kings-work/001-the-kings-work.md#t015-successor-learns)

On screen: “661, military affairs in Liaodong and Gungnaeseong.” The meal Seokbu receives is also placed before Namsaeng. They eat in the cold, and Namsaeng asks about the road. After the meal, Seokbu takes him to the work route. He points out separately where one person can pass and where a load can turn.

**Namsaeng**　I came this way. Why can the load not get through?

**Seokbu**　A place where one body can step aside is different from one where a load can turn. If you send the rear on the strength of the person who went first, it will jam here.

Namsaeng listens to the experienced man's explanation to the end.

**Namsaeng**　Please take charge of the road. I will obtain what you need. I will report the reason for the delay too.

Elsewhere, Namgeon has cavalry heading for a narrow place change routes.

**Namgeon**　Do not stop here; turn toward the open side. Spread out so those behind can turn after you.

Having received an answer from the royal house, Namsan checks its contents again with a person waiting on central business.

**Namsan**　Pass the answer from His Majesty to this side too. Read it first so different words do not go out.

Gaesomun opens and reads the local report that has arrived.

**Gaesomun**　He entrusted it to Seokbu. He did not cling to work he did not know. If he takes responsibility even after delegating... he may be able to do it longer than I could. Send the next result too.

Behind the sound of Gaesomun folding the report, voices from the first council hall return. It is a place where Go Hwi's opposition is not yet over.
