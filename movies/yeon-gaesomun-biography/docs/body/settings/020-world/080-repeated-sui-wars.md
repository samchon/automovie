## Sui’s armies return after victory {#repeated-sui-wars}

**Definition and scope**

Each time Goguryeo repelled a major Sui invasion, people hoped peace might come. After diplomacy settled events in 598, they hoped for recovery, but a large army returned in 612. After defeating it they hoped expeditions would cease; Sui invaded again in 613. Hearing of rebellion within Sui in 613, they waited to see whether the turmoil would stop the campaign, but a final expedition followed in 614. Each character’s knowledge of force size and the war varies with the route by which news reaches them. Sui’s proclaimed two million is received as an enemy’s display of threat, not known as a number matching later historical counts.

**Established facts**

The 614 record says Sui could not assemble conscripted forces on time and Goguryeo was also exhausted. After defeat in the Bisaseong direction, Goguryeo requested surrender and sent Husi Zheng; Emperor Yang recalled Lai Hu’er. In the work, the request seeks time to stop further destruction of livelihood while keeping the country’s name. Limit the defeat to the Bisaseong direction; do not expand it into complete capture of Bisaseong or occupation of all territory.

The generation inheriting victory stories first learns that a huge army was defeated. Those who survived the wars and carried out recovery know food and people were lost long after victory. Some younger people know the damage, but do not feel the persistence of repeated invasions with the same weight as their elders. This difference produces Yeongnyu’s caution and Yeon Gaesomun’s confidence, and they prescribe different solutions for the same country.
