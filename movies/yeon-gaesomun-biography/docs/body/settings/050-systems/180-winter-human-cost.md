# The returning person’s hands and the next war

Canon guide: [Location of this subject](../outline/index.md#050-systems--180-winter-human-cost).

## The returning person’s hands and the next war {#winter-human-cost}

**Definition and scope**

The king’s reply mentions soldiers and animals frozen to death, injured hands and feet, and people dying on the road. The work sets 2,000 of the 30,000 Silla transport party as dead or unable to return and 3,000 of the survivors as unable immediately to resume service or transport work. The source does not preserve those exact totals. The detachment returning by sea is included in the total and is not added again to the main body’s return.

**Established facts**

Chano’s injuries and his companion’s death are invented individual events within this cost. Cold does not fell only the disloyal, and a body does not revive from admiration for a commander’s spirit. A returning member can be proud of delivering rations while resenting injury and the absence of a companion. Kim Yusin is a commander with both achievement and loss.

Goguryeo’s leaders are surprised less by the enemy having stronger blades than by its ability to move necessary quantities along a route and in time they thought impossible. The opportunity to eliminate Su Dingfang is gone, and Silla’s execution must be reassessed. This supports Namsaeng’s reconstruction; the prior expectation that Silla is weak and easily conquered after Tang is defeated does not simply persist.

In 733 Silla’s expedition against Balhae returns without success after losing more than half its soldiers to heavy snow and difficult roads. The work reconstructs it as passing the same difficult route as this winter transport, a connection used only in a side story to look back at Kim Yusin’s achievement and cost. The main story does not move the later failure forward as a cause or result of the transport, and does not additionally design the side-story chronology to complete main-story canon.
