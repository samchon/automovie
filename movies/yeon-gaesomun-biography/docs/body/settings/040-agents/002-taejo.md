## The learning Taejo permits {#taejo}

**Definition and scope**

Taejo lets son Gaesomun use family people and equipment while teaching that talent alone cannot move people. Proud of his skill, he guards against the habit of winning before persuading. Authority over family soldiers and resources belongs to Taejo; Gaesomun cannot take them at will.

**Established facts**

Taejo’s mangniji office is based on epitaph genealogy. The Samguk Sagi calls him eastern great man (also said western) and daedaero. His private teaching and death season are invented; he dies in late 633 and Gaesomun inherits command the next year. His birth year is unset.

A soldier who endured the Sui wars, Taejo basically supports Yeongnyu, understands recovery, and provides family troops under royal orders. After 618 he nevertheless argues that striking hard once during Chinese division will secure later peace. He does not join opposition to the king; he is one side among people who shared war. His son remembers the argument more loudly as if it were his whole position. His opponent at home is fellow Sui veteran [Seogu](117-seogu.md#seogu); neither regards the other as coward or reckless. Taejo proposes striking west of the Liao for one winter and returning, later after 630 preferring elites and cavalry before the wall. [Debate over the divided world](../020-world/090-second-restoration-and-tang.md#great-turmoil-debate) owns specifics.

When his son excels at Taehak, Taejo asks questions before praising. After defeating someone, will that person follow next time? When using family people, did they agree? This teaching opens the route to the son’s bowed promise before opposing nobles in 634, but does not become his leading value for life.

Taejo is about 172 centimeters tall, broad-bodied, with a long face and heavy lower jaw, brown eyes beneath drooping black eyebrows, blunt nose, broad mouth, and tawny skin. Hair is tied back and beard reaches below the chin. He wears dark brown cloth and dark sash, adding torso lamellar armor in training. Body, face, and clothes remain through youth; age appearance changes.

From 614 to around 620 he appears in his forties, thick-shouldered and chest, quick-stepping, black hair and beard with few white strands. From late 620s to 633 he appears late fifties, slight belly, hand on knee when rising, much white in hair and beard, deeper lines around eyes and forehead. Around 630 his son nears his height; by 634 the son is a palm taller.

His low clear voice has long resonance. He gives an order once, slowly. Even reprimanding his son, he lowers the ending and uses silence rather than shouting.
