## Li Shiji’s forward camp holds {#li-shiji}

**Definition and scope**

Li Shiji commands the forward camp in the 645 battle and leads the conquest army against Goguryeo in 668. In 645 he holds a position intended to draw the enemy in. To hold, he must know his own strength and when [Zhangsun Wuji](049-zhangsun-wuji.md#zhangsun-wuji)’s separate force will enter. The commanders coordinating reports about what they must await, rather than praising one another in identical words, is an invented consultation. Their deployment at Jupilsan rests on the Samguk Sagi record.

**Established facts**

The invented Li Shiji is 172 centimeters tall, with lean shoulders, long fingers, and a long square face. He has brown eyes, sparse black eyebrows, a long nose, small mouth, and sun-darkened tawny skin. His black hair is tied and his beard has white strands from 645, more gray by 668.

He wears black clothes, a dark reddish-brown sash, and sparsely decorated armor. He makes small hand movements; his voice is low, dry, and rough. Reports are brief, and before deciding action he asks what conditions will let his unit hold.

When again tasked with conquering Goguryeo in 668, he has experience from the earlier war. He uses cooperation and division among regional powers around routes and fortresses rather than assuming the entire country suddenly weakened. Information and troops provided by Namsaeng expand what Li can actually accomplish.
