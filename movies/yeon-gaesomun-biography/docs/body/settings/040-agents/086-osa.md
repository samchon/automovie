# Osa’s gate shifts

Canon guide: [Location of this subject](../outline/index.md#040-agents--086-osa).

## Osa’s gate shifts {#osa}

**Definition and scope**

Osa is a junior commander who cooperates with Tang from inside alongside Sinseong. The work makes him a subordinate commander able to relay orders for night shifts and troops entering and leaving Pyongyang’s west postern. This gate and area are invented; do not write as if the historical record names the gate opened. Sinseong’s secret letter does not automatically open every gate: people must actually direct shifts and entry.

**Established facts**

The invented Osa is 169 centimeters tall, with a short neck, thick hands, rounded square face, small brown eyes, short black eyebrows, low nose, broad mouth, and sun-darkened tawny skin. He ties his black hair and wears a short beard. He wears lamellar armor over dark brown clothes and a black sash. His voice is low, thick, and rough. He repeats short orders only to nearby soldiers. Do not infer a birth year or other exploits from the record. In 668 he is an adult commander responsible for orders to his troops.

He cannot represent all Goguryeo nobles and regions. His invented motive for cooperating weighs the treatment he may gain by opening the gate against the danger of staying to fight. He is not certain he can protect every resident after Tang enters. His access authority depends on [Yomyo](087-yomyo.md#yomyo)’s control of the approach and Sinseong’s command. [The fall of Pyongyang](../050-systems/340-pyongyang-fall.md#pyongyang-fall) owns the actual execution constraints leading from shifts to opening the gate.
