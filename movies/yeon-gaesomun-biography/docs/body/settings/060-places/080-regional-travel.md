## Travel time between Gungnaeseong and Liaodong Fortress {#regional-travel}

**Definition and scope**

The work's baseline for Namsaeng's journey between the two centers, with attendants and baggage, is nine days one way.

**Established facts**

In winter it rises to twelve days; an urgent messenger with remounts takes four days one way. These are chosen times, not precise measurements of ancient roads, intended to prevent travel from being reduced to a single day for a scene's convenience. Days spent in refugee villages outside a fortress are added separately. See [the second war](../outline/index.md#050-systems) and [the two coups](../outline/index.md#050-systems) for the conditions at the Yalu front in 661 and the submission at Gungnaeseong in 666, respectively.
