## The Gungnaeseong living region {#gungnae}

**Definition and scope**

Gungnaeseong is the living region where Namsaeng gathers support gained on the common front and in reconstruction and enters to escape central suppression in 666. Do not treat it as the same place as Liaodong Fortress or a single headquarters of one faction. The work’s scene area has a five-meter-wide road running 100 meters north from the south gate by the river to a provisions assembly yard. The yard is 30 by 40 meters, with two storehouses east and an alley to noble residences west. A noble house’s courtyard requires a separate gate from that alley; villagers outside the fortress cannot enter directly. Military service, marriage, and protection ties bridge that distance. This partial plan is an invented layout, not an excavation survey of the whole fortress.

**Established facts**

The house where Namsaeng meets is a noble residence about 200 meters west of the yard. Its hall is 8 by 5 meters; daylight enters the south door and commanders report from a low place to the north. Beyond the courtyard, yard orders and carts can be heard, but low conversation in the hall is indistinguishable with the door shut. Light gray-brown stone and earth of walls and storehouses mix with dark brown timber and dark tiles. The provisions assembly place and residents’ fields do not all fit in one yard within the walls.

The meeting hall has one story, three meters from floor to beneath the beam, eaves at 3.3 meters, and an east–west ridge at 4.8 meters. Its dark tiled roof slopes north and south and projects 0.6 meters beyond each wall. Beams and pillars are visible inside, but the space above is not a second meeting room. Each of the two storehouses is one story, with 2.8 meters beneath the beam, three-meter eaves, and a 4.5-meter east–west ridge. These heights are invented and preserve separate access between storehouses and residence and the south door for receiving reports.
