In 677 Tang sends King Bojang back to Liaodong and orders the return of residents earlier moved into the interior. Unable to govern the region with conquest's military force alone, it chooses to use Goguryeo's name and royal relationships again. Bojang sees the same symbol as an opening to move his country and region again. Do not close with stability restored because Tang and the king have identical purposes.

The return order is not an event in which every relocated household recovers its old home on the same day. Do not establish that all four of Haeun, Janggeun, Seou, and Chamu returned. The last confirmed life for this family is the livelihood they maintain together in Henan. Distinguish the wider view of an order from the household's present, which that order has not restored, so their movement and loss in 669 are not canceled.

Do not bring the king's later plotting with Malgal, recall and exile, or death forward before 679. The change here is that limits on Tang's direct rule cause it to summon old relationships again, and the intentions of the royal house and region relying on those relationships are not finished. In the same year Namsaeng receives a task to administer that land again under Tang authority.

## Namsaeng administers for Tang the region he meant to restore {#namsaeng-administers-the-land-he-meant-to-restore}

By imperial order in 677, Namsaeng undertakes pacification of Liaodong and organization of its prefectures and counties. Do not assert that this was the date of a new appointment as grand governor of Liaodong. In the past he connected people and roads so residents could prepare the next sowing and defense; now he is tasked with moving those people and regions again for the conqueror's administration. His ability to carry out reconstruction remains, but whose authority he exercises and what he restores have changed.

He receives reports that local cooperation does not return easily and that labor demands generate resentment. Do not summon Sokro's eastern community or Seokbu to a particular residence in Liaodong for an invented private conversation. Reports tell Namsaeng that former supporters no longer receive his orders with the same meaning. The audience has already seen their actual lives, so it need not understand the community's judgment through one report to him.

Namsaeng acknowledges that Tang office and duties cannot restore the Goguryeo he meant to create. He comes to recognize that the roads and command relationships he surrendered contributed to deaths and forced migration, and that he cannot reduce responsibility by saying he aimed only at revenge on the regime. Even so, he cannot forgive Namgeon and the royal house for causing Heonchung's death. Continuing the administrative work he can do and resolving his responsibility for the lost country never coincide, leading to his sickbed in 679.

## Namsaeng dies, leaving Heonseong's life unresolved {#death-leaves-heonseong-living}

In 679, ill at the Andong Prefecture residence, Namsaeng receives remaining reports and work but does not regain command of old Goguryeo. Heonseong is beside him. The resting room and office quarters are separate; Heonchung's coffin or remains are not moved beside the sickbed. Memory of the dead son, care from the living son, and Tang duties still arriving share his final life.

Before Heonseong, Namsaeng acknowledges responsibility for contributing to the country's collapse. Do not wash it away with one repentance or turn it into forgiveness for Heonchung's death. Heonseong, too, supported his father's defection and acted as envoy, so he cannot remain only a comforter. He attends his father's end while sharing the burden left by the choice made to survive. Neither can absolve the other.

Namsaeng dies on the twenty-ninth day of the first lunar month. The final actual event of the main work is this death and Heonseong alive there. Without erasing Gaesomun's victory defending the country or the possibility of reconstruction Namsaeng prepared, it closes with the son's responsibility for admitting a foreign army while knowing it would harm the defense. Heonseong has a life still to bear, and the communities remain where they have already chosen their own paths. End here without leaping to Balhae's success or forgiveness after death.
