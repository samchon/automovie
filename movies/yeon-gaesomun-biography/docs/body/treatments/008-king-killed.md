In 642 Gaesomun confirms that a plot to kill him has leaked and acts first. He does not believe simple submission will buy safety and rejects withdrawing only his troops while leaving the king. He gathers over a hundred opposing nobles at a review and meeting south of Pyongyang, attacks them, and secures palace approaches with his force ([fixed course of the coup](../settings/050-systems/240-642-coup.md#642-coup)). Do not invent names of individual plotters or a separate heroic story for the source of the leak.

Only after the nobles’ killing does the audience learn Geonmu has been captured. Gaesomun’s speed made a way to survive, but this is no lawful replacement under a royal order. His ability to gather people and secure approaches rests on cooperation and authority already gained; now it is used to kill people who helped defend the country. He chooses not to avoid facing the king.

In a [palace private room](../settings/060-places/010-pyeongyang-palace.md#pyeongyang-palace), Geonmu does not ask for his life. He argues from experience: Pyeongwon and Yeongyang reconnected families and eastern communities, enabling resistance to Sui; Sui returned despite defeat, leaving him recovery after victory. Families who survived the earlier civil war and relied on kin on Silla’s frontier, and families who followed southern trade and marriage ties to Baekje, are different examples still not returned. A royal declaration that turmoil is settled is not the same as reversing a family’s choice to stay in a new country to survive ([Silla families](../settings/040-agents/091-silla-exiles.md#silla-exiles), [Baekje families](../settings/040-agents/092-baekje-exiles.md#baekje-exiles)). More time was needed to maintain cooperation than for one military feat. To the charge that he should have foreseen Tang’s rapid unification, he answers with uncertainty at the time. Gaesomun argues the country must first show strength to survive. The king leaves the thought that Gaesomun will understand him when he occupies the highest position ([final defense](../settings/040-agents/015-geonmu.md#geonmu-last-defence)). Exact dialogue is not fixed here.

Gaesomun weeps at killing the person he once admired most, but does not change his decision. Do not make the king approve having been understood, or Gaesomun kill under pressure without conviction that it is necessary. He kills the king and installs Bojang. The audience follows a protagonist who has saved himself and bears responsibility for regicide. Do not show corpse mutilation as a settled scene. The historian’s necessary distance distinguishes the hostile record from the work’s chosen interpretation; do not end the death with a joke.
