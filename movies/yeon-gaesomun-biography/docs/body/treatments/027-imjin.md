Around the fifteenth day of the second lunar month, Silla's main body reaches the north bank of the Imjin River. While the leading carriers and injured cross to the south bank, the escort faces the pursuing Goguryeo force with its back to the river. Water and wind prevent spoken orders from reaching both formations immediately, so drums, flags, and nearby messengers coordinate the sequence ([the crossing front](../settings/060-places/180-chiljung.md#chiljung)). Chano must cross too, but has less and less strength to carry both Gwidol's knife and himself.

Kim Yusin faces two dangers: if everyone crosses first, the rear guard is isolated; if everyone remains, the whole column is trapped on the riverbank. He keeps the crossing going while the escort holds the front. The Goguryeo pursuers do not emerge from a newly invented forest or hidden crossing; they press from the north, targeting Silla's narrow waiting area.

The next day, Kim Yusin leaves a north-bank escort to support the crossing and sends only part of it along the western route. Before departure, he assigns the escort remaining by the river to counter at close range and hold the leading Goguryeo formation if it tries to pull away, without abandoning the crossing to pursue it to the end. The western escort moves north along the western edge of the Goguryeo assembly area. A Goguryeo flank observer sees and reports this movement.

The pursuers judge that if they lift the pressure on the riverbank and withdraw together to the northern assembly area, more Silla troops will cross in the gap. They decide to maintain pressure while pulling part of the front back to meet the western approach. As that formation tries to break contact, the Silla riverbank escort advances briefly and holds it; the escort that went west attacks from the northwest toward the southeast of the assembly area. The leading formation cannot pull back, and the troops at the assembly area take the western blow before they can receive it and form a single line. This is not an army ignoring a report: two real attacks combine with its choice to keep blocking the crossing, leaving front and flank unable to support one another.

Kim Yusin does not pursue the divided enemy to destruction. He returns the escort that moved west to the riverbank by the same route to support the remaining crossing. The brief north-bank counterattack and return follow duties assigned before departure; do not make a spoken order cross the river instantly to move two forces. He does not abandon the whole rear guard for the flank attack.

The Goguryeo pursuers are defeated, and Silla's main body gains a way home. The work's 2,000 killed, seriously wounded, or captured are included in Goguryeo's 15,000 losses on other fronts. The historian distinguishes the Silla record's 10,000 beheaded and 5,000 captured as the victor's claimed result. Namsaeng's Yalu force and the central army at Sasu have not suffered another defeat in this battle. Kim Yusin's return closes as a military achievement separate from the delivery; the remaining contest is at Pyongyang.
