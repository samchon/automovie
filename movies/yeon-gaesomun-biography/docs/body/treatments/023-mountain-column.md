# A path for one person and a road for provisions

After the twenty-third day of the first lunar month, Kim Yusin's column takes roughly two weeks to travel the difficult 240-kilometer detour from the Chiljung River to Yango. The advance party checks the next passage, the transport teams move in groups, and the rear guard gathers those falling behind. At narrow sections, carts are unloaded, people and animals carry the loads across, and the loads are tied again on the far side ([the actual width of the difficult route](../settings/050-systems/140-supply-route.md#supply-route)). Kim Yusin does not simply send the first troops across onward; he leaves time for the grain behind them to pass.

Drawing on transport experience, Chano rebalances loads on either side; Gwidol shares the burden of an exhausted companion. Sacks soaked by snow and water grow heavier, and some carts and cargo that cannot be moved are abandoned. Rationing their own food to protect the grain for Tang harms the people carrying it. Kim Yusin chooses to feed them some of what is needed while managing a separate share to preserve the delivery target. Do not make a myth of discipline in which not a handful was touched.

At a [representative camp](../settings/060-places/210-winter-camp.md#winter-camp), only the roughly 2,000 people in Chano's section rest; the others are divided into areas ahead and behind. Moving baggage down to get water means carrying it up again, so they do not move everyone to the water. As Chano unwraps the cloth from his wet feet, Gwidol worries about his mother having to work the fields alone. Brief self-deprecating humor may surface, but a companion who has collapsed in the cold is not its target.

Kim Yusin separates those who will rest from the loads to be sent ahead, and leaves an escort for injured people returning. The pace behind and the food ahead diminish accordingly. Chano begins to lose sensation in some fingers and his feet, but has no authority to stop the whole column for himself, so takes a smaller load ([Chano's injuries](../settings/040-agents/074-chano.md#chano)). A commander's example does not heal frozen bodies. What allows Chano to continue is the strength others still have.

Goguryeo's watch receives reports of people moving along mountain paths. At first the leadership interprets them as small scouts or communications parties and does not redirect the central counterattack plan. The transport column has left the main road and is passing over several days; a single sighting cannot reveal the whole quantity. Only when later reports repeatedly add cargo, livestock, and road clearing does the scale of the supply transport become clear.

Within that information gap, Kim Yusin divides the duties of the delivery force and the main body returning home. He has Kim Inmun and Kim Yangdo confirm Tang's handover conditions, rather than sending the entire column into an unfamiliar perimeter at once. Even after the arduous mountain crossing, supplies become useful only when the actual recipient accepts and moves them. Yango is that final threshold.
