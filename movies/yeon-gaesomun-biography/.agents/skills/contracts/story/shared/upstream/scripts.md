# Upstream repair exposed by scripts

Each script H2 answers about its actual treatment parents and, through them, settings causes. Repair follows the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes).

## What did scene execution prove missing or unusable upstream? {#script-upstream-repair}

Identify exact treatment decisions about event, cause, knowledge, relationship, time, place, appearance, object, or inherited work constraint that are missing, contradictory, prematurely fixed, or impossible to execute. Trace a settings cause to its exact settings owner rather than bypass a missing treatment with a direct settings acknowledgment. Also inspect parent design that forces a named failure or blocks adopted goals and promises: the same recognition, lesson or debate assigned with the same force to several units; prewritten explanation, record or anticipation assigned to a narrator; a list of arguments a person must recite; a setup without a payoff or with its payoff prohibited; or a promised experience assigned nowhere. Continuity records of existing knowledge or state are not repeated assignments to enact it. Correct the exact treatment body yourself, tracing a settings cause to that settings body, before writing from the repair. The answer names the owner, defect and corrected passage; reporting it while covering it downstream fails. If no repair is needed, identify actual parent decisions executed unchanged and the concrete test they survived. Do not invent core events or canonical facts in scripts, and do not call an unexamined parent sound.
