# Final screenplay population obligations

The completed final screenplay population answers once at its current stage. Construction assesses the complete plan and provisional material and timing; delivery also assesses its actual realization. A script draft, partial animatic, or local shot success cannot answer for a completed delivery.

## Does the complete audiovisual plan realize its parents and delivery promise? {#complete-realization}

Map every script execution to final screenplay units in a long work, and every final unit to its actual parents. A short work authorized to begin here maps its actual brief requirements and screenplay-owned scene decisions without inventing skipped planning populations. Assess the complete audiovisual plan and sufficient material at screenplay construction, marking future source, performance, sound, edit, and output as pending where they do not yet exist. Connect those real descendants and observations as they become available and at delivery. Preserve identity, events, choice, consequence, state, relationship, knowledge, and reveal order while inspecting the new image-sound relationships, attention, duration, and transitions across the whole. A reference in code or a successful source check is not proof that the output realizes the screenplay. Record provisional performance, placeholders, partial scope, and the limits of actual observations. A future descendant does not block screenplay construction merely because it is unmade; an absent required part at delivery remains a gap.

At screenplay construction, report provisional timing, its basis, and the performance or editing measurements still needed to confirm it. Once assembled output exists and at delivery, measure that output and its real delivery units by the production's declared duration and format, distinguishing estimated script timing from recorded speech, performed action, edit timing, and observed playback. Report actual total duration and the effect of removing only repetitions actually judged redundant; functional refrains still count. Below-target duration alone is not a defect, but compressed required experience, omitted event assignment, or a poor partition may be. Report justified delivery ceilings and format constraints too. Repair the earliest cause without restoring removed padding, inventing events, or stretching sound to fill a target.

## Are revisions accountable to the delivered version? {#published-version}

For an existing public release, identify its exact version, changed scope, reason, semantic effect, and required notice across the release population. Distinguish spelling or metadata correction, display or rendering repair, factual error, semantic revision, and changes required by the applicable delivery conditions. Changed goals, relationships, or results require accountable version handling rather than an unnoticed replacement. Follow applicable platform correction requirements and align settings, treatments, scripts, screenplay, source, media, edit, and review with the new version. Audience reaction or a long release gap alone does not justify changing canon. If no public version exists, identify that population explicitly; future editability never excuses skipping review before delivery.
