## Does the Korean wording preserve the owned historical value? {#korean-values}

Keep the actual owner of a name, title, date, age convention, quantity, color, material, dimension, and unit. Distinguish elapsed years from counted age, lunar dates from a supported calendar conversion, claimed casualties from adopted losses, troops from households, and a capacity from a unit name. Write elapsed time as 「16년 전」. 租 in the winter supply account means grain tax; it does not establish millet 粟. Preserve the owned ranges of 「회갈색」 and 「옅은 황갈색」 rather than vivid yellow, pure white, or red ochre. Fluent wording preserves invention, inference, dispute, and uncertainty instead of silently making them verified facts.
