ride

ride

ParameterTypeMeaning
onstringThe moving prop ridden: its subject id in the performance.
secondsnumberSeconds ridden, more than none.
rising?number | objectA rising trot: the mount's stride time, seconds, or the cue track of the performance that holds its strides (the footfalls of one diagonal, as the mount's own ride publishes them). The rider stands in the stirrups and sits again once a stride, the seat rising `POST` thigh lengths off the saddle, so both feet must rest on the support. On a cue track the seat is down at each of its instants and seated before the first and after the last. Seated throughout when absent.
feet?{ left, right }The support's point each foot stays on, by its id, for a part that moves within the support: a pedal on its turning crank, a stirrup on a trotting horse (`IHumanProp.point`). The foot keeps the place it had on that point when the ride began. A foot not named, or a point that only moves with the support, is carried rigidly.
seat?stringThe support's point the seat stays on, by its id, where the seat itself moves within the support: a saddle on a horse's trunk, which rises and falls with the stride over the course its root follows. The body is carried with the support alone when absent.
hands?{ left, right }The support's point each hand holds, by its id: a handlebar grip, a rein, a wheel's rim. The hand goes to it over `TAKE` seconds from the ride's start and stays on it, following a part that moves within the support. The arms are still yielded, so an arm action over the ride takes a hand off for as long as it lasts. The hand turns to the point and closes on it (`HumanMountRider.hold`).
