reachHolster

ParameterTypeMeaning
site?object | object | object | objectWhere on the region the first hand lands (`touchSite`): an offset, a place the seed draws, or the place a rule chooses; the region's own point when absent.
prop?stringThe subject id of the prop that hand holds, whose point touches in place of the hand part: a cigarette's lit end tapped on an ashtray, a pen's tip on a page. No prop when absent.
point?stringThe held prop's point that touches, one of its affordance ids; required with `prop`.
grasp?{ handle, line }With `prop` on a region of the performer's own, the grasp the tool was taken up by for its use (`pickUp`'s `handle` and `use.line`): the stroke holds the tool as that grasp presents it at the region (`HumanGraspUse.presented`, the region as the use's place) and moves only its point, as a razor keeps its angle on the jaw. Absent, the prop's point faces into the region.
clear?numberA miss staged as a touch: the effector passes the region this far off its surface along its normal, in the hand's lengths, at the stroke's full speed, and no contact is declared (a stage slap). A touch that lands when absent.
elbow?"level" | "natural"Where the elbow lies while the hand is on the region: `natural`, the place of least strain, or `level`, raised to the shoulder's height so the upper arm is horizontal, as a salute or a hand shading the eyes holds it. `natural` when absent.
other?object | object | object | object | objectThe other hand's region, touched the same way at the same time; one hand when absent.
rests?object[]The arms' segments that lie on the performer's own body while the hands are on their regions, as a self-hug's forearms lie on each other and on the chest (`AutoMovieMotionHumanTouchContacts.sided`); none when absent.
count?numberRepetitions; 3 for a tap, a pat, a rub or a scratch and 1 otherwise when absent.
tangent?"across" | "up"The travel's direction on the region; when absent, `up`, or `across` for a hand on the performer's own region whose travel up and down would sink it into another part of the body (`touchHold.lay`).
manner?{ effort, intensity, pace, proficiency, variation }How it is performed.

surrender

ParameterTypeMeaning
side?"left" | "right"The hand of a one-hand gesture; `right` when absent.
target?object | object | object | object | objectWhat to point at; required by `point`.
region?"chin" | "earLeft" | "earRight" | "forehead" | "mouth" | "nape" | "noseBridge" | "templeLeft" | "templeRight"The face region a `touchFace` reaches; `forehead` when absent.
count?integer from 1Stroke repetitions (waves, nods, claps); the gesture's own count when absent.
depth?numberA bow's depth, degrees in (0, 60], refused outside that range only for a bow, so it carries no schema bound; 30 when absent.
effort?{ weight, time, space, flow }Movement quality.
turn?numberDegrees the moving hands are turned in the plane of their own palms from the gesture's attitude, the thumb's side rising for a positive turn (provisional sign, held by `test_human_motion_emblem_thumbs`): a quarter turn stands a held-out fist's thumb up, the opposite one points it down. No turn when absent.
shape?"count1" | "count2" | "count3" | "count4" | "count5" | "crossed" | "ok" | "quotes" | "snap" | "thumbUp" | "victory"A hand shape with a meaning of its own, taken by the moving hands in place of the gesture's working shape: a thumbs up, an OK ring, a V, a count; the gesture's own shape when absent.
aim?{ digit, toward }A digit of `shape` aimed straight at the world's up or down: the moving hands turn by the least rotation from the gesture's attitude, after `turn`, that points the digit's own direction (`HumanHandShape.digit`) there, since a straight digit does not lie along the hand's axes (a thumbs up's thumb stands about 35 degrees off the knuckle line). Requires `shape`; no aim when absent.
manner?{ proficiency, variation }How much repeats of the gesture differ: `variation` in [0, 1] scales the spread of the preparation's length and of where a `wave` or a `stopPalm` holds the hand within its named place, and `proficiency` replaces the performer's skill for those spreads. Absent or 0, every repeat is the same.

Used in Standoff. Try it alone
