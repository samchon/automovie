# Citizen house spatial preservation contract

**Status:** production decision, a local contract for the fixed spatial graph of the user's authoring brief.

## Citizen house spatial preservation {#citizen-house-spatial-requirements}

One 11×12m rectangular main house has two storeys. The ground-floor entry has one central dog-leg stair, the continuous living, dining, and kitchen at the rear, the directly connected flex workroom at the front, and the opaque service core on the right. On the upper floor, a short straight corridor starting from the stair landing connects directly, through actual doors, the primary bedroom, the two small bedrooms, the bathroom, and storage.

The source produces a result in which every room belongs to a storey, every required room is connected by a door, the corridor, and the single stair, and the curtainwall bays align with the floor lines and room boundaries. A result that introduces a courtyard, annex, bridge, cantilever, double-height living void, second stair, branch corridor, or disconnected room to resemble the exterior references fails this contract. The floor opening needed for the single stair's inter-storey passage is not included in the forbidden double-height living void.

Privacy is expressed with electrochromic and translucent glass and exterior shading, and a glass surface is not replaced with an image texture or billboard. This contract does not certify the actual building's structural, energy, or barrier-free performance, and items without such measurement remain `unverified`.

Review question: do the spaces, boundaries, openings, and connections of the compiled environment preserve this fixed graph and the curtainwall/privacy relation at the same time?
