The envelope of [house](001-citizen-house.md#citizen-house-space) is five complete surfaces: the [front](#front-face), [rear](#rear-face), [left](#left-face), [right](#right-face), and [roof](#roof-face). Each face has one owner that owns the stable addresses of all its structural returns, openings, frames, shades, and exposed finish geometry. The finish binding of those addresses is decided by materials, and the elevation owner carries the decided id. The author and source file follow the [decomposition declaration](../settings/003-spatial-basis.md#surface-decomposition). An opening H2 is an address inside the same elevation and does not split the author.

The [mass and storey datum](002-spatial-graph.md#mass-and-storeys) owns the perimeter, storey lines, and thickness. The elevation joins the interior clear face and the exterior face within that exterior wall thickness. The exterior corners meet at the diagonal division of the [corner junction](#envelope-corners), and the inter-storey spandrel follows the corresponding floor line. The storey owner makes the final horizontal interior floor/ceiling faces and the interior structural wall bodies, and the elevation owner closes the exterior walls and glazing assemblies. Each room owner handles only the interior finish of the exterior wall and does not generate a final face such as the glass a second time. The front exterior wall in front of the roomless [stair void](002-spatial-graph.md#stair-enclosure) has no room lining, so the front elevation owner handles its interior face too.

The enclosing space of the four whole elevation boundaries is house. The room/storey that each opening faces is compared by the corresponding opening H2 with the actual cell position. Unlike an interior shared boundary, two different rooms are not listed in the spaces list of one exterior face. The vertical extent of an elevation is from the ground floor to the upper ceiling, and the floor band in between is closed by the same elevation. Below it, the outer face from the site ground y=-0.45 to the ground floor is closed by the foundation plinth of the [ground-floor owner](002-spatial-graph.md#ground-level) on the same outer plane as the elevation, and the site owner's ground meets that plinth at y=-0.45. Below the clear width of the [entrance](002-spatial-graph.md#front-entry), the [threshold plate](002-spatial-graph.md#door-interface) fills -0.016..0 directly below the ground floor, and that threshold plate is made together by the front elevation owner, which cuts the door opening. From the upper ceiling above the elevation, the structure of the [roof owner](#roof-face) closes as far as the exterior wall section. No unowned face or gap with the ground therefore remains at any height of the main house's outline.

The opaque service wall, metal frames, glass, and shading distinguished by the [visual grammar](../settings/001-production.md#production-visual-grammar) have different actual occupancies within the same perimeter. Only that spatial boundary is set here, and the implementation of colour, optics, and light consumes this canon in later source.

The actual boundary normal, thickness, junction lines, opening profile, and floor line agreement of each face are checked in the [full spatial observation](001-citizen-house.md#spatial-observation). For the -0.45..0 band below the elevations, the same output is read for whether the actual bounds of the foundation, bearing ring, and threshold plate continue without an empty section along the full length of the four outer planes and whether the site ground and walking surfaces end at the outline. The envelope design verdict of v-096 is PASS, and the material binding and new GPU exterior observation of the current tree are unverified.
