The texture families are limestone-grain, cassette-grain, seal-grain, oak-grain, paint-grain, felt-grain, woven-grain, screen-grain, tile-grain, worktop-grain, and the retained canopy-pv-cell. All are deterministic RGBA set by the source, and no photograph or reference image is used. screen-grain is an independent asset that separates out the problem of using the low-luminance woven-grain with a double tint. The new cassette and seal bindings use surface-metres and repeat, and the application range of the other textures is set by each finish H2.

The two finishes meet at the actual depth and joints of the stone panel and wall body and the metal cassette and seal outside them, and the stone hidden by the metal plate is not repainted. The nominal surface layers are interior paint .15mm, cassette and coated-metal film .08mm, joinery film .1mm, floor and stair clear coat .06mm, door and storage veneer .6mm + clear .06mm, furniture veneer .6mm, glaze .5mm, textile .001m, and fibre .003m, and stone, glass, metal, tile, seats, and screen fabric are the material of the geometry itself without a layer. On a shared wall, the lining of each room receives its own finish, and at a threshold the oak plate meets the tile floor. Junction exposed faces take the wall finish of the room they face, and the same face is not assigned to two finishes.
