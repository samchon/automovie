The architectural geometry of the delivered house, rooms, doors, stair, envelope, and site and the repetition of architectural modules are the target of spaces design and spaceSources implementation. Within the whole interior, the reusable shapes and part/face addresses of furniture, services, and props are decided by models, per-room placement and object repetition by instances, the emission process by systems, and the finish binding of architectural and object faces by materials, and each source branch implements them. This division consumes the [settings coverage map](../../settings/001-production.md#settings-coverage-map) and [Surface decomposition](../../settings/003-spatial-basis.md#surface-decomposition). The objects of the current room sources are a temporary path preserved until the later source review, not permanent object ownership of spaces/spaceSources. No separate character, film, photoreal service, or externally downloaded asset is added. A result the public engine cannot interpret remains an open problem of the source, and the coordinator handles package installation and server start.

Every settings obligation is decided in the actual H2s linked above. This account is not a second owner of room dimensions, object placement, or apparatus output. The architectural source and the WebGL2 viewer are implemented and were used for the observations of independent verdicts, but the later object handover, material implementation, and full observation are not complete. The GPU result of the new tree, the safety of actual people, and the apparatus performance are not verified by this declaration alone, and approval of the whole production remains open.
