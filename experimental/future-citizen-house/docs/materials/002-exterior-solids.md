The finish `limestone-honed` is the bright matte stone of the envelope. The geometry of the existing 0.016m panels and the wall bodies behind them is kept. Each envelope owner assigns the per-elevation `*-stone-panels` and the corresponding wall bodies, corner prisms, and under-window `*-drip` stone, and the roof owner assigns `roof-slab` (the outer edge face y=6.108..6.40 and the remaining faces buried behind roof-weather and the ceiling) and the exterior exposed faces of the `roof-bearing-*` ring that closes the exterior wall section below it (including the outer face 6.10..6.108). The final waterproof face `roof-weather`, down to its edge faces, is a [retained role](006-wet-and-joinery.md#retained-surfaces). The panels and wall bodies behind the metal cassettes of the [floor band](#opaque-floor-band) also keep this stone finish. In addition, the faces exposed to the stair void, namely the two bands of the interior face of the front wall body (3.184..3.25 below the window cut and 6.04..6.10 above the head at x=-1.24..1.58), the interior end face of `front-stair-glazing-upper-drip`, which projects 1mm beyond the body face at 3.25..3.28 between them, and the underside of `roof-bearing-front` visible as a 6mm strip above the band over the head (z=-5.766..-5.76, y=6.10), are assigned by each owner to [interior paint](005-soft-finishes.md#plaster-paint). It is not assigned to the site paving or interior tiles. Of the face tangent axes of the [metric coordinates](001-binding-and-scale.md#metric-texture-coordinates), the elevation's local horizontal is used as U and the vertical as V.

The rule of bright achromatic exterior walls and not warming white walls with paint comes from the [visual grammar](../settings/001-production.md#production-visual-grammar), and the bright stone-family opaque faces from [Envelope and openings](../settings/003-spatial-basis.md#envelope-and-privacy). The colour, roughness, and grain values below are this layer's authoring choice. The colour is #c9c3b5, roughness=.82. `limestone-grain` is 512², repeating every 0.64×0.64m. It is made as a linear achromatic texture to multiply with the base colour, with a mean of .98 and a range of .94..1.00, mixing 2..8mm grains and gentle 40..100mm cloud patterns. There are no sharp black dots, brick lines, or fake cracks. The base colour variation of separate members allows only the same sRGB factor of .98..1.02 on each RGB channel. Light and shadow are made by geometry and lighting.

Actual panel gaps and opening recesses are not covered with texture. The front/rear corners are the edges where the projections of each actual face meet and are not disguised as one huge seamless piece of stone. [Exterior and junction checks](007-observation.md#reference-material-samples) confirm the bright panel character and low gloss of ref01, the corners and ground/upper joints, and the reading of front and right as the same material. The close-up grain and distant mean colour, the joint of the elevation top end with roof-slab and the bearing ring, and the joint of the bottom end with the foundation bearing ring are viewed separately in the [distance and junction checks](007-observation.md#scale-and-junction-samples).
