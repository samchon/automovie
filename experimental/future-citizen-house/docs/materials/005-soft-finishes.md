A separate `screen-grain` 256² at .064×.064m with the same 1.5..2mm weave period is bound, lowering the linear variation width to about .96..1.00. It follows the vertical V of the shade panel. The existing roller states, heights, counts, and actual screened area stay owned by the glazing owner.

The shared woven-grain carries .89..1.00 modulation to the furniture textiles, so without a shader the contrast of the screen alone could not be lowered. This H2 separates a screen-only colour resource. The amplitude of the same weave waveform is lowered to 1.4 and the low-frequency fleck amplitude to .6, and the mean encoded value is raised to 253/255. The 8-bit quantized minimum and maximum linear values are confirmed by a full measurement of the resource. The existing PNG and the furniture textile resource are preserved.

No transparent holes are punched in the texture and no exterior louvres are drawn. Only the reading of the cloth is improved, and it does not replace the later design of the glass states and shading layout. The [state check](007-observation.md#material-state-samples) looks at the raised/lowered range of each actual screen and its distinction from the glass. The additional performance that the screening of ref03 and 05 requires is before a separate verdict, and the light transmission performance of the weave is unverified.
