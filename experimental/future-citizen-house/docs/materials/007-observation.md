The check input is the actual models, members, materials, and UVs made by the `buildHouse()` return of the current state and the public lowering/instanceSlot. For each role of the [binding interface](001-binding-and-scale.md#surface-bindings), it enumerates the source owner, element or set/member, part/face, material ID, texture ID, physical tile, and UV basis. Members divided per face are written per face: the 16 exposed faces of the 12 junction solids and the space and finish each faces, the number of hidden faces, the four upper slab pieces' cut faces toward the stair hole, the top of the front strip, and the remaining faces including the faces where pieces touch, the two interior bands that the front exterior wall body exposes to the stair void and the remaining body faces, the cassette plates, returns, slot strips, and seals of the five floor bands, the separate metal clips and steel anchors and the limestone panels and wall bodies behind them, the threshold plates filling the host wall thickness at each clear opening (11 including the entry-common opening), the outer faces and remaining faces of roof-slab and roof-bearing, and the end faces of the upper drip and the underside of roof-bearing-front in the stair void. For wood, the number of faces that received a texture and the number of untextured [end faces](004-wood.md#wood-end-faces) are counted separately. Retained roles are also written per role named by the [retention H2](006-wet-and-joinery.md#retained-surfaces) and not grouped into one catch-all line. Roles with no application also keep 0, and missing or duplicate roles, unsupported channels, and wrong colour spaces are reported as failures. Undecided assignments are not replaced with plaster or retained.

What is compared with the existing state is the house/storey/room/connector/opening addresses and every actual member ID, count, transform, world bounds, and ownership relation. These addresses come from the citizen-site→house→storey→room hierarchy and house-root visible root of the [citizen house space](../spaces/001-citizen-house.md#citizen-house-space) and the questions of [stage 1 verification](../spaces/002-spatial-graph.md#stage-one-verification), and the check input from the public `@automovie/engine` API. The enumeration items and failure classification are this layer's choice. The new model variant count must be explained by the original prototype/finish/scale. For each member, the prototype and modelRecipe returned by instanceSlot are written, so a path that turned the native Error of a missing explicit prototype into a default model or an empty population is exposed. The native model variant count, the texture resource count, and the RGBA bytes are recorded together, and it is compared whether the models and textures of the viewer payload pass the same native material and texture records as `buildHouse()` to the uploader. Topology and canopy audit errors and invalid texture bindings are not hidden. At the implementation stage, `sourceBasis` must include docs/materials and the new material source as current inputs, and an old payload is not cited as verification of the new documents.

The physical layers distinguish the member ownership documents/geometry from the nominal surface layer choice, and the optical values are read from the native material. The retained texture assets of the cassette and seal are also checked together with their native binding. The census returned by the source is read together with the actual frames below, and success of the text check is not read as a visual pass. Unimplemented and unmeasured scopes remain unverified.

For every new texture family, one face of the minimum and one of the maximum size of the current applied faces and each UV direction are selected. The basis is the actual native area and extent, with ties broken by ID in lexicographic order. The same source geometry is viewed at 1m, 3m, and 12m distances, frontally and obliquely at 30° to the tangent. In a narrow actual room the camera shoots only up to the maximum distance that does not leave its own space, and the impossible distances and reasons are recorded. The remaining distance samples use the same models, finishes, and placement in an inspection-only isolated scene and do not replace the actual room check.

The neutral check places an sRGB #808080 background, a white hemisphere close to D65 at intensity=1, and a white directional at intensity=2 at a 45° position from the local surface normal. Tone mapping is the same as the existing viewer, fixed at exposure=1, 1600×1000/DPR1, and the same camera FOV50. A 1m solid-colour reference bar is placed in inspection mode only and shot together with the texture period. The existing illumination/PMREM of the production beauty is not changed. The luminance units and this scene are not measured lighting certification.

The required junctions are: the stone corner prisms of the front/right corners, the elevation top end with the roof slab and bearing ring, the elevation bottom end with the foundation bearing ring, the top and bottom, outer frame line, actual panel joints, slots, seals, and junction under the stone drip of the five floor cassette bands and their depth separation from the stone panels behind, whether the slab outer edge comes out to the cladding plane, the two painted junction faces of the common area front wall and the junction face of the bathroom's east tile wall (the -X face at x=-3.02, z=2.40..2.58 visible from `upper-bathroom/corner-1`), the continuity of the stair hole's lower ceiling end, slab cut face, stair-lining, and edge junctions and the front strip top, the two exterior wall interior bands, the upper drip end face, and the roof-bearing underside, the door leaf/jamb/head, the continuation of the first stair step, turning landing, and last step with the storey floors and the gloss difference of the railing and stringers from the wood, the solid board end faces at the stair side ends, landing ends, and corridor arrival, the oak threshold plates and tile floors, the dining table top edge and leg ends, the worktop/edge/sink, the flat/curved seams of the sofa and the lower poles and rear seams of the pillows, and the boundaries of two repeat periods of each texture. The member ID and surface normal direction of each junction are recorded. Grain stretching, flipped faces, double tint, conspicuous tile boundaries, moiré, and fake geometry readings are failures.

The native function is used as `import { validateTextureScale } from "@automovie/engine"`. The [implementation module](../../../../packages/engine/src/validation/validateTextureScale.ts) is published from the [validation index](../../../../packages/engine/src/validation/index.ts) and the [engine root index](../../../../packages/engine/src/index.ts). It is not a contract to import `@automovie/engine/validation` or internal repository files. The input is `{ models: readonly IAutoMovieModel[] }` and the return is `IAutoMovieValidation`, both of which are root exports of `@automovie/interface`. The input is the current `environment.models` made by `buildHouse()`, in their original order. Texture-bearing variants must already have a primary UV reflecting the actual scale. This function does not modify models or UVs and neither reads nor performs placement/part transforms, tessellation, or image files.

The return of [IAutoMovieValidation](../../../../packages/interface/src/validation/IAutoMovieValidation.ts) is `{ success: true; warnings?: IAutoMovieConstraintViolation[] }` or `{ success: false; violations: IAutoMovieConstraintViolation[] }`. The warnings of a success exist only when they occur. The violations of a failure keep errors and warnings together. Each [IAutoMovieConstraintViolation](../../../../packages/interface/src/validation/IAutoMovieConstraintViolation.ts) has `kind`, `severity: "error" | "warning"`, `path`, `expected`, `value`, and optional `overshoot` and `node`. This function's path is `$input.models[i].parts[j].material.<slot>.<u|v>`, and the position is interpreted by recording the input order together with the model/part IDs. It is not assumed to return separate `errors`, `findings`, or `checkableCount` fields or an output file.

The function measures the finite, positive U/V span of a mesh. If a structured texture binding with `coordinateSource: "normalized"` has span > 1 + 1e-9, it returns `kind: "type"` with severity error. For `surface-metres`, if the tile=1/abs(scale) of an axis with a finite non-zero transform scale is greater than span×(1+1e-9), it is `kind: "range"` with severity warning. If that axis's sampler is clamp, this tile warning is not issued. Primitives, missing UVs, non-finite or degenerate spans, missing or unresolved materials, string bindings, an omitted coordinateSource, and `source-uv` are not verified by this measurement. A surface-metres axis without a scale, or with a 0 or non-finite scale, is not checked either, so an error of the separate binding check is not turned into success. The severity and original text of these native errors and warnings are preserved, and no failure or visual pass is made on the ground that there are only warnings.

The production call owner to implement in materialSources is `auditMaterialTextureScale` of `src/materials/observation.ts`, which imports the engine function and interface types above from the package root. The input is `{ models: readonly IAutoMovieModel[] }`, and the return format is `MaterialTextureScaleAudit` below. This wrapper passes the same models to the native function once and puts the result into `validation` as it is. It separately tallies only the check scope that native does not return and does not replace measurements.

```typescript
type MaterialTextureScaleAudit = {
  validation: IAutoMovieValidation;
  models: number;
  parts: number;
  partsWithoutTexture: number;
  axes: {
    path: string;
    model: string;
    part: string;
    slot: "baseColorTexture" | "metallicRoughnessTexture" |
      "normalTexture" | "occlusionTexture" | "emissiveTexture";
    axis: "u" | "v";
    status: "checked" | "clamped" | "unverified";
    reason: string;
  }[];
};
```

axes enumerates every existing texture binding in input model/part order, the slot order written in the schema, and u/v order. null/undefined slots are left out of the binding count, but parts with no texture are counted in `partsWithoutTexture`. If the material is null or unresolved, that part is also included in that count, and the material problem stays in the [binding check](#binding-census). `checked` is an axis on which the native comparison above can run, `clamped` an axis with a valid scale and span in surface-metres but whose tile comparison is skipped because of clamp, and the rest are `unverified`. reason is exactly one of `normalized-span`, `surface-metres-tile`, `clamp-fit`, `primitive`, `missing-uv`, `invalid-or-degenerate-uv`, `string-binding`, `missing-coordinate-source`, `source-uv`, `invalid-or-missing-scale`, the first condition that actually applies. The priority of unchecked reasons is geometry → UV → binding format → coordinateSource → scale → clamp. Per-condition counts are tallied from axes, and the checkable count is the count of `checked`. A result with 0 input models/parts or 0 checkable is not read as passing the whole check. The fit of clamped faces and the untextured faces each remain targets of junction and gloss observation.

[src/house/build.ts](../../src/house/build.ts) calls this wrapper after the model generation of every surface owner and the existing house/canopy audits. The model array of the returned environment and the check input are the same. The existing canopy report argument is kept, and the same result is passed through a third optional `materialReport: (audit: MaterialTextureScaleAudit) => void` callback. The check runs regardless of whether there is a callback, and after passing the original result to the callback, if native success=false it throws an `Error` whose message contains the path/expected of every violation, preserving the original audit as the cause. An unexpected API exception is not replaced with success either. [src/viewer/payload.ts](../../src/viewer/payload.ts) does not rerun the wrapper separately and passes the callback result of the same build call as the `materialTextureScaleAudit` field. The library's `citizenHouseSpaceSource` therefore runs the same check too. This schema, callback, and the calling files are verified together in the actual source.

The difference between the original unit mesh and the actual scale is confirmed by a separate world-space U/V length comparison. The native span check does not certify the equidistance of actual lengths, UV seams, curved poles, or the material's visual pass, and its result is read together with the distance and junction samples above. The README's `npm run lint` is the canonical source/evidence check, and it is not claimed that this static command also ran the geometry measurement. No separate substitute CLI or workaround setting is made, and if the corresponding measurement of the actual producer cannot be run and read, it is reported as unverified.

One exterior setting, every exposed elevation, corner, roof, and underside, the openings and entrances, and every space's threshold, four inner corners, and four directions from the centre are viewed from within their own space. failure/null positions are not deleted.
