`oak-floor` is assigned only to the `<room.id>-floor-boards` of the nine rooms (entry, flex-workroom, common-room, storage-1f, upper-corridor, primary-bedroom, child-bedroom-1, child-bedroom-2, upper-storage), excluding the three tile rooms (powder-utility, upper-bathroom, upper-service). storeys/floors.ts keeps the 0.18m-wide, 1.8m-long pitch, the board joints staggered by 0.6m per x column (x%3), the cut boards, the 0.016m board thickness, and the 1.5mm gaps between boards. The floor finish of the host wall thickness under the doors is filled by the threshold plates of [door-interface](../spaces/002-spatial-graph.md#door-interface), and the oak-floor grain is not extended into that section. It is a nominal .06mm matte clear finish layer and adds no geometry. Base colour #a78965, roughness=.55. In `oak-grain` 1024² with a U=.36m, V=1.8m repeat, the grain runs parallel to each board's local +Z.

The texture is a linear achromatic texture with a mean of .96 and a range of .86..1.00, with wavy longitudinal grain at 1.5..4mm spacing and gentle bands 20..50mm wide. The lateral deviation of the grain is at most 8mm per period, with no protrusions or black knot holes. The per-board phase follows the [metric rule](001-binding-and-scale.md#metric-texture-coordinates), and the per-board sRGB base colour factor is .96..1.04. It is a failure if the grain width grows on cut boards or the board joints are duplicated as black lines in the texture.

The floor's world +Z grain is kept across all rooms and does not rotate at cell boundaries. In the four in-room directions and thresholds of the [shortest/longest board check](007-observation.md#scale-and-junction-samples) and the [ref03 and 05 room samples](007-observation.md#reference-material-samples), boards and low-gloss wood must read at the same time. Identification of the actual species, wear resistance, and slip performance are unverified.
