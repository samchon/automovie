The interior is made as a blocking pass in which warm wood floors and cabinetry, low-saturation plaster, metal, and glass, and living furniture and building services read together. Glass privacy is expressed by a combination of the electrochromic state, a translucent layer, exterior shading, and interior shades.

The daytime basis is the soft daylight of an overcast sky with the warm interior lights on. Even when the day, private, and night states of the glass change, the camera, default light, and exposure stay fixed during a comparison. [Delivery fidelity](#delivery-fidelity) owns the reading of actual members and the limits of the blocking pass, and [Build or adopt scope](#build-or-adopt) owns the use of the source and the public engine API.
