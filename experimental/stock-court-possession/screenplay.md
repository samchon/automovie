# Stock court possession

## Court, interior

[Teams](../../brief/court/court.md#teams) fill the left and right halves of the floor. The blue ball handler is near the bottom of the wide frame, already holding the ball. A blue teammate is ahead. The rim and the open space under it share the frame with both teams.

### Dribble and gather

The blue handler walks forward. The ball drops to the floor and comes back into one downward palm. It bounces again. The handler closes both hands on it and keeps walking.

### Pass and contest

Blue's handler plants and passes across the lane. The receiver takes the incoming ball in both hands. Clay's nearby defender has stepped in facing the play. The defender drives upward from both feet with both hands high, then lands over the same footprints.

### Score

Once the defender is down, the blue receiver raises the ball and sends it into the arc. The ball falls through the opening and strikes the floor. The court stays in view through the bounce.

In the recovery version, one clay athlete comes toward the basket from behind the opening lane. The athlete reaches both hands up to the rising ball and holds it as the cut ends.
