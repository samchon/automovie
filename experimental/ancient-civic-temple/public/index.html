Controls

    
Drag: rotate · Right drag: pan · Wheel: zoom. Keyboard: arrow keys rotate, Shift+arrow keys pan, +/− zoom, R resets.

    
Vertical section (inspection mode): choose an X or Z section under Section and enter its offset (m). The server cuts the current source's wall, roof, coping, plinth and floor solids with that plane and draws the exact pieces (walls rust, roofs blue-grey, coping and plinth yellow, floors green) and the site ground line (green). Orthographic views the plane head-on and reads heights on a 0.5m grid (the red line is Y=0). A blank between pieces is a gap and a stacking is an overlap. Example address: ?inspect=1&section=cut-x&offset=0&ortho=1&span=2

    
Stations are derived from the current source's spaces, openings and elevations. A camera inside a room is not evidence that the room is reachable on foot. Labels, support, surface owner colours and sections are shown only in inspection mode.

    
Self-check account

    
Before requesting judgment, measure the current source with the same producer as npm run self-check: an envelope overlap scan (1cm grid), observation poses inside solids, a topology account of every model (degenerate, open and non-manifold edges, winding, gaps after T-junction resolution, connected components, volume) and closure contract violations. It takes a few seconds.

    
A defect location the account reports reopens the same view through ?camera=x,y,z&target=x,y,z.
