# Stage integrity

This target records the user's ordered authoring boundary for the building library.

## Rough layers close in order and stay closed {#rough-layers-close-in-order}

The author closes massing and the spatial graph first, and submits the surface decomposition with that stage. The following stages are actual envelope members, repeated envelope modules, fit-out, and finish. A closed stage is not reopened without an explicit recorded reason and the affected descendant consequences. The author does not claim a final result from source compilation alone; observer measurements and the separate read-only review are required before a stage or the whole production is called complete.

Authority: the user's 2026-09-16 brief. Success is a monotonically advancing stage record with a coherent owner and downstream consequence for each change. Failure is starting finish before topology is stable, silently reopening a closed stage, or declaring completion because one side of the author's and observer's unfinished lists is empty.

Review question: is the current change inside the earliest still-open layer, and are all later claims explicitly withheld until its required observations exist?

Sources: user brief, 2026-09-16; [NASA systems engineering handbook on configuration and verification control](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)
