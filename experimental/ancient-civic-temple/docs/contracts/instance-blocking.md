# Ancient civic temple instance contract

This production-local target binds the complete temple instance population to reviewed space, model, and material interfaces without turning instances into a second topology or prototype owner.

## Temple instance blocking boundary {#temple-instance-blocking-boundary}

Authority: the reviewed temple settings, spaces, models, and materials handoffs, interpreted by the instance author only for repeated membership and placement. Applicability: the fourteen authored instance populations in `docs/instances/temple.md`, including the centered `altar/sanctuary` singleton and repeated loop, roof, door, room-furniture, storage, vessel, lamp, and bench members. Success is a deterministic population whose IDs, prototype bindings, host contacts, transforms, bounded variation, and route clearances are all addressable before source construction. Failure is an unnamed member, a prototype or material override that changes model ownership, a lost floor or host contact, a duplicate or traversal-derived identity, an overlap with a threshold or protected observation route, or a transform that leaves its reviewed room, loop, roof, or service boundary. The instance layer may not add a room, connector, socket, axis, model part, material family, or motion state.

Review question: can the complete temple fit-out population be derived from reviewed hosts and prototypes with stable identities and deterministic refusal boundaries without changing the space, model, or material owners?
