## Reference images guide expression, not topology or measurement {#reference-images-guide-expression}

The five images in `D:/AutoMovieBench/refs-20260916/1951/` are ordered as exterior, section axonometric, courtyard fountain, sanctuary, and records/service area. They are broad visual references for envelope expression, atmosphere, material family, and spatial relationships. They are not dimension drawings, and no pixel proportion or embedded number is a source of dimension. The authored dimensions come from the fixed graph, structural plausibility, circulation clearance, and explicit production decisions. The images must not be pasted into the scene as a background, billboard, or surface texture. The section axonometric may be used to inspect the graph but is not a delivery frame.

Authority: the user's 2026-09-16 brief. Success is an authored scene whose forms and materials answer the reference questions while remaining independently generated from typed source. Failure is any dimension justified only by pixels, any reference image used as scene geometry, or any visual detail that changes the fixed room graph.
