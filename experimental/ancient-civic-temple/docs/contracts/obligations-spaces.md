# Additional obligations for the temple space population

This document assigns two independent conditions from the user brief to spaces: sole ownership of complete surfaces and observation of the entire topology. General space-use, geometry and material facts belong to settings and the shared space contracts. Settings and this contract/claim declaration establish neither completion of spatial design nor compiled observations.

## Ownership of complete surfaces {#surface-ownership}

The authority is the user's direct instruction. Before the initial massing and spatial graph close, the complete spaces population must declare one sole owner per complete visual surface for every exposed facade, every room's interior surfaces, and the single storey's floor and ceiling/roof underside. Finishes on opposite sides of a shared physical wall are different visual surfaces, while the physical wall itself has one topology owner. Roof tops, eave undersides and room ceilings must also be included. The ownership map connects to actual boundaries/hosts and specifies design and downstream source ownership paths. Putting the whole large building in one file first and splitting surfaces later is disallowed. If a required space emerges later, revisit the initial boundary owner.

Review question: does each complete visual surface of the actual space belong exactly once to the facade, room and storey ownership map, leaving no opportunity for different people to split authorship of the same surface?

Sources: surface decomposition and stage instructions in the user's "Authoring brief — small-to-medium ancient Mediterranean civic temple", 2026-09-21.

## Compiled observation denominator {#compiled-observations}

The authority is the user's direct instruction. The spaces observation design must derive, without omission, one external setting, every exposed facade and the corners where those facades meet, every exposed roof and underside, and every opening and entrance from actual compiled topology. Each space answers one threshold inside itself, four inner corners and four directions from its center. A concave space or one with a hole, such as the continuous colonnade, adds inner boundary corners and center observations of each segment instead of reducing them to four representative corners. Building exterior and space-interior observations cannot substitute for one another. The five reference comparisons are additional separate questions. After source implementation, every round updates the actual observation list and unresolved items from current compiled IDs, positions and bindings. Artifacts answer numeric questions; current frames answer whether the subject reads as itself and as that room. An inspection cutaway cannot substitute for an exterior delivery frame.

Review question: is there any exposed surface, boundary, space or opening in the actual topology that has no observation question linked to a current result or an honest unverified state?

Sources: instructions on what must read in every round and on the five references in the user's "Authoring brief — small-to-medium ancient Mediterranean civic temple", 2026-09-21.
