This architectural/interior library takes authority from the user brief. Deliver one approximately 430㎡ single-storey civic temple, complete interiors, adjacent site needed to read the building, deterministic design/source and an actual 3D review viewer. Timed film, shots, narrative and character performance are outside scope. Exterior, courtyard, sanctuary, offering room, administration, records, storage and service yard must be accessible and viewable as actual spaces. Documents or section axonometrics alone cannot replace delivery.

Author decision: operators inspect current source through exterior orbit, zoom, interior-space selection and fixed observation viewpoints. Viewpoint selection is an inspection control moving the virtual camera there. Camera presence in a room proves neither walking nor carrying from the entrance to that room; actual access connections are reviewed separately through space/floor/door artifacts. Space names and selection controls belong in a panel outside the view. Labels, routes, boundaries and sections default off and turn on through explicit inspection modes. A section axonometric is an inspection instrument, not an exterior/interior delivery-frame substitute. Users neither edit geometry nor create source-absent spaces in the UI.

Under the user's full-English translation instruction, standard modern English is the primary language of production documents and viewer operating explanations. Technical prose lets authors, observers and reviewers read the same decisions and limits; viewer space names and controls also use English. File paths, APIs, source identifiers, commands and fixed contract markers retain exact notation. Technical terms operators need receive English explanations on the first screen or document using them. stage means a declared contract stage; threshold means an observation position immediately inside a space's doorway; fit-out means furniture, equipment and prop placement; compiled topology means spaces, boundaries and connections obtained by compiling current source. Ancient-language dialogue/inscriptions and a separate translated edition are not delivered. This is a production/review language choice rather than a historical claim about fictional inhabitants' actual language.

External ground/water heights are spatial-design values relative to it. All directional expressions mean these authored coordinates rather than actual geographic surveys.

<!--

@evidence principles/core/settings.md#constraint-sufficiency Corner clearance at least 0.3m, lens clearance/near plane, exterior occupancy 70~85% and recorded coordinates/lens are specified.
@evidence principles/core/settings.md#observable-identity Interiors face four level directions within their own space; exterior roof/eave tilts are recorded to expose unfavorable views too.
@evidence obligations/core/settings.md#delivery-review-condition Frames at 1600×1000/50 degrees, interior positions, exterior occupancy and camera records reproduce review distance.
@evidence obligations/core/settings.md#design-dependent-subject-conditions Lens radius 0.10m and near 0.05m constrain unobstructed observation positions, separately from pedestrian envelopes.
-->

Author decision: default comparison uses 1600×1000, perspective camera and vertical field of view 50 degrees. Interior observations remain level; exterior roof/eave-underside observations may tilt vertically to show their subject, recording that value. Each interior center observation faces four directions 1.6m above its own floor. threshold observations look across the space immediately inside its doorway. Four inner-corner observations look diagonally from internal points at least 0.3m from walls. Authored virtual-rig conditions are a collision-free radius 0.10m around lens center and near-clipping distance 0.05m. These are neither human movement envelopes nor actual camera specifications. Neither lens center, surrounding region nor near plane may cut walls, columns or furniture. A collision at a required position preserves the original question unresolved; add the nearest valid position inside the same space as an auxiliary observation, recording original/actual coordinates and movement reason. The reviewer judges whether that auxiliary frame answers the original space, direction and question. Neither selecting only favorable compositions nor moving outside the room replaces required observations. Exterior distance makes the subject's whole form occupy approximately 70~85% of the frame; record coordinates, target and lens with the observation. The [observation contract](../contracts/obligations-spaces.md#compiled-observations) determines exact subject counts and positions.

Author decision: English space descriptions, textual observation-subject lists and keyboard-operable space/view/inspection selection are required; the viewer owns their implementation. Color alone cannot distinguish states. With no film, audio or dialogue, caption, dialogue-transcript and audio-description tracks are intentionally absent. Direct shape comparison by blind operators is unsupported; space descriptions provide auxiliary access to numbers and use. Modern architectural accessibility certification is outside scope, but access barriers and level changes remain visible for review in actual geometry.
