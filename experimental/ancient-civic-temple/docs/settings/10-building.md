No explanation asserts historical orthodoxy, and no invented ancient-language inscription is made.

Preserve the user instruction literally. One central courtyard lies within a rectangular single-storey outline. The entrance leads directly to the courtyard, and one continuous colonnade is the sole circulation loop. A small sanctuary lies on the rear axis; the communal offering room is on the left; administration, records and offering storage form one front-to-rear line on the right; and a small service yard sits at rear right. Author decision: the three right-side rooms run administration→records→storage from front to rear. Courtyard, colonnade, entrance, sanctuary, offering room, three workrooms and service yard all belong to the same storey. Every room has an actual direct door to the colonnade; successive room-to-room passage is not its sole route. Hidden rooms, disconnected passages, multiple storeys and additional courtyards are prohibited. The service yard may have a direct colonnade door and external service door, without creating a second circulation corridor. Left/right and front/rear follow the [coordinates](00-delivery.md#coordinates).

Targeting the user's approximately 430㎡, the author sets the horizontal area of the single-storey rectangle enclosed by exterior wall lines to 410~450㎡. Courtyard and service yard are included; adjacent road and site are excluded. The rectangle's long-side/short-side ratio is 1.0~1.25. These are admissible design ranges rather than image-survey results. spaces reviews room use, wall thickness and colonnade clear width to adopt one coherent dimension set and record its reasons. A grand hall's form is not forced into a small room, and the altar is not reduced to a plate to obtain passage.

Author assumption: the building is in a static open state without character meshes. The review movement envelope for one adult pedestrian is width 0.6m, depth 0.4m and height 1.9m. The offering-carrying envelope, including the person, is width 0.8m, depth 0.8m and height 1.9m. These are spatial inspection inputs rather than anthropometric statistics or guarantees for every user. The colonnade must let two pedestrians pass and small carried objects travel, maintaining at least 1.5m clear width after columns, leaves and fixtures. Room doors have minimum clear width 0.9m and height 2.1m; the main entrance has minimum clear width 1.4m with both leaves open. Movement may turn in plan, but the same envelope must avoid fixed members throughout each turn. Carrying is restricted to handheld small offerings and proves neither initial large-furniture delivery nor wheeled transport passage. Authored threshold/step limits and service delivery follow [floors and ground access](#ground-access). Occupant capacity and crowd simulation are not declared.

The service yard's [small handcart](35-objects.md#handcart) is permitted only as a stationary external prop. It demonstrates neither wheel rolling, doorway passage nor load performance, so it does not conflict with the wheeled-passage exclusion above.

Author decision: one above-ground storey, with no basement or internal stair storey. Low stone platforms at the main approach, around the sanctuary altar and at the courtyard edge, plus low room thresholds, are allowed. These are local level changes on the same ground storey, creating neither another storey nor hidden space. Authored geometry ranges are 0.10~0.15m per platform riser, minimum effective tread depth 0.30m and threshold height 0~0.02m above adjacent floors. These are walking-route design inputs, not claims about ancient construction, modern regulations or usability by everyone. The colonnade walking floor has one continuous height, and shallow courtyard-edge steps must read visibly. Where a connection meets a level change, source must provide actual tread surfaces and connecting regions. A semantic connector that does not physically reach is not passage. The service delivery route remains continuous from external door through yard/colonnade to storage, with no level change beyond threshold limits. If stairs become necessary there, review small-offering carrying conditions and ground heights together at the earliest owner; a drawn connection alone cannot declare passage. Do not confuse main-entrance approach platforms with the service route's circulation conditions.
