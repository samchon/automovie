These are the earth-red curved tiles of the [pitched roof and eaves](../settings/20-envelope.md#roof-form). The basis is the roofs of images 01 and 03, where rows of semicircular cover tiles run down the slope and semicircular sections show at the eave ends. One flat tile and one round tile covering its seam form one unit, and the irregularity or surface grain of individual tiles is not claimed.

In the building, the slab top this unit lies on comes from the [west](../spaces/roofs/west.md#west-roof), [south colonnade](../spaces/roofs/colonnade.md#south-canopy) and [east](../spaces/roofs/east.md#east-roof) roofs and the [composite boundary](../spaces/roofs/assembly.md#roof-junctions). The underside 4.69m of the [coping section](../spaces/junctions.md#plinth-coping) is the upper limit after the unit height is added.

The local origin is the centre of the eave-side lower edge line of the unit and lies on the roof slab top. Local +Z is the direction up the slope, +Y is the normal of the roof plane, and X runs across the slope. The flat tile is a board of X=−0.20~+0.20m, Z=0~0.52m and thickness 0.02m. The basic board over Z=0.08~0.52m is Y=0~0.02m, and along the two long edges X=−0.20~−0.10m and +0.10~+0.20m, only within the stretch Z=0.08~0.44m, a ledge of width 0.10m at Y=0.02~0.04m is attached. Over the last Z=0.44~0.52m the lifted front end of the next flat tile occupies Y=0.02~0.04m, so the ledge is left out there and the lap volume is avoided. In the eave-side stretch 0≤Z<0.08m, the board bottom and top are raised to Y=0.02/0.04m respectively, and the ledge is not made as a separate volume. Behind the vertical joint face at Z=0.08m, the board is Y=0/0.02m. When the upper unit of the same row is placed every 0.44m, the lifted 0.08m underside of the upper unit and the lower unit's top face Y=0.02m are in face contact, so the volumes of the two boards do not overlap. The X centre of the round tile is the unit's right seam line X=+0.20m, and its axis is +Z. The outer radius is 0.085m at Z=0.08m and 0.075m at Z=0.52m, decreasing linearly; the thickness is 0.015m, and the semicircle has 8 segments. The X of the two feet is 0.115/0.285m at the start and 0.125/0.275m at the end. Over Z=0.08~0.44m the left foot on this unit's +X ledge (+0.10~+0.20m) and the right foot on the −X ledge (+0.20~+0.30m) of the next unit placed at X=+0.40m stand at Y=0.04m, touching the ledges. The last 0.08m is a short projection without a contact band and hangs from the two-foot support of the preceding 0.36m. The 0.015m width of both feet lies within each ledge and spans the middle seam X=+0.20m. The shell occupies only Z=0.08~0.52m, and at the seam the end of the lower unit and the start of the upper unit abut at Z=0.52m. The exposed length of one row is 0.44m, and the maximum height is 0.04+0.085=0.125m. Because the imbrex extends into the neighbouring unit, the occupancy range of one unit is X=−0.20~+0.285m, Y=0~0.125m, Z=0~0.52m (box 0.485×0.125×0.52m).

Part map: `tegula`=flat tile; `lip`=lifted front end; `imbrex`=round tile. `tegula` includes the basic board and the ledges on both sides. `lip` meets the board and ledges only along a line at the Z=0.08m boundary, so it is kept as a separate closed member whose shape and position follow the joint dimensions above.

Parts and surfaces are split into `tegula`, `lip` and `imbrex` so that materials can vary the colour of the two tiles slightly. The rear underside of the flat tile touches the slab top, and the eave-side lifted 0.08m lap underside is a hidden contact face that touches the board below. The open bottom of the round tile shows at the eave end, and the contact bands of both feet hide behind the ledges. Placement count, X row spacing 0.40m, row direction, and the real cut faces at piece boundaries and valley lines are derived by instances from the composite roof pieces. A tile straddling a boundary is not dropped whole. The model's boundary variant cuts the original tile with the vertical boundary half-spaces of the roof polygon, preserves the thickness, normals and metre UVs of the remaining parts, and closes the new cut faces. A unit lying wholly inside the union of pieces on the same plane reuses the prototype. Only units straddling a boundary piece use the real per-polygon cut variant. The cut face UV is a horizontal U and an in-plane V orthogonal to it. The last 0.16m ridge seat stretch keeps the board and ends the ledges and the round tile. Where this stretch lies over a lifted front end, that part also continues as the low board, preserving the contact height. Under the coping, adding the maximum tile height 0.125m to the slab top of about 4.56m at the west mono-pitch roof end gives 4.685m, which differs from the coping underside 4.69m by 0.005m and falls short of the required clearance 0.01m. instances adds the real slab top of each roof piece and the 0.125m member ceiling, places whole units only up to 0.01m below the coping underside, and finishes the cut end before it meets the coping. On the last 0.16m of slope where it meets the gable ridge, the flat tile makes neither the round tile nor the two ledges and continues only the Y=0~0.02m top of the flat tile board up to the ridge, bearing both feet of the ridge cover. The cut position and unit count of this joint are calculated from the reviewed roof and coping faces.

On the review board, one unit and a 3×3 array are viewed from the front, side and 3/4 views to confirm that the semicircular cover rows, the lap steps and the eave-end semicircular sections read. In building observation, the roof at review distance must read as a tiled surface of semicircular cover rows, not as a striped board. A pattern on a flat board, a row direction misaligned with the slope, or a tile that pierces the parapet is a failure.

The top UV0 of the flat tile `tegula` and `lip` takes local +X across as U and +Z up the slope as V. The hollow semicircular shell of `imbrex` is unwrapped with the arc length from the eave-side −X foot to the +X foot as U and the +Z length as V, with seams at the unit ends and at the boundaries of the three parts.

## Ridge tile {#ridge-tile}

The end unit is a variant of the same lineage cut to 0.45m or less at the real south end face set by placement. It keeps the 0.40m repeat origin and does not pull the last whole unit back. If the cut length is 0.05m or less only the front nose remains; if it is longer, the rear shell is made up to that length. The new section UV0 of the end face is a metre unwrap in X/Y.

This is the semicircular cover over the ridge of a gable roof. The basis is the thick tile rows running along the sanctuary and east wing ridges of image 01. It is used on the three ridges of the sanctuary, the east gable and the porch, and not at the parapet-side high ends of the mono-pitch roofs or on the four valley lines. A valley line reads only as a slab fold and the tile ends cut at that line, and making no separate valley tile is the limit of this representation ceiling.

The local origin is the unit front end Z=0 on the slab ridge line, with local +Z along the ridge, +Y vertically up, and X running across both slopes. For the slope angle α of that gable (east 19°, sanctuary and porch 22°), the Y datum of the cover's lower edge is `Y0=0.02m/cos(α)−0.13m×tan(α)`. The 0.02m thickness of the flat tile is along the slope normal, so the top of the whole section is `Ytile(X)=0.02m/cos(α)−|X|tan(α)`. The half-cylinder shell has length 0.45m and 12 segments around the semicircle, and its rear 0.40m stretch (0.05≤Z≤0.45m) has outer radius 0.13m and thickness 0.02m. The front 0.05m lap nose has outer radius 0.15m and inner radius 0.13m, raised outward, so that when placed at a 0.40m pitch its inner face is in contact with the outer face of the lower unit's rear stretch. For the outer radius r of each stretch (rear r=0.13m, nose r=0.15m), the outer upper section of both stretches is `Youter(X)=Y0+√(r²−X²)`. The lower section is `max(Y0+√((r−0.02m)²−X²),Ytile(X))` for |X|≤r−0.02m and `Ytile(X)` for r−0.02m<|X|≤r. In addition to each vertex of the 12-segment semicircle, the intersections of the inner arc with Ytile are found by 24 bisection steps and inserted as vertices. The foot undersides follow the roof slope from the intersection to the end of the radius, so the rear band does not cut into the flat tile. The two feet of the nose at |X|=0.13~0.15m have a short end face running down from Y0 to Ytile(0.15m), so they do not float. The nose of the exposed first unit of a row also uses this section cut formula and closes its open end only at Z=0. At Z=0.05m there is a step end face of radius 0.02m. End faces that overlap at internal seams are not made as duplicate caps; the thickness section is closed only at exposed and cut ends. The Y occupancy runs from `Ytile(0.15m)` to `Y0+0.15m`, with X=−0.15~+0.15m and Z=0~0.45m. On both slopes the [flat tile board](cladding.md#roof-tile) stops the round tile and ledges 0.16m before the ridge and continues only the basic board, bearing the rear cover feet X=±0.13m and the nose feet X=±0.15m.

Part map: `ridge`=cover.

Parts and surfaces are the single `ridge`. The rear feet X=±0.13m and the lap nose feet X=±0.15m are hidden contact faces that touch the flat tile boards on both sides. instances derives the ridge length and placement count from each ridge line. At the south end of the east gable, the slab ridge top of about 4.53m plus the 19° `Y0+0.15m` is about 4.656m. With about 0.034m up to the coping underside 4.69m, the 0.01m clearance is secured. The ridge cover row of the east gable stops at south-inner−0.06m set by the [gable closure](../spaces/junctions.md#gable-closures), which is the inner projecting end of the coping. instances places whole units up to that position, cuts the last unit at that plane and closes its south end face. The real height plus the `Y0+0.15m` of the relevant slope angle is also compared with the coping underside, and the row stops short of it if no 0.01m clearance remains. Space interface report: when the east gable was 22°, the slab ridge rose to about 4.73m above the coping underside 4.69m, and spaces changed the [east gable](../spaces/roofs/east.md#east-roof) to 19° so that it could take the cover. The sanctuary ridge (about 7.67m) and the porch ridge (about 4.67m, between the two return wall copings) have no blocking member.

The review of the three-unit row uses the 1600×1000 pixels and 50° vertical field of view of the [common frame](../settings/00-delivery.md#review-condition), and both feet and the flat tile bed are checked in a separate close view.

The UV0 of `ridge` takes the semicircular arc length from the local −X foot to the +X foot as U and the horizontal ridge +Z direction as V. Seams lie at both ends of the 0.45m unit and at the radius step of the nose.

On the review board, one unit and a three-unit row are viewed, and building observation checks that the sanctuary and east gable ridges read as tile rows and that a semicircular section shows at the gable end. A gable where two slopes meet sharply without a ridge, or a cover floating above the ridge, is a failure.
