# Model obligations of the model design population

## Addresses of independent model decisions {#addressable-decisions}

<!--

Three shared scale, articulation and review-board items moved to production contracts, while structural members split into prototypes with different contacts and dimensions. portable's 13 H2s and ritual's 3 H2s distinguish bench/fabric size variants from separate censers, floor cushions and single-jar stands; records/storage chests retain one shared base prototype.

A model decision has its own H2 when its consumer differs or it can change independently. [Shared scale and representation ceiling](../../contracts/principles-models.md#temple-reference-scale) is a principle each prototype H2 answers separately through its own occupancy and UV0. The [articulation rule](../../contracts/obligations-models.md#temple-articulation-map) and [review board](../../contracts/obligations-models.md#temple-model-review-board) account for role assignment across the model population. Colonnade and porch round columns share a family but remain separate because their dimensions, placement counts and supported beams differ; double and single door leaves also split because their joint counts and face composition differ. Flat tiles and ridge tiles have separate addresses because they follow different roof lines and coping relationships.

Conversely, variants differing only in dimensions while sharing construction, surfaces and consumers stay within one H2. These include eight door-frame combinations, writing desks and reading tables, display units and wall shelves, rolled/bundled/open scrolls and cover variants of folded fabric. Records/storage chests copy the same base prototype. Floor cushions, low censers and single-jar stands have [separate addresses](../../models/ritual.md) because construction and consumers differ. Stable part surfaces and UV0 belong to the model prototypes and [shared principle](../../contracts/principles-models.md#temple-reference-scale); repeat lengths, fallback and bitmap bindings belong to [materials](../../materials/10-model-bindings.md#binding-map).

## Accounting for representation-layer completion {#representation-completion}

The representation layer is accounted for by 49 prototype-defining H2s listing parts and surfaces, member-correspondence addresses, contact faces, voids and occupied extents. The 13 portable-object H2s state member dimensions and occupancy in their prose. Each H2 answers scale/UV0 principles; articulation and neutral-review-board duties belong to production obligations and emit no parts. Scale relationships come from proportions against the walking envelope of the [shared datum](../../contracts/principles-models.md#temple-reference-scale) and reviewed spaces clear dimensions, not primitive defaults. Review distance permits reading silhouettes, parts and voids; carving, weathering, tile-by-tile irregularity and exact historical-style restoration are excluded shape conditions. Hierarchies follow support chains: column→beam→rafter→roof underside, platform→altar→bowl, compartment shelf→scroll, and handcart deck→axle/wheel. Only the double/single door leaves expose the `hinge.<leaf ID>` articulation interface; handles/hinges move with their leaf and the other 47 prototypes are rigid. The neutral prototype board observes six whole directions and opposite-side close joint views, closed/open leaves, rolled/bundled/open scrolls and 3×3 tile repetition under matching light and camera proportions.

Declared limits are the deterministic blocking-geometry ceiling, valley lines without valley tiles, untiled slab roofs on background neighbor shells, omission of rafters hidden above boards, and static shapes without flames or flowing water. Each H2's review-board and building-observation sentences own observation, while the complete spaces observation set separately examines in-building placement/contact.

Structural validity and semantic completion are judged separately. `modelSources` is at evidence, with contract ownership and concrete-class/prototype-H2 correspondence inspected under the evidence graph. Compiled parts, surface IDs and contact quantities require actual source-output inspection, while visual completion of silhouettes and close joints is judged through real model-review-board observations.
