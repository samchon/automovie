# Temple model articulation and review board account

These two production contract items account for the role allocation and observation plan of the whole model population. Each prototype's scale and UV0 are answered by its own model H2 under separate principles.

## Articulation role allocation {#articulation-allocation}

<!--

A door's handle and hinges move with the leaf node.
-->

Only the [double door leaf](../../models/openings.md#double-door-leaf) and the [single door leaf](../../models/openings.md#single-door-leaf) have a published hinge state. Closed and open are two states of the same leaf, not new model shapes. The [shared articulation obligation](../../contracts/obligations-models.md#temple-articulation-map) keeps the other 47 prototypes rigid. Changes over time in water level, flame and placement belong to the later systems, motions and instances.

## Neutral review board role {#neutral-review-allocation}

<!--
@evidence contracts/obligations-models.md#temple-model-review-board The size, contact and openings of the 49 prototype H2s are compared on one neutral board. The six whole directions are joined by the opposite close-up direction of each member joint, the two door states, the rolled, bundled and open scrolls, and a 3×3 roof-tile repeat.
-->

The [neutral review board](../../contracts/obligations-models.md#temple-model-review-board) compares individual prototypes under the same background, light, envelope and camera ratio. Its observation addresses are the six whole directions, the opposite close-up views of each H2's contacts, voids, boundaries and dimensional variants, the two door states, the scroll variants and the 3×3 roof-tile repeat. Placement and contact inside the real building belong to the [spaces observations](../../spaces/observations.md#geometry-observations), so this board's isolated shape review does not count as the building delivery review.
