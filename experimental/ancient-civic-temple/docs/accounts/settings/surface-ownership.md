# Settings surface-ownership account

## Surface-ownership coverage {#surface-ownership-coverage}

The settings surface-ownership conclusion is contributed by `docs/settings/temple.md#delivery-scope`, `#subject-breakdown-production-scope`, `#settings-coverage-map`, `#operative-subject-inventory`, and `#subject-observable-identity-and-fit-out-limits`. A complete visual surface has one canonical owner: space owns envelope, floor, wall, roof, opening, and graph boundary; model owns each blocking prototype; material owns construction, finish, and response; instance owns repetition, placement, and bounded variation; and the neutral observation population derives review questions rather than splitting a surface. This account substantively covers the settings ownership declaration and leaves later implementation to the concrete future branches named by the settings owner.
