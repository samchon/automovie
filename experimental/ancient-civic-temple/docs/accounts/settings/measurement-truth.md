# Settings measurement-truth account

## Measurement-truth coverage {#measurement-truth-coverage}

The settings measurement-truth conclusion is contributed by `docs/settings/temple.md#coordinate-unit-convention`, `#audience-operator-access`, `#accessibility-deliverable-states`, `#delivery-review-condition`, and `#subject-observable-identity-and-fit-out-limits`. The coordinate intervals are explicitly author-adopted, and `24.00m × 18.00m = 432.00㎡` is derived from those intervals rather than measured from a reference image. The 1.50m loop width, 1.10m ordinary door, 1.60m entrance, 0.08m threshold, 3.60m clear height, observation camera rules, and fit-out bounds are declared blocking or review conditions rather than legal or historical measurements. Image-derived dimensions, jurisdictional certification, and any quantity without a measurement means remain `unverified` or `outside-scope` and are not silently replaced by a similar command. This account therefore substantively covers the settings measurement-truth policy.
