# Settings observation-set account

## Observation-set coverage {#observation-set-coverage}

The settings observation-set conclusion is contributed by `docs/settings/temple.md#delivery-review-condition`, `#delivery-scope`, `#operative-subject-inventory`, and `#stage-policy`. The denominator is fixed as a graph-derived population: one setting, every exposed elevation and its meeting corners, every exposed roof and underside, every opening and entrance, and for every room one threshold, four inside corners, and four cardinal in-space directions, with the five reference questions added as supplementary prompts. The neutral camera, output, lighting, background, and control conditions are fixed here for repeatability, while identities, positions, bindings, and dimensions must be derived from the later compiled topology. The population is therefore substantively covered at the settings layer without claiming that its concrete observations or a viewer already exist.
