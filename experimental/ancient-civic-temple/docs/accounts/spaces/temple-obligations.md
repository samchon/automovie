# Accounting for the temple's additional space obligations

## Scope of complete surface assignment {#surface-population}

<!--

This enumeration does not establish absence of coincident geometry.

[ownership](../../spaces/ownership.md#surface-map) assigns south/north/west/east outer faces to their facades; inner faces/floors/ceilings of nine spaces to their rooms; datums and shared structural joints to storey; roof tops/external eave undersides to roof owners; and site soil bands, curbs, paving and distant ridges to the [site](../../spaces/site.md) owner. Each row's source address assigns a sole owner. Actual implementation and surface assignment are checked separately through the emitted surface-ID reverse inspection below. Every room, facade and roof document was read, and the relationship agreed: a facade or boundaries alone makes the physical wall, while its two opposing finishes remain with their respective spaces.

[threshold-support](../../spaces/storey.md#threshold-support) gives the whole threshold to the room rather than the colonnade, covering reservation within actual wall thickness and cell extension together. [junctions](../../spaces/junctions.md) owns butt joints, gable extensions and exterior plinth/coping section calculations, without becoming a new complete-surface owner. Coping/plinth surfaces retain each facade's coping/plinth IDs; coping above yard walls is service-yard wall-top, and even corner cells receive only one surface. The [interior lower band](../../spaces/ownership.md#interior-dado) divides colonnade/sanctuary wall finishes at 0.60m into two dado surfaces, still owned by each room. Face splitting in [roof assembly](../../spaces/roofs/assembly.md#roof-junctions) also retains original surface IDs, distinguishing external eaves from interior ceiling undersides. One separate model owner creates every surface of each independent column, door, basin and fixture prototype; room files consume its placement/contact.

This accounts for the initial design's ownership decomposition and reverse inspection. Enumerating emitted surface IDs by owner with `npm run self-check` yields 21 owners: four facades; nine spaces comprising entrance, courtyard, colonnade, sanctuary, offering room, administration, records, storage and service yard; five roofs; boundaries; site; and site-distant. Only boundaries reveal surfaces, the inner jamb/lintel faces of doors/windows in internal boundary walls, previously lacked an owner row; boundary/opening rows were added to the ownership table to close that gap. storey owns only shared joints and emits no surface. Each part references one surface ID, but separate parts can still emit coincident faces; compare their actual geometry and exposed faces with the authored ownership map. Apart from storey with only shared joints, the recorded enumeration found emitted output for every table owner. The listing does not automatically validate the table or complete the user's massing/spaces stage. Site surfaces are owned separately from the building; neighbor/vegetation objects belong to models/instances and are not merged into building-surface authorship.

## Complete observations and preservation of unresolved items {#observation-population}

<!--

-->

[geometry-observations](../../spaces/observations.md#geometry-observations) derives exposed facades/corners/roofs/undersides/entrances/openings with setting/boundary addresses from one generation's built environment and lowering. Twenty exposed joint-band segments outside facade host centerlines, including two upper segments beneath sanctuary/spine eaves, bind to the corner, T-joint and parapet questions and explicitly unverified sections in observations#geometry-observations. Two anta ends and both porch gable faces have separate facade addresses. Each space has its own internal threshold, four corners and four directions from its center; the colonnade adds six planar regions and hole/notch boundaries instead of substituting its bounding-box center. The service yard adds two thresholds; the long offering room adds both major-axis ends; the entrance adds center/end/column-support sections; and the site adds zoning overhead, two approaches, alley slope and distant-ridge views to test local failures. Beyond the engine's four outer corners, the colonnade has four courtyard-side inner corners and two entrance-body corners as source poses. Joint observations provide poses for four valleys, beneath sanctuary eaves and above parapets, three parapet meetings, the south end of the east gable and stone bands at four outer corners. The `section` group supplies exact viewer section poses for longitudinal/transverse door and ridge sections, threshold longitudinal sections, lower gable ends, light openings, wing-roof level-change end faces, eave projections, L/T joints, lower external walls and entrance parallel sections. Only the full-length diagonal valley section, which X/Z planes cannot follow, remains an unverified item without a pose and with its reason stated. The complete external-facade/opening set includes six constant-height hosts, four upper outward boundaries of two return walls, four high outward-facing north/side faces and four low single-space boundaries of setback-wall end cells, two free porch ends and eight sanctuary windows. Four invalid window thresholds previously bound inside the offering room/colonnade are preserved as unverified items without poses. Emitted-face rays measure visibility of each free porch end's own face; portions hidden from one viewpoint remain unverified. Computed cell interfaces are not new room corners or surface ownership.

When an observation function returns a different eye height or an interior point far from a door, preserve that original result and compare it separately with the settings-required position. Collisions/nulls remain in the list as unverified items with auxiliary positions in the same space. Solid sweeps of frames, leaves and hardware differ from zero-thickness panel boxes; without an actual instrument, similar-looking numbers cannot pass them. Full northeast canopy coverage, lowest exterior-wall ground contact and full threshold width/depth likewise cannot be replaced by a one-point check.

The five reference questions for exterior, section inspection, courtyard, sanctuary and records/service add to this complete set. Source provides five poses in the `reference` group, unverified until judgment. A cutaway is an inspection instrument rather than a delivery frame. `npm run self-check` counts observation poses inside wall/roof solids alongside its envelope-overlap scan. [viewer-path](../../spaces/observations.md#viewer-path) transfers current source solids, IDs, transforms, materials and observation generation together, without labels or semantic support over the default view. Viewer payload and display derive the census, observation count and GPU RENDERER each time. In the recorded building inspection, 303 actual topology observations were compared with 298 PNGs and five items without poses, and all 298 images were read directly. The four invalid room-bound window thresholds and full-length diagonal valley section retained their original no-pose state; dedicated inside/outside views of the actual eight windows, vertical sections and exterior/joint views of the four valleys addressed each question separately. Empty roof/underside census populations were examined through separate roof/underside views; low ceilings and curbs omitted by cutaways were read through actual room/site 3D views. These are recorded observations, not new captures made by a document translation. This account distinguishes design responsibility allocation from building observations, without claiming completion of all unfinished objects, GI or structural certification.
