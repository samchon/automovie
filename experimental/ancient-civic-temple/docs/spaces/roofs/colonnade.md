The north canopy is a monopitch covering the [north colonnade cell](../rooms/colonnade.md#ring-volume). At support line court-back its top uses the colonnade-eave [shared support height](assembly.md#roof-junctions). The plane rises northward at 12 degrees and ends at north-ring, the colonnade face of the [sanctuary south wall](../openings.md#boundary-ownership). Input arithmetic puts the top there around 3.65m, below the sanctuary gable clerestory sill. The former N(Z)/G(X) minimum-height restriction became unnecessary when the sanctuary rose above the wings and was retired.

The candidate extends in X from west-ring to east-room plus the shared yard-side projection, and in Z from north-ring to court-back plus the shared courtyard projection. Its west-ring~west-court portion composes with the [west roof](west.md#west-roof), and east-court~east-ring with the [east gable](east.md#east-roof), making two corner valleys. The east end adjoining the yard is a gable end projecting 0.35m from east-room; the yard itself remains open sky. Sanctuary south eaves float above this canopy without deleting it.

Source `src/spaces/roofs/colonnade.ts` owns the north canopy top and exterior end thickness. Beam/rafter undersides above columns bind to the colonnade's complete interior surface. The northeast colonnade cell by the yard must also be covered. Observe the sanctuary-door meeting, both sides of northwest/northeast valleys, yard-side end, sanctuary-south-eave gap, and courtyard eave underside. Covered windows or corner holes fail. Cover and composition are design decisions; confirm actual member support and visual results in source and GPU observations.

The south canopy is a monopitch. At court-front its top uses the colonnade-eave [shared support height](assembly.md#roof-junctions). It rises southward at 12 degrees to south-inner, the [south parapet](../facades/south.md#south-envelope) inner face. Input arithmetic puts the top there around 3.95m. The former X-direction ridge gable and porch-eave-width notch were retired in favor of this monopitch behind parapets.

There are three candidates. From the courtyard eave line at court-front minus shared projection to entrance-back, use the full west-inner~east-ring width. Over entrance-back~south-inner, use the tails west-inner~west-porch-outer and east-porch-outer~east-ring. Leave the entrance body occupied by the recessed wall, two returns, and [porch](porch.md#porch-roof) empty. The westward extension to west-inner composes with the [west roof](west.md#west-roof), and east-court~east-ring with the [east gable](east.md#east-roof), forming southwest/southeast valleys. Both tails terminate at the colonnade faces of returns, which rise as parapets above this canopy.

This source shares the north canopy's colonnade roof owner. These are north/south components of one complete canopy, not separate authors splitting the same face. Interior beam and exposed-ceiling surfaces remain with the colonnade space. Observe the recessed-wall rear meeting, return-wall exterior junctions, southwest/southeast valleys, and courtyard south-eave underside; do not count the porch alone as covering the rear colonnade.
