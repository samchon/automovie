Consume the [roof setting](../../settings/20-envelope.md#roof-form). Roofs form three composition units. The wing unit combines the [west monopitch](west.md#west-roof), [north](colonnade.md#north-canopy) and [south](colonnade.md#south-canopy) colonnade monopitches, and [east gable](east.md#east-roof) into one tiled roof around the courtyard. The [sanctuary gable](sanctuary.md#sanctuary-roof) and [porch gable](porch.md#porch-roof) are separate units. Derive heights from each owner's top-surface height at its support line. The courtyard colonnade eaves support lines (west-court, east-court, court-back, court-front) have top Y=3.20m; the east gable's east outer-wall center line is 3.55m, sanctuary lines 5.35m, and porch lines 4.00m. These reference roof tops, not actual beam or wall bearing heights.

Monopitches slope 12 degrees, sanctuary/porch gables 22 degrees, and the east gable 19 degrees; mass normal thickness is 0.18m. The underside is a parallel plane vertically 0.18/cos(slope) below the top. Match wall tops and bearings to it. For example, input arithmetic puts the west monopitch top at west-inner around 4.56m, below [outer parapet coping](../facades/south.md#south-envelope) at 4.85m. The sanctuary ridge is around 7.67m. Three roofs meet or pass above the coping underside at 4.69m; [gable and parapet closures](../junctions.md#gable-closures) tabulate those relations. The former 22-degree east gable had a south-end ridge around 4.73m piercing the south coping's inward projection, while the former sanctuary support at 5.05m made its west eave pierce the offering-room north coping. Therefore only the east gable was reduced to 19 degrees and sanctuary support raised to 5.35m. These are neither surveyed nor compiled results; measure overlaps with the full envelope scan. Envelope/repetition stages own detailed beam, rafter, and tile sections against these support and thickness inputs.

Only open ends have eave projections: horizontally 0.35m outward from each roof owner's reference face. Open ends are courtyard colonnade eaves, east outer-wall eaves, yard-side ends of the east gable and north monopitch, all four sanctuary ends, and the porch front. Ends meeting parapets or higher walls terminate at their inner faces without projection. Establish horizontal end lines first, then evaluate the existing sloped plane at those positions; thickness does not expand the plan again.

Where wing candidates overlap, retain only the region with the greatest top height as the exterior top. Inward monopitches and the east gable's courtyard-facing plane meet this way, forming valleys rising outward from the four inner courtyard corners. Cut at plane intersections without retaining duplicate faces. Equal-height ownership priority is west→colonnade (north/south)→east, used only to deduplicate coincident regions. Remove the underside slabs of removed regions too. Where adjacent wing pieces meet at different heights and the higher underside lies above the lower top, close the gap with a vertical end face owned by the higher piece. This is one zero-thickness sheet with a front facing the lower piece and a back facing the pocket below the higher piece. The front belongs to the higher roof owner. According to the subdivision below, the back belongs to a room's ceiling or, outside, that roof owner's eave underside. The back is the other side of the same sheet, not a second member. Currently one such face occurs at Z=-2.8m between the north colonnade monopitch and east gable; its back is colonnade ceiling. Without that back, the colonnade sees outside through the face. Add no exposed end caps at internal roof contacts.

Do not compose sanctuary or porch with the wings. Wing candidates leave the sanctuary body (west-room~east-room, north of north-inner~north-ring) and entrance body empty. Sanctuary west/south eaves and yard-side eaves float above the offering/north colonnade roofs or yard. Higher eaves must not erase lower roofs and leave holes below. [Gable and parapet closures](../junctions.md#gable-closures) assign walls closing beneath and between roofs; add no new infill walls to the open colonnade.

Composition source `src/spaces/roofs/assembly.ts` combines other owners' mass inputs without taking complete visual surfaces. Cut plane pieces and height-difference front faces retain the original roof owner's surface IDs. Roof owners own exterior eave undersides; rooms own ceilings/roof undersides visible inside. Do not duplicate a physical member from both sides. Nonhabitable structural gaps above low plank ceilings create no new spaces or access paths.

Observe sections through all four valleys and intersections, height-difference faces, the sanctuary-eave/lower-wing gap, roof/parapet meetings, and every exposed top and eave underside. Repair composition and the relevant roof owner together for light leaks, duplicate faces, open sections, holes below high eaves, or gaps above columns. Read actual face subdivision, normals, and shadows again in source and GPU observation; this document's arithmetic cannot replace them.
