Support lines are west-porch-inner/east-porch-inner, the inner return faces beside the [entrance](../rooms/entrance.md#entrance-volume). Returns rise to the [south parapet](../facades/south.md#south-envelope) coping height and close the porch sides, so the roof sits between them without side eaves. Support-line top Y=4.00m uses the [shared roof inputs](assembly.md#roof-junctions) for gable slope, normal thickness, and underside derivation. The ridge faces the frontage along X=0; its top is around 4.67m by input arithmetic, below coping. The small triangular gable sits centrally between broad plaster parapets.

Z runs from entrance-back to the eave 0.35m forward of south-outer. The rear end is the recessed entrance wall's north face; the wall rises to the porch underside to support it. Only the front end projects beyond the outline; floor and columns remain inside the rectangle. Beam top Y=3.50m meets the [triangular infill](../facades/south.md#south-envelope) underside, whose south-facade-owned top meets the porch underside. At support lines X=±1.65m, triangular infill fills around 0.306m between beam and roof underside Y≈3.806m; do not count the beam as touching the roof directly.
