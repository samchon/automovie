The support line is west-court in the [courtyard west datums](../building.md#plan-datums); its top consumes the colonnade-eave [shared support height](assembly.md#roof-junctions). The top is one monopitch plane rising westward at 12 degrees, shedding rain courtyardward from the colonnade eaves. It covers offering and west colonnade together without extending roof planes into the central courtyard. Only the narrow projecting eaves cross that boundary.

There are two candidates: the full offering-room length west-inner~west-room, north-inner~south-inner; and west colonnade with northwest/southwest corners, X west-room~west-court plus shared projection, north-ring~south-inner. Leave the sanctuary body for the [sanctuary gable](sanctuary.md#sanctuary-roof). West/north/south ends terminate at the inner faces of [west](../facades/west.md#west-envelope), [north](../facades/north.md#north-envelope), and [south](../facades/south.md#south-envelope) parapets without exterior eaves. Input arithmetic puts the west-inner top around 4.56m, below coping. Northwest/southwest corners compose with the [colonnade monopitches](colonnade.md) into valleys.

Source `src/spaces/roofs/west.ts` owns the complete west-wing roof top and exterior underside. Consume the [ceiling setting](../../settings/20-envelope.md#ceilings): offering has low timber planks, while colonnade exposes beams and rafters. Each space owns its interior ceiling; do not split the exterior roof among coauthors.

Observe northwest/southwest valleys, ends before west/north/south parapets, the sanctuary west wall and eave above it, and courtyard eaves. Intersecting duplicate sloped panels that flicker or roofs disappearing inside fail. Detailed member spacing and tile repetition belong to later stages; do not present the current box slab as a finished roof.
