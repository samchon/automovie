Support lines above the [sanctuary space](../rooms/sanctuary.md#sanctuary-volume) are X=±5.75m. Their tops consume sanctuary [shared support height](assembly.md#roof-junctions) 5.35m; the ridge is a straight Z-direction line at X=0, with gables facing north/south. Its calculated ridge around 7.67m lies within the [permitted](../../settings/20-envelope.md#roof-form) 6.8~7.8m and reads as the highest mass above outer parapet coping. West/east eave-end tops are around 5.15m and undersides around 4.95m. The west eave passes above the offering-room north parapet beyond north-outer, so its underside must remain around 0.10m above coping top 4.85m. [Gable/parapet closures](../junctions.md#gable-closures) owns this relation.

Candidate end reference faces are west-room, east-room, north-outer, and north-ring in the [datums](../building.md#plan-datums). Apply the shared projection outward from each. Sanctuary is a separate unit, uncomposed with the wings: west eaves float above the [west monopitch](west.md#west-roof), south eaves above the [north colonnade monopitch](colonnade.md#north-canopy), and east eaves above the yard without deleting roofs below. These end lines change neither storeys nor sanctuary wall positions.

Source `src/spaces/roofs/sanctuary.ts` owns both sloped tops, ridge, exterior eave underside, and end thickness together. North facade and the colonnade south of the sanctuary own gable exteriors; west facade owns the west wall exposed above wing roofs; yard owns the east wall facing it. Sanctuary owns the exposed interior underside. High windows consume the same walls' [voids](../openings.md#clerestories).

Observe whether the gable reads highest above wings and parapets from front/rear, whether the high timber section remains inside, whether roof thickness fits above windows, and whether gaps beneath floating eaves leak. This selects roof mass; it does not complete actual tile/timber modules or rendering.
