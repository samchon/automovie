The west outer wall spans west-outer~west-inner and north-outer~south-outer in the [datums](../building.md#plan-datums). One long offering room lies inside; add no door or window to this face. The wall is the outer parapet in the [roof setting](../../settings/20-envelope.md#roof-form), consuming coping and plinth heights from the [south](south.md#south-envelope) owner. The high end of the inward-sloping [west monopitch roof](../roofs/west.md#west-roof) terminates at its inner face, without an exterior eave.

Source `src/spaces/facades/west.ts` owns the full exterior plaster, plinth, coping surfaces, and wall solid. [Band division](../junctions.md#gable-closures) splits the inner face into offering-room finish below the roof, contact within roof thickness, and parapet rear face above it. Offering owns the first and west owns the last. West also owns the sanctuary section of the west spine exposed above the offering roof. [Diagonal junctions](../junctions.md#wall-junctions) cut the solids at both north/south corners while preserving the complete west exterior face. Finish owners bind continuous stone and plaster to that complete surface without arbitrary patches.

Observe the west front, both ends, roof junction behind the parapet, and sanctuary west wall for length, plinth contact, and coping height. A wall without openings still requires observation. Later rendering must verify that the offering interior reads through its colonnade door and actual environmental light without adding windows.

The wall bottom follows the [storey ground junction](../storey.md#wall-ground-contact). Maintain wall-solid contact with exterior ground throughout its length while preserving the offering-room floor and space lower boundary at their existing storey heights. Below-ground extensions remain part of the west envelope owner's same solid.
