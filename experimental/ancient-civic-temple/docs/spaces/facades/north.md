Along north-outer~north-inner in the [datums](../building.md#plan-datums), the rear joins the west offering room, central sanctuary, and right service yard. Its outline is straight, with heights distinguished by the offering parapet, tallest sanctuary gable, and low yard wall. The offering section (west-outer~west-room) has the same coping height as the [south](south.md#south-envelope); the north end of the [west monopitch roof](../roofs/west.md#west-roof) terminates at its inner face. The sanctuary section (west-room~east-room) is a gable end wall closing to the actual underside of the [sanctuary roof](../roofs/sanctuary.md#sanctuary-roof). The service-yard wall top is Y=2.55m.

Consume only the high sanctuary windows from [clerestories](../openings.md#clerestories), adding no arbitrary rear or closed-room doors. Ochre surfaces and plinth follow the envelope material canon; the yard boundary reads as stone. Source `src/spaces/facades/north.ts` owns the complete rear exterior surfaces, coping, parapet rear faces, and wall solids. The offering section's inner face is [divided into bands](../junctions.md#gable-closures): offering-room finish below the roof and parapet rear face above it. Sanctuary and yard inner faces remain with their rooms.

Meet the end-wall solids through [corner junctions](../junctions.md#wall-junctions), and use [gable closures](../junctions.md#gable-closures) for high end walls. Observe the rear facade, northwest/northeast corners, high windows inside and outside, roof junctions behind parapets, and sections above the yard wall. Treating the rear as invisible and leaving empty walls or missing roof surfaces is a failure.

All sanctuary, offering-room, and low yard-wall bottoms follow the [storey ground junction](../storey.md#wall-ground-contact). This facade also owns below-ground wall extensions without changing yard-wall tops or interior floor levels.
