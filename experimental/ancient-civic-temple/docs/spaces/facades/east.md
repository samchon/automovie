The east exterior wall occupies east-inner~east-outer in the [datums](../building.md#plan-datums). From south to north it encloses administration, records, storage, and the service yard. The administrative-room wall supports the east eaves of the [east gable](../roofs/east.md#east-roof), closing to its underside. The southeast corner bay adjoining the south parapet (south-inner~south-outer) rises to the [south](south.md#south-envelope) coping height and terminates the parapet. The low yard wall consumes the [north](north.md#north-envelope) height. Take the actual exterior service-door void from the [door owner](../openings.md#doors); do not add exterior access to every room.

Source `src/spaces/facades/east.ts` owns the complete east envelope, wall solid, and southeast corner-bay coping, leaving inner faces to each room. Both ends consume the [corner junctions](../junctions.md#wall-junctions). The administrative-wing roof ends at the yard boundary to preserve open sky; the [existing shared wall](../junctions.md#gable-closures) closes its north gable. Deep jambs, lintel, and timber leaf must remain legible at the service door, whose threshold meets the actual exterior contact at Y=0.

Observe the east front and both corners, the southeast bay's junction with the parapet, inside and outside the service access, and the section changing from eaves wall to low wall. If the exterior door floats or the height transition suggests a nonexistent room, repair this facade together with the yard owner.

The wall bottom follows the [storey ground junction](../storey.md#wall-ground-contact). Within the service doorway's wall thickness, leave the volume occupied by the yard-owned threshold slab empty. Distinguish a below-ground wall meeting the slab underside from a wall blocking passage; do not copy exterior ground to Y=0 everywhere.
