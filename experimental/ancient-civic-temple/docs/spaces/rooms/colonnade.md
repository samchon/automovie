Space ID `colonnade` is an interior/semi-exterior connection on the [ground storey](../storey.md#ground-storey). Its plan is one connected region in the [shared datums](../building.md#plan-datums), spanning west-ring~east-ring and north-ring~south-inner minus the courtyard and entrance recess. Do not declare the entire bounding box, including the courtyard hole, as interior.

The plan regions have disjoint interiors: north (west-ring~east-ring, north-ring~court-back), west (west-ring~west-court, court-back~court-front), east (east-court~east-ring, court-back~court-front), south (west-ring~east-ring, court-front~entrance-back), southwest tail (west-ring~west-porch-outer, entrance-back~south-inner), and southeast tail (east-porch-outer~east-ring, entrance-back~south-inner). Return-wall thickness remains outside these regions and outside the entrance void. This decomposition describes the space; it creates neither new rooms nor independent corridors. Adjacent regions meet without closed walls.

Public type `IAutoMovieBuiltSpace` represents one space as a union of convex `cells`. The implementation puts this union in one record with `id: colonnade` and `parent: temple-ground`, without also declaring `shell`. Apply the [finished floor](../storey.md#ground-storey) and [composed roof underside](../roofs/assembly.md#roof-junctions) to the six plan regions to derive actual three-dimensional cells. If roof junctions require more subdivisions to preserve convexity, add cells within the same space. Six plan regions therefore do not fix the compiled cell count. Computational interfaces create no physical walls, doors, or surface owners, and do not split ownership of the colonnade's complete floor and exposed ceiling.

Each cell is an intersection of world-coordinate half-spaces `dot(normal, point) <= offset`; public `builtSpaceContainsPoint` reads their union. Repository source confirmed this path and `lowerBuiltEnvironment` preservation of the original built environment. In actual output, compare points inside every region, on both sides of open interfaces, in the courtyard hole, in the entrance recess, and in return walls against the design boundaries. Read threshold connections separately from actual openings and connectors. Actual containment and observation-position checks had 0 failures. Door-passage and ring-turn SAT checks on actual world triangles, including open leaves and columns, also had 0 collisions. Actual sections separately established support continuity. These building results do not pass unfinished fixture placement and contact.

Place the [colonnade](../../settings/20-envelope.md#colonnade) column rows toward the courtyard boundary, limiting the base/capital plan projection into the colonnade to 0.35m from that boundary. Nominal north, east, and west widths minus this projection leave 1.75m; the south side before the recessed entrance wall leaves 1.60m. These are pre-placement budgets. Recheck [use-profile](../../settings/10-building.md#use-profile) usable widths after placing leaves, columns, and fixtures. A repetition rule that puts columns across doorways is unacceptable.

Source `src/spaces/rooms/colonnade.ts` owns one space and all inner-wall finishes, floor, and exposed ceiling surfaces. Models own the column prototype as a whole; do not split its round surfaces here. Observe four directions from an actual interior center of each of the six plan regions, outer/courtyard/recess corners, every direct door, and floor continuity. A bounding-box center in the courtyard does not justify observing the colonnade center from outside the space.
