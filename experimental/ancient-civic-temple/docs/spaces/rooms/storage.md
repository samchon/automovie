Space ID `storage` occupies [datums](../building.md#plan-datums) east-room~east-inner, storage-back~storage-front, between service yard northward and records southward. Its direct west door reaches colonnade. Required yard delivery follows yard door→colonnade→storage door, without a separate passage room through the shared north wall.

The threshold floor and passage volume of `door-storage` within the east spine belong to storage under [storey assignment](../storey.md#threshold-support). Service delivery height remains continuous from colonnade to storage floor at this interface too.

Following [storage identity](../../settings/30-interiors.md#storage), place large vessels, boxes and baskets along north/east/south walls and reserve empty west-door-to-center entry. Central passage retains the use-envelope minimum and the complete leaf swing. Its floor matches colonnade height, with no stairs required for small-offering carrying.

Source `src/spaces/rooms/storage.ts` owns the space and complete inner-wall/floor/ceiling surfaces. Inspect threshold, four corners, four center directions and bidirectional yard delivery. If large jars hide the door or placement turns storage into records shelving, repair placement and these reserved regions together.
