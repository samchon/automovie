Space ID `service-yard` spans east-room~east-inner and north-inner~yard-front in the [datums](../building.md#plan-datums). Its west side adjoins the sanctuary and north colonnade; storage lies south, and low outer walls bound the north and east. The west interface with the colonnade is north-ring~yard-front. Place the direct door on that interface, without extending it into the sanctuary.

The yard owns the threshold floors and passage volumes of west `door-yard` and east `door-service-exterior` under the [storey allocation](../storey.md#threshold-support). They extend to the west colonnade wall face and east outer wall face respectively; they do not assign external ground to the yard.

Both doors connect at the [storey floor](../storey.md#ground-storey) Y=0 with thresholds no higher than 0.02m. The exterior door must meet actual continuous ground. The yard is open sky, without extending the east wing roof over it. A few vessels and baskets occupy the northeast corner while leaving the path clear.

Its logical floor is Y=0 and cap is Y=3.20m from the [roof assembly](../roofs/assembly.md#roof-junctions). This is the same low air-region convention as the courtyard; the facade owners determine the actual north and east wall heights. The cap is not a roof, ceiling, screen, or walking support. Finite body cells and two door-passage cells belong to the same space without guaranteeing clearance from actual vessels, leaves, or eaves. The cap assigns observations without clipping views or light, so sky and roofs beyond it remain visible.

The source owns the inner enclosure, paving, and wall-top sections. The north and east facade owners provide exterior faces without duplicate walls. Observe both thresholds, all four corners, four directions from the center, and the path from outside to the colonnade. Do not add a door, corridor, second central courtyard, or circulation loop.
