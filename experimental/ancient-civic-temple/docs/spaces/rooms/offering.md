Space ID `offering` is one long room at [datums](../building.md#plan-datums) west-inner~west-room, north-inner~south-inner. It lies west of colonnade/sanctuary and meets west/north/south exterior facades. The east door directly reaches colonnade without sanctuary detours. Its length does not permit a hidden rear-room split.

The threshold floor and passage volume of `door-offering` in the west spine belong to offering under [storey assignment](../storey.md#threshold-support), continuing to east colonnade floor.

Following the [communal offering room](../../settings/30-interiors.md#offering-room), align the long table with Z and leave use on both sides. Keep the colonnade door-axis crossing clear between table and display units; prioritize north/west wall display. Add neither stage, chair rows nor another ritual space. Low beam/board ceilings consume the actual [west roof](../roofs/west.md#west-roof) underside.

Source `src/spaces/rooms/offering.ts` owns complete inner-wall/floor/ceiling surfaces and this single space identity. Objects use distinct offering-table/display prototypes rather than copied altars. Supplement four corners and four center directions with two major-axis-end views toward the central table, keeping room ends visible. Inspect door swing/table-side clearance after actual placement.
