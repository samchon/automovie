This changes no room count, boundary, door, or access; it prevents two solids from occupying the same wall intersection. Source `src/spaces/junctions.ts` computes cut inputs and contact relations without owning complete facade or room surfaces. Existing facade or boundaries owners build physical walls from those inputs.

The rectangle's four corners butt along vertical diagonal planes joining outer and inner vertices. Within the square overlap of equal-thickness outer walls, assign volume to the wall with the shorter horizontal distance to its facade's exterior plane. The equidistant diagonal is a shared zero-volume interface. Each west/east/south/north complete exterior face therefore remains with its original facade over its full length; do not overlay another facade's end cap at a corner. Their outer X faces meet the central cut ends of the south wall. South envelope physically owns recessed wall/returns; boundaries must not create second solids.

Preserve element and face addresses on both contact sides without rendering internal contacts as exposed finishes or thick gaps.
