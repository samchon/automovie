Doors/windows share the same cut internally and externally. Attaching a frame or leaf does not establish a void.

Public `IAutoMovieBuiltBoundary.face` holds the uncut host's position, rotation, outline, and thickness; `IAutoMovieBuiltOpening.profile` holds its actual local-XY void. Door/clerestory profiles are four-vertex rectangles derived from each section's clear dimensions plus edge-frame thickness. Relationship records without profiles are not physical openings. Wall cutting and frame/leaf placement consume one host/profile/ID without separate screen coordinates or hole lists.

Wall solids are unions of convex column prisms subdividing the host plan (convex trapezoid with diagonal butt joins, or rectangle) by longitudinal cuts and roof pieces. Cuts occur at adjacency changes, opening-profile ends, and neighboring roof-piece boundaries crossing wall faces. Column bottoms use the common bottom; tops follow that composition unit's roof underside or horizontal coping/yard-wall height. Opening columns retain only solids below the [threshold slab reservation](storey.md#threshold-support) and above lintels (below/above sills for clerestories), producing actual through-voids and reveals. Subtract opposite-normal coplanar touching faces between neighboring columns, retaining only exposed jamb reveals and height-difference ends. Where long faces meet roofs, [band division](junctions.md#gable-closures) separates below-roof, within-thickness, and above-roof portions. Public `extrudeAutoMovieRegion` constant-section extrusion was rejected because it cannot represent diagonal ends and sloped tops changing across wall thickness. Do not substitute `builtBoundaryWallCut` bounding rectangles for gable outlines. Construct columns from convex planar faces through public `buildAutoMoviePolyhedron`; every surface part must pass 2-manifold validation.

Pair both faces of every boundary and sections through all voids. Wrong space connections, residual wall on one door side, or exposed internal column contacts fail. Never place an invisible semantic door in a closed boundary. Confirm actual mesh cuts, reveals, and shadows in source and GPU observations.
