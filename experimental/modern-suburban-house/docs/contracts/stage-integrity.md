# Stage integrity

This target records the production-specific ordered lifecycle and its change boundary.

## Close the coarse layer before adding detail {#close-the-coarse-layer-before-adding-detail}

The production proceeds through massing and space graph, surface decomposition, envelope, envelope modules, fit-out, and finish. Surface decomposition is delivered with stage one. A closed stage is not reopened without a recorded reason. Unsupported, discarded, regressed, failed, and unverified work is retained in the worklog with successful work rather than hidden.
