Adopt the large front tree in reference 01 and backyard tree in 02 as two height variants. Branches do not copy photographic outlines.

[Site and planting](../settings/10-house.md#site-identity) requires irregular branches, crowns, foliage clusters and grounding shadows for the left-front mature tree and rear tree. This H2 owns two prototype-size drafts rather than placement. The local origin is the trunk's ground-contact center, +Y is up, and +X/+Z are horizontal crown axes. The intended front parameters are H=8.00 m and maximum crown radius R=3.00 m; the intended rear parameters are H=6.00 m and R=2.00 m. Those authoring choices were motivated by house eave/roof comparisons rather than photo measurements, but the retained crown formula below does not attain either intended H. No achieved above-roof silhouette is claimed. Subsequent instances consumes maps reference points to own position, yaw and terrain grounding without scaling a tree to conceal this draft mismatch.

The trunk uses three 12-sided rings tapering from base radius 0.045H to radius 0.025H at first-branch height 0.32H and top radius 0.010H, with a closed base. Six branches extend from the first-branch height at 60° intervals; branch i has azimuth `i × π/3 + (i mod 2) × π/12`, length `R × (0.72 + 0.04 × (i mod 3))`, and end height `H × (0.69 + 0.025 × (i mod 3))`. Each branch tapers from base radius 0.018H to end radius 0.006H and has a closed end. The crown comprises 13 closed rounded clusters at different heights. The central cluster has center Y = 0.76H and radius 0.35R; the 12 outer clusters have angle `j × 5π/6`, horizontal center distance `R × (0.48 + 0.07 × (j mod 3))`, height `H × (0.68 + 0.04 × (j mod 4))`, and radius `R × (0.30 + 0.025 × (j mod 3))`. Each cluster is a closed ellipsoid with 8 latitude rings and 12 longitude vertices, and vertical radius 0.72 times its horizontal radius. This rule regenerates without a seed or arbitrary array. `bark` covers every trunk/branch face and `foliage` every cluster face, each providing circumference U and height V in metres. Part boundaries remain distinct closed meshes, and hidden faces inside contacts retain their surface ids.
The third 12-sided trunk ring is at Y=0.76H, aligned with the central crown center. The two axial segments between the existing first-branch ring Y=0.32H and base ring Y=0 each taper linearly, and the upper trunk ends inside the central cluster. Ring heights are 0·2.56·6.08 m for the front tree and 0·1.92·4.56 m for the rear tree; branches and foliage clusters provide the remaining top crown.

For the twelve outer clusters j=0…11, the highest top occurs at j=11: Y=0.80H+0.72×0.35R=0.80H+0.252R. It is 7.156 m for H=8/R=3 and 5.304 m for H=6/R=2, below intended H by 0.844/0.696 m. Central-cluster and branch ends stay lower. These are bounds of the authored draft formula, not measured tree meshes or proof that its intended physical height is complete; the discrepancy is retained without changing the formulas or generating new geometry.

Individual leaves, species identification, growth, wind and root structures are not represented. The planned source owner is `src/models/planting.ts`; this tree family is unbuilt. The front, side and 45° [fixed model views](00-model-frame.md#model-review-set) must inspect both actual outlines, branch/crown contacts and non-box silhouettes when source exists. After terrain placement, site observations inspect grounding and shadows. Actual renders and terrain junctions remain unverified.

Adopt reference 01's low shrubs at the entry/garage base. Height and width are chosen from pedestrian and below-window clearances.

The shrub is one prototype repeated along the right fence and low planting beds of [site and planting](../settings/10-house.md#site-identity). Its local origin is the terrain-contact center, with an outline 0.80 m high and 0.90 m in diameter. Eight branches extend from a base of radius 0.035 m at angles `i × π/4`, each ending 0.27 m horizontally from the origin and at height `0.42 + 0.04 × (i mod 3)` m. Clusters comprise one central volume (center Y 0.57 m, radius 0.23 m) and eight closed ellipsoids at branch ends (horizontal radius 0.18 m, vertical radius 0.14 m). Branches are closed 8-sided volumes tapering from base radius 0.025 m to end radius 0.006 m. `bark` and `foliage` cover every branch and cluster face respectively, with circumference U/height V provided in metres. The furthest horizontal extent is 0.27 + 0.18 = 0.45 m, giving an actual diameter of 0.90 m. Subsequent instances determines position, yaw and population from maps reference points. Individual leaves, roots and wind are not represented. The source owner is `src/models/planting.ts`. Front, side and 45° [fixed model views](00-model-frame.md#model-review-set) inspect the low silhouette and clusters; after fence placement, inspect walkway intrusion, grounding and shadows. Actual renders remain unverified.
