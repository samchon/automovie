Per the [surface decomposition handoff](../settings/20-verification.md#surface-allocation), the [complete surface contract](../contracts/surface-ownership.md#whole-surface-owner) is applied to source authoring. The table below assigns the responsibility of the spaces wall, roof, and substrate files. The author in charge is in every case this production's single author. One complete face is handed over whole, and the members, repetition, and finish of the same face are not handed over separately. The spaces skeleton source and part measurements exist, but the later model fill members have no source yet, so this table is not read as a completion declaration of the whole surface id list, face count, or missing/duplicate census.

The UV coordinate ownership of [common materials and envelope impression](../settings/20-verification.md#visual-grammar) is received by the complete face owners. The exposed vertical faces of the four exterior walls set U to X for the front and rear faces, U to Z for the side faces, and V to Y, from the world origin. The vertical brick faces of the base and chimney also use the same U as that face's horizontal tangent and V as Y, sharing horizontal course heights across storeys and corners. A face ends at wall cut faces and at the real ends of plates and openings, and a new member starts its own UV, but splitting one exterior wall into several meshes alone does not restart its phase. The weather face of a roof slope sets U to the world axis parallel to that face's eave and V to the signed distance up that face's slope from the world origin. A face ends at valleys, ridges, steps, and chimney cuts, and the separate face on the other side of a junction receives its own slope and axis. The sloped roof weather face of the porch also uses this slope rule, and its underside and the columns, beams, and bases consume the per-member local UV of the [porch face owner](porch.md#porch-roof-columns). Repeat length and optical response are set by materials, consuming these coordinates.

`src/spaces/building.ts` is the assembly owner of the outline and shared coordinates and does not author the individual members of complete elevations directly as a giant array. For the roof joining lines, `src/spaces/roof/junctions.ts` derives the [single height/intersection boundary](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-shared-edges), and each slope owner consumes it. The per-roof-face documents are in the `docs/spaces/roof` files of the same name. The shared calculation is not a second geometry that owns a slope. The real measurement of the spaces skeleton is taken by the source audit, and the integrated surface census and GPU readability after the model fill are unverified.

The [common opening handoff](06-openings.md#external-opening-interface) of `src/spaces/openings.ts` is a calculation boundary that shares the coordinate format and member reservations and is not a separate owner of window/door geometry. Each elevation owner owns the wall body, its own voids, and the wall cut faces, and the room interior owner takes the interior reveal/finish of the same void. The separate closed members, the outer trim, window frame, sill, door frame, leaf, and glass, are owned by the [model fill prototype](../models/00-model-frame.md#model-representation-ceiling) and generated after modelSources opens. The same cut face is not covered again with trim, and the same trim is not built by both the elevation and a model at once. Door leaf glass and the inner divisions of windows are not hidden from real observation either.

The common body of an ordinary interior partition is generated by the single source owner of the [shared boundary assignment](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-ownership), and the rooms on both sides keep their own complete finishes. `src/spaces/boundaries.ts` has only the calculation responsibility of passing on the same boundary of [intersections, openings, and thresholds](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-junctions) and makes no separate wall or finish. The garage shared wall follows the two owners by height in the table above, and the stair structure the existing stair owner.

The [exterior wall corner and roof step junctions](07-boundary-assembly.md#exterior-boundary-junctions) distinguish the single structural body that the front and rear elevations/shared wall receive from the finish of each complete elevation. The [roof contact at the wall head](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-wall-head-junctions) is received from the roof intersection calculation and applied across the whole wall thickness. The elevation, roof, and room surface ownership of the table above is not changed on the ground of shared calculation or a common body.

The [interstorey edges](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions) connect the thickness zone of the exterior walls and the top and bottom contacts of interior walls as the same boundary. The body of the stair hole is the upper floor plate owner's, the visible continuous finish of its thickness section the stair owner's, and the visible floor of the arrival the upper-hall owner's. No second floor plate or rim finish is generated at the same edge.

The [support under the ground floor](10-ground-floor.md#ground-support-handoff) distinguishes the zone under the main block/garage interior substrate from the base zone of the exterior walls/shared wall. The exposed vertical finish of the base is integrated by the existing complete elevation owners, and the floor owner does not overlay a separate cladding band. The real ground, support bottom, foundation, and contact census are not complete yet.

`src/spaces/site.ts` takes on only the [assembly of exterior zones/connections](site/00-access.md#site-access-interface). The earlier reservation of all paving in one file was made concrete, before source authoring, into the complete walking surface, driveway, and terrace. The waiting area under the porch and the waiting area outside the garden door belong wholly to their continuous paving owners and are not split into separate plates. The side service walk keeps the same continuous face owner even though it has front and back zones. The fixed panels and gate posts of the wooden fence and the independent leaf of models are different closed surfaces and divide only at the handoff that states the [retirement and later generation point](../models/02-exterior-doors.md#side-yard-gate). The ownership decomposition of the site boundary, sidewalk/road, ground, and planting and the real parcel inclusion and ground junction of the fence are incomplete because maps do not exist yet. This table is not read as completion of the whole site surface census.

The [exterior paving substrate](site/01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) and the [support of raised flat surfaces](site/01-paving-support.md#raised-platform-support) are also owned together by each complete paving/porch file above. The site assembly file makes no separate substrate or support box. The [driveway/walk contact and gate post junction](site/01-paving-support.md#paving-contact-handoff) close at the same end line of the original owners, and the real support bottom/ground waits for later input.

For the complete faces inside each room that the [surface decomposition handoff](../settings/20-verification.md#surface-allocation) requires, the owners below have the responsibility for one author to integrate all inner wall, ceiling, and floor finish zones and opening surrounds of each room. Structural walls and floor plates consume the outline/shared references and invent no room coordinates separate from the envelope. The real voids of doors and windows are cut once by the boundary owner. The room face owners below check that their own reveal and finish faces and the members of the [interior door prototype](../models/03-interior-doors.md#interior-door-members) and [window prototype](../models/01-windows.md#window-member-sizes) fit the same void and interface, and make no leaf or window meshes. A [separate closed baseboard plate](../models/06-interior-trim.md#wall-baseboard) attaches at the wall/floor tangent of dry rooms and the laundry, and the room owner makes only the wall and floor finish faces and provides that plate's length input and end interfaces. That plate is not placed in the garage or on bathroom tile walls. Every responsibility belongs to the same single author.

At each finished floor and ceiling height inside the main block inner outline, a plan point must belong to exactly one of a room finish plate, a partition or wall body, a stair solid, or a stair hole. Even a reservation strip subtracted from a passage outline does not leave empty a plan that has no real wall or stair solid. The floor finish is taken by the room owner that reaches that open plan, and the ceiling finish of the lower storey by the room owner of the same plan excluding the real stair hole. A finish plate does not share the plan interior at the same height with another room's finish plate or a solid, and butts at the joint line. The vertical rim finish of the stair hole is taken by the [interstorey edge owner](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions) between the top of the lower ceiling finish and the underside of the upper floor finish, and is not overlaid on those two horizontal finishes or on the partition body. The case in which the inside of shallow storage is outside the passage outline of the room using it is included in this rule as well. The room owner in charge of the storage makes the finish across that whole inner X/Z down to its storey's finished floor, and must also connect with the neighbor's finished floor under the storage opening. If the owners on the two sides of an opening differ, each fills only its own half per the [threshold midplane handoff](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-junctions), and if the owner is the same, the whole opening width is filled once. The floor and ceiling boundaries [beside the open stair in the entry](rooms/entry.md#entry-plan), of the [coat closet](rooms/entry.md#entry-coat-storage), and of the [linen closet](rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-linen-storage) are currently set by this covering rule.

The interior UV coordinates of [common materials and envelope impression](../settings/20-verification.md#visual-grammar) are set by the owner of the visible face. The inner walls of each room consume world coordinates with U=X for front and rear faces, U=Z for side faces, and V=Y. Floors and ceilings share U=X, V=Z and keep the world X/Z origin, so the phase does not change across room boundaries on the same storey. Each face ends at door, window, and niche cuts in walls, at material thresholds and stair holes in floors, and at real holes and height steps in ceilings. A face that remains on the same plane after a cut keeps the same world phase, and walls of another direction or ceilings of another height project their own plane separately. The per-member coordinates of stair treads, risers, and landing consume the UV of the [stair route owner](02-stair.md#stair-reservation), and the axes and seams of posts and handrails made by spaces consume the UV of the [protective boundary owner](02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights). Balusters are made by the [model prototype](../models/04-stair-members.md#stair-balusters), and models also set the UV of their faces. The local UV of casings, leaves, window frames, and independent handles that models make stays with those model owners.
