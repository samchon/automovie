`pantry` is a room of ground-storey entered directly from the back of the [service access](service.md#service-access-plan). The inner finish is X = [3.22, 5.50], Z = [-6.05, -4.70] m. The front is the laundry partition, the back the [common area](common.md#common-room-plan) boundary, and the right the [garage shared wall](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent). Per the [service band setting](../../settings/10-house.md#service-band), there is no door passing through to another room. The service passage opens directly to the common area, so one does not pass through the laundry to bring food.

`service-pantry-door` takes Z = [-5.75, -4.80], Y = [0, 2.20] m in the west partition as its rough opening and targets an effective width of at least 0.85 m. It opens from hinges on the +Z jamb into the interior in the +X direction. The shelves sit inside an L-shaped reservation 0.25 m deep in +Z from the back wall and 0.30 m deep in -X from the right wall. The reason for choosing the back shelf shallower than the earlier 0.30 m is to hold the [open door/handle and the passage](#pantry-use-route) below together. No further shelf is placed on the front wall to reduce the room depth from both sides.

`src/spaces/rooms/pantry.ts` owns the room. The entry and shelf access reflecting the shelf ends, the 90° open leaf, and the real door frame, and the observation inside the narrow room are checked. Food brought out goes by way of the service access to the [main route of the common area](common.md#common-clear-routes) and the kitchen, and no second pantry door is added to the back wall. The reserved dimensions are inputs for the [identity of food storage](../../settings/10-house.md#pantry), and the real storage, fixture occupancy, and turning are unverified.

It consumes the [shelf plan](#pantry-plan) of the same pantry/ground-storey. The overlapping corner of the back strip and the right strip is made into one L-shaped shelf face, without overlapping boards or placing corner posts twice. Posts, brackets, and lips are reserved inside this plan and do not project further into the passage. No separate storage room or floor furniture is added.

The shelf top heights are derived as five shelves from 0.20 m above the [ground-storey finished floor](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums) at 0.40 m spacing. The shelf thickness reservation is 0.03 m, placed downward from the top. This is the design input of the height repetition, not five hand-copied records or the current compiled member count. Supporting members, repeated boards, and finish are integrated in later authoring by this room's complete face owner.

The food of the [pantry setting](../../settings/10-house.md#pantry) is divided into airtight containers, small grocery boxes, and low baskets and placed within the range set back 0.02 m from each shelf plan end. The maximum depth of objects on the back shelves is reserved at 0.20 m and on the right at 0.25 m, and each object's height within 0.30 m above its shelf. The lower shelves hold low baskets/containers, the middle frequently used food boxes and containers, and the upper small containers. The food quantity, prototypes, and placement are later models/instances authoring, and this document invents no unseen objects or whole inventory.

Objects are not stacked on the floor or behind the door to reduce the passage. No deep object that can be taken out only by putting one's body into the corner, or a required movable step stool, is placed. Rather than filling the shelves to their ends, the taking-out path is also compared with the [use passage](#pantry-use-route) below. The real prototypes, handles/lids, shelf supports and object contact, taking things from the upper shelves, access to the L-shaped corner, and the readability of a food storage room are unverified. The plan/section with filled shelves and the complete interior viewpoints of the room are the inspection addresses.

The state of opening the [room door](#pantry-plan) above 90° and using the storage inside pantry is reserved. The -Z occupancy of the open leaf and handle must be within 0.08 m from the +Z jamb plane. The threshold effective width including the door frame keeps the existing room door target. This projection is an authored upper limit, not a real handle product or measured value. When setting the back shelf depth, the passage after subtracting up to this limit was considered, and if real members exceed the reservation, it is resolved in the door/shelf owner without shrinking the person.

The main use strip connects, in the X direction, from the room entrance to the right shelf front, and in the Z direction, between the back shelf front and the rearmost limit of the open door/handle above. Per the shelf clear width condition of the [pantry setting](../../settings/10-house.md#pantry), shelf objects, brackets, and baseboards do not project into this strip either. The continuous passage past the threshold is checked against the 0.90 m target of the [use conditions](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) and is not confused with the separate effective width target at the entrance.

The turning reservation at the right end is a 0.90 m square placed 0.50 m in -X from the right shelf front at the Z center of the use strip above. It is space for the body and the held object to turn, not an independent room or floor plate. This zone is kept clear even with the door open. When taking things from the right shelf, one faces +X with 0.15 m between the shelf front and the front of the body. At the back shelf, one faces -Z with 0.20 m between the front and the front of the body, at a position where the whole body width does not go into the walls/shelves on either side. The person width, depth, and width including a basket consume the existing use conditions and are not reduced to fit the shelves.

The moment of operating the door from the service passage, the moment of passing the open door to enter, and the moment of taking things out in front of a shelf and turning around are distinguished. This small room is checked in the state of one person using it, and there is no claim that another person can pass behind the person using the shelves at the same time. After taking things out, one returns through the same door → [service access](service.md#service-access-plan) → common area → kitchen. A temporary basket or step stool is not set down in the service passage with its occupancy omitted.

The checks add the whole plan of the open room door/handle and shelf objects, sections of the threshold, shelf fronts, corner, and each height, the route of passing in both directions while holding objects, and views inside the room. The turning zone dimensions are not proof of real turnability and must be confirmed with the final geometry and occupancy body. The real door clear width, turning, taking objects out, and contacts/collisions are unverified. The threshold, four corners, and center directions of the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) are kept even in this narrow room.
