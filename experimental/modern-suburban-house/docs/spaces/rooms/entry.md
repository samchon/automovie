`front-entry` is an interior distribution space of ground-storey. It consumes the [main block inner limit](../00-building.md#main-building-extent) and the [storey reference](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The inner finish plan vertices in (X, Z) m are (-1.80, -0.25), (2.02, -0.25), (2.02, -3.41), (-0.65, -3.41), (-0.65, -1.45), (-1.80, -1.45). It closes from the last point back to the first. The lower flight route of the [single stair](../02-stair.md#stair-reservation) is excluded from the entry floor, and the lower waiting area X = [-1.80, -0.65]·Z = [-1.45, -0.25] is included. X = [-0.65, -0.50]·Z = [-3.41, -1.45] on the right of the stair hole is beside an open railing on the ground storey, not a full-height wall, so the entry owner finishes the floor Y = [-0.025, 0] m and the ceiling Y = [2.75, 2.765] m there. The floor shares no plan interior with the real bodies of treads and railings and abuts them at X = -0.65 m. The ceiling continues to the same hole line, but at its top Y=2.765 m it butts against the lower end of the [vertical rim of the stair hole](../08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions) without overlapping.

`front-door` belongs to the main block front wall between the porch and this entry. The rough wall opening is X = [0.40, 1.40], Y = [0, 2.20] m, with Z as the wall thickness direction. A door frame leaving an effective width of at least 0.90 m, above the [door clear width target](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile), is fitted as a later member. A leaf with hinges on the +X jamb swinging into the interior in the -Z direction is reserved. Closed is the baseline state, and even when opened for interior observation the floor up to the stair waiting area is kept clear. The porch height follows the [threshold reference](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums).

The living room is connected directly by [its own entrance](living.md#living-plan), and the first step of the [central single turning stair](../../settings/10-house.md#stair) is also exposed to the same entry. The right opens to the [service access](service.md#service-access-plan). The way from the entry to the living room, stair, and service passes through no other room. The source assignment is `src/spaces/rooms/entry.ts`. The swing of the entrance leaf, the lower waiting area and the living room door swing, and the complete observation poses inside the entry can refute this plan. The current door binding, door frame clear width, passage, and views are unverified.

It consumes the [wooden entry door and shallow mat](../../settings/10-house.md#entry) of the same front-entry/ground-storey. The mat is at world X = [0.45, 1.35], Z = [-1.95, -1.30] m, within 0.006 m thick above the ground-storey finished floor. It is placed behind the end of the door opened 90° and checked against the real operating range of the door bottom/handle. No bench, console, or planter is added to this distribution floor, and coats are sent to the [storage under the stair](#entry-coat-storage).

A person who opens the door and comes in branches to the lower stair waiting area and the living room door on the left and to the service connection at the back and right. The lower stair waiting area and the space in front of the living room door are one open floor, with no claim that the door operator and a person coming down the stair occupy the same spot at the same time. A person carrying a basket also uses the [same user body](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile), and the turn including the stair railing end, door frame, and baseboard is checked. When the coat closet is not in use, one must be able to go back from the entry directly to the service passage.

The plan/section with the [living room door](living.md#living-plan) and the entry door both open, the moment of opening the door and the moment of passing through the open door, and the distribution directions seen going up and down the stair are added to the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The coat closet, the [coat storage near the entry](../../settings/10-house.md#entry) reached along the service access connected from the [entry](#entry-plan), is placed at the high end under the [upper flight](../02-stair.md#stair-reservation). The storage body reservation is X = [1.10, 1.75], Z = [-4.56, -3.51], Y = [0, 2.15] m. Its X start is derived 0.07 m inward from the start of the seventh tread of the upper flight, and the body end 0.65 m back from there. The top Y = 2.15 m is set as this body owner's value, and the structural underside of treads 7–9 above consumes this value. X = [1.75, 2.02] in front of it is continued as an open reveal between the closet and the service passage. `entry-coat-opening` makes an opening Z = [-4.51, -3.56], Y = [0, 2.15] m at the X = [1.87, 2.02] boundary at the +X end under the stair. No blocking panel is left in front of the doors to hide the storage.

Inside the closet and the open reveal, X = [1.10, 1.87]·Z = [-4.56, -3.51] m, the entry owner makes the [per-room floor finish](../03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff) at Y = [-0.025, 0] m. This range does not overlap the entry passage outline. The opening floor X = [1.87, 2.02]·Z = [-4.51, -3.56] m is divided at the X = 1.945 m midplane, with the inner half finished by entry and the service passage half by [service-access](service.md#service-access-plan) at the same height. The wall opening is not treated as a floor hole.

The doors are chosen as two overlapping sliding leaves on the X = 2.02 m face aligned to the service passage. They are storage doors for taking out clothes by partial opening, not room doors a person passes through, and the 0.65 m deep body holds hangers and shelves. The empty box without clothes is not used as a room a person walks into. There is no through floor or back door.

The rod is placed 0.325 m in +X from the back X end of the body, 1.65 m above the floor, hanging in the Z direction. The upper shelf top is at 2.00 m, and the occupancy including clothes/hangers stays inside the existing body. The maximum passage-side projection of the doors/handles is up to X = 2.07 m. The front use reservation is X = [2.10, 2.70], Z = [-4.40, -3.65] m facing -X, with body depth applied to X and width to Z. Taking clothes out of the closet uses part of the [service band](service.md#service-access-plan), so it alternates with others passing through. After the user steps back, the [main passage clear width target](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) must remain even behind the closed/open sliding hardware and the opposite casing. The real clothes depth, the two door overlap, the section under the stair, and the route of taking out a coat and returning to the entry are unverified.

Whether the stair tread heights and structural underside at that position are higher than the closet top is confirmed in member sections. Tread positions alone do not settle that the closet fits. The source responsibility for the closet walls, opening, and top height is `src/spaces/rooms/entry.ts`, the leaf, rod, and shelf prototypes belong to the [storage members](../../models/05-closet-fittings.md#coat-closet-doors), and the positions and underside shape of the stair treads are owned by the stair owner. The underside height of treads 7–9 consumes this closet top value. The current storage/stair structure interference and the use space in front of the doors are unverified.
