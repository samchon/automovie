`laundry-mudroom` is a real room of ground-storey. The inner finish is X = [3.22, 5.50], Z = [-4.55, -2.05] m. The west is the [service partition](service.md#service-access-plan), the east the [main block/garage shared wall](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent), the front the powder room partition, and the back the pantry partition at Z = [-4.70, -4.55]. The [laundry equipment and buffer function](../../settings/10-house.md#laundry-mudroom) are carried out in the same room.

`service-laundry-door` and `laundry-garage-door` each own a rough opening Z = [-4.40, -3.35], Y = [0, 2.20] m in the west partition and the east shared wall respectively. Both doors target a final effective width of 0.95 m and open into the mudroom from their -Z jambs. The openings face each other, and the open leaves point toward the back edge. The two doors are not merged into the same connector. The 0.15 m step on the garage side receives the [existing threshold datum](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums).

The machines, folding top, upper storage, and bench/hooks consume the [laundry work reservation](#laundry-equipment-use). The front work is distinguished from the [crossing route](#laundry-through-route) between the two room doors at the back, so the only interior route between the house and the garage, [which the garage interior observation also passes through](../../settings/10-house.md#garage), is not cut because a machine is left open.

The garage-side lower waiting area X = [5.75, 6.80]·Z = [-4.43, -3.38] and the mudroom upper waiting area X = [4.45, 5.50]·Z = [-4.43, -3.38] m are reserved. The amount by which the door frames and the 90° open leaves cut into these areas is also included in the real clear width. `src/spaces/rooms/laundry.ts` and the shared wall owner consume the same two opening ids. The threshold section, two-way basket movement, and interference between laundry work and crossing are unverified.

The [floor handoff of the single step](../10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions) bears the high threshold with the main block substrate up to the garage-side face of the shared wall and assigns its top/exposed riser face to this room owner. The existing garage lower waiting area is not covered with a high tread, and it meets the garage floor at the same end line.

Inside the same laundry-mudroom/ground-storey, a right machine wall and a left shoe bench are assigned. No separate room or door is added. The closed occupancy strip of the machines runs from X = 4.75 m to [the room's right inner face](#laundry-plan), with Z from -3.35 m to the front inner face. Each machine is at most 0.65 m wide, 0.75 m deep, and 0.88 m tall, in the order of the washer at the back and the dryer at the front. The two Z centers are derived at 0.65 m spacing starting from the point half a width in from the back end. These values are not particular product specifications, and the body, closed round door, handle, and rear hose connection clearance are all held inside this occupancy.

The fronts of the two machines face -X. The operating reservation of each round door is at most 0.50 m in -X from the front, with Z limited to inside its own machine width. The work of a person holding a basket in front of an open door is reserved at X = [3.80, 4.25] m, with Z = [-3.35, -2.60] m in front of the washer and Z = [-2.80, -2.05] m in front of the dryer. The depth of the [user body](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) is applied to X and the width including the basket to Z. The work floors of the two machines are used by one person in sequence, and their partly overlapping area is not added as simultaneous independent work. If the real hinges, door diameter, or handles exceed this space, it returns to the machine prototype and placement for correction. Baskets are not set down in the back crossing strip. The machine-front work and the state of another person carrying a basket at the back are observed together.

The folding top is reserved at a top height of 0.94 m in the same plan as the two machine strips. The height is relative to the room's finished floor, and its support members must not hide the machines' round doors. The upper storage runs from X = 5.20 m to the right inner face, with Z the same two machine strips, a bottom height of 1.50 m, and a top of 2.30 m. Projections that interfere with the use of the leaves/machines below are not allowed. No laundry, electrical, or exhaust performance or product installation certification is claimed.

The shoe bench is reserved from the left inner face to X = 3.62 m, with Z from -2.85 m to the front inner face, a seat height of 0.45 m, facing +X. The maximum projection of the hooks above the bench and the hanging coats stays within the same X depth at a height of 1.10–1.85 m. The occupancy for putting on or taking off shoes runs from the bench front to X = 4.25 m, with Z inside the bench width. This floor partly shares the machine-front work occupancy, so putting on shoes and the machine work right in front alternate. The two tasks are not added as independent areas usable at the same time. The number and shape of coats/shoes are decided by later prototypes and placement, but hanging clothes do not cross into the back passage.

The earlier bench reservation facing the front wall was discarded because its body could overlap the open machine-front work, and it was changed to face the left wall. The room outline, doors, and storey datum were kept. The spatial input is consumed by models/instances, and the complete faces of the room are taken by `src/spaces/rooms/laundry.ts`. The machine count, body/operation ranges, folding top, the real occupancy of the bench/coats, the separation of work and crossing, and the interior readability of the small room are checked in the [observation set](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) and are currently unverified.

This route is a floor strip inside the same room connecting the [two entrance doors and the waiting areas on both sides](#laundry-plan). X runs from the room's left inner face to its right inner face, and Z = [-4.32, -3.42] m is kept as a crossing reservation that use occupancy does not intrude on. With each of the two room doors opened 90° as the reference, the 0.90 m width of the [continuous passage width target](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) and the [door effective width target](#laundry-plan) must remain in this strip even behind the door frames, leaf thickness, and handles. So that [the basket working space in front of the washer and the route from the garage into the house do not overlap and block each other](../../settings/10-house.md#laundry-mudroom), the open washer/dryer and machine-front workers are in the [front work zone](#laundry-equipment-use), and the basket carrier passes along this back strip. The two uses are not simply marked in different colors while the real collision is ignored.

The fan swept while a room door rotates is different from the passage reservation. One operates the door and then passes, and the moment someone on the other side opens or closes the door is not claimed as simultaneous passage. Going down from the mudroom upper waiting area to the garage lower waiting area consumes the [existing single step height](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums) and the threshold boundary. The lower waiting area continues to the [garage interior route](garage-interior.md#garage-use-routes) and is not counted in the object storage area. The plan/threshold section of a person holding a basket passing in both directions with both doors open, the state adding each machine's open door and a worker, and the waiting position during door operation are checked. For now this is design input, and the real collisions, rotation, and reachability are unverified.
