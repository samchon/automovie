`service-access` is an open L-shaped circulation of ground-storey. Its inner finish joins the right strip X = [2.02, 3.07]·Z = [-6.05, -0.25] m and the strip behind the stair X = [-1.80, 2.02]·Z = [-6.05, -4.71] m. The right strip is 1.05 m wide and the back strip 1.34 m deep. It goes around the outside of the [stair protective boundary](../02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening) and does not go under the stair.

The segment X = 2.02, Z = [-3.41, -0.25] m meeting the [entry](entry.md#entry-plan) is a connection without a door or full-height partition. The west shared partition of the service rooms is X = [3.07, 3.22] m. The doors of the [powder room](powder.md#powder-plan), [laundry and mudroom](laundry.md#laundry-plan), and [pantry](pantry.md#pantry-plan) are each owned as openings in this partition by their own room owners. Per the [service band setting](../../settings/10-house.md#service-band), one branches from this passage to each room and comes back. The only interior route to the garage goes through the mudroom.

The back consumes the [open connection of the common area](common.md#common-room-plan). The coat closet doors are the sliding doors of the [entry storage](entry.md#entry-coat-storage), so no swinging door projecting into this strip is added. The service-side half X = [1.945, 2.02] m under that owner's `entry-coat-opening` Z segment is finished by this passage owner at Y = [-0.025, 0] m, butting against the entry-side half at the midplane. `src/spaces/rooms/service.ts` is the complete interior owner. The narrow 1.05 m strip is checked against the target [clear width](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) including trim and baseboards, and an edge connected in name only is not read as real passage. The two-way turning of a person/basket at the two turning points and every threshold and the additional observation of the hidden corner of the L are unverified.

The [coat closet use reservation](entry.md#entry-coat-storage) takes part of this passage at the under-stair interface at the back. The areas of a person taking out clothes and a person passing beside are not added as simultaneous passage. With the user out of the way, the clear width behind the remaining sliding door/handle projection and the opposite casing is checked. The existing connections from the entry to the service rooms and the common area stay as they are.
