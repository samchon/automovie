Storage is gathered on the rear wall of the same garage/ground-storey, keeping the central floor and the west mudroom waiting area clear. The plan reservation of the shelves is X = [7.15, 8.85] m, with Z from the [garage rear wall inner face](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) to -5.85 m and a total height of 2.05 m above the garage floor. The top heights of the five shelves are derived from 0.20 m above the garage floor at 0.40 m spacing. Posts, shelf thickness, and boxes/containers stay inside this body and are not stacked forward to reduce the [passage](#garage-use-routes). The floor where a person stands in front of the shelves has X equal to the shelf width and Z = [-5.85, -4.80] m.

The tool workbench is a reservation at X = [9.00, 10.20] m with the same rear wall depth in Z as the shelves, and its top is 0.90 m above the garage floor. The tool drawers below extend at most 0.45 m in the +Z direction, and the human work floor in front of the open drawers is Z = [-5.40, -4.80] m at the same X width. The tool board/hanging tools on the rear wall are in the same X range, within 0.15 m forward from the rear wall, at a height of 1.10–2.10 m above the floor. The tool types, count, and detailed prototypes are later authoring, but no live machine operation or electrical performance is claimed.

These heights are relative to the [garage finished floor](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums), not the main block floor. The shelves/workbench are behind the [upper door travel reservation](../envelope/front.md#garage-front-opening), and no tall side wall storage crossing the right window is placed. The inner depth of the [side fixed window](../envelope/right.md#garage-right-window) and the access including its handle/sill are checked on the internal routes. All bodies, prototypes, repetitions, box/tool placements, and their shadows are not implemented yet. The complete interior owner stays `src/spaces/rooms/garage-interior.ts`, and the real occupancy/clear height/interference and the readability of the empty garage are unverified.

From the mudroom's [garage-side lower waiting area](laundry.md#laundry-plan), one moves to the shelves, workbench, side window, and inside of the closed front door inside the same garage. The west longitudinal route is X = [5.95, 6.95] m, with Z from the [front of the rear wall storage](#garage-storage-use) to -0.95 m. The middle cross route runs in X from the left end of the west longitudinal route to the point set back from the [garage right inner face](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) by the [interior window projection limit](../06-openings.md#external-opening-interface), at Z = [-4.75, -3.85] m. In the design input the overlapping parts of the two routes and the waiting area in front of the door are handed over as connections, and no new room or hidden passage is made. The longitudinal access on the right window side runs from X = 10.35 m to the same inner limit, with Z from the front of the rear wall storage to the front end of the middle cross route.

The [work in front of the shelves/drawers](#garage-storage-use) is behind the cross route above, and the door rails are excluded from the routes at the real occupancy the [front door owner](../envelope/front.md#garage-front-opening) set. The closed front door is checked at the front end of the longitudinal route, and when the front door is opened to go out to the [driveway](../site/driveway.md#driveway-plan), one turns to enter within the real effective width of the front opening. No route going straight out through posts/vertical rails is made. In both door states, [entry and exit through the mudroom](../../settings/10-house.md#garage) are kept.

A plan placing storage bodies, objects, open drawers, workers, and basket movement together, storage/window/ceiling sections relative to the garage floor, the closed/open front panels and rails, and the two-way route crossing the mudroom step are added to the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). Because [the family is a use condition and vehicles are outside the delivery](../../settings/00-production.md#operative-subjects), this is not an instruction to author characters/vehicles but a passage check of the existing [user body](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile). The basic observations of the remaining corners and floor are not omitted either. The real geometry, storage heights, basket turning, poses inside the own space at every corner, and frames are unverified.
