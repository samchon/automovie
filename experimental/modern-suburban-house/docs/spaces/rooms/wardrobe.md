The [rear storage reservation](#primary-wardrobe-plan) of the same primary-wardrobe/upper-storey is used as the hangers/shelves of the [storage setting](../../settings/10-house.md#storage). The hanging segment runs from the left end of the storage to X = 4.25 m, and the folded clothes/shoe shelf segment from X = 4.40 m to the right end. The hanging rod is placed 0.28 m forward from the rear, 1.65 m above the upper finished floor, and the top shelf at 2.05 m. The tops of the four right shelves are derived from 0.20 m above the floor at 0.45 m spacing. The depth including clothes, hangers, and containers must stay inside the existing storage reservation.

From the turning floor inside the door, one follows the front passage to the storage at both ends and returns through the same door. No storage that puts floor baskets or drawers out into the passage is added.
