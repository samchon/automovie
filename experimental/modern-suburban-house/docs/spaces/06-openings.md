The exterior openings consume the [room connections](05-route-network.md#room-route-network) and the [roof heights](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-profile-datums). The opening H2 of each elevation is the world coordinate input of a rough rectangular opening to be made in the real exterior wall. The X or Z interval is the horizontal width, and the Y interval is the world height, not relative to the floor. In the wall thickness direction it passes from the inner face to the outer face of the [main block](00-building.md#main-building-extent)/[garage](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) outline. The ids, positions, and counts planned here are not compiled output.

The opening coordinates of the front, rear, left, and right are owned by the [front](envelope/front.md#front-openings), [rear](envelope/rear.md#rear-openings), [left](envelope/left.md#left-openings), and [right](envelope/right.md#right-openings) elevation owners respectively. Only the existing front-door takes the decision of the [entry](rooms/entry.md#entry-plan) as it is. The room owner consumes the same id and void, takes the inner finish/reveal, and does not copy the window with independent coordinates. The elevation declares the number of vertical divisions of a window group, and the frame/sash/mullion are derived repeatedly from the effective width and the number of divisions. The upper glass in a leaf is a fill member of that leaf and is not counted as a separate exterior wall hole.

The later real wall is generated from the face outline and opening profile of the same boundary. The installed `builtBoundaryWallCut` returns the face and the opening each as an enclosing rectangle, so it can be applied as is only to rectangular walls/voids. The original outline of a gable or concave wall is not replaced with that bounding panel. When passing to `extrudeAutoMovieRegion`, a window lying wholly inside the wall is represented as an inner ring, and a door reaching the wall bottom as an open notch in the outline. Touching outer/hole rings are not input, and a thin fake threshold wall is not left in order to pass. The extrusion is generated on both halves of local Z, but the boundary thickness runs in the local +Z direction, so the real wall interval is matched with the same face's origin and rotation and a shift of half the thickness. This is a handoff condition set after reading the public API, and the real cut and placement result is unverified.

As a common space reservation, the exterior trim is chosen within 0.10 m on the left, right, and top of the rough opening, and within 0.10 m under windows as well. No trim of the same width that blocks the floor is wrapped under entrance doors. The outer face of the window frame is set back 0.04 m into the interior from the exterior wall weather face, and the frame, sash, and glass are placed inside a depth of 0.14 m. This is a reservation that secures the window frame depth inside the wall and is not yet a real member layering. The projection of the sill and handles from the inner finish face is reserved within 0.06 m and included in the check of the use space in front of the window. Glass is not replaced with a black opaque plate.

The vertical windows of the living room, bedrooms, kitchen, and family room are chosen as vertical sliding sashes, the small stair window as fixed, the high bathroom windows as top-hinged with obscured glass, and the garage side as fixed. In the baseline state the exterior doors and all windows are closed. The passage clear width is judged as a derived value of the rough width minus the real occupancy of frames, open leaves, and handles, and a pass is not claimed from the rough width alone. The opening, privacy, and daylighting of windows are unverified until the real members, views, and lighting exist. This layout is not a claim of conformance to egress, ventilation, thermal, or structural codes.

`src/spaces/openings.ts` is planned to provide the common reservations above and the handoff format of rooms and elevations, and does not take the complete elevation geometry. Every real opening and fill member is included in the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The inspection addresses are the section passing through the same exterior wall void from each room's inner reveal, the front and side of the closed fill members, door opening and the waiting area in front of the door, the whole elevations, and all interior observations. The real host/storey/room bindings, voids, members, and GPU readability are unverified.

Only the six windows [living room front](envelope/front.md#living-front-window) W=2.80 m, [living room left](envelope/left.md#living-left-window) W=1.20 m, [family room rear](envelope/rear.md#family-rear-window) W=2.00 m, [family room right](envelope/right.md#family-right-window) W=1.70 m, [second bedroom front](envelope/front.md#bedroom-two-front-window) W=2.10 m, and [third bedroom front](envelope/front.md#bedroom-three-front-window) W=2.10 m are the targets of this H2. The curtain reservations of the two windows of the [primary bedroom](rooms/primary.md#primary-furniture-use) are left as they are. The living room, family room, and small bedroom documents already check the access in front of the curtains and the interior projection, so real curtain prototypes are bound to these windows.

For each window, the inner lower left corner on the room side is the local origin, U is along the window width, V is upward, and N is the interior normal. The horizontal range of the curtain occupancy strip is U=[-0.10,W+0.10] m, its top end is V=0.12 m above the window head, and its bottom end is 0.10 m above the [finished floor datum](01-storeys.md#storey-datums) of that storey. The depth is N=[0,0.12] m from the interior finish face, and the passage clear width including the window frame and handle projections is measured again in later instances. W+0.20 m is 3.00, 1.40, 2.20, 1.90, 2.30, and 2.30 m respectively. This strip is not a real wall or window frame face and does not copy the [window fill prototype](../models/01-windows.md#window-local-frame) or the interior reveal. The curtain member is taken by models, and the position and count of the six hosts by instances. Each window is checked from the front and the interior side for collisions with the ceiling, window frame, handles, furniture, and access routes. The real model source and GPU frames are unverified.
