The interstorey floor between [the finished surfaces of the two storeys](01-storeys.md#storey-datums) is one structure of the upper-storey floor. `src/spaces/floors/upper.ts` generates this common structural substrate once, and the ground-storey ceiling consumes the lower boundary of the same structure. `src/spaces/floors/ground.ts` owns the main block ground floor substrate and generates no second body of the interstorey floor or separate ground-storey ceiling plate. Independent structure boxes are not stacked per room on the ground that the room division of the upper storey differs from that of the lower storey. This assignment does not change the [per-room ownership of complete visual faces](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff).

The visible floor uses the world X/Z origin and U=X, V=Z of the [room interior UV handoff](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff). Even when one floor plate is divided into per-room meshes, the UV phase of the same plane does not restart. The face is cut at the real thresholds of the upper bathrooms and at the stair hole, and the structural section under the floor is not unwrapped into the UV of the visible finish.

Inside the existing interstorey height reservation, 0.015 m is assigned to the lower ceiling finish and 0.025 m to the upper floor finish bundle. The ceiling finish goes upward from the ground-storey finished ceiling, and the floor finish goes downward from the upper-storey finished floor. The structural reservation left between them is 0.270 m, the existing interstorey height minus the two finish reservations. Even when a thin finish material is chosen, the finished surface of the bundle including substrate/underlay is matched to the same datum. No new step is made at room doors on the ground that bathroom tile and hall carpet differ in thickness. This is an authoring choice of spatial occupancy, not a result that calculated timber member sizes, spans, loads, fire resistance, or sound insulation performance. The real structure and materials are composed inside this reservation in later envelope/finish stages, and members that could not carry the load are not replaced with a simple thick plate and claimed complete.

The plan of the structural substrate consumes the [main block inner finish outline](00-building.md#main-building-extent) and continues under ordinary interior partitions too. The one place emptied for passage is the [existing L-shaped stair hole](02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening). That hole is passed with the same boundary to the upper floor finish, the common structure, and the lower ceiling finish. Each room's finish zone consumes its own storey's real room outline and threshold handoff. The inside of shallow storage outside the passage outline is also finished separately in the same 0.025 m layer by the [per-room finish owner](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff), so a logical storage space record does not stand in for the floor plate. The lower storey's room boundaries are not copied onto the upper floor, and the lower ceiling is not divided by the upper storey's room boundaries. The lower waiting area floor of the entry under the stair hole stays on ground-storey as it is, and the main block upper ceiling above the stairwell is kept too. The interstorey hole is not applied wholesale to every floor plate.

This L-shaped empty area reaches the inner front outline, so it is not a closed interior hole of the plan mesh. The outer ring of the remaining floor plate has one notched outline that goes from the front around the stairwell and back out to the front. `triangulateAutoMovieRegion` and `extrudeAutoMovieRegion` reject touching outer/hole rings, so the outline is composed with the existing front end at the same coordinates. No gap is opened to pass, and no floor plate strip is left in front of the stairwell. The same outline is generated in the thickness direction, while the existing ownership of the floor and ceiling finishes and the vertical finish on the stair side is kept. This comparison with the public input conditions does not replace the connectivity, gap, and normal checks of the real floor plate.

Under upper-storey room doors, the [finish handoff at the threshold midplane](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-junctions) is received, and the common structure is not cut again at each door width. The same interstorey substrate continues under the hall linen closet too, and the visible inside of the closet stays with the existing upper-hall owner. The open entry/service connection, the common area openings, and the area under the stair on ground-storey consume the separate substrate of the ground floor owner. This interstorey finish thickness reservation is not carried over to the ground floor. The main block interstorey structure is not extended to the low garage, porch, or terrace either.

The checks are the result of overlaying the two storey plans in the same coordinates, the whole interstorey section, and the upper and lower finish/body census of the segments where room divisions differ. The [existing comparison tolerance](00-building.md#main-building-extent) is applied to the finished heights, the reservation sum, and the stair hole match. The structural body generation responsibility, the finish owners, and the real surface ids are read separately, and this document's assignment table and height arithmetic are not counted as output measurements. The real structure, finish layering, hole, clear height, and frames are unverified.

The [interstorey structure](#interstorey-floor-boundary) reaches [the same inner face of the exterior walls](00-building.md#main-building-extent) inside the main block. The thickness zone of the exterior walls stays with the existing elevation/shared wall owners, and the floor plate edge is not extended past the cladding to expose a strip between the siding or brick. The exterior wall in the height interval between the two storeys is also closed continuously. That segment of the exterior wall is not emptied on the ground that interior floors and ceilings are attached, and conversely the exterior wall body is not extended into the room to reduce the interior clear width. Real structural junctions such as later bearings and joist ends must share one contact boundary between the existing wall owner and the interstorey structure owner inside the same wall reservation, and overlapping solids are not used as evidence of support.

Ordinary interior partitions keep their [per-storey height roles](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-ownership). The tops of lower partitions reach the lower finished ceiling, and the bottoms of upper partitions reach the upper finished floor. The hidden support junctions at the bottoms and tops of partitions are resolved inside the structural reservation, and if a beam or wall comes down below the finished ceiling, the clear height of that room, opening, and route is judged again. A conflict of space inputs is not considered hidden inside the ceiling. A room's visible ceiling/floor closes at its own inner wall faces, and the whole plan of the substrate is not used as is as the visible finish.

Where an interior structure continuing up to the height between the two storeys, such as the [separation wall behind the stair](02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights), meets the interstorey substrate, the overlapping body zone is generated only once by the upper floor plate owner and subtracted from that wall body. This is a division of the junction body, not an instruction to delete the height role of the rear separation wall or the upper hall railing. The wall/floor plate section finish visible from the stairwell in front of it is joined as one continuous face by the stair owner, and two finishes are not stacked at the same height. The exterior wall thickness zone keeps the exterior wall owner assignment of the previous paragraph.

The vertical thickness section of the stair hole consumes the common structural edge of `src/spaces/floors/upper.ts`, while the continuous finish visible from the stairwell is taken by `src/spaces/stair.ts`. The lower and upper ends of the rim board are set at the wall top or horizontal finish section that really meets each straight segment. At an edge without a room ceiling, the top of a ceiling is not assumed as the junction point.

The horizontal finish of the rear protection strip reaches to Z=[-4.71,-4.56] m and also covers once the narrow top X=[-1.80,1.87] m·Z=[-4.575,-4.56] m above the rim board. The board thickness of 0.015 m is placed set in toward the remaining floor plate, and the structural substrate steps back by that much. The lower 0.015 m on the west and back butts against the top of the lower wall, and the upper 0.025 m at the front linen corner butts against the bottom of the upper partition. The other segments butt face to face with the lower ceiling Y=[2.75,2.765] m and the upper floor Y=[3.035,3.06] m and share no volume. The finished boundary of the [original passage hole](02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening) is not narrowed inward by the board thickness. At convex/concave corners and at the end reaching the front exterior wall, the same boundary is shared, and the room finish owners do not overlay a separate rim. Segments where a protective wall already covers the section meet the [height-specific boundaries of the stair](02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights), and no hidden overlapping decorative strip is made.

The last riser of the upper flight meets the floor end of the [existing upper hall arrival](rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-hall-plan). The common structure at that end is taken by the upper floor plate owner, the visible hall floor by the upper-hall owner, and the last riser and stair handrail by the stair owner. The arrival floor is not stacked once more as a separate stair tread, and no thin gap is left. The protective walls/railings needed around the rest of the stair hole are not extended to the open arrival end. The garage-side shared wall keeps the [existing garage structure owner](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent), and the garage interior ceiling or the mudroom step is not turned into a main block interstorey edge.

The inspection addresses are the whole perimeter where the exterior walls meet the interstorey structure, the shared wall contact, the top and bottom contacts of every interior partition, each turn, the front end, and the upper arrival section of the stair hole, and two-way stair/hall views. These are added to the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation), and the real overlaps, gaps, exposed floor plate strips, blocked arrivals, and headroom after finishing are read. Structural safety, expression in the public engine, the real surface census, member junctions, and GPU frames are unverified.
