The outline choice of these spaces follows [scale](../settings/10-house.md#house-scale) and [coordinates](../settings/00-production.md#coordinate-units). The main block exterior-face reference is X = [-5.75, 5.75] m, Z = [-10.70, 0] m. Both storeys use the same rectangular outline. The product of the 11.50 m width and the 10.70 m depth is 123.05㎡ per storey, and 246.10㎡ for both storeys together. This is arithmetic of the authoring input, not a compiled area measurement. The garage, porch, and site are not included in this value. The front gable is made as a crossing shape of the roof and does not project the room floor.

The spatial reservation value of the exterior wall thickness is 0.25 m. The inner finish limit of the main block is X = [-5.50, 5.50] m, Z = [-10.45, -0.25] m. Later member design sets the structure and cladding assembly inside this reservation value, and does not encroach on the inner limit on the ground of adding finish thickness. Interior partitions reserve 0.15 m. The effective dimensions of a room must be read between final inner faces, not as wall centerline spacing.

The basis for choosing the 11.50 m width is room to place the left living room, the central stair and entry, and the right service access and small service rooms together. The 10.70 m depth is the range for holding the front living zone and the rear continuous common area. Room division and door positions are written in the per-room owners that the [circulation handoff](05-route-network.md#room-route-network) connects. Whether door operation, fixture occupancy, and the real areas/boundaries fit inside this reservation is still unverified. The space graph is governed by [the settings entry](../settings/10-house.md#entry), [service band](../settings/10-house.md#service-band), and [upper hall](../settings/10-house.md#upper-hall), and this outline choice grants no authority to change that graph.

The verification address is the main block outline and two storeys of the future `src/spaces/building.ts`, and the area and boundary report that will consume its output. The needed observations are the two storey plans, the front, rear, left, and right elevations, and a central stair section. The area tolerance is chosen as 0.01㎡ in the comparison of the authoring input arithmetic with the output outline, and the boundary match tolerance as 0.001 m. These are not claims of survey precision or construction tolerance.

The [empty garage](../settings/10-house.md#garage) attaches to the right of the main block as one single-storey volume. The outline is chosen as X = [5.50, 11.70] m, Z = [-6.70, -0.30] m. The 6.20 m width and 6.40 m depth are inside the garage outline range. The front is set back 0.30 m from the main block and does not pass the main block rear. The wall the main block and garage share is one 0.25 m structural reference inside X = [5.50, 5.75] m. The overlap of the two outlines is not an instruction to draw two overlapping walls; it means the area boundaries on both sides reference one shared wall boundary. Per [surface assignment](03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff), the body with the door below the garage roof is `garage.ts` and the exposed siding body above the roof is `envelope/right.ts`, meeting by height on the same X/Z reference.

The other garage exterior walls also use the 0.25 m reservation. The 5.70 m × 5.90 m obtained from the inner finish X = [5.75, 11.45] m, Z = [-6.45, -0.55] m is the reserved clear interior before furniture and door rails are added. It is not used as a measured value of clearance after storage installation or of two-car parking performance. No vehicle is authored. The garage front opening and the mudroom door must each be authored as a real void in the front face and the shared wall. The mudroom door's coordinates and waiting space take the decision of [laundry and mudroom](rooms/laundry.md#laundry-plan). The direct interface between the garage door to the house and the mudroom that the [service band](../settings/10-house.md#service-band) requires is not paid by merely naming an arbitrary service passage the mudroom.

The failures to verify are a double thickness of the shared wall or a gap at roof height, a garage door connected outside the mudroom, a detached garage, and interior observation possible only by removing the closed front door. The garage floor, ceiling, rails, and storage are the completion scope of later stages, and this outline declaration does not claim their implementation now. The shared wall is one boundary consumed by height by `src/spaces/garage.ts` below the roof and `src/spaces/envelope/right.ts` above it, and `src/spaces/envelope/front.ts` owns the whole front including the garage front.
