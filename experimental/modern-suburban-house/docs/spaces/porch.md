`front-porch` is an exterior annex space of ground-storey. It keeps the [existing floor/approach heights and the riser heights and tread depth of the three steps](01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums). The floor outline is X = [-5.75, 2.20], Z = [0, 2.20] m. Behind it is the [main block front](00-building.md#main-building-extent), in front the walking approach, and above the porch roof below. The interior entry door consumes [front-door](rooms/entry.md#entry-plan). One enters the entry from the porch without passing through the living room.

The entry stair width is 1.50 m, placed aligned to the center X of the entry door opening. The three 0.15 m risers rise from the existing -0.45 m approach to the porch floor. There are two separate horizontal treads, each 0.30 m deep, and the last riser arrives at the porch floor. The porch floor is not generated twice as a third independent tread. In front of the first riser is a flat waiting area of the same width and 1.20 m deep. The overall Z position is derived in reverse from the porch front end and the tread depth.

The front is chosen as an open porch like reference 01, with no exterior railing. This is not a choice to omit the [interior stair railing](02-stair.md#stair-clearance). The low planter and mat are placed outside the entry width. The [entry walk](site/front-walk.md#front-walk-plan) consumes the lower flat waiting area set in the previous paragraph to reach the first riser, owning the waiting area and the walk as one complete face. `src/spaces/porch.ts` owns the porch floor, risers, and treads and does not generate the lower waiting area floor twice. The ground finish and the connection to the exterior sidewalk are incomplete. The axis of the entry door, three steps, and lower waiting area, the way back down from the top, and the porch edges with their grounding and shadows are unverified.

The floor top plate, edge support walls, step bodies, and the support under the three columns consume the [raised flat surface section](site/01-paving-support.md#raised-platform-support). They keep the existing top, step count, and clear width while matching the substrate underside of the lower waiting area, and that junction reference is not read as the real ground or foundation depth.

On the [porch floor](#porch-platform-access), three columns are placed at 3.65 m spacing from center X = -5.40 m. The column shaft width/depth is 0.25 m, and the base/capital width/depth is 0.35 m. The common center Z is chosen as 1.975 m, the existing effective depth of 1.80 m plus half the base depth. 1.80 m is preserved up to the inner face of the widest base, and the clear depth is not claimed against the shafts alone. The base is Y = [0, 0.15], the shaft Y = [0.15, 2.33], and the capital Y = [2.33, 2.45] m. Coordinates are derived repeatedly from the column count and spacing.

The reservation of the front beam is X = [-5.75, 2.20], Z = [1.85, 2.10], Y = [2.45, 2.70] m. The porch weather face is `P(Z) = 3.50 - (1/4) × Z`, and the underside is the face 0.22 m lower than P in the Y direction. The roof outline is X = [-6.10, 2.55], Z = [0, 2.35] m. The back, meeting the main block front, has a wall junction, and the short back edge outside the main block left outline is closed with a free edge thickness. Between the beam top and the roof underside, bearing members derived from that spacing are placed so the roof does not float above the beam.

This roof covers the living room window and the entry door together and does not pass the garage front. The upper windows and trim of the [front window layout](envelope/front.md#front-openings) stay above the roof-wall junction, and the entrance door head and the height under the porch beam are checked in the same section. The lowest structural point of the clear height is read again including the beam, capitals, and bases. The long roof is not replaced with a plate without thickness. The capacity, material layering, and junction details of the structural section are incomplete in later stages.

The complete surface owner of the roof, underside, columns, capitals, bases, and beam is in every case `src/spaces/porch.ts`. The inspection addresses are the whole front, both end corners, the entrance axis section, and four directions under the porch. The real roof/column junctions, column overlaps with the stair/door, and material and shadow readability are unverified.

The three white columns keep the shaft, capital, and base at each center X=−5.40+3.65k m (k=0,1,2), Z=1.975 m as separate UV members. Each vertical face starts U along the real horizontal width and V along the Y height from its own lower left corner, and breaks the seam at the four corners and at section changes. The exposed horizontal ledges of the capitals and bases start U=+X and V=+Z from each member's small-X, small-Z corner and are cut at the shaft contact line and the outer corners. The front beam starts the length U=+X from its left end at X=−5.75 m and V=Y along the height of the beam side. The beam underside starts U=+X and V=−Z at (X,Z)=(−5.75,2.10) m, and the two end faces start U=+Z and V=+Y at Z=1.85 m, Y=2.45 m, breaking at the side and underside corners. Each bearing member restarts the real length U and width V from its own lower left corner touching the beam top. The white underside of the porch roof takes X=−6.10 m and the free front eave Z=2.35 m as origin, with U=+X and V as the real slope distance rising toward the wall in −Z. The exposed fascia uses the 3D length U from the lexicographically smaller (X,Z) end of each free edge and the vertical thickness of 0.22 m from the weather face to the underside as V, and is cut at the main block wall junction and the beam/column junctions. The world-coordinate UV of the upper roof shingles and the separate paving UV of the porch platform are not copied onto these white faces.
