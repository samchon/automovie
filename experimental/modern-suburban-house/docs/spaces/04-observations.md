Receiving the [observation allocation](../settings/20-verification.md#observation-allocation), the [complete observation denominator](../contracts/observation-denominator.md#compiled-denominator) is applied as is to this spaces layer. No fixed view count is declared from the current outline and storey reservations. After the real space, boundary, opening, and roof records are made, the questions of the exterior setting, every exposed elevation and the corners where they meet, the roof and its underside, and every opening and entrance are derived from the same output. For each room, one threshold, four inner corners, and four directions from the center are generated as poses inside its own space, and the hidden parts of non-rectangular shapes add questions. The 01–05 reference questions are added to this denominator.

A room's engine station is the starting point that sets the identifier and direction of a question, not proof of a place where a person can stand. An in-room pose is compared against the footprint of a use-profile person 0.60 m wide and 0.45 m deep standing in the original gaze direction and a height of 1.90 m. The corners of that footprint must lie inside its own space and must not overlap furniture, fixture, and storage bodies or door/drawer swing reservations. Covers and use/passage reservations stay as walking surfaces. If the original station breaks this condition, candidates at 0.10 m spacing within the range of that space's cell are checked together with the original point, the valid candidate closest to the original point is chosen, and the cause of the move is recorded. The four center directions keep their gaze direction, and corners and thresholds keep a gaze that reads the original target and their own room. The same question is not removed or moved to another room. If there is no valid candidate, it is a failure that includes the question id and is not completed as an empty observation list.

The numeric checks use two storey plans, stair, mudroom threshold, and porch sections, four-direction elevations, and the roof joining boundaries. Every room has a storey, real boundaries, an entrance opening, and a hall or stair route, and both the way from the entry to the destination and the way back are checked. A pass is not gained by shrinking the person and basket sizes of use-profile. The clear width after door, furniture, and appliance occupancy enters must be read again from the final output. Per [the responsibility of measurement and frames](../settings/20-verification.md#data-authority), the real topology and route checks are currently unverified.

The [interior shared boundaries](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-ownership) add the two counterpart spaces and the single structure/both-side finish responsibility, and the [intersections, openings, and thresholds](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-junctions) add height-specific sections and the in-room views of both rooms for every real junction. They find duplicate bodies, gaps at intersections, blocked walls behind doors, and floor holes under doors that are easy to miss when each room is viewed separately. They also check the case in which the stair's protective boundary was made into a blocked wall two storeys high from plan overlap alone. The design's owner table is not a substitute for a real boundary/surface census.

In the same opening handoff, the whole supporting interface and contents of wall attachments and the door/window voids, frames, and open members are placed together on interior elevations and height-specific sections. They find a towel bar that passed on the room floor plan alone but crosses a door hole, or an upper cabinet or mirror that blocks a window. The [tub bathroom towel bar](rooms/tub-bath.md#tub-fixture-use) reads the real closed wall behind the door, the projection including the hanging towel, and the occupancy during use and during passage. Support or passage is not passed on declared room bindings alone, and the current result without real members is unverified.

The [height-specific boundaries of the stair](02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights) compare in the same section the open railing of the lower flight, the closed wall of the upper bedroom, the hall fall edge, and the open end of the arrival with the coat closet below it. Handrail height and hall protection height are distinguished, and the real headroom and clear width behind members are read at each tread. The mere name of a protective member is not counted as confirming passage or the fall boundary.

The [interstorey floor](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary) compares in plan and section the different room divisions of the two storeys, one structural substrate, the finished surfaces on both sides, and the same stair hole. The [edge junctions](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions) add the whole exterior wall perimeter, the shared wall, the tops and bottoms of partitions, the hole turns and front end, and the open upper arrival. They find two duplicated floor plates, a substrate broken per room, a floor plate projecting between the cladding, a stair hole narrowed by finish, and an arrival plate stacked on the last riser. Structural substrate and visual face ownership are distinguished, and the clear height and headroom after the real members are read again.

The [main block top ceiling](09-ceiling-assembly.md#upper-ceiling-closure) adds the reflected ceiling plans/sections of the whole upper storey and the stairwell, and the [garage ceiling](09-ceiling-assembly.md#garage-ceiling-closure) the front door rails and the four wall contacts. The [clearance under the low roof](09-ceiling-assembly.md#ceiling-roof-clearance) reads together, up to the real members, the interior end lines, corners, roof steps, the space above the stair, and the garage shared wall. They find a stairwell cut through to the roof, a low ceiling blocking the stair, collisions of ceiling with roof or rails, and missing or duplicated ceiling strips above storage and railings. The design formula's clearance does not replace real measurement, and every per-room basic observation is kept.

The [roof contact across the whole exterior wall thickness](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-wall-head-junctions) adds sections through the outer, middle, and inner parts of the front and rear walls and the gable intersection lines. The [single body of exterior wall corners/steps](07-boundary-assembly.md#exterior-boundary-junctions) is read together in those sections and in height-specific plans. They find a gap left inside where only the outer elevation line matches, a wall raised to the inner height that pierces the roof, overlapping bodies of the step and the front and rear walls, and a main block shared wall cut off at the garage roof. With no real members, this check is currently unverified and does not replace the existing complete exterior/interior observations.

The [main block ground substrate](10-ground-floor.md#main-ground-floor-base) and the [garage substrate](10-ground-floor.md#garage-ground-floor-base) add the whole floor plan, the feet of partitions, stair, and storage, and the finish transition sections. The [four building entrance sections](10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions) read the support substrate, door frame, and finished floor together at the center and at both jambs. After the real ground is received, the building perimeter, the shared wall, and the exterior paving contacts of the [support bottom handoff](10-ground-floor.md#ground-support-handoff) are checked. They find a ground floor into which the interstorey hole was wrongly copied, an empty strip or overlapping wall under a door, a missing mudroom step, a substrate floating in the air, and an entrance buried in the ground. The current state without a ground is not counted as a grounding pass.

The [entry distribution](rooms/entry.md#entry-use-routes) compares the opening of two room doors, the mat, and the stair waiting area; the [coat closet](rooms/entry.md#entry-coat-storage) the objects/sliding doors, the user, and the service band; and the [living room seating](rooms/living.md#living-furniture-use) and [front-to-back route](rooms/living.md#living-through-route) the fireplace projection, table, seating and bookcase use, and the two windows, each in plan/section and from viewpoints inside its own room. They distinguish door operation from passage and reading-chair use from crossing the bookcase, and do not narrow the main front-to-back route by seating occupancy. The entry, living room, and stair relations of 02/04 and all the basic complete viewpoints are kept.

The [use/passage reservations of the common area](rooms/common.md#common-clear-routes) add a plan with closed appliances and pulled-out dining chairs and stools, the work in front of each of the open refrigerator, oven, and dishwasher, and sections of the sink island, rear window, and upper cabinets. The route from the central service entrance to the family room and garden door, and the route from the living room through the kitchen to the same rear, are compared in both directions. The union of per-seat occupancy is distinguished from its whole bbox, and the real appliance doors, handles, and curtains are read. A place blocked by appliance work is not counted twice as main circulation, and the six usable dining seats are not reduced to pass.

The [laundry work](rooms/laundry.md#laundry-equipment-use), [mudroom crossing](rooms/laundry.md#laundry-through-route), [powder room use](rooms/powder.md#powder-fixture-use), and [garage storage/routes](rooms/garage-interior.md#garage-use-routes) are included in the same check of going from the service passage to each use point and back. The moment of operating a room door, the state of passing through with the room door open, and the state of working with appliances or drawers open are distinguished. Shared area, such as putting on shoes at the bench and laundry work right in front of it, is not counted as simultaneous use. The plans/sections and interior views of the two laundry machines with their doors open, basket work and passage, the mudroom step, toilet/washbasin access, the garage shelves and tools, the front panels/rails, and the side window are added, and the basic questions of the small service rooms and the empty garage are kept too.

The [pantry shelves/food](rooms/pantry.md#pantry-storage-use) and the [use passage with the door open](rooms/pantry.md#pantry-use-route) are compared in plans/sections that place the leaf, handle, L-shaped corner, shelf lips, and contents together. Operating the door is distinguished from entering, taking things out, and turning around, and one returns to the kitchen through the same door and service passage. A nominal passage width or empty shelves alone do not pass real retrieval and turning. The basic complete interior viewpoints of the pantry and the functional readability of a small storage room are kept too.

On the upper storey, the [primary bedroom](rooms/primary.md#primary-furniture-use) with the bed sides and foot, two windows, open drawers, and wardrobe door; the [olive bedroom](rooms/bedroom-two.md#bedroom-two-furniture-use) and [blue-gray bedroom](rooms/bedroom-three.md#bedroom-three-furniture-use) with door/bed, pulled-out chair, closet, and window; the [shower bathroom](rooms/shower-bath.md#shower-fixture-use) with the room door, sliding glass, and three fixtures; and the [tub bathroom](rooms/tub-bath.md#tub-fixture-use) with the left passage, window operation, curtain, and fixtures are compared in plan/section and from views inside their own rooms. The [wardrobe storage](rooms/wardrobe.md#wardrobe-storage-use) and the [linen closet](rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-linen-storage) distinguish real object depth from occupancy during operation and during passage. One goes from the stair arrival to each room's destination and back, and the basic observations and the additional questions of 02 and 05 are all kept.

The [site internal access](site/00-access.md#site-local-routes) adds plans/sections of the porch axis of the walk, the T intersection and the driveway connection, the garage threshold and the road-side end, and the garden door waiting area, the central terrace route, the exterior steps, and the lower waiting area. It includes the threshold, corner, and center directions of the exterior paving zones and observes two-way views going from the front sidewalk into the house and from the common area out to the garden and back. Maps boundaries, ground, and road connections that do not exist yet are not counted as passed edges. Occupancy after planting and furniture and the questions of every real exposed face are added as they are, and the denominator is not fixed by the contacts listed here alone.

The [side service walk](site/side-walk.md#side-walk-plan) adds the height interpolation of the driveway connection, the merging of the three strips, and the end line with the waiting area under the terrace, and the [gate](site/side-walk.md#side-gate-interface) adds closed, door operation, 90° open, and the front and back waiting areas. The basic viewpoints of each of the front and back exterior zones and the hidden corner of the turn are kept, and the routes carrying a basket from the driveway to the terrace and from the terrace back to the driveway are compared. The fact that ground, fence, and planting do not exist yet is not interpreted as an empty obstacle list or a finished garden.

The [exterior paving thickness](site/01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) adds not only the end lines of the sloped connecting path but also its intermediate sections and underside, and the [raised porch/terrace](site/01-paving-support.md#raised-platform-support) the whole outline, both sides of the steps, and the support under the columns. The [contact with other substrates](site/01-paving-support.md#paving-contact-handoff) reads together both sides of the driveway, the low waiting area/service walk, the building thresholds, and the gate post foundations. They find thin floating plates, support walls rising above the top, duplicate bodies at merges, step heights or walking surfaces changed to fit the substrate, and paving cut off by a foundation. The current state without ground or foundations is not a grounding result.

Once the [maps input handoff](site/00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs) is realized, a plan of parcel/building/paving containment at the same revision, full-width sections of the two front ports, and ground contact sections of the low paving edges and the sides of the raised terrace are added. They find places where the ground drops away under the eaves or overlaps the paving, ground that buries steps, and places where only the node point matches while the port width or height differs. The route going from the exterior sidewalk into the two ports and back can be checked only after a real map connection exists and is currently unverified. These junction questions are added to the existing complete exterior/interior denominator and the five reference questions.

The [fence closure](site/fence.md#fence-enclosure-plan) adds a plan that shows the whole line together with the building envelope, inside and outside views of every long side and turn, and sections of the [remaining panels beside the service gate and the wall contacts](site/fence.md#fence-gate-junction) and of the [ground height and foundation occupancy](site/fence.md#fence-ground-profile). It confirms that the gate is in a real void, that short panels were not dropped or end posts put inside walls or paving, and whether branches or shrubs hide junction defects. The closed boundary is distinguished from the two-way service walk passage after opening. Every visible face of the fence is included from the real output and is not replaced with one front view.

The camera conditions of each observation pose, such as eye height, field of view, and near, consume the [frame conditions](../settings/20-verification.md#frame-condition), and if a viewpoint was moved inward into its own space because of a boundary, the reason is recorded with the pose. What this H2 hands over is the derivation rule of the questions and the poses inside their own spaces. The rendering, run, and port conditions of the viewer that shows those observations are owned by the expression and verification conditions of settings and are not set in this space document. The space source that draws the house and its GPU frames do not exist yet, so these observations are unverified.

The installed public engine's `lowerBuiltEnvironment` validates the environment, lowers only elements that have a real model into the set of world transforms, and also preserves the original built environment. Logical room, boundary, and opening declarations alone produce no visible walls, floors, or windows and doors. Therefore each complete face these space documents set must be made as an element with a model, from the same authoring input, by the same source owner of the [elevation, roof, and storey surface](03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff) and [room interior face](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff) assignments, and no house copied with separate coordinates is kept. A non-rectangular space uses one representation of `IAutoMovieBuiltSpace`, a union of convex cells or a closed shell, and its whole bbox is not turned into the room volume. For now this is a condition read from the public engine source, and there is no environment input, call, or render result.

A space's standable surface is a floor declaration for movement and observation checks, not a floor plate or site visible in the [real 3D render](../settings/20-verification.md#renderer-boundary). Visible floors are made by the elements of the [ground substrate](10-ground-floor.md#main-ground-floor-base), the [interstorey floor](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary), and each room and paving owner. The members of those faces are not omitted on the ground that a standable surface exists. The viewer's render conditions, including camera, lighting, shadows, and materials, are owned by settings and are not set in this space document.

Even when `builtEnvironmentBuildingCensus` and `builtSpaceObservationStations` are used, the [complete denominator](#spatial-observation-derivation) is not reduced. The latter derives from the space volume the four center directions, four viewpoints moved inward from the bbox corners, and the threshold of each boundary opening, and can return null when it finds no interior pose. Null or viewpoints gathered at the same place are not counted as successful observations. Real inner turns missed in this house's L-shaped stairwell, hall, and the like are added from the same compiled boundaries, and each pose's position inside its own space must be confirmed. This document does not declare in advance the count or coordinates of helper results or claim that the four default corners answered every concave corner. The real calls, complete observation, and comparison with the five references are unverified.

The [circulation handoff](05-route-network.md#room-route-network) separates boundary adjacency from real human passage. On the stair, the [three stops of the single connector](02-stair.md#stair-connector-handoff), the route position that reached the landing and the real landing surface, and the per-step members are read together. The public landing verification checks the reference id and the at range but does not guarantee that the point lies inside the target space, so a misplaced landing is not passed on a valid id alone. A connector without a steps tally is not reported as a stair whose per-step check is done. Real space containment, support, door operation, dimensions, and the current result without frames are all unverified.
