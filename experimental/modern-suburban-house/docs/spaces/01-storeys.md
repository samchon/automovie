Receiving the [coordinate reference](../settings/00-production.md#coordinate-units), the finished floor of `ground-storey` is set at Y = 0 m and `upper-storey` at Y = 3.06 m. The main block has only these two storeys. The garage and the entry porch are annex spaces of ground-storey, and the space inside the roof is not made into a third storey that people use. Each room's space record must reference its storey directly, and storey membership is not omitted on the ground that the room is a child of the whole building.

The ground-storey finished ceiling is Y = 2.75 m and the upper-storey finished ceiling is Y = 5.66 m. The interior clear heights are 2.75 m and 2.60 m respectively, inside the [main block height range](../settings/10-house.md#main-mass). The 0.31 m between the ground-storey ceiling and the upper finished floor is the reservation for the whole ceiling, floor structure, and finish. This declaration is not a result that verified the detailed layering or structural safety. `src/spaces/storeys.ts` owns the datums once, and each room, opening, stair, and floor plate consumes those values.

The shared upper floor and the ground-storey ceiling need the same stair passage hole. The hole's plan consumes the [stair hole owner](02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening), and the finishes on either side do not independently make different holes. The [single interstorey assembly](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary) sets the structure/finish occupancy inside this height reservation and the ownership of one body, and the [edge junctions](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions) connect the exterior walls, interior walls, and upper arrival. The effective stair headroom under the hole is checked in a real section against the 2.00 m target. Turning the empty space into a double-height void by cutting through to the roof is not allowed.

The needed checks are an output comparison of every space's storey reference, the two storeys' finished-surface heights, the corresponding holes in ceiling and floor plate, and the stair's upper arrival height. The tolerance for height and junction comparison is 0.001 m. The real storey records and boundary outputs do not exist yet, so these checks are unverified.

The [top ceiling](09-ceiling-assembly.md#upper-ceiling-closure) consumes this document's upper-storey ceiling datum to close over the rooms and the stairwell. Its owner assigns the substrate/finish occupancy and does not copy the holes and layering of the interstorey structure. The [roof comparison](09-ceiling-assembly.md#ceiling-roof-clearance) uses the roof underside above the interior outline.

The porch floor is Y = 0 m, the same as the ground-storey finished floor of the entry. The walk in front of the porch is reserved at Y = -0.45 m, and one climbs up by three steps 0.15 m high. The horizontal depth of each tread is chosen as 0.30 m, with flat waiting surfaces before and after the three steps. The effective porch depth is 1.80 m outward from the front wall. The 0.45 m rise and 1.80 m effective depth are choices inside the 0.35–0.55 m rise and 1.6–2.0 m effective depth range of the [entry porch](../settings/10-house.md#porch-entry). The approach in front of the steps is connected by the [entry walk](site/front-walk.md#front-walk-plan), which consumes the first riser position and this front walk height; contact with the exterior sidewalk is reviewed later together with the site input, and slope or step count is not back-calculated from reference pixels.

The garage finished floor is chosen as Y = -0.15 m and the garage ceiling as Y = 2.55 m. The mudroom uses Y = 0 m of ground-storey. In front of the door from the garage into the mudroom, a flat waiting area on the garage side and one step 0.15 m high are reserved. Leaf operation and the waiting areas on both sides are set by [laundry and mudroom](rooms/laundry.md#laundry-plan), and the crossing with a basket by [mudroom crossing](rooms/laundry.md#laundry-through-route), each consuming this step; whether those motions collide at the step edge is unverified because there is no real section. A plan connection alone is not recorded as a passage at the same height.

The source responsibility of this item is `src/spaces/porch.ts` for the porch and `src/spaces/garage.ts` for the garage, and `src/spaces/storeys.ts` provides the canonical datum values. The exterior walk and driveway must contact the site input. The needed observations are a front entry section, a garage/mudroom threshold section, and two-way entry views, and the real passage and fixture interference results are unverified.

The upper boundary of the garage is set by the [garage ceiling closure](09-ceiling-assembly.md#garage-ceiling-closure) between this ceiling datum and its own roof/walls. The main block interstorey floor or the exterior porch roof does not stand in for the garage ceiling.

The [main block ground substrate](10-ground-floor.md#main-ground-floor-base) and the [garage substrate](10-ground-floor.md#garage-ground-floor-base) are each reserved downward from their finished heights. The [building entrance section](10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions) assigns the support inside the wall thickness and the existing single mudroom step, and the [support bottom](10-ground-floor.md#ground-support-handoff) is incomplete until the real ground input is received.
