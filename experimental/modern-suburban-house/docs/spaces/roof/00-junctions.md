The [roof group of the settings](../../settings/10-house.md#main-mass) is laid out while keeping the [main block](../00-building.md#main-building-extent) and [garage](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) outlines and the [two storey ceilings](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The ridges of the main block main roof and the low right roof run in the X direction, and the ridge of the front-left gable in the Z direction. The right roof is a part lowering inside the main block and makes no independent building, additional storey, or floor projection. The garage has a lower X-direction gable roof above its own single-storey outline.

The plane dividing the main roof and the right roof is X = 1.60 m. The common ridge plane Z of the two roofs is derived at the middle of the main block front and rear exterior walls. The main roof covers from the main block left side to this division plane, and the low roof from the division plane to the main block right side. Both ends of the front gable on the wall reference are X = [-5.75, -1.80] m, and the front wall is the main block's Z = 0 m. The center is the average of the two ends. This width is a choice to cover the front living room/left child's room and to keep the [small stair window](../02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening) to its right, [a smaller window for the stair/hall](../../settings/10-house.md#openings), in a separate front segment.

The Z of the garage ridge is the average of the [garage front and rear exterior walls](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent). On the west side touching the main block, the roof joins at the outer face X = 5.75 m of the shared wall, and the roof plate is not pushed into the main block interior. The front porch is covered by [its own roof/column owner](../porch.md#porch-roof-columns). This layout is the authoring input of the mass to read from outside, and the real silhouette comparison is unverified.

Here a height function means the Y of the topmost roof weather face. The rise over a horizontal run of 12 is used as the slope. The main block main roof is chosen as 8/12, the front gable as 9/12, and the low right roof as 7/12. The angles are `atan(8/12)`, `atan(9/12)`, and `atan(7/12)` respectively, authoring choices inside the 30–38° range of the settings. The garage's 5/12 is the choice for a [low annex roof](../../settings/10-house.md#garage) and is not mixed with the main block pitch range. The final angles and heights are read again from the source output.

| Roof | Weather face Y at the exterior wall line, m | Free edge overhang, m | Vertical reserved thickness of the underside, m |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main roof | 6.30 | 0.40 | 0.24 |
| Front gable | Consumes the main roof's exterior wall line height | Cut by the intersection line within the 0.40 outer zone | 0.24 |
| Main block right low roof | 5.95 | 0.40 | 0.24 |
| Garage | 2.95 | 0.35, no overhang at the main block junction | 0.24 |

The front face of the main block main roof is `Mfront(Z) = 6.30 - (8/12) × Z`, and the back face `Mback(Z) = 6.30 + (8/12) × (Z + 10.70)`. The low roof uses `Rfront` and `Rback`, substituting a height of 5.95 and a slope of 7/12 at the same front/back wall and ridge positions. The garage front face is `Gfront(Z) = 2.95 - (5/12) × (Z + 0.30)`, and the back face `Gback(Z) = 2.95 + (5/12) × (Z + 6.70)`. Each roof divides at a common ridge where the front and back heights are equal. The wall coordinates of the formulas are not independent inputs but notations substituting the values of the [main block](../00-building.md#main-building-extent)/[garage](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) outline owners, and when an outline changes they are recomputed from that owner.

For the front gable, with the left end of its wall reference interval as `a` and the right end as `b`, `F(X) = 6.30 + (9/12) × min(X - a, b - X)`. The two faces meet at the gable center, and the same formula is extended over the front eave zone. The slope underside is the plane 0.24 m lower in the Y direction than the corresponding weather face. This value is a vertical reserved thickness and is not confused with a material thickness perpendicular to the roof. The real layering of structure, boards, and roofing is implemented inside the reservation by later envelope/module stages.

The plans of the main/right roofs apply only the free edge overhang to the [assigned zones](#roof-mass-allocation) and add no overhang at the X division plane. The Z zone of the two roofs runs from the main block rear wall to the back overhang and from the front wall to the front overhang, divided front/back at the common ridge. The candidate zone of the front gable is X = [a - e, b + e], Z = [main block ridge Z, main block front Z + e], where e is the front gable overhang value of the table. The intersection formula below is applied only in this finite zone, so F is not extended to the main block back roof. The garage also sets its zone by its own front and rear walls and free overhang, and ends on the west at the outer face of the shared wall.

The underside input at the lowest main block exterior wall line is 5.95 - 0.24 m, so it leaves 0.05 m above the [upper ceiling](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The underside at the garage exterior wall line is also reserved above the [garage ceiling](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums). The underside of an eave projecting outward can be lower but does not intrude into the interior ceiling. This is arithmetic of the formulas, and the real member headroom, structural safety, and construction verification are unverified.

The [upper occupancy of the ceiling substrate](../09-ceiling-assembly.md#ceiling-roof-clearance) is compared with this underside above the real interior outline, not the exterior wall outer line. The 0.05 m of the previous paragraph is arithmetic between the finished ceiling and the exterior wall line, not a value claiming to accommodate the ceiling substrate thickness too. The ceiling owner consumes the same roof functions of the main block/garage and invents no separate pitch or eave height.

The [front gable](#roof-mass-allocation) joins the front of the main roof. In that outline zone, only the part where `F(X) > Mfront(Z)` shows as gable slope, and the rest is the main roof front face. Two roof plates are not overlaid at the same position. `Z = -(9/8) × min(X - a, b - X)`, where the heights are equal, is the same boundary of both valleys. The gable ridge runs from the front overhang end to the center point of this contour boundary, and behind that no unnecessary plate buried in the main roof is left.

The equality is not rounded separately on each face, and the same segments/vertices of `src/spaces/roof/junctions.ts` are consumed. The main ridge, the front gable ridge, the two valleys, the free eaves and gable rake edges, the main block right step, and the garage wall junction are preserved as different boundary roles. No horizontal trim blocking the valley drainage end or fake eave crossing the gable triangle wall is made.

The eight slope owners apply the [UV handoff of envelope faces](../03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff) to their own real slopes. Faces parallel to an X-direction ridge use U=X, and faces parallel to a Z-direction ridge U=Z. V is the real slope distance upward from the projection of the world origin onto that slope, obtained as `Y / sqrt(nx²+nz²)` as long as the face normal's horizontal length is not 0. Pieces of the same face and remainders after cuts share the phase, and the UV of different faces is not joined at valleys, ridges, or wall junctions. The eave is a line of equal V on each slope, so the material courses run parallel to it.

At X = 1.60 m the main roof and the low roof differ in height, so they are not forced into the same slope. The [right elevation owner](../envelope/right.md#right-roof-closures) owns the step wall. This is a junction inside the main block outline, and the main roof is not projected twice above the low face like a free eave. The wall closing from the step to the main roof underside is distinguished from the wall junction flashing of the low roof. There is no roof passage or third room.

The main roof front face where the chimney reaches is cut along the [chimney interface](../envelope/left.md#chimney-roof-interface). The source generates the face outlines deterministically from the formulas and boundaries above and copies no opaque vertex arrays. The existence of general Boolean operations or the combined performance of mesh merging is not assumed. If the explicit face composition of this boundary is not expressible on the public engine path, that limitation is recorded and handed over to the coordinator. The current engine execution, boundary census, normals/UV, gaps/overlaps, and frame comparison with 01 are unverified.

The exterior walls of the main block and garage occupy the space between the existing outlines and inner faces of the [main block](../00-building.md#main-building-extent) and [garage](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) respectively, and close under their own roofs. The upper boundary of the habitable space is kept as a [horizontal ceiling](../09-ceiling-assembly.md#upper-ceiling-closure). This junction is a boundary of the wall top and adds or changes no room, storey, passage, roof pitch, or ceiling datum. The line where a lower garage/porch roof attaches beside the main block wall is not the top of the main block wall. The [chimney](../envelope/left.md#chimney-roof-interface) rising through the roof keeps its own top height and roof cut and is not cut off at this top.

The inner and outer top lines of each exterior wall and the top between them consume the [roof underside function](#roof-profile-datums) really assigned at that X/Z. The height of the outer wall line is not extruded as is through the thickness direction, and conversely the outer wall is not raised to the inner height to pierce the roof plate. In segments where one slope is assigned across the whole thickness of the front and rear walls, the inside is higher than the outside by `(8/12) × exterior wall thickness` for the main roof, `(7/12) × exterior wall thickness` for the low roof, and `(5/12) × exterior wall thickness` for the garage roof. With the current thickness substituted, the differences are about 0.1667/0.1458/0.1042 m respectively. These values are design formula arithmetic, not measurements of real walls or gaps.

The wall thickness zone where the front gable meets the main roof is divided at the [same intersection line as the finite assignment zone](#roof-shared-edges), and each part receives its underside. F of the gable front is not applied unconditionally across the whole wall thickness, and a slope formula extended outside the assignment zone is not adopted as the wall top. Where the main block front and rear walls pass the high/low roof division plane, the height also changes at the same division. The ridge turns of the left and right gable walls and the separate garage roof keep their own real assignments. A segment without a roof function is not closed at an arbitrary height.

`src/spaces/roof/junctions.ts` passes on the calculation of this top boundary and makes no wall body. Each elevation/shared wall owner ends its own body at the same top per the [corner and step junction assignment](../07-boundary-assembly.md#exterior-boundary-junctions). The roof underside and the wall top are one contact boundary, and no separate decorative wedge covering the space between them is generated twice. The real structural bearing, material layering, and flashing are the responsibility of later members that consume this reservation.

The checks are the outer/middle/inner sections of every front and rear exterior wall, every exterior wall corner of the main block/garage, the parts where the front gable intersection line passes through the wall thickness, the front and back ends of the high roof step, and the garage shared wall. The wall top and the assigned roof underside of the same output are compared with the [shared tolerance](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums), and views under the eaves and of the interior ceiling edges are added to the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The real contact, gaps/piercing, support, surface census, and GPU frames are unverified.
