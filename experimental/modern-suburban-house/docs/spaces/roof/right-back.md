`roof.right.back` and its underside are complete faces of `src/spaces/roof/right-back.ts`. It uses [Rback](00-junctions.md#roof-profile-datums) in the back half of the [right zone](00-junctions.md#roof-mass-allocation) and connects the low ridge at the front with the free eave at the back. The left is the [step](../envelope/right.md#right-roof-closures) with the main roof, and the right the main block gable rake edge.

The bathroom and wardrobe under this face are not lowered or given a new exterior wall line because of the roof. The back eave, the back end of the step, and the right triangle wall top that the [right elevation](../envelope/right.md#right-roof-closures) closes to the low roof underside are matched with this face's underside as the same boundary. The inspection addresses are the rear/right elevations and the low ridge section. The real face, storey ceiling interference, eave members, and frames are unverified.

The U of the white eave soffit this owner makes is the distance measured in +X from the left end X=1.60 m of the free back eave Z=−11.10 m, and V is the real slope distance rising from that eave in +Z toward the low ridge. The rear fascia closing the 0.24 m roof thickness starts U along the free edge from the same X=1.60 m end and sets V as the real thickness distance from the weather face to the underside. The exposed thickness faces of the right rake and the step also restart the real 3D length U at the lexicographically smaller (X,Z) end of each free edge. The UV is cut where the face ends against the ridge, step, and triangle wall, and no white trim is attached twice on shared internal faces. The shingle weather face keeps the [common slope UV](../03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff).
