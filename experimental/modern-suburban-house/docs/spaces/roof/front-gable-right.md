`roof.front-gable.right` is the face on the +X side of the [gable center](00-junctions.md#roof-mass-allocation) of the [forward gable at the front left](../../settings/10-house.md#main-mass), owned by `src/spaces/roof/front-gable-right.ts`. It receives [the right slope of F and its underside](00-junctions.md#roof-profile-datums) and the [same valley](00-junctions.md#roof-shared-edges). The left is the gable ridge, the front the front rake edge, and the back the valley meeting the main roof front face.

Whether this face descends in front of the opening of the [stair window](../envelope/front.md#stair-front-window) and covers the window is checked together with the [front boundary](../envelope/front.md#front-roof-closures). The gable trim, underside, and main roof join are not overlaid as separate decorative plates. The inspection addresses are the front/side sections of the right valley end and the eave above the stair window, and the rake of the gable front. The opening interference, outline, and shadow judgment are unverified.

This owner's white gable underside sets U as the length measured from the free front end back in −Z, and V as the real slope distance rising toward the gable ridge in −X from the point where each Z meets the right valley. U=0 at the front and V=0 at the valley, and it breaks at the valley and ridge seams. The shingle weather face keeps the [world slope UV](../03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff). The white fascia starts the real 3D length U at the lexicographically smaller (X,Z) end of the exposed front rake and V as the 0.24 m vertical thickness from the weather face to the underside. The right valley and the left ridge have no exposed free thickness face, so no fascia is placed there, and no separate white cover is added in front of the stair window.
