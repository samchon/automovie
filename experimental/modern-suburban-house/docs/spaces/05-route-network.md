The hall linen closet and entry coat closet are storage with a real opening and interior depth of the space that uses each, and are not counted as room route edges. The porch floor, three risers, and lower waiting area input are owned by the porch owner above, and the lower waiting area surface is consumed by the continuous walk. The [exterior window/door handoff](06-openings.md#external-opening-interface), the [site internal access](site/00-access.md#site-local-routes), and the front and back connections of the side service walk were reserved, but the real members, the maps sidewalk/road and ground bindings, and the whole fence boundary are incomplete. The earlier topology discarded in the from-the-origin review and its observation count are not used again.

The needed check is the route that reaches each destination of the table from the entry and returns in the same boundary order. The common room, garage, and two bathrooms must be reached without passing through the pantry, powder room, or bedrooms. The fact that rough opening coordinates lie inside a wall and the passage behind the real door frame/leaf are different checks. If even one room of the table lacks a real storey, boundary, opening, or connector, the source handoff is incomplete. The current topology report and two-way passage are unverified.

`builtEnvironmentAdjacentSpaces` returns the space on the other side of the same boundary regardless of whether there is an opening. Using that list directly as the human reach graph misjudges a blocked partition or floor plate as passed through. The human routes of the table above must rest on the real boundary and void of passage doors/open connections with the corresponding operating state, or on an authored connector. Adjacency that shares only a window, wall, or ceiling is not a passage edge. A connection one can go through by operating a door is also distinguished from a connection immediately passable in the current state with the door closed. The connector's intermediate landing is read as the space of the real landing, and `main-stair` is reached from both the entry and the upper hall as the middle stop of the [same stair connection](02-stair.md#stair-connector-handoff). This handoff adds no existing space, door, or storey, and real reachability is judged only after compiled members and states exist.

The exterior starting point of this table is still the paving inside house-site. Once the [map input handoff](site/00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs) joins the real exterior sidewalk with the two ports of one node, the approach and return starting from that sidewalk are attached in front of the same route. The center points of the ports alone are not chained, and the service walk is not treated as a third road port. The current table without parcel or ground alone does not report reaching each room from the road.

From the [distribution floor of the entry](rooms/entry.md#entry-use-routes), the stair, living room, and service [branch directly](../settings/10-house.md#entry). The living room continues to the kitchen by the [front-to-back route outside the seating](rooms/living.md#living-through-route), and from the service one uses the [coat storage](rooms/entry.md#entry-coat-storage) and returns by the same way. The room/opening connections of the table above stay as they are, and the sequential states of door operation, seating, and storage use are distinguished from the passage state.

The [main route and kitchen access](rooms/common.md#common-clear-routes) inside the rear common area are passage inside the same room. No separate door or hall edge is added to the kitchen, dining, and family room. Going from the service entrance to the garden door consumes the main route that goes around furniture use occupancy, and the kitchen entry from the living room side and the work in front of open appliances are checked as separate states. Food carrying keeps the existing pantry → service access → common area route.

The edges between rooms stay exactly as in the table above, and the new H2s are the owners of the order and occupancy of using doors and equipment inside them.

There is no new edge through the pantry back wall. Door operation and retrieval/turning states are checked separately, and passage is not judged with objects set down in the service passage erased.

The interior use routes of the upper storey from their own doors to furniture and fixtures are owned by the [primary bedroom](rooms/primary.md#primary-furniture-use), [olive bedroom](rooms/bedroom-two.md#bedroom-two-furniture-use), [blue-gray bedroom](rooms/bedroom-three.md#bedroom-three-furniture-use), [shower bathroom](rooms/shower-bath.md#shower-fixture-use), and [tub bathroom](rooms/tub-bath.md#tub-fixture-use). Passage in the room connection table above is not turned into a pass by omitting chairs, open storage, or door operation.

This check is a section judgment of the current spaces reservations and does not certify the real headroom of later models.
