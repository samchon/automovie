The ground-storey substrate of the [two-storey main block](../settings/10-house.md#main-mass) is chosen as continuous ground support that receives the whole [inner finish outline](00-building.md#main-building-extent). No basement, crawl space that people enter, or new stair is made. The finished height consumes the [ground-storey datum](01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The substrate continues under interior partitions and the stair, and the [interstorey L-shaped hole](02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening) is not subtracted from this floor. The lower waiting area in front of the first riser is the existing entry floor, and no separate stair tread is added.

Below the finished floor, a 0.025 m finish/underlay bundle is reserved, and below it a 0.15 m support substrate. The substrate underside is lower than the finished floor by the sum of the two reservations. This reservation is a spatial choice to distinguish the flat interior finish from the bearing below it, not a value that verified the capacity or material performance of structural members. Even when the real finishes such as wood flooring and tile differ, the substrate height is matched inside the same bundle, and no unintended step is made in front of the powder room or laundry door. Even though the interstorey structure has the same numbers, this is not a rule that copies [the layering of that structure](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary) to the ground storey.

`src/spaces/floors/ground.ts` owns the common substrate once. The visible floor of each room is made by the [existing room owner](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff), consuming its own inner outline and [threshold transition line](07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-junctions). The inside of the coat storage under the stair is the entry owner's, and the support substrate hidden by the stair body is the ground owner's. Per-room substrate boxes are not stacked, and the storage floor is not added as a human pass-through route. The support contacts of partitions and the stair are assigned in section so they are not broken even inside the finish bundle, and the finish plates alone are not counted as supporting the structure.

The checks are the whole ground floor plan, under every interior door, the feet of partitions and the stair, the coat closet, and sections between different finishes. The finished height, substrate continuity, and overlaps/gaps are compared with the [shared tolerance](01-storeys.md#storey-datums), and these are added to the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The real substrate, finish layering, support, and passage are unverified.

The garage substrate receives the [garage inner outline](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) and reserves 0.15 m downward from [its own finished floor](01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums). The main block floor is not extended into it, and the garage is not raised to the main block height. This garage is planned as a concrete floor continuously supported on the ground, and there is no underground space. The interior floor keeps the flat surface that the existing waiting, storage, and door travel reservations consume. A drainage slope is not newly invented to change the mudroom step or the front threshold height. The real drainage and structural performance have not been verified yet.

The common substrate is owned by `src/spaces/garage.ts`, and the exposed top of that substrate and the interior finish by `src/spaces/rooms/garage-interior.ts`. The exposed concrete face is a boundary of the same substrate, so no second opaque floor plate is stacked at the same height. If a finish treatment is needed, it keeps the existing finished height and is assigned to engage the support substrate only once. The feet of storage and rails consume this real floor, and no vehicle is added.

The checks are the whole garage floor plan, both ends of the front door and the mudroom step, the feet of the shelves/workbench, and sections at the bottom of the four walls. The [garage internal access](rooms/garage-interior.md#garage-use-routes) and the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) are kept, and bodies overlapping the main block floor, floating rails/shelves, and thresholds with differing finished heights are found. The real substrate, clear height, grounding, and concrete readability are unverified.

The interior floors of the main block and garage end inside the walls in plan, but under the entrance openings a support substrate passing through the wall thickness is needed. The four boundaries below consume the rough void widths and the inner and outer wall faces set by the spaces opening owners of the [per-room route table](05-route-network.md#room-route-network). The substrate bears that whole strip at its own interior floor reservation height, and the final clear width of the threshold is read after the door frames and leaves made by models are put in. This is not a rule that extends floor finishes under windows or closed walls.

The zone where the substrate reservation under each door overlaps the wall body is generated only once by the floor owner above, and the wall owner excludes that reservation. The floor is not stacked while a wall is left below the opening's lower Y limit. The base/support junctions below that follow the [ground handoff](#ground-support-handoff), and the whole wall under the door is not deleted down to the ground. The jamb bearing and the threshold must engage in the same section. The visible threshold/riser finish is also closed by the one owner of that boundary, leaving no separate face overlapping the existing substrate/wall.

The checks are the center and both jamb sections of all four entrances, the inner and outer waiting areas, and under closed and open doors. Following the different floor owners of porch/terrace/driveway and rooms, gaps, overlaps, unintended lips, the position of the mudroom step, and the garage lower waiting area are read. The door, room, and exterior zone questions of the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) are not reduced. The design connection is separate from the real threshold members, clear width, door bottom operation, and two-way passage, and the latter are unverified.

This building is planned with fill and edge support that continuously bear the underside of the main block/garage substrates above. No invisible empty floating layer is left under the substrate, and grounding is not claimed with a thick floor plate alone. The fill bottom, the base/foundation depth and width, and the real structural members are currently undecided. There is no [maps ground input](site/00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs), so no arbitrary horizontal ground or soil performance is fixed. Nor is it assumed that the exterior finished ground has the same value as the fill support surface or the foundation bottom.

While there is no maps ground, the temporary bottom for reading the exposed faces of the exterior walls in the viewer is placed at the already set [front walk top](01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums) Y = -0.45 m. This value is only a display cut line of the exterior wall faces, not the ground g, the fill support surface, the base, or the foundation bottom. It is marked `map-ground-pending` in the source and output, and after the real ground is received, the exposed segment and structural support of each elevation are set again. Grounding completion is not claimed merely because the wall looks closed at the display cut line.

The support zone under the main block interior substrate is taken by the ground floor owner, and the garage's by the garage structure owner. The base in the exterior wall/shared wall thickness zone is owned by the existing wall structure owner, and the vertical finish exposed to the real ground is integrated by each complete elevation owner. The interior substrate edge is not pushed out past the cladding to generate a duplicate base band. The porch, terrace, and driveway keep the support of their own owners, and the main block substrate is not extended under all of them. Even in underground junctions the bodies of two owners must meet at the same face, and duplicate occupancy is not counted as evidence of support.

What must be received from maps is the real exterior ground and contact line in the existing coordinate system. After receiving that input, the building owner places the substrate underside, exterior walls/thresholds, and ground together and decides the support bottom and base, and an impossible junction goes back to the original input to be corrected. The finished floor is not covered with ground, and the building is not moved wholesale to hide a junction. The finish boundary of brick/siding and moisture, insulation, drainage, and structural performance are incomplete items for the later corresponding owners and are not paid by this spatial reservation.

The checks are height-specific sections and low exterior views including the two floor undersides, the whole building perimeter, the shared wall, the four entrance boundaries, and the porch/terrace/driveway contacts. Questions that read the counterpart owners and contacts of the real ground, support, and finish together are added to the [site handoff](site/00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs) and the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The current maps binding, fill/base/foundation, grounding, structural performance, and GPU shadows are unverified, and no completion of the ground floor implementation is claimed.
