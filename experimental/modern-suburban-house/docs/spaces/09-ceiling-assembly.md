## The Same Upper Boundary for the Main Block Upper Storey and the Stairwell {#upper-ceiling-closure}

The top interior boundary of the main block is a horizontal ceiling at the height of the [upper-storey finished ceiling](01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The plan of the common substrate consumes the whole [main block inner finish outline](00-building.md#main-building-extent). It is a boundary separate from the [interstorey floor](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary), and that L-shaped stair hole is not copied into this ceiling. The empty volume under the roof is not a habitable storey, room, hall, or storage passage, and no door or ladder climbing up from the interior is made. The underside of the sloped roof does not stand in for the room ceilings to change the bedroom height.

The vertical occupancy reservation combining the ceiling finish and substrate is 0.18 m above the finished ceiling. The lower 0.015 m of it is chosen as the visible ceiling finish and the remaining 0.165 m as the reservation of substrate/support members. The underside of the ceiling finish is the existing finished height, and no thickness is added toward the interior. This value is a spatial occupancy choice, not a result that verified joist size, spacing, span, or load. Later members compose the real support relations inside the reservation, and buildability is not claimed merely by filling the whole hidden substrate with an opaque solid plate.

`src/spaces/floors/upper.ts` owns this common substrate of the main block once, and [each upper-storey room owner](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff) owns the ceiling visible inside its own room. The per-room finished ceiling outline ends at the inner wall faces of that room, and adjacent rooms do not make the same finish plate again. The hidden substrate above partitions is continuous, and no hole open up to the roof is left because of low built-in closets. The room/storage divisions consume the existing owners, and no new space is made on the ground of the ceiling division.

The finish over [main-stair](02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening) takes its complete face through `src/spaces/stair.ts`. The plan joins the projection of the existing stair hole with those parts of the protection/separation strips around it that are exposed at ceiling height. Consuming the [per-boundary height roles](02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights), it excludes the parts of the bedroom separation wall that reach the ceiling, and includes in this stair ceiling the strips above the low rear wall and railing up to the corners. The rear finish end meets the inner end of the existing upper hall, so no gap or duplicate plate is left over the protection strip. This is the assignment of the high ceiling finish and does not change the interstorey hole/floor or the railing heights. This stair, which has [no double-height void](../settings/10-house.md#stair), consumes the upper-storey finished ceiling height for its own ceiling above even though it belongs to ground-storey. A plate at the ground-storey ceiling height is not overlaid partway up the stair. Over the open arrival of the upper hall too, two owned faces of the same height meet so the upper boundary is not broken. The existing front stair window stays bound to the stairwell as it is.

The visible horizontal ceiling uses the world X/Z origin and U=X, V=Z of the [room interior UV handoff](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff). Even when the room owners and the stair owner divide the upper boundary at the same height, the projection phase does not restart. The face is cut only at the outline of the stair finish zone, real height steps, and the individual member boundaries of light fixtures. The hidden upper face of the structural substrate is not counted as a seam of the visible ceiling finish.

The checks are the upper reflected ceiling plan, the section from the stair start up to the high window/ceiling, and observations looking up from every upper room and the stairwell. They find holes that copy the stair hole, missing strips above low storage/railings, overlapping finishes at room boundaries, and lower ceiling plates that block the stair. Heights and contacts are compared with the [shared tolerance](01-storeys.md#storey-datums), and the real substrate, finish, space bindings, clear height, and frames are unverified.

The garage is a ground-storey annex space with [its own floor/ceiling datums](01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums). The ceiling substrate covers the whole [garage inner finish outline](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) and closes under the [garage roof](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-mass-allocation). The main block floor/ceiling is not extended into it, and no room at the same height is made across the shared wall. The vertical occupancy and finish thickness consume the [ceiling reservation above](#upper-ceiling-closure) relative to the garage finished ceiling. No separate room, storage loft, or additional stair is placed in the space under the garage roof either.

The substrate is owned by `src/spaces/garage.ts` and the whole ceiling finish visible from inside by `src/spaces/rooms/garage-interior.ts`. The same ceiling edge reaches the inner faces of the shared wall and the other three exterior walls, and the structure/cladding owners of the four walls stay as they are. The eave soffit outside the garage is a separate exterior surface of the roof owner, so the interior ceiling plate is not pushed out to the eave end.

The [reservation of the open front panels/guides](envelope/front.md#garage-front-opening) is kept below the finished ceiling. The height difference between the ceiling datum and the top of that reservation is 0.05 m in the inputs. This difference is not the result of a real rail/ceiling interference check. When later beams, hangers, fasteners, and light fixtures are placed, they are checked not to block the whole occupancy of the moving panels, and the collision is not hidden by raising the ceiling height or thinning the panels. The fixed junctions that support the rails themselves are distinguished from the moving panels that pass between them. The height of the rear wall shelves/workbench consumes the [garage interior owner](rooms/garage-interior.md#garage-storage-use) as it is.

The checks are the garage reflected ceiling plan, a section looking up along the front rails to the roof, the shared wall contact, the four inner corners, and the interior views with the door closed and open. They find a ceiling wrongly generated as an upper floor, roof/ceiling overlaps, collisions of rails with fixed members, and gaps between ceiling finishes. The real clear height, door operation, member support, finish, and frames of the empty garage are unverified.

The ceilings reach the inner faces of the [main block interior plan](00-building.md#main-building-extent) and the [garage interior plan](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) respectively, and the roofs have [their own outlines and intersection boundaries](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-shared-edges). The top of each ceiling reservation is compared with the underside of the roof really assigned above that plan. No arbitrary thickness is set again from the roof weather face; the [existing underside function](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-profile-datums) is consumed. The lower height of the main block exterior wall outer line or the eave end is not confused with the height of the interior ceiling outline.

A minimum separation clearance of 0.010 m between the top of the flat ceiling reservation and the roof underside is the design target. In the current inputs, the lowest comparison segment of the main block is the front and back interior end lines under the right roof. The difference between that underside and the top of the upper ceiling reservation is `(5.95 + (7/12) × 0.25 - 0.24) - (5.66 + 0.18)`, about 0.0158 m. At the front and back interior end lines under the [low gable roof of the garage](../settings/10-house.md#garage), it is `(2.95 + (5/12) × 0.25 - 0.24) - (2.55 + 0.18)`, about 0.0842 m. The values of the formulas are notations that substitute the roof and exterior wall thickness of the links above, the finished ceilings of the [two storeys](01-storeys.md#storey-datums) and the [garage](01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums), and this document's [occupancy reservation](#upper-ceiling-closure). They are not copied as independent dimension inputs and are recomputed by the same relation when the original owners change. This is a design formula comparison, not a real output measurement or a result of structural, ventilation, or insulation conformance.

The ceiling substrate ends at the inner wall faces, and the wall thickness zones are closed by the elevation/shared wall owners up to [the roof underside at each position](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-wall-head-junctions). The outer wall height is not extended horizontally inward to leave a gap beside the ceiling. An end bearing on a wall shares one contact boundary with that wall, and two owners do not stack the same junction body. The real members at the roof steps, gable crossings, and around the chimney consume each roof/elevation owner, and if a member comes down outside the ceiling reservation, the section and the clear height of that room are checked again. Intended junctions of support members are stated distinctly from the separation clearance between these flat reservations, and penetration/overlap is not accepted as an arbitrary junction.

The checks are the ceiling sections of every roof-assigned zone, the low front and back end lines and the four exterior wall corners, below the main block roof step, above the stairwell, and the garage shared wall/rail segments. The roof underside, ceiling reservation, real members, and junction ids are read from the same output, and inside and outside views are added to the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The remaining members are not passed by matching two numbers alone. The real minimum clearance, duplicates/gaps, surface census, headroom, and GPU observation are unverified.
