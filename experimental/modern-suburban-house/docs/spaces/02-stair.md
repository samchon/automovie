The L-shaped route below is adopted as the design input of the [single turning stair](../settings/10-house.md#stair). `main-stair` rises in the -Z direction from the lower waiting area of the [entry](rooms/entry.md#entry-plan), turns once in the +X direction at the intermediate landing, and continues to the arrival surface of the [upper hall](rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-hall-plan). The stair space of the two flights and the intermediate landing belongs to ground-storey, and the destination of the connection is the upper arrival surface of upper-storey. Only this one stair connection exists between the two storeys. No route is made that reaches the living room or the rear common area by passing under the stair.

The 3.06 m of the [storey reference](01-storeys.md#storey-datums) is divided as 18 × 0.17 m. The lower run has 8 risers and 7 treads, and the upper run 10 risers and 9 treads. The tread depth is 0.28 m. The last riser of each run reaches the next landing, so a landing is not counted once more as a tread. The intermediate landing height is 1.36 m, and the horizontal run lengths are 1.96 m below and 2.52 m above.

| Route part | Plan reservation, m | Height and connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower flight | X = [-1.80, -0.65], Z = -1.45 → -3.41 | The first riser is at Z = -1.45, the first tread height is 0.17. The eighth riser rises to the intermediate landing. |
| Intermediate landing | X = [-1.80, -0.65], Z = [-4.56, -3.41] | Y = 1.36. Turns at a right angle and enters the +X flight. |
| Upper flight | X = -0.65 → 1.87, Z = [-4.56, -3.41] | Starts from the ninth full riser and arrives at upper-storey with the eighteenth riser. |

The planned source owner of the stair is `src/spaces/stair.ts`. Per-tread positions are derived repeatedly from the count, run direction, and start point above, and the same record is not copied by hand. The comparison targets are the step count, run direction, and the connection of the two arrival surfaces with the real steps, and the coordinate tolerance consumes the [design comparison reference of the outline](00-building.md#main-building-extent). The current values are authoring input, and the stair output, section, and two-way passage are unverified.

Each tread starts its own width direction U and run direction V. The lower flight rises toward −Z, so U=X+1.80 starts at the left end of the nosing X=−1.80 m, and V is the −Z run distance from the front nosing of each tread. The upper flight rises toward +X, so U=Z+4.56 starts at the left end of the nosing Z=−4.56 m, and V is the +X run distance from the front nosing of each tread. The vertical front face of a lower riser starts U=X+1.80 at its own left X=−1.80 m, an upper riser starts U=Z+4.56 at its own left Z=−4.56 m, and V is measured as Y height from each riser's bottom. The landing top uses U=+X, V=−Z from the left front corner (X,Z)=(−1.80,−3.41) m and is cut at the perimeter of the Y=1.36 m plate and at the nosing interfaces of the two flights. Treads and the landing receive the same oak grain but do not continue each other's origin, and the white-painted risers start again from the origin of their own vertical face.

The [stair space and per-step dimensions](#stair-reservation) that realize the [single turning stair](../settings/10-house.md#stair) are kept. The later `IAutoMovieBuiltConnector` is authored as one `main-stair-connection` with kind stair, from front-entry, to upper-hall, and bidirectional true. The two ends of the route are placed inside the existing lower waiting area and the upper arrival surface respectively, and follow the real run order lower flight → one turn at the intermediate landing → upper flight. The two flights are not registered as separate interstorey stairs, and the two storeys are not connected with a straight line that does not pass through the stairwell. The real two flights, landing, and protective members are handed over as elements of the same connector.

The existing main-stair space keeps its ground-storey membership and is also referenced as the connector's intermediate landing. The landing position is set at the X/Z center of the intermediate landing plan and at that landing's height. This point is included as the turning station of the route, and `landings.at` is the 3D polyline length from the start to that point divided by the whole route length. It is not simply fixed at the middle index of the station array or at 0.5. The intermediate landing is not made into a separate room, storey, or second stair, and `landings.space` points to main-stair. The physical boundary and real supporting surfaces of the landing stay with the stair owner.

The public type's `steps` is one bundle of count/rise/run, and `validateBuiltEnvironment` compares count × rise with the height difference of the whole route and count × run with the whole horizontal route length including the landing and approach parts. This house has, besides equally spaced treads, the turn of the intermediate landing and the connections of the lower and upper waiting areas, so that uniform repetition summary is not entered for the whole connector. This input form was confirmed by reading the optional-field condition of the public implementation and the steps-free spiral stair case in the repository's `test/src/features/architecture/test_architecture_built_connector.ts`. It does not mean the test was run or that this house's stair was verified. The connector representation is chosen in this form, but the limit that omitting steps does not pay the step count and dimension checks is kept. Options that make an average run or change the existing step count, landing, or route to fit the tally are not adopted.

The connection check and the real step check are distinguished. The connector lookup must find the same connection from front-entry/main-stair/upper-hall, and the derived landing position must lie on the same landing. `builtConnectorGeometry` reads the route's height difference, length, and stations and does not measure the real treads or railings. The riser count, each height, tread depth, landing, and clear width and headroom behind protective members must be read from the real members that rendering consumes. While that measurement path and geometry do not exist, these stay unverified and are not replaced with route arithmetic or a test case. The [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) includes the threshold, inner corner, and center directions of the stair space itself and two-way passage as they are.

Limited to the [route above](#stair-reservation) and the lower waiting area, the floor plate hole is an L that joins the longitudinal part X = [-1.80, -0.65]·Z = [-4.56, -0.25] and the transverse part X = [-0.65, 1.87]·Z = [-4.56, -3.41]. The void and heights consume [stair-front-window](envelope/front.md#stair-front-window), and the real window assembly is implemented in a later member. The space behind the window is not turned into a bedroom, and no fake window is stuck on the front.

The hole is the real passage opening the [single turning stair](../settings/10-house.md#stair) requires and belongs only to the stairwell above the two flights, the intermediate landing, and the lower waiting area. There is no double-height void that further empties the living room, entry distribution floor, or bedrooms. The same boundary of the passage hole is consumed by `src/spaces/floors/upper.ts` of the [single interstorey structure](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary) and by the per-room finish owners of the upper floor/lower ceiling. The vertical thickness finish visible from the stair side and the upper arrival follow the [edge handoff](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions). The space above the stairwell is closed by the main block's upper ceiling. The hole is not extended to the roof.

The protection/separation boundaries are 0.15 m reservations: X = [-1.95, -1.80] on the left of the hole, X = [-0.65, -0.50] on the right of the longitudinal part, Z = [-3.41, -3.26] at the front of the transverse part, and Z = [-4.71, -4.56] at its back. A reservation does not mean a full-height wall body. The ground storey at X = [-0.65, -0.50]·Z = [-3.41, -1.45] on the right of the longitudinal part is beside an open railing, so [the entry floor and ceiling](rooms/entry.md#entry-plan) reach the X = -0.65 m boundary of the stair hole. The 0.015 m thickness of the ceiling ends below the height at which the [interstorey vertical rim](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions) starts, so it does not make the same volume. The other parts also abut the inner ends of adjacent rooms along the real walls and stair solids and the [plan covering](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff) of room finishes. The [high ceiling closure](09-ceiling-assembly.md#upper-ceiling-closure) above the stairwell consumes as its finish zone the same hole and those protection strips around it that are exposed at ceiling height. The common substrate is the upper storey owner, and the finish visible from the stairwell is this stair owner. A low ceiling plate is not generated over the stair on the ground of ground-storey membership.
