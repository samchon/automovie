Of the [exterior access of house-site/ground-storey](00-access.md#site-local-routes), the tops of the entry walk, side service walk, and garden lower waiting area are owned by [front-walk](front-walk.md#front-walk-plan), [side-walk](side-walk.md#side-walk-plan), and [garden-lower-landing](terrace.md#garden-lower-landing-plan) respectively. The substrate thickness of these three walking surfaces is reserved as 0.12 m in the Y direction from the top, and 0.15 m for the [driveway](driveway.md#driveway-plan). The concrete garage driveway and entry walk of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity) receive this thickness reservation. This is a spatial occupancy choice including the finish, not a value that verified concrete mix, reinforcement, joints, bearing capacity, or vehicle loads. No car or separate parking zone is added.

The visible horizontal paving faces of the porch, entry walk, driveway, service walk, terrace, and garden lower waiting area use U=X, V=Z from the world X/Z origin. Even when each paving owner divides into several slabs, the phase of the same top continues, and the face is cut at real slab joints, thresholds, curbs, and outlines. Sloped connecting faces keep the same X/Z projection. The vertical riser faces and the outer side faces of the paving, including the porch floor and the two step blocks, start U as the horizontal tangent length from the lexicographically first (X,Z) end of each exposed end line and V as the +Y height from the substrate bottom at that position, and break at block corners, steps, and junctions with other paving. The substrate and ground under the paving are not counted as UV seams of the visible top.

The underside of each substrate subtracts its own thickness vertically from the original top height formula. It is not confused with a material thickness perpendicular to the slope. The connecting paths on both sides of the driveway apply above and below the original interpolation formula that consumes both position X and Z. An arbitrary plane is not made from the four corner points alone to change the intermediate heights. No duplicate substrate or fake side is generated inside the union of the T-shaped walk and the three strips of the service walk. The substrate and finish are owned together by the existing complete paving owner, and no second opaque plate is overlaid on the same top.

When the bilinear tops of the two connecting paths are approximated by triangles, the height match at the vertices alone does not pay the [shared tolerance](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). Writing the normalized coordinates of each connecting path rectangle as u/v and the four corner heights as h00/h10/h01/h11, the cross term that makes the curvature is `q = h11 - h10 - h01 + h00`. The maximum vertical difference between the two planes that split a quadrilateral along one diagonal and the original interpolation formula is `|q| / 4`, occurring at the diagonal midpoint. Applying the same relation to cells divided evenly into nX/nZ in the X/Z directions, the overall maximum difference is `|q| / (4 × nX × nZ)`. The underside subtracts only a constant thickness from the same top, so it has the same error bound.

Currently the Z length of the two connecting paths is 1.20 m in each owner, and consuming the 0.30 m height difference between the two ends of the driveway and its length of 6.80 m gives `|q| = 0.30 × 1.20 / 6.80 m` for each. Therefore covering one quadrilateral with only two triangles gives a maximum difference of about 0.013235 m. Dividing the sloped segment of each connecting path into at least four intervals in each of X/Z reduces the theoretical maximum difference to about 0.000827 m. This division is an approximation reservation of the surface and does not make concrete joints or a separate surface owner. If the height formula, end lines, or length change, the cross term and division are derived again in the original owner. These figures are interpretations of the authoring formulas, not results that measured the compiled mesh or GPU precision.

The walkable height and the real opaque substrate consume the same original height formula. The division vertices are placed on that formula, and the X/Z divisions of the top/underside correspond. The division of the connecting path end lines is also passed to the shared boundaries of the driveway and the flat walks, leaving no mismatch in which one side's edge ends in the middle of the other side's triangle edge. The line passing into the flat surface is kept, and the sloped segment is not widened to the whole surrounding walk. A state with only a walking surface declaration and no visible substrate is not counted as complete.

The checks are the top/underside and longitudinal and transverse sections of each paving, every cell diagonal midpoint and the shared end lines of the two sloped connecting paths, the T junction, and the service walk turns. Not only the vertices but the real heights inside the triangles are compared with the original interpolation formula, and the shared boundaries and vertical thicknesses of adjacent paving are read from the same output. Height formulas, vertical thickness, and gaps/duplicates are compared with the [shared tolerance](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums), and the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) is kept. The thickness inputs and the interpretation of the approximation error are not measurements of real support or sloped connections, and the substrate, surface, passage, and grounding are unverified.

The [front porch](../porch.md#porch-platform-access) and the [garden terrace](terrace.md#garden-terrace-plan) keep their own raised flat surfaces and exterior steps. The condition that the porch floor is higher than the surrounding walk and that real steps lead to it comes from [front porch and exterior entry](../../settings/10-house.md#porch-entry). The top slab thickness of the two flat surfaces consumes the [walking paving reservation](#paving-depth-reservation). A 0.15 m wide edge support wall is reserved inside the outline of each flat surface and closes from under the top slab down to the substrate underside of its own low waiting area. This bottom is the building-side reference for joining the existing waiting areas in front of the porch/behind the garden, not the map ground or the foundation bottom depth. The support wall is not raised above the top to make a new curb or railing or to reduce the existing clear width.

The inside under the flat surfaces is planned as a fill zone bearing the top slab, and no empty space for people is kept. The support walls and fill meet at the same boundary, and two bodies are not overlapped in the same volume. The body of the exterior steps closes from the section of the original risers/treads down to the same low waiting area substrate underside. The end support wall of the raised flat surface and the step body meet at a shared face, and the underside of each tread is not left as an empty floating plate. The body where top slab, support wall, and steps meet is assigned only once, and no overlapping faces are left inside. The top, riser faces, and exposed sides are integrated by the existing complete owners.

The waiting area floor under the porch stays with the front-walk owner and is not copied by the porch. The garden steps and lower waiting area are joined by the terrace owner with one body boundary. The relation of the original three risers, two horizontal treads, and arrival floor is kept, and the step heights/depths are not changed on the ground of thickness. The back end touching the main block wall consumes the [building threshold handoff](../10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions), and the top slab and support walls are not pushed into the main block interior.

Under the [three column bases](../porch.md#porch-roof-columns) of the porch, a vertical support zone consuming that base plan is assigned from under the top slab down to the common bottom above. Separate column positions or counts are not copied. The parts overlapping the support walls are merged into one body and excluded from the interior fill. The base tops and the column heights above the floor keep their existing values, and the floor slab alone is not counted as verifying the column loads. The connection of this support with a later real foundation is remaining work of the porch owner.

The checks are the whole outline of the two raised flat surfaces, the center/sides of each step, the meeting of the top slab and end support wall, the low waiting area contacts, and vertical sections at the three porch columns. These are added to the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) to find floating plates/columns, steps buried in support walls, duplicate waiting area floors, and support projecting above the top. The real members, soil/foundation, drainage, structural performance, shadows, and the 01/03 reference readability are unverified.

Each complete paving owner ends at its own plan outline and consumes the counterpart's top/underside in the same coordinates. At the contact of a walk and the thicker driveway, the tops must match first, and the walking surface is not lowered to match the undersides too. The contact segment of two substrates whose heights overlap is a hidden shared boundary, and the remaining section of the deeper driveway substrate is the driveway owner's responsibility. No independent finish strip covering that difference is made on either side. The garden lower waiting area and the service walk meet at the end line with the same thickness reservation, and the floors are not stacked.

On the building side they meet at the outer wall face of the [four entrance sections](../10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions), and the front end waits for the [two ports of maps](00-access.md#site-access-interface). The bearing/fill under the low paving substrates and the foundations under the high support walls are incomplete items each paving/porch owner will own. After the [real ground](00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs) is received, the exposed sides, buried zones, and the support under the substrate are set together, and the exterior finished ground is not treated as the structural support surface. The whole eave projection is not erased from the terrain, and the ground is not raised to cover steps.

In the right wooden fence of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity), the existing exception that only the foundation of the [fence gate post](fence.md#fence-gate-junction) may enter under the service walk does not allow substrate duplication. The real occupancy of the gate post foundation and the paving substrate is divided in the same section, and the foundation top does not rise above the finished walking surface. If the bearing the paving needs disappears or the gate clear width/waiting area is intruded on, it is corrected in that foundation/paving owner. In the current state without a foundation depth, the crossing section is not counted as passing.

The height check of the ground handoff is done after first identifying the real footprint the check point belongs to and the id, owner, walkability, and height of every candidate surface. Even if only the height of the one surface a lookup selected first matches, the exposed top of another ground/paving may overlap, so that is not used as evidence of single occupancy. A buried support surface at a different height is distinguished from an exposed top duplicate. Two tops on a shared end line are distinguished as boundary contact, and at interior points on either side of the line, whether only the intended finished surface continues is also read. An extended height obtained at a point outside the footprint or a height clamped at the grid end is not counted as contact. This check does not replace the real mesh heights and side/substrate sections above and does not merge the map ground with paving ownership.

The checks are the whole sloped connections on both sides of the driveway, the garden lower waiting area end line, the four building entrance contacts, the full width of the two front ports, the exposed paving outlines, and the gate post section. The [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) and the [map handoff check](00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs) are kept, and the counterpart owner, upper and lower heights, exposure, and real contact are read from the same output. The current map connection, foundation/fill, gaps/overlaps, finish/joints, and GPU observation are unverified.
