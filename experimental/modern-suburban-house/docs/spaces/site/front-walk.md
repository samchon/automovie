`front-walk` is the entry walk of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity) and an exterior walking zone of house-site/ground-storey. It continues from in front of the first riser of the [front porch](../porch.md#porch-platform-access) to the [front paving end](00-access.md#site-access-interface). The width and center X consume the porch entry stair as is, and the inner end Z is derived from that first riser position. The top Y is the [front walk datum](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums). The whole lower flat waiting area the porch requires is included inside this one face, and no second floor is overlaid there.

The cross connecting path to the driveway is Z = [4.25, 5.45] m, with X from the right end of this walk to the left end of the [driveway](driveway.md#driveway-plan). The longitudinal and cross paths are merged into one T-shaped walking zone, and no lip, curb, or planter is placed at the contact. The height of the connecting path at each cross section linearly interpolates between the walk top on the left and the driveway top D(Z) on the right. `t = (X - walk right X) / (driveway left X - walk right X)`, and the top is `(1 - t) × walk Y + t × D(Z)`. The driveway side is not cut into a horizontal floor leaving a small step.

The 1.20 m width of the cross connecting path is an authoring choice for the way the [occupancy body carrying a basket](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) returns to the entry. The outer 0.20 m from the edge is reserved as clearance into which the stems, branches, and planters of later planting do not come, and the walking surface is not counted as widened by that clearance. The sloped interpolation of the cross path does not enter the waiting area in front of the first porch step. Concrete planting objects are placed by a later owner, not by this face.

The owner of the complete walking surface and edges is `src/spaces/site/front-walk.ts`. The substrate consumes the [low paving thickness](01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) and the [contact handoff](01-paving-support.md#paving-contact-handoff), and joints and concrete finish are later authoring of the same face. The boundary with the first porch riser, the T contact, the oblique connection line with the driveway, and the exterior port end are checked in sections/plans and two-way approach. The junction height comparison consumes the [shared tolerance](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The real clear width, gaps, grounding, and the walk readability of 01 are unverified.
