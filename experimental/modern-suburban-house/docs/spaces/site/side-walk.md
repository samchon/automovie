`side-walk` is a continuous exterior walking surface of house-site/ground-storey. It comes out from the right of the [driveway](driveway.md#driveway-plan), passes the right of the garage, and reaches the outer end of the [garden lower waiting area](terrace.md#garden-lower-landing-plan). It adds no exterior node or third road port to the [maps connection](00-access.md#site-access-interface). What follows is an internal walking surface reservation to hand over to maps, not a claim that an adopted parcel or ground already exists.

In world X/Z m, the left end of the longitudinal path is 0.60 m in +X from the [outer right face of the garage](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent), and its width is 1.20 m. The front connecting path is a 1.20 m strip from where Z is 1.40 m less than the [front paving end](00-access.md#site-access-interface) to where it is 0.20 m less, and in X it runs from the right end of the driveway to the right end of the longitudinal path. The front end of the longitudinal path is the front end of this connecting path. The longitudinal path is not extended to the paving end to make a separate entrance onto the sidewalk.

The back cross path is 1.20 m wide in the -Z direction from the outer end of the garden lower waiting area, with X from the left end of that waiting area to the right end of the longitudinal path. The longitudinal path comes down to the outer end of this cross path. The three strips form one complete walking surface as a union, and the floor at the contacts is not copied. It meets the lower waiting area only at the end line and does not cover the existing terrace, exterior steps, or waiting area. No garage side door or second stair into the house is made.

The top S of the longitudinal/back paths consumes the height of the garden lower waiting area. On the front connecting path, between the right end of the driveway and the left end of the longitudinal path, it is interpolated with `t = (X - driveway right X) / (longitudinal path left X - driveway right X)` and the top `(1 - t) × D(Z) + t × S`. D(Z) is the top of the driveway owner. Inside the longitudinal path, S is kept. Making the front connection a horizontal plate would create a small lip against the driveway slope, so it is not adopted. This formula is a height reservation for exterior passage, not a judgment of drainage performance or code conformance.

The 0.20 m outside the walking surface is clearance into which stems, rocks, and the shrubs and right wooden fence foundations of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity) do not come. That clearance is not added to the passage width. Only the [gate post connection](#side-gate-interface) is allowed along the boundary, and the clear width after subtracting the real posts and leaf/handle is checked separately. The parcel range maps must receive has to include this whole walking surface and clearance, and if it does not match the real boundary, it is resolved in this layout or in the authoring owner of that boundary. Ground duplication over the walking surface, gaps under the paving, and the headroom under eaves/branches are unverified.

`src/spaces/site/side-walk.ts` owns the continuous top, sides, and the driveway connection. The substrate consumes the [walking paving thickness](01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) and the [contact/gate post handoff](01-paving-support.md#paving-contact-handoff), and paving prototypes, joints, and finish are later authoring of the same owner. The two-way movement of a [person/basket](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) at the two turns, the two ends of the sloped connection, and the boundary with the lower waiting area is added to plans/sections and the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The real zones, surfaces, contacts, passage, and the yard readability of 01/03 are unverified.

On the [walking surface](#side-walk-plan) above, the two exterior zones are chosen as `side-front-access` in front of the gate and `side-rear-access` behind it, and both belong to house-site/ground-storey. The two sides share the same continuous paving owner, and each has the threshold, corners, four center directions, and additional questions at the hidden turn. The questions of zones split by the gate are not reduced to one representative view.

The boundary plane Z of `side-yard-gate` consumes the garage front outer face. The X interval between the inner faces of the gate posts equals the width of the longitudinal walking surface, and it opens from hinges on the +X side toward the garden in the -Z direction. The final passage width target is 1.05 m. The gate posts sit outside this interval, and no upper beam/header crossing above the walking surface is placed. The wooden leaf is reserved within 0.05–1.70 m above S, and the rotation radius including the handle within 1.20 m. With the gate opened 90°, the hardware occupancy coming inward from the +X boundary must be within 0.10 m. The real width, height, and opening behind the hinges/frame/latch are checked in later members.

The front waiting area is +Z 0.10–1.60 m from the gate plane and the back waiting area -Z 1.30–2.80 m, and both use the whole width of the longitudinal path. The two waiting areas are not new slabs but use zones of the same walking surface. The moment of operating the latch at the closed gate is distinguished from the moment of passing through the gate opened 90°, and on the way back one steps back into the back waiting area while the leaf rotates. The default state is closed, and in the circulation check the real gate is opened and passed in both directions. This does not mean vehicle entry or barrier-free certification.

The owner of the complete wooden fence, the gate posts, and the empty opening is `src/spaces/site/fence.ts`, and the prototype owner of the leaf, hinges, and handle is the [side-yard gate](../../models/02-exterior-doors.md#side-yard-gate). `fence.ts` and the walking surface owner do not generate the leaf. The [whole fence line](fence.md#fence-enclosure-plan) is derived from the outlines of the building and service walk, and the [remaining panels/gate posts](fence.md#fence-gate-junction) close up to this gate. The real ground contact and the maps boundary to be adopted are still incomplete. Because it must also be checked that the surrounding fence really closes up to the gate posts and that only this gate divides the front and back zones, the gate alone is not currently claimed to complete the garden boundary. The real gate operation, foot/hand occupancy, the whole fence junction, the wooden boundary of 01, and the observations of the two zones are unverified.
