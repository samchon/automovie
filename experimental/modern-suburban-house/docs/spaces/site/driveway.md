`driveway` is the concrete driveway into the garage of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity) and an exterior paving zone of house-site/ground-storey. It opens at the back to the outer wall face of [garage-front-door](../envelope/front.md#garage-front-opening), at the front to the [front paving end](00-access.md#site-access-interface), on the left to the [entry cross connecting path](front-walk.md#front-walk-plan), and on the right to the [front connecting path of the side service walk](side-walk.md#side-walk-plan). The X range is set by adding 0.20 m on each side of the garage rough door opening. A center different from the door is not authored separately. The right/left edges outside the two connecting paths are boundaries meeting the ground and are not connected to a separate road.

The top D(Z) linearly interpolates between the [garage finished floor](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums) at the garage end and the walk height set by the same owner at the front end. `u = (Z - garage door outer Z) / (front paving end Z - garage door outer Z)`, `D(Z) = (1 - u) × garage floor Y + u × front walk Y`. No cross slope is added. The threshold floor inside the exterior wall thickness is extended up to the outer wall face by the garage floor owner, and the driveway ends at that line, so the floors do not overlap. The rough door width is distinguished from the clear width after the final door frame/rails.

This slope is an authoring dimension connecting the two existing floors, not a verified value of geographic survey, drainage performance, or vehicle drivability. The door is closed in the baseline state, and the [empty garage](../rooms/garage-interior.md#garage-interior-plan) is observed from the mudroom. No vehicle, parking marks, or fake silhouette are added. No planting or storage is placed on the driveway/connecting paths, and a 0.20 m planting occupancy exclusion clearance is left on both sides. The connection to the exterior sidewalk and road is left as an incomplete map/space interface.

The owner of the complete driveway top and edges is `src/spaces/site/driveway.ts`. The substrate consumes the [driveway thickness reservation](01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) and the [walk contact](01-paving-support.md#paving-contact-handoff). Joints and material are later authoring of the same face, and arbitrary rectangular patches are not copied. The longitudinal and transverse sections of the garage threshold, both jambs, the connecting path contacts, and the outer port are put into the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The slope/width/connection height of the final surface, interference with the open door mechanism, and the readability of the concrete, shadows, and 01 are unverified.
