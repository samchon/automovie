`house-site` is the site that will contain the [main block](../00-building.md#main-building-extent), [garage](../00-building.md#attached-garage-extent), porch, and the exterior access zones below. The walks, driveway, and terrace attached to the building are also bound as exterior zones of [ground-storey](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). No interior room or separate storey is added. Coordinates use the [common reference](../../settings/00-production.md#coordinate-units). `src/spaces/site.ts` is the assembly owner of this containment and connection and does not directly generate paving or terrain twice.

This document places at Z = 6.50 m the outer end of the front paving where the garage driveway and the entry walk reach the front sidewalk of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity). This line is the paving end spaces requires, not a declaration of a parcel boundary or public sidewalk line. On the same line, the end of the [entry walk](front-walk.md#front-walk-plan) and the end of the [garage driveway](driveway.md#driveway-plan) both reach the existing [front walk datum](../01-storeys.md#ground-threshold-datums). Maps must own the real parcel boundary, sidewalk, curb, and road and one named `house-site-access` node, and receive these two connection sections as the walk/driveway ports of that node. Two separate exterior networks are not invented.

Maps is currently disabled and has no adopted boundary/node. Therefore the world boundary of house-site and the exterior connection of these two ports are incomplete, and the internal routes below are not reported as continuing to the exterior road. Because maps has not been authored yet, map geometry is not copied into this document in advance. Once the upper boundary is adopted, the paving end, coordinate transform, heights, and widths are compared on both sides, and mismatches are corrected in the relevant design owner. The real map/space binding is unverified.

This H2 connects, as segment orders inside house-site, the direct porch and entry access of [front porch and exterior entry](../../settings/10-house.md#porch-entry) and the rear terrace and right wooden fence of [site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity). The entry route is [front-walk](front-walk.md#front-walk-plan) → lower waiting area of front-porch → three exterior steps → [porch](../porch.md#porch-platform-access) → [front-door](../rooms/entry.md#entry-plan) → front-entry. Walking from the driveway to the entry uses [driveway](driveway.md#driveway-plan) → the cross connecting path of front-walk → the same porch route. The garage's interior entry is the existing [mudroom connection](../rooms/laundry.md#laundry-plan), and the exterior garage door keeps its [default closed state](../../settings/10-house.md#garage). The driveway/garage threshold connection in the open state is observed only when checking garage door operation. No car or vehicle driving task is added.

The rear route is kitchen-dining-family → [garden-door](../envelope/rear.md#garden-door) → waiting area of [garden-terrace](terrace.md#garden-terrace-plan) → central walking strip → [terrace exterior steps](terrace.md#garden-steps-plan) → [garden-lower-landing](terrace.md#garden-lower-landing-plan). The outer end of the lower waiting area continues to the driveway through the [side service walk](side-walk.md#side-walk-plan). The front and back zones of that walk and the [fence gate](side-walk.md#side-gate-interface) are separate internal connections and add no exterior node. The [fence line](fence.md#fence-enclosure-plan) is derived from the building/service walk, but the real garden ground, parcel containment, and closure remain items left with the maps input. The statement that one can cross the lawn does not substitute for an open connection.

The paving connections handed to maps are, besides the existing two front ports, the whole outline and planting exclusion clearance of the side service walk and the [ground junction inputs](#map-handoff-inputs) below. The high terrace top and the low lower waiting area are not both treated as connecting level to the lawn. These are not values that settle the parcel shape or fence line in their place. The [whole surface handoff](../03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff) assigns the file responsibility of the service walk and the wooden fence in advance and keeps the real world boundary, ground, and planting census incomplete.

The logical volume of the exterior walking zones is temporarily extended up to 2.00 m above each walking surface top and marked `map-ground-pending`. This value is a representation height for the current zone selection and observation, not the [stair headroom](../02-stair.md#stair-clearance) or a certification of passing real overhead obstacles. Once the maps ground and later occupancy arrive, the measured effective height of each route is checked again.

Each route consumes the [user occupancy body](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) and the basket width. Sections of the walking surfaces, views coming back down from the top, and two-way passage including leaf opening and later furniture/planting occupancy are added to the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The exterior zones also carry the questions of threshold, each corner, and center directions, and questions are added at the concave contacts of the T-shaped walk. The design routes do not replace the compiled topology or the observation count, and the real passage and surface connections are unverified.

This H2 owns the inputs `house-site` must receive from maps and the mismatches it refuses. [Site and planting](../../settings/10-house.md#site-identity) keeps the concrete boundaries, planting positions, and ground slope as authoring choices of maps, so the real parcel, exterior network, and terrain are the answers of the maps owner, and the requirements below alone do not mean maps has been adopted or connected. The source of coordinates and heights stays with the linked spaces/settings owners, and the building or steps are not moved separately in the viewer to fit the ground.

The exposed face of the ground does not overlap the paving tops, and the grounding and vertical sides under the foundations and paving supports must be closed with real sections. If the whole eave projection area is subtracted from the terrain, the ground under the porch/eaves disappears, so that projection line is not used as a ground removal outline. The later terrain owner must receive the height and counterpart owner of each contact line, and the paving thickness or support shapes are not newly invented at this interface.

The [support under the main block/garage floors](../10-ground-floor.md#ground-support-handoff) sets the bottom of the building-side fill and base/foundation after receiving this ground input. The exterior finished ground of maps does not stand in for the support surface or foundation bottom depth. The finished heights and steps of the [four building entrance boundaries](../10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions) are kept, and covering a threshold with exterior ground is not treated as making the contact.

The inputs of the exterior substrates come from the [low paving thickness](01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) and the [high porch/terrace support](01-paving-support.md#raised-platform-support). The [paving contact](01-paving-support.md#paving-contact-handoff) distinguishes different substrate undersides from exposed sides and buried zones, and that construction bottom is not adopted as the exterior finished ground of maps. The real support surfaces/foundations and the connection of the two front ports are still incomplete.

The handoff check adds the parcel containment plan, the whole sections of the two ports, the junctions on both sides of the driveway and the two sloped connecting paths, and a section passing through the terrace top, risers, lower waiting area, and ground together. The tolerance consumes the [shared reference](../01-storeys.md#storey-datums). Once real source exists, the world/site/space identifiers, transforms, boundaries, and contacts of the same output are read, and two-way views are confirmed in the [complete observation](../04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation). The row count of the input table is not a surface or observation count, and the current containment, grounding, connection, and planting interference are unverified.
