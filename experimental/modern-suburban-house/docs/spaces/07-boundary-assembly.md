This design is a spatial handoff for generating the partitions between existing rooms only once within the [two storeys](01-storeys.md#storey-datums). The room inner finish outlines, partition reservation thickness, and door positions consume the original owners that the [room connections](05-route-network.md#room-route-network) point to. Room coordinates are not set again here, and differing inputs are not hidden by averaging the midline of two rooms. If the inner boundaries of two rooms abutting on the same storey and the wall reservation between them do not match, the assembly stops and the original plan is corrected.

The structural substrate owner in the table below generates the common body and opening cuts of the shared partition only once. The inner finishes and reveals visible from both sides stay with [each room's complete face owner](03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff). No second opaque plate is made in which the prototype or surface of the structural substrate overlaps the finish at the same position. The author in charge is in every case this production's single author, and the names in the table are names of design connections. The real boundary ids and counts are read from the output generated from source.

| ground-storey | living-room ↔ front-entry, and the segment of living-room ↔ service-access outside the stair | `src/spaces/rooms/living.ts` |
| ground-storey | The front partition of kitchen-dining-family ↔ living-room / service-access / pantry | `src/spaces/rooms/common.ts` |
| ground-storey | powder-room ↔ service-access / laundry-mudroom | `src/spaces/rooms/powder.ts` |

Each row applies only to the segments that really exist between rooms. No new wall is made between counterparts grouped by a slash in the table. The [open connection of the entry and service](rooms/service.md#service-access-plan) and the [hall arrival at the stair end](rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-hall-plan) are kept as wall-free connections. Between the living room/service and the common area there is a large void under the existing lintel, and the rest of the front partition is closed. The whole wall is not deleted on the ground that it is a connection edge.

For the [main block/garage shared wall](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent), `src/spaces/garage.ts` below the garage roof and `src/spaces/envelope/right.ts` for the exposed siding above the roof meet by height on the same plan reference, and the [stair and protection/separation boundaries](02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening) keep the structural responsibility of the existing `src/spaces/stair.ts`. The interstorey body that consumes the stair owner's passage hole and the wall/floor plate junctions are assigned by the [single interstorey assembly](08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-edge-junctions). The fixed bodies, openings, and surrounds of the entry coat closet and the upper linen closet are owned by [entry](rooms/entry.md#entry-coat-storage) and [upper-hall](rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-linen-storage) respectively, and the closed leaves, rails, and hardware of those openings are owned by the [storage prototype](../models/05-closet-fittings.md#closet-fitting-surfaces). The stair structure is not made again. The stair surroundings consume the [height-specific closed walls/open protective boundaries](02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights), and the plan reservations are not extruded wholesale into blocked walls two storeys high. The height of an ordinary interior partition is from that storey's finished floor to the ceiling and does not overwrite the separate heights of the stair/storage.

`src/spaces/boundaries.ts` is planned to take on the handoff of the same boundary of rooms, walls, and openings and the junction calculation below, and owns no wall geometry or elevation/room finish. The assembly passes together, for each boundary, the two counterpart spaces, storey, segment, height role, structural substrate owner, and the finish owners on both sides. An interior wall with only one counterpart, unexplained overlapping rooms, an interface without an owner, or a state in which two structural owners generated the same interface is a failure. Where one side of the interior is the non-passage zone under the stair, that role is stated, and it is not counted as a new room or a hidden passage. The whole storey plans, the sections at the relevant heights, and the shared face census are the inspection addresses, and the real assembly is unverified. The local +Z of a wall boundary with one exterior counterpart is authored to point from the interior space to the exterior. The engine's short probe at the wall midplane may reach no room, so outward is not guessed by leaving the default axis rotation as is. When +Z is flipped, the local horizontal coordinate and the outline/opening coordinates are mirrored too, so every world vertex and void keeps its original position. The boundaries between two interior spaces keep the existing coordinate direction. Storage enclosures and body/floor plate junctions are interior contacts recorded in kind and are not paid by the mere name of an envelope face.

The shared partitions above share the end, L, T, and cross junctions where they meet at the same height. The end of one straight wall is not unconditionally extended to the center of another wall, and no hole is left at a corner because both sides end at their inner faces. `src/spaces/boundaries.ts` derives the plan zone of the meeting from the [original room outlines and thicknesses](#interior-boundary-ownership), and treats as one junction only the part where the height intervals overlap. Faces on different storeys or above and below the stair are not merged merely because their plan projections overlap.

The junction zone is generated only once as a common body, and that zone is subtracted from each straight body. The responsibility for generating a junction between ordinary partitions is assigned to the first owner when the source paths meeting in the table above are sorted lexicographically. The responsibility does not change with document row order or runtime evaluation order. Where a partition meets an exterior wall, the garage shared wall, or the stair structure, that existing structural owner takes the junction and the ordinary partition ends at that interface. This assignment is a responsibility for the calculated common body, not a rule that hands the visually complete face of the room wrapping the corner to another author. The wall finish of the same room closes its own corner continuously.

The position of a wall opening comes from the [original room owner](05-route-network.md#room-route-network) and is passed with the same cut boundary to the common body and the finishes on both sides. The single spaces room H2 designated in that table as the design boundary/opening owner of each door opening makes only the wall cut, the reveal, and the floor finish transition below. The [interior door model prototype](../models/03-interior-doors.md#interior-door-members) receives this cut and makes the closed jambs, casing, leaf, hinges, and handles once, and the room on the other side observes the opposite faces of the same members. A leaf is not generated in each of the two rooms. The doorless openings from the living room/service into the common area use the same cut too. Exterior doors and windows keep the elevation/room roles of the [exterior opening handoff](06-openings.md#external-opening-interface). It is a failure if an opening is blocked again by another junction body or the closed-door state is mistaken for a void member of the wall.

The supporting interfaces of wall attachments also consume the real wall face after the same cut. Towel bars, mirrors, upper cabinets, and shelves are not assigned by room name and plan wall direction alone. The whole interface, with the height reference of that storey applied, must lie on a closed wall segment, and no fake support crossing an opening is generated. Each room owner compares in the same coordinates the maximum occupancy of its attachments and contents with the closed frames/casings and the opening occupancy of [interior doors](../models/03-interior-doors.md#interior-door-members), [exterior doors](../models/02-exterior-doors.md#exterior-door-surfaces), and [windows](../models/01-windows.md#window-surface-partitions). On a collision, the attachment placement is corrected in the original room, and the door is not blocked and the wall of the opposite room is not moved. Movable objects that deliberately cover an opening, such as curtains in front of a window, also distinguish the wall support position from the use state.

Under interior doors at the same height, the [floor owner of that storey](03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff) provides a substrate continuous through the wall thickness direction. The finish transition line on the floor is set at the midplane of the rough partition thickness, and each room's finish meets at that line. The floor boundary is not moved by the hinge position of the leaf. The floor threshold/transition face is integrated by the one spaces room H2 that is the owner of that door opening in the 05 table above, and both rooms consume the same height and interface. The separate door fill is a member of models/03 and does not copy this floor face. This same-height rule is not applied to [the mudroom/garage step](rooms/laundry.md#laundry-plan), the porch and garden door, or the stair floor hole, and each existing datum/passage boundary is preserved.

Under the four building entrances of the main block and garage, the [separate handoff of the ground substrate](10-ground-floor.md#ground-threshold-junctions) assigns the substrate and threshold passing through the exterior wall/shared wall thickness. The reservation under the lower limit of the wall opening is divided too, so the supporting substrate and the wall body do not stack, and the central transition line of ordinary room doors is not moved to exterior doors or the mudroom step.

The check adds height-specific sections and in-room views from both sides for every interior shared wall on both storeys, its real openings, and every junction that wall meets. Closed/open doors, different floor finishes, and the upper and lower boundaries beside the stair are read together. The [boundary tolerance](00-building.md#main-building-extent) is used for the match comparison of the same boundary input and is not an authority to hide small gaps. Whether the public engine can express this common body/cut/face handoff has not been run yet, and if it is not supported, the expression limit is recorded and raised to the coordinator. The real ids, duplicates/gaps, threshold continuity, clear width, surface census, and frames are unverified.

This junction connects the exterior boundaries that meet inside the reservations of the [main block exterior walls](00-building.md#main-building-extent) and the [garage exterior walls/shared wall](00-building.md#attached-garage-extent). The outer face of each elevation, the inner faces of rooms, and the existing door, window, and storey relations are kept, and no separate room or entrance is made. The tops that reach the roof consume the [roof contact across the whole wall thickness](roof/00-junctions.md#roof-wall-head-junctions), and different roof heights are not averaged. The junctions of interior partitions keep the [separate assignment](#interior-boundary-junctions) above.

| Zone that really meets | Single assignment of the common body |
| --- | --- |
| Two front corners of the main block | The front elevation owner takes the corner body inside the front wall thickness. The left/right straight bodies end at the inner face of the front wall. |
| Two back corners of the main block | The rear elevation owner takes the corner body inside the back wall thickness. The left/right straight bodies end at the inner face of the back wall. |
| Right front/back corners of the garage | Taken by the front/rear elevation owners respectively. The garage right straight body ends between the inner faces of the two walls. |
| Left ends of the garage front/back walls | The existing garage shared wall owner takes priority. The two garage walls end at the garage-side face of the shared wall and make no overlapping end post inside the main block wall thickness. |
| Front/rear exterior wall junctions of the [main block roof step](../settings/10-house.md#main-mass) wall | The bodies inside the really overlapping front/rear exterior wall thickness are taken by the front/rear owners respectively. The step wall of the right owner excludes that zone and connects the remaining eave segment outside the exterior wall and the roof segment inside the exterior wall. |

This assignment is not a rule that copies the plan corner rectangle wholesale to every height. Only the height intervals where the lower/upper boundaries of each body really overlap are shared, and door and window voids are excluded in the same way. The main block/garage shared wall divides, on one X/Z structural reference, into the garage body up to the weather line of the garage roof top and the right siding body above it. The two bodies meet at that weather line, and the upper one continues up to below the main block roof, leaving no gap at the boundary. The overlap zone of the left chimney and the main block wall is also integrated into one body boundary inside the existing left owner and does not block the living room's firebox interface again.

`src/spaces/boundaries.ts` calculates and passes on the zones and height boundaries of the same meeting and adds no geometry. The structural substrate the table assigns is distinguished from the [complete visual surfaces](03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff). For example, the side finish of a corner body taken by front remains a continuous face of the left/right elevation owner. The outer intersection line of those two weather faces is handed once to the [closed corner trim prototype](../models/15-outdoor.md#exterior-corner-trim), and the spaces elevation and boundary sources do not make that board. The corner trim or one room's finish is not split because of the body assignment. The author in charge is, as before, this production's single author.

The checks are height-specific plans and top sections of every exterior wall corner of the main block and garage, both ends of the garage shared wall, the crossings of the step wall with the front and rear exterior walls, and the chimney contacts. The union and intersection of real body occupancy, whether openings are blocked again, and the connections of each complete elevation/room are read from the same output. The [shared tolerance](01-storeys.md#storey-datums) and the [complete observation](04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) are applied, and the table's row count is not used as a junction/surface census. The real division, closure, voids, and GPU frames are unverified.
