Realize the entry-side wall sconce from the [entry porch settings](../settings/10-house.md#porch-entry) as one point light, `light:front-porch:door-sconce`. The rough [front-door](../spaces/rooms/entry.md#entry-plan) opening lies on the main house's front wall at X = [0.40, 1.40], Y = [0, 2.20] m, and the [porch floor](../spaces/porch.md#porch-platform-access) is Y = 0 m over X = [-5.75, 2.20], Z = [0, 2.20] m. Place the sconce light beside the -X jamb opposite the door hinge, at X = 0.15 m, Z = 0.10 m, projecting 0.10 m from the front wall's outer face Z = 0 m, and Y = 1.80 m above the porch floor. The fixture body's frontmost point is Z = 0.17 m under the [sconce prototype](../models/17-light-fixtures.md#porch-wall-sconce), so the 0.10 m light point is inside its shade. This height is below the [porch front beam](../spaces/porch.md#porch-roof-columns) at Y = [2.45, 2.70] m, and the leaf opens indoors toward -Z, so it does not overlap door operation. Subtracting the 0.17 m body projection still leaves 1.63 m of the porch's effective 1.80 m depth. The light is approximately 2,700 K (1.00, 0.72, 0.45), with `intensity` 15 cd and `range` 3.0 m; it has no reason to be on in the [daytime reference state](../settings/20-verification.md#lighting-state), so it is off in review frames. Under the [static reference state](00-lighting-frame.md#lighting-static-state), off means creating no light record while retaining the fixture geometry. Settings requires no other exterior fixtures, so none is placed at the garage front, rear garden door or elsewhere.

The source owner is `src/systems/lighting.ts`. The actual 32-light list contains no `light:front-porch:door-sconce`, retaining only the `fixture:front-porch:door-sconce` body in instances. In the original whole-building exterior and entry-side close-up, the dark shade/glass body at X=0.15m·Y=1.80m reads without emission to the left of the wooden entry door. It is below the porch beam, and the door opens in the opposite indoor direction without intersecting the body.
