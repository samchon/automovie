# Site map

## Site boundary and orientation {#site-boundary-and-orientation}

The site is a shallow suburban lot with the street on negative Z, a front walk and concrete driveway at the front, a modest lawn on the remaining visible ground, and a fenced rear edge. The house and attached garage sit inside one footprint; there are no detached structures. The front walk aligns to the entry door and the driveway aligns to the two-car garage door.

## Building placement and access {#building-placement-and-access}

The main house occupies X -5.5 to 5.5 and Z -4.8 to 4.8. The garage occupies X 5.5 to 11.3 and Z -4.8 to 1.4, sharing the east wall interface with the front-end service band. A 1.35 m front walk reaches the porch. A 5.6 m driveway reaches the garage apron. The mudroom door is the only interior-to-garage service connection; the front door remains the public entry.

## Site review consequence {#site-review-consequence}

The site must be inspected from front, rear, left, and right exterior views and from an overhead placement view. The acceptance question is whether the one house and one attached garage read as one plausible lot arrangement, with no accidental second building or vehicle filling the garage. Slope, drainage performance, and property-line compliance are unverified because no surveyed site or terrain source was provided.
