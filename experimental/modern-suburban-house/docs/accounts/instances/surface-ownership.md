The [complete-surface contract](../../contracts/surface-ownership.md#whole-surface-owner) assigns a surface's author and file in phase 1. This account examines whether authorship divides when instances consumes the [exterior-surface handoff](../../spaces/03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff) and [room-interior handoff](../../spaces/03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff). The placement documents here contain 5 rule/lighting H2s in 00, 11 ground-floor/shared-interior H2s in 01, 4 upper-floor H2s in 02, 6 exterior/site H2s in 03, and 6 opening H2s in 04, totaling 32 H2s.

| Instance set | Surface datum and instance responsibility | Duplication boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [siding course](../../instances/03-exterior-repetition.md#siding-course-law) | Receives each elevation owner's base, wall-top, window-void and trim boundaries to cut and place the kth prototype board on that surface. | Wall body, base and casing are made by the existing spaces/models owners; a course is not another complete wall panel in front of the wall. |
| [shingle course](../../instances/03-exterior-repetition.md#shingle-course-law) | Receives the eave, ridge, valley and chimney boundaries of eight roof slopes to determine course order and cutting. | Roof structure, eave undersides and chimney remain with spaces owners; course-mesh geometry remains with the shingle model. |
| [Window/door filling](../../instances/04-opening-fill.md#opening-fill-membership) | Selects prototypes corresponding one-to-one with spatial opening ids and places them within the same outlines. | Spaces makes opening cuts, wall reveals and threshold plates; an instance neither cuts a new hole nor covers a wall face. |
| [Garage and storage doors](../../instances/04-opening-fill.md#exterior-door-fill-placement) | Closed garage-door panels/rails and sliding storage doors transform each model's surface partitions only once. | Does not make a new rail per rail reservation or count garage-door panels again as siding courses. |
| [Interior furniture/fixtures](../../instances/01-ground-rooms.md#kitchen-fixtures), [Upper-floor furniture](../../instances/02-upper-rooms.md#primary-furniture) | Room reservations supply position and use clearance, models supply closed prototype surfaces, and materials supply their final finishes. | Furniture does not replace complete wall/floor/ceiling surfaces or shrink prototypes to obtain placement clearance. |
| [Curtains](../../instances/01-ground-rooms.md#window-curtain-members) | Places an interior model with the same `rod`, `bracket` and `curtain` surfaces at each of eight windows. | These are separate from glass, frames and interior walls, and frame setback is not applied twice. |
| [Planting and terrace furniture](../../instances/03-exterior-repetition.md#planting-individuals) | Grounds model trunks/crowns on the map-datum terrain and places terrace tables/chairs on spatial paving. | Terrain, paving and fences are spaces/maps-owned surfaces; tree shadows are not plates for concealing gaps. |

[Exterior repetition exclusions](../../instances/03-exterior-repetition.md#exterior-exclusions) remove porch columns, railings, garage-door panels and joints from the repetition population. Material bindings for siding, shingles and furniture respond only to model surface ids; instances creates no new material face. Existing opening fills, fixed fit-out, exterior repetition and fixed lighting supply a partial compiled population. Complete duplication/omission/gap review still needs the `(instance id, prototype id, face id, surface owner)` correspondence across all design families and actual junction frames; existing producers do not close unbuilt furniture/covering/prop or inactive planting drafts.
