[coverage-map](../../settings/00-production.md#coverage-map) connects the delivery, living-space and review file groups, and [operative-subjects](../../settings/00-production.md#operative-subjects) links not only the house, lights and doors but also operators, measuring tools and judging parties to dedicated H2s. The primary bedroom, two children's bedrooms, shower bathroom and tub bathroom have separate H2s in 10 because their door affiliations and furniture decisions can change independently. The service band owns connections rather than individual rooms' interior finishes, and openings owns the member/state criteria for windows and doors shared by rooms. Execution, installation, server and Git are also separated in 20 because their change authorities differ. These distinctions are not collapsed into a single evidence address for the whole file.

The two-floor area condition of [house-scale](../../settings/10-house.md#house-scale) excludes the garage and porch. For example, applying an admissible 11.6 m × 10.6 m to both floors gives 245.92㎡, within 235–255㎡. This calculation demonstrates that the ranges are consistent; it does not select a final outline or inspect projection areas. A combination such as 11.8 m × 11.0 m × 2 = 259.6㎡ exceeds the area ceiling even though width and depth are individually admissible, and is refused by the body's simultaneous-satisfaction condition. This account does not settle new building dimensions.

In [coordinate-units](../../settings/00-production.md#coordinate-units), +X points toward the garage on the right when viewed from the front, and +Z toward the front pavement, using the same front/back orientation as the front living room and rear common area in 10. rad is the shared angle unit, while ° in 10's roof slope and 20's FOV is human-readable range notation and must be converted to the same angle when used as an actual design value. Geographic north and real solar time were not adopted as coordinate or lighting grounds.

The 0.60 m human occupancy width and 0.75 m basket-inclusive width in [use-profile](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) are smaller than the 0.80 m door, 0.90 m route and 0.95 m stair targets, and the 1.90 m height is below the 2.00 m headroom target. These differences show only the arithmetic compatibility of inspection targets. Actual passage around furniture, door operation and bends must be inspected separately in compiled spaces. The stair's two flights and intermediate landing arrive at one upper corridor, and both bathrooms are entered directly from that corridor without requiring a primary-bedroom-only route. Wall separation for the tub bathroom and carpet termination at the shower bathroom disallow a single shared bathroom under two names.

As a static library, the production invents no event chronology, travel time or running time, and uses s only when a time unit is needed. Lights are on, but water/drainage, appliances and weather do not move; render operation is not confused with simulation of living functions. The CJS server in [execution-boundary](../../settings/20-verification.md#execution-boundary) delivers engine outputs to the browser, while [renderer-boundary](../../settings/20-verification.md#renderer-boundary) requires an actual WebGL display of that result, so server interpretation and client 3D are compatible. Public APIs and resource suitability remain source-review duties requiring the installed packages to be read.

The vehicle in original image 02 and the stair differences between interior references are subordinate to garage's vehicle prohibition and stair's single-stair selection respectively. Self-review carries no approval authority. The author owns root `pnpm install` and joint lockfile submission in a dependency-changing turn, explicit-path commits/pushes, and a short server-startup check in an implementation turn; the coordinator owns continuously running and maintaining the server. The instruction to omit pull --rebase in the shared checkout is reflected in submission-boundary. At the original settings-stage comparison, downstream outputs had not yet been authored. They exist in the current checkout, but this comparison of adopted conditions is not a current-output inspection and does not establish visual quality, the actual observation set or completion.
