[Observation allocation](../../settings/20-verification.md#observation-allocation) adopts the [original denominator](../../contracts/observation-denominator.md#compiled-denominator). Exterior setting, exposed elevations and corners, roofs and undersides, openings and entrances, and every room's threshold, four inner corners and four directions from its center are questions derived from actual compiled boundaries. The original also retains additional duties for occluded regions in nonrectangular spaces. 10's service band, two children's rooms and two bathrooms require actual separation and access, while storage prevents a walk-in room from being concealed under the name of a built-in closet. Settings therefore permits no reduction of obligations by merging room names or selecting representative views.

[data-authority](../../settings/20-verification.md#data-authority), [frame-condition](../../settings/20-verification.md#frame-condition), and [operator-access](../../settings/00-production.md#operator-access) respectively divide authority over positions/dimensions, viewpoint conditions and operation. The intact-envelope and interior observations of 01·03·04·05 have a different role from the cutaway inspection of 02, and all are added to the derived set. This settings-stage account supplies no implementation room/view counts or pose/binding lists: allocating duties does not execute them. The current checkout has compiled-space and observation producers, whose reachability, viewpoint inclusion and image results require their own review; they are not established by this account.

[Completion and recording](../../settings/20-verification.md#completion-boundary) requires simultaneous resolution of author and observer lists and the modeller's own image judgment; [role-boundary](../../settings/20-verification.md#role-boundary) does not treat observation as approval or a cause. An undelivered observer list is not regarded as empty. This settings evidence allocation means neither visual approval of the five scenes nor completion of the house. It does not verify current GPU RENDERER, frames or complete observation results.
