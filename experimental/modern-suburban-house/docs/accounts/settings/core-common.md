The comparison population comprises all of [Production scope and use conditions](../../settings/00-production.md), [Identity of the house and living spaces](../../settings/10-house.md), and [Representation and verification conditions](../../settings/20-verification.md). Each file has the document role of settings; this account answers only their allocation across files. It does not replace principle answers for individual H2s.

The users and operators in 00 provide conditions for the doors, stairs and rooms in 10, and the house in 10 is exposed to the current frames and complete observation in 20. Keeping only 10 would describe the visible house without deciding who inspects it through which routes. Keeping only 20 would provide GPU and observation procedures without the identity of the house and rooms to be read. 00 connects their delivery scope, users, coordinates and language. No film, dialogue or family-biography file was created because it has no role in this static library.

The stair addresses relationships among its single route, landing, floor opening, railing and dimension derivation, whereas the pantry addresses the narrower decisions of shelves, door and food access. The two children's bedrooms share a function while defining color, their own windows, separate doors and observation; the two bathrooms distinguish the booth from the tub and require direct corridor access. Ceiling, door rails, floor and storage are allocated even to the empty garage, so vehicle omission does not lead to omission of its interior.

The settings-stage comparison preserved the three approved-draft file roles and room-specific bodies. The submission-boundary correction removed pull --rebase and reflected shared-checkout and non-fast-forward handling, following the user's instruction. Increased comments are not new spatial authorship. Settings inherits no authored parent layer, so a narrative parent-length comparison does not apply. Design/source had not yet been authored at that settings-stage comparison; they exist in the current checkout and require their own realization review. This account allocates the two floors and complete interior of one house and does not establish their geometry, reachability or visual completion.
