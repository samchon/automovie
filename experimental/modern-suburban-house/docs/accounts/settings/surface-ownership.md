[surface-allocation](../../settings/20-verification.md#surface-allocation) makes the [original complete-surface contract](../../contracts/surface-ownership.md#whole-surface-owner) a phase-1 closure condition. [lifecycle-boundary](../../settings/20-verification.md#lifecycle-boundary) requires closing the mass/spatial graph and this declaration together before proceeding to the envelope and repeated modules, so it does not permit building the envelope in a large source file first and dividing it later. Even implementation by one person does not justify omitted surface or file boundaries.

The spaces, models, materials and instances in [build-allocation](../../settings/00-production.md#build-allocation) are independent kinds of work, not authority to own the same wall again with different coordinates and repetition criteria. Exterior elevations and room interiors are different complete surfaces, but the shared wall-body datum must be singular under the original contract. Opening perimeters, stair openings and floor-slab junctions are also within the assignment scope, and surface allocation must preserve openings' and stair's requirements for actual thickness and passage openings.

This account connects the handoff responsibilities borne by the complete settings population. The actual list by surface id, boundary, assigned author and source file is a phase-1 output produced with topology. There is no claim that the list has been authored or checked for omissions/duplicates yet, and actual satisfaction of face assignments is unverified. Role allocation among surface-sharing branches does not replace the actual census.
