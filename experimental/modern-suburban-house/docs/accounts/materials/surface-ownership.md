The [original contract](../../contracts/surface-ownership.md#whole-surface-owner) assigns one owner to each complete visual surface. Materials binds only finishes to [exterior](../../spaces/03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff)/[interior](../../spaces/03-surface-owners.md#interior-surface-handoff) spaces faces and [model partitions](../../models/00-model-frame.md#model-surface-partition-naming). One **final material instance** is attached per face; lines where different final materials meet align with host-member boundaries under the [binding rule](../../materials/00-material-frame.md#material-binding-rule).

The four files currently in `docs/materials` contain 49 H2s. The 5 in `00-material-frame.md` are common rules/review addresses and receive no surfaces directly. The 12 in `01-exterior.md`, 13 in `02-interior-shell.md`, and 19 in `03-furnishings.md` are 44 finish responses. These counts are an H2 denominator; they do not mean actual binding inspection has completed for every `(host surface id, final material)` pair enumerated by each finish H2. Current `src/materials/model-bindings.ts` contains the building catalogue and rejects zero/multiple finish matches for each emitted model face. That source check concerns produced members; it does not complete absent object families or the full design pair census and current rendered response review.

Structural boundaries are allocated as follows. Siding/brick meet at the base's upper line; wood/carpet/tile/laundry flooring meet at [interior transitions below doors](../../spaces/07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-junctions); wall paint/wall tile meet at the host's tile-region termination. [White exterior trim](../../materials/01-exterior.md#trim-white) also binds to `exterior-trim` on the [L-shaped corner board](../../models/15-outdoor.md#exterior-corner-trim). [Brick joints](../../materials/01-exterior.md#brick-red-brown) and [tile joints](../../materials/02-interior-shell.md#tile-grout) are respectively color, roughness and normal UV masks **within one material** bound to existing brick/tile faces. There is no recessed-joint mesh, separate `grout` face id or second material binding.

The `curtain` partition of [Primary-bedroom and six-window curtains](../../models/13-bedrooms.md#primary-window-curtains) is assigned the [textile finish](../../materials/03-furnishings.md#towel-curtain-textile); those curtain bodies and their bindings remain an unbuilt draft. The [fireplace's metal firebox and wooden mantel](../../models/11-living.md#fireplace-insert-mantel) are separate from the brick structure and receive [black firebox](../../materials/03-furnishings.md#firebox-black) and [furniture wood](../../materials/03-furnishings.md#furniture-wood) respectively. Current fixed fit-out produces the insert, and the building catalogue supplies its model-qualified finishes. Source existence and declared face ids do not establish a new contact, optical-response or GPU verdict.
