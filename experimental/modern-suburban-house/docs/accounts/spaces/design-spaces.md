# Addresses and closure of the spatial population

## Addresses of independent spatial decisions {#spatial-addressability}
<!--

Independently changeable interior and entry-door openings belong to their room plan H2s, garden/garage-front doors to elevation H2s, and the maintenance gate to its path H2; 05's connection table cites them by id, while plot, terrain and external roads are explicitly maps inputs rather than hidden decisions.

File groups were compared to establish whether every spatial decision can be cited independently. This account answers only allocation of H2 boundaries.

- Exterior openings: each elevation has closure and placement H2s, with a separate H2 for each window and door. For example, the [stair window](../../spaces/envelope/front.md#stair-front-window) can change independently of living-room or bedroom windows.
- Stair: [spatial reservation](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-reservation), [connector](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-connector-handoff), [floor opening](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-floor-opening), [clear width](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-clearance), and [height-dependent boundaries](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-boundary-heights) each have an H2.
- Rooms: fourteen rooms excluding the [service passage](../../spaces/rooms/service.md#service-access-plan) each have a plan H2 defining boundaries/entrance and a separate furniture/fixture use or route H2; the service passage defines boundaries, entrance and passage in one H2. The [common area](../../spaces/rooms/common.md#common-room-plan) adds five H2s for kitchen wall, island, dining table, family room and routes.
- Interior doors: because a door opening is a room entrance, that room's plan H2 owns coordinates, hinge and opening. Among exterior doors, the [entry plan](../../spaces/rooms/entry.md#entry-plan) owns the entry door, elevation H2s own the [garden door](../../spaces/envelope/rear.md#garden-door) and [garage-front door](../../spaces/envelope/front.md#garage-front-opening), and the maintenance-path H2 owns the [maintenance gate](../../spaces/site/side-walk.md#side-gate-interface). The [connection table](../../spaces/05-route-network.md#room-route-network) cites those values by id/link without copying them.
- Shared boundaries: H2s in 07–10 and roof/00 own walls, floor slabs, ceilings, ground floor and roof intersections, with rooms/elevations consuming them through links.
- Site: pedestrian path, driveway, maintenance path, terrace and fence have separate files; terrace and fence each divide further into three H2s.

Some decisions do not yet have addresses. Plot boundary, external pavement/roads, terrain and planting locations are written only as inputs for the [maps handoff](../../spaces/site/00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs). These are not decisions hidden in another owner's prose; they remain unauthored because maps is disabled.

Datums and topology: all spaces lie within `house-site` in the [common coordinates](../../settings/00-production.md#coordinate-units). The [main-house](../../spaces/00-building.md#main-building-extent) and [garage](../../spaces/00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) outlines, [two storeys](../../spaces/01-storeys.md#storey-datums), each room's plan H2 and exterior zones are named. The [connection table](../../spaces/05-route-network.md#room-route-network) connects departure, boundary/opening owner, arrival and storey from exterior paving to the upper dressing room. Room plan H2s also state their own storey and adjacent rooms, allowing the table and room files to be compared.

Envelope and interior: window/door H2s on the front, rear, left and right each bind to one room; only the [stair window](../../spaces/envelope/front.md#stair-front-window) binds to main-stair's inter-floor space rather than a room. The [opening handoff](../../spaces/06-openings.md#external-opening-interface) assigns void coordinates to elevations and reveals to rooms. The [outline](../../spaces/00-building.md#main-building-extent) defines exterior-wall thickness and inner limits, [storeys](../../spaces/01-storeys.md#storey-datums) defines floor heights, and [exterior-wall junctions](../../spaces/07-boundary-assembly.md#exterior-boundary-junctions) defines corner bodies once. [Right-side openings](../../spaces/envelope/right.md#right-openings) explicitly includes windowless sections such as the service rooms behind the shared garage wall.

Access and circulation: the represented exterior entrances are the four entry, garden, garage and maintenance doors. Every interior door between rooms and doorless open connection is also in the connection table; coat-closet and linen-closet doors are separate storage interfaces rather than room routes. Fourteen rooms excluding the primary bedroom consume the [use-profile](../../settings/00-production.md#use-profile) human/basket occupant in plan or use/route H2s. Open working states, such as [mudroom crossing](../../spaces/rooms/laundry.md#laundry-through-route) and the [pantry use route](../../spaces/rooms/pantry.md#pantry-use-route), are inspected separately from passage states. The stair connects the entry and upper corridor through a [single connector](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-connector-handoff).

Inspection set: [observation derivation](../../spaces/04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) creates exterior/per-room questions from compiled topology. The 54 room/site H2s (38 room, 16 site) each add their own verification items marked unverified; room-file door operation, use states with open appliances/drawers and site-file paving/step sections enter those additional questions, with the [five reference comparisons](../../spaces/04-observations.md#reference-spatial-comparisons) added above them. Observation counts are not declared as constants.

This closure compares design documents. Current spaces source produces storey bindings, opening cuts, compiled routes and observation poses; their existence does not establish every current reachability/containment question, later object contact or direct GPU judgment. This account supplies no new output inspection or complete realization verdict.
