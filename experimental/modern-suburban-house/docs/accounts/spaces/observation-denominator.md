The [original denominator](../../contracts/observation-denominator.md#compiled-denominator) requires exterior setting, every exposed elevation and corner, roofs and undersides, every opening and entrance, and one threshold, four inner corners and four center directions for each room. In spaces, [observation derivation](../../spaces/04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) connects this rule to spatial outputs. Observation counts derive from actual space, boundary, opening and roof records rather than being declared in documents.

Each owner adds its own questions. Room files describe separate observations for operating a door and passing through it, and rooms with appliances, drawers, wardrobes or chairs separately specify open working states. Elevation and roof files identify corners, eave undersides and intersections as inspection addresses. Site files specify section questions for paving, steps and contacts. Occluded parts of nonrectangular spaces also add questions. Examples include the two corners beside the [blue-gray bedroom](../../spaces/rooms/bedroom-three.md#bedroom-three-plan)'s linen closet and bends in the L-shaped [entry](../../spaces/rooms/entry.md#entry-plan), [upper hall](../../spaces/rooms/upper-hall.md#upper-hall-plan), and [service passage](../../spaces/rooms/service.md#service-access-plan). The walk-in [dressing room](../../spaces/rooms/wardrobe.md#primary-wardrobe-plan) is not omitted from observation because it is called storage.

The [five reference comparisons](../../spaces/04-observations.md#reference-spatial-comparisons) add to this denominator rather than replacing it. The 02 cutaway is an inspection-mode comparison and does not substitute for interior observation with an intact envelope.

The [completion boundary](../../contracts/observation-denominator.md#dual-completion) closes only when the author and delivered observer unfinished lists are both empty and the modeller directly judges and repairs the actual images. An undelivered list is not empty, and no separate verdict agent substitutes for that judgment. Current spaces source produces compiled topology and observation questions/cameras; recorded earlier frames and successful checks do not close all current room/elevation verification items. This account therefore claims neither visual completion nor a fresh GPU/RENDERER verdict from document relationships.
