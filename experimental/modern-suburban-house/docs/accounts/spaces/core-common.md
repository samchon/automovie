The comparison population comprises all 47 files under `docs/spaces`. This account answers only role allocation and layer boundaries across files; it does not replace each H2's principle answers.

Common files own datums that must be settled once. The [outline](../../spaces/00-building.md#main-building-extent) and [two storeys](../../spaces/01-storeys.md#storey-datums) provide room/elevation/roof coordinates and floor heights, while the [single stair](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-reservation) is the only connection between floors. The [room-connection table](../../spaces/05-route-network.md#room-route-network) hands off the boundary sequence from the entry to every room. [Surface ownership](../../spaces/03-surface-owners.md#exterior-surface-handoff) assigns complete surfaces to files before source authorship, and [observation derivation](../../spaces/04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) creates inspection questions from those outputs. [Openings](../../spaces/06-openings.md#external-opening-interface), [walls](../../spaces/07-boundary-assembly.md#interior-boundary-ownership), [floor slabs](../../spaces/08-floor-assembly.md#interstorey-floor-boundary), [ceilings](../../spaces/09-ceiling-assembly.md#upper-ceiling-closure), [ground floor](../../spaces/10-ground-floor.md#main-ground-floor-base), and [roof intersections](../../spaces/roof/00-junctions.md#roof-mass-allocation) each define shared boundaries between two owners once.

Of the remaining thirty-five files, thirty-three each own one complete surface or room. Their units are four elevations, eight roof slopes, the porch, fifteen interior spaces and five site files for pedestrian path, driveway, maintenance path, terrace and fence. The remaining two site files own no surfaces: [site access](../../spaces/site/00-access.md#site-access-interface) defines house-site containment, internal routes and maps handoff once, while [paving support](../../spaces/site/01-paving-support.md#paving-depth-reservation) defines the support thickness, support and contact rules shared by paving/porch owners once. This decomposition matches the file boundaries required by the [surface-ownership contract](../../contracts/surface-ownership.md#whole-surface-owner). Removing one file would move that surface/room's boundaries, openings and use reservations into another file's prose or lose them.

Each owner states its layer boundary. Furniture/appliance/shelf tables are upper-bound occupancy reservations; models consumes prototypes, legs and handle geometry, instances consumes placement of three stools and six chairs, materials consumes material/optical values, and systems consumes per-room lights. Plot, external pavement, terrain and planting are written only as inputs for the [maps handoff](../../spaces/site/00-access.md#map-handoff-inputs), rather than being decided by spaces. The [render handoff](../../spaces/04-observations.md#engine-render-handoff) retains only spatial decisions: spatial faces must become model elements of the same surface owner; nonrectangular spaces use convex-cell unions or closed shells; a walking standable surface is not the visible floor; an observation-helper pose returning null does not count as success; and landing validation does not guarantee containment inside the landing. [Observation derivation](../../spaces/04-observations.md#spatial-observation-derivation) likewise receives pose-camera conditions only from [frame conditions](../../settings/20-verification.md#frame-condition). [Viewer execution handoff](../../settings/20-verification.md#viewer-handoff) owns viewer port 4173, `--port`, runner and startup checks; [actual 3D rendering boundary](../../settings/20-verification.md#renderer-boundary) owns camera, materials and depth; [lighting and reference state](../../settings/20-verification.md#lighting-state) owns lighting and shadows; spatial documents do not decide these values. Current spatial, model, placement, material, lighting and viewer source exists. Its partial realization does not establish complete compliance across unbuilt furniture/props or every actual rendered contact and observation.

## Consistency of the working language {#population-language}
<!--

Under the [working-language decision](../../settings/00-production.md#working-language), explanations, decisions and inspection sentences use English. Spatial ids (`front-entry`, `kitchen-dining-family`, `garage-front-door`, etc.), source paths (`src/spaces/rooms/entry.ts`, etc.), public API names (`lowerBuiltEnvironment`, `builtSpaceObservationStations`, etc.) and coordinate formulas (`D(Z)`, `q = h11 - h10 - h01 + h00`) retain their original form. They must match the code's strings and are therefore not translated.

Architectural/rendering terms including threshold, reveal, trim, sash, flight, connector, bbox, pose and census are used consistently. For example, the inner face around a door is a reveal, and the room-entry observation is a threshold observation. Sentence clarity remains a matter for independent review.

## Allocation proportionate to the declared scale {#population-proportion}
<!--

The narrower service passage and dressing room have less relational detail; each roof slope consumes the shared height/intersection calculations in roof/00 instead of duplicating them. The user-defined scale is an approximately 246㎡ two-floor main house, a right-side two-car garage and the complete interior. The [main-house outline](../../spaces/00-building.md#main-building-extent), totaling 246.10㎡ across two floors, and the attached [garage](../../spaces/00-building.md#attached-garage-extent) have fifteen interior-space owners, four elevations and eight roof slopes, matching the decomposition required by the [surface contract](../../contracts/surface-ownership.md#whole-surface-owner) for ownership by room, elevation and floor. No room or floor beyond this scale was added.

Decisions with greater consequences receive more detail. The [stair](../../spaces/02-stair.md#stair-reservation) separately addresses tread division, connector, floor opening, clear width and height-dependent protective boundaries. The [common area](../../spaces/rooms/common.md#common-room-plan) separates kitchen wall, island, dining table, family room and routes. The [service passage](../../spaces/rooms/service.md#service-access-plan) and [dressing room](../../spaces/rooms/wardrobe.md#primary-wardrobe-plan) make narrower decisions. The eight roof-slope files consume heights and intersections calculated once by [roof intersections](../../spaces/roof/00-junctions.md#roof-profile-datums). Repeating the same calculations for every slope would increase text while splitting the owner in two.

The population assigns decisions to room, face and junction owners and includes use/passage reservations. Current spaces source produces compiled geometry, topology and observation cameras, while actual reachability/containment measurements remain distinct from direct pixel judgment of every current view. This ownership account supplies no new geometry, gate or GPU verdict, and source existence does not close unbuilt later families or all unfinished observations. Allocation between design→source is assessed by each H2's actual realization and consumers rather than prose-to-TypeScript matching.
