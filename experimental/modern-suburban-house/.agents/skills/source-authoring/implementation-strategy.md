# Implementation strategy

Use this procedure when an implementation method is uncertain, an existing representation may be inadequate, or repeated corrections fail to improve the consumed result. The requirement and design owners define success. This procedure owns how to compare implementation paths and turn research into a tested choice.

## Establish the decision

Name the observable result, the current failure, and the joint conditions the replacement must satisfy. Include affected neighbors, intermediate states, invalid inputs, recovery, and the actual consuming path. Distinguish required behavior from an assumption introduced by the current implementation. A missing degree of freedom, an incorrect parameter, a faulty adapter, and a display error require different repairs.

Compare the current path with applicable existing operations, reusable representations or data, and new implementation. Inspect the actual source, required assets, supported controls, coordinate and identity conventions, permissions, dependencies, and consumer boundary. An available algorithm does not imply that its required data or adaptation is available. Verify a suspected external defect through its supported path before attributing an adapter's failure to the underlying method.

## Discriminate with experiments

State the hypothesis, competing explanation, fixed inputs, changed quantity, measurement basis, and result that would change the decision. Use an independent small case to check the instrument, then exercise the actual consumer on a representative unit with its coupled parts and states. Choose that unit to expose the uncertainty; an attractive neutral example alone is insufficient.

Compare correctness and cost under equivalent contracts. Separate preparation, repeated evaluation, validation, and presentation costs. A precomputed deformation and a general constraint solve are different workloads. A structural comparison can still inform the choice when workloads differ, but it cannot establish an equivalent-work speedup or quality ranking.

Retain source revisions, inputs, results, failed candidates, and unresolved questions in the existing research and review chronology. Distinguish an external author's reported result, a local reproduction, and a result observed through the intended consumer. A method can contribute useful principles or data even when its implementation is not selected.

## Integrate before expanding

Prove a complete representative path through authored input, implementation, consumer, and observable output before expanding an uncertain method's generality or the subject population. Check the joint conditions defined for that path, including interpolation and combinations where they apply. An isolated numerical improvement is a research result until the consumer realizes it.

Consider separating preparation from repeated evaluation when their inputs change at different rates. Reusable topology, basis data, attachments, and precomputed operators need explicit dependencies and invalidation; they cannot silently outlive a changed input. Preserve the required output and failure behavior when changing the computation. Performance does not justify reducing the acceptance conditions.

If the same failure returns or local improvements do not reach the consumer, revisit the formulation, representation, data, and assembly order before another correction. Choose the next experiment by the uncertainty it can resolve. Prior effort and implementation familiarity do not establish a reason to retain a method. Adopt the path supported by the complete conditions and record remaining limits with its canonical design owner.

Report research findings, integrated behavior, and verified delivery separately. Base remaining-time estimates on concrete remaining work, verified integration state, and unresolved dependencies. Report measured execution costs separately from estimates of implementation and verification effort. Neither experiment count nor a passing component check measures completion of the requested result.

## Workflow handoff

Production research uses [Research](../production-lifecycle/research.md) for external evidence and its interpretation. Narrowing the declared production population uses [Pilot](../production-lifecycle/pilot.md), which owns eligibility, selection, and resumption of the full population. A diagnostic sample does not itself narrow the evidence population or advance a stage.

A changed design follows [Upstream revision](../production-lifecycle/upstream-revision.md); experiments do not bypass its preservation or stage rules. [Review verification](../review-verification/SKILL.md) owns observation and completion, and [Evidence graph](../evidence-graph/SKILL.md) owns contract relationships. Use the host repository's corresponding owners when this procedure is invoked for library development.
