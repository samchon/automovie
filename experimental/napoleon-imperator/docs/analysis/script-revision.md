## Continuing this edition

Start with [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md), the current bodies and the [task router](../../.agents/skills/screenwriting/SKILL.md). The long-work route is settings construction → treatments construction → scripts construction → screenplay construction → screenplay naturalness. The following choices apply to this commissioned maintenance:

| Change | Edit and compare | Required handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact, character, relationship, place or object constraint | Its semantic owner in `docs/body/settings`, then every treatment that uses the changed value | Carry the corrected fact through treatments before scripts or screenplay consume it |
| Event, consequence, reveal or decisive choice | Its owner in `docs/body/treatments`, then affected scripts and screenplay | Preserve the complete causal progression and each consumer's inherited limits |
| Script action, blocking, dialogue or wording | `docs/body/scripts`, with the actual treatments and selected construction and expression questions | Complete content and expression together; compare the affected screenplay rather than create a Scripts naturalness edition |
| Screenplay event, knowledge, role or chronology | A separate construction repair at the earliest defective owner and its consumers | Record the changed meaning and repair scope; expression revision cannot conceal that change |
| A new screenplay expression pass | The reviewed construction frozen under `docs/revision/screenplay`, with work under `docs/naturalness/screenplay` | Follow the [expression procedure](../../.agents/skills/screenwriting/rewriting/procedure.md), independent closing review and exact comment-free transfer |
