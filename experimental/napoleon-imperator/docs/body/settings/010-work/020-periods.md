# The Era Across Eight Parts

## From the peak back to the final return {#periods}

The eight parts below are reference divisions for finding periods and people's scope of activity, not the actual number of released units. Part numbers in character canon also refer to the time ranges in this table. Current release arrangement and editing budget are set by the [production instructions](../../../instructions/001-adaptation.md#provisional-delivery); treatments and scripts own actual event order and scene partition. Whether reference divisions are split or combined into released units, preserve the fixed boundary of the first released unit and the scope of the whole life.

Parts 2 and 4 are especially dense with events and require editing attention so different battles are not replaced by one short montage.

## The last coffin and the person who remains {#ending}

Death on May 5, 1821 and return on December 15, 1840 are separate endings. Part 8 does not end the work at death; it also treats who rewrote and used the life. The final close view places Bertrand or Marchand, already encountered in scenes of exile and recording. Treatments makes the final choice, but does not replace the weight of the ending with a new anonymous spectator. Temporary placement at Les Invalides in 1840 differs from the tomb completed in 1861; do not put the latter on screen. The final author commentary also explains the boundary between fact and invention. Do not settle disputed figures such as crowd size and bequest amounts as a measure of glory.
