# Headquarters and Austerlitz in 1805

[Outline](../outline/index.md#100-history--010-austerlitz)

## Displaced outposts and an army not yet assembled {#november-28}

On November 28 French outposts at Vyškov, pressured by Bagration's vanguard and Kienmayer, withdraw behind Rousínov along the road to Brünn. Rousínov is also evacuated in the evening. Soult's French corps is dispersed around Austerlitz and nearby, Lannes's at Bošovice, Holubice, and Velešovice, Murat's cavalry on the Brünn–Vyškov road, and the Guard and grenadier reserve toward Brünn. Bernadotte has not yet arrived and Davout's reinforcements have not assembled. The marshals' anxiety arises from the actual state of the lost road and separated forces, not a judgment knowing the enemy's total strength or final victory in advance.

Prisoner counts differ: the bulletin gives 50, Thiébault 104, and Stutterheim about 100 and four officers. Seven lines of campfires are not a troop count either. Adopt retreat from the night of the 28th into the 29th and withdrawal from Austerlitz on the 29th, but do not combine differing accounts of 3 a.m., before dawn, full daylight, and 10 a.m. into one exact departure time. Source: [original headquarters record](../../../references/novels/settings/100-history/028-nov-28-headquarters-doubt.md#nov-28-headquarters-doubt), carrying forward its attributions to Thiébault pages 443–449, bulletin 30, Stutterheim, and Correspondence no. 9531.

## The post-house letter and the duel directed only at Soult {#post-house}

Thiébault's posthumous memoir conveys the evening of the 28th through Lannes's aide Subervie, who heard the exchange outside the door. Murat and Soult entrust Lannes with a joint recommendation to retreat. Newly arrived Lannes writes the letter from their explanation; the Emperor enters, picks it up, and reads it. Asked by the Emperor, Soult changes to an answer of loyalty that his corps can handle twice its number. Lannes protests that he only recently arrived and wrote on their entreaties and information, then challenges Soult alone. The Emperor walks on, apparently unaware of or not intervening in the quarrel, then agrees a retreat is necessary. After the marshals leave he dictates maneuver orders to Berthier. Agreement comes after Soult's answer and Lannes's protest.

Use the old Pozořice post house adopted by the source through comparison of local accounts and memoirs. Do not claim it has been proved identical to the memoir's “post house behind Velešovice.” A large stove, principal room, letter, door, and Subervie are in the memoir; dimensions and other furniture are unknown. A cold, low overcast night without moon, stars, rain, or snow carries forward the source's explicitly invented weather. Around 9 p.m. in the memoir is not a time confirmed by a contemporary document. If that value is followed, the meeting is after concentration orders sent at 7 and 8, while allowing that the memoir's clock may be wrong.

Lannes sends a challenge but receives no answer. Its date and time of dispatch are unknown. Just before battle on December 2 the memoir continues with Lannes's renewed protest that he thought Soult knew how to use a sword, Soult's answer that there are more important matters today, and Lannes's insulting retort. The three utterances are reconstructions conveying meaning, not recordings from the scene. On the 28th do not draw swords or fix a time and place for a duel. Murat does not also become a challenged party, nor does the Emperor forbid the duel. Do not say fighting in the south had still not begun when this exchange ended on the 2nd. Source: the original above and Thiébault pages 446–448 and 456. Do not merge Rapp's anecdote about unnamed marshals as independent testimony for this Soult scene.

## A maneuver that appears weak and the opponent's plan {#plans}

The French vacate the Pratzen and form a line with an apparently weak right. Adopt the French explanation that they hoped a moderate envoy and meeting would appear fearful, without claiming the Emperor completely controlled every enemy judgment. Dolgorukov's visit is within November 29–30, not fixed to the 28th. Dates of Savary's first mission, armistice proposal, and arrival of full powers differ between letters and bulletins; do not combine dates of different documents into a new single day. Do not bring the December 1 proclamation forward to November 29.

The Allies interweave Alexander's court, nominal commander Kutuzov, Weyrother's plan, and Austrian and Russian armies with different authority. Moving south to turn the French right and cut the road to Vienna creates risk of emptying the center. Do not write the opposing command as stupid from the start because the result is known. Numbers and organization of marching columns on the 27th and battle columns on the 1st differ; do not transfer forces of the same number unchanged. Source: original `100-history/028`, `029-moravia-approach-march`, `027-austerlitz`; also follow command, organization, and arrival limits of institutions `008-allied-hq-1805`, `017`–`020`, and `098` in the [battlefield adoption register](../120-research/020-source-register.md#armies-and-battles).

## Fog and sun, ponds and losses {#light-and-loss}

The Goldbach valley lies west, the low Pratzen ridge east. Dawn light seen through fog, calculated sunrise, and the sun visible from the heights are different observations. Do not unify a memoir's “fog lifted at seven” into a clear battlefield with the sun fully risen. Also follow adoption of exact bearings, elevations, and light for the Pratzen ridge, southern ponds, causeways, mud, and villages in original `080-places/036-austerlitz-ground-and-light` and `037-austerlitz-bivouac-night`.

Troop strength can be summarized only as study ranges of about 65,000–75,000 French and 73,000–89,000 Allied. Strength in an individual scene is a separate value for that unit and day. About 9,000 French total casualties and 1,600 killed are the source's adoption distinguishing killed, wounded, mortally wounded, and later roster analysis. “8 ou 900” is 800–900, not 8,000–9,000, and is a claim in an early letter saying the Emperor did not then know the total. Bulletin claims for captured standards and guns increased by date. Do not map columns and regimental reclassification in Russian loss tables directly onto battle columns, or count returnees as bodies.

Retain the danger of ice and ponds but do not fix 20,000 drowned as a spectacle of bodies. Exaggeration in the bulletin and two or three bodies recovered after later drainage are different populations. Nor does that latter small number prove only two drowned in the entire battle. The Russian Guard's capture of the French 4th Line's eagle remains an achievement of the defeated within French victory. Source: individual original `027-austerlitz` H2s on strength, loss, trophies, Russian losses, and pond accounts are carried forward.

## Conditions of negotiation and requisition after victory {#after-victory}

The emperors' December 4 meeting and armistice agreement, the formal December 6 armistice between Berthier and Johann Liechtenstein, and the December 26 Treaty of Pressburg have different dates and documents. They are a state after the first part's night, not brought forward to close that part. Requisition and resistance borne by Moravian residents are a cost distinct from military victory. The source's Telnitz requisition example is post-battle material; do not bring it forward as a cause of outpost withdrawal on the 28th. The November 28 order for 100 million francs in requisitions is not actual receipts or a food budget. Source: original `030-moravia-camps-and-supply`, `031-dec-4-interview`, `020-pressburg`.
