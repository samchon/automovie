## Caulaincourt {#caulaincourt}

He serves the Emperor while warning of Russian conditions and diplomatic limits. Warning and loyalty coexist. He is distinct from his younger brother Auguste; do not transfer the brother’s death in battle to him.

Basis: relevant statements and work decisions in the [retained individual record](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/092-caulaincourt.md#caulaincourt).

Performance adoption: The entire appearance and voice paragraph beginning “Work decision (appearance and voice)” in the [relevant H2 of the individual source](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/092-caulaincourt.md#caulaincourt) is directly adopted. Its body, face, hair, clothes, carried objects, voice, pace, emotional changes, and source or invented status are active performance values. Age follows the birth date and variants in the same H2, subject to these limits: Warnings and diplomacy in parts 5 and 6. He is twenty-seven in October 1801, thirty-eight on December 5, 1812, and thirty-nine after his birthday on the 9th during return. Beard, fur boots, and loosened clothing are reported conditions of that winter return. Do not make him Duroc’s successor as grand marshal of the palace.
