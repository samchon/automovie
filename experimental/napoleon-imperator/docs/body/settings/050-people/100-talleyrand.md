## Talleyrand {#talleyrand}

He wants balance, security, wealth, and diplomatic power and resists destructive expansion. He limps, speaks slowly and with refinement, and receives orders after delay. Preserve attribution for total bribes and famous witty sayings.

Basis: relevant statements and work decisions in the [retained individual record](../../../references/novels/settings/050-people/078-talleyrand.md#talleyrand).
