# The People with the Map

## The day after victory {#settlement}

Parents: [020#negotiation](../../treatments/020-the-price-of-victory.md#negotiation), [020#requisition](../../treatments/020-the-price-of-victory.md#requisition).

Caption: December 3, 1805.

Headquarters by day. Documents and maps lie where men spent the night of victory. Napoleon's hand moves from the battle area to borders. See the two sovereigns' meeting and armistice agreement on the 4th together from a distance. For the formal armistice on the 6th and Treaty of Pressburg on the 26th, change dates and open documents separately. One handshake does not complete every agreement.

Meanwhile Moravian residents' provisions lie before the army's demands. See a courtyard in daylight from the side. A sack stands between soldiers and residents.

RESIDENT: We must keep this share.

SOLDIER: We have to take that amount.

RESIDENT: People stay here. Leave us something to eat.

The resident and people behind block the entrance. More soldiers enter and move the load. The resident points empty-handed to what was not left. The cart departs. See the group left behind rather than an unrecorded death or speech by a particular farmer.

## Victory where the Emperor is absent {#two-fields}

Parents: [021#two-fields](../../treatments/021-two-battles-and-a-report.md#two-fields), [021#report](../../treatments/021-two-battles-and-a-report.md#report).

HISTORIAN (off screen): In 1806 Prussia demanded French troops withdraw beyond the Rhine. This can be seen as a war between Prussia seeking room to act in Germany and France unwilling to give up deployment and alliance order.

Caption: October 14, 1806.

See the attack at Jena high and wide. French pressure breaks the Prussian left and center. A separate corps counterattacks afterward but cannot reverse the retreat.

Caption: Auerstädt.

HISTORIAN (off screen): The army Davout met was nearly twice his size.

Look out from inside squares of Davout's leading infantry. A line of bayonets trembles before approaching cavalry. A soldier's heel slides back.

SERGEANT: Don't turn. Close the gap!

Before the aimed muskets, cavalry turns aside and passes. Enemy troops gather again beyond smoke. Widen the view as French divisions behind arrive. Space once containing only the first square fills.

DAVOUT: Forward now. Keep your line.

The French counterattack. Enemy cavalry, unable to unite, cannot interrupt their advance. Close on a result gained on a different field from Jena.

Next morning Napoleon reads a report.

NAPOLEON: Davout faced this army?

The reporter opens the paper again. Napoleon reads to the end and places it beside a map of his own field.

Prussia's Louise looks at a map after defeat.

LOUISE: We must protect the country that remains. This line is not all we stand to lose.

Where she points, people receive demand documents. Preparation begins to rebuild the empty place in the army.

## An army remains {#eylau-and-tilsit}

Parents: [022#eylau](../../treatments/022-blood-and-tilsit.md#eylau), [022#tilsit](../../treatments/022-blood-and-tilsit.md#tilsit), [022#new-map](../../treatments/022-blood-and-tilsit.md#new-map).

Eylau, 1807. Follow infantry ranks through snow from the side. Augereau's corps, veering left, appears before Russian batteries. Cannon leave gaps in the line. An advancing soldier collides with someone coming back.

SOLDIER: Where do we go?

The person beside him grips his arm and withdraws with him. Murat's cavalry enters afterward. At a horse's chest height move through snow, then widen to see the charge pass through Russian lines. Russian ranks remain afterward. There are empty places where the French who held prepare food, and wounded still receive care.

Napoleon receives the next operations map. Russian command also calculates the army left. See preparations on both sides before moving to the next battle. Do not cover bodies lost today with words that war is over.

After victory at Friedland, frame the two emperors at Tilsit in a medium view at the same height.

NAPOLEON: We can decide together.

ALEXANDER: Russia must retain what it needs to protect.

Both study the map. Prussia's demands do not carry equal weight at this table. Louise looks from paper to the other faces.

Later reforms and reorganization proceed in different spaces as dates move forward. Households still receive service demands.

HISTORIAN (off screen): Even when freed from personal serf status, obligations remained. The 1808 troop ceiling also bound the size of the army Prussia could maintain.

Do not write this restriction as a signed clause on the day of Tilsit.

## A road to live on and the Emperor's road {#blockade}

Parents: [023#blockade](../../treatments/023-the-blockade-enters-homes.md#blockade), [023#crowns](../../treatments/023-the-blockade-enters-homes.md#crowns).

HISTORIAN (off screen): By closing the continent's doors, he sought to weaken the power sustaining British trade and war.

A port in daylight after Tilsit. An enforcer's arm blocks a merchant's parcel. Widen to other ships and closed warehouses.

MERCHANT: If we cannot sell, we cannot live either.

ENFORCER: Those goods cannot enter.

The merchant turns the load around and sends it another way. Stricter enforcement follows on that route. Show enforcement and evasion in the Netherlands, Italy, and Warsaw as distinct places and groups. Do not move the same merchant among them.

Naples, 1807. Show Joseph's and the Emperor's desks separately. Voices reconstructing the meaning of letters do not sound as a face-to-face conversation or original quotation.

JOSEPH'S VOICE: I am responsible for governing this country. If I meet every demand, how am I to continue here?

NAPOLEON'S VOICE: Blockade and war come first. The kingdom can be protected by that strength too.

Joseph pulls paper forward to answer.

HISTORIAN (off screen): Joseph was the elder brother. The younger brother who gave him his crown was Emperor.

There is no nameplate for the king of Spain yet.

Before expanded territory under control, Talleyrand speaks to Napoleon.

TALLEYRAND: More land means more relationships to protect.

NAPOLEON: If enforcement is insufficient, we must be stricter.

Talleyrand looks at another border. Beyond that line lies the next object of coercion.

## Changing the king does not end a country {#spain}

Parents: [024#bayonne](../../treatments/024-spain-will-not-close.md#bayonne), [024#bailen](../../treatments/024-spain-will-not-close.md#bailen), [024#peninsula](../../treatments/024-spain-will-not-close.md#peninsula).

Madrid in daylight, 1808. Citizens hold their ground before marching troops. A close hand grips a neighbor; another person rushes forward. Keep executions after suppression distant and remain at the height of family left behind. Joseph has not yet arrived.

Then see the Bayonne abdications and change of throne through documents and ceremony. Stop before the seat where the new king Joseph will sit. Authority and resistance of another country reach him after his authority in Naples ends.

News arrives of French defeat at Bailén. A person looking at an image of invincibility receives news of troops captured. Resistance groups' movements do not follow one commander's gesture.

See the two emperors at Erfurt from afar.

HISTORIAN (off screen): The promise remained that Russia would enter war if Austria declared on France. Demands to control the judgment of Russian entry and placement of corps did not remain.

Napoleon's army heads for Spain. In 1809 a document on Joseph's desk instructs marshals to correspond directly with the minister of war. He sees a route for military reports bypassing his desk.

JOSEPH: The army goes around me, and I am supposed to govern the country.

Keep this as reconstructed speech expressing the contradiction in correspondence, without marking it as direct quotation. Do not make Joseph's anger the cause of all resistance in Spain.

In a separate space Wellington watches supplies leave a port for inland.

WELLINGTON: Keep the route open. This is not an army that fights once and is done.

Different British, Portuguese, and Spanish forces, politics at Cádiz, and guerrillas move in their respective spaces.

HISTORIAN (off screen): Earlier, the Portuguese royal family moved to Brazil. Opening the ports also changed relations across the sea. Sidney Smith's 1807 escort was a different mission from Acre.

Return to Iberia's present. France's war continues.

End of main work. A separate author voice.

AUTHOR: We saw the map of victory alongside the people living within it. Joseph's Naples and Spain were powers at different times. Even with a crown, not all marshals acted through him. New words at meetings and in letters reconstruct their meaning; they are not quotations from actual literature.
