# The Returning Coffin

## People deciding where he may stay {#house}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/043-a-house-under-guard.md#longwood) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/043-a-house-under-guard.md#governor) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#house)

Return to island life with a caption indicating after arrival in October 1815. After wind on the shore, transition to a separate caption: moving into Longwood in December. Name chief valet Marchand at his first appearance. He places nearby necessities and checks where Napoleon can rest his body.

MARCHAND: I will put it here, Sire.

Bertrand finishes work needed for his family's life and returns to the Emperor's practical affairs. Las Cases translates and conveys language and records in his own notebook. The picture follows in turn Marchand placing objects, Bertrand coming and going, and Las Cases sitting and writing. Keep language work in this wide view too distant to understand individual words.

After April 1816. Hudson Lowe's office. Documents controlling movement and communication are stamped and sent. Do not create a new direct meeting with Napoleon. Distinguish the substance of titles on the governor's documents as “General Bonaparte” and at Longwood as “Sire,” without marking them as quotations of original document text.

Longwood. Napoleon hears the governor's title and conditions. Remain before the people dealing with his daily life instead of moving to Lowe's space.

NAPOLEON: I cannot accept treatment that does not recognize my status as Emperor.

Back in the governor's office, Lowe continues handling government-imposed communication and movement restrictions. Stay with public documents and Lowe performing that work.

At Longwood, dealing with expenses, Marchand checks expenditure and remaining objects. Napoleon's silver plate lies on the table. He looks at its shine, then pushes it aside for disposal.

NAPOLEON: Dispose of this.

MARCHAND: Yes, Sire.

The plate leaves their hands and its disposal provides resources for living. The next meal is laid with the place where silver stood empty. Napoleon sits and looks there. Marchand puts necessities down and stays beside him.

## A campaign moved by words {#dictation}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/044-the-people-who-write.md#dictation) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#dictation)

Longwood, autumn 1816. Napoleon walks, stops, and dictates. The words are audible to people in the room but low background in which individual phrases cannot be identified by the audience. The historian later conveys only the necessary claim. Las Cases's son takes dictation. The father keeps his own record in another notebook. Show hands and papers separately enough that the manuscripts do not mix.

NAPOLEON: Read from that part again.

The son finds the passage and retraces it with his eyes. Keeping the sound of a pen, transition to a later historian's comparison. Do not limit the later explanation to a sentence dictated in this room now or a particular revision day that autumn.

HISTORIAN (voice): The Marengo report revised in exile claimed victory according to a plan made from the start.

Brief images return to the 1800 field: only an army actually retreating, the subsequent arrival of Desaix's troops, and the counterattack. After the distance from present dictation is clear, return to the Longwood pen. No caption gives an exact revision day.

Elsewhere Bertrand dates and records nearby work. Marchand records what he saw in daily life. Notebooks close separate covers. Montholon's later memoir appears only as a separate source title.

HISTORIAN (voice): These were neither the same manuscript nor testimony at the same distance.

Napoleon begins another dictation and only one pen moves again. Do not fill the background sound with newly intelligible historical text.

## A man who wants to be closer {#rivalry}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/044-the-people-who-write.md#rivalry) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/044-the-people-who-write.md#departures) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#rivalry)

An undated moment at Longwood. Caption: Relationship reconstructed.

Gourgaud tries to approach Napoleon. Napoleon's attention stays with another companion. Gourgaud waits for the exchange, then raises what he prepared to say.

GOURGAUD: Sire, I have something to tell you too.

Napoleon looks toward him, then back to the unfinished matter. Gourgaud waits. As Napoleon continues other work, Gourgaud's mouth closes. Returning to his place, he opens a diary. Do not read the wording as private historical speech; watch a hand speed up and stop.

Screen text separates time: “1816, Las Cases arrested and removed.” Mark the boundary of a record unable to directly hear Longwood conversations after he leaves. After the father's notebook closes, do not use his voice to explain the later room.

February 1818. Cipriani dies. Those remaining fill the absence of the steward who handled household and food procurement. They receive objects and seek necessities again. Do not fix an exact day.

Gourgaud sails in March. Those remaining watch the ship, then return to daily life that must continue. Treat his British statements only after screen text identifying them as material different from the island diary. Distinguish the two records' places without direct quotation of content.

## The patient's room and the governor's report {#care}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/045-the-patient-and-the-reports.md#o-meara) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/045-the-patient-and-the-reports.md#antommarchi) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#care)

O'Meara opens his visiting bag and waits for the patient. Napoleon points to the painful place and moves a little.

O'MEARA: Does it hurt more when you move?

NAPOLEON: Here. Like this, it gets worse.

O'Meara observes and writes briefly. Later elsewhere he opens a notebook to report to the governor. His hand hesitates while attempting to write what he heard from the patient. From Lowe's side a document goes out demanding a report.

HISTORIAN (voice): The physician's treatment and duty to report to the governor conflicted. What O'Meara and Lowe left also carried their respective interests.

Dismissal in July 1818. O'Meara closes his bag and leaves. When the patient's room is shown later, that physician has not returned.

Antommarchi arrives September 20, 1819. The new doctor turns through earlier records, looks up, and examines the patient himself.

In the garden after arrival, Marchand stays beside Napoleon while Antommarchi helps him walk.

ANTOMMARCHI: Will you try moving a little?

Marchand walks with Napoleon. When Napoleon must stop and rest, both stop too. Antommarchi helps him rest and Marchand waits beside him.

At a meal another day, the portion he can eat diminishes. His dictating voice breaks off sooner. Watch a life gradually losing strength to continue walking, eating, and speaking. Do not transition to a new treatment or cure.

## The work of what will be left {#will-and-death}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/046-death-and-burial.md#will) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/046-death-and-burial.md#death) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#will-and-death)

A room handling the will. Work recording gifts and people continues. “Gift instructions” appears briefly on screen, without leading to an actual payment scene. The historian conveys the substance of Napoleon's burial wish.

HISTORIAN (voice): It was a wish to be buried on the banks of the Seine, among the French people he loved.

The historian's words are heard only by the audience. In the room Marchand and caregivers handle written documents, the will still being completed, and objects to leave. Do not synchronize their hands to the later voice. Move from the document's wish to control outside the room and the distant sea.

May 5, 1821. People who watched Napoleon's breathing remain beside him. Marchand's hand stops its work and selects needed cloth again. Someone starts to speak as if to a living patient, then stops. Give distant wind and nearby cloth sounds time before the date changes.

Autopsy, May 6. Hands observing the body and records. The historian's voice is heard at a distance from the observers.

HISTORIAN (voice): The judgment was stomach cancer. Differences remained in contemporary findings. Those differences cannot be evidence of a poisoner.

Insert no face or object indicating a poisoner.

Island burial that month. People finish placing the coffin and step back. The wish for the Seine does not change burial here. After those remaining return, island sounds continue.

## A book arrives and a son departs {#memory-and-son}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/047-the-name-outlives-the-family.md#publication) · [Event parent 2](../../../body/treatments/047-the-name-outlives-the-family.md#son) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#memory-and-son)

1823. Las Cases's published book reaches the hands of a reader absent from that room. Turning pages meets the distant reappearing image of Napoleon, but retain a cover showing that the text the reader sees is a later book.

HISTORIAN (voice): This book and the revised Marengo report were different manuscripts. Cambronne himself denied the sayings spread as a legend of refusing defeat. Bourrienne's memoir came from another position too.

Briefly show Bourrienne as secretary. He turns an answer he heard into a document. Then show time text “after dismissal” and a later memoir cover with resentment over money. Do not extend the secretary's voice into later private life he did not know.

Vienna. The child who was his father's King of Rome is now called Franz. Through the title Duke of Reichstadt in 1818 he becomes a young adult. On a white uniform after November 1830 are lieutenant colonel's epaulettes. His thin body is taller than his father's but breath is short. Alone, Franz looks down at the uniform.

FRANZ (to himself, work's reconstruction): What am I in France?

No new person or letter answers. As he seeks his place attached to his father's name, there is no French state or army given to him. He braces a hand to sit and lowers himself slowly.

HISTORIAN (voice): In 1815 his father declared his succession as Napoleon II. He never actually ruled.

Schönbrunn, July 22, 1832. The life of the young man with short breath ends. Do not put the later name “the Eaglet” in voices calling him during life.

Letizia sits in family life. She places aged hands on her knees, then adjusts her clothes. Watch this remaining life without reading grief over her son through a new letter. The sound of pages turning elsewhere returns a little later.

Saint Helena. Bertrand is sixty-seven, Marchand forty-nine. Their aged bodies take up work beside the coffin. Identify accompanying Las Cases: “The son who took dictation at Longwood.”

## The person beside the coffin {#ending}

[Event parent 1](../../../body/treatments/048-the-last-return.md#arrival-and-ending) · [Scene parent](../../../body/scripts/001-chronicle/010-coffin.md#ending)

Paris, December 15, 1840. A procession of remains received by the state. Hear public sound wide, then narrow to footsteps matched close to the coffin. Caption: Les Invalides, temporary placement.

The coffin is set in place. Carrying hands fall away one by one. Marchand remains beside it to check his work one last time. He straightens small disarrangements of cloth and position and lowers his hands. He looks at Bertrand.

MARCHAND: It is done.

Bertrand moves his head slightly in answer. Marchand steps back and walks away. Even after his footsteps recede, the picture does not leave the coffin and Bertrand. The sixty-seven-year-old adjusts his standing posture once. The coffin does not move. While Bertrand remains, the screen darkens.

Keep the black screen ending the main work. Then “Author's Commentary” appears and a separate voice is heard.

AUTHOR (voice): The ending is temporary placement in 1840. We did not show the tomb completed later in advance. The will's wish and gift instructions, actual burial, and permission for transfer were different things. We also separated records by who left them and from what position. Work caring for a living person continued into moving a coffin. Marchand finishes and withdraws; Bertrand remains beside it. The son's question to himself is a reconstruction by the work, not a verbatim quotation from the record.
