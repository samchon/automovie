# The Man Who Could Not Return Alive

## His wishes become documents in place of his body {#will}

Factual parents: [death and grave](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#exile-and-memory), [Bertrand](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#bertrand), [Marchand](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#marchand), [Antommarchi](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#antommarchi), [the final return](../settings/010-work/020-periods.md#ending).

As illness deepens in 1821, the will becomes a means of passing property, people, and the meaning of his life beyond death. Distinguish Napoleon's self-defense from his wishes toward remaining family and companions. Do not add amounts from separate bequest documents into one settled compensation or make them money already paid. Rather than establish a new performer taking dictation, concentrate on the document being completed and the work remaining for Bertrand and Marchand to organize and care for records and belongings.

The wish to return and rest is not yet the same as where the remains can actually be placed. Do not bring the document's future immediately forward to a scene in 1840. For those who cared for his body, responsibility begins to shift from keeping the Emperor alive to handling what he will leave.

Additional factual parent: [roles and last wishes](../settings/050-people/020-roles-and-last-wishes.md#will).

In this performance, which does not show actual amounts, do not have the audience read the internal warning not to total them. Retain only the distinction that directions for gifts are not completed payments. Make the desired burial place clear as the banks of the Seine among the French people, distinct from actual permission to return.

## Death closes a life and leaves a contest over the record {#death}

Napoleon dies at Longwood on May 5, 1821. Do not provide a maxim synthesizing different witnesses' last words. Give sufficient time to the result that the waiting and care of those near him can no longer obtain a response from his body. It is a loss on a small scale opposite the crowd's cheers in the first battle, but do not invent more complete solitude than actually existed to make death nobler.

At the autopsy on the 6th, Antommarchi and British physicians record bodily findings and interpretations differently. Distinguish stomach cancer adopted as cause of death from differences in contemporary diagnosis; do not open arsenic poisoning as a factual event or mystery with a hidden culprit. The island grave in May is the actual immediate end. The possibility of return alive has closed, but authority to move the coffin and write the life passes to surviving people and states. The final return of the remains is not resurrection canceling this death.
