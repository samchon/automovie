# Binding Neighbors to Starve an Enemy

## Orders try to fill the gap at sea {#blockade}

Factual parents: [blockade and occupation](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#expansion), [finance](../settings/030-society/010-power-and-cost.md#finance), [satellite states](../settings/030-society/010-power-and-cost.md#occupation), [family kingdoms](../settings/030-society/010-power-and-cost.md#dynasty-and-belief), [Joseph](../settings/050-people/080-joseph-bonaparte.md#joseph-bonaparte).

Even after the agreement at Tilsit in 1807, the Continental System begun the previous year demands actual obedience in ports and daily life. Napoleon, seeking to weaken British maritime and commercial power, wants the continent to move together like his army corps. But the circumstances of buyers and sellers and tax-collecting governments differ from the Emperor's timetable for subduing an enemy. Smuggling and evasion are not accidental confusion by people ignorant of the law, but the result of livelihoods and interests failing to obey an order.

People who chose France's side may make different choices in the face of blockade and conscription. Do not combine the Netherlands, Italy, Warsaw, and other regions into the response of one household. Without erasing rights gained through reform or livelihoods lost to economic obedience, merchants and residents protect their lives and interests through evasion and smuggling instead of cutting off trade, and the empire tries to stop evasion with stricter enforcement. The more control tightens, the more people who cooperated step aside from orders to protect their livelihoods, increasing the need for enforcement again. Carry this cycle forward to later expansion of coercion.

In 1807 Joseph rules Naples. As eldest brother he wants precedence and authority to govern his own country, but Napoleon, the younger brother who gave him the crown, gives priority as Emperor to the demands of war and blockade. For Joseph, who seeks to continue governing with regard for local needs, those demands divide responsibility for his country from political obedience to family. Do not make this a time when his authority as king of Spain or the 1809 instructions for marshals' separate correspondence are already in effect. Leave the experience that family cooperation does not automatically become a single national will. Do not add other siblings whose performance is conditional as new court characters; convey the separate interests of each country through groups and institutions.

Talleyrand judges diplomatically that expanding domination may harm security and balance. Do not settle his interests as pure conscience or greed alone. For Napoleon wider control is still the way to solve problems; for those around him that control weakens their own authority and countries. The next Spanish event makes this gap erupt in military occupation.
