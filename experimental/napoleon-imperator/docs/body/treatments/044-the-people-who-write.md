# Different Histories Come from the Same House

## A war he can no longer command is revised in words {#dictation}

Factual parents: [the weight of the record](../settings/120-research/010-source-policy.md#weight), [timing of entry and departure](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#exile-and-memory), [Las Cases](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#las-cases), [Gourgaud](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#gourgaud), [Bertrand](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#bertrand), [Marchand](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#marchand), [the actual proposition compared in the Marengo report](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#consulate).

Dictation and recording continuing at Longwood in autumn 1816 are remaining powers Napoleon can exercise over past campaigns. He seeks to control the meaning of the past by saying which choices were necessary and who failed to execute his wishes. The diary written by Las Cases, dictation taken down by his son, and campaign memoirs Napoleon dictated separately are not the same manuscript. Recorders select and later edit while gaining loyalty and a place of their own.

The historian briefly withdraws from this one autumn's daily life to compare the Marengo battle report revised again in exile with the field previously seen. A victory changed by Desaix's arrival and counterattack after actual pressure and retreat becomes, in the revised account, strategy calculated from the start. The audience knows that the crisis already experienced at Marengo differs from the claim of a plan now presented. Even though the Emperor can no longer bring his army back, he retains power to erase the possibility of past defeat and gather the meaning of victory to himself.

This comparison is a brief extension about the formation of the exile-period report, whose exact revision date is unknown. Do not fix an action revising that sentence on a particular autumn 1816 day or dialogue at the scene, and do not invent the revised original wording. Preserve the relationship of the contemporary bulletin and later report revision; do not transfer this claim to Las Cases's diary or the 1823 publication. After the comparison return to the companions' daily life in 1816.

Bertrand's dated record and Marchand's close daily life are not testimony of the whole battlefield either. Introduce Montholon's memoir only as a record with different formation and credibility; do not open a new individual performance. The audience distinguishes not only who was close and when, but what explanations they use to revise the same life, before watching the next rivalry for position.

Gourgaud wants a place close to the Emperor, but boredom, jealousy, and distance from other companions narrow his life. He reads intimacy gained by others as a sign of losing his own place and, seeking greater reassurance of closeness, increases his distance from them. Living together becomes a process accumulating comparison and dissatisfaction as well as cooperation, and in his record he receives the same daily life from the perspective of his own exclusion. Wanting to be closer instead makes that life harder to bear. Develop the adopted relationship of jealousy and competition in action without making an argument, duel, or private remark on a specific date a new historical fact. Do not make his emotions the key exposing truth about the whole island. Las Cases too gains a place through the usefulness of his record, but arrest and removal take him from that life within 1816.

Do not continue supplying conversations after his departure as scenes witnessed in his memoir. The audience receives the result that when one recorder disappears, the voices available and gaps left behind change. Rivalry over records has matters of face and small disputes that can be funny, but do not erase anxiety about removal from the island or family life behind the laughter.

Convey Cipriani's death in February 1818 as the loss of one person from the companions' daily life. Add no dialogue or performer for a new death scene. In this narrowed community, distinguish what Gourgaud says in Britain after sailing in March from what he said to the Emperor on the island.
