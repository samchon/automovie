# The Captured City Gives No Answer

## Even a decisive battle does not make the enemy army disappear {#borodino}

Factual parents: [Borodino and Moscow](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#russia-and-coalition), [Kutuzov](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#kutuzov), [Alexander](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#alexander), [Napoleon](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#napoleon), [layers of the record](../settings/120-research/010-source-policy.md#layers).

At Borodino the French gain the great battle they wanted, but its sacrifices further reduce their capacity for the next movement. The Russian army under Kutuzov also loses bodies and units that day. Whatever name each side's propaganda gives the fighting, do not carry its result forward as the disappearance of the enemy state's will. Napoleon's body is not exempt from the campaign's attrition, but do not let one illness explain every judgment and defeat.

Separate Bourgogne's survival from the battle's achievement as assessed by command. A soldier's need for meaning in what he endured and a commander's need for an army still able to receive the next order read the same loss differently. Moscow, the next objective, becomes both a reward and a hope of obtaining the political answer that will end the war.

## Holding Moscow does not obtain the opponent's decision {#moscow}

The French enter Moscow after the Russian army withdraws. For Kutuzov, preserving the army and the political weight of failing to hold the city coexist; Alexander retains the choice to continue war after losing it. Do not explain that choice through Napoleon's dialogue or monologue. The audience distinguishes the place the French have gained from the decision Russia has not yet surrendered.

In the burning city, the benefit of occupation shrinks further. Do not settle the whole war by naming a single culprit for the fire; retain different accounts and divided causes. Napoleon seeks political victory by waiting, but the desired answer of peace does not arrive. The costs in supply and bodies accumulate while he waits. The choice now changes from completing conditions for victory to how to extract the army before it is damaged further.
