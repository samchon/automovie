## The army changes direction before crossing the sea {#continental-turn}

After the breakdown of Amiens, the army preparing at Boulogne turns toward the continent in 1805 before the result of the naval battle. Training and concentration do not mean an invasion actually occurred. Napoleon can use the accumulated strength against continental opponents, but the problem of directly subduing Britain remains. Establish this order first so the audience does not mistake the later Trafalgar for the cause of the earlier movement.

Britain fears invasion while sustaining war through trade, credit, Parliament, and taxation. The benefits of that system rest on the cost of forced impressment of sailors and limited political rights. Preserve the condition that ships and money move outside the French Emperor's orders. If the opponent wins at sea, it preserves possibilities for the next continental war beyond defeating one general.

Additional factual parent: [credit, knowledge, and industry](../settings/030-society/020-credit-knowledge-and-industry.md#jacquard).

Before departure for the continent, place the April 1805 visit to see Jacquard's loom at the Palais Saint-Pierre in Lyon. Holes in linked cards select which warp threads to lift, and a person continues weaving. Jacquard shows the achievement of having the machine perform this selection. The state accepts it as a subject for industrial encouragement and supports it through rewards for looms put into operation. A weaver present sees skilled work diminishing and asks whether work will remain. Invent neither a real guarantee of employment nor immediate unemployment in answer. The visit ends with both sides present. This is an independent event about changing production and labor, not a logistical illustration explaining the army's shoe production. The weaver's exchange is a reconstruction, not recorded speech from that day.

## Rapid concentration makes the reputation for the next war {#ulm}

In 1805 on the continent, the army achieves success at Ulm by concentrating faster than the enemy can respond. Organization prepared at Boulogne, soldiers who have marched, and orders Berthier divides into executable form turn Napoleon's judgment into actual encirclement and victory. Even for an audience that already knows the opening success at Austerlitz, this campaign is not a victory table but a process of learning what preparation and speed narrowed the opponent's choices.

That same speed consumes food, shoes, and horses and requires local procurement. Do not separate military gain from the burden on residents. As the army shifts war's costs onto conquered territory, it can keep moving, and resentment left behind grows too. A condition emerges that the method enabling the Emperor's maneuvers may not work at the same speed in other lands. First receive the achievement at Ulm here, then move to the October 21 battle at sea in the next file.
