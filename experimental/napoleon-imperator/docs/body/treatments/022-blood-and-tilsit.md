# An Opponent Who Does Not Disappear in Defeat

## At Eylau the language of victory cannot cover every body {#eylau}

Factual parents: [war and settlement in 1807](../settings/100-history/020-life-and-memory.md#expansion), [Alexander](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#alexander), [Louise](../settings/050-people/010-ensemble.md#queen-louise), [Prussian reform](../settings/030-society/010-power-and-cost.md#recruitment), [occupation](../settings/030-society/010-power-and-cost.md#occupation).

Eylau, 1807. The rapid concentration and talent for command familiar to the audience meet their limits in an attack through a snowstorm. The infantry attack of Augereau's corps veers left, suffers heavy losses to Russian batteries, and collapses. Let the audience experience this failure first, so it understands what the later cavalry action answers. Do not open a new close encounter for Augereau or give him unrecorded orders in dialogue.

Murat's cavalry then breaks through Russian lines, and the French hold. Give the achievement of continuing the fight through an intervention after a broken attack, but do not close it with a single charge securing the whole army or producing complete victory. Armies remain on both sides, and bodies and units already lost do not return. Do not restore generals' lost units as clean, full-strength forces in the next file. Let the audience first know this endurance and loss, then receive announcements declaring victory or defeat, so the claim of who won does not have the same clarity as the soldiers' day.

Even after this experience Napoleon presses on, believing he must defeat the opponent to end the war. Alexander too must protect his army and country's strength, so fighting continues. Rather than accumulating fatigue alone for the audience, establish that unresolved political objectives call forth another battle.

After the French victory at Friedland, the two emperors at Tilsit seek to arrange an order together rather than eliminate one another. Napoleon's victory becomes diplomacy requiring Alexander's consent and choice. For Russia's sovereign, agreement is a way to preserve his country's safety and future possibilities, not surrendering every interest to personal fascination.

The demands of defeated Prussia and Louise cannot be reflected with equal force in the two powers' bargain. Humiliation remains, but that society's survival and reform do not end. Obligations remaining on people whose status has been freed, changes to the military system, and the reorganization of talent create conditions for the next army over time. Do not bring the 1808 troop limit forward as a clause on the day of Tilsit or combine several reformers' work as an achievement completed in one day.

The new map creates changes and expectations including Warsaw, alongside anxiety in neighboring countries. People gaining opportunities for reform and advancement also bear the burden of providing men and money to France. Do not turn the agreement between Alexander and Napoleon into new loyalty from every resident of Europe. Give the audience both closeness between rulers and the divergence of interests below.

Conditions that will prevent this agreement from forestalling a later break already exist. If different countries maintain the same blockade, someone must continue to accept its cost. The next event moves to the obedience an empire unable to defeat its enemy directly at sea demands from allies and occupied lands.
