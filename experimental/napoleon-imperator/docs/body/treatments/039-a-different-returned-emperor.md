# A Country Sets Conditions for the Returned Emperor

## Support for a comeback is not a blank check for old powers {#constitution}

In Paris in 1815 Napoleon must accept constitutional promises and limits. The strength given by the returning army is not the same as the strength of those demanding political participation. Lucien helps his brother while maintaining independence as a republican; Fouché seeks to preserve a place in information and negotiation rather than loyalty useful to only one regime. People gathered around Napoleon for different reasons are not one group seeking to restore the old empire unchanged.

The Emperor has returned, but the state faces more demands. Davout's severity and loyalty to the state do not mean agreement with every choice of the Emperor. Reorganizing army and administration requires actual labor; shortages cannot be filled by one name. The audience understands enthusiasm for return and limited governing capacity as part of the same present.

Additional factual parent: [additional historical decisions](../settings/100-history/030-decisions-and-results.md#additional-act).

To recover support, the Emperor proposes a system accepting annual parliamentary approval of direct taxes and ministerial responsibility. Parliament does not treat this limitation as a mere signature of loyalty. A hereditary upper chamber remains, so do not close it as the achievement of complete democracy. The audience knows what is more limited than before and moves to the question of authority to bear war.

## Diplomatic isolation demands a quick military decision {#isolation}

For the Allies, Napoleon's return is not a problem resolved by cheers within France. They have reasons to protect their own security and the order already made, and prepare for war again. Napoleon needs a chance to separate and fight larger opponents before they can fully move together. Do not close the process making war unavoidable around an individual's addiction to war or British money alone.

France must find time both to execute constitutional promises and prepare for war. Napoleon goes to Belgium seeking to create conditions for political survival through military ability. The expectation opens that rapid concentration learned before may again be possible, but this time staff and army executing orders and enemy cooperation cannot be assumed the same as before.
