# Prepare a usable motion hint

Use this branch when delivering a motion-video hint or preparing visual controls for a selected generator. Read the commissioned scope, actual programmed shots and intended receiving contract. A complete hint can be delivered without selecting a generator; report that compatibility as unverified rather than require an uncommissioned generation job. [Generation](generation.md) owns submitting inputs and adopting takes, not producing the source performance.

## Choose the controls the recipient needs

Select the supported motion video, reference images, pose, depth, normal, outline, semantic mask or other inputs the actual receiver needs. No project must produce every pass. Read installed viewer, engine and render contracts for their real outputs and limitations; a mode name or visually plausible image does not establish another tool's expected representation. Keep required action, identity, camera and timing readable through [staging](../motion/staging.md), regardless of surface detail.

Record the chosen channel meanings and their recipient requirements with the adopted production setup and actual capture source. Distinguish a convenient preview from a machine-consumed input. If no available path can produce an essential control, repair the responsible integration or state the missing capability instead of renaming another image to match it.

## Keep channels aligned

Give time-matched control outputs one authoritative frame format, camera/crop and time mapping. Separately supplied appearance references and other unpaired inputs keep the receiver's own meaning; do not force them into a per-frame correspondence. Resolve output frame, shot-local time and any trim or transition through the actual [timeline](../motion/timeline.md). Capture each channel from the corresponding evaluated performance and view; changing render mode must not advance or restart motion. A matching frame count cannot establish matching poses, cameras or event times.

Use the same intended subject transforms, visibility and deformation across relevant passes. Read each pass's actual exceptions: the installed skeletal pose overlay cannot represent anonymous instanced members without a bone graph, and a display mask without an attached semantic palette uses positional colors rather than stable identity. Preserve those limits or adopt a supported alternative; a populated image cannot prove every required subject is represented.

Interpret depth range, coordinate/normal convention, background and occlusion from the actual capture path. Camera clipping and depth normalization are different controls; clamped depth can hide relevant separation. A structural pass must retain its admitted values rather than inherit beauty exposure, tone mapping or decorative overlays. Apply only conversions admitted by the recipient and retain their original inputs and settings.

For semantic masks, keep each actual palette and sidecar with the capture and source population it describes. Retain semantic identities and reported coverage gaps, and compare identities through those records rather than assuming identical RGB values across different populations. A valid payload digest does not establish scene ownership, visibility or faithful captured pixels. Restoration/disposal belongs to the host: return borrowed scene/renderer state after each pass and release owned overlays before later captures.

## Preserve and inspect the actual handoff

Retain accepted source frames or supported control files, mappings and sidecars with exact content identities. Keep quantitative or categorical controls separate from a viewing encode when their consumers require their original values. Lossy encoding, color conversion, interpolation or transitions can change depth values or invent mask colors; inspect the actual delivered format against the receiving contract rather than infer value preservation from a successful encode. A picture edit does not automatically define meaningful blending for every control channel.

Inspect the complete required sequence and corresponding channels at matched frames, including interaction extrema, cuts, trims and the ending. Compare subject coverage, pose, camera, geometry and timing, then inspect the converted files through the actual input path when a receiver is selected. Keep unsupported or unobserved compatibility explicit. These observations supplement [delivery review](../review/video.md); they do not establish a generated final film.

Hand fixed hint outputs, source revision, frame/camera mapping, channel conventions, palette/sidecars and observed limits to [finishing](finishing.md) or [generation](generation.md). A later source, camera, take, palette, conversion or timing change invalidates affected companion observations under [execution traceability](../evidence/execution.md). Preserve closed creative proof throughout.
