# Treatments

Turn reviewed settings into decided events: causes, choices, exchanges, costs, results, and the state and information later events inherit. Use clear explanation, lists, or tables so another author can recreate the same storyline. Complete [discovery](brief.md#discovery), read the settings and instructions relevant to each event, and apply treatments questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md).

Write ordered `NNN-event-title.md` files directly under `docs/body/treatments`, with one H1 per file and explicitly anchored H2s for meaningful decisions. Treatments is the event sequence; [scripts](scripts.md) later determines actual part, episode, sequence, and scene partitions. Do not create release directories here.

Use the production's tensions, beliefs, and systems to design what happens. Work from the first encounter with its promised experience through accumulating consequences to the ending and aftermath. Keep the next author's actual decisions in view; [treatment principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md) and [population duties](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/treatments.md) judge the resulting chain and whole-work material.

Inherit settings by citing owners and carrying facts necessary for execution. Choose material that changes progression or the intended experience instead of copying biographies, appearance specifications, and institutional explanations into lists the next author must recite. Resolve ownership through the [settings boundary](../contracts/story/shared/principles/settings.md#record-and-boundary) before repairing a missing fact or event decision.

Decide a detail here when it determines an event's cause or result; leave full dialogue, scene blocking, performance, and expressive audiovisual choices downstream. Where narration has a necessary role, decide its target and added judgment or perspective without prewriting its sentences or restating the scene's result.

Read the complete sequence in its intended screen order before evidence, including parallel progressions and the ending, to find missing causes and changes hidden by a clear individual event. Correct explanation and organization in construction; treatments has no separate naturalness pass. Complete construction under [workflow](workflow.md#construction) before scripts relies on this sequence. Repair upstream defects at their first owner under the [upstream repair](repair.md).
