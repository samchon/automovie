# Screenwriting

Make the commissioned audience experience and a usable handoff to its next author. Read the request, adopted project context and actual work to identify the current task, layer, pass and edition. Continue authorized work from those inputs; a stage flag or an old review does not establish that the present body is complete.

Choose the task below and read its complete document. Read workflow when choosing or resuming a route, opening or closing a pass, or handing work onward. During an existing pass, use its selected method and current parents without loading unrelated stages.

The selected [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) own quality questions and their populations. Read the families for the current layer and pass; prose explaining compliance cannot supply missing work. Preserve user-fixed decisions and use delegated freedom to make the layer's own contribution.

Independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) judges submitted work; author inspection and evidence do not approve it. Open [evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md) when changing graph definitions, stages or scope, and installed package documentation for its APIs. A request to write does not itself authorize weakening a requirement or its selection.

For commissioned video, hand the final screenplay to [production](../production/SKILL.md). Actual motion, models and sound belong to their production tasks. A screenplay-only commission follows its required text passes and [delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md) without inventing media work.
