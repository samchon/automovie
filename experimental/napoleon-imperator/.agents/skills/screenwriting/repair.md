# Repair and changed scope

Identify the earliest owner, actual consumers, current pass and closed boundaries before changing the work. Repair facts, contradictions or unworkable design at that construction owner, then change downstream bodies only where they consume the changed value. Keep an expression-fidelity repair in its working edition against the unchanged frozen original.

## Cause rounds

Every author reading, evidence correction, independent review and author response finishes its inspection round before edits. Before judging, collect literal candidates across the whole declared scope for every failure named by applicable contracts, rather than only passages noticed while reading. Use the same layer-specific expression questions as independent review during draft rereading and expressive rewriting. Record candidates and decisions in author notes or the review record.

Group findings by cause and affected consumers. For an unnamed failure, find its whole-scope class too. Search repeated words and constructions as well as reading; a few examples cannot establish a class's extent. Repair the causes together, placing missing material where its action or explanation belongs rather than appending an answer at the end.

After repair, compare changed passages together for the same failure in different words or a new uniform mold. Compare meaning with actual parents and any frozen original. Within the submitted pass, recheck dependent sentences, quotations, positions, counts, order, screen action, inherited state, exclusions and reasons for preservation; a surviving quotation alone does not keep an answer true. Complete related [answer corrections](evidence/writing.md) in the same repair. Keep unaffected bytes and historical judgments attributable to their actual snapshot; changes involving completed layers follow [closed-pass repair](repair.md#closed-passes).

Judge retained and unadopted candidates under the canonical [retention test](evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate), keeping the comparison available to independent review. Finish the next whole inspection round before adopting further repairs. Author corrections finish when a complete round adopts no further edit; independent acceptance follows [review rounds](../review/rounds.md).

## Closed passes

Closed layers stay closed, whether downstream work repairs their bodies or upstream changes produce diagnostics in them. An upstream change reopens neither layer's evidence, reviews nor approvals. Remove answers for deleted H2s and write answers for newly created H2s only; report other upstream-change diagnostics without repairing them. Preserve original failed reports and their actual statuses.

After an already completed expression transfer, hand off the repaired body and its repair scope with the unfiltered report, preserving that transfer's working edition and frozen original. Stale or pending-transfer results remain failed checks; do not promote the old working edition over the repaired body. Whole-work review does not renew frozen construction approval against transferred expression. Hand off the current result under the [completion and qualified-delivery boundary](workflow.md#completion).

When replacing a unit from scratch, remove obsolete current construction answers, frozen original, working version and corresponding current review records, together with obsolete descendants being replaced. Preserve completed material outside the replacement scope and history in version control. A reused filename carries no old approval into new content. A new construction starts its own [completion](workflow.md#completion) and expression cycle.

## Scope expansion

The default narrative scope is complete work. A first-part pilot requires the user's commission and an exact selected part. Settings remains work-wide; treatments, scripts and screenplay cover the declared slice with its lineage, applicable obligations and current reviews. State whole-work obligations that the pilot cannot establish.

Expand after required pilot passes and independent slice review are current. Switch scope through [evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md), reset affected narrative passes together, design complete treatments including the pilot's material, and reconsider script partition. Establish new evidence and reviews for the whole population instead of extending pilot approval by implication. Preserve out-of-scope content until the authorized rewrite includes it.
