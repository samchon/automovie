# Settings

Make facts that later authors and artists can find and use without reconstructing the world themselves. Read [instructions](brief.md#requirements), complete [discovery](discovery.md) before bulk writing, and apply the settings questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). Resolve the work's scope and recurring form before detailing its people and places; use retained references and the intended production use to choose the needed specificity.

When the request supplies a premise rather than episode counts or duration, derive a provisional delivery plan from its audience promise, story material, and production limits. Identify it as an author choice and revise it when event design or performed timing gives better grounds; do not make the user invent numbers before work can begin. Work backward from planned scenes to discover needed facts, conditions, and information boundaries. This identifies dependencies; it does not assign actual events, choices, changes, or reveal order, which [treatments](treatments.md) owns. Separate baseline relationships and fixed historical states from the events that change or reveal them.

Use [record organization](settings-records.md) for character, relationship, place, building and object ownership, section choices and safe splitting.

For a new classified layout, write `docs/body/settings/NNN-domain/NNN-subject.md`, one H1 per file and one explicitly anchored H2 per independently usable fact or constraint. Group inseparable properties under their owner. Give later authors a consistent way to find values, conditions, limits, references, and consequential unresolved decisions. Name actual subjects rather than checklist headings.

Existing flat settings remain valid until a layout migration is adopted; preserve their paths and file-stem outline anchors, and never mix flat and classified layouts. For a migration, separate path changes from content rewriting, map old and new file/H2 addresses, and update outline links, citations, selectors, detached answers and review paths together through [layout ownership](../production/layout.md#project-ownership-and-customization). Preserve historical judgments at their actual edition; a moved path or refreshed hash is not new approval.

Maintain `docs/body/settings/outline/index.md` as a finding guide: domain H2s and file H3s in reading order, explaining what to find and why it belongs there. A file's H3 uses its H1 and an explicit anchor formed by removing `.md` from the settings-relative path and replacing `/` with `--`. Each settings file cites that entry. Update map and references with the files; the outline belongs to settings completion rather than a separate creative approval.

User-fixed interpretations are canon for this production. Research open facts under [usable facts and their limits](../contracts/story/shared/principles/settings.md#usable-facts), keeping support at their owner. Report a conflict between a fixed instruction and its source without silently weakening the instruction. Open supplied reference images before deciding appearance, and point to work contracts for style and audiovisual form.

Complete facts, explanation, organization, and document correction in construction; settings has no separate naturalness pass. Complete construction under [workflow](workflow.md#construction) before event design relies on it. Repair missing facts here, then propagate through the [upstream repair](repair.md).
