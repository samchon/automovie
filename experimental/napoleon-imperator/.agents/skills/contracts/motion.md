# Motion

Apply these questions to the actual performance controller, accepted clip or scoped observation report that can establish them. A whole-sequence promise needs the complete sequence respondent; a single pose or source function does not answer for the delivered film. Select the applicable scope through evidence, preserving the narrative contracts and their completed proof.

## Does the performance realize its actual source and timing? {#source-and-performance}

Identify the actual source decisions, canonical identities and required action/result, or the standalone brief when no narrative parent exists. Trace the invoked controls and accepted motion to their actual shot use and time range. Establish source/target rig interpretation, units, frames, root trajectory, meaningful proportions and sound/caption dependencies where they affect that performance. Shared timing and state have one owner. A renamed clip, uninvoked function, copied angle sequence or altered outcome cannot establish the intended action. Explicit stylization is judged against its adopted promise rather than an invented realism requirement.

## Do required contacts and constraints survive the performance? {#contacts-and-constraints}

Inspect every consequential support, grasp, impact, attachment and release through the complete affected motion and actual consumer. Compare positions, sliding, penetration, reach, limits, support transfer and subsequent state with the chosen contract and meaningful independent tolerances. Confirm the required bodies/effectors and objects were actually checked; missing targets, sampled-only checks and numerical admission have their stated limits. Use supported solver feedback in the executed performance, preserve residual warnings and refused results, and distinguish kinematic, physical and observed-appearance claims. A good average or one successful key pose cannot prove a sustained interaction.

## Is the accepted performance reproducible and actually consumed? {#replay-and-consumption}

Identify fixed source and adopted derivative bytes, rig/control revision, chosen time schedule, initial state and actual shot/edit consumer. Compare the replay paths the host promises, including fresh sequential playback and seeking/retry/split capture when supported. Stateful history, random consumption and contact settling must match those paths or remain an explicit limitation. Link current measured and rendered observations to the accepted inputs; changed rig, take, timing or edit invalidates affected observations. An accepted clip does not prove final edit inclusion or independently approve the screenplay.
