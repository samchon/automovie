# Treatment principles

Each treatment H2 answers for its event or inseparable progression. Treatments establish event order before scripts choose acts, sequences, or episode boundaries.

## Is the event decided with its inherited meaning? {#decided-event}

Does the body settle what happens, who or what participates, why it happens, the alternatives and consequential choice or process, its costs and result, and the state inherited by the next event? Establish the time, place, exchange, or clue when it changes causality. Inherit the meaning of settings and instructions as well as their values: goals, beliefs, judgment, institutional roles, knowledge, and operating rules cannot be contradicted while their numbers remain unchanged. Work within established knowledge and action limits rather than replacing an inconvenient premise.

Assign each promised experience to the event or intended narrator contribution that will carry it: humor and tone in every delivered unit, including heavy ones, and promised precedents, wider perspective and character facets somewhere in the relevant part. An unassigned promise will not appear by itself downstream. For each person, object or utterance set up as a promise, assign its payoff too; if historical evidence prevents the proposed payoff, choose another way for the audience to experience it or omit that setup.

Do not assign the same recognition, lesson or realization to be spoken or experienced repeatedly. Once realized, that knowledge must produce a new consequence or choice at its next use. Recording what someone already knows or their current state in several H2s for continuity is legitimate and is not another assignment to enact the recognition.

A result label or writing policy that leaves the core progression for scripts to invent fails. Treatments create event connections; new world facts return to their settings owner. Exact speech, detailed blocking, and audiovisual execution remain for scripts unless a specific decision itself determines the cause or result. Concise prose, lists, and tables may fully decide an event without performing it. Overprescribing scripts' work also fails: ordered argument inventories, prewritten recognitions or lessons and narrator sentences, incidental action order, and directions or prohibitions addressed to the next author leave no realization to create. Preserve only the detail that determines the event's cause or result.

## Is its information design usable? {#information-design}

Can the next author follow the settled event, its causal connections, what the audience already understands, what is revealed or withheld here, and the understanding left at the end? Place the people, relationships, time, space, and pressure needed for the current choice. A later reveal must grow from earlier facts without invalidating them. Secrets and reversals are optional; comprehension and the chosen release order are not. Keep one event decision with one owner, ordered events in order, and reasons connected to their choices. Scattered facts, repeated decisions, or a uniform paragraph template must not make the handoff ambiguous.
