# Speech expression population obligation

The completed revised script or final screenplay population answers once from all its actual exchanges. Local plausible lines do not establish distinct voices across a work.

## Do voices and exchange habits remain distinct across the whole? {#voice-distribution}

Compare each recurring speaker's vocabulary, address, register, pace, breath, sentence lengths, interruptions, avoidance, silence, argument shape, and openings and endings against their changing relationships and situations. Find cross-speaker convergence, repeated explanatory exchanges, common metaphors, uniform courtesy or hostility, and repetitive response tactics that replace a character's choices. Preserve a person's deliberate habit or an adopted ritual through its actual function rather than assigning every speaker a different gimmick. Record all found members and test retained forms as in [common expression obligations](common.md#recurrent-frames). Preserve the inherited exchange and outcome; a missing character distinction returns to its owner rather than prompting new conflict or facts.
