# Script requirement discovery

The script discovery result answers for the complete declared executable scene and partition population.

## Are the work's execution and presentation rules discovered? {#script-requirements}

Find uncovered rules for partition, action, audience access, speech and silence, inserted forms, transitions, and exits that change what scenes must actually perform and can be refuted in their selected hosts. Inspect positive conventions for scene entry and exit, action-line placement, block content and withholding, release boundaries, direction register, scene headings, views, and sound notation. Distinguish how a view is notated from whether its meaningful decision exists. Identify the actual form expression revision must preserve and final screenplay inherits, with its canonical owner. Apply the [common discovery boundary](common.md#shared-work-boundary) to findings, covered requirements, and inspected negatives rather than declaring the whole work covered after finding one rule.
