## Do Korean speech levels, reference, and rhythm make the relationship audible? {#korean-register-and-rhythm}

Can a listener identify who addresses whom without reconstructing omitted subjects or tangled modifiers? Do endings, address forms, pronouns, and sentence rhythm follow the setting's relationship, authority, privacy, and changing distance? Keep the historian's measured declarative voice, the author's friendly polite voice, and each character's actual speech distinct. Does the line remain immediately speakable without literal French or English word order, stacked abstract nouns, habitual passive endings, ornamental archaism, modern internet speech, or repetitive translated vocatives? An intentional hesitation, abrupt insult, or difficult phrase needs its scene-specific effect; polish must preserve the decision, source meaning, and interpersonal cost.

A representative failure lets formal Soult speak like intimate Lannes, translates every vous into repeated “당신,” or gives a frightened recruit polished historical commentary. Repair the language while preserving the frozen construction's event and relationship. If the intended relationship itself is missing or contradictory, repair settings before changing its descendant.
