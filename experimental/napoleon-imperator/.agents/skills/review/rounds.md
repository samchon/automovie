# Current units and a whole-pass verdict

Use the configured current review tree, mirroring every authored file and H2 in `docs/review/<draft|final>/<layer>/round-N`. Construction reviews target construction; naturalness reviews target working text. After [expression transfer](../screenwriting/rewriting/procedure.md#transfer), mark transferred units outside construction rounds even if body fingerprints changed; those judgments remain with the frozen edition. Each unit without a body defect carries its native current fingerprint review, including a body-clean unit with a separately recorded evidence-statement finding. The fingerprint covers the body, not the truth of its answers. `verdict/index.md` answers whole-pass review duties; a stage flag or fresh hash alone grants no acceptance.

Preserve reviewed revision and inputs, scope, literal findings and support, causes and occurrences, protected achievements, author decisions and reasons, unresolved limits and outcome. Identify independent review and self-review honestly. Use [review principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md) to support findings, their owner and consequences; preference alone is no defect and an earlier pass cannot excuse a current failure. A broad question can establish recipient loss beyond its examples; send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../screenwriting/brief.md#discovery).

Complete the inspection before correction under [cause rounds](../screenwriting/repair.md#cause-rounds). The author records acceptance, partial acceptance or rejection with evidence and repairs accepted body defects together with dependent answers. Rejection or partial acceptance of a named failure must pass the [retention test](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate); unsupported rejection is treated as accepted for repair. Disputed findings return to the reviewer who raised them in the same record. End the exchange when neither side supplies new evidence; repeating a rejected reason cannot establish it.

Use scoped rounds only to verify previous findings in an open pass. Reread the complete affected dependency closure; carry unaffected judgments only when their body, inputs and requirements are unchanged, without searching those units for new defects. Record original rounds, grounds, readers and limitations without calling a carry a new reading. The highest round contains the whole inventory and a population verdict, including explicitly carried units; never borrow missing units implicitly from older folders.

A closing round judges every H2 afresh without carries. A complete round with no edits or findings closes the pass. A whole round with no body findings and only evidence-statement findings also counts as dry: after the author corrects and rechecks those statements under [evidence writing](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md), close without another review round, preserving findings and decisions. This exception requires unchanged body, upstream inputs and creative requirements. Body repair requires a new reviewer in the next round. Retained candidates and protected achievements still face concrete alternatives and current contract failures.

Keep only the current completed round in the working tree once carried judgments and findings are accounted for; previous rounds remain in version history. Preserve original [closed-pass diagnostics](../screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes) instead of renewing old judgments. Mechanical checking can locate stale relationships and cannot grant semantic acceptance.
