In August 661, Tang's navy reaches Pyongyang and Su Dingfang's army surrounds the capital. This is not an enemy that must first bring down every fortress in Liaodong to reach the capital. Pyongyang's residents lose access to the fields and firewood routes outside the walls, and Haeun must reduce both the work she takes in for repairs and food for her family. Yu Hye's son is still in the capital. The residents experience the siege through their own mobilization and deprivation; they do not explain the center's strategy ([the capital's isolation](../settings/050-systems/070-pyongyang-isolation.md#pyongyang-isolation)).

Su Dingfang presses the fortress with troops and supplies brought by sea. Leaving forces to defend the capital, Gaesomun begins moving in August with the central mobile army toward the northern operational area. Namgeon accompanies him with the heavy cavalry under his command, and travel and assembly take time before they can link with the mobile forces in Liaodong. Meanwhile, troops already assigned sectors and missions continue to cut land connections. When Sokro's light cavalry targets small transport parties, Tang increases their escorts. Sokro does not try to break the strengthened escort head-on; he cuts communications and transport at other chokepoints. A few successes do not eliminate all the food, but the stores and connections of individual camps gradually diminish. Gaesomun's delegation of the local judgment Sokro needs actually buys time.

The historian connects the audience to reports of the southern outcome in the same year, 661, that reached Liaodong and Pyongyang, showing that Goguryeo too, with its multiple fronts, could not overcome Silla's resistance with limited siege resources. After failing to attack Sulcheonseong, the armies of Noeumsin and Saenghae moved to Bukhansanseong. There, Dongtacheon and male and female residents held out: catapults damaged the battlements and buildings, but defenders diverted timber from storage to the watchtower and used rope nets hung with leather and padded garments against incoming stones. Ballistae and caltrops also impeded approaches. The defense was no miracle without damage; it required fresh expenditure of storage space and repair labor. Do not turn the reported 2,800 men and women who held out for more than 20 days into a count of combatants alone. The attackers, too, consumed food and siege labor before withdrawing amid a change in weather. This 661 outcome differs from the earlier siege in 603, when a relief army caused a withdrawal. The report is a wider view connected by a later historian; do not make the full account instant knowledge for a field commander in August that changes another operation ([Noeumsin's change of objective](../settings/040-agents/105-noeumsin.md#noeumsin), [Saenghae's joint attack](../settings/040-agents/106-saenghae.md#saenghae), [Dongtacheon's defense](../settings/040-agents/104-dongtacheon.md#dongtacheon), [resources and withdrawal in 661](../settings/060-places/190-bukhan.md#bukhan), [catapults](../settings/070-objects/060-pocha.md#pocha), [ballistae](../settings/070-objects/070-nopo.md#nopo), [caltrops](../settings/070-objects/080-caltrops.md#caltrops), [improvised rope nets](../settings/070-objects/090-defensive-net.md#defensive-net)).

Turning to Baekje, Boksin and Buyeo Pung's forces calculate the survival of their own country, not Goguryeo's. They have support requested from Wa since 660 and fortresses they have recovered, but believe they cannot hold alone if Tang destroys Goguryeo and returns. Present the prospect that Goguryeo's several fronts will collapse as their judgment, not the historian's certain prophecy ([the restoration war](../settings/050-systems/290-baekje-restoration-war.md#baekje-restoration-war)).

Boksin seeks to expand the support already requested into an offensive to destroy Silla in a short campaign and gain its people and productive base. Buyeo Pung invokes royal authority and his marriage ties and connections in Wa, but cannot move Boksin's troops by his own orders alone. They are separate actors who choose an insufficiently prepared offensive in anticipation of defeat, not passive allies waiting for Goguryeo to survive. Connect Wa's dispatch of forces and the marriage through communications reports; do not give direct speech to an unconfirmed Wa commander.

The marriage report identifies the spouse as a woman of Wa's ruling class and the younger sister of Komoshiki. That family connection shows that the Wa support already requested also involves the king's personal family and political commitments. Do not make her brother a supreme commander who alone decided the marriage, or fill in her name, whether she accompanied Pung home, or her later fate. Having a family connection differs from having authority to command all Wa troops and nobles ([the unnamed spouse's relationship](../settings/040-agents/075-pung-wife.md#pung-wife), [her brother's family](../settings/040-agents/076-ta-shikibu.md#ta-shikibu)).
