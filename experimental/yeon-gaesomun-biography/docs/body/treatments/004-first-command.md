Around 627 Gaesomun moves to Taehak. When Mugok asks students to interpret a precedent for conditions in the field, Gaesomun answers first and cuts off [the first pupil](../settings/040-agents/120-taehak-moving-pupil.md#taehak-moving-pupil). Mugok does not withdraw the correct answer; he requires an explanation others can use. Questions from that pupil and [the pupil beside him](../settings/040-agents/121-taehak-receiving-pupil.md#taehak-receiving-pupil) force Gaesomun to restore steps he skipped in his rush. Mugok lets questions come instead of punishing him by publicly crushing his talent. Only after the quickest pupil explains the longest does he win recognition. The reversal passes lightly: Gaesomun keeps evidence that he thinks faster, Mugok the lesson that an answer must be communicated to be useful ([Taehak](../settings/030-society/040-taehak.md#taehak), [Mugok](../settings/040-agents/005-taehak-participants.md#taehak-participants)).

Meeting on the street, Eulbo does not regard his failure to enter Taehak as defeat in talent: he must carry loads and lacks wealth and recommendation. Gaesomun sees his friend from the shared bow remain in another life but does not intend to give up his own open path. They do not announce a parting; the life in which they often met ends.

In 628 news that Tang has reunited China reaches Taejo’s house. Taejo sees a shrinking chance to strike a divided enemy; Seogu sees greater need to buy time with a stronger neighbor. Hearing opposite courses from the same news, Gaesomun sides with his father’s call to act first ([Tang’s reunification](../settings/020-world/090-second-restoration-and-tang.md#tang-unification-and-wall)).

In spring 629 Taejo permits Gaesomun to command a family-soldier training contest for the first time. Racing to a stone pile on the [northern field](../settings/060-places/270-pyongyang-north-woods.md#pyongyang-north-woods), he chooses the stream shortcut over the daylight main road. Others object that rocks and darkness hide footing, but he presses on because he and the leaders can make it. They arrive first; an older soldier injures his left ankle. Training victory and household loss occur together ([the soldier’s condition](../settings/040-agents/119-yeon-house-soldiers.md#injured-house-soldier)).

At first Gaesomun thinks letting the injured man rest has settled it. Taejo asks what the man’s household can no longer do; Gaesomun cannot answer. Taejo does not expand his training authority into ownership of the family, but sends him to find out personally. This causes the visit.

By day Gaesomun enters [the soldier’s small house](../settings/060-places/280-injured-soldier-home.md#injured-soldier-home). The soldier receives the young master from his resting place rather than stepping on his injured foot to meet him. Asked about the wound and recovery, he calmly explains that the season’s field work and wages have stopped. He neither lists grievances nor sends him away with praise of his talent ([fixed visit](../../instructions/011-inherited-scene-constraints.md#injured-soldier-encounter)).

Gaesomun begins to justify the training’s purpose, then hears the full cost borne first by this household. Back home he reports both victory and its loss to Taejo, who sees that his son has learned more of what using people requires. Do not add recovery or new property compensation to solve the loss. Gaesomun gains an experience that makes the cost impossible to ignore, the beginning of a rule that will later demand it knowingly.
