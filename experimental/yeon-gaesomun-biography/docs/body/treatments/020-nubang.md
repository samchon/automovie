# Two roads for the army left behind

## Borrowing infantry needed for defense {#local-concentration}

Late in the tenth lunar month, Gaesomun's mobile army cooperates with forces from the Sinseong area to attack Zheng Mingzhen's camp west of Yodongseong. Namgeon joins the northern operation with the central heavy cavalry assigned to him in spring. Local infantry come from a share that leaves the fortress defended, and go through assembly and coordination of signals. Their origin at Sinseong does not make Sinseong the battlefield ([local cooperation](../settings/050-systems/020-goguryeo-force-system.md#goguryeo-force-system), [the relative positions at the Nufang camp](../settings/060-places/070-yodong.md#yodong)).

Gaesomun could charge the front immediately to make up for time spent moving in search of the enemy, but chooses to use both the two western transport routes and the eastern sortie route from the fortress. Sokro's light cavalry cuts communications and cargo on the northwest and southwest routes, while local forces press the camp from the fortress side. Do not place the same people simultaneously on the routes and in the frontal attack.

Knowing the northern armies have left, Zheng Mingzhen sends escorts out to protect the remaining supplies. Troops going west and incoming cargo meet on the same road, slowing the camp's response. As Goguryeo's pressure from the east increases, Tang must divide its efforts between recovering the transport route and supporting the front. Rather than break the entire escort head-on, Sokro moves to keep the connections from being restored.

Gaesomun holds the front and concentrates his forces when that division occurs. With the connections through which relief and supplies could reach them already cut, the elite troops cannot act as a single army even though some continue to fight. Survivors escape through a gap south of the camp, so this is neither a complete circular encirclement nor total loss of life. The Nufang organization loses its ability to continue operations ([loss of organization and loss of people](../settings/050-systems/130-second-war-losses.md#second-war-losses)). Do not add Zheng Mingzhen's death as a battlefield death confirmed here.

Instead of pursuing spoils, Gaesomun selects and reorganizes the troops and horses available for the next movement. Subtracting roughly 5,000 immediately lost to duty from the mobile army leaves about 35,000. The local infantry return under their own fortress officers and continue its defense. The victory is not a magical increase in numbers, but the result of fortress action, interdiction, and concentration working together.

Gaesomun has learned that this method can defeat elite troops, but three armies remain before the capital. He must check supplies and routes for a southward march through winter. Do not bring him straight from victory late in the tenth lunar month to Pyongyang: after time to deal with injuries, equipment, horses, and regional cooperation, the southbound column assembles around Sinseong early in the twelfth lunar month.
