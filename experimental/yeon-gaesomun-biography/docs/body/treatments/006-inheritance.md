Gaesomun and Aran marry in spring 632. Her family hopes for central standing; his mother and Aran share household management. When Gaesomun does not wait for someone defeated in training, Aran chooses to send that household food. She does not exercise his command and he does not interpret this small act as political opposition ([Aran](../settings/040-agents/118-yeon-wife.md#yeon-wife)). Do not merge domestic and military cooperation under the same authority.

When Taejo dies in late 633, Mother manages mourners and the household while Gaesomun seeks to inherit troops and equipment. Aran is not yet pregnant. Do not use Taejo’s death to blur succession and birth. The visiting [first](../settings/040-agents/122-first-household-representative.md#first-household-representative), [second](../settings/040-agents/123-second-household-representative.md#second-household-representative), and [third representative](../settings/040-agents/124-third-household-representative.md#third-household-representative) withhold cooperation, turning loss at once into a crisis of authority.

In spring 634 the same three noble household representatives object not to the young master’s ability but to his potential use of their people and equipment at will. Gaesomun invokes the offices of [grandfather Jayu](../settings/040-agents/003-jayu.md#jayu) and father Taejo to claim the family’s established standing and resources, adding training results as evidence of his ability. The households do not deny the Yeon share carried under Jayu’s name, but say that reputation is not Gaesomun’s own achievement or their consent to unrestricted use of their contributions. They cite household costs he did not pay. Even with ancestral office and personal talent, he must newly obtain the future cooperation his father asked about. He accepts that force cannot give him the whole family ([terms of succession](../settings/040-agents/004-inheritance-opponents.md#inheritance-opponents)).

Gaesomun bows and apologizes, asks to act in office, and pledges he will not regret removal if unsuitable. He adds a promise not to infringe each household’s share. Moved by his bowed head and pledge, and having obtained practical conditions, the representatives consent. Do not make succession solely a cold calculation or universal admiration for talent. He inherits the eastern great-man office and family command and becomes the king’s subject.

That autumn Aran bears Namsaeng. Gaesomun welcomes the next generation into a household where he has just gained authority. His mother cannot guarantee how long he will keep his promise; Jeongto remains the younger brother excluded from the agreement. Do not immediately prophesy the family’s later civil war.

The youth phase closes as a child who admired a hero gains command over people through others’ consent. He is disappointed in the king’s policy but has not yet plotted a coup. The historian marks the twenty years from 614 to 634; the next event passes to the adult actually expanding this authority.
