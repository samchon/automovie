# An order to surrender the seal and guards

## Damseok enters believing he can take control {#damsuk-demands-seal-and-separation}

With Namgeon's order and the royal command as his authority, Damseok leads roughly 1,000 men to seize the Yeon residence. About 200 in the lead enter the guard-rotation courtyard inside the south gate; the rest hold the outer wall and alley entrance. Heonchung's guards remain north of the courtyard. No one in the office or family quarters can see the whole deployment at once. Authority to enter the palace and command of the Yeon family's guards do not transfer to one another.

Damseok demands the seal and separation of the guards. He does not believe this procedure will itself give him Namsaeng's nationwide authority, but thinks the royal order and Namgeon's strength can end resistance in this house. His immediate aim is to take Heonchung alive, hold him separately, and detach the family's troops. Armed soldiers occupying the house and escape routes make the demand coercive.

The audience first understands the scope and arrangement of the seizure. This is not a decisive battle in Pyongyang between two national field armies. But Heonchung also sees that the royal order is being used to take away arms rather than guarantee the family's safety. He is under pressure to decide whether fear will make him comply or whether to move the guards he is permitted to command.

## Heonchung reads protective delay as betrayal {#heonchung-rejects-waiting}

Durim objects and urges Heonchung to hold inside the walls and send word first. He has no authority to hand the roughly 300 men Namsaeng entrusted to him over to Damseok at will, so his objection is not a call for immediate surrender. He judges, however, that even if they drive back the lead group in the courtyard, troops outside the gate and in the alley will remain; if the young lord goes forward, the family could lose both its means of negotiation and the person it must protect.

Heonchung believes that if the seal and guards are separated while they wait for a message, even the place to which his father could return will disappear. Mistaking Durim's objection for abandonment of himself and his father, he leads roughly 300 household guards out to suppress the seizure personally. His quick judgment and formidable martial skill achieve results at the front, but he has not inherited his grandfather's experience or nationwide command. The seizure force and guards clash in a narrow space, breaking down the separation procedure Damseok expected.

Even after being mistaken for a traitor, Durim does not leave; he follows the troops out to protect the young lord. Damseok wants capture and separation, but cannot stop every blade of soldiers fighting armed opponents before them. The coercive seizure and Heonchung's rash sortie combine to turn a coup with no murder plan into a fight in which survival cannot be guaranteed. The next event begins where this loss of control inflicts an actual wound.
