After 634 Gaesomun expands influence by quickly connecting reports in family mobilization and central military affairs. He now demands the people and equipment he once used with Taejo’s permission. As results accrue, he takes final judgments into his own hands; nobles defending the king’s peace policy acknowledge his ability but fear he may overstep royal orders ([his view of rule](../settings/040-agents/001-yeon-gaesomun.md#yeon-rule), [royal and noble authority](../settings/030-society/010-royal-and-aristocratic-power.md#royal-and-aristocratic-power)). Do not explain his rise with new offices or unrecorded lists of achievements.

Geonmu wants to spend time bought by the wall and diplomacy on actual production recovery. Gaesomun argues concessions cannot stop an opponent intent on conquest. From the same reports of moving resources, they respectively demand defenses to hold longer and an army to meet the enemy first. The king refuses to surrender final responsibility for allocating scarce resources; Gaesomun believes that judgment endangers the country. The audience receives both their shared goal and increasingly incompatible authority.

Gaesomun uses the danger to gain support for a hard line. Seeing his growing power, Geonmu and the great men secretly plot to kill him. Do not invent the meeting place or individual participants as a scene; the leaked fact reaching Gaesomun starts the next event. Do not judge the king’s concern as mere jealousy.
