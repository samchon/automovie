In late spring 661, the story reaches the time of the opening council and monologue. Rather than replaying the reports and strategy at length, it confirms the end of the decision, with Go Hwi's objection still standing, and moves on to military business. The audience now knows the defeat at Jupilsan, the failure and delivery of protective supplies, the light cavalry's cooperation, and the cost of repeated invasions. The grounds for a plan that first sounded reckless are visible, but its success remains uncertain ([scope of the opening](../settings/050-systems/035-late-spring-661-decision.md#late-spring-661-decision)). The framing story closes here and does not return to his boyhood.

Gaesomun has the infantry to be sent in advance to local fortresses and their rendezvous points determined, and separates the duties of the central mobile army and the Liaodong light cavalry. He assigns Namsaeng to block the Yalu and cut land supply. He does not take all 180,000 with him: defense, field forces, and support remain in their respective places ([available strength](../settings/050-systems/040-goguryeo-661-strength.md#goguryeo-661-strength)). Namgeon is confident in his force's striking power, but Gaesomun says he will not use it until the defenders and those cutting the enemy's routes have bought time. Namgeon and the central heavy cavalry under him are assigned to the mobile force for the northern operation; they do not arrive on the northern battlefield immediately from this room. Namsan connects the royal orders with cooperation at the center. To the father this distribution is cooperation; to the brothers it offers different places to earn distinction.

Go Hwi intends to remain at his post while sending his wife, nine-year-old daughter, and property east to his wife's family. He tries to attach three registered defenders as family escorts, but Durak, the officer responsible for the east gate, stops him. Durak does not prohibit the family's passage; he asks for documents releasing the three registered men from duty. When Go Hwi argues that he armed them at his own expense, Durak sends the roster and the objection to the center ([authority over passage](../settings/030-society/010-royal-and-aristocratic-power.md#royal-and-aristocratic-power)).

At the same gate, Yu Hye is told that she cannot leave with her husband, a defender. Her family may depart, but her husband is the only person who knows the road beyond the ferry. The cart driver goes only as far as the ferry, and Go Hwi's two unregistered guides know a different road. If Go Hwi's situation alone becomes an exception, there is no basis for keeping Yu Hye's husband either. Before the ruling, the audience sees the different means available to the two households and understands that equal passage rights do not mean equal ability to travel ([Durak](../settings/040-agents/033-east-gate-officer.md#east-gate-officer), [Yu Hye](../settings/040-agents/034-yu-hye-gate-household.md#yu-hye-gate-household)).

Gaesomun does not use Go Hwi's argument for holding the fortress as grounds for punishment. He rules that family and unregistered attendants may leave, but registered defenders cannot be released from duty without documents. Go Hwi retains both his post and the defenders. Durak announces the ruling to both households at the east gate, so it does not remain a gatekeeper's private whim or an exception known only to nobles.

Go Hwi's wife gives up some silk and jewelry boxes to leave room for her daughter's clothes, food, and valuables to conceal, making a load the two unregistered attendants and a handcart can carry. She does not abandon the journey because the escort her husband chose is gone; she chooses the baggage herself to meet the actual risks. When her daughter tries to follow the porters ahead, her mother holds her back so they move together. Show the audience their journey from the gate to the ferry, but do not give Durak knowledge of their arrival ([the east gate and evacuation route](../settings/060-places/040-pyongyang-east-route.md#pyongyang-east-route)).

## The cart turns back {#household-remains}

Unable to find suitable company, Yu Hye turns the cart back home. Do not secretly release her husband or turn the Go household's guides into people who know her route. The state has not confined the family, but her husband's defense duty has taken away their ability to travel. Yu Hye secures food and a place for her son in the capital and asks around for another companion. Both remain inside the city when news of the first invasion arrives.

After sending his family away, Go Hwi returns to duty. The ruling does not make him agree with Gaesomun's field strategy, nor does Gaesomun make troops gained by eliminating an opponent a condition of victory. The spring's military preparations end with both men obliged to defend the same capital while carrying their own fears. Tang's army is coming toward it.
