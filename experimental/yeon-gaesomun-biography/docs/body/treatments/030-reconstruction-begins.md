# A spring to prepare for the next war

## Dividing the work instead of ending mobilization {#return-and-repair}

After the war in 662, Namsaeng prepares together for return home and the recovery of life, production, and defense in Liaodong and Gungnaeseong. Send everyone home and the fortresses and roads are left empty; keep everyone and the next sowing is lost. He separates food to eat from seed to plant and assigns livestock, tools, and return-home work, releasing people while retaining defenses ([the reconstruction program](../settings/030-society/100-reconstruction-program.md#reconstruction-program)). Seokbu guides people along routes and to workers, using the trust he gained in his commander for local work.

From the explanation, Gaesomun understands that his son has not retreated for fear of war but is building the country's strength for the next one. Instead of holding every task in his own hands as he does, Gaesomun recognizes the use of capable people and gives Namsaeng real work to do. He does not suppress his son's reputation as a threat to his own authority. Namsaeng uses that authority to distinguish those who can return from the defenses that must remain, rather than to magnify his own achievements.

## People unable to wait for results {#demands-after-victory}

Requests rise from Pyongyang and the south that their regions' defense and burdens are being pushed back. Namgeon says they should attack south while the army that defeated Tang's elite still exists; Namsan seeks more opportunities to gain cooperation from the royal house and central nobles. Namsaeng explains why restoring defense differs from abandoning attack, but expects that because his brothers fought the same war they will understand the costs in the same measure ([the brothers' different judgments](../settings/040-agents/007-namsaeng.md#namsaeng-restoration), [Namgeon](../settings/040-agents/008-namgeon.md#namgeon), [Namsan](../settings/040-agents/009-namsan.md#namsan)).

The younger brothers feel their elder treats their experience and abilities as secondary. They have not yet decided on a coup; each performs his own work competently and expects greater authority. While Gaesomun lives, King Bojang does not publicly use their dispute to restore royal power. As victory removes the immediate crisis, the old argument over where to use the same national strength finds room to be voiced again.

## A country to leave to a son's son {#future-for-heonchung}

Namsaeng is proud of Heonchung's quick talent, but tries to teach him that talent alone cannot move people. Heonchung is frustrated by his father listening so long to residents and wants to resemble his grandfather's quick judgment. Rather than wait until he can hand every task to his son, Namsaeng begins the reconstruction he can do now ([Heonchung's learning and limits](../settings/040-agents/010-heonchung.md#heonchung)).

Seeing the different strengths of father and son together, Gaesomun hopes the country he defended will continue into the next era. There is no guarantee that Tang will never attack again, and the Baekje restoration forces are still fighting in the south. Reconstruction is a choice to use time gained, not a project in a completed peace. News from the south the following year makes that choice more urgent.
