When the grain arrives, Su Dingfang does not treat it as grounds for optimism that he can immediately rescue all the other camps. He restores rations for his main force and prepares the capacity to gather equipment and people and get out. Ma Jeong does not make that decision for him; he passes on the food received. Kim Yangdo and some others return by boat from a separate waterside point, not along the main body's land route.

Gaesomun learns the scale of the delivery late. The chance to eliminate the starving Su Dingfang has diminished, but two other Tang camps remain. Instead of diverting the entire central army to pursue Kim Yusin, he continues the planned concentration. Unlike the historical connection in which the provisions led directly to Tang's withdrawal, the audience follows the work's chosen sequence of delivery, Sasu, then Su Dingfang's withdrawal ([the calendar's distinction](../settings/020-world/030-second-war-calendar.md#second-war-calendar)). Do not make news of a Tang withdrawal that has not yet happened the reason the transport's main body returns.
