After the defeat at Jupilsan, the castellan of Ansi divides the fortress's 8,000 armed personnel between defense and repairs. Do not count the relief army outside as troops inside the fortress again. Residents provide water, food, and labor on the walls; the castellan answers the center's expectations with the people and provisions still available on the ground ([defense of Ansi](../settings/050-systems/210-ansi-defence.md#ansi-defence)). Rather than replace the castellan who defied him, Gaesomun supports the conditions that let him hold out. Here begins the actual exercise of rule that puts the ability to defend the country ahead of personal obedience.

Tang attacks damage the walls and exhaust the people. The castellan accepts that patching one place may delay work elsewhere, and uses nighttime labor to repair damage exposed during the day. When the earthen mound Tang is building begins to overlook the adjacent wall, the defenders redistribute people between the frontal defense and the response on the slopes ([the limits of walls and mound](../settings/060-places/110-ansi.md#ansi)). Do not make this the work of one heroic commander who sees the whole fortress at once. Reports reach the center late, and local decisions buy the next stretch of time.

Below Geonan Fortress, Zhang Liang's naval force is surprised before its camp is complete. The returning routes of workers outside the eastern side and horses from the southern pasture overlap with the battle formations. Through the unfinished defenses and the congestion at the entrances, a Goguryeo raiding party reaches the ships and unloading lines on the west while Tang cannot form a front. When Zhang Liang is unable to speak, Zhang Jinshu beats a drum to gather nearby troops at the center, but spoken orders cannot move the distant unloading stations all at once ([the naval battle](../settings/050-systems/220-645-naval-failure.md#645-naval-failure), [the camp](../settings/060-places/130-tang-naval-camp.md#tang-naval-camp)).

Some raiders who reach the west first attack the crews and set fire to the wooden hulls and ropes of major ships. A counterattack by the troops Zhang Jinshu has gathered drives the Goguryeo force back, and Tang retakes the land camp. But when order is restored, the counterattack has not brought back the ships already burned beyond use or the crew members killed or captured. Damage to timber and ropes cutting transport capacity follows from [the ships' established conditions](../settings/070-objects/140-ships.md#ships); the fire and its specific sequence are choices made by the work. Do not cite the scene as a historical record confirming that fire.

Zhang Liang sends the remaining ships and troops back. Having lost the core of his force and fleet in the raid and subsequent withdrawal, he cannot establish a land base and secure supply link to continue the direct attack on Pyongyang. Tang withdraws amid the delay at Ansi and the limits of season and supplies. The people inside the fortress see the enemy turn back and know they have survived, but they do not immediately recover their fields and roofs outside. Gaesomun is left with both the real achievement of preventing conquest and the failure of losing people in the field. Instead of preserving his confidence by placing all blame for defeat on the two relief commanders, he revisits his own decision to trust frontal strength first without severing the enemy's connections.

The historian briefly contrasts two elements of the 645 campaign record so the withdrawal is not understood as the defeat of an incompetent enemy. In the same year, Zhishi Sili commanded outside the Goguryeo front, checking Xueyantuo on the Jinshan route. Tang concentrated on Liaodong without stripping all its forces from the north.
