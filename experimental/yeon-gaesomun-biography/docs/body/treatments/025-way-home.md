Around the seventh day of the second lunar month, Kim Yusin sends the main body back by land with its delivery cargo reduced. The sea detachment is part of the transport's total, not an addition to the returning main body. With the large loads gone, the return average can rise to 27–30 kilometers a day, but the injured and rear guard cannot keep the same stride ([the return schedule](../settings/050-systems/170-return-and-pursuit.md#return-and-pursuit)). Kim Yusin assigns troops to go ahead and escorts to take in those who fall behind.

Ten thousand troops available for pursuit from Goguryeo's southern watch follow them. They did not leave Liaodong on the day word arrived, but had already marched south with separate supplies. This is not an operation that empties the central counterattack force of 50,000 to pursue Kim Yusin alone. The two operations continue their separate responsibilities on the same days.

On the return, Gwidol's frozen feet and strength worsen. Chano hides his own injuries and shares his companion's load, but cannot walk for him. Gwidol worries that his mother will have to shoulder the work left behind alone. Instead of making a new promise or a grand last speech, he is unable to continue speaking and dies on the road ([Gwidol](../settings/040-agents/077-guidol.md#guidol)).

Chano has neither the authority nor the means to carry the body home or return later to bury it. He takes his companion's [small everyday knife](../settings/070-objects/050-guidol-knife.md#guidol-knife) to give to his mother. The choice leaves both the shame of leaving the body and the duty to return alive and carry the news. The unit continues moving without pretending the death did not happen. The audience first experiences the ending from which one person cannot return despite the transport's success.

## Fires left behind {#deception-at-night}

As the pursuit draws close, Kim Yusin uses the fires and traces of camp left behind to make it appear that his force is still there, and moves the main body at night. Goguryeo initially follows the traces of a halt, then confirms that the actual movement was different. One fire does not deceive the entire enemy forever; the time spent checking creates room to prepare the crossing.

The rear guard receiving the injured is not made wholly safe by that time. People and formations stretch apart along the road until they reach the north bank. Around the fourteenth day of the second lunar month, before Kim Yusin's return is complete, Gaesomun begins an attack on Ren Yaxiang's camp at Pyongyang. The two decisions in the same winter do not wait for one another to end.
