## An improvised rope net to catch stones {#defensive-net}

**Definition and scope**

A rope net hung with leather and padded clothing is an improvised defense against incoming stones at Bukhansanseong. It is not an iron-like ballistic barrier: impact can stretch or break ropes, and people behind must repair it. Dismantling a wooden storehouse to make a watchtower loses both the building and its original function.

**Established facts**

The work’s net uses ropes about 2 centimeters thick woven a handspan apart, layered with leather and padded clothing, in 3-by-2-meter sections hung before damaged wall areas. Its two upper corners and two middle points are tied to a temporary wooden crossbeam, with the sides secured to vertical posts. The beam and posts are fixed to wooden supports behind undamaged wall. The net hangs about 0.5 meters outside the wall with its bottom loose; people stand back inside the wall, leaving room for it to bow inward. A large stone may stretch ropes, dislodge knots, or crack supports; it is no guarantee that people behind escape impact. The fastening, slack, and dimensions are invented specifications absent from the 661 account; event design determines actual impacts and repairs.
