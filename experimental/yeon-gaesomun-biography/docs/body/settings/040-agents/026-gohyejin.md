## Go Hyejin checks the conditions for advance {#gohyejin}

**Definition and scope**

Go Hyejin commands the relief force with Go Yeonsu and examines the conditions needed for attack. He knows that the entire formation cannot turn at once or move supplies easily. Nevertheless, trusting earlier victories and the size of the relief force, he cooperates in the advance. Even while questioning practical details, he expects to win in the field.

**Established facts**

In 645 he is invented to look in his late forties, about 172 centimeters tall, thick-bodied and short-limbed. His square face has small brown eyes, gently arched black eyebrows, a blunt nose, and a thin mouth. His skin is tawny; he ties thick black hair back and wears short whiskers on both cheeks. He wears lamellar armor over dark cloth clothes, a reddish-brown sash, and an undecorated helmet.

He points out positions with his hand while speaking. His low voice is breathy and muffled, quieter than Go Yeonsu’s. When anxious he slowly repeats the same question.

After defeat he surrenders with Go Yeonsu and receives a Tang office. Even though he checked practical details, he shares responsibility for choosing to cooperate in an advance he expected to win. [Jupilsan](../050-systems/200-jupilsan-defeat.md#jupilsan-defeat) establishes the causes of surrender and campaign outcome.
