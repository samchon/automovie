## Nyuil divides reconstruction work {#nyuil}

**Definition and scope**

Nyuil is an invented military officer born in 607. In Namsaeng’s reconstruction program he adjusts construction labor to eastern sowing and rest. He has experience mobilizing people at the front, but learns that not all labor can be handled under military-service discipline alone. If changing the allocation delays completion, he must report its cost and responsibility to Namsaeng. The campaign treatments own his actual choice in response to Miran and its result.

**Established facts**

After hearing the reasons for opposition, he can change the allocation, but cannot cancel all construction. Education at Gyeongdang and rations are not his sole responsibility either.

He is about 174 centimeters tall, broad-bodied and short-armed, with an angular chin, small brown eyes, and dark gray-black eyebrows. White strands show in his tied black hair; he has a short beard and tawny skin. He wears brown leather protection over a dark outer robe.

His voice is low, even, and clear. After raising it, he takes a long breath and slows his speech.
