## Eulbo, who grows up at Gyeongdang {#eulbo}

**Definition and scope**

Eulbo is an invented commoner child growing up with Gaesomun at Pyongyang Gyeongdang. Born in 613, one year earlier, he is eldest son of a cart-carrying household on the southern street. His father injured a leg in the 614 mobilization and reduced cart work; unpaid debt remains. If it cannot be repaid, a child must become nobi to repay it, so Eulbo carries loads early while attending Gyeongdang.

**Established facts**

He competes most often with Gaesomun. Initially a better archer, he falls behind in bow and reading as Gaesomun grows. Gaesomun regards him as a friend but first as someone to beat. Eulbo acknowledges Gaesomun wins through skill, not noble birth, but remembers that after winning he does not wait for the loser.

After Gaesomun moves to Taehak around 627, Eulbo stays at Gyeongdang. They meet only in passing on street and yard. He most wants military merit to escape debt and earn a name, believing Ondal’s story an example. He later becomes a commoner soldier able to recognize Gaesomun when they meet again in war; event design decides meeting and outcome.

Around 620, seven years after birth, he is about 120 centimeters tall, thicker-bodied and broader-cheeked than peers, with short black hair, running barefoot or in straw shoes. His high voice is rough.

Around 626 he is about 150 centimeters, broader-shouldered and larger-handed than peers, with round face, short chin, thick straight eyebrows, small brown eyes, broad nose, and sun-darkened tawny skin. Short black hair is tied, palms callused by cart handles. He wears mended gray-brown cloth and a cord belt, using shared bows. His voice is lower and muffled; he speaks quickly, laughs loudly, and when losing holds words before a one-line retort. Even to noble children he does not prolong honorifics.

Around 630–634, near twenty, he becomes a 170-centimeter porter with thick shoulders and arms and short beard. He keeps gray-brown cloth and wraps a thick load cord around his waist; his voice settles into low muffled chest resonance.

In 645 he is among [Ansi’s 8,000 armed personnel](../050-systems/210-ansi-defence.md#ansi-defence), carrying and repairing walls. This invented assignment is not additional reinforcement. He is an adult 32 years after birth, without exact birthday age, retaining adult build, clothing, cord, and voice. He can perform work and speak of its burden, but cannot command the whole fortress or exempt himself or another soldier from service. Treatments decide actual reunion, choice, and outcome.

**Conditions and limits**

Debt and nobi risk follow [recognition through achievement](../030-society/020-martial-status.md#martial-status). The lender is a grain merchant household south of the fortress, appearing only through a demand, not as an individual. First service date, separate merit, debt resolution or nobi status, and fate after 645 are unset. The 645 assignment does not prove those outcomes.
