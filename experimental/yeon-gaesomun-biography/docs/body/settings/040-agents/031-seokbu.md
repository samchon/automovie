## The two commanders Seokbu follows {#seokbu}

**Definition and scope**

Seokbu is an invented Liaodong soldier born in 633. Son of a household combining farming and hunting, he learned archery at Gyeongdang but cannot afford expensive heavy-cavalry equipment. He believes military service can bring fame and reward and learns to survive actual combat. In 661 he serves under Namsaeng in the Yalu defense. He begins as a soldier experiencing the front, not an observer explaining policy from the outset.

**Established facts**

He is about 163 centimeters tall, short-legged with sturdy thighs. His broad round face has small brown eyes, short thick eyebrows, a low nose, and a large mouth. His tawny skin is deeply sun-darkened, his straight black hair gathered and tied short, his beard short and sparse. He wears leather torso protection over tawny cloth and a dark sash; his quiver is mended. His right shoulder rises when he draws a bow, and one fingernail bears an old injury.

His voice is muffled and below the middle register. With familiar comrades he exchanges short remarks, but before an officer catches his breath with each statement. Fear makes his voice catch at the ends and stops his jokes.

Enduring the same front as Namsaeng, Seokbu also earns trust. He comes to trust Namsaeng by seeing him suffer the same cold and not shift responsibility for losses to others. People and objects left behind in retreat prevent him from taking defeat lightly. After war he joins Namsaeng’s reconstruction and guides people along routes and to work. On hearing of the coup, trust in the commander he fought with conflicts with aversion to bringing in foreign troops. He ultimately does not follow Namsaeng’s submission and remains with Goguryeo. Later he experiences the threat to his region from routes Namsaeng has disclosed to the enemy. Their real accumulated trust gives this choice weight.
