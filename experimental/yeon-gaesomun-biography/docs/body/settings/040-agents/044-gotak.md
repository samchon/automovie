## Gotak, the replaced transport officer {#gotak}

**Definition and scope**

Gotak is an invented regional military officer born in 610. He has assembled eastern transport labor since before 645, but sends protective supplies late, leaving the central government’s promise to residents unfulfilled. The delay cannot be explained solely by personal enrichment: his decision to assign animals and people first to other duties holds up delivery of the survivors’ share.

**Established facts**

Yeon Gaesomun holds him responsible for the failed support, transfers the work to Gubin, and removes Gotak from the post. This does not lead to his death or punishment of his entire household. Gotak undertakes no separate political activity in the main story after his replacement.

He looks to be in his forties, short and round-bodied, with a broad forehead, thick beard, tied black hair, brown skin, and dark brown clothes. His voice is low and muffled; he lists his reasons at length.
