## Porters at the handover site {#yang-o-porters}

**Definition and scope**

The Tang handover force is an unnamed group that, on Ma Jeong’s orders, puts down sacks, moves them to dry ground, and hands them over to the garrison. Do not merge them with the Silla transport party. The currently planned narrative has no separate military histories, betrayals, or individual families for them. They are soldiers assigned to the handover while waiting for rations; already hungry and cold, they work more slowly than in ordinary times. Ma Jeong distinguishes a report that goods have been received from one that they have actually been moved.

**Established facts**

Their shared design is sun-darkened skin, hair tied back in black, gray-brown clothing with black sashes, mended leather outerwear, and cloth wrapped around their hands. Their brief responses when receiving sacks are breathless and hoarse; the current scene requires no distinctive individual voice.
