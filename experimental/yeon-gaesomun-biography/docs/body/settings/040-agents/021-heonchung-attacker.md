# The leading soldier who cuts Heonchung

Canon guide: [Location of this subject](../outline/index.md#040-agents--021-heonchung-attacker).

## The leading soldier who cuts Heonchung {#heonchung-attacker}

**Definition and scope**

The man who cuts Heonchung’s left side with a blade is a leading soldier in Damseok’s receiving force. Ordered to subdue guards defying the king’s command, he gives priority once engaged to stopping the armed enemy before him. He is not an assassin who recognized Heonchung and had agreed in advance to kill him. He serves under Damseok and must obey an order to take prisoners alive. [The injury constraints](../050-systems/270-heonchung-injury.md#heonchung-injury) own his recognition of the order and withdrawal after the blade lands.

**Established facts**

His name and family are not established. There is no later development in which his identity is reliably communicated to Namsaeng and becomes a personal revenge plot. He has no separate dialogue or inner life story in this event.

His invented appearance is a thin man in his twenties, 169 centimeters tall, with a round chin, brown eyes, short black eyebrows, and tawny skin. He ties his black hair beneath his helmet and has a sparse beard. He wears lamellar armor over gray-brown underclothes and a black sash.

His voice is rough and above the middle register; in the clash, the ends of his words are lost in his breath.
