## Xue Rengui’s selection and the limits of fame {#xue-rengui}

**Definition and scope**

Xue Rengui is selected for advancement after conspicuous combat at Jupilsan in 645. Start from the record of unusual clothing and a charge, without exaggerating him into the cause of all the battle’s casualties. The work makes his light clothing visible amid dark military gear and his long-armed handling of weapons apparent from a distance. See [the light outer garment](../070-objects/170-xue-wear.md#xue-wear) for material, dimensions, and color, and the object canon for weapons.

**Established facts**

His invented design is 180 centimeters tall, with long arms, broad shoulders, and a long square face. He has dark brown eyes, thick straight black eyebrows, a high nasal bridge, broad nose tip, thin lips, and sun-darkened tawny skin. His black hair is tied; in 645 he has a short sparse beard, later a short even one. Light gray-brown outer clothing and a black sash stand out amid iron lamellar gear. His voice is clear and below the middle register, strong enough to carry. He speaks more concretely when regrouping troops after battle than when boasting of prowess. Success creates confidence for new duties, but not every command situation resolves the same way. The epilogue uses the 670 failure against Tibet as a report of a new limit to Tang’s capacity for conquest, not as proof that a secret of the six-flower formation was solved once and all Tang troops became incompetent.
