## King Uija’s offensive and defeat {#uija}

**Definition and scope**

King Uija was the Baekje ruler who attacked several Silla fortresses, including Daeyaseong, in 642 and lost his dynasty to the Tang–Silla alliance in 660.

**Established facts**

Goguryeo’s leaders receive different reports of Uija’s offensive and defeat as they assess the balance of power in the south beyond their own war. Do not merge him with Buyeo Pung, king of the Baekje restoration. The currently planned narrative gives Uija no independent court scenes or direct dialogue, so do not invent a face or voice.
