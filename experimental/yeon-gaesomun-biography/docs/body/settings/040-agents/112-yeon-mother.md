# The mother who raised Gaesomun

## The mother who raised Gaesomun {#yeon-mother}

**Definition and scope**

Gaesomun’s mother is Taejo’s wife, invented for the work and born around 594; she is unnamed in the text. She manages Yeon family living quarters, food, cloth, and servants. Authority over family troops and arms belongs to her husband and later son. She is not a recorded person; her genealogy and natal family are unset.

**Established facts**

She wants her son to become a family head trusted like his father. Welcoming him home after a Gyeongdang victory, she first asks about the losing child’s family. She sees that he remembers his father’s calls to fight most strongly, but has no place in guests’ debate; she hears politics through the door while serving.

After her husband dies in late 633, she directs mourning household work and hospitality. She serves the three opposing household representatives visiting in 634 but cannot set agreement terms. When her son returns after bowing and promising, she cannot guarantee how long he will keep his word.

Her son says less before her; rather than scolding, she provides food and clothing and leaves questions. She repeatedly tells second son [Jeongto](113-yeon-jeongto.md#yeon-jeongto) not to fight with his brother.

She is about 158 centimeters tall, narrow-shouldered and straight-waisted, with a round face, thin eyebrows, brown eyes, small nose, thin upper and full lower lip; Gaesomun’s thick lower lip resembles hers. Her skin is light tawny, black hair lifted and tied back. She wears dark reddish-brown outerwear and a dark sash, undyed light tawny cloth during mourning. Her appearance changes with time.

At Gaesomun’s birth in 614 she is around twenty, round-cheeked and thick-haired, with hair raised high and a small wooden comb. In the 620s around thirty, her cheeks thin, cheekbones show, and backs of hands roughen. Around 634 she appears near forty, with forehead and mouth lines and a few white hairs at the temples; hair is tied low without a comb.

Her middle-register voice is soft and slightly breathy. She speaks unhurriedly, repeating short questions when worried; before guests she lowers volume and ends clearly.

**Conditions and limits**

Her fate and actions after 634 are unset; supplement this record first to include her later.
