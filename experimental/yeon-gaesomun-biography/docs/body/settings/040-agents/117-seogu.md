# Seogu argues that now is the time to rebuild

## Seogu argues that now is the time to rebuild {#seogu}

**Definition and scope**

Seogu is an invented middle-aged military noble serving King Yeongnyu, born in 568. In 612 he ambushed Sui troops dispersed by looting at Pyongyang’s outer fortress with royal prince Go Geonmu. After Sui’s final 614 expedition he supervised recovery between Liaodong and Pyongyang; after Geonmu’s 618 accession he joins royal councils. He voices “now is the time to rebuild” in debate over the divided world. He can participate in the king’s decision but cannot replace royal orders or move other nobles’ forces.

**Established facts**

His grounds are personal: after 612 Sui returned the next two years, and in villages he supervised more died in subsequent winters than in the victory year. He first counts fields and fortresses that would be empty while attacking divided China. He says he does not know how long division will last and cannot stake the country’s remaining people on an unknown. This is not fear but the judgment of someone who paid a different price in the same war.

He and Taejo are old comrades from the Sui wars. Both support Yeongnyu; neither thinks the other cowardly or reckless. Seogu opposes Taejo at his house and makes the same case at palace council. The king accepts his calculation and launches no expedition, but does not dismiss Taejo. To young Gaesomun hearing only voices at the corridor’s end, Seogu remains the man blocking his father; only later does the boy learn he fought at the outer fortress in 612.

In 618 Seogu has lived fifty years, about 176 centimeters tall, slightly taller than Taejo, narrow-shouldered and thick-bodied, dragging his right leg slightly. His lean face has high cheekbones, narrow brown eyes beneath short straight eyebrows, straight nose, and thin lips. His tawny skin is sun-darkened, black hair streaked gray and tied back, beard short. A long scar runs from above his right ear to cheek. He wears dark brown outerwear and dark sash, adding black leather belt and sword at council.

Around 630 he appears over sixty, thinner and slightly stooped, hair and beard mostly gray. His slower dragging gait needs a cane; clothing colors and scar remain.

His voice is quiet, dry, and below the middle register. He speaks slowly through numbers and years, resuming where interrupted rather than restarting. Even opposing Taejo he does not raise his voice, and has a habit of a short laugh at the end.

**Conditions and limits**

Seogu is not recorded. The scope of the 612 fight follows [Go Geonmu’s heroic story](015-geonmu.md#geonmu-war-story). His actions after 631 and fate in the 642 coup are unset; supplement this record before later appearance.
