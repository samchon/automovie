## Emperor Taizong’s experience of victory {#taizong}

**Definition and scope**

Li Shimin is the emperor who personally fought wars during division and founded a unified dynasty. He won conquests through personal martial skill and command of multiple generals. He intervenes directly in the 645 Goguryeo campaign as well; the Samguk Sagi separately records the force he led at Jupilsan. Its detailed organization and course follow campaign canon.

**Established facts**

The invented Li Shimin is 176 centimeters tall, broad-shouldered and thick-bodied, with a round face, heavy chin, high nose, and broad mouth. His eyes are dark brown, eyebrows thick and black, skin tawny. His black hair is tied and his beard trimmed short. He wears dark reddish-brown clothes and a black sash; in battle he wears armor and a helmet with metal ornaments.

His invented voice is thick, clear, and resonant below the middle register. Hearing battlefield news, he changes questions quickly and even in formal settings often asks for the conclusion before another finishes answering. When praising a general he elevates both the achievement and his own eye for recognizing it. His pride in confronting Gaesomun appears in this speech; the two express pride differently.

Despite prejudice that looks down on Goguryeo, he can acknowledge an enemy general or castellan who proves capable in battle while maintaining his conquest objective. Though judged a generous and accomplished ruler, his record also includes the violence of treating surrendered prisoners differently.

Taizong achieved real successes that prevent Goguryeo’s memories of victory from reading him merely as a repetition of Emperor Yang of Sui. How far does that accumulated success lead him to misread the conditions of a new country, Goguryeo? His story leaves that question.
