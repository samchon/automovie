## The two positions Yeon Namsan moves between {#namsan}

**Definition and scope**

Yeon Gaesomun’s third son Namsan moves between royal house and brothers, believing he can control their dealings. The work sets his birth in 639, consistent with common epitaph-based reconstruction.

**Established facts**

His invented design is 173 centimeters tall, narrow-waisted and straight-shouldered, with a smoother oval face than his brothers. Brown eyes lie beneath thin black eyebrows and narrow first when he smiles. He has a small nose tip, evenly thin lips, light tawny skin, neatly tied black hair, and a very short beard. He wears fitted dark brown everyday clothes, a black sash, and small metal ornaments. Though carefully dressed, he has military experience and can handle equipment at the front.

His middle-register voice is clear and soft. He starts by taking up another’s words, prolongs request endings slightly, lowers his voice while smiling, but ends official orders firmly. In crisis he adds words quickly to delay another’s decision; this does not always work.

He recognizes what others want to hear and moves skillfully between opposing sides. Knowing royal ritual and Pyongyang nobles’ demands, he can use this ability to gain support for Namsaeng’s policy. During the 661–662 campaign he helps with ritual and meetings at the palace and hears king and nobles closely, an invented position. Yet when he feels his elder brother treats his opinions as secondary, royal praise rewards him more. He adds legitimacy of royal order and rank to Namgeon’s overt ambition.

Namsan knows the king wants power back but believes he and Namgeon can handle him. The king likewise thinks two sons dividing power will leave a gap he can exploit. Both transact partly aware of the other’s intentions while overestimating control.

Namsan also bears responsibility for failing to prevent Heonchung’s death: he joined the coup and justified troop entry, so blame cannot rest only with Namgeon. At the fall Namgeon holds out, but Namsan surrenders to Tang, judging survival differently despite the same family conflict. The main story ends by 679 and does not continue to his death in 701.
