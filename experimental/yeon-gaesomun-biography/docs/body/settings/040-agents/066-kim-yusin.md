# Kim Yusin’s march and its cost

Canon guide: [Location of this subject](../outline/index.md#040-agents--066-kim-yusin).

## Kim Yusin’s march and its cost {#kim-yusin}

**Definition and scope**

Goguryeo leaders first know Kim Yusin as a capable Silla general fighting Baekje, then reassess him after the 662 provisions transport. He takes a large force and carriers through a difficult route considered impassable to the enemy, delivers food, and returns troops through deception and combat. Do not rate the march’s achievement below battle size: command that brings food and people on time changes an enemy operation’s outcome.

**Established facts**

The work’s 662 appearance is 174 centimeters tall, broad-chested and thick-legged, with a long square face, dark eyebrows flecked white over brown eyes, high nose, and broad mouth. His tawny skin is sun-darkened, tied black hair streaked gray, beard heavily white. He wears long-mended armor and cold-weather leather over gray-brown underclothes, dark brown outerwear, and a black sash. He pauses when rising. His voice is low, thick, dry, and rough. On the march he briefly and clearly separates who departs, waits, or crosses first rather than making long speeches. He does not pretend not to know soldiers’ fear and first demonstrates responsibility for the choice. A commander’s resolve cannot remove cold or heal frozen bodies. Appearance and private speech are invented.

Use the record that before departure King Munmu gave him a personally written delegation of rewards and punishments beyond the frontier. He grants [Yeolgi](079-yeolgi.md#yeolgi) and [Gugeun](080-gugeun.md#gugeun) geupchan in the field for expedience, then asks and receives royal permission to promote them to sachan. Do not make this a bone-rank promotion or seizure of unapproved appointment power. Field authority to bear danger connects to responsibility for merit after return.

Munmu’s 671 diplomatic reply recalls the 662 transport and return, frozen and injured hands and feet, and deaths on the road. This is the king emphasizing Silla’s service and loss to Tang. Do not treat frostbite and deaths as wholly unsupported invention; exact losses and a particular person’s amputations remain invented. Do not adopt that Kim suffered a stroke here and left history.
