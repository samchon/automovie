## Aran, wife and mother of Namsaeng {#yeon-wife}

**Definition and scope**

Aran is Gaesomun’s wife and mother of Namsaeng, Namgeon, and Namsan. The record does not preserve Gaesomun’s wife or their mother; her name, origin, birth year, and marriage date are invented. Born in 616 to a western Pyongyang noble family with unspecified surname, she marries Gaesomun in spring 632, eighteen years after his birth, and enters Yeon family quarters. She learns and assumes household management from her mother-in-law but cannot move family troops or arms.

**Established facts**

After Taejo’s death in late 633, succession in spring 634 precedes Namsaeng’s birth that autumn. Aran is not pregnant when Taejo dies. After Gaesomun bows and promises before opposing nobles and inherits eastern great-man office and family command in spring, she bears eldest son Namsaeng in autumn. Thus he is born in the year his father becomes head. His birth year follows the epitaph; the post-succession autumn is invented. Aran also bears Namgeon in 637 and Namsan in 639.

From the first year she sees Gaesomun’s habit of defeating people. She does not tell him to change, but sends food to a loser’s household when her husband will not wait. She shares management and closeness with her mother-in-law. Her natal family hopes the marriage will gain central standing, which Aran knows; she has no place in her husband’s decisions.

At marriage in 632 she has lived sixteen years, about 155 centimeters and slender, with a narrow face, pointed chin, round brown eyes, short eyebrows, small mouth, and light tawny skin. Her black hair is braided long and raised; she wears dark reddish-brown outerwear, dark sash, and a red thread knot from home.

In 634 she mourns and carries her first child. Around the spring agreement she is about three months pregnant without a visible belly. Beyond five months in summer her belly projects; she leans slightly back, walks slowly, and cannot stand long. At autumn delivery her belly is large, one hand supports her back while walking, and cheeks round. In mourning she wears undyed light brown with gray, removes the natal red thread knot, and adds an undyed hemp knot to her sash. One long braid hangs down her back.

Side by side in mourning, Aran is slightly shorter than her mother-in-law (about 155 versus 158 centimeters), and from summer her projecting belly and backward-leaning waist alter her profile. Her mother-in-law is straight-waisted with low-tied hair and light tawny mourning clothes; Aran has a single braid down her back and gray-brown mourning clothes.

Her voice is high, clear, and quiet. Before strangers she speaks briefly and trails off; with family she speaks distinctly. She hums softly to soothe a child.

**Conditions and limits**

Her appearance, actions, and life dates after 634 are unset; supplement this record before later appearance. Namsaeng’s birth may close the youth phase; its scene choice belongs to youth event design.
