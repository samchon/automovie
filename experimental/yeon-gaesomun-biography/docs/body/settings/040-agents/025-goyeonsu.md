# The relief force Go Yeonsu believes in

## The relief force Go Yeonsu believes in {#goyeonsu}

**Definition and scope**

Go Yeonsu commands a large relief force in 645, seeking to end the war in the field. He believes his generation can reproduce on the battlefield the glory of the generation that defeated Sui. Knowing the harm long confinement in fortresses causes residents and production, he sees practical grounds for demands for a quick decision. Pride that underestimates the enemy adds to his calculation that the relief force’s size will restrict enemy options. Even when [Go Hyejin](026-gohyejin.md#gohyejin) asks about practical attack constraints, both share the premise that victory is possible.

**Established facts**

His birth year is unresolved; the work portrays him looking in his forties in 645. He is about 185 centimeters tall, long-limbed and broad-shouldered, with prominent cheekbones, high nasal bridge, broad mouth, and narrow brown eyes beneath dark eyebrows. His skin is deeply sun-darkened, straight black hair tied, and beard short. He wears a reddish-brown outer robe, dark sash, well-mended lamellar armor, and a decorated helmet.

His thick, bright voice below the middle register carries long across a wide space. Hearing disagreement, he speaks faster and ends orders firmly. At surrender, his short breath prevents him projecting as usual.

With many units to move, Go Yeonsu seeks to settle disagreement quickly. The resulting advance ends in defeat and surrender at Jupilsan. Failure to protect people he thought his rank as general could protect leads to fear and choices for survival after defeat. Survival alone does not make him a coward. The record says he receives Tang office after surrender, but that result does not justify the earlier decision to advance.
