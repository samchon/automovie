## Buru, the veteran at Pyongyang Gyeongdang {#gyeongdang-teacher}

**Definition and scope**

Buru is an invented veteran who teaches archery and formation movement to children at [Pyongyang Gyeongdang](../060-places/260-pyongyang-gyeongdang.md#pyongyang-gyeongdang). A commoner born in 570, he fought Sui in 598 and 612 and was at the fighting in Pyongyang’s outer fortress in 612. After losing his left ring and little fingers he could no longer draw a bow for long and teaches at Gyeongdang. He lives on grain and firewood provided by street households and cannot command troops outside the school.

**Established facts**

Buru tells children how royal prince Go Geonmu led a picked force to defeat Sui. He knows the actual attack was an ambush inside the outer fortress against Sui troops dispersed by looting. Before children, however, he inflates it into a charge by the prince leading heavy cavalry, because it makes them hold their bows longer. If asked separately, he briefly explains the ambush and looting; the children remember the charge.

He judges children by archery and formation, not family. Even a noble child who breaks turn is sent to the yard’s edge. He recognizes Gaesomun’s talent early, but sets him back in line whenever he rushes ahead and disrupts the formation.

In the 620s Buru appears in his fifties, about 168 centimeters tall, broad-shouldered and slightly stooped. His angular face has sparse eyebrows, deep brown eyes, and a curved nasal bridge. His deeply sun-darkened skin and black hair heavily streaked with gray are visible; his hair is tied in one bundle and his beard short and rough. Two fingers are missing from his left hand. He wears gray-brown cloth and a dark brown sash, always carrying a small knife and thread bundle for bow repair at his waist.

His voice is low and hoarse. He shouts briefly to be heard across the yard, lowers his voice and leaves long pauses when telling a story, and when angry calls a child’s name once rather than raising his voice.

**Conditions and limits**

Buru’s knowledge of the actual 612 conditions is invented; [Go Geonmu’s heroic story](015-geonmu.md#geonmu-war-story) establishes the record’s scope.
