## The Wa woman married to Buyeo Pung {#pung-wife}

**Definition and scope**

Buyeo Pung’s spouse is a woman of the Wa ruling class whom the Nihon Shoki identifies as Komoshiki’s younger sister. Do not reconstruct a name absent from the record as her historical name. Adopt the marriage before Pung’s return in 661 as a concrete family connection between the two countries. Reports seen in Goguryeo convey the marriage as well as the forces the king obtained in Wa. In the currently planned narrative she neither speaks directly nor recalls events privately, and does not act at Baekgang. Do not assert whether she accompanied his return or what became of her afterward. Therefore, no individual portrait or voice is established for a scene.

**Established facts**

Her marriage does not prove that every Wa noble was an illegitimate son of a Baekje noble. Unlike a commercial relationship that can simply return to Wa after defeat, this small connection shows that the king’s personal family and political commitments already bind the two countries.
