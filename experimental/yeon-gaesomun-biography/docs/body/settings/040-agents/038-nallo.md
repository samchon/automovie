## Nallo, who cannot return {#nallo}

**Definition and scope**

Nallo is an invented soldier born in 626, Miran’s husband and Sokro’s cousin. In 645 he uses bow and spear in the unit Sokro commands. Before capture he tries to find a way out of the fighting, but is trapped with the formation. Captured and executed at Jupilsan, he cannot return home. The bow Miran keeps is the one he left at home; the weapons he took to battle do not return. Alongside Miran’s loss, portray Nallo’s own attempt to survive, leaving at the end the question of whether his family can confirm his death in battle.

**Established facts**

He appears around twenty, a handspan shorter than Sokro, with thick arms, a round nose, large mouth, short tied black hair, and deeply sun-darkened skin. He wears brown leather protection over tawny cloth clothes. His voice is rough but bright in the middle register.

Nallo’s personal scenes in the main story run from before Jupilsan to his capture. No new speech or memories from him follow his execution.
