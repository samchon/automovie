# Conditions under which Gaesomun moves first in 642

Canon guide: [Location of this subject](../outline/index.md#050-systems--240-642-coup).

## Conditions under which Gaesomun moves first in 642 {#642-coup}

**Definition and scope**

The coup of 642 begins with the king and great men secretly plotting first to kill Gaesomun. Samguk Sagi vol. 49 says that after he succeeded to office he was cruel and lawless, and the great men secretly consulted the king to kill him (嗣位而凶殘不道，諸大人與王密議欲誅). Treat that stated cruelty as an assessment from the same hostile perspective as the account of mutilating the corpse. The work portrays the plot as a choice by king and great men afraid of his growing power. Once it leaks, Gaesomun acts first to survive. Amid growing Tang pressure, a generation resents the king’s defenses and diplomacy as weakness, while Gaesomun’s central power itself threatens the royal house. Do not divide people into those who only love the country and those who have only private ambition.

**Established facts**

Gaesomun uses his own people to secure the assembly and palace approaches. Using a military review and gathering south of Pyongyang, he brings about 100 opposing nobles together and attacks them. The account’s more than a hundred ministers is retained, but participant numbers and detailed guard organization are invented; do not claim a surviving list naming every victim. Seizing military power is not described as a legitimate replacement authorized by royal order.

After capture, the king dies leaving final words that do not seek a way to survive. See [King Yeongnyu](../040-agents/015-geonmu.md#geonmu-last-defence) for their meeting. Gaesomun installs Bojang and takes actual power while retaining the state, not planning to eliminate the whole royal family and found a new dynasty. He needs royal orders and the country’s name, but power gained by killing the king leaves royal resentment.

Histories say he cut the king’s corpse into pieces and threw it in a ditch. Since this conflicts with the coup’s character of preserving the Go royal house and state, the work treats it as exaggeration in a hostile record and does not assert corpse mutilation in the narrative.

**Conditions and limits**

The record does not identify the plot’s exact participants or location; the work does not fix them.
