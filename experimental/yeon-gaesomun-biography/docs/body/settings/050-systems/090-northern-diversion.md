## The opening created by the northern crisis {#northern-diversion}

**Definition and scope**

The Tiele and Khitan crisis in late 661 prevents Tang from emptying other frontiers solely for the Goguryeo campaign. Strategic connection between Goguryeo and Khitan is adopted by the work; not every Tiele revolt follows Gaesomun’s direct order. [The recorded calendar](../020-world/030-second-war-calendar.md#second-war-calendar) owns diversion dates; this record owns delays in orders and reconnaissance and conditions remaining afterward.

**Established facts**

Baseline delivery of the northern diversion order from Tang’s center is 18 days to Xiao Siye, 20 to Ashina Zhong, and 28 to Qibi Heli’s Yalu position. Departure preparation after receipt takes two days each for Xiao and Ashina and three for Qibi. These are work values, not measured post roads; receipt and departure are not simultaneous. Dispatch to departure totals 20, 22, and 31 days. The calendar owns the early dispatch window and latest departures. Qibi leaves after the frozen crossing and before the Nufang counterattack. Do not combine the Zizhi Tongjian’s September withdrawal placement and a scholarly reconstruction of an edict after October 11 as the same dating evidence. This is not a timetable applying Lee Min-su’s late-dispatch hypothesis unchanged.

Liaodong scouts confirm movement two days after departure in the Buyeo direction, report to Namsaeng in two days, and take eight more from him to Pyongyang even with relay horses. The first report knows direction and remaining camp conditions, not the original order or reason; prisoner information and separate trade contacts must be compared. Field commanders can judge within delegated authority without waiting for central confirmation of the reason.

After diversion, Zheng Mingzhen’s Nufang force alone remains in Liaodong; northern support capacity decreases. Individuals may survive even after organizational loss.

Later fates of the diverted commanders follow [Qibi Heli](../040-agents/054-qibi-heli.md#qibi-heli) and [Xiao Siye](../040-agents/059-xiao-siye.md#xiao-siye). Zheng Rentai’s light-cavalry desert losses occurred outside the Goguryeo front.

Ashina Zhong’s inscription says he greatly defeated Khitan aligned with Goguryeo. The work follows it, without Goguryeo troops participating.

Reference: treatments own actual links between edict receipt, march, and reports, and Liaodong interdiction and organizational collapse.
