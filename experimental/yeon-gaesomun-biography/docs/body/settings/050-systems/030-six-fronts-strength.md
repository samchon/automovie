## Six directions and the work’s 350,000 {#six-fronts-strength}

**Definition and scope**

The work adopts Lee Min-su’s 35 expeditionary routes and 350,000 as Tang’s total 661 mobilization. Popular estimates of 700,000–1,000,000 lack strong support. The research organization includes multiple routes such as Jangjam, Hamja, and Yalu, not only six names. The six groupings below are the work’s organization by operational main force, not exact directional figures established by source or paper. Do not divide equally.

**Established facts**

| Operational grouping | Principal commander | Work-specific initial mobilization | Mission and subordinate connections |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Pyongyang route main force | Su Dingfang | 70,000 | Naval pressure on Pyongyang, including some Hamja-route troops |
| Paegang route main force | Ren Yaxiang | 60,000 | Siege around Pyongyang and inland connection |
| Okjeo route main force | Pang Xiaotai | 50,000 | Sasu positions and southern interdiction |
| Liaodong route main force | Qibi Heli | 60,000 | Yalu breakthrough and supply connection, including Yalu-route troops |
| Buyeo direction | Xiao Siye and Ashina Zhong | 60,000 | Northern pressure with separate Buyeo and Jangjam commands |
| Nufang direction | Zheng Mingzhen | 50,000 | Pressure on Sinseong area and Liaodong defenses |
| Total | — | 350,000 | Not one army under one field commander |

Buyeo’s 60,000 divide into Xiao’s 35,000 and Ashina’s 25,000. They do not share one camp or have Xiao command all Ashina’s force. Troops retaining route names differs from Goguryeo identifying both in one strategic direction. Subordinate routes are included; do not add separately appearing Hamja and Yalu troops again.

Initial groupings range from 50,000 to 70,000. Excluding transport, rear garrisons, and sickness, each generally fields 40,000–60,000 in one battle, similar to Gaesomun’s maximum 50,000 military mission personnel, 46,000 fighters and 4,000 support ([Goguryeo availability and losses](040-goguryeo-661-strength.md#goguryeo-661-strength)). Each is [elite Tang](010-tang-military-confidence.md#tang-military-confidence). Defeating them separately is not choosing weak enemies, but a plan to face similarly sized elites one at a time six times. Do not turn every campaign departure or survivor into field annihilation. Goguryeo’s advantage lies in isolating enemies and gathering its forces on time, not numbers.

Initial mobilization counts military personnel performing combat and transport, not precise Tang fighter statistics including every outside civilian carrying household. Only some enter actual combat; supply, rear, and sickness remove others. Goguryeo characters do not know this table exactly at the outset, estimating groupings and relative size from reports and prisoners.
