## The local battle for Dolsaseong in 664 {#664-dolsa}

**Definition and scope**

In the seventh lunar month of 664, Silla orders Kim Inmun, Pumil, Gungwan, and Munyeong to lead troops from Ilseonju and Hansanju and the forces of the Ungjin subordinate prefecture against Goguryeo’s Dolsaseong, which they capture. The work adopts this as a local battle over southern fortresses and routes even after the conquest expedition stops. As Goguryeo receives remaining Baekje forces including Buyeo Pung and seeks to extend control and trust in the south, the report of the loss of an actual fortress adds to the anxiety of reconstruction.

**Established facts**

Historical sources do not establish Dolsaseong’s exact location; no direct siege scene is planned. The explanation that the attack was prompted by the exile of remaining Baekje forces is a scholarly view introduced in the National Institute of Korean History annotation. The work reads that possibility as context for southern pressure, without writing as though the Silla king actually confessed that motive. Do not invent figures for casualties or the battle’s duration. The loss of this fortress does not itself mean that Tang and Silla resumed a war to conquer the whole country.
