## Family conflict changes under royal orders in 666 {#666-coup}

**Definition and scope**

Namgeon and Namsan launch a coup to remove their elder brother and govern aggressively; they have no prior plan to kill him. The king actually issues orders enabling them to control him, taking their seizure beyond a private family conflict.

**Established facts**

Namsaeng, in the regions, sends [Du Gyeom](../040-agents/018-namsaeng-messenger.md#namsaeng-messenger) to investigate the center. The accompanying driver returns reporting detention. Namsaeng judges his brothers intend more than grievance: they want to bring him centrally and strip power. Yet refusing the summons gives Namgeon’s side grounds to claim defiance of a royal order. Before his regional support hardens, the royal house seeks to sever central command and its basis for legitimacy.

When coup forces come to take the Yeon residence and troops, Heonchung moves about 300 remaining guards. The work sets about 1,000 mobilized by Namgeon and Namsan. The side first securing capital approaches and power spaces near the palace expands its orders’ reach. This is not all national field forces gathering in the capital, and Heonchung cannot from the outset defeat all 1,000 uncles’ troops.

After Namsaeng refuses the summons and enters Gungnaeseong, the king orders his arrest and execution for defiance, actually approving that order.

It reaches Namsaeng and Gungnaeseong supporters only after Heonchung dies. They judge the royal house has also cut off a chance to end matters as removal and renegotiation between brothers. Namsaeng can no longer trust a king ordering his execution as a negotiating partner guaranteeing safety.

Fixed detention and takeover constraints: Du Gyeom waits at Namsan’s work residence, is handed to the receiving commander and detained; the driver confirms only absence and detention, waits, leaves Pyongyang, and reports. Namsaeng knows of detention after that report. The driver does not witness interrogation or know the king’s whole intent. About 200 of Damseok’s leading troops deploy in the Yeon south inner courtyard, the rest at alley entrance and outer wall. Damseok intends capture alive and guard separation but remains responsible for failing to control every blade. After injury he reports it to Namgeon, waits for an order to bring back Heonchung under treatment, and reports death three days later. This order and deployment are invented fixed inputs; treatments complete actual exchange, movement, and disclosure.
