# Expression that preserves facts

## Remove repetitive phrasing while preserving facts {#fact-preserving-expression}

Remove repetitive AI slop from settings, treatments, scripts, and final screenplay without adding facts. Revise expression while preserving the distinction between fact and invention, negatives and uncertainty, people, events, relationships, quantities, appearance, and outcomes. Reduce findings of an independent audit below the level found in comparable human writing, with zero high-confidence findings.

Upstream patterns carry into later layers, so improve expression from settings onward without losing established facts through concision.
