# Production code

## Implementation and ownership

Does the declaration serve the requested behavior through a justified method, with its assumptions, units, failure effects, and resource ownership clear? Explain any non-obvious tradeoff or unresolved limit. Keep each fact or calculation with its owner, repair causes through their consumers, and use fixed inputs wherever replay must be reproducible. Identify any consumer-specific compensation and its contractual basis.

## Authoring interface

Can the next author use and change the declaration from its types and documentation? Document purpose and the non-obvious constraints, coordinate frames, timebases, optional states, or parameter relationships that apply. Keep public controls meaningful for the production rather than adding abstraction for hypothetical work. State what the caller must supply or verify.
