# Delivery

## Complete presentation

Does the actual delivered artifact meet the request's scope, format, duration, and intended audience experience? Trace required screenplay material through executing shots and actual edit inclusion; source names and citations alone prove no visible result. Compare the complete opening, middle, joins, part boundaries and ending with the commissioned content, titles, captions and sound. For an asset delivery, compare its controls and required views instead.

For a motion hint, inspect the intended action, staging, identity, contacts, camera and timing at its chosen representation level; coarse geometry is valid when it carries that performance.

For commissioned time-matched controls, establish matching performance, camera/crop and frame-time mapping, declared channel conventions, actual subject coverage and the palette/sidecars used by semantic identity. Separately supplied reference inputs follow their receiver's contract without invented frame correspondence. Verify that conversions and delivered formats preserve the values the recipient requires; a viewing encode or matching frame count cannot prove aligned usable controls.

When a generator is selected, state its actual observed input compatibility. A finished direct 3D or generated film additionally needs its actual visual and sound result: identity, spatial/temporal continuity, required action and information, intelligibility of included speech/text, and the intended audience effect. Conditioning alone cannot establish it.

Verify the actual delivered file's agreed dimensions/aspect, frame rate, container/codec, picture/color handling, audio and caption tracks where the commission specifies them. Use measured media timing and actual playback through the intended consumer, distinguishing observed results and unverified limits. An encoder exit, preview or successful proxy proves no unobserved final profile.

## Reproducible handoff

Can the recipient locate the fixed inputs, actual source entry, dependencies, executable commands and exact final outputs needed to reproduce or use the result? Identify the reviewed revision, accepted takes and content digests, observed delivery profile, external dependencies, rights or attribution, completed checks and remaining limitations. Keep external generation requests, provider/model/job identity and original outputs traceable to the accepted derivatives, without credentials. Replaying fixed bytes does not promise deterministic regeneration from a seed or transient URL. Preserve the programmed hint and generated final as distinct artifacts when both were commissioned. A successful intermediate render or encoder exit does not prove the final handoff.
