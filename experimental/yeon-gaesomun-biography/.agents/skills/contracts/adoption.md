# Adopt a requirement

## Find the missing responsibility

Compare the adopted instructions, material, and requested deliverable with existing questions before adding a rule. Keep creative user choices with [instructions](../instructions/SKILL.md), operating scope and choices with [project context](../production/profile.md), facts and constraints with their creative owner, and new production-specific judgment in the matching story work-contract family. [Discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md) records the investigation and any adequate existing owners when no independent requirement remains.

For a new duty, identify its distinct requirement, the narrowest honest respondent population, an observable desired difference, a representative failure, and the basis for requiring it. Group requirements judged together rather than copying one rule into several owners or the screenplay. Adopt the question and its actual population, layer, and pass together through [evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md).

## Preserve the work's actual language

Shared questions retain language discovery, value fidelity, idiomatic writing and speech, relationship language, and cross-population interference. Adopt additional language-specific standards at the narrow owner when the actual work needs them. The scaffold does not force Korean, novel-only prose conventions, or speculative signal bans. A new language question supplements the shared responsibilities rather than replacing them.

## Choose respondents that can establish the result

Engineering, creative, asset, and output duties have different respondents. Unit questions concern the selected unit; a whole-work duty needs the actual population respondent. [Evidence writing](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md) owns accountable answers and [respondent placement](../evidence-writing/respondents.md). Installed package documentation owns native annotations and selectors.

For commissioned execution, preserve formal final-screenplay-to-shot and shot-to-edit coverage under [scope and graph ownership](../evidence-configuration/scope.md#preserve-actual-relationships). Asset builders or scoped observation and handoff reports can answer [asset](assets.md) and [delivery](delivery.md) duties where they establish actual facts. Source citations cannot prove edit inclusion, appearance, intelligibility, or playback.

Match output obligations to the commission. A generation hint can use coarse geometry or stick figures while needing accurate movement, staging, and camera relationships. A direct image, direct 3D asset, or recovered model is a production choice governed by the needed controls and deformation. Inspect a delivered hint at its intended use; do not require a later diffusion result when it was not commissioned. A finished-film commission needs that actual delivered film inspected through [delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md).

## Change the rule and its consumers together

When adopting, changing, merging, or retiring a question, update its owners and selected targets together. Keep respondents, inherited relationships, and review mirrors consistent. Route changed narrative requirements, answers, scope, and reviews through [upstream repair](../upstream-repair/SKILL.md); historical proof remains attributable to its own snapshot. A renamed rule, previous approval, or passing graph does not establish a new obligation's truth.

## Retain provenance

The production workflow and story contracts adapt [`wrtnlabs/novels` skills](https://github.com/wrtnlabs/novels/tree/6a9178bb2d96038d93b5313cd28682061cf44a03/packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills) at commit `6a9178bb2d96038d93b5313cd28682061cf44a03`; retain their MIT notice in [LICENSE.novels](../LICENSE.novels). The installed `@automovie/evidence` package owns the reusable adapted graph, checking implementation, and its source notice.

Video realization replaces final prose with image, sound, performance, and editing decisions; actual performance and delivery promises replace novel length recommendations. The upstream reader-book exporter and central baseline migration commands belong to that distribution system. Installed production instructions, contracts, configuration, and authored files remain project property. These distribution choices waive no creative pass or check; short direct entry and standalone assets keep their explicit routes.
