# Contracts

Find the existing obligation before adding another. Exported source declarations answer [common](common.md), and exported test functions answer [testing](testing.md). [Story contracts](story/index.md) retain unit principles, population obligations, discovery, upstream repair, expression revision, and independent review as distinct families. Apply their selected questions to the actual work and respondent, rather than treating a scene as proof of a whole population.

When a requirement is missing or changes, use [adoption](adoption.md) to choose its canonical owner, observable difference, and narrowest honest scope. [Instructions](../screenwriting/brief.md#requirements) preserves creative user choices; [project context](../production/profile.md) owns operating scope and choices; [discovery](../screenwriting/brief.md#discovery) investigates what existing owners do not cover. Work and language duties supplement shared questions; never weaken a question or its selected population to conceal a defect.

Use [evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md) to wire populations and relationships while preserving the engineering baseline. It owns graph configuration, not answer prose. Use [evidence writing](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md) to compare the complete respondent with the complete selected requirement, correct the work, and write its grounds.

Assets and delivery retain their own [questions](assets.md) and [handoff criteria](delivery.md). Select evidence for the requested screenplay, generation hint, finished film, or asset; required motion and controls do not imply a particular figure system, reconstruction method, or photorealistic finish. [Delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md) checks the actual commissioned result.

Each selected question needs a respondent-specific answer and unresolved limits. Mechanical coverage establishes connections; independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) tests their truth and the recipient's experience. [Writing workflow](../screenwriting/workflow.md#completion) owns pass completion, and [upstream repair](../screenwriting/repair.md) owns the closed-proof boundary when requirements or their consumers change.
