# Review unit principles

Each review H2 answers for the authored H2 or precise relationship it inspected. These questions assess the review record, not the creative quality of the work being reviewed.

## Is its judgment grounded in an independent reading? {#independent-reading}

Before consulting acknowledgments or earlier verdicts, did the reviewer read the current body as its recipient and record an initial observation with at least one exact body quotation and locator? Keep this evidence for units without findings too. Headings, annotations, another unit, remembered wording, and quotations chosen only after deciding a verdict cannot establish that reading. Findings must identify the actual passage, violated obligation and failure condition, what the recipient loses or must invent, the strongest defensible interpretation and why it fails here, and the earliest owner that must become true. A valid counterreading withdraws a finding with its reason; author confidence, repair cost, previous verdicts, length, and reviewer taste do not. Were named expression failures classified under the [selected decision and exception rule](../naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written) before consulting author defenses? Withdraw or protect a named failure only when its passage proves an allowed contractual exception. For other candidates, apply the [retention test](../../../../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate). Unsupported withdrawal remains a finding.

Record fragile achievements with quotations and functions, or explicitly state that none were identified. Protect their function rather than freezing a defective sentence; a named failure without a proven contractual exception is not a protected achievement. Give repair directions rather than prose for adoption; counterfactual sentences belong only to the retention comparison. Review narrative and expressive quality separately from the truth of evidence answers.

Judge the applicable question's defined scope: examples may permit another proven loss, but a named-failure boundary cannot be expanded by reviewer taste. A revised passage requires a clear applicable failure before another yes; unchanged classifications require new body or source evidence to reopen, under [inspection](../../../../screenwriting/inspection.md#verify-changed-work).

## Were related failures and claimed relationships fully audited? {#literal-and-class-audit}

For each finding class, record the inspected scope, reproducible search or comparison, every discovered member and affected relation, and the earliest owner; reference other review units where they inspected a member. A genuinely isolated finding needs its reason. Audit each acknowledgment against the full target requirement and complete host support, including host kind, family, semantic role, lineage, exact predicate, and actual realization. A claim about checking itself, repeated vocabulary, another event, or partial support cannot prove the relationship. Quotations and locators must match the inspected snapshot; an exclusion needs its entire defined negative scope and inspection method.

Did the [inspection record](../../../../screenwriting/inspection.md) cover every required sentence and parent comparison at the actual snapshot, before author evidence, and recheck all changed dependencies without silently reversing unchanged tests?

Was the body-derived element inventory audited independently of these relationship rows, following the [reading and source audit](../../../../review/reading.md)? Verify every element against its actual source or inherited parent, including the full relevant source context for checkable settings and treatment claims. Matching a quotation or completing a checklist cannot replace that independent factual and expressive audit.

Compare nearby relationships by exchanging their names, predicates, and conditions: support and refutation must still be specific to the actual relation. Similar wording is a prompt to inspect, not automatic failure. Repair a false edge at its owner; do not change the story merely to make a pasted answer true. Structural validity belongs to the evidence checker and never substitutes for this semantic audit.
