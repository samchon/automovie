# Inspect before repairing

Use this procedure for author inspection, evidence correction and independent review. The selected contracts own failure conditions and permitted exceptions; a table records their application rather than creates another checklist.

## Isolated context

Use an available subagent or separate-session tool, within its authorization rules, to launch the author's model with no inherited conversation or creative work memory. Pass only the assigned job and input file paths; the worker reads those files directly. Naming the current context "fresh" does not isolate its memory. If that tool or authorization is unavailable, report the missing task instead of performing it in the current context.

## Independent classification

Give the classifier the exact current body and necessary neighboring context, selected contracts, and actual parents or frozen original needed for that comparison. It handles classification alone, excluding author explanations, evidence, prior verdicts and other judging tasks.

For scripts and screenplay construction or expression revision, classify every sentence against every applicable named failure, including speech and the layer-specific questions. For narrative construction, also pair every downstream paragraph with its actual parent passages, or the brief for a direct short, and test [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation); word matching cannot detect a paraphrased sequence. Independently inventory inherited names, forms of address, quantities, dates, times, places, consequential states and certainty and compare their values with the actual parents.

Record the snapshot, files and covered ranges. List the tested failures once, then use one row per sentence with its exact locator and yes-pattern names, or `-` when all are no. This records negative coverage without repeating the whole checklist in every row. For each yes, retain the passage and any quoted contractual exception. Uncertain named failures follow the selected contract's decision rule. Keep these records with the current author notes or review rather than inside the creative body.

The author or reviewer uses the classifications to repair or judge. Lock the initial complete table before consulting author defenses; those records may identify an actual exception or a false assertion, but cannot replace the body comparison. Search recurring literal forms and record all members of each discovered cause. Recipient losses outside named patterns still require judgment under the applicable broader question.

## Verify changed work

Keep the same author through its correction cycle. Compare each changed passage with its previous form, then classify the changed sentences, their neighbors and every statement whose meaning depends on them. Changed parents or requirements invalidate all affected comparisons too. Relocate unchanged rows when line numbers move without calling their carried classification a fresh reading.

Do not reverse an unchanged classification merely because a later reader prefers another interpretation. Reopen it for new body, parent, source or contract evidence; record that evidence. A complete closing review still rereads every selected body and makes its own current unit and population judgments under [review rounds](../review/rounds.md); stable sentence tests do not carry an old unit approval into new text.

An author correction cycle is dry only when initial coverage is complete and no unprotected yes remains after the affected rows are rechecked. Check changed passages together for a new common mold, fidelity losses and dependent-answer errors under [cause rounds](repair.md#cause-rounds). A dry classification table is not independent approval or whole-work completion.
