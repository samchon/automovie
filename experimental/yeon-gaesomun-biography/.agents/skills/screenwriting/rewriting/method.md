# Expressive rewriting

Preserve a reviewed construction while writing its expression anew. Scripts becomes a script performers and filmmakers can execute naturally; screenplay becomes the production's final audiovisual experience. Neither rewrites facts or results. Use the selected naturalness questions in [story contracts](../../contracts/story/index.md).

Rewrite from the scene's actual people, purpose, pressure, and rhythm. Read dialogue with adjacent action and consecutive replies as if spoken, using [realized exchange](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/realized.md#speech-yield) and [common expression](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md) to diagnose it. Delete narration that repeats meaning the scene already shows. Where summary or copied realization still carries needed meaning, recompose it as performed action and sound.

| Layer | Rewriting method |
| --- | --- |
| Scripts | Follow the order of filming and performance. Read ambiguous directions beside the action they govern and exchanges beside their replies; reshape blocks around the changing pressure. |
| Screenplay | Follow the audience's attention through image and sound. Rework selected detail, juxtaposition, reaction, silence, and transitions to realize the moment's intended effect. |

Compare the working version with its frozen original in both directions under [expression fidelity](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#expression-fidelity). Repair lost meaning in the working version and report a construction defect to its owner. After each annotation-free whole reading, use the same layer-specific naturalness questions in [story contracts](../../contracts/story/index.md) as independent review to collect and judge whole-scope candidates in rewriting notes under [cause-based correction](../repair.md#cause-rounds). Repeat until a complete round adopts no further edit; record retained candidates beside the passage under the [retention test](../evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) for independent review.

## Revision cycles

One construction cycle receives one comprehensive expression rewrite. Finish its complete author reading and cause-based corrections before opening evidence. Evidence correction, independent review, author decisions, and repairs then finish that working edition; do not defer known defects to another wholesale rewrite.

For a first rewrite, use the complete construction as material and compose in the layer's own voice and rhythm rather than polish its sentence order. Preserve passages already doing their work. The number of changed sentences, distance from the original, and added detail do not measure improvement.

When a further expression revision is requested, begin from the previous working edition, its reviews, and the author's decisions. Retain those comparison inputs before replacing a working file. Compare meaning in both directions with the initial frozen construction, and compare expressive gains and losses with the previous working edition. The previous edition is not a new construction baseline, and old evidence or acceptance is not an answer for new text.

Keep the initial frozen original throughout repairs to the same construction. Repair facts, events, decisions, or results at their earliest owner under [upstream repair](../repair.md); keep that repair distinct from replacing a unit from scratch, and never hide it by replacing the frozen original during expression work. Preserve work outside the authorized scope.

Use [complete cause rounds](../repair.md#cause-rounds) for every correction. An already settled choice needs new evidence before reopening, except that a selected contract's failure still requires current judgment.

Use [rewriting procedure](procedure.md) for frozen editions, evidence, review and transfer.
