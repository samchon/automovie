# Organize settings records

Use this guide when planning settings files, adding a recurring subject or separating an overloaded record. [Settings](settings.md) owns the research and construction process; the [settings questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/settings.md) and [population obligations](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/settings.md) own completeness and truth. Organization must preserve their responsibilities, not manufacture empty fields.

## Domains and subjects

Use the needed domains under `docs/body/settings/NNN-domain/NNN-subject.md`. Typical subjects include work scope; world and chronology; institutions and systems; agents; relationships; places; objects; background and research. Domain numbers order the actual work, not a universal taxonomy. Preserve adopted names and numbers, leave retired numbers unused, and never reuse an old address for a different subject. Folder order numbers are unique at settings root, and file order numbers are unique within their folder. The installed layout admits this one folder level; use another file rather than a character/building subdirectory.

One independently used person normally owns one character file. An unnamed group may share a file while it acts as one; separate a member when individual speech, choice, reaction or relationship needs an independent owner. A cast list can navigate to those files but must not become a second source of their facts. Brief participants need only their consequential role and recognizable attributes; recurring or central agents need the actual depth required by the contracts.

## Character sections

Give the file one H1 naming its subject and stable, explicitly anchored H2s for independently cited decisions. Choose sections from the material actually needed:

| Subject, when distinct | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and life stage | Names and address, age/date uncertainty, role, status and the scope in which they hold |
| Appearance and voice | Recognizable body, face, hair, clothing, carried items and audible identity, with retained references and meaningful constraints |
| Drives, capacities and limits | Priorities, fears, beliefs, resources, abilities, refusal, costs and credible conditions for changing them |
| Relationships | The applicable two-sided dependency, knowledge, obligation, asymmetry, debt and change conditions, or links to their shared owner |
| Knowledge and access | What the agent knows, believes, misunderstands, can observe and cannot obtain |
| State variants | Consequential differences by age, era, world or established condition without copying the stable identity |

These are section choices, not mandatory headings. Keep inseparable attributes together; a short complete profile may need one H2. Attach sources, adopted inventions, uncertainty and limits to the facts they qualify rather than creating a separate reference H2 that supplies no world decision. Actual changes, revelations and consequences in the film remain treatments' work.

A shared relationship fact has one owner. A simple relationship can be one H2 in a participant's file, recording both sides while the other file links to it. A complex relationship network can have its own file in a needed relationships domain. Distinct personal interpretations may have their own owners, but do not copy the same debt, history or agreed condition into every participant's profile. Shared world history and chronology remain at their own owners rather than reappearing as biographies in every file.

## Places, buildings, backgrounds and objects

Give an independently used location or building its own file. Separate spatial organization, entrances/routes, dimensions, fixed furnishings, materials/colors, light/sound and consequential state variants into H2s when they are independently used. A room or outdoor zone may be an H2; give it another file in the same domain when independent design, reuse or size needs that boundary. Keep connections to the containing building or landscape explicit. Repeated generic streets or incidental background need the shared world grammar, not an invented file for every visible object.

Settings fixes the world facts needed by asset, motion and sound authors. Source code owns meshes, rigs, weights, shaders and render implementation; production owns incidental design and adopted delivery choices. Link realized assets and performances to the actual settings facts and screenplay use through [execution traceability](../evidence/execution.md). A technical implementation must not silently become a new canon fact.

## Change existing records safely

Split a multi-person or multi-location collection by its actual subjects before expanding content. Preserve each existing H2's meaning, literal body, limits and source adoption. Map old file/H2 addresses to their new owners, rebase relative and same-file links, and update actual consumers and outline entries together. An anchor collision after merging a subject needs an explicit old/new mapping, never an ambiguous duplicate. Moving paths and creating new semantic H2s are separate operations: do not redistribute an old H2's meaning while its callers still cite it as the complete decision.

Keep the outline a current finding guide with each actual file's H1, canonical path-derived H3 anchor, content and placement reason. Preserve historical evidence, reviews and source snapshots at the edition they observed; address migration and matching hashes grant no new approval. Apply the [closed-pass boundary](repair.md#closed-passes) to any diagnostics, and leave unused domains absent.
