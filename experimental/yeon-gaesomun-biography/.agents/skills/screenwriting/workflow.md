# Writing workflow

Read the request, project context, adopted instructions and current bodies before choosing the commissioned route. A screenplay is a text deliverable; implementing that text as a motion hint or finished film is a separate production responsibility. Supplied work needs assessment under this production's requirements, not inherited approval.

## Creative ownership

A long film or series retains settings → treatments → scripts → final screenplay. Keep all four responsibilities even when one is brief; compress or regroup content rather than replacing a layer with a description of its parent.

| Layer | Owns | Hands onward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Settings](settings.md) | World facts, baseline states, constraints, characters and relationships, places, objects, knowledge boundaries, and sensory consistency | Usable facts with conditions, uncertainty, sources, and one semantic owner |
| [Treatments](treatments.md) | Events, causes, choices, exchanges, consequences, setup and payoff, information release, and the complete progression | A storyline another author can execute without inventing decisive events |
| [Scripts](scripts.md) | Part and episode partition; fully executed scenes with blocking, actual dialogue, reactions, meaningful performance, stage, light, sound, and viewing position | Complete playable scenes rather than an outline with scene headings |
| [Final screenplay](screenplay.md) | The audience's complete audiovisual experience: selected image and sound, access, attention, duration, juxtaposition, and transitions | Final production text traceable into executed shots and the edit |

Scripts already supply enough detail for a performer and filmmaker to execute each scene. Final screenplay makes independent decisions about what receives attention, what image and sound reveal together, which reaction holds, what silence changes, and how a transition carries the effect. Reformatting a script or appending shot numbers does not supply that realization. Preserve inherited events, decisions, results, fixed values, and required information order while choosing their audiovisual expression.

A baseline relationship belongs in settings; its change belongs to the event causing it. A historical outcome can constrain the work without settings owning its chosen dramatic path. Possible knowledge, the moment someone learns something, and the audience's access to evidence are distinct. Cite their owners rather than making each layer a second biography or encyclopedia.

## Route and parents

Long work follows settings construction → treatments construction → scripts construction → scripts naturalness → screenplay construction → screenplay naturalness. Short work enters at screenplay construction and retains its expression, evidence and independent review duties. An asset-only task has no narrative passes. Only an explicit user waiver omits an expression pass.

For an expression pass, use the [rewriting method](rewriting/method.md) for composition and revision cycles, and the [rewriting procedure](rewriting/procedure.md) for freezing, submission, review and exact transfer.

Choose form and provisional scale through brief, without imposing conflict, a protagonist, a cliffhanger or uniform units on a demonstration, observation, ensemble, music video or abstract work. Complete work is the default; a pilot requires its own commission. A short's brief may live in the screenplay header; invent no empty upstream populations or false lineage.

## Construction

Read current parent bodies and reviews when starting, resuming, or changing a source. The dependencies are:

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, material and discovered work requirements |
| Treatments | Completed settings construction |
| Scripts | Completed treatments construction |
| Screenplay in long work | Treatments and reviewed, transferred scripts after naturalness; an explicit waiver may use scripts construction |
| Screenplay in short work | The request and adopted constraints directly |

Facts pass through the actual parents. Direct settings evidence cannot repair an incomplete treatment handoff. [Discovery](brief.md#discovery) compares requirements and current owners before bulk settings and at downstream design reviews.

1. Finish the whole declared body while its pass is disabled. Read selected contract goals without shaping each paragraph as a checklist response. Preserve an identifiable complete-draft snapshot in version history or an immutable copy before opening evidence; a partial progress snapshot is insufficient.
2. Open the corresponding evidence stage and claims together through [evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md). Compare the complete body with every selected requirement, complete [cause-based inspection and repair](repair.md#cause-rounds), and write [evidence](evidence/writing.md) from the corrected work.
3. Submit the whole selected population, including boundaries and global obligations, to independent [review](../review/SKILL.md). Supply scope and actual inputs rather than a conclusion to confirm.
4. Record reasoned author decisions, repair accepted findings through their owners, and satisfy the [closing-round rule](../review/rounds.md). Keep the current completed review and the pass in its evidence stage.

Settings includes its outline in the same completion. Unit answers cannot establish whole-population coverage, character development, middle accumulation, partition, or complete handoff; use the selected population respondent and compare every member. Settings and treatments correct their documents within construction; scripts and screenplay next receive their separate expression passes.

## Completion

Run the installed production checker for the submitted layer and pass, then read the complete report and unfiltered validation. A target check establishes only its target. Captured reports describe their actual inputs; reevaluate after source or filesystem changes. Readiness can identify a body ready for review while completion still fails, and cannot grant artistic acceptance. Installed `@automovie/evidence` documentation owns commands, fields, and exit behavior.

Keep completed passes in their evidence stage. Existing closed layers follow [upstream repair](repair.md), preserving failed reports and actual exit statuses. Strict whole-work completion requires the selected passes, passing unfiltered validation and a clean package build, followed by [delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md) of the actual commissioned output. Failed required checks leave that completion unavailable.

When preserved closed-pass diagnostics prevent mechanical readiness, continue authorized author work and independent reading against the actual current bodies and parents, reporting the failed checks separately. After the current independent closing review and commissioned output checks are complete, a manuscript may be delivered with its exact revision, review outcome, repair scope and named mechanical limits. This qualified delivery does not satisfy a request for fully validated completion. It waives no current creative defect, required parent, unfinished initial expression transfer, independent review or commissioned media verification; pending review means a draft submitted for review. A screenplay or diffusion hint commission does not imply a finished post-diffusion film.
