# Final video screenplay

Make the work the audience will see and hear. For the long route, begin with reviewed treatments and scripts after scripts [rewriting](rewriting/method.md). Read [discovery](discovery.md) and screenplay questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). This is its own construction, followed by its own expression pass.

Turn the inherited decisions into a fresh audiovisual progression. Select what the audience sees and hears and when it attends, recognizes, waits, and connects images and sounds. Create action and reactions specific to these people and this moment; interchangeable gestures, empty responses, and summaries of what the scene already conveys do not supply that contribution. [Screenplay principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/screenplay.md) and [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation) judge that contribution and its inherited limits.

For long work:

1. Extract brief notes containing episode/H2 identities, values used in decisions or continuity, who decides what with which result, and the audience's access to character knowledge. Include age, rank, appearance or history only where the scene uses that value. Preserve consequential chronology and required staging. Leave out interpretations of exchanges, category labels, argument lists, mood instructions, upstream wording and incidental action sequences; these become repeated explanations in the draft.
2. [Launch an isolated drafting context](inspection.md#isolated-context) with only the paths to those notes, adopted requirements and contracts, required identities and explicitly fixed quotations. Keep upstream sentences and unfixed dialogue outside it. Compose the audiovisual progression there. If that context is unavailable, report the missing step rather than claim a construction drafted in the upstream context is complete.
3. Use [independent parent and value comparisons](inspection.md) against the actual treatments and scripts in both directions. Record missing decisions in your own words, close the parents, and realize those notes through fresh image, sound, action and speech. Changing tense or formatting does not transform a copied realization.

A short's direct entry has no upstream expression to isolate and composes from its actual brief. Missing inherited decisions return to [upstream repair](repair.md#closed-passes).

Mirror scripts' part directories, index H1s, episode filenames, H1s, H2 identities, and order in `docs/body/screenplay`. Within each unit, give the complete viewing and listening progression with actual spoken words, important reactions, sounds, and screen text, using the [working notation](notation.md). A shot plan may accompany it; it cannot replace it. Cite the realized treatment and corresponding script. World facts arrive through those parents; missing inherited decisions require upstream repair rather than direct settings evidence.

For a short, enter here directly and use the short mode's screenplay population under `docs/body/screenplay`. Its header can hold a sufficient brief. No imaginary parents or unused world documents are required. Retain [instructions](brief.md#requirements), discovery, selected screenplay contracts, construction, expressive rewriting, evidence, and independent review. Direct entry skips the first three layers, not this layer's responsibilities.

Before [evidence](evidence/writing.md), read the complete final screenplay in viewing order, then complete [independent classification](inspection.md) and [repair by cause](repair.md#cause-rounds), using the common, speech, and screenplay expression questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) used by independent review, plus parent differentiation. A search for three consecutive shared words locates candidates; it cannot prove or disprove copied realization. Judge the complete parent comparison, preserving required terms, fixed quotations and already complete short utterances without ornamental variation. Remove sentences whose loss changes no information, attention, or pressure; intended duration does not require filling their place. Complete the selected passes under [workflow](workflow.md), then hand the current result to [production](../production/SKILL.md). Keep traceable relationships to actual executable shots and the edit. Estimates remain provisional until performed action, fixed speech and playback establish timing. A screenplay-only commission ends with its required text review; inspect the actual assembled output when video delivery is commissioned.
