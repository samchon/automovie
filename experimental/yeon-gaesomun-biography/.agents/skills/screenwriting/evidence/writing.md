# Creative evidence

Read the complete selected target, respondent, and necessary parent material. Compare every requirement with every relevant part of that respondent; one correct quotation cannot pass the rest of a unit, file, or population. If the selected contracts include an independent overview question, resolve it before detailed rows so local repairs follow the same direction. Use [story contracts](../../contracts/story/index.md) to find questions, and installed `@wrtnlabs/evidence` documentation for annotations and `@automovie/evidence` documentation for production composition and checks.

Before answering, find possible failures across the whole respondent and complete [cause rounds](../repair.md#cause-rounds), including the [retention test](#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate). Answers diagnose the corrected work or grounds for retaining a candidate; they do not argue missing material into existence. Group a repeated cause and reference its already established grounds rather than reciting them in every answer.

Support assertions with literal quotations and real owner addresses. Verify quotations as exact strings in the current body or parent, and verify claimed counts, sequence, causes, places, and states against the whole relevant scope. Answer what the question asks; unnecessary inventories become stale downstream facts. For an inapplicable question, state inspected scope and precise reason through the mechanism that relationship permits. Whole-population exclusion requires the whole population to be inapplicable.

Use [respondent placement](respondents.md) for detached construction answers, working-version comments, and permitted exclusions. A population answer must inspect the whole selected population. [Evidence configuration](../../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md) owns the graph and its protected-input boundaries; answering a question does not authorize changing its scope.

After an edit within the submitted pass, reconcile its dependent answers and observations in the same repair, including claims that an inherited value is absent after the body supplies it. Recheck new and revised answers against the complete relevant body before handoff. [Upstream repair](../repair.md) owns changes touching closed bodies, proof, or scope. Preserve unrelated bytes when the requested operation changes only evidence metadata.

Hand the complete body and accountable answers to [writing workflow](../workflow.md) for construction submission and pass checks, or [rewriting procedure](../rewriting/procedure.md) for expression submission and transfer. Inspect real selections and complete diagnostics through the installed APIs; filtered checks establish only their scope. Independent [review](../../review/SKILL.md) checks truth and the recipient's experience. Preserve failed reports and exit statuses, including diagnostics left closed by upstream repair. A stale-review refresh is not new creative judgment, and workflow readiness is not an artistic PASS.

## Grounds for retaining a candidate

For a named expression failure, use the selected contract's [decision and exception rule](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written); an alternative cannot create an unlisted exception. For other candidates, retaining or withdrawing one requires a written alternative giving the same information, access and pressure without that form, plus a quotation showing what the original adds at that occurrence. Deletion is not that alternative. Record the alternative and supporting quotation together; if the alternative is equally good or better, use it, and if that evidence is missing, repair the candidate. A function label, the next sentence relying on it, loss on deletion, inherited precedent, or earlier protection does not pass this test.
