# Place the answer with its respondent

Construction answers remain detached from creative bodies. Put positive H2 answers in `docs/evidence/construction/units/<layer>/...md` and file answers in `docs/evidence/construction/files/<layer>/...md`. The document's H1 identifies the exact body file or file-and-anchor address, followed by Evidence headings and supporting prose. Read installed `@wrtnlabs/evidence` documentation for native address and annotation syntax.

Detached documents do not support exclusion or exclusion-review rows. Put a permitted exclusion in the actual host unit's HTML comment, or use the designated discovery carrier for a discovery negative. State the inspected scope and exact inapplicability; a population exclusion requires the entire population to be inapplicable.

Naturalness answers stay in HTML comments attached to their working unit in `docs/naturalness`. File answers attach before its first heading. Keep them with that edition when reviewed text is transferred; [rewriting procedure](../rewriting/procedure.md#transfer) owns the comment-free transfer and [upstream repair](../repair.md) its closed-proof boundary.

Review records mirror the actual units of their declared edition and include a verdict for the whole selected population. They are the reviewer's judgments, not substitutes for the author's evidence. [Review](../../review/SKILL.md) owns their reading and finding exchange.

Work-specific and language-specific questions belong in their matching families under `.agents/skills/contracts/story/{work,language}`. [Contracts](../../contracts/SKILL.md) owns adoption and [evidence configuration](../../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md) selects their actual populations and owners. Use a unit for its behavior and a designated population respondent for a whole-work duty. Asset and delivery respondents can be builders or scoped observation and handoff reports when they can establish the commissioned result.
