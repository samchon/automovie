# Video and conditioning review

Watch and, when present, listen to the entire assembled artifact as an audience member who has not read its planning, then lock that response before consulting screenplay or code. Include opening, ending, sustained middle, joins and part transitions. For a separately commissioned still asset, inspect its promised views and shot role; stills alone establish no motion-video result. A whole-video hint must contain the programmed main scenes and complete assembled timeline, including joins and ending.

A diffusion hint must carry the intended staging, actor and object identity, camera, action and timing at its chosen representation level. Stick figures and coarse meshes can fulfill that brief; inspect the complete relevant motion for contacts, support, trajectories, facing, reactions, occlusion and continuity through [rendered-use inspection](../3d-modeling/inspection.md). Verify low-cost motion before selectively raising geometric detail through code options. Inspect upgraded objects at their intended use and budget. A description can supply an omitted object only when its visible geometry or interaction is unnecessary for that hint. When a generator is selected, verify its actual input contract instead of assuming it accepts a custom skeleton, depth image or rendered video. If generation is authorized and available, inspect a representative result before scaling that integration; otherwise disclose compatibility as unverified without producing an uncommissioned final film.

Trace each required screenplay unit into executable shots, the edit's actual inclusion and the resulting output. These relationships can be many-to-many. A function's name or a source reference proves neither invocation nor visible delivery. Check source placement, camera and framing against the intended experience, then trace any missing result through accepted media, captions and transitions. Installed packages own capture, import and encoding capabilities; the production supplies its actual host.

Use performed and measured timing from actual action, accepted speech, pauses, captions, music and transitions; page or word count cannot establish runtime. Apply [audio acquisition and listening](../audio/acquisition.md) to takes and sound with picture. Changing a voice take can change reactions, subtitles, shot lengths and subsequent timing even with identical words. Return those changes to affected owners and consumers together. A silent motion hint may be complete for its declared timing and action; it cannot establish speech intelligibility or a finished soundtrack.

For commissioned final video, inspect the actual direct 3D or post-diffusion output for preserved action, identity, spatial and temporal continuity, information, emotion, intelligibility of included speech and text, and intended audience effect. Record generator inputs and fixed output bytes separately from the reproducible hint; deterministic replay of adopted bytes does not make external generation deterministic. Judge observed output rather than assuming conditioning guaranteed it.
