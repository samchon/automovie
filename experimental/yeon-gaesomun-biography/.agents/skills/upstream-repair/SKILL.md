# Upstream repair

Identify the earliest owner, actual consumers, current pass and closed boundaries before changing the work. Repair facts, contradictions or unworkable design at that construction owner, then change downstream bodies only where they consume the changed value. Keep an expression-fidelity repair in its working edition against the unchanged frozen original.

Every author reading, evidence correction, review and author response follows [complete cause rounds](cause-rounds.md). Finish an inspection before editing, repair the complete affected cause and its open-pass answers together, then perform the next complete inspection.

Use [closed passes and replacement](closed-passes.md) when a repair touches completed layers, transferred bodies or units replaced from scratch. Use [scope expansion](scope-expansion.md) when expanding a commissioned pilot. [Evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md) owns selection; [narrative completion](../narrative-completion/SKILL.md) owns completion and initial expression transfer. Changed media and execution still need [delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md) within the affected output scope.
