# Production

Read the request, project-root [PROJECT.md](../../../PROJECT.md), current source and outputs. Record adopted scope, fixed choices, available inputs, target environment and setup authority through [production decisions](profile.md). A screenplay, whole motion hint, finished direct 3D film and post-diffusion film have different delivery boundaries. Resolve routine choices within the user's authority.

Use [screenwriting](../screenwriting/SKILL.md) for narrative planning, writing and revision. An asset-only task has no narrative pipeline. The requested output determines which asset and [delivery questions](../contracts/delivery.md) apply; [contracts](../contracts/SKILL.md) owns their adoption and [evidence configuration](../evidence-configuration/SKILL.md) owns technical graph changes.

For commissioned video, [motion](../motion/SKILL.md) programs the main scenes' performance and staging and assembles the complete timeline. Simple figures and coarse objects can carry it; choose representations through [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md) and commissioned sound through [audio](../audio/SKILL.md). A motion hint needs no finished diffusion film, and a requested direct 3D film needs no diffusion step. Stills and pilot clips cannot complete a whole-video commission.

Read [layout and intake](layout.md) when adapting supplied material or choosing working files. Implement through [development](../development/SKILL.md), proving a representative execution and media path before multiplying it. Finish source [review](../review/SKILL.md) and [delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md) of the actual commissioned output, disclosing unobserved stages.
