# Transfer reviewed expression

[Rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md) owns the frozen construction and matching working edition, with [cycle rules](../rewriting/cycles.md) for their comparison. Finish the whole working version while its pass is disabled, including the author's complete cause-based inspection. Open evidence only when complete, attach answers and rewriting notes in comments, then submit the working version and frozen construction to independent review.

After the [closing round](../review/rounds.md), transfer every supplied working file to its actual `docs/body` counterpart with HTML comments removed and every other byte preserved. Supplied navigation indexes participate; absent optional indexes need no fabricated copies. Pending initial transfer prevents downstream consumption and completion even when the working edition is ready to reread.

Retain the working edition, its evidence, frozen original, findings and author decisions. Construction evidence, review and approval remain closed against the frozen construction; report construction diagnostics without rewriting answers or renewing its approval against transferred expression. An unchanged working edition keeps its naturalness review.

Exact equality with the comment-free reviewed working edition governs this initial transfer. Subsequent semantic body repairs follow [closed-pass repair](../upstream-repair/closed-passes.md); preserve their repair instead of transferring the old working edition over it. A new construction starts a new cycle and receives its own evidence and review.
