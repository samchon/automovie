# Yeon Gaesomun

This English document edition translates the Korean adaptation. Read the final audiovisual screenplay in [docs/body/screenplay](docs/body/screenplay). Its supporting canon, treatments and executable scripts remain in [docs/body](docs/body); creative requirements are in [docs/instructions](docs/instructions).

The [project brief](PROJECT.md) owns the document scope, and the [translation checks](docs/translation/validation.md) record its verification and limits. The [source manifest](docs/translation/korean-source-manifest.json) identifies the exact Korean input captured before translation, and the [source archive](docs/translation/korean-source.zip) preserves its original bytes, including line endings. The baseline commit preserves the readable Korean edition. Retained references and source comments remain historical material; their Korean approvals do not approve the English edition.

This is a document translation commission. It has no runnable production package, rendered video, soundtrack or measured screen time. Historical source-analysis links may refer to records retained in the original writing workspace rather than this translation branch.

Further planning, writing, expression revision and narrative evidence use the [screenwriting task guide](.agents/skills/screenwriting/SKILL.md). Independent review, contract questions and graph configuration retain their own roles.
