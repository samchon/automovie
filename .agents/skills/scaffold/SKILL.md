# Maintaining the scaffold

The scaffold supplies project-owned instructions, contracts and configuration. [Ownership](../../../packages/template/scaffold/README.md#ownership) defines placement; [Static-document updates](../../../packages/template/scaffold/README.md#static-document-updates) defines adoption.

The shipped skill is [3D modeling](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/3d-modeling/SKILL.md). Runtime APIs and graph mechanics are documented by their installed packages.

## Shared inventory

Contract family/domain filenames and ordered H2 anchors are pinned by `@automovie/evidence`. Change targets, selectors, citations, inventory and consumers coherently; do not weaken a population to hide debt. Keep reusable targets independent of a production's content.

Maps own broad-world content and site interfaces; spaces own building/site topology inside them. Preserve distinct design and source ownership.

## Verification

Run applicable package build/evidence and pure tests. Prove changed graph refusals with authorized negative inputs and restore them. For changed installation behavior, observe a generated consumer and verify its installed entry point, preserved content and repeatable adoption. Protect other owners' files and processes.

Use [documentation](../documentation/instructions.md) for instruction edits, [evidence graph](../evidence-graph/SKILL.md) for repository citations and [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md#visual-debugging) for visual changes.
