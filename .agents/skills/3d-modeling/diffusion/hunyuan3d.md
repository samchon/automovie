# Hunyuan3D-2.1

Open weights; later versions are hosted only. Values were read from the linked documentation in November 2026; it owns them, so read it again when choosing.

- **Input:** one image. Its app removes the background of an image without transparency and centers the subject with a margin on a 512 px square, so a cut-out subject is kept as given.
- **Hardware:** about 10 GB of GPU memory for shape, 21 GB for texture and 29 GB for both.
- **Beyond the mesh:** material channels; its own pipeline removes floating pieces and reduces the faces.
- **License:** it does not apply in the European Union, the United Kingdom or South Korea, needs a separate license above 1 million monthly active users, and forbids using the output to improve other AI models.
