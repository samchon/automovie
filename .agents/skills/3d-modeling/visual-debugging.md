# Visual Debugging

Readiness or numerical success proves no appearance; only an observed render does.

## Viewer

Recover a broken video or 3D viewer before other work. Confirm the current displayed artifact and relevant controls, seek/playback and capture through the actual consumer, and protect other owners' processes meanwhile. Repair missing engine, interface, viewer or render capability at its reusable owner, then reconnect the production and apply explicit instruction sync for changed guidance; a build or server response alone does not prove recovery.

Inspect the actual consumer with current source on a hardware renderer, recording source, input, renderer, and view.

## Correction Rounds

Before editing, inspect the whole affected asset at every scale, moving the camera freely through whole views, regions, neighbors and close-ups from every needed direction, and write down every observed defect with its construction and comparison conditions. Trace causes through generation and display, including shared boundaries and derived outputs. Where causes or their interactions are unclear, plan the controlled comparisons that separate them as one set, run the whole set without changing the source, and record the results. Map each proposed change to all defects and parts it affects.

Fix all identified causes in one combined change at their sources, and judge it on the assembled asset. A cause that crosses a part boundary is changed by both owners in the same round. Editing after each observed defect, changing one value and rendering to see its effect, rendering after each single fix, and leaving a traced cause to a later round or finer scale are the whack-a-mole patterns this procedure replaces, however cheap a render is.

Rerun the original comparison across every affected part, view, identity, resolution and motion state, checking both the recorded defects and regressions. Account for every recorded defect before accepting the round; new or unexplained defects keep it open.

## Coverage

Front and side alone hide oblique sections and junctions, so inspect:

- every direction, back quarters and high and low obliques included;
- each defect with a free camera, framing its close-up on the affected region from outside the model;
- assembled neighbors and distance;
- the motion states: rest, intermediate, supported extremes, return, and shape-motion combinations.

## Construction

Judge a surface only with the construction that generated it, because the surface shows the symptom and the construction shows the value that causes it:

- a curve network's sections, guides, and cage;
- code CAD's sketches, axes, and boolean operands;
- a procedural rule's skeleton;
- an implicit surface's primitives;
- a field's defining curves.

Every set of views, whole sweeps and each defect's close-up alike, shows the same cameras three times: surface, construction over the surface, and construction alone.

## Light And Material

Judge form under raking light with fine material detail such as normal maps turned off, and judge materials and lighting in beauty. Use structural passes only for the quantities they document.

## Confirmation

Compare the render with the engine result to separate calculation from display faults, and compare before and after under matching conditions.

After each round's combined correction, render again and look yourself. Stale, blank, or unobserved output stays unverified.
