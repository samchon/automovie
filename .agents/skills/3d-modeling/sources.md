# Sources

Adopt an existing asset when the shot needs it or the user requests it. Follow [Conditioning](conditioning.md).

## Channels

### Poly Haven

[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) offers photoreal models, HDRIs and textures under CC0, with a [public API](https://api.polyhaven.com) for scripted fetching. Its HDRIs suit environment light; its models are fewer, so expect common props and furniture rather than a specific subject.

### ambientCG

[ambientCG](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) offers PBR materials and HDRIs under CC0, for surfaces on crafted or recovered geometry rather than meshes.

### Kenney

[Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/category:3D) offers consistent low-poly kits and characters under CC0, whose coarse, uniform style fits identifiable stand-ins and fast blocking.

### codepawl GLB repacks

[codepawl](https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/abo-glb) repacks Amazon Berkeley Objects (`abo-glb`), Google Scanned Objects (`scanned-objects-glb`) and NVIDIA SimReady (`simready-glb`) as real-scale GLB files. For CC BY 4.0 attribution, credit the original creator.

### Quixel Megascans

[Quixel Megascans](https://www.fab.com/sellers/Quixel%20Megascans) offers photoscanned assets, surfaces and vegetation with a Fab account. The [Fab Standard License](https://www.fab.com/eula) allows commercial use and modification. Keep source scans out of public commits and simplify where detail is unnecessary.

### 100STYLE

[100STYLE](https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/) offers stylized locomotion motion capture in BVH under CC BY 4.0, as a gait or style reference to retarget or recover, with its credit.
