# Sources

An existing asset is a source beside crafting and generation. Propose, fetch or adopt one when a shot needs it or the user asks, and take it in under [Conditioning](conditioning.md), which decides whether it is used as supplied, rigged, varied by a parameter or recovered.

The channels below are examples, not a complete list; search further for the subject at hand.

## Poly Haven

[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) offers photoreal models, HDRIs and textures under CC0, with a [public API](https://api.polyhaven.com) for scripted fetching. Its HDRIs suit environment light; its models are fewer than its HDRIs and textures, so expect common props and furniture rather than a specific subject.

## ambientCG

[ambientCG](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) offers PBR materials and HDRIs under CC0. Use it for surfaces on crafted or recovered geometry, not for meshes.

## Kenney

[Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/category:3D) offers consistent low-poly kits and characters under CC0. Their coarse, uniform style fits identifiable stand-ins and fast blocking.

## codepawl GLB repacks

The [codepawl datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/abo-glb) on Hugging Face repack Amazon Berkeley Objects (`abo-glb`), Google Scanned Objects (`scanned-objects-glb`) and NVIDIA SimReady (`simready-glb`) as GLB under the originals' CC BY 4.0, so each use credits the original creator, not the repacker. These are real household and product objects at real-world scale, downloadable in bulk.

## Quixel Megascans

[Quixel Megascans](https://www.fab.com/sellers/Quixel%20Megascans) on Fab offers free photoscanned 3D assets, surfaces and vegetation under the [Fab Standard License](https://www.fab.com/eula): commercial use and modification in any tool, but no standalone redistribution, so the files stay out of public commits. Downloads need a Fab account. The scans are dense; choose a lower level of detail or simplify when an identifiable form is enough.

## 100STYLE

[100STYLE](https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/) offers stylized locomotion motion capture in BVH under CC BY 4.0. Use it as a gait or style reference to retarget or recover, with its credit.
