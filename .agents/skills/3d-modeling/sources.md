# Sources

An existing asset is a source beside crafting and generation. Use it as supplied when it serves the shot, or [recover](recovery.md) its curves when the shot needs dimensions, deformation or controls the asset cannot give. Propose, fetch or adopt one when a shot needs it or the user asks.

Adopt an asset only when its licence permits everything the production does with it: commercial release, modification, rendering into conditioning and redistributing the committed bytes. Terms as permissive as MIT or Apache, such as CC0 and CC BY 4.0, pass. Read the licence of the item itself when fetching it, because one channel can mix terms and change them later, and record its source, licence, required credit and content digest in the project's media record.

These channels are representative examples, not a complete or required list. Search further for the subject at hand and apply the same licence test to any other source.
