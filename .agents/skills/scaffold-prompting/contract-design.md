# Contract design

The production's [contracts](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/contracts/SKILL.md) define respondents. See [adoption](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/evidence/adoption.md) for adding requirements and [configuration](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/evidence/configuration.md) for selection.

Follow [items and statements](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/contracts/items.md#items-and-statements). Group each family by owner. Confirm that `@automovie/evidence` selectors reach items and exclude statements.

Keep H2 anchors stable. When an item or source folder moves, update callers, selectors and respondents together. Verify changed obligations afresh within the production's [closed-pass rules](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).

Record production criteria's sources and limits in [research routes](research-routes.md). Keep project-specific criteria local. Record unverified quality claims as candidates.
