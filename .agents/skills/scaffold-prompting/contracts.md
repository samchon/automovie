# Design the quality questions

Ask what the respondent can establish with its actual work. Use the scaffold's [adoption guide](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/evidence/adoption.md) for production requirements and [graph configuration](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/evidence/configuration.md) for selection and ownership. A scene can establish its experience; cross-scene continuity needs the relevant population, and a rendered-output promise needs the actual output observations.

## Scope and failure

Write an intelligible question, an observable failure and the legitimate neighboring form it protects. State whether listed failures are examples of a broader achievement or an exhaustive judgment boundary, so review cannot silently turn taste into a new requirement. A broad question still needs the passage or output, context and concrete recipient loss supporting its finding.

Judge sufficient and excessive detail in the same achievement. More explanation, geometry, emphasis or variation can displace the required action or experience. An individual function, compliance elsewhere or a paraphrase of intended meaning cannot excuse a different failure in the actual expression. Protection must answer that same alleged failure; preserve useful execution notation, deliberate stillness and adopted stylization where they work.

Keep an overall achievement independently answerable when detailed negatives cannot establish it. Reuse a question that already covers the loss before adding another. Split only when the evidence, scope or repair responsibility differs; a new sentence mold is not a new family.

## Coherent repair and adoption

Compare proposed questions with existing ones for overlap, contradiction and feasible joint satisfaction. Trace a failing case and a similar legitimate case through judgment and repair. Check that preserving meaning does not freeze defective wording, that removing a failure does not delete setup or promised tone, and that correcting expression does not invent new facts or events.

Keep stable family paths and explicit H2 identities where Evidence addresses them. Change callers, selected respondents and actual dependencies with a moved question; its existing answers and approvals do not certify a changed obligation. Follow the production's [closed-pass boundary](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes), preserving historical proof and unresolved diagnostics.

Keep the reason for a failure or protection in the text the recipient reads. Retain verified source support and limits in the appropriate source record or comment, without assuming the materializer strips comments or provides a novel-style migration tool. Shared criteria must work across subjects; local observations remain local until their proposed generalization is supported.

Distinguish clarified ownership, a corrected contradiction or a direct user requirement from a new claim that a writing or production prescription improves quality. Record an unverified prescription as a candidate rather than silently make it mandatory. Authoring and actual recipient observations establish any claimed effect under [verification](verification.md).
