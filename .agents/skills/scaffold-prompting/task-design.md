# Task design

A shipped task skill tells an author how to reach a commissioned result. Work out from the actual request and current work who receives the result, what it must achieve and what makes it usable at the next stage. An executable scene, a final audiovisual plan and a rendered performance serve different recipients even when they realize the same event.

## Creation and judgment

Give the author the achievement, the decisions that make it possible and what the next stage may decide afresh, rather than a list of symptoms to avoid, because writing only to avoid failures can remove motivation, humor, rhythm or the work's promised form. Detailed failures guide diagnosis after creation, in the contracts that [contract design](contract-design.md) shapes.

Judge three things apart, because none proves another: whether the content is true and preserved, whether its expression makes the intended experience, and how that experience is spread across the whole work. A passage's story function can justify keeping information whose language, performance, timing or image-sound relation still needs rewriting. Neither that function, compliance elsewhere nor a paraphrase of the intended meaning excuses a failure in the actual expression. A style label alone does not prove a failure.

After detailed repairs, read the complete result to check that the goal and the achievements worth protecting survive together. The existing authors and reviewers make that judgment; a new approval layer would only duplicate them.

## Production handoff

Trace a proposed instruction through the production's actual handoff: the brief or parent layers it starts from, the author's decision, the contract respondent, the repair owner, and the stage that consumes the result. The production's [writing workflow](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/story/workflow/SKILL.md), [source layout](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/development/source-layout.md) and [delivery review](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/review/delivery-review.md) set those boundaries.

A changed instruction must still let the whole motion hint pass through coded performance, camera staging, the complete edit and the observed output, with identities, contacts, timing and requested controls kept on the coarse forms the [product contract](../project/SKILL.md#coarse-models-detailed-motion) sets.

Judge audiovisual expression as a plan at screenplay time and as the captured and encoded result at delivery, where the user hears its sound; neither observation establishes the other.

A repair stays inside the work's route and adopted form. It never makes a new gesture, close-up, cut, narration or realism compulsory to escape a wording defect, because a silent hint, documentary, abstract short or asset may keep its form.

Only installed APIs and actual execution provide controls; research or an instruction alone cannot.
