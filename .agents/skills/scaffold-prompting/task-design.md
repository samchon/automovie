# Task design

A shipped task skill tells an author how to reach a commissioned output. Start from the user's goal and the failure the change must fix, and work out who receives the result and what makes it usable at the next stage: an executable scene, a final audiovisual plan and a rendered performance serve different recipients even when they realize the same event.

## The failure first

Decide whether the agent missed an existing rule or the rules permit the failure. When a contract item already covers it, repair how that item is applied and answered; change the design only when the needed judgment is absent or contradictory. Compliant records do not substitute for a usable film, so trace each observed failure to its repair owner.

Borrow from other crafts only what fits film production; a serial novel's prose interiority, reading rhythm and publishing steps do not.

## Creation and judgment

Give the author the achievement, the decisions that make it possible and what the next stage may decide afresh, not a list of symptoms to avoid, because writing to avoid failures removes motivation, humor, rhythm or the promised form. Detailed failures belong to the contracts, where they guide diagnosis after creation.

Judge three things apart, because none proves another: whether the content is true and preserved, whether its expression makes the intended experience, and how that experience spreads across the whole work. A passage's story function can justify keeping information whose expression still needs rewriting, but neither that function nor compliance elsewhere excuses the expression. Naming a style, such as "too literary", is not a failure.

A repair stays inside the work's route and adopted form. It never requires a new gesture, close-up, cut, narration or realism to escape a wording defect, because a silent hint, a documentary, an abstract short or an asset may keep its form.

## Production handoff

Trace an instruction through the actual handoff: the parent it starts from, the author's decision, the contract respondent, the repair owner and the stage that consumes the result. The production's [writing workflow](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/story/workflow/SKILL.md), [source layout](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/development/source-layout.md) and [delivery review](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/.agents/skills/review/delivery-review.md) set those boundaries.

No change may break the path from coded performance through camera staging and the complete edit to the observed output. Identities, contacts, timing and requested controls stay carried by the coarse forms, and only installed APIs and actual execution provide them; research or an instruction alone cannot.

Audiovisual expression is judged as a plan at screenplay time and as the captured and encoded result at delivery, where the user hears its sound; neither observation establishes the other.

## The default path

A production's default is to compose what its installed packages provide: the polygon man, the archetypes and the motion vocabulary, as its [principles](../../../packages/template/scaffold/instructions/AGENTS.md#principles) set. Every task page states that path first, as prompting's [default route](../prompting/SKILL.md#default-route) requires.

A route that leaves it, such as a commissioned detail option, a registered law or a simulated response, sits behind the condition that opens it. A page that describes such a route in more detail than the default sends every author down it.

A source folder, command or stage the default path does not need is named as conditional, because an author treats a listed folder as one to fill.

## Revising a page

Revise a page as [Writing](../documentation/writing.md#revising) sets. After detailed repairs, read the complete result to check that the goal and the achievements worth protecting survive together. The existing authors and reviewers make that judgment, and a new approval layer would only duplicate them.
