# Design the task strategy

Identify the task's recipient, needed achievement and usable handoff from the actual request and current work. Give the author choices that lead toward that result rather than a list of symptoms to avoid. An executable scene, final audiovisual plan and rendered performance serve different recipients even when they realize the same event.

Keep the entry point short enough to reveal its task, goal, authority and next resource. Put a branch behind its actual trigger; always-needed decisions stay together. Move callers and references with a renamed resource so installed agents can still reach it. Production entry points must not depend on repository skills that will not ship.

## Connect creation to judgment

Describe the achievement, the decisions that make it possible and what the next owner may choose afresh. Connect it to [contract design](contracts.md) without restating the questions. Detailed failures guide diagnosis after creation; writing only to avoid them can remove motivation, humor, rhythm or the work's promised form.

Separate content being true or preserved, expression making the intended experience, and the distribution of that experience across the whole. None proves the others. A function may justify keeping information while its actual language, performance, timing or image-sound relation still needs rewriting. Conversely, a stylistic label does not establish a failure.

Read the complete result after detailed repairs to check whether the goal and protected achievements survive together. Let the existing authoring and review owners perform that judgment; adding a new approval layer would duplicate responsibility rather than improve the work.

## Follow the production handoff

Trace a proposed instruction through the actual brief or parents, author's decision, contract respondent, repair owner and next consumer. Use the production's [writing workflow](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/screenwriting/workflow.md), [execution traceability](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/evidence/execution.md) and [delivery review](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/review/delivery.md) for those boundaries.

A whole hint must still reach coded performance, usable camera staging, the complete edit and observed output. Preserve identities, contacts, timing and requested controls at low cost before detail upgrades. Inspect audiovisual expression as a plan at screenplay time and the actual captured, heard and encoded result at media delivery; neither observation establishes the other.

Keep route and fidelity constraints compatible with repair. Do not make new gestures, close-up shots, cuts, narration or realism compulsory to escape a wording defect. A silent hint, documentary, abstract short or asset can use its adopted form. Installed APIs and actual execution determine available controls; research and an instruction alone cannot provide them.
