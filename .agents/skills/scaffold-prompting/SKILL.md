# Scaffold prompting

Design task guidance and its quality questions around the same commissioned result. A task skill gives the goal, priority judgments, necessary order and freedom to create. Its contracts test the resulting work at the scope that can establish it. Connect the observed failure back to the author or production owner that can repair it; compliant records cannot substitute for a usable film.

Apply [prompting](../prompting/SKILL.md) and the documentation [writing rules](../documentation/writing.md). Use [task design](design.md) when changing strategy, responsibility or routing, [contract design](contracts.md) when changing a question or its judgment boundary, and [verification](verification.md) before reporting the result.

Start with the user's goal and the actual authoring or production failure. Distinguish a rule the agent missed from a result the current rules permit. Repair application and evidence when the existing question already covers the loss; change the design when the needed judgment is absent or contradictory. A new symptom does not automatically need a new question.

Keep the scaffold production-neutral and usable without repository operating skills. Adopt work-specific genre, language, voice and delivery decisions in the production's own instructions, project context or work contracts. Upstream novel workflows can inform ownership and review, but prose interiority, mobile reading, serial rewards and their distribution commands do not define film production.

The [project contract](../project/SKILL.md) fixes AutoMovie's purpose. Preserve its complete deterministic motion hint, with separately commissioned screenplay, asset, direct 3D film and generated film routes. Use the production [task router](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/production/SKILL.md) and [screenwriting router](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/screenwriting/SKILL.md) to find actual owners rather than copy their layer definitions here.

Retain the [upstream notice](../../../packages/template/scaffold/.agents/skills/LICENSE.novels) when adapting its material. Use [primary research routes](references.md) to find relevant originals and record their support and limits; a source's existence or another medium's successful example does not establish improvement here.

Hand the change to [scaffold](../scaffold/SKILL.md) for distribution and project ownership checks, and implementation to [development](../development/SKILL.md). This skill adds no creative pass, graph stage, publication authority or automatic migration of existing productions.
