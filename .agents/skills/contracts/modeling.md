# Modeling Principles

These chapters add to the common chapters for declarations that define, build, or measure a form, human or not.

## Part Identity and Grouping

A part is the smallest piece with its own identity, such as a door or a bone, and has one named declaration. A group owns its members and their order, copies no member's shape or values, and lets a change to one part reach its neighbors only through shared boundaries. State the part or group, its members, and why the division falls there, or why one declaration holding several parts cannot be split.

## Parameter Channels

- Each channel varies one named trait that no other channel varies; document independent controls and intentional coupling.
- A normalized offset is zero at its declared neutral with a stated positive direction. Absolute measurements keep their units and observed grades their protocol, and one declaration owns each conversion.
- Paired features carry left and right values with an explicit neutral and symmetry rule.

State each channel's trait, neutral, positive direction, pair representation, and dependence on other channels.

## Emitted Geometry

Derive the emitted primitives from the representation the form requires, using a parametric surface, subdivision, card, instance, or level of detail where the form allows, so counts grow with resolution parameters rather than with authored features. State the representation, the parameters that set each count, the counts at the supported extremes, and why no smaller representation suffices.

## Spatial Conventions

Keep one unit and one frame, with one handedness, axis order, and origin, inside a declaration, and convert only where units or frames meet, as a named step with one owner. State the unit, frame, origin, and axes of each input and output, and the conversions at the boundary.

## Shared Boundaries

Build a boundary between parts from one definition both sides share, so they meet without a gap, an overlap, or, where the form is smooth, a jump in the normal, under every admitted configuration of both parts. State the boundaries built or consumed, the shared definition, the continuity it guarantees, and each configuration in which the join opens.

## Rendered Observation

- The owner of a part, a group, or a joint observes the current final output under the 3D modeling skill's visual debugging procedure; a transport or numerical helper has no whole-render obligation.
- Cover opposing views, rest, intermediate states, supported extremes, return, and shape-motion combinations, close and at use distance, in assembled context with beauty or clay and the structural pass that exposes the suspected failure.
- Record the source revision, inputs, views, and limits in the owning issue or review. Missing, stale, or occluded output stays unverified.

State the observation responsibility and the supported result, with accepted defects as named ceilings.
