# Variation

Two takes of one action differ and a novice differs from an expert, yet the same take replays exactly. Parameters, skill and seed give all three.

## One law, many performances

A different performance of an action, such as a tired walk or a second take, is the same law at other parameters, another skill or another seed. A copied law with edited constants is a variant the next repair does not reach. When a performance needs something the law cannot express, give the law that parameter with the study that bounds it.

## Skill

Express skill as a value from novice to expert, and let each quantity of an action move between the novice and expert values its study measured, such as a phase duration or a scatter. Each family of actions takes these values from its own studies, because another family's numbers give a walker a boxer's timing. Name a quantity with no located study as provisional.

Skill changes how well the same action is done. It never widens a range of motion and never changes an outcome.

## Variability across tasks

Scale each draw's spread by two regularities that hold across tasks. The variance of a timed interval is a constant motor part plus a part that grows with the interval (Wing and Kristofferson 1973). Endpoint scatter grows with the distance moved and shrinks with the movement time (Schmidt et al. 1979), because motor noise grows with the size of the command (Harris and Wolpert 1998).

## Independent draws

Take each random draw from a stream keyed by the seed and by stable identities: the performer, the action, its instance and the quantity drawn. Adding, removing or reordering one action then leaves every other draw unchanged, where a single stream consumed in order changes the whole film.

A seed varies the motion inside the bounds its study gives. It never chooses an outcome, nor how many members of a group an outcome takes: the caller gives that count, or a studied rate the code turns into a count, and the seed chooses only which members.
