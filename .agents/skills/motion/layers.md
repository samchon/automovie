# Layer what runs over every action

Gaze, breathing and displays of feeling run on top of whatever action the body performs. A layer adds its contribution to the action's pose. It never starts, cuts or ends an action, so an action plays the same with or without it.

A layer leaves every contact goal untouched, because planted feet and gripping hands are solved from those goals and must not slide. It uses the joints the action leaves free, such as the eyes, the head and the chest, or adds small offsets to the trunk that the [contact fits](authoring.md#order-the-fits) then absorb.

A layer that begins, ends or changes its target blends from where it is, never from neutral, so the head does not snap. Its timing and amplitude come from its own study and vary under [skill and seed](variation.md) like any action's.
