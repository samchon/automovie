# Construction

## Form

- Build both sides through one canonical construction and a declared reflection, including boundary maps, derivatives and orientation.
- Build each anatomical part as an independently deformable module in its own frame under a common body frame, joined to its neighbours by a declared composition rule. Keep common mathematics in one shared module, and give each shared boundary one owner that its neighbour copies.
- Loft each limb and digit on its own bone.
- Divide and conquer: a layer need not be one ring drawn at once. Split a part by sub-part, sector of its girth, layer span and axis, only along named anatomical loci, as finely as makes each piece's rule simple to estimate, and build each piece by its own rule in its own frame, so it can be built, checked and transformed alone.
- Join pieces only through the shared composition module: each shared boundary has one owner that publishes it with a declared continuity, and the neighbour copies it. A piece that invents its own join, or fades a mismatch, breaks the merged skin at its seam; never close a junction by a global fit or a smoothing pass.
- Give every piece its own file, named for its part and piece, so it is found, read, tested and replaced alone; a part's file only composes its pieces, and shared mathematics stays in the shared modules.
- Emit `IAutoMovieHumanLoftChain` under the existing chain names, so the body surface, event joins, skin, appearance and construction keep working.
- Keep the section representation until a part shows it cannot carry the law; a new representation spreads into joins, caps, normals and sampling.
- Make skin smooth through its generating mathematics alone.
- Keep full skin, the generation path, rigs, export and other consumers working at every commit.
- Leave genitals and nipples out of the modelled skin.

## Numbers

- Reduce independent numbers while keeping curve and section density at or above the current figure.
- Hold shape priors as named constants in normalized part coordinates, shared by every person.
- Give every constant a published source, a population measurement or a derivation, and turn a value repeated across features into the rule that produces it.
- When an input or its definition changes, replace a prior fitted to it by the definition, and solve the measure against the construction's own surface.

## Inputs

- Construct and measure each input at its published site and by its published definition: ISO 8559-1 and ANSUR II for the body, Farkas for the head.
- Check an input against its definition and its population relations before relying on it, and correct a wrong input at the input.

## Evidence

- Read every difference from a reference or an earlier figure as evidence for or against a law.
- Fix every defect you find, whatever an earlier figure had.
- Use reference meshes and curves as observations for rules, and judge appearance against the original [photographs](screenshot.md#references).
- Generalize a law only from people held out of its fit.
