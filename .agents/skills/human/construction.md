# Construction

## Tree

- Build the whole figure as one construction tree of the interface's construction types (`packages/interface/src/construction`). The engine's `realizeAutoMovieConstruction` realizes it once into chains, joins, trims and one skin. The Human package derives the nodes from the person's inputs and laws, and reads what the realization publishes. Lofting, joining, trimming and tessellation belong to the engine, so every person and every other form share one tested kernel.
- Compose compatible original profiles and section pieces in one declared physical plane and frame before lofting. Their guides, extrema, fullness, anatomical controls and measurement constraints remain explicit, so the generated surface traces back to those definitions. A logical anatomical part need not be an independent loft or solid.
- Choose the operator from that source domain under [Connections](#connections). Continue compatible regular open ends, connect declared original ports by their admitted bands, and retain a diff or trim for genuine removed volume or an opening. A volume merge is used only where the active construction retains it. Derive blend widths from anatomy or source curvature, and preserve the logical names through a mapping to actual subdomains and charts.
- Derive ports, retained join seeds, trim landmarks and regional domains from shared landmark curves, courses and the bind skeleton in relative coordinates, so each follows the person's edits. Do not rediscover a known original connection by intersecting two already lofted surfaces.
- Take a form the tree cannot express to the kernel as a general capability. Design it in the interface and self-review it, then implement it in the engine with its operator tests before any part uses it, because a part-local workaround forks the kernel and escapes its tests.
- Publish the realized chains and body as the one source for every stage after the skin (rig, binding, appearance, garments), because a second body drifts from the first and doubles the work.

## Connections

Apply [Modularization](../3d-modeling/modularization.md#frames-and-connections) for general ownership and coordinate rules. Before lofting, one generating owner supplies the common section/control family or both complete original port curves and their transverse data. Declare the physical domains, station/phase coordinates, frames, units and direction/scale maps, with the available physical order 0/1/2, phase, mixed, frame and lift derivatives and boundary relief. Capturing a boundary from an already generated chain cannot alone establish original ownership. A shared centre or four edge values leave independent interiors that can separate when a dimension changes.

Read the parent's generating section at the actual attachment station and derive the contact chord at the actual position across that section. A whole-region support maximum, an independently estimated radius or depth, reverse measurement of the output mesh, or a later fit is no substitute for that local boundary. Public measurements may constrain the source but do not themselves define its contact geometry.

Keep both production consumers connected to that same generating owner; declaring a helper or sharing only placement data leaves separate shape definitions. Preserve an already continuous source family across its anatomical names. An interval adapter retains the original shape controls, fullness and available derivatives through its declared transforms. Mark a derivative the owner cannot provide as unavailable; zero would assert geometry the source has not supplied.

Separate interpolation support from the part's actual physical domain. Support knots and parameter endpoints may remain outside a restricted domain to preserve its original functions. Lofting and display consume the declared domain. Removing support and reinterpolating, coarsening one side, or changing valid inputs to satisfy connection admission changes the geometry and cannot serve as an equivalence proof.

For a shared measurement or attachment plane, author its complete section family before the differently oriented constituents consume it. Reuse a solve or cut only while its actual source inputs and trial state are invariant. A candidate that changes parent values, frames or jets needs its own consuming source; a dependency cycle remains a coupled solve. Keep a tape hull that bridges concavities distinct from the coplanar union boundary. Projection loses depth, and agreeing at finitely many section planes does not prove equivalent solids between them.

A continuation needs two actual open ends and compatible whole boundary functions and jets under the declared maps. A side footprint, branch, disconnected section or hole requires its own supported domain construction. Preserve real recesses and openings; a common outward envelope cannot replace them. Confirm untouched valid intervals as well as any explicitly changed adjoining strips, because an endpoint copy can deform a previously valid source.

A known port declares its boundary, material coordinates, orientation, correspondence, neighboring source strips and available transverse derivatives before either consumer is lofted. Its band consumes the actual owned neighboring source fields through the declared C2 chart maps, with regularity, continuity and failure admission in the engine. Sampled display curves, a refit and Taylor extrapolation of collected boundary jets cannot replace those fields. A branch remains a branch; a port does not turn it into a one-to-one continuation or prove its topology by a finite set of coincident samples.

Merge and diff combine defined volumes; they do not establish shared original ownership or repair independent boundaries. Verify the source connection with [Shared Connections](verification.md#shared-connections), then judge the actual band, continuation, trim or retained Boolean separately, so an operator failure never justifies distorting valid anatomical inputs.

## Parametric Identity And Binding

Keep logical anatomical names, source controls, generated streams and surface charts as distinct identities. Map each logical region to its actual source subdomain, ports and material charts; do not duplicate its geometry to preserve an old chain name. Local edits invalidate the true dependency graph. A coupled measurement may legitimately propagate to several regions, while unrelated valid source domains retain their promised behavior.

Keep semantic measurement constraints distinct from direct curve authority. A curve edit that changes a locked measurement needs its declared constraint solve or an explicit refusal; a coincident source address is not proof that the published measurement still holds.

Publish shared boundary bindings and material coordinates with the realized atlas. Every copy of a physical boundary consumes the same binding field through its declared chart map. Derive anatomical joint datums from the common source and relative skeleton, not the new tessellation or a surface-patch centre. Keep rest geometry, joint datums, bind transforms and inverse binds under explicit source dependencies, so an edited frame or anatomy cannot reuse a stale binding.

Coincident contact is not sewn identity. Upper and lower lips retain distinct material sheets and binding weights at their contact even when their rest positions agree; do not weld them by position or copy one sheet's weights to the other.

A topology or chart edit invalidates affected addresses and bindings; a changed rest skeleton also invalidates its inverse binds. Preserve an unaffected bone's identity when a surface is repartitioned. Verify the current neutral/bind consumers and their mappings in the active task; this contract alone does not start another pose or rigging feature.

## Form

- Author original curves through the [2D sketch and control workflow](curves.md), then keep that same source authoritative through composition and lofting.
- Build both sides through one canonical construction and a declared reflection, including boundary maps, derivatives and orientation.
- Keep anatomical controls and relative frames in named modules under a common body frame, with common mathematics in shared modules. Independent controls and deformation regions do not require independent lofts.
- Build limb and digit sources in their declared bone-relative frames. A continuous source may cross several bones while retaining each anatomical joint datum.
- Split source modules along anatomical responsibilities, and split surface domains where topology, continuity or the editing contract requires it. A smaller anatomical unit may remain an editable subdomain of one composed stream.
- Let one section contain several pieces or loops. Structures with different centres retain their source frames and explicit maps into a composed physical section. A single closed star-shaped contour cannot represent every branch, undercut, cavity or disconnected section.
- Differentiate only smooth functions, analytically or on a smooth fit; a finite difference across a clamp or a step throws the surface.
- Let a part or join that cannot keep its contract leave the tree with one diagnostic, as the realization rolls back a failed subtree. Generation then returns a figure for every valid person, and the diagnostic names the defect.
- Give every piece its own file, named for its part and piece, so it is found, read, tested and replaced alone; a part's file only composes its pieces, and shared mathematics stays in the shared modules.
- Choose a representation that carries the anatomy, topology, measurement and editing contracts. An exact adapter proves preservation of the original functions; a deliberately changed law or bounded approximation declares its basis and independent error instead of claiming byte or derivative identity. Preserve valid public meanings, not obsolete decomposition.
- Make smooth skin and junctions through their generating mathematics, and preserve declared creases. Apply C2 or G2 only on the domains that promise it; no global fit or smoothing pass hides a seam.
- Keep full skin, the generation path, rigs, export and other consumers working at every commit.
- Leave genitals and nipples out of the modelled skin.

## Numbers

- Reduce redundant independent numbers while retaining the curves, guides, sections and controls the anatomy needs. A sourced replacement may change their representation; fewer files, streams or patches alone do not prove a better construction, and a domain too sparse to carry its shape is raised.
- Hold a number that is a fact of the world as a named constant shared by every person: a definition, a unit factor, a published range, a cited anatomical ratio, or a shape prior in normalized part coordinates. Compute every value that must follow the person's inputs, so that no constant fixes one person's value.
- Give every constant and every curve a published source, a population measurement or a derivation, and state that reason in its doc comment, so a reader can judge it against the anatomy. Turn a value repeated across features into the rule that produces it.
- When an input or its definition changes, replace a prior fitted to it by the definition, and solve the measure against the construction's own surface.

## Inputs

- Construct and measure each input at its published site and by its published definition: ISO 8559-1 and ANSUR II for the body, Farkas for the head.
- Make every public parameter an anatomical measurement with a published definition, a unit and a population range, and replace a control without a definition, such as a blend slider, by the measure it stands for.
- Check an input against its definition and its population relations before relying on it, and correct a wrong input at the input.

## Evidence

- Read every difference from a reference or an earlier figure as evidence for or against a law.
- Resolve every finding in the active correction round's authorized scope together by cause, including inherited defects. Record other findings and keep their acceptance unclaimed.
- Use reference meshes and curves as observations for rules, and judge appearance against the original [photographs](screenshot.md#references).
- Check a law estimated from observed people on people outside its fit, and replace it with a published source when one is found, because an estimate reproduces the people it came from.
