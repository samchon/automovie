# Verification

## Seeing before believing

Open each frame that supports a shape finding beside its reference under matching camera, lighting and shade. Inspect the named region on both sides. Read measurements from their output files and trace the failing premise to its code and a passing control.

Inspect original curves, loft curves, clay and final skin at their own stages. Use the [shared instrument](screenshot.md) for pictures and [tests](testing.md) for measured contracts.

## Form before deviation

Preserve convexity, concavity, silhouette runs, muscle maxima and anatomical features. Each feature keeps its sign, landmark and at least half its reference depth. Unexpected lumps, level bands, steps, creases or dents fail the figure. Report deviation in each round to locate differences.

## Curves against the reference

Inventory each definition's owner, domain and frame map, including contributing pieces, loops, guides and ports. Verify curves before lofts. Record an unavailable reference comparison under [reference comparison](screenshot.md#reference-comparison).

1. Overlay each curve in its own front, side, top, back and relevant oblique views.
2. Inspect each part with its host, children and neighbours at their shared joins.
3. Inspect the whole figure for coverage and proportions.

Measure maximum and mean deviation in millimetres at stations, guides and section diagonals. Investigate differences beyond a few millimetres. Record each expected change before fixing all causes in one batch. Remeasure, and revisit the cause when the measurement misses its prediction.

If a law misses its reference, verify the definition, frame and population. Use the person's measured value by the published definition when needed. Reopen comparisons for neighbours affected by a changed host or frame. Take accepted originals through the [inspection round](#inspection-round) with their neighbours.

## Steps one and two

Use these gates for the first two [steps](SKILL.md#three-steps).

- Step 1. Match every overlay and compare each part's clay from standard and oblique views.
- Step 2. Return the rebased coordinates to the reference pose exactly.
- Both. Realize the joints and pass anatomical order and surface integrity checks.

Verify traced knots against reference artifacts such as hatching, holes or jagged slices. Before each fix, inventory whole-figure defects and their measured owners.

## Published values

In step 3, evaluate a published relation at the person's covariates or take the person's value by the published measurement definition. Record the input covariates and their source. A plain population mean describes the source population.

## Shared connections

Trace each [shared boundary](construction.md#connections) through both inputs and their raw and realized consumers. Check the complete boundary, its jets, frame maps, domains and neighbouring intervals.

For a port, overlay both sides' lines at the joint in every view. Inspect the realized band and neighbouring strips. A spanning gap or steep turn sends the repair back to the lines.

Take the admission checks from the engine operators. Check section star shape, conic layout, rings, band directions and correspondence before realization. Use the operator's units and bounds.

## Inspection round

Apply [visual debugging](../visual-debugging/SKILL.md) and [form inspection](../3d-modeling/inspection.md) to each affected part and join. Match camera, lighting, shade, size and backend to the earlier figure and reference.

1. For accepted originals, capture the [three passes](screenshot.md#the-three-passes) with matched cameras.
   - Check parts alone, with neighbours and assembled.
2. Check the whole person in clay and beauty, in neutral, bind and commissioned poses.
3. Inspect regions and joins in close-up.
   - Use face owner colours for ownership and faces shade for open or flipped surfaces.
4. Overdraw suspect surface lines with the source curves, sections and family axis.
5. Record each finding with its location, measured cause and owner.

## Review

Record the exact revision, region, interval and views inspected. Mark each result pass, fail or unobserved. Unexplained spikes, bulges, indentations and crossings remain open until resolved.

Build a baseline for originals, references, neighbour groups and the whole figure. Recapture changed originals and affected neighbours, adding views for unresolved questions. Batch findings by cause.

## Render audit

Capture after a generated output changes. Open new frames or the latest unreviewed frames if output is unchanged. At minimum capture the [three passes](screenshot.md#the-three-passes) from front, left and top without underwear or hair. Add other angles, close-ups and neighbour views as needed.

Save rounds in the Human package's ignored shots folder under [capture](../visual-debugging/capture.md#rounds). If capture fails, [restore the instrument](../visual-debugging/capture.md#restoring-the-instrument) before judging the affected output.
