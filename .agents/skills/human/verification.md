# Verification

Read this guide before implementation. Complete at least one full inspection round after each geometry or integration change, including in solo work. Report unavailable checks as unverified; do not claim visual acceptance without inspecting actual renders.

## Actual Generated Form

- Apply the [essential server rule](SKILL.md#human). Isolate heavy/failure-prone models in request/child execution; preserve the host and return explicit diagnostics.
- Bind source, emitted JS/maps, actual loaded imports, inputs, skin/construction and PNG bytes.
- Trace actual API/root consumption; unused code, empty requests and successful fits do not prove changed geometry.
- Separate acquisition, source-conditioned reproduction, common fit, integration, visual acceptance and prediction.

A stale ownership record for an already dead service never authorizes killing a live process. Use the single [web service](screenshot.md#human-url-catalog), not another viewer.

## Inspection Round

1. Compare all four specimens and independent generated results under the same camera, light, material, resolution and renderer backend.
2. Inspect whole body, multiple directions, enlarged defects and neighbors in surface, overlay and construction passes.
3. Show unattached regions separately as generated, integrated or not renderable; state missing anatomical close-ups.
4. Collect all defects and latent effects, trace their common causes, and correct them in one batch.
5. Repeat the complete affected population and views under [Visual Debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md); new/unexplained defects keep the round open.

Source-only overlays and bounds-relative tiles do not establish independent skin or dedicated close-ups. If no broad-form progress appears, reconsider correspondence and representation rather than increasing precision, layers or wrappers.

## Audit And User Reports

During authorized active modeling, deliver these reports directly to the user, including in solo work and when progress is unchanged. The implementer owns verification and reporting unless the user explicitly delegates them. The [explicit stop](SKILL.md#human) suspends captures and automatic resumption.

- Every fifteen minutes, inspect and report new f1/f2/m1/m2 whole-front and whole-back surface captures, eight photographs total.
- Extra details, one-person rotation and cache hits do not replace these eight.
- Check regional image inspection, actual code consumption, uncovered regions and violations; follow [Work Allocation](SKILL.md#work-allocation) for audit delegation.
- Preserve/open/display exact local PNGs under [Screenshot reporting](screenshot.md#user-facing-image-reports), with captured UTC, SHA and code/input/output binding.
- Distinguish capture, local-save and report times. Report defects, unsupported scope and remaining comparisons.
- On delay, report cause/current work/last actual capture immediately; never skip the report or regenerate old evidence under its original timestamp.

## Separate Qualifications

- Check independent input changes, interactions and restoration only within the user's authorized experiment scope; deferred experiments stay unperformed.
- Reproduction does not establish unseen-shape prediction.
- Human and Human-viewer tests must not be written, restored or run, including renamed/relocated substitutes.
