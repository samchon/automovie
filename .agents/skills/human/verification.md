# Verification

## Bind The Actual Result

Bind source bytes to emitted JavaScript and source maps, actual HTTP imports, resolved inputs, generated skin and construction, then capture hashes. A successful build, HTTP status or root URL does not prove that the displayed model uses current source. Record renderer, camera and material conditions, capture time and report time separately.

Recover a broken capture or viewer before further shaping. Isolate local failures as diagnostics with explicit partial status, exclude invalid primitives, and preserve usable controls or a clearly labelled previous valid display. That previous display is not evidence for changed source.

## Inspect The Whole Model

Use [Visual Debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md) for the author's actual-viewer inspection of the assembled model, construction, enlarged regions, neighbors and required directions. Check silhouette, proportions, volume, features, omitted or added surface, folds, orientation, seams and self-intersections before interpreting numerical residuals as progress.

For each new Human stage, inspect and attach one original-versus-independent-generated full-body comparison for each of the four fixed reference people under the same camera, material, lighting and resolution. Attach local images with absolute paths. Deliver the first normal capture immediately, then the completed comparisons; name each missing person's evidence and its owner rather than reusing an old image as new output.

Inspect regional methods after assembly as well as alone. Report actual-viewer coverage, numerical fit, generated domain coverage and visual quality as separate claims. A good curve fit or local seam does not establish a complete person.

## Correct By Cause

Finish the complete discovery round before editing: inspect observed defects and their latent effects on neighboring regions, shared boundaries, parameter responses and downstream consumers. Analyze their common causes through extraction, Hermite conversion, correspondence, rule identification, interpolation and display. Correct all identified causes together at their sources, then repeat the full affected inspection under matching conditions and account for regressions and new findings. Fixing only the first symptom or deferring a known finding is forbidden regardless of the reason; report such a violation under [Ownership And Supervision](SKILL.md#ownership-and-supervision). A combined correction does not promise perfection in one attempt; each new round must discover and resolve the remaining causes as a whole.

If two consecutive matching rounds do not improve the broad form, reconsider correspondence, hierarchy or representation before adding precision, layers, order or traits. Small numerical improvements do not justify continuing a visibly failed whole-body method.

When a method fails, stop local repairs that preserve its disproven premise. Revise the hypothesis, correspondence or representation, research alternative methods in primary literature and compare their assumptions with the observed cause before implementing the next coherent approach. Monkey patches, wrappers and repeated tolerance changes cannot substitute for that investigation.

## Check Parameter Meaning

Observe independent parameter changes, relevant interactions and return to the starting values on the actual generated curves and skin. Preserve physical meanings and shared boundaries throughout. Separate reproduction of the fixed references from prediction on reserved observations or unobserved combinations; do not infer population coverage from four people or synthetic samples of the same generator.

Keep unsupported regions and parameter responses explicit. Implementing a new formula, compiling it or fitting observations remains unverified visually until the actual corresponding display has been inspected.
