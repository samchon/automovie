# Verification

## Calibration Bodies

Calibrate and judge form on lean 20-year-olds: a slim, model-grade woman and man, and a low-fat muscular woman and man for muscle relief. Fat, sagging skin, and wrinkles hide wrong form, so other people only confirm the converter's range after these four pass.

## Judgment

Every Human part is generated from curves, so every judgment of it shows its [construction](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md#construction).

Apply [Correction Rounds](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md#correction-rounds) to the assembled calibration bodies and their nearest references.

Keep numerical, clinical, and visual judgments distinct.

When two consecutive correction rounds show no gain on matching renders, change the source, correspondence, or representation, not a solver or tolerance.
