# Human verification

## Form and measurements

Verify realistic skin through visual debugging. Personally open matched clay, curves over clay and close views of the generated part, its neighbours and the assembled figure. Inspect silhouettes, anatomical landmarks, recesses and contact folds. Record the exact revision, input, camera and reason for each decision. Use measurements at their anatomical sites and capture diagnostics to locate problems in those images. [Inspecting a form](../3d-modeling/inspection.md) · [Correction rounds](../visual-debugging/correction-rounds.md).

For Campaign 3, compare against the accepted local Campaign 2 F1 assembly in its accepted pose, with matched cameras and lighting. Capture neutral separately when the change affects a joint. Adopt a shape replacement only when it has a stronger scientific basis and visibly improves the changed form, while preserving the quality of its neighbours. Prefer fewer conic loft curves when those conditions hold. Exemplar differences are diagnostic observations. Judge the shape by its anatomy, silhouette, fold flow and shared boundaries. [Anatomical consistency](../../../packages/human/docs/testing/consistency.md).

## Shared connections

Inspect the original boundary and jets on both sides under their frame and parameter maps. Check the preserved adjacent domains and every applicable premise in the engine's admission path before the full fit. For a parameterized connection, render the ordinary case and the affected parameter's declared bounds through the same neighbouring owners. Inspect the realized connection with its neighbours through the same cameras. [Human connections](construction.md#connections).
