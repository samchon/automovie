# Verification

The [shared instrument](screenshot.md) finds defects and accepts a candidate, and the [tests](testing.md) confirm it, in the Human skill's [order](SKILL.md#order). A candidate needs both, because numbers alone do not establish a sound human shape and a still shows defects no test reads yet. Judge original 2D definitions, actual raw loft curves, their overlap/default clay, and final realized skin as separate stages. Each stage keeps its own failures and unverified work; a smooth downstream surface cannot accept a wrong original.

## Curves Against The Reference

A surface is only as right as its original 2D definitions, so complete their inspection before correcting its shape. The instrument draws no reference yet: until the reference is handed to it as a subject or as marks, a comparison against the reference is [unavailable evidence](screenshot.md#reference-comparison), recorded as such and never passed.

For a composed source, inspect its original contributing curves and physical-plane/frame maps, then the composed section/guide family and declared ports before judging its loft. Keep logical anatomical selections mapped to actual subdomains of that same source; a former part name does not require a duplicate independent loft. Record any source or port the current inspection consumer cannot yet display as unavailable.

1. Inventory every original definition by its stable identity, source owner, valid physical domain and frame/plane map. For the existing view primitive, inspect `front.pos`, `front.neg`, `side.pos`, `side.neg`, `axis` and each available `top.<k>` fullness ring. For a composed source, inventory and observe every contributing piece, loop, component, guide and port, including its actual endpoints, valid intervals and correspondence; four supports cannot replace that inventory. Keep rejected or missing support explicit. Capture each original curve separately over the reference, then inspect front, side, top, back and high/low obliques that reveal depth and endpoints. Set camera direction, target and scale freely when a preset hides the question. A source view name belongs to its own frame, not necessarily to the anatomical camera of that name.
2. Chosen groups of curves and parts together against the same reference: a part with its host, children and neighbours sharing a join, such as the head mass with nose and lips, or the trunk with an arm and its palm and digits. Inspect their placement, crossings and continuity in the same frame; separate pictures cannot establish their relationship. Keep the reference clay for front and side silhouettes and its section curves for the top; horizontal reference curves alone can hide the side boundary.
3. The whole figure with all its view curves over the reference, to check proportions and coverage. Whole front, side and top frames are an overview, not a replacement for the individual and grouped readings.

A changed host or frame reopens the original-curve comparison of every affected neighbour. Use the selected canonical original producer for this stage. Unrelated regional sources, upper constraint solves, raw lofts, full preparation/calibration and Boolean realization are not prerequisites for reading an available original. Record a genuinely required unavailable dependency instead of requesting unrelated work to reach it.

For repeatable evidence, plan the round as one capture with a [sweep](screenshot.md#selecting-curves-and-parts), then judge its individual curves and neighbour groups before accepting the whole overview. A fitted named view is a visibility aid; a curve that remains edge-on, hidden or unsupported still needs the reverse or oblique reading. A completed capture is coverage, not a 2D form or shared-connection pass.

The reference comparison observes the production source definition before final realization. Read its `observation`, original `diagnostics` and `sourceRecords`: tree membership, unconnected studies, definition-only parts and unavailable results mean different things. Inspect studies as studies; their visible curves do not prove they belong to the generated skin. Distinguish the original interpolation support from the actual part domain, and verify that displayed curves and top rings lie in that domain. `finalRealization: not-run` is no final calibration, join or rig pass. A source with no admitted loft sections can still have original outlines to inspect.

Completing the observation round is not 2D acceptance. Measure every curve's deviation from the registered reference as a table: maximum and mean in mm at sampled stations, guides and section diagonals both. Investigate every deviation beyond a few millimetres to its cause in the code, trace and published sources, and write the predicted numeric effect of each fix before changing anything. Correct the affected original 2D definitions in one batch, and repeat their complete individual and grouped reference comparison until placement and form hold. A citation does not excuse a departure. When a population law or published norm misses the person's reference, the law is the wrong one, or it is applied by the wrong definition, frame or population. Find that cause, or take the person's own value by the published definition as a measured input with a receipt. A re-measured table that misses a prediction means the investigation was incomplete. Investigate again; do not patch the visible symptom and re-render to see what happens. As soon as a part reaches that state, proceed to its [3D inspection](#inspection-round) with its neighbours; unrelated parts need not be complete. A surface-first repair can conceal the wrong input the algorithm received.

Read the actual matched part and curve ids, briefly enabling labels to establish identity and then hiding them when they obscure the shape. Record missing parts, empty selections and unsupported comparisons explicitly; a palm incorporated in an arm's walk needs inspection there, and an absent digit is not a passed digit. For each curve record the observed departure, its direction and scale where measurable, the camera and reference conditions, and the input, law, frame or construction choice responsible. Use one frozen source for the inspection round and personally open every frame cited.

## Steps One And Two

The [three steps](SKILL.md#three-steps) define the work; these are the gates of the first two.

- Step 1 passes when every traced line lies on the reference in every view, and same-camera clay renders of each part and of the reference mesh agree at every standard and oblique angle. A curve overlay alone does not show volume.
- Step 2 passes when the re-based coordinates return to the reference pose exactly.
- Both steps also need the joints realized, the anatomical order detectors passing and the surface-integrity detectors passing.

The reference mesh has hatching, holes and saw-toothed slices, so an extracted knot is accepted only after it is checked against the reference mesh itself.

A defect visible in a frame must be removable in one batch. Before each batch, inventory every visible defect of the whole figure in every view and close-up, and assign each defect a cause and an owner. Research whether a better or more certain method exists than the current one, and adopt it when it does. A defect left out of the inventory is an incomplete investigation.

## Published Values

In step 3, a published value enters only in one of two ways:

- as a relation in the source's own covariates, evaluated at this person's values: a regression, a ratio to a girth or length, or the subgroup matching the person's sex, build, fat and muscularity;
- as the person's own measurement, taken by the source's definition.

A sample's plain mean or median describes the average subject, not this person. Before using a source, read the parameters the reference was authored with: its body controls, such as height, fat, masculine-feminine and muscularity, beside its measured girths and lengths. State in the comment which covariate values select the relation. A difference left by a population default is unfinished work, not an accepted reason.

## Review

Build one baseline covering every available original, its individual reference views, actual neighbour groups and the whole figure. Reuse source preparation across the batch. Later rounds regenerate changed originals and affected neighbours; add angles or close-ups for unresolved questions rather than repeat unchanged images. Missing or failed originals remain named gaps in coverage.

Inspect the actual originals and retain each completed region's source bytes, identities, domains, frame maps, images and unresolved conditions. Record each completed review round's findings together by cause.

A verdict names the exact source version, curve, physical interval and personally inspected views. Use pass, fail or unobserved only for that scope. A material-clock guide is not an anatomical plane cut, and neither accepts an uninspected section piece, loop or complete part. Require plausible basic form and placement over the reference; unexplained spikes, bulges, indentations or crossings remain defects even when calipers, continuity checks or tests pass. Completed captures and numerical residuals are not visual acceptance.

## Shared Connections

Trace the [generating connection](construction.md#connections) into both actual production source inputs before lofting, then into their actual raw and realized consumers. Verify the whole boundary and its qualified jets under the declared maps, the actual open-end or side-footprint domains, and the promised preservation of neighbouring source intervals. Equal names, centres, four supports or object identity alone do not establish that geometry. Record missing support and finite-sample limits explicitly.

For known ports, inspect both complete original port curves, their correspondence and transverse data, then the actual realized band and its neighboring strips. Follow logical-region, material-chart and shared binding identities through the same atlas. Check joint-datum and inverse-bind dependencies in the active neutral/bind consumers without inferring a new pose qualification from this construction inspection.

Inspect both parts' original 2D definitions individually and with their neighbours over the reference, then follow the [inspection round](#inspection-round) through actual 3D loft curves, their default-clay overlap and clay alone at matching cameras. Read front, side, top and needed obliques of the connection. Keep source form and sharing findings separate from final merge/diff outcomes: prejoin curves or clay do not establish a successful Boolean connection.

## Inspection Round

Inspect the candidate's generating curves, skin and joins following [visual debugging](../visual-debugging/SKILL.md) and [form inspection](../3d-modeling/inspection.md), for the affected person and all affected parts, and compare it with the existing figure under matching camera, light, shade, size and backend in every affected region and join, including changed inputs. Expand to other people when the task requires it. Inspect each selected part alone, then with its host and neighbours, then assembled, using the [selection recipes](screenshot.md#selecting-curves-and-parts); include the realized blends so a hidden blend cannot manufacture a gap. Keep partial work visible with what is unverified marked. Every key below is a request key of the [shared instrument](screenshot.md).

1. For each part whose original 2D curves are accepted, draw its sections, guides and axis alone (`marks=curve/*`, `surface=0`), then those curves over the clay surface (`marks=curve/*`, `shade=clay`), then the clay alone (`shade=clay`), through the same cameras. Inspect the part and its chosen neighbours immediately; unrelated parts need not be complete.
2. Inspect the whole current person and affected neighbours from every needed view, under the clay and the beauty shade, in the anatomical neutral, bind and any poses required by the active task. Check proportions, silhouettes and missing or disconnected anatomy. View the face with a focused view such as `front@focus=f1/nose` with `marks=curve/*`.
3. View each region and connection in close-ups with a focused view under `shade=clay`, and with `shade=faces` where an open end or a flipped surface is suspected. Check the declared C2/G2 conditions on smooth keys and strips, and inspect explicit creases, topology transitions and trims under their own contracts; `marks=edge` shows how the triangles follow the form there.
4. Trace every line or ripple to the curves that make it:
   - Find the family that carries it by drawing and colouring candidate families' curves over the skin with `marks=curve/*` and `color`; the skin itself is the one piece `f1/body`.
   - Draw the sections either side of it and the axis of that family, such as `parts=f1/arm/section/3,f1/arm/section/4,f1/arm/axis` with `marks=curve/*`, through the camera of the preceding surface pass.
   - Name what is drawn with `label` while establishing identity, then hide the labels where they cover the shape.
5. Write every finding, with its cause, into the round's findings table.

A missing or culled surface cannot be accepted from its curves.

## Render Audit

- Capture only after the generated output changes, and read only new stills.
- After a figure changes, the whole-person minimum is the three passes above from the front, left and top, with underwear and hair hidden by `hide`. Inspect these stills yourself and keep the capture's record. They accompany a shape claim; unavailable final skin remains unverified.
- Include back, oblique, individual and neighbour views as the affected geometry requires; the whole-person minimum does not replace those readings.
- Keep each round's capture under `packages/human/.shots/`, in a named round of its own, as [capture](../visual-debugging/capture.md#rounds) keeps rounds.
- Open every still a finding cites.
- If a capture fails, stop visual claims that depend on it and restore the instrument first.
