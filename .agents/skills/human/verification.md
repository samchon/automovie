# Verification

## Seeing Before Believing

A shape claim is accepted only from renders opened by the person making the claim, beside the reference, under matching camera, light and shade. Numbers, passing tests and a worker's report are pointers to where to look, not acceptance: a figure has passed every check while its armpit read wrong, and a defect that no test lists yet shows only in a still.

- Open every frame a finding or claim cites, and read the named pixel region on both sides, because light hides a defect on one side.
- Read numbers from the output files, not from a message.
- Accept a cause only when it is measured: it names the failed premise with its numbers on the failing case and on a passing counterpart, and it agrees with the code that produced the diagnostic.
- Judge original 2D definitions, raw loft curves, their clay and the realized skin as separate stages. A smooth downstream surface cannot accept a wrong original.

The [shared instrument](screenshot.md) finds defects and accepts a candidate; the [tests](testing.md) confirm it.

## Curves Against The Reference

A surface is only as right as its original 2D definitions, so inspect them before correcting a shape. A comparison the instrument cannot draw yet, because the reference is not handed to it, is [unavailable evidence](screenshot.md#reference-comparison), recorded as such and never passed.

1. **Each curve.** Inventory every original definition by its identity, owner, valid domain and frame map, including every contributing piece, loop, guide and port. Capture each curve over the reference in front, side, top, back and the obliques that show depth and endpoints. A view name belongs to the curve's own frame.
2. **Neighbours.** Inspect a part with its host, children and neighbours sharing a join, in one frame, because separate pictures cannot show a relationship.
3. **The whole.** Inspect the whole figure with all its view curves over the reference for proportion and coverage. The whole is an overview, not a replacement for 1 and 2.

Then measure every curve's deviation from the registered reference as a table, with maximum and mean in mm at sampled stations, guides and section diagonals. Investigate every deviation beyond a few millimetres to its cause and write the predicted effect of each fix before changing anything. Correct the affected originals in one batch and repeat the comparison until placement and form hold. A re-measured table that misses its prediction means the investigation was incomplete.

When a published law misses the person's reference, the law is the wrong one, or it is applied by the wrong definition, frame or population. Find that cause, or take the person's own value by the published definition as a measured input.

A changed host or frame reopens the comparison of every affected neighbour. As soon as a part's originals hold, take it to its [inspection round](#inspection-round) with its neighbours.

## Steps One And Two

These are the gates of the first two [steps](SKILL.md#three-steps).

- Step 1 passes when every traced line lies on the reference in every view, and same-camera clay renders of each part and of the reference agree at every standard and oblique angle. A curve overlay alone does not show volume.
- Step 2 passes when the re-based coordinates return to the reference pose exactly.
- Both need the joints realized and the anatomical order and surface-integrity detectors passing.

The reference mesh has hatching, holes and saw-toothed slices, so an extracted knot is accepted only after it is checked against the mesh itself.

Before each batch, inventory every visible defect of the whole figure in every view and close-up, and give each a cause and an owner. A defect left out of the inventory is an incomplete investigation.

## Published Values

In step 3 a published value enters in one of two ways:

- as a relation in the source's own covariates, evaluated at this person's values: a regression, a ratio to a girth or length, or the subgroup matching the person's sex, build, fat and muscularity;
- as the person's own measurement, taken by the source's definition.

A sample's plain mean describes the average subject, not this person. Read the parameters the reference was authored with before using a source, and state in the comment which covariate values select the relation.

## Shared Connections

Trace each [connection](construction.md#connections) into both production source inputs before lofting, then into their raw and realized consumers. Verify the whole boundary and its jets under the declared maps, the open-end or footprint domains, and the preservation of neighbouring intervals. Equal names, centres or four supports do not establish that geometry.

For a port, overlay both sides' 2D lines at the joint in every view, then inspect the realized band and its neighbouring strips. A band that spans a gap or turns steeply fails here, at the lines.

Before a connection or a family is realized, pre-check every premise the engine admits it on, not one of them: the sections' star-shape and their conic layout's admissibility at every station, the rings and loops the bands start and land on, every band column's direction, and the correspondence's own condition, such as a monotone angle. A premise left out of the pre-check surfaces as a refusal after the build, when a fit and a figure have already been spent on it.

## Inspection Round

Inspect the candidate following [visual debugging](../visual-debugging/SKILL.md) and [form inspection](../3d-modeling/inspection.md), for every affected part and join, against the existing figure and the reference under matching camera, light, shade, size and backend.

1. For each part whose originals hold, take the [three passes](screenshot.md#the-three-passes) through the same cameras: curves alone, curves over clay, clay alone. Inspect the part alone, then with its neighbours, then assembled.
2. Inspect the whole person from every needed view, under clay and beauty shade, in the neutral, the bind and the poses the task requires.
3. Inspect each region and connection in close-ups. Colour the triangles by owning face to name what makes a defect, and use a faces shade where an open end or a flipped surface is suspected.
4. Trace every line or ripple to the curves that make it: draw the candidate families' curves over the skin, then the sections either side of it and the family's axis, through the camera of the surface pass.
5. Write every finding with its owner and cause into the round's table.

A missing or culled surface cannot be accepted from its curves.

## Review

A verdict names the exact source version, part, interval and the views personally inspected, and uses pass, fail or unobserved only for that scope. Unexplained spikes, bulges, indentations or crossings remain defects when calipers, continuity checks or tests pass. A completed capture is coverage, not acceptance.

Build one baseline of every original, its reference views, its neighbour groups and the whole figure. Later rounds regenerate changed originals and affected neighbours, and add angles or close-ups for open questions. Record each round's findings together by cause.

## Render Audit

- Capture after the generated output changes, and read the new stills. When nothing new was rendered, open the latest stills not yet read.
- After a figure changes, the minimum is the three passes from the front, left and top, with underwear and hair hidden. Add back, oblique, close-up and neighbour views as the affected geometry requires.
- Keep each round's capture under the Human package's ignored shots folder, in a named round, as [capture](../visual-debugging/capture.md#rounds) keeps rounds.
- If a capture fails, stop the visual claims that depend on it and restore the instrument first.
