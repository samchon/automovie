# Verification

## Actual Generated Form

Bind source, emitted JavaScript and maps, loaded imports, inputs, generated skin and construction to the captures. A successful command or HTTP response does not establish current displayed geometry; a retained previous display remains labelled as such. Recover broken display or capture before further shaping.

For each modeling stage, inspect and attach one reference-versus-independent-generated full-body comparison for each of the four reference people under matching camera, material, lighting and resolution. Use absolute image paths, deliver the first normal capture promptly, and distinguish capture time from report time; identify missing comparisons rather than substituting old images.

Apply [Visual Debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md) to the whole body, regions, neighbors, multi-angle views and enlarged defects with their generating construction. Separate coverage, numerical fit and appearance. Check each parameter's declared physical effect on the generated curves and skin through independent changes, interactions and return to the starting values; reference reproduction does not establish prediction on unobserved forms.

Human and Human-viewer tests must not be written, restored or run, including renamed or relocated substitutes; reference experiments do not authorize such tests.

## Correction Rounds

Finish discovery of observed defects and their latent effects on neighbors, shared boundaries and downstream consumers, then analyze and correct all identified common causes together before repeating the complete affected inspection. Fixing one symptom or deferring a known cause is forbidden.

When the method fails or matching rounds show no broad-form progress, reconsider the hypothesis, correspondence, representation and algorithm, and research alternatives before continuing. Increasing precision, layers or traits, monkey patches and repeated local repairs cannot substitute for a supported common model.
