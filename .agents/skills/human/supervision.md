# Human supervision

## Assignment

Use the current checkout and assign one writer per coupled source. Share the measured cause, governing geometry, scope and expected evidence. Scale the team to independent work. Workers make routine decisions within their scope. The supervisor reads the relevant primary sources and existing implementation, inspects results and coordinates coupled fixes.

## Every ten minutes

For supervised Human campaigns, inspect at the start and at fixed ten-minute deadlines while work runs. Log the scheduled, actual and next times together with the revision, inspected evidence and next actions. Record any missed deadline when noticed.

Review saved changes and active processes. Open the matched images and read numerical receipts and exact-revision checks. Distinguish a rejected form from independently valid implementation work and preserve the latter. Group findings by measured cause before the next correction round. Report progress and unresolved gates in the user's language. [Verification](verification.md) · [Correction rounds](../visual-debugging/correction-rounds.md).

## Before delivery

Inspect the affected assembly and neighbors on the delivery revision. Apply the issue's whole-figure and CI gates before claiming acceptance. Stop work on request and preserve existing diffs, captures and local references.
