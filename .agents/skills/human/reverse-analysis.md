# Human Reverse Analysis

Use [Mesh Recovery](../3d-modeling/recovery.md) for fixed-source observation, coefficient reconstruction and actual reverse-rendered comparison. This guide owns the additional task of identifying shared Human equations, not a second recovery pipeline. Read [References](references.md), [Construction](construction.md) and the existing research before choosing measurements or a method; apply Human's author workflow, delegation and reporting.

## Establish Anatomical Correspondence

Register posture and local anatomical frames before comparing people. Treat rig markers, heuristic extrema and clinical landmarks as different observations, and retain uncertainty instead of assigning unsupported anatomical names. Convert measurement protocols explicitly before fitting.

Identify physical section families, longitudinal guides and feature boundaries for each region and its neighbors. Preserve correspondence through the shared skeleton and measurement definitions; a valid geometric contour need not be an anatomical measurement plane.

## Identify Shared Equations

Choose independent observables under [Parameter-Driven Curves](construction.md#parameter-driven-curves), which owns the production rules and the boundary between absolute-coordinate research and parameter-generated Human curves.

Fit a compact shared coefficient set to corresponding observables across multiple identities. Preserve independent measurements rather than compensating for a wrong junction, registration or surface operator with another dimension. Compare representations by observable error, complexity, identifiability and stability under parameter changes; report free coefficients, spans and constraints alongside residuals.

## Validate Variation

Plan identities across independent dimensions, proportions, local shape, asymmetry and relevant combinations, including interiors, supported extremes and transitions. Expand where uncertainty or residuals concentrate. More cuts on the same people improve observation density, not population coverage.

Reserve identities, section locations, oblique directions and parameter combinations before fitting, and keep that split fixed through a correction round. Samples evaluated from fitted curves are not independent evidence. A small seed set cannot establish a population equation.

Evaluate reserved shapes and independent parameter changes, interactions and a return to starting values. Check that measurements retain their meanings, neighboring boundaries remain compatible, and the same equations generate the displayed skin and construction. State unsupported regions and extrapolations rather than claiming all parameter combinations from finite observations.

Use [Verification](verification.md) for calibration and its representation-change rule, and [Mesh Recovery](../3d-modeling/recovery.md#compare-and-correct) for source-versus-reconstruction evidence. Numerical fit or additional curve count alone establishes no visual result.

## Coordinate Experiments

Apply [Delegation](SKILL.md#delegation) to shared frames, boundaries, schemas, operators and consumers. Give each run immutable inputs, an isolated entry graph, an owned output directory and a capture page; integrate versioned artifacts through manifests rather than shared live files. Keep source-copy and compiler leases separate from long read-only measurement or rendering, reuse immutable compiler work and bound worker pools without removing guards.

At each scheduled audit, compare the method with alternatives that could remove observed causes more directly. Record the hypothesis, measurement evidence, uncertainty, owner and next discriminating experiment under Human's reporting schedule.
