## Realistic skin

Use visual debugging to verify realistic human geometry. Open the actual generated skin in matched clay, curve-overlay and close views for every affected region and its neighbours. Add no automated tests for realistic human geometry. Record measurements alongside the visual evidence when they locate a cause.

Start Campaign 3 joint work from the accepted Campaign 2 F1 assembly and its Phase 2.5 continuation. Hold the neighbouring form while changing a connection. Treat the parameterization of regional form as separate work and order it according to the geometry dependencies. Keep restricted exemplar data local.

## Polygon man

The polygon man is the plain rigid figure used by motion verification and diffusion hints. Correct measured radii, lengths and frames while keeping its primitives, segment count and style. Refer any change to that representation to the user. Verify it separately from the continuous skin. [Polygon man](../../../packages/human/README.md#polygon-man).
