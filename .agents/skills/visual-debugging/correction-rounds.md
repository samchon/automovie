# Correction rounds

A correction round gathers every finding of one capture, traces each to its cause, repairs the causes in one change and compares again. This page owns that order.

## Measuring and looking

Settle by measurement what a number decides, because an eye on a picture misjudges position, depth and timing; the skill of each subject names its measures. Then [plan the capture](capture.md#plan).

## Inspection

Inspect the whole affected subject at every scale before changing anything: the whole, its regions, its neighbors and its close-ups, from every needed view and at every needed time. Write down every observed defect with its cell and its conditions.

## Causes

Trace causes through generation and display, including shared boundaries and derived outputs. Where causes or their interactions are unclear, plan the controlled [comparisons](comparison.md) that separate them as one set, run the whole set without changing the source, and record the results. Map each proposed change to every defect and part it affects.

## One combined change

Fix all identified causes in one combined change at their sources, and judge it on the assembled subject. A cause that crosses an ownership boundary is changed by both owners in the same round, or told to the user where the project cannot change an owner.

However cheap a capture is, this replaces fixing one finding at a time:

- editing after each observed defect;
- changing one value and capturing to see its effect;
- capturing after each single fix;
- leaving a traced cause to a later round or finer scale.

## Comparing again

Repeat the round's capture after the change and compare it with the earlier one across every affected part, view, time, identity, resolution and motion state, checking both the recorded defects and regressions. Account for every recorded defect before accepting the round; a new or unexplained defect keeps it open.

When a fix is tried over several rounds, keep a round only if nothing got worse than in the best earlier round, and record every remaining failure with its numbers.

Look at the new capture yourself, because stale, blank or unobserved output stays unverified.
