    Namespace HumanBlade

    Blade work under film names, each a row of a law of HumanSword, HumanSwordGuard, HumanSwordParry or HumanSwordThrust. The composition gives the held blade (blade, a sword prop the hand picked up) and the target; with neither, the row is a solo drill of the bare hand. Register the ones a film uses.

    The guard names are Liechtenauer's four, as Ringeck's gloss describes them: vomTag (from the roof, the hilt at the shoulder), ochs (the ox, the hilt beside the head and the point at the face), pflug (the plough, the hilt at the hip and the point at the face) and alber (the fool, the point at the ground).

    An exchange is three rows on two people: the attacker's cut or thrust with outcome: "parried" and an anchor, the defender's parry with against that anchor, and the defender's riposte after it.
