    Namespace AutoMovieHumanNailFold

    The nail folds: the horseshoe of skin that begins behind the nail's root and runs along both sides of the plate, bevelling below its free end (Bridgman 1920, docs/anatomy/hand.md, Form), written as a relief window over the dorsal skin in the plate's own frame.

    The fold stands proud of the skin in a band of width BAND mm around the plate's outline: behind the root and beside the edges, open at the free edge. In the plate's chart (u, v) (root 0 to free edge 1, radial edge -1 to ulnar 1) the distance from the plate's outline, in millimetres, is

    d(u, v) = hypot(L max(0, -u), (W / 2) max(0, |v| - 1))

    and the height is HEIGHT_SHARE * W * bump((d - BAND / 2) / (BAND / 2)) for 0 < d < BAND: zero at the plate's edge and at the band's outer edge with its slope and curvature, so the fold meets the plate under it (G0 with the plate's edge going under the fold, a declared step) and the dorsal skin around it (G1 and in fact C2) without a crease. The window ends at the free edge: where u > 1 the sides' distance stays the same and the fold fades by bump of u - 1 over BAND.

    Dimensions are CONVENTIONS with the plate's (no nail source is read).
