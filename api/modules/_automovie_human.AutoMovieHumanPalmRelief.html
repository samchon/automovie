    Namespace AutoMovieHumanPalmRelief

    The palm's reliefs on its own sections, in the hand form's axes (a along the hand toward the fingers, b across toward the thumb, c through toward the palm), each its own piece on its own axis:

    • the interosseous valleys (AutoMovieHumanInterosseousValley) between neighbouring finger metacarpals on the back of the hand, each as deep as its share of the metacarpal arches' radius (AutoMovieHumanMetacarpalArch), growing from the palm's first section to the knuckles;

    • the hypothenar eminence (AutoMovieHumanHandWindow.HYPOTHENAR) along the fifth metacarpal on the palm's ulnar side, its window between the palm's first section and the fifth finger's root.

    • the palmar sector's pad (AutoMovieHumanPalmPalmarSector), the aponeurosis pad's half ellipse between the section's side points, joined to the palmar face by the smooth union (AutoMovieHumanSeamUnion.body), growing from its chord at the palm's first section to its full depth at the knuckles.

    All three vanish at the palm's first section, the seam the palm shares with the heel, so that seam is unchanged.

    A metacarpal's place across the hand is its finger root's, its length in the palm the way from the palm's first section to that root. A relief moves a point through the hand, toward the palm for a valley and away from the bones for the eminence, weighed by how far the point lies on its face of the section (dorsal for a valley, palmar for the eminence), so the reliefs fade to nothing at the side lines, where the faces meet. Every window vanishes with its slope and curvature at its edge.
