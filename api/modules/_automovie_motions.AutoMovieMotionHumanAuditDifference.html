    Namespace AutoMovieMotionHumanAuditDifference

    How far two displayed motions are apart: the largest distance between the same joint centre of both at the same film second.

    Three properties of a motion are stated as a difference of two runs:

    • determinism: the same seed and the same chain give a difference of zero;
    • seed sensitivity: another seed gives a difference above zero when the action varies, and still a clean audit;
    • left and right symmetry: the motion of the mirrored order (every side swapped) is the mirror image of the first, so with mirror the second motion is reflected in the sagittal plane x = plane and its left bones are compared with the first one's right bones.

    The two motions are read over the shorter one's length, and the difference of their lengths is reported beside the distance, since two motions that agree while both last still differ when one is longer.
