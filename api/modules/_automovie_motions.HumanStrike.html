    Namespace HumanStrike

    The strikes as rows on the grammar (#3362): each named technique is a row of the shipped law that owns its geometry and its outcome, the effector and the side fixed, the target and the accuracy the composition's.

    Row Law Fixed
    jab, cross punch straight, lead or rear hand
    leadHook, rearHook punch hook, lead or rear hand
    leadUppercut, rearUppercut punch uppercut, lead or rear hand
    roundKick, switchKick kick round, rear or lead leg
    pushKick, teep kick push, lead or rear leg
    leadKnee, rearKnee knee lead or rear leg

    A strike with a held weapon is the weapons' rows on the blade laws (HumanWeaponMelee) and a slap the stage fight's (HumanStageFight), so every strike shares the stroke and the outcome law of its family. The receiver's reaction is synced to the strike's hit (sync({ hit }), #3480) and planned by the perturbation law (#3430).
