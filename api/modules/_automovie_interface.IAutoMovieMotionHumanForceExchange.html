    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanForceExchange

    The mechanics of a force exchanged through grips (#3439): a pull that draws a partner off balance (kuzushi), the counter-lean that resists it, and a throw's rotation about the thrower's fulcrum.

    • Off-balancing. The partner is a linear inverted pendulum of its own mass M and length l (resolveAutoMovieCenterOfMass.pendulum), with eigenfrequency ω = sqrt(g / l) (Hof, Gazendam and Sinke 2005). A constant horizontal pull F at rest gives x'' = ω² x + F / M, so the centre moves x(t) = (F / M ω²)(cosh ωt - 1) along the pull and its extrapolated centre x + x' / ω is (F / M ω²)(e^{ωt} - 1). It leaves the base where the base ends along the pull, e from the centre, at T = ln(1 + e M ω² / F) / ω; the pull that breaks the base at a declared T is F = e M ω² / (e^{ωT} - 1).
    • Resistance. A partner who resists keeps its centre over the base by leaning against the pull until the moments about the base balance: the pull at grip height h against the weight shifts the centre of pressure F h / (M g) toward the pull (static moment balance, the form of HumanLoad.lean), and the resistance holds while that shift stays within the base's extent along the pull.
    • Throw. Once the base is broken the thrower's fulcrum carries the partner, who turns about it as a rigid body: I φ'' = M g r sin φ + τ, with φ the angle of the centre from the vertical through the fulcrum, r its distance, I = I_c + M r² (parallel axes) and τ the pull's moment about the fulcrum while the pull lasts: a horizontal pull F at a grip h above the fulcrum turns the body by F h. It starts from the momentum the pull left, φ'(0) = v_t / r, the centre's speed across the fulcrum line, and is released where the centre comes level with the fulcrum on its far side, φ = π / 2. A swing whose rate falls to zero before the vertical cannot carry the partner over, so the throw is not produced.

    The phases' timing (kuzushi, tsukuri, kake) is not taken from a study of seoi-nage: the instants are the mechanics' own, from the forces and the bodies.
