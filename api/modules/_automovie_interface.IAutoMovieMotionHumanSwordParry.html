    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanSwordParry

    The parry of blade work: the strong of the blade brought across an incoming blade where its attack is stopped.

    A parry answers an attack the author declared parried (HumanSword), named by its anchor. It reads the attacker's blade at the attack's decisive instant, where that blade stands stopped, and puts its own blade across it there: point up for a high line and down for a low one, on the defender's side, the two centre lines HumanSword.GAP apart. The parry waits in the hold it was entered in for as long as puts its meeting (stroke) on the attack's decisive instant, so a parry that starts in time meets its attack; sync with align: "stroke" starts it in time. With no attack named it closes a line as a drill.

    It meets the attacking blade on its weak and, while the arm cannot hold its own blade across it there, slides the meeting back along that blade (SLIDE), where the stopped blade stands farther from the defender. A blade to meet that lies beyond the arm is refused. First pass: the meeting is where the stopped attack stands, so a parry does not beat the attacking blade aside, and a bind is not planned.
