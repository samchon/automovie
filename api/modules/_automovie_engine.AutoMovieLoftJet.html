    Namespace AutoMovieLoftJet

    A chain's surface point with its exact first and second derivatives in station and phase.

    A chain expanded with its jets (every layer carrying its plane, the plane's station derivatives and the coefficients' second derivatives) is, between two layers, P(s, t) = o(s) + x(s, t) u(s) + z(s, t) v(s) with coefficient Hermite factors in physical station or a declared pole coordinate, actual analytic frames or the existing component-plane fit, and the actual local source/continuation total2 or legacy phase spline, plus a lift along w = v x u and its reliefs along their axes. Each derivative is then the product rule over those factors, each factor's derivative exact:

    P_s  = o' + x_s u + x u' + z_s v + z v'
P_ss = o'' + x_ss u + 2 x_s u' + x u'' + (the same in z, v)
P_st = x_st u + x_t u' + z_st v + z_t v'
P_t  = x_t u + z_t v,   P_tt = x_tt u + z_tt v

    The strips, the trims' and lids' cross derivatives and the shading normals read these instead of differences of sampled points.
