    Namespace AutoMovieHumanBodyCourses

    Defaults of the foot's girths and lengths and the crotch's courses that AutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy.resolve reads: each an ordinary least squares line by sex on the Human input that predicts it best, or a surveyed share.

    Surveys: ANSUR II (Gordon et al. 2014, NATICK/TR-15/007; 1,986 women and 4,082 men, docs/sources/ansur-ii.md) and ANSUR 1988 (Gordon et al. 1989, NATICK/TR-89/044; 2,208 women and 1,774 men, docs/sources/ansur-1988.md). Every value is printed from the public files by test_human_survey_foot and test_human_survey_pelvis (test/src/features/human/survey/).
