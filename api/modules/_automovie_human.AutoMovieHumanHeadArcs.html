    Namespace AutoMovieHumanHeadArcs

    The head's surface arcs over the head from tragion to tragion and the tragion's drop below the vertex: the girths the head mass's fullness is solved to and the ear's height on the side outline.

    Source: ANSUR 1988 (Gordon et al. 1989, NATICK/TR-89/044; 2,208 women and 1,774 men, docs/sources/ansur-1988.md) takes six bitragion arcs, from tragion to tragion over the coronal plane, the crinion, the minimum frontal breadth, the subnasale, the menton and under the mandible; ANSUR II (Gordon et al. 2014; 1,986 women and 4,082 men) takes the tragion to the top of the head. Each is the ordinary least squares line by sex on the Human head input with the best R2 (test_human_survey_head_arcs): the head circumference for the vault's arcs and the tragion's drop, the bizygomatic breadth (the Human's faceWidth, zy-zy) for the face's arcs.

    Limits: the head's arcs follow any one input weakly (R2 0.14 to 0.46), so a default places a person near the survey's arc for the input, not near their own; the arcs are surface lengths over the skin and hair-free scalp as the survey's tape takes them.
