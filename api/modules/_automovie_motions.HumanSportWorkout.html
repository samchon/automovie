    Namespace HumanSportWorkout

    Exercises, stretches, yoga poses, dance holds and turns, and a few sport acts under the names a screenplay uses (#3377, #3378), each a row of the workout law with its kind fixed, or of a shipped law. A row leaves open the count (reps), a held pose's hold, whether the body stays on its support (stay), a bar's place and the manner.

    What a row shows and what its first pass leaves out is stated on its kind (WorkoutFloor, WorkoutStanding, WorkoutHanging): equipment is not shown and nothing is held. A flow (a sun salutation, a kata) is these rows in sequence in the composition.
