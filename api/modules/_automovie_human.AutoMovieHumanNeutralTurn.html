    Namespace AutoMovieHumanNeutralTurn

    The turns every piece of the neutral posture is made of: the shortest turn between two directions, the turn that also matches a side, and the vectors they are written in.

    Neutral reaches each bone from the bind by one rotation about the bone's own joint. A bone's rotation is composed from its parent's, so a piece states only the turn it adds: the least that brings a direction to the one the posture wants.
