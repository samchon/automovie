    Namespace AutoMovieHumanMouthPrior

    Published anatomy the mouth's interior is generated from, shared by every person. Lengths are millimetres of an adult; a person's own head sets where they stand, never their size, because the permanent teeth vary little between adults and do not grow with stature.

    • Teeth: crown dimensions of the permanent dentition from the standard table of Wheeler's Dental Anatomy (Nelson and Ash, Wheeler's Dental Anatomy, Physiology and Occlusion, 9th ed., 2010, Table 1-1), central incisor to second molar of one quadrant. Third molars are left out, as they are often absent or unerupted.
    • Arch: teeth stand side by side along the beta-function arch of Braun et al. (1998, Angle Orthod 68:29-36), whose width is set so that the first molars' mesiobuccal cusps stand the adult intermolar width apart.
    • Occlusion: overjet and overbite of a normal occlusion, about 2 to 3 mm each (Proffit, Contemporary Orthodontics, 6th ed., ch. 1); the occlusal plane rises backward from the Frankfort plane by Downs' mean cant (Downs 1948, Am J Orthod 34:812-840).
    • Lips over the teeth: the upper incisal edge shows below the upper lip at rest by 3.40 mm in women and 1.91 mm in men (Vig and Brundo 1978, J Prosthet Dent 39:502-504); the soft tissue over the upper incisors is about 13 to 15 mm thick at the vermilion (Holdaway 1983, Am J Orthod 84:1-28).
