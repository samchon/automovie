    Namespace HumanThrownWeapon

    Thrown weapons (#3490) as rows of throwObject, each throwing the held prop the composition names (prop), which rides the hand to the release and then flies (#3479):

    • throwJavelin and throwSpear: overarm, the shaft along its velocity, released at the middle of the release angles Campos, Brizuela and Ramón (2004) measured in elite men at the 1999 World Championships (27.7 to 40.1 degrees), 33.9 degrees; a soldier's lower speed follows from the range equation to the target. The athletes' run-up stays with #3377.
    • throwKnife: overarm, spinning at the throwing arm's rate rounded to a whole number of half turns over the flight to the target, so the stand-off decides the turns (throwObjectLever.spin).
    • throwGrenade, hurlStone and hurlBottle: overarm at the style's angle, the body tumbling as it was released.
    • sling and castAtlatl: overarm from the implement the composition names (implement, a sling's pouch, an atlatl's spur), which lengthens the lever the launch leaves from (throwObjectLever).
