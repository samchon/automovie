    Namespace HumanSportSwing

    Swings of a held implement under sport names, each a row of the cut that blade work plans: the implement's striking part is carried through the target on one of the eight lines, the edge leading, by the stroke planner.

    The composition gives the implement the hand holds (blade, with tip naming its striking contact, such as a bat's strike), and the target (the ball's place at contact); with neither, the row is a shadow swing of the empty hand. First pass: the body's law is the arm's stroke with the step or lunge the cut offers; the hips' and the trunk's turn of a real swing are not planned, and the ball is not moved. A ball that flies is a prop with a motion of its own, released by the scene at the row's stroke.
