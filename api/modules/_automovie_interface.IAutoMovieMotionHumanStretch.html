    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanStretch

    A standing static stretch or yoga pose held and released (#3246, M24): the neck side bend, the cross-body shoulder stretch, the overhead side bend, the standing quadriceps stretch holding the ankle, and the tree pose (ACSM's Guidelines, 11th ed.; the hold of 30 s, Bandy et al. 1997).

    Each pose is a phrase: enter to reachEnd over 1.5 s (provisional, a stretch is entered slowly), the hold, and the release over 1.5 s. The intensity names the share of the joint's own range a stretch uses (mild half, moderate deep, AutoMovieMotionHumanRange), never more than the range table allows.

    • Neck side bend: the neck and head bend to one side by the share of the neck's lateral range.
    • Cross-body: the stretched arm crosses the chest until its wrist is beyond the other shoulder (provisional place), and the other palm presses the stretched arm near its elbow (stretchCrossBody).
    • Overhead side bend: both arms overhead, palms facing, the spine and the chest bent to one side by the share of their lateral ranges, the pelvis level over the planted feet and the arms riding the chest.
    • Quadriceps: the hips move over the standing foot, the other knee bends to at least 130 degrees (provisional) and as far as the same-side hand needs to hold the ankle (stretchQuadriceps).
    • Tree: the hips over the standing foot, the other foot's sole on the inside of the standing calf, the knee turned out, the palms together before the chest.

    Events: enter, reachEnd, release, with the phrase instants.

    Not drafted: seated and floor stretches, PNF, dynamic stretches and the flows (sun salutation); they need floor supports for the hands and seat.
