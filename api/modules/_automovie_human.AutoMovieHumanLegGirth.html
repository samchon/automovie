    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLegGirth

    The girth the leg's section asks for along the walk: each tape at its published site, the landmark shares between, and a monotone cubic through them (Fritsch and Carlson), so every run between two knots rises or falls once and no combination of tapes makes a section overshoot its knots.

    Sites: the calf tape at the leg's maximum girth below the knee (the shank landmark where the references' calf peaks), the knee tape at mid-patella, MID_PATELLA under the knee centre (the references: 36 mm; ANSUR II puts it 16.9 mm below the lateral epicondyle in women and 3.3 mm in men, with the joint centre at the epicondyles), and the thigh tape at the widest girth just under the crotch (ISO 8559-1 5.3.20). The shares between are the references' girths over their own calf maximum (shank) or thigh maximum (thigh) at each landmark row, their medians over f1, f2, m1 and m2.
