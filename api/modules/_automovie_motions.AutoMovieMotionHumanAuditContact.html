    Namespace AutoMovieMotionHumanAuditContact

    Whether the feet's contact with the floor, inferred from the displayed motion itself, agrees with the contact the actions declare.

    The slip and lift readings of the ground audit look only inside declared plant windows, so a foot that skates along the floor while no window is declared on it goes unread. This reading infers contact the way gait analysis does from kinematics alone: a sole socket (heel, ball or toe) is on the floor while it is no higher than HEIGHT, and a socket on the floor is at rest while its horizontal speed is no more than SPEED. Each step between two grid instants is then read twice:

    • skate: outside every window declared on the foot, a socket on the floor at both ends of the step travels horizontally: a foot dragged or skated along the floor. share is the part of the film's steps in which some socket does so faster than SPEED.
    • loose and afloat: inside a plant window on the ground, the foot's lowest socket must be the contact: loose is its horizontal speed while the same socket stays the lowest, and afloat its height off the floor.

    A window of any kind declared on a foot (a plant, a pivot, a strike) declares its contact, so only steps wholly outside all of them are read for skate: a step a window's edge falls inside, between two grid instants, is partly declared and is not read.
