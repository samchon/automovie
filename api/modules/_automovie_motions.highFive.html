    Namespace highFive

    A high five, a low five or a fist bump between two standing partners: both bring one hand to one shared point between them and meet there at one shared instant (#3284), the mutual goal the handshake uses (AutoMovieMotionMeeting).

    The meeting point is the pair's mutual goal (resolveAutoMovieMutualGoal on the true wrist reach of the two opposed hand frames): the action prefers the mean of the two crown heights for a high five, of their waist heights for a low five and of their chest heights for a fist bump (provisional), and the point lies at that height where both arms are the same share of their comfortable reaches, moved down or up only as far as both arms' reach demands. Partners of unequal stature therefore meet where the shorter one can reach. Seated partners whose arms alone fall short lean their trunks forward over the seat, the arm's target first, in equal shares of each hip's flexion room (HumanSeatedReach, Chan and Dubey 1995); partners no point lies within both arms of, leaned as far as they can, are refused, never stretched. Each role's hand reaches it in the free-space transport time of its distance (AutoMovieMotionHumanTiming.transport), and at least the time its arm's arc takes within the audit's travel bound (touchReach.meeting); the pair meets when the slower arrives, both stay 0.1 s (provisional, the rebound) and go back in the reach's time. The palms (or the fists) face each other, each surface's skin on the point.

    With prop, each role brings the prop its hand holds instead of the hand: the prop's point (a glass's lip) comes to the shared point with the vessel upright, so two glasses meet rim to rim as in a toast, and the hand keeps its prop afterwards (HumanHeld).

    Events: the phrase instants, with meet at ready and rebound at relax; both partners share meet.
