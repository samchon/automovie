    Namespace AutoMovieHumanCanonicalProfile

    The contact and mass profile of a polygon man, built from the same joints and girth radii its drawing is built from, at the canonical T-pose rest.

    Colliders are the drawn solids. The trunk, the neck, the head, each limb segment, the palm, the rear foot and every toe phalanx collide as the convex hulls of the very ring points the drawing lofts its mesh through (AutoMovieHumanCanonicalSolids), so the collision surface is the drawn surface: no corner stands outside what is seen and no drawn surface lies outside its collider. A rig bone carries the hulls of every drawn module that rides it: the foot its two modules through the subtalar and midfoot joints, the toes bone every toe phalanx, so the toes bend at the ball as drawn. Each phalanx of the hand is a capsule of its girth radius from its joint to its child joint, a distal one ending with its cap on the fingertip, because a curl solves its contact in closed form on capsules and the drawn phalanx is that round.

    Masses are Dempster's shares of body mass as tabulated by Winter (Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement, 4th ed., table 4.1): hand 0.006, forearm 0.016, upper arm 0.028, thigh 0.100, leg 0.0465, foot 0.0145, thorax 0.216, abdomen 0.139, pelvis 0.142 and head with neck 0.081, the head taking de Leva's (1996) 0.0694 and the neck the rest; the hand's share is split over the palm and the phalanges by collider volume, a uniform density within the hand, and the foot's over the rear foot and the toes the same way. Each segment's centre of mass and inertia are those of its colliders as uniform solids, combined by the parallel-axis theorem, the way physics engines derive inertia from shapes. Joints sit at each child joint with identity frames; every clinical axis of DEFAULT_HUMANOID_ROM becomes a physical axis along its HUMANOID_JOINT_AXES direction with limits read through HUMANOID_REST_FRAME, so the physical zero is the T-pose. Drive gains are placeholders kinematic motion never reads: 50 N m per downstream kilogram. Self-contact is excluded between joint neighbours, among the palm and digits of one hand, between the abdomen and each upper arm and between the two thighs. The last two are body pairs that overlap in the reference pose: the shoulder lies inside the chest's girth, so a hanging arm's root runs about 47 mm into the ring the chest shares with the abdomen, and the hip joints sit closer together than a thigh's root is wide, so the two roots cross the midline by 12 to 17 mm. Neither overlap leaves within a natural arm or leg abduction, and left live, the solver ejects those limbs on the first substep and splays the planted feet. Disabling collision for pairs that overlap in the reference pose is the standard practice (Unreal Engine's Physics Asset collision-disable table; MuJoCo's <contact> <exclude> pairs). The known limitation: an upper arm passes through the abdomen and a thigh through the other thigh, as in crossed legs or a clinch, while every other pair, such as the shins with each other and the forearms with the abdomen, still collides.
