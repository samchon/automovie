    Namespace AutoMovieHumanHeadMassPart

    The head mass and its neck (cranium, face and neck without the nose, lips and ears) as a part drawn from its three views, the input of the view-to-section operator (AutoMovieViewLoft).

    • Side view: the actual upper front source and lower conic-template band share their endpoint value and parameter jets through one root-clock bridge. A clinical cohort's C is not a skin point or flat skin jet. B, the median back profile (AutoMovieHumanHeadBackProfile), down the nape to the neck ring.
    • Front view: L, the head's front-view silhouette, one outline through the vertex, the eurion's level (half the head breadth), the tragion's (half the cranial base width t-t), the gonion's (half go-go) and the neck's original radius source, whose normal plane and named base course have distinct measurement definitions.
    • Axis: parallel level planes whose source origin drifts from the neck centre to the skull's centre. An axis leaning to the neck tilted the sections under the tragion, so the lowest ran from behind the ear to under the chin and their outlines flared out past the jaw.
    • Fullness: the vault's quarters take the conic shoulder of the retained horizontal super-ellipse proxy (AutoMovieHumanHeadVault.closing, r = 2^(1 - 1/n) - 1, the share of the diagonal point of |x/a|^n + |z/b|^n = 1); the face's front quarters depart to the face plan's exponent under the nasion and return to the vault's over the forehead.

    The vertex is a pole. The actual lower domain starts at the supplied regular neck boundary. Original support controls below it preserve the same scalar functions without exposing an overlap. Public head-tape closure on the actual oblique source course remains distinct from the horizontal proxy and the final joined skin.
