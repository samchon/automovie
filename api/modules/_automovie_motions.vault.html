    Namespace vault

    A handspring vault (#3377): a front handspring whose hands land on a prop's top, a vaulting table, a box or a plyometric box, and fly from it to the floor or named landing mat beyond. The post-table flight is timed from its release to the landing surface's height, a separate phase in the vault observations of Chen et al. (2026, PMC13350820).

    The motion is the handspring's (handspring.over) with the hands bearing on the prop's surface affordance, read where the prop stands as the action starts. The hands come down where the lunge brings them, so a surface that is not there is refused: the hands' middle must lie inside the top's declared width and depth; a top that declares neither is taken as wide as it needs to be. The higher hands give the flight a longer fall, so the rate that lands it is lower than on the floor.

    First pass: no run-up, hurdle, springboard or flight onto the table; the body is entered standing before the prop and lunges onto it, and whether the legs clear the prop's edge is not checked.
