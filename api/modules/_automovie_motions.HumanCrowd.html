    Namespace HumanCrowd

    A crowd that keeps no formation, steered: each member (or each walking group, as one agent) heads for its goals at its own drawn speed and avoids the others and the obstacles, by one plan-time pass of the engine's steerAutoMovieCrowd. The pass gives each member a course; the member's own walk or run then keeps station on it (walkStation), so its feet are still whole steps.

    A member's agent is a disc of its shoulders' half breadth (the shoulder joint's half width plus the upper arm's radius) at its desired speed: its gait's speed at its leg length by dynamic similarity (the walk's normal Froude number, or the run's), scaled by a normal draw with the coefficient of variation of pedestrians' desired speeds, 0.26 over 1.34 m/s (Helbing and Molnar 1995), by fatigue, and in flight by morale (HUMAN_ATTRIBUTE_EFFECTS). Its start is the cue plus its latency (HumanResponse), and in flight its herding share is morale's. Every draw is keyed by the member; adding a member changes the others' courses (they avoid it) but never their draws.
