    Namespace HumanTravelLeaf

    Opening a door by its handle and holding it: the hand reaches the handle, closes on it and goes with it while the leaf swings, the person standing, and walking round with the leaf when the arm alone would lose the handle. Register HumanTravelLeaf.openDoor to use it.

    The leaf's swing is the scene's: the door is a prop whose handle moves within it (IHumanProp.point, as HumanObject.prop gives an archetype's articulated contact), and the hand follows the handle at every instant. The hand turns to the handle's frame and closes on the door's own colliders (HumanMountRider.hold, HumanMountRider.closed), reaching it over TAKE seconds on the minimum-jerk profile (Flash and Hogan 1985). A handle the standing arm does not reach as the hand takes it, such as a car door's below the shoulder, is taken leaning toward it, the body lowered as a pick up lowers it. While the arm's own fit keeps the wrist within HOLD of the hold from where the person stands the feet stay there; otherwise the person walks with the leaf, backward and round it, facing it, keeping the place relative to it the handle was taken from, at the handle's pace (HumanTravelLeafWalk). A swing the hand still loses, faster or farther than the person can walk with it, is refused as a reach refusal on handle naming the largest swing followed. First pass: the closure is the one at the instant the hand arrives.
