    Namespace AutoMovieHumanCanonicalBind

    The polygon man's bind joints, placed from the person's parameters by closed-form placement laws alone, in calibration millimetres (forward +X, up +Y, left +Z).

    The polygon man is a coarse stand-in on Human's skeleton and carries only the form it needs, so nothing here builds, fits or measures a curve of the detailed figure: no trunk tape or course is closed, no neck girth is solved, no head mass or surface is prepared. The detailed figure keeps its own bind skeleton (AutoMovieHumanBodyLaw.prepare), whose trunk joints stand on its converged surface.

    What each joint is placed from:

    • Legs, feet and toes: AutoMovieHumanLimbRest from the public limb inputs and the body frame's perineal datum, as the detailed figure places them.
    • Neck base, neck radius, atlas, head and crown: the neck prior's affine axis and tape radius (AutoMovieHumanNeckSource) and the head's landmark layout (AutoMovieHumanHeadLandmarks.layout), the head joint on the cranial centre at the tragion's published drop under the vertex. These are the values the detailed head lane publishes.
    • Jaw and eyes: the same layout's median profile at the nasion and its eye openings, by the rule of AutoMovieHumanHeadLane.prepare.
    • Shoulders and arms: the acromion and the glenohumeral centre of AutoMovieHumanShoulderDatum, its neck support the neck's tape radius at the neck base. The detailed figure reads the support from its solved neck-base course instead, which moves the glenohumeral centre by a few hundredths of a millimetre at most on the motion population.
    • Spine, chest and the sternoclavicular joints' forward place: on the straight line from the mid-hip to the neck base, at the heights the skeleton law already gives them. This is the polygon man's own definition of its trunk joints, directed by the project owner: no published source in the package gives the vertebral column's forward place at these levels, and the detailed figure's column axis, the middle of its converged sections' depth, belongs to that figure's surface. The line is the simplest construction through the two joints the skeleton level does place.
