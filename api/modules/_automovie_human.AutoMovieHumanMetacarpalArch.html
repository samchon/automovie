    Namespace AutoMovieHumanMetacarpalArch

    The dorsal arch over one metacarpal: the back of the hand's skin over a metacarpal shaft in a section square to the hand, its own piece with the metacarpal's own axis.

    The palm's section is a ring about the hand's axis in the plane (b, c), b across the hand toward the thumb and c through it toward the palm. Over each of the four finger metacarpals the dorsal skin is an arc of a circle about that metacarpal's axis, the circle of the bone plus the dorsal soft tissue; between two neighbouring metacarpals the arcs meet in the interosseous valley (AutoMovieHumanInterosseousValley). The circle's radius is BONE_SHARE of the finger's skin half width at its root plus TISSUE mm of dorsal tissue.

    Both numbers are CONVENTIONS, written so a published bone cross-section can replace them: the repository holds the metacarpals' lengths (Reddy 2024) and not their cross-sections, and the plan's packing rule gives each finger's skin width within the hand breadth.
