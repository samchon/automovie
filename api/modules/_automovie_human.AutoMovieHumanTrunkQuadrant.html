    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkQuadrant

    The shared mathematics of the trunk's section pieces: the quadrant rule the guides stand on, the jets of a guide on it, the sector arc between two guides and the reflection of the canonical left side.

    A section's left half runs from the front midline (phase 0) round the body's left (+z) to the back midline (phase pi). In normalised quadrant coordinates zeta = z / width and xi = (x - middle) / depth, the front and the back quadrant each lie on zeta^n + xi^n = 1. The guides stand on that curve at fixed lateral shares and own its point, tangent and curvature there; at the midlines and at the widest point, where a quadrant with n > 2 has no curvature, a guide takes the osculating parabola's through its next guide instead, so no guide flattens into a stripe. A sector between two guides is the quintic Hermite of their two jets, so it meets both with their curvature (G2). Section vectors are [x, z] in calibration millimetres.
