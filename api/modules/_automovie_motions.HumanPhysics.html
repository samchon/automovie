    Namespace HumanPhysics

    The one reader of the balance option (IAutoMovieMotionHumanBalance): the shift an action applies, by how much at a film second, and whether the audit judges the body's balance over it. Absent on the params, none: a posed, planted action computes no centre of mass and is not checked against gravity unless its law stages a physical outcome (IAutoMovieMotionHumanActionLaw.physical). Given, each field is the caller's where given, else the law's recommendation (IAutoMovieMotionHumanActionLaw.balance), and a field neither gives is refused, naming it, except for the explicit weight and ramp defaults. Its bounds are events, resolved as the scheduled instants are (HumanPerformance.instant). The law only asks for a correction where the prepared window has positive weight.
