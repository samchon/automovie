    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanChain

    The fluent authoring surface of one person's actions: one typed method per action of the vocabulary, act for the same action by its name, registered for a production's own law by its name, until to cut the last one, sync to time it to another action or a cue, over to perform other actions on it at once (HumanChainCore), and next and with (from AutoMovieChain) to sequence and parallel whole chains, people with people. Its statics start a chain (subject), give registered laws typed methods (extend) and join people in a mutual action (mutual, and pair for a handshake and a handover).

    const a = HumanChain.subject("a").stand({ seconds: 1 }).until({ share: 0.5 }).stand();
    new HumanPerformance({ seed: "film", performers, composition: a.node });

    A chain is immutable; every method returns a new chain. The chain only names actions and their parameters: bodies, places and skill belong to the performance, which plans every action and refuses an undeclared cut with the legal windows.

    The vocabulary methods are inherited in families (travel, posture, the hands, striking, partners and objects, sport), each family a class of this one's ancestry, so every action of HumanActionName is a method here and each takes its law's own parameter type.
