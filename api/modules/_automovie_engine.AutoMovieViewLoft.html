    Namespace AutoMovieViewLoft

    Loft a view part through its four signed source outlines and fullness owner. Direct shoulder/top fields retain their original scalar generators for final points, phase derivatives and available physical total2. Emitted uniform-polar cubic coefficients remain the explicit compatibility representation; generic girth retains that existing representation. Analytic frames keep actual source jets, while numerical axes retain their existing smooth plane fit.

    A top fullness declaring touch offsets or splits makes each station a conic chain (AutoMovieViewChain, through the native reader's mapping), returned in chains; its compatibility rows sample that chain and carry no four-quarter metadata, and its outlines join the same knot coverage as the extents and shoulders. Such a result declares C1 in phase and in station: a chain join moves with the station at phase theta(s), and where its two arcs' curvatures differ the radius keeps r and r_phi continuous across it while r_phiphi jumps; differentiating the two continuities along the join gives the jumps [r_sphi] = -theta' [r_phiphi] and [r_ss] = theta'^2 [r_phiphi], so C2 holds only within the smooth pieces between joins.

    Regular C2 requires the admitted scalar/section/frame derivative premises. Physical pole jets and geometric G2 at an apex are not supplied here. The surviving-layer domain and the original polar phase meaning stay unchanged.

    Broken boxes/fullness omit the station with its named numeric diagnostic. A direct source with any rejected prepared station publishes only inspection sections, because skipping a row cannot remove that invalid original field. Unreachable girth keeps its reach endpoint with a diagnostic. Fewer than two surviving sections publish no final chain. Source records and inputs remain immutable.
