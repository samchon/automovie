    Namespace AutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy

    The body's measures under their published names, every field of IAutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy: stature, tapes, segment lengths, skeletal breadths, landmark heights and fat percentage, each the person's own measure or the default its survey regression derives from the person's sex, stature and tapes.

    A default follows the input that predicts the measure best in the survey: a bone breadth the girth that wraps the same joint, a height the stature or the crotch, a breadth of the foot or hand that segment's own measure. Every coefficient is an ordinary least squares fit of the named survey's public data by sex, listed with its source and its coefficient of determination in packages/human/docs/work/parameter-anatomy-survey.md, and the trunk's breadths and depths are reproduced by the survey readings under test/src/features/human/survey/. At the chest, the natural waist and the hip, the breadth, depth and girth defaults are held inside the range the supplied measures leave in their box (AutoMovieHumanBodyBox.place), so a default never causes a contradiction.
