    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanAthletics

    The sprint start's crouch into the set position and its hold until the gun (#3250): the hands on the start line, the front foot and the rear foot on their blocks.

    The set position is solved from measured block settings and one measured angle: the anthropometry-driven setting of Cavedon et al. (2019, PLoS One 14:e0213979, table 2) puts the front block 0.60 of the leg's length behind the line and the rear block 0.45 of it behind the front, and the front knee stands at 90 degrees (their 90, SD 8; Harland and Steele 1997's "about 90"). The hands lie on the line a shoulder width apart with the four support frames fixed by the measured body. One whole-body fit (sprintStartSet) keeps those supports, the front knee at 90, rear knee within 117 +/- 11 and front hip within 43 +/- 6 degrees, and the sex-matched centre of mass behind the line. Arm extension is free, rather than a fixed fraction consuming a degree of freedom the measured posture needs. Hip height and the trunk's inclination are results of that shared constrained fit.

    The crouch takes 1.5 s (provisional): the front foot steps back to its block over the first half, the rear foot over the second, while the hips travel and the trunk pitches down; the set is held, and the action ends at the gun by default. With drive, the hands leave at the gun and the rear foot leaves before the front foot. The final contact is blockExit.

    The knee-down marks posture, the first free sprint stride, hurdles and relay exchanges are outside this block-start action.
