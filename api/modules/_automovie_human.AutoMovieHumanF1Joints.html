    Namespace AutoMovieHumanF1Joints

    f1's joint centres on the +Z side in its reference pose: the datums every authored course of the figure runs through.

    Frame: millimetres, forward +X, up +Y, +Z the stored side. Each value is stored in the frame of the segment it belongs to (AutoMovieHumanF1Placing.POINTS) and read here through the chain at the reference pose (AutoMovieHumanF1Placement.point), so this module holds no coordinate. The joint centres are the reference rig's bind joint centres, read once offline by test/src/reference-conics/FamilyConicBody.ts --emit-joints and re-based by FamilyConicRebase.ts --tables. A family names its course by these datums and holds no joint coordinate of its own, so a law that places the joints from a person's measurements replaces the table and every course follows.
