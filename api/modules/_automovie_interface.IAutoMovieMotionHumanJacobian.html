    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanJacobian

    The analytic Jacobian of a posed body: how each of a posture's variables (the root's place, its yaw, pitch and roll, and any joint angle) moves the bones, read from the one forward kinematics that posed them.

    Each variable is a twist: the bones it moves turn about a world axis through a point, or move along a direction (Siciliano et al. 2009, section 3.1.3). A bone's segment then has the twist's angular velocity and its origin the linear velocity v + ω × (o − pivot), and any point on it moves at v_origin + ω × (p − o), so a caller with many probes on few segments reads each segment's twist once (twist) and a caller with few points reads their columns directly (velocity). The root's place moves every bone along its axis; its heading turns every bone about the root, yaw about the world's up, pitch about the yawed left axis and roll about the yawed and pitched forward axis (resolveHumanPosture.heading). A joint's three angles compose its rotation in the engine's order (jointToQuaternion): twist, abduction, flexion for a proximal twist, so the flexion axis is the basis axis turned by the twist and the abduction before it, and abduction, flexion, twist for a distal one. The frame before the joint is its parent's world rotation turned by its rest rotation, and a clinical angle is the rig's angle mapped by the rest frame, so a turn per clinical degree is the axis scaled by the frame's sign. The angles are the clamped ones forward kinematics reads, so at a range limit the motion is the one the joint makes inward.
