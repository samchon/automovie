    Namespace AutoMovieHumanArmLaw

    The arm's law of one side: its bones, walk and tube from the arm's pieces, and the family of section planes the chain is cut by.

    The bones stand on the glenohumeral centre at their published definitions (AutoMovieHumanArmBones), the walk on the bones turns at the elbow over a length scaled by the elbow's radius (AutoMovieHumanArmAxis), and the tube about the walk is the spans' guides, sectors and girth knots (AutoMovieHumanArmPieces, AutoMovieHumanArmGuides, AutoMovieHumanArmGirth, AutoMovieHumanArmTube), each girth input honoured at its site. The parameter is the hand's station -a up to the wrist crease, where the hand's heel piece takes over (AutoMovieHumanHandHeel), and past it the walk's distance back toward the shoulder, so it rises from the hand to the shoulder; the planes turn from the hand's axis onto the forearm's over WRIST_TURN. The arm's own square sections end one scye radius down the walk from the trunk's level ring, and the root strip (AutoMovieHumanLimbRootStrip) carries the skin from there to the ring, its parameter running on as the strip's mean chord.
