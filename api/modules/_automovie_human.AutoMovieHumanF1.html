    Namespace AutoMovieHumanF1

    The f1 figure in its reference pose, generated from its authored conic families alone: no reference mesh, recovered curve or observation file is read anywhere on this path.

    The body is one sewn skin, built by the engine's construction operators: the trunk from the crotch to the crown, the nose's free end, and on each side an arm that runs from the armpit's sagittal plane round the shoulder and through the wrist to the finger webs, a leg that runs from below the crotch round the ankle to the toe webs, an ear, five fingers and five toes. The trunk's open lower end is split into the two legs along the midsagittal chord, and each arm, each breast and the nose is attached at a port on the trunk; those bands span from a curve on the trunk to a ring of the part and meet both G2 without a traced intersection. Each finger and toe is a capsule merged onto its limb's cap along the curve where it comes out, and each ear is its plate with the helix and the antihelix merged on it as tubes, merged onto the head along the curve round the plate's root. The crown, each hand, each foot, each ear, the nose and each digit close in a pole.

    The hand's sections leave out the thumb past where it stands free, as the trunk's leave out the arms, the ears and the nose. The face's eyes and mouth are height fields over the trunk's wall. A hollow with a wall of its own is a loft cut out: each nostril and each ear's bowl from the head.

    Every family is placed through f1's segment frames (assemble): its course, its chart's up and its reliefs' directions are stored in the frames of the segments they belong to (AutoMovieHumanF1Placing) and mapped to the figure's frame at a pose, the reference pose unless another is given. Whatever reads the figure's frame here (the port loops, the fan seams, the fills) reads it through those frames.
