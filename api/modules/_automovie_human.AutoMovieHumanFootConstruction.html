    Namespace AutoMovieHumanFootConstruction

    Both feet generated from foot length, foot breadth and lateral malleolus height by one construction in the left foot's frame and a declared reflection (AutoMovieHumanFootReflection); each side takes its own dimensions into the same construction.

    This module only composes the foot's pieces, each in its own file under ./foot:

    • AutoMovieHumanFootBones: the anatomical centres, before any skin, which bones hands to the skeleton.
    • AutoMovieHumanFootFan: the body's section planes.
    • AutoMovieHumanFootGuide: the five guides, each owning its junction's jet.
    • AutoMovieHumanFootBody: each section from the dorsal arcs, the lateral border's plantar piece, the sole's contact and the medial arch.
    • AutoMovieHumanFootMalleolus: the malleoli's relief windows.
    • AutoMovieHumanFootBodyChain: the body's chain to the ankle ring.
    • AutoMovieHumanFootWebRing: the ring the toes leave through the webs' surface (AutoMovieHumanBodyWeb, event web: true).
    • AutoMovieHumanFootToeSpan, AutoMovieHumanFootToeSection and AutoMovieHumanFootToeChain: each toe on its line.

    The ankle centre stands at the origin here; the stance belongs to AutoMovieHumanLegConstruction.stand.
