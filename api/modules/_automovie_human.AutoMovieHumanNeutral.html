    Namespace AutoMovieHumanNeutral

    The anatomical neutral of a generated body, derived from its bind skeleton.

    Generation achieves a posture: an A-pose with relaxed hands. That posture is an observation and the skin's bind, not a clinical zero. Neutral is the standing body the clinical angles are measured from (the Neutral-0 position of DGUV F 4030: upright, arms hanging, thumbs forward, feet parallel), reached from the bind by one rotation per bone, and those rotations are the body's achieved offset. A bone that no piece names rides its parent, so its offset is zero. Each joint's rule is its own piece, composed here parent before child; a hinge whose neutral is straight on its parent (Neutral-0: extension is the zero of flexion) is AutoMovieHumanNeutralTurn.straighten of its bone along the limb's line:

    • Arm: AutoMovieHumanNeutralShoulder (resting at the side), the elbow straight (the forearm on the arm's line), AutoMovieHumanNeutralForearm (thumbs forward) and the wrist straight (the hand on the forearm's line, before the forearm's twist).
    • Thumb: AutoMovieHumanNeutralThumbCmc (toward the index metacarpal, clear of the hand), and its two hinges straight on the line it lies along.
    • Fingers: AutoMovieHumanNeutralKnuckle, its two interphalangeal hinges straight, the finger straight on the hand, parted by the least spread at which neighbours do not cross.
    • Leg: AutoMovieHumanNeutralHip (the least abduction at which the thighs do not cross), the knee straight (the shank plumb under the knee) and AutoMovieHumanNeutralAnkle (the sole level, the foot straight ahead).
    • Spine, neck, head, jaw, eyes, girdle: as they stand. A standing column is its own neutral, and the head is built in the Frankfort plane, so no reference says otherwise.

    The rig's rest stays the bind, since a skin has one rest frame: neutral is a pose of the rig, and every clinical pose is a further pose of neutral.
