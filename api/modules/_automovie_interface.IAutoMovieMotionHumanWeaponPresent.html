    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanWeaponPresent

    Handling a firearm with the weapon as the effector: presenting it to an aim or a ready position, the shot's recoil, and the draw and the return to a holster.

    The firearm is a prop the hand holds (HumanObject.prop of a handgun or a long gun, taken by its grip); presenting and firing are the generalised launcher laws of HumanWeaponPresent under the firearm's names, reading the gun's muzzle and sight and aiming from the eye (a handgun) or the cheek (a long gun) unless a row names another tip, edge or anchor.

    A shot is three actions in a chain: draw (a pick up of the holstered pistol by its grip), present (the muzzle brought onto the line from the eye to the target and held) and fire (the trigger's press, the muzzle's rise about the hand and its return onto the line). present with a ready position lowers the weapon, and holster puts it back.

    A handgun is held in both hands unless one is asked, the support hand wrapped beside the shooting hand; a long gun (a prop that declares a foreEnd) takes the support hand on its fore-end and is refused in one hand. A target more than TURN degrees off the heading is refused: the performer turns first.

    First pass: the target is read once at the action's start, the holster is a prop where the scene stands it and does not ride the body, and no reload is planned. The recoil's size and times are provisional: no recoil-recovery study was read (#3348 lists it as research to do).
