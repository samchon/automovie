    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanAuditTangle

    How deep a displayed body's own parts pass into one another: the tangle a limb crossing through the trunk, a hand buried in the head or two legs run through each other shows on screen.

    Every collider segment carries probes: the corners of its shapes as drawn, and three points along its bone from its joint to each child joint, so a limb that crosses another like an X, with no corner inside, is still read by its axis. A probe's depth in another segment is how far it lies below that segment's surface, exactly for a convex hull (the least distance to its face planes) and by the engine's shape distance otherwise.

    Two readings are removed because they are the body's build and not the motion's doing:

    • pairs that are one part or share a joint: a bone and its parent, or the nearest ancestor that carries a collider when the bones between carry none (the upper arm and the chest meet at the shoulder girdle, whose clavicle bone has no collider of its own); their colliders overlap at the joint by construction and fold into each other as it bends, within its range of motion. Also the hand with its own fingers, and the foot with its toes;
    • what two segments already share in the body's two reference postures, the rest T-pose it was modelled in and the quiet stance every performer starts from (stand.posture): the thighs meet at the crotch as the body was modelled, and the hanging upper arm lies against the ribs, where flesh gives and a convex collider does not. Two such surfaces slide along each other as the body moves (a walking arm passes the flank it hangs against), so a corner's depth counts only beyond the deepest any corner of its segment lies in the other in either reference posture: the practice of a self-collision matrix, which sets aside the pairs that collide in the default pose (Chitta, Sucan and Cousins 2012, MoveIt, IEEE Robot Autom Mag 19:18). Where that reference depth is itself more than the skin gives (BUILT), the two convex colliders occupy one volume by their build, as the upper arm's and the trunk's do along the flank, and their corners are set aside altogether, as that matrix does; below it the reference depth is kept as the allowance. A point of a bone's axis keeps its own reference depth in every pair, so a limb that crosses another is read in full however much their surfaces share. A tangle of the quiet stance itself is not this reading's to find.

    A caller may also exempt the hand a law declares in contact with the person's own body from the bone that carries the touched region, for the span of that contact window (exemptions): the hand is meant to lie there. An arm's segment a law declares lying on a segment of the same person (a self-hug's forearm on the other forearm or on the chest) is read against that segment only beyond BUILT over its window: a resting forearm may press the skin as far as it gives, and no deeper.
