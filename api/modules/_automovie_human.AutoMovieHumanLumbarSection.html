    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLumbarSection

    The lumbar section's box at a girth: the breadth and depth of the trunk at a level between the natural waist and the hip that has a girth and no published breadth or depth, such as ISO 8559-1 5.3.12's top hip level and iliocristale.

    Source: the navel section, the one lumbar level where a survey takes a breadth and a depth with the girth: ANSUR 1988's waist breadth and depth at omphalion, each on the omphalion girth by ordinary least squares by sex (AutoMovieHumanBodyBreadths.WAIST_BREADTH_ON_WAIST, WAIST_DEPTH_ON_WAIST; R2 0.85 to 0.92; test_human_survey_waist_section). Omphalion lies between the natural waist and the top hip level of every surveyed adult (ANSUR II omphalion at 0.6017 and 0.6014 of stature, docs/sources/ansur-ii-trunk-ratios.md). Derivation: the two lines evaluated at the level's own girth, then held so the girth lies inside their box (AutoMovieHumanBodyBox.place), premised on the lumbar section keeping the navel section's law over the levels it serves. The natural waist's default breadth and depth are the same law.
