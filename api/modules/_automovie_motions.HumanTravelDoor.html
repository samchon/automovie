    Namespace HumanTravelDoor

    Going through a doorway: a walk that squares up before a named opening of one of the performance's obstacles, passes through its middle and comes out beyond it. Register HumanTravelDoor.passThrough to use it.

    The walker passes two places on the opening's own line, one of its own steps at its pace before the sill and one beyond it (walk.walking, the step 1.2 l Fr^0.25 of Alexander's walking model), so the last footfall before the opening and the first after it lie on that line and the body crosses the opening at right angles to its width wherever the walk began. The walk itself asks the obstacles about each stretch (walkClear), so an opening narrower than the walker's chest or lower than the walker stands is refused there with the measure it would need. First pass: the leaf of a door is not opened or held; an opening is a hole.
