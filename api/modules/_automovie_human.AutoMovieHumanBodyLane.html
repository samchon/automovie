    Namespace AutoMovieHumanBodyLane

    The body lane's subtree (#3047 to #3051) as one construction tree realized by the engine (AutoMovieHumanBodyTree): every part a view node built from its views, every join a merge node with a search seed and fold widths, published as chains and the realized body for the calibration, from the laws the caller binds.

    Parts: the trunk with its breasts and gluteal masses (AutoMovieHumanTrunkBuild), each leg as one walk over femur and tibia (AutoMovieHumanLegWalk), each arm with its palm as one walk (AutoMovieHumanArmWalk), each foot (AutoMovieHumanFootPart), each finger and thumb as a joint-normal walk (AutoMovieHumanDigitWalk), and each toe in its fixed foot-axis observation planes (AutoMovieHumanToePart).

    Joins, the part being the solid whose station lines leave the host across the fold, the host the whole subtree built before it: masses after their trunk and same-leg parents; each toe onto its foot, the leg onto that foot (seeded at the ankle's front), and the leg with its foot onto the trunk (seeded at the inguinal fold, R the caller's operational join softness, capped at half the gap between the two hip centres so the two legs' rails never cross at the perineum); each digit onto its arm's palm, and the arm with its hand onto the trunk (seeded at the axilla vault). A digit's or a toe's web is capped at half the gap to its nearest neighbour's base, so neighbouring webs' rails never cross.

    Failed original definitions keep their diagnostics. Admitted originals remain attempt leaves: the engine performs their lofts and names failed leaves/joins and its rollback. A source tree is not a completed skin.
