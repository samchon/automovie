    Namespace AutoMovieHumanArmRootArc

    The arm's arc of the shoulder ring: where the arm's horizontal ellipse leaves the torso and returns to it, as two sectors between the anterior and the posterior axillary guides through the lateral guide at the ellipse's outer extreme.

    The guides are the axillary folds. At the vault they are the torso's own anterior and posterior axillary guides, so the ring there is the torso's outside the arc and the trunk's top ring, which crosses the armpit below, agrees with it on every sector the arm leaves alone. As the arm merges with the shoulder they move, by the torso weight (1 at the vault, 0 at the glenohumeral centre's height), to where the ellipse crosses the torso's quadrant: the anterior at CROSS of the ellipse's forward half size ahead of its centre, the posterior as far behind it. Each guide keeps the torso's own jet there, so the arc joins the torso's sectors G1 and the fold's softness is the arc's own. The lateral guide is the ellipse's extreme: tangent along the body, curvature rz / rx^2. Each sector is the quintic Hermite of its guides whose end speed is the arc length of a quarter turn over the chord, pi / (2 sqrt 2) times the chord (a quarter circle's arc over its chord).
