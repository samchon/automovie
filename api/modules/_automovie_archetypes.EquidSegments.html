    Namespace EquidSegments

    Equids' limb and back segments measured on the body's surface, by breed, and the bone fractions an equid derives from them against the horse's.

    The measures are Brooks et al. 2010, Morphological variation in the horse: defining complex traits of body size and shape, Animal Genetics 41 (Suppl. 2):159, its protocol read at p. 160 (https://srvanderplas.github.io/stat-computing-r-python/data/bonus_horse_morphometric_dataset/Brooks_et_al-2010-Animal_Genetics.pdf); each breed's means are computed from the paper's published per-animal rows, in inches, converted at 2.54 cm (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/srvanderplas/stat-computing-r-python/main/data/bonus_horse_morphometric_dataset/Brooks Sutter Horse Morphometrics.txt): no paper prints these means, so they are this campaign's arithmetic on the paper's data. The forearm runs from the point of the elbow to the back of the knee, the cannons from the knee or the point of the hock to the ergot, the gaskin from the stifle to the point of the hock, and the back from the withers to the point of the croup; none is a bone's length, and neither the humerus nor the femur is measured.
