    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkGuides

    The trunk section's seven guides of the canonical left side and the six sectors between them, as one table: each guide a longitudinal reference line at a fixed knot of the 256-control section with the lateral share it stands at, each sector the quintic Hermite of its two guides' jets at the quadrant's own speeds (AutoMovieHumanTrunkQuadrant.between), so it meets both with their point, tangent and curvature (G2) and holds no number of its own. The reference lines are Gray 1918's and ISO 8559-1's (docs/techniques/surface-composition.md, Sectors and guides):

    • front midline M, knot 0: the linea alba and the sternum. It owns the front place, the tangent across the midline and the curvature of the front quadrant's parabola through the bust line guide, the crotch fold's curvature added.
    • bust line K, knot 24: the mid-clavicular vertical through the bust point at the chest, at the clavicle's midpoint, half of the sternoclavicular joint's and the acromion's half breadths (about 20 and 183 mm, ANSUR II biacromial breadth), over the chest's half breadth below the axilla (about 150 mm on the four references): share 0.65.
    • anterior axillary line A, knot 46: the lower border of pectoralis major from the anterior axillary fold point, at the section's own lateral share, which the front interscye length sets at the axilla and which falls back to 0.9 below it. Its knot is where the arm's arc of the shoulder ring starts.
    • mid-axillary line L, knot 70: the section's widest point. Its tangent runs straight back; its curvature is the mean of the front and back quadrants' parabolas through the anterior and posterior axillary guides, declared once so both flank sectors share it; the crotch fold has ended before it.
    • posterior axillary line P, knot 94: latissimus dorsi and teres major down from the posterior axillary fold point, at share 0.9 or the section's own. Its knot is where the arm's arc of the shoulder ring ends.
    • scapular line E, knot 108: the vertical through the inferior scapular angle, the erector column's lateral edge below it, the inferior angle about 80 mm off the midline (Gray 1918) over the chest's half breadth: share 0.55.
    • back midline S, knot 128: the spinous processes and the sacrum. It owns the back place, the tangent across the midline and the curvature of the back quadrant's parabola through the scapular guide, the gluteal cleft's curvature added.

    The sectors, each after the guide of the same index:

    • sternal, M to K: the linea alba and rectus below, the sternum and the pectorals' medial part above; the crotch fold acts on it.
    • pectoral, K to A: pectoralis major over the ribs above, the rectus' lateral edge and the oblique below; the crotch fold acts on it and ends at A.
    • anterior flank, A to L: serratus anterior and the external oblique over the ribs, the iliac roll at the pelvis.
    • posterior flank, L to P: latissimus dorsi over the ribs, the iliac crest's back at the pelvis.
    • scapular, P to E: the scapula's lateral border and teres major above, latissimus below, the gluteus medius at the pelvis; the gluteal cleft's displacement ends at P.
    • erector, E to S: the erector spinae column, the sacrum and the gluteal cleft at the pelvis; the spinal furrow is a relief over it, not this arc.
