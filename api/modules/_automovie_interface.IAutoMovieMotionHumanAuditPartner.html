    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanAuditPartner

    How deep two people's bodies pass into one another: the hand that sinks into a partner's shoulder, the fist that ends inside a head, the two trunks of an embrace run through each other.

    It is the two-body counterpart of the self-penetration audit and reads the same probes against the same colliders, in both directions, since a small part may lie wholly inside a large one whose own corners stay outside. Nothing is removed from the reading: two bodies share no joint and no rest pose, and a declared contact between them is a statement that the skins meet, never that one body may enter the other. How much a pressed skin gives is the caller's limit to state.
