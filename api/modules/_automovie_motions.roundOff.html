    Namespace roundOff

    A round-off (#3377): a cartwheel that travels along the way the body faced, turns half round about the vertical and lands on both feet at once, facing back where it came from, the entry to a back handspring or a back salto.

    The body rolls as the cartwheel's wheel (GymnasticsWheel): the arms raised wide and the legs straddled over the first cartwheel.SHAPE degrees, the lowest spoke bearing at every angle. On the way to the hands it turns a quarter about the vertical through what bears, so the wheel rolls along the heading; past the handstand it turns the second quarter about the bearing hand while the legs close, so both feet come down together, knees bent to take the landing. The roll is one axis and each quarter turn is another, about the vertical, and the two run at once while a quarter is being made (over the wheel's first ENTRY degrees and over SNAP degrees past the handstand): the posture's heading turns and its roll grows in the same instant, composed as heading then roll, not solved as one rotation about a moving axis (#3416 keeps that multi-axis case).

    The angle follows minimum-jerk progress over the seconds; nothing is integrated, and no hurdle step, push off the second hand or flight before the landing is shown, a first pass.

    Events: handsDown (the first hand bears), inverted (half the wheel), feetDown (the feet bear again), relax (the end).
