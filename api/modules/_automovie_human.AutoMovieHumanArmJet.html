    Namespace AutoMovieHumanArmJet

    Second-order forward-mode jets for the arm's posed course (AutoMovieHumanArmF1Pose): a scalar jet is a value with its first and second derivatives in one parameter, [f, f', f''], and every operation applies the exact chain and product rules (Leibniz to second order), so a law written once on jets yields its value and its analytic derivatives together, with rounding error only (Griewank and Walther 2008, Evaluating Derivatives, ch. 13, univariate Taylor propagation). A vector jet is three scalar jets.
