    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanTouchEffectors

    A touch while standing: one hand, or both, laid on a region of a partner or of the performer's own body and moved on it by a kind of social touch (Jung et al. 2017): a tap, a pat, a stroke, a rub, a press, a squeeze, a hold or a scratch. It is the one law of the affectionate and social touch family (#3284: a pat on the head, a tap on the shoulder, a hand on the arm) and of the self-directed family (#3285: a belly pat, a nape rub, a forearm scratch, a self hug, hands over the ears), each item a row of (effector, region, kind, timing).

    The hand reaches the region in the free-space transport time of the distance (AutoMovieMotionHumanTiming.transport), repeats the kind's stroke count times (AutoMovieMotionHumanTouchForm.FORMS), dwells for the hold and goes back in the reach's time; the manner's tempo scales every span and its extent the lift and the travel. The feet stay planted and the body keeps the entry posture. A region of the performer's limbs is read on the entry pose, so it must ride a limb the touch does not move, except a region on the other arm: a person scratching or patting their own forearm first brings it up, so that arm is carried on the same envelope to its forearm held across the front of the waist, palm down, and the region is read on the arm held there (present). A region above the shoulder that the standing arm does not reach (a taller person's cheek) is risen to on the toes, and a region beyond the standing arm (a thigh slapped while standing) is leaned into, each as far as the hand needs (AutoMovieMotionHumanTouchStance), a leg's region then read on the legs as the lean leaves them; one still out of reach at the lean's limit is refused. A seated or carried body does not rise.

    Events: the phrase instants (ready on the region, strokeStart to strokeEnd the repetitions, relax leaving). A touch may be cut before it arrives or from relax on, into standing, a walk, a gesture or another touch, the declined handshake's rule.
