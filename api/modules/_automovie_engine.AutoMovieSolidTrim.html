    Namespace AutoMovieSolidTrim

    A through-cut: an opening in the skin (the palpebral fissure, the oral fissure) as a closed trim loop in the skin's own chart.

    Nothing lies behind such a cut for a blend to reach, so it is not a diff: the margin is a closed curve on the surface and the chart's domain gets it as a hole (Sederberg et al. 2008, watertight trims as shared parameter curves). The margin's points come from the landmarks that define it (endocanthion, palpebrale superius, exocanthion, palpebrale inferius and the courses between them; the cheilia and the stomion line), each taken to its foot on the surface (AutoMovieSolidFoot), and the loop is the periodic cubic B-spline through the feet at uniform loop parameters (AutoMovieSplineCurve.interpolate), so it is C2 in the chart and on the skin, and passes through its feet to the inverse filter's stated numerical accuracy. The skin is the surface outside the loop; its tessellation stops at the loop's points, which the margin's consumers (a lid band, a rolled rim, a lining) read from the same function.

    A rolled rim, where the skin turns into the opening (the lid margin's rounded edge, the vermilion's inner turn), is one more blend (AutoMovieSolidBlend) from ITrim.ribbon, the skin's jet across the loop toward the hole, to the inner surface's ribbon.

    Landmarks must be ordered on one chart sheet, with any periodic phase unwrapped consistently by the guesses. Admission checks only a 16-sample-per-span polygon's proper crossings and sampled chart regularity; it does not certify the entire cubic loop is simple or regular between those samples.
