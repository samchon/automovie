    Namespace AutoMovieMotionHumanBend

    Bend hints: the world point an elbow or a knee bends toward, from a named preset on a posture.

    A limb goal places the wrist or the ankle and leaves one freedom, the turn of the middle joint about the line from the limb's root to its end. The hint fixes it. An elbow's hint is a point beside the shoulder, stated in upper-arm lengths outward, upward and forward of the shoulder's rest place and carried by the posture's root rotation, so it follows a turned and a leaned trunk. A knee's hint is a point one metre ahead of the knee's rest place along the foot's heading, so the knee bends over the foot (Perry and Burnfield, Gait Analysis, 2nd ed.).

    A law takes a preset and passes its own point only where its action bends the limb another way, with the reason in a comment. A hand goal that names no preset takes the elbow place solve finds: the one of least joint strain among the places the ranges of motion and the trunk allow. The search over those places is AutoMovieMotionHumanBendSearch's, on the arm chain AutoMovieMotionHumanBendChain prepares.
