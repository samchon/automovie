    Namespace AutoMovieHumanBodyWeb

    The skin between a parent's ring and children that start apart from it: the webs of a hand.

    A child that inherits its parent's arcs keeps their corners in its own sections for as long as it takes to round them, and two children that part at a pair of pinches meet across a chord that leaves the parent's surface at a right angle. Here each child starts from its own section past its webs, round from its first ring, and the skin from the parent's ring to those sections is built here as one surface with a saddle at every web.

    The surface is meshed in rows and columns. A row is a level of depth on the way from the parent's ring (row 0) to the children's sections (the last row); a column runs down that way from one vertex of the parent's ring to its child's section, the steepest line of the rows. Each child owns the columns of its arcs, and they arrive on its section in proportion to their length along the arc. At a web the two pinches' columns are the web's margin: a quarter round from each pinch, leaving it along the parent's own surface, to the web's crest S, which stands half the pinches' chord past their middle, so the margin is round across the hand. Above the crest's row the two children are one sheet, the parent continued, and the pinches' columns are its lips; from S each child's own column runs down to the point of its section that is level with the crest across the margin, leaving S across the web. Those four separatrices cross at S, eight cells meet there, and the skin about S is the saddle x^2 - y^2.

    Near a web a column's level and its distance along the ring from the pinch are the chart z = x + i y of the saddle at S: x the level from the crest's row in margin radii, y the distance over the margin's radius. The branch point is w = u + i v = sqrt(z). A column there is the translational surface of the web's separatrices, F(u, v) = D(u) + lip(v) - S, which near S is the saddle S + alpha u E_eta + beta v E_xi + rho (u^2 - v^2) E_a and on the chart's axes the separatrices themselves, so the columns about a pinch stay ordered however close they run; its ends are moved onto its own ring vertex and arrival in proportion to the level. Away from the web a column is the Hermite from the parent's surface to the child's, leaving the ring along the parent's station derivative and arriving along the child's, and the two blend over FADE margin radii.

    The mesh is even in w, not z, about every crest: the rows' levels close on the crest as the square of the row, and the parent's ring takes phases about each pinch at distances growing as the square of their count (AutoMovieHumanBodySurface), so the cells about S are as small as the margin's radius needs. Even in z they stretched there by the square root, to edges of 4 mm across a 2.5 mm saddle.
