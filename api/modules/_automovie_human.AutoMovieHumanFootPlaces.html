    Namespace AutoMovieHumanFootPlaces

    The standing foot's observed yaw, ankle skin-loop place, dorsum heights and toe skin reaches (TOES, from test_human_estimate_sites). Their source normalizers differ: body-X projection for ankle/dorsum, rotated heel-to-foremost length for toes.

    Estimated from the private reference set; no published source found. The reading test_human_estimate_body_places aggregates six of the reference feet (f1, f2, m1, m2, where the foot reads) on their horizontal sections: the yaw of the heel's rearmost to the toe's foremost point, toe out, 2.30 (SD 1.02) degrees; the ankle loop's centre 0.1706 (SD 0.0326) of the foot's body-X span ahead of the heel; and the dorsum's height, the highest section whose loop spans a body-X place, divided by that same projected span, at the forefoot's projected shares (FOREFOOT). The dorsum at half that projected span reads 0.209 (SD 0.063). AutoMovieHumanFootArch instead uses a published standing ratio, 0.214 of foot length. These distinct length normalizers and station definitions do not establish numeric agreement.

    Limits: six feet of four people; the sections stand 10 mm apart, so the heights are read to about 0.04 of the foot's length; the replacement stays open. The observed loop centre is not an independently measured ankle joint centre; applying its offset to that joint is a placement assumption. Reusing body-X ratios as rotated along-axis ratios is another assumption, not an exact frame conversion recovered by these aggregates.
