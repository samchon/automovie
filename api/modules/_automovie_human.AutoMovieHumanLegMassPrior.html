    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLegMassPrior

    Where the leg's mass stands about its bone: at each station share of a segment, the share of the section's front-to-back depth that lies ahead of the bone's line, the front share. A shape prior in normalized part coordinates, shared by every person.

    Direction from cross-sectional anatomy (Gray 1918): the quadriceps stand ahead of the femur and the hamstrings behind it, with the adductors medial, so the thigh's mass lies a little ahead of its bone; the gastrocnemius and soleus stand behind the tibia, whose anterior border is subcutaneous, so the shank's mass lies well behind its bone and comes forward toward the ankle, where the calf's bellies give way to their tendon.

    Fitted on the references, so a prior awaiting a published law: the mean of f1, f2, m1 and m2's level leg_l sections read against their own rigs (by the historical reference reader), by bins a tenth of the segment long, each person's own reading predicted within about 0.025 by the other three. The proximal tenth of the thigh lies above the crotch and carries no section; the joints' own tenths are left out.

    The deleted reader remains in git at 3672a547f0, path test/src/features/human/reference/curves/test_human_reference_limb_mass.ts. It sampled each cubic section span eight times, took the longest level loop's body-X supports and used q=(front-boneX)/(front-back), with boneX linearly interpolated on that reference's old rig. Its quality="all" included suspect/broken source curves. This is sampled support, not an exact continuous extreme, and does not establish the present pure joints or bone-normal frame. Transferring q to that frame remains a placement proxy. The retained table's final aggregation weighting was not recovered by finding this reader; no new fit or private person reading was made.
