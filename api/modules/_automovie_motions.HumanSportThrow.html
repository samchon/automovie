    Namespace HumanSportThrow

    Throws, catches, kicks, jumps and runs of sport under their names, each a row of the shipped law that plans it. The composition gives what the law leaves open: the ball the hand holds and what it is thrown at, the ball kicked and where, the thrower caught from, the place jumped or run to.

    The laws own the physics a row inherits: a throw's kinetic chain and the ball's ballistic flight from its release, a catch's interception, a kick's ball flight, a jump's flight from its take-off speed. First pass: a row fixes only the style its name means; an event's own technique (a pitcher's windup, a jump shot's leap before the release, a hurdle's trail leg) is not planned, and the row says so; a high jump's arch over the bar is HumanGymnastics.fosburyFlop.
