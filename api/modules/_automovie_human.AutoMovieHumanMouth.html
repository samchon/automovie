    Namespace AutoMovieHumanMouth

    The mouth of one person: the oral fissure the skin is opened along, and the frame the teeth, the tongue and the cavity's lining stand in, all placed from the head's own lips in calibration millimetres (forward +X, up +Y, left +Z).

    Centres first. The lips' contact line, which the head construction draws, is the fissure: a slit along it, a stadium in the face's plane from one cheilion to the other whose half-height is the 0.5 mm station spacing the skin takes near an opening, so no skin edge steps over it and its rim is one loop. The lips meet at rest: across the slit the lining's two inner faces come together at the contact line, so no gap and no tooth shows until the lips part. The upper incisors stand behind the upper lip by the lip's published thickness, their edges below the lip by the published rest display; the occlusal plane runs back from them at Downs' cant. Teeth take their crown sizes from the dental table and stand side by side on the beta arch, the lower arch inside the upper by the overjet. The cavity's lining runs from the slit round the outside of the gums to the pharynx above the angles of the jaw, and the tongue fills its floor inside the lower arch.

    Everything here is data, so the calibration that carries it can be cloned and compared.
