    Namespace StrikingStance

    The fighting stance uses separate tracks, soft knees, a leading shoulder and glove goals measured from the performer's own head.

    IBA coaching manual pp. 34 to 39 and the kickboxing/Muay Thai guides in #3201 give these relations; the numbers are named readings:

    • stance length along the line 1.2 shoulder widths (guides: 1.0 to 1.3);
    • feet on their own hip tracks, spaced by the hip joints;
    • 30 degree body turn with lead shoulder forward, lead toe 15 degrees toward that heading and rear toe 45 degrees out (IBA p.34 readings);
    • a sunk root flexes both knees 32 degrees, within the 25 to 45 degree band;
    • both gloves at ear height the same short distance ahead of their jaw, the rear one outside its ear and the lead one before its own shoulder, chin tucked and eyes on the opponent (IABA, A Beginner's Guide: "tuck your chin down and look forward"; the boxing and MMA guard as coached: the rear hand "as if holding a phone to your ear and then pushed forward just in front of your face", the front hand "at the same height"; the Muay Thai guard: "arms projected out further", "nearly vertical forearms"). The resting arms leave the ribs to be defended by an action: a body kick is checked with the raised shin and the elbow brought down to the knee.

    Orthodox leads left and southpaw right; distances read the body's measure.
