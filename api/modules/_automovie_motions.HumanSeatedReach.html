    Namespace HumanSeatedReach

    A seated reach with the trunk below the arm in task priority: the hand's target first, and when the arm alone falls short, a lean of the trunk forward, which the seat bears: the hips flex the trunk over the seat and the spine's three joints flex above them, each within the flexion room its range leaves. The joints share the lean in one share of each one's room, and two seated partners in one share of each one's chain, the joint-limit-weighted least-norm resolution of the redundancy (Chan and Dubey 1995, "A weighted least-norm solution based scheme for avoiding joint limits for redundant joint manipulators", IEEE Trans. Robot. Autom. 11(2)): each joint's motion weighted by its range, so none reaches its limit before another.

    A standing body leans none here: its reach stays its arm's, as the standing laws already plan it.
