    Namespace AutoMovieHumanEarPlace

    The ear's place on the head: how far the auricle's top stands above the tragion, which with the tragion's own height (AutoMovieHumanHeadArcs.tragionDrop) places the ear, replacing the head prior's observed ear.drop; its fore-aft place (FORWARD, for ear.forward) and the concha's depth (CONCHA_ON_PROTRUSION) are reference estimates.

    Source: ANSUR 1988 (Gordon et al. 1989; 2,208 women and 1,774 men) takes the ear length above tragion with the ear length; the line by sex (test_human_survey_head_arcs): 6.96 + 0.3645 L in women and 8.12 + 0.3673 L in men, R2 0.34 and 0.40, residual SD 1.9 mm; the means are 28.7 (2.3) and 31.9 (2.5) mm. The retained ANSUR survey and the public Cho CT ear length use distinct protocols; this file does not establish their orientation equivalence. HeadLane and its source observer use aboveTragion. The current EarFrame no longer reads FORWARD; forward and concha have no production caller.
