    Namespace AutoMovieHumanHeadDome

    A small part rising from its base to an apex along its own axis, drawn from its views: the shared form of the face's masses (brow ridge, zygoma, cheek, chin pad, jaw angle), the nose's and the lips' bodies, the auricle, and of the cutters that hollow the face (orbit, temple, sulci).

    In the part's frame the unit axis w points from the cap base toward its apex, inward for a cutter; u is the footprint's long direction and v = u x w. A constant-footprint stem starts behind the nominal cap base by twice the host-sagitta estimate. Each cap extent falls to the apex along the quarter super-ellipse e(s) = e0 (1 - (s/h)^p)^(1/p), the part's projection profile in its side views, and the apex is the part's pole. Each quarter's fullness is one conic shoulder. The declared profile is preserved even for a shallow cap: an engine join limitation must remain a join diagnostic rather than reshape its source.
