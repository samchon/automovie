    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkViewPart

    The trunk base as a part lofted from its own views (campaign #3044, construction step 1; #3047): the chain trunk from the perineum's pole to the neck, every station a level section that touches the box of the front view's half breadth and the side view's two profiles.

    Views to the kernel's chart. The planes are level. TrunkAxis adds the actual back and AP radius in their common root/angle source parameter, retaining both jets at their combined knots; world Y stays physical height. u forward and v left, so the kernel's front pair (the extremes along u) is the anatomical side view and its side pair (along v) the anatomical front view. Every edge is given relative to the axis's origin, the mid-depth c = (front + back) / 2 at each named site: its profiles become +-(front - back) / 2, and the lateral outline +-half, the right side the left's reflection (J6). At the perineum all four meet and the chain closes there by its pole.

    Fullness and stations follow AutoMovieHumanSiteViewPart: a tape's site solves its common conic shoulder on its own box, so every trunk tape reads back at its site; an untaped site keeps its sourced shoulder. The box is symmetric along u about the mid-depth, so one common shoulder serves the four quarters with no departure until a sourced law of the front and back quarters lands. The head supplies the same upper scalar-support datum as before, preserving the original angle parameter and lower polynomials. A shared neck ends the actual part at its regular connection: the support above that end is not a lofted cap. Signed scalar hull ranges retain the original noncrossing support span. Its upper support tangent is the secant from the last neck radius to the pole in the shared angle parameter, with zero second parameter derivative. It is nonzero; a range filter must not flatten a wrong-way pole tangent to zero. Physical station derivatives remain singular there, and this source condition is not a final-surface G2 proof.
