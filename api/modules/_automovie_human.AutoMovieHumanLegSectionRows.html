    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLegSectionRows

    The landmark rows of the leg's section, as one table: the eight guides' [rho, rho_theta, rho_thetatheta] and the sixteen sectors' [fullness] (a thigh and a shank compartment after each guide), the medians over the four reference bodies f1, f2, m1 and m2 of the horizontal sections of the left leg (the legs stand within 3.7 degrees of vertical). A guide's values are read about each section's area centroid by least squares of a quadratic over +-12 degrees about its angle, rho in units of the section's girth over 2 pi and its derivatives in the section angle (radians); a sector's fullness is its bulge against the quintic Hermite arc of its two guides' jets, normal to its chord at its middle (AutoMovieHumanChordArc), outward positive. The thigh rows stand at 0.25 to 1 of the thigh from the hip centre (0.18 is the crotch ring), the shank rows at 0.07 to 0.9 of the shank from the knee centre. AutoMovieHumanLegGuides carries every channel between its rows by the natural cubic spline (C2). Each entry's note gives the structures it runs over and the largest held-out deviation of a person's value from the median of the other three.
