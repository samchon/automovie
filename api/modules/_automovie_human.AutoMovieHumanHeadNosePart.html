    Namespace AutoMovieHumanHeadNosePart

    The nose's body (the dorsum from the nasion down to the tip and the columella under it, without the tip's ball and the alar lobules, which are its own parts) as a part drawn from its views, in level sections as the reference's skin is read.

    • Side view: the soft-tissue profile follows two straight chords that meet at pronasale (a declared convention, DEBT; see profile), nasion-pronasale and pronasale-subnasale, rounded at the tip over the tip lobule's own radius and buried at both poles (see profile).
    • Front view: the dorsum's half width, the prior's face.dorsum row times the half nose width.
    • Axis: level planes whose origins run depth behind that nasal chord, so every section holds its axis.
    • Fullness: the ellipse's shoulder.

    What is input and what is prior. Public inputs (FaceBase 3D Facial Norms inter-landmark protocol, IAutoMovieHumanNose): nasion-subnasale (height), subnasale-pronasale (protrusion) and alare-alare (width). Published prior: nasion-pronasale, the bridge length, is the norms' share of the nasal height (profile.bridge, 47.4 / 54.7 in women, 49.9 / 56.6 in men), applied by AutoMovieHumanHeadLandmarks, which places pronasale by closing the triangle of these three lengths. No input or prior here describes a dorsal hump, concavity or supratip break, and no scan value enters the law.

    Both ends close on poles inside the face: under the tip at the profile row's lowest knot, and over the nasion where the ridge has fallen to the face.
