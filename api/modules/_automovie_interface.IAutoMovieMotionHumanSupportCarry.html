    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanSupportCarry

    The one owner of goals in a moving support's frame (#3491): a posture planned in the world at an action's start, expressed in the support's frame there and carried back to the world at every second by the support's rigid motion since then. A stance on a cart's bed, a bow held on its target from a cantering horse and a carbine presented from a moving car all plan once at their start and are carried this way, so a goal on the support stays on it and the plan stays a pure function of its seconds.

    The carry is the rigid transform D(t) = F(t) F(start)^-1 of the support's frame F: a point p goes to F(t) F(start)^-1 p, a rotation is turned by the support's turn, and the heading by that turn's yaw about the vertical. A goal attached to a bone (IAutoMovieHumanLimbGoal.on) rides its bone already and is left alone. On the ground the carry is the identity, so nothing changes there.
