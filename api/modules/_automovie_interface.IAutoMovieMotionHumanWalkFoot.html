    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanWalkFoot

    One foot of a stepping gait at an instant: the rockers of its stance on a footprint and its swing between two reached stances.

    In stance a foot rolls on its heel from 15° toes-up to flat over 12 % of the stance and, from 60 % of it, on its ball to its push pitch at toe-off (provisional angles inside the ranges of Perry and Burnfield's rockers), the toes extending at the ball by the same angle so they stay flat on the floor. The forefoot rocker starts at rest and ends still turning (LEAVE), the rate the swing then carries on from. In swing the ankle leaves the placement and the velocity its stance ended with and arrives at rest on the placement its next stance begins with, on a quintic Hermite curve (the minimum-jerk profile of Flash and Hogan 1985 plus the engine's carriedRate term for the initial velocity), lifted at mid-swing on a raised-cosine bump, sin²(π u), whose slope is zero at both ends, so the lift adds no velocity at toe-off and the foot meets the floor at rest (the heel's vertical velocity at contact is near zero, Winter 1992), and raised further wherever the toe or heel would come within CLEARANCE of the floor. The travel, the turn and the descent of the placements end early, at the share of the swing where the lift has fallen to the contact height of the floor (AutoMovieMotionHumanAuditContact.HEIGHT, half the minimum toe clearance): in terminal swing the foot comes to rest over its landing and only then sets down, the heel's horizontal velocity falling through terminal swing as it descends (Winter 1992), so no sole is on the floor while it still travels. The toes relax over the first half and take the landing's flexion over the second.
