    Namespace AutoMovieMotionHumanArm

    Arm primitives: a hand surface carried to a target, kept on it and taken back.

    A target is a frame evaluated at each second on the posture the action has so far (a region of the performer's own trunk, a partner's hand, a prop, a point of the world), so the same row fits every body and follows whatever the target rides on. The hand is an effector goal solved by the arm's inverse kinematics: the goal carries the surface as its contact, so where the arm cannot turn the hand as asked the resolver re-aims the wrist until the surface stays on its point and the rotation gives way (HumanPosture.IGoal.contact).

    Between its origin and its target the surface moves on a straight path, or one that bows to the arm's own side when the movement states a curvature, and the hand turns by spherical interpolation, both by one weight, and the fingers go from the relaxed cascade to the target's shape by the same weight (Jeannerod 1984: the hand is shaped during its transport). The origin is held in the performer's own floor frame, so it follows the body.
