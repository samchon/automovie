    Namespace AutoMovieHumanFootKnots

    The foot's guide regions and the five guides they compose, as one table.

    A guide is its regions' knots, front to back, joined by the guide's own C2 spline (AutoMovieHumanFootGuide.of); each region has its own centre and its knots in the foot frame's normalized coordinates (foot lengths a, foot breadths u, and heights in foot lengths or lateral malleolus heights). The regions with a rule of their own keep their files (the crest's forefoot, the lateral forefoot, the two columns, the heel's corner and the Achilles); the others are knot lists in this table, each a reference median awaiting a published value:

    • crest: the instep's summit line from the first toe web back and up the column to the ankle ring, the guide the section's two dorsal pieces start from: the forefoot's part and the ankle's (crestAnkle: over the talus's neck to the ankle ring, centred at the lateral malleolus height, the last knot on the ring).
    • lateral: the greatest across point of each of the fan's own planes, the fifth metatarsal's side, the heel's lateral wall (lateralHeel: the calcaneus's wall at shares 0.55 to 0.65; on every reference the extreme passes from this wall to the malleolus between 0.65 and 0.75, a branch change the guide bridges to the column's first knot) and the column up to the lateral malleolus.
    • contactLateral: the sole's lateral boundary on the floor under the ball (contactLateralBall, round the fifth metatarsal head, a = 0.86 to 0.6), the midfoot (contactLateralMidfoot, along the cuboid and the fifth metatarsal's base, a = 0.5 to 0.2, where the lateral arch is lowest) and the heel (contactLateralHeel, round the calcaneal tuberosity to the contact's rear end, a = 0.1 and 0.075), then round the heel's posterior corner and up the Achilles.
    • contactMedial: the sole's medial boundary under the ball (contactMedialBall, round the first metatarsal head and the sesamoids, crossing the sole as the arch begins), the medial arch's footprint (contactMedialMidfoot, centred on the navicular) and the heel (contactMedialHeel), then round the heel's corner and up the Achilles.
    • medial: the least across point of each of the fan's own planes, the first ray's side (medialForefoot, from the web ring to 0.45 of the stations), the navicular's arch (medialNavicular, the medial longitudinal arch's crown at shares 0.5 to 0.65) and the column up to the medial malleolus.
