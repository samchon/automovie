    Namespace AutoMovieHumanF1Landmarks

    f1's bony and facial landmarks on the stored side (+Z) in its reference pose: the points the segment frames need that the joint table lacks.

    Frame: millimetres, forward +X, up +Y, +Z the stored side, the side whose datums the joint table holds (the reference rig's _r joints). Its twin is the reflection in z = 0, because the reference is mirror-symmetric.

    Each value was read once from the reference skin at its palpation site by the written definition in its comment, with test/src/reference-conics/FamilyConicLandmarks.ts (from test/: pnpm conic:build, then node .shots/esbuild/FamilyConicLandmarks.cjs), reading of 2026-11-14 test/.shots/family-conic-landmarks/2026-11-14T08-08-13.033Z, rounded to 0.01 mm. Each is stored in the frame of the segment it belongs to (AutoMovieHumanF1Placing.POINTS, re-based by FamilyConicRebase.ts --tables) and read here through the chain at the reference pose, so this module holds no coordinate. A skin reading stands on the soft tissue over the bone, so a breadth between two of them is a skin breadth and reads wider than the caliper's bony breadth (Heinz et al. 2003, women, per side, docs/sources/heinz-2003-body-dimensions.md).

    Not stored, because the reference skin does not show them unambiguously:

    • the anterior and posterior superior iliac spines, the xiphoid process, T8, orbitale and the scapular points: no mark on the skin;
    • the malleoli: over sections square to the shank from 25 mm below to 25 mm above the ankle centre, the lateral extreme stands within 0.33 mm of the slab's end (no prominence), and the skin bimalleolar breadth (61.3 mm) falls below the bony mean (65.2 +- 4.4 mm);
    • the vertebra prominens: on the back median profile the point farthest behind the chord from the cervical lordosis's apex (1,510 mm) to the thoracic kyphosis's apex (1,283 mm) stands 6.6 mm behind it but at 1,384 mm, 0.814 of stature (ANSUR II women cervicale 0.857 +- 0.007, -6.3 SD) and 31.5 mm below suprasternale, where cervicale stands 0.0405 +- 0.0071 of stature above it.

    The frame each would set takes a declared substitute in the frame tree.
