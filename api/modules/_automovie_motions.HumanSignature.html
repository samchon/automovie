    Namespace HumanSignature

    The kinematic readings of an act's signature: what human-movement studies measure on a performer, read off the displayed bones of a performance.

    A production checks its own composition with these the way the vocabulary is checked: perform it, then read, at the instants the act's events name, the quantities the literature reports (the trunk's inclination from vertical in the sagittal plane, joint flexions, the whole-body centre of mass, the head's pitch, which feet bear on the floor, the duty factor and the cadence), and compare each with a cited value in a unit test. No reading knows an action; the bounds and their sources belong to the test.

    const reader = HumanSignature.of(performance, "a", body, 0);
    const lean = HumanSignature.trunk(reader, seconds) - HumanSignature.trunk(reader, 0);

    Conventions: the performer's forward is the heading given to the reader, so a positive inclination tips the shoulders ahead of the hips; a flexion is 0 with the joint straight and grows as it folds; a head pitch is positive when the face turns down. A foot bears on the floor when its lowest sole socket (heel, ball or toe) lies within CONTACT of the floor's height and is nearly still over it (SLIDE). The trunk reading of a quiet stance is not exactly zero on every body, so a lean is read against the same body's standing value.
