    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkTape

    The trunk base's fullness at a tape site whose tape wraps the merged skin: the chest tape over the breasts, the hip tape over the gluteal masses (ISO 8559-1 5.3.4 and 5.3.13). A tape bridges every concavity, so it reads the convex hull of the level cut through the base, masses and named parent cuts; the base's common conic shoulder there is the root that puts that hull's perimeter at the tape (#3047).

    The source cut at height y contains the base's original section, the masses' original side curves and explicitly supplied original leg cuts. A leg cut retains its own oblique chart and is not cast to an AP mass. The actual straight AP declaration of each mass has world-Y up and a constant axis height equal to this site. Its horizontal cut is therefore exactly the two +/-v source loci, including the pole; no intermediate loft or final skin supplies the cut. Each edge contributes its named/scalar-critical points and 64 station intervals, while the base retains 512 angular samples. Their polygon hull perimeter is a numerical approximation, not an exact continuous-tape or final-merge certificate. Unsupported charts or another cutting level retain a source diagnostic.

    The perimeter rises with the base's shoulder (the base's section grows outward and the masses stay), so the root is bracketed by the base box's valid shoulder range. An unreachable tape returns no solved shoulder and one diagnostic; the caller retains its admitted original rather than authoring an extreme failed estimate as a new source knot.
