    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkReliefNet

    The merge of a feature into the trunk: a relief window over the trunk's chart whose control net is the feature's height sampled at the base surface.

    A window's net has its three outer rings zero (IAutoMovieLoftRelief), so the relief's height, slope and curvature vanish at the window's edge (rule J7). With n controls control k peaks at the window fraction (k - 1) / (n - 3), so the inner controls 3 .. n - 4 span 2 / (n - 3) to (n - 5) / (n - 3) of the window. The window is therefore the feature's footprint grown by 2 / (n - 7) of its length on each side, so the first and last inner controls stand on the footprint's edge, where the feature's own profile meets the wall C2, and no inner control is cut while the feature still stands. Where the grown window would cross one of the chart's ends (the crotch ring, the neck's ring, both shared with a neighbour), it stops there and the feature is drawn toward its apex on that side, so its edge still falls on the first inner control. The evaluated cubic B-spline of a sampled net is a little lower than the samples, so the net is scaled until the relief reaches the feature's extreme height at its apex.
