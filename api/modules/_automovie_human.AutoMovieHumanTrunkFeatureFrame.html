    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkFeatureFrame

    The frame of a trunk feature that has its own centre: the breast, the gluteal mass, the umbilicus and the spinal furrow are each built in a frame of their own and merged with the trunk at a declared seam, not drawn in the trunk's ring about the trunk's axis.

    A frame has an origin (the feature's centre on the wall), an outward axis (the direction its height is measured along) and two in-plane axes, up and lateral, so a point p of the body has local coordinates (u, v, w) with u along lateral, v along up and w along axis. All vectors are body-frame millimetres, forward +X, up +Y, left +Z; the right side's frames are the left's reflections (rule J6).
