    Namespace AutoMovieHumanNeutralThumbCmc

    The thumb's carpometacarpal neutral: the metacarpal turned from the bind toward the index metacarpal's line about the saddle joint at the trapezium, as far as its skin stays out of the hand's.

    Neutral-0 measures the thumb from its adducted position, against the hand in the hand's plane and in the plane square to it (DGUV F 4030), but a thumb laid exactly along the index metacarpal swings its own skin through the thenar and the palm. The neutral therefore takes the nearest direction to the index metacarpal's at which no point of the thumb's skin past its root ring (where it leaves the hand's) lies inside the hand's skin: the turn's share from the bind direction to the index line is scanned down from the whole in steps of STEP, each candidate turning the thumb's bind skin rigidly about the joint. Without the two chains the thumb lies on the index line.
