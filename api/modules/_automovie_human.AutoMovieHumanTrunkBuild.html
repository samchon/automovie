    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkBuild

    The trunk subtree built and joined (#3047): the base lofted from its views, the breasts and the gluteal masses as parts of their own on it, the base's fullness at the chest and the buttock solved on sampled original cuts, and fold declarations for later body-tree assembly. Each declared AP apex fixes its own height, transverse place and full-body front/back target. The original trunk section at that Y/Z supplies the local wall, so actual amplitude is their AP difference, not a whole-depth difference at another place.

    Order. The masses stand on the base's surface and the chest and hip tapes wrap base and masses together, so the build closes the two in two passes: the base with the ellipse's shoulder at those two sites, the masses on it, the two shoulders solved on the hull of the base's section, the masses' level cuts and, at the hip, the same original legs' horizontal cuts (AutoMovieHumanTrunkTape), then the base again with them and the masses again on it. A mass's place reads only the base's outlines near its centre, so a changed shoulder can change local projection. This retains the existing two-pass tape construction; it does not establish final-union tape closure. AP apex coordinates remain fixed in both source passes.

    Masses. Each side is the same construction from the left side's law mirrored (J6): the target point and the up direction reflected in the median plane, the footprint and profile kept in their up, lateral, down and medial naming, and the side's lateral direction (+Z on the left, -Z on the right) handed to the part, which reads off its own frame which chart side is lateral. The original-source wall point and AP axis pass to the mass builder directly; its original world jet only estimates embedding. A source-to-loft discrepancy does not move the apex to conceal it.

    Folds. Each mass declares a fold for the body's construction tree (AutoMovieHumanBodyTree), its seed searched along the mass's lower station line (the footprint's lower edge) from inside the base out, its fold radius the caller's declared join softness, its width capped at half its smallest footprint edge (a fillet wider than that swallows the mass) and at the gap from its footprint's medial edge to the median plane (beyond it the pair's rails cross: the cleavage and the cleft are corners the two-part merge does not close, operator gap 3). A pair that reaches the median plane is reported and left unjoined.
