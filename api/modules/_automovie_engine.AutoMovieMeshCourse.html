    Namespace AutoMovieMeshCourse

    Sections of a dense mesh on the planes of a course: one exact plane cut per station (AutoMovieMeshExtract.outline on that station's own plane, so a turned family needs no common axis), the outer piece holding the course point, that loop's own plane readings and the family's neighbour jumps.

    Each section reports the loop's Newell vector direction and area centroid read in its own plane, its four chart extents and four conic quarter shoulders (read), each support direction's crests and notches (the loop's points whose tangent is square to that direction, from a persistence walk) and its reach from the course point. A caller that follows guides from section to section climbs to each one's hill (ascend) and reads the quarters between them split at their shoulder bars (pieces). A course's turn regularity is a property of the course itself and belongs to its owner (the axis and its analytic jet), not to these sampled planes.

    The jump check reads the holding loop's own values: an extent, or a quarter's shoulder point (rho times its corner reach, millimetres), that leaves the station-linear interpolation of its two neighbours by more than the tolerance is flagged as a jump, so a neighbouring body in the same cut (a hand beside the hip) does not flag the part.
