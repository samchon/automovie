    Namespace armWrestle

    Arm wrestling, one role of it (#3350): the elbow planted on a table's top, the hand clasped with the partner's above it, the struggle and the pin.

    Both roles read one geometry from the table's point and the two bodies, so neither waits on the other. The clasp stands over the table's point at 0.85 of the shorter forearm (provisional), the two wrists GAP apart across the table; each elbow lies on the table's top where its own forearm reaches its wrist from, toward its own side. The trunk leans from the hips until the shoulder is an upper arm from the elbow, so the elbow, which is no hand surface, is put where it must be by the arm's own lengths: the hand's goal is the wrist and the elbow's hint is its place on the table. A table too low or too far for the lean to close is refused.

    The forearms turn about the line through the two elbows: upright from set, swaying SWAY degrees through the struggle from go, then down to the pin's angle (PIN degrees when none is asked) toward the winner's left by pin, and back up to leave from relax. An angle past what the arm holds with its elbow in place (the fitted elbow within HELD of its place, as far as upright) is refused with the angle it holds. The winner's trunk holds its lean; the loser's bends toward the pin as the pin comes, by the force exchanged (#3439, HumanForceTrunk): its shoulder resists at its eccentric internal-rotation peak per body mass (Tasiopoulos et al. 2018, provisional beyond men at 60 degrees a second) and its trunk holds that torque at the side-bending share its stiffness gives (Watt, Callaway and Williams 2022), but no further than keeps its elbow on the pad, which the sport's rules hold down throughout (World Armwrestling Federation rules: lifting the elbow off the pad is a foul): the shoulder stays an upper arm, within HELD, from the elbow's place. The author declares the winner (#3350's outcome-first rule).

    First pass: the elbow's bearing on the table is not declared as a contact window, the fingers are left as they were and do not clasp, and the free hand hangs.
