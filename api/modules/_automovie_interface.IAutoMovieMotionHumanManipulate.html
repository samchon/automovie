    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanManipulate

    A keyed contact sequence: digits of one hand or both pressing sites of a prop one after another, timed by the caller or by skill. Typing, a piano's keys, a guitar's frets, a phone's keypad and a dial are the same structure and are rows of this law (HumanManipulate).

    The prop's sites are its affordances: each contact names one, whose frame's translation is the site and whose +Y is the site's outward normal, read in the prop's frame at the act's start. A contact names its digit, or leaves it to the fingering: the digit chosen is the one whose press keeps the hand still, by dynamic programming over the sequence of each hand (the Viterbi path over digits, cost the distance the hand's wrist must travel between consecutive presses), so a run of nearby keys stays under one placement of the hand. This is the outcome Handrix reports for its fretting hand, which begins on the middle finger to keep the wrist still (ElKoura and Singh 2003, "Handrix: animating the human hand", SCA 2003, pp. 110-119); its own cost terms (finger strength, stretch) were not read at the source, so the wrist's travel alone is the cost here, a first pass. A text with the touch-typing layout assigns every letter's digit by the standard home-row chart instead.

    Each press is two goals. The arm carries the hand so the pressing digit's tip, the digit straight and the others curled clear of the keys (manipulateShape), stands over its site at the hover height, drawn back by as much as its swing down to the key shortens its reach (manipulateReach), moving between placements on minimum-jerk paths; the digit's own inverse kinematics (IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture.digits) then takes the tip down by the key's travel and up again. Times come from at when every contact gives it, or from typing skill otherwise (the interval of typeText, Salthouse 1986), each press taking the first 40 % of its interval to reach the key and the rest to lift, as typeText does. The body holds the posture it is entered in. Events press1 to pressN.
