    Namespace AutoMovieHumanFacePlan

    The face's plan: the exponent of the super-ellipse |x / a|^n + |z / b|^n = 1 the face's front quarter follows in the top view at the subnasale's level, from the tragion (a, the half bitragion breadth, across) to the subnasale (b, its depth ahead of the tragions' line), replacing the head prior's observed frame.plan.

    Derivation: the bitragion subnasale arc, the tape from tragion to tragion under the nose (ANSUR 1988, the person's by AutoMovieHumanHeadArcs), is twice the quarter's arc length; the quarter's ends are the 3D Facial Norms' (Weinberg et al. 2016, docs/sources/weinberg-2016-3d-facial-norms.md) cranial base width (t-t, 139.0 and 147.9 mm) and middle facial depth (t-sn, 123.5 and 132.8 mm, a 3D distance, so the depth is sqrt(t-sn^2 - (t-t / 2)^2)), women and men, scaled by the person's face width over those norms' maximum facial width (zy-zy, 129.8 and 137.6 mm). The exponent is the one whose quarter arc equals half the arc, found by bisection; the arc runs from the chord (n of about 1) to the box's two sides (n large), so a plan exists for every arc between them and is held at the nearer end otherwise. At the norms' means, with the arc at the mean face width, the quarter stands within two millimetres of an ellipse's (n near 2), beside the old prior's 2.5 read on the references at the zygomatic level.

    Limits: two surveys of different people meet in one quarter (an Army tape and a 3D facial scan); the tape crosses under the nose and over the cheeks' skin, so the plan is the skin's; the head modeler converts the exponent to its conic shoulder.
