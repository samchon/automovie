    Namespace AutoMovieHumanSeam

    The shared joining mathematics of divided parts: how an owner publishes a seam's jet, how a neighbour copies it, and how a seam is measured.

    • J1, curves: the owner publishes (p, T, kappa N) by arclength (publish); a neighbour of parameter speed lambda and tangential acceleration mu writes q_v = lambda T, q_vv = lambda^2 kappa N + mu T (adopt). The curve so copied has the owner's point, tangent line and curvature vector whatever lambda > 0 and mu: the curvature of a parametric curve is the part of q_vv across q_v over |q_v|^2.
    • J4, surface ribbons: across a seam curve c(t) with the owner's cross derivatives D1, D2 and d_t D1, a neighbour leaves along q_w = alpha D1 + beta c' (G1 for alpha > 0, since it stays in the owner's tangent plane) and bends by q_ww = alpha^2 D2 + 2 alpha beta d_t D1 + beta^2 c'' (G2: its normal part is the owner's second fundamental form in that direction, the chain rule of P(t + beta w, w alpha)); see ribbon.
    • J6, reflection: a mirrored piece mirrors every vector and reverses its along parameter, so along and twist change sign and the outward orientation is kept (reflect).
    • J8, observation: gap, normal angle and the jump of normal curvature in the plane normal to the seam's tangent (measure).

    Knot jets and sector assembly (J2) live in AutoMovieHumanSeamKnot, and the station rule along a chain (J3) in AutoMovieSeamStation. Vectors are plain arrays of any dimension unless a function says 3.
