    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkLandmarks

    The trunk's landmark heights and the rules that place them from the inputs.

    Heights stand at shares of the span from the crotch (the leg law's) to cervicale, each share one pooled mean of ANSUR II's women and men (Gordon et al. 2014, public working databases; docs/sources/ansur-ii-trunk-ratios.md and ansur-ii.md), grade measured: with the crotch at 0.4809 S and cervicale at 0.8605 S, a landmark at height h over stature S has the share (h - 0.4809) / (0.8605 - 0.4809). No share carries a sex term.

    Three heights are solved later on the construction's own surface, so they are only started here: the natural waist (by neckToWaist), the bust point (by shoulderToApex) and the shoulder point's breadth (by acrossShoulder).
