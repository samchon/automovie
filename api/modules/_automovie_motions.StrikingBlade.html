    Namespace StrikingBlade

    The stroke of a held tool, shared by every blade and firearm action: the tool is taken from where the hand holds it to a ready pose, carried along a path to its decisive pose, kept there and returned, while the feet stay or the leading foot steps or lunges under it.

    The action states only tool poses (hand frames built by StrikingHeld.hand); this owns the time course and the body. The clock is a phrase with the standard instants: the tool leaves the entry hold for the ready pose by ready, runs its path from strokeStart, is at its decisive pose at stroke, follows through to strokeEnd, stays until relax and is back in the entry hold at end, or stays where the stroke left it for an action that ends in its pose (a guard, a levelled aim).

    An advance carries the body with the stroke: the leading foot leaves the floor at strokeStart and lands at stroke, the pelvis travels a share of the foot's travel and sinks until both legs reach their feet, and the foot and the pelvis come back from relax to end (StrikingAdvance).
