    Namespace AutoMovieMotionHumanAudit

    The generic audit of a displayed human motion: every check that holds for any action whatever it means to do, read from the displayed bones alone.

    • tangle: no part of the body passes into another part of it.
    • floor: no collider sinks below the floor.
    • slip, lift: a foot declared planted stays where it stands, on the floor.
    • jump, reversal, speed: every joint centre travels a continuous path with no out-and-back spike and no impossible speed.
    • balance: while both feet alone bear the body, its extrapolated centre of mass stays over them.

    Each check has a limit; a reading above its limit is a finding. A limit of null leaves the check unread, for an action a check does not apply to (a fall is meant to lose its balance). What an action intends (which hand reaches where, at which beat) is not the audit's: that is pinned by the action's own tests.
