    Namespace HumanDefence

    The defences as rows on the grammar (#3362): each named defence is a row of defend, the law that owns the defender's role and the outcome shared with the strike it answers, and the brace a row of brace, a base adaptation. The composition gives the strike answered (against) and, where the scene decides it, the outcome.

    Row Law Fixed
    cover, handParry, elbowBlock, highBlock, checkKick defend the block
    slip, rollUnder, swayBack defend the evasion
    braceImpact, bracePush brace what is braced for

    handParry, rollUnder and swayBack are named apart from the blade's parry, the regrasp's roll and the drill's sway, so a name a performance registers is one row.
