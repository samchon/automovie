    Namespace StrikingGuardYield

    How far a guard facing a person steps back so that the two fighters' guards, both held, stand clear of each other: no joint of either body inside the other's colliders (the partner verdict's reading, HumanColliders.distance), whatever the two bodies' sizes.

    Each fighter reads the other as it perceived them when its guard began, where the guard's line reads them (StrikingTarget.LOOK and a reaction time, HumanPerception.REACTION), so two guards reading each other never read each other's guard while it is planned. From that reading it anticipates the other's guard held at set: the stance the guard law lays for that body about its feet, its lead the foot ahead, facing this fighter (held). The two guards held where they stand would pass into each other by some step along the floor's line between them; each fighter takes half of it, the reciprocal share of optimal reciprocal collision avoidance (van den Berg, Guy, Lin and Manocha 2011), so the two halves clear them together. The step is the least at which they clear, bisected (bracketedBisection) from a bracket doubled until it clears.
