    Namespace EquidEarProfiles

    Published ear references transferred explicitly to the current equid bodies.

    Donkey: Patel et al.2025, Indian Journal of Animal Sciences95:322-328, Table2 p326, https://doi.org/10.56093/ijans.v95i4.154768. Male Halari means are ear length28.64, breadth10.64 and withers108.04cm (n28). Its adult table heading and mixed-age methods are not perfectly reconciled; these are reported reference means, not a validated Veracruz population law.

    Mule: Salamanca-Carreño et al.2020, Animals10:679, TableS1 pp6-7, https://doi.org/10.3390/ani10040679; actual raw15 mules,7 male/8 female, ages4-22 in these rows. ALC/LORE/ORE are withers/ear length/ear width in cm. The supplement is https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/animals/animals-10-00679/article_deploy/animals-10-00679-s001.pdf.

    Each ratio is length/breadth mean over the SAME donor cohort's height mean. Current Veracruz donkey and Velásquez2020 sex-specific mule h/g/mass remain unchanged. Their ear-only uniform transfer is provisional cross-cohort construction, not measured pairing, individual allometry or a population band.
