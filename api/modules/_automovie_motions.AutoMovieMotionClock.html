    Namespace AutoMovieMotionClock

    The clock of one action: its duration and its named instants, and the only owner of progress between two of them.

    A primitive asks the clock how far the action is between two instants and never does window arithmetic of its own, so every channel of an action moves on the same instants and a partner's action or an interrupt row reads the same names. The shared names are Kendon's gesture phases with a contact (Kendon 1980; McNeill 1992): prepare, strokeStart, contact, strokeEnd, retract. A family adds its own (chamber, takeoff, landing). start and end always exist, at 0 and at the duration.
