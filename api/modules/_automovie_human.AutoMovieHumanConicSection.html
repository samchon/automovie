    Namespace AutoMovieHumanConicSection

    One section of a conic family as a short vector of named shape numbers, read through a layout of anchors.

    A section is a closed chain of conic arcs through its anchors in increasing phase. Every anchor is a point whose tangent the layout fixes by rule, so no number is spent on a direction that anatomy already gives:

    • u: a point where the section runs parallel to the chart's v axis: a front or back extreme. Its numbers are its u and v; a mid anchor stands on the median line v = 0 and has only u.
    • v: a point where the section runs parallel to the u axis: a side extreme. Its numbers are its u and v.
    • m: the median point of the conic triangle its two neighbours span: S = M + f (A - M) on the segment from the chord midpoint M to the meeting A of the neighbours' tangent lines, with the tangent parallel to the chord, as every conic has at its own median point. Its number is f. Its neighbours are the nearest anchors of a lower level, so medians nest: a level 2 median halves an arc a level 1 median already made.
    • i: an inflection between two neighbours whose tangents are parallel. An S-shaped stretch crosses its own chord once, at the inflection, so the point is P0 + t (P2 - P0) and its tangent is the chord's direction turned by d. Its numbers are t and d.
    • f: a point on one of the part's feature lines (a tip's ridge, a wing's top, a groove's floor or the inflection beside it), its tangent free. Each number is a field along the stations like every other, so the feature line is a smooth longitudinal control curve, the way an aircraft's lines are drawn, and a feature that fades away leaves its anchor on a smooth stretch. Every feature of a run stands on one chord, its frame's (frame: the extremes either side of the run), so the features keep their order round the section wherever they fade, as an i anchor keeps it. Its numbers are its share of what is left of that chord past the feature before it (along), its offset o square to the chord in millimetres outward, and its tangent's turn h from the chord's direction, radians (place). Read as free chart coordinates, f1's nose's six feature lines left that order at 146 of 157 stations where its alar crease and wing fade; each on the chord from the feature before it, a tenth of a millimetre at one feature turned the next one's chord past its arc's own turn and the fit stood against those walls in both directions.

    After the anchors come the shoulders, one per arc between consecutive anchors: the conic shoulder rho = |M S| / |M A| of the engine's native chain (AutoMovieViewChain). A mirror layout lists the anchors of the half v >= 0 from the front median anchor to the back median anchor and reflects them, so a bilateral section spends its numbers once.

    Shares (f, t, shoulders) are confined to [EDGE, 1 - EDGE] inside the model itself, and shoulders to at most FAIR, so an offline fit, the authored numbers and the generated surface read one curve.
