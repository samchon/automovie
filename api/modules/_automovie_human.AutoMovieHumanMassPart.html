    Namespace AutoMovieHumanMassPart

    A mass that rises from its host along the host's outward normal (a breast on the chest wall, a gluteal mass on the pelvis, the patella on the knee, the olecranon on the elbow), lofted as a straight part in its own frame and merged onto the host by a fold join of the body's tree (AutoMovieHumanBodyTree), so the fold under it (the inframammary fold, the gluteal fold) is the merge's blend (#3044, construction step 2; #3047, #3048, #3050).

    Frame. The axis stands at the mass's centre on the host (a host chart point the caller places from landmarks), along the host's outward normal there; u is the caller's up direction (the body's +Y for the trunk's masses) made square to the normal and v = u x n, so the straight axis's stations, which advance along v x u, run out along n. The kernel's front pair is the footprint's upper and lower edges and its side pair the lateral and medial edges, +v taking whichever of the two the caller's lateral direction leans to: v turns from one side to the other with the normal (a breast's faces forward, a gluteal mass's back), so the side is read off the frame, never fixed per mass. A caller whose target is the skin's extreme along the depth asks for the normal levelled (IInput.level): the finished skin's normal there is level, while the base under it may slope (the sacrum's line under the buttock, the upper chest under the breast). An explicit original-source frame instead fixes its wall point and axis; the same actual original world jet supplies the retained embedding clock. An anatomical AP extreme can keep its declared Y/Z rather than following the base's oblique normal or a sampled closest point.

    Views. The footprint is the box at its named wall station, offset from the internal start at station zero: four edges from the centre, each a law of the caller's (the breast's from the inframammary fold, the second rib, the sternal edge and the anterior axillary line). The profile is the share of each edge left at each share of the amplitude, one knot per station of the caller's profile law, down to the apex's pole at the amplitude. The caller owns the distance from the same original wall point to its declared apex. Whole-depth differences at different heights do not independently define that local projection.

    Embedding. The centre's actual world2 supplies tilt and an osculating-circle sagitta R - sqrt(R^2 - e^2), with R = 1 / kappa, on four footprint rays. Clipping e to R and adding one tenth of the amplitude is the retained legacy estimate. It is exact for that circle, not a bound on the entire host, its opposite wall or every phase's crossing. Production reads the same jet directly from the original source, without a raw loft.

    The named footprint/profile values own the radial source. Explicit C2 jets keep each interval between its endpoint values and hold the internal embedded-to-wall footprint, rather than letting a global natural solve inflate it from the external taper. This changes the old interpolated exterior between its preserved named values; it is not exact replay of the analytic bump that supplied those samples.

    A supplied shared connection owns the actual root and embedding from its whole-parent local chords. MassSource.rebase preserves this declared source when translating its clock. Neither the root mapping nor a held internal footprint proves whole-parent containment, all-phase crossing, exact G2 at a pole or a successful final join.
