    Namespace AutoMovieHumanTrunkViews

    The trunk base's views from a person's measures: every site's height, half breadth, front and back profile and fullness owner, in calibration millimetres (#3047).

    Sources, site by site:

    • Heights: the crotch (the caller's, ISO 8559-1 5.7.6), the buttock point as an ANSUR II share of the crotch-to-cervicale span (AutoMovieHumanTrunkLandmarks, measured there); the chest at the caller's same generating bust-apex height (currently an old course-placement proxy, not a second independent survey site); the natural waist at the person's waistHeight (the 1988 Army survey's natural waist, where its breadth and depth are taken); the top hip midway between the waist and the hip (ISO 8559-1 5.3.12); the inframammary fold two intercostal spaces under the chest point (Gray 1918, AutoMovieHumanTrunkLandmarks.FOLD_DROP, conventional); the axilla, the acromion and cervicale as inputs (ANSUR II).
    • Boxes: the chest's breadth, the natural waist's breadth and depth, the hip's breadth and the buttock's depth from #3045 (AutoMovieHumanBodyBox.of, each tape held inside its box's reach). The top hip's and the underbust's boxes from their girths by the caller's laws (ILaws.lumbar, the navel section's ANSUR 1988 law; ILaws.chestWall, the thorax without the breasts).
    • Side placement: the back median's forward place at each height from the caller's back profile law (ILaws.back), the front the back plus the site's base depth.

    The base is drawn without its protrusions (#3047): the caller gives each mass's retained wall setback. This fixes the bare median box, not the local amplitude at the mass's transverse place. The actual original wall/AP-apex difference supplies that amplitude in the assembly. At the chest the base's depth is chest depth less the setback (the whole chest depth for a man, whose chest carries none). At the buttock the caller's back line is the gluteal apex (ILaws.back, the median section's back extreme), so the base's back stands the gluteal amplitude in front of it and its front the buttock depth, and the two gluteal masses stand on it apart. Those two sites' tapes wrap the merged skin, so their fullness is solved by the trunk's assembly on the hull of the base's and the masses' sections; here they carry the ellipse's shoulder (the least-information conic) to start from. The waist's, the top hip's and the underbust's tapes wrap the base alone and are its owners.

    Above the chest no breadth or depth is published: the caller's ILaws.upper gives each upper site's half breadth, front and back (the back profile's where it gives none), its sourced shoulder too. Where the female axilla table contains the breast envelope, the bare wall instead continues the same chest depth; this is an explicit thorax transfer convention.
