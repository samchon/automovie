    Namespace AutoMovieMotionHumanSite

    Where on a target a contact is made, chosen among the places the target allows (#3357): a place drawn by the seed among weighted allowed places, and the place along a vertical handle that leaves the hand comfortable at the end of the move (end-state comfort).

    End-state comfort: people grasp an object so that the posture at the end of the move is comfortable, so the grasp height on a vertical cylinder falls as the height it is moved to rises (Cohen and Rosenbaum 2004, "Where grasps are made reveals how grasps are planned", Exp Brain Res 157:486; Rosenbaum, Halloran and Cohen 2006). The rule here takes the comfortable end height of the hand as the elbow's height with the upper arm hanging, the forearm level (the body's shoulder height less its upper arm), and grasps where the hand lands there, as far as the handle allows: the grasp falls one for one with the target's height inside the handle and stays at its end beyond it. The slope of one is the rule's, not a measured one: the studies report the inverse relation, and its measured slope is to be read before it becomes a constant (#3357's research list).
