    Namespace AutoMovieLoftSectionNative

    Cartesian readings of the original native scalar generating field. Explicit numeric rejection uses the internal admission error; exceptions raised by input getters are not intercepted or reclassified here.

    A source that declares touch offsets or quarter split points (IAutoMovieViewPart.ITop) is read as its conic chain (AutoMovieViewChain): the guide on support line q stands at its extent along the chart axis and its touch offset across it, with the tangent of that support line; each split point of a quarter stands at its own (u, v) with the tangent at its angle, in the quarter's order; each arc keeps its own shoulder. Every chain reading takes its point and direction from the chain's one ray reader, so Point0 and value read the arc point alone and the derivative capabilities add their jets on that same ray. The chain is G1, so its radius is C1 in phase; at a join whose curvatures differ the second phase derivatives are the starting arc's alone. Such a source therefore has no eight-scalar boundary law (AutoMovieLoftSectionBoundary refuses it) and its loft declares C1 (AutoMovieViewLoft). A source without them keeps the four-quarter reading unchanged.
