    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanRange

    Amounts by level: how far a joint turns as a share of its range of motion, how far the trunk leans as a share of the reach's lean limit, and how far the root shifts as a share of the stature.

    A law states "light" neck flexion and the joint's own range supplies the degrees, so one word fits a neck and a knee: a level is a share of the distance from the joint's rest (0 degrees) to the limit of the named motion in DEFAULT_HUMANOID_ROM. The limit a motion reads:

    Motion Limit
    flexion flexion.max
    extension flexion.min
    abduction abduction.max
    adduction abduction.min
    external twist.max
    internal twist.min

    The result is signed as the posture's clinical angles are, so an extension is negative.

    An amount has three rungs: a level, a level with a scale in [0.5, 1.5], or explicit degrees (a magnitude, signed by the motion) with their source.
