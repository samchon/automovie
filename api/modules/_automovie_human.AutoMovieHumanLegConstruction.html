    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLegConstruction

    Both legs generated on their own bones, from the ankle ring, the crotch ring, the calf, knee, thigh and hip tapes and the masculine-feminine control, by one construction in the left leg's frame and a reflection.

    This file only composes. The foot places the ankle ring, the trunk places the crotch ring, and the skeleton places the hip, the knee and the ankle before any limb; the leg starts on the one ring and ends on the other. Each rule lives in its own piece under leg/:

    • AutoMovieHumanLegBones, AutoMovieHumanLegCrotchHeight and AutoMovieHumanLegStations: the joints, the crotch height and the stations, from the inputs at their published definitions;
    • AutoMovieHumanLegAxis: the section planes along the bones;
    • AutoMovieHumanLegLaw: the fitted section law (until the span pieces replace it) and AutoMovieHumanLegSection, a station's section;
    • AutoMovieHumanLegAnkleSeam: the foot's ring, read as the leg's start (owner: the foot);
    • AutoMovieHumanLegCrotchRoot and AutoMovieHumanLegCrotchFillet: the trunk's ring with its jet and the fold's rounded corners (owner: the trunk), joined to the leg's own section by the root strip (AutoMovieHumanLimbRootStrip) the leg chain composes;
    • AutoMovieHumanLegChain and AutoMovieHumanLegReflection: the chain leg.<side> and the right side as the left's reflection;
    • AutoMovieHumanLegStand: the feet moved under the stance.
