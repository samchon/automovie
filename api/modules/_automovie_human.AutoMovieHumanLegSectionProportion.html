    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLegSectionProportion

    The leg section's breadth over depth and its fullness in its box along the leg, from the ankle (share 0) to the crotch (share 1): the width-to-depth law for the leg's tape sites, which publish a girth and no breadth or depth (the thigh's depth aside, AutoMovieHumanLegSites.thighDepth).

    Estimated from the private reference set; no published source found. The reading test_human_estimate_leg_sections aggregates eight legs (both legs of f1, f2, m1 and m2) on their horizontal sections at each twentieth of the span between the reference curves' ankle and crotch cuts: the section's breadth over depth and its girth's place in its box range, 0 the rhombus 2 sqrt(W^2 + D^2) and 1 the box 2 (W + D) (ROWS, means; the SDs run 0.009 to 0.043 for the ratio and 0.002 to 0.028 for the fullness). With a girth G, the box's breadth W and depth D follow from the ratio r = W / D and the fullness f: G = 2 D (sqrt(r^2 + 1) + f (r + 1 - sqrt(r^2 + 1))).

    Limits: four people; the sections are horizontal, standing for the section normal to the near-vertical leg; the replacement stays open.
