    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanPushSled

    A pushed load's handles held in both hands through a walk: a sled push. The trunk leans into the load while the body stands, the hands go to the handles where the load stands, close on them, and from then on are carried with the chest along the floor at the handles' own height. The body then walks to to, slowly with no arm swing, while the arms take the walk's rise and fall and the gripped load stays on the floor.

    The lean is the trunk's incline from the vertical, a hinge at the hips shared by the pelvis and the spine (at least lean, if asked), coming in over SETTLE seconds with the arms; lower handles are pushed with the trunk nearer the horizontal, as far as measured pushers incline at the handles' height (pushSledStance), and from bent knees, the body sinking as far as keeps each wrist within the arm's reach as the walk rises; the hands reach the handles in that time and grip them from its end, each fist's grip socket on its handle as a grasp lays it (HumanHand.reach: the palm into the bar along the handle's -Y, the thumb's side along its +X), turned about the handle as far as its symmetry makes the same grasp and slid along its grip band to whichever grasp the arm, its wrist within range, keeps nearest the handle through the push (the shared chooser, HumanFreedomChoice), so the handle is in the fist. Each hand's place is then kept in the chest's frame for the rest of the push, its turn kept with it; a turn the arm cannot meet gives way about the grip socket.

    The same hold serves the other ways a load is moved by its handles (pushPull): an incline asked outright, leaning back for a pull; a walk facing the load, so the body walks backward and draws it along; a stand in place (seconds) instead of the walk; and a load that does not move, whose handles the hands press (a bear contact bearing none of the body's weight) instead of gripping, so it stays where it stands.

    First pass: the load slides with the grip and is not dragged by a friction; the push's force and the slowing it brings to the walk are not modelled.
