    Namespace AutoMovieHumanLegLaws

    A leg's site laws bound for a person (#3048), in calibration millimetres, for AutoMovieHumanLegWalk:

    • Heights: the thigh at the gluteal furrow (ANSUR 1988, the site ISO 8559-1 5.3.20 seeks just below it); the lower thigh at the suprapatella (AutoMovieHumanSuprapatella: mid-patella plus half the patella's length); the knee at mid-patella, ANSUR II's midpatella height over stature by sex (MIDPATELLA, measured); the calf at ANSUR 1988's calf height share between the lateral malleolus and mid-patella (AutoMovieHumanLegSites.calf); the ankle's least girth at the caller's height (its site law is pending).
    • Boxes: no standing depth is published at any site (the survey's thigh depth, AutoMovieHumanLegSites.thighDepth, is taken seated and compressed, short of the standing thigh's), so every box is the estimated breadth over depth at the site's share of the ankle-to-crotch span (AutoMovieHumanLegSectionProportion, estimated from the private reference set). Each box splits its whole skin depth by the retained skin-support front share (AutoMovieHumanLegMassPrior), not a one-site muscle-thickness ratio. Those old level, sampled body-X supports and reference-rig lines transfer here to the current pure bone-normal chart as an explicit proxy. A natural C2 profile connects the actual segment-share rows across their unmeasured knee gap; values beyond the outer rows are held, with no invented joint measurement. The breadth splits by the lateral share about the bone line (AutoMovieHumanReferenceOutlines.THIGH_SPLIT from the knee toward the crotch, SHANK_SPLIT from the ankle's site to the knee, estimated from the private reference set; no published source), the lateral side +Z on the left leg and -Z on the right (J6). The tape owns the fullness at its site.
    • Intermediate thigh shape: the retained LegGirth.THIGH normalized girth rows between the public thigh and lower-thigh sites are placed on the current hip-to-knee height span. The same box/split owners read them for all four edges. Only that existing table is consumed, not its old C1 girth interpolator or old skin. This preserves a declared aggregate proxy instead of allowing the sparse five-site/pole spline to invent the intervening bulge; continuous world admission remains separate.
