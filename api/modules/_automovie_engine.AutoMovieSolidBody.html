    Namespace AutoMovieSolidBody

    A solid built by merges and diffs, as trimmed faces that share their boundaries: the base, every blend and every part, each a surface with its trim cycles in its own chart (AutoMovieSolidCycle), the shared boundary vertices held once in the body's mesh.

    A merge (merge) traces its part against the body as an atlas (atlas), so its curve and its host rail cross from face to face (the thigh against the trunk and the gluteal mass, a malleolus on the shank-to-foot blend, the second breast against the first). It adds the blend face (the join's blend chain, its two rails its station-end cycles), gives the part face its rail cycle (a part merged again at its other end keeps both, a limb merged at its root and at its foot), and trims every host face the host rail runs over: a rail on one face adds its loop, a rail that crosses faces is clipped into each face it runs on (AutoMovieSolidClip). Where two blends meet (the perineum, the webs, the cleavage), the later merge's rail simply runs over the earlier blend face, so no corner needs its own patch.

    draw tessellates every face on its own grid trimmed by its cycles (AutoMovieSolidMesh.face), shared boundaries vertex for vertex.
