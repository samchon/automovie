    Namespace IAutoMovieMotionHumanOperateDrive

    The part an operation moves on a prop that articulates, and the drive the operation publishes for it: the hand and the part follow one function of the stroke's progress, the prop posed at the articulation's travel there.

    The operated contact is the prop's articulation itself (a trigger, a button, a knob) or a contact that rides one of the mechanism's kinds, however far up its chain (a door's lever on its latch on its hinge, a drawer's pull on its slider, IAutoMovieAffordance.rides). The stroke moves that articulation from where the performance has it at the operation's start by the mechanism's amount, converting a prior writer's coordinate of that degree through the engine's articulation map, in the articulation's own unit and sign: degrees for a hinge's swing or a knob's turn, metres for a slider's or a button's travel. The hand's goal at a progress is the contact of the prop posed at that travel, so the hand cannot drift from the part; the drive writes the same travel at the same progress.
