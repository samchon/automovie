    Namespace sit

    Sitting down on a chair, bench or stool from standing in front of it: the body's seat socket is lowered onto the seat while the trunk leans forward and the feet stay where they stand.

    Stand-to-sit is the rise reversed under eccentric control (Kralj, Jaeger and Munih 1990, J Biomech 23:1123): the hips travel back and down, the trunk flexes forward to keep the weight over the feet until the seat takes it, then the trunk settles into the seated posture. The goal is the seat socket on its point on the seat (seated); the root follows from that goal and the trunk's pitch, the planted legs and the hands are solved by inverse kinematics. The socket moves back over the first 70 % of the descent, no further than keeps the centre of mass where it stands over the feet until a pendulum's time constant before contact (Hof, Gazendam and Sinke 2005), over which the body comes down onto the seat, and down from 10 % to its end, so it reaches the seat from above; the trunk pitches forward to its peak by 45 % of the descent and settles after contact; each hand is brought to its rest along the arm trajectory planner's path, onto its armrest as the seat takes the body, the two supports loaded together where the armrest lies nearest the shoulder, or, with none, onto its thigh from contact to seated, once the thighs no longer rise under it, the fingers straightening to lie flat.

    Peak trunk flexion 35 + 50 (1 - r) degrees and descent time 2 (1 + 0.6 (1 - r)) seconds for a seat at r of the popliteal height (provisional coefficients; a lower seat needs more trunk flexion and more time, Janssen, Bussmann and Stam 2002, and sit-to-stand takes about 1.5 to 2.5 s in healthy adults). Contact comes at 75 % of the descent. The seated back is upright (pitch 0), lean (12 degrees back onto a backrest, which the seat must have) or slump (pelvis 10 degrees back, chest 12 and spine 8 flexed), all provisional.

    The performer must stand facing within 20 degrees of the seat's heading with feet the legs can reach from the seat; otherwise the sit is refused with the range of foot distances that work. Events commit (the last instant the sitting can be abandoned for standing), seatContact and seated; the seat window starts at contact.

    A seat above 1.2 of the popliteal height, or one sat astride, is got onto instead (HumanMountOn): the feet leave the floor for the seat's rests or hang, under the same parameters, events and cancel table.
