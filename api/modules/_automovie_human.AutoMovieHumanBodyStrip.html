    Namespace AutoMovieHumanBodyStrip

    The strip joining an appendage's end ring to its root loop on a base (the redesign plan's section 3).

    At each phase u of the appendage's end ring, the strip's column is a clamped cubic B-spline in w from the appendage (w = 0) to the base (w = 1) on uniform knots. Its three control rows at each edge are set by that edge's point and its first and second cross derivatives by arc length, scaled by the edge length l (the root's reach times the chord between the edges) and l^2, so the strip meets both surfaces with their positions, tangent planes and curvatures (G2) by construction. With knot span h, at w = 0

    P_0 = A,   P_1 = A + h A' / 3,   P_2 = A + h A' + h^2 A'' / 3

    and symmetrically at w = 1 with the derivatives taken toward the base. Each interior row is the quintic Hermite blend of the two edges at its Greville abscissa, offset in the blend's local frame (its unit tangent along the ring, its unit normal and their cross product) by the root's fitted rows, periodic cubic B-splines in u, so whatever moves an edge carries them. Edge derivatives come from the chains' jets where they carry them, otherwise from differences of the chain functions.
