    Namespace WorkoutPlace

    Where a workout's bearing bones stay: the one place the kind's pin keeps while the body works, decided once per plan from how the kind is anchored.

    • stance: where the entry shows those bones, so a foot stays where it stands and not where the keys' own joints would stand it.
    • hips: where they are when the first posture rests with the pelvis over its place. Feet or toes that bear on the floor are as high above it as when the body stands on them, and hands as high as a laid palm's wrist; a body that bears on its trunk is rested by its shapes; a body on a raised support has its hip joints as high above it as above the floor when it lies flat.
    • bar: the prop's with the joints' own height above it, or, with none, the places a support exercise's bars stand at (WorkoutKind.bars) beside the place stood on, or the floor rule for a kind done on equipment (an imagined support). A hanging kind hangs from the bar it names and is refused without one; hands that grip a bar are pinned by their grip sockets, so the bar lies in the fists.
