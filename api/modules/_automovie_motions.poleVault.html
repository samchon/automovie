    Namespace poleVault

    A pole vault (#3377): out of a run, the hand that holds the pole plants its end on the floor ahead at the take-off, the body swings over the planted end with the pole, lets go as the pole stands upright, and flies on to come down lying on the landing surface.

    The pole is a held prop (AutoMovieMotionHumanHeld): taken up before, typically by a pick up with a use, and rigid in the hand. Its planted end is the prop's tip affordance, or the one named.

    1. Take-off: the jump's push-off (GymnasticsTakeoff) at the run's speed, kept, rising at ANGLE. Over it the holding hand carries the pole from the entry to the plant: the hand as high above the shoulder as the arm reaches, unless the pole is too short to reach the floor from there leaning LEAN or more, then lower, and the pole's end on the floor ahead along the run.
    2. Swing, about one axis, the body's left one through the planted end: body and pole turn as one rigid pendulum. At the plant the centre's velocity across the pendulum's radius is kept, as the angular momentum about the planted end is, and the part along the radius is lost to the plant; the turn then follows the energy of a point mass on a rigid massless pole, ω² = ω₀² + (2g/R)(cos θ₀ − cos θ), integrated once into a table of time by angle. The hand lets go when the pole stands upright. A run too slow to bring it upright is refused with the speed needed.
    3. Flight: the centre on the parabola of its velocity at the release, the body keeping the swing's rate, until its lowest shape meets the landing surface; then it lies there (GymnasticsMat), on its back or its front as its turn brought it.

    First pass: the pole does not bend and the body does not swing about the hands, so there is no inversion, no pull and turn and no push off the pole, which a bending pole and a body that rocks back over its hands give a vaulter (the multi-axis case is #3416); the pole's own mass and the body's inertia about its centre are left out of the pendulum; the other hand keeps the jump's arm swing; the released pole is not planned.

    Events: takeoff (the plant), release, touchdown, settled, relax.
