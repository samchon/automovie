    Namespace instance

    An instanced member: a performer that displays a template member's placed posture, read whole film frames earlier and moved rigidly by the offset it stands at from the template, so a rear rank marching with its front rank costs no plans and no fits of its own.

    At its start the law reads where it stands relative to the template, in the template's heading frame: an offset and a turn. Each posture is the template's at t - shift / 24 with the root, every limb goal's world point and bend, and every goal's rotation moved by that offset and turn about the template's root; a goal's contact lies in its end joint's own rest frame and turns with the joint, so it is kept; joint angles are the template's. So the instance's feet are planted where the template's are, moved, and a gaze layer on it still looks at its own target. The move stays rigid only while the template keeps its heading, so a span in which the template turns by more than TURN refuses (a wheel needs full members), and so does a template on another body, whose limbs the instance would carry. A goal attached to a bone is in that bone's frame and is kept; a goal that keeps clear of or rests on world colliders is refused, since those belong to the template's place. The instance gives up its own per-step draws for one whole-frame shift: the declared price of IHumanEnsemble.detail.

    Its contact windows are none: a planted goal it carries is the template's, moved, and the slip of an instance is the template's own.
