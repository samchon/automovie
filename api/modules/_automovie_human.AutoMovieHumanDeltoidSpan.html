    Namespace AutoMovieHumanDeltoidSpan

    The deltoid span: the upper arm from the arm's first own section, below the trunk's scye ring, to the deltoid insertion.

    Gray 1918 puts the deltoid's insertion, the deltoid tuberosity, at the middle of the lateral humerus. This legacy span instead uses an acromion-to-elbow midpoint projection; neither that convention nor the active half-AR proxy independently locates olecranon or a measured deltoid insertion. The span owns two observed rows, at 0.3 and at the insertion, where the reference sections first stand free of the trunk on all four references (nearer the root, m1's and m2's sections still merge with the chest). Above the first row the section is held, so the root strip's last section is this span's first row, not an invented shape; the deltoid's three heads and the deltoid-pectoral groove are to be read where a reference section is free of the trunk, which this sampling does not give.

    Rows are the medians of f1, f2, m1 and m2, read on each reference's skin square to its own humerus (the centroid line of its upper arm sections), with the guides' angle derivatives by least squares over +-12 degrees about their angle; the sector model leaves 0.2 to 0.8 % of the radius unexplained. The row at 0.45 of the humerus stands for the insertion row (the insertion is at 0.4388 of it for a hanging arm, 0.011 of the humerus or 3 mm away).
