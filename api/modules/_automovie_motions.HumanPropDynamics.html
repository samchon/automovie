    Namespace HumanPropDynamics

    Props with mass and friction moved by the hands' force (#3433): a prop on the floor slides under Newton's second law and Coulomb friction, a hinged prop turns under its torque, and the pushing body leans so its weight balances the reaction of the force it applies.

    • Slide. A prop of mass m at rest stays while the applied horizontal force is within the static friction μs m g; once moving it accelerates at (F - μk m g) / m against its motion (Coulomb's law), until it stops, where the static friction holds it again. Its position and speed are integrated at plan time by the engine's integrator, each stop located inside its step and the integration restarted there, so playback and seek read the same path.
    • Hinge. A door of moment of inertia I about its hinge turns at θ'' = τ / I under the torque applied at its handle.
    • Lean. A body pushing horizontally with a force F at the height y of its hands leans until its weight's moment about its feet, M g h sin θ, balances the push's reaction, F y (static moment balance).

    The prop's mass and its static and kinetic friction are its model's (IAutoMovieBody), read by the caller; this module reads coefficients only. First pass: rolling resistance is the kinetic coefficient the producer gives; the push's force profile comes from the caller, since no pushing-strength study has been read for it. slideProp offers the trajectory as the frame of a prop in a performance. hingeProp supplies an articulation track. The film authors each drive independently of the poses that follow the prop.
