    Namespace StrikingSkill

    The striking family's measured timing, speed, reaction and guard numbers, from novice to elite, as HumanSkill.quantity reads them. They belong to striking alone: no other action family reads them, and striking reads no other family's numbers (only the cross-task variability laws of HumanSkill).

    Elite phase durations are the measured means of trained populations; the novice end scales them by the ratios of Vagner et al. 2023 (a systematic review of the roundhouse kick, PMC10459763): the time from launch to target is about equal across levels (280 to 300 ms, so the strike phase scales x1.06 to x1.0), while the whole kick takes 1.06 s for novices against 0.84 s for elites, which the chamber and the recovery absorb (x1.4 to x1.0). Peak distal speed differs far more (6.5, 11.4 and 14.3 m/s for novice, sub-elite and elite foot speed), so with near-equal time the novice covers a shorter path: proficiency scales the share of the available range a strike uses (its chamber and its trunk turn), never the range itself.

    Constants with no located study are marked provisional, with the measurement that would settle them.
