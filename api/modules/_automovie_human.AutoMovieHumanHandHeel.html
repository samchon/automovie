    Namespace AutoMovieHumanHandHeel

    The wrist heel: the hand's skin from the wrist crease to the thumb's branch, where the forearm's bone tube hands over to the hand, its own piece with the carpus's axis (the hand's own frame, along the third metacarpal's direction) and not the forearm's.

    At each station a of the hand's own axis (the crease at PRIOR.hand.crease, the branch at PRIOR.hand.branch) and phase t, the heel's point is the bone tube's point there (bone, the arm's cut of its tube by the plane square to the hand: the arm owns the tube and publishes where it ends, AutoMovieHumanWristSpan.end) drawn to the hand's skin by the quintic smooth step v of the way from the crease to the branch:

    p(a, t) = bone(a, t) + v (skin(a, t) - bone(a, t))
v = smoother((a - crease) / (branch - crease))

    where skin is the hand form's heel star (wholeSkin) handing over to the palm's run and the thumb's opening across the thenar's reach. At the crease v is 0, with its first and second derivatives, so the heel there is the bone tube's own section and the seam with the forearm is G2 by construction: the heel publishes the crease layer's jet (creaseJet) by arclength and the forearm copies it (AutoMovieHumanSeam, J1). At the branch v is 1 with zero slope and curvature, so the palm's and thenar's chains inherit the hand's skin untouched.
