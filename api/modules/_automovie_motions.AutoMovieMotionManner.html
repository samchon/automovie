    Namespace AutoMovieMotionManner

    How an action is performed, as distinct from which action it is: how fast, how large, how forceful, how skilled and how varied.

    A manner is resolved once into modifiers. The tempo and the hold factor change durations through the clock (time); the extent, the curvature and the bend change sizes through the phrases. A manner never changes a goal on a solid place: a phrase that states a contact keeps its place, and only a place in the air, an excursion about the base or a free-space amplitude is scaled. The absent manner is neutral and leaves every action as it is.
