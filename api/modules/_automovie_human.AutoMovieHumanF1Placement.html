    Namespace AutoMovieHumanF1Placement

    f1's placing read at a pose: the stored local tables (AutoMovieHumanF1Placing) mapped through the chain of segment frames into the figure's frame.

    A pose is one rotation per joint (AutoMovieHumanSegmentFrame.world); a joint the pose leaves undefined keeps its reference rotation, so no pose at all is the reference pose, the pose f1 was traced in. A point of the table is o + A q in its segment's posed frame and a direction A q, so a rotation of a joint moves every datum of the segments past it rigidly and leaves every stored value as it was.

    A family is placed by three kinds of field and no other: its course's centres, each a datum of the segment it stands in; its up, a direction of its course's first segment; and each relief's axis and turn, directions of the segment its window lies on. Its section table is local to that course already (AutoMovieHumanConicLoft.axis), so place carries it untouched. Where a pose changes a course's joint angles, the family is carried by AutoMovieHumanConicPose: its turns keep their set-backs, its stations are mapped and its chart takes the roll its joints' twists ask for, each an exact no-op at the reference pose.
