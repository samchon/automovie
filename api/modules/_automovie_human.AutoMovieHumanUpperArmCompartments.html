    Namespace AutoMovieHumanUpperArmCompartments

    The upper arm's compartments: the span from the deltoid insertion to the supracondylar least girth above the elbow.

    The four guides run on the compartments' boundaries: forward on the biceps crest, lateral on the lateral intermuscular septum, back on the triceps and medial on the medial septum with the neurovascular course; the sectors between them are the anterior compartment (biceps and brachialis) split in two, and the posterior compartment (triceps) split in two. The span only falls in girth from the insertion to the supracondylar ridge: in a relaxed arm the biceps belly is a sector's shape, not a girth maximum (Gray 1918).

    Rows are medians of f1, f2, m1 and m2, read on each reference's skin square to its own humerus, at 0.6 and 0.75 of the humerus' joint-centre length and at the supracondylar least girth. The span copies the insertion row from the deltoid span, which owns it, and owns the supracondylar row for the elbow span to copy.
