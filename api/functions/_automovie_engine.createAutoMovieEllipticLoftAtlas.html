      Form a shared bicubic atlas from cubic centre and ellipse-radius profiles. Each quarter uses the tangent-matching circular cubic with k = 4 (sqrt(2) - 1) / 3. Its radial deviation from the unit circle is below 0.000273: differentiating x(t)^2 + z(t)^2 locates the interior extrema. Applying the diagonal ellipse scale bounds the section's Hausdorff deviation by that constant times its largest half-axis. This is a separate geometric approximation from the atlas-to-triangle tolerance and any observation error. Clockwise angular U and increasing physical Y make U cross V outward. Adjacent spans share coefficient identity at equal endpoints; each patch keeps its own analytic normal fan so a C0-only axial join retains both limits. C1 remains the caller's constraint rather than an inferred property.
