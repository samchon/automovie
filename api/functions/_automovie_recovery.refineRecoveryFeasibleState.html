      Refine a feasible fixed-population recovery state with strictly inward models. A pure tilted QP supplies descent and active-constraint inwardness. Actual retraction, all nonlinear gates and Armijo decrease must also pass. Only an original-coordinate normal KKT certificate terminates successfully. Optional acceleration separately certifies an untilted affine QP, mixes its physical step with this baseline, and may reuse its factor for one same-base curvature arc with a 2.5 ceiling and dual-priced descent cap. Qualified same-base full candidates and one half on the accelerated arc compete by original cost; ties retain the earlier one. A mixture is not the baseline QP minimizer. Every evaluated candidate, including qualified unselected candidates, consumes maxTrials allowance. The last accepted endpoint receives a charged model check, not another step. An optional owner notification consumes the exact selected increment before changing the current state; it cannot replace candidate or normal checks.
