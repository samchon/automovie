    Function createQuadruped

    • createQuadruped(
          params: createQuadruped.IParams,
          seed?: string | number,
      ): Archetype<createQuadruped.Affordance, createHorse.Dimension>

      A four-legged animal of a kind, with its kind's tack: every kind shares one body of the horse's bone vocabulary (QuadrupedBody) and one ride (rideQuadruped), and differs from the others only by its species' data, its proportions, its gaits and its tack.

      The four equids retain their existing producers and tack. A dog is createDog's coarse Husky cohort prototype with required authored mass and optional authored collar. A bare adult domestic cat supplies a level walk. Ox, single-hump camel and Boso sika are coherent coarse cohort references, with actual identifying parts and optional authored equipment. Source limitations remain with each body/gait owner; fixed cohort means are not measured population variation or complete animal dynamics.

      Parameters

      • params: createQuadruped.IParams

        The kind, and the kind's own parameters.

      • Optionalseed: string | number

        The seed that draws every dimension left free inside its band; absent, each takes the middle of its band.

      Returns Archetype<createQuadruped.Affordance, createHorse.Dimension>

      The animal.

      Throws

      For a kind that is not one of KINDS, and as the kind's producer refuses.
