    Function nearest

    • nearest(
          body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          bones: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>,
          a: IAutoMovieVector3,
          b: IAutoMovieVector3,
      ): IAutoMovieMotionHumanColliders.INearestResult

      Where a segment comes nearest a posed body: the signed distance and the bone of distanceToSegment, with the point of that bone's colliders nearest the segment and their outward normal there, read by the engine's one proximity owner (physicalShapeNearest). A flight's contact reads where it struck and which way the surface faced.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The body.

      • bones: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>

        Its posed bones, world frame.

      • a: IAutoMovieVector3

        One end, world metres.

      • b: IAutoMovieVector3

        The other end, world metres.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanColliders.INearestResult

      The distance, the bone, the surface point and its outward normal.

      Throws

      When the reduction remains positive Infinity, including an empty observed profile.
