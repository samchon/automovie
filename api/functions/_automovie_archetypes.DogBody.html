    Function DogBody

    • DogBody(cohort: DogBody.Cohort): QuadrupedBody.IBody

      A coarse Husky control rig from one whole morphometric cohort mean.

      Huson, Srikanth and Ellis 2025, Genes 16:1355, Table 1 reports tape-measured surface landmarks, not skeletal lengths (https://doi.org/10.3390/genes16111355). Pet, show and sled means remain separate. The foot measurement is divided equally into two control bars; their historical Cannon/Pastern names do not turn that discretization into measured canine metapodial or digit lengths. Hind angles are the two-link triangle reaching the measured tail-base height with a vertical foot. Initial fore controls are vertical; the shared QuadrupedBody parent-solid construction connects an outside-body root by articulating its two proximal controls with unchanged lengths and foot. That neutral construction is authored geometry, not measured canine pose. Circular cross sections, straight neck/tail and the head's eye-width radius are construction proxies. No canine segment masses or paw material are known here.

      Parameters

      • cohort: DogBody.Cohort

        The complete measured cohort, never a blend of its traits.

      Returns QuadrupedBody.IBody

      Its coarse control proportions; no anatomical or visual validation is claimed.
