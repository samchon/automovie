      What the solve's knuckle section, read on the palm's rings and the border fingers' roots, must measure for the meshed skin to measure the breadth and the hand tape.

      The skin at the knuckles is the palm's and, at its borders, the webs' columns out to the index and little fingers' roots, which the surface builds after the chains; read on the chains alone the breadth missed by -5 to +4 mm, depending on how the border was approximated. So the hand is solved, its palm and fingers are meshed by the body's own surface, the skin's section is measured (AutoMovieHumanKnuckleSection), and the solve's targets move by the skin's ratio to the inputs, in SKIN_ROUNDS rounds; the ratio of skin to chain changes little with the scales, so each round shrinks the miss severalfold. Measured in the left frame for either side, since the right is its reflection; cached per mirror-free inputs, so one side serves the other's mirror image, and because the skeleton asks for the same bones first.
