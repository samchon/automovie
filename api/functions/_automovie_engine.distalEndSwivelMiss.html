    Function distalEndSwivelMiss

    • distalEndSwivelMiss(
          props: distalEndSwivelReadings.IProps,
      ): ((low: number, high: number) => number) | null

      A certified lower bound, over an interval of swivels, of how far the end joint of a chain whose mid segment twists distally (a wrist beyond a forearm) must miss a requested rotation, degrees.

      Whatever twist the mid segment takes and wherever in its box the end joint stands, the rotation the chain shows carries the mid segment's world axis u to d(f, a) = (-sin a, cos a sin f, cos a cos f) in the end joint's basis, with (f, a) inside the box (distalEndSwivelReadings), while the requested rotation carries it to x(s), the direction the readings give at swivel s. A rotation by an angle moves no unit vector by more than that angle, so the angle between the shown and the requested rotation is at least the angle from x(s) to the nearest d(f, a) of the box: the spherical distance from a point to a rectangle of latitudes (abduction) and longitudes (flexion) about the flexion axis. Inside the rectangle's longitudes that distance is the abduction's excess past its interval; outside them the nearest point lies on one of the two bounding meridians, where the direction's cosine with the meridian point of abduction a is -x_f sin a + c cos a, c = x_a sin e + x_t cos e, greatest at the abduction nearest atan2(-x_f, c).

      x(s) circles an axis as the swivel turns, on a circle whose radius is the norm of its sinusoids' cosine (or sine) coefficients, so it moves by at most that radius per unit of swivel and the distance, which changes by no more than its point moves, is bounded over an interval by its value at the middle less the radius times the half width.

      The bound is lowered by the joints' tolerance (ROM_TOLERANCE), within which a projection onto the box may stand past its ends, so it stays below every miss it bounds.

      Parameters

      • props: distalEndSwivelReadings.IProps

        The end joint and the mid segment's direction by swivel.

      Returns ((low: number, high: number) => number) | null

      The bound over low to high degrees, never negative; null when the analysis does not apply (distalEndSwivelReadings.of).
