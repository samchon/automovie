      ): IAutoMovieLoftRelief[]

      The reliefs of one leg.

      Parameters

      • bones: IAutoMovieHumanLegStations.IBones

        The joints' heights, mm.

      • girth: OfGirth

        The girth the law asks for at a station, mm.

      • span: OfSpan

        The leg's own stations, from the ankle ring to the crotch ring, mm.

      • OptionalcrotchReach: number

        The span under the crotch ring over which the trunk's ring hands the skin over to the leg's law, mm; a window anchored at the crotch stands at its end. Absent (a leg read without its crotch ring), every window stands at its share.

      • diagnostics: IAutoMovieLoftContract.IDiagnostic[] = []

        The list a relief that cannot be placed is recorded in; the leg keeps its base there (level 3 for a bump, 4 for a recess).

      • Optionalfurrow: number

        The person's gluteal furrow height, mm: a window anchored at the crotch stands there when it lies inside the leg, the fold being where the furrow is.

      Returns IAutoMovieLoftRelief[]
