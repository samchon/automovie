    • superposeMinimumJerk(
          origin: IAutoMovieVector3,
          units: readonly IUnit[],
          seconds: number,
      ): IAutoMovieSuperposeMinimumJerk.IState

      A point moved by overlapping minimum-jerk units, each adding its own displacement over its own time window.

      Flash and Henis (1991, J. Cogn. Neurosci. 3:220) and Henis and Flash (1995) found that when a target jumps during a reach, the observed hand path is fitted best by superposing a second minimum-jerk unit, from the old target to the new one, on the unmodified first, rather than by aborting and re-planning; Meyer et al. (1988) model corrective submovements the same way. Because every unit is a quintic with zero velocity and acceleration at its ends, the sum is continuous in position, velocity and acceleration at every unit's start and end, ends at rest at the origin plus every displacement, and equals the first unit alone when a later unit's displacement is zero. position reads only that same accumulated point, omitting derivative profiles and velocity/acceleration/state records. Every unit still receives its original positive-duration check and normalized-time admission; an empty unit list performs no time admission, as in the complete reader.

      Parameters

      • origin: IAutoMovieVector3

        Where the point starts, metres.

      • units: readonly IUnit[]

        Displacements in metres with their start and duration in seconds.

      • seconds: number

        The instant.

      Returns IAutoMovieSuperposeMinimumJerk.IState

      Position (m), velocity (m/s) and acceleration (m/s²).
