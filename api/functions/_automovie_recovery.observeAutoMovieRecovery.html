      Observe a registered mesh and return source-free cubic coefficients and incidence. Inputs are borrowed and remain unchanged. Numerical fitting failures are reported by the observation owner; unsupported local components retain their diagnostics. No original position, triangle, normal or rig array enters the returned bundle. curveFormat selects every nested cubic basis; omitted means bezier. Conversion retains original phase widths and binary64 arithmetic limitations. onSourceLineage optionally observes copied original face incidence during fitting; those provisional acquisition records never enter the source-free result. Observer exceptions fail the operation, and cooperative cancellation stops further delivery.
