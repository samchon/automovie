    Function stageScene

    • stageScene(
          script: IAutoMovieScript,
          staging: IAutoMovieStage,
      ): IAutoMovieStagedSet

      The STAGING consumer: fold the script's cast and the staging write's placements into the IAutoMovieScene every later stage performs into, or return every violation for the correction round.

      Referential integrity is the core check: every placement names a cast member, every cast member is placed, ids are non-blank and unique across actors, set pieces, cameras and lights, a mount rides another placed actor, and a camera's lookAt node is a placed actor, set piece or camera, the same table the performance stage resolves positional targets against. Scalars are range-checked where a later stage could not use them: finite positions and turns, a unit set rotation not combined with facingDeg, a positive finite set scale, a field of view in (0, 180) degrees, 0 < near < far, the camera's depth precision and clearance envelope, a non-negative light intensity, each light's shape, and a finite derived camera look direction that does not collapse to its own position. Geometry is converted, not judged: whether 0.7 m is striking range is the reviewer's business.

      Conversions: an actor's facingDeg, about +Y with 0 facing +Z, becomes its rotation; a set piece becomes a static node through setPieceTransform; a camera's lookAt resolves to a point and the level-horizon look basis aims its -Z there; a light lowers through lowerLightPlacement to the scene light its type names.

      The optional space, the ground's walkable meaning, is accepted by validateSpace with its paths re-rooted under $input.space, and is composed as space: null when omitted, the scalar ground plane. An authored fog lowers verbatim, while fogVisibility solves the density from camera-axis depth and transmittance. resolveStagedFog admits one form and leaves fog absent when neither is authored. The optional environment is checked by validateSceneEnvironment against the staged lights and lowered verbatim.

      Parameters

      • script: IAutoMovieScript

        The screenplay whose cast staging must place.

      • staging: IAutoMovieStage

        The staging write.

      Returns IAutoMovieStagedSet

      The composed scene with its persistent mounts and authored actor support scopes, or every violation.
