    Function boxExcludedSwivels

    • boxExcludedSwivels(
          props: IAutoMovieBoxExcludedSwivels.IProps,
      ): (low: number, high: number) => boolean

      The swivels a joint's three-angle box provably refuses: for a joint whose bone-local rotation is a fixed rotation turned about a fixed axis by the swivel (the chain root of hingedArmSwivel.pose), a test of whether every swivel from low to high degrees reads past the joint's box in both of the charts decomposeJointRotationOnBasis chooses between, so that jointRomOvershoot scores each of them above zero.

      A rotation turned about a fixed axis by ψ has a matrix whose every entry is a + b cos ψ + c sin ψ (Rodrigues' formula between two fixed rotations), so every entry the decomposition reads is such a sinusoid, fitted exactly from the rotation at 0, 90 and 180 degrees. Each clinical bound is then a sinusoid's sign. The middle angle of the composition (atan2(s, cos) with a non-negative cosine, so asin(s)) lies past a bound q where s - sin q is positive; an outer angle atan2(y, x) lies past the upper end β of an interval no wider than a half turn where y cos β - x sin β (the vector's length times sin(δ - β)) is positive, and past the lower end α where x sin α - y cos α is. The alternate chart (alternateRigJointAngles) turns each outer angle by a half turn, negating its vector, and reflects the middle angle about its right angle. Where one such sinusoid exceeds MARGIN, its exact roots bound an arc of swivels (φ ± acos((MARGIN - a) / R)), and a reading is refused on the union of its axes' arcs.

      Every refusal is certain. A sinusoid above MARGIN puts its angle past the bound by more than MARGIN radians, the joints' tolerance (ROM_TOLERANCE, about 1.7e-4), far beyond the rounding of the fit, of the arc's ends and of the decomposition itself (near 1e-15); and an outer angle's vector is then longer than MARGIN, past the gimbal band (sqrt(ε), 1.5e-8) inside which the decomposition pins that angle instead. A joint gated by swing and twist, which the box does not score, is read by its range instead (swingTwistExcludedSwivels) when the frame the overshoot reads it in is given, and refuses nothing otherwise; an axis without an interval, an outer interval wider than a half turn (whose refusal is an intersection, not a union, of two arcs) and a middle interval met twice by the half turn the middle angle covers refuse nothing, so the test only ever says less than the box would.

      Parameters

      • props: IAutoMovieBoxExcludedSwivels.IProps

        The joint and its rotation by swivel.

      Returns (low: number, high: number) => boolean

      Whether every swivel in [low, high], degrees, is refused; a joint with nothing to refuse refuses no swivel.
