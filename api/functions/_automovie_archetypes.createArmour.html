    Function createArmour

    • createArmour(
          params: createArmour.IParams,
          seed?: string | number,
      ): Archetype<"wear", createArmour.Dimension>

      A piece of armour worn on one bone: a helmet (open, closed or brimmed), a cuirass, a mail shirt, a gauntlet, a vambrace or a greave.

      Actual closed wall cells leave face, neck, arm, hem and limb cavities open. Museum H/W/D and total mass remain the selected record's; fitted body boundaries retain their exact measure, plate and provisional helmet pad. Unfitted elliptical walls use the plate band as a minimum normal gauge; their varying gauge preserves shared inner edges and the record envelope. This coarse construction does not claim a uniform historical plate gauge. Fitted mail places its wall outside the body's breadth/depth. Its wall is the recorded mass's equivalent volume under the existing structural-steel density, not a measurement of wire, weave or historical alloy. A gauntlet's forearm radius sizes its cuff; its rigid mitten/thumb regions are coarse authored envelopes, not a measured hand. The selected armet23232 retains its missing visor and arm-defense35850 excludes its separate elbow reinforce. Coarse opening fractions distinguish the kinds without claiming calibrated artifact profiles. Each piece stays on one bone, +Y up, +Z front; wear is its origin. Brim depth and plate metadata retain their existing contracts.

      Parameters

      • params: createArmour.IParams

        The kind, the read piece, the body's measure, and any dimension the author fixes.

      • Optionalseed: string | number

        The seed that picks the read piece left free and draws every dimension left free inside its band; absent, the first piece and the middle of each band.

      Returns Archetype<"wear", createArmour.Dimension>

      The piece.

      Throws

      For an unsupported piece/dimension, invalid used body measures, inverted cuirass heights or a mail mass/coverage that cannot contain a positive wall and cavity.
