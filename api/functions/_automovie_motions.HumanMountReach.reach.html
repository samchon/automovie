    • reach(
          body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          seated: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture,
          sides: readonly AutoMovieHumanSide[],
          bow?: (degrees: number) => IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture,
          span?: readonly [number, number],
      ): (
          points: Partial<Record<AutoMovieHumanSide, IAutoMovieVector3>>,

      How far a seated rider leans the trunk forward to reach what the hands hold: the least flexion of the spine, up to LEAN, that brings each shoulder within ARM of its arm's length of its point, as a cyclist leans to the bar and a fixed-seat rower to the catch.

      Flexing the spine turns everything above it rigidly about the spine joint's flexion axis, a line fixed in the world while the other angles hold (jointToQuaternion applies flexion innermost), so each shoulder runs on a circle: s(θ) = a + b cos θ + c sin θ, with b and c of one length and at right angles. The three vectors are read once, from the shoulder at three flexions by forward kinematics. A point's squared distance from the shoulder is then K + B cos θ + C sin θ, and the least flexion bringing it within the arm's reach is a closed-form arccos root, so a reach that follows a moving grip costs no forward kinematics per instant.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The body.

      • seated: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture

        The seated posture.

      • sides: readonly AutoMovieHumanSide[]

        The holding hands.

      • bow: (degrees: number) => IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture = ...

        The optional rotation about the seat or the hips in place of spine flexion.

      • span: readonly [number, number] = ...

        The bounds of that rotation, degrees from the entered posture.

      Returns (points: Partial<Record<AutoMovieHumanSide, IAutoMovieVector3>>) => number

      For the hand goals in the seated frame, the rotation nearest the entered posture that reaches both, or the least worst excess reach when the arcs do not overlap.
