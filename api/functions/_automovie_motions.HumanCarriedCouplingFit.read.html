    • read(
          bearer: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          borne: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          row: IAutoMovieMotionHumanCarried.IRow,
          held: Grip,
          bends: boolean,
          grips?: Grip,
          planted?: IPlantedContact,
      ): HumanCarriedCouplingFit.IRead

      Register the carried region on the bearer at the authored key. If any wrist cannot reach its grip, find the least change to the bearer's stance and borne legs that preserves the resting contact and reaches every grip.

      Parameters

      • bearer: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The bearer's body.

      • borne: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The borne body.

      • row: IAutoMovieMotionHumanCarried.IRow

        The carry's row.

      • held: Grip

        The borne region each bearer hand holds, left then right.

      • bends: boolean

        Whether the bearer may leave upright: not when a second helper shares the body, whose contacts a bend of the first would carry away.

      • grips: Grip = held

        The borne region each hand grips where the bearer takes a nearer grip than the row describes (HumanCarriedCoupling.NEARER), left then right: the arms must reach these, while the turn stays fitted to the described holds, so a grip never turns the borne body; the described holds when absent.

      • Optionalplanted: IPlantedContact

        The additional height fixed by a second bearer standing on the common floor.

      Returns HumanCarriedCouplingFit.IRead

      The placement with the bones used to judge its reach.

      Throws

      A reach refusal on carry when no stance and legs within their ranges bring every held region within its arm.
