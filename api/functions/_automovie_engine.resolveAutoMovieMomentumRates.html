    Function resolveAutoMovieMomentumRates

    • resolveAutoMovieMomentumRates(
          model: IAutoMovieResolveCenterOfMass.IModel,
          posed: resolveAutoMovieMomentum.IPosed,
          bones: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>,
          rates: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, resolveAutoMovieBoneRates.IRate>,
      ): IAutoMovieBodyMomentum

      Instantaneous centroidal momentum from actual bone-origin rates.

      The model, posed parts, bones and rates must describe the same instant and the same unchanged mass model, in one common frame. Bone linear rates are metres per second and angular rates radians per second in that frame. Parts and their centre come from the existing mass-model pose owner; principal axes retain that owner's actual principal-to-common rotation. A part's centre moves at v_bone + omega_bone cross (c_part - p_bone). The result has the existing momentum shape, with centre and rates at this instant rather than at the middle of a finite interval.

      Every part remains present, including zero-mass parts whose intrinsic inertia can still contribute spin under the existing mass-model contract. The whole body needs finite positive mass. Inputs are borrowed for this synchronous reduction, and every returned vector is fresh. This function does not replace the two-pose momentum API or certify callback derivatives.

      Parameters

      • model: IAutoMovieResolveCenterOfMass.IModel

        The same ordered SI mass model that produced posed.

      • posed: resolveAutoMovieMomentum.IPosed

        Its rigid parts and mass-weighted centre at this instant.

      • bones: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>

        The actual common-frame bone poses used to produce posed.

      • rates: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, resolveAutoMovieBoneRates.IRate>

        Actual common-frame origin and angular derivatives of bones.

      Returns IAutoMovieBodyMomentum

      Instantaneous centre, centre velocity and linear/angular momentum.

      Throws

      When mass, rates or results are nonfinite, total mass is not positive, part count/masses do not match, or a segment's bone/rate is missing.
