    Function resolveHydrodynamicForce

    • resolveHydrodynamicForce(
          props: IAutoMovieResolveHydrodynamicForce.IProps,
      ): IForce

      The buoyant and drag forces water puts on one body segment (#3432), the per-segment structure of SWUM (Nakashima, Satou and Miura 2007, J Fluid Sci Tech 2(1):56-67): the limbs move by their joints and the water's forces on every segment move the root, as #3432 describes it (the paper itself not yet read).

      The segment is taken as the sphere of its colliders' volume, centred on its centre of mass, so its submerged volume below the water plane is the spherical cap's, π d² (3 r - d) / 3 for a depth d of the sphere below the plane. The buoyant force is that volume's weight of water, upward (Archimedes). The drag is the quasi-steady drag of a sphere, ½ ρ C_d A |v| v against the velocity, C_d the sphere's textbook 0.47 in the subcritical range of Reynolds numbers a limb in water reaches (provisional: no drag study was read for it), and A the sphere's section scaled by the share of its volume under water.

      First pass: SWUM's own coefficients, its normal and tangential drag of elongated segments and its added mass are not read; the sphere stands in for every segment, so a limb's drag along and across its axis is the same. Unsteady effects and the free surface's waves are outside a quasi-steady model.

      Parameters

      • props: IAutoMovieResolveHydrodynamicForce.IProps

        The segment's volume, its centre and velocity, the water plane and the water's density.

      Returns IForce

      The buoyant force, the drag and the submerged volume.

      Throws

      When the volume or the density is not positive.
