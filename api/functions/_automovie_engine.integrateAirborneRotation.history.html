    Function history

    • history(

              | prepareAutoMovieMassModel.IPrepared,
          shape: (
              seconds: number,

      A shape's history: at each second, the composite inertia of the posed bones and the relative momentum from the bones DELTA either side, second order in DELTA for a sufficiently smooth shape. Each query's inertia and relative momentum are computed once and kept by its exact second for the history's lifetime. Each reduction poses the parts once at each distinct instant, sharing their transformed centres with the mass-weighted centre. Exact nodes share their sufficient numeric mass parts across all overlapping stencils, stages and shooting trials of this history. The first demand reads the actual pure shape; later exact-node demands use those same numbers without retaining a full pose or raw bone map. The two fixed boundary stencils separately share their numeric moments.

      Given the flight's span, the three-point stencil slides up to its boundary and then stays inside it. At a requested instant t away from the stencil's centre s, its central momentum is corrected by (t-s)/δ times the difference of its right and left half-interval momenta: the derivative of a quadratic interpolant, second order in δ for smooth trajectories (Fornberg 1988, Mathematics of Computation 51:699-706). Its weights and nodes are continuous at the centred switch. For continuous interval momenta this adds no switch-induced value jump. An actual shape jump at a boundary can propagate through this stencil.

      Parameters

      • model: IAutoMovieResolveCenterOfMass.IModel | prepareAutoMovieMassModel.IPrepared

        The body's unchanged mass model for this reuse epoch, or an admitted planning-owned snapshot released separately after this history is cleared.

      • shape: (seconds: number) => ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>

        The posed bones at a second, in the root's own frame (the root at the origin, unturned): the composed shape without the flight's rotation. Pure, and read up to DELTA outside the flight, or up to 2 DELTA inside it at its ends when span is given.

      • Optionalspan: IHistorySpan

        The flight's take-off and touchdown, seconds; the shape is read across them when absent.

      • Optionalobserver: IObserver

        Optional native callback-free work counter from observe(); omitted histories allocate no counter record and retain the ordinary physical result/error behavior. Explicit handles accumulate across cache clears.

      Returns ICachedHistory

      The history.

      States root-frame shape, millisecond central difference, unchanged boundary correction, exact-time memo and separate prepared-model ownership. AirborneShapeHistory reduces the same physical tensor and two-pose momentum with the unchanged stencil and quadratic boundary correction; only I and h enter the flight ODE. The public history entry selects the established DELTA and delegates memo, stencil and model reduction to one history owner. Every first demanded exact mass node reads its actual pure shape; no time grid, shape interpolation, stencil reduction or tolerance change is introduced. A new query uses at most four distinct numeric nodes and O(S) mass work. Each exact node performs shape/IK/FK only on its first demand, even when other queries separate repeated endpoint reads; actual current orbital terms, tensor sums and physical checks remain unchanged. Exact time under one unchanged model, pure shape and span identifies equivalent I/h and numeric mass parts across this operation. Fixed boundary moments are separately shared; no time is rounded. A prepared token supplies both model reducers without unrelated-model pairing, and no shooting target or IK answer enters node storage. For N distinct I/h queries and S fixed model parts, at most 3N+6 numeric nodes cost O(N*S), alongside O(N) I/h and two boundary moments. Private moments share 3S numeric fields after exact checks, leaving at most 8S+3 per node; exact unchanged principal quaternions can additionally share their four fields. O(S) last-value references and map keys/object/array overhead depend on runtime. clear releases every node/shared-vector list/map/inverse registration; no raw bone map/full pose is retained. The caller separately releases its borrowed O(S) model.
