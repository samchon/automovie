    Function EquineJointLimits

    • EquineJointLimits(bone: Bone): IReference

      Look up clinical horse joint-angle observations for an actual distal bone.

      The bone identifies its proximal joint. Right-side lookup returns the same study population, with its measured side retained; it asserts no symmetry. These are included landmark angles, not offsets from the model's rest pose. Means, standard deviations and standing extension are not anatomical bounds. Shoulder, hip and axial observations, and every local rotation limit, remain unknown. Skin-marker rays also differ from the model's coarse bone bars.

      Parameters

      • bone: Bone

        The actual bone whose proximal joint is being inspected.

      Returns IReference

      Read-only source observations and explicitly unknown runtime limits.
