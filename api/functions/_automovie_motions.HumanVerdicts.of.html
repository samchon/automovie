    • of(
          performance: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPerformance,
          rate?: number,
          span?: IOfSpan,
      ): IAutoMovieMotionHumanVerdicts.IReport

      Read every bound on a performance, or on one span of its film.

      A span lets a long film be read in parts, each by its own caller: the bounds are read over the span as a film of its own, starting at its from, and every finding is placed at its film second. A bound that reads a step between frames reads it only where both frames lie in the span, so parts that are to cover a film whole overlap by two frames of the grid. The timing faults belong to the schedule, not to a span, and are read whatever the span.

      Parameters

      • performance: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPerformance

        The performance.

      • rate: number = 24

        Grid samples per second for the bounds read on a grid; 24 when absent.

      • Optionalspan: IOfSpan

        The film seconds the bounds are read over; the whole film when absent.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanVerdicts.IReport

      The report.

      Throws

      When the span does not lie within the film or ends before it starts.
