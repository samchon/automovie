    Function arc

    • arc(points: readonly AutoMovieVector3Tuple[]): IAutoMovieSolidTraceLaw.ILaw

      The reference-guide law used for requested arc parameterization through equal-chord walked points; it is not an exact arclength reparameterization. The periodic cubic B-spline Q through them (AutoMovieSplineCurve's interpolation, component by component), and g = (P - Q(t)) . Q'(t), whose zero is the point's foot on Q. With P' = A_s s' + A_phi phi': g_t = -Q' . Q' + (P - Q) . Q'' and, past the terms of J x'' and Q_A . Q', d^2 g / dt^2 keeps 2 P' . Q'' - 3 Q' . Q'' + (P - Q) . Q'''.
