      Build a polygon actor and its source-frame sampler for a compiled shot. A film host mounts the drawing in the shot scene and supplies drawLayer as a layer preparation callback. Its source frame accounts for cuts and trims. If an actor frame is supplied, the sampler also places the drawing at that world frame under its rigid parent. Without a supplied frame the cue's shot axes coincide with the drawing's axes at the origin. Dispose the drawing with the usual polygon drawing owner.
