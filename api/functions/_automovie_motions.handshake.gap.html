    Function gap

    • gap(
          a: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          b: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          side?: AutoMovieHumanSide,
      ): number

      The horizontal sternum-to-sternum gap at which two partners facing each other shake hands, the gap an approach stops at.

      Each offering arm reaches forward u sin(handshakeOffer.SHOULDER) + l cos(slope) from its shoulder, the forearm l carrying the palm socket as handshakeOffer.forearm does and sloping 90 - SHOULDER - ELBOW degrees below level, further down by handshakeOffer.shape under the higher elbow and less under the lower, the higher upper arm lowered as that shape says; the two reaches sum to the shoulders' horizontal distance, across which the shaking shoulders sit each to its own side, and each sternum leads its shoulder as the standing body places them (stance). Partners whose floor points stand this gap plus both sternum offsets apart therefore meet palm to palm where handshakeOffer.point places the point, both upper arms in the offering shape.

      Parameters

      • a: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        One partner.

      • b: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The other.

      • side: AutoMovieHumanSide = "right"

        The shaking hand of both; right when absent.

      Returns number
