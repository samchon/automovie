    • formationSubjectBox(
          props: IAutoMovieFormationSubjectBox.IProps,
      ): IAutoMovieSubjectBox

      The world-space box, in metres, that one formation's members occupy at a shot-local instant.

      The unit's designed bounds go through transformFormationBounds under the cue sampleFormationMotion reports at that instant, so a mass that has marched, wheeled, or closed its ranks is framed where it is. Both reads are the builder's own: the ground gate that refuses a member standing off its staged surface asks the same two functions the same way, so a gate and a camera cannot disagree about where a unit is. A time before 0 reads the unit as it stands at 0.

      The transformed slot box is then widened on X and Z by the member radius the compiler derived for LOD projection, ignored when negative, and its Y range raised by the member's own extent, because the box of slot positions is a footprint: the outermost member's body hangs over its edge and its head stands above it.
