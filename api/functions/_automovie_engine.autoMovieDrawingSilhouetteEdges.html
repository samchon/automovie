    Function autoMovieDrawingSilhouetteEdges

    • autoMovieDrawingSilhouetteEdges(
          frame: IAutoMovieDrawingFrame,
          triangles: readonly IAutoMovieDrawingTriangle[],
      ): IAutoMovieDrawingEdge[]

      Silhouette of a triangle soup under the view's orthographic direction.

      An edge is drawn when the faces meeting along it disagree about facing the viewer, or when only one face owns it. A face's facing is the sign of its winding normal against the frame normal, and an edge-on face counts as facing away, which is what makes a plan of an upright box its four top edges: the top faces the viewer, the walls are edge-on, and the disagreement along each top edge is the outline. Degenerate triangles are skipped.

      Corners are welded on the rounded output grid before edges are keyed, so two triangles meeting at one corner are recognised as meeting there even when the arithmetic that produced it differed in a last bit. Two corners closer than the grid but on opposite sides of a rounding boundary are not welded.

      Parameters

      • frame: IAutoMovieDrawingFrame

        Page basis whose normal points back at the viewer.

      • triangles: readonly IAutoMovieDrawingTriangle[]

        World triangles of one drawable, in their source winding.

      Returns IAutoMovieDrawingEdge[]

      One world edge per silhouette or border edge, in first-seen order.
