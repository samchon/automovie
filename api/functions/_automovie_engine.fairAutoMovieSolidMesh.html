    Function fairAutoMovieSolidMesh

    • fairAutoMovieSolidMesh(
          mesh: IAutoMovieSolidMesh.IMesh,
          regions: IRegions,
      ): number

      Fair a drawn mesh over the regions about given vertices: the discrete thin-plate fill of a hole, the triangles within each region moved so that the bi-Laplacian of their positions vanishes, with the two rings of vertices outside the region held where they are.

      Where a construction leaves a corner that no single surface owns, as at a split's fork, where the host's end and two bands meet, every band turns sharply beside the corner and the skin shows a notch or a crease there; where a part is merged onto its host, the narrow blend between them leaves a notch wherever the trace turns sharply. The orthodox remedy in the branching-surface literature trims a neighbourhood of the corner or the seam from the meeting surfaces and fills the hole with a patch G1 to its rim (Gabrielides, Ginnis and Kaklis 2007). On the realized mesh that patch is the minimizer of the discrete thin-plate energy sum_k |U x_k|^2 over the region's vertices x: U the umbrella operator, U x_k = sum_j (x_j - x_k) over the neighbours j of k (Kobbelt, Campagna, Vorsatz and Seidel 1998, "Interactive multi-resolution modeling on arbitrary meshes"), its rows taken at the region's vertices and the first ring outside it. Its stationary point is U^2 x = 0 on the region, the fairing fill of Botsch and Kobbelt 2004 ("An intuitive framework for real-time freeform modeling"), whose solution meets the fixed first ring with the tangent planes the second ring gives it, so the fill is G1 to the skin it leaves. The umbrella's weights are all one, so the system is linear and its weights cannot change sign. The cotangent Laplacian (Pinkall and Polthier 1993), which keeps a planar patch where it is, was tried first: a split's bands are meshed in graded rows and fans, and round f1's forks their obtuse and folded triangles gave negative weights that turned the fill's own triangles over (edges folding by 90 degrees or more round the toes rose from 70 to 106 to 170, as the negative weights were clamped at zero or kept); the umbrella also slides each vertex toward its neighbours' mean, which evens those fans out, and folded fewer (51).

      The mesh's coincident vertices are read as one (distinct IDs a shared boundary may hold at one position) and moved together, so no seam opens. A region is the vertices whose shortest path along the mesh's edges from the nearest of its sources is shorter than its radius: one source is a disc about a point, a curve's vertices a band along the curve. A region does not reach across a gap to skin the path does not cross. Overlapping regions are solved as one. The system is solved by conjugate gradients on the displacements, symmetric and positive definite on a region whose rim holds it; each coordinate is solved apart.

      A moved vertex keeps its owner and chart coordinates, which then name the surface point it was moved from: the fill belongs to no surface. Topology, indices and every vertex outside the regions are unchanged.

      Parameters

      • mesh: IAutoMovieSolidMesh.IMesh

        The drawn mesh, mm; its positions are changed in place.

      • regions: IRegions

        Each region's sources as vertex indices of the mesh, at least one, and its radius along the mesh, mm, positive and finite.

      Returns number

      How many distinct positions were moved.
