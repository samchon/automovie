    Function resolveAutoMovieMomentum

    • resolveAutoMovieMomentum(
          model: IAutoMovieResolveCenterOfMass.IModel,
          before: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>,
          after: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>,
          seconds: number,
      ): IAutoMovieBodyMomentum

      Linear and angular momentum of a posed body from two of its poses seconds apart, at the interval's middle, read from the mass model resolveAutoMovieCenterOfMass builds once per body: every profile segment a rigid part riding its bone, with its mass, centre and principal inertia.

      Airborne rotation, the response to an impulse and a pair's balance read the momenta and the composite tensor about the centre of mass (#3422). Units are kilograms, metres and seconds: linear momentum in kg m/s, angular momentum in kg m²/s, in the frame the bones are given in (the world, or a root's own frame when a caller passes root-frame bones).

      Each segment's velocity is the chord of its centre over the interval and its angular velocity the rotation vector of its relative turn Q1 Q0⁻¹, taken on the shorter arc (samplePoseVelocity.rotationVector), over the interval: both are central differences about the middle, with second-order error in seconds for sufficiently smooth segment trajectories. Each segment's position and principal frame are read at the middle (the mean of its centres and the half-way slerp of its frames), and the angular momentum about the middle's centre of mass is Σ m (c - C) × v + R diag(I) Rᵀ ω, the segments' orbital and spin momenta. A rigid turn of the whole body by θ over the interval introduces an O(θ²) orbital error through the mean positions and velocity chords. The logarithmic spin rate has no such universal scalar bias, and the complete relative momentum error depends on the mass distribution and motion; this stencil does not certify a relative error budget for a chosen interval.

      Parameters

      • model: IAutoMovieResolveCenterOfMass.IModel

        The body's mass model.

      • before: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>

        The posed bones at the interval's start.

      • after: ReadonlyMap<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, IAutoMovieResolvedBone>

        The posed bones at its end.

      • seconds: number

        The interval, seconds; positive.

      Returns IAutoMovieBodyMomentum

      The momenta.

      Throws

      When the interval is not positive or a segment's bone is not posed in both.
