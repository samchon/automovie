    Function measureAutoMovieMeshCrossings

    • measureAutoMovieMeshCrossings(
          first: IAutoMovieMesh,
          second: IAutoMovieMesh,
          options?: IAutoMovieMeshCrossingOptions,
      ): IAutoMovieMeshCrossing[]

      Report every triangle of first that a triangle of second crosses.

      Both meshes share one metre frame and are only read. An entry names the first-mesh triangle, one second-mesh witness, and whether the pair crosses transversally or overlaps flat in one plane; it is empty when the surfaces are disjoint or merely touch. Entries follow first-mesh ordinal, and the witness is the first crossing candidate in the order collectAutoMovieSpatialQueryCandidates gives: the first grid cell, at the second mesh's mean triangle size, the pair shares in X, Y, Z order, then the second ordinal. A transverse witness is preferred over a coplanar one. With allPairs every crossing witness is reported instead, which a corrective that must clear every contact needs; it then decides which shared vertices or intended contacts are exempt.

      Touching is not crossing. Two shells meeting along a seam share vertices and edges by construction, so every test is strict: a triangle edge pierces only strictly inside both the edge and the other triangle, a segment ending at or starting from the other triangle's corner is excluded by exact coordinate identity before any arithmetic, and coplanar triangles overlap only through properly crossing edges or strict containment. A fold through a shared corner still reports, because its other edges cross. interiorTolerance, a dimensionless margin in [0, 0.5) of the edge and triangle coordinates, lets a caller classify sub-resolution seam residues as contact; omitted, it is zero. acceptTransversePoint sees each strict transverse intersection point and may reject it, which removes that one witness and continues with the other edges and candidates; it cannot add a crossing the strict test did not find.

      The numerical cutoffs are classification thresholds, not proofs: a Moller-Trumbore determinant below 1e-15 counts as parallel and is left to the coplanar test, a face normal shorter than 1e-15 is never coplanar, and plane distances below 1e-9 count as in-plane. This measures overlap only, not depth, which measureAutoMovieMeshClearance gives along a chosen axis, nor which surface should move. Passing one mesh as both arguments tests it for self-intersection, as measureAutoMovieModelCrossings does with withinParts. Throws on a malformed index buffer, a non-finite position or a tolerance outside [0, 0.5).

      Parameters

      • first: IAutoMovieMesh

        Mesh whose triangles are reported.

      • second: IAutoMovieMesh

        Mesh tested against it, in the same frame.

      • Optionaloptions: IAutoMovieMeshCrossingOptions

        Pair population, contact tolerance and point classifier.

      Returns IAutoMovieMeshCrossing[]

      Crossings in first-triangle and candidate order.
