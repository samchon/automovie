    Function resolveBeatOpening

    • resolveBeatOpening(props: IAutoMovieResolveBeatProps): IAutoMovieBeatEndState

      Every scene actor at the shot's opening instant, t = 0, before any of its motion has played: where each stands, faces and is coupled as the beat begins.

      It has the shape of resolveBeatEnd's state, so the gait phase, root velocity and foot plants are the resumable fields at the opening. The continuity check compares it with the previous beat's end state to catch a cut that does not resume where that beat left off, a character who drifts or a prop that disappears. compileDefinedShot returns it as the opening continuity, overlaying a supplied previous beat's gait phase, root velocity, foot plants and mount per actor.

      Throws on duplicated motion ids, performance nodes, mounts or plant entries, and on a motion reference props.motions does not supply.

      Parameters

      • props: IAutoMovieResolveBeatProps

        The compiled shot, its staged scene and motions, and the mount and plant facts to carry.

      Returns IAutoMovieBeatEndState

      One opening state per scene node, in scene order.
