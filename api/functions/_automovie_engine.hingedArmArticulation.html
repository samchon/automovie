    Function hingedArmArticulation

    • hingedArmArticulation(
          props: hingedArmArticulation.IProps,
      ): IAutoMovieHingedArticulation | null

      Two-bone arm IK whose mid joint bends only about its own flexion hinge, with the chain root's free swivel spent on the rig's range of motion.

      The elbow rotates by θ about hinge alone, so its abduction and twist decompose to zero, which is what an elbow's immobile axes require, and the bend plane is the hinge's own plane. Splitting the far segment into the part along the hinge and the part it swings gives the root-to-effector span

      |m(θ)|² = k + α·cos θ + β·sin θ

      so the span equals the target distance where R·cos(θ − φ) = distance² − k, with R = hypot(α, β) and φ = atan2(β, α): an arccos whose two roots are the chain's two bend branches. A target outside the reachable span takes the fully extended or fully folded root, so the effector stops on the reachable shell along the line to the target. A root outside the mid joint's own range stops at the nearer limit in the same way, the effector resting on the legal shell along that line and the result reporting the shortfall; of two branches the one stopping nearer the target is swept, because a candidate past the hinge's limit would be clamped by the caller afterwards and land somewhere no candidate was scored at.

      The root joint first aims m(θ) at the target, then any extra rotation about m(θ) leaves the effector where it is. The function sweeps that swivel in SWIVEL_STEP increments on each branch, ordering candidates by the least overshoot (legal, zero, ahead of any other exactly; two positive ones tied within the decomposition's rounding, 1e-9 degree), then the least deviation, both from score. Every local minimum of a sweep is then refined between its neighbouring steps by golden-section search under the same ordering, and the best of the sweeps and the refinements is returned. A pass whose two probes tie closes the bracket onto both, so a request mirrored left to right, whose probes are the same two exchanged, refines to the mirrored swivel. So the chosen pose follows a moving target continuously rather than in swivel steps, a legal interval narrower than one step is still found from the dip it leaves in the sweep, and between two basins the truly better one wins rather than the one a sample happened to fall nearer the bottom of. The caller owns the decomposition and the rig's ROM, so the geometry here holds no rig policy.

      A caller that can certify swivels its score refuses (excluded) spares their scoring, and the pose is still the full sweep's. A step is skipped when every swivel within two steps of it is refused, and a step whose bracket, a step either way, is refused is not refined; every other step is scored and compared with its true neighbours, since a step that is not skipped and could be refined has both neighbours scored. When the pruned sweep finds a legal candidate, the full sweep's answer is that candidate: a refused swivel, a step or a refinement inside a refused bracket, is never legal, so it can never be ahead of a legal candidate (legality is compared exactly); every legal candidate of the full sweep is a scored step or the refinement of a step that is scored with the same neighbours, so it is the same candidate in both; and the ordering among legal candidates, met in the same order, is the same. When the pruned sweep finds none, the full sweep decides, so a chain that cannot reach legally still returns its least overshoot.

      A cold fit with closed candidates and no tie or minimum scores them alone and returns the best when it is legal and met accepts it: where a requested term is met only at isolated swivels, those are the whole admissible set and no search can add to it.

      A cold fit with a pole and no tie or minimum searches locally from it (hingedSwivelPole) and returns a legal result without the sweep, so it follows the bend hint rather than jumping to a far, marginally better swivel.

      A cold fit whose caller certifies that no candidate can pass met (unmet) skips the pole start, whose result met would refuse.

      A caller continuing a fit of a nearby target (near) skips the sweep: a closed candidate less than a step from the seed answers when legal and met (hingedSwivelNear), and otherwise the seed's branch, its swivel refined within one step either way by the same golden-section search under the same ordering, to the seed's tolerance (IAutoMovieSwivelNear). Every result names its branch and swivel, the seed a nearby fit continues from. If dividing a positive subnormal tolerance by the initial bracket width underflows, the difference of logarithms keeps the mathematically finite refinement count finite; ordinary ratios retain their original expression. With certified global score minima and no second key, the first scored coarse candidate ends a cold search only if its actual two keys equal both minima exactly. No later candidate can be strictly ahead, so the original candidate and complete-tie seed are preserved; later coarse candidates cannot use this shortcut because earlier refinements precede them.

      Returns null for a degenerate chain: a mid or end offset shorter than 1e-6, a target within 1e-6 of the chain root, a hinge shorter than 1e-6, or R below 1e-9, a hinge parallel to the far segment that cannot change the span (armChainFault reports that rig).
