      The arm of a planned carry that follows its goal.

      The correction is zero up to ready. On the frame grid from there, each frame takes one damped least-squares step (HumanArmDifferential.step) on the contact's Jacobian at the corrected arm, toward the goal's motion since the plan (moved) less the contact's motion the correction has already made, so a goal that has not moved leaves the correction exactly zero and the arm exactly the plan. The step is shortened to keep the elbow, the wrist and the fingertip within the plan's own pace (its fastest probe's travel over a frame of the span, the law's own timed arm) beyond the plan's own travel that frame, and halved while it would take the arm deeper past the tangle audit's skin than it is. After relax the correction returns to zero within the same bound, so the return rejoins the plan. Between grid frames the correction is interpolated, so the arm is a function of time: the grid is filled from ready once, in order, and kept.
