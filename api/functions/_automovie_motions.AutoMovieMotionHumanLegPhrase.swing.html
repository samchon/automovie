    Function swing

    • swing(
          props: IAutoMovieMotionHumanLegPhrase.ISwingProps,
      ): IAutoMovieMotionHumanPrimitive

      A swing: the foot is drawn to a chamber by ready, extends from strokeStart and passes its target at stroke at speed. With a follow phase it travels on past the target and slows to rest at strokeEnd (a kick in the air); with none it is arrested at the target (a kick met by a body). It stays until relax and stands on its stance again at end.

      Parameters

      • props: IAutoMovieMotionHumanLegPhrase.ISwingProps

        The phrase, the chamber, the target and the weapons the swing carries.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanPrimitive

      The primitive, with a contact window from stroke to relax when the phrase states a contact; the foot is planted only at the two ends.
