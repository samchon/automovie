      A hand holding a grip, a rein or a rim: where its wrist goes, how the hand turns and how its fingers close, a share of the way from where the posture's own joints hold the hand.

      An articulated round handle uses the measured depth of the gripping palm. On other supports the wrist stays PALM of the hand's length plus the grip's radius (half the width the object states across it; none when it states none) off the point along the point's outward normal and CENTRE behind it along the fingers, so the grip lies across the middle of the palm, the palm faces the point, and the fingers lie across the grip's own axis on the side the body faces, so they wrap a bar that runs across the hand; with no axis they point the way the body faces. The fingers close from the relaxed hand to the closure given, which closed curls on the support's own colliders.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The body.

      • side: "left" | "right"

        The hand.

      • posture: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture

        The rider's posture at the instant.

      • grip: IHoldGrip

        The held point and its outward unit normal, world, the unit axis the grip runs along when it has one, its radius, metres, when the object states one, and the side of the axis the fingers lie on (the sign of the axis crossed with the normal) when a hold keeps the side it was taken from; the side the body faces when absent.

      • share: number

        0 where the joints hold the hand to 1 holding.

      • closed: Partial<Record<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, readonly [number, number, number]>>

        The finger joints of the hand closed on the grip; none leaves the fingers as they are.

      • Optionalresolution: IAutoMoviePoseResolution | (() => IAutoMoviePoseResolution)

        Optional exact FK reader or lazy supplier borrowed only for this synchronous partial hold.

      • Optionalprojection: (pose: IAutoMoviePose) => IAutoMoviePose

        Optional genuine same-body ROM metadata projector borrowed from this same operation.

      Returns IHoldResult

      The arm's goal and the hand's finger joints.
