    • seeded(
          body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          posture: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture,
          base?: IAutoMoviePose,

      ): ISeeded

      resolveHumanPosture with each limb's fit continued from a nearby one where near names it (IAutoMovieSwivelNear), reporting the seed each limb's fit was chosen at: the same pose, solved the same way, with what a fit of the same limb a moment away continues from.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The performer's body.

      • posture: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture

        The posture.

      • Optionalbase: IAutoMoviePose

        A pose whose joints replace clinical zero for unset bones.

      • near: INear = {}

        Each limb's nearby fit to continue; a limb not named is solved cold.

      • Optionalresolution: IAutoMoviePoseResolution | (() => IAutoMoviePoseResolution)

        Optional exact reader or lazy supplier borrowed from this same synchronous person evaluation; never retained by the result.

      • Optionalrequested: ReadonlySet<AutoMovieHumanoidBone>

        Optional observational bone demand: only these frames equal full resolution; unneeded world limb goals may remain at clamped authored joints. Foot contacts retain re-aim/toe correction; continued, unsupported or arm-dependent context keeps full resolution.

      • Optionalprojection: (pose: IAutoMoviePose) => IAutoMoviePose

        Unread: assembly projects through the body's own projector (IHumanBody.projection), which every caller's projection is.

      Returns ISeeded

      The canonical pose and each actually fitted limb's seed; omission of requested preserves complete resolution.
