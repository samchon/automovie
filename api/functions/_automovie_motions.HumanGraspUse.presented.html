    Function presented

    • presented(
          props: IPresentedProps,
          closure?: IAutoMovieRigidTransform,
      ): IAutoMovieMotionHumanGrasp.IKey

      The pose a tool is presented in for its use, as a key the grasp must also hold: the grip at the place a law presents it (place), and the hand turned so that the tool's line lies on the line the use names, in the plane the body faces.

      Parameters

      • props: IPresentedProps

        The action's context, the prop and its handle, the hand, the hand the arm shows for the candidate grasp, the use, and how far the closed hand at a wrist keeps out of the body's other parts, metres, negative inside (every roll alike when absent).

      • Optionalclosure: IAutoMovieRigidTransform

        The prop's world frame at grasp closure, or the entry frame when omitted.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanGrasp.IKey

      The key pose.

      Throws

      An affordance refusal on use when the prop declares no such working point, listing its contacts; a parameter refusal on use for an along line ahead of the body, listing the other lines.
