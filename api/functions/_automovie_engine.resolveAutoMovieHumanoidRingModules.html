    Function resolveAutoMovieHumanoidRingModules

    • resolveAutoMovieHumanoidRingModules(
          bones: readonly IAutoMovieResolveHumanoidRingModules.IBone[],
      ): IAutoMovieResolveHumanoidRingModules.IModule[]

      The rigid ring modules of a humanoid body, each a short stack of eight-sided sections around one joint's segment, from the rest skeleton alone: joint positions, tips and girth radii.

      This is the one statement of the body's simple solid form. The polygon drawing lofts a mesh through each module's rings and the physical profile takes the convex hull of the same ring points as that segment's collider, so what is seen and what collides cannot drift apart.

      The skeleton is in the Human frame at rest: +X forward, +Y up, +Z left, metres. The trunk's modules share their boundary rings (pelvis floor, hip, waist, chest, shoulder, neck base), widened to the hip and shoulder joint spans; the neck runs from the neck joint to the head joint; the head widens to its girth and closes on a pole at its tip; a palm runs from the wrist toward the mean of the four finger roots as far as the farthest of them, so no knuckle is bare beyond it; a foot is two modules through the subtalar and midfoot joints to the toe roots; every other segment is three round rings from its joint to its end, narrowing to the next segment or bevelled at a leaf. Shoulder, scapula, foot, eye, jaw and atlas joints drive others and carry no module.

      Parameters

      • bones: readonly IAutoMovieResolveHumanoidRingModules.IBone[]

        The rest joints, parent before child, in the Human frame.

      Returns IAutoMovieResolveHumanoidRingModules.IModule[]

      The modules in drawing order: hips, spine, head, chest, neck, each side's hand and two foot modules, then every remaining segment in joint order.

      Throws

      When a joint a module is built from is missing.
