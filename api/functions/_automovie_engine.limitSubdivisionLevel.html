    Function limitSubdivisionLevel

    • limitSubdivisionLevel(
          level: IAutoMovieSubdivisionLevel,
      ): IAutoMovieLimitSubdivisionLevel.IResult

      Evaluate the limit position and face-varying limit normals of every vertex of a level.

      A vertex's limit is the limit of its child, so the level's points are refined once without building the next level's connectivity, and each vertex reads its child's quad one-ring from them: its vertex point, the edge points of its fan edges and the face points of its fan faces. That ring is all quads even when the level has other polygons. While any ring edge keeps a semi-sharp sharpness between 0 and 10, the ring is refined on its own, so a crease that outlasts the requested level still rounds off in the limit exactly as further refinement would; at most ten such steps are needed. limitSubdivisionRing then applies the limit masks.

      Normals are numbered vertex by vertex, sector by sector in fan order, and each corner names the normal of the sector its face belongs to.

      Parameters

      • level: IAutoMovieSubdivisionLevel

        The final level.

      Returns IAutoMovieLimitSubdivisionLevel.IResult

      Packed limit positions, packed unit normals, and each corner's normal index.
