    Function CamelBody

    • CamelBody(withersHeight?: number, cohort?: Name): QuadrupedBody.IBody

      A coarse dromedary body from explicit body and limb reference cohorts.

      Diop et al. 2020, REMVT 73:247-254, measured 131 Mauritanian adult females aged about 7-15 years (https://doi.org/10.19182/remvt.31948). Neck length is larynx to brisket attachment, head length is occipital crest to nose, head breadth is a tape path between ears via the crest, and chest girth excludes the hump. Mapping those surface measures to circular bars is a declared coarse interpretation, not recovered camel anatomy.

      Mostafa and Khalil 2018 Tables 2-3 supply separate Egyptian external limb controls (https://doi.org/10.5958/2277-8934.2018.00022.X). Their overall sample had 208 camels, 159 males and 49 females; the normal-subgroup size and withers mean are not supplied. Dividing those lengths by Diop's height is an explicitly provisional cross-cohort normalization. Table 5 standing internal joint angles do not determine absolute bar pitches or passive ROM; this profile keeps its authored standing controls separate.

      The five morphotype means in Diop Table II retain their complete body vector. Egyptian limb controls keep the existing ratios to the 1.847 m pooled reference, uniformly scaled to the selected height as provisional construction. They are not new measurements of that morphotype's limbs.

      Parameters

      • OptionalwithersHeight: number

        Admitted instance height in metres; only undefined defaults to the selected reference, while null is invalid.

      • cohort: Name = CamelCohorts.DEFAULT

        Whole pooled or classified adult-female reference; pooled by default.

      Returns QuadrupedBody.IBody

      Fresh coarse body controls; the actual producer owns the separate hump and tack.

      Throws

      For nonpositive or nonfinite instance height.
