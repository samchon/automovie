    Function subdivideAutoMovieSurface

    • subdivideAutoMovieSurface(
          props: IAutoMovieSubdivideSurface.IProps,
      ): IAutoMovieSubdivisionSurface

      Refine a polygon cage into a Catmull-Clark subdivision surface and evaluate its limit positions and normals.

      Each level applies Catmull and Clark's (1978) face, edge and vertex rules. Boundary edges are infinitely sharp, so a boundary follows the cubic B-spline of its cage edges, and a boundary vertex with one face is rounded rather than kept, OpenSubdiv's edge-only boundary interpolation. Creases follow DeRose, Kass and Truong (1998): a vertex with two sharp edges takes the crease rule, one with three or more is a corner, and semi-sharp edges blend into smooth ones as their sharpness decays. After the first level every face is a quad, so the cage may mix triangles, pentagons and other polygons with its quads.

      The limit position and normal of each vertex come from the published limit masks, evaluated after any semi-sharp sharpness left at the final level is spent, so they are what infinite refinement gives and do not depend on the level. A vertex on an infinitely sharp crease or boundary gets one normal per side. The exceptions are boundary or crease sides of one face, such as a cage corner under edge-only interpolation, or of four or more faces: there the limit surface has no tangent plane containing the crease, and the normal is OpenSubdiv's approximating mask, as limitSubdivisionRing explains.

      UVs refine face-varying with every chart boundary pinned, so an authored UV layout keeps its chart outlines exactly. The surface is deterministic: the same cage and level give the same numbers in the same order on every host.

      Refused before refinement: every defect admitSubdivisionCage lists, and a level that is not an integer from 1 to 6. The cap exists because each level multiplies the quad count by four, so the output is at most 1,024 quads per cage corner. A vertex whose limit normal vanishes, such as an interior vertex of valence two or a collapsed cage, is refused after refinement.
