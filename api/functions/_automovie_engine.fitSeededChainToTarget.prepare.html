      Prepare one source request for a synchronous operation that varies only its target, such as contact re-aim. The source owns its cold/near mode; neither the previous target nor its answer becomes a new seed. A supplied authored reader must include the source effector frame under this exact rig/pose/clinical mapping and admit every authored joint in order; extra frames may serve the caller. It shares completed FK at first use. An optional resolution reader belongs to the same synchronous operation and shares exact computation across its source, zeroed and reference FK. A supplied poseResolution must snapshot this exact source rig, topology and clinical tables; it enables requested-ancestor FK with fresh outputs. Omission preserves the raw full-FK admission path. An optional plant places the chain from its rig constants (placeChainPlant); such a chain carries its rig-only readers, built for these same clinical tables, and the workspace reads through them.

      States fixed dependencies, synchronous ownership and target-independent cold/near policy. Shares exactly the prepared rigid frames, domains, bases, clamps, fixed projector coefficients, source/reference FK and angle maps whose inputs remain fixed, then invokes the same bend search and final orientation for each target. prepareChainFit owns invariant data; fitChainBend owns candidate search and orientChainEffector owns final articulation; the rotation reader uses the same prepared frames. No obstruction/strain result, target-dependent candidate score, seed, target or final pose is memoized across targets; only fixed preparation, exact authored-FK readings, prior conversion and source continuity are shared. Invalid preparation retains the one-shot fallback and full FK rotation read. Rig preparation and at most two lazy source/reference FK reads occur once per workspace; an authored reader can reuse its exact FK. An explicit immutable rig computes only requested source/zeroed/reference ancestry and null-preparation effector rotation, with O(J) ordered joint admission and exact dependency reuse. Without it, raw full-FK admission remains. Each target retains its search; valid chain rotation uses a joint scan and constant chain FK. The fixed input scope proves equivalence; rig preparation getters and an optional exact authored-FK reader retain their original lazy admission sites, returned poses preserve fixed non-chain dependencies, and every target invokes all required candidate callbacks and projection queries. The three returned methods own one preparation workspace with O(J+B) borrowed input reachability and O(J) derived maps; no global cache or handle survives caller release.
