    Function manipulateReach

    • manipulateReach(
          body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          side: AutoMovieHumanSide,
          digit: string,
          site: IAutoMovieVector3,
          normal: IAutoMovieVector3,
          depth: number,
          rotation: IAutoMovieQuaternion,
      ): IAutoMovieVector3

      Where the wrist stands for one press of a keyed contact sequence (manipulate): the hand turned as the act keeps it, the pressing digit straight, its tip over the site at typing's hover height (typeText.HOVER) and advanced along the digit by as much as its swing down to the key's bottom shortens its reach.

      A straight digit of length r from its first joint, swung down by the hover height and the key's travel d, reaches sqrt(r^2 - d^2) ahead of that joint, not r: a tip placed straight over the key would stop short of it by the difference. The knuckle advances along the digit by r - sqrt(r^2 - d^2), and the press is a swing about the first joint that ends on the key, which the digit's own inverse kinematics then fits.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The body.

      • side: AutoMovieHumanSide

        The hand.

      • digit: string

        The pressing digit, as manipulate.Finger names it.

      • site: IAutoMovieVector3

        The site, world metres.

      • normal: IAutoMovieVector3

        The site's outward unit normal, world.

      • depth: number

        The key's travel, metres.

      • rotation: IAutoMovieQuaternion

        The hand's world rotation from its rest for the act.

      Returns IAutoMovieVector3

      The wrist's world point.
