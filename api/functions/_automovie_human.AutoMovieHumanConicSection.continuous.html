    Function continuous

    • continuous(values: readonly number[], layout: ILayout, k: number): number[]

      The field vector with the shoulders of the two arcs that meet at anchor k set so the section's curvature is continuous there, each arc keeping its triangle (its two end points and the meeting of their tangents).

      Two conics that meet at an anchor share its tangent but not its curvature, and where one arc ends nearly flat and the next starts sharply bent the wall turns within a few millimetres along the anchor's whole station line: f1's trunk over the hips met at its first median with curvatures of 1.2 and 308 per metre and drew an upright line down each hip. For a rational quadratic with end weights 1 and middle weight w = rho / (1 - rho) the curvature at an end is |(P1 - P0) x (P2 - P1)| / (w^2 |leg|^3), the leg the side of the triangle at that end (Farin, rational quadratic Bezier curves; Pottmann, curvature continuous conic splines). Both curvatures are brought to their geometric mean, so each arc's weight moves by the same factor; the shoulders stay within the model's bounds, where the joint is as near G2 as they allow. Merged into one conic instead (both halves of one arc split at its shoulder), the hip's shape moved further.

      Parameters

      • values: readonly number[]

        The section's field vector.

      • layout: ILayout

        Its layout, not a mirror's last anchor: k must have an arc on either side in the listed anchors.

      • k: number

        The anchor.

      Returns number[]
