      Plan one staged, level boarding transfer. LA Metro asks passengers to wait for a complete stop before boarding (https://www.metro.net/riding/how-to-ride-metro-rail/). Stationary docking is the default. The co-moving mode stages a scenic gangway whose platforms share upright rigid motion through trailing touchdown. Both soles and the root ride the deck after that touchdown. The dock check samples at intervals of at most 1/120 s, with 1 mm and 0.1 degree tolerances on stationary prop frames or, in co-moving mode, relative deck alignment at its centre and corners.

      The lead sole lands while the trail sole bears on the platform. The deck and platform must be level within 2 mm. Provisional staging limits are a 0.13 m projected gap, 0.45 hip heights of ankle travel and a 65 mm lift. Articulated surfaces, unsampled track excursions and obstacles between the supports require a broader law.

      Throws
