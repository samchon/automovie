    Function eulerYXY

    • eulerYXY(r: AutoMovieMatrix3Tuple): EulerYXYResult

      The ISB shoulder angles, a Y-X-Y Euler sequence (Wu et al. 2005 2.4.7, the humerus relative to the thorax): R = Ry(a) Rx(b) Ry(c) with a the plane of elevation (0 abduction, 90 degrees forward flexion), b the elevation and c the axial rotation (internal positive), radians. The text writes "elevation (negative)", and its footnote 5 that the second rotation is by definition in the negative direction as a consequence of the chosen axes, so the branch with b in [-pi, 0] is returned: an arm raised sideways reads a plane of elevation near 0 and a negative elevation. From the product, R[1][1] = cos b, R[0][1] = sin a sin b, R[2][1] = cos a sin b, R[1][0] = sin b sin c, R[1][2] = -sin b cos c, so with sin b <= 0: a = atan2(-R[0][1], -R[2][1]), c = atan2(-R[1][0], R[1][2]).
