    Function touch

    • touch(props: ITouch): IAutoMovieMotionHumanPrimitive[]

      A touch: the arm and the fingers on one clock. The arm's whole touch is planned as one trajectory (AutoMovieMotionHumanArmPlanned): its reach from its entry angles, its hold on the goal laid on the region from ready to relax and its return, clear of the body all the way; a hand path solved second by second would let the elbow change sides between them. Between strokeStart and strokeEnd the goal is lifted along the region's normal by lift sin^2(pi n u), which touches down at every repetition's end, and moved along its tangent by the clock's windowed wave of n cycles. A held vessel keeps its attitude on the way (AutoMovieMotionHumanDrinkingArm.carried).

      Parameters

      • props: ITouch

        The touch.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanPrimitive[]

      The arm's and the hand's primitives, the arm's alone when the fingers are kept; the arm carries the contact window from ready to relax when a support is given and the hand, not a held tool, is what touches it, and one lie window over the same span for each of its segments declared resting (ITouch.rests).

      Throws

      When the count is not a positive whole number.
