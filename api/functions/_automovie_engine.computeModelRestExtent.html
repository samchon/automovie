    Function computeModelRestExtent

    • computeModelRestExtent(
          model: IAutoMovieModel,
      ): IAutoMovieComputeModelRestExtent.IResult

      A model's rest-pose box in model space: the axis-aligned range, in metres, of its portable part geometry, on all three axes. External asset bytes are not opened here, so proxy parts do not establish the imported asset's pixel extent.

      Every vertex is placed the way the renderer places it: by its part's own transform through placeTransformedPoint, then by the rest frame of the bone the part rides, or left in model space when it rides none. The canonical geometry realizer supplies primitive and bicubic recipe vertices, retaining finite partial output, so framing does not reject an otherwise displayable recipe. A trailing partial x, y, z triple in a mesh is ignored.

      computeModelRestExtentY is this box read on the vertical axis. The horizontal half is what a subject wider than it is tall is framed and graded from: a 60 m facade authored outward from its element origin has a drawn box of x 0…60, y 0…24, z −1…1, where the vertical read alone reports y 0…24.

      Returns null when no part has a vertex, leaving the caller's own fallback in charge rather than inventing an extent. Throws, through skeletonRestFrames, when the model's skeleton is malformed.
