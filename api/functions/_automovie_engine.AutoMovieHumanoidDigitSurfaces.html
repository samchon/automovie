    Function AutoMovieHumanoidDigitSurfaces

    • AutoMovieHumanoidDigitSurfaces(
          rays: Pick<IAutoMovieHumanoidSurfaceReader, "at" | "local" | "surface">,
          profile: IAutoMoviePhysicalProfile,
      ): Partial<
          Record<AutoMovieHumanoidRegion, IAutoMovieHumanoidSurfaces.IFrame>,
      >

      The tip and the pad of every digit of both hands, as bone-local frames on each digit's distal phalanx.

      A digit's tip is where a ray from its last joint, along its distal phalanx (the line from the joint before it), leaves the phalanx's colliders, with the exited collider's outward normal: the index fingertip as resolveAutoMovieHumanoidSurfaces has always placed it, now for every digit. Its pad is the palmar face of the same phalanx: where a ray from the middle of the phalanx, between the last joint and the tip, leaves the colliders toward the palm's side. The palm faces -Y in the canonical rest (HUMANOID_REST_FRAME), so the palmar direction is -Y made perpendicular to the phalanx; the rig's thumb lies in the palm's plane with its nail up, so its pad faces the same side. A profile with no segment for a phalanx reads the hand's colliders instead, as the index tip did.

      Both sides are resolved; the caller replaces each left frame with its right twin's mirror image, as it does for every region.

      Parameters

      • rays: Pick<IAutoMovieHumanoidSurfaceReader, "at" | "local" | "surface">

        The rest-pose readers of the body (AutoMovieHumanoidSurfaceRays.tools).

      • profile: IAutoMoviePhysicalProfile

        The body's physical profile, segments keyed by rig bones.

      Returns Partial<Record<AutoMovieHumanoidRegion, IAutoMovieHumanoidSurfaces.IFrame>>

      The tip and pad frames of every digit, keyed by region.

      Throws

      When a digit bone is missing or a ray misses its colliders.
