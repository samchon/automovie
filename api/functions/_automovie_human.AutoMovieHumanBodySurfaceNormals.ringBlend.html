      Shading normals blended across the event rings.

      Parent and child are fitted on their own and meet at a shared ring with one tangent plane (AutoMovieHumanEventJoin) but not one curvature, so each side's surface normals turn at their own rate and the ring shades as a line under raking light: across the face at the 1543.2 mm ring under the nose and at the 1595.5 mm ring beside the eyes, in clay as in colour, although the normals there agree with the local surface to 1.7 degrees. Every vertex within BLEND mm of station from one of its chain's event rings therefore averages its normal with its mesh neighbours' over PASSES passes, fully at the ring and fading to nothing at the band's edge, so the turn spreads over the band on both sides. A neighbour whose normal differs by more than FOLD degrees is left out: the contacts' real folds (the axillae, the crotch, the webs, the auricle's rims) keep their shading. Positions are not moved.
