    Function digit

    • digit(
          body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          side: AutoMovieHumanSide,
          joints: Partial<
              Record<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, readonly [number, number, number]>,
          >,
          name: "Index" | "Thumb",
      ): IAutoMovieVector3

      A digit's own direction under a set of finger angles: from its first joint (the knuckle of a finger, the thumb's middle joint) to its last, in the hand's rest frame. A shape's straight digit does not lie along the hand's own axes: the forefinger leans off the knuckle line toward the thumb, and a straight thumb stands about 35 degrees off it, so a request that means a digit to point somewhere aims this direction, not an axis of the hand.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The body, whose rig places the digit's joints.

      • side: AutoMovieHumanSide

        The hand.

      • joints: Partial<Record<AutoMovieHumanoidBone, readonly [number, number, number]>>

        The finger angles, clinical degrees by bone; a bone left out stands at rest.

      • name: "Index" | "Thumb"

        The digit: the thumb or the forefinger.

      Returns IAutoMovieVector3

      The unit direction.
