    Function performShot

    • performShot(props: IAutoMoviePerformShotProps): IAutoMoviePerformedShot

      Fold one beat's action calls into an IAutoMovieShot through compilePerformance, gating both sides of the seam.

      The performing list is revise.final when the revise pass produced one and draft otherwise. The stages run in order, and the first stage group that reports a violation returns every violation of that group and stops:

      1. Input: unique script beat ids, the shot's lightMotions against the staged lights and its polygon body cues against staged rigs and practical surface tracks against the staged nodes and registered models, a planned beat, a positive duration, authored editorial markers, every action by gatePerformShotActions, the live camera's moves by gatePerformShotFrameOverlaps, same-actor content overlaps by gatePerformShotRegionOverlaps, the blocking by gatePerformShotBlocking, and the cameras by gatePerformShotCamera.
      2. Launches: each flight is solved and its recoil scheduled by compilePerformShotLaunches.
      3. Performance: the stage list compiles, stance feet are planted by plantPerformShotStanceFeet, every channel a region mask would drop is reported at the author's region (or onHit) field, and every clip must pass validateMotion against its rig.
      4. Coupling: per-beat attachTo handoffs and staged mounts are baked by coupleObjects.
      5. Authored object clips are gated by gateAuthoredObjectMotions.
      6. Practical lights bind staged emitters to the final object clips and share light channel admission with authored clips.
      7. Cameras: the live camera's move and each coverage take compile through the framing grammar, subjects measured by createPerformShotSubjectFramer, and any physical clearance envelope is checked by compileCameraClearanceReports.

      A shot with no frame action is locked off on the scene's first camera. Positional targets resolve against every staged placement, cameras included, so an actor may look down the lens, but a camera performs only frame. Only a camera subject may name a formation. The assembled artifact is checked independently and a failure throws as a compilation defect. This does not prove every possible authored failure was located earlier: typed outer containers, generated event-id collisions, mutable callbacks and downstream arithmetic remain distinct exception boundaries.
