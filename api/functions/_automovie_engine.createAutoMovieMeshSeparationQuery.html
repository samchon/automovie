    Function createAutoMovieMeshSeparationQuery

    • createAutoMovieMeshSeparationQuery(
          mesh: IAutoMovieMesh,
          representation?: "source" | "float32",
      ): IAutoMovieMeshSeparationQuery

      Compile a resident surface into a conservative whole-feature separation query.

      The mesh is snapshotted, so changing its positions or indices later needs a new query. Open sheets, disconnected components and degenerate triangles are admitted, since unsigned separation needs no winding, closure or volume. representation chooses whether the snapshot measures the source binary64 coordinates or their Float32 roundings, the buffer an exporter will write; both keep the binary64 coordinates as the reference for attachment seats. Original triangle ordinals survive the hierarchy's reordering.

      A call takes a feature, a nonempty finite XYZ vertex run in the same metre frame measured as its convex hull, a nonnegative requested clearance and a caller-owned work budget. It bounds the feature's separation from every resident triangle: a hierarchy box is skipped only when an enclosed axis projection already proves the requested clearance, and every remaining triangle is bounded by boundAutoMovieConvexSeparation. This is neither ray sampling nor a nearest-corner test. certified means the complete feature is at least the clearance away, and at zero clearance strictly positive; false means unproved, including touches, crossings and rounding at an exact limit, and neither diagnoses penetration nor selects a closest point. lowerBound is capped at the clearance, or is the smallest positive double at zero clearance, which is a proof threshold rather than a distance estimate; triangle is the original ordinal of the triangle that lowered it below that cap, the lowest on ties, or -1 when none did.

      At zero clearance, a three-vertex fan may carry attachment metadata: its root's canonical barycentric seat on a source triangle and the original triangles incident to that seat. The root must equal that seat exactly, in the snapshot's representation, and each support must contain the seat's supporting corners. Each support must then bound the whole fan's contact through boundAutoMovieTriangleAttachmentContact, reported as an attachmentCaps entry, and every other triangle still needs strict positive separation. Such a result reports lowerBound zero, because certification is that combined proof and not positive separation. Without metadata a fan touching its root is simply unproved.

      Compilation throws on an unknown representation, a malformed or empty index buffer, a non-finite position, or a Float32 rounding that overflows. Every visited box, with its up to three axis projections, spends one budget unit, and every tested support, triangle, normal triple, projection axis and edge pair spends one more before its work. Exhaustion and malformed input throw by name; the caller owns the spent count and must not reset it during refinement. A failed call accepts no geometry and retries nothing. The returned query's separated runs the same walk and stops at the first triangle whose bound falls below the target; since the running bound only falls, its decision equals the full measurement's and it never spends more.

      Parameters

      • mesh: IAutoMovieMesh

        Resident surface, snapshotted.

      • representation: "source" | "float32" = "source"

        Coordinates the snapshot measures.

      Returns IAutoMovieMeshSeparationQuery

      The compiled query.
