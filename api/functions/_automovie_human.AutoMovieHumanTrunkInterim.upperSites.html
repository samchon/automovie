      Torso-only axilla and acromion boundaries above the chest. The old table's upper half-breadth includes the arms, so the shoulder's public biacromial breadth supplies the acromion's lateral place; an upper-arm circle radius medial to it defines the axilla attachment convention. Front/back and quadrant fullness retain the existing outline table. These anatomical knots do not by themselves bound the natural cubic spans: their slope/curvature and reference comparison remain required. The last site is the neck seat, the neck's bottom ring, where the trunk takes the neck's ring by the head's down event: its half width and its front and back midline points are the ring's own (AutoMovieHumanTrunkNeckSeat), so the trunk's top meets the ring it continues. Remaining: the neck seat's height is the old solve's.
