    Function createCareItem

    • createCareItem(
          params: createCareItem.IParams,
          seed?: string | number,
      ): Archetype<createCareItem.Affordance, createCareItem.Dimension>

      A personal care item: a tissue, a comb, a toothbrush, a cream tube, a razor or a lipstick.

      Each lies at the origin with its long side along +Z, its working end at +Z: a comb's teeth point down along its length, a toothbrush's bristles and a razor's blades face up at its far end, a tube's cap is at its far end and a lipstick's bullet points out of its far end. A tissue lies flat.

      Parameters

      • params: createCareItem.IParams

        The kind, the kind's model where it has read models, and any dimension the author fixes.

      • Optionalseed: string | number

        The seed that picks the model when none is given and draws every dimension left free inside its band; absent, the first model and the middle of each band.

      Returns Archetype<createCareItem.Affordance, createCareItem.Dimension>

      The item.

      Throws

      For a model the kind does not have, or a fixed dimension outside its band.
