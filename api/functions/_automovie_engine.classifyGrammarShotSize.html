    Function classifyGrammarShotSize

    • classifyGrammarShotSize(
          frameOccupancy: number,
      ): "wide" | "full" | "medium" | "close"

      The framing a subject's measured frame occupancy reads as: the framing whose FRAMING_HEIGHT_FRACTION is nearest, by ratio, to how many subject sizes the frame spans.

      frameOccupancy is the subject's share of the frame, in (0, ∞): its height over the frame's height for a subject the frame holds vertically, or its width over the frame's width for one whose width placed the camera. The framing solve applies FRAMING_HEIGHT_FRACTION to both axes, so the axis that fills more of its own side states the size delivered, and measureGrammarShotSize passes the larger of the two. Nearness is measured on a log scale, so close at 0.28 and medium at 0.62 are separated where their ratio is even; an exact tie keeps the framing listed first, wider before closer. Throws when the occupancy is not finite and positive.

      Parameters

      • frameOccupancy: number

      Returns "wide" | "full" | "medium" | "close"
