    Function CatBody

    • CatBody(): QuadrupedBody.IBody

      A coarse adult domestic cat assembled from separate male reference samples.

      Poradowski et al. 2024 (https://doi.org/10.3390/vetsci11110522), Tables 1–3, provide withers height and proximal limb lengths. Madrigal-Valverde et al. 2025 (https://doi.org/10.3390/ani15152191) report bare mass in Section 2.2 and tape landmarks in Section 2.4 and Table 6 for a separate crossbred sample. Section 2.4 defines the snout-to-S3 measure as head plus body: its 55.77 cm and the Cd1–Cd15 tail's 27.38 cm sum to the reported 83.15 cm total. The torso control subtracts the snout-to-C1 head length and an authored neck allowance from snout-to-S3. Circular head width comes from tape circumference and is a shape proxy. Distal bars, stance, pinna plates, soft-tissue radii and the tail carriage are authored.

      Returns QuadrupedBody.IBody

      A fresh independent profile for the common body builder.
