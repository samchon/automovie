    • held(
          body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody,
          posture: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture,
          side: AutoMovieHumanSide,

      ): IAutoMovieMotionHumanTouchDrink.IHeld

      The least lean toward the face at which a vessel the hand holds by a kept grasp is brought to the region by a drinking arm, and the wrist's goal there. The grasp fixes the vessel in the hand, so only its lean is free: the hand's rotation at the region is the grasp's turned by the lean (tip), and the wrist stands where that rotation puts the vessel's point on the region, as the touch stroke places a held rotation. Leans are tried as drinking tries them, every COARSE and then every LEAN_STEP inside the first admitting bracket up to LEAN, each screened by its fit on the posture and admitted by its fit held to its own reference, the arm the stroke shows.

      Parameters

      • body: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBody

        The body.

      • posture: IAutoMovieMotionHumanPosture

        The posture the arm is solved on.

      • side: AutoMovieHumanSide

        The hand.

      • effector: IEffector

        The effector: the vessel's point in the hand.

      • place: Pick<IAutoMovieMotionHumanTarget.IPlace, "point" | "normal">

        The region.

      • hint: IAutoMovieVector3

        The elbow's hint, world.

      • rotation: IAutoMovieQuaternion

        The hand's rotation the grasp presents the vessel at the region with, world.

      • Optionalafter: IAfter

        Where the vessel is held while the drinker drinks; the arrival alone when absent.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanTouchDrink.IHeld

      The least admitted lean and the wrist's goal at it.

      Throws

      When no lean up to LEAN holds a drinking arm.
