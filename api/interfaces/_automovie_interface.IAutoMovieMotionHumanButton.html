    Interface IAutoMovieMotionHumanButton

    Push a button through the rectangular clear opening of a fixed garment prop.

    The garment supplies a rigid straight slider carrying the button geometry and a stationary opening and rim. The author supplies a sphere enclosing the button and the thickness of the stationary panel. Those dimensions bound the passage without cloth deformation or a geometry-specific solver.
