    Interface IAutoMovieBodyMeasure

    The anthropometric measures of one humanoid body that motion laws scale from: joint-centre heights and spacings, segment lengths, girths and the foot and head extents, all in metres, and the mass in kilograms.

    Measures are read on the body's canonical rest (the VRM 1.0 T-pose, +Y up, facing +Z, left toward +X, the floor at Y = 0), so a height is a rest height above the floor and a length is a joint-centre-to-joint-centre distance, which does not change with pose. Lengths follow the segment definitions of biomechanics texts (Winter, Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement, 4th ed., ch. 4): the thigh runs from the hip joint centre to the knee, the shank from the knee to the ankle, the upper arm from the glenohumeral centre to the elbow, the forearm from the elbow to the wrist.

    Every field is a finite positive number; the type cannot enforce that, so a producer validates it. The record carries no pose, no identity and no skeleton reference: the same measure may describe a generated Human, a parametric humanoid or an imported character.
