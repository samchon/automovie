    mutual

    • mutual<Params extends object, Role extends string>(
          mutual: IAutoMovieMotionHumanMutual<Params, Role>,
          cast: Readonly<Record<Role, IAutoMovieMotionHumanChainCore>>,
          ...args: {} extends Params
              ? [params?: Params, anchor?: string]
              : [params: Params, anchor?: string],
      ): IAutoMovieChain

      Join people's chains in a mutual action: one thing they do together, such as a handshake, a handover or a lift.

      Every chain of the cast plays first, side by side, and then every role performs its part of the mutual action at once, so each person's earlier actions are placed before any role plans the meeting. HumanMutual holds the vocabulary's mutual actions; a production states its own as an IAutoMovieMotionHumanMutual over its registered laws.

      HumanChain.mutual(HumanMutual.handOver, { giver: a, receiver: b }, { prop: "cup", handle: "body" });
