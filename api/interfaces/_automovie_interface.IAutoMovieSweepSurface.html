    Interface IAutoMovieSweepSurface

    A sweep compiled for evaluation, from compileAutoMovieSweep.

    u is the chord-length parameter along the spine and v the turn around it, both from 0 to 1. Under direction correspondence v is polar angle divided by 2*pi; under parameter correspondence it is the section curve parameter and need not be polar angle or arc-length share. Position, section and frame queries clamp u and wrap v. Differential queries require u in [0,1]; their endpoint stencil is one-sided. Every query is deterministic. Angular linear reconstruction and a local feature nearest-branch change limit the smoothness guarantees. Coordinates use the authored model frame and metres. Returned points and frames are fresh mutable records. The compiled surface retains its source declaration and coefficients; preserve authored data while using it. compileAutoMovieSweep admits authored geometry, while individual queries refuse invalid generated radii or section coordinates; finite query arguments remain the caller's responsibility.
