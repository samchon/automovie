    Optionalbeside

    beside?: ITogether

    The arms planned over the same span on one base, each as a problem of its own: every member is solved on the posture before the group's first member, which shows the others at their rest, so the plans do not depend on the order the arms are written in and two arms raised alike are each other's mirror. For arms that keep to their own sides (a stretch, a winner's two fists): arms whose paths may cross are one problem (together). When both are given, beside decides.

      The hand's world rotation on the way, for a hand that holds an object whose attitude matters (a glass kept upright), solved at the wrist on the arm the blend places (HumanHandAttitude.held); the blend when absent. Read only between the ends, it must meet the held end's hand at weight 0 and the goal's at 1, or the hand steps as the carry leaves or joins.
