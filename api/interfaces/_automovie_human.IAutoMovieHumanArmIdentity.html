    carryingAngle

    carryingAngle: number

    Carrying angle: the angle in the frontal plane between the long axes of the arm and the forearm with the elbow fully extended and the forearm supinated, degrees, positive for valgus, the forearm turned away from the body. Slightly varus at birth (-1.5), 6 degrees at 2 to 6 years and 11 from 15 on, in 921 Thai children measured with a goniometer, and larger in girls (8.5 against 6.9 over all ages; Kaewpornsawan et al. 2018, Siriraj Med J 70(4):284-288). Schema constraints: Minimum: -30; Maximum: 40; retained by Human's schema refinement.

    dorsalHandFat

    dorsalHandFat: number

    Fat over the back of the hand, millimetres: the dorsal superficial, intermediate and deep fatty laminae over the metacarpals, measured by ultrasound. It thins with age, about 4.3 mm in the 20s and 2.4 mm over 60, which is why old hands show their knuckles, tendons and veins (Hung et al. 2022, Aesthet Surg J 42(10):1119-1126). Schema constraints: Minimum: 0; Maximum: 30; retained by Human's schema refinement.
