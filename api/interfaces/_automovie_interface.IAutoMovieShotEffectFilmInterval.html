    Interface IAutoMovieShotEffectFilmInterval

    One shot-owned effect interval projected onto the film clock.

    projectProductionShotEffectFilmIntervals emits one per film occurrence of a shot cue that overlaps its segment, trimmed to that segment. The builder supplies these intervals to materializeProductionFilmEffects only to prove that one world zone has one effect owner at every realized film frame; they are not substituted for film-owned runtime effects. Frames are half-open: startFrame is owned and endFrame is not.
