    cues?: Readonly<Record<string, readonly IAutoMovieScheduleCue[]>>

    Tracks of named instants that belong to no intent, by track name: a dialogue's line starts and accents, a score's beats. A part aligns to one and an intent is cut on one as on an anchored intent's events.

    Optionalinstants

    • instants?(
          of: string,
          entries: readonly IAutoMovieScheduleEntry[],
      ): readonly IAutoMovieScheduleCue[] | undefined

      The instants of a track the laws compute from what is placed so far, read when an at names neither an anchor nor a track of cues: the hits a struck person has received, known only once their strikers are placed. Undefined for a name the laws do not know, which the walk then refuses as a missing anchor. Pure in the entries given.

        The track's name.
