    Interface IAutoMovieRecoveryImageFit

    Qualified fit and acquisition evidence kept outside source-free coefficients. Pixel residuals compare silhouette limits in supplied charts, not material correspondence, surface distance or a rigidity proof. The QR diagonal ratio is an operational rank diagnostic, not a covariance or certified condition.
