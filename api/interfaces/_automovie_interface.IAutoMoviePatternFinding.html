    Interface IAutoMoviePatternFinding

    One structured defect or unsupported case a pattern run reports.

    The unit of measured and the meaning of limit follow kind:

    • sliver: one occurrence; surviving coverage fraction against the pattern's minimumPiece.
    • unsupported-piece: one occurrence; square metres an exclusion punched out, against zero.
    • module-overlap: two occurrences; their negative separation in metres, against zero.
    • joint-deviation: two occurrences; their gap in metres against the nominal joint, reported when the difference exceeds jointTolerance.
    • grain-break: two occurrences; grain deviation in degrees, read modulo 180, against grainToleranceDeg.

    Per-occurrence findings come first in placement order, then pair findings ordered by their first occurrence and then their partner.
