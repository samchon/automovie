    Interface IAutoMovieMotionHumanProp

    A prop actions can aim at and hands can hold: a subject id, its world frame at rest, and its named points in that frame, such as a heavy bag's striking surface, a pad's face or a cup's handle.

    The prop stays at its rest frame, on the bone it is worn on, or at its initial mount until a hand grips it; while gripped it rides the hand, unless it is a fixture, worn it rides its bone, and it stays where the last grip released it, or flies from a launch and rests where it struck (see HumanPerformance.prop). A point's normal points out of the prop's surface.

    Optionalarticulate

    • articulate?(
          state: Readonly<Record<string, number>>,
      ): IAutoMovieMotionHumanProp.IPosed

      The prop posed at stated travels of its articulations, for a prop whose parts an act moves (a door's leaf, a trigger, a bowstring): its contacts and colliders in its own frame with the parts moved. The performance reads it at the travels the drives and tracks give at a second (HumanPerformance.state), so a hand reaching a handle after the door was opened goes to where the handle is. A rigid object's model adapter can also expose this reader.

      Parameters

      • state: Readonly<Record<string, number>>

        Travel by articulation id, in each articulation's own unit; an articulation left out stays at rest.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionHumanProp.IPosed

      The posed contacts and colliders.

      Throws

      When a travel lies outside its articulation's range.

    Optionalpoint

    • point?(id: string, seconds: number): IPlacedPoint | undefined

      A named point in the world at a film second, for a prop whose parts move within it: a pedal on its crank, a stirrup on a trotting horse, a handle on a door that swings. points holds a point where it is in the prop at rest, and the prop's frame alone carries it rigidly; a point this knows is read here instead, so a foot or a hand aimed at it follows the part and not the place the part had at rest.
