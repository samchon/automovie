    Optionalbounded

    bounded?: {
        below: (low: number, high: number) => number;
        key: (candidate: T) => number | null;
    }

    Branch and bound over the arcs (Land and Doig 1960): below is a certified lower bound of key over every candidate at a swivel from low to high degrees, and key the number a candidate is ranked by among those that can be chosen, or null for a candidate no bound may be compared with (an illegal one). Arcs are then visited in the order of their bounds and the visit ends at the first whose bound exceeds the least key read; absent, every arc is visited.

    • Defined in packages/engine/src/kinematics/swivelArcMinimum.ts:100
