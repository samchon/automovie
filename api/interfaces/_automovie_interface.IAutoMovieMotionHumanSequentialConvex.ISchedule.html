    Interface ISchedule

    How the region moves: a step is taken where the ratio of the actual to the predicted decrease exceeds eta, and the region shrinks by shrink on every step not taken, at most tries times a convexification; a step taken shrinks the region where its ratio is below low and grows it by expand, to widest, where its ratio exceeds high (on a step at the boundary when boundary holds), Nocedal and Wright's Algorithm 4.1 in its general form.
