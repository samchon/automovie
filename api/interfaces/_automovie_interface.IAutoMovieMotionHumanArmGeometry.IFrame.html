    Interface IFrame

    What the arm is read against at one instant (HumanArmFrame.of). The frame is memoised by base-object identity, upper-arm bone and alone flag. Mutating the base or body does not invalidate that cache: producers and consumers must keep the borrowed input geometry and returned arrays stable during reuse. surface, owners, segments and corners have equal lengths and correspond by index.
