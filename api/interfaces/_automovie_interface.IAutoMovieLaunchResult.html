    Interface IAutoMovieLaunchResult

    What compileLaunch yields for one launch action: the projectile's baked flight, the solved impact, and the reaction and events that impact schedules. compileLaunch returns null instead when no flight reaches the target at the action's speed.

    performShot shifts the clip onto the shot clock, refuses a launch landing after the shot ends, and folds react into the action list so the recoil passes the same synthesis and range-of-motion gate as an authored react.

    World point of impact in metres, where the solved flight lands. Against a target faster than the projectile it is the closest lead found, not the target's own position.

    react

    react: IAutoMovieReactAction | null

    The target's recoil, scheduled at the computed contact: a synthetic IAutoMovieReactAction to fold into the action list, or null when the launch carries no onHit or aims at a point or group with no single node to recoil. Its start is the shot-local instant the projectile lands, and its from point sits one metre upstream of the impact along the incoming velocity, so the reference synthesizer recoils the body along the projectile's travel, for a lobbed or a flat flight alike.

    velocity: IAutoMovieVector3

    Solved launch velocity in metres per second, of magnitude action.speed.
