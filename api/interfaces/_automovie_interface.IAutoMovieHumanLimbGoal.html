    Interface IAutoMovieHumanLimbGoal

    An arm or leg effector goal with its bend direction, optional contact constraints and reference configuration. Coordinates are world metres unless on names the posed bone frame; resolveHumanPosture admits attachment order and fits the goal.

    Optionalelbow

    elbow?: Elbow

    Where an arm's elbow goes when the law leaves it to the body: "strain" takes, among the places that leave the hand's rotation error least, the one where the arm strains least, read from the fit's own sweep (AutoMovieMotionHumanBend.strainOf), with bend serving only where no place is legal. "placed" says bend is itself the elbow's place, which the law's own elbow search chose (AutoMovieMotionHumanBend.solve): the fit takes the swivel that puts the elbow there, refined locally, and sweeps only where that is not legal, so the place is searched once. Absent, bend decides; a leg ignores it.

    Optionalon

    on?: AutoMovieHumanoidBone

    The bone the goal is attached to, when it moves with the body rather than staying in the world: point and bend are then in the bone's posed frame (relative to its posed world position and rotation) and rotation composes after the bone's. The resolver carries them into the world from the pose being fitted, after the trunk, the head, any layer and any transition offset have placed the bone, so a palm on the chin or in a pocket stays there when breathing or a glance moves the body. A limb attached to another limb's bone is fitted after it; a bone of the limb's own chain, or two limbs attached to each other, is refused (resolveHumanPosture.order, resolveHumanPosture.world).

    Optionalreference

    reference?: IAutoMovieHumanLimbReference

    The limb's own solution at one instant of the action the law names (the grasp, the clasp, the placement): the clinical angles of its upper and lower bones there, as resolveHumanPosture.reference reads them. Where the goal can be met by more than one arm or leg configuration, the fit keeps the one nearest this reference, so the limb stays on one side of the choice for the whole stroke. It is a constant of the plan, never a previous frame, so the pose stays a function of time alone. A degree of joint turn from the reference counts like a degree of hand or foot rotation error, so a law gives it in full from the named instant on (the hold after a grasp) and, over the reach toward that instant, raises its weight from 0 to 1 with the reach's own progress: the reference is that instant's solution, so the reach arrives at it exactly, and on the way the growing weight keeps the limb on the reference's side where two configurations nearly tie instead of letting it switch between them.
