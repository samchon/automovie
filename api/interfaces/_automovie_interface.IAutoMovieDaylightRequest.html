    Interface IAutoMovieDaylightRequest

    Everything one daylight or artificial-light study is configured with.

    analyzeAutoMovieDaylight throws on a blank id, subject, revision or sky name, an invalid environment context, an instant the context does not declare, a malformed workplane, luminaire or shading solid, a sky sample count that is not a positive safe integer, or a malformed target. A null instant makes the run an artificial-light run, otherwise it is daylight. The settings digest covers the context's id, ground, north and occluders, the resolved instant record, and every other field except id, subject and inputRevision.
