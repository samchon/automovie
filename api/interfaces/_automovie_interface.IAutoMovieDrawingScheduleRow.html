    Interface IAutoMovieDrawingScheduleRow

    One scheduled type and every occurrence of it.

    Opening and connector occurrences that agree on kind, model, width, height and basis share a row; a space is always a row of its own. Every field but members and place is shared by all members of a row.

    basis

    basis: "fill" | "profile" | "unmeasured"

    What width and height were measured from.

    profile is the thing itself: an opening's own void on its host boundary, a connector's own declared section, or a space's declared volume box. fill is an opening's filling element extent, which is the leaf's size and not the hole's. unmeasured is neither, and the sizes are null. A connector is never unmeasured, because validation requires it to state a section.

    mark: string

    Deterministic type mark, <kind>-NN, numbered from 01 per kind in row order.

    The mark comes from the row's position in the canonical order rather than from an authored label, so the same design marks the same type identically on every run and two derivations of one revision compare mark by mark.

    place

    place: IAutoMovieDrawingSchedulePlace

    Where the occurrence sits, as the place variant of the schedule's subject.

    A space row carries its own place. An opening or connector row carries the place of its first member in members, because place is not a grouping column; other members may stand elsewhere, so read their locations from the design.

    width

    width: number | null

    Nominal width in metres, rounded to six decimals, or null when basis is unmeasured.

    An opening reports its void's or leaf's width, a connector its narrowest stated section, and a space the wider of its declared box's world X and Z extents. null states that nothing in the design answers, not a zero-width door.
