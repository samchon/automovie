    Optionalcorrect

    • correct?(
          x: X,
          step: IAutoMovieMotionHumanSequentialConvex.IStep<X>,
          trial: E,
      ): X | null

      The second-order correction of a rejected step, against the Maratos effect (Nocedal and Wright 2006, section 15.6, pp. 443-444): the step p plus a correction p^ that meets the constraints' linearisation at the trial, A(x) p^ + c(x + p) = 0 on the rows the step meets, its minimum-norm solution being their (15.36). Asked only for a step whose ratio did not exceed ISchedule.eta and whose predicted decrease is positive, once per step and before the region shrinks; the corrected point is judged on the step's own predicted decrease, taken where its ratio exceeds eta and dropped otherwise, never searched along (their Algorithm 15.2, p. 443, in the trust region of their Algorithm 18.4, p. 549). Absent, no correction is tried.

      Parameters

      • x: X

        The iterate.

      • step: IAutoMovieMotionHumanSequentialConvex.IStep<X>

        The rejected step.

      • trial: E

        The reading at the step's trial.

      Returns X | null

      The corrected point x + p + p^, or null where the caller has none.
