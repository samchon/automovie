    Interface IAutoMovieGrammarInput

    One edited sequence, observed shot by shot, and the two thresholds its mechanical read uses.

    readFilmGrammar consumes it. It throws on a malformed input instead of reporting it: a duplicate or blank shot id, a threshold or duration that is not finite and positive, or any defect the shot observation documents.
