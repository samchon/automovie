    Interface IAutoMovieSurfaceLocation

    Where one vertex of a subdivided surface network lies in the network's own terms: its surface coordinate, its patch's name and, on a sweep or transition patch, the point of the sweep's (u, v) domain.

    The domain point is the patch's (s, t) carried by its ranges, u from t and v from s, so it follows the cage's chart rather than a projection onto the sweep. Sweep cell averaging, inverse fitting and the finite chart reconstruction all separate this domain address from the displayed skin; no nearest-point or universal distance bound is promised. Each vertex reports one incident-face address, not every adjacent chart. Fields crossing patch seams must agree at the corresponding addresses. v stays in the patch's range, so on a patch spanning a whole turn a seam vertex reads v at either end of the range, one turn apart; give a field over such a patch a period of one turn in v so both read alike.
