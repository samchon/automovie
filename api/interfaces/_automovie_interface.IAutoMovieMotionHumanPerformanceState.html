    Interface IAutoMovieMotionHumanPerformanceState

    Everything a performance resolves and its readers share: the people with their placed actions, the props, the laws, the cue tracks and the schedule, built once by the constructor.

    HumanPerformance is the public surface over it. The state's members are public so the HumanPerformance* namespaces, which hold the performance's work by responsibility (placing, contacts, layers, the schedule, aiming and reading), can share them; the public helper signatures expose this shared state contract transitively, while HumanPerformance remains the normal author-facing entry point.
