    Interface IAutoMovieSweepRig

    One sweep of a surface rig, from rigAutoMovieSweep: the frames, scale factors and surface normals a cage reads from it, at parameters registered once and refreshed per pose in place.

    Registration (frame, scale, probe, sample) runs while a cage is planned and returns slot indices; seal then sizes the arrays and fills them at rest. pose refreshes them for the current bone motions, or at rest for null.

    girth

    • girth(factor: (u: number) => number): void

      Multiply every scale slot by a fixed factor of its u, at rest and in every pose from now on, the radial correction a surface network solves to hold a measured girth. Each call replaces the previous factor, and the rest slots and probes are refreshed after every factor, scale product and probe is admitted. A rejected factor, product or probe leaves the previous scales and probes in place.

    sample

    • sample(
          u: number,
          v: number,
          cell: readonly [number, number, number, number],
      ): (out: Float64Array, at: number) => void

      Register a surface point and make its writer.

      The point is averaged over its cell, which reaches half the spacing to the neighbouring sample on each side along u (clamped to the spine) and v, by the trapezoid rule on the cell's two halves: weights b / (2 (b + a)), 1/2 and a / (2 (b + a)) at the lower end, the point and the upper end for halves b below and a above, which is 1/4, 1/2, 1/4 on an even grid. Direction correspondence averages radii about the authored direction, so a round section keeps its size. Parameter correspondence averages the actual Cartesian section points, with each u-dependent scale inside the average; it preserves an off-origin curve and permits a legitimately cancelling origin. Signed means and products that cannot be represented are refused. A cell of zero extent is the section point itself, as IAutoMovieSweepSurface.point places it. Shapes are read now; the writer scales and places them in the current frame.

      Parameters

      • u: number

        Along the spine

      • v: number

        Around it

      • cell: readonly [number, number, number, number]

        Spacing to the neighbouring sample below and above along u, then below and above along v

      Returns (out: Float64Array, at: number) => void

      The writer, three numbers in metres at an offset
