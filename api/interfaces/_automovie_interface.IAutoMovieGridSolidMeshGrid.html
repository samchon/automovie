    local

    local: (
        ids: readonly number[],
        charts: readonly (readonly [number, number])[],
    ) => [number, number]

    A quadrilateral's own first fundamental form, as the map (u, v) -> (u + shear v, scale v) under which its chart lengths are its skin's up to one factor: the least-squares linear map J from its four chart points to their skin points about their centroids, with E = |J_u|^2, F = J_u . J_v, G = |J_v|^2, shear = F / E and scale = sqrt(G / E - shear^2). Residual legalization reads it, so a face whose metric changes across it is made Delaunay in the metric each quadrilateral actually spans, not the face's mean: beside the armscye's loop the trunk spans 231 mm a radian where its mean is about half that, and triangles fair in the mean metric stood 4 to 7 degrees thin on the skin and folded 135 degrees. Read instead from the grid jets of the cell holding the centroid, a band's corner column at f1's crotch fork, where the band's columns converge within one cell, took the wrong diagonal and folded 63 degrees. Missing skin points, a singular chart fit, or a nonfinite/nonpositive fitted metric use [0, metric], the face mean.

    metric

    metric: number

    Phase scale of the face's uniform metric, length units per radian: the mean first fundamental form over the grid jets without its shear term, sqrt(mean G / mean E) with E = |S_u|^2 and G = |S_v|^2. It carries the length unit, so a body in another unit legalizes alike.
