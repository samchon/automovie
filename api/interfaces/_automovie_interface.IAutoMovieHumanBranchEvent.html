    web?: boolean

    Whether the children start apart from the parent's ring, joined to it by the webs' own surface (AutoMovieHumanBodyWeb): each child's adjacent end is its own section past the webs' crests, and the skin from the parent's ring to those sections, the saddle of every web included, is built by the surface. Absent, the children's ends are the arcs themselves, closed across each span by a chord.
