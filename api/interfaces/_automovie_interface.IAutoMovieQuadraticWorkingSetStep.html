    Interface IAutoMovieQuadraticWorkingSetStep

    One equality-constrained step of the quadratic polisher, returned by solveAutoMovieQuadraticWorkingSetStep for the current working set.

    The step minimizes the unchanged diagonal objective on the affine space where every working row equals its chosen endpoint. It is a candidate only: the polisher still checks every original row and the full optimality conditions, and neither the basis nor this reduced solve establishes them. Exactly one of three outcomes holds. A refusal names why no step could be represented; otherwise flatDirection is set when the objective decreases without bound on the working space; otherwise primal and dual hold the minimizer. Multiplier vectors are indexed by the working rows passed to the step, with zero for a row the basis found dependent.
