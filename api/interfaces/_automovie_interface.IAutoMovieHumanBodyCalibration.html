    Interface IAutoMovieHumanBodyCalibration

    Shared shape coefficients (theta) of the generated body surface.

    The chains are principal layers fitted from saved Recovery/Hermite sections of a calibration specimen in the registered frame: forward +X, up +Y, lateral +Z, millimetres, floor at y = 0. The generator reads this object and the public parameters only; it never reads reference files.

    Chain tree

    Chains are joined by IAutoMovieHumanBranchEvents. At an event the parent's end section is the contact of several child sections touching at pinch points: a down event sits at the parent's bottom with its children below (a crotch, an axilla, the webs between fingers), an up event at the parent's top with its children above (an ear rim leaving the head). The parent generates the contact ring once; each child's adjacent end is the arcs of that ring along the parent's phases, so the chains share those vertices. A chain end with no event closes at an IAutoMovieHumanSurfacePole.

    Calibration is one stage of the campaign plan: the coefficients first reproduce one specimen, then are replaced by laws in the public inputs identified across all references.

    Optionaldiagnostics

    diagnostics?: IAutoMovieLoftContract.IDiagnostic[]

    Contracts the body law's pieces broke while building this calibration, each piece degraded to its base (construction-levels.md, Totality and diagnostics). The body law creates the list once and every stage that spreads the calibration shares it, so a piece records into it where it degrades; AutoMovieHuman.generate carries it into the shape's own diagnostics. A calibration built by hand, as a part viewer's or a test's, may leave it out.
