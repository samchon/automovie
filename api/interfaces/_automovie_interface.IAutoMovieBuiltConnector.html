    Interface IAutoMovieBuiltConnector

    A navigable relation between logical spaces, and the shape it has.

    The record is the traversal geometry a later analysis reads, not a verdict about that analysis: whether a person can pass, how they would route, and how a building evacuates are separate work. It states where the route runs, which way each station faces, how wide and how clear it is there, how steeply it climbs, what one step is, which further spaces it stops at, and where its car stands, so later work reads exact values instead of re-deriving them from modelled geometry.

    Optionallandings

    landings?: IAutoMovieConnectorLanding[]

    Further spaces this one run serves between its two endpoints.

    A lift passing five floors is one shaft, not five relations, and a stair with a half-landing stops somewhere its two ends do not name. Adjacency and connector queries answer with these stops, so the floors a run reaches stay in the space graph.

    Absent, the run serves only its two endpoints. Each landing names a resolving space other than either endpoint, appears once, and lies at a route fraction within (0, 1), strictly increasing in IAutoMovieConnectorLanding.at.

    Optionaloperation

    operation?: IAutoMovieConnectorOperation

    Travelling cars and the named states they stand in, or absent for a run that never moves.

    A stair is the whole of itself at all times; a lift, escalator, moving walk, or turning gate is not. Absent, the connector is static and lowers with no carriage or state.

    Optionalorientations

    orientations?: IAutoMovieQuaternion[]

    Per-station facing, one unit quaternion per route point.

    Absent, the route is a bare position polyline. A spiral stair needs it: its centre route is nearly a vertical line, so consecutive treads differ only by the turn between them. The facing is a full quaternion rather than a heading because a tread is also pitched and a helical ramp is also banked.

    • Defined in packages/interface/src/architecture/IAutoMovieBuiltConnector.ts:112

    Optionalslope

    slope?: number

    Slope of the travelled surface in radians, measured from horizontal.

    It is the climb taken against the horizontal length of the route, not of the straight line between its endpoints, so a switchback's slope is the gradient actually walked.

    The route already implies it, so a stated slope is a claim the engine checks: the implied value is atan2(|last.y - first.y|, horizontal route length), the net climb averaged over the whole route rather than its steepest segment, and a stated slope must lie within [0, PI / 2] and agree with it to 1e-6 radians. Absent, nothing is checked.
