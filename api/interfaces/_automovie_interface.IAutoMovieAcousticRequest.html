    Interface IAutoMovieAcousticRequest

    Everything one room-acoustic study is configured with.

    analyzeAutoMovieAcoustics throws on a blank id, subject or revision, a non-positive volume, an empty surface list, any malformed or repeated member, more receivers than AUTOMOVIE_ANALYSIS_MAX_SAMPLES, or a malformed target. Every field except id, subject and inputRevision enters the settings digest; those three go into the run header.
