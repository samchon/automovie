    Interface IAutoMovieHumanArmPiece

    One span of the arm between two named anatomical loci, built from fixed inputs alone.

    A piece owns two channels over its interval of the walk: the girth the section must measure (knots of a monotone curve, one per named site) and the section's normalised shape (rows of the four guides' radius jets and the four sectors' fullness, read by the polar sector rule). The arm is the pieces laid proximal to distal; where two meet, the proximal piece owns the shared row and girth knot and the distal piece copies them (rule J3, the station rule), so no piece invents its side of a boundary.

    A piece reads only IAutoMovieHumanArmPiece.IInputs: the bones, the walk on them and the four tapes. It reads no skin, no other person and no other piece except the boundary row its neighbour owns, which it receives from that neighbour's end function. So a piece can be built, checked and looked at alone.
