    Interface IAutoMovieJointAxes

    The bone-local axes the three clinical angles rotate about.

    Lets a rig whose bone frames are not aligned to the default clinical planes, such as a T-pose arm pointing along its local X, declare which local axis flexion, abduction and twist each turn about, so flexion stays anatomically sagittal instead of rolling the bone along its length.

    A rig declares them on the bone itself, IAutoMovieBone.axes, which takes precedence over the per-bone table a host passes to the engine, such as HUMANOID_JOINT_AXES; a bone that declares none reads that table, then DEFAULT_JOINT_AXES.

    Each axis is a direction in the bone's local frame; any nonzero length is accepted and normalized before use. The three must be finite, longer than 1e-9, and mutually orthogonal, the normalized axes' dot products within 1e-6 of zero, in either handedness; otherwise normalizeJointAxes throws, validateJointAxesBasis reports an issue, and validateModel refuses a bone that declares them. DEFAULT_JOINT_AXES maps flexion to X, abduction to Z and twist to Y, and HUMANOID_JOINT_AXES remaps the arms and turns hip, ankle and toe flexion to −X, so positive flexion swings the thigh forward and lifts the toes.
