    Interface IAutoMovieHumanSurfaceAppendage

    One appendage: a chain whose end meets a closed root loop on its base chain through a transition strip.

    The base is not lofted inside the loop. Between the loop on the base and the appendage's end ring, a strip T(t, w) runs from the appendage (w = 0) to the base (w = 1) as the quintic Hermite of the two curves, their cross derivatives and their second cross derivatives, so the skin is continuous in position, tangent plane and curvature across both edges (G2). Ring phase t of the appendage's end and loop parameter t name the same strip column.

    root

    root: number[]

    Root loop in the base's chart as a periodic cubic B-spline of M control points [s_0 .. s_{M-1}, t_0 .. t_{M-1}]: station in mm and phase in radians, the phases continuous (unwrapped) round the loop. Either turning sense is accepted. With a winding, the phase is the winding times the loop parameter plus this remainder.

    Optionalrows

    rows?: number[][]

    Interior rows of the strip, from the appendage toward the base: each the offsets of one row from the edges' quintic blend at its Greville abscissa, in the blend's frame there (along the ring, the normal and their cross product), as three periodic cubic B-splines in the ring phase of M control points, [a_0 .. a_{M-1}, b_0 .., c_0 ..] in mm (AutoMovieHumanBodyStrip). Absent means none: the strip is then the six edge rows alone.

    Optionalwinding

    winding?: 1 | -1

    Turn of the root loop's phase round the base's cap pole, for a loop that winds once round it (a digit's or toe's on the palm's or the sole's cap): the base is left out beyond the loop toward the pole. Absent means none: the loop encloses its region of the chart.
