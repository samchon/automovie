    Interface IAutoMovieFurnishingLayout

    A rectangular room in which a furnishing kit places its props.

    The kit's placement frame is at the floor centre, with Y up. All plan dimensions are metres. The producer checks that dimensions and the aisle are finite and positive, and that reserved rectangles and overridden props fit the useful room. Use the room dimensions inside its walls.
