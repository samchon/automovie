    Optionalaxis

    • axis?(
          a: IAutoMovieVector3,
          b: IAutoMovieVector3,
          seconds: number,
          stops: IStops,
          taper?: readonly [number, number],
      ): number

      The signed distance from a segment to the body's surface at a second, metres: the least signed distance over the segment's points, positive when the whole segment is outside. Required to meet capsule weapons. Given a taper, the segment is widened by a radius running linearly from its first end's to its other end's, and the distance is that tapered capsule's: the least over its points of the distance less the radius there.

      Parameters

      • a: IAutoMovieVector3

        The segment's first end, world metres.

      • b: IAutoMovieVector3

        Its other end, world metres.

      • seconds: number

        Seconds.

      • stops: IStops

        The stops so far.

      • Optionaltaper: readonly [number, number]

        The radii at a and at b, metres, for a tapered capsule weapon.

      Returns number

      Metres.

          a: IAutoMovieVector3,
          b: IAutoMovieVector3,
          seconds: number,
          stops: IStops,
          by: string,
      ): string | undefined

      The body's own weapon carried by the part a striking weapon met, when that weapon is still on its free path at the second: the contact is mutual, so it stops with the striker. Absent, or undefined, when the part met carries none.

      Parameters

      • a: IAutoMovieVector3

        The striking weapon's centre, or its axis's first end, world metres.

      • b: IAutoMovieVector3

        The axis's other end; the centre again for a sphere.

      • seconds: number

        The contact second.

      • stops: IStops

        The stops before this contact.

      • by: string

        The id of the body carrying the striking weapon.

      Returns string | undefined

      The id of one of this body's weapons, or undefined.

    Optionaldeparture

    • departure?(contact: IInitialContact): Departure | undefined

      Certify departure of an already-present pair only when its physical model establishes that phase. A clearance must have no new entry before it; no-entry covers only this search span. Undefined or absent keeps the original initial overlap/touch report. Opaque distance samples alone do not justify a certificate. The resolver validates clearance against at(seconds), and every other body keeps its original eligibility.

      Parameters

      • contact: IInitialContact

      Returns Departure | undefined

    Optionalspeed

    • speed?(grid: IAutoMovieResolveContactEvents.IGrid, stops: IStops): Speed

      The body's speed bound on one search's grid (Speed), prepared once a search under that search's stops and read with a weapon's own (IWeapon.speed), over the same doubling window from a grid second the scan read and before the weapon's, so a body that holds still only for a while is still bound there and one that cannot be bound costs the weapon no read; absent, every grid step is read against this body.
