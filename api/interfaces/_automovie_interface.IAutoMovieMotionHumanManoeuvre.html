    Interface IAutoMovieMotionHumanManoeuvre

    A change of formation, planned as each member's own travel from its old slot to its new one: a wheel, a column deploying into line, a square, a line opening or closing order, a crowd re-forming.

    The target formation is data (IAutoMovieMotionHumanFormation); the manoeuvre adds the four choices that decide it, each a small closed union: how members take the new slots (assign), what way each goes (path), who steps off when after the word (order), and what each rank does on arrival (arrive). A drill is a row of those four (HUMAN_MANOEUVRES). Each member's part is then appended to its chain as the vocabulary's travel synced to the word (HumanManoeuvre.append), so the cuts, refusals and verdicts are the march's and the stand's.

        to: IAutoMovieMotionHumanFormation;
    }
