    Interface IAutoMovieHumanBodyIdentity

    Body heights, breadths and circumferences, with one record per limb.

    Heights are measured from the standing surface. Surface landmarks, mechanical joint centres and support-to-support chord lengths have distinct measurement protocols. Measurements taken at different respiratory states or tissue pressures remain independent observations.

    Every measure the public input also carries takes its declaration, definition and range from IAutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy, so no measure has two ranges; this record requires each of them and adds the measures the anatomy record does not carry.

    bicristalBreadth: number

    chestBreadth

    chestBreadth: number

    Chest breadth: across the rib cage under the arms at the chest circumference's level, by beam caliper at the maximum point of quiet respiration (ANSUR 1988 chest breadth), the front outline's knot there.     thyroidAngle

    thyroidAngle: number

    Thyroid angle: the internal angle between the two laminae of the thyroid cartilage at the level of the vocal folds, degrees. The narrower the angle, the further the laryngeal prominence, the Adam's apple, projects in front of the neck. On computed tomography of 283 people aged 8 to 20 the female mean stayed between 91 and 97 degrees at every age while the male mean fell from 92 to 67, most steeply at 14 to 15 years (Glikson et al. 2017, Laryngoscope 127(10):E354-E358); adult cadaver larynges measure 74.4 in men and 92.4 in women (Rawal et al. 2014, Natl J Integr Res Med 5(3)). Schema constraints: Minimum: 40; Maximum: 140; retained by Human's schema refinement.

    waistBreadth

    waistBreadth: number

    Waist breadth: the torso's horizontal breadth at the natural waist, the waist circumference's level, standing, tissue uncompressed; the front outline's knot there. No survey takes it at this level: ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II take the waist breadth at the navel (ANSUR II: 299.9 and 326.4, means), so the default is a derivation: the navel section's breadth-on-girth law (ANSUR 1988) at the natural waist girth, 268.0 (20.6) and 302.1 (23.6) at the surveyed natural girths. Schema constraints: Minimum: 40; Maximum: 800; retained by Human's schema refinement.
