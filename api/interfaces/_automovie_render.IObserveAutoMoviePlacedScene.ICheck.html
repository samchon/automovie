    Actual touching evidence for the same declared pair at one film second, in one of three classes. measured carries one exact geometric or completed solver separation in metres, with a null impulse when no solver evidence exists. absent states that the pair was completely observed at that second and did not touch. unobservable states that the producer could not observe the pair and why. The evidence supplements the measured relation and its coverage. It never becomes the expected target, and it never decides intended support or grip ownership on its own.
