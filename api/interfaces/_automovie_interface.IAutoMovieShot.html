    Interface IAutoMovieShot

    A shot: one continuous take (a scene, the camera that frames it, the camera's move, and what every placed node performs) over a local time range. This is the unit an LLM renders ("render this shot") and the rung above a single clip on the road to assembling a film from objects and motion.

    Time is local to the shot (origin 0, seconds); a IAutoMovieSequence composes shots into a global timeline. The camera is a scene node, so its move is an ordinary IAutoMovieClip of node transform tracks, with no special camera-animation concept.

    performShot assembles the shot and validateShotArtifact is its structural contract, which every producer and consumer shares. A clip field admits only the channels its own applier writes: cameraMotion and objectMotions take node channels, and lightMotions takes light pointers, so a pointer such as a camera's field of view is refused rather than stored and ignored.

    OptionalcameraClearance

    cameraClearance?: IAutoMovieCameraClearanceReport[]

    Swept-clearance reports for hero and alternate takes declaring physical envelopes, one per such camera. The performing producer checks the runtime revision; the stored report has no revision pair for an artifact reader to re-establish freshness independently.

    Omission is admitted only when no delivered camera declares an envelope. A present list must carry clear reports with empty findings; blocked or stale evaluation refuses publication by the performing producer.

    OptionalcameraIntent

    cameraIntent?: IAutoMovieCameraIntent[]

    The camera's directorial intent per frame span: framing, move, the resolved focus point, and the lens intent. Structural guide metadata a diffusion or render host reads beside cameraMotion, as it reads events. The deterministic camera solve never consumes it. Absent means legacy; an empty array means the shot was assembled with no frame actions.

    Optionalcoverage

    coverage?: IAutoMovieShotCoverage[]

    The alternate camera takes covering the same beat: one staged camera per additional angle, each with its own compiled move and intent. The hero take stays the singular camera and cameraMotion every consumer already reads; coverage rides beside it as structural guide metadata a diffusion or render host uses to render the beat from the other staged angles. The cut is untouched: coverage takes are alternates of this shot, never separate timeline entries. Absent means legacy or single-camera data; an empty array means the shot was assembled with one camera.

    OptionallightMotions

    lightMotions?: IAutoMovieClip[]

    Clips changing this shot's lighting over its local clock: a candle going out, a sunset. Each track addresses one staged light by the pointer /lights/<id>/<property>, where the property is intensity, color, range, coneAngle, position or rotation, and no two tracks across the list may address the same light property. Absent means legacy; an empty array means constant light.

    objectMotions

    objectMotions: IAutoMovieClip[]

    Node-transform clips for non-skeletal scene objects the shot animates (a launched projectile's baked flight, a prop carried along a path, a door panel or prop joint the source authored), each an ordinary IAutoMovieClip with a unique id. Distinct from performances (skeletal pose motions played through a rig) and from cameraMotion (the one live camera): an object has no skeleton, so it moves the way the camera does, by a clip of transform tracks. Empty when the shot animates no such object.
