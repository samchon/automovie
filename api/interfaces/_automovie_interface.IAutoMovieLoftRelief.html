    Interface IAutoMovieLoftRelief

    A relief: a feature written as a height over its base chain's surface, such as the nose over the face or a breast over the thorax.

    Over a window of the base's chart (station and phase), the skin is the base point moved along the relief's fixed axis by a height d(s, t), a tensor-product uniform cubic B-spline whose three outer rings of control points are zero (a uniform cubic takes three control points at a knot, so the height, its slope and its curvature then vanish there). d and its first and second derivatives therefore vanish at the window's edge, so the skin is as smooth across the edge as the base itself and no boundary exists. A fixed axis chosen across the feature's underside (forward and down for the nose and the breasts) keeps every point a single height, so the feature cannot fold back.

    The window and the control net are the relief's transformation handles: a gain on the net changes the feature's projection, a change of window its extent.

    Optionalturn

    turn?: [number, number, number]

    A second unit world direction, square to axis, that makes the height's direction turn with the phase instead of staying fixed: at phase t it is cos(t - c) axis + sin(t - c) turn, c the window's middle phase. With axis the radial direction of the base's chart at c and turn the chart's direction of increasing phase there, the height is cast square to a round base all the way round, so one window can go round a part whose sides a fixed axis would only graze. Absent, the axis is fixed.
