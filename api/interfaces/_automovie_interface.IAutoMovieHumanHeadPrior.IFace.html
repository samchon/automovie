    ala

    ala: readonly number[]

    Alar lobules: height, reach in half nose widths, centre above the subnasale in spans of nasion to tip, half-heights above and below likewise, half-width in half nose widths. Reach and half-width put the lobule's fall at the nose's width, so the width input is where it ends. A crest's radius is the square of the half-height over twice the height, so the lower half-height keeps the alar rim round.
