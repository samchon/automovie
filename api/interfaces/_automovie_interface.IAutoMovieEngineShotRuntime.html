    Interface IAutoMovieEngineShotRuntime

    Host-owned capabilities and compiled facts compileDefinedShot needs to turn a shot program's thin verbs into dense, validated motion.

    The shot program states what happens; this record supplies what only the host knows: the rig and model of each staged node, the action synthesizer, the delivery raster, builder-owned formations and clips, the previous beat's end state, and any collision advice. compileDefinedShot forwards the capabilities to performShot, the measurement inputs to realizeShotContract, previous to blockBeat, plants to the closing continuity snapshot, and advice to its own validation. Every optional member, when omitted, disables only the check or measurement it feeds, as stated on that member.
