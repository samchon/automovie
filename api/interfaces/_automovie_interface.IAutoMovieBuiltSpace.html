    Interface IAutoMovieBuiltSpace

    One named semantic region, independent of visible walls and slabs.

    A space states its volume at most one way, in cells or in shell. Stating both is refused, because two spellings of one region can be edited apart and the containment query would have to pick a winner nobody authored. Stating neither makes a purely semantic container that locates nothing and contains no point.

    cells

    cells: IAutoMovieConvexSpaceCell[]

    World-space convex cells whose union locates the region. An empty list with no shell is a semantic container; a non-convex region may be split into cells. Individually convex cells do not imply their combined union is convex.

    A finite convex decomposition can represent a bounded polyhedral region in exact geometry, including concavity and voids. The stored plane values and binary64 query tolerances delimit the actual answer; downstream cell-volume totals can also count overlaps more than once. Curved boundaries require a faceted declaration, while a shell can state a void without a manual cell decomposition.

    Optionalfidelity

    fidelity?: "exact" | "faceted"

    What the stated volume claims to be, when it claims less than the region.

    Absent, or exact, says the cells or the shell are the region: a rectilinear room, a chamfered lobby, a slab with a rectangular void.

    faceted says they only stand in for it. A dome, a barrel vault, and a free-form soffit have curved boundaries, and this record carries no curved primitive, so an author can write only flats. Every derived quantity, section, and containment answer over such a space is the facets' answer, and the drawing schedule and quantity take-off report that.

    It is a declaration, not a measurement: nothing can look at flats and tell whether a curve was meant. Declaring it on a space that states no volume is refused, because there is nothing for it to approximate.

    Optionalshell

    shell?: IAutoMovieSpaceShell

    The region as its own closed triangle boundary, when half-space cells would state it awkwardly.

    A storey pierced by an atrium is one shell rather than a hand decomposition into cells, which IFC likewise allows through a faceted B-rep with voids. A shell is authoritative for containment wherever it is stated, it is mutually exclusive with cells, and it is flat-faced: a dome authored here is the facets it is written as, which fidelity declares.
