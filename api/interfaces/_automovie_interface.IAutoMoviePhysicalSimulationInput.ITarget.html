    Interface ITarget

    Authored world poses and causal ownership/control policy for one cache sample.

    Sample k supplies continuous poses at k/rate seconds but its discrete policy owns the preceding interval [(k-1)/rate,k/rate); sample zero initializes ownership. AutoMoviePhysicalSimulation clones targets before admission and identity hashing; physical target fitting may derive different motor goals without rewriting these authored samples. admitPhysicalTargets requires complete segment coverage, finite native-range positions, unit quaternions and strengths in [0,1]. dynamicSegments must explicitly include descendants of every selected parent.

    OptionalcoupledGroup

    coupledGroup?: string

    Exact nonblank id of a mutually bound group for this output interval. Active targets with the same id at step k share one target fit against the simultaneous scene snapshot. Frame zero seeds the simulation. Omission keeps ordinary individual tracking. Admission refuses a member with releaseOnContact, and a member whose native root also takes balance or supportTracking goals at that step.
