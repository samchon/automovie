    Interface IAutoMovieMeshSectionCut

    The section at one station.

    Section arrays transfer to the caller, but extrema may share point tuples and outer boundaries may reuse loop points. Flags also include bridged gap lengths and chains still unclosed after bridging; an empty cut has empty arrays, null extremes and no flags.

    Extremes along +a, -a, +b, -b (a and b the two in-plane frame axes after the station axis in cyclic order: for axis 1 they are axes 2 and 0), and the quarter diagonals (+a+b), (+a-b), (-a+b), (-a-b) over the square root of two; null when the plane misses.

    flags

    flags: string[]

    Why this sample cannot be trusted as it stands, empty when sound: "open" when the cut leaves dangling crossings (a hole, a gap or a broken slice of the mesh; the flag names the dangling crossings and broken chains), "jump " when an extreme leaves the mean of its two neighbouring stations' by more than the tolerance (a spike or saw tooth). A flagged sample is to be checked in the viewer or excluded.

    outer

    outer: IAutoMovieMeshSectionChain[]

    The cut's outer boundaries, largest enclosed area first: the closed pieces after open chains were joined end to end across gaps up to the bridge (each bridged gap flagged), less every piece enclosed by another (eyes, teeth, a mouth bag). Disjoint bodies (two legs) give one each; a reference with holes and slits still gives its outer contour here.
