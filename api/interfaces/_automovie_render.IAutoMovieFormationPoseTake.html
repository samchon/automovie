    Interface IAutoMovieFormationPoseTake

    One cycle of a performed member's poses for the population layer to play: the poses at even phases of one period, as a motion law displayed them (HumanPopulation.cycle in the motions package), so a background member plays the march the foreground members march instead of a generic gait.
