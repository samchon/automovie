    Interface IAutoMovieMotionHumanActionContext<P>

    Everything one action instance plans from: whose body, with which parameters and skill, entered from which state, at which film second, and how to find its targets and its seeded draws.

    start is the film second of the action's own time zero; an action entered at a later share of itself (a redirect continuing its family's clock) starts before its entry. aim resolves a target to a world point and outward normal at a film second: a person's region on that person's displayed body, a prop's named point, a free point, or the virtual opponent of none. random returns the stream of one channel of this instance, keyed by the chain seed, the subject, the action's id and its instance.

    The arm solutions handed between the plans of this one action: the solutions the last plan of the same action in the same performance reached (an alignment trial's, a replan's over another span), by the arms solved together, and the map this plan's solutions are written into for the next.     meeting

    • meeting(
          kind: IAutoMovieMotionMeetingKind,
          role: string,
          partner?: IPartner | readonly IPartner[],
          window?: IAutoMovieMotionMeeting.IWindow,
      ): IAutoMovieMotionMeeting

      The meeting of an action this performer does with others: the one record every role reads, computed from where all stand at this action's start by whichever role is planned first, and the same whichever that is. With no partner it is this role's own meeting on its default places, which is not solid. It may be read at plan time, so the partners' earlier actions must be placed first, as HumanChain.mutual places them.

      Parameters

        The kind of mutual action.

      • role: string

        This performer's role in it.

      • Optionalpartner: IPartner | readonly IPartner[]

        The other performer and their role, or every other one of a meeting of three or more; absent when alone.

      • Optionalwindow: IAutoMovieMotionMeeting.IWindow

        The film seconds the meeting holds over, which every role states alike (IAutoMovieMotionMeeting.IWindow); its natural instants when absent.

      Returns IAutoMovieMotionMeeting

      The meeting.

      Throws

      When no such performer exists, or the kind lacks a role.

      Throws
