    Optionalaim

    aim?: { digit: "thumb" | "forefinger"; toward: "down" | "up" }

    A digit of shape aimed straight at the world's up or down: the moving hands turn by the least rotation from the gesture's attitude, after turn, that points the digit's own direction (HumanHandShape.digit) there, since a straight digit does not lie along the hand's axes (a thumbs up's thumb stands about 35 degrees off the knuckle line). Requires shape; no aim when absent.

    Type Declaration

    • digit: "thumb" | "forefinger"

      Digit extended for the directed hand gesture.

    • toward: "down" | "up"

      Whether that digit points upward or downward.

    Optionalbalance

    balance?: IAutoMovieMotionHumanBalance

    A balancing shift (IAutoMovieMotionHumanBalance): one closed-form shift of its part, scaled by its weight between its two event-named instants, each field absent taking the law's recommendation. Absent, none: the posture is the law's own kinematic one with no balance shift or centre-of-mass reading.

    Optionalmanner

    manner?: Pick<IAutoMovieMotionManner, "variation" | "proficiency">

    How much repeats of the gesture differ: variation in [0, 1] scales the spread of the preparation's length and of where a wave or a stopPalm holds the hand within its named place, and proficiency replaces the performer's skill for those spreads. Absent or 0, every repeat is the same.
