    Interface IAutoMovieBuiltEnvelopeFace

    One exposed separation of a building unit, placed in world space.

    A separation is exposed when it encloses exactly one logical space, because the record's other spelling names the two regions a separation stands between. What is left over is the envelope, which is precisely the population a facade, corner, and roof review is counted over.

    Produced by builtEnvironmentEnvelopeFaces, sorted by boundary id in code-unit order. A boundary with no face, with no space or more than one, or whose one space is not in the record yields no record; a space no building unit owns makes the producer throw.
