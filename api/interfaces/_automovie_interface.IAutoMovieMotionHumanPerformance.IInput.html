    Optionalcues

    cues?: Readonly<Record<string, readonly IAutoMovieScheduleCue[]>>

    Tracks of named instants that belong to no action, by track name: a dialogue's line starts and accents, a score's beats (HumanCue builds both). An action is synced to one (HumanChain.sync) and cut on one (until) as on another action's events; a track's name is used like an anchor, so no action may be anchored under it.

    Optionalflight

    flight?: "physical" | "staged"

    Authored airborne turns use deterministic staged rotation by default, preserving the law's centre path and fitted articulation without claiming angular-momentum conservation. The existing landing pose requires complete revolutions on each axis; nonclosing fractional turns need a compatible landing outcome and are refused. Physical solves turns from the composed body's inertia and take-off limit but does not repair that legacy landing limitation. A flight with explicitly supplied momentum always remains physical.

    forces?: readonly IAutoMovieMotionHumanForce[]

    Forces on the performers (#3429): impulses at an instant and forces sustained over a span, read by the reactions the composition places (IAutoMovieMotionHumanActionContext.forces). A force starts no action. None when absent.

    Optionallaws

    laws?: readonly IAutoMovieMotionHumanActionLaw<never>[]

    Laws of the production's own, performed like the vocabulary's under the names they carry: an act the vocabulary lacks, written on the same kernel (a clock, a base and part primitives), or a row of an existing law with some of its parameters fixed. A name the vocabulary already has, or one given twice, refuses the performance. A registered law draws from streams keyed by its own name, so registering it changes no other action's draws. HumanChain.extend gives its typed method.
