    brief?: ProductionStage

    Sets the short route's brief construction stage.

    The brief is the short route's source layer and has no upstream references. Omitted, it is disabled.

    Optionalindex

    index?: ProductionStage

    Sets the long-route settings outline document's own construction stage.

    The outline's part principle and arrangement obligation claims follow this stage, so the outline's evidence can open or close apart from the settings files. The file-to-outline-entry claim stays on the settings stage. Omitted, the outline follows the settings stage.
