    Interface IAutoMovieConstructionBandProfile

    One cross profile of a port's band: a depth along the band's own inward normal over a window of its loop clock.

    At an interior section of clock c and cross share t (0 at the port curve, 1 at the part's rail) the supplied point is the plain blend's, carried in by w(c) D(t) along the plain blend's inward unit normal there. D is the cubic spline through (0, 0), the knots and (1, 0) with zero slope at both ends, so the depth starts and ends flat; it is laid in as interior cross sections at the knots' shares, each with the depth's own slope and bend across the band, and the band's rails keep the two sides' 2-jets, so it stays G2 to the host and to the part. w is 1 inside [start, end] and falls to 0 over transition on each side by the quintic smoothstep, as a split's crease window does, so the band stays C2 along its loop. Where windows overlap their depths add; every window's knots are laid at every window's shares. The section derivative construction omits the inward normal's own derivatives across the band. The resulting piecewise quintic passes through those supplied section jets and keeps the original rail jets; it is not the exact normal-displaced plain surface between sections. Port realization converts metre depths to IAutoMovieConstructionBandProfile64 millimetres once before this kernel evaluation.

    Engine admission: at least one knot; every share finite in (0, 1) and above the one before it; every depth finite; start and end finite with start <= end < start + 2 pi; transition finite and positive.
