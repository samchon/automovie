    Interface IAutoMovieConstructionPort

    A part attached at a known port of a host face: a closed port curve on the host's chart, an ellipse about a published centre, is cut out of the host, and a band spans from that curve to the part's ring at rail (an arm on its armscye, a branch on a stem's side). The parent owns the port loop and the region inside it; the part keeps its stations beyond the rail.

    The port curve at clock c in [0, 2 pi) is the chart point (centre[0] + semi[0] cos c, centre[1] + sense semi[1] sin c): station in metres, phase in radians. The band is the engine's join strip between two ribbons: the host surface's own 2-jet across the port curve, toward the curve's centre (the chart direction (-semi[0] cos c, -sense semi[1] sin c)), and the part's own 2-jet at its rail toward its kept side, both scaled by one factor at which no column turns back along its chord. The band is G2 to the host along the port curve and G2 to the part along its rail. A failed premise leaves the part, the band and the host's port as they were, with one diagnostic naming the port.

    Engine admission: hostFace a view part of the host's subtree and partFace one of the part's; the band name unique in the tree; count a multiple of 4 of at least 8; rows a positive integer; rail positive; rails, when given, at least 3 finite positive distances; the loop's centre finite, both semi-axes positive and the phase semi-axis under pi; a loop's shape, when given, count finite positive shares; each profile as IAutoMovieConstructionBandProfile states.

    Optionalcorrespondence

    correspondence?: "angle" | "length"

    How the part's rail is matched to the port curve column by column. angle (the default): the rail point at the curve point's angle about the rail ring's centroid, which both curves must turn round once monotonically. length: the first column by that angle, every other at the same share of the rail's length round it as the column's of the port curve's, so neither need be star-shaped about the centroid, as a nose's rail is not where it runs back behind each nostril and its underside is lifted onto the skin.

    Optionalrails

    rails?: number[]

    The rail as a closed curve round the part instead of one station: its distance from that end at the part phases 2 pi j / m, j = 0 .. m - 1, metres, positive, joined round the part by the periodic cubic spline through them. The part keeps, at each phase, the stations beyond the curve. It serves a part whose root does not lie in one section plane, as a nose's does: its wings leave the face by the crease round them, far back, while its middle meets the lip far forward. Absent, the rail is the one station rail.

    • Defined in packages/interface/src/construction/IAutoMovieConstructionPort.ts:54

    Optionalribbons

    ribbons?: "common" | "column"

    How far each column's ribbons reach into the band. common (the default): every column's ribbons are scaled by one factor, the largest at which no column turns back along its chord, so the hardest column sets the reach of all. column: each column takes its own factor from a field smooth along the loop that stays under every column's own largest, so a column where the port's loop and the part's ring disagree shortens only its neighbourhood and the band elsewhere turns from the host to the part over its whole length. Either way the band stays G2 to both sides.
