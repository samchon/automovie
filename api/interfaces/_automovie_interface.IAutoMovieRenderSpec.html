    Interface IAutoMovieRenderSpec

    Render parameters for turning a shot or sequence into frames and video. Kept separate from the shot so the same shot renders at a draft 12 fps or a final 30 or 60 fps without editing the content.

    The pipeline: N = round(duration * frameFormat.fps) frames, sampled at t = i / frameFormat.fps (rational, not accumulated, for determinism), each rendered headless to an sRGB image, then encoded by ffmpeg -framerate fps -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf crf -r fps -s widthxheight -movflags +faststart. The encoder never tone maps; the curve is a renderer setting applied before capture.

    toneMapping

    toneMapping: "none" | "acesFilmic"

    Tone mapping applied before the sRGB encode: none for the stylized toon path, acesFilmic for the photoreal path.

    This is the delivery default, and a scene overrides it. A scene's optional environment owns the photographic response of that scene (its image lighting, exposure and curve), and one render spec covers a whole sequence of scenes, so a spec-level curve forced onto every shot would flatten a night interior and a noon exterior into one response. A scene that declares no environment keeps this value.

    Structural passes (depth, normal, mask, outline) ignore both. Their pixels are geometric facts, so tone mapping and exposure are bypassed there.
