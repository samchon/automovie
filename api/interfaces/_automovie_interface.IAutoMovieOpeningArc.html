    Interface IAutoMovieOpeningArc

    The circle one bulged opening edge runs on, in the profile's planar frame.

    Produced by autoMovieOpeningArc. The arc starts at the edge's from point, at angle start about center, and turns by the signed sweep to reach the edge's to point; a negative sweep runs clockwise.
