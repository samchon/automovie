    Interface IAutoMovieHumanArmLaw

    The arm's sections for one side, in the left frame: a one-parameter family of planes from the hand's branch (low parameter) through the heel, the forearm and the upper arm to the level shoulder ring (high parameter). The hand's solve reads its wrist, crease and girth; the chain reads its layers, planes and points.
