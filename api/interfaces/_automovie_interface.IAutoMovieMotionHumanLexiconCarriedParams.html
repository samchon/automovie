    Open parameters of the carried lexicon family, keyed by each runtime export name. Omitted keys are fixed by the row before its underlying law plans; the remaining members retain that law's defaults, units and required status. HumanPerformanceParams checks these open inputs using the generated row schema before planning or resolving a row's fixed values.

    bearing

    bearing: IBearing

    Hold a person in a named carry: the carrier's hands where the carry takes the carried body. Performed over the carrier's own action (carrier.walk(...).over((o) => o.registered("bearing", ...))), it claims only the arms that hold. With two helpers each names the other by beside, the one whose carry is the other side's being the second. With the carried person named and the hold performed as an action of its own, the carrier tips about its feet by the pair's own balance as its hands take the body (HumanCarriedCouplingLoad.lean); over a walk, which keeps its trunk, the composition schedules that tip as a layer (HumanCarriedLean.coupled), and with no person named the table's lean (HumanCarriedLean.layer).

    With the carried person named (of), each holding wrist goes where the coupled carry lays the held region, in the frame of the carrier's own displayed bone the borne body rides (HumanCarriedCouplingPose.holds); otherwise to a place measured from one of the carrier's own regions on the trunk the carrier's action has placed, in arm lengths. A named person's pair tips the carrier about its feet by its static balance. Either way the hold follows the walk, and the arm is carried in joint space from what it held (AutoMovieMotionHumanArm.carried). One arm primitive per holding hand over the inherited base. The hands do not read the carried body, which reads the carrier, so the two cannot chase each other; the holds' places are provisional. One trunk resolution per holding arm per posture. Planned once per instance; nothing is shared between instances. The plan closes over its constants and the entry state, released with the plan.

    borne

    borne: IBorne

    Be carried in a named carry: the body takes the carry's posture and rests on the carrier by every contact of the carry's coupled meeting (#3437, HumanCarriedCoupling): its resting region on the carrier's region and its held regions on the carrier's palms, each window with its load share, its clinging hands on the carrier's regions. The carrier performs bearing over their own action. The lift into the carry and the set-down are the lift and setDown seconds (HumanCarriedLift). A second helper named by with takes the other side of a carry that has one (HumanCarriedCoupling.PAIRED), and the body rests on both. A person named as their own carrier is refused on by, a second helper on a carry with no other side on with, and a borne mass no lean of a carrier balances on mass with the feasible mass.

    The coupling is read from the meeting, computed once whichever role plans first; at each second the carrier's displayed bones are read and the carry's key posture rides the carrier's displayed bone its resting region lies on, at the coupling's key offset (HumanCarriedCouplingPose.posture). A meeting that is not solid falls back to the single-region placement on the carrier's region, found by one forward kinematics of the trunk. The row, the coupling, the windows, then one posture function. The posture is rigid in the carry (no sag, no sway) and its angles are provisional; with no carrier the posture is taken in place and declares no support. One key resolution, one read of the carrier's bones and one frame composition per posture. Planned once per instance; nothing is shared between instances. The plan closes over its constants and the entry state, released with the plan.
