    Interface IAutoMovieSubdivisionTopology

    Connectivity of one subdivision level: polygons, edges with sharpness, and the ordered fan of edges and faces around every vertex.

    Faces are stored as compressed rows: face f owns corners faceOffsets[f] to faceOffsets[f + 1] - 1, and corner c is vertex faceVertices[c]. A corner's out-edge runs from its vertex to the next corner's. Every edge is the out-edge of one corner per adjacent face: the first in corner order, then the opposite one, or -1 on a boundary.

    A vertex's fan lists its faces counter-clockwise about the outward side, as corners, and its edges between them: edge i is the out-edge of fan corner i, and fan corner i lies between edges i and i + 1. An interior fan is cyclic with as many edges as corners and starts at the vertex's lowest-numbered corner; a boundary fan starts at the boundary edge leaving the vertex and ends at the boundary edge entering it, one edge more than corners.
