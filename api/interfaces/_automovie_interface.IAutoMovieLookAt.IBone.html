    Interface IBone

    Gaze turns the eye bones.

    Each eye turns by clinical flexion, positive down, and twist, positive toward the model's own left, the conventions the head's gaze uses; in the canonical rest frame the default basis puts them on the eye's +X and +Y, and a rig whose eye frames differ declares IAutoMovieBone.axes so the two still mean those directions. A channel at full weight turns the eye by the range in its direction, and partial weights scale linearly, so a weight w is VRM's range map evaluated at w of its input maximum. Inner and outer are relative to each eye: looking left turns the left eye outward and the right eye inward. The turn adds to any angle the pose authors on that eye.

    validateModel requires a skeleton declaring leftEye and rightEye, and each range finite, at least 0, and within both eyes' range of motion in its direction.
