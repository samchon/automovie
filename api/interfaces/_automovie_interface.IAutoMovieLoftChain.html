    Interface IAutoMovieLoftChain

    Principal layers and explicit source owners of one lofted chain.

    View lofts, fitted sections and continuation operators publish this record for AutoMovieLoftChain and AutoMovieLoftJet. A published chain and its nested arrays/source records must remain unchanged while readers retain it: point and jet caches key preparations by chain identity and physical station. Copies with equal numeric data retain source bindings; changing values requires the owning operator to re-express or remove those bindings. The interface does not freeze or validate storage; construction operators own admission, and readers reject stale explicit owners when reached.

    Optionalmirrored

    mirrored?: boolean

    Whether the chain bounds a hole, such as the pocket under an ear's helix.

    A hole's sections run clockwise (front toward -Z), so its own rings face into the hole, which is outward from the body there. Phases of a hole's sections, in the fitter and for vertices it inherits, are taken from the outline mirrored as (x, z) -> (x, -z), which makes them increase along the clockwise ring order the zipper expects. Absent means an ordinary tube.

    Optionalorientation

    orientation?: 1 | -1

    Explicit outward sign of S_station x S_phase, when the producer knows the oriented boundary it continues (a join's blend). Absent, a tube's radial direction and mirrored flag decide it. This sign takes precedence over mirrored. Reversing one chart coordinate or reflecting world space reverses it; orientation-preserving rigid motion and translation keep it. The producer still owns regularity of the connected surface: a sign is not a certificate that its chart has no degenerate or folded point.

    Optionalphases

    phases?: number[]

    The phases of the chain's own rings, radians, strictly increasing within one turn from the first, samples of them, when they are not uniform: a section that turns fast over a few phases, as a sole's edge does, takes its rings' vertices closer there. Absent, the samples uniform phases 2 pi k / samples.

    samples: number

    Number of vertices in the chain's own rings, a positive integer chosen by the producer.
