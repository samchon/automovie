    Interface IAutoMovieInteractionEvent

    One engine-visible interaction on a shot-local clock.

    actor is the initiator or affected performer when one is known, target is the receiver or parent when one is known, and object is the prop or projectile involved when the event is object-mediated. reaction names the actor whose downstream motion was scheduled from this event, or null when the event is only observational.

    compileLaunch, the attachTo handoff, and the collision, free-fall and toppling detectors produce events; sequenceEventTimeline places them on the output clock and reviewVisualRead measures point against the target body to flag a contact that reads as a miss.
