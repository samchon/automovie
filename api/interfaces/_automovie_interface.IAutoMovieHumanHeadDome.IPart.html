    Interface IPart

    A cap retaining its actual original material contact for downstream consumers.

    The same complete source/frame/contact used to author embedding remains attached to the original part. One record preserves geometry and its actual borrowed material owner. Retaining a contact claims neither whole-footprint agreement nor Boolean acceptance. Null means a caller supplied no original material parent. Data performs no work. Regional metadata borrows the identical prepared contact. The composition owns the part and its transitive original-parent source.

    Optionalpole

    pole?: "both" | "bottom" | "top"

    The end that closes in a point, or both ends (both: a foot from the heel to its forefoot cap; a fingertip, a toe tip, the heel, the perineum's cap), where all four outlines and the girth have a knot of value 0 at that end's station. The chain then carries that point as its pole. Radial source preparation uses a signed square-root parameter for one pole and an angle parameter for two by default. An explicit original parameter must have the same singular endpoint and inward side at every actual pole; its other endpoint may lie outside the retained interval. Parameter endpoints alone declare no cap. Preserving an old native field requires the removed cap's actual effects to vanish on the kept interval. The loft adds rings at equal steps of the root of pole distance and moves shoulders toward the conic ellipse value by a quintic smoothstep over the nearest outline-knot span. A top fullness with touch offsets or splits keeps its own angles and shoulders instead: its touch offsets and split coordinates also have a knot of value 0 at the pole, and the cap is the end section shrunk to that point. These construction choices require regular compatible source/frame jets for a smooth dome; this field does not prove G2 regularity of the resulting pole. Absent, both ends are open sections.

    stations

    stations: number[]

    Stations of the emitted chain's layers in mm, strictly increasing. They For a direct shoulder/top source, its continuous functions own every knot even without a layer there. Generic girth's coefficient representation requires each retained contour/fullness knot as a declared station. Controls outside that interval may remain to preserve the original scalar function and explicit parameter; they emit no layer. Between sites, stations follow the surface's needs. Generic girth checks all those declared knots regardless of site label; internal interpolation and axis-source knots require no extra layers.
