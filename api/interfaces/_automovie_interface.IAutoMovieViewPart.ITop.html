    Interface ITop

    A part whose third view is the top (the nose's base, the sole, the lips' arch): the top outline gives the section's shape at its key stations as each quarter's shoulder value, carried by that outline's selected scalar interpolation. The top outline's extremes are the front and side outlines' values there; the outlines keep owning them.

    Optionalpieces

    pieces?: IQuarters<IAutoMovieViewPart.ISplit[]>

    Each quarter split into conic pieces at its ordered points of the section (#3158), in increasing phase: quarter q runs from guide q through pieces[q][0], pieces[q][1], ... to guide q + 1, its first piece with shoulder shoulders[q] and each later piece with the shoulder of the point that starts it, each a rational quadratic on its two end points and tangent lines (AutoMovieViewChain). An empty list leaves the quarter one conic. Absent: one conic per quarter.

Absent: one conic per quarter.

    Optionaltouch

    touch?: IQuarters<IAutoMovieViewPart.IOutline>

    Where each guide touches its support line, millimetres along it from the chart axis: the v of the +u and -u extremes and the u of the +v and -v extremes, so a section's extreme point stands where the section actually reaches it (classic conic lofting's maximum half-breadth line has its own height). Absent: every guide on its axis line, the four-quarter section's convention.
