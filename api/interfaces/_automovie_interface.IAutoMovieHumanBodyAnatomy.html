    Interface IAutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy

    The body's measures: stature, tapes, segment lengths, skeletal breadths, landmark heights and composition, each a published measurement at a published site.

    Every field is optional: an omitted field takes the default AutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy.resolve derives from the same person's sex, stature and tapes by a regression fitted on public survey data, so every person carries every measure. A paired site takes the right side, as ISO 7250-1 and ANSUR II do. Lengths are millimetres.

    Population figures are the female and male mean (SD) of the named survey: ANSUR 1988 (Gordon et al. 1989, NATICK/TR-89/044, 2,208 women and 1,774 men), ANSUR II (Gordon et al. 2014, NATICK/TR-15/007) and the body dimensions data of Heinz et al. (2003, J Stat Educ 11(2), 260 women and 247 men, skeletal breadths given there as the sum of both sides and here per side). The static bounds are envelopes wider than every surveyed adult.
