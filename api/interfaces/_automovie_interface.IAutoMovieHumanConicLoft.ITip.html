    Interface ITip

    One closed end of a part.

    Between from and tip the part is its section at from shrunk by one factor, with that section's tangent angles and shoulders kept. With t = d / H, d the distance to the tip and H that of from, the factor is g = sqrt(P(t)) for the cubic P(t) = a t + b t^2 + c t^3: upright at the tip (a > 0, so g grows as sqrt(t)), 1 at from, leaving from at the wall's own taper q = H mu (mu the wall's relative growth per millimetre away from the tip) and bending there as the wall bends (g'' = k, the wall's relative second derivative times H^2), so the cap continues the wall without a crease in its slope or its curvature. Those three conditions at t = 1, P = 1, P' = 2 q and P'' = 2 q^2 + 2 k, give c = (1 - q)^2 + k, b = 2 q - 1 - 2 c and a = 1 - b - c = 2 (1 - q) + c; c is held at 0 or more, which keeps P positive on the cap. With c = 0 this is the conic P = 2 (1 - q) t + (2 q - 1) t^2, which bends by -(1 - q)^2 at from: the earlier law, which met the slope of every wall but not its bend and left a ring round every finger's and toe's tip, where the wall runs on straight.

    Optionalcentre

    centre?: [number, number]

    The point of the chart, [u, v] mm, the cap's sections gather about while it closes, when the end's top does not stand over the course. A coordinate x of the cap is x g + 2 c g (1 - g)^2 for its centre's coordinate c: at from it and its slope in g are the plain cap's, so the cap leaves the wall as before; it bulges toward the centre over its middle, by 8 c / 27 at most at g = 1 / 3; and it still closes on the course, where the pole is, since x / g tends to x + 2 c, which keeps the course inside every section while |2 c| is less than the section's extent on the far side. f1's crown: the course runs 16 mm behind the skull's top (the reference's top contour at 1,699 mm spans x -4 to 40), and the plain cap shrank the last section, 34 mm behind the course and 83 mm in front, toward it, so the back of the crown stood 4 mm proud of the reference at x -20 and the top was a flat lid with a corner behind.

    Optionalcovered

    covered?: boolean

    The end is not the figure's skin: buried inside another part, as a digit's root inside its limb, or under the digits merged onto it, as a hand's or a foot's closed end. It closes as the conic (c = 0), the slimmest the law allows, instead of bending on as its wall bends: the fuller cap brought the foot's end 5.9 mm below the sole and moved where the outer digits come out of their limbs' ends.

    Optionalown

    own?: boolean

    Each coordinate of the cap takes the taper and the bend of its own outline at from, instead of the mean extent's. One factor for the whole section met the mean slope but not each side's: f1's crown section narrows 0.95 mm a millimetre in front (on 83 mm) and 1.2 behind (on 34 mm), and the shared factor took 1.9 in front and 0.77 behind, a dip at the crown's front and a corner at its back. A coordinate within OWN_LEAST of the section's size of zero keeps the shared factor, since its relative change has no meaning there.
