    Interface IAutoMovieAimDriver

    Orient an owner so one of its axes points at a target (eyes, head, camera).

    Resolved in the world-space pass after the hierarchy is composed. The owner's world rotation becomes the shortest arc taking aimAxis onto the direction toward the target's world position, followed by a twist about that direction that brings upAxis nearest to worldUp. The result is slerped from the owner's composed rotation by influence, the owner's world position and scale are kept, and its subtree recomposes. Channel limits run before this pass, so they never clamp the aimed rotation. A non-finite or zero axis, or an influence outside [0, 1], throws.

    When worldUp is parallel to the aim direction the twist is undefined and skipped. A target at the owner's own position gives no direction, and the owner then turns toward the identity world rotation.
