    Type Alias IScale

    Compile one feature curve of a sweep into the radius change it makes at a point of the sweep's domain.

    The curve is the centripetal Catmull-Rom spline through its points in the (u, angle) domain, each angle unwrapped so consecutive points take the shorter way round and a point repeating its predecessor dropped, continued straight beyond its ends through reflected phantom points. Its knots are spaced by the square root of each chord measured in metres, u by the spine's length and angle by the sweep's mean radius, so each segment has no cusp or self-intersection however unevenly its points are spaced (Yuksel, Schaefer and Keyser 2011, Computer-Aided Design 43(7):747-755), and it is C1 in that knot parameter. Its radius or offset follows the same parameter by interpolateAutoMovieSpline, so it too runs smoothly along the curve. At a query point a nearest candidate on the curve is searched in a local cylindrical metric: u scaled by the spine's length and angle by the query's radius before features. This approximates surface distance; it is not the arc metric of a noncircular section. The search seeds sixteen times the number of segments uniformly over the knot span and refines the nearest seed by four Newton steps. It keeps the seed if refinement moves further away; this local search does not guarantee the global nearest point. Its smooth course removes polyline corners, though a nearest-branch change inside a bend tighter than the band can still crease the field. The radius moves by the residual there, the declared radius less the surface's radius at the curve or the declared offset, times (1 - (d / width)^2)^3 within width, a falloff with continuous curvature at the band's edge.
