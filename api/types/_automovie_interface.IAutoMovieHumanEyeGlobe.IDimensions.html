    Type Alias IDimensions

    Biometric means in millimetres, with their basis.

    • radial: half the mean of the transverse 24.16/24.32 and vertical 23.80/23.75 globe diameters (Bekerman 2014, Table 1, CT, n = 250); measured.
    • axialLength: corneal apex to posterior pole, 23.48 (Chen 2016, IOLMaster, n = 1015); measured.
    • cornealRadius: 337.5 / 44.20 D = 7.64 (Chen 2016, keratometric index 1.3375); derived from measured keratometry.
    • limbusRadius: half the horizontal visible iris diameter, 11.7 mm (white-to-white, Rüfer et al. 2005, J Cataract Refract Surg 31(1):107-112: 11.71 +/- 0.42); measured, relayed.
    • anteriorChamberDepth: corneal apex to the lens face on which the pupillary iris rests, 3.03 (Chen 2016); measured.
    • pupilRadius: 2.0, a 4 mm pupil, usual in indoor light between the photopic 3 mm and the dark-adapted 7 mm; conventional, since no lighting or expression input sets it.
    • irisRecession: 0.5, how far the iris root lies behind its pupillary margin (the iris is a shallow cone over the lens); conventional.
