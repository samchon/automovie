    Type Alias OnBonesLegs

    OnBonesLegs: readonly { direction: AutoMovieVector3Tuple; length: number }[]

    The legs argument of AutoMovieHumanHandDigitBones.onBones. A digit's line, sections, tip and first section on its legs. A finger's sections follow the phalanx span law from its own root section (the form's, scaled by the hand's across and through scales), the thumb's are the form's read at the same share of the way from the root to the tip; both carry the joint swellings, creases, pulp and nail of AutoMovieHumanDigitProfile, each drawn as the four-guide ring (AutoMovieHumanDigitRing); the line turns at each joint over the elbow's rule, half a length of softness times the joint's radius; the tip closes where its radius equals the length left to the apex (AutoMovieHumanDigitTipCap).

    Param: rootAt

    Length along the line of the digit's root, where the form's sections start; 0 for the thumb, whose line starts at its root.

    Param: joints

    Lengths of its joints along the line, proximal first. * * *
