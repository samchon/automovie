    A single action verb an actor performs: the thin unit the model emits and the engine expands into dense motion. The model says what ("jab", "walk to the door", "look at her", "get knocked back"), and the engine's primitives (locomotion bakers, two-bone IK, aim, spring, projectile, impact) synthesize the per-frame IAutoMovieMotion.

    Discriminated on verb. Every action carries an actor and a placement on the shot's local timeline. compilePerformance composes an actor's actions into one performance clip, layering disjoint body regions and arranging the rest with arrangeMotion, which holds the last pose across gaps. The camera is an actor too; its IAutoMovieCameraActions are how it moves.

    The engine's reference synthesizer animates locomote, hold, lookAt, emote, gesture, reach, and react; attachTo, launch, and frame are compiled by their own passes, and enact is resolved by the host from its clip table. A verb the injected synthesizer answers with null is skipped without a violation.
