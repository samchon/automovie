    plate

    plate: AutoMovieVector3Tuple[]

    The ear's plate's course, along the auricle's long axis from below the lobule to above the helix's top, through the middle of the plate's lens at each station: the lens is the auricle's cranial skin and the floor of its hollows, read off the reference's sections square to the course (FamilyConicBody.ts plate --kernels, which also gives each lens's centroid in its chart). Laid through those centroids where it can be, with the turn at 1,566 mm 2 mm farther back than they stand, since there the lens is 4 mm thin and the course fell on its floor, and its last leg aimed 5 mm behind the top lenses' centroids: on the leg through them (to [13, 1600, 80.5]) the planes leaned 30 degrees forward and three of them cut the helix's front limb with the plate, a lens three times as long. The last centre stands under the helix's top (its course 88.6 mm out at 1,599.3 mm), so the plate's top cap closes into the tube: ended at [8, 1602, 80.5] the cap closed 8 mm inside the tube's line and its square end showed under the helix's arch (ear.left). Every section holds the course.
