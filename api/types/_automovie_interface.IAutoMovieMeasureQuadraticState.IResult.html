    Type Alias IResult

    Measure homogeneous equations and original-coordinate termination quantities. Residual signs are rx=-Px-A'z-qtau and rz=Ax+s-btau. Costs divide by tau and undo objective scaling; infeasibility quantities remain homogeneous. Stable Euclidean norms retain the preceding solver's residual definitions. Product/addition corrections remain through sparse residuals and costs; the gap is formed before the two reported costs round to individual numbers.
