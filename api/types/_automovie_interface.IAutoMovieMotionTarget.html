    What a directed action aims at: another person's anatomical region, a region of the performer's own body, a named point on a prop, a free point, or nothing at all.

    • person: a region of another subject's body; the action commits to its position at its commit event and tracks it afterwards.
    • self: a region of the performer's own body (a hand to the chin, to the hip, to the chest), read on the posture the performer is in.
    • prop: a named point of a prop (a bag's centre, a pad's face, a handle), as the prop's profile declares it.
    • point: a fixed point in the world or in the performer's own frame (+Z forward, +X left, +Y up, origin on the floor under the root), in metres, or in that frame in statures (stature), the performer's own measure, so one point fits every body. Its normal says which way the surface at the point faces (a table top faces up, a wall faces the room); without one the point faces back at the performer.
    • none: shadow work and drills in the air; the aim point is that of a virtual opponent of the performer's own measure at range metres (default: the technique's own range), at the line height (default middle), so contact checks become extension checks.
