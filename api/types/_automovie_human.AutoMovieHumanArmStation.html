    The landmark stations at which the arm's shape rule is read, from the shoulder to the wrist, by name:

    • u0.3, u0.45, u0.6, u0.75: shares of the humerus' joint-centre length from the glenohumeral centre (the deltoid and the upper arm's compartments; u0.45 stands for the deltoid insertion);
    • ridge: the supracondylar ridge above the elbow;
    • elbow: the elbow centre, in the plane bisecting the corner;
    • f0.1, f0.22, f0.4, f0.6, f0.8, f0.95: shares of the elbow centre to stylion along the forearm (the elbow's distal edge, the belly, the junction and the wrist).

    Every column of the shape rule (a guide line or a sector) keeps one value set for each of these stations.
