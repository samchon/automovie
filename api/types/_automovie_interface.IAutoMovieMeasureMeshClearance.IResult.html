    Type Alias IResult

    IResult: { minimum: number; triangle: number }[]

    Front-minus-back depth measurements along one axis over the complete projected overlap of two triangle meshes.

    Each entry belongs to one front triangle, by ordinal, and gives its smallest signed depth separation from any back triangle in mesh-local metres: positive is clear, zero is contact, negative breaks the declared front-back ordering. A front triangle with no projected overlap is not reported. The projection plane is Y/Z for depth X, Z/X for depth Y and X/Y for depth Z. Triangles parallel to the depth axis are skipped, so this is directional surface ordering, not a closed-volume collision test. Both meshes must already share a frame; callers own transforms and which pairs may touch. Neither input is changed.

    The optional visit receives every overlap polygon vertex as a witness, clear ones included, so a later coupled displacement can keep every one of those inequalities and not only the violated ones. Throws on an invalid axis, a malformed index buffer, a non-finite position, and projected area, clipping or depth arithmetic that overflows.

    The list and entries are newly allocated and mutable. The optional measurement visitor runs synchronously for every clipped overlap vertex, may retain its fresh witness arrays, and is not retained after return; a visitor exception propagates and prevents return of the list.

    Type Declaration

    • minimum: number

      Minimum front-minus-back axial depth over all projected overlaps for this triangle, metres; positive is clear, zero contact, negative reversed ordering.

    • triangle: number

      Zero-based ordinal of the original front triangle, in ascending order; absent ordinals have no nondegenerate projected overlap.
