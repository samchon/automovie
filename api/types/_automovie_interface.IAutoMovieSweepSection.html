    Type Alias IAutoMovieSweepSection

    IAutoMovieSweepSection: ISuperellipse | IBSpline | IBezier | IPolygon | IRadial

    One closed cross-section curve of a sweep, in the plane of its station.

    Coordinates are metres in the station's section plane: x along the plane's first axis and y along its second, both measured from the spine point, which is the section's origin. Under the sweep's default direction correspondence a section is read as its radius at every polar angle about that origin, so it must be star-shaped about it: every ray from the origin crosses the curve exactly once; angles grow counterclockwise from +x toward +y, and a curve must run that way. Under parameter correspondence a section is read by its own parameter instead, so it need only be simple.

    The kinds cover the forms a section family needs: an analytic superellipse with a half-axis and an exponent per quadrant for rounded or boxy, symmetric or egg-shaped sections; a periodic cubic B-spline whose control polygon a junction can share; a closed piecewise cubic Bezier for authored outlines with tangents; a polygon for sections with corners; and radii at even angles for sections composed from additive terms. A new kind joins the union with its own evaluation and leaves the others unchanged.
