    Type Alias SquareMetric

    SquareMetric: readonly [number, number, number]

    The metric argument of AutoMovieHumanSeam.square. A chart direction leaving a curve on a surface square to it on the skin (J4 in the surface's chart): w = D1 + beta c' with beta = -<D1, c'> / <c', c'> in the first fundamental form [E, F, G] of the chart's two coordinates, so w is D1 with its part along the curve removed in the metric of the skin, not of the chart. A chart whose coordinates differ in scale (phase in radians against station in mm) turns a direction square in the chart far off square on the skin; near where the curve runs along one coordinate the turned direction then swings through a right angle within a tiny step. Here alpha = 1 keeps w on D1's side of the curve.

    Param: metric

    [E, F, G]: dot products of the surface's derivatives along the first and second chart coordinates.

    Param: cross

    D1, a chart direction across the curve on the side to leave toward.

    Param: along

    c', the curve's chart tangent.

    Returns

    w in the same chart coordinates. * * *
