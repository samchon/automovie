    Compile a bridge patch's interior for per-pose evaluation, returning the writer that fills its rows into a target buffer.

    Column k joins loop point k of the first loop to loop point k of the second by the cubic Hermite curve h00 A + h10 m T_A + h01 B + h11 m T_B, m the tension times the chord, row j at t = j / rows. A loop point's direction follows its loop's rule: at a sweep point the sweep's outward normal for normal, its opposite for inward, and its unit derivative along the spine, forward or backward, for forward and backward; at a curve point the curve's declared direction. Under across the direction is the normal, the sweep's or the curve's blended one, crossed with the loop's chord between the points one first-row length, m / rows, before and after it along the loop, at least reaching both neighbours. That chord is the loop's tangent at the scale of the first row, so near a corner of the loop the columns turn over the points within one row of it rather than at the corner alone. This local construction does not certify that columns never cross or that the resulting patch is free of self-intersection. Nondegenerate loop lengths and direction vectors are caller premises; this kernel can write nonfinite controls for a degenerate tangent, which the owning network rejects after evaluating all writers. No rollback of earlier interior writes is provided.
