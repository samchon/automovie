    Type Alias IPrepareOptions

    The options argument of AutoMovieHumanCanonicalBody.prepare. Prepare a Human's polygon man on the canonical rig from its parameters, at the skeleton level: no skin, loft or head surface is built. The bind joints come from Human's own source preparation and skeleton law (AutoMovieHumanBodyLaw.prepareSources, its head source and AutoMovieHumanSkeletonLaw.complete), about 2 s a body. Each joint's girth radius is perimeter / 2π of the person's own resolved circumference (AutoMovieHumanBodyAnatomy.resolve): hip, waist, chest, neck and head for the trunk and head, the arm at the axilla, the forearm, the hand, the thigh, the calf, the ankle and the ball of the foot (0.8 of it at the midfoot, as Human's rig has it); the jaw is 0.12 of the head radius as Human's rig has it, the eye a 12 mm globe (Bekerman et al. 2014), and a digit the stature girth Human uses for an unlofted digit (finger and lesser toe 0.03, hallux 0.04 of stature). The joints then move onto the canonical rig: reflected from Human's registered frame, each arm straightened along ±X by shortest arcs about the shoulder, the elbow and the wrist and pronated until the palm faces -Y, each finger straightened in the palm's plane along its own knuckle line and the thumb's two hinges along its metacarpal (the bind holds a relaxed hand, and a finger angle of zero is the extended finger, as in the VRM T-pose and the Neutral-0 position), each thigh and shank straightened along -Y with the foot keeping its bind orientation, and the whole body raised or lowered so its lowest foot joint sphere touches the floor. Every humanoid-named joint becomes a rig bone with an identity rest rotation, the toes bone at the hallux's metatarsophalangeal joint, and every other joint rides its nearest humanoid ancestor. The contact profile is built from the same joints and radii (AutoMovieHumanCanonicalProfile), its colliders the convex hulls of the drawn ring points, so contacts meet what is drawn.
