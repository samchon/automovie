    Type Alias ILandmarks

    The L argument of AutoMovieHumanF1Frames.segments. Every segment parent-first, in the reference pose, by its rule.

    Param: J

    The joint table the rules read; absent, f1's own at the reference pose.

    Param: L

    The landmark table the rules read; absent, f1's own at the reference pose. * * *

    epicondyleLateral

    epicondyleLateral: AutoMovieVector3Tuple

    EL, the lateral humeral epicondyle (Wu et al. 2005, 2.3.4): on the skin's section through the elbow centre (lowerarm_r) square to the humerus's long axis (upperarm_r to lowerarm_r), the extreme along the elbow's flexion axis, the normal of the plane the humerus and the forearm span (the reference's elbow stands flexed 37 degrees, so that plane is defined), on the lateral side.

    suprasternale

    suprasternale: AutoMovieVector3Tuple

    IJ, suprasternale, the deepest point of the jugular notch (Wu et al. 2005, 2.3.1): on the median section (z = 0), the front profile's most posterior point between 1,330 and 1,430 mm high, the pit where the neck's front meets the manubrium, 3.2 mm deeper than the profile at the nearer window end. Its height is 0.833 of stature (the skin's highest point, 1,699.7 mm), against ANSUR II women's 0.8164 +- 0.0071 (+2.3 SD).

    tragion

    tragion: AutoMovieVector3Tuple

    Tragion, read at the tragus's crest: on the head's level section through the concha's deepest point (1,563 mm, AutoMovieHumanF1Joints.conchaRoot), the outline point farthest out (+Z) from 0 to 20 mm in front of that point. Farkas's tragion is the notch at the tragus's upper margin, a few millimetres above this crest; the head frame reads it only for its origin, the bitragion midpoint.
