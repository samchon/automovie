    Type Alias IRule

    Lid rule constants in mm.

    • band: width, measured across the margin in projection, of the skin the lid band replaces outside the contact line. The lofted skin there reproduces the reference's inward roll into the conjunctival sac only coarsely, so the band rebuilds the last millimetres of the lid by rule.
    • upper, lower: depth of the lid margin along the line of sight, from the contact line to the anterior edge, for each lid. The margin is about 2 mm wide (anterior lamella with the lashes to the posterior edge on the eye, Gray 1918) and inclined to the line of sight; the reference's anterior lid edge lies 0.6 to 1.1 mm in front of its contact line.
    • offset: how far the anterior edge lies outward of the posterior edge in projection.
    • taper: arc length from a canthus over which the margin depth grows from merge of its full value; the lids thin where they meet.
    • gap: clearance of the posterior edge off the globe surface.
    • clearance: least lid thickness over the globe across the band.
