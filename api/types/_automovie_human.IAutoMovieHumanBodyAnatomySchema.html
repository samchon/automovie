    Type Declaration

    • OptionalacromialHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<600> & tags.Maximum<1900>

      Acromial height: the right acromion above the standing surface (ISO 7250-1 6.1.4 shoulder height; ANSUR II acromial height). ANSUR II: 1335.1 (58.1) and 1440.7 (63.3).

    • OptionalacrossBackShoulderWidth?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Across back shoulder width: shoulder point to shoulder point over the back neck point (ISO 8559-1 5.4.3). No survey takes it; the references read 395.6 to 451.9.

    • OptionalankleCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<120> & tags.Maximum<400>

      Ankle circumference: the minimum horizontal girth of the leg above the malleoli (ANSUR II ankle circumference). ANSUR II: 215.7 (14.9) and 229.3 (14.6).

    • OptionalaxillaHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<550> & tags.Maximum<1800>

      Axilla height: the lowest point of the armpit's skin above the standing surface, arm hanging (ANSUR II axilla height). ANSUR II: 1239.0 (55.8) and 1329.1 (59.5).

    • OptionalaxillaryArmCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<900>

      Axillary arm circumference, mm: the circumference of the upper arm perpendicular to its long axis at the level of the axilla, a taut tape (ANSUR 1988 ARM CIRC-AXILLARY, Gordon et al. 1989 NATICK/TR-89/044). ANSUR 1988: women 291.6 (24.4), men 335.0 (27.1). Absent, it resolves to the upper arm circumference, which keeps the arm's documented law (the upper arm leaves its shoulder port at the mid-arm girth, flat to the mid-arm site).

    • OptionalbackNeckToWaist?: number & tags.Minimum<100> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Back neck point to waist: over the back's median skin from cervicale to the natural waist (ISO 8559-1 5.4.5; ANSUR 1988 natural waist back length). ANSUR 1988: 367.3 (25.0) and 412.0 (23.2).

    • OptionalballOfFootCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<120> & tags.Maximum<400>

      Ball-of-foot circumference: the girth of the foot over the first and fifth metatarsal heads, standing (ANSUR II ball of foot circumference). It wraps the forefoot's section at the metatarsal heads, so it fixes the forefoot's fullness with the foot breadth there. ANSUR II: 228.1 (11.8) and 252.0 (12.9).

    • OptionalballOfFootLength?: number & tags.Minimum<100> & tags.Maximum<300>

      Ball-of-foot length: the back of the heel to the head of the first metatarsal, along the foot's axis, standing (ANSUR II ball of foot length). It places the metatarsal heads. ANSUR II: 182.1 (9.6) and 200.9 (10.5).

    • OptionalbiacromialBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<200> & tags.Maximum<550>

      Biacromial breadth: acromion to acromion, the lateral edges of the acromial processes, a bony breadth by caliper (ISO 7250-1 6.2.7; ANSUR II biacromial breadth). It fixes the shoulder tip, the point the ISO 8559-1 5.4.3 across back shoulder width projects to the skin. ANSUR 1988: 362.6 (17.4) and 397.0 (18.0).

    • OptionalbicristalBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<50> & tags.Maximum<500>

      Bicristal breadth: between the highest palpable points of the two iliac crests, tissue firmly compressed, a bony breadth by caliper (ANSUR II bicristal breadth; ANSUR 1988 takes none). It places the iliac crests in the skeleton and is not a knot of the skin's outline. ANSUR II: 273.3 (22.3) and 275.4 (17.5).

    • OptionalbideltoidBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<90> & tags.Maximum<900>

      Bideltoid breadth: the maximum horizontal breadth across the deltoid muscles, upper arms hanging against the sides, a soft-tissue breadth (ISO 7250-1 6.2.8; ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II bideltoid breadth, seated). It is the arms' lateral outline at the deltoids, the shoulder width the front view shows; bideltoid over hip breadth is 1.28 in ANSUR II women and 1.48 in men, the outline's V-taper. ANSUR 1988: 432.6 (22.6) and 491.8 (25.9); ANSUR II: 450.3 (28.7) and 510.4 (32.5).

    • OptionalbimalleolarBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<40> & tags.Maximum<110>

      Bimalleolar breadth: between the most medial point of the medial malleolus and the most lateral point of the lateral malleolus, standing (ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II bimalleolar breadth). ANSUR 1988: 64.4 (3.1) and 72.8 (3.9).

    • OptionalbodyFatPercentage?: number & tags.Minimum<3> & tags.Maximum<70>

      Whole-body fat mass over body mass, percent, by dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (Kelly et al. 2009, PLoS ONE 4(9):e7038). Omitted, it is the US Navy circumference estimate from the person's neck, waist, hip and stature (Hodgdon and Beckett 1984, NHRC report 84-11 and 84-29).

    • OptionalbustPointWidth?: number & tags.Minimum<50> & tags.Maximum<450>

      Bust point width: the straight breadth between the bust points (ISO 8559-1 5.2.3; ANSUR 1988 bustpoint-to-bustpoint breadth). ANSUR 1988: 184.8 (16.2) and 215.9 (18.9).

    • OptionalbuttockDepth?: number & tags.Minimum<30> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Buttock depth: horizontal, from the abdomen to the buttocks' maximum posterior protrusion, at the buttock circumference's level, standing (ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II buttock depth). It is the side outline's knot at that level. ANSUR 1988: 227.1 (21.0) and 248.6 (20.7); ANSUR II: 232.9 (24.3) and 245.8 (26.5).

    • OptionalcalfCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<90> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Calf girth: horizontal at the widest part of the calf (ISO 8559-1 5.3.24; ANSUR II). ANSUR II: 373.3 (28.5) and 392.3 (29.7).

    • OptionalcalfHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<700>

      Calf height: the level of the maximum horizontal circumference of the calf above the standing surface (ANSUR 1988 (29)); the calf girth is taken there. Absent, the leg places it by its own fibular relation.

    • OptionalcervicaleHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<650> & tags.Maximum<2000>

      Cervicale height: the tip of the seventh cervical vertebra's spinous process above the standing surface (ANSUR II cervicale height). With stature and head height it fixes the neck's span. ANSUR II: 1395.7 (59.5) and 1517.3 (63.3).

    • OptionalchestBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<500>

      Chest breadth: across the rib cage under the arms at the chest circumference's level, by beam caliper at the maximum point of quiet respiration (ANSUR 1988 chest breadth), the front outline's knot there. ANSUR 1988: 279.6 (19.7) and 321.5 (25.5). ANSUR II takes its chest breadth at maximum inspiration with tissue compressed, a different measure: 577 of its 4,082 men have a chest girth above the box of that breadth and their chest depth, against none in ANSUR 1988 (test_human_survey_box_consistency).

    • OptionalchestCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<180> & tags.Maximum<2000>

      Bust (chest) girth: horizontal at the bust point, the most anterior point of the bust or chest (ISO 8559-1 5.3.4). ANSUR 1988: 907.1 (63.5) and 991.4 (69.1).

    • OptionalchestDepth?: number & tags.Minimum<30> & tags.Maximum<700>

      Chest depth: horizontal, from the most anterior point of the chest, the bust in women, to the back, at the chest circumference's level, standing (ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II chest depth). With the chest breadth it is the chest's box, the side outline's knot at that level. ANSUR 1988: 239.4 (21.1) and 243.2 (21.5); ANSUR II: 247.4 and 253.8 (means).

    • OptionalcrotchLength?: number & tags.Minimum<250> & tags.Maximum<1400>

      Crotch length: from the navel's level at the front midline, down over the skin through the crotch and up the back midline to the same level (ANSUR II crotch length, omphalion). With its posterior part it places the perineum along the trunk's median section. ANSUR II: 620.8 (42.4) and 622.3 (46.7).

    • OptionalelbowBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<35> & tags.Maximum<110>

      Humerus biepicondylar breadth: between the medial and lateral epicondyles of the humerus, elbow flexed 90 degrees, caliper with firm pressure (ISAK humerus breadth; NHANES III elbow breadth). It is the bone the elbow's girth wraps, so the epicondyles' relief is its half less the tube's radius. Heinz 2003: 61.9 (4.2) and 72.3 (4.4).

    • OptionalelbowCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<600>

      Elbow girth over the olecranon, arm extended (ANSUR 1988 elbow circumference). ANSUR 1988: 238.3 (13.4) and 277.1 (15.3).

    • OptionalforearmBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<20> & tags.Maximum<200>

      Forearm skin breadth, mm: a caliper's light-contact reading across the forearm along the epicondyle line, at Perry et al. 2021's station 0.2 radiale-stylion distal of radiale, arm hanging, palm forward. Absent, it resolves to Perry's supination relation to the forearm girth (the fitted ellipse's major semi-axis 0.1749 of the girth, doubled).

    • OptionalforearmCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<70> & tags.Maximum<600>

      Relaxed maximum forearm girth below the elbow (Heinz et al. 2003 forearm girth, extended, palm up). Heinz 2003: 237.6 (16.8) and 282.4 (17.8).

    • OptionalforearmDepth?: number & tags.Minimum<15> & tags.Maximum<200>

      Forearm skin depth, mm: the same caliper across the epicondyle line at the same station. Absent, Perry's minor semi-axis 0.1365 of the forearm girth, doubled.

    • OptionalforearmLength?: number & tags.Minimum<40> & tags.Maximum<500>

      Radiale-stylion length (ANSUR II). ANSUR II: 241.3 (15.2) and 267.9 (15.4).

    • OptionalglutealFurrowHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<350> & tags.Maximum<1200>

      Gluteal furrow height: the lowest point of the gluteal fold above the standing surface (ANSUR 1988 gluteal furrow height). The ISO 8559-1 5.3.20 thigh girth is sought just below this level. ANSUR 1988: 744.3 (43.0) and 814.4 (45.6).

    • OptionalhandCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<60> & tags.Maximum<400>

      Hand circumference over metacarpals II to V, thumb excluded (ANSUR 1988 hand circumference at metacarpale). ANSUR 1988: 186.2 (8.5) and 213.8 (9.7).

    • OptionalheelAnkleCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<180> & tags.Maximum<500>

      Heel-ankle circumference: the girth round the back of the heel and over the front of the ankle at the instep's crease, standing (ANSUR II heel ankle circumference); the foot's diagonal girth, which bounds the dorsum's height at the ankle's front. ANSUR II: 310.3 (15.3) and 343.5 (16.6).

    • OptionalheelBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<35> & tags.Maximum<120>

      Heel breadth: the maximum breadth of the heel behind the malleoli, standing (ANSUR 1988 heel breadth). ANSUR 1988: 63.0 (4.8) and 70.1 (5.3).

    • OptionalhighHipCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<200> & tags.Maximum<2200>

      Top hip girth: horizontal midway between the waist and hip levels (ISO 8559-1 5.3.12). No survey takes it; omitted, it is the mean of the waist and hip girths.

    • OptionalhipBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Hip breadth: the maximum horizontal breadth across the hips, between the most lateral points of the buttocks, standing, soft tissue included (ISO 7250-1 6.1.12 hip breadth, standing; ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II hip breadth). It is the front outline's knot at its own widest level. ANSUR 1988: 342.7 (22.4) and 341.8 (20.3); ANSUR II: 353.8 (26.7) and 345.7 (24.2).

    • OptionalhipCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<200> & tags.Maximum<2200>

      Hip girth: horizontal at the buttocks' greatest posterior projection (ISO 8559-1 5.3.13; ANSUR 1988 buttock circumference). ANSUR 1988: 966.9 (60.2) and 983.7 (62.2).

    • Optionalinseam?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<1300>

      Inside leg length: crotch height less the outer ankle point's height (ISO 8559-1 5.7.6). ANSUR 1988 crotch height less lateral malleolus height: 710.8 and 770.1 (means).

    • OptionalinstepLength?: number & tags.Minimum<90> & tags.Maximum<300>

      Instep length: the back of the heel to the instep's highest point, along the foot's axis, standing (ANSUR 1988 instep length). It places the dorsum's crest. ANSUR 1988: 179.4 (9.6) and 196.0 (10.5).

    • OptionalkneeBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<55> & tags.Maximum<150>

      Femur biepicondylar breadth: between the medial and lateral epicondyles of the femur, knee flexed 90 degrees, caliper with firm pressure (ISAK femur breadth). Heinz 2003: 90.5 (6.0) and 97.8 (5.4).

    • OptionalkneeCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<900>

      Knee girth: horizontal at mid-patella (ISO 8559-1 5.3.22; ANSUR 1988 knee circumference). ANSUR 1988: 365.2 (23.3) and 386.4 (22.1).

    • OptionalkyphoticAngle?: number & tags.Minimum<10> & tags.Maximum<100>

      Thoracic kyphotic angle, degrees: the angle between the back surface's sagittal tangents at the cervicothoracic (ICT) and thoracolumbar (ITL) inflection points, standing relaxed (DIERS formetric surface topography). Reference Values for 3D Spinal Posture Based on Videorasterstereographic Analyses of Healthy Adults, Bioengineering 2022 (PMC9774435), Table 4, 201 adults aged 18 to 70: 50.1 (8.2) and 49.6 (8.6).

    • OptionallordoticAngle?: number & tags.Minimum<5> & tags.Maximum<90>

      Lumbar lordotic angle, degrees: the angle between the back surface's sagittal tangents at the thoracolumbar (ITL) and lumbosacral (ILS) inflection points, standing relaxed (DIERS formetric surface topography). Same source as the kyphotic angle: 44.0 (8.5) and 34.9 (7.3).

    • OptionallowerThighCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<900>

      Lower thigh circumference: at suprapatella, the upper border of the patella, perpendicular to the thigh's axis, standing (ANSUR II lower thigh circumference). It is the thigh's tape just above the knee. ANSUR II: 400.7 (35.2) and 409.3 (31.9).

    • OptionalmidpatellaHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<200> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Mid-patella height: the centre of the kneecap above the standing surface, the level of the knee girth (ISO 8559-1 5.3.22; ANSUR II midpatella height). Absent, the leg takes ANSUR II's relation on stature (0.276 S women, 0.278 S men).

    • OptionalneckBaseCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Neck base girth: over the back neck point, the side neck points and the front neck point (ISO 8559-1 5.3.3; ANSUR 1988). ANSUR 1988: 346.2 (16.3) and 408.4 (20.5).

    • OptionalneckCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<100> & tags.Maximum<700>

      Neck girth: just below the thyroid cartilage, perpendicular to the neck's axis (ISO 8559-1 5.3.2; ANSUR II). ANSUR II: 329.8 (19.2) and 397.6 (25.8).

    • OptionalposteriorCrotchLength?: number & tags.Minimum<120> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Posterior crotch length: the crotch length's part from the crotch point up the back midline to the navel's level (ANSUR II crotch length, posterior, omphalion); less than the crotch length. ANSUR II: 341.9 (28.6) and 355.9 (29.5), 0.551 (SD 0.033) and 0.573 (0.037) of the crotch length.

    • OptionalsideNeckPointHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<650> & tags.Maximum<2000>

      Side neck point height: the side neck point, where the neck base meets the shoulder's top line, above the standing surface (ANSUR 1988 lateral neck height; the ISO 8559-1 side neck point). It stands 62.9 (11.1) mm above the acromion in women and 67.0 (11.7) in men. ANSUR 1988: 1396.5 (58.4) and 1509.5 (61.6).

    • OptionalsideNeckToBustPoint?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<600>

      Side neck point to bust point over the skin (ISO 8559-1 5.4.10; ANSUR 1988 neck to bust point length). ANSUR 1988: 268.0 (22.3) and 272.4 (18.1).

    • Optionalstature?: number & tags.Minimum<400> & tags.Maximum<2500>

      Stature: the vertex above the standing surface, standing erect (ISO 7250-1 6.1.2; ANSUR II). ANSUR II: 1628.5 (64.2) and 1756.2 (68.6).

    • OptionalsuprasternaleHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<600> & tags.Maximum<1900>

      Suprasternale height: the jugular notch, ISO 8559-1's front neck point, above the standing surface (ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II suprasternale height). ANSUR 1988: 1330.3 (56.0) and 1438.4 (59.3).

    • OptionalthighCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<120> & tags.Maximum<1300>

      Thigh girth: horizontal at the widest part of the thigh below the gluteal fold (ISO 8559-1 5.3.20; ANSUR 1988 proximal thigh circumference, just below the gluteal furrow). ANSUR 1988: 580.3 (45.1) and 596.5 (49.3).

    • OptionalthumbBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<10> & tags.Maximum<40>

      Thumb breadth: the maximum breadth of the thumb at its interphalangeal joint (ISO 7250-1 6.3.16; ANSUR 1988 thumb breadth). ANSUR 1988: 20.7 (1.3) and 24.1 (1.4).

    • OptionalunderbustCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<2000>

      Under bust girth: horizontal at the inframammary fold (ISO 8559-1 5.3.8; ANSUR 1988 chest circumference below the bust). ANSUR 1988: 769.7 (51.0) and 926.1 (65.5).

    • OptionalupperArmCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<80> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Relaxed upper arm girth midway between acromion and olecranon, arm hanging (NHANES mid-upper arm circumference). NHANES 2015-2018, 20 to 29 years: 315 and 341 (means).

    • OptionalupperArmLength?: number & tags.Minimum<50> & tags.Maximum<600>

      Acromion-radiale length, arm hanging (ANSUR II). ANSUR II: 311.2 (17.2) and 335.2 (17.5).

    • OptionalwaistBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<40> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Waist breadth: the torso's horizontal breadth at the natural waist, the waist circumference's level, standing, tissue uncompressed; the front outline's knot there. No survey takes it at this level: ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II take the waist breadth at the navel (ANSUR II: 299.9 and 326.4, means), so the default is a derivation: the navel section's breadth-on-girth law (ANSUR 1988) at the natural waist girth, 268.0 (20.6) and 302.1 (23.6) at the surveyed natural girths.

    • OptionalwaistCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<180> & tags.Maximum<2200>

      Natural waist girth: horizontal at the torso's least girth between the lowest rib and the iliac crest (ANSUR 1988 natural waist circumference). ANSUR 1988: 725.5 (63.0) and 839.9 (74.0).

    • OptionalwaistDepth?: number & tags.Minimum<30> & tags.Maximum<800>

      Waist depth: horizontal, abdomen to back, at the natural waist, the waist circumference's level, standing; the side outline's knot there. No survey takes it at this level (ANSUR II at the navel: 213.0 and 237.8, means), so the default is the navel section's depth-on-girth law (ANSUR 1988) at the natural waist girth, 185.6 (17.5) and 220.0 (20.5) at the surveyed natural girths.

    • OptionalwaistHeight?: number & tags.Minimum<150> & tags.Maximum<1600>

      Natural waist height: the level of the waist circumference above the standing surface (ANSUR 1988 waist height, natural indentation: the torso's greatest lateral indentation, or midway between the tenth rib and iliocristale where none is clear). The waist breadth and depth are taken at this level. ANSUR 1988: 1056.5 (51.7) and 1127.1 (52.0), at or just above the tenth rib (1044 and 1121) and 74 and 68 mm above the navel.

    • OptionalwristBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<30> & tags.Maximum<90>

      Bistyloid breadth: between the radial and ulnar styloid processes, by caliper with firm pressure (ISAK wrist breadth; NHANES III wrist breadth). Heinz 2003: 49.4 (3.3) and 56.3 (3.2).

    • OptionalwristCircumference?: number & tags.Minimum<50> & tags.Maximum<300>

      Wrist girth over stylion (ANSUR II wrist circumference). ANSUR II: 154.8 (7.8) and 175.9 (9.0).

    • OptionalwristDepth?: number & tags.Minimum<15> & tags.Maximum<120>

      Wrist skin depth over stylion, mm: the same caliper across the styloid line. Absent, the same ellipse's depth.

    • OptionalwristSkinBreadth?: number & tags.Minimum<20> & tags.Maximum<120>

      Wrist skin breadth over stylion, mm: a caliper's light-contact reading along the styloid line (the skin's, not the bony bistyloid wristBreadth). Absent, the ellipse of the wrist girth at Perry's circularity 0.674, its breadth.
