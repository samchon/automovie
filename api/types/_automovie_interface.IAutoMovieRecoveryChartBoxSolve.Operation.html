    Minimize sum_axis(0.5x' H x - rhs' x) on the supplied original control box. H is an exactly symmetric sparse positive-semidefinite Gram matrix supplied by the caller. This helper validates finite entries, distinct sparse addresses, reciprocal symmetry and nonnegative diagonals; it does not certify PSD or rank. An exactly zero diagonal requires an exactly zero row and three zero RHS entries. Such an unseen coordinate uses scale one and retains its feasible initial value. Dii=1/sqrt(Hii), x=Dy, Hs=DHD and bs=Db give the same real-arithmetic objective and box after scaling. FISTA projects in y, with objective-based momentum restarts. Numeric scaling is binary64; failed finite/feasible transformations throw rather than adding a regularizer or perturbing H. Active bounds retain original endpoints. Termination uses the original infinity norm of G_L(x)=L(x-proj(x-gradient/L)). An equivalent component formula avoids cancellation of two nearly equal x values. The best finite feasible original objective is returned, with its own gradient metric; exhausting the attempt budget is not convergence. Iterations counts all projected proposals, including rejected momentum attempts. Empty systems return empty. The attempt budget is a positive safe integer and gradient tolerance is finite and nonnegative. Reported gradients/objectives are binary64 evaluations, not exact KKT, uniqueness, eigenvalue or geometric accuracy certificates. Optional initialValues are copied original normalized coordinates, interleaved by three axes per control. They must be finite and feasible without clipping; omission starts at projected zero. initialObjective records their original Q, and every invocation starts with fresh momentum and retains that feasible best.
