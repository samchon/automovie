    SPREADS: Readonly<
        Record<
            | "place"
            | "strike"
            | "direction"
            | "amount"
            | "duration"
            | "pause"
            | "swivel"
            | "bend"
            | "curvature",
            readonly [novice: number, elite: number],
        >,
    > = ...

    The spread of each slot type at variation 1, for a novice and for an elite:

    • place: where a performer means to put a hand within what the name allows, arm lengths (provisional, checked by the visibility check of test_motion_compose_variation_place);
    • strike: where a performer means to land within a region, a share of the region's radius (provisional, checked by test_motion_compose_variation_strike);
    • direction: degrees about the named direction (provisional);
    • amount: a factor on a level's share (provisional);
    • duration: seconds, the motor term of HumanSkill.jitter (Wing and Kristofferson 1973);
    • pause: a factor on the pause, the coefficient of variation of HumanSkill.jitter (Grondin and Killeen 2009);
    • swivel: degrees about the shoulder-to-wrist line (provisional);
    • bend: a share of the upper arm added to a preset's outward share (provisional);
    • curvature: a share of the path's length (provisional).

    A slot the action's goal depends on narrows by about 3 from novice to elite, the ratio of HumanSkill.scatter and of dart throwers' error variability (65.0 mm to 21.3 mm; Nasu, Matsuo and Kadota 2014, PLoS ONE 9:e88536). A slot it does not depend on narrows by at most 1.5 (provisional).
