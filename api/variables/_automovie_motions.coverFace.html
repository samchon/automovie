        Pick<IAutoMovieMotionHumanHandToFace.IParams, "seconds" | "manner">,
    > = ...

    Cover the face with both bare hands. The open palms settle on the cheeks with the fingers upward toward the eyes. The head bows into the cover as both hands arrive at atFace. The seconds parameter sets the additional hold after taskEnd before applying the action's manner and time scale.

    Wróbel, Zickfeld and Ciesielski (2025) describes face hiding as part of crying behavior. The bilateral pose, 15 degrees of head flexion and the default 1.2 s additional hold are authored choices.
