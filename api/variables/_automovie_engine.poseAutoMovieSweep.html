    poseAutoMovieSweep: (props: IAutoMoviePoseSweep.IProps) => IAutoMovieSweep = ...

    Re-sweep a sweep in a pose: move its spine and section planes with the bones they ride and rebuild the surface from them, sweep-based deformation after Hyun, Yoon, Chang, Seong, Kim and Juttler (2005, "Sweep-based human deformation", The Visual Computer 21(8-10):542-550).

    Each spine point moves rigidly with its bone, and the spine's starting direction and any declared end tangents turn with the first or last point's bone. Each station's rest plane, its own rotation or the rest sweep's frame at its at, turns with its bone; a station on two bones takes the spherical midpoint of the two turned planes. The posed sweep carries those planes as station rotations, so between stations the turn relative to the posed spine's rotation-minimizing frame is interpolated by normalized linear quaternion interpolation: a twist at one station spreads evenly to the next instead of wringing the surface beside one joint, and sections at a bent joint stay across the tube rather than collapsing into it. Sections, guides, scale and features are kept as they are, so the surface keeps its shape in each section plane.

    With preserveVolume, the sections of stations on two bones are scaled together by one factor until the posed volume, by measureAutoMovieSweepVolume, equals the rest volume: the scale profile gains a knot at every station, the factor at joint stations and 1 at the others, multiplied into any existing profile, and the factor is found by secant steps, as the paper first rescales the key section at the bent joint (Section 4.4), by settleAutoMovieSweepVolume. Without joint stations the sweep is returned unscaled. The scale applies after features, so the section's shape is unchanged and only its size at the joints moves.

          • binding: IAutoMovieSweepBinding

            Bone identities aligned with spine points and stations. poseAutoMovieSweep checks both array lengths and resolves every named bone; each station tuple supplies one bone or a two-bone spherical midpoint.

          • posed: readonly IAutoMovieResolvedBone[]

            Corresponding posed transforms in the same frame. A bound identity missing from either resolved list throws when its motion is requested.

          • OptionalpreserveVolume?: boolean

            Omitted means false. True settles one joint-station scale factor against the authored rest volume; sweeps without two-bone stations skip compensation. Volume measurement or settlement failures propagate.

          • rest: readonly IAutoMovieResolvedBone[]

            Resolved rest transforms, indexed by bone identity, whose worldPosition/worldRotation are in the sweep's model frame. Only read during this call.

          • sweep: IAutoMovieSweep

            Rest sweep in the same model-metre frame as resolved bones. compileAutoMovieSweep validates its section and spine data; the returned sweep shares unchanged section/guide/feature data.

        Returns IAutoMovieSweep

        The posed sweep, a new record sharing the rest sweep's sections.

    Throws

    Error when the binding does not align with the sweep, a bone is missing, or the rest sweep is refused by compileAutoMovieSweep.
