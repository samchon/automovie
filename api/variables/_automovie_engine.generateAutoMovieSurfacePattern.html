    Lay one pattern and measure exactly what was laid.

    With stable input and a pure generator, the fixed sequence is repeatable in the same numerical runtime: zones in declaration order, rows outermost, columns innermost and candidates in generator order. Engine code samples neither clock nor Math.random; variants use seededValue with the pattern seed and an FNV-1a occurrence-id hash. The callback's own purity is a caller premise.

    Every module is clipped to its own zone region, then measured against every exclusion. A module the region rejects entirely is not a zero-area occurrence, it is absent, and so is one an exclusion covers entirely. What survives carries the coverage it kept, why it was cut, and the identity that the mesh, the instance slot, the finish, and the take-off all cite, so a quantity can be traced back to the exact piece it counted. Quantities are reported per zone and in total, and quality is judged by autoMoviePatternFindings.

    Coordinates are the surface's planar (u, v) metres and angles are degrees. A malformed declaration throws an Error naming the field: blank or duplicate ids, non-finite or non-positive sizes, a non-convex region or exclusion, overlapping zones or exclusions, ambiguous surviving occurrence ids, a zone spanning more than AUTOMOVIE_MAX_PATTERN_CELLS lattice cells, non-safe-integer derived lattice bounds, or a generated module reaching beyond its cell's declared reach. Quality defects are not thrown; they are returned as findings (sliver, unsupported-piece, module-overlap, joint-deviation, grain-break).
