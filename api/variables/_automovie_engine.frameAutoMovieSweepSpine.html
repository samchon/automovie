    frameAutoMovieSweepSpine: (spine: IAutoMovieSweepSpine) => IAutoMovieSweepPath = ...

    Compile a spine into its curve, tangent and rotation-minimizing frames.

    The curve is the centripetal Catmull-Rom spline through the points (Yuksel, Schaefer and Keyser 2011, Computer-Aided Design 43(7):747-755), which has no cusp or self-intersection within a segment and follows a bent chain closely; the ends continue straight through phantom points reflected from the second and second-last points. Each segment is evaluated in its equivalent cubic Hermite form, its end derivatives the spline's knot tangents (P_k - P_k-1) / d_k-1 - (P_k+1 - P_k-1) / (d_k-1 + d_k) + (P_k+1 - P_k) / d_k over the centripetal spacings d, so positions and tangents are closed forms with no difference step and scale with the points exactly. A per-coordinate monotone interpolant would stop each coordinate flat at a bent joint and leave a corner there. A declared end tangent replaces the first or last derivative's direction and keeps its length, so the end segment stays a cubic of the same speed that leaves or reaches its point along the declared direction. Within each segment the parameter runs linearly from its start point's chord-length share to its end point's, so stations at those shares sit on the points. Frames are transported by the double reflection method of Wang, Juttler, Zheng and Liu (2008, ACM Trans. Graph. 27(1):2): the first axis starts as spine.normal made perpendicular to the first tangent, and each step reflects it across the bisector plane of the step's chord and then across the plane that maps the reflected tangent onto the next tangent, which approximates the rotation-minimizing frame to fourth order in the step. The table holds 64 steps per spine segment; a query between two entries reflects once more from the entry below it.

    Throws

    Error when the spine has fewer than two points, a point, the normal or an end tangent is not finite or an end tangent is zero, two consecutive points coincide, the path reverses at a point, the normal is parallel to the first tangent, or the tangent vanishes somewhere on the path.
