    settleAutoMovieSweepVolume: (
        target: number,
        volume: (factor: number) => number,
    ) => number = ...

    Find the factor on a posed sweep's joint sections that restores its rest volume, by secant steps.

    The first two trials are 1 and sqrt(target / V(1)), the factor that would restore the volume if it scaled as an area; then up to eight secant steps, each factor held at 0.1 or more, stop when the volume is within 1e-6 of the target or two trials give the same volume. Volume preservation in sweep-based deformation rescales the key section at the bent joint the same way (Hyun et al. 2005, The Visual Computer 21(8-10):542-550, Section 4.4).
