    rigAutoMovieSweep: (props: IAutoMovieRigSweep.IProps) => IAutoMovieSweepRig = ...

    Compile one sweep of a surface rig: its rest surface, and, when it rides bones, the buffers that re-sweep it per pose as poseAutoMovieSweep does.

    At rest every slot reads the compiled rest surface. Posed, each spine point moves with its bone, the starting direction and any end tangents turn with the first or last point's bone, and the spine is re-transported in place; each station's rest plane turns with its bone, or takes the spherical midpoint of its two bones' turns, and the orientation is recomputed; every registered plane is then read from it. With volume preservation and joint stations, the scale profile gains a knot at every station and rest scale knot, the rest scale times a joint factor at joint stations and times 1 elsewhere, and settleAutoMovieSweepVolume finds the factor whose volume, from the rest section moments and this pose's frames by integrateAutoMovieSweepVolume, equals the rest volume. Without either the scale stays the rest profile. A girth factor set by girth then multiplies every scale slot, at rest and after settling, so the settled volume is the rest volume before that correction. Shapes before scale come directly from the shared shape evaluator and are read once at registration; no scaled result is divided back into a shape. Parameter-correspondence cells average actual signed section coordinates; radial cells retain radius averaging about their authored direction. Valid Cartesian cancellation at the origin is allowed. Unrepresentable nonzero cell averages, scaled samples and girth products are refused. Girth admission stages every factor, product and probe before committing.

          • Optionalbinding?: { points: number[]; stations: number[][] }

            Optional compiled bone-buffer indices, distinct from the authored bone-identity binding. Omission keeps all frames at rest. Outer array lengths are checked against the sweep; index validity and one/two station cardinality are caller preconditions.

            • points: number[]

              One nonnegative index in the later pose bone buffer per spine point, in spine order. The array is retained for pose updates.

            • stations: number[][]

              One array of one or two bone-buffer indices per station. Two indices mark a joint and blend its rotations at their spherical midpoint. The arrays are retained and must remain stable.

          • preserveVolume: boolean

            Whether bound two-bone stations settle their radial scale to the authored rest volume before multiplying girth factors. Required explicitly; unbound sweeps or bindings without joints perform no settlement.

          • sweep: IAutoMovieSweep

            Rest sweep in model metres, validated by compileAutoMovieSweep and retained by the rig. Keep authored geometry stable while compiled slots are in use.

        Returns IAutoMovieSweepRig

        The rig, open for registration.

    Throws

    Error when compileAutoMovieSweep refuses the sweep, the binding does not align with it, a slot is registered after seal, or a probe cell is negative or nonfinite; and from pose whatever the spine transport refuses.
