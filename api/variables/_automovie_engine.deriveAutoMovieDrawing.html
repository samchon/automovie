    deriveAutoMovieDrawing: (
        props: IAutoMovieDeriveDrawing.IProps,

    Derive one drawing from one design and one view.

    Throws an Error when the design fails validateBuiltEnvironment, naming the first violation, because a drawing of a graph that does not resolve would be a picture of nothing handed on as evidence. Throws when the view is degenerate, as autoMovieDrawingFrame describes, and when it declares two dimensions or two annotations with one id, since a consumer addresses them by id.

    Everything is computed from the design: lines from element and face-only boundary geometry in each element's current operating state, regions from the cells a cutting view passes through, opening marks from each opening's void or fill, and dimensions and notes from the features their targets pin. Nothing produced here may be read back into the design. What cannot be computed is reported in IAutoMovieDrawing.gaps rather than omitted, because a sheet that silently leaves something out reads as a building without it. Openings are marked whatever the kind filter says, because the schedule counts them all, and only the space filter selects them.

    The digest is SHA-256 over the canonical JSON of every other field. Identical canonical payloads give identical digests; a shared digest does not prove field equality because SHA-256 collisions are possible.
