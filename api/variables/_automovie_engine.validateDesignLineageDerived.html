    Validate the derived artifacts of one design lineage: their identity, their freshness, and the fairness of every comparison they take part in.

    Each artifact needs a unique non-blank id that is not a subject's, a non-blank kind, at least one input naming a subject or another artifact once, and lowercase sha256: digests for its bytes and its configuration. It is stale, and refused, when its stamp names a revision other than head, an unknown variant, a variant based on another revision, or an unknown phase; when an input artifact was stamped differently; when it reads an input subject carrying imported bytes without citing exactly that subject's current digest once; or when it cites bytes of a subject that is not such an input. The input graph must be acyclic. Within one decision, two artifacts of the same kind stamped with that decision's alternatives must share a configuration and a phase, because a comparison shot under two cameras compares the cameras.

    Findings are pushed to out with <root>.derived[i] paths, in artifact order and then decision order for the fairness check; nothing throws.
