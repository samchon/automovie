    meshAutoMovieSweep: (props: IAutoMovieMeshSweep.IProps) => IAutoMovieMesh = ...

    Triangulate a sweep directly into a closed mesh, for hard-edged members such as profiles, rails, pipes and tapering piers that need no subdivision cage.

    Rings sit at the given spine parameters, by default the chord-length shares of the spine's own points, so a sweep along a polyline gets one ring per corner and straight walls between them. Each ring is every loop of the sweep at that parameter: a polygon section under parameter correspondence gives exactly its corners, so corners stay corners; any other loop gives columns points at even parameters. Rings are laid out by canonicalRegion, outer loop counterclockwise and holes clockwise, so the walls between consecutive rings face out of the solid and the hole walls into the holes. Both ends are capped by triangulating the end rings as planar regions (trianglesOf) unless caps is false.

    flat shading gives every wall triangle its own corners and plane normal, so section corners and ring edges stay creases; smooth shares each ring point between its walls and takes area-weighted vertex normals (meshOf), so a polygon approximating a round section reads round. The atlas is metric: on the walls u is the distance round the ring and v the spine parameter times the spine's polygon length, the distance along a path of straight legs; caps take their section-plane coordinates. A smooth wall repeats each ring's first point at the end of the ring so the seam can carry both u 0 and the ring's length, and pools the two normals there.

    Whether the solid intersects itself is the author's: a spine turning tighter than the section is wide folds the walls through each other.

          • Optionalcaps?: boolean

            Whether to triangulate both end regions, including holes; omitted means true. False returns the open walls.

          • Optionalcolumns?: number

            Safe integer samples per nonpolygon loop, at least three; omitted means 32. Parameter-correspondence polygons use their authored corner count instead, but this option is still validated.

          • Optionalrows?: readonly number[]

            Normalized spine coordinates of successive rings. The mesher copies and checks at least two finite strictly increasing values, exactly starting at 0 and ending at 1. Omitted uses the spine control points' polygonal chord-length shares.

          • Optionalshading?: "flat" | "smooth"

            Wall normal sharing: flat duplicates triangle corners, smooth shares ring vertices and pools seam normals. Omitted means flat; end caps keep separate planar normals in either case.

          • sweep: IAutoMovieSweep

            Authored sweep in model metres, borrowed for this synchronous meshing call and admitted by compileAutoMovieSweep. Self-intersection of the swept solid is not tested.

        Returns IAutoMovieMesh

        A new mesh with normals and metric UVs and no skin.

    Throws

    Error when compileAutoMovieSweep refuses the sweep, the rows or columns are invalid, a ring is not a valid planar region, or a ring winds the other way from the first.
