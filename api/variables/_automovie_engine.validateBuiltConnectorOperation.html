    Validate the travelling carriages, named states, and stops of one run.

    An operated connector must name the elements it is built from, and declare at least one carriage and one state. Each carriage needs a unique non-blank id, an element that resolves and is one of the run's elements or below one, and a valid travel by validateBuiltTravelMotion; no element may be driven by two members of the whole work, which driven records across every opening and connector and is updated here. Each state needs a unique non-blank id and a known drive, may drive in reverse only on a bidirectional run, and gives every carriage exactly one value inside its travel; a carriage's serves must be one of the run's stops, its endpoints or landings. The current state must resolve. Findings are pushed under path; a run with no operation is skipped.
