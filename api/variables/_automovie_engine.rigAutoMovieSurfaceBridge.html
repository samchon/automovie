    Compile a bridge patch's interior for per-pose evaluation, returning the writer that fills its rows into a target buffer.

    Column k joins loop point k of the first loop to loop point k of the second by the cubic Hermite curve h00 A + h10 m T_A + h01 B + h11 m T_B, m the tension times the chord, row j at t = j / rows. A loop point's direction follows its loop's rule: at a sweep point the sweep's outward normal for normal, its opposite for inward, and its unit derivative along the spine, forward or backward, for forward and backward; at a curve point the curve's declared direction. Under across the direction is the normal, the sweep's or the curve's blended one, crossed with the loop's chord between the points one first-row length, m / rows, before and after it along the loop, at least reaching both neighbours. That chord is the loop's tangent at the scale of the first row, so near a corner of the loop the columns turn over the points within one row of it rather than at the corner alone, and no column overtakes its neighbour.

          Compile a bridge patch's interior for per-pose evaluation, returning the writer that fills its rows into a target buffer.

          Column k joins loop point k of the first loop to loop point k of the second by the cubic Hermite curve h00 A + h10 m T_A + h01 B + h11 m T_B, m the tension times the chord, row j at t = j / rows. A loop point's direction follows its loop's rule: at a sweep point the sweep's outward normal for normal, its opposite for inward, and its unit derivative along the spine, forward or backward, for forward and backward; at a curve point the curve's declared direction. Under across the direction is the normal, the sweep's or the curve's blended one, crossed with the loop's chord between the points one first-row length, m / rows, before and after it along the loop, at least reaching both neighbours. That chord is the loop's tangent at the scale of the first row, so near a corner of the loop the columns turn over the points within one row of it rather than at the corner alone. This local construction does not certify that columns never cross or that the resulting patch is free of self-intersection. Nondegenerate loop lengths and direction vectors are caller premises; this kernel can write nonfinite controls for a degenerate tangent, which the owning network rejects after evaluating all writers. No rollback of earlier interior writes is provided.

          • from: IAutoMovieSurfaceSample[]

            First loop's ordered sample records, borrowed by the returned writer. Each sample's sweep or curve rig supplies its bridge direction; preserve order and references while the writer lives.

          • fromIndex: number[]

            Vertex indices of first-loop points in the packed target buffer. Copied into an Int32Array during compilation; buffer positions are read at three times each index.

          • fromRule: IAutoMovieSurfaceNetwork.ILoop["tangent"]

            First loop's direction rule: sweep normal/inward/spine forward/backward, or normal crossed with a scale-aware loop chord for across. A curve supplies its declared direction except under across.

          • interior: number[]

            Borrowed target vertex indices, column-major: row j of column k is at k*(rows-1)+j-1 for j=1..rows-1. The returned writer mutates those packed XYZ positions in metres.

          • probes: number[]

            Borrowed sweep probe slots for all first-loop points followed by all second-loop points. A curve point uses -1. Probe fields must reflect the current pose before writing.

          • rows: number

            Positive integer number of faces along each bridging column. The compiler writes rows-1 interior points; parent patch planning owns count admission.

          • tension: number

            Positive finite dimensionless tangent-length/chord-length ratio, admitted by parent patch planning. Each Hermite endpoint tangent is scaled by this ratio times the current endpoint separation.

          • to: IAutoMovieSurfaceSample[]

            Second loop's samples in correspondence with from, with exactly the same count. Network patch planning admits matching loops before calling this compiler.

          • toIndex: number[]

            Corresponding second-loop vertex indices, copied during compilation. Loops must already be written before the returned interior writer runs.

          • toRule: IAutoMovieSurfaceNetwork.ILoop["tangent"]

            Second loop's direction rule in the same convention as fromRule; it determines the receiving Hermite tangent.

        Returns (targets: Float64Array) => void

        The writer, which reads the loops' vertices and writes the interior into the same buffer.
