    Sample a section's radius from its origin at evenly spaced polar angles.

    Radius k lies along angle 2 pi k / count, measured counterclockwise from the section plane's +x axis. A radial section is read through its periodic spline, interpolateAutoMovieSpline. A superellipse centred on its origin is read in closed form: in the quadrant holding the angle, with that quadrant's half-axes a along x and b along y and exponent n, the curve is |x / a|^n + |y / b|^n = 1, so the radius is (|cos a / a|^n + |sin a / b|^n)^(-1/n), and it is star-shaped and counterclockwise by construction. Any other curve is first walked at 64 points per segment (at least 1,024) to bracket each angle between two curve parameters, then the parameter is bisected forty times against the curve's own polar angle, so the radius lies on the exact curve rather than on a chord.

    The walk checks the sampled curve for star-shapedness about its origin and winding counterclockwise: the polar angle must strictly increase between successive points and turn once in total, or the curve is refused, because the sampled walk would not pair its points by direction. This finite walk does not certify monotone polar angle between probes.

          • count: number

            Safe integer angular sample count of at least three, checked by the sampler. Output has exactly this many newly allocated radii at 2pik/count radians, with no repeated endpoint.

          • section: IAutoMovieSweepSection

            Borrowed section in metre x/y coordinates. The sampler validates radial data or delegates curve admission to parameterizeAutoMovieSweepSection; its finite walk rejects observed origin crossings and nonincreasing polar angles, without certifying unsampled curve intervals.

        Returns number[]

        Radii in metres, one per angle, all positive.

    Throws

    Error when the count is not an integer of at least 3, the section's numbers are not finite or out of range, or the curve passes through its origin, winds clockwise or is not star-shaped about it.
