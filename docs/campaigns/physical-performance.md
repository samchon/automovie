- The kicking-side hand posts forward near the head, and the opposite hand protects it. The same semantics apply on either side.

## Research and delivery

Inertia scaling uses [LAPACK DSYEV](https://netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/dd/d4c/dsyev_8f_source.html). Applying those principles is an implementation inference, not qualification of this controller.
