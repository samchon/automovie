## Ownership and performance

- Engine, Interface, Viewer and Render have no Human dependency. Human may consume Engine and Interface; its anatomical laws and physical provider remain in Human.
- Kickboxing uses the original polygon people, with rig points and sticks on by default. Its choreography, fixtures and tests stay inside the movie.
- Strikes bind to actual opponent geometry and provider dimensions. Footwork resolves necessary approach together with planted-support reach; fixed world-coordinate nudges do not qualify contact.
- Related parts share their actual coordinate dependencies. Reuse unchanged topology and completed calculations, propagate an ancestor change through its affected branches, and reevaluate only relationships whose inputs change. Shared ancestors and moving receivers remain coupled; a cache key alone does not establish equivalence.
- Dynamic control preserves authored root motor intent while fitting joint goals about the actual root. Complete support patches, ROM, native effort limits and original world anchors remain authoritative.
- Recoverable disturbances require bracing or actual recovery. Confirmed landings require positive native floor receipts and the declared endpoint position/speed. A failed attempted step is not a landing.
- The kicking-side hand posts forward near the head, and the opposite hand protects it. The same semantics apply on either side.

## Research and delivery

Controller research reads [Caron, Pham and Nakamura](https://arxiv.org/html/1501.04719v1), [SIMBICON](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~van/papers/2007-siggraph-simbicon.pdf), [Mason](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.08179v1) and [Kuindersma](https://arxiv.org/html/1311.1839v1) against their force, contact and actuation assumptions. Signed-distance differentiation follows [Drake's derivative construction](https://github.com/RobotLocomotion/drake/blob/bdcd5ff96a1dfed77791302fb499ec2aa831a03e/multibody/inverse_kinematics/distance_constraint_utilities.cc). Inertia scaling uses [LAPACK DSYEV](https://netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/dd/d4c/dsyev_8f_source.html). Applying those principles is an implementation inference, not qualification of this controller.

Typia ^15.3.0 and ttsc ^0.36.0 are installed as 15.3.0 and 0.36.3. The package PR keeps movie content out apart from its minimal compiler-range manifest changes. Final delivery still requires a clean Overall Self-Review, appropriate unit results and exact-head CI, with the owner's explicit exclusions reported accurately.
