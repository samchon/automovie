# Connected body basis

`basis.json.gz` is the first CC0 numerical body basis, revision `mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-20-joints-and-measures`. It is the remainder of the MPFB base mesh below the face basis's neck clip, with the shape channels, joint landmarks, skin weights, clinical joint limits and macro combination correctives the body editor evaluates. The generic evaluator lives in `@automovie/human`; this payload is study content. Replay needs this exact basis, not Blender.

The sources, their licenses and the anthropometric and clinical records are in the [parent study](../README.md). [`extraction-receipt.json`](extraction-receipt.json) pins this revision: the script digest, Blender version, frame offset, correspondence and ring checks, neutral recovery, mirror figures, per-endpoint displacement, the corrective sampling, the skin truncation and the constraint source of every joint.

## Derivation

`test/scripts/body-review/extract-body-basis.py` runs inside Blender 5.2 with the pinned MPFB revision enabled from source (`.references/mpfb2/src/mpfb`, junctioned into Blender's user extension repository). It creates the default human (every macro axis 0.5, equal ethnic mixture), binds the CC0 `weights.game_engine.json` as vertex groups, masks the helpers and applies one limit-surface Catmull-Clark round, which reproduces the face basis's head vertices exactly. Every state is read from Blender's evaluated depsgraph, so an endpoint is what the add-on itself shows.

Coordinates are metres, Y up, Z forward, in the face basis frame: `x`, `z_blender - 1.5194313526153564`, `-y_blender`. The ground plane sits at Y = -1.5187 m in the neutral. The clip keeps Y <= -0.145 m. Each subdivided quad is split along the diagonal from its first loop corner before clipping, because that is how the face was clipped: 104 quad edges and 96 diagonals cross the plane, giving the 200 ring vertices that coincide with the face ring to 0.0 m. Ring vertices are affine combinations `a + t (b - a)` of their edge ends, evaluated with the neutral `t` for every endpoint, so a state that moves the neck moves the ring identically on both sides of the seam. 35,787 original vertices plus the 200 ring vertices make 35,987; 71,772 triangles; one material region with the source UVs (V inverted for the glTF convention, verified corner by corner against the face).

## Channels and endpoints

132 signed channels, each `[-1, 1]` with zero the source neutral, generated from MPFB's own `targets/target.json` and macro axes rather than typed by hand:

- 124 regional channels over `torso`, `arms`, `legs`, `hands`, `feet`, `neck`, `hip`, `pelvis`, `stomach`, `buttocks` and `breast`. A sided category becomes a `Left` and a `Right` channel that name each other in `mirror`. Endpoint names are the MPFB target paths. Twenty of them are MPFB's `measure-*` targets (bust, underbust, waist, hip, neck, upper arm, wrist, thigh, knee, calf and ankle girths; shoulder, front chest, nape to waist, waist to hip distances; upper arm, forearm, upper leg, lower leg and neck heights), which are the channels the editor prices in millimetres.
- 8 macro channels: `macroGender` (female at -1, male at +1), `macroAge` (child node 0.1875 at -1, old at +1), `macroMuscle`, `macroWeight`, `macroHeight`, `macroProportions` (uncommon at -1, ideal at +1), `macroCupsize`, `macroFirmness`. MPFB's macro system is piecewise linear between nodes, so each channel reproduces it exactly along its segment.
- 78 combination correctives over macro axis pairs. MPFB blends macro targets as products of node weights, so a pair at its extremes differs from the sum of its two endpoints; the residual is sampled directly (`f(a, b) - f(0) - Δa - Δb`) and published as a corrective driven by the two sides with the product activation of `createHumanFaceBasisBuilder`. All 112 pair states were sampled; 34 fell below 0.5 mm and are listed in the receipt as unpublished (every pair of height or proportions with cupsize or firmness, every gender x cupsize and gender x firmness pair, height x proportions, child x minimum height, and a few muscle or weight pairs with the breast axes, all at a micrometre except muscle-min x firmness-max at 0.42 mm). The largest is child x maximum height at 125.5 mm, which is what makes a tall child a different body from a scaled adult.

Excluded by decision: `genitals/*`, `pelvis/bulge-*`, `breast/nipple-*`, every face folder, `asym/*`, and the macro ethnicity axes. Neck channels are kept although the neck itself is above the clip: they move the ring band by up to 2.4 mm (and `neckDouble` by micrometres), and the seam with the face's own neck controls (`neckWidth`, `neckDepth`, `neckUnderChinFullness`, `globalAgeStructure`, `globalSexualDimorphism`, `globalAdiposity`, `globalMuscularity`) is owned by the separate combination stage, which also has to translate the face by the ring's rise under `macroHeight` because the face subtracts its binocular frame while the body keeps its feet on the ground.

Endpoint rows are `[vertex, dx, dy, dz]` rounded to a micrometre with exact-zero rows omitted. Every state restores to the neutral to the last bit (`neutralRecoveryMaximumMetres` 0.0). The neutral mirrors onto itself with a pairing RMS of 63 nm (worst 0.36 micrometres, 143 midline vertices, a proper involution); each right endpoint is the mirror of its left to within 0.067 mm.

## Joints, landmarks and skin

125 landmarks are the centroids of MPFB's `joint-*` helper cubes, published with their own sparse endpoint deltas so a joint follows the shape that moves it. 52 joints map the game_engine rig one to one onto the VRM humanoid slots (eyes and jaw are the face's): `hips` is the root at the pelvis cube, and each joint names its head and tail landmark. Each carries the flexion reference direction of its anatomical frame (anterior for the trunk, shoulder, elbow and hip; posterior for the knee; superior for the ankle and toes; the measured palmar direction for the wrist and fingers, and the across-palm direction from thumb to little finger for the thumb), the measured clinical signs of abduction and twist under that frame, and its range of motion from the pinned clinical record: 38 CFR 4.71 Plates I and II for the limbs, 4.71a Note (2) split equally over the segments of each spinal chain, AAOS where the regulation is silent, and the engine's fallback where nothing clinical was pinned (shoulder girdle, foot inversion, toes, finger phalanges). The receipt names the source of every joint.

Skin weights are Blender's interpolation of the game_engine weights through the subdivision, truncated to the four largest influences per vertex and renormalized; the largest weight dropped anywhere is 0.042. The neck joint sits 33 mm above the clip and is kept because the ring band carries its weight.

## Limits

An authored upstream prior, not individual anatomy. Joints are hinge and ball approximations at cube centroids with no scapulohumeral rhythm, knee screw-home or carrying angle. Nothing here establishes collision-free or physiological combinations; the census and pose correctives of later topics measure that.
