# Connected body basis

`basis.json.gz` is the CC0 numerical body basis, revision `mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-23-sym+digits+rhythm+envelope+correctives-r4`. It is the remainder of the MPFB base mesh that the face basis does not keep, with the shape channels, joint landmarks, joint couplings, skin weights, clinical joint limits, macro combination correctives and pose correctives the body editor evaluates. The generic evaluator lives in `@automovie/human`; this payload is study content. Replay needs this exact basis, not Blender.

The sources, their licenses and the anthropometric and clinical records are in the [parent study](../README.md). [`extraction-receipt.json`](extraction-receipt.json) pins the extraction (`mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-21-coronal-limits`): the script digest, Blender version, frame offset, correspondence and boundary checks, neutral recovery, mirror figures, per-endpoint displacement, the corrective sampling, the skin truncation and the constraint source of every joint. The publication steps that follow the extraction each carry a receipt and a revision id, and the shipped file is the last: [`individuality-receipt.json`](individuality-receipt.json) adds the twelve individuality channels; [`envelope-receipt.json`](envelope-receipt.json) widens the envelopes of the channels whose source extremes stop short of the population; [`envelope-detail-receipt.json`](envelope-detail-receipt.json) extends them by volume rather than by detail and records the reach the simple tier then has; [`symmetry-receipt.json`](symmetry-receipt.json) makes the skin weights bilaterally symmetric; [`digits-receipt.json`](digits-receipt.json) puts the finger and toe joints where the articular heads are; [`couplings-receipt.json`](couplings-receipt.json) declares the scapulohumeral rhythm; and [`pose-correctives-receipt.json`](pose-correctives-receipt.json) adds the pose correctives solved on that skeleton under dual quaternion skinning and records the hashes of the shipped file. The tooling that performs these steps is kept outside the repository; each receipt states the method and the digests of what it produced, which is what a reproduction is checked against.

## Derivation

The extraction script the receipt pins by digest runs inside Blender 5.2 with the pinned MPFB revision enabled from source (`.references/mpfb2/src/mpfb`, junctioned into Blender's user extension repository). It creates the default human (every macro axis 0.5, equal ethnic mixture), binds the CC0 `weights.game_engine.json` as vertex groups, masks the helpers and applies one limit-surface Catmull-Clark round, which reproduces the face basis's head vertices exactly. Every state is read from Blender's evaluated depsgraph, so an endpoint is what the add-on itself shows, with one edit applied to every state before the cut: the nipple region is removed (below).

Coordinates are metres, Y up, Z forward, in the face basis frame: `x`, `z_blender - 1.5194313526153564`, `-y_blender`. The ground plane sits at Y = -1.5187 m in the neutral. The cut is not a plane: the body is the complement of the triangles the shipped face basis keeps. The face was cropped at the collar (`crop-face-to-neck`), where its photographic observation stops, and is open along a loop of 120 vertices between Y = -0.0808 and -0.0902 m that follows the neck's own slope; the body keeps every triangle of the same fan-triangulated source that the face does not, so the two meet along that loop by vertex identity (`boundary_agreement`: 120 face loop vertices, 120 body boundary vertices, worst distance 0.0 m). No vertex is interpolated: every body vertex is a source vertex. The first revision cut at the source asset's own shoulder plane Y = -0.145 m and left the neck between that plane and the face's collar to neither basis, which the editor showed as a head floating over the shoulders. 36,810 vertices; 73,498 triangles; one material region with the source UVs (V inverted for the glTF convention, verified corner by corner against the face).

## No nipple geometry

The product excludes genital and nipple geometry from the base model, its controls, presets and exports. The genitals are helper geometry the mask already drops. The nipples are skin, so the extraction replaces them: the region is the 538 subdivided vertices that the source's own `breast/nipple-size-incr` and `breast/nipple-point-incr` targets move, and it is refilled by a biharmonic solve with two rings of surrounding vertices (128) held fixed, so the fill continues the chest's curvature instead of a flat membrane (a harmonic fill was tried first and read as a flat disc). The fill is linear in the surrounding vertices, so it is applied identically to the neutral and to every endpoint, the neutral still recovers to the last bit, and a breast still forms under `macroGender`, `macroCupsize` and `macroFirmness` without its nipple. At the neutral the fill moves the region by 2.1 mm on average and 5.7 mm at most.

## Channels and endpoints

132 signed channels, each `[-1, 1]` with zero the source neutral, generated from MPFB's own `targets/target.json` and macro axes rather than typed by hand:

- 124 regional channels over `torso`, `arms`, `legs`, `hands`, `feet`, `neck`, `hip`, `pelvis`, `stomach`, `buttocks` and `breast`. A sided category becomes a `Left` and a `Right` channel that name each other in `mirror`. Endpoint names are the MPFB target paths. Twenty of them are MPFB's `measure-*` targets (bust, underbust, waist, hip, neck, upper arm, wrist, thigh, knee, calf and ankle girths; shoulder, front chest, nape to waist, waist to hip distances; upper arm, forearm, upper leg, lower leg and neck heights), which are the channels the editor prices in millimetres.
- 8 macro channels: `macroGender` (female at -1, male at +1), `macroAge` (child node 0.1875 at -1, old at +1), `macroMuscle`, `macroWeight`, `macroHeight`, `macroProportions` (uncommon at -1, ideal at +1), `macroCupsize`, `macroFirmness`. MPFB's macro system is piecewise linear between nodes, so each channel reproduces it exactly along its segment.
- 78 combination correctives over macro axis pairs. MPFB blends macro targets as products of node weights, so a pair at its extremes differs from the sum of its two endpoints; the residual is sampled directly (`f(a, b) - f(0) - Δa - Δb`) and published as a corrective driven by the two sides with the product activation of `createHumanFaceBasisBuilder`. All 112 pair states were sampled; 34 fell below 0.5 mm and are listed in the receipt as unpublished (every pair of height or proportions with cupsize or firmness, every gender x cupsize and gender x firmness pair, height x proportions, child x minimum height, and a few muscle or weight pairs with the breast axes, all at a micrometre except muscle-min x firmness-max at 0.42 mm). The largest is child x maximum height at 125.5 mm, which is what makes a tall child a different body from a scaled adult.

Excluded by decision: `genitals/*`, `pelvis/bulge-*`, `breast/nipple-*`, every face folder, `asym/*`, and the macro ethnicity axes. Neck channels are kept: the neck below the collar is the body's now, and the seam with the face's own neck controls (`neckWidth`, `neckDepth`, `neckUnderChinFullness`, `globalAgeStructure`, `globalSexualDimorphism`, `globalAdiposity`, `globalMuscularity`) is owned by the separate combination stage, which also has to move the face when a body channel moves the collar loop (the editor seats a neutral face on the head bone and shows that gap when it opens).

Endpoint rows are `[vertex, dx, dy, dz]` rounded to a micrometre with exact-zero rows omitted. Every state restores to the neutral to the last bit (`neutralRecoveryMaximumMetres` 0.0). The neutral mirrors onto itself with a pairing RMS of 0.06 µm (worst 0.36 µm, 162 midline vertices, a proper involution); each right endpoint is the mirror of its left to within 0.067 mm.

## Individuality channels

[`individuality-receipt.json`](individuality-receipt.json) records the twelve endpoints and the numbers they were authored from. The source's regional controls grow and shrink tissue but do not express where a real body's tissue sits or shows: gluteal ptosis, the lower abdominal apron, android flank fat and gynoid outer-thigh fat, the hip dip, the definition of the rectus, deltoid and scapula, and the skeleton showing through a lean body. Twelve channels add those (`buttocksPtosis`, `stomachOverhang`, `flankFat`, `outerThighFatLeft/Right`, `hipDipLeft/Right`, `absDefinition`, `deltoidDefinitionLeft/Right`, `scapularDefinition`, `skeletalProminence`), each a procedural displacement field on the neutral surface written entirely as numbers in a parameter table the receipt reproduces: a tissue mask read off the source endpoint over the same anatomy (the buttock is what `buttocks-volume` grows) or gates over the surface, a coordinate over that mask (polar between its lower and upper rims, geodesic from the most lateral point of a slab, a height above an anchor, or the mask itself), and displacement terms along the normal or gravity with a plateau, bell or step profile, or a relief of grooves and ridges along skin curves drawn from landmarks (the linea alba and three tendinous intersections; the clavicle, costal margin, four ribs, iliac crest and fifteen spinous processes). The generator is only the engine that evaluates those rows; a trait is added or tuned as a row. The right side of a mirrored pair is the exact reflection of the left (residual 0.0000 mm in the receipt). The clinical scales behind the rows (Gonzalez 2006 for ptosis, Igwe's panniculus grades, the WHO android/gynoid split, the surface anatomy of the rectus sheath) are pinned in the receipt's `research` entry. Each trait was rendered on a real GPU at its extremes and on the macro extremes it meets, and the `traits` census below reads every one of them clean.

## Envelopes and reach

The source's macro nodes are authored bodies, not population limits: with every channel at its authored extreme the basis reached 47 to 65 kg on a 1.66 m woman and 65 to 97 kg on a 1.80 m man, with the hip channel and the weight macro at their authored minimum its hip tape girth bottomed at 0.80 m, and with the waist channel and the weight macro at their maximum its waist topped at 0.85 m, which left the obese, the emaciated, the narrow-hipped and the apron-bellied outside the editor. An endpoint is a linear displacement field, so a weight past one is more of the same field, and the envelope is data on the channel. The envelope step publishes these envelopes: `macroWeight` [-2.5, 5.5] and `macroMuscle` [-1, 2], whose fields cover the whole skin; the waist girth [-2, 3.5]; the hip, underbust, upper-arm and calf girths and the shoulder breadth [-2, 2]; the bust girth [-2, 1.25] and the thigh girth [-2, 1.75], where the rest pose starts to cross (the hanging arm, the other thigh); `stomachPregnant` [-1, 2] and `stomachOverhang` [0, 1.5]. The regional fat fields (arm, thigh, calf, buttocks) and the banded depots (flank, outer thigh) stay at their authored reach of one: rendered past it, each field's edge at the knee, the elbow or the gluteal fold stood as a shelf, and a bell band as a lump, so a heavier body rides the weight macro instead. Past its node a channel grows by volume, not by detail. An endpoint's low spatial frequencies carry where tissue is added and its high frequencies the rolls and creases of the one body the source sculpted; worn linearly to four times its node, the heaviest source body's centimetre rolls at the flank and under the scapula stood as four-centimetre shelves on a 105 kg man, although each input alone was clean. [`envelope-detail-receipt.json`](envelope-detail-receipt.json) records the repair. An endpoint both moves the skin and adds tissue along its normal, so each extended side is separated in two passes of implicit heat diffusion over the skin (cotangent Laplacian with a lumped mass, the neck boundary held): a vector pass at a segment's scale (150 mm) keeps the motion, a translation of a whole arm included, and the residual's thickness along the rest surface's normal is then split at 40 mm, between the 5 to 10 cm width of a roll and the 20 to 40 cm scale of a depot, into a smooth part and a detail part. A thickness pass alone read a translated arm's normal component, which swings from outward to inward around every finger, as detail and crumpled the fingers of a widened shoulder; a vector pass alone cancelled a limb's uniform growth across its opposite sides. One corrective per side ramps from the node to the envelope's end and subtracts the detail's extrapolation, so past the node the field is the endpoint at its node plus the smooth part times the excess and nothing inside the node changes. A deeper roll or apron is the individuality channels' to make, on their clinical grades, and the rule leaves those channels to their linear extension: they are fields a generator computes from a grade rather than one sculpted body, their crease is their content, and held to their node the apron at 1.5 read as the apron at 1. The receipt records the reach the simple tier then has: along its mass direction (the weight macro alone, whose field covers the whole skin) at 30 years, 1.55 m woman 31 to 87 kg, 1.55 m man 40 to 93 kg, 1.75 m woman 44 to 112 kg, 1.75 m man 54 to 118 kg, 1.95 m woman 60 to 143 kg, 1.95 m man 72 to 151 kg; and each tape measurement's span (a girth read as a tape reads it, the section's convex hull), waist girth 0.50 to 1.01 m, hips girth 0.79 to 1.28 m, bust girth 0.69 to 1.04 m, underbust girth 0.64 to 0.79 m, shoulder breadth 0.29 to 0.44 m, thigh girth 0.48 to 0.69 m, upperarm girth 0.26 to 0.42 m, calf girth 0.20 to 0.53 m. The channel census reads every channel at its widened extremes, which is where an overextended field would show as a crossing.

## The simple tier

`@automovie/human` expands five numbers a person knows about a body (sex, age in years, stature, mass in kilograms, a muscularity) and four optional tape measurements (waist, hip and bust girth, shoulder breadth) into this basis's channel weights (`expandHumanBodySimpleShape`), and reads them back off any detailed shape (`projectHumanBodySimpleShape`). The table it evaluates, `HUMAN_BODY_SIMPLE_SHAPE`, is numbers only: the age nodes of the source (child 11 years, young 25, old 90), a sarcopenia loss of a third of the muscle between 30 and 80, gluteal ptosis rising with age and weight, the apron with the body mass index, flank fat on men and outer-thigh fat on women, the definition of the rectus, deltoid and scapula and the skeleton showing through, gated by [Deurenberg et al. 1991](https://doi.org/10.1079/BJN19910073)'s separate child-through-15 and adult-from-16 body-fat estimates (linearly joined for fractional ages between 15 and 16) less the sex's essential fat, and firmness falling with age. Stature is solved by measurement against this basis's height rule plus the head allowance of the face basis (0.2196 m), mass through the skin volume the boundary-capped mesh encloses at Siri's density over an age-specific head-and-neck share ([Jensen 1989](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9290(89)90004-3) boys' longitudinal polynomial through 15, bridged at 15–16 to the 8.1% adult Dempster estimate), and each girth against its rule, together until the weights settle. The detailed tier stays canonical: applied over a detailed shape, the expansion keeps whatever the simple tier does not name. The archetypes of [`archetypes.json`](archetypes.json) (a slender woman, a small-busted wide-hipped one, a narrow-hipped thin one, an emaciated man, a model, a bodybuilder, a heavy muscular man, a thin man with a pot belly, an obese woman, a giant, an elderly woman and man) are the review population: verification samples of the reach, not targets.

## Joints, landmarks and skin

125 landmarks are the centroids of MPFB's `joint-*` helper cubes, published with their own sparse endpoint deltas so a joint follows the shape that moves it. 52 joints map the game_engine rig one to one onto the VRM humanoid slots (eyes and jaw are the face's): `hips` is the root at the pelvis cube, and each joint names its head and tail landmark. Each carries the flexion reference direction of its anatomical frame (anterior for the trunk, shoulder and hip; toward the shoulder for the elbow, so it folds in the plane of the arm; posterior for the knee; superior for the ankle and toes; the measured palmar direction for the wrist and fingers, and the across-palm direction from thumb to little finger for the thumb), the measured clinical signs of abduction and twist under that frame, the clinical angle each axis rests at, and its range of motion from the pinned clinical record. The receipt names the source of every joint:

- 38 CFR 4.71 Plates I and II for the limbs; 4.71a Note (2) split equally over the segments of each spinal chain; 4.71a's note on the digits for the fingers (MCP 0-90, PIP 0-100, DIP 0-80, with the engine table's 20 and 10 degrees of hyperextension kept at MCP and DIP, below the AAOS normals); AAOS for the thumb (CMC flexion 15 / extension 20, MCP 60 / 10, IP 80 / 20), the shoulder girdle (elevation 40 / depression 10; protraction and retraction stay at the engine table's 30 and 15 because this frame's sign for them is not pinned), hip extension and rotation, shoulder extension, and the first metatarsophalangeal joint (extension 70, which is this frame's positive toe flexion, and flexion 45).
- Adduction of the upper arm and of the thigh is limited to 10 degrees. The clinical figures (30 to 45) are measured with the limb carried in front of the trunk; a single-axis range describes the coronal plane alone, in which the limb can only press against the side of the chest or the other thigh, and the first census's `abduction@-30` states put the arm five centimetres inside the chest wall. The coupled adduction-with-flexion path is not modelled and is listed as a limit.
- The hip's swing cone equals its pinned flexion (125), so every pure-plane clinical maximum stays admissible; the engine table's 120 sat below it and refused the pose, which the rig admission now rejects as a contradiction, and which cost the first census 51 states before it was found.

The rest is not the clinical zero. The source stands in an A-pose, so each joint records the clinical angle its rest direction sits at (`neutral`) and the builder hands it to the engine as the rest frame's neutral, which turns a document's clinical angle into `(clinical - neutral) / sign` of rig rotation. Goniometry takes the relaxed standing posture as zero for the trunk, neck, hip, knee, ankle and toes, so those rest at zero; the upper limb measures its rest against the anatomical position: upper arm abduction 41.75 degrees, elbow flexion 43.06, wrist flexion 6.58 and radial deviation 10.99, index proximal 12.32 (identical on both sides). A T-pose is therefore `abduction 90, elbow 0`, and `abduction 180` raises the arm overhead rather than 180 degrees past the A-pose.

Two landmark groups were then moved off the helper-cube centroids, and [`digits-receipt.json`](digits-receipt.json) records the measurements. The metacarpophalangeal landmarks of digits 2-5 sat too far along the hand: the proximal phalanx measured 21 to 34 mm against a middle phalanx of 16 to 28 mm, so each moved proximally along its metacarpal until the proximal phalanx matched the Buchholz, Armstrong & Goldstein (1992) proximal-over-middle link-length ratio (index 35.7, middle 43.2, ring 37.8, little 28.1 mm), and the skin between the old and the new joint handed its hand weight to the proximal phalanx over a 22 mm linear band about the new joint. Then the centres of rotation were read across the limb in the flexion plane: the metacarpophalangeal and proximal interphalangeal centres of digits 2-5 sat 0.17 to 0.31 of the knuckle's thickness in from the dorsum and the metatarsophalangeal centre 0.23 of the forefoot's thickness up from the sole, so the skin on the far side of each hinge swept into the neighbouring segment from the first tens of degrees: a hinge at distance a from the concave skin folds the distal skin into the proximal segment over a length of a·tan(θ/2). A hinge's centre of rotation is the centre of its articular head, which lies on the shaft axis of the bone, so each of those landmarks moved along the flexion normal to the midpoint of the skin thickness at the joint (3.2 to 8.9 mm), the flexion normal read from the public builder so the move follows the rig's own sign convention (the toes flex dorsally on the positive side of this frame, as the ankle does). The thumb stays on its centroid: at its metacarpophalangeal joint the section runs through the thenar mass and the rule has no tube to read, and its folds stayed within budget. Before any corrective, the index knuckle's crossing at 90 degrees fell from 54/62 to 23/25 triangles, the fist's proximal-interphalangeal crossings vanished and the toe's crossing onset moved from 22 to 60 degrees.

The shoulder girdle follows the arm. [`couplings-receipt.json`](couplings-receipt.json) declares, per side, the scapulohumeral rhythm as two couplings on the basis (`couplings`): the upper arm's clinical elevation, the swing cone of its flexion and abduction from the anatomical zero, drives the girdle bone's elevation (abduction) to 11 degrees and its retraction (this frame's negative flexion) to 16 degrees at 180 of elevation along a piecewise-linear curve whose first knot is the A-pose rest elevation with a zero ordinate, both capped by the girdle's pinned range (Ludewig et al. 2009; Inman 1944). The builder applies the couplings before the pose is validated, so the range check sees the document's angle plus the coupled angle and refuses the sum past the range instead of clamping it; the editor shows the coupled contribution and the document never stores it. At abduction 180 the glenohumeral centre rises 28.4 mm and the arm overhead is a smooth shoulder rather than a deltoid folded into the neck.

Skin weights are Blender's interpolation of the game_engine weights through the subdivision, truncated to the four largest influences per vertex and renormalized; the largest weight dropped anywhere is 0.042. The interpolated weights were not bilaterally symmetric although the neutral surface and the landmarks are: 1,760 of the 18,486 mirror vertex pairs differed by more than 0.01 and 389 by more than 0.10 (worst 0.52, at the ankle and the hip), which folded the two knees at different angles under the same pose. [`symmetry-receipt.json`](symmetry-receipt.json) records the repair: each mirror pair takes the mean of its two weight sets with the bone names mirrored, a midline vertex the mean of its own and its mirrored set, truncated again to four influences (largest dropped 0.016). The surface is posed by dual quaternion skinning (Kavan et al. 2007): each vertex blends the unit dual quaternions of its influences, so a joint carries its skin as a rigid arc with no candy-wrapper collapse under twist and no pinch at a fold, where the linear blend the previous revisions were skinned with lost girth (below). The correctives are solved on that skin; the previous revision's 1,357 linear-blend correctives were retired rather than carried, since a corrective that refills a linear blend's collapse is a bulge under a rigid one. The neck and head joints carry the neck below the collar, and the face the editor seats on the head bone follows the head rigidly, so a turned head opens a crack at the collar loop until the combination stage skins the face with the same weights.

## Measured: rigid segments, arcs, ranges

[`joints-receipt.json`](joints-receipt.json) records, for each mobile joint axis posed alone at half its range from the rest on the skin before the pose correctives (which by design move the fold), how the skin follows the bone. Over the axes whose segment has vertices bound by at least 0.999 to that bone, those vertices move as one rigid body with the bone: the Kabsch residual is at most 0.011 mm and every vertex keeps its distance from the pivot to below a micrometre, against a chord sagitta of up to 57.4 mm that a linear endpoint read at the same half angle would have produced. That is the number the joint contract exists for. The whole dominated segment, blend zone included, departs from rigid by an RMS of up to 17.2 mm, which is the blend zone bending about the joint. With the pose correctives worn the same test reads up to 2.1 mm at the elbow, the corrective's own displacement inside the half-range ramp. The receipt also lists every axis whose range differs from the engine's fallback table, which is the clinical record's side of the comparison in the [study README](../README.md).

## Measured: girth under the pose

Each posed limb was cut by nine planes perpendicular to its bone and the closed section loop compared with the rest. Under the linear blend skinning of the previous revisions the elbow's girth fell 16% at forearm pronation 85, the knee's 7 to 8% at 140 degrees of flexion, the hip's 11% at 125 of flexion and the upper arm's 6% at 90 of twist: the candy wrapper and the pinch of the fold, measured rather than assumed. Under the dual quaternion skinning of this revision the same stations lose 0.0% at all four, and the largest loss anywhere off a fold is 2.5% (the thigh at 10 degrees of adduction, where the section plane clips the other thigh). The station inside a fold reads the two touching segments as one loop and is not a girth.

## Pose correctives

[`pose-correctives-receipt.json`](pose-correctives-receipt.json) records the correctives, the states they were solved on, and the states that stayed beyond their budget. A pose corrective is a rest-space endpoint driven by a clinical joint angle as a ramp, `{bone, axis, side, onset, full}` in degrees past the rest, applied before skinning (`IAutoMovieHumanBodyBasis.correctives`); RigLogic's conditional table, applied to a joint. Every mobile joint axis was visited on each side of its rest at the census's two sample angles, in ascending travel, on a working basis that already wore the correctives accepted at the smaller angles of the same axis, and each combination finding of the census (below) was then visited on the basis that wore every single-axis corrective:

- **Onset** is measured, not assumed: the largest fraction of the pose at which nothing crosses, bisected to 2.5 degrees, so a corrective ramps from where the contact starts and not from the rest. A combination corrective is the product of one ramp per input: a channel ramp from zero to the shape's weight, so the push grows with the tissue and stays off the bodies short of it, and a joint ramp over each posed axis from the measured onset to the full angle.
- **Push**: the crossing corners of both segments are moved apart in the posed frame and the displacement carried into the rest frame through the inverse of each vertex's blended dual quaternion, which is exact under dual quaternion skinning (the carry was checked against the builder to the last bit). Rules are tried from the same start and the first that clears the pair is kept: a surface rule that moves each entered corner past the other surface by half the penetration plus a 1.5 mm clearance, repeated each sweep until the corner is out; the same rule holding its first target; a plane through the joint (the fold plane for an adjacent pair, the contact plane for any other); and a plane estimated from the contact itself, through the midpoint of the two crossing corner sets and normal to the difference of their centroids, which parts a fold whose joint sits far from it (the hip centre lies centimetres behind the groin crease). Every separation a pair wins is kept as a constraint the later pairs of the same state must hold, since a segment between two contacts (the pelvis between the thighs) is pushed by each in turn and a relaxation that knew only the pair in hand undid the other. Eight rings of neighbours share each push and forty Laplacian sweeps relax the patch per round, for up to 24 rounds, inside a tissue budget per pair: 8 mm between digits, 10 at the toes, 25 for a limb against the trunk or the other limb, 50 at the knee and hip folds, 15 elsewhere.
- **Verified** through the public builder and the census instrument at the state, at the midpoint of the joint ramp and at half the shape's weight; the target pairs had to be gone, and a midpoint that still crossed was queued as a state of its own. The first round visited 295 single-axis states: 248 were clean, 43 needed a corrective, and with the queued midpoints the published single-axis correctives number 63. Four stayed beyond their budget in that round, both thighs at 125 degrees of pure flexion and both arms at 180 degrees of flexion; the thighs were solved in the third round as census findings once the contact solver held every separation a segment had already won and estimated a contact's plane from the crossing skin itself, and the arms were solved in the fourth round (Limits).
- **Combination**, `state/<set>:<finding>`: a state the census found crossing where each input alone did not, a published pose corrective's full angle worn by an extreme shape (the `shapes` set), a channel at its end (the `channels` set) or two joints at their extremes together (the `combos` set), solved with the same push and published as the product of one ramp per input. 1010 state correctives are published across four rounds, one per state and per queued midpoint, and the receipt names the round that solved each. The final round added 286 state correctives; 20 attempted states stayed beyond their tissue budget (13 combinations and seven shapes), recorded with the pairs that remained. The final census separately finds 16 crossing shape states and five crossing joint combinations; some have not been attempted in this round, so those counts are not interchangeable.

The first solver of this basis went through six rules before one held, and the failed ones are recorded here so they are not tried again: a nearest-surface push made a 145-degree elbow worse under linear blend skinning (the crease's triangles invert, so their normals point anywhere), a retreat along carried rest normals stalled on the Z-fold a fingertip makes over its knuckle, a fold plane with the seam pinned on it laid whole triangles of both segments coplanar, and one with the seam a clearance off it let the other segment's mixed triangles dip through the sheet. Under dual quaternion skinning the surface rule above is what holds, the planes are the fallback, and the contact-estimated plane with the kept constraints is what parted the thighs at 125 degrees of flexion and the heaviest body's groin.

## Measured: self-crossing census

[`census-receipt.json`](census-receipt.json) is merged from the shards of the census. Segments are the dominant-bone partition of the skin (`segmentHumanBodyModel`), and the instrument is the engine's `measureAutoMovieModelCrossings`, whose shared-corner exclusion is exact for a partitioned surface. A shard records every state it was asked for, a pose the builder refuses as a refusal rather than a crash, and the merge refuses a shard set with a hole. On the shipped revision:

The rest pose crosses on no pair, so every finding is absolute.

- `channels` (every channel at each endpoint, alone): 279 states, 279 clean, 0 with a crossing the rest does not have, 0 refused by the builder.
- `joints` (every mobile joint axis at the four fractions of its range, the other axes at the rest): 294 states, 294 clean, 0 with a crossing the rest does not have, 0 refused by the builder.
- `combos` (the arm raised straight up from the anatomical zero at 90, 135 and 180 of flexion, every parent-child flexion pair and the shoulder/hip abduction against elbow/knee flexion at their extremes): 158 states, 149 clean, 5 with a crossing the rest does not have, 4 refused by the builder. The four refusals combine shoulder retraction at its limit with upper-arm flexion and exceed the coupled clinical range; the builder refuses rather than clamps. The five crossings are `leftUpperArm.flexion@180+leftUpperArm.abduction@0` (neck x shoulder and shoulder x upper arm), `leftShoulder.flexion@30+leftUpperArm.flexion@180` and its right counterpart (upper chest, neck, shoulder and upper arm), and `leftUpperArm.flexion@180+leftLowerArm.flexion@145` and its right counterpart (upper chest, neck, shoulder and upper arm or lower arm). The receipt identifies every pair.
- `shapes` (every published pose corrective's full angle on each macro extreme and on every channel end past the source's unit node): 1596 states, 1580 clean, 16 with a crossing the rest does not have, 0 refused by the builder.
