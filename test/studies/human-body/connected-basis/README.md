# Connected body basis

`basis.json.gz` is the CC0 numerical body basis, revision `mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-21-envelopes`. It is the remainder of the MPFB base mesh that the face basis does not keep, with the shape channels, joint landmarks, skin weights, clinical joint limits, macro combination correctives and pose correctives the body editor evaluates. The generic evaluator lives in `@automovie/human`; this payload is study content. Replay needs this exact basis, not Blender.

The sources, their licenses and the anthropometric and clinical records are in the [parent study](../README.md). [`extraction-receipt.json`](extraction-receipt.json) pins the extraction (`mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-21-coronal-limits`): the script digest, Blender version, frame offset, correspondence and boundary checks, neutral recovery, mirror figures, per-endpoint displacement, the corrective sampling, the skin truncation and the constraint source of every joint. Three publication steps follow the extraction, each with its own receipt and revision id, and the shipped file is the last: [`pose-correctives-receipt.json`](pose-correctives-receipt.json) adds the pose correctives, [`individuality-receipt.json`](individuality-receipt.json) the twelve individuality channels, and [`envelope-receipt.json`](envelope-receipt.json) widens the envelopes of the channels whose source extremes stop short of the population and records the reach the simple tier then has, with the hashes of the shipped file.

## Derivation

`test/scripts/body-review/extract-body-basis.py` runs inside Blender 5.2 with the pinned MPFB revision enabled from source (`.references/mpfb2/src/mpfb`, junctioned into Blender's user extension repository). It creates the default human (every macro axis 0.5, equal ethnic mixture), binds the CC0 `weights.game_engine.json` as vertex groups, masks the helpers and applies one limit-surface Catmull-Clark round, which reproduces the face basis's head vertices exactly. Every state is read from Blender's evaluated depsgraph, so an endpoint is what the add-on itself shows, with one edit applied to every state before the cut: the nipple region is removed (below).

Coordinates are metres, Y up, Z forward, in the face basis frame: `x`, `z_blender - 1.5194313526153564`, `-y_blender`. The ground plane sits at Y = -1.5187 m in the neutral. The cut is not a plane: the body is the complement of the triangles the shipped face basis keeps. The face was cropped at the collar (`crop-face-to-neck`), where its photographic observation stops, and is open along a loop of 120 vertices between Y = -0.0808 and -0.0902 m that follows the neck's own slope; the body keeps every triangle of the same fan-triangulated source that the face does not, so the two meet along that loop by vertex identity (`boundary_agreement`: 120 face loop vertices, 120 body boundary vertices, worst distance 0.0 m). No vertex is interpolated: every body vertex is a source vertex. The first revision cut at the source asset's own shoulder plane Y = -0.145 m and left the neck between that plane and the face's collar to neither basis, which the editor showed as a head floating over the shoulders. 36,810 vertices; 73,498 triangles; one material region with the source UVs (V inverted for the glTF convention, verified corner by corner against the face).

## No nipple geometry

The product excludes genital and nipple geometry from the base model, its controls, presets and exports. The genitals are helper geometry the mask already drops. The nipples are skin, so the extraction replaces them: the region is the 538 subdivided vertices that the source's own `breast/nipple-size-incr` and `breast/nipple-point-incr` targets move, and it is refilled by a biharmonic solve with two rings of surrounding vertices (128) held fixed, so the fill continues the chest's curvature instead of a flat membrane (a harmonic fill was tried first and read as a flat disc). The fill is linear in the surrounding vertices, so it is applied identically to the neutral and to every endpoint, the neutral still recovers to the last bit, and a breast still forms under `macroGender`, `macroCupsize` and `macroFirmness` without its nipple. At the neutral the fill moves the region by 2.1 mm on average and 5.7 mm at most.

## Channels and endpoints

132 signed channels, each `[-1, 1]` with zero the source neutral, generated from MPFB's own `targets/target.json` and macro axes rather than typed by hand:

- 124 regional channels over `torso`, `arms`, `legs`, `hands`, `feet`, `neck`, `hip`, `pelvis`, `stomach`, `buttocks` and `breast`. A sided category becomes a `Left` and a `Right` channel that name each other in `mirror`. Endpoint names are the MPFB target paths. Twenty of them are MPFB's `measure-*` targets (bust, underbust, waist, hip, neck, upper arm, wrist, thigh, knee, calf and ankle girths; shoulder, front chest, nape to waist, waist to hip distances; upper arm, forearm, upper leg, lower leg and neck heights), which are the channels the editor prices in millimetres.
- 8 macro channels: `macroGender` (female at -1, male at +1), `macroAge` (child node 0.1875 at -1, old at +1), `macroMuscle`, `macroWeight`, `macroHeight`, `macroProportions` (uncommon at -1, ideal at +1), `macroCupsize`, `macroFirmness`. MPFB's macro system is piecewise linear between nodes, so each channel reproduces it exactly along its segment.
- 78 combination correctives over macro axis pairs. MPFB blends macro targets as products of node weights, so a pair at its extremes differs from the sum of its two endpoints; the residual is sampled directly (`f(a, b) - f(0) - Δa - Δb`) and published as a corrective driven by the two sides with the product activation of `createHumanFaceBasisBuilder`. All 112 pair states were sampled; 34 fell below 0.5 mm and are listed in the receipt as unpublished (every pair of height or proportions with cupsize or firmness, every gender x cupsize and gender x firmness pair, height x proportions, child x minimum height, and a few muscle or weight pairs with the breast axes, all at a micrometre except muscle-min x firmness-max at 0.42 mm). The largest is child x maximum height at 125.5 mm, which is what makes a tall child a different body from a scaled adult.

Excluded by decision: `genitals/*`, `pelvis/bulge-*`, `breast/nipple-*`, every face folder, `asym/*`, and the macro ethnicity axes. Neck channels are kept: the neck below the collar is the body's now, and the seam with the face's own neck controls (`neckWidth`, `neckDepth`, `neckUnderChinFullness`, `globalAgeStructure`, `globalSexualDimorphism`, `globalAdiposity`, `globalMuscularity`) is owned by the separate combination stage, which also has to move the face when a body channel moves the collar loop (the editor seats a neutral face on the head bone and shows that gap when it opens).

Endpoint rows are `[vertex, dx, dy, dz]` rounded to a micrometre with exact-zero rows omitted. Every state restores to the neutral to the last bit (`neutralRecoveryMaximumMetres` 0.0). The neutral mirrors onto itself with a pairing RMS of 0.06 µm (worst 0.36 µm, 162 midline vertices, a proper involution); each right endpoint is the mirror of its left to within 0.067 mm.

## Individuality channels

[`individuality-receipt.json`](individuality-receipt.json) is written by `test/scripts/body-review/merge-individuality.ts` from the endpoints `generate-individuality.ts` authors. The source's regional controls grow and shrink tissue but do not express where a real body's tissue sits or shows: gluteal ptosis, the lower abdominal apron, android flank fat and gynoid outer-thigh fat, the hip dip, the definition of the rectus, deltoid and scapula, and the skeleton showing through a lean body. Twelve channels add those (`buttocksPtosis`, `stomachOverhang`, `flankFat`, `outerThighFatLeft/Right`, `hipDipLeft/Right`, `absDefinition`, `deltoidDefinitionLeft/Right`, `scapularDefinition`, `skeletalProminence`), each a procedural displacement field on the neutral surface written entirely as numbers in `individualityParameters.ts`: a tissue mask read off the source endpoint over the same anatomy (the buttock is what `buttocks-volume` grows) or gates over the surface, a coordinate over that mask (polar between its lower and upper rims, geodesic from the most lateral point of a slab, a height above an anchor, or the mask itself), and displacement terms along the normal or gravity with a plateau, bell or step profile, or a relief of grooves and ridges along skin curves drawn from landmarks (the linea alba and three tendinous intersections; the clavicle, costal margin, four ribs, iliac crest and fifteen spinous processes). `generate-individuality.ts` is only the engine that evaluates those rows; a trait is added or tuned as a row. The right side of a mirrored pair is the exact reflection of the left (residual 0.0000 mm in the receipt). The clinical scales behind the rows (Gonzalez 2006 for ptosis, Igwe's panniculus grades, the WHO android/gynoid split, the surface anatomy of the rectus sheath) are pinned in the wiki research note and the parameter file's comments. Each trait was rendered on a real GPU at its extremes and on the macro extremes it meets, and the `traits` census below reads every one of them clean.

## Envelopes and reach

The source's macro nodes are authored bodies, not population limits: with every channel at its authored extreme the basis reached 47 to 65 kg on a 1.66 m woman and 65 to 97 kg on a 1.80 m man, with the hip channel and the weight macro at their authored minimum its hip tape girth bottomed at 0.80 m, and with the waist channel and the weight macro at their maximum its waist topped at 0.85 m, which left the obese, the emaciated, the narrow-hipped and the apron-bellied outside the editor. An endpoint is a linear displacement field, so a weight past one is more of the same field, and the envelope is data on the channel. `extend-envelopes.ts` publishes these envelopes: `macroWeight` [-2.5, 5.5] and `macroMuscle` [-1, 2], whose fields cover the whole skin; the waist girth [-2, 3.5]; the hip, underbust, upper-arm and calf girths and the shoulder breadth [-2, 2]; the bust girth [-2, 1.25] and the thigh girth [-2, 1.75], where the rest pose starts to cross (the hanging arm, the other thigh); `stomachPregnant` [-1, 2] and `stomachOverhang` [0, 1.5]. The regional fat fields (arm, thigh, calf, buttocks) and the banded depots (flank, outer thigh) stay at their authored reach of one: rendered past it, each field's edge at the knee, the elbow or the gluteal fold stood as a shelf, and a bell band as a lump, so a heavier body rides the weight macro instead. The receipt records the reach the simple tier then has: along its mass direction (the weight macro alone, whose field covers the whole skin) at 30 years, 1.55 m woman 34 to 68 kg, 1.55 m man 43 to 74 kg, 1.75 m woman 47 to 89 kg, 1.75 m man 57 to 95 kg, 1.95 m woman 64 to 115 kg, 1.95 m man 76 to 122 kg; and each tape measurement's span, waist girth 0.50 to 0.98 m, hips girth 0.91 to 1.43 m, bust girth 0.70 to 1.19 m, underbust girth 0.64 to 0.79 m, shoulder breadth 0.29 to 0.44 m, thigh girth 0.48 to 0.73 m, upperarm girth 0.27 to 0.44 m, calf girth 0.20 to 0.54 m. The channel census reads every channel at its widened extremes, which is where an overextended field would show as a crossing.

## The simple tier

`@automovie/human` expands five numbers a person knows about a body (sex, age in years, stature, mass in kilograms, a muscularity) and four optional tape measurements (waist, hip and bust girth, shoulder breadth) into this basis's channel weights (`expandHumanBodySimpleShape`), and reads them back off any detailed shape (`projectHumanBodySimpleShape`). The table it evaluates, `HUMAN_BODY_SIMPLE_SHAPE`, is numbers only: the age nodes of the source (child 11 years, young 25, old 90), a sarcopenia loss of a third of the muscle between 30 and 80, gluteal ptosis rising with age and weight, the apron with the body mass index, flank fat on men and outer-thigh fat on women, the definition of the rectus, deltoid and scapula and the skeleton showing through, gated by Deurenberg's body fat estimate less the sex's essential fat, and firmness falling with age. Stature is solved by measurement against this basis's height rule plus the head allowance of the face basis (0.2196 m), mass through the skin volume the boundary-capped mesh encloses at Siri's density over the head-and-neck share (8.1%, Dempster), and each girth against its rule, together until the weights settle. The detailed tier stays canonical: applied over a detailed shape, the expansion keeps whatever the simple tier does not name. The archetypes of [`archetypes.json`](archetypes.json) (a slender woman, a small-busted wide-hipped one, a narrow-hipped thin one, an emaciated man, a model, a bodybuilder, a heavy muscular man, a thin man with a pot belly, an obese woman, a giant, an elderly woman and man) are the review population: verification samples of the reach, not targets.

## Joints, landmarks and skin

125 landmarks are the centroids of MPFB's `joint-*` helper cubes, published with their own sparse endpoint deltas so a joint follows the shape that moves it. 52 joints map the game_engine rig one to one onto the VRM humanoid slots (eyes and jaw are the face's): `hips` is the root at the pelvis cube, and each joint names its head and tail landmark. Each carries the flexion reference direction of its anatomical frame (anterior for the trunk, shoulder and hip; toward the shoulder for the elbow, so it folds in the plane of the arm; posterior for the knee; superior for the ankle and toes; the measured palmar direction for the wrist and fingers, and the across-palm direction from thumb to little finger for the thumb), the measured clinical signs of abduction and twist under that frame, the clinical angle each axis rests at, and its range of motion from the pinned clinical record. The receipt names the source of every joint:

- 38 CFR 4.71 Plates I and II for the limbs; 4.71a Note (2) split equally over the segments of each spinal chain; 4.71a's note on the digits for the fingers (MCP 0-90, PIP 0-100, DIP 0-80, with the engine table's 20 and 10 degrees of hyperextension kept at MCP and DIP, below the AAOS normals); AAOS for the thumb (CMC flexion 15 / extension 20, MCP 60 / 10, IP 80 / 20), the shoulder girdle (elevation 40 / depression 10; protraction and retraction stay at the engine table's 30 and 15 because this frame's sign for them is not pinned), hip extension and rotation, shoulder extension, and the first metatarsophalangeal joint (extension 70, which is this frame's positive toe flexion, and flexion 45).
- Adduction of the upper arm and of the thigh is limited to 10 degrees. The clinical figures (30 to 45) are measured with the limb carried in front of the trunk; a single-axis range describes the coronal plane alone, in which the limb can only press against the side of the chest or the other thigh, and the first census's `abduction@-30` states put the arm five centimetres inside the chest wall. The coupled adduction-with-flexion path is not modelled and is listed as a limit.
- The hip's swing cone equals its pinned flexion (125), so every pure-plane clinical maximum stays admissible; the engine table's 120 sat below it and refused the pose, which the rig admission now rejects as a contradiction, and which cost the first census 51 states before it was found.

The rest is not the clinical zero. The source stands in an A-pose, so each joint records the clinical angle its rest direction sits at (`neutral`) and the builder hands it to the engine as the rest frame's neutral, which turns a document's clinical angle into `(clinical - neutral) / sign` of rig rotation. Goniometry takes the relaxed standing posture as zero for the trunk, neck, hip, knee, ankle and toes, so those rest at zero; the upper limb measures its rest against the anatomical position: upper arm abduction 41.75 degrees, elbow flexion 43.06, wrist flexion 6.58 and radial deviation 10.99, index proximal 12.32 (identical on both sides). A T-pose is therefore `abduction 90, elbow 0`, and `abduction 180` raises the arm overhead rather than 180 degrees past the A-pose.

Skin weights are Blender's interpolation of the game_engine weights through the subdivision, truncated to the four largest influences per vertex and renormalized; the largest weight dropped anywhere is 0.042. The neck and head joints carry the neck below the collar, and the face the editor seats on the head bone follows the head rigidly, so a turned head opens a crack at the collar loop until the combination stage skins the face with the same weights.

## Measured: rigid segments, arcs, ranges

[`joints-receipt.json`](joints-receipt.json) is written by `test/scripts/body-review/review-joints.ts`. For each mobile joint axis the joint is posed alone at half its range from the rest. Over the axes whose segment has vertices bound by at least 0.999 to that bone, those vertices move as one rigid body with the bone: the Kabsch residual is at most 0.011 mm and every vertex keeps its distance from the pivot to within 0.109 mm, against a chord sagitta of up to 61.5 mm that a linear endpoint read at the same half angle would have produced. That is the number the joint contract exists for. The whole dominated segment, blend zone included, departs from rigid by an RMS of up to 16.3 mm, which is the linear-blend deformation the pose correctives repair. The receipt also lists every axis whose range differs from the engine's fallback table, which is the clinical record's side of the comparison in the [study README](../README.md).

## Measured: girth under the pose

`test/scripts/body-review/measure-girth.ts` cuts each posed limb by nine planes perpendicular to its bone and compares the closed section loop with the rest. Under linear blend skinning alone the elbow's girth fell 16% at forearm pronation 85, the knee's 7 to 8% at 140 degrees of flexion, the hip's 11% at 125 of flexion and the upper arm's 6% at 90 of twist: the candy wrapper and the pinch of the fold, measured rather than assumed. These are the losses the volume correctives below give back; the girth receipt after correction is recorded in `girth-after` of the review outputs and summarized in the wiki.

## Pose correctives

[`pose-correctives-receipt.json`](pose-correctives-receipt.json) is written by `test/scripts/body-review/merge-pose-correctives.ts` from the shards of `generate-pose-correctives.ts`. A pose corrective is a rest-space endpoint driven by a clinical joint angle as a ramp, `{bone, axis, side, onset, full}` in degrees past the rest, applied before skinning (`IAutoMovieHumanBodyBasis.correctives`); RigLogic's conditional table, applied to a joint. Every mobile joint axis was visited on each side of its rest at the census's two sample angles, in ascending travel, on a working basis that already wore the correctives accepted before:

- **Volume**, `vol/<bone>.<axis><side>@<angle>`: the rigid (dual quaternion) blend of the same skin minus the linear blend, where a millimetre or more was lost, outside the fold (inside a fold the rigid blend swings the crease out into the segment the joint closes against, where real skin folds in), ramping from the previous sample to this one. 129 of them.
- **Crossing**, `pose/<bone>.<axis><side>@<angle>`: where two skin segments still crossed with the volume worn, both were pushed apart in the posed frame and the displacement carried into the rest frame through the inverse of each vertex's blended bone rotation, which is exact under linear blend skinning. The `onset` is measured, not assumed: the largest angle at which nothing crosses, bisected to 2.5 degrees from the last clean angle, so a corrective ramps from where the contact starts and not from the rest. The push is a plane: through the joint bisecting the two rays that leave it for an adjacent pair (the fold plane, with the parent's ray flipped where the parent bone points away from the joint, as the hips bone does under the thighs), or perpendicular to the shortest line between the two bones for any other pair (the contact plane), whichever asks the crossing patches for less movement once it is centred between the deepest vertex of each side (the groin at 62 degrees of hip flexion is the inner thigh against the pubis, a contact, not a fold), then slid a millimetre a round toward whichever side still has budget; each side keeps two clearances (1 mm) off it, seam vertices one, and the crease gutter (a seam vertex that is a corner of the other segment's crossing triangle) sits on it. The budget per vertex is the tissue the pair gives: 8 mm for a digit, 10 for the toes, 15 default, 25 for arm against arm or trunk and leg against leg, 50 for the knee fold, whose calf stands 46 mm past the fold plane at 140 degrees on this rig, and 50 for the hip fold, a thigh against the pelvis or the belly. A vertex whose blend of bone rotations is near singular (half its weight on each side of a joint bent toward 180 degrees, where the rotations cancel) gets no rest row and is counted as inexpressible: its inverse would be exact at that one angle and a spike everywhere else on the ramp, and its direction is one the linear blend cannot express there. When a full angle stays beyond its budget, the midpoint between the last clean angle and it is queued as a state of its own, so the corrective reaches as far as the tissue gives and the receipt says where it stopped. 72 of them; 19 were queued midpoints (a corrective whose ramp still crossed half way got an in-between of its own, RigLogic's in-betweens).
- **Verified** through the public builder and the census instrument at the angle and at the midpoint of the ramp; the target pairs had to be gone. 6 states stayed beyond their budget and are unpublished, listed in the receipt with the pairs that remained: `leftUpperArm.flexion@180` (upperChest x leftShoulder, upperChest x leftUpperArm, neck x leftUpperArm, leftShoulder x leftUpperArm); `rightUpperArm.flexion@180` (upperChest x rightShoulder, upperChest x rightUpperArm, neck x rightUpperArm, rightShoulder x rightUpperArm); `leftUpperLeg.flexion@125` (hips x leftUpperLeg, spine x leftUpperLeg, chest x leftUpperLeg); `leftUpperLeg.flexion@95.703125` (hips x leftUpperLeg, spine x leftUpperLeg); `rightUpperLeg.flexion@125` (hips x rightUpperLeg, spine x rightUpperLeg, chest x rightUpperLeg); `rightUpperLeg.flexion@95.703125` (hips x rightUpperLeg, spine x rightUpperLeg). The two are the extremes this rig cannot fold: an arm straight overhead crosses the head, neck and chest with half its blend zone inexpressible, and a thigh past about 81 degrees of flexion stands centimetres inside the belly, which a real body reaches only by tilting the pelvis and flexing the lumbar spine (the `hips` and `spine` joints, which the document can add).

The solver went through six rules before these held, and the failed ones are recorded in the wiki decision rather than here: a nearest-surface push made a 145-degree elbow worse (linear blend skinning inverts the crease's triangles, so their normals point anywhere), a retreat along carried rest normals stalled on the Z-fold a fingertip makes over its knuckle, a fold plane with the seam pinned on it laid whole triangles of both segments coplanar, and one with the seam a clearance off it let the other segment's mixed triangles dip through the sheet.

## Limits

An authored upstream prior, not individual anatomy. The mass the simple tier reaches is what the weight macro's field gives when worn to 5.5 before it stops reading as more tissue: about a body mass index of 36 to 39 depending on sex and stature (class II obesity), and 14 to 16 at the thin end. Class III obesity needs an authored endpoint beyond the source's heaviest node, which no field here extrapolates; a request past the reach is refused with the reach. Two single-axis extremes stay uncorrected and are listed in the pose receipt: an arm at 180 degrees of pure humeral flexion and a thigh past about 81 degrees of pure hip flexion. Neither is a pose a body makes on one joint: an arm straight overhead is scapulohumeral rhythm, girdle elevation (`leftShoulder` abduction, up to 40 here) plus about 140 of humeral flexion, and a knee to the chest is hip flexion plus posterior pelvic tilt and lumbar flexion (`hips` and `spine` flexion); those chains are the anatomical route to both poses, the combination census below reads each parent-child pair at its extremes, and the clinical single-axis totals remain pinned as ranges so a goniometric angle is still a valid input, reported by the contact check when it crosses. Joints are hinge and ball approximations at cube centroids with no scapulohumeral rhythm, knee screw-home or carrying angle. Adduction across the trunk and across the other thigh needs flexion the single-axis ranges do not couple, and is not reachable. The face the editor seats on the head bone is a neutral face placed rigidly; a turned head or a body channel that moves the collar opens the loop until the combination stage skins the face with the body's weights. The pose correctives are solved one axis at a time and verified at their own angles and at adjacent pairs' extremes; a combination outside that census is not claimed.
