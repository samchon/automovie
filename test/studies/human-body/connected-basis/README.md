# Connected body basis

`basis.json.gz` is the CC0 numerical body basis, revision `mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-26-anatomy-r10`. It is the remainder of the MPFB base mesh that the face basis does not keep, with the shape channels (the source's and two musculature definition channels), joint landmarks, joint couplings, skin weights, clinical joint limits, macro combination correctives, pose correctives and the soft-tissue sag the body editor evaluates. The generic evaluator lives in `@automovie/human`; this payload is study content. Replay needs this exact basis, not Blender.

The sources, their licenses and the anthropometric and clinical records are in the [parent study](../README.md). [`extraction-receipt.json`](extraction-receipt.json) pins the extraction (`mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-21-coronal-limits`): the script digest, Blender version, frame offset, correspondence and boundary checks, neutral recovery, mirror figures, per-endpoint displacement, the corrective sampling, the skin truncation and the constraint source of every joint. The publication steps that follow the extraction each carry a receipt and a revision id, and the shipped file is the last: [`individuality-receipt.json`](individuality-receipt.json) adds the twelve individuality channels; [`envelope-receipt.json`](envelope-receipt.json) widens the envelopes of the channels whose source extremes stop short of the population; [`envelope-detail-receipt.json`](envelope-detail-receipt.json) extends them by volume rather than by detail and records the reach the simple tier then has; [`symmetry-receipt.json`](symmetry-receipt.json) makes the skin weights bilaterally symmetric, widens the shoulders' weight transition and records each symmetrization of the correctives; [`digits-receipt.json`](digits-receipt.json) puts the finger and toe joints where the articular heads are; [`couplings-receipt.json`](couplings-receipt.json) declares the scapulohumeral rhythm, read from the thorax-relative humerus, and the pelvifemoral rhythm; [`neutral-receipt.json`](neutral-receipt.json) parts the toes the source neutral lays into each other; [`pose-correctives-receipt.json`](pose-correctives-receipt.json) adds the pose correctives solved on that skeleton under dual quaternion skinning, the shoulder's on the lattice its census grid lays, and records the hashes of the shipped file; [`nipple-areola-receipt.json`](nipple-areola-receipt.json) refills the nipple-areola complex with a geometric fill; [`gluteal-receipt.json`](gluteal-receipt.json) adds the gluteal projection and records the simple tier's calibration against ANSUR II; [`census-receipt.json`](census-receipt.json) reads the result. The tooling that performs these steps is kept outside the repository; each receipt states the method and the digests of what it produced, which is what a reproduction is checked against.

## Derivation

The extraction script the receipt pins by digest runs inside Blender 5.2 with the pinned MPFB revision enabled from source (`.references/mpfb2/src/mpfb`, junctioned into Blender's user extension repository). It creates the default human (every macro axis 0.5, equal ethnic mixture), binds the CC0 `weights.game_engine.json` as vertex groups, masks the helpers and applies one limit-surface Catmull-Clark round, which reproduces the face basis's head vertices exactly. Every state is read from Blender's evaluated depsgraph, so an endpoint is what the add-on itself shows, with one edit applied to every state before the cut: the nipple region is removed (below).

Coordinates are metres, Y up, Z forward, in the face basis frame: `x`, `z_blender - 1.5194313526153564`, `-y_blender`. The ground plane sits at Y = -1.5187 m in the neutral. The cut is not a plane: the body is the complement of the triangles the shipped face basis keeps. The face was cropped at the collar (`crop-face-to-neck`), where its photographic observation stops, and is open along a loop of 120 vertices between Y = -0.0808 and -0.0902 m that follows the neck's own slope; the body keeps every triangle of the same fan-triangulated source that the face does not, so the two meet along that loop by vertex identity (`boundary_agreement`: 120 face loop vertices, 120 body boundary vertices, worst distance 0.0 m). No vertex is interpolated: every body vertex is a source vertex. The first revision cut at the source asset's own shoulder plane Y = -0.145 m and left the neck between that plane and the face's collar to neither basis, which the editor showed as a head floating over the shoulders. 36,810 vertices; 73,498 triangles; one material region with the source UVs (V inverted for the glTF convention, verified corner by corner against the face).

## No nipple geometry

The product excludes genital and nipple geometry from the base model, its controls, presets and exports. The genitals are helper geometry the mask already drops. The nipples are skin, so the extraction replaces them: the region is the 538 subdivided vertices that the source's own `breast/nipple-size-incr` and `breast/nipple-point-incr` targets move, and it is refilled by a biharmonic solve with two rings of surrounding vertices (128) held fixed, so the fill continues the chest's curvature instead of a flat membrane (a harmonic fill was tried first and read as a flat disc). The fill is linear in the surrounding vertices, so it is applied identically to the neutral and to every endpoint, the neutral still recovers to the last bit, and a breast still forms under `macroGender`, `macroCupsize` and `macroFirmness` without its nipple. At the neutral the fill moves the region by 2.1 mm on average and 5.7 mm at most.

That fill weighs every neighbour alike, so it follows the mesh's sampling rather than its shape: the breast is sampled about ten times more densely than the abdomen, and the same uniform fill misplaces a featureless abdominal disc by up to 15 mm. Over a breast it left the apex standing up to 4 mm above the dome around it, more as the cup grows, and in a three-quarter view that point read as a nipple. [`nipple-areola-receipt.json`](nipple-areola-receipt.json) records the refill that replaces it. Every vertex within 20 mm of the fill's centre is refilled; the mean adult areola is 38 to 40 mm across ([Hussain et al. 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12792541/); [600 breasts](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18626683/)). That is 480 vertices over both sides, refilled in the neutral and in every endpoint by the biharmonic fill in the cotangent Laplacian and lumped mass of the neutral (`Q = L M⁻¹ L`), with two rings held. The map is linear, so channels combine as before. The neutral moves by 3.1 mm at most, and 157 endpoints change inside the region by up to 8 mm. On built bodies with small and large cups, low firmness and at 55 years, the apex is a smooth dome.

## Channels and endpoints

132 signed channels, each `[-1, 1]` with zero the source neutral, generated from MPFB's own `targets/target.json` and macro axes rather than typed by hand:

- 124 regional channels over `torso`, `arms`, `legs`, `hands`, `feet`, `neck`, `hip`, `pelvis`, `stomach`, `buttocks` and `breast`. A sided category becomes a `Left` and a `Right` channel that name each other in `mirror`. Endpoint names are the MPFB target paths. Twenty of them are MPFB's `measure-*` targets (bust, underbust, waist, hip, neck, upper arm, wrist, thigh, knee, calf and ankle girths; shoulder, front chest, nape to waist, waist to hip distances; upper arm, forearm, upper leg, lower leg and neck heights), which are the channels the editor prices in millimetres.
- 8 macro channels: `macroGender` (female at -1, male at +1), `macroAge` (child node 0.1875 at -1, old at +1), `macroMuscle`, `macroWeight`, `macroHeight`, `macroProportions` (uncommon at -1, ideal at +1), `macroCupsize`, `macroFirmness`. MPFB's macro system is piecewise linear between nodes, so each channel reproduces it exactly along its segment.
- 78 combination correctives over macro axis pairs. MPFB blends macro targets as products of node weights, so a pair at its extremes differs from the sum of its two endpoints; the residual is sampled directly (`f(a, b) - f(0) - Δa - Δb`) and published as a corrective driven by the two sides with the product activation of `createHumanFaceBasisBuilder`. All 112 pair states were sampled; 34 fell below 0.5 mm and are listed in the receipt as unpublished (every pair of height or proportions with cupsize or firmness, every gender x cupsize and gender x firmness pair, height x proportions, child x minimum height, and a few muscle or weight pairs with the breast axes, all at a micrometre except muscle-min x firmness-max at 0.42 mm). The largest is child x maximum height at 125.5 mm, which is what makes a tall child a different body from a scaled adult.

Excluded by decision: `genitals/*`, `pelvis/bulge-*`, `breast/nipple-*`, every face folder, `asym/*`, and the macro ethnicity axes. Neck channels are kept: the neck below the collar is the body's now, and the seam with the face's own neck controls (`neckWidth`, `neckDepth`, `neckUnderChinFullness`, `globalAgeStructure`, `globalSexualDimorphism`, `globalAdiposity`, `globalMuscularity`) is owned by the separate combination stage, which also has to move the face when a body channel moves the collar loop (the editor seats a neutral face on the head bone and shows that gap when it opens).

Endpoint rows are `[vertex, dx, dy, dz]` rounded to a micrometre with exact-zero rows omitted. Every state restores to the neutral to the last bit (`neutralRecoveryMaximumMetres` 0.0). The neutral mirrors onto itself with a pairing RMS of 0.06 µm (worst 0.36 µm, 162 midline vertices, a proper involution); each right endpoint is the mirror of its left to within 0.067 mm.

## Individuality channels

[`individuality-receipt.json`](individuality-receipt.json) records the twelve endpoints and the numbers they were authored from; a thirteenth, `glutealProjection`, is written in the same engine and recorded with its calibration in [`gluteal-receipt.json`](gluteal-receipt.json) (below). The source's regional controls grow and shrink tissue but do not express where a real body's tissue sits or shows: gluteal ptosis, the lower abdominal apron, android flank fat and gynoid outer-thigh fat, the hip dip, the definition of the rectus, deltoid and scapula, and the skeleton showing through a lean body. Twelve channels add those (`buttocksPtosis`, `stomachOverhang`, `flankFat`, `outerThighFatLeft/Right`, `hipDipLeft/Right`, `absDefinition`, `deltoidDefinitionLeft/Right`, `scapularDefinition`, `skeletalProminence`), each a procedural displacement field on the neutral surface written entirely as numbers in a parameter table the receipt reproduces: a tissue mask read off the source endpoint over the same anatomy (the buttock is what `buttocks-volume` grows) or gates over the surface, a coordinate over that mask (polar between its lower and upper rims, geodesic from the most lateral point of a slab, a height above an anchor, or the mask itself), and displacement terms along the normal or gravity with a plateau, bell or step profile, or a relief of grooves and ridges along skin curves drawn from landmarks (the linea alba and three tendinous intersections; the clavicle, costal margin, four ribs, iliac crest and fifteen spinous processes). The generator is only the engine that evaluates those rows; a trait is added or tuned as a row. The right side of a mirrored pair is the exact reflection of the left (residual 0.0000 mm in the receipt). The clinical scales behind the rows (Gonzalez 2006 for ptosis, Igwe's panniculus grades, the WHO android/gynoid split, the surface anatomy of the rectus sheath) are pinned in the receipt's `research` entry. Each trait was rendered on a real GPU at its extremes and on the macro extremes it meets, and the `traits` census below reads every one of them clean.

## Gluteal projection

The buttock of the source stood too little behind the pelvis and too high, and no source channel carried its projection. This was measured by reproducing people of [ANSUR II](https://www.openlab.psu.edu/ansur2/), the 2012 anthropometric survey of US Army personnel (public data). Each person was built from their own sex, age, stature, mass and chest and buttock girths, one per stratum of body mass index (150 women and 150 men). Their unpinned measures were then read the way the survey reads them.

On r9:

- **Men.** The buttock stood 32 mm flatter than the person it reproduced (1.2 population standard deviations), with its greatest protrusion 45 mm too high.
- **Women.** The buttock stood 13 mm flatter, with its protrusion 16 mm too high.
- **Both sexes.** The flattening grows by about 3.5 mm per unit of body mass index.

The source channels do not reach it:

- The weight macro adds a thicker body's depth at the front.
- `buttocksVolume` grows the buttock sideways and low, with its largest backward move at 0.466 of stature.
- The source's muscle adds 3 to 7 mm there.

`glutealProjection` is written in the individuality engine:

- **Tissue.** The tissue is what `buttocks-volume` grows, facing back, with the cleft held.
- **Profile.** A normal bell over height, centred 1.5 cm below the hip joint on the neutral, where the survey measures the greatest protrusion (0.512 of stature). Its σ is 4.5 cm, so it fades up into the lumbar curve and down into the fold.
- **Reach.** The channel reaches +25 mm and −20 mm.

**ANSUR II people in the simple tier.** The same reproduction showed that women's bodies departed from the survey in these ways:

- **Waist.** Women carried their waist 32 mm too wide at the omphalion, and more so at a higher body mass index, while its depth was right.
- **Pinned hips.** With the hips pinned, a heavier woman's mass went to the waist's sides.
- **Bust point.** Their bust point stood 11.5 mm high below a body mass index of 20 and 11 mm low at 26 to 30.
- **Crotch.** Their crotch stood 23 mm low at every body mass index, so their legs read short.

The table's ANSUR II people rows set a woman's `glutealProjection`, `hipScaleHoriz`, `hipScaleDepth`, thigh and calf fat and `breastTransDownUp` over body mass index knots 18, 22, 26, 30 and 35:

- **Pelvis.** A shorter pelvis (`hipScaleVert`) is set to −0.6 for women from a body mass index of 18 to 35 and fades like the other rows, since a thin woman of 90 folded her thigh with it. Per unit, with stature, girths and mass re-solved, it raises the crotch by 20 mm and lowers the buttock's greatest protrusion by 6 mm. At −1 the pelvis also raises the hips' smallest reachable girth past ordinary women's, so −0.6 is the largest that still builds every surveyed woman of the subset.

## Musculature definition

[`definition-receipt.json`](definition-receipt.json) records how two channels carry the definition of the whole superficial musculature, `musculatureDefinition` and `chestDefinition`. The hand-authored reliefs above cover only the rectus, the deltoid and the scapula. The source's own sculpted muscle is read instead of drawing each muscle by hand: its lean muscular body (muscle at its node, weight at its minimum) less its lean average body, along the neutral's normals, is broad volume plus the bellies and the grooves between them. A surface band-pass separates the two: the difference diffused by 4 uniform-Laplacian sweeps, less the same diffused by 200. That leaves the relief of the superficial muscles alone (median 0.02 mm, 95th percentile 4.2 mm, largest 11.3 mm).

- **Removed**:
  - The relief fades to nothing within 40 mm of the nipple fill's centres, so the fill's boundary does not read as a ring. The centres are recovered from the neutral itself, where the fill's uniform bilaplacian vanishes: (±0.102, −0.311, 0.146).
  - It fades likewise at the crotch cut on the midline.
  - It steps aside where an authored relief already carves, so no groove is cut twice.
- **The breast mound**: the part over it is split into its own channel, because on a woman the gland and its fat cover the pectoralis. The mound is the region the source's breast volume endpoint moves.
- **Symmetry**: both channels are made exactly bilaterally symmetric, in the field and in the normals (residual 0.0000 mm).

The simple tier drives them from muscle and from the fat the definition reads (below). `chestDefinition` is gated by sex, 0.15 on a woman and 1 on a man.

## Body composition in the simple tier

Deurenberg's regression gives body fat from the mass index, age and sex, and knows no muscularity. A trained body at an athlete's mass index therefore read the fat of an untrained one, and its definition gates stayed shut.

The fat the definition rows read now subtracts the fat-free mass the muscle parameter adds: `100 · Δ · muscle / BMI` points, with Δ the fat-free mass index one unit of muscle adds. The median young man's fat-free mass index is 18.9 and the young woman's 15.4 ([Schutz et al. 2002](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ijo.0802037)); natural male athletes average 21.8 ([Kouri et al. 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7496846/)). So Δ is 2.2 for a man, which takes an average young man at a mass index of 24 to that 21.8, and 1.8 for a woman, the same share of her median.

The references are adult, and so is every row calibrated on training. Training builds little muscle before puberty: children's strength gains are neural rather than hypertrophic ([Faigenbaum et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e31819df407); [Lloyd et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2013-092952)). The muscle spurt follows peak height velocity, at 11.8 years in girls and 13.5 in boys ([Baxter-Jones et al. 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18467546/)); lean mass grows fastest in the year after it, and the spurt lasts about two years ([Tanner et al. 1981](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7337414/)).

So a maturity ramp runs from a year before peak height velocity to three years after it: 10.8 to 14.8 years for a girl, 12.5 to 16.5 for a boy. The ramp is an authored bridge over those findings, not a fitted curve. The developed muscle is the muscle parameter times that ramp. The shift above reads it, and so do the rows calibrated on adult training: the regional muscle, the gluteal mass, tone and lift, the abdominal tone, the trained V, the adipose chest the pectoral holds back, the definition reliefs and the skeletal prominence. A child keeps the regression and the source's own child node, and the mass model's density keeps the regression too.

Before this ramp, the reliefs and the gluteal rows reached children at an adult's gain. At r6 that crossed the pelvis of a muscular obese 11-year-old boy (97 triangles) and the upper arms of a muscular thin 11-year-old girl, whose musculature relief read 0.865.

A swimmer of 1.90 m and 86 kg at muscle 1.1 reads 7.4% fat, inside the 8–12% competitive swimmers report, where the regression alone read 17.6%.

The simple tier's muscle now reaches 2, the source's competition node, a fat-free mass index near the natural limit of 25 at an athlete's mass index.

The definition bands are one curve over the fat above each sex's essential fat. The rectus shows in outline at about 10–12% on a man and 20–22% on a woman, and distinctly below about 9% and 16% (consumer body-composition guidance, not a clinical study).

## Soft-tissue sag

Soft tissue hangs from its body under gravity. The source's rest shape already carries that hang, so only a change of gravity's direction in the skin's own frame moves the tissue. The basis declares a sag on its surface.

- **Tissue.** A vertex carries the tissue the document's rest body has over the same body made lean, measured along the rest normal. The lean body sets the weight macro to the source's thin node, the regional fat channels to zero and the cup size to its minimum.
- **Compliance.** Compliance is that tissue times a gain of 0.2 and a softness.
  - Softness is 0.6 + 0.7 · `macroAge` − 0.3 · `macroMuscle`, held in [0.2, 1.5].
  - Cutometer elasticity falls with age at every measured site. Muscle keeps tissue firm.
- **Displacement.** A vertex moves by the compliance times `down − R · down`, where `R · down` is read by skinning a point a centimetre below the vertex. The field is smoothed over eight half-steps, is held at the neck's cut, and is zero at the rest pose.
- **Calibration.** A breast about 8 to 10 cm thick moves 3 to 6 cm between prone and supine in MRI ([Sci Rep 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-61802-9)). That is 0.3 to 0.6 of its thickness for gravity reversed, which the model gives as `gain · softness · 2`.

Measured on the published revision, as the largest displacement:

| Body | Prone or supine | Forward bend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60-year-old woman, BMI 32 | 23 mm | 13 mm |
| 25-year-old swimmer, 1.90 m and 86 kg | 1.5 mm | 0.6 mm |

The field is smoothed, so the peaks of a thick breast or belly move less than a local model would move them.

## Envelopes and reach

The source's macro nodes are authored bodies, not population limits: with every channel at its authored extreme the basis reached 47 to 65 kg on a 1.66 m woman and 65 to 97 kg on a 1.80 m man, with the hip channel and the weight macro at their authored minimum its hip tape girth bottomed at 0.80 m, and with the waist channel and the weight macro at their maximum its waist topped at 0.85 m, which left the obese, the emaciated, the narrow-hipped and the apron-bellied outside the editor. An endpoint is a linear displacement field, so a weight past one is more of the same field, and the envelope is data on the channel. The envelope step publishes these envelopes: `macroWeight` [-2.5, 9] (5.5 in the previous revision; widened with its detail separation regenerated, below, so the simple tier reaches class III obesity) and `macroMuscle` [-1, 2], whose fields cover the whole skin; the waist girth [-2, 3.5]; the hip, underbust, upper-arm and calf girths and the shoulder breadth [-2, 2]; the bust girth [-2, 1.25] and the thigh girth [-2, 1.75], where the rest pose starts to cross (the hanging arm, the other thigh); `stomachPregnant` [-1, 2] and `stomachOverhang` [0, 1.5]. The regional fat fields (arm, thigh, calf, buttocks) and the banded depots (flank, outer thigh) stay at their authored reach of one: rendered past it, each field's edge at the knee, the elbow or the gluteal fold stood as a shelf, and a bell band as a lump, so a heavier body rides the weight macro instead. Past its node a channel grows by volume, not by detail. An endpoint's low spatial frequencies carry where tissue is added and its high frequencies the rolls and creases of the one body the source sculpted; worn linearly to four times its node, the heaviest source body's centimetre rolls at the flank and under the scapula stood as four-centimetre shelves on a 105 kg man, although each input alone was clean. [`envelope-detail-receipt.json`](envelope-detail-receipt.json) records the repair. An endpoint both moves the skin and adds tissue along its normal, so each extended side is separated in two passes of implicit heat diffusion over the skin (cotangent Laplacian with a lumped mass, the neck boundary held): a vector pass at a segment's scale (150 mm) keeps the motion, a translation of a whole arm included, and the residual's thickness along the rest surface's normal is then split at 40 mm, between the 5 to 10 cm width of a roll and the 20 to 40 cm scale of a depot, into a smooth part and a detail part. A thickness pass alone read a translated arm's normal component, which swings from outward to inward around every finger, as detail and crumpled the fingers of a widened shoulder; a vector pass alone cancelled a limb's uniform growth across its opposite sides. One corrective per side ramps from the node to the envelope's end and subtracts the detail's extrapolation, so past the node the field is the endpoint at its node plus the smooth part times the excess and nothing inside the node changes. A deeper roll or apron is the individuality channels' to make, on their clinical grades, and the rule leaves those channels to their linear extension: they are fields a generator computes from a grade rather than one sculpted body, their crease is their content, and held to their node the apron at 1.5 read as the apron at 1. The receipt records the reach the simple tier then has: along its mass direction (the weight macro alone, whose field covers the whole skin) at 30 years, 1.55 m woman 31 to 117 kg, 1.55 m man 40 to 122 kg, 1.75 m woman 44 to 149 kg, 1.75 m man 54 to 154 kg, 1.95 m woman 60 to 186 kg, 1.95 m man 72 to 194 kg; and each tape measurement's span (a girth read as a tape reads it, the section's convex hull), waist girth 0.50 to 1.01 m, hips girth 0.79 to 1.28 m, bust girth 0.69 to 1.04 m, underbust girth 0.64 to 0.79 m, shoulder breadth 0.29 to 0.44 m, thigh girth 0.48 to 0.69 m, upperarm girth 0.26 to 0.42 m, calf girth 0.20 to 0.53 m, read with that step's rules (the bust is now read at the nipple's height and the upper arm from its midpoint; `HUMAN_BODY_MEASUREMENTS`). The channel census reads every channel at its widened extremes, which is where an overextended field would show as a crossing.

## The simple tier

`@automovie/human` expands five numbers a person knows about a body (sex, age in years, stature, mass in kilograms, a muscularity) and seven optional tape measurements (waist, hip, bust, thigh, upper arm and calf girth, shoulder breadth) into this basis's channel weights (`expandHumanBodySimpleShape`), and reads them back off any detailed shape (`projectHumanBodySimpleShape`). The table it evaluates, `HUMAN_BODY_SIMPLE_SHAPE`, is numbers only: the age nodes of the source (child 11 years, young 25, old 90), a sarcopenia loss of a third of the muscle between 30 and 80, gluteal ptosis rising with age and weight, the apron with the body mass index, flank fat on men and outer-thigh fat on women, the definition of the rectus, deltoid and scapula and the skeleton showing through, gated by [Deurenberg et al. 1991](https://doi.org/10.1079/BJN19910073)'s separate child-through-15 and adult-from-16 body-fat estimates (linearly joined for fractional ages between 15 and 16) less the sex's essential fat, firmness falling with age, and the ANSUR II people rows (Gluteal projection above). Stature is solved by measurement against this basis's height rule plus the head allowance of the face basis (0.2196 m), mass through the skin volume the boundary-capped mesh encloses at Siri's density over an age-specific head-and-neck share ([Jensen 1989](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9290(89)90004-3) boys' longitudinal polynomial through 15, bridged at 15–16 to the 8.1% adult Dempster estimate), and each girth against its rule, together until the weights settle. The detailed tier stays canonical: applied over a detailed shape, the expansion keeps whatever the simple tier does not name. The archetypes of [`archetypes.json`](archetypes.json) (a slender woman, a small-busted wide-hipped one, a narrow-hipped thin one, an emaciated man, a model, a bodybuilder, a heavy muscular man, a thin man with a pot belly, an obese woman, a giant, an elderly woman and man) are the review population: verification samples of the reach, not targets.

## Joints, landmarks and skin

125 landmarks are the centroids of MPFB's `joint-*` helper cubes, published with their own sparse endpoint deltas so a joint follows the shape that moves it. 52 joints map the game_engine rig one to one onto the VRM humanoid slots (eyes and jaw are the face's): `hips` is the root at the pelvis cube, and each joint names its head and tail landmark. Each carries the flexion reference direction of its anatomical frame (anterior for the trunk, shoulder and hip; toward the shoulder for the elbow, so it folds in the plane of the arm; posterior for the knee; superior for the ankle and toes; the measured palmar direction for the wrist and fingers, and the across-palm direction from thumb to little finger for the thumb), the measured clinical signs of abduction and twist under that frame, the clinical angle each axis rests at, and its range of motion from the pinned clinical record. The receipt names the source of every joint:

Its generic axes are held. Its record is the total elevation 0 to 180 (38 CFR 4.71 Plate I), axial rotation -90 to 90 (Plate I) and the humeral joint sinus, the largest elevation each plane admits: `[-180, 10]` (the coronal adduction the chest wall stops, as the first census's `abduction@-30` states put the arm five centimetres inside it), `[-90, 60]` (extension, AAOS), `[-45, 90]` (horizontal extension 45 at 90 of elevation, AAOS 1965), `[0, 180]` and `[90, 180]` (abduction and flexion, Plate I), `[135, 90]` (horizontal flexion 135, AAOS 1965), linear between knots and periodic in the plane. The rest is the measured A-pose, plane 0.28 and elevation 41.75 on both sides.
- Adduction of the thigh is limited to 10 degrees for the same reason: a single-axis range describes the coronal plane alone, in which the thigh can only press against the other thigh.
- The hip's swing cone equals its pinned flexion (125), so every pure-plane clinical maximum stays admissible; the engine table's 120 sat below it and refused the pose, which the rig admission now rejects as a contradiction, and which cost the first census 51 states before it was found.

The rest is not the clinical zero. The source stands in an A-pose, so each joint records the clinical angle its rest direction sits at (`neutral`) and the builder hands it to the engine as the rest frame's neutral, which turns a document's clinical angle into `(clinical - neutral) / sign` of rig rotation. Goniometry takes the relaxed standing posture as zero for the trunk, neck, hip, knee, ankle and toes, so those rest at zero; the upper limb measures its rest against the anatomical position: elbow flexion 43.06, wrist flexion 6.58 and radial deviation 10.99, index proximal 12.32 (identical on both sides), and the humerus at elevation 41.75 in the lateral plane. The pelvis follows the thigh. The basis declares a pelvifemoral rhythm (`pelvifemoral`): the legs' document flexion is trunk-relative, and the larger lift tilts the pelvis posteriorly by 18.1% of it, 22.6 degrees at the leg's clinical 125 (Murray et al. 2002's standing unilateral figure, measured throughout the movement). Only the pelvis turns, about the line through both hip centres, so the hip centres, the trunk, the lifted thigh's authored direction and the other foot stay where they were; the pose is judged both as written and as the pelvic-relative hips and lumbar angle the tilt produces. Tully et al. 2002 measured the lumbar flexion that goes with it, and Dewberry et al. 2003's 13.1 to 35.5% for suspended bilateral lifts bounds the tilt a bilateral lift shares. The hip-flexion pose correctives of the previous revision were solved on a pelvis that did not turn and were retired with the rhythm.

Skin weights are Blender's interpolation of the game_engine weights through the subdivision, truncated to the four largest influences per vertex and renormalized; the largest weight dropped anywhere is 0.042. The interpolated weights were not bilaterally symmetric although the neutral surface and the landmarks are: 1,760 of the 18,486 mirror vertex pairs differed by more than 0.01 and 389 by more than 0.10 (worst 0.52, at the ankle and the hip), which folded the two knees at different angles under the same pose. [`symmetry-receipt.json`](symmetry-receipt.json) records the repair: each mirror pair takes the mean of its two weight sets with the bone names mirrored, a midline vertex the mean of its own and its mirrored set, truncated again to four influences (largest dropped 0.016). Around each shoulder the transition from the upper arm's weight to the girdle's and the trunk's was a band a few triangles wide, so under the blend below the armpit and pectoral skin bound to the arm swung with it into the chest when the arm moved forward and across, and a relaxed arm just below the A-pose pressed its own skin into the chest wall. The upper arm's blend zone (every vertex whose upper-arm weight lies strictly between zero and one, none influenced by the hand or a digit) is grown by six rings over girdle and trunk skin and its weights over the upper arm, the girdle and the trunk diffused for forty Jacobi sweeps with the outer ring held, then truncated to four influences. Both sides are diffused together, in one pass over the union of their regions: diffusing one and then reading its result for the other made the midline, where the regions meet, differ by side. Measured with no shoulder corrective, the left humerus's grid and between states crossed in 86 of 159 before and 65 after (9,808 triangles to 8,332), and the arm lowered to 10 degrees of elevation went clean in every plane from -45 to 45 degrees. The surface is posed by dual quaternion skinning (Kavan et al. 2007): each vertex blends the unit dual quaternions of its influences, so a joint carries its skin as a rigid arc with no candy-wrapper collapse under twist and no pinch at a fold, where the linear blend the previous revisions were skinned with lost girth (below). The correctives are solved on that skin; the previous revision's 1,357 linear-blend correctives were retired rather than carried, since a corrective that refills a linear blend's collapse is a bulge under a rigid one. Each bone's dual quaternion takes its sign once per pose, aligned parent to child, so every vertex travels the arc the skeleton travelled and a blend does not depend on the order its influences are listed in. Flipping each influence against a vertex's first one, as the fourth revision's evaluator did, tore the shoulder into strips with the arm raised forward overhead, a half turn from the A-pose: 1,363 vertices listing the arm first flipped the girdle against the chest. Only those forward-overhead states skin differently under the fix.

The upper arms, the forearms and the thighs spread their axial twist along their skin (`distributeTwist`), a rig's twist joints made continuous: each bone's rotation relative to its parent is split into a swing and a twist about its rest axis, and a vertex takes the swing whole and the twist in proportion to where it lies from the bone's head to its child's. With the twist taken at the joint, an upper arm turned about its own axis wrenched the armpit and pectoral skin round at the shoulder: with no corrective, the arm raised forward to 90 degrees and turned out 90 crossed on 112 triangles, and 6 with the twist spread; the arm raised forward in the plane 15 degrees inward of it went from 105 to 45. Elbow, forearm, hip and knee poses read the same with and without it.

## Skin colour by site

A document may name the cheek albedo the face wears (`skinColour.cheek`), and the body's skin is then coloured by anatomical site from it. The [International Skin Spectra Archive](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04857-5) measured the same subjects at the cheek and at body sites. Integrated under D65 and the CIE 1931 2° observer to linear sRGB, the reduction the face's skin-albedo norms use, and averaged per ancestry group and sex, each site is fitted per channel in logarithms, `albedo = exp(a) · cheek^b`, over 12 to 16 group means ([`skin-sites-receipt.json`](skin-sites-receipt.json)). Pigment absorbs multiplicatively, so a site follows the cheek as a power, and the cheek explains 74 to 98% of the site's variance across the groups. One cheek reading measures a person's pigmentation with noise, so a fit across individuals regresses toward the archive's mean: it predicted the darkest group's inner arm 30 to 36% too light in green and blue, where the group-mean fit is within 7%.

Every group reads lighter and less red on the protected inner arm than on the sun-exposed outer arm, and lighter on the palm than on the back of the hand. The palm's exponent is the smallest, so the darker the cheek, the lighter the palm relatively.

The archive has no trunk or leg site. The trunk, the thighs and the rest of the covered skin take the inner arm, constitutive pigmentation. The forearms take the outer arm, and the shanks and the backs of the feet half of each. The soles take the palm. The palm and the back of the hand are told apart by the normal along the fingers' flexion direction, read off the rig. Four centimetres below the neck's cut, the colour blends from the cheek's to the sites', so the body meets the face in its colour.

## Skin micro-relief

A document may turn on the skin's micro-relief (`skinDetail.strength`). The skin material then takes a tileable normal map generated from measured statistics, `HUMAN_BODY_SKIN_DETAIL`. Primary lines are 20 to 100 µm deep and secondary lines 5 to 40 µm, perpendicular to them (Hashimoto 1974, as quoted by [Zahouani et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/483/1/012012)). On a young adult's forearm the two primary families cross near right angles ([Diosa et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0241533)) and close about one polygon per square millimetre (Trojahn et al. 2015).

So the tile carries two primary families 0.8 mm apart and 40 µm deep, and their secondary lines a quarter millimetre apart and 12 µm deep. Each groove wanders, deepens and breaks along its length. Pores are 22 per cm², the middle of the body sites [Otberg et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.0022-202X.2003.22110.x) measured (14 on the calf to 32 on the upper arm), each about 90 µm across.

The tile is 10 mm square. It repeats over the body's own UV layout at that layout's median scale, 1.84 m of neutral skin per UV unit, from 1.18 to 2.66 m between the 5th and 95th percentile of triangles. The relief's strength rises with age by the forearm roughness Ra, 16.9 µm at 20 to 29 years and 28.5 µm at 60 to 74 ([Li et al. 2006](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00403-005-0628-y)). The elderly skin's loss of one line orientation ([Corcuff et al. 1991](https://doi.org/10.1093/geronj/46.6.m223)) is not modelled.

## Neutral repair

The source neutral lays neighbouring toes into each other: each toe segment crosses itself on 13 triangles before any channel or pose. The repair is in the neutral, not in a corrective every document would wear: the rest skin is measured segment against itself, each crossing's two sheets are parted by the pose correctives' contact rule inside the toes' 10 mm budget, and the displacement is added to the neutral positions (472 vertices, at most 4.1 mm, bilateral residual 0.03 µm). Endpoints are differences from the neutral and keep their meaning. [`neutral-receipt.json`](neutral-receipt.json) records it. ## Measured: rigid segments, arcs, ranges

[`joints-receipt.json`](joints-receipt.json) records, for each mobile joint axis posed alone at half its range from the rest on the skin before the pose correctives (which by design move the fold), how the skin follows the bone. Over the axes whose segment has vertices bound by at least 0.999 to that bone, those vertices move as one rigid body with the bone, except the twist of a bone that spreads it (below), whose skin shears along the bone by design: the Kabsch residual is at most 0.13 mm (a thigh's flexion) and every vertex keeps its distance from the pivot to below a micrometre, against a chord sagitta of up to 48.3 mm that a linear endpoint read at the same half angle would have produced. That is the number the joint contract exists for. The twist of a bone that spreads it shears its skin by 2.7 mm (the thigh) to 4.5 mm (the forearm) RMS at half its range, which is the spread. The whole dominated segment, blend zone included, departs from rigid by an RMS of up to 14.9 mm (the upper arm's elevation), which is the blend zone bending about the joint. ## Measured: girth under the pose

Each posed limb was cut by nine planes perpendicular to its bone and the closed section loop compared with the rest. Under the linear blend skinning of the previous revisions the elbow's girth fell 16% at forearm pronation 85, the knee's 7 to 8% at 140 degrees of flexion, the hip's 11% at 125 of flexion and the upper arm's 6% at 90 of twist: the candy wrapper and the pinch of the fold, measured rather than assumed. ## Pose correctives

[`pose-correctives-receipt.json`](pose-correctives-receipt.json) records the correctives, the states they were solved on, and the states that stayed beyond their budget. A pose corrective is a rest-space endpoint driven by a clinical joint angle as a ramp, `{bone, axis, side, onset, full}` in degrees past the rest, applied before skinning (`IAutoMovieHumanBodyBasis.correctives`); RigLogic's conditional table, applied to a joint. Every mobile joint axis was visited on each side of its rest at the census's two sample angles, in ascending travel, on a working basis that already wore the correctives accepted at the smaller angles of the same axis, and each combination finding of the census (below) was then visited on the basis that wore every single-axis corrective:

- **Onset** is measured, not assumed: the largest fraction of the pose at which nothing crosses, bisected to 2.5 degrees, so a corrective ramps from where the contact starts and not from the rest. A combination corrective is the product of one ramp per input: a channel ramp from the measured shape onset to the shape's weight, and a joint ramp over each posed axis from the measured onset to the full angle. The shape onset is read the same way on the finished stack: with every other corrective worn and the state's own switched off, the largest fraction of the shape, bisected to 1/32, at which the state in its full pose crosses on no more triangles than the neutral body in that pose. Worn from zero instead, a push solved on an extreme shape reached every milder body in part, and the milder bodies crossed where the shapeless correctives alone left them clean.
- **Shoulder lattice**: a thorax-relative humerus is corrected by orientation kernels (`{shoulder, orientation, innerDegrees, outerDegrees}`, the SO(3) distance from the kernel's centre, which reads the same for the equivalent triples at the 0 and 180 degree poles) centred on the nodes of the census's shoulder grid, one kernel per node and family, whose window reaches the nearest other node and never the rest. The builder divides a family's kernels (one humerus, every other input of their correctives identical) by their sum wherever it exceeds one, so that, as in pose space deformation (Lewis et al. 2000), the corrections solved at example poses are interpolated rather than summed (here by the normalized overlap of the kernels, not a radial-basis solve), and a pose wears at most one whole correction of a family and between nodes the neighbouring corrections blend. Each node is solved on its own, and a node that already carries a corrective of its family has that corrective's rows extended rather than a second stacked there. The kernels this replaces were centred wherever a state was solved (grid nodes, between samples, ramp midpoints, re-solves) and summed: a forward raise wore 18 to 32 of them at once with an activation sum of 6.5 to 11, and pushes each verified alone drove skin through skin together.
- **Shape examples**: a corrective read by a pose and by channels was solved on one shape in that pose, so the correctives of one pose solved on different shapes are examples of one correction across the shape space, and the builder interpolates them as it does the shoulder kernels: each set of channels and sides is an example, its factor the largest channel-ramp product among its correctives, and a body that reaches several examples of one pose has their factors divided by their sum where it exceeds one. Summed, the examples of a man's weight, age and sex all fired whole on a heavy old man with a bent knee (143 correctives whose activations summed to 116) and drove his thighs through each other; three held-out bodies bent at the knee crossed on 39 to 181 triangles with the examples summed, 18 to 57 with them interpolated, and 0 to 5 with no shape-and-pose corrective at all, which is what the measured shape onset then restored on the milder bodies.
- **Push**: the crossing corners of both segments are moved apart in the posed frame and the displacement carried into the rest frame through the inverse of each vertex's blended dual quaternion, which is exact under dual quaternion skinning (the carry was checked against the builder to the last bit). Rules are tried from the same start and the first that clears the pair is kept: a surface rule that moves each entered corner past the other surface by half the penetration plus a 1.5 mm clearance, repeated each sweep until the corner is out; the same rule holding its first target; a plane through the joint (the fold plane for an adjacent pair, the contact plane for any other); and a plane estimated from the contact itself, through the midpoint of the two crossing corner sets and normal to the difference of their centroids, which parts a fold whose joint sits far from it (the hip centre lies centimetres behind the groin crease). Every separation a pair wins is kept as a constraint the later pairs of the same state must hold, since a segment between two contacts (the pelvis between the thighs) is pushed by each in turn and a relaxation that knew only the pair in hand undid the other. Eight rings of neighbours share each push and forty Laplacian sweeps relax the patch per round, for up to 24 rounds, inside a tissue budget per pair: 8 mm between digits, 10 at the toes, 25 for a limb against the trunk or the other limb, 50 at the knee and hip folds and at the shoulder's root fold (the upper arm against the chest and girdle it folds onto, as the thigh folds onto the belly, and any segment such a fold joins, the upper arm, the girdle, the trunk or the thigh, folding onto itself), 15 elsewhere. A segment passing through itself (the inside of a bent elbow, an armpit, a roll lying on the skin below it) is parted the same way once its two sheets are named: the crossing relation between its triangles, two-coloured, gives each tangle its two sides even where they meet at the crease. A pair in which one segment's bone, the line between its joint centres, passes through the other's skin is a body passing through a body rather than tissue a pad can part, and is recorded as such without a push. So is a hand, forearm, digit, shank or foot meeting a segment outside its own limb (the forearm across the belly, the hand against the thigh behind the back): that contact is the pose's, not a fold's, and a pad of skin that parted it would hide what the editor's contact reading must report, so such a state is recorded as limb contact without a push.
- **Verified** through the public builder and the census instrument at the state, at the midpoint of the joint ramp and at half the shape's weight; the target pairs had to be gone, and a midpoint that still crossed was queued as a state of its own (a shoulder node has no ramp midpoint: its neighbours are the lattice's other nodes). The previous revision's single-axis correctives, solved on the neutral body one axis at a time in ascending travel, are kept wherever their joint is not the humerus or the hip; the receipt names the round that solved each.
- **Combination**, `state/<set>:<finding>`: a state the census found crossing where each input alone did not, a published pose corrective's full angle worn by an extreme shape (the `shapes` set), a channel at its end (the `channels` set) or two joints at their extremes together (the `combos` set), solved with the same push and published as the product of one ramp per input. A shape-only state's ramps are one-sided and hold past their full weight, so its corrective stays on at every body further along the same channels; solved side by side on separate shards, two such correctives were added together wherever both held and the heavier bodies crossed worse than before either was solved. Shape-only states are therefore solved on one shard in order of generality, fewest channels and then least weight first, so each corrective carries only what the more general ones left. The correctives are bilaterally symmetric, as the neutral, the landmarks and the skin weights are: solved one state at a time, a left state and its right counterpart each wore the basis its own shard had, and after two rounds a symmetric heavy body lowered its left arm to 36.5 degrees and its right to 32.0. After every merge each sided corrective is therefore replaced by the left one of its pair and that one's exact mirror (the vertex mirrored with its x displacement negated, the input names with their side swapped; a TT kernel's orientation reads the same on either arm), a sided corrective without a partner gains its mirror, and a corrective whose inputs name no side takes the mean of its field and the field's mirror; later rounds solve the left and midline states and mirror them. The receipt lists every published corrective with its round, and the states still beyond their tissue budget with the pairs that remained; the census below is read separately and its counts are not the solver's.

The first solver of this basis went through six rules before one held, and the failed ones are recorded here so they are not tried again: a nearest-surface push made a 145-degree elbow worse under linear blend skinning (the crease's triangles invert, so their normals point anywhere), a retreat along carried rest normals stalled on the Z-fold a fingertip makes over its knuckle, a fold plane with the seam pinned on it laid whole triangles of both segments coplanar, and one with the seam a clearance off it let the other segment's mixed triangles dip through the sheet. Under dual quaternion skinning the surface rule above is what holds, the planes are the fallback, and the contact-estimated plane with the kept constraints is what parted the thighs at 125 degrees of flexion and the heaviest body's groin.

## Rounds

The publication is iterated: census every set, solve the findings, merge, make the correctives symmetric, store them at 10 µm, census again. The previous revision's four rounds on dual quaternion skin left the correctives of every joint but the humerus in place; this revision retired its 408 fixed-axis upper-arm correctives and its hip-flexion correctives with the thorax-relative shoulder and the pelvic rhythm, and its rounds are:

1. The shoulder grid solved as orientation kernels, and the neutral toe repair.
2. Every census finding solved on that basis. Its shape-only correctives, solved side by side on separate shards, stacked on the heavier bodies (the simple tier's body matrix went from 44 crossing bodies to 60); the 142 of them were retired and the shape-only states re-solved on one shard in order of generality, which took the body matrix to one crossing body.
3. The first symmetrization.
