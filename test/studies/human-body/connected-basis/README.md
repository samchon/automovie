# Connected body basis

`basis.json.gz` is the first CC0 numerical body basis, revision `mpfb-connected-body-2026-09-20-joints-and-measures`. It is the remainder of the MPFB base mesh below the face basis's neck clip, with the shape channels, joint landmarks, skin weights, clinical joint limits and macro combination correctives the body editor evaluates. The generic evaluator lives in `@automovie/human`; this payload is study content. Replay needs this exact basis, not Blender.

The sources, their licenses and the anthropometric and clinical records are in the [parent study](../README.md). [`extraction-receipt.json`](extraction-receipt.json) pins this revision: the script digest, Blender version, frame offset, correspondence and ring checks, neutral recovery, mirror figures, per-endpoint displacement, the corrective sampling, the skin truncation and the constraint source of every joint.

## Derivation

`test/scripts/body-review/extract-body-basis.py` runs inside Blender 5.2 with the pinned MPFB revision enabled from source (`.references/mpfb2/src/mpfb`, junctioned into Blender's user extension repository). It creates the default human (every macro axis 0.5, equal ethnic mixture), binds the CC0 `weights.game_engine.json` as vertex groups, masks the helpers and applies one limit-surface Catmull-Clark round, which reproduces the face basis's head vertices exactly. Every state is read from Blender's evaluated depsgraph, so an endpoint is what the add-on itself shows, with one edit applied to every state before the clip: the nipple region is removed (below).

Coordinates are metres, Y up, Z forward, in the face basis frame: `x`, `z_blender - 1.5194313526153564`, `-y_blender`. The ground plane sits at Y = -1.5187 m in the neutral. The clip keeps Y <= -0.145 m. Each subdivided quad is split along the diagonal from its first loop corner before clipping, because that is how the face was clipped: 104 quad edges and 96 diagonals cross the plane, giving the 200 ring vertices that coincide with the face ring to 0.0 m. Ring vertices are affine combinations `a + t (b - a)` of their edge ends, evaluated with the neutral `t` for every endpoint, so a state that moves the neck moves the ring identically on both sides of the seam. 35,787 original vertices plus the 200 ring vertices make 35,987; 71,772 triangles; one material region with the source UVs (V inverted for the glTF convention, verified corner by corner against the face).

## No nipple geometry

The product excludes genital and nipple geometry from the base model, its controls, presets and exports. The genitals are helper geometry the mask already drops. The nipples are skin, so the extraction replaces them: the region is the 538 subdivided vertices that the source's own `breast/nipple-size-incr` and `breast/nipple-point-incr` targets move, and it is refilled by a biharmonic solve with two rings of surrounding vertices (128) held fixed, so the fill continues the chest's curvature instead of a flat membrane (a harmonic fill was tried first and read as a flat disc). The fill is linear in the surrounding vertices, so it is applied identically to the neutral and to every endpoint, the neutral still recovers to the last bit, and a breast still forms under `macroGender`, `macroCupsize` and `macroFirmness` without its nipple. At the neutral the fill moves the region by 2.1 mm on average and 5.7 mm at most.

## Channels and endpoints

132 signed channels, each `[-1, 1]` with zero the source neutral, generated from MPFB's own `targets/target.json` and macro axes rather than typed by hand:

- 124 regional channels over `torso`, `arms`, `legs`, `hands`, `feet`, `neck`, `hip`, `pelvis`, `stomach`, `buttocks` and `breast`. A sided category becomes a `Left` and a `Right` channel that name each other in `mirror`. Endpoint names are the MPFB target paths. Twenty of them are MPFB's `measure-*` targets (bust, underbust, waist, hip, neck, upper arm, wrist, thigh, knee, calf and ankle girths; shoulder, front chest, nape to waist, waist to hip distances; upper arm, forearm, upper leg, lower leg and neck heights), which are the channels the editor prices in millimetres.
- 8 macro channels: `macroGender` (female at -1, male at +1), `macroAge` (child node 0.1875 at -1, old at +1), `macroMuscle`, `macroWeight`, `macroHeight`, `macroProportions` (uncommon at -1, ideal at +1), `macroCupsize`, `macroFirmness`. MPFB's macro system is piecewise linear between nodes, so each channel reproduces it exactly along its segment.
- 78 combination correctives over macro axis pairs. MPFB blends macro targets as products of node weights, so a pair at its extremes differs from the sum of its two endpoints; the residual is sampled directly (`f(a, b) - f(0) - Δa - Δb`) and published as a corrective driven by the two sides with the product activation of `createHumanFaceBasisBuilder`. All 112 pair states were sampled; 34 fell below 0.5 mm and are listed in the receipt as unpublished (every pair of height or proportions with cupsize or firmness, every gender x cupsize and gender x firmness pair, height x proportions, child x minimum height, and a few muscle or weight pairs with the breast axes, all at a micrometre except muscle-min x firmness-max at 0.42 mm). The largest is child x maximum height at 125.5 mm, which is what makes a tall child a different body from a scaled adult.

Excluded by decision: `genitals/*`, `pelvis/bulge-*`, `breast/nipple-*`, every face folder, `asym/*`, and the macro ethnicity axes. Neck channels are kept although the neck itself is above the clip: they move the ring band by up to 2.4 mm (and `neckDouble` by micrometres), and the seam with the face's own neck controls (`neckWidth`, `neckDepth`, `neckUnderChinFullness`, `globalAgeStructure`, `globalSexualDimorphism`, `globalAdiposity`, `globalMuscularity`) is owned by the separate combination stage, which also has to translate the face by the ring's rise under `macroHeight` because the face subtracts its binocular frame while the body keeps its feet on the ground.

Endpoint rows are `[vertex, dx, dy, dz]` rounded to a micrometre with exact-zero rows omitted. Every state restores to the neutral to the last bit (`neutralRecoveryMaximumMetres` 0.0). The neutral mirrors onto itself with a pairing RMS of 63 nm (worst 0.36 micrometres, 143 midline vertices, a proper involution); each right endpoint is the mirror of its left to within 0.067 mm.

## Joints, landmarks and skin

125 landmarks are the centroids of MPFB's `joint-*` helper cubes, published with their own sparse endpoint deltas so a joint follows the shape that moves it. 52 joints map the game_engine rig one to one onto the VRM humanoid slots (eyes and jaw are the face's): `hips` is the root at the pelvis cube, and each joint names its head and tail landmark. Each carries the flexion reference direction of its anatomical frame (anterior for the trunk, shoulder and hip; toward the shoulder for the elbow, so it folds in the plane of the arm; posterior for the knee; superior for the ankle and toes; the measured palmar direction for the wrist and fingers, and the across-palm direction from thumb to little finger for the thumb), the measured clinical signs of abduction and twist under that frame, the clinical angle each axis rests at, and its range of motion from the pinned clinical record: 38 CFR 4.71 Plates I and II for the limbs, 4.71a Note (2) split equally over the segments of each spinal chain, AAOS where the regulation is silent, and the engine's fallback where nothing clinical was pinned (shoulder girdle, foot inversion, toes, finger phalanges). The receipt names the source of every joint.

The rest is not the clinical zero. The source stands in an A-pose, so each joint records the clinical angle its rest direction sits at (`neutral`) and the builder hands it to the engine as the rest frame's neutral, which turns a document's clinical angle into `(clinical - neutral) / sign` of rig rotation. Goniometry takes the relaxed standing posture as zero for the trunk, neck, hip, knee, ankle and toes, so those rest at zero; the upper limb measures its rest against the anatomical position: upper arm abduction 41.75 degrees, elbow flexion 43.06, wrist flexion 6.58 and radial deviation 10.99, index proximal 12.32 (identical on both sides). A T-pose is therefore `abduction 90, elbow 0`, and `abduction 180` raises the arm overhead rather than 180 degrees past the A-pose.

Skin weights are Blender's interpolation of the game_engine weights through the subdivision, truncated to the four largest influences per vertex and renormalized; the largest weight dropped anywhere is 0.042. The neck joint sits 33 mm above the clip and is kept because the ring band carries its weight.

## Measured: rigid segments, arcs, ranges

[`joints-receipt.json`](joints-receipt.json) is written by `test/scripts/body-review/review-joints.ts`. For each of the 77 mobile joint axes the joint is posed alone at half its range from the rest. Over the 48 axes whose segment has vertices bound by at least 0.999 to that bone, those vertices move as one rigid body with the bone: the Kabsch residual is at most 0.011 mm and every vertex keeps its distance from the pivot to within 0.109 mm, against a chord sagitta of up to 61.5 mm (knee), 57.4 mm (shoulder flexion) and 48.3 mm (hip flexion) that a linear endpoint read at the same half angle would have produced. That is the number the joint contract exists for. The whole dominated segment, blend zone included, departs from rigid by an RMS of up to 16.3 mm at the shoulder and 14.3 mm at the hip, which is the linear-blend deformation the pose correctives inherit. The neck and head dominate no skin vertex below the clip and are listed as such. The receipt also lists every axis whose range differs from the engine's fallback table, which is the clinical record's side of the comparison in the [study README](../README.md).

## Measured: self-crossing census

[`census-receipt.json`](census-receipt.json) is merged from the shards of `test/scripts/body-review/census-crossings.ts`. Segments are the dominant-bone partition of the skin (`segmentHumanBody.ts`), and the instrument is the engine's `measureAutoMovieModelCrossings`, whose shared-corner exclusion was made exact for a partitioned surface in this change (two neighbouring skin triangles used to report each other through a rounding residue). The rest pose crosses on no pair. Every one of the 264 channel endpoints, alone, crosses on no pair either. Of the 249 single-axis joint states (each mobile axis at -1, -1/2, 1/2 and 1 of its range), 173 are clean and 76 create crossings, all of them the fold or contact of a limb into the tissue it bends toward: the arm into the chest at adduction (-15 to -30) and at flexion 180 and twist 90, the forearm into the upper arm at 145, the calf into the thigh at 140 and one side already at 70, the thigh into the other thigh and shank at hip adduction -30, the thigh into the pelvis at hip flexion 62.5, the toes into the foot at dorsiflexion 40 and 80, and every finger phalanx into its parent at 100 with the thumb from 50. These are the states the pose correctives (next topic) address, with a push-out budget and a receipt of what stays over budget; the list is finite and enumerated here rather than found by eye. One asymmetry is recorded rather than smoothed over: `leftUpperArm.abduction@-15` and `rightLowerLeg.flexion@70` cross while their mirrors do not, which the mirror residual of 0.067 mm between the source's left and right endpoints and the two sides' independently interpolated weights explain and the next topic re-measures after correction.

## Limits

An authored upstream prior, not individual anatomy. Joints are hinge and ball approximations at cube centroids with no scapulohumeral rhythm, knee screw-home or carrying angle. Nothing here establishes collision-free or physiological combinations; the census and pose correctives of later topics measure that.
