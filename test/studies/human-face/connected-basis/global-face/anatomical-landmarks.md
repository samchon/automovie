# Candidate anatomical landmarks on the selected shared face basis

The [candidate map](anatomical-landmarks.json) is an immutable source annotation for `basis.json.gz` at SHA-256 `c2e9c3fbb73d1ea0a9e9e4ec6808a6ccc7082730007f6418de38812b6be1acde`. It is not a personal document, a face-edit input, or an accepted clinical correspondence. Its protocol names the 3D Facial Norms cheilion definition: the soft-tissue labial commissure on each side ([3DFN measurement table](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4841054/), [3D landmark-definition study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/joa.12407)).

The neutral source `Human` surface has 16,942 vertices and separate `skin`/`lips` material regions. The maximum and minimum X coordinates among its 3,040 lip-region triangles occur at source vertices 9183 (+X, anatomical left) and 1390 (-X, anatomical right). Each vertex participates in both `skin` and `lips` triangles (three of each). Their neutral positions are `[0.026431992650032043, -0.032529234886169434, 0.12743662297725677]` and `[-0.026431992650032043, -0.032529354095458984, 0.12743662297725677]` metres, in the source frame. A neutral GPU render with those vertices marked places both at the visible oral commissures in the front view; the marking geometry is a disposable diagnostic, not part of the model.

Under each of the eighteen published identity shapes with expression removed, the fixed pair's **3D Euclidean** separation is 37.647–56.778 mm. The original X-only probe was independently checked against all three coordinate differences; its non-X pair separation reaches at most 0.0011 mm on these shaped faces. In seventeen, the pair is still the lip region's left/right X extrema. In `generated-black-boy-01`, two adjacent vertices are 0.165 and 0.166 mm farther out, so using the *current* extrema as landmark identities would change the measured site. With each document's requested expression also applied as rest-space residual rows, the fixed pair's X span is at most 0.877 mm narrower than the then-extreme lip X span. These comparisons use `humanFaceBasisWeights` and `evaluateHumanFaceRest` without the subsequent articulation/contact pass; they establish source-topology stability, not posed or exported measurement accuracy.

Holding every other published shape weight fixed and setting `mouthWidth` to -1, 0 and +1 yields strictly increasing fixed-pair Euclidean widths in each of the eighteen examples. The selected subject intervals span 34.565–66.050 mm across the observed minimum/maximum endpoints; this is source *reachability*, not an age- or population-supported human interval. The endpoint computation was cross-checked independently to within 2e-14 mm.

The basis declares `mouthWidth` on [-1,1] and has no corrective driven by that channel. `evaluateHumanFaceRest` therefore makes the fixed-pair difference vector affine on each sign segment while all other shape weights are held fixed. If `d` is the pair difference at a segment start and `s` is its per-unit slope, the derivative of its Euclidean length has the sign of `d · s`. That numerator increases by `s · s` per unit of weight. The smallest start numerators among the eighteen published identities are 277.097 and 368.096 mm² on the negative and positive segments, so each of those identities is strictly monotone over the entire channel domain, not merely at three samples.

A conservative extension bounds every other shape channel independently over its declared endpoint interval and bounds every corrective activation independently in [0,1]. Summing each contribution's smallest dot product with the corresponding `mouthWidth` slope still leaves positive lower bounds of 25.320 mm² on [-1,0] and 96.602 mm² on [0,1]. Thus the fixed-pair neutral Euclidean distance is strictly increasing for **every admitted identity shape combination on this exact basis**, with expression absent. This bound intentionally ignores correlations among corrective drivers, making it weaker but safe. It does not show that the fixed vertices remain anatomically correct commissures at every extreme, that posed articulation/contact preserves the metric, or that any particular width is physiologically valid. The source map remains `candidate` until these separate conditions and the final generated surface are reviewed.
