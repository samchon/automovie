# Candidate source cheilion correspondence

The [basis-bound annotation](anatomical-landmarks.json) uses SHA-256 `c2e9c3fbb73d1ea0a9e9e4ec6808a6ccc7082730007f6418de38812b6be1acde` of `basis.json.gz`. It is a shared source hypothesis, never an individual's face-edit input. The intended landmark is the soft-tissue labial commissure at which the upper and lower lips meet under the [3D Facial Norms protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4841054/). The [first lateral lip-extrema pair](rejected-cheilion-extrema.md) was rejected because skin occluded both vertices by 10.4 mm despite a monotone pair distance; its distance proof cannot validate a cheilion.

The `Human` surface has 152 vertices shared by its `skin` and `lips` triangle regions. A frontward XY ray test through each shared vertex on the neutral source identifies visible candidates at vertex 13738 (+X, anatomical left) and 6074 (-X, anatomical right). Their neutral source coordinates are respectively `[0.025320883840322495, -0.03548741340637207, 0.13793005049228668]` and `[-0.025320883840322495, -0.03548765182495117, 0.1379300355911255]` metres. They are material-boundary vertices near the visible outer ends of the oral fissure, not the lateral extrema of all lip-region triangles.

All eighteen published identity documents were evaluated with expression absent through `humanFaceBasisWeights` and `evaluateHumanFaceRest`. The frontmost `Human` triangle along the source XY ray at each fixed candidate has zero anterior separation from that candidate in all eighteen documents. On the real AMD Radeon 780M ANGLE renderer, exact-position 2.5 mm diagnostic octahedra remain visible at the fissure ends in front and both quarter views for the shared neutral source and the -1/+1 `mouthWidth` endpoints of `alan-rickman-connected` and `generated-black-boy-01-connected`. The renderer's red disk outside the face is an independent camera calibration marker. The diagnostic octahedra are not product geometry; the images are source rest surfaces before articulation/contact.

The selected basis declares `mouthWidth` on [-1,1] with no corrective driven by it. Holding all other shape coordinates fixed makes the candidate pair's 3D difference vector affine on each sign segment. If `d` is the pair difference at a segment start and `s` its per-unit slope, the Euclidean distance derivative has the sign of `d · s`, and that numerator rises by `s · s` with weight. The minimum segment-start numerators among the eighteen documents are 322.851 and 372.977 mm². Conservatively bounding every other shape channel over its own declared envelope and every corrective activation independently in [0,1] still leaves positive lower bounds of 10.770 mm² on [-1,0] and 105.605 mm² on [0,1]. Thus the fixed-pair neutral 3D distance is strictly increasing over the channel's entire domain for every admitted identity shape combination on this exact basis, with expression absent. These are basis-response bounds, never population or physiological ranges.

The actual `createHumanFaceBasisPoseEvaluator` was then run for all eighteen published documents twice: with expression omitted, and with each document's requested expression. Its source-indexed `Human` array includes the basis rest deformation, joint pose, aperture-scaled closure, passage and contact resolution before output material/UV splitting. The frontmost `Human` triangle on each candidate's XY ray remains at its depth in all 36 evaluated states; the maximum anterior occlusion is 0 mm. Neutral candidate separations span 34.517–55.068 mm, while the eighteen posed separations span 44.642–66.221 mm. Contact resolution was active, for example moving 665 `Human` vertices by up to 1.095 mm in the Alan Rickman posed case. These data establish visibility in the published population after the actual pose/contact stage, not monotonic response of the final metric over all source weights.

The status remains `candidate`. A front-ray hit and marker placement do not prove that each vertex is the anatomically exact cheilion under all shape combinations, or that every lip, tooth and oral-lining contact is valid after lowering. The final GLB/Float32 surface, full combined-shape and expression domains, and age/population-supported admission remain unreviewed. No anatomical `fissureWidthMm` edit consumes the map until these conditions are established.
