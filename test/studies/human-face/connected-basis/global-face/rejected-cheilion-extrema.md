# Rejected lip-extrema cheilion candidate on the selected shared face basis

The [rejected map](rejected-cheilion-extrema.json) preserves a tested source hypothesis for `basis.json.gz` at SHA-256 `c2e9c3fbb73d1ea0a9e9e4ec6808a6ccc7082730007f6418de38812b6be1acde`. It is neither a personal document nor an accepted edit input. The intended 3D Facial Norms cheilion is the visible soft-tissue labial commissure on each side ([3DFN measurement table](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4841054/), [3D landmark-definition study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/joa.12407)). The vertices below fail that identification.

The neutral source `Human` surface has 16,942 vertices and separate `skin`/`lips` material regions. The maximum and minimum X coordinates among its 3,040 lip-region triangles occur at source vertices 9183 (+X, anatomical left) and 1390 (-X, anatomical right). Each vertex participates in both `skin` and `lips` triangles (three of each). Their neutral positions are `[0.026431992650032043, -0.032529234886169434, 0.12743662297725677]` and `[-0.026431992650032043, -0.032529354095458984, 0.12743662297725677]` metres, in the source frame. The first diagnostic raised its markers 30 mm toward the camera, so their front-view alignment established only projected XY location, not visible surface depth.

Under each of the eighteen published identity shapes with expression removed, the fixed pair's **3D Euclidean** separation is 37.647–56.778 mm. The original X-only probe was independently checked against all three coordinate differences; its non-X pair separation reaches at most 0.0011 mm on these shaped faces. In seventeen, the pair is still the lip region's left/right X extrema. In `generated-black-boy-01`, two adjacent vertices are 0.165 and 0.166 mm farther out. With each document's requested expression also applied as rest-space residual rows, the fixed pair's X span is at most 0.877 mm narrower than the then-extreme lip X span. These comparisons use `humanFaceBasisWeights` and `evaluateHumanFaceRest` without the subsequent articulation/contact pass; they establish a stable source-vertex identity, not anatomical cheilion correspondence or posed/exported accuracy.

Holding every other published shape weight fixed and setting `mouthWidth` to -1, 0 and +1 yields strictly increasing fixed-pair Euclidean widths in each of the eighteen examples. The selected subject intervals span 34.565–66.050 mm across the observed minimum/maximum endpoints; this is source *reachability*, not an age- or population-supported human interval. The endpoint computation was cross-checked independently to within 2e-14 mm.

The basis declares `mouthWidth` on [-1,1] and has no corrective driven by that channel. `evaluateHumanFaceRest` therefore makes the fixed-pair difference vector affine on each sign segment while all other shape weights are held fixed. If `d` is the pair difference at a segment start and `s` is its per-unit slope, the derivative of its Euclidean length has the sign of `d · s`. That numerator increases by `s · s` per unit of weight. The smallest start numerators among the eighteen published identities are 277.097 and 368.096 mm² on the negative and positive segments, so each of those identities is strictly monotone over the entire channel domain, not merely at three samples.

A conservative extension bounds every other shape channel independently over its declared endpoint interval and bounds every corrective activation independently in [0,1]. Summing each contribution's smallest dot product with the corresponding `mouthWidth` slope still leaves positive lower bounds of 25.320 mm² on [-1,0] and 96.602 mm² on [0,1]. Thus the fixed-pair neutral Euclidean distance is strictly increasing for **every admitted identity shape combination on this exact basis**, with expression absent. This bound intentionally ignores correlations among corrective drivers, making it weaker but safe. It is a true statement about the wrong anatomical pair and must not be used as `fissureWidthMm` lowering evidence.

A second diagnostic put 2.5 mm octahedra at the exact two source vertices and rendered the neutral source and `mouthWidth` endpoints of `alan-rickman-connected` and `generated-black-boy-01-connected` in front, mouth-close, and both quarter views on AMD Radeon 780M through ANGLE. No coloured marker appeared at a visible commissure; the red disk outside the face in full-front captures is the capture page's hand-typed camera calibration marker. An independent XY ray/triangle test at the neutral anchor coordinates found `Human/skin` in front of each anchor by 10.401516 mm on the left and 10.401490 mm on the right. The candidate points are hidden under the visible skin at neutral. The map is marked `rejected`, and neither it nor its monotonic source distance may drive the anatomical editor. A replacement requires independently identified visible commissure points on the final connected surface, followed by the same measurement, full-domain and contact review.
