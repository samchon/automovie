# Facial representation reassessment

## Decision and status

On 2026-09-22 the user required a fresh investigation of the architecture after repeated local fixes failed to resolve the face editor. This supplements [#2533](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/issues/2533) and [#2535](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/issues/2535) in the same [Draft PR #2534](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/pull/2534). Body implications belong to [#2536](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/issues/2536). This record stands alone without `.wiki`.

Checkpoint `28db475a38a831879cc40fcf0f68c29c52e15af8` establishes numerical personal state, editor replay and common generation. It does not establish adequate anatomical coupling or hairstyle coverage. Its GitHub build, test and deploy checks passed; the local full-suite timing failures remain separately recorded in [the migration receipt](numerical-hair-migration.json). All eighteen published study documents completed native preview/export and real-GPU capture in their saved expression states. This is not a sweep of their expression spaces. Observed visual defects prevent acceptance.

**Stop using personal parameter adjustments and additional isolated corrective fields as the main route to anatomical correctness.** Reassess the evaluation model before expanding its parameter inventory. The proposed replacement separates identity geometry, articulated performance, soft-tissue attachment and hair construction into explicit stages with shared correspondence. Choosing the shared geometry source remains subject to the comparison below; replacing its name or increasing its coefficient count is insufficient.

## Articulated evaluator, 2026-09-23

The replacement architecture's items 2, 3 and 4 are implemented in `mpfb-connected-head-2026-09-23-articulated`; the [study README](README.md#articulated-revision) records the data and its receipts, and this section records the design decisions and what they were measured against.

**Shared source, not a person.** The joints are the source's own joint cubes (`joint-mouth`, `joint-l-eye`, `joint-r-eye`), extracted from the pinned MPFB revision with the body extraction's Blender session and published as landmarks with the cube motion of every shape endpoint that moves them, so a joint follows identity exactly as the source's rig does. No per-person coordinate, fitted pivot or named exception exists; every one of the 274 shape endpoints' MPFB recipes was recovered by measurement and is recorded.

**Attachment weights are measured, not painted.** The MPFB `default` rig's painted weights were transported exactly through the subdivision and compared: they leave a larger residual against the source's own `jawOpen` (4.19 mm rms) than the weights the source's pure-translation mandibular endpoints reveal (2.85 mm rms), because a translation endpoint exposes each vertex's attachment directly. The revision uses the measured weights and records the comparison. This is the "candidate source, not clinical truth" the handoff warned about, resolved by measurement rather than by preference.

**The jaw is rotation plus coupled translation, not a hinge.** The source's full-open transform is one screw whose pivot lies 17 mm below the source's jaw cube; the clinical literature (Gallo 1997; Chen 1998 and 2021; Jász 2024) reports that habitual opening is never a pure rotation and that its instantaneous axis lies below the condyle, which is exactly what a rotation about the condyle plus a forward condylar translation looks like. Decomposing the source screw under Chen et al. 2021's measured coupling (14.3 mm forward, 3.5 mm down at 27.4 degrees) places the axis 18.6 mm below the globe centres, where the condyle is. The coupling and the sagittal budget (14.72 mm) are cited figures; the angle, translations and axis point are the source's own. A document past the budget is refused by name. The population's larger opening (47 to 52 mm interincisal, 27 to 32 degrees) and the rotation share of laterotrusion (Chen 2021: 27 percent) are recorded as known deviations of the authored source, not modelled.

**The globes turn about the source cube with the source's eccentric shift.** The cube sits 15.2 mm behind the corneal apex, inside the 13.5 to 15.3 mm range of Ohlendorf 2022 and the earlier 13.5 to 15 mm figures; the source's gaze endpoints turn about a point up to 1.85 mm from it, which the revision keeps as each channel's translation so the lids the source sculpted stay coherent. A blink does not turn the globe (Doane 1980; Takagi 1992). Lids remain tissue rows; lid-over-cornea sliding is the ocular coupling still owed.

**Residuals replace correctives that cheated.** Expression rows are rest-space residuals over the articulation; `jawOpenMouthCloseEase`'s 19.6 mm lift of the mandibular arch and the 19 correctives that compensated `tongueOut`'s folded-in jaw are retired. The counterexample this document opened with (a 30 degree endpoint shrinks transverse distances 3.4 percent at half weight) is closed for every attached vertex: the arch is on the arc at every fraction, and the residual rows that remain are the only linear part.

**What the census now shows and does not solve.** On the neutral and two subjects across fourteen coupled states, rest, protrusion, laterotrusion and blink are identical to the previous revision, gaze and tongue-with-open-jaw replay their authored poses, full opening crosses the skin less and half opening slightly more (the lips were authored to the old chord). Two combinations became visible: lips fully closed over a fully open jaw, and the tongue out through closed teeth. They are the contact problems the reassessment's item 4 names; the articulation states them (crossing census, refusal of the budget) and does not resolve them. The next owners are, in order: a coupled contact evaluator over lips, teeth and tongue in the same articulated state; lid-over-cornea coupling; the hierarchical hair generator (item 5); then the eighteen-subject census and views (item 6).

## Contact evaluator, 2026-09-23

Item 4 of the replacement architecture, coupled validity, is implemented in `mpfb-connected-head-2026-09-23-contact`; the [study README](README.md#contact-revision) records the data and this section the decisions.

**Closure is a coupled quantity, not a shape.** The source's `mouthClose` is a delta over the open jaw. Decomposed as a companion of `jawOpen` and applied scaled by the aperture it has to close, one channel seals the lips at every opening without a corrective; the corrective that had done it (`jawOpenMouthCloseEase`) was, to the millimetre, the rotation error of applying the delta at the wrong joint state. The seal domain is the source's authored range; the literature reports no maximum interincisal opening at which lips can still seal (Stavness 2013: closure "achievable for various jaw heights"), so no constant was invented.

**Impossible combinations are refused, not clamped or hidden.** The tongue through closed teeth or sealed lips is a geometric impossibility for a constant-volume hydrostat and is refused naming the short channel and the millimetres it needs. The refusal reads the posed lower incisal edge, because the tongue rides the mandible: protrusion and laterotrusion carry tongue and lower incisors together. An earlier frame taken from the chord of the opening at the incisors tilted 21.7 degrees backward and read protrusion as a rise; the frame is the cranial vertical, the axis every clinical aperture is measured along.

**Contact is resolved by a rest floor, within a stated budget.** Tissue keeps the clearance the source authored at rest and is moved back to it when a pose takes it deeper; rims of the open gum sheets read no side, seam copies move together, and a push past the tissue budget refuses the document. The budgets are a cited lip thickness and the tongue's own height, not a noise floor: the source's own `jawOpen` target reshapes the tongue 1.76 mm into the molars, and a budget fitted to that noise refused the population's combinations at 1.99 mm, which is the whack-a-mole the reassessment forbids.

**Lids ride the cornea by the same floor.** The globes are open at their posterior poles by a ring of thirty-two vertices, sealed like a crown; with them as colliders and the lashes as a soft surface, every lid vertex keeps the clearance it has over the sclera at rest as the cornea turns under it and as it closes, which is the radial push of Neog 2016 without a depth map. Measured on the neutral before the change: lateral gaze cut 848 lid vertices up to 1.83 mm and a blink 781 up to 2.67 mm into the globe; vertical gaze cut nothing beyond 0.13 mm because the source authored those rows on the globe. No lid rule was invented; the contact block gained a collider.

**Hair is a hierarchy.** A layer may integrate only a fraction of its roots as guides and grow the rest from their nearest same-side guides by scalp distance, projected by the same contact rule; with an eighth of the roots as guides three published subjects built 2.2 to 2.5 times faster and no ribbon vertex lies inside the skin (the unprojected blend had put 1,082 sampled vertices up to 5 mm inside). Under the population the scalp now takes the hair's colour by a rule that reads only the hairline, root region and colour every document already has; on the published long dark hair the bright skin patches at the crown are gone, and a lighter hair leaves the skin as real light hair does. Strands can also clump toward their guide, from apart at the root to the chosen fraction of the way at the tip, which on long hair turns scattered ribbons into locks. Density-derived ribbon width is the remaining step of item 5; the physical and render width are still one number.

**What remains.** The rest of the hair hierarchy (item 5) and the eighteen-subject census and views (item 6). The dental arches themselves are rigid and no channel brings them closer than rest; occlusal overlap under laterotrusion stays a crossing census fact.

## Verified causes and hypotheses

| Surface | Evidence in the current implementation | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facial motion (previous revision) | `createHumanFaceBasisBuilder` added shape and expression displacements, added corrective displacements, then `applyHumanFaceRigidGroups` independently projected selected components to rigid motion. | Teeth could retain their dimensions while neighboring lips, tongue and oral lining took a different motion. Resolved by the articulated evaluator above: teeth, lining, tongue and lip tissue receive one mandibular transform through shared attachments before their residual rows. |
| Joint placement (previous revision) | The basis had fitted rigid groups but no shared articulated jaw chain with identity-dependent joint frames and tissue weights. | Resolved above: the source's joint cubes are landmarks that follow identity, the mandible turns about a condylar axis with coupled translation, and the weights are measured from the source. A fitted proper rotation was not evidence of a physiological joint path. |
| Hair construction | `createHumanFaceHairBuilder` integrates each sampled root directly into a rendered strip. `evaluateHumanFaceHairDirection` combines a global flow, root-dependent part bias, lift and curl. | There is no intermediate description of hairstyle volume or guide neighborhoods. Changing strip count/width changes apparent coverage without establishing the intended hairstyle volume. |
| Hair scale | `integrateHumanFaceHairCurve` uses the same `width` for contact clearance that `buildHumanFaceHairMesh` uses for strip width; clearance also includes half the integration step. A point root fans immediately into the full strip. | Physical fiber size, aggregate rendering width and numerical integration error are mixed. Increasing card width to hide gaps also changes the generated path; broad hairline triangles follow from this representation. The replacement needs explicit scale separation and a convergence check. |
| Hair boundaries | Current actual exports show broad steps at hairlines and posterior gaps. The posterior scalp triangles and root population are present. A Daniel counterexample with only its part field removed substantially reduced the central gap while retaining identical roots. | The field and strip coverage require examination together. Neither missing roots nor the part field alone explains every observed defect. |
| Control validity | Individual finite ranges are admitted for shape/expression channels. | Membership in a numeric box does not imply a valid combination of jaw, lips, lids, eyes and tongue. A coupled validity domain is missing. |
| Simple controls | The current residual-preserving group conversion is deterministic authoring arithmetic. | It preserves edits but does not establish empirical age, anatomy or population semantics. That evidence must be supplied separately. |

These are distinct claims. Numerical serialization, reproducibility, physical geometry and useful authoring are separate acceptance conditions.

There is also an identity-independent counterexample to treating endpoint addition as articulation. Interpolating between a rest point and its rotation uses `A(t) = (1-t)I + tR`. In the plane perpendicular to the rotation axis, its length scale is `sqrt(1 - 2t(1-t)(1-cos(theta)))`; at half weight this is `abs(cos(theta/2))`. Thus a 30-degree rotation shrinks transverse distances by about 3.4% at half weight. Correcting an isolated dental group afterwards restores that group's distances but does not apply the same rotational trajectory to attached tissue. The replacement must evaluate the rigid transform and its tissue attachments together; denser sampling of the same linear path does not change this counterexample.

## GNM source audit

The official repository was cloned at `a424b5153eec9154f3dfa5ee7f214e5817918d54`. Its `gnm/shape/data/versions/v3_0/gnm_head.npz` is 53,305,389 bytes with SHA-256 `67fef851c2e74ecad590dccf1b336afbb6bf0ae7cf981e5efeaf2160f178e745`. This is one shared model, not personal data. No GNM asset or implementation has been added to the application.

Direct non-pickle NumPy inspection finds 17,821 vertices, 35,324 triangles, 253 identity coefficients and 383 expression coefficients. The four joints are `neck`, `head`, `left_eye`, `right_eye`; there is no jaw joint. The stored pose-corrective regressor is entirely zero. The paper's table lists 382 expression dimensions, so consumers must bind to the actual versioned data rather than copying that table.

An independent SVD audit evaluated the documented bind-pose expression sum, with zero identity and joint motion. Each of the 383 expression coordinates was evaluated at -1, -0.5, 0.5 and 1 on each 1,440-vertex dental group. Proper-rigid registration was first checked against an analytic rotated/translated tetrahedron and a nonrigid scaled negative control. Upper-group maximum RMS residual was `1.5763507821070616e-17` source units. Lower-group maximum was `0.00011397858832435184` source units at `lower_face_region_001 = -1`. This is a check of the published linear representation, not execution of its full runtime, a clinical magnitude threshold, a tooth-only measurement or a collision test: the groups include gums.

The [audit receipt](gnm-representation-audit.json) preserves those observations. They rule out adopting GNM unchanged on the assumption that all internal parts already have rigid articulation. They do not rule out using its shared anatomy as a source or imply that the current basis is superior.

## Replacement architecture to evaluate

1. **One fine document.** Versioned shared-basis coordinates, anatomical dimensions, performance controls, procedural appearance and hair operations are the personal state. Easy controls compile into this state using explicit, reproducible transformations and preserve independently edited fine values. No personal vertex residual, guide array or image lookup is admitted.
2. **Identity before performance.** Evaluate neutral morphology and all dependent component locations together. Bind eyes, teeth, tongue, oral lining, lids and scalp to that same result. Shared attachment data belongs to the model and must be reproducible from its source.
3. **Articulation before tissue detail.** Jaw and eye transforms own rigid motion. Associated tissue follows the same articulation before local expression and pose detail are evaluated. The evaluator must not repair a tooth in isolation after the rest of the mouth has already taken another path.
4. **Coupled validity.** Joint limits, dental occlusion, eye/lid contact, lip aperture and tongue/teeth clearance are jointly evaluated on the same state. Establish the supported domain and the response to conflicting controls; do not silently clamp an unrelated control or hide visible tissue. Shared correctives are permitted only with an identified residual cause and evidence of behavior between their samples.
5. **Hierarchical procedural hair.** Numerical scalp regions and hairline define coverage; regional style operations generate a volume/guide field; generated strands follow that field; strips or other rendering geometry are derived from those strands. Density, physical width and rendering resolution have distinct meanings. Fine hair at boundaries is generated from the same boundary definition. Gathered styles need a general numerical gathering operation, not a named person's stored curves.
6. **Common measurements.** Compare candidates on identical neutral, identity-extreme, intermediate and combined-control cases before fitting the eighteen examples. Check rigid distances, attachment error, signed clearance, valid triangle area, symmetry/equivariance, deterministic replay and preservation of intended motion. A lower error in one region cannot compensate for breaking another constraint.

Items 2, 3 and 4 of this direction are implemented and measured in the [articulated evaluator](#articulated-evaluator-2026-09-23) and the [contact evaluator](#contact-evaluator-2026-09-23) above, lids included; hierarchical hair and the population census remain. GNM, FLAME and the existing MPFB prior remain candidates for the shared neutral/identity source. Final visual acceptance still requires actual editor and exported output after the shared model passes those checks.
