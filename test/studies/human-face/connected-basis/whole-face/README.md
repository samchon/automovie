# Whole-face regional basis

The connected-face playground selects this CC0 prior. It retains the preceding basis's neutral geometry, materials, UVs, connectivity and every existing endpoint exactly, then expands from 12 to 80 shape controls while retaining all 52 expression controls. Its immutable identity is `mpfb-connected-head-2026-09-17-complete-regions`. The earlier [64-control basis](../README.md) remains available as a separate revision; saved documents bind to their exact revision and are never silently converted.

## Source and derivation

The [parent source and license record](../README.md#sources-and-license) applies unchanged. The additional endpoints come from the pinned MPFB2 target files. Every signed control uses its two actual authored endpoints, including pairs named down/up, backward/forward or in/out; a negative value does not extrapolate a positive target. MPFB's official refit recalculates the same five attached assets for each shape endpoint. No MetaHuman proprietary mesh, DNA, sculpt database or runtime code is included.

Skin is subdivided and clipped as one surface before material separation. The identical affine clipping stencils sample every endpoint, and runtime evaluation reconstructs common normals before splitting UV/material seams. All six surfaces returned exactly to their neutral coordinates after extraction. [The receipt](extraction-receipt.json) records source and payload digests and the exact comparison with the preceding basis.

## Scientific interpretation

The applied lessons are a coherent neutral prior, separation of identity from performance, shared facial connectivity, common attachment evaluation, prepared correspondence and explicit source revisions. MetaHuman's neutral and expression guidance additionally motivates inspecting external skin, lips, dental pocket, eyes and attached tissues together at intermediate and combined poses. Its PSD/RBF arithmetic does not supply anatomical corrective meshes; none are fabricated here.

These controls interpolate authored CC0 targets. Their weights are dimensionless, and the age-structure control is not an age in years. Linear interpolation does not become rigid jaw articulation, collision prevention or physiological validation merely because more controls exist. Hair grooming, custom dental morphology, validated combination correctives and a clinical anatomical error bound are not provided by this asset. The existing procedural face path remains available with its independent capabilities. Numerical admission, actual render inspection and input-person likeness are separate results.

The fitting experiment used 468 detector correspondences on the neutral prior, fixed barycentric anchors, signed endpoint sums, bounded weak-perspective pose/scale/translation and robust least squares. Pose-only fitting establishes the baseline before jointly solving shape and performance. Residuals are normalized by detected facial height. Two neutral projection misses use quarter weight. Identity regularity is zero-centred with weight 0.003; all 52 performance controls use the already recorded detector expression estimates as their prior, with weight 0.015. These are numerical stabilization choices, not measured anatomical distributions. The [official model card](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/Model%20Card%20Blendshape%20V2.pdf) describes the expression coefficients and their limitations; their transfer to this artistic prior is not an anatomical calibration. Unseen depth retains the prior and a smaller residual does not establish likeness.
