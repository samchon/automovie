## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The recipe states the open bag floor, the chain's required placement and the authored pickup outcome.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Both outcomes append the same fingertip touch. A found-item request adds the pickup with its authored grip and lift. A no-find returns the chain after the touch, so a subsequent search retains the written attempt order.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

The recipe appends one touch and, when the item is found, one pickup. It retains no search state between calls.
