## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

The caller's prepared endpoint reader, or one prepared here; one local exact workspace then connects course lowering, actual posture fitting and final endpoint FK.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No pose, time, target, seed, stop state, fit or sampled endpoint is retained. Every current authored joint/digit/needed fit and unsupported-context fallback still executes; arms keep the complete course and coupled clearance.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

A plan prepares each limb's fixed name set once for its chain (`StrikingWeapon.frames`), so its contact scans pay the canonical sorting/demand preparation once; a cold call pays it itself. Every call retains its actual course/physical fit/admission and requested FK ancestry with fresh maps.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Only the plan-owned reader of one fixed requested set is shared, across that plan's seconds; course, channels, actor, seconds and stops determine the separately evaluated pose and cannot share answers or workspaces across calls.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The reader belongs to the plan that prepared it and is released with it; no module store holds it. Each call lazily owns one genuine ROM mapper only within its unchanged-table course/final-fit operation; it releases that mapper and its local workspace/pose on return or throw, and outputs own fresh frames.
