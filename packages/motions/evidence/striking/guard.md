## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Each foot steps on a minimum-jerk unit to its measured stance place in turn, the root sinks and turns, the gloves rise to the guard, then a bounce through the knees; the limbs are solved by the shared fit. Facing a person, the stance is laid back by its reciprocal half of the least step that keeps both guards held clear (`StrikingGuardYield.back`).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Every actual leg/guard/native fit remains. Facing a person adds the yield's resolves and bisection (`StrikingGuardYield.back`) when the geometry is built; fixed guard rig demand and each moving foot's exact Slerp arc prepare once; current raw entry heading is still read by the original per-query expression, and one current native workspace serves lower and guard ancestry, retaining all progress-dependent quaternion weights and normalization. Speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The built native geometry fixes body, entry/stance feet, heading and lead. Only those arc/name/scalar invariants are shared; every current root/joint/foot/goal and query-scoped genuine FK/ROM input reaches its original numerical owner. Settle drops geometry when the faced target can change, without sharing any posed result across times.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Holds one settled geometry and at most two lazy fixed-foot arc readers until settle or plan release. Each posture owns its exact getter and fresh authored goals; optional parent FK/ROM loans end on return/failure and enter no retained output or pose/time history.
