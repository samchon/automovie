## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The aimed point is read a look before the start; the line, the height band, the travel and the turn follow from it and the body's measure; the launch's contact is decided on the fitted figure and its chamber folded from it by the skill's range share.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

The reading and what it gives, the comparison with the launch before, then the contact's solver and the launch's contact.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

A launch before is returned only when its aimed point, its predicted goal and what a redirect keeps are the same in every component; every band, share and refusal is the law's own.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Three reads of the target and constant arithmetic to compare; the carriage search (up to eight leans of four step rounds of eight whole-posture fits) runs once for a launch, where it ran at each of a kick's three or four geometry builds.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Equivalence is the launch's own reading: the same aimed point and predicted goal on the same body, entry, timing and kept line give the same contact, so the plan's one launch serves every geometry built on it.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan holds one launch, with its contact's fitted pose and path, until it is released or the launch reads differently.
