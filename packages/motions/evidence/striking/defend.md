# src/striking/defend.ts#defend.plan

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States its input, its plan and its refusals.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The outcome law times the start from the strike's launch and contact; the goal is built from what the defender sees at that read second, the guard held before it.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Decides the timing once, builds the geometry on first use after the read, and evaluates each posture from it.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

A strike that cannot be defended throws; nothing about contact is decided here, so no outcome is faked geometrically.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

The original reaction decision, attacker reads and all current native posture fitting remain. Waiting and built-defence branches prepare fixed foot geometry and demanded rig names once, evaluate the movement envelope once per query and borrow genuine current FK/ROM inputs; speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The decision is made once and built geometry is dropped on settle when the strike may change. The native context fixes entry feet and body, so waiting shares only their authored geometry/name preparation; every posture remains fresh, and query-supplied exact FK/ROM loans are forwarded without retaining them.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Holds one built geometry cleared by settle and one fixed-entry waiting reader prepared with the plan. Plan release drops fixed metadata, and current maps, pose fits and borrowed query sources end with each synchronous return or failure.
