# src/striking/StrikingWeaponTable.ts#StrikingWeaponTable

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the reader, the first knot and the result.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Each knot is the course's own fit at that second, solved as the displayed posture is, so the contact scan meets the limb the figure shows; no seed is carried between knots, because a continued swivel was measured to leave the shown fit.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

A read is a knot's row, filled on its first read, or one fit of a second off the knots.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

A knot is recognised only when the scan's own expression `from + k * STEP` gives the second bit for bit; any other second is fitted itself.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

One fit a knot actually read, the fit the figure itself pays at that second, and a lookup for a knot read again; no cheaper exact method exists, since a fit continued from the previous knot is a different, inexact solution branch.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

A knot's row is the course's value at that knot on one built geometry; the owner drops the table with the geometry (`StrikingWeapon.capsules`).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The rows grow to the knots read, at most a hundred and twenty a second of weapon window, and are released with the table by its owner.
