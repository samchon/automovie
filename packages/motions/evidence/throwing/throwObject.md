# src/throwing/throwObject.ts#throwObject.plan

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The law states launch/release, carry/follow/recovery, style and support behavior; this planner retains original events and actual wrist/contact goals while preparing fixed hand/path geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Keeps original launch, Hermite acceleration/follow curves, rigid carry interval, trunk envelope and complete default-bend/contact arm goal under the existing composed clock/base.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Existing launch/lever/path owners produce the course; one actual grip-to-wrist placement feeds both Bend.solve and ArmTarget.fromWrist goal assembly.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No release time/velocity, curve sample, actual Bend solve, contact pivot, physics bound or ROM policy changes; rest geometry is omitted only in phases that do not consume it.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Fixed curves/boundaries prepare once; windup/recovery alone evaluate their actual rest frame. One lazy grip offset and one actual wrist replace three offset readings and duplicate placement per arm frame. The arm's elbow is solved once over the span when the action is planned and continued through it (`AutoMovieMotionHumanBendTrack`), one refinement a frame in place of one cold search; every fit remains. Speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Body/side/grip/relaxed geometry stays unchanged for this plan; shared rigid placement/goal owners retain original arithmetic/contact admission. Every current posture, curve parameter, wrist and bend remains actual.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns original launch/path/clock/lever/base data and one lazy fixed grip offset until release. Wrist/contact outputs are fresh; no fitted answer, FK reader, query/time history or handle is retained.
