## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Entry FK requests chest/hand only and its actual grip-point read omits two unused normal/forward rotations. Each used fist/flat local offset prepares once rather than three times per writer; one current wrist serves bend plus goal, while actual chest FK and the full bounded bend remain. Speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The immutable body, hand and each of the two original grip shapes fix only their local offset. Current world chest, rotation, point, shape selection and bend remain inputs; composer FK loans reach the original raw chest policy without applying ROM projection.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns fixed entry frames and at most two copied local offsets until release. Each write releases its borrowed FK source on return or throw and returns fresh point/contact payloads, without retaining a query, reader, sampled pose or history.
