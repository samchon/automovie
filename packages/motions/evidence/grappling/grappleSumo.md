Foot and fist targets in the performer's own frame. The gap at contact is the two bodies' own: each chest's front ahead of its hips at the charge's pitch, from the bodies' chest and hip heights and chest depths; the root stands over the ankles, so a foot's target, its ball, lies the rest foot's ankle-to-ball length ahead of the place the root is to reach. The charge steps both feet, one key each, so the root over them closes the gap; the receiver's drive mirrors the initiator's, both stepping the same distance on the same keys.
