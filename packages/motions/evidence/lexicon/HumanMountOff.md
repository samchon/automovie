## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The end is the stand law's own root over the floor places, so the next action starts from a quiet stance. Every path is a minimum-jerk share (Flash and Hogan 1985). A foot lands only when its hip brings the floor place within the leg's reachable band, and keeps a rest only while it is within that band, so no plant is held by a leg that cannot reach it.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Choose the side, place the standing feet, time the descent, find when each foot leaves and lands, course the legs, then one posture function: the legs' goals, their planned seeds, then the hands.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The side defaults to the left only for a seat the entered sit names as astride; after any other entry the composition states it. The timing shares and the constants of `HumanMount` are provisional and named.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Planning scans the time at 60 Hz three times, each step one forward-kinematics pass of the pelvis, and courses the legs once (`courseHumanLegs`); a posture costs one such pass, the legs placed in closed form at their planned seeds and the hands laid with the palms prepared once (`prepareSeatedPalms`).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The floor places, foot instants, leg course and palms are computed once per instance, so the thighs the hands rest on are the thighs shown; each posture passes its caller's forward-kinematics reader and range-of-motion projector to the hands' fit (`seated.arms`).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources
