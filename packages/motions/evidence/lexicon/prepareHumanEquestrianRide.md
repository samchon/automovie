## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Resolve finite local times from film cues at planning. Use endpoint-flat functions and a rotation about the carried seat point. A raised seat transfers support to the existing stirrup goals. The film turn uses the available joint ranges.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Prepare the three optional timelines separately, then sample their offsets once before ordinary limb fitting.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The horse remains the original moving carrier. No aim target, projectile, joint-bound override or ground contact is fabricated.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Prepare and sort the cue tracks and their times once. A sample uses one binary search for the track and one for its stride, then does fixed pose math.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The ride provides the current support frame and measured seat drift and keeps ownership of its existing goal fits. The clocks, saddle offset and joint bounds are prepared once.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Holds only this ride's sorted cue times, ordered jump instants and body reference. Sampling keeps no history.
