## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Maximizing a scalar product selects the closest permitted clavicle tip to each wrist. Intersecting both resulting arm spheres with the seat line selects one bearing station. Later pelvic inclination carries the two fixed shoulder frames about the same bearing.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Reads only the two clavicle frames at preparation. At playback each reachable wrist keeps its own angle. Only a wrist needing a scapular turn solves its scalar reach boundary.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Reads the authored seat, actual wrists and per-rig clinical limits. A different articulation or an empty witness keeps the existing posture.
