## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States context/plan ownership and seat, facing, leg-reach and backrest refusals; the namespace gives world-metre socket goals, timing and contact conventions.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The seat socket follows its original descent/balanced travel while planted-leg and achieved hand goals retain canonical fitting; high seats delegate to the existing mount law; a seat on a prop that may move carries the whole plan in that prop's frame (`sitCarried`). The legs are coursed once over the sit and each hand's span is the body's own (`sitHandSpans`): a hanging hand its rising thigh meets before seat contact lands on the thigh there.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Fixed descent geometry and the original balance table feed one body base, the legs' course and the hands' spans (`sitHandSpans`), and two carried hand rows.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Original seat/facing/reach refusals, all balance samples/bracket trials, contact times and authored interpolation remain; no fit or tolerance is relaxed.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Planning retains the original SHARES balance brackets and actual complete mass-centre fits, with no unused support geometry. Moving queries retain actual root lowering and hand trajectory/fits; the native settled hold reuses its delivered lower-body key; fixed intervals, joint entries and frame coefficients prepare once.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Fixed descent/socket/joint metadata share across queries; balance construction lends one synchronous FK/ROM source and returns only scalar travel. Current base lowering and carried thigh goals borrow composition loans without sharing a posed answer.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns finite clock/geometry/travel and arm plans until release. Construction/current-query FK/ROM sources end on synchronous return or throw and never enter those records.
