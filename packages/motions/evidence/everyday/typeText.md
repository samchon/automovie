# src/everyday/typeText.ts#typeText.plan

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the plan and its refusals; the namespace gives the goals, timing and contacts.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Fingertip goals on key centres fitted along the course by `AutoMovieMotionHumanArmChain`; the fit checks read the entry pose's shoulder joints by FK; the contacts are the entry's own supports.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Board, keys and fit checks, then press times, then the fingertip reader and one posture writer.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Unfit desks and unknown keys are refused; every threshold is a named provisional constant.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

O(N) planning for N keys and one shoulder FK; each posture costs two O(log N) key lookups and the arm chain's fit.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Key layout, timing, hand rotations and the two fingertip-surface goal builders are prepared once per plan.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns its key and press records and the prepared goal builders until released; no sampled posture is retained.
