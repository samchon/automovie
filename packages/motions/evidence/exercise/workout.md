## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

One clock of spans, each between two keys; the posture at a second is the two keys' blend placed by the kind's pin, and on the way from and to standing the root is carried between the standing root and the pinned one; on the way off a bar the body is raised by whatever its colliders would sink beyond the share of the supported posture's own sink the way has reached, the reading the floor audit makes, so the floor is a constraint and the way meets the supported posture continuously.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Each time query finds its span in O(log spans), blends prepared joints and requests only observed pin/collider ancestry. Actual neutral collider names, including mirrored entries, prepare floor selection once; changing floor readings and laid-hand fits remain where needed.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Fixed key pairs share joint preparation, repeated endpoint keys share one supported floor reading, and the first laid-hand targets share one exact hand-bone map. The time-only primitive reads no earlier channel; its synchronous parent FK/ROM loans serve actual pin, floor and load observations without retaining a current pose.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns O(spans) clocks/blend closures and at most one floor scalar per fixed endpoint key; no pose reader or time-query history is retained.
