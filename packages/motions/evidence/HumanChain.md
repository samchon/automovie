## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the surface with its operators and statics, its immutability, what the performance owns and where the vocabulary methods are declared.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The engine's immutable chain of one subject with the whole vocabulary as typed methods; a mutual action joins several subjects' chains.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Four statics (`subject`, `extend`, `mutual`, `pair`); the vocabulary and the core operators (`act`, `registered`, `until`, `sync`, `over`) are inherited.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

No action is reachable only through an untyped call.
