## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

O(KJ) preparation removes per-query route joint unions and fixed progress-name lookup; native clock guards retain custom-callback behavior. First actual floor/fold demand prepares native collider selection or folded-seat target/name metadata once. Every changing query retains actual bounds/fold solve and synchronous FK/ROM; the fully arrived hold performs no new observations. A standing entry's first phase adds one demanded feet FK per query; the bearing sockets, the first phase's two ends, the root's offset over its rest, the feet's pins, the release into the second phase and the rolling heel are prepared once at plan time through a fresh exact FK reader and the body's own projection, which give the values a query's loaned reader gives. Each blend's arm-room scan (`floorArmRoom`) runs once when placement is final, through the prepared-playback setup hook, not on the first displayed second of the blend.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns finite route/clock/joint readers, at most one body-fixed floor reader, one folded-seat reader and one arrived posture until release. Current FK/ROM and actual maps/placements end on return or throw and never enter those readers, the endpoint or the plan.
