## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Each placement of the four limbs is a stance of the support planner, whose keys are the balanced postures on those contacts nearest the chain's shape there, each solve started from that shape, and each step one limb released, swung and set down (Escande, Kheddar and Miossec 2013).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

A placement schedule, its stances, one support plan and the walk's events named on it.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

One limb moves at a time, so three always bear; the feet come in by exactly what the hands went out, so each cycle ends in the fold it began in.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

The chain, fixed floor conversions, two sole prerequisites and palm geometry prepare once across the N actual stance preferences. Each preference retains its actual chain search and complete pose solve, and the support plan retains every original key fit.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Unchanged body/chain/floor frame share native geometry and one synchronous exact FK/genuine ROM scope across actual preference construction. Every current foot/wrist placement still builds independent raw preferences, actual full fits and contact points; actual support, chain and entry behavior retain their owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

A dedicated stance-construction scope releases all FK/ROM/preference callbacks before support planning; only original numeric preferences, contact targets and step records leave it. Returned support keys/intervals retain no construction loan, callback or query history and forward original settlement.
