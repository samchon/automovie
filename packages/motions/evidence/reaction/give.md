## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Reads only head ancestry after full authored-joint admission: one original entry read and the two original neck-axis trials for a head reaction. Fixed head demand prepares once rather than indexing names at each read; written postures retain constant work and speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One synchronous observation lends its exact FK workspace and body-bound head-demand producer to the entry and both actual joint-override trials; every authored joint is still admitted and each changed neck pose is read afresh, while direction is still read once.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The observation IIFE releases its lazy FK workspace, fixed head-demand reader and fresh bone maps before constructing the returned primitive; only its direction and computed head angles escape, never an FK reader, callback or posed-result cache.
