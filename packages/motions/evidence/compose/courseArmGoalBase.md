## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States what the base holds before, over and after each arm's span, the demand it makes once laid, and what it reads.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Before its course an arm is the plan's own; over its span its goal is left out; after it the arm keeps the angles its course ended with or stands where its course returns it, so the fit after the seam continues the course's swivel and branch (Tolani, Goswami and Badler 2000: the feasible swivel nearest the previous solution).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

The course's demand and base posture, moved verbatim from `courseArmGoal`, read the scope the course hands them; whether the course is laid and its composition are read through the scope's getters, since both are set after the base is built.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No arm is frozen at a rest: each angle read is the course's or the plan's own.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Once laid, a second where every arm in its window is on its course asks the law for every channel but those arms, as an engine's animation graph evaluates only its required bones.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Each set of skipped arms keeps one channel list, so the reads of a second share it; each arm's ending and return angles are read once.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Retains one channel list per set of skipped arms and the scope, released with the plan.
