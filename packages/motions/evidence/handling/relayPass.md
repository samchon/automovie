## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Inactive pass windows return the carrier directly; partial blends retain their original solves, and full share skips the unused carrier-angle solve. Each active current posture shares one yaw transform across its original axes/point/bend. Incoming contact requests only partner hand output and point-only palm geometry, omitting two unused direction rotations. Speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Current-person FK/ROM loans pass through complete carrier, actual shoulder/baseline observations and arm carry. Exact unchanged partner-body identity permits one local palm reader; opaque partner lookup, film second and displayed bones remain current and use that other person's own pipeline.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns base/baton geometry and one last immutable partner-body point reader, replaced on body change. Optional current-person FK/ROM inputs are borrowed only during the call; no loan, current bones/point, pose/time history or handle enters the plan or outputs.
