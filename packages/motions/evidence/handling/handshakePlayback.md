# src/handling/handshakePlayback.ts#handshakePlayback

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Specifies the motion phases, unchanged base and endpoint ownership.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The prepared transports and digit schedules give the reach and withdrawal. A finite sinusoidal pump is applied to the shared clasp frame with its closed grip held throughout.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Each phase supplies one hand result on the same current base. The raw action and placed display use those same evaluators in the same endpoint order.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The base, exact timed contact, arm reference and existing downstream fit remain current. Foreign base readers retain their time and FK/ROM arguments.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Evaluate only one transport or pump and the actual base at a requested second. No contact placement, meeting solve or curve construction is repeated.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The grip digits, wrist attitude and phase coefficients come from the established clasp for every pump. Changed posture can still affect the original bend and fit.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The readers own the three phase formulas and original fixed data for one handshake. The current posture, observations and result are never retained.
