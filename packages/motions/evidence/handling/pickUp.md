## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The law and parameters define original grasp/use/lift/steadied-hand goals, contact events, variation and refusals; this plan preserves complete position/attitude requests through the actual lift.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Existing stance/grasp/prehension owners choose and transport the grip, close its actual contacts, carry the original complete arm/digit/reference goals and apply the original lift-height judgement.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Grasp/stance admission precedes finger/transport preparation, original phase body construction and complete arm goals; optional same-person observations follow those existing owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Original grasp/lift targets, contact timing, candidate fits, ROM, reference, planting and acceptance bounds remain; no shown endpoint replaces an asked goal and no attitude-only post-fit twist discards position.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Fixed target/hand/digit/transport data prepare once. Active body lowering, shoulder reading and initial carry borrow exact current-person FK/ROM where supplied; every original phase goal and actual solve remains. Speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One arm course serves the reach, the load and the lift, so the load shows the course's own hold rather than a posture frozen at the close. The moving phases use current-call FK and ROM loans through their original stance, lift and carry paths.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns original chosen grasp/stance/transport/finger geometry; current FK/ROM arguments are used synchronously and never stored in the plan, returned goals or query history.
