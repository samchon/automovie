## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One constructor owns the full finite joint-only snapshot and weak registration; prepared performs one weak provenance lookup, metadata acquisition and original canonical solve, returning the genuine preparation and a permanently invalidating guard. The actual gymnastic placement consumer owns COM reduction and changing rigid placement. Unsupported authoring retains its existing reader.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Limb/digit goals, whole-body rotations, unknown top-level context and nonfinite fields cannot obtain proof. Unissued callbacks do not execute or gain preparation. The resolver retains all authored joints and admission, while wrappers, layers, carry, contacts, balance fits, cuts and IK keep ordinary evaluation. No public flag, sample-equality verdict, solver replacement, fixture or action-name branch exists.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Construction copies/freezes O(J) direct coordinates once; each supported read copies O(J) output. prepared performs one complete ordinary canonical solve and O(B+T) rig checking for B rig records and T ROM/frame table entries without demanding an unused public pose copy. Additional capture/guard work has no measured speedup claim; ordinary dynamic bases retain their original constant-cost borrowed reads.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Private immutable coefficients establish the fixed canonical source independently of callback identity; identity only locates their genuine constructor. The actual placement consumer checks the returned unchanged guard before reuse. Any observed mismatch permanently invalidates that acquisition, so restoring earlier metadata cannot revive its preparation; changed bodies or unissued callbacks receive no reused preparation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The callback and weak entry own one O(J) immutable source until the callback releases. Each prepared acquisition owns one actual resolver preparation and genuine rig checker. The placement consumer replaces its current acquisition on invalidation and releases it with its plan, without query history, FK workspace or external handles.
