## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

IEEE-754 multiplication by a nonfinite operand distinguishes an actual zero term from an absent term; reconstructs the original coefficient sequence using the same finite positive scales and completed nonzero values.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One exceptional-row owner serves ADMM, residual, certificate, outer-product and polishing readers; their finite paths retain the existing compressed loops.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Restores every original signed zero, index, duplicate and ordering position without a fabricated NaN, tolerance, retry or blanket rejection of truly empty rows.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

First restoration takes O(k) time/storage for k original row entries; repeated exceptional reads take O(1) lookup. Normal finite products allocate no restored row.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Admitted raw layouts and prepared scales stay unchanged for their synchronous one-shot loan or fixed-model snapshot; each original nonzero maps in order to one CSR entry. Complete zero-free rows need no raw copy, while zero-bearing layouts preserve every omitted entry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Lazily retains at most one restored row per original row, O(R) total entries; zero-free model rows copy both arrays and other indices borrow unchanged layouts. One-shot maps release at return, fixed-model maps at owner collection, and neither enters preparation or public state.
