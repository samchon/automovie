## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Every distinct triple runs actual projection/composition, failures are never stored and signed zero stays distinct. ## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Local hits compare three current scalars without constructing lower/hand input tuples. Misses keep the actual projection's tuple, private three-angle snapshot and quaternion copy; all world FK/fresh output remains. At most three current entries are retained and runtime speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Only exact completed local work under the one unchanged derivative scope is reused; coordinate keys are scalar snapshots, each incoming world prefix is recomputed and neither caller buffers nor final chain results become memo keys or values.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The caller's local reader owns one shoulder entry and at most two lower/hand entries plus borrowed setup/frame/functions; completion or failure releases the reader and its constant-size state, with no global map, pose population, handle or task.
