## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Assembly owns fresh direct records/root and admission; the refit applies the solved chain edits and digit fitting retains its original owner. An absent base with an absent or native (factory/engine) reader fits on the assembly's own records, since assembly creates every result record and a native reader lends only exact FK; explicit base and foreign-reader sources clone at the boundary, and a no-goal refit keeps its borrowed-pose contract.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No public resolved flag, achieved-target substitution, relaxed ROM/acceptance or prior-time result is accepted. Original raw goals remain auditable; unsupported/changed dependencies, base and near context retain their original solve. Weak issued provenance and independently mutable result copies prevent forged or changed public outputs from certifying another request.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Ordinary work scales with rig/joints and actual selected search sizes. Explicit observations omit only proven irrelevant fits, and checked source transport replaces a duplicate eligible solve with linear scalar checking/copying. Every retained target/search remains actual and speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Same-operation FK readers share exact scalar prerequisites. Whole-source reuse requires the actual constructor-owned graph plus current rig/table/native-loan proof; arbitrary raw getters are never sampled to certify partial-leg output, and copies or mismatches fall back normally.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Invocation-owned FK ends on return/throw and borrowed readers remain with their synchronous parent. Issued preparation and transported raw objects weakly own bounded source/signature/seeded data. Results own fresh mutable data; no limb fit is remembered, and no reader, query history or handle is retained.
