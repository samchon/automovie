## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Ordinary resolution clamps direct joints before the original range-clamped pole-hint IK. A posture placed from a preparation carries its own solve (`HumanPosturePreparation.IPlaced`) for its reader; every posture given here is fitted.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Assembly owns fresh direct records/root and admission; the refit applies the solved chain edits and digit fitting retains its original owner. An absent base with an absent or native (factory/engine) reader fits on the assembly's own records, since assembly creates every result record and a native reader lends only exact FK; explicit base and foreign-reader sources clone at the boundary, and a no-goal refit keeps its borrowed-pose contract.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No public resolved flag, achieved-target substitution, relaxed ROM/acceptance or prior-time result is accepted. Original raw goals remain auditable; unsupported/changed dependencies, base and near context retain their original solve. Results are independently mutable copies.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Ordinary work scales with rig/joints and actual selected search sizes. Explicit observations omit only proven irrelevant fits. Every retained target/search remains actual and speedup is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Same-operation FK readers share exact scalar prerequisites. A placed preparation's solve travels with its posture instead of being looked up here.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Invocation-owned FK ends on return/throw and borrowed readers remain with their synchronous parent. Results own fresh mutable data; no limb fit is remembered, and no reader, query history or handle is retained.
