## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Cold frame/bone reads retain nothing. A prepared point reader owns one fixed local vector and original fixed geometry callback until release; digital fallback borrows original body/geometry. Returned points/maps/frames remain fresh with no current bones, time, result history, handle or task retained.
