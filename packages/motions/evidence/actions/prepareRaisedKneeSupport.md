## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

A raised knee and contralateral foot determine the pelvic placement before the unsupported upper body reaches. The hand opposite the knee completes the diagonal support chain (Escande et al., 2013, doi:10.1016/j.robot.2013.01.008). The contralateral order is a candidate construction, never an admission criterion.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

The lower-chain solve excludes all upper joints. The tripod adds the trunk and one arm; only the final closure opens the whole body's variables.
