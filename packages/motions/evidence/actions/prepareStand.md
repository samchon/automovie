## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the probe/final-duration use, fixed inputs, varying positive seconds and stream ownership.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Retains stand's exact contact-preserving rocker course, midpoint/root alignment, minimum-jerk settle and seeded smooth sway with the same duration-specific normalization and moving-support carry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Entry geometry and support choice are prepared once, then each requested duration owns its settle, fresh random channels, events, contacts and joint map.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Neither a probe's trajectory nor an entry posture substitutes for the duration-specific stance; each query evaluates the original equations and actual support frame.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

A shared entry performs its socket FK and geometric preparation once; each duration prepares its two fixed four-term sway spectra lazily, while every posture still evaluates the original sines, window, rockers and carry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Fixed entry/body/alignment/support data serve both durations. Each child owns its pure indexed random channels and duration-specific spectra, whose exact coefficients are reused without sharing any time-dependent result with another child.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The owning action retains one finite two-foot preparation until release; each child plan owns two lazy four-term noise readers and its joint map. No FK reader, evaluated pose or query history is retained.
