## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the result and the refusals.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Each key is the whole-body fit on its contacts, seeded from the key before; a transition key holds both stances' contacts at once; the course between keys is a minimum-jerk blend of each joint on its range.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Keys and their transfer writers are prepared once. Bare support playback dispatches to the interval writer at its ordered contact event, and authored reads select the same writer after clamping their time.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

A contact the fit misses is refused, never left where the fit stopped. Every hand contact window keeps its arm and hand, regardless of kind.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

At most two fits per stance after the first, three for a step, run once at plan time. Authored queries find an interval in O(log K) for K ordered keys. Bare playback selects the prepared phase once. Fixed pairs, the common pin and the source point prepare once, while current interpolation and floor readings remain.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Solved keys and contact order define immutable interval geometry; entry-target/source-point observations share only within one schedule generation and settle clears them. Current posture calls forward parent FK/ROM loans to actual contact/floor observations, with every current solve retained.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The plan owns keys and one finite preparation per adjacent interval until release. Intervals retain fixed endpoint metadata and at most one source/target record until settle, without current poses, queried times or borrowed FK/ROM workspaces.
