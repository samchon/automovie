## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Read wrists or ankles and their bending joints at both keys. Above-floor contacts reuse the key fitter's toe, palm or grip datum. Floor feet retain the heel or ball socket their window holds (else the lower at the key before), and floor hands retain the wrist.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Select the anatomical point and needed frames. Observe both keys and re-express each pivot in its end bone's local frame.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The world pivot uses achieved bone frames, including the point offset of a raised grip. No authored target replaces an achieved key.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

One fixed bone demand for the two key poses. Each limb contributes one frame pair and at most one bearing pivot.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Reads all retained limbs from the same two resolved bone maps and uses the existing support-contact form rules.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources
