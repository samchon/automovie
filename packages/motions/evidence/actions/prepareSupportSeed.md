## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

With one raised knee the hip lies on its thigh-length sphere inside the other endpoint reach constraints. Otherwise translation is the least-squares world displacement on the specified axes. Each anchored wrist and ankle keeps its observed offset from the contact and uses two-bone IK; the normal and coupled constraints remain the whole-body fit's.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Read the moved contact frames once, calculate the root's displacement, then resolve the anchored limbs at that root.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The candidate runs through the same constraint merit as the predecessor and preference. No contact is moved to make its target easier.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

One demanded FK and one analytic fit for each anchored limb. Used only when the original candidates leave a contact outside its geometric tolerance.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

All contact offsets and bend poles are observed together on the unchanged predecessor.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Returns a new posture. Observed frames, goals and any borrowed kinematics are released with the call.
