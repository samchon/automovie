## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States what the scene holds and how each object is named, what seeking does and refuses, where the marks come from, and the delivery camera.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Figures are posed through Human's canonical adapter in `MotionCaptureFigure`; the grid and lights are the harness's scene dressing, kept in the subject as the campaign asks, and the grid is measured from the posed figures and boxes of every instant so the framing covers the whole case.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Build the scene, measure the floor, add the grid and camera, return the subject.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Poses are the case's own samples, never interpolated; an unsampled second is refused.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Building is linear in figures times instants for the floor; a seek is linear in the figures' joints.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One scene and one set of marks serve every draw of the page.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Figures, ghosts, boxes and the grid are released by the subject's `dispose`.
