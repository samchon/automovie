Surface targets are metres: every path the package's exterior target table binds (for example surface.leftLowerLimb.foot.length) is solved on one shared exterior, and each result states its instrument's departure from the survey definition. Head radii (leftLowerLimb.thigh.femur.sphereFittedHeadRadius and the humeral counterpart) are millimetres. One document is one inspection, so targets and radii are inspected separately. Observed values, gap paths, measurements no product path consumes and a bound channel also written in shape are refused by name. Save preserves the document; export writes the static candidate.
