# src/scene/disposeFormationRepresentations.ts#disposeFormationRepresentations

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Collects actual owned tier geometry, shadow handles, visible materials and every declared gait texture by native identity, then independently invokes each release; iterable tiers must represent resources this owner may retire, and physical GPU release is not certified by public listener completion.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One tier retirement projection serves both constructor rollback and runtime close, with shadow-pair ownership delegated to its own independent disposer and no hero/input model disposal.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No first throwing geometry/material can skip later resource roles, and shared identities are not retried within this attempt; callbacks are actual public disposers rather than replaced methods or synthetic success counters.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Gives all owned resource roles, all declared takes rather than active-only texture, shared identity dedup and borrowed-hero/input exclusions.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Visits tier/material/take entries once to build identity Sets and then R unique resource operations, O(entries+R) time and O(R) temporary references independent of anonymous member count.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One attempt deduplicates shared geometry/material/shadow/texture objects; constructor and runtime callers reuse this implementation, while their lifetime owner must memoize completion rather than re-invoking this effectful primitive.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Acquires no new GPU resource and borrows the tier collection only for snapshot/retirement; attempts every role before reporting failures, and the calling lifetime owner must retain failed/indeterminate handles until its supported physical boundary ends.
