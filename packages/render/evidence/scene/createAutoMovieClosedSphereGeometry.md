## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The returned geometry owns Three's position, normal, UV and index buffers. Failed output admission disposes them before throwing. The producer retains no closure, actor, renderer or task; callers dispose accepted geometry after its last mesh consumer.
