## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Construction regenerates N anonymous slots and fills each resident tier, bakes each tier once and locates E exceptions by scanning C chunks then the matching chunk's slots. Updates visit chunks/tiers and heroes, sample cue lists for each exception, and rewrite N matrices when spacing/reform changes. Cue sampling and native graph updates add their actual costs; retained buffers scale with N times resident tiers rather than one Object3D per member.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Shares tier geometry/material/cycle textures across chunk meshes, retains tier hysteresis and only rewrites arrangement when spacing/reform inputs change; absolute cue/cadence inputs drive updates, and failed/successful disposal completion is reused without physical retries.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Construction scope immediately owns tier retirement and each chunk geometry/mesh, rolling them back independently on later refusal; returned runtime owns all anonymous resources until disposal and borrows hero objects. Disposal stops updates, attempts every unique resource, retains operation closures on failed release and preserves its original completion until the runtime is released.
