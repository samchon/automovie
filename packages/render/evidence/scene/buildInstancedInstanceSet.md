## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Regenerates seeded compact slots, admitted static prototypes, scales/traits and sRGB-decoded palettes, then uses engine crop-aware culling/LOD with native instance matrices; rigid representation requirements and finite admitted transforms are premises. ## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Separates prototype-id and tier keys, one shared flattened representation per pair and chunk-owned instance buffers; generated materials stay owned while borrowed loaded materials receive white clones. The Manor assembly consumes the result through its existing instance consumer, while website baking and final draw/capture observations have their own owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Gives compact population purpose, supported prototype boundary, loaded/generated ownership, regenerated attribute/color roles and the existing Manor assembly connection, without certifying actual raster or compiled-shot delivery from constructor reach.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Regenerates N slots and filters them by P prototypes per chunk, then fills matrices/colors/traits across resident LOD tiers; construction is O(N*P plus resident buffer filling/prototype flattening), update visits chunk/prototype tiers and scans supported visibility state without creating member objects. Buffers grow with admitted members times resident tiers and authored trait count.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Shares flattened geometry and instance-only materials by prototype/tier, decoded swatches by exact authored palette string and resident meshes/hysteresis across updates; separate prototype identities retain distinct geometry and mutable per-runtime retirement state.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Construction scope registers prototype/chunk geometry, owned generated or cloned materials and native instance meshes from acquisition; refusal independently rolls back them while loaded sources/textures remain borrowed. Returned dispose closes updates, deduplicates/attempts all resources once, clears healthy registries and retains failure/operation references for uncertain retirement until owner lifetime ends.
