## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Null selects a real renderer default, while blank, absent, conflicting and unrequested ids refuse; construction or disposal failure never becomes an untextured substitute or successful empty-table claim.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Deduplicates requested ids before engine resolution and builds each admitted material once; definition admission searches model/material declarations per requested id, texture prime deduplicates assets, and retirement visits each built material once.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One material object per requested id is lent to all surfaces in the shot; the supplied cache shares decoded assets, and completed disposal or its stored failure is reused without native retries.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The returned owner retains its material Map until dispose; successful releases remove entries while failed entries and their original completion remain retained. Construction rollback attempts every earlier built material, and texture lifetimes stay with the external shot cache.
