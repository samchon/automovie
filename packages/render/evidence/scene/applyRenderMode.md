Structural installation traverses scene helpers plus builder mesh/bone populations; the generic override allocates per mesh, legacy masks/shells share by their declared child/cycle keys, and pose/outline overlays scale with the subject graph. Healthy beauty returns a constant no-op handle.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Three boundary callbacks retain helper visibility, fog and environment/background snapshots; builders nest their own background transitions and own pass resources. Failed returned-handle restoration retains the complete boundary callback and cause under the scene guard. Builder throw still independently restores boundary snapshots with its primary error, while partial acquisition depends on that builder's rollback capability.
