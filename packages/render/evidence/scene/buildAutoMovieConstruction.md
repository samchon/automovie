## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Validated finite float32-representable generating samples become native Line/LineLoop objects; line closure and identity are explicit and geometry is never inferred from a displayed mesh.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

This generic viewer producer owns rendering and resource lifetime for construction while recovery and other generators remain the authorities for the lines' numerical meaning.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No curve refit, source overlay, coordinate weld, native method replacement or construction inferred from visible triangles enters display; invalid points and point budgets refuse before native allocation.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States metre units, closed-line endpoint convention, float32 display admission, caller mounting, first-allocation rollback, owned resources and disposal limits.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Validation and position conversion cost O(P) for P supplied points, bounded by maxPoints, with one geometry per line and one material shared by the call's lines.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

All same-colour lines in this returned construction share one owned material; geometry is created once and no cross-request cache retains caller records.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Construction rollback immediately owns every native allocation; successful output transfers geometries/material and one shared retirement promise to the caller, with no renderer, RAF, listener or global registry acquired.
