## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Stage membership comes from its independently authored ID list. A run binds measured quantities to the check definition, observed source and input digests. An explicit capture inspects the record its own attempt published and confirms that it remains current before retaining its digest.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One evaluator coordinates selection, stage omissions and retained measurement or capture evidence. The media readers keep their own scope, observation and publication rules.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Unknown and excluded observations remain explicit. Selecting another check cannot remove a required row. A damaged known record keeps its path and repair action. A cached display outcome cannot replace a measurement, and the latest file cannot substitute for another capture attempt's result.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Walks the distinct selected targets and their check and required lists. An evaluated local receipt hashes its selected source and declared inputs, repeating observations during an explicit run. Media checks reuse the digest of an unchanged file snapshot for the command.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

A retained measurement is recomputed against its criterion only when the definition, scoped input bytes and measurement binding (`signature`) agree. A refreshed capture additionally binds the record digest. Legacy capture currentness needs a matching clean revision at the checkout root. A nested production can name shared files by project-relative paths within the same checkout.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Each selected check retains its latest receipt and first witnessed failure under the owning production cache. The first complete failure is linked from separate staged bytes before latest is replaced. An explicit refresh rebuilds latest from current observations independently of its prior contents. Temporary receipt writes are atomically published and their staging paths are released.
