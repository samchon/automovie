## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The limbs' taper and bend, a recurve's tips and the horn nocks are not drawn: each limb turns stiff about the grip; no contact reads more. The string's radius is half the read arrow nock width it fits. A crossbow's stock is a box of its fore-end's section, its taper not drawn; its cranequin's axle direction and its stirrup's height are unread and placed provisionally; its draw weight is unread, so it has none.
