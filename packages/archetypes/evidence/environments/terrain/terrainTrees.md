## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Protect the entire placement disk plus the maximum trunk radius. Every uniform seeded slot is then safe by construction, and existing instance regeneration evaluates the same height at its placed XZ.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

This owner handles vegetation footprints and named stream derivation. The existing tree producer owns form, and instanceSlot owns population placement, scale and ground evaluation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Refuse unbounded population counts, collapsed scale and disks approaching an exclusion. Acceptance is independent of the sampled seed and uses no rejected-slot retry.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Gives metre disk and world height semantics, independent streams, exclusions and the compact output.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Each named disk needs one prototype and one bounded clearance query. Construction retains only O(disks) prototype and placement records plus the already owned height rule.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

A disk shares one referenced terrain rule and one model across all of its slots. Each named stream derives from the occurrence and disk name so adding another disk does not consume its values.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The caller owns the compact definitions and models. Preparing the scatter issues no renderer handles or deferred tasks.
