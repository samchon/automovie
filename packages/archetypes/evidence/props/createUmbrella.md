## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The canopy is a flat disc, not a ribbed dome, and a closed umbrella's fabric is not drawn, only its ribs; the handle's length is CCOHS's, and the shaft's radius and the canopy's thickness are construction estimates that no act reads and no band declares; the ferrule is not drawn. A closed umbrella opens by its runner's articulation, the ribs' span giving the canopy's size; open builds the opened disc whole.
