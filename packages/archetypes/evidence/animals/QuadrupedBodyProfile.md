# src/animals/QuadrupedBodyProfile.ts#QuadrupedBodyProfile

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Divide lengths by reference height and project straight controls with sine/cosine. A radius-w withers sphere centered at height-w reaches exactly height. Removing both head caps from its total axial length gives the bar span; reserving a positive pelvic run avoids a zero-length hindquarters bar.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Admit source and construction scalars, derive the common controls once, then return the existing body representation to all three species adapters.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No species branch, borrowed horse profile, measured-pose claim, fitted patch, arbitrary numeric fallback or physical coefficient is supplied. The source adapter declares every measurement transfer and authored estimate.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Admission and construction each inspect a fixed number of scalars and eight limb controls; O(1) time/space and no mesh generation, geometry search or iterative numerical solve occurs.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One shared mapping serves ox, camel and deer. Immutable-in-type source data is read directly, and fresh arrays/records prevent a result mutation from changing source choices or a later result.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Retains no props, query, cache or external handle. The caller owns all returned records; no segment shares or foot material are added, leaving their absence explicit for the existing builder.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Defines reference/instance metres, control directions, circular/capsule interpretations, producer-owned provenance, resource ownership and refusals; no anatomical or visual result is asserted.
