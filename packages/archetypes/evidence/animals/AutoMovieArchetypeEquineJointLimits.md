## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The closed anatomical lookup copies the primary rows in their original landmark conventions. Compare selects one requested DOI, orders the caller's same-convention angle against its endpoint means, and divides signed differences by the fixed positive reported SDs. No local Euler calibration is implied.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

The lookup switch selects one table row set and copies its nested statistics/source records; compare validates the angle, reuses the lookup, refuses a missing source and computes one diagnostic.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Standing extension stays labelled as standing, distinct cohorts and marker rays are not pooled, and no mean, SD multiplier, unmeasured axis or uncalibrated landmark becomes an anatomical limit or a zero rotation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Lookup and comparison take O(S) time for S source rows, at most two; independent copied output uses O(S) space and comparison adds constant working storage.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The ten fixed primary rows and their three source descriptions have one module owner; compare reuses the lookup and recomputes only caller-dependent arithmetic without caching temporal/model results.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The module retains ten rows and three source records; lookup clones all exposed nested records/tuples so returned data cannot mutate the reference table. Calls acquire no handles or retain caller observations.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The lookup and compare operation define proximal bone mapping, study-specific included-angle degrees, paired mean/SD tuples, source identity/conditions, unsupported joints and unknown runtime limits. Compare requires one retained DOI and a finite [0,360] angle and reports signed endpoint-SD differences, not probability or pose admission.
