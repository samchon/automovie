# src/animals/createPony.ts#createPony

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The breed drawn whole over the measured breeds, then the horse's equid builder on that breed's profile: its own height and girth, its mass by `AutoMovieArchetypeEquine.mass`, bone fractions from its own measures (Brooks et al. 2010), and the horse's mass shares by Nauwelaerts et al. 2011's size-independence below 600 kg. The law gives the Shetland 174 kg, the Welsh Mountain Pony 250, the Dartmoor 255, the Welsh Pony 330 and the Connemara 427. The weighed ponies read beside it are checks, not anchors, their heights unread: van den Bogert's five of 165 to 240 kg (as Nauwelaerts et al. 2011 report) and Pearson et al. 1998's three of 174, 184 and 220 kg, so the Welsh Pony's and the Connemara's distance above them is no evidence against the law; breed pages give the Connemara about 300 to 450 kg, secondary.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts
