# src/animals/CamelGait.ts#CamelGait

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Distinguishes Bactrian walking from dromedary running pace, identifies source variables and averaging, and states phase, variance, shape and morphology-transfer limits.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

One walking speed/period and separate fore/hind contact fractions enter the shared walk key; phase data remain species-owned and absolute scaling preserves their experimental timebase.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The camel producer reads one table through the common quadruped ride; no species branch, duplicate solver or runtime source-file loader is added.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

No horse trot is relabelled as a camel pace, no walking delay becomes coincident running contacts, and no unreported stride SD, speed band or anatomical ROM is fabricated.
