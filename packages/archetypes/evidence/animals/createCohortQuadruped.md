## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Admit profiles and non-emitting gear plans for the entire unfixed catalogue, including its seedless default, before one whole-row draw. Thus equipment refusal cannot depend on seed. A fixed cohort admits only itself. The selected prepared profile/additions build one model with source or authored mass and unknown foot friction.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Prepare source figures, rigs and physical additions for the request's complete choice domain, make one cohort draw, record tempo and emit the selected profile and plans once.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No independent trait sampling, unreported population band, equine weight law, invented mass/foot coefficient or renderer-only feature is substituted. A point profile is not presented as completion of population variation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

O(K) fixed-size profile/rig/addition planning for K declared reference rows precedes one model construction; no unselected model geometry, mesh or physical simulation is built. Selected body lay reads the same generating definition for its rigid geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One model-axis draw owns reference/provenance selection. Prepared body profiles and selected lead/equipment plans are reused for emission; no seed retry or selected gear replanning occurs.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The returned model owns its records. The O(K) profile/plan catalogue, draw session and construction callbacks are call-local and become unreachable after emission; no process, global cache or external handle is retained.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States exact whole-reference ownership, fixed/seedless/seeded selection, authored catalogue frequency, source versus equipment mass and declared timing spread.
