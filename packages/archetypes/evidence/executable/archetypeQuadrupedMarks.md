## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States model-metre coordinates, neutral/posed ownership, independently carried parent attachment expectations, exact primitive floor heights and binary64-only gap admission. Marks expose geometry, not anatomical ROM or force fidelity.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Neutral bar frames supply parent-local joint offsets once. Each drawn model supplies fresh endpoints and parent frames; their rigidly carried expected origins are compared with child origins. The existing exact primitive support owner measures floor clearance. Support functions locate terminal soles, and only explicitly supplied producer goals are compared with terminal axes.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Recognize the quadruped vocabulary, prepare its neutral hierarchy, then index one current model and emit separately named bone, joint, attachment-gap, sole-height and floor marks.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No renderer proxy, second pose sample, achieved-point-as-goal assertion, strict posed-chain decoder, biological tolerance or guessed foot radius hides a failure. A floor-height witness is explicitly a vertical projection at the part centre, not a claimed physical contact point.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

O(P+B) time and storage for parts and 21 bones per draw; source hierarchy preparation is O(P+B) once per member.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Retains only the source hierarchy and reuses Quaternion, Vector3 and QuadrupedRestFloor. One sampled model supplies both drawing and every current observation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The draw subject owns the prepared closure; returned marks contain fresh numeric arrays and acquire no geometry, process, cache, file or renderer handle.
