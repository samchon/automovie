# src/executable/subjects.ts#subjects

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States draw/mark ownership, native rig families and same-sample rest goals, model-space anchoring, surface/xray inspection, scene layout, seeking and the delivery camera.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Models use render's real buildModel; affordances use AutoMovieArchetypeMarks and quadruped diagnostics independently observe those same sampled parts against neutral sources or explicit producer goals. Anchored marks inherit the exact rendered member transform. Floor and camera extents cover the subject's whole clock.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Lay the members out, build them at a second, measure the floor, add the grid and the camera.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No substituted geometry or second pose sample supplies marks; only explicit producer goals receive effector-error marks. The grid is dressing and diagnostics are labelled construction observations, not anatomical or dynamic proof.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

A seek rebuilds each member and observes its parts/bones linearly; a moving subject's floor is measured at 24 evenly spaced samples of its clock.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Member offsets, arrow lengths and neutral diagnostic hierarchy are prepared once; one sampled model supplies geometry, contacts and all diagnostics per placed frame.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Each seek releases replaced geometry/materials, and any failed seek releases all partial current builds before rethrowing. Dispose releases the last models and grid; plain diagnostic records retain no rendering handles.
