## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States canonical dog and saddle-horse sources, admitted minimum stirrup drop and reference withers height, geometric support pitch, authored clock/carriage, contact consumer and model-space placement rule; no horse clinical trajectory is claimed.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Public dog/horse producers supply bodies and tack. AutoMovieArchetypeQuadrupedRest computes supported poses from bars and one explicit floor. Each sampled animal already includes the root placement; that same sample supplies terminal goals for independent geometry marks, without another root transform or sampler call.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Prepare two source/law pairs with explicit authoring values and complete carriers, then a shared adapter exposes their events, posed models and terminal targets to the existing instrument.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No static fallback, substituted geometry, fake readiness or measured-timing claim hides a failed sample; the law's ordinary refusals propagate to capture.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Two bounded source/law preparations and one O(P+B+A) pose with four goal references per request; each event mapping contains four entries.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Every subject shares its immutable source and prepared time-only law across seeks; the same sample supplies marks and geometry without a completed-frame cache.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Each subject retains its source body and law for its catalogue lifetime, acquires no renderer/process/file, and returns fresh posed transforms per request.
