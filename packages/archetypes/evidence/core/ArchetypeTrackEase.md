## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

ArchetypeTrackProfile owns the unchanged quintic and its conditioned value/inverse/rate. The original sample is compared against held boundaries before elapsed/remaining time; FastTwoSum's prepared end residual preserves the remaining time when whole-span subtraction rounds to duration.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Snapshot/admit input scalars, prepare one profile and clock residual, then return the scalar reader and optional crossing.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No range margin, curve, measured bound or sample time is changed or clamped. Unsupported clock resolution refuses instead of moving an event.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Two range admissions, constant-time playback and one profile crossing when requested; range admission owns its existing neutral-pose cost.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Clock/range/profile owners prepare one independent track, with no geometry rebuilding or inverse calculation per sample.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Only prepared scalar values and the profile closures escape; the mutable input record and model are not retained by playback.
