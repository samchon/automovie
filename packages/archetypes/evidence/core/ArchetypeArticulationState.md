## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the units, permitted reversal, result, immutability and refusals including duplicate inputs.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The engine's AffordanceArticulation.input admits each named input through explicitly reversible maps and exact forward endpoint images. An independent degree of freedom receives exactly one authored input, including when redundant authored coordinates agree.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One engine admission per authored input, a producer-specific structured refusal on failure, then the independent leader map consumed by forward articulation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No producer names, clamping, duplicate-input precedence or inversion of an unmarked follower.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

O(S times (D plus T)) for S authored inputs, follows depth D and table rows T along each path; state and visited maps are bounded by the model's articulations.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Shares input admission and travel coordinates with engine and motion consumers; independent inputs are normalized once per pose.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The leader map lives for one pose; the engine releases each temporary chain before the next input.
