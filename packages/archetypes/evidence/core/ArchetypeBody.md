## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States body construction, absent-coefficient meaning and fresh ownership of every mutable nested record.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Copies SI mass properties and only declared contact coefficients into the existing model body representation; null is preserved as absence rather than zero. Builder owns material and restitution admission, while validateBody owns the full model scalar ranges; this projection does not repeat either gate.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One body-construction owner serves the AutoMovieArchetype constructor and keeps serialization and nested ownership independent of geometry and articulation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Adds no coefficient, material inference or geometry estimate and preserves the existing field insertion order.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

A fixed number of scalar reads and at most three small nested copies take constant time and storage.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Reads the existing scalar metadata and mass properties without recomputing or revalidating them; every body's mutable records remain distinct.

## @evidenceExclude contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Only the fresh body escapes, with no retained archetype, builder, query or external handle.
