# Independent narrative and delivery review

A review reads as the next recipient, not as the author defending the text. Use a fresh context for the same pass and scope. If an independent context is unavailable, report the missing review rather than substituting self-review.

## Read before auditing

Read the complete selected body first and record the first response and every concrete loss of understanding, intent, tension, performance, or continuity before opening author evidence. Then read the relevant contracts, actual parents, adopted work requirements, and evidence; audit the grounds against the entire unit rather than a matching quotation.

After locking that reading, build an inventory from the actual body independently of its contract answers: facts, quantities, chronology, names, titles and forms of address, number and date notation, recurring words and constructions, literal quotations, and consequential action, image, sound, and continuity values. Compare every element with its actual source or parent and its adopted language and work requirements. Contract questions and relationship rows cannot replace this inventory. Account for each first-reading concern as a supported finding or a withdrawal supported by the actual passage.

For settings and treatments, independently compare externally checkable facts and causal claims with the original source's whole relevant sequence and context. A matching word or number cannot establish that an inference or comparison follows. Scripts and screenplay inherit facts through their actual parents; compare those values there and report a doubtful parent value at its earliest owner instead of silently correcting it downstream.

Settings is read as the treatment writer and production designer; treatments as the script writer; scripts as the final screenplay writer and performer; screenplay as the filmmaker and audience. Do not excuse a weak final realization because the parent was less expressive. Protect a deliberate choice only by showing what would be lost against a concrete alternative.

## Current units and a whole-pass verdict

Use the configured current review tree, mirroring each authored file and H2 in `docs/review/<draft|final>/<layer>/round-N`. Construction reviews target body; naturalness reviews target working text. Each clean unit carries its native current fingerprint review, and `verdict/index.md` answers the whole-pass review duties. A stage flag or a fresh hash alone is not acceptance.

Collect all findings and their affected occurrences before any correction. State the source, failure, owner, and consequences. The author records acceptance, partial acceptance, or rejection with evidence; disputed findings return to the reviewer. Repair the responsible owner and all affected consumers together. A new clean complete round closes the initial pass. A later scoped reopening rereads the complete affected dependency closure and may carry an unaffected judgment only when its body, inputs, and requirements remain unchanged. Record its original round and grounds without calling it a new reading. The highest round must contain the whole current review inventory and a renewed population verdict, including those explicitly carried units; missing units cannot be borrowed implicitly from older folders.

The reviewer who raised a disputed finding answers a partial acceptance or rejection in the same record. End that exchange when neither side supplies new evidence; repeating a rejected reason does not establish it. A new reviewer verifies repaired text in the next round. Retained candidates and protected achievements still face their concrete alternatives and current contract failures.

Keep only the current completed round in the working tree once its carried judgments and findings are accounted for; previous rounds remain in version history. Changed inputs invalidate the relevant judgments, including answers that quote or depend on changed facts. A mechanical checker can locate stale relationships but cannot grant semantic acceptance.

## Whole work and actual audience experience

After every required pass is current and the full graph is clean, perform a separate whole-work review. Read all current bodies, sources, frozen editions, selected contracts, evidence, and configuration. Check beginning against later consequences and ending, all part boundaries, promises, recurrence, information states, and duration distribution. A selected part or pilot cannot satisfy obligations of an unfinished whole.

For delivered video, first watch and listen to the entire assembled artifact as an audience member who has not read its planning documents, and lock that response before diagnosing it against the screenplay and code. Then trace missing or changed experience through shot source, actual edit inclusion, accepted audio, captions, transitions, and output bytes. Use measured/performed timing, not word count.

Report the exact revision, media and output identities, selected scope, completed checks, actual observations, and anything unverified. Full semantic acceptance requires a complete round with no edits and no findings; local graph success or a collection of passing shots cannot replace it.
