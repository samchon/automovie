# Narrative lifecycle

The long route preserves four creative layers and two expression passes:

`settings construction → treatments construction → scripts construction → scripts naturalness → screenplay construction → screenplay naturalness → production → whole-work review`

This carries the source architecture's distinction between facts, events, scene execution, and final-medium realization into video. Each layer completes evidence and independent review before consumers rely on it. Only an explicit user waiver omits an expression pass. Short mode begins at screenplay construction and retains its naturalness, discovery, contracts, evidence, independent review, and actual-output review; asset mode has no narrative layers.

## Owners and preparation

[Instructions](../../.agents/skills/instructions/SKILL.md) records adopted requirements and delegated direction. [Discovery](../../.agents/skills/discovery/SKILL.md) compares requirements, material, references, and current work with existing owners before bulk settings and at downstream design reviews. Facts belong to settings, events and information order to treatments, executed scenes and release partition to scripts, and final audiovisual realization to screenplay. Work contracts own production-specific judgment and form.

Read current parent bodies and reviews when starting, resuming, or changing a source. Treatments depends on settings construction; scripts depends on treatments construction; screenplay depends on treatments and reviewed, transferred scripts after naturalness. An explicit waiver may use scripts construction instead. Facts pass through those parents. Direct settings evidence cannot repair an incomplete treatment handoff. A short without those parents follows its request and adopted constraints directly.

Installed `@automovie/evidence` documentation owns configuration, supported layout, diagnostics, and checker APIs; `@wrtnlabs/evidence` owns general annotation syntax. Keep technical details with those owners. Add narrative options and the actual production mode to `evidence.config.ts` before narrative work, following the [evidence guide](evidence.md); a disabled draft stage is not permission to delete a required layer.

## Complete a construction

1. With the pass disabled, finish its whole declared body before writing evidence answers. Read selected contract goals without shaping each paragraph as a checklist response. Preserve an identifiable snapshot of that complete draft in version history or an immutable copy before opening evidence; a partial progress snapshot does not authorize the transition.
2. Open the corresponding evidence stage and its claims together. Compare the whole body with every selected requirement, repair all findings by cause, and write [evidence](../../.agents/skills/evidence-writing/SKILL.md) from the corrected work.
3. Review the whole selected population as its recipient, including boundaries and global obligations. An independent reviewer follows the [reading procedure](narrative-review.md#read-before-auditing) to prepare the record before reading the annotation-free body, locks that reading, then examines parents, creative contracts, evidence, and prior judgments.
4. Record reasoned author decisions and repair accepted findings through their owners. Follow the [review rounds](narrative-review.md#current-units-and-a-whole-pass-verdict) until a complete round has neither edits nor findings. Retain the current review and keep the completed pass in its evidence stage. Closed layers follow [upstream repair](#upstream-repair-and-scope).

A target check establishes its target, not a complete pass. Check the complete current structure through the installed checker, for example `npm run production:check -- --layer scripts --pass construction`, and run unfiltered production validation before handoff. Report the actual diagnostics and readiness without reopening closed passes under the boundaries below. Readiness diagnoses preparation and evidence structure; independent judgment establishes creative quality. Whole-work completion still requires a clean package build.

Settings includes its outline in the same completion. A unit answer cannot establish whole-population coverage, character development, middle accumulation, partition, or complete handoff. Use the selected population respondent and compare all members.

## Complete expressive rewriting

[Rewriting](../../.agents/skills/rewriting/SKILL.md) preserves construction in `docs/revision/<layer>/...` and develops matching files in `docs/naturalness/<layer>/...`. Scripts and screenplay each have their own cycle. Keep paths, H1/H2 identities, titles, and order; compare semantic fidelity with that cycle's original in both directions.

The [cycle rules](../../.agents/skills/rewriting/cycles.md) distinguish the initial meaning baseline from a previous working edition's expressive achievements, and a comprehensive rewrite from the local repairs that complete its evidence and review. Preserve both comparison inputs when revisiting expression.

Finish the whole working version while its pass is disabled, using [repair by cause](#repair-by-cause) for the author's complete reading and correction. Open evidence when complete, attach answers and rewriting notes in comments, and submit working version and frozen construction to independent review.

Retain working version, evidence, frozen original, findings, and author verdicts. After a clean whole round, transfer the working version to `docs/body` with HTML comments removed, preserving every other byte. The transferred unit's construction evidence, review, and approval remain closed against the frozen construction. Do not rewrite those answers or renew construction review or approval for the transferred text; report any construction diagnostics without repairing them. An unchanged working edition keeps its naturalness review. Downstream work receives the body only when it exactly equals the comment-free reviewed working edition.

Expression repairs preserve the frozen original and correct the working version. A defect in original facts, events, or execution follows upstream repair. A new construction starts a new cycle; previous approvals and evidence cannot become proof of the new body.

## Independent review and records

Use a reviewer who does not share the author's creative work memory. Supply declared scope, current inputs, and contracts rather than a conclusion to confirm. Identify independent review and self-review honestly. Read every file and unit in the snapshot; divide a large scope into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling.

Construction records use `docs/review/draft/<layer>/round-N/`; expression records use `docs/review/final/<layer>/round-N/`. Follow the installed package's actual review format. Preserve reviewed revision and inputs, scope, literal findings and support, causes and occurrences, protected achievements, author decisions and reasons, unresolved limits, and outcome. Keep rejected or retained candidates accountable through passages and alternatives. Preference alone is not a defect; an earlier pass does not excuse a current failure.

Use the [review principles](../../.agents/skills/contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md) for findings and their support, and [repair by cause](#repair-by-cause) for correction rounds.

## Repair by cause

Every author reading, evidence correction, independent review, and author response completes its inspection round before edits. Group findings by cause, find their occurrences and affected consumers across the declared scope, then repair them together. Search repeated words and constructions as well as reading; a few examples do not establish the class's extent. Put missing material where its action or explanation belongs rather than appending an answer at the end.

After repair, compare changed passages together for the same failure in different words or a new uniform mold. Compare their meaning with actual parents and any frozen original. Within the submitted pass, recheck dependent sentences, quotations, positions, counts, order, screen action, inherited state, exclusions, and reasons for preservation; a surviving quotation alone does not keep an answer true. Upstream changes follow the closed-pass boundary below. Keep unaffected bytes and historical judgments attributable to their actual snapshot.

For a questionable expression retained on function, write a workable alternative giving the same information, access, and pressure without that form, then show what the original adds in its actual context. Keep that comparison available to independent review. A functional label, parental precedent, previous approval, or loss on deletion alone cannot replace it. The selected contract judges the result. Complete the next whole inspection round before adopting further repairs.

## Upstream repair and scope

Repair missing facts, contradictions, or an unworkable design at the earliest construction owner. Change downstream bodies only where a sentence actually consumes the changed value. Closed layers are not reviewed again, whether downstream work repairs their bodies or upstream changes produce diagnostics in them. An upstream change does not reopen either layer's evidence, reviews, or approvals. Remove answers for deleted H2s and write answers for newly created H2s only; report other upstream-change diagnostics without repairing them. Keep expression fidelity repairs in the working edition with its frozen original unchanged.

When replacing a unit from scratch, remove its obsolete current construction answers, frozen original, working version, and corresponding current review records, together with obsolete descendants being replaced. Preserve completed material outside that scope and history in version control. A reused filename does not carry old approval into new content.

The default narrative scope is `complete-work`. Choose `first-part-pilot` only when the user commissions that route and identify the exact part. Settings remains work-wide, while treatments and scripts/screenplay cover the declared slice. Preserve slice lineage, applicable obligations, and current reviews. Explicitly identify whole-work obligations the pilot cannot establish.

Expand only after required pilot passes and independent slice review are current. Switch scope together, reset affected narrative passes, design complete treatments including the pilot's material, and reconsider script partition. Establish new evidence and reviews for the whole population rather than extending pilot approval by implication. Preserve out-of-scope content until the authorized rewrite includes it.

## Complete the delivered work

After all required creative passes and unfiltered validation, independently review the current whole work across its layers and rendered delivery. Inspect all promised material, cross-part consequences, ending, duration, and assembled image and sound. A pilot, correct tags, separate shot captures, or silent animatic cannot establish a finished film or series.

Trace screenplay units into shot source and the actual edit, then judge the experience without supplying information from planning. Record output identities, observed playback conditions, speech and text intelligibility, continuity, pacing, transitions, limitations, and unrendered scope. Repair findings together by earliest cause and obtain a new complete clean round. For a published version, record the original edition, changed scope and meaning, reason, and required recipient notice, keeping references aligned with the new edition.
