# Commissioned soundtrack

A motion hint and a direct 3D film are silent unless the commission asks for sound, and no stage waits on a soundtrack nobody commissioned. A direct 3D film's dialogue belongs to its soundtrack, and [direct film](../video/direct-film.md#faces-and-speech) times the mouths to it.

When one is commissioned, record its tracks in the [delivery profile](../production/project-context.md) with the [loudness](film-sound.md#loudness) target, acquire its material under [acquisition](acquisition.md) and build it under [sound effects](sound-effects.md). Have the user listen at every acceptance, namely each adopted take, each mixed passage with its picture and the delivered file, and record which ones the user heard, because a measurement cannot stand for a listening.

Write a generated score's request under [music prompts](diffusion/prompts.md#music), then adopt its actual take under acquisition.
