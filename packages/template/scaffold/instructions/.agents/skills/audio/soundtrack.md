# Commissioned soundtrack

Record a commissioned soundtrack's tracks in the [delivery profile](../production/project-context.md) with the [loudness](film-sound.md#loudness) target, acquire its material under [acquisition](acquisition.md) and build it under [sound effects](sound-effects.md). Have the user listen at every acceptance [listening](acquisition.md#listening) names.

A direct 3D film's dialogue belongs to its soundtrack, and [direct film](../video/direct-film.md#faces-and-speech) times the mouths to it.

Write a generated score's request under [music prompts](diffusion/prompts.md#music), then adopt its take under acquisition.
