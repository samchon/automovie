# Adoption

Before adding a question, compare the request and adopted instructions with the existing contracts; most needs already have an owner. Creative user choices stay with [instructions](../story/planning/requirements.md#requirements), operating choices with [project context](../production/project-context.md), and an uncovered work requirement goes through [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

A new question names one achievement, why its recipient needs it, its observable failure and the similar legitimate case it must leave alone. Its checkable detail goes into [statements](../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) under the item, not into more questions.

Give it the narrowest respondent that can establish it: a unit answers for itself, and a whole-work duty needs its population respondent.

Put a production- or language-specific question in the production's own work or language contracts, supplementing the shared questions rather than replacing them, and its statements in the production's statements folder outside those two families, because the graph reads every document in them as contracts and respondents. [Document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents) gives each location.

Adopt the question, its respondents and their [configuration](configuration.md) together, and update every consumer when you change, merge or retire one. Changes that touch closed narrative work follow [repair](../story/correction/repair.md). Never weaken a question or narrow its population to hide a defect.
