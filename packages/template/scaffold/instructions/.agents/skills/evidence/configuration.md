# Evidence configuration

Make `evidence.config.ts` select the work the commission actually has. The installed evidence package's README and types own its options, their spelling and its selectors and annotations; this document says only what to select and when.

## Engineering

Keep the default engineering claims: every source declaration and test answers its [development contracts](../contracts/development/SKILL.md). Turn on the entities, behaviors and composition stages when the entity folders, the behavior folders and the composition folder gain their first declaration, so entities cite the facts they realize, behaviors the studies they follow, scenes the screenplay scenes and beats they realize and films the screenplay files they deliver.

Source stages follow the code: a folder with declarations and no stage answers nothing.

## Story

Choose the route option that matches the route [workflow](../story/workflow.md#route-and-parents) adopted, or the option for [no narrative pass](../story/workflow.md#who-writes-the-screenplay) when the user's choice of writer runs none, under which the code realizes the instructions in place of the narrative passes.

Narrative stages follow a finished draft, never the presence of files: enable each layer's construction, and the screenplay's naturalness stage, when its complete draft is ready, because a stage opened on a half-written layer demands answers for text that will change.

Turn on the research stage when its first study holds what its first consumer uses. Complete work is the default scope; a pilot follows [scope expansion](../story/correction/repair.md#scope-expansion).

Scripts expression and population questions belong to scripts construction, and only screenplay expression belongs to the final pass, as the [workflow](../story/workflow.md#route-and-parents) sets. A contract folder named `naturalness` does not choose its pass.

No stage selects a [statements](../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) document, and none should be added to a selector.

## Changing the graph

Inspect every affected population and relationship together, including those outside a pilot and the engineering claims. Closed narrative work keeps its boundary under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
