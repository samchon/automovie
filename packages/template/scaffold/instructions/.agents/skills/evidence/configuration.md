# Evidence configuration

Make `evidence.config.ts` select the work the commission actually has. The installed evidence package's README and types own its options, their spelling and its selectors and annotations; this document says only what to select and when.

## Engineering

Keep the default engineering claims: every source declaration and test answers the shared engineering checklists of the [development contracts](../contracts/development/SKILL.md), and the entities, behaviors and composition stages add the production's own.

Turn on each of those stages when the entity folders, the behavior folders and the composition folder gain their first declaration, unless the route's steps open them later; a folder with declarations and no stage answers nothing.

## Story

Choose the route option that matches the [route](../story/workflow/SKILL.md#choosing-the-route) adopted, or the option for [no narrative pass](../story/workflow/SKILL.md#who-writes-the-screenplay) when the user's choice of writer runs none, under which the code realizes the instructions in place of the narrative passes.

Narrative stages follow a finished draft, never the presence of files, because a stage opened on a half-written layer demands answers for text that will change. Each route's steps say when its stages open and close, and turning a closed layer's stage back to disabled breaks no layer still running.

Except on the short route, whose studies answer no research contract, turn on the research stage when the first study is written. Complete work is the default scope; a pilot follows [scope expansion](../story/correction/repair.md#scope-expansion).

The `naturalness` contract folder holds questions scripts construction answers and chooses no stage of its own.

No stage selects a [statements](../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) document, and none should be added to a selector.

## Changing the graph

Inspect every affected population and relationship together, including those outside a pilot and the engineering claims. Closed narrative work keeps its boundary under [closed passes](../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
