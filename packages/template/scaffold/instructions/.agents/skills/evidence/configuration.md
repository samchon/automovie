# Evidence configuration

Make `evidence.config.ts` select the work the commission actually has. The installed evidence package's README and types own its options, their spelling and its selectors and annotations; this document says only what to select and when.

## Engineering

Keep the default engineering claims: every source declaration and test answers the shared engineering checklists of the [development contracts](../contracts/development/SKILL.md). The entities, behaviors and composition stages add their own checklists from the same family, and the behaviors and composition stages also check the citations those checklists name; entity citations are judged by review.

Turn on each of those stages when the entity folders, the behavior folders and the composition folder gain their first declaration; a folder with declarations and no stage answers nothing. The short route is the exception: it opens the stages its code needs at its [evidence step](../story/workflow/short.md#steps).

## Story

Choose the route option that matches the [route](../story/workflow/SKILL.md#choosing-the-route) adopted, or the option for [no narrative pass](../story/workflow/SKILL.md#who-writes-the-screenplay) when the user's choice of writer runs none, under which the code realizes the instructions in place of the narrative passes.

Narrative stages follow a finished draft, never the presence of files, because a stage opened on a half-written layer demands answers for text that will change. On long work, enable each layer's construction when its complete draft is ready, and a closed layer's stage may go back to disabled without breaking the layers still running. The short route sets both moments in its [steps](../story/workflow/short.md#steps).

Except on the short route, whose studies answer no research contract, turn on the research stage when the first study is written. Complete work is the default scope; a pilot follows [scope expansion](../story/correction/repair.md#scope-expansion).

The `naturalness` contract folder holds questions scripts construction answers and chooses no stage of its own.

No stage selects a [statements](../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) document, and none should be added to a selector.

## Changing the graph

Inspect every affected population and relationship together, including those outside a pilot and the engineering claims. Closed narrative work keeps its boundary under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
