# Video models

Each row shows how a current model takes the motion hint, sets [adherence](../requests.md#adherence) and continues a take; the user may choose any other. Read the chosen model's values from its documentation, as [model documentation](SKILL.md#model-documentation) sets.

| Model | Takes the hint as | Sets [adherence](../requests.md#adherence) by | Continues a take by | Documentation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Runway Aleph 2.0 | One video to edit or restyle, with timed keyframe images | No setting; it keeps what the prompt does not ask to change | A chain frame as the first keyframe | [Models](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/models.md), [prompting](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/52150503729171-Aleph-2-0-Prompting-Guide) |
| Kling Omni | A base video to edit beside other videos and images as references | Wording or input type: editing the base video or referencing it | Not documented beside the hint | [API](https://kling.ai/document-api/api/video/4-0/video-omni), [guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/kling-4-model-user-guide) |
| Kling Motion Control | One figure's body motion, the largest person's, and none when several are alike in size | A character orientation setting: matching the video keeps its camera and facing and is required for binding an element; matching the image takes the camera from the prompt | A chain frame as the character image | [Guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide) |
| Seedance | A video to edit or extend, or several reference videos | A task type: edit, extend or reference, and wording that says what a reference supplies | Extending one video while another is a reference | [Model](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md), [prompting](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5-prompt-guide.md) |
| fal H3 Max 3D to video | A 3D render of up to 15 s and 32 shots, with up to 8 reference images and no first frame | No setting; it keeps camera, paths, timing and cuts closely but not exactly, and invents references when given none, so always give the canon references | None documented; end at a cut | [3D to video](https://fal.ai/models/minimax/h3-max/3d-to-video/llms.txt) |
| Gemini Omni | A video to edit, with reference images and a first or last frame | Wording only | Extension by further turns | [Omni](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/omni) |
| Veo | Still frames: a first frame, or a first and last frame, with asset images for identity | None; it keeps only the frames | An extension from a prompt alone, returned joined to its input | [Model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/veo/3-1-generate), [best practices](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/video/best-practice) |
| Luma Ray | One video to edit, with keyframes | Strength presets, and per-signal controls for pose, depth, normals, trajectory and face | A chain frame as the first keyframe | [Editing](https://docs.agents.lumalabs.ai/guides/videos/editing) |
| LTX, open weights | The RGB hint through a gray-layout adapter with an art-directed first frame, or a union control adapter for depth, edge or pose, or a motion adapter for drawn tracks | Adapter strength, masked over frames and regions | No documented extension beside a control channel | [Layout-To-Render](https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/vfx-post-production/layout-to-render.md), [IC-LoRA](https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/usage-guides/ic-lo-ra.md) |
| Cosmos Transfer, open weights | Segmentation, depth, edge and blur videos, alone or together | A weight per channel, varied over frames and regions, in its own framework; some serving backends take the channels unweighted | The previous output's frame as an input | [Transfer 2.5](https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-transfer2.5), superseded by [Cosmos 3](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Cosmos) |
| DiffusionRenderer, open weights | Base color, normal, depth, roughness and metallic channels, with an HDR environment map for light | None; it keeps the channels and computes the light | Not documented | [Repository](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/diffusion-renderer) |
| Wan VACE, open weights | Depth, pose, scribble, flow, layout or mask channels | Each channel's strength and masks | Extension from the previous clip's first or last frames | [VACE](https://github.com/ali-vilab/VACE), [Wan2.1](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.1) |

Hosted models take the hint as RGB video at most, deriving any other channel from it themselves; open models with control adapters also take rendered depth, edge or pose videos.

Read each model's terms before adopting it: territory limits, rejected reference content such as real human faces or minors, revenue thresholds and labeling duties for open weights, and price per second.

## Other roles

A few models serve one role beside the hint: [Wan2.2-S2V](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2) generates from dialogue audio with a pose video, Wan2.2-Animate in the same repository moves one character by a performer's video, and dubbing models such as [InfiniteTalk](https://github.com/MeiGen-AI/InfiniteTalk), or [MultiTalk](https://github.com/MeiGen-AI/MultiTalk) for several speakers each bound to its own audio track, re-sync a face to accepted speech.
