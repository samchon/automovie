# LTX

Open weights, LTX-2.5 with the LTX-2.3 control adapters. Values were read from the linked documentation in November 2026; it owns them, so read it again when planning.

- **Takes the hint as:** the RGB hint through the Layout-To-Render adapter, which turns a clay or blocky render into the shot; or one depth, edge or pose pass per run through the Union Control adapter.
- **Output:** for Layout-To-Render, a frame count of 8n+1, 24 fps recommended, and sides divisible by 64. The hosted API gives 720p to 4K at 24, 25, 48 or 50 fps.
- **Adherence:** Layout-To-Render at strength 1.0. A [control adapter](../../motion/control-channels.md#dense-passes) from 0 to 1, where 0.5 to 0.8 gives a softer guide, optionally masked over frames and regions.
- **References:** Layout-To-Render requires a first frame made from the hint's first frame, never the raw gray frame. Union Control can take a first-frame image too.
- **Prompt:** for Layout-To-Render, one or two sentences describing the finished shot, not the layout.
- **Sound:** generated by the Python pipeline and the Union Control workflow; the Layout-To-Render ComfyUI graph is silent.
- **Continuing a take:** the hosted extend needs LTX-2.3 Pro. The open pipelines generate a long clip in overlapping windows within one run, and document no extension from a previous take beside a control pass.
- **After generation:** spatial upscalers of 2x or 4x, and temporal upscaling that doubles the frame rate.

Documentation: [Layout-To-Render](https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/vfx-post-production/layout-to-render.md), [Union Control](https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/feature-guides/structural-control/union-control.md), [IC-LoRA](https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/usage-guides/ic-lo-ra.md), [prompting](https://docs.ltx.io/api-documentation/implementation-guides/prompting-guide.md), [pipelines](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/packages/ltx-pipelines/docs/pipelines.md).
