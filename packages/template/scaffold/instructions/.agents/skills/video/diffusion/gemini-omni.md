# Gemini Omni Flash

Values were read from the linked documentation in November 2026; it owns them, so read it again when planning.

- **Takes the hint as:** a video to edit of up to 15 s, or reference videos of up to 3 s: 3 of them for a 3 to 10 s output, 1 for a longer one. Editing or extending an uploaded video is unavailable in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom.
- **Output:** 3 to 15 s, 360p to 4K, with 1080p and 4K upscaled.
- **Adherence:** a task setting, such as edit, extend or reference to video, adds strict constraints; the guide suggests trying without it first.
- **References:** up to 6 images, at most 3 per character for 2 characters. Recognizable people are not supported, nor are minors in the regions above.
- **Prompt:** no negative field; only English is fully supported.
- **Sound:** generated; no voice editing or audio references.
- **Continuing a take:** extend adds 3 to 10 s per turn from the last 10 s of the video, and takes no reference video. Name again, or give as a reference image, whatever appeared only before those 10 s. A request cannot reason across several videos.

Documentation: [Omni](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/omni).
