# Video diffusion models

The models below are representative examples, not a complete or required list; any model the user prefers or a newer release can serve. Their values change between versions, so the linked documentation, read when planning, owns them; the table only shows how differently models take a motion hint and how a request sets its [adherence](requests.md#adherence).

Proprietary hosted models take the hint as RGB video at most and derive any structure from it themselves; open models, including their hosted endpoints, take depth, edge or pose videos directly. Choose an open model with control channels when exact positions, contacts and camera outweigh its setup cost; even it keeps each channel only at the strength the request sets.
Read each model's terms before adopting it: territory limits, rejected reference content such as real faces, revenue thresholds and labeling duties for open weights, and price per second.
