Direct the commission within the user's [delegated choices](../story/planning/direction.md).

## Assignments

Confirm [scope and authority](project-context.md#intake). [Register required production checks](checks.md) before drafting. Give the writer the instructions, inputs and deliverable. Follow the user's authority over roles and worker count. Assign [separate contexts](../story/workflow/routes.md#separate-contexts) to the writer and, when required, the Evidence answerer and independent reviewer. The independent reviewer owns the verdict. [Resume interrupted workers](../multi-agent/continuing.md) when needed.

## Judgment and revision

At each handoff:

- Inspect the current artifact, subject records, cited studies and [commissioned references](../review/delivery-review.md#assets).
- Read the screenplay in viewing order. Follow people and props and check timing, space and intended effect.
- Test any standalone asset in its commissioned use. Record your judgment.
- Use reports to locate issues. Verify independent verdicts and active Evidence against that revision.
- Leave unobserved work pending. Only active Evidence checks the current respondent.

Batch findings. Give each repair owner the location, observed loss and what to preserve. Inspect the revision and any retake joins. Honor [closed passes](../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes) and refer changes to [fixed text](../story/workflow/routes.md#fixed-screenplay) or reserved decisions to the user.

For a programmed film, inspect the [registered cases](../motion/catalog-cases.md#record) and require [source review](../review/source-review.md#the-actual-consumer). Watch the exact cut at 1× and inspect decisive frames yourself. Give the independent viewer the complete candidate. Approve only after independent viewing and verified repairs. [Record your decision](../review/delivery-review.md#acceptance-record) on that cut.
