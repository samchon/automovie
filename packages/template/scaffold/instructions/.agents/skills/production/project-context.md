# Project context

The production's `PROJECT.md` is its one current account of what is made and how, written for the user and the next agent. It states only the current choices; Git keeps the superseded ones.

## What to record

Record only topics the commission needs.

Keep every other fact with its owner and link to it: creative instructions in `docs/instructions`, canon and text in their layers, findings and reviews in their records, and setup and secrets under the development skill's source layout.

### Overview and look

State in a sentence or two what is made, its audience and the intended experience. Record the look only for a generated or direct 3D film, by the facets settings [cinematography](../story/layers/settings.md#records) lists.

On long work a settings record owns both, and a link to it may replace them.

### Commissioned scope

Record the commissioned output and its boundary, with any authorized slice, deferred work and exclusions.

### Representation

Record that people are polygon men and other subjects coarse forms, and which delivery, if any, selects which entity's detail option.

### Adopted approach

Record these choices, each with the reasons and limits that affect later choices:

- the story route;
- the required control channels;
- how the video model takes the hint, and its default adherence;
- the handle length at cuts;
- any simulated window a scene uses;
- how sound is made.

### Finishing approach

For a generated film, record the frame-rate conform and working color space. For a direct 3D film, record the exposure and tone curve.

### Tools and operating constraints

Record what the work runs on and within:

- the chosen providers or local tools;
- the video, image, 3D and motion generation models;
- the takes per video request;
- the runtime, hardware, time, cost and rights limits;
- the approved setup.

### Authority and open choices

Record what the user fixed, what the agent decided within delegation and why, and what remains open.

### The delivery profile

The profile records, for each delivered video:

- dimensions, scan, running time and frame rate as an exact fraction;
- container and codec;
- color primaries, transfer, matrix and range;
- caption tracks, with their format and style guide;
- for generated video, the disclosure its release owes.

For a film with sound, it also records the audio tracks and sample rate, the loudness target with its measurement method and true-peak limit, and any stems or music-and-effects mix the release requires.

This file owns the profile. The film implements it, and long-form settings state matching runtime and frame conditions. Resolve conflicts from the user's commission through the profile.

## When to read and update it

Read project context when starting or resuming. Record adopted changes before consumers use them. Update affected owners together and follow [repair](../story/correction/repair.md) for closed narrative work.

Ask for operating choices the user has not delegated when a task first needs them. Record the answer here. For story choices including runtime and frame, follow the story skill's creative requirements.

## How it controls the work

The recorded scope sets the acceptance boundary. An adopted user decision is fixed within its scope, and the agent makes routine delegated decisions and records them without another permission round.

Preserve adopted exclusions in planning and production.

Mark undecided choices explicitly. Continue independent work.
