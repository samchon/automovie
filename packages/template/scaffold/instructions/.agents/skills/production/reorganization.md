# Reorganization

Package upgrades never rewrite this production, and only part of its organization may change.

## Ownership

Keep shared workspace instructions at its root, local decisions with production owners and reusable checks in the installed Evidence package. Preserve route duties and graph relationships.

## What a production may reorganize

Keep the [source layout](../development/source-layout.md#source) folders because Evidence selects them by path. Add subjects, disciplines, scenes and films within them.

Reorganize only paths no installed stage selects by name. Map old addresses to new owners and update links, citations, commands and consumers together. Follow [evidence answering](../evidence/answering.md) and [closed narrative repair](../story/correction/repair.md) where applicable.
