# Research

Study what the film must show truthfully before the screenplay decides it or code programs it: each practice, the conduct of officials and crowds, and how the genre is actually shot and heard. Settings or brief, screenplay beats, entity measurements and every production [behavior](../../motion/SKILL.md) cite the study.

## What needs a study {#what-needs-a-study}

Read the installed human motion vocabulary's README before deciding what to study. Its actions carry their own studies, so a movement it performs, everyday movement included, needs no production study: the screenplay names the action and the scenes compose it. Study what the vocabulary does not carry: a specialist practice, or the level and patterns of one, what is not a person, the conduct of officials and crowds, and how the genre is shot and heard. A study that no behavior follows has no consumer, and the evidence graph reports it.

## Performance study {#performance-study}

Study real practice at the depicted level, such as a sport, fight, dance, craft or procedure, and the broadcast or film practice of shooting it; an invented action studies its closest real analogue and states where it departs. Record what the screenplay and code need so neither invents the practice: its laws, its outcome table, the patterns of top-level performance and the measurable targets for what a viewer sees and hears. The [depicted-practice question](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice) and its statements hold the study to them.

## Sources

Use originals: rulebooks, coaching and training texts, measured studies, analyses of real top-level performances and productions, official documentation and footage of qualified practitioners; search results only locate them.

Read each passage in context and study footage as consecutive frames through its connected phases. Keep each value as a range from the depicted discipline and level with its unit, method and conditions; a value taken from another discipline or read from an adjective states its translation.

Cross-check disputed claims and record what stays unavailable.

Save permitted originals in `references/`, rights-unconfirmed ones in ignored `references/private/` and footage in ignored `media/acquired/videos/`, then open the saved files. Keep one `docs/research/index.md` naming each original's creator, URL, retrieval date, locator, rights and use, and write each study in `docs/research/<discipline>/<topic>.md`. A URL or an empty index is not a retained source.

## Use

Test the adopted values in the real entity, behavior or sound and compare the result with the footage frame by frame. The study informs the work; [delivery review](../../review/delivery.md) measures its targets on the picture and judges what it produced.
