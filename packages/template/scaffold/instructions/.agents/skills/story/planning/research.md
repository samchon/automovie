# Research

Study the practices, conduct and audiovisual conventions the film must depict. Cite each relevant study from settings or brief, beats, measurements and added laws.

## Study scope

### When to study

Before choosing a direction, study the facts that could change it. Once chosen, study the practice at the level the production depicts.

On shorts, the brief or screenplay author studies the facts their layer needs. On long projects, settings own their facts and the first treatment or script using an action studies its law and limits. A [direct writer](../workflow/routes.md#direct-writing) owns the studies their screenplay needs. With fixed text or a standalone asset, the maker studies the production choices the commission leaves open. Code and delivery review own their measurements.

### What needs a study

When depicting skilled practice, ground the choice and sequence of actions in a [performance study](#performance-study), including when `@automovie/motions` supplies every action. Reuse a study if its observations cover the depicted conditions. Read the package README for the mechanics each module supplies and the [shot grammar](../layers/shots.md) for camera vocabulary.

### Performance study

Study real practice at the depicted level, such as a sport or a craft, and the broadcast or film practice of shooting it; an invented action studies its closest real analogue.

Observe a continuous passage from the initiating cue through the response. Record starting positions, what prompts each choice, who moves or waits at the same time, what supports or obstructs the action and how long each phase takes. For skilled practice, establish action density and variation at the depicted level. Carry the relevant observations into the beat or module under [depicted practice](../../contracts/story/principles/research.md#depicted-practice).

## Sources

Use original rulebooks, teaching texts, measured studies, practitioner footage and production accounts appropriate to the depicted level. Search results only locate them.

Read passages in context and inspect consecutive frames across phases. Mark [traced measurements](../../motion/diffusion/tracing.md) as estimates. Record values, units and conditions. Verify each value against its source once, cross-check disputes and report gaps.

Retain and open study originals, studies and their index under [document layout](../document-layout.md#documents). Keep footage in ignored `media/acquired/videos/`. Index creator, URL, retrieval date, locator, rights and use.

## Use

Test the adopted values in the entity, behavior or sound. Compare corresponding phases of the performance with what the source establishes under [depicted practice](../../contracts/story/principles/research.md#depicted-practice). Use matched frames for a trace of the same action. Delivery review checks the result in the film.
