# Research

Study what the film must show truthfully: each depicted practice, the conduct of officials and crowds, and how the genre is actually shot and heard. The settings or brief, screenplay beats, entity measurements and every production [behavior](../../motion/SKILL.md) cite the study they rely on.

## When to study

Study a fact when a decision needs it, as deep as the depicted level requires, and gather no value that nothing written yet uses, because its consumer often needs a different value once it is written. Before choosing the [direction](direction.md), study only what could change it. After that, the route assigns each study:

| Where | Who studies, and what |
| --- | --- |
| Short route | The brief, the screenplay or the code, at the moment one of its decisions needs the fact |
| Long route | Settings, each fact as they fix it; the treatment or script that first names an action, its law and outcomes |
| [No narrative pass](../workflow/routes.md#who-writes-the-screenplay) | The code, the facts, laws and outcomes the narrative layers would have studied |
| Every route | Code and delivery review, the measurements they program or measure |

## What needs a study

Read the installed packages' READMEs first, because what they carry needs no production study: a movement the motion vocabulary performs, everyday movement included, which the screenplay names and the scenes compose; a rule or table a package exports; and the conventional film grammar [shots and cuts](../layers/shots.md) follows. A study records only where the production departs from them. Study how a depicted practice is filmed where it departs from that grammar, such as a dance or a sport.

Study what the packages do not carry: a specialist practice, its level and patterns, the size of a force a decided outcome turns on, such as a shove, the rate behind a group's counted outcome, such as misfires in a volley, and a body no package moves. A study that no behavior follows has no consumer, and the evidence graph reports it.

## Performance study

Study real practice at the depicted level, such as a sport, fight, dance, craft or procedure, and the broadcast or film practice of shooting it; an invented action studies its closest real analogue and states where it departs.

Start with the practice's density and distribution targets at the depicted level, because a screenplay without them writes the practice as turns of single actions, far sparser than the real thing. Rules, procedures and conventions follow as far as the writing needs them. The [depicted-practice question](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice) and its statements list what each consumer needs recorded, so none invents the practice.

## Sources

Use originals: rulebooks, coaching and training texts, measured studies, analyses of real top-level performances and productions, official documentation and footage of qualified practitioners. Search results only locate them.

Read each passage in context, and study footage as consecutive frames through its connected phases, measuring it where needed by [tracing](../../motion/diffusion/tracing.md), whose values are estimates. Keep each value as a range from the depicted discipline and level, with its unit, method and conditions; a value taken from another discipline or read from an adjective states its translation. Check each value against its source once, when it is written, cross-check disputed claims and record what stays unavailable.

Save permitted originals in `references/`, rights-unconfirmed ones in ignored `references/private/` and footage in ignored `media/acquired/videos/`, then open the saved files, because a URL or an empty index is not a retained source. Keep one `docs/research/index.md` naming each original's creator, URL, retrieval date, locator, rights and use, and write each study in `docs/research/<discipline>/<topic>.md`.

## Use

Test the adopted values in the real entity, behavior or sound, and compare the result with the footage frame by frame. The study informs the work; [delivery review](../../review/delivery-review.md) measures its targets on the picture and judges what it produced.
