# Discovery

Before bulk writing, assign each consequential instruction, reference, finding and delegated choice to an owner under [requirement ownership](../../contracts/story/discovery/common.md#requirement-ownership). Define what the chosen form presents, withholds and makes visible to its recipient.

Put facts in settings or within the [brief's boundary](../layers/brief.md#what-it-holds). Assign events and release to their layers. Adopt production-specific judgments and their populations through [adoption](../../evidence/adoption.md).

## Checkpoints

On the long route, repeat discovery at each applicable checkpoint. Inspect available material within its scope.

| Checkpoint, in order | What it looks for |
| --- | --- |
| After settings | What the canon needs that direction did not foresee |
| Before composition, when commissioned | What no source folder, research or contract covers yet |
| After output review | What the output or handoff shows the work still needs |

Before composition, look for unowned element kinds, unsupported behavior, camera or staging choices, and failures no contract covers.

Update the discovery result at each applicable checkpoint. Leave an applicable checkpoint open if it has not run. Mark checkpoints outside the commission as inapplicable.

## Result

Record actual findings under [document layout](../document-layout.md#documents). For a negative, name the inspected scope, tested risks and existing owners.
