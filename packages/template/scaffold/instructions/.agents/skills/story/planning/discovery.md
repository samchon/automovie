# Discovery

Before bulk writing, assign each consequential instruction, reference, finding and delegated choice to an owner under the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md). Define what the chosen form presents, withholds and makes visible to its recipient.

Put facts in settings or within the [brief's boundary](../layers/brief.md#what-it-holds). Assign events and release to their layers. Adopt production-specific judgments and their populations through [adoption](../../evidence/adoption.md).

## Checkpoints

Repeat discovery at each checkpoint the route requires.

| Checkpoint, in order | What it looks for |
| --- | --- |
| After settings | What the canon needs that direction did not foresee |
| Before composition | What no source folder, research or contract covers yet |
| After delivery review | What the acceptance record shows the work needed and lacked |

Before composition, that is an element kind no source folder owns, a behavior, camera or staging choice no research supports, and a failure no contract asks about.

Update the discovery result at each one, and leave a checkpoint that was not run recorded as open.

## Result

Record actual findings under [document layout](../document-layout.md#documents). For a negative, name the inspected scope, tested risks and existing owners.
