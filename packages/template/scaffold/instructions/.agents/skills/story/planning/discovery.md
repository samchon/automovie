# Discovery

Before bulk writing, break the adopted instructions, references, research and delegated choices into consequential elements and assign each to its existing owner or a missing one, under the discovery questions in the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md). A genre label or a general reading of the brief does not establish coverage. Discover the chosen form too: what each unit presents or withholds, how it guides its recipient, and the visible difference a named style makes.
Facts and constraints belong in settings or a short's brief, events and release in their layer, and a production-specific judgment in the production's own work contracts, adopted with its population through [adoption](../../evidence/adoption.md). A requirement that lives only in status notes, selector names or repeated guidance needs a canonical owner.

## Checkpoints {#checkpoints}

Discovery runs again at four points, because each one exposes kinds of requirement the one before could not see:

1. after research, for what the studied practice needs that no instruction named;
2. after settings or the brief, for what the canon needs that the research did not foresee;
3. before composition, for an element kind no source folder owns, a behavior, camera or staging choice no research supports, and a failure no contract asks about;
4. after delivery review, for what the acceptance record shows the work needed and lacked.

Update the discovery result at each one, and leave a checkpoint that was not run recorded as open.

## Result

Write the discovery result where the [document layout](../document-layout.md) puts it, from actual findings and inspected negatives. If no independent requirement remains, record the inspected scope, the risks examined and the owners that cover them. A finding in one area does not establish absence elsewhere, and a blank production never receives a fabricated negative.
