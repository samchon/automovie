# Scripts

Realize completed treatments as scenes performers and filmmakers can execute, under the [script principles](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/scripts.md) and [population duties](../../contracts/story/shared/obligations/scripts.md).

After [discovery](../planning/discovery.md), map treatments to their scenes and settle the scene, sequence and release units by actual viewing material, keeping stable episode files and scene anchors, because the screenplay [realizes each script scene in its own file](screenplay.md#scenes).

Build each scene around what its participants do, encounter and change: actual dialogue, action, reaction and performance in playable order, with usable space, light, sound and view, in readable [notation](notation.md). Study what [when to study](../planning/research.md#when-to-study) assigns to a script.

Keep the inherited event chronology while creating the scene's own execution under [parent differentiation](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation), cite every realized treatment, and repair a defective parent under [upstream repair](../correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).

Before evidence, read the whole script as reader, performer and filmmaker, then inspect and repair under [cause rounds](../correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), comparing neighboring units for the [distribution](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/obligations/scripts.md#execution-distribution) of execution. Scripts complete content and expression together in [construction](../workflow/long.md#each-layers-pass).
