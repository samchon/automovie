# Layers

Each layer makes its own contribution and hands usable decisions to its next recipient, in the order its route runs.

| Layer | Hands onward |
| --- | --- |
| [Settings](settings.md), long route | Specific decisions with stable addresses and one owner |
| [Brief](brief.md), short route | Decisions with stable addresses the screenplay cites |
| [Treatments](treatments.md) | A progression that needs no invented decisive event |
| [Scripts](scripts.md) | Playable scenes for the final screenplay |
| [Screenplay](screenplay.md) | Complete production text the scenes realize |

Settings and the brief own what the later layers keep true, each on its own route. Settings own the facts of every [domain](settings.md#records), with baseline states, relationships and knowledge boundaries. The brief owns only the decisions [it holds](brief.md#what-it-holds).

Treatments, scripts and the screenplay own the story in turn. Treatments own events, causes, choices, consequences, setup and payoff, and information release. Scripts own the partition and fully executed scenes: speech, action, reactions, performance, stage, light, sound and view. The screenplay owns the audience's image, sound, attention, duration, juxtaposition and transitions, scene by scene.

[Notation](notation.md) gives the blocks and headings scripts and the screenplay use, and the screenplay's records. [Shots and cuts](shots.md) says how both choose the view and the edit.
