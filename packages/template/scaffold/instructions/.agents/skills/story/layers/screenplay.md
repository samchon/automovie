# Screenplay

Write what the audience sees, hears and learns in viewing order. Shape pressure, density and motifs across sections. Use the [screenplay criteria](../../contracts/story/principles/screenplay.md), [blocks](notation.md) and [shots and cuts](shots.md). Compare [recurring forms, voices and rhythm](../../contracts/story/obligations/screenplay.md#recurrent-frames).

## Scenes

Use one file per continuous place and time. With long-route narrative passes, map each script scene to one file in its episode folder. Follow [document layout](../document-layout.md#documents) and [heading identities](notation.md#identities).

## Realization

On the long route with narrative passes:

1. Extract the required identities, choices, states, chronology and space.
   - Include audience knowledge. Use the treatments and scripts as sources.
2. Draft from the notes, requirements and fixed quotes in a [separate context](../workflow/routes.md#separate-contexts).
3. Write dialogue from each exchange's intent, information and outcome.
   - Preserve event order and [compose afresh](../../contracts/story/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation).
4. Check the draft against the actual parents.

On the short route with narrative passes, take instructions as the basis for events, consequences, setups, payoffs and ending. Decide when the audience and characters learn dangers and secrets. Cite the [brief](brief.md) and complete assigned [research](../planning/research.md#when-to-study).

For direct writing, base creative decisions on adopted instructions and assigned research.

Stage for the representation in [project context](../../production/project-context.md). A faceless figure can turn toward someone, hesitate or step away. Use the installed distance bands when specifying spacing. Carry details beyond the coarse figure through a [direct film's detail option](../../video/direct-film.md) or a generated film's canon reference and prompt.

## Beats

Read every beat in viewing order. For skilled practice, choose and time exchanges from the [performance study](../planning/research.md#performance-study). Check cues, reactions, timing and outcomes. Repair unsupported choices.

Write one beat per exchange, ending when intent or initiative changes. Give speakers an aim and let reactions follow the cue the participant can see or hear. Record consequential departures from default listening or breathing in the [beat record](notation.md#records).

Replay changed beats and joins firsthand when staging exists. Send unstageable story decisions to their [earliest owner](../correction/repair.md#upstream-defects) and errors in staging to the module owner. Finish required inspections and repairs before enabling Evidence.
