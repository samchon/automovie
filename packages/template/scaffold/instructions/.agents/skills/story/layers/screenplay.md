# Screenplay

Compose the audience's complete viewing and listening experience, understandable without its parents, under the screenplay questions in the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md). Choose what is seen and heard, how attention moves, and when the audience recognizes, waits and connects, through speech, action, reactions, sound, detail, duration and transitions in [readable notation](notation.md). A fixed view, silence, caption or narration serves the form when its contribution earns the attention.

## Scenes

Each film's screenplay is a folder, and each scene is one file in it, because a long film cannot fit one file and a scene is the unit code composes. A scene is one continuous place and time: a cut to another place or time starts the next scene, and no stretch of continuous place and time is split across files. On the long route each script scene becomes one scene file of its episode's folder. The [document layout](../document-layout.md#documents) gives the folders and [notation](notation.md#identities) the headings.

## Realization

On the long route, extract concise notes of the required identities, decisions, states, chronology, space and audience knowledge from the completed treatments and scripts, draft in an [isolated context](../correction/inspection.md#isolated-context) with those notes, the requirements and fixed quotations, then compare against the actual parents. The decided event order holds while the realization is composed afresh under [parent differentiation](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation).

On the short route, decide the events, action and realization here from the instructions, citing the [brief](brief.md) for the people, places and recurring props it holds, and study what [when to study](../planning/research.md#when-to-study) assigns to the screenplay.

On either route, write only what the adopted representation and frame can show, as [project context](../../production/project-context.md) records them; for a commissioned generated film, the reactions also state the facial expressions they need, which the generation prompt carries. A polygon man has no face, so its attention is the turn of its head and torso, and a fact it cannot carry reaches a generated film through canon references and the generation prompt.

## Beats

Before writing beats, plan the whole film's performance: how densely each section's action comes against its study's density and distribution targets, where the practice has one, where pressure rises, and where a recurring motif returns and what varies around it. Beats written first and counted afterward come out as sparse turns of one shape. Keep the plan as working notes in ignored `.cache/`, never as a section or file of the screenplay, because every screenplay heading owes realization, answers and review.

A beat is one exchange, ending when an intent or the initiative changes. Its action lines name every action it chains, in order, and its record decides the exchange once, because a record per single action buries the performance under bookkeeping and splits a combination into turns.

The screenplay is the last place a decision can be made: motion, sound and assembly realize it, and a film [only composes](../../contracts/development/composition.md#composition-only). So write every beat in terms the modules can perform, from the decisions about the people, objects and places in it, with each action named as one the installed motion vocabulary performs, alone or joined with others, or as the research law that describes it, under [what needs a study](../planning/research.md#what-needs-a-study).

Decide the beat completely in its [beat record](notation.md#records), as the [beat-decisions question](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/screenplay.md#beat-decisions) asks. A beat that no composition of installed actions, cited law or outcome table can produce is repaired here, or at the parent that owns the decision under [upstream repair](../correction/repair.md#upstream-defects), before code is written from it.

Before evidence, read the whole progression in viewing order, then inspect and repair as the [route](../workflow/SKILL.md) sets.
