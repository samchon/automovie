# Brief

A short's canon: the few decisions its screenplay cites, under the [brief criteria](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/brief.md). Write it after discovery, studying what [when to study](../planning/research.md#when-to-study) assigns it.

## What it holds

Use the [needed-decisions question](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions) for shared people, places and props. Decide scene-only subjects, camera and sound in the screenplay.

## Records

Record each cast member, set or recurring prop under [canon records](../document-layout.md#canon-records).

## Growth

The brief grows as the screenplay is written. After the pass closes, a decision of a kind the brief holds that one scene makes and another must keep is added under [concretization](../correction/repair.md#concretization).
