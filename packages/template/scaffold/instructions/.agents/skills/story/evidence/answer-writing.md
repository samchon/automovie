# Answer writing

The evidence answerer writes the answers: a context that did not write the body, so its answers state what the body holds rather than what its author meant. It changes no body. A question it cannot answer truthfully goes to the author as a gap, with the passage and the statement it fails, and is answered after the author fills it.

Read the whole selected respondent and the parents it needs, and find its questions through the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md).

Answer an overview question first, because the local answers follow its direction. An answer states the current work; it cannot argue missing material into existence or overturn a locked recipient loss.

Support each assertion with exact quotations and owner addresses checked against the current body or parent, and check claimed counts, sequence, causes, places and states across the whole scope.

Where an item has statements, follow [items and statements](../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) before answering.

Answer only what the question asks; an unneeded inventory becomes a stale fact. Group a repeated cause and cite its grounds once.

An inapplicable question states its inspected scope and reason through the mechanism its relationship permits, and a population exclusion requires the whole population to be inapplicable. [Answer placement](answer-placement.md) says where each answer goes.

After the author edits within the open pass while its stage is enabled, the answerer reconciles the dependent answers; changes touching closed work follow [upstream repair](../correction/repair.md).

Pass the body and answers to the next step of the [route](../workflow/SKILL.md). Independent [review](../../review/narrative-rounds.md) judges only the body, and a refreshed hash grants no creative judgment.

## Grounds for retaining a candidate

A named expression failure follows its contract's [decision and exception rule](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written). For another candidate, write an alternative carrying the same information, access and pressure without the form and compare the two, recording the gain or loss in ignored notes and the decision in the accountable record.

Deletion, a function label, precedent or earlier protection cannot establish what the expression achieves. After an independent finding, the comparison guides repair rather than acquittal; a working function whose form fails is recomposed, and a defective inherited value returns to its earliest owner under [upstream repair](../correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).
