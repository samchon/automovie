# Repair and changed scope

Identify the earliest owner, the consumers that use it, the current pass and the closed boundaries before changing the work. For a requested revision, name the change, what the user wants preserved and the smallest useful scope, then trace its impact through dependent facts, scenes, assets, performance and edit.

## Upstream defects

A construction author who finds a missing, conflicting or unrealizable parent decision repairs its earliest owner before writing from it, using the selected [upstream questions](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md); a downstream workaround leaves every other consumer with the defect. During expression work, report it instead of changing frozen content.

## Cause rounds

Finish the whole inspection round before editing, under [inspection](inspection.md). Group the findings by cause, including every member of a recurring class, and repair each cause where its action or explanation belongs, recomposing a passage when cutting would lose its meaning.

Reconcile dependent statements, quotations, values and [answers](../evidence/writing.md) in the same repair, then read the changed passages and the whole progression for new uniformity, fidelity losses and broken seams before the next round.

## Closed passes

Completed layers stay closed when their bodies or parents change: do not reopen their evidence, reviews or approvals. Remove answers for deleted H2s, write answers for new ones only, and report other diagnostics without repairing them, keeping the original failed reports.

After expression transfer, hand off the repaired body with its working edition, frozen original and unfiltered report; a pending or stale transfer check remains a failure.

Replacing a unit from scratch removes its obsolete answers, editions, reviews and replaced descendants while keeping other material; a reused filename grants no approval, and the new unit begins its own cycle.

## Concretization {#concretization}

When scene composition must make a decision the screenplay left vague, the decision belongs to the screenplay, not to the code. Add it to the beat's record in the screenplay body in the same change, and when another scene or film must keep it, add it to settings or the brief as a new anchored fact citing the beat.

A closed screenplay stays closed: the edited beat keeps its answers and reviews, its stale fingerprint is reported as a remaining diagnostic under [closed passes](#closed-passes), and the next review of that screenplay reads the beat afresh.

An exchange, an outcome or a reaction is never a concretization. A screenplay that lacks one is defective and returns to its author under [upstream defects](#upstream-defects).

## Scope expansion

A first-part pilot needs the user's commission and its exact part. Settings stay work-wide; the other layers cover the slice with its lineage, and the result states what the slice cannot establish.

To expand, reconfigure through [evidence configuration](../../evidence/configuration.md), reset the affected passes together, design the complete treatment sequence including the pilot, and establish whole-population evidence and review; pilot approval cannot stand for the whole work.
