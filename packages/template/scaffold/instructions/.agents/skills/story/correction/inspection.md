# Inspection

Use this method on the long route for construction inspection, Evidence gap repairs and independent review. Take failure conditions and exceptions from the selected contracts.

## Independent classification

Give the classifier, a [separate context](../workflow/routes.md#separate-contexts), the current body and needed neighbors, the selected contracts and the actual parents, and nothing of the author's explanations, evidence or earlier verdicts.

Check script scenes and draft exchanges for story gaps and continuity. For the screenplay, classify each sentence against the [named expression failures](../../contracts/story/principles/screenplay.md#reads-as-written) and any selected language or work criteria. Decide borderline cases by their effect on the recipient.

Pair each paragraph with its parent under [parent differentiation](../../contracts/story/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation). Compare inherited names, address, quantities, dates, places, states and certainty. Keep source checks with the [parent](../planning/research.md#sources).

Record revision, coverage and tested failures once. For sentence classification, give each sentence a locator and failure names or `-`. Note other recipient losses. Keep the table in ignored `.cache/` and supported findings in their record.

Lock the findings before reading the author's response. The author repairs or objects on factual grounds. The classifier decides objections by recipient loss. Record all members of recurring causes and send uncovered classes to discovery.

## Changed work

The body's author makes every repair until the cycle closes. Recheck the changed passage, its neighbors and statements that depend on it. Where sentence classification applies, update the affected rows. Changed parents or requirements reopen their comparisons.

Change an earlier classification only on new body, parent, source or contract evidence. Flag clear applicable failures in the repair and reread changed passages under [cause rounds](repair.md#cause-rounds).

Finish classification when coverage is complete and no applicable failure remains in the rechecked work. Independent review closes the pass.
