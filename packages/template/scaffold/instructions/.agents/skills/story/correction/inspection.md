# Inspection

Author inspection, evidence correction and independent review share this method. The selected contracts own the failure conditions and exceptions; the table records their application and the other losses it meets.

## Isolated context

Launch the author's model through an available subagent or separate-session tool, within its authorization, with no inherited conversation or working memory, passing only the job and input file paths. Calling the current context fresh does not isolate it; if no tool or authorization is available, report the task as unperformed.

## Independent classification

Give the classifier the current body and needed neighbors, the selected contracts and the actual parents or frozen original, and nothing of the author's explanations, evidence or earlier verdicts.

For scripts and screenplay, classify every sentence against every applicable named failure. For narrative construction, also pair each paragraph with its parent passages, or the brief for a short, to test [parent differentiation](../../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation), and compare inherited names, address, quantities, dates, places, states and certainty with the parents.

Record the revision and covered ranges, list the tested failures once, and give one row per sentence with its locator and the names of the failures found, or `-`. Note beside a row, or for the unit, any other loss the recipient would suffer and what is lost, because review counts such a loss only when the reviewer and the classification both record it.

An ambiguous candidate is decided in context by the recipient loss; ambiguity alone is not yes. Keep the raw table in ignored `.cache/` and its supported findings in the accountable record.

Lock the whole initial table before author defenses. The author repairs or raises a factual objection, as [narrative rounds](../../review/narrative-rounds.md#findings-and-objections) defines one. In the author's own inspection the classifier resolves it without substituting intent for the recipient's judgment; in review, narrative rounds set who does. Record every member of each recurring cause, and send uncovered recurring losses to discovery.

## Verify changed work

Keep the same author through the correction cycle.

The author's first table and the reviewer's first table cover the whole body. Each one's later tables classify only each sentence changed since its own last table, with their neighbors and every statement whose meaning depends on them, and carry the other rows; changed parents or requirements reopen their comparisons. Classifying the whole body again after each repair judges settled sentences anew, and every new reading becomes another repair that widens the change.

Within the author's tables, or the reviewer's, do not reverse an unchanged classification for a preferred reading, only for new body, parent, source or contract evidence, an objection the reviewer upheld, or membership in a finding's class. A repaired passage receives another yes only for a clear applicable failure.

The cycle is complete when coverage is complete and no unprotected yes remains after the affected rows are rechecked.

Read the changed passages together for a new common pattern or fidelity loss under [cause rounds](repair.md#cause-rounds). A clean table is neither independent approval nor whole-work completion; independent review closes the pass under [narrative rounds](../../review/narrative-rounds.md).
