# Story routes

## Recorded choices

### Choosing the route

Use the long route for causes, setups and payoffs that span many scenes or releases. Use the short route for films of a few minutes whose events fit in one screenplay, including shorts that share a world.

Record the choice in [project context](../../production/project-context.md). In an AutoMovie workspace the production's folder fixes it: `movies/shorts/<name>` runs the short route and `movies/series/<name>` the long route.

Asset-only work has no narrative: choose its form and scale through [direction](../planning/direction.md), and make the complete work unless the user authorizes a slice.

### Who writes the screenplay

At intake, use the user's explicit choice or ask which approach they want. Record their answer in `PROJECT.md`. Follow the chosen route in order. Changing or skipping a required stage needs the user's explicit direction. Creative delegation covers only the choices delegated.

| Choice | Narrative passes | Screenplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This workflow, recommended | The route's | The writer develops the screenplay through them |
| Without this workflow | None | The writer [writes it directly](#direct-writing) |
| The user's, refined | The route's | Material, through [intake](../document-layout.md#intake-of-existing-work) |
| The user's, fixed | None | [Used exactly as given](#fixed-screenplay) |

### Direct writing

Settle [undelegated creative choices](../planning/requirements.md#asking-the-user) and record the [instructions](../planning/requirements.md#requirements). Write the screenplay directly from them.

### Fixed screenplay

[Take in the supplied revision](../document-layout.md#intake-of-existing-work) verbatim. Ask the user to supply a missing exchange, outcome or reaction, or rewrite an unperformable beat. Put [implementation-only decisions](../correction/repair.md#concretization) in the instructions.

## One chain

Trace commissioned video from settings or brief through screenplay, entities, needed motion laws, [verified capabilities](../../motion/catalog-cases.md), scene compositions and film. With no narrative pass, use instructions in place of settings or brief.

Cite the preceding decisions each stage realizes.

## Separate contexts

Use separate contexts for the author, Evidence answerer and independent reviewer.

Start an authorized fresh context with the author's model. Pass the job and input paths without inherited conversation or memory. Report the task as unperformed if isolation is unavailable.

## Completion

For each narrative pass, read the unfiltered Evidence report while its required stages are active. Check parent coverage and the reviewer's unit records and verdict against the manuscript revision. Finish the route's repairs and hand off the revision reviewed, subsequent changes and any open diagnostics under [closed passes](../correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
