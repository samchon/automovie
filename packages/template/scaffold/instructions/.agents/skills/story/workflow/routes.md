# Routes and completion

Which route a work takes, who writes its screenplay, how its layers chain and when it is complete.

## Recorded choices

### Choosing the route

Choose the long route when the work's causes, setups and payoffs span many scenes or releases, such as a feature or a series, because treatments must design those events and scripts partition them before the screenplay. Choose the short route when each film runs a few minutes at most and its events fit one screenplay, alone or as several shorts sharing a world.

Record the choice in [project context](../../production/project-context.md). In an AutoMovie workspace the production's folder fixes it: `movies/shorts/<name>` runs the short route and `movies/series/<name>` the long route.

Asset-only work has no narrative: choose its form and scale through [direction](../planning/direction.md), and make the complete work unless the user authorizes a slice.

### Who writes the screenplay

The user chooses, whatever the route, and the choice is recorded with the route; ask when they supply a screenplay without saying which.

| Choice | Narrative passes | Screenplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This workflow, the default | The route's | The agent writes it through them |
| Without this workflow | None | The agent writes it directly from the instructions |
| The user's, refined | The route's | Material, through [intake](../document-layout.md#intake-of-existing-work) |
| The user's, fixed | None | Used exactly as given |

A fixed screenplay is never edited, reviewed or rewritten. An exchange, outcome or reaction it lacks, or a beat that cannot be performed, goes back to the user to write into it, because only the screenplay decides them.

## One chain

Commissioned video follows one traceable chain: settings or a short's brief, the screenplay, the entities and the laws the installed motion vocabulary lacks, with their tests and accepted [catalog cases](../../motion/catalog-cases.md), the scene composition, then the film. When [no narrative pass runs](#who-writes-the-screenplay), the instructions stand in for settings or the brief.

Each link cites the addresses it realizes from the links above it, so no code exists without a cited basis and no requirement stays unrealized.

## Separate contexts

Keep the author, the evidence answerer and the independent reviewer in separate contexts, because an author answering for its own body states what it meant, not what the body holds, and a reviewer reading answers judges paperwork instead of the work.

## Completion

Whole-work completion needs the route's passes with their closed review, passing unfiltered validation, the compiler and source checks, the required tests, and delivery review of any commissioned media. Run the README's evidence command and read its whole report, because a passing graph shows only the recorded relationships, and review judges the creative result.

A creative defect, a missing parent, an unfinished review or unverified media blocks completion, and until review finishes the result is a draft. A manuscript with its closed review may be delivered with its revision and its named remaining diagnostics, but that is not fully validated completion. Changes to completed layers follow [closed passes](../correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
