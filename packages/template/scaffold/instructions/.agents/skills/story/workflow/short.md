# Short route

A short is drafted all the way to its film and checked once at the end. The brief and the screenplay are written together, the film is drafted from them, and only then are the contracts answered and one independent review run, because answers and reviews written earlier would describe text the draft still changes.

A short has no settings, treatments, scripts or outline. Wherever a skill or contract cites settings or brief facts, a short cites its brief, or its screenplay for what the [brief leaves to it](../layers/brief.md#what-it-holds).

## Steps {#steps}

1. Questions.

    Ask the user under [asking the user](../planning/requirements.md#asking-the-user), and run [discovery](../planning/discovery.md) once on the answers; a short runs none of its later checkpoints.

2. Brief.

    Write the [brief](../layers/brief.md).

3. Screenplay.

    Write the whole [screenplay](../layers/screenplay.md) in detail, studying a fact when a decision needs it, under [when to study](../planning/research.md#when-to-study). When writing exposes a missing or wrong brief decision, repair the brief at once; both are still drafts.

4. Film draft.

    For a commission with a film, build the entities, the scene composition and the film from the screenplay. Judge the film on stills under [visual debugging](../../visual-debugging/SKILL.md) and ask the user to watch playback for continuous motion and sound. Repair each cause where it lies, in the screenplay, the brief, the code or an [extension](../../motion/extend.md) of the motion vocabulary, until the film reads as intended. A screenplay-only commission skips this step.

5. Evidence.

    Read the brief and screenplay once, whole, against the contracts the evidence stages will select, and repair what fails by cause, in the code and film too. No independent classification runs, because the review that follows reads afresh. Then enable the brief and screenplay construction stages, and the development stages the code needs, through [evidence configuration](../../evidence/configuration.md). The [evidence answerer](../evidence/answer-writing.md) writes the answers, and the author fills the gaps it returns.

6. Review.

    One independent reviewer reads the brief and screenplay under [narrative rounds](../../review/narrative-rounds.md). Once the reviewer's findings reach the author, the brief and screenplay stages go back to disabled, so the repairs that follow owe no story answers and later edits reopen none. The author decides each finding and repairs the accepted ones, in the code and film too, keeping the code's development answers current, and that closes the pass. The film's [delivery review](../../review/delivery-review.md) belongs to [completion](SKILL.md#completion).
