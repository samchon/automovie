# Short route

A short writes a minimal brief and the whole screenplay, drafts the film from them, then answers the contracts and takes one independent review. Every narrative stage stays disabled until the evidence step, because answers written before the film draft would describe text the draft still changes.

A short has no settings, treatment, script or outline files. Wherever a skill or contract cites settings or brief facts, a short cites its brief, or its screenplay for what the [brief leaves to it](../layers/brief.md#what-it-holds).

## Steps {#steps}

1. Questions.

    Ask the user under [asking the user](../planning/requirements.md#asking-the-user), and run [discovery](../planning/discovery.md) on the answers.

2. Minimal brief.

    Write the [brief](../layers/brief.md).

3. Detailed screenplay draft.

    Write the whole [screenplay](../layers/screenplay.md) in detail. Whenever writing exposes a missing or wrong brief decision, repair the brief at once; both are open drafts, so no closed-pass procedure applies.

4. Code and film draft.

    For a commission with a film, build the entities, the scene composition and the film from the screenplay draft. Judge the film on stills under [visual debugging](../../visual-debugging/SKILL.md) and ask the user to watch playback for continuous motion and sound. Repair each cause where it lies, in the screenplay, the brief, the code or an [extension](../../motion/extend.md) of the motion vocabulary, until the film reads as intended. A screenplay-only commission skips this step.

5. Evidence once.

    Read the brief and screenplay once under [short-route inspection](../correction/inspection.md#short-route) and repair by cause, in the code and film too when one exists. Then enable the brief and screenplay construction stages, and the development stages the code needs, through [evidence configuration](../../evidence/configuration.md). The [evidence answerer](../evidence/answer-writing.md) writes the answers and hands the author the gaps it cannot answer truthfully; the author fills them.

6. One independent review.

    One round of [narrative rounds](../../review/narrative-rounds.md) reads the brief and screenplay; the film's [delivery review](../../review/delivery-review.md) belongs to [completion](SKILL.md#completion). The brief and screenplay construction stages then go back to disabled, so the repairs that follow owe no story answers and later edits reopen none. The author decides each finding and repairs the accepted ones, in the code and film too when one exists, keeping their development answers current, which closes the pass.
