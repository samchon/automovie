# Long route

Long work runs settings → treatments → scripts → screenplay, one layer at a time, each a pass that closes before the next layer builds on it. Its settings exist because every later layer realizes and cites their decisions and cannot cite what was never written. Scripts settle their own expression in construction.

## Parents {#parents}

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, discovered requirements and the [research](../planning/research.md) the facts draw on |
| Treatments | Completed settings |
| Scripts | Completed treatments |
| Screenplay | Completed treatments and scripts |

Ask the user under [asking the user](../planning/requirements.md#asking-the-user) first. Inherit through actual parents, repairing a missing handoff rather than bypassing it, and run [discovery](../planning/discovery.md) before bulk settings and again at each of its [checkpoints](../planning/discovery.md#checkpoints).

## Each layer's pass {#each-layers-pass}

1. The author finishes the whole body while its stage is disabled, and completes [inspection and cause repair](../correction/repair.md#cause-rounds).
2. The [evidence answerer](../evidence/answer-writing.md) enables the stage through [evidence configuration](../../evidence/configuration.md), writes the answers and hands the author the gaps it cannot answer truthfully; the author fills them.
3. The body, boundaries and population go to independent [narrative rounds](../../review/narrative-rounds.md), which judge only the body.
4. The author [decides](../../review/narrative-rounds.md#findings-and-decisions) each finding and repairs the accepted ones at their owners.

A pass has at most two review rounds, because reviewers keep finding matters of their own style and a review that waited for no findings would never close. A round in which the author accepts no finding closes the pass; otherwise a second round reads the repaired body, and the pass closes once its accepted findings are repaired. The evidence answerer then brings the answers up to date with the final body.
