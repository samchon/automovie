# Expressive rewriting

Recompose the final screenplay's audiovisual expression while keeping its completed construction, under the naturalness questions in the [story contracts](../../contracts/story/SKILL.md).

Read the annotation-free frozen edition as its performer or audience first, recording both its losses and what already works.

Compose from the scene's people, purpose, pressure and rhythm, choosing the audience's access, distance and emphasis through image, sound, speech, performance, juxtaposition, silence and transitions, and read each exchange in order with its action.

Compare meaning in both directions with the frozen construction under [expression fidelity](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#expression-fidelity), and report a construction defect to its owner instead of changing it.

Reread the whole progression against the first experience and the [audiovisual voice](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice), then correct details under [inspection](../correction/inspection.md) and [cause rounds](../correction/repair.md#cause-rounds); a clean sentence table does not establish the continuous effect.

## Revision cycles

One construction cycle receives one comprehensive rewrite, completed under [procedure](transfer.md). Changed-sentence counts and distance from the original do not measure improvement.

A further requested revision starts from the previous working edition and its reviews, compares meaning with the initial frozen construction and expression with the previous edition, and keeps that frozen baseline throughout; a unit replaced from scratch starts new construction.
