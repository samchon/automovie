# Story

Make the commissioned audience experience and a usable handoff to the next layer, keeping what the user fixed and deciding what was delegated. Find the task, route, layer and pass from the request, [project context](../production/project-context.md) and the current work, then open only the page it needs. The production workflow connects the screenplay to the film.

| Read | For |
| --- | --- |
| [Workflow](workflow/SKILL.md) | Choosing the route and screenplay writer, running a route, completing the work |
| [Planning](planning/SKILL.md) | Settling requirements, direction and uncovered requirements, studying a practice |
| [Layers](layers/SKILL.md) | Writing settings or a brief, treatments, scripts or the screenplay, with shots and cuts |
| [Inspection](correction/inspection.md) | Classifying a body's sentences and verifying repairs |
| [Repair](correction/repair.md) | Repairing by cause and in closed work, concretizing a vague beat, expanding a pilot |
| [Answers](answers.md) | Writing and placing the answers to the story contracts |
| [Document layout](document-layout.md) | Finding where a document, answer or review lives, or taking in supplied work |

The [story contracts](../contracts/story/SKILL.md) hold the quality questions, and independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) judges the result. A creative body follows the production's adopted language, voice and form under those contracts, not the documentation rules, so fixed quotations, purposeful repetition, nonstandard speech and punctuation stay as written.
