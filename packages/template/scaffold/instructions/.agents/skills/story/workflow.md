# Writing workflow

Choose the commissioned route from the request, project context, adopted instructions and current bodies.

## Creative ownership

| Layer | Owns | Hands onward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Settings](layers/settings.md) | Facts about the world, cast, crowds, props, sets, cinematography and sound, with baseline states, relationships and knowledge boundaries | Specific decisions with stable addresses and one owner |
| [Brief](layers/brief.md), short route | A short's fixed conditions and the world, people, places, objects, tone and look its films share | Decisions with stable addresses the screenplays cite |
| [Treatments](layers/treatments.md) | Events, causes, choices, consequences, setup and payoff, and information release | A progression that needs no invented decisive event |
| [Scripts](layers/scripts.md) | Partition and fully executed scenes: speech, action, reactions, performance, stage, light, sound and view | Playable scenes for the final screenplay |
| [Final screenplay](layers/screenplay.md) | The audience's image, sound, attention, duration, juxtaposition and transitions | Complete production text the scenes realize |

## Route and parents

Commissioned video follows one traceable chain: research, settings or a short's brief, screenplay, entities and the laws the installed motion vocabulary lacks, with their tests and accepted [catalog cases](../motion/SKILL.md#catalog-cases), scene composition, then the film. Each link cites the addresses it realizes from the links above it, so no code exists without a cited basis and no requirement stays unrealized; a later link can only realize what an earlier one decided, and motion written before the screenplay settles its action is fitted to guesses.

Long work runs settings → treatments → scripts → screenplay construction, then screenplay naturalness; only the final screenplay has a separate expression pass, which only an explicit user waiver omits. Its settings exist because every later layer realizes and cites their decisions and cannot cite what was never written.

A short runs brief → screenplay construction, then screenplay naturalness. Its [brief](layers/brief.md) is canon like settings, with its own folders, contracts, evidence and review, and holds the fixed conditions and the decisions the films share; the screenplay decides everything else and repairs the brief upstream when writing exposes a gap. There are no settings, treatment, script or outline files. Wherever a skill or contract cites settings facts, a short cites its brief.

Choose the long route when the work's causes, setups and payoffs span many scenes or releases, such as a feature, an episode or a series, because they need treatments to design events and scripts to partition them before the screenplay; choose the short route when each film is a few minutes at most and its events fit one screenplay, alone or as several shorts sharing a world. Record the choice in [project context](../production/profile.md); in an AutoMovie workspace the production's folder fixes it: `movies/shorts/<name>` runs the short route and `movies/series/<name>` the long route. Asset-only work has no narrative passes. Choose form and scale through [direction](planning/direction.md); complete work is the default.

## Construction

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, discovered requirements and the [research](planning/research.md) the facts draw on |
| Brief | Adopted instructions, discovered requirements and the research its decisions draw on |
| Treatments | Completed settings |
| Scripts | Completed treatments |
| Screenplay, long work | Completed treatments and scripts, including scripts expression |
| Screenplay, short | Completed brief and the research it cites |

Inherit through actual parents, repairing a missing handoff rather than bypassing it, and run [discovery](planning/discovery.md) before bulk settings or brief and again at each of its [checkpoints](planning/discovery.md#checkpoints).

1. Finish the whole body while its pass is disabled.
2. Complete [inspection and cause repair](correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), enable the stage through [evidence configuration](../evidence/configuration.md), and write [answers](evidence/writing.md).
3. Submit the body, boundaries and population to independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) through creative closure and evidence audit under [review rounds](../review/rounds.md).
4. Repair findings at their owners until the pass closes under [review rounds](../review/rounds.md).

## Completion

Run the README's evidence command and read its whole report; a passing graph shows the recorded relationships, and review judges the creative result. Whole-work completion needs the selected passes, current closed review, passing unfiltered validation, the compiler and source checks and required tests, and [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) of any commissioned media.

A manuscript with its closed review may be delivered with its revision and named remaining diagnostics, but that is not fully validated completion. A creative defect, missing parent, pending transfer, unfinished review or unverified media blocks its completion, and until review finishes the result is a draft. Changes to completed layers follow [closed passes](correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
