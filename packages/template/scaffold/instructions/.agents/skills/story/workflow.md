# Writing workflow

Choose the commissioned route from the request, project context, adopted instructions and current bodies.

## Creative ownership

| Layer | Owns | Hands onward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Settings](layers/settings.md) | Facts about the world, cast, crowds, props, sets, cinematography and sound, with baseline states, relationships and knowledge boundaries | Specific, checkable facts with stable addresses, sources and one owner |
| [Treatments](layers/treatments.md) | Events, causes, choices, consequences, setup and payoff, and information release | A progression that needs no invented decisive event |
| [Scripts](layers/scripts.md) | Partition and fully executed scenes: speech, action, reactions, performance, stage, light, sound and view | Playable scenes for the final screenplay |
| [Final screenplay](layers/screenplay.md) | The audience's image, sound, attention, duration, juxtaposition and transitions | Complete production text the scenes realize |

## Route and parents

Commissioned video follows one traceable chain: settings and research, screenplay, laws and entities with their tests and accepted [catalog cases](../motion/SKILL.md), scene composition, then the film. Each link cites the addresses it realizes from the links above it, so no code exists without a cited basis and no requirement stays unrealized; a later link can only realize what an earlier one decided, and motion written before the screenplay settles its action is fitted to guesses.

Every film has settings, because every later layer realizes and cites their facts and cannot cite what was never written; a facet that does not apply says so with its reason. Long work runs settings → treatments → scripts → screenplay construction, then screenplay naturalness; only the final screenplay has a separate expression pass. A short runs settings → screenplay (`short-with-settings`), keeping expression, evidence and review without empty treatment, script or outline files. Asset-only work has no narrative passes, and only an explicit user waiver omits screenplay naturalness. Choose form and scale through [direction](planning/direction.md); complete work is the default. In an AutoMovie workspace the production's folder fixes the route: `movies/shorts/<name>` runs settings → screenplay, and `movies/series/<name>` runs the whole long route.

## Construction

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, discovered requirements and the [research](planning/research.md) the facts draw on |
| Treatments | Completed settings |
| Scripts | Completed treatments |
| Screenplay, long work | Completed treatments and scripts, including scripts expression |
| Screenplay, short | Completed settings, the brief and the research the settings cite |

Inherit through actual parents, repairing a missing handoff rather than bypassing it, and run [discovery](planning/discovery.md) before bulk settings.

1. Finish the whole body while its pass is disabled.
2. Complete [inspection and cause repair](correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), enable the stage through [evidence configuration](../evidence/configuration.md), and write [answers](evidence/writing.md).
3. Submit the body, boundaries and population to independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) through creative closure and evidence audit under [review rounds](../review/rounds.md).
4. Repair findings at their owners and complete the closing review.

## Completion

Run `npm run evidence` and read its whole report; a passing graph shows the recorded relationships, and review judges the creative result. Whole-work completion needs the selected passes, a current closing review, passing unfiltered validation, the compiler and source checks and required tests, and [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) of any commissioned media.

A manuscript with its closing review may be delivered with its revision and named remaining diagnostics, but that is not fully validated completion. A creative defect, missing parent, pending transfer, unfinished review or unverified media blocks its completion, and until review finishes the result is a draft. Changes to completed layers follow [closed passes](correction/repair.md#closed-passes).
