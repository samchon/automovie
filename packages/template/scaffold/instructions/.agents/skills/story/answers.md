# Answers

Answer the body's story contracts in a [separate context](workflow/routes.md#separate-contexts) from its author. The Evidence skill owns graph configuration.

## Answer writing

Leave the body with its author. Return any unanswerable item with the passage and failed criterion.

Read the full current respondent, required parents and cited sources firsthand. Answer the [selected items in full](../contracts/items.md#item-structure), including work and language. Answer overviews first and record waived stages.

Answer only what the item asks from the current body and parents. Check exact quotations and owner addresses. Verify claimed counts, order, causes, places and states across the scope. Cite a shared cause once.

Update affected answers after repairs in an open pass. Apply [closed-pass rules](correction/repair.md#closed-passes) after closure, even if the stage remains enabled. Return the body and answers to the director, who gives the reviewer the annotation-free body first. Narrative rounds govern review records and fingerprints.

## Answer placement

Keep construction answers separate under [document layout](document-layout.md#documents). The H1 names the exact file or file and anchor. Follow it with Evidence headings and support using the installed package's syntax.

Record a supported exclusion in the same answer document, as an `@evidenceExclude` heading under the respondent's H1 with its scope and reason. The body stays free of answers and exclusions. Use the designated discovery carrier for a discovery negative and the relationship's allowed form. Exclude a population only when all of it is inapplicable.

A work- or language-specific question is adopted through [adoption](../evidence/adoption.md), which also chooses its respondent.

## Grounds for retaining a candidate

For screenplay expression, apply the [decision and exception rule](../contracts/story/principles/screenplay.md#reads-as-written) to named failures. For other candidates, compare a variant that preserves information, access and pressure without the disputed form. Record the material comparison and decision in its record.

Demonstrate the form's effect in the text. Use comparisons to repair independently found failures. Preserve the function in a rewrite and send inherited defects to their [earliest owner](correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).
