# Answers

The evidence answerer writes the answers a narrative body gives its story contracts, in a [context separate](workflow/routes.md#separate-contexts) from the body's author, and places them where [answer placement](#answer-placement) puts them. [Evidence](../evidence/SKILL.md) owns graph configuration.

## Answer writing

The answerer changes no body. A question it cannot answer truthfully goes back to the author as a gap, with the passage and the statement it fails, and is answered once the author fills it.

Read the whole selected respondent and the parents it needs, find its questions through the [story contracts](../contracts/story/SKILL.md), and where an item has statements, work through them as [items and statements](../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) sets. Answer an overview question first, because the local answers follow its direction.

An answer states the current work: it cannot argue missing material into existence or overturn a locked recipient loss. Support each assertion with exact quotations and owner addresses checked against the current body or parent, and check claimed counts, sequence, causes, places and states across the whole scope. Answer only what the question asks, because an unneeded inventory becomes a stale fact, and group a repeated cause, citing its grounds once.

When the author edits an open pass whose stage is enabled, the answerer reconciles the dependent answers; changes touching closed work follow [closed passes](correction/repair.md#closed-passes). The body and answers then go to the next step of the [route](workflow/SKILL.md). Independent [review](../review/narrative-rounds.md) judges only the body, and a refreshed fingerprint grants no creative judgment.

## Answer placement

Construction answers stay detached from creative bodies, in the unit and file locations the [document layout](document-layout.md#documents) gives. Each answer document's H1 names the exact body file or file-and-anchor address, followed by Evidence headings and supporting prose; the installed evidence package's documentation owns the native address and annotation syntax.

A question that does not apply is answered through the mechanism its relationship permits. Put a permitted exclusion in the respondent unit's own HTML comment, because a detached document holds no exclusion or exclusion-review row; a discovery negative goes in the designated discovery carrier. Place an H2's exclusion comment directly beneath it, before its H3 headings, and a file's before its first heading. State the inspected scope and exactly why the question does not apply; a population exclusion requires the entire population to be inapplicable.

A work- or language-specific question is adopted through [adoption](../evidence/adoption.md), which also says where it lives: a unit answers for its own behavior, and a designated population respondent for a whole-work duty. Review records and their fingerprints follow [narrative rounds](../review/narrative-rounds.md).

## Grounds for retaining a candidate

A named expression failure follows its contract's [decision and exception rule](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written). For any other candidate, write an alternative that carries the same information, access and pressure without the form, and compare the two, recording the gain or loss in ignored notes and the decision in the accountable record.

Deletion, a function label, precedent or earlier protection cannot establish what the expression achieves. After an independent finding, the comparison guides repair rather than acquittal: a working function whose form fails is recomposed, and a defective inherited value returns to its earliest owner under [upstream defects](correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).
