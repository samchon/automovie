# Segment review

After [testing the central passage](judgment.md#before-widening), review contiguous segments in film order.

## What to look at

Capture the full segment in the film camera at the [specified times](../visual-debugging/capture.md#times). Use [motion judgment](judgment.md) for the complete exchange, including action outside the viewer's immediate focus. At each join, check support, direction, speed and possession on both sides.

## Review rounds

Group findings by cause. Return a failed module to its [catalog case](catalog-cases.md). Recapture affected intervals and joins, including the central passage if its lead-in or consequence changed. Review the assembled film under [delivery review](../review/delivery-review.md#video).
