# Segment review

Review each finished short segment with its adopted scene, view and clock before extending it, so defects do not spread through unreviewed motion. [Visual debugging](../visual-debugging/SKILL.md) owns repeatable observation conditions and cost.

## What to look at

Read the whole segment on the contact sheets of the [review segments](../review/delivery-review.md#measurement-and-comparison) that cover it, each one capture of a stretch of the film. Then capture its exchanges, contacts, cuts and joins on the film's own consecutive frames, as [times](../visual-debugging/capture.md#times) requires, using the README's capture options for an exact frame grid or exact seconds. Open the full-size stills as [stills and sheets](../visual-debugging/capture.md#stills-and-sheets) sets, add a second named view as [views](../visual-debugging/capture.md#views) sets, and use `joint` marks where the capture offers them.

Judge the segment's staging, camera and timing and how its accepted cases join. A case that no longer convinces as the depicted practice returns to its [catalog case](catalog-cases.md). Numbers, hashes and receipts support that judgment but cannot close it.

## Review rounds

Each review segment, taken in film order, is one round: capture its stills, review them all, repair every finding by cause at its owning module, then recapture only the interval the repair touches and the seam to the next segment. Move on only when that interval is clean. Rechecking an interval costs a capture, not a render, so no fix waits for the whole film.

Accepted segments do not establish the whole film; [delivery review](../review/delivery-review.md) reviews the assembled one.
