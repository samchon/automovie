# Physical feedback

How a production chooses each scene's physical mode, and uses the installed engine's physical computations for a body no package moves. The installed packages resolve a person's supports, contacts and forces, which a composition states under [forces and outcomes](compose/composition.md#forces-and-outcomes), so a production solves none of them here.

## Physical mode

Choose each scene's physical mode in its composition, with a scene default and explicit overrides for a body or a window, among the modes the installed packages document:

- the kinematic default, planned by the vocabulary;
- a simulated window, in which the engine's solver produces the bodies' response and hands back to the composed motion after it;
- a stated departure from the solved result when impulse, gravity or compliance is exaggerated.

Simulate a window only where the promise needs a response the kinematic mode cannot show, because authored timing and dramatic intent still choose what the audience sees. In every mode a solver realizes the [decided outcome](compose/composition.md#forces-and-outcomes) and is audited against it.

Record each departure's reason in the scene's comment, because an implicit change can conceal a failed screenplay outcome. Every mode audits its promised contacts, misses, falls and timing against its actual receipt source, keeping geometric and solved receipts distinct: a proximity measurement cannot establish a solved impulse.

## Cached simulation

Simulate fixed numerical inputs once as a [stage cache](../production/stage-caches.md) and draw cached states by film time, so playback, seek and capture consume the same performance. A late edit may reuse only a verified unchanged input prefix with its complete solver and controller checkpoint.

Keep dimensions, mass or material assumptions, initial state, time sampling, transforms and constraints explicit where the chosen API needs them, with simple collision or control representations separate from display tessellation.

## A production law's goals

A law for [what is not a person](laws/extension.md#what-is-not-a-person) realizes its [effector goals](laws/authoring.md) with the engine's solvers, as its README and public types give them. Read the engine's public contracts for the interaction at issue, and use its kinematics, range-of-motion checks, rigid impacts or other computations to derive consequential action or expose an unworkable performance. Order the fits as [fit order](laws/authoring.md#fit-order) sets, and solve several goals together where a solver accepts several targets.

A residual, refusal or range-limited pose marks an unreachable goal, repaired at its owner as [staging](staging.md#blocking) directs. A solver the engine lacks follows [a defect or a gap](../development/reuse.md#a-defect-or-a-gap).

## Review

Feed returned motion, contacts or violations into the performance and check them against measurable production requirements. Then review support, impact order, reaction, recoil and the following state on consecutive captured frames across the complete affected sequence under [segment review](segment-review.md), the hand-back at each end of a simulated window included. When detail, proportions or attachments change, recheck that the physical assumptions and visible contact still agree.
