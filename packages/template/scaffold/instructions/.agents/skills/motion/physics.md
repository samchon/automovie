# Physical feedback in performance

Read the installed engine's public contracts for the physical interaction at issue, and use its kinematics, range-of-motion checks, rigid impacts or other relevant computations to derive consequential action or expose an unworkable performance. Authored timing and dramatic intent still choose what the audience sees, so simulate only where the promise needs it.

Realize [effector goals](authoring.md) with the installed solvers by role: whole-body fitting reaches one goal from a held support, stance planting pins each foot's support run to the ground and fits the legs, chain solvers reach a target along a bend hint, aim solvers turn a head, eye or held tool toward a target, and attachment carries a prop on a hand or a rider on a mount. Order the fits as [effector goals](authoring.md#order-the-fits) sets, and solve several goals together where a solver accepts several targets. Under the single transform owner [staging](staging.md) requires, a carried prop rides one hand and receives the other hand as a goal, and a mount carries the rider's seated support and receives the hands and feet as goals on its regions. A residual, refusal or range-limited pose marks an unreachable goal, repaired at its owner as staging directs; a missing solver is added at its package owner.

Choose the physical mode in each scene's composition, with a scene default and explicit body overrides: `kinematic` for an authored hint or deliberate stylization, `simulated` when the promise requires solved physical performance, and `exaggerated` when stated impulse, gravity or compliance factors depart from that simulation. Record each departure's reason in the scene's comment, because an implicit change can conceal a failed screenplay outcome. Every mode audits its promised contacts, misses, falls and timing against its actual receipt source, keeping geometric and solved receipts distinct: a proximity measurement cannot establish a solved impulse.

Simulate fixed numerical inputs once as a [saved stage](../production/stages.md) and draw cached states by film time, so playback, seek and capture consume the same performance. Late edits may reuse only a verified unchanged input prefix and its complete solver/controller checkpoint.

Keep dimensions, mass or material assumptions, initial state, time sampling, transforms and constraints explicit where the chosen API needs them, with simple collision or control representations separate from display tessellation. Feed returned motion, contacts or violations into the performance, check them against measurable production requirements, then review support, impact order, reaction, recoil and subsequent state in consecutive frames across the complete affected sequence with the review commands and overlays in the project README. When detail, proportions or attachments change, recheck that the physical assumptions and visible contact still agree.
