# Motion judgment

Judge the performed action in its catalog case, scene or finished film.

## Questions

Use [diagnostic stills](../visual-debugging/capture.md#times) for spatial questions.

### Form

At the film camera's scale, do the proportions, orientation and attachments hold alongside the bodies and objects they meet?

### Action

<!-- @reference [Kovar, Schreiner and Gleicher](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~kovar/footskateCleanup.pdf) describe sliding during an intended footplant. -->

Do posture, preparation, support and recovery read as this person's behavior and practiced style? For a reaction to contact, locate the surface meeting at or before its onset. Check clearance and plants or grasps relative to their moving support. If perspective hides the contact, use another view or measure the posed surfaces under [frame evaluation](frame-evaluation.md).

### Pace

Do each participant's speed, effort, spacing and timing perform what the beat shows? For skilled practice, do the cues, spacing, simultaneous responses and elapsed times match the study? Measure intervals and spacing at times sampled under [frame evaluation](frame-evaluation.md).

### Staging

Can the viewer follow the required geography, action and response through the cut? Check the [intended view](staging.md#the-view), including what it deliberately withholds.

### Outcome

What in the picture delivers the intended outcome?

## Findings

Record the time or frames, the failed requirement and the repair owner. Trace the cause under [correction rounds](../visual-debugging/correction-rounds.md#causes).

## Before widening

<!-- @reference [Disney Animation](https://disneyanimation.com/process/layout/) on staging with the camera. Blender Studio on the [timed edit](https://studio.blender.org/tools/pipeline-overview/pre-production/editorial) and [blocking pass](https://studio.blender.org/tools/artist-guide/shot-production/animation). -->

Stage the decisive passage and its lead-in and consequence with coarse cast, props, obstacles and timing cues. Watch it at 1× in the intended view and clear segment review before widening.

If the preview drops frames, use a timed export. Keep the pace judgment open until someone watches the passage at speed. Measure evaluation and capture cost before planning the whole-film render. Check new conditions as they enter the film.
