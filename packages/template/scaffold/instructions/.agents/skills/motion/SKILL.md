# Programmed motion

A scene starts from its screenplay and the study [research](../story/planning/research.md#what-needs-a-study) requires, a standalone case from its subject facts and promised behavior.

## The motion vocabulary

The screenplay owns what happens and when it matters. The composition directs kinematic performance through the [entities'](../development/entities.md) capabilities. The installed vocabulary resolves body movement, fitting, supports and contact bounds. Read its [parameters and operators](../automovie/SKILL.md#reading-a-package) before authoring.

## Order

1. Prepare the coarse [entities](../development/entities.md#coarse-first).
2. [Compose the performance](compose/SKILL.md), repairing refusals at the named axis.
3. Accept each module in its [catalog case](catalog-cases.md).
4. [Stage](staging.md) and [review](segment-review.md) each segment before extending it.
5. Hand it to [assembly](../production/assembly.md) or [video finishing](../video/SKILL.md), as commissioned.

[Motion judgment](judgment.md) decides every acceptance in steps 3 and 4, and its [gate](judgment.md#before-widening) comes before the film widens.

Use [frame evaluation](frame-evaluation.md) for clocks, seeking and split renders. Render commissioned [control channels](control-channels.md) from the same performance.
