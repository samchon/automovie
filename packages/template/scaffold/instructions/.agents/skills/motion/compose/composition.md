# Composing a performance

A screenplay beat becomes each person's composition in the installed motion vocabulary: actions with their parameters, joined by operators. The vocabulary's README and public types own every call and value this page names by role, so read them under [reading a package](../../development/reuse.md#reading-a-package) before writing a composition.

## Action axes

For each thing a person does in the beat, settle these axes from the beat record and its [performance study](../../story/planning/research.md#performance-study), top to bottom, because each narrows the next:

| Axis | Settle |
| --- | --- |
| Base | What carries the body: the floor, a seat, a wall, a bar, water, a moving vehicle or mount, or another person |
| Travel | Whether the body travels, where to and at what gait |
| Action | The vocabulary's action, and its named entry where the action has a table of them, such as a kind of gesture, touch or task |
| Effector | The hand, fingers, foot or other part that performs it or bears weight, and what each hand already holds |
| Target | A region of another person or of the performer's own body, a place on an object, a point in the world or none, with the contact site on it |
| Object | The object involved and how the body relates to it: holding, using, operating, resting on, carrying, riding, throwing or launching it |
| Partner | The performer's role where several perform one action, such as giver and receiver, leader and follower, or lifter and lifted |
| Force | What acts on the body from outside, such as a hit, a shove, a load or a slip, and the outcome the beat decides |
| Manner | Effort, pace, count and how long it is held |
| Layer | What runs under everything the person does, such as gaze, breathing, listening or a mood |
| Timing | When it happens against other actions, partners, hits, commands, dialogue and beats |

Write those decisions as the composition and nothing else. A value the beat does not decide stays at the action's default, because the default is the studied value and a guessed number is not. A decision the beat left vague is made in the screenplay, under [concretization](../../story/correction/repair.md#concretization).

Everyone present breathes, and a listener's gaze follows the conversation unless the beat departs from it. Time a reaction by a sync on the hit, sight, sound or command that causes it; the vocabulary adds the studied reaction time.

## Forces and outcomes

The vocabulary moves a body by the forces on it, so give it the force and write no reaction by hand. A strike's hit carries its own force; a force the beat does not size, such as a shove, takes its size from the study. The outcome, such as whether the shoved man sways, steps or falls, is the one the screenplay decided: state it in the composition rather than leave the vocabulary to choose, because an outcome the vocabulary picks changes with the body or the mass. An outcome the force cannot produce is refused and repaired under [refusals](refusals.md).

## Operators

The vocabulary joins actions with a few operators:

| The beat shows | Operator |
| --- | --- |
| One action, then the next | A sequence |
| An action that ends early, at a moment or when something comes near | A cut on that action, inside a window it declares |
| One person doing two things at once, such as reading while walking | An overlay: the action that moves the body carries, and the other takes the parts the carrier yields |
| Something running under everything a person does | A layer, which adds to every action and takes no part away from one |
| Several people each doing their own thing at once | Their compositions in parallel |
| Two or more people performing one action together, such as a handshake, a handover, a lift or a throw | A mutual join, which plans every role from one meeting |
| An action on an object, such as sitting on it, gripping it, opening it or riding it | Attaching the action to the object's [affordance](../../development/entities.md#objects) |
| An action timed to another's instant or hit, a partner, a line of dialogue, a command or a beat | A sync between the named instants |

Start a following action inside the window where the previous one lets the next interrupt it, rather than letting each return to rest, because a person begins the next thing before finishing the last.

## Groups

People who act as one, such as a marching unit or a fleeing crowd, are composed through the vocabulary's group capability, and each member still performs through the axes above. What the group does is the screenplay's, and how many members an outcome takes is given as a count or a studied rate; the seed and each member's traits vary only how and when, under [independent draws](../laws/variation.md#independent-draws).

## Seed

Every performance takes the film's [one seed](../../development/source-layout.md#how-the-parts-work-together). The vocabulary draws each variation from the seed, the person and the action, so people given the same action already differ and a crowd needs no hand-set offsets. Another take of an action is another seed or parameter, never a copied composition, under [one module](../../development/reuse.md#one-module).

## Handoff

A cast or crowd entity's capability returns the composition and a scene joins capabilities, as [source layout](../../development/source-layout.md#how-the-parts-work-together) sets. A composition the vocabulary cannot perform is refused when the performance is built; repair it under [refusals](refusals.md), and accept one that plans in its [catalog case](../catalog-cases.md).
