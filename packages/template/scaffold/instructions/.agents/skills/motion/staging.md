# Staging

Staging turns screenplay beats into scene compositions, and returns whenever blocking, cameras or continuity change. [Entity work](../development/entities.md) chooses representations, [frame evaluation](frame-evaluation.md) owns time sampling and the screenplay owns every edit decision.

## The observable performance

Read each scene and its beat records in context: the required action or change, who starts it, the intended reaction and the resulting state, with consequential props, supports, entrances and exits. Silence, stillness or an off-screen event can be the decision.

One beat may need several camera setups and one setup may span several beats; each scene cites what it realizes under the [composition contract](../contracts/development/composition.md).

## Blocking

Stage in the set's zones and landmarks, never in coordinates: each beat's spatial change moves its participants between them through entity capabilities, and the set entity resolves where that is.

Program each person's actions, reactions, pauses and result through their [composition](compose/SKILL.md) and the interactions that relate the participants, with [physical feedback](physical-feedback.md) and [retargeting](retargeting.md) where a scene needs them. An installed action carries its own preparation and follow-through, so add only the anticipation the screenplay decides, such as stepping nearer or looking at the target first.

Program everyone present as [living scene](../contracts/development/composition.md#living-scene) asks, so an official repositions, a crowd reacts and a partner keeps reading the action. Take each background mover's purpose and destination from the screenplay, or record them as a [concretization](../story/correction/repair.md#concretization); cross the mover behind the principal action rather than through it, and quiet it at the beat's focal moment, as the DGA's [background guidance](https://www.dga.org/the-guild/councils/west/western-adupm-council/committees-of-the-adupm-west/standing-ad-upm-committees/first-ad-committee/setting-background) advises.

Each entity has one transform owner, so a clip, an attachment and a procedural driver never fight for the same state; a reach or scale mismatch is fixed at its owner, not by changing the outcome.

## The view

Cameras and lights are entities whose behavior follows the action. A camera realizes the screenplay's shots and cuts, decided under [shots and cuts](../story/layers/shots.md), through the installed camera grammar the interface package's types define: it frames subjects by relation, reframes as they move and follows motivated action. A decided lens reaches the picture only through the camera's field of view, so set the field of view from it as those types document. A view the grammar cannot give follows [a defect or a gap](../development/reuse.md#a-defect-or-a-gap).

Light each scene from the sources its set and story day give, such as a window, a lamp or the sun, and keep each source's direction and color across the scene's cuts, because light that jumps at a cut reads as another place or time.

Inspect every shot at the delivered aspect and scale through the whole action, in the actual camera frames rather than in subject bounds:

- the subject the beat names reads at the decided size;
- at the beat's key action, the moving limb and the turned head stand clear of the torso and the background;
- no head or limb is cut at a joint or crowded against the frame edge unless the shot intends it;
- nothing clips or hides a contact or a reaction, and no detail competes with it;
- screen direction, eyelines and light hold across each cut, apart from a discontinuity the screenplay decided.

A pan that crosses the frame too fast for the frame rate judders, the more so without motion blur, and stills cannot show it. Measure each camera move's speed in frame widths per second from the resolved camera path, read what motion blur the renderer draws, and ask the user to watch the fast moves.

A framing failure is repaired in the camera's placement within the beat's camera entry, or at that entry or the space entry in the screenplay, never by moving the performance. A light that jumps goes to its light entity, and a wrong gaze, contact or clipping body to the module that performs it.

## Segments and handoff

Build contiguous segments and pass [segment review](segment-review.md), carrying each accepted ending into the next and checking both sides of the join, and hand accepted scenes to [assembly](../production/assembly.md).
