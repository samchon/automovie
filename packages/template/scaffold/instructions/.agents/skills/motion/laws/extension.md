# Vocabulary extension

Use the first applicable route below. User-chosen generated or traced motion follows [motion diffusion](../diffusion/SKILL.md).

## Routes

### Composing again

Check the closest action's parameters and the [operators](../compose/composition.md#operators). Try another target, effector, object, manner or seed, or compose actions with an overlay or mutual join.

Send an installed action that fails under documented use to its [package owner](../compose/refusals.md#when-no-repair-holds).

### Registering a row

Register a missing table row as the README specifies. Its owning action keeps the open parameters and operators.

### Registering a law

Write a law only when the missing action owns what no shipped action plans:

- a change of base, such as a way onto or off a support;
- an outcome rule between performers;
- a planner of its own, for a path, a pattern of steps or a sequence no shipped planner lays out.

Register the law with the vocabulary as its README shows, and the vocabulary composes, cuts, refuses and checks it like its own actions.

### Other moving bodies

Use the installed model and motion capabilities for other moving bodies. Extend a missing reusable capability at its owner, following [physical feedback](../physical-feedback.md) when a scripted response requires simulation.

## What an extension owes

Confirm the [research](../../story/planning/research.md#what-needs-a-study) first. Put the extension in its [laws folder](../../development/source-layout.md#source) and answer the [behaviors contract](../../contracts/development/behaviors.md). [Test](../../development/testing.md#motion-checks) against the study and accept a [catalog case](../catalog-cases.md).

Model the extension on the nearest shipped action in the installed `@automovie/motions` `src/`, or in its repository at the installed version's revision when source is absent. Follow that action's frames, units, ownership and validation conventions.

A row takes only its study's values. A law follows the pages this skill's router lists.

## Sending it upstream

Share a row or a law other productions would need as a [shared module](../../development/modules.md#shared-modules) of the workspace, tell the user, and keep the registered one in the production until the package ships it. Tell the user of an action registration cannot express.
