# Vocabulary extension

What to do when a beat needs an action or a moving body the installed packages do not seem to have. Take the first route below that stages the beat, because each later one costs more to write and leaves more of the checking to the production. Generated or traced motion the user chooses follows [motion diffusion](../diffusion/SKILL.md) instead.

## Composing again

Read the nearest action's parameters and the [operators](../compose/composition.md#operators) again in the vocabulary's types. Most missing actions are an existing one with another target, effector, object or manner, two joined as an overlay or a mutual join, or the same one at another seed. Most such motion is the vocabulary's own at other inputs, as its types show.

## Registering a row

A row is one named entry of an action's table, such as a kind of gesture, touch or task. When what is missing is a new entry of a table the vocabulary has, register the row with the vocabulary as its README shows. The action that owns the table then performs the row, with every axis the row leaves open and every operator.

## Registering a law

Write a law only when the missing action owns what no shipped action plans:

- a change of base, such as a way onto or off a support;
- an outcome rule between performers;
- a planner of its own, for a path, a pattern of steps or a sequence no shipped planner lays out.

Register the law with the vocabulary as its README shows, and the vocabulary composes, cuts, refuses and checks it like its own actions.

## What is not a person

Write a production law for a moving body no installed package provides, such as an animal or a machine no archetype offers. The installed packages already move what people use and ride, so those need none. Contact and reactions of a body the production moves use the installed engine through [physical feedback](../physical-feedback.md).

## What an extension owes

Confirm its [research](../../story/planning/research.md#what-needs-a-study) study first, because a row or a law written without one invents the practice. Put it in the laws folder [source layout](../../development/source-layout.md#source) gives, where its declarations answer the [behaviors contract](../../contracts/development/behaviors.md), and unit-test it against its study's values through [testing](../../development/testing.md#motion-checks). Then accept it in a [catalog case](../catalog-cases.md) like any other module.

A row takes only its study's values. A law follows [effector goals](authoring.md) and the other shared law rules the [motion laws](SKILL.md) router lists where it needs them, and [method references](method-references.md) help choose its algorithm.

## Sending it upstream

A row or a law other productions would need belongs in the vocabulary itself. Share it inside a workspace as a [shared module](../../development/source-layout.md#shared-modules), hand it to the user for the package under [a defect or a gap](../../development/reuse.md#a-defect-or-a-gap), and keep the registered one until the package ships it. An action that registration cannot express is a gap under the same route.
