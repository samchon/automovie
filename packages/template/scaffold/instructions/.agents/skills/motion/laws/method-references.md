# Method references

These primary sources help choose the algorithm of a law written under [vocabulary extension](extension.md), and inform the retargeting and control-channel checks. The installed motion vocabulary has already made these choices for its own actions, and the installed engine may already provide a method, so read its README before adopting one.

The sources inform implementation methods and failure checks only; movement evidence comes from the [performance study](../../story/planning/research.md#performance-study). Their experiments, embodiments and hardware neither validate this production nor supply an installed solver, so check an adopted principle on the current host and the commissioned output.

| Primary source | Decision informed here |
| --- | --- |
| [Motion Graphs](https://graphics.cs.wisc.edu/Papers/2002/KGP02/mograph.pdf), Sections 3–4, and [parameterized motions](https://research.cs.wisc.edu/graphics/Gallery/kovar.vol/ParamMotion/motionFamilies.pdf), Section 1.1.2 | Reuse motion units through compatible transitions and meaningful bounded parameters; these captured-motion methods inform composition without supplying a procedural controller or contact solver |
| [Animation sync markers](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/animation-sync-groups-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.8) and [target warping windows](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/motion-warping-in-unreal-engine) | Align meaningful phases and limit target adaptation to the relevant interval; synchronization and target fitting do not establish collision response or balance |
| [Full Body IK](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/control-rig-full-body-ik-in-unreal-engine) and [Two Bone IK](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.animation.rigging@1.3/manual/constraints/TwoBoneIKConstraint.html) | Pose a body from effector goals with per-goal strength, a bend hint and per-joint limits, keeping the input pose where no goal pulls |
| [Footskate Cleanup](https://research.cs.wisc.edu/graphics/Gallery/kovar.vol/Cleanup) | Hold each foot plant exactly and blend the correction into neighboring frames, because plants switching on and off pop the knee and root; the method cleans existing motion and may stretch legs slightly, which a rigid rig does not allow |
| [Semi-Procedural Locomotion](https://runevision.com/thesis/) | Adapt example gait cycles so each foot lands on the actual ground, with heel and toe aligned as one constraint; built for keyframed or captured cycles, it informs a law for a gait no installed package carries, such as that of an animal no archetype offers |
| [Spatial Relationship Preserving Character Motion Adaptation](https://history.siggraph.org/?p=105435) | Close contact between characters or with objects, such as wrestling, dancing or entering a car, survives editing when their spatial relations are kept rather than their joint angles; it says what to preserve, not how to solve it here |
| [Retargeting Matters / General Motion Retargeting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02252) | Inspect retargeting artifacts such as foot sliding, self-penetration and physically infeasible motion before expecting a downstream tracker or generator to repair them |
| [Shooting for Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03116) | Kinematic similarity and dynamically feasible contact are different claims; use actual supported contact or physics evidence when the task requires it |
| [Spatiotemporal Retargeting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30540) | Object interaction depends on preserving body–object relations at critical stages and key body parts; inspect those actual contacts instead of weighting every frame and part alike |
| [Wan-Animate preprocessing](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2/blob/main/wan/modules/animate/preprocess/UserGuider.md) | Inspect the actual pose and mask intermediate outputs, body-proportion retargeting and the selected mode's supported subject interactions before generation |
| [ControlNet](https://github.com/lllyasviel/ControlNet) | A conditioning mechanism has a concrete input contract; an arbitrary stick figure or depth-like image is not automatically a compatible signal |

[Retargeting](../retargeting.md), [physical feedback](../physical-feedback.md) and [frame evaluation](../frame-evaluation.md) own the production procedure, [control channels](../control-channels.md) the hint's channels, and [video generation](../../video/generation.md) service integration and take adoption.
