# Review

Review in a fresh context without the author's working memory, because an author reads their own work as intended. Identify the requested judgment, its exact scope, current inputs and recipient; a self-review is labeled as one and never stands for independent acceptance, and an unavailable context or output is an unperformed check. Repeat rounds until the current scope closes under its round rules.

| Task | Read |
| --- | --- |

Reviewing segments while implementing motion belongs to the author, under [motion segments](../motion/segments.md).

## What a reviewer observes

A reviewer sees pictures only as stills and cannot hear. Judge motion and picture on stills at the spacing [delivery review](delivery.md#measure-and-compare) gives, and report playback and sound only as what the user watched or heard. A review that claims an observation it could not make is worse than one that names the gap.

## Cited sources are trusted {#cited-sources}

Check that the work follows what its cited study or record says, and that each citation carries a locator. Do not open the original behind a recorded citation to verify it again: the study's author already answered for it, re-reading references is the costliest read in a production, and a second reading of the same page reopens settled facts without new evidence. Report a source only when the work contradicts its own record of it, or when a claim has no citation at all.
