# Independent reading

An independent reader is a [separate context](../story/workflow/routes.md#separate-contexts) that reads a narrative body as its recipient before any author record.

## Reading as the recipient

Give the reviewer the task, the recipient and the annotation-free body before parents, contracts or earlier verdicts, and never the [evidence answers](narrative-rounds.md#reviewers).

Read the whole declared scope, splitting a large one into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling, and lock the first response and concrete losses under the [independent-reading questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading). Read that item's statements before locking, because a lock that fails them, such as one without a quotation, has to be read again.

Read each layer as its recipient:

| Layer | Read as |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Treatment writer and designer |
| Brief | Screenplay writer and designer |
| Treatments | Script writer |
| Scripts | Screenplay writer and performer |
| Screenplay | Audience, then filmmaker |

A recipient's loss stays a finding even when upstream knowledge explains it, and a defective parent is reported with its earliest owner under [upstream defects](../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).

## Comparison with parents and contracts

Compare the body's decisions, names, notation, quotations and consequential action, image and sound with the selected contracts, the parents and the requirements, looking for contradictions; matching quotations do not replace this comparison. Do not recompute fiction's own quantities, as [factual basis](../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) sets.

Where the route runs [independent classification](../story/correction/inspection.md#independent-classification), lock it in a separate context before reading author defenses. Apply the contract's decision rule to named failures and the [retention test](../story/answers.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) to other candidates; intent or a function label cannot overturn the locked judgment.

## Expression

Scripts receive content and expression together under their expression questions. The screenplay's speech is judged under [speaker plausibility](../contracts/story/shared/principles/realized.md#speaker-plausibility), and its idiom and action lines under the language statements of [audiovisual realization](../contracts/story/shared/principles/screenplay.md#audiovisual-realization).

Scene headings, useful parentheticals, first-entry appearance and the screenplay's standing state and [beat records](../story/layers/notation.md#records) are notation, not expression failures. Treatment prescriptions are judged under their [completion question](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md#decided-event).
