# Source review

Identify the requested judgment, scope, current inputs and recipient. Use a separate review context. Label any self-review and report an unavailable review or output as unperformed. Repeat source review until its scope closes.

Read the whole requested change with its callers, inputs, consumers, error paths and tests, then check it against the [development contracts](../contracts/development/SKILL.md) its declarations answer.

## Truth of answers

Verify each answer against its [contract item](../contracts/items.md#item-structure), the implementation and the sources it cites. Report any behavior, measurement or limit it claims without support.

## The actual consumer

Verify affected and preserved behavior through the consumer. For a motion change, trace the visible pose at its film time through the registered scene to the action that supplied it. Check any local layer that replaces part of the pose. Judge the result at the [capture times](../visual-debugging/capture.md#times). A distance between roots can establish placement. A claim about a shoe, sleeve or held object needs the corresponding posed surfaces.

For a scene that realizes a screenplay beat, read the beat and its study before its action comments. Check each comment against them, the parameters against the comment, and the [replayed exchange](../evidence/answering.md#film-composition) against the parameters. Return a misread beat or a parameter that contradicts its comment to the film author, a performance that contradicts its parameters to the owner of its action, and a defect in the beat itself through [film repairs](delivery-review.md#repairs).

Attribute playback and [listening observations](../audio/listening.md#user-listening) to the person who watched or listened. Report unavailable hosts or platforms.
