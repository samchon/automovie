# Read before auditing

Use a fresh context of the author's model, without the author's working memory, and give it the task, recipient and annotation-free body before parents, contracts, evidence or earlier verdicts; if none is available, report the review as unperformed.

Read the whole declared scope, splitting a large one into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling, and lock the first response and concrete losses under the [independent-reading questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading).

Then compare the body's decisions, names, notation, quotations and consequential action, image and sound with the selected contracts, actual parents and requirements, looking for contradictions.

Fiction's own derived quantities belong to research and code, so do not recompute them. Lock the [independent classification](../story/correction/inspection.md) in an isolated worker before reading author defenses; answers and matching quotations cannot replace this comparison.

Read each layer as its recipient: settings as treatment writer and designer, a brief as screenplay writer and designer, treatments as script writer, scripts as screenplay writer and performer, and the screenplay as filmmaker and audience.

A recipient's loss stays a finding even when upstream knowledge explains it, and a defective parent is reported with its earliest owner under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).

Apply the contract's decision rule to named failures and the [retention test](../story/evidence/answer-writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) to other candidates; intent or a function label cannot overturn the locked judgment.

For screenplay expression, judge the [audiovisual voice](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice) of the whole unit from its actual speech, performance, image, sound and timing.

Scripts receive content and expression together; scene headings, useful parentheticals, first-entry appearance and the screenplay's breakdown and beat records are notation, not expression failures. Treatment prescriptions are judged under their [completion question](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md#decided-event).

Finish creative correction before the evidence audit under [rounds](rounds.md), then compare each answer with its whole respondent, its parents and the item's [statements](../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements), searching the whole scope for counterexamples to negatives. A truthful answer and a usable scene are separate achievements.
