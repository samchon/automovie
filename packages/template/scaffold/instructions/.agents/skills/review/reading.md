# Read before auditing

Use a fresh context of the author's model, without the author's working memory, and give it the task, recipient and annotation-free body before parents, contracts, evidence or earlier verdicts; if none is available, report the review as unperformed. Read the whole declared scope, splitting a large one into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling, and lock the first response and concrete losses under the [independent-reading questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading).

Then inventory the body's facts, quantities, chronology, names, notation, quotations and consequential action, image and sound, and compare it with the selected contracts, actual parents and requirements. Lock the [independent classification](../story/correction/inspection.md) in an isolated worker before reading author defenses; answers and matching quotations cannot replace the body inventory. For settings and treatments, check external facts and causal claims against the whole relevant source.

Read each layer as its recipient: settings as treatment writer and designer, treatments as script writer, scripts as screenplay writer and performer, and the screenplay as filmmaker and audience. A recipient's loss stays a finding even when upstream knowledge explains it, and a defective parent is reported with its earliest owner under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects). Apply the contract's decision rule to named failures and the [retention test](../story/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) to other candidates; intent or a function label cannot overturn the locked judgment.

For screenplay expression, judge the [audiovisual voice](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice) of the whole unit from its actual speech, performance, image, sound and timing. Scripts receive content and expression together; scene headings, useful parentheticals, first-entry appearance and the screenplay's breakdown and beat records are notation, not expression failures. Treatment prescriptions are judged under their [completion question](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md#decided-event).

Finish creative correction before the evidence audit under [rounds](rounds.md), then compare each answer with its whole respondent, parents and sources, searching the whole scope for counterexamples to negatives. A truthful answer and a usable scene are separate achievements.
