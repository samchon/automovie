# Narrative rounds

A narrative pass closes through independent review. Its [route](../story/workflow/SKILL.md) sets how many rounds it has and what follows its closure.

## Records

Keep current reviews in the review locations of the [document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents), mirroring every authored file and H2, with one verdict document for whole-pass duties. Raw independent reads and sentence tables stay in ignored `.cache/`.

Each unit with no body finding carries its current review, which the installed evidence package binds to the body by a fingerprint of its text.

## Reviewers

A fresh reviewer judges the body against the commission, [project context](../production/project-context.md) and parents. Read as the recipient first. Leave the evidence answers to the answerer.

Consult studies only for applicable facts and targets. Lock the initial reading before consulting earlier verdicts for declined findings. Add a reader only for a question beyond the reviewer's competence.

Record the revision, inputs, scope, findings with literal support, causes, protected achievements, author decisions, limits and outcome. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

A whole-work review rereads every selected body afresh and carries no unit verdict.

## Findings and decisions

Apply the [independent-reading statements](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading). Raise a wording finding when it violates a contract, parent or requirement, or the user directs it.

The author records acceptance or refusal of each finding, with reasons. The reviewer withdraws or rebuts refusals. The author's decision after rebuttal stands. Reopen only with new body, parent or source evidence.

The author repairs only accepted findings under [cause rounds](../story/correction/repair.md#cause-rounds). Close the round on the repaired result.
