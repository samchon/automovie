# Narrative rounds

The narrative route sets the rounds and what follows. Independent review closes a pass.

## Records

Keep current reviews in the review locations of the [document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents), mirroring every authored file and H2, with one verdict document for whole-pass duties. Keep raw independent reads and sentence tables.

When a unit review is carried forward, the installed Evidence package checks its body-text fingerprint. A whole-work review reads every selected body afresh.

## Reviewers

Lock your [independent reading](independent-reading.md#reading-as-the-recipient). Open the parents and cited studies yourself. Check findings against them and the commission before reading past decisions. Leave Evidence answers with the answerer. Add a reader only for a question beyond the reviewer's competence.

Record the revision, inputs, scope, findings with literal support, causes, protected achievements, author decisions, limits and outcome. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

## Findings and decisions

Apply the [independent-reading criteria](../contracts/story/principles/review.md#independent-reading). Raise a wording finding when it violates a contract, parent or requirement, or the user directs it.

The author records each decision and its grounds. The reviewer withdraws or rebuts a disputed finding. The author's final response closes that exchange. Carry unresolved losses into the verdict for the director. Reopen a settled finding only with new body, parent or source evidence.

The author repairs accepted findings only, in [cause rounds](../story/correction/repair.md#cause-rounds). Close the round on the repair.
