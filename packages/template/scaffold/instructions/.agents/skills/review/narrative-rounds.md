# Narrative rounds

A narrative pass closes through independent review: a fresh reviewer reads the body, the author decides each finding, and each round leaves a record and a verdict. How many rounds a pass has, and what follows its closure, is set by its [route](../story/workflow/SKILL.md).

## Records

Keep current reviews in the review locations of the [document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents), mirroring every authored file and H2, with one verdict document for whole-pass duties.

Each unit with no body finding carries its current review, which the installed evidence package binds to the body by a fingerprint of its text. That review covers the body, not the truth of its answers.

Raw independent reads and sentence tables stay in ignored `.cache/`.

## Reviewers

Each round uses a fresh reviewer, and the reviewer judges only the body: against the commission, [project context](../production/project-context.md) and the actual parents, reading it first as the recipient that [independent reading](independent-reading.md#read-as-the-recipient) assigns its layer. It never opens the evidence answers, whose truth the [evidence answerer](../story/evidence/answer-writing.md) owns. Research and earlier reviews are never its subject: it consults a study only for the practice facts and targets the body must meet, and an earlier verdict, after locking its own reading, only for the findings the author declined. Add another reader only for a question the reviewer cannot judge.

Follow [independent reading](independent-reading.md) before opening author records, and record the revision, inputs, scope, findings with literal support, causes, protected achievements, author decisions, limits and outcome. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

A whole-work review rereads every selected body afresh and carries no unit verdict.

## Findings and decisions

Every finding meets the [independent-reading statements](../contracts/story/shared/statements/principles/review.md#independent-reading). A matter of how a passage reads that fails no contract statement and contradicts no parent or requirement is no finding unless the user names it, because one reader's taste alone would keep every round open.

The author decides each finding and records in the verdict whether it accepts or declines it, with the reason. The reviewer answers each declined finding in the verdict, withdrawing it or rebutting the reason, and the author decides a rebutted finding again; that decision stands. A later round raises a declined finding again only on new body, parent or source evidence.

Only accepted findings are repaired, under [cause rounds](../story/correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), by the author's method and changing only what they need. Close with the actual result, not a stage flag.
