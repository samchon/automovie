# Narrative rounds

A narrative pass closes through rounds of independent review, each leaving a record and a verdict.

## Records

Keep current reviews in the review locations of the [document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents), mirroring every authored file and H2, with one verdict document for whole-pass duties. Construction reviews target construction and naturalness reviews the working text.

Each unit with no body finding carries its current review, which the installed evidence package binds to the body by a fingerprint of its text. That review covers the body, not the truth of its answers. After [expression transfer](../story/naturalness/transfer.md#transfer), construction judgments stay with their frozen edition.

Raw independent reads and sentence tables stay in ignored `.cache/`.

## Reviewers

Each round uses a fresh reviewer, and a pass has at most two rounds. A reviewer judges only the body, as its recipient receives it, and never opens the evidence answers, whose truth the [evidence answerer](../story/evidence/answer-writing.md) owns. Add another reader only for a question the reviewer cannot judge.

Follow [independent reading](independent-reading.md) before opening author records, and record the revision, inputs, scope, findings with literal support, causes, protected achievements, author decisions, limits and outcome. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

A whole-work review rereads every selected body afresh and carries no unit verdict.

## Findings and decisions {#findings-and-decisions}

A loss that rests only on how a passage reads, failing no contract statement and contradicting no parent or requirement, is a finding only when both the reviewer and its [independent classification](../story/correction/inspection.md#independent-classification) record it, or the user names it, because one reader's taste alone would keep every round open.

The author decides each finding and records in the verdict whether it accepts or declines it, with the reason. The reviewer answers each declined finding in the verdict, withdrawing it or rebutting the reason, and the author decides a rebutted finding again; that decision stands. Only accepted findings are repaired, under [cause rounds](../story/correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), by the author's method and changing only what they need.

When screenplay naturalness traces a finding to frozen construction content, record its cause and earliest owner as an upstream report in the verdict instead of repeating it each round. It is repaired under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects) and remains an open limit until then.

## Closure {#grading-and-closure}

A round in which the author's final decisions accept no finding closes the pass. Reviewers keep finding matters of their own style, so a review that waited for no findings would never close.

Otherwise a second round, by a fresh reviewer, reads the repaired body. Its accepted findings are repaired, and the pass closes without a third round. The second reviewer reads the first round's verdict only after locking its own reading, and raises again a finding the author declined only on new body, parent or source evidence.

Once the pass closes, the evidence answerer brings the answers up to date with the final body, then sets the pass's stage to completed, because later changes, such as the expression transfer, leave stale answers and reviews that no role may refresh.

The stage records the closure without proving it; close with the actual result.
