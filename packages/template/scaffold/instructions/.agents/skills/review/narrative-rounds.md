# Narrative rounds

A narrative pass closes through rounds of independent review, each leaving a record and a verdict.

## Records

Keep current reviews in the review locations of the [document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents), mirroring every authored file and H2, with one verdict document for whole-pass duties. Construction reviews target construction and naturalness reviews the working text.

Each unit with no body finding carries its current review, which the installed evidence package binds to the body by a fingerprint of its text. That review covers the body, not the truth of its answers. After [expression transfer](../story/naturalness/transfer.md#transfer), construction judgments stay with their frozen edition.

Raw independent reads and sentence tables stay in ignored `.cache/`.

## Reviewers

One author and one independent reviewer carry a pass. The same reviewer reviews every round, resolves objections and confirms corrected answers; in a later round it locks its reading of the changed text before opening the author's new decisions and repairs. Add another reader only for a question the reviewer cannot judge.

When the reviewer's session cannot be resumed, a fresh reviewer takes over, locking its own reading of the affected H2s before opening the record. A standing finding its reading misses is not withdrawn.

Follow [independent reading](independent-reading.md) before opening author records, and record the revision, inputs, scope, findings with literal support and who found each, causes, protected achievements, author decisions, limits and outcome. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

## Findings and objections {#findings-and-objections}

A finding the text itself shows needs one finder. The reviewer's [independent classification](../story/correction/inspection.md#independent-classification) decides the named failures and parent comparisons it records. The reviewer's quoted comparison alone shows a passage that contradicts or leaves unmet a parent, the frozen original, a requirement, a cited record, or a contract statement whose failure needs no judgment of how it reads.

Any other loss rests on how the passage reads to its recipient. It is a finding only when the reviewer's locked judgment and its independent classification both record it, or when the user names it, because one reading of an experience may be that reader's own, and every finding reopens the work. Below that threshold the loss stays in the verdict as an observation.

Once a loss is a finding, every member of its class that a search finds belongs to it.

Before repairing a review finding, the author records its decision beside that finding in the verdict, accepted or a factual objection, and after repairing, the repair. Repair begins once the reviewer has resolved every objection of the round, so the round's standing findings are repaired together by cause. A factual objection quotes its evidence and is raised only when:

- the quotation is absent from the body;
- the passage does not literally match the definition the finding names;
- a permitted exception applies;
- the parent, the frozen original, a requirement or a cited record contradicts the finding;
- the finding lacks the finders this section requires.

Intent, taste, repair cost and earlier approval are not factual objections. The reviewer resolves every objection in the verdict from the actual inputs. An upheld objection withdraws the finding; otherwise it stands.

The author repairs every finding that stands under [cause rounds](../story/correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), choosing the method, and the repair goes to the round the [grading](#grading-and-closure) requires.

When screenplay naturalness traces a finding to frozen construction content, record its cause and earliest owner as an upstream report in the verdict instead of repeating it each round. It is repaired under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects) and remains an open limit until then.

## Scoped rounds

A scoped round verifies repairs in an open pass and rereads their whole affected dependency closure, carrying an unaffected judgment only with its source and grounds. A fact, number or conclusion a repair introduces is verified across its whole range, through every passage and consumer that depends on it.

A whole-work review rereads every selected body afresh and carries no unit verdict.

An item judged an observation in an earlier round becomes a finding only on new body, parent or source evidence, or when the user names it.

## Grading and closure {#grading-and-closure}

Grade each body finding by what its repair changes:

| Grade | Its repair changes | Creative review closes by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minor | Wording only, with no fact, decision, event or audience experience beyond that passage | The scoped round that confirms it |
| Moderate or higher | Anything more | A closing round that rereads every current H2 and judges afresh |

A complete round with no body finding, or whose every body finding an upheld objection withdrew, closes creative review directly.

Once creative review closes, audit the evidence once under [answer writing](../story/evidence/answer-writing.md). A round whose only findings are evidence statements closes when the author corrects those statements and the reviewer confirms them in the verdict, without a new round, provided the body, inputs and requirements are unchanged. A body defect found there returns to creative review.

Close with the actual result, not a stage flag.
