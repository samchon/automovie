# Narrative rounds

A narrative pass closes through rounds of independent review, each leaving a record and a verdict.

## Records

Keep current reviews in the review locations of the [document layout](../story/document-layout.md#documents), mirroring every authored file and H2, with one verdict document for whole-pass duties. Construction reviews target construction and naturalness reviews the working text.

Each unit with no body finding carries its current review, which the installed evidence package binds to the body by a fingerprint of its text. That review covers the body, not the truth of its answers. After [expression transfer](../story/naturalness/transfer.md#transfer), construction judgments stay with their frozen edition.

Raw independent reads and sentence tables stay in ignored `.cache/`.

## Reviewers

One author and one independent reviewer carry a pass, the same reviewer through every round. Add another reader only for a question the reviewer cannot judge.

Follow [independent reading](independent-reading.md) before opening author records, and record the revision, inputs, scope, findings with literal support, causes, protected achievements, author decisions, limits and outcome. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../story/planning/discovery.md).

## Findings and objections {#findings-and-objections}

A loss that rests only on how a passage reads, with no contract failure, parent or requirement it contradicts, is a finding only when both the reviewer and its [independent classification](../story/correction/inspection.md#independent-classification) record it, or the user names it.

Before repairing, the author records in the verdict its decision on each finding: accepted, or a factual objection:

- the quotation is absent;
- the passage does not match the definition;
- a permitted exception applies;
- the parent contradicts the finding.

The reviewer resolves each objection from the actual inputs in the same record. Intent, repair cost or earlier approval cannot dismiss an established loss. The author then repairs the findings that stand under [cause rounds](../story/correction/repair.md#cause-rounds), choosing the method.

When screenplay naturalness traces a finding to frozen construction content, record its cause and earliest owner as an upstream report in the verdict instead of repeating it each round. It is repaired under [upstream repair](../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects) and remains an open limit until then.

## Scoped rounds

A scoped round verifies findings in an open pass and rereads their whole affected dependency closure, carrying an unaffected judgment only with its source and grounds. A fact, number or conclusion a repair introduces is verified across its whole range, through every passage and consumer that depends on it.

A whole-work review rereads every selected body afresh and carries no unit verdict.

An item judged an observation in an earlier round becomes a finding only on new body, parent or source evidence.

## Grading and closure {#grading-and-closure}

Grade each body finding by what its repair changes:

| Grade | Its repair changes | Creative review closes by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minor | Wording only, with no fact, decision, event or audience experience beyond that passage | The scoped round that confirms it |
| Moderate or higher | Anything more | A closing round that rereads every current H2 and judges afresh |

A complete round with no body finding closes creative review directly.

Once creative review closes, audit the evidence once under [answer writing](../story/evidence/answer-writing.md). A round whose only findings are evidence statements closes when the author corrects those statements and the reviewer confirms them in the same record, provided the body, inputs and requirements are unchanged. A body defect found there returns to creative review.

Close with the actual result, not a stage flag.
