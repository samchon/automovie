# Brief principles

Each brief H2 answers as one facet of its subject, owning the anchored H3 decisions beneath it. It does not answer for every film or scene.

## Needed decisions

Does the record name its subject, decision, scope and applicable limits so each screenplay can use it consistently?

Identify cast members by build, appearance and wardrobe, and give the proficiency or habit that affects their choices. For a group, establish its role. Describe a place by the routes, barriers and sightlines the scene uses. For a prop, establish its use and initial state. Distinguish similar objects when exchanging them would change the action.

Where a rule shapes an outcome, state what it permits or prevents and cite its basis. Cite the [study](../../../story/planning/research.md) for skilled practice and sources for external claims under the declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis). Keep story-defining measurements here.
