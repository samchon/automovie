# Common authoring principles

Each authored H2 answers these questions from its own body and identified parents. Population coverage belongs to obligations, not to an individual H2.

## Scope preservation

Does the body deliver its title, instructions and lineage through specific decisions the next author can use?

Check each promised element in this unit's body. Distinguish a decision delegated downstream from an unfinished decision due here. A population answer cannot supply missing unit content. Even a story about records must deliver its events and relationships. Use authoring terms in the world only for a creative reason.
