# Settings population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering settings file, whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned scenes need, without requiring unused domains or empty files.

## World and scope

Do the promise, scope and representation give later layers one basis? Can they find one consistent owner for each decision a scene relies on, with enough detail for its effect?

### Commission

State the audience, intended experience, questions, recurring rewards and title promise. Preserve wording fixed by the user. Record exclusions once and identify what the representation chosen in `PROJECT.md` can convey.

Give the release count, duration range and relation to any larger work. Ground delegated estimates in the material and its limits. Mark uncertainty and revise estimates when their basis changes. For a series, identify what recurs, develops or concludes. Scripts set the release boundaries.

### World

Give each consequential rule, power, institution, person, relationship, place and object one record that later authors can find. Establish recurring characters' situations, drives, values, fears, abilities and limits. Include what they know or misunderstand and how their behavior, appearance and voice distinguish them.

Establish what each participant, including an official or a crowd, does throughout their appearance. Describe relationships from both sides and identify what could change them. For a nonhuman force, give its inputs, operation and thresholds. Use places, props, costume, materials, image and sound to distinguish locations, eras and groups where a scene depends on that distinction.

### Coherence

Check abilities against rules, knowledge against its source, relationships from both sides and changes against baseline conditions. If someone could end an initial threat, injustice or mystery, establish what prevents them. Repair contradictions at their earliest owner.

Under a declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), identify consequential participants in the source. Reconcile chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities, including the rules for invented quantities.

### Records

Use one prose file per independently used subject. Use one term per concept, link to shared facts and present definitions, settled content, conditions and open matters in a consistent order.

Settings own baselines, historical results and lasting states, with a citation to the unit that established a later state. Treatments own events, causes, choices, setups, payoffs and reveals. Cinematography owns the look and source code owns rendering controls. Production instructions or work contracts own editing and audience access.
