# Settings population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering settings file, whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned scenes need, without requiring unused domains or empty files.

## World and scope

Do the promise, scope and representation give later layers one basis? Can they find one consistent owner for each decision a scene relies on, with enough detail for its effect?

- **Commission.** State audience, intended experience, questions, recurring rewards and title promise. Preserve user-fixed wording. Name the delivery scope, relation to a larger work and release counts and duration ranges. Ground delegated estimates in material and limits, mark uncertainty and revise when those change. A series identifies what recurs, closes or develops. Scripts decide release boundaries. Record exclusions once and the affordances of the representation chosen in `PROJECT.md`.
- **World.** Give consequential rules, powers, institutions, people, relationships, places and objects one usable owner each. Establish recurring agents' situations, drives, values, fears, abilities, limits, knowledge, errors, behavior, appearance and audible voice. Give nonhuman forces inputs, operations and thresholds. Every presence, including an official or crowd, has a role throughout its appearance. Define relationships from both sides and the conditions for change. Places, props, costume, materials, image and sound distinguish locations, eras and groups where needed.
- **Coherence.** Check rules against abilities, knowledge against its source, both views of relationships and baseline states against change conditions. Identify who could end an initial threat, injustice or mystery and what prevents it. Repair contradictions at their earliest owner. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), identify consequential source participants and reconcile chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities, including the rules for invented quantities.
- **Records.** Use one prose file per independently used subject, one term per concept and links to shared facts. Give definitions, settled content, conditions and open matters in a consistent order. Keep rendering controls in source. Settings own baselines, historical results and lasting states established downstream, with a citation to the unit. Treatments own events, causes, choices, setups, payoffs and reveals. Cinematography owns the look. Production instructions or work contracts own editing and audience access.
