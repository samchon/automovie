# Common expression statements

Contract: [naturalness/common.md](../../naturalness/common.md).

## Reads as written

These statements are the item's named failures and their exceptions, its whole boundary; a finding needs one of them.

Padding and explanation:

1. No sentence explains again the meaning of an action the scene already shows. Exception: recalling an earlier event or sharing a later action's goal is not repetition by itself, as the passage read with its neighbors shows.
2. No passage ends on a closing aphorism or a balanced summary.
3. No background postpones entry into the scene, and no research or later commentary stops it.
4. No list of absent acts or sounds manufactures stillness.
5. No delivery label repeats its line. Exception: speaker identifiers and useful performance parentheticals are execution instructions, and parenthetical excess is judged under [script execution](../../principles/scripts.md#executable-scene).
6. No appearance or rank introduction is repeated. Exceptions, because removing those cues can make the people impossible to identify:
    - first-entry appearance and costume instructions;
    - a role, relationship or single scene-used identifying trait conveyed through action, address or perception.
7. No habitual gesture, filler, empty link, arbitrary sentence pattern or authoring jargon appears. Exception: reusing an object's name, a word twice or a script's framing is not by itself habitual.
8. No misunderstanding is invented only to be negated.
9. No modifier, qualification, contrast or formatting appears without information, access or pressure. Padding that was removed and came back in different words counts.
10. No character's thought merely adds the conclusion or emotion shown or about to be shown. Exception: a calculation, memory or expectation that reveals new present understanding, hope or burden.

Idiomatic language:

11. The language reads and sounds idiomatic within its adopted variety, period, register and relationships, with no translated construction, imported syntax or expression nobody would use there, even where its abstract meaning is correct.
12. Word combinations, verbs, agreement, word order, subjects and references, pronouns, nominal chains and clauses give an immediate understanding of who does what to whom.
13. Functional dialect, quotation, institutional terms, fragments, passive forms, inversion and deliberate repetition are preserved; no grammatical form is banned, and formal words are not replaced mechanically.
14. A language repair adds no action, cause or opposition.

Concrete realization:

15. Viewpoint and movement, object use, and bodily conditions such as cold, hunger, weight or injury are concrete enough to understand and feel the current action.
16. Scripts serve executable placement and conditions, and leave audience emphasis to the final screenplay.
17. None of these remains:
    - an unlocated inventory;
    - an unmarked change of gaze;
    - a vague impression in place of a required color, material, scale or position;
    - an interchangeable metaphor that erases a distinction;
    - a physical limit reduced to a name or a number.
18. Inherited values and permitted access are used, and no canonical fact is invented.
19. Detail does not displace the present danger, choice or relationship by making the audience decode its sequence. Exception: selective detail, and restrained or documentary form.
20. Sufficient specificity and excessive emphasis are judged together, never one maximized and undone elsewhere.

What cutting must not lose:

21. Successive paragraphs and exchanges keep an identifiable speaker or actor, the object of each reaction, and their causal and spatial connections.
22. No performed exchange is reduced to a progress report, no emotion lacks its occasion, and the adopted voice and humor are not replaced by neutral statements. Exceptions:
    - recoverable omitted subjects;
    - deliberately chosen summary.
23. Uniform short beats do not flatten changing pressure.
24. A needed experience is restored through expression, never through added events or explanatory padding.

Deciding and retaining:

25. Every sentence was tested against the applicable named failures by the [independent classification](../../../../../story/correction/inspection.md#independent-classification), and their connections were read for insufficiency.
26. An ambiguous candidate was decided by the independent classifier, who read its context for whether the named failure causes the recipient's loss.
27. The author decided each finding and chose the repair under [narrative rounds](../../../../../review/narrative-rounds.md#findings-and-decisions).
28. A revised passage was classified again under [inspection](../../../../../story/correction/inspection.md#changed-work).
29. A named failure is retained only under one of these exceptions, supported by the passage, and no author's intention, function label, nearby achievement, previous protection or weaker alternative stands as another:
    - useful execution directions;
    - meaningful silence;
    - thought carrying new understanding, expectation or burden;
    - adopted narration and form;
    - functional lies, refrains and speech habits;
    - formal regularity.
30. Retained narration realizes its adopted contribution through wording, placement or image-sound relations.

## Expression fidelity

1. Compared with its parent treatments, the script keeps:
    - the world facts, events and causal connections;
    - people and relationships, motives, choices and consequences;
    - knowledge boundaries, chronology and reveal order;
    - values such as names, quantities, dimensions and colors;
    - the degree of certainty.
2. Realized setup and payoff, motivation-setting speech, the promised narrator voice and humor survive as functions the audience experiences.
3. Where the current expression of such a function is a named failure, the function is recomposed without the failure, not deleted.
4. Every value the script states is accurate.
5. Nothing omitted changes inherited meaning, required execution, knowledge or reveal order, or removes information the audience needs to understand a choice and its result or that the adopted work requires it to receive.
6. An upstream measurement or condition the audience does not need is not displayed, and execution constraints go to their actual consumer.
7. A claimed loss is shown in the passage, and no upstream parameter inventory is restored to answer it.
8. The script stays inside the established storyline and adopted form. Exception: it may clarify an implied existing actor and develop bodily performance, image, sound and subtext.
9. The script adds no dependency, changes no result, resolves no open relationship and replaces no concrete value with an impression.
10. The script preserves meaning, not an artificial register, negation patterns, source disclaimers or annotation vocabulary.
11. A needed semantic change was reported and not made, and a construction repair returned to its earliest owner under [upstream defects](../../../../../story/correction/repair.md#upstream-defects).
12. Surplus removed below a duration target was not refilled with new facts, events or replacement padding.
