# Screenplay statements

The detailed statements of the [final screenplay principles](../principles/screenplay.md). Check each scene against them before answering, as [items and statements](../../../SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Beat decisions {#beat-decisions}

What each beat decides:

1. The beat names its participants by their settings facts, or by brief decisions on a short.
2. Each participant has an intent.
3. The beat states its outcome, and the consequence for each body with its duration and the mark it leaves.
4. It states the change in where everyone stands among the set's zones and landmarks. Exception: stillness the scene decides.
5. It states the camera's size, angle and movement.
6. It states all five sound layers: dialogue, foley, ambience, effects and music with its usage. A layer with nothing in it says `none`.
7. It states the continuity state it leaves.
8. The beats cover the whole running time with the continuous response of everyone present. No stretch is summarized, as in "they dance".

What the modules can perform:

9. Each action is one the installed human motion vocabulary performs, or the one its cited research law describes.
10. Each outcome is one the cited outcome table permits for the written intents and the performers' settings. An outcome the table forbids, or one no table covers, is undecided, not dramatic license.
11. The beat asks nothing the adopted representation cannot show, such as a facial expression of a faceless figure.

Measurements:

12. Every measurement is left to the research laws that determine it and the code that derives it: no coordinate, offset or time between exchanges is written. Exception: a true anchor, namely a line, lyric or music event the picture syncs to, a count the audience perceives, or a key event the edit meets.
13. No decision is expressed only as a number, because the number leaves its meaning undecided.

Breakdown and addresses:

14. The scene's breakdown names by settings or brief link every cast member, crowd, prop, set, wardrobe and makeup state, effect, sound, camera and lighting element its beats use, and nothing they do not.
15. A scene or beat removed after others cite it keeps its address as `OMITTED`, and no address is reused or renumbered.
