# Narrative population obligations

Each completed treatment, script and final screenplay population answers once per question across all units and boundaries. Major treatment spans follow supplied closing turns; scripts and screenplay use their actual partition.

Statements: [statements/obligations/narratives.md](../statements/obligations/narratives.md).

## Are progression, state, and long pursuits continuous? {#continuity-and-progression}

Followed across every unit and boundary, do events, states and pursuits continue or change for a reason? Local passes cannot show an unsupported reversal or a pursuit that silently stops.

## Are promises, rhythm, and variation realized across the whole? {#promises-and-distribution}

Does the whole keep its promises, and does its treatment of time, speech and recurrence follow each span's pressure? Uniform speed, mechanical alternation, habitual aphorisms, uniform voices and functionless recurrence can fail despite local passes.
