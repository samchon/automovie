# Brief discovery statements

The detailed statements of the [brief requirement discovery](../../discovery/brief.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Instructions and backcast {#instructions-and-backcast}

1. Every instruction and adopted promise is broken into its consequential elements: premises, people and names, the delivery conditions and representation limits, the audience's capacity for names and information, asset constraints, source boundaries and sensitive subjects.
2. Each element has an existing owner or a needed decision. Instruction conflicts are reported, and neither side is silently overwritten.
3. Premises are checked against sources only under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) or where the work claims an external fact.
4. The planned films were read as a consumer, and the rule, relationship, knowledge, place, appearance or behavior they would otherwise invent was backcast. Events, action and reveal order stay with the screenplay.
5. An honest negative names the concrete planned risk and its adequate existing owner.
