# Brief discovery statements

Contract: [discovery/brief.md](../../discovery/brief.md).

## Instructions and backcast

1. Every instruction is broken into its consequential elements:
    - premises;
    - people and names;
    - the delivery conditions and representation limits;
    - the audience's capacity for names and information;
    - asset constraints;
    - source boundaries;
    - sensitive subjects.
2. Each element has an existing owner or a needed decision. Where two instructions conflict, the conflict is reported, and neither instruction silently overrides the other.
3. A premise is checked against a source only where a [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) is declared or the films claim an external fact.
4. The planned films were read as a consumer, and what several scenes would otherwise invent about people, places, recurring props and a commissioned generated film's look was traced back to the brief decision that must supply it.
5. Events, action and reveal order stay with the screenplay.
6. Where nothing is missing, the answer names the planned risk it examined and the brief record that already covers it.
