# Script expression principles

Each script construction H2 answers for the clarity and rhythm of its executable scene, in the same pass as its content. Compare the inherited storyline with its treatments.

## Execution rhythm

Do blocks, speech, directions, silence, interruptions and simultaneous actions make the scene playable at its required pace and pressure? Preserve necessary execution language.

- Look for uniform beats, a gesture or delivery label on every line, decisive dialogue buried in explanation, summary in place of an exchange, and directions that obscure a response. Judge urgency, comedy, weight and stillness at the pace the scene needs.
- Preserve playable staging, sound, viewing position, inherited facts and reveal order. Replace interpretation with an action the existing scene can perform. Apply [the classification and exceptions](common.md#reads-as-written).
