# Common authoring principles

Each authored H2 answers these questions from its own body and identified parents. Population coverage belongs to obligations, not to an individual H2.

Statements: [statements/principles/common.md](../statements/principles/common.md).

## Does this unit fulfill its assigned scope? {#scope-preservation}

Does the body deliver the facts, constraints, events, relationships, interval, or result promised by its title, instructions, and lineage? A heading, placeholder, research list, or promise that another author will decide the essential content leaves the scope unfilled.

## Do its choices come from this work? {#specificity}

Do the chosen facts, decisions, actions, images, and details follow this work's circumstances rather than familiar labels or habitual gestures? Specificity means useful distinctions, not more detail.
