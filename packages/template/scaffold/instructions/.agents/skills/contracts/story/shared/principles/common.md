# Common authoring principles

Each authored H2 answers these questions from its own body and identified parents. Population coverage belongs to obligations, not to an individual H2.

Statements: [statements/principles/common.md](../statements/principles/common.md).

## Scope preservation

Does the body deliver the facts, constraints, events, relationships, interval or result its title, instructions and lineage promise, chosen from this work's circumstances rather than familiar labels or habitual gestures? A heading, a placeholder, a research list or a promise that another author will decide the essential content leaves the scope unfilled; specificity means useful distinctions, not more detail.
