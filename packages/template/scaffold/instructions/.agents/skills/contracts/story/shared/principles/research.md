# Research principles

Each study in `docs/research/` other than the index answers these items under an H1 naming its path. Keep originals in `references/` and index them. Its consumers cite the study.

Statements: [statements/principles/research.md](../statements/principles/research.md).

## Depicted practice

Can an author use the study to write or program the practice at the depicted level? Include its laws, limits, sourced values and any required action density and distribution. Record deliberate stylization.

## Traceable sources

Does each observation identify its retained source, locator and observation type so a reader can check it?
