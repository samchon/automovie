# Settings index population obligations

The complete index answers once per question against all sections and actual settings files; individual sections cannot establish whole-index coverage. Check each item's [statements](../statements/obligations/index.md) before answering it.

## Does the index give every subject one clear reference home? {#canonical-navigation}

Can a reader predict the next location from each sibling level's stated organizing axis, distinguish group purposes and reach every actual owner? A subject scattered without owner routes is rebuilt differently by every reader.

## Does its order and size support finding rather than rereading? {#reference-order}

Can a reader find and understand the needed fact without guessing owners, traversing unexplained levels or reassembling copied fragments?
