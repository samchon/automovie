## Whole event design

Information:

1. The answer maps what the audience knows, does not know, is led to believe and waits to learn throughout the sequence.
2. Consequential reveals, reversals and decisive uses of powers or objects have earlier establishment.
3. Every setup or promise has a payoff, a transformation or an owned unresolved ending.
4. Unsupported payoffs and abandoned setups are reported in both directions.
5. The answer locates the questions that open and resolve, how long they stay open, the oldest outstanding ones and any deferral of all satisfaction. Counts locate the burden; reading judges it.

Entry, middle and ending:

6. The first event and perception are decided, introducing people, relationships, time, space and pressure as current choices need them.
7. The middle accumulates costs, narrowed possibilities, changed relationships or expectations and does not reset.
8. Parallel progressions have consequential order, intersections or contrasts, not alternating summaries.
9. Events convey emotional movement and tone instead of leaving a mood for scripts to invent, and opposing agents act from their own priorities where present.
10. The ending follows established choices and costs, shows what closes and what remains open with its aftermath or changed state, and decides the actual last delivered event and stopping reason.
11. Chance may begin a crisis, while outside resolution needs an owned work premise. Exception: an open ending needs grounds, not total explanation or a compulsory epilogue.

## Material and handoff

1. A script author can understand the essential events, motives, choices, relationship turns, information releases, costs, results and inherited state.
2. Necessary canonical facts are passed in usable context, and full dialogue, precise blocking and release partition are left to scripts.
3. Ordered event and process material maps to the declared delivery scale, with substantial turns that can close major units. Scripts choose the actual boundaries.
4. The answer reports unsupported promised intervals, repetition posed as development and core progression left for the next author. Exception: a concise treatment can establish extensive action.
5. No prescription consumes script execution choices.
6. Event material is repaired at its earliest treatment owner, or an unsupported promise returned to its authorized settings owner. Downstream inflation supplies no missing decision.
