## Audiovisual voice {#audiovisual-voice}

1. Preserved meaning and useful function are judged separately from the experience the actual expression makes.
2. No habitual opening, explanation, montage pattern, musical emphasis, reaction pause or closing image is imposed on different material.
3. No frantic moment is buried in duration, no weight is lost to a uniform short beat, and no grouping conceals the movement of attention.
4. Speaker, actor, spatial and causal orientation were rechecked after rewriting. A formerly approved construction does not establish that shortened expression still conveys them.
5. These failures are examples, not the boundary of the question: any finding is established with the actual passage, the neighboring progression and a concrete audience loss within the adopted form.
6. Long duration, flat delivery, a fixed view, recurrence, metaphor, direct statement, captions and summary may work when their effect is realized. The actual effect is protected, not the form's name or a paraphrase of intended meaning.
7. The sequence conveys the present pressure. Listing each image's or sentence's function excuses no ineffective expression or delayed continuous experience, and forms are not varied merely for variety.

## Upstream transposition {#upstream-transposition}

1. The unit holds no copied planning label, source-status or research commentary inserted into the scene, explanatory category name nobody in the world would use, or shot list with no finished audiovisual relation.
2. Repeated introductions, redundant delivery labels and authoring jargon are judged under [common expression](common.md#reads-as-written), not here.
3. Genuine world names, titles and terms, useful time transitions, adopted documentary quotations, captions, and narration that realizes the adopted voice and audience access are preserved.
4. Named failures follow the decision and exception rules of [common expression](common.md#reads-as-written), and no event or canonical value is changed to escape an awkward register.
