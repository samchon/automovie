## Screenplay upstream repair

1. The exact earliest owner of each defective inherited decision is identified, including a design that forces a defined downstream failure or blocks an adopted goal or promise.
2. A script defect is traced to its treatment or settings cause, and direct settings evidence replaces no absent script decision.
3. Legitimate constraints and source-status records are told apart from prescriptions that leave no audiovisual realization to create.
4. Each defect was corrected in its earliest owner's body before the screenplay was written from it, and the answer names that owner, the defect and the corrected passage.
5. Where no repair was needed, the answer identifies concrete parent decisions tested unchanged and how they survived realization.
6. A short tests its brief and its screenplay-owned decisions without inventing skipped parents, and moves into the brief a decision another film or scene must keep when it is of a kind the [brief holds](../../../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds).
7. Performance and output verification still to come is named as pending, because this stage establishes a realizable plan, not a film.
