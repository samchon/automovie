# Final screenplay expression revision principles

Each revised final screenplay H2 answers for the finished image, sound, performance, attention, and temporal experience specified by that unit.

Statements: [statements/naturalness/screenplay.md](../statements/naturalness/screenplay.md).

## Do audiovisual voice and rhythm belong to this moment? {#audiovisual-voice}

Do the chosen image and sound, distance and access, duration, interruption, transition, and arrangement of performance carry this scene's pressure within the work's adopted voice? Understandable information can still make the audience decode a forced image, spoken construction or image-sound relation instead of experiencing the moment.

## Does the final medium transform the upstream register? {#upstream-transposition}

Does the plan convey inherited content as the audience's image, sound, action and permitted narration rather than import the author's management language? Useful camera, sound, edit and performance instructions remain necessary production notation; the failure is their substitution for an audience experience, not their presence.
