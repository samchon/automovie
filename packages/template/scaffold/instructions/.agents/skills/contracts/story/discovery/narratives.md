# Narrative requirement discovery

The discovery result covers the complete current treatment, script or final screenplay population under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership), not every H2 independently.

## Narrative requirements

Did discovery assign each recurring narrative requirement to the smallest population that can answer for it? Use a whole-population obligation only when it applies to the whole population.

Look for recurring conditions of authenticity, representation, agency, causality, form and audience experience. For each uncovered condition, name its audience effect and a representative failure under [common discovery](common.md#requirement-ownership).
