# Settings principles

Each settings H2 answers as one facet of its subject, owning the anchored H3 facts beneath it. It does not answer for every person or place in the work.

## Does the record settle a decision the planned scenes need? {#usable-facts}

Does it name its subject and settle a value, scope and the conditions, limits, costs or exceptions that affect use, specifically enough that a later author or reviewer can hold the work to it? "A nervous partner" binds no scene; "hesitates before every jump, and the hesitation shows in his shoulders" does. Settings record decisions: the world and its rules, people's personality, values, motives, abilities and knowledge, relationships, the identity and use of places and objects, and the tone and look. A bounded unknown may stay unknown when its established effects and limits constrain later solutions; a consequential open decision states its permitted range and decision condition rather than pose as mystery.

Write only what the planned scenes and arcs need. Filling a facet nothing uses, inventorying incidental defaults or writing "not applicable" for an absent one fails, because it buries the decisions in material nobody can be held to. A measurement such as a dimension, light position or timing belongs here only when it is itself a story decision; otherwise its source goes to [research](../../../../story/planning/research.md) and its value to the code and tests that realize it. A practiced activity records its level and style decisions and cites the study that holds its vocabulary.

Keep adopted invention distinct from any fact claimed as true. An external fact the record asserts, such as a sport's rule, cites its source; under a declared [factual basis](../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), each covered claim needs direct primary or authoritative evidence at its edition, passage, date or page, with meaningful disagreement, and reduced precision where the evidence is weaker. Invented choices need no outside authority.

## Is the record findable and owned at the right layer? {#record-and-boundary}

Can the next author find the definition, settled content, conditions and open matters in a consistent order for this kind of record? Each independently used subject has one file with its inseparable attributes together; use one term per concept and one owner per fact, linking shared facts to their owner. The record states facts in prose for readers and never describes code; rendering and generation controls belong to the production's source.

Does the record establish world facts, baseline states and change conditions while leaving the chosen events, causal progression, choices, results, setups, payoffs and audience reveal order to treatments on the long route, or to screenplay on the short route? A fixed historical result or time-specific status may constrain treatments without prewriting how the film reaches or reveals it. A lasting state a later layer established, such as a wound or a revealed relationship, belongs here once established, citing the unit that established it. The cinematography domain owns the film's look; editing and audience access belong to production instructions or their work contracts, while a character's actual voice remains a world property.
