# Settings principles

Each settings H2 answers as one facet of its subject, owning the anchored H3 facts beneath it. It does not answer for every person or place in the work. Check each item's [statements](../statements/principles/settings.md) before answering it.

## Does the record settle a decision the planned scenes need? {#usable-facts}

Does it name its subject and settle a value, scope and the conditions, limits, costs or exceptions that affect use, specifically enough that a later author or reviewer can hold the work to it? "A nervous partner" binds no scene; "hesitates before every jump, and the hesitation shows in his shoulders" does. A record filled with facets nothing uses buries its decisions in material nobody can be held to.

## Is the record findable and owned at the right layer? {#record-and-boundary}

Can the next author find what the record settles, and does it hold world facts while leaving events to treatments? A fact with two owners, or an event prewritten here, gives later layers two versions to choose between.
