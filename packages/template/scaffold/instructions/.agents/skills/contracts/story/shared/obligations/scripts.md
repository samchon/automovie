# Script population obligations

The completed script population answers once per question for its partition, titles, material allocation and executable handoff. Check each item's [statements](../statements/obligations/scripts.md) before answering it.

## Does the chosen partition give the audience meaningful units? {#partition-and-titles}

Do the boundaries fall where the form delivers change and an earned reason to continue or stop, and does each title gain meaning in its unit? A scene quota or an unfinished exchange cut cannot establish a boundary.

## Does every assigned event become a complete executable handoff? {#complete-execution}

Can a filmmaker stage every assigned event from the scripts without inventing it? Missing decisions and decisions buried under directions both make a handoff unusable.
