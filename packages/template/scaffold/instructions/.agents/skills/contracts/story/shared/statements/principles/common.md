# Common authoring statements

Contract: [principles/common.md](../../principles/common.md).

## Scope preservation {#scope-preservation}

1. Every element the unit's title, instructions and lineage promise is present in the body.
2. A decision deliberately left to the next layer is told apart from an unfinished decision owned here.
3. No answer about the whole population stands in for this unit's missing content.
4. The author's process vocabulary, such as checking, reporting and judging, has not become the world's institutions, objects or character interests. Exception: a creative reason this work owns.
5. A work about records still realizes events and relationships, not its management procedure.
6. Settings, a brief and treatments hold specific decisions, not literary decoration.
