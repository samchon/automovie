# Common requirement discovery

The discovery result answers for the entire inspected layer population. Authors write it where the [document layout](../../../../story/document-layout.md#documents) puts it, from actual findings and inspected negatives; the scaffold supplies no completed negative.

Statements: [statements/discovery/common.md](../statements/discovery/common.md).

## Requirement ownership

Was everything the work must keep, in its instructions, its language and its production, found and given one owner that can be held to it? A requirement left only in guide prose, selectors, status notes or repeated explanations binds nobody, and a selector or a discovery answer cannot supply a missing creative decision.
