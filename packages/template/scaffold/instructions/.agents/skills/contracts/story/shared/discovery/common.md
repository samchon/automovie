# Common requirement discovery

The discovery result answers for the entire inspected layer population. Authors write it where the [document layout](../../../../story/layout.md#documents) puts it, from actual findings and inspected negatives; the scaffold supplies no completed negative.

## Does every requirement the work needs live at one canonical owner? {#requirement-ownership}

Inspect instructions, promises, subjects, sources, assets, genre, form and the current body for conditions no active shared, language, work or settings requirement owns. A finding changes what may honestly be created or omitted, has an observable application and failure, and names the smallest respondent population that can establish it; aspirations, genre labels and already owned conditions need no duplicate authority. For an adopted form, state its observable choices, scope, audience effect, representative failure, semantic owner and repair boundary. Language baselines belong to their language owner, creative form to the work, world facts to settings, and a possibly universal candidate remains research until adopted into shared contracts. A requirement left only in guide prose, selectors, status notes or repeated explanations moves to its owner with its consumers updated, under the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes); a selector or discovery answer cannot supply a missing creative decision.

Inspect language separately: regional, period, social, medium, translation, register, address and honorific boundaries; names and titles; and number, date, age, color, material, dimension and unit wording. Identify affected speakers and passages, audience cues and canonical owners, and record the concrete language risks existing owners cover. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), check period usage against the cited record or identify the adopted departure. Notation serves the actual audience and medium rather than artificial period weight.

Inspect the settings, research, screenplay, source and, after delivery review, the acceptance record for an element kind no source folder owns, a behavior, camera or staging choice no research supports, a failure no contract asks about, and a skill, folder or instruction the work needed but lacked. Answer each by adopting a work-specific contract or folder, by recording a scaffold candidate with its motivating evidence, which stays local until other productions show the same need, or by recording its absence with the inspected scope. Was this inspection repeated at each [discovery checkpoint](../../../../story/planning/discovery.md#checkpoints) reached so far? An unchecked checkpoint stays open.

Account for findings, already covered choices and the remaining inspected scope together. A negative names the concrete risks inspected and their adequate existing owners; one finding cannot establish absence elsewhere, and unread or unresolved scope stays open.
