# Review population obligations

One current verdict document answers for the complete declared review surface, identifying its creative reading. Unit notes do not substitute for this population record.

## Complete current round

Did the round cover the scope its review requires and locate every member of each finding class in this revision?

Identify the revision, pass, layer, files, H2s, relationships, descendants and observations actually reviewed. Compare this inventory and its measurements with the reading required by [narrative rounds](../../../review/narrative-rounds.md). For material carried over, identify the source, reader and grounds in the current tree. State where coverage was partial or unavailable and respect [closed passes](../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).

Where [classification](../../../story/correction/inspection.md#changed-work) ran, stable sentences can retain their classifications. They still need the applicable whole reading and unit verdict.

Trace each class of findings to the units and reproducible search that support it. Identify missing members or affected relationships. Where relevant, check completeness and navigation across settings. Test a claim about silence, refrain or another recurring form against its function in the passages. Repeated syntax, lengths and annotation ratios can identify where to read.

## Round accountability

Does the verdict record the inspected scope, findings, repairs and open items for the next round or delivery?

Identify the source revision, review kind, achievements and functions to preserve. Group findings by cause and contributing factors, including author decisions, reasons, accepted repairs and outstanding decisions.

Distinguish surplus removed below a duration target from missing event material. Trace accepted repairs through affected descendants and the required [route](../../../story/workflow/SKILL.md). State whether the pass is open or clean and which host or output claims remain unverified.
