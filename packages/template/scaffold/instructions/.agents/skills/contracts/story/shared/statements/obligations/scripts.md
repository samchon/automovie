# Script population statements

The detailed statements of the [script population obligations](../../obligations/scripts.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Partition and titles {#partition-and-titles}

1. Act, part, sequence, episode or season boundaries sit where the actual form delivers change and an earned reason to continue or stop. Units need not share the same crisis.
2. After a release interval, action, goals, address, objects or other retrieval cues restore needed state without a mandatory recap.
3. Where purchase boundaries are declared, the prior material has delivered the promised experience and reward.
4. Each audience-facing title is compared with its unit's content and prior viewing: the promised event, object, conflict, image or question gains meaning there. Absent promises and interchangeable titles are reported.
5. Exception: no plot summary or spoiler is demanded, numbers, ambiguous or poetic titles, part-numbered repetition and refrains may work, and user-fixed wording is preserved.
6. Semantic mismatches are repaired at scripts, which own the titles and identities descendants inherit.

## Complete execution {#complete-execution}

1. Treatments are mapped to implementing scripts and scripts to actual parents, many-to-many where needed, exposing missing, orphaned and duplicated progression.
2. Assigned execution material is compared with the declared scale, so later authors need not invent essential progression, repeat or pad. Text-length ratios do not establish sufficiency; the boundary, event assignment or delivery promise that causes a gap is repaired.
3. Every H2 reads as an independent initial execution script: a filmmaker can stage essential action, process, exchange, transition, consequence, appearance, blocking, light, in-scene sound, atmosphere, viewing position and meaning-changing delivery without inventing them.
4. No treatment summary, elapsed-action report, dialogue buried in explanation or result without intervening execution remains, even where later specialists could rescue it.
5. Composition, lens values and incidental acting detail stay open only where they replace no script-owned decision.
