# Review unit principles

Each review H2 answers for the authored H2 or precise relationship it inspected, identifying a creative reading or evidence audit. These questions assess the review record, not the creative quality of the work being reviewed.

Statements: [statements/principles/review.md](../statements/principles/review.md).

## Is its judgment grounded in an independent reading? {#independent-reading}

Before parents, contracts, acknowledgments or earlier verdicts, did a fresh context read the annotation-free current body as its recipient and lock what it found? A judgment formed after the author's records reads the work as intended, not as received.

## Were related failures and claimed relationships fully audited? {#literal-and-class-audit}

Did the review find every member of each finding class, and compare every answer with its full requirement and host? A finding repaired alone leaves the rest of its class, and a matching quotation can stand beside a false relationship.
