# Review unit principles

Each review H2 answers for the authored H2 or precise relationship it inspected, identifying a creative reading or evidence audit. These questions assess the review record, not the creative quality of the work being reviewed.

## Is its judgment grounded in an independent reading? {#independent-reading}

Before parents, contracts, acknowledgments or earlier verdicts, did a fresh context read the annotation-free current body as its recipient and lock an observation with at least one exact body quotation and locator? Keep this evidence for body-clean units too. Headings, annotations, another unit, remembered wording and quotations selected after a verdict cannot establish that reading. In an evidence audit, identify the completed creative reading instead of claiming another blind read.

Does every finding identify its passage, violated obligation and failure condition, recipient loss, and earliest repair owner? Resolve a factual objection with the actual quotation, definition, permitted exception and parent or cited record. Upstream knowledge cannot fill a gap experienced by the recipient; author confidence, repair cost, previous verdicts and taste cannot dismiss that loss. Were named failures classified under the [selected decision rule](../naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written) before author defenses? The independent classifier resolves ambiguity in context. Protect a named failure only under a proven contractual exception; other candidates use the [retention test](../../../../story/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate).

Record fragile achievements with quotations and functions, or explicitly state that none were identified. Protect their function rather than freezing a defective sentence; a named failure without a proven contractual exception is not a protected achievement. Give repair directions rather than prose for adoption; counterfactual sentences belong only to the retention comparison. Review narrative and expressive quality separately from the truth of evidence answers.

Judge the applicable question's defined scope: examples may permit another proven loss, but a named-failure boundary cannot be expanded by reviewer taste. A revised passage requires a clear applicable failure before another yes; unchanged classifications require new body or source evidence to reopen, under [inspection](../../../../story/correction/inspection.md#verify-changed-work).

## Were related failures and claimed relationships fully audited? {#literal-and-class-audit}

For each finding class, record the inspected scope, reproducible search or comparison, every discovered member and affected relation, and the earliest owner; reference other review units where they inspected a member. A genuinely isolated finding needs its reason. Quotations and locators must match the inspected revision.

During the evidence audit after creative correction, compare each acknowledgment with the full target requirement and complete host support: host kind, family, semantic role, lineage, predicate and actual realization. A claim about checking itself, repeated vocabulary, another event or partial support cannot prove the relationship. An exclusion needs its complete defined negative scope and inspection method. A creative reading records that the literal audit is pending rather than claiming to perform it.

Did the [inspection record](../../../../story/correction/inspection.md) cover every required sentence and parent comparison at the actual snapshot, before author evidence, and recheck all changed dependencies without silently reversing unchanged tests?

Was the body compared with its parents and requirements independently of these relationship rows, following the [independent reading](../../../../review/reading.md)? Compare every decision with its inherited parent. Matching a quotation or completing a checklist cannot replace that independent comparison.

During that audit, compare nearby relationships by exchanging their names, predicates and conditions: support and refutation must remain specific to the actual relation. Similar wording prompts inspection rather than automatic failure. Repair a false edge at its owner; a pasted answer cannot require changing the story. Structural validity belongs to the evidence checker and never substitutes for semantic truth.
