# Realized speech expression principles

Each script construction H2 answers from actual exchanges with their neighboring action. A settings voice description or treatment plan cannot answer for speech realization.

Statements: [statements/naturalness/realized.md](../statements/naturalness/realized.md).

## Speech yield

Followed in order with its surrounding action, does each exchange's language serve the listener, the situation and the immediate tactic, and does the exchange leave an actual recognition, refusal, changed position, broken silence, or other inherited result? An exchange that only passes information leaves the scene where it began. The fit of words to speaker is judged under [speaker plausibility](../principles/realized.md#speaker-plausibility), not here.
