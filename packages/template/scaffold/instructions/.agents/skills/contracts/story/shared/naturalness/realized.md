# Realized exchange expression principles

Each script construction H2 answers from actual exchanges with their neighboring action. A settings voice description or treatment plan cannot answer for speech realization.

Statements: [statements/naturalness/realized.md](../statements/naturalness/realized.md).

## Does a person say this to this person here? {#speech-yield}

Follow each exchange in order with its surrounding action. With the fit of words to speaker judged under [speaker plausibility](../principles/realized.md#speaker-plausibility), does its language serve the listener, the situation and the immediate tactic, and does the exchange leave an actual recognition, refusal, changed position, broken silence, or other inherited result? An exchange that only passes information leaves the scene where it began.
