## Voice distribution

Compared across the whole, does each recurring speaker keep a voice of their own as relationships and situations change? Plausible lines in each scene do not establish distinct voices across a work.

Compare recurring speakers across situations and relationships for vocabulary, address, register, breath, pace, length, interruptions, avoidance, silence, argument shapes and openings or endings. Locate shared explanatory tactics, metaphors, courtesy or hostility that erase a person's choices. Record all cases and test a retained habit or ritual under [common expression obligations](common.md#recurrent-frames). Preserve exchange and outcome. A missing distinction goes to its owner.
