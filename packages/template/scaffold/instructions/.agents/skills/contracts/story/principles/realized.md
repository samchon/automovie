# Realized speech principles

Each script and final screenplay H2 answers from its speech and neighboring action. Treatment plans and settings voice descriptions do not establish the quality of an executed exchange.

## Speaker plausibility

Does each consequential line fit the speaker, listener and situation? Can the audience distinguish the speakers and tell how each action relates to the line?

Read each exchange for the speaker's age, situation, status, knowledge, aim and relationship. Vocabulary, address, register, rhythm, regional usage, avoidance and silence can distinguish speakers who sometimes share an ordinary reply. Preserve the purpose and outcome in the adopted form. Ground social signals and world-specific jargon in this speaker and situation.

Place cues, action and delivery where they clarify what prompted the line or followed it. Let the exchange demonstrate the voice.
