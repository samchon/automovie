# Script principles

Each script H2 answers for an executable scene or inseparable audiovisual progression within the script's chosen partition. Scripts already contain full action, actual dialogue, staging, and a viewing position.

Statements: [statements/principles/scripts.md](../statements/principles/scripts.md).

## Can the scene be performed in its written order? {#executable-scene}

Can the scene be performed as written, from an executable opening through action, exchange and reaction to an observable exit state? A reported exchange or a result without its execution leaves the next author to invent the scene, and redundant explanation hides the decisive operation.

## Can the established scene be seen and heard consistently? {#audiovisual-staging}

Does the scene establish where it is, who is where, what the view shows and what is audible, and keep them consistent? Missing decisions make the next recipient invent the scene, and excessive incidental directions bury the decisions needed to perform it.
