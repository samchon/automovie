# Script principles

Each script H2 answers for an executable scene of one continuous place and time within the script's chosen partition. Scripts already contain full action, actual dialogue, staging, and a viewing position.

Statements: [statements/principles/scripts.md](../statements/principles/scripts.md).

## Executable scene

Can the scene be performed in its written order, from an executable opening through action, exchange and reaction to an observable exit state, with where it is, who is where, what the view shows and what is audible established and kept consistent? A reported exchange, a result without its execution or a missing staging decision leaves the next author to invent the scene, and redundant explanation or incidental directions bury the decisive operation.
