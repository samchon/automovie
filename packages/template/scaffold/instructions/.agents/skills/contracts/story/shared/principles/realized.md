# Realized speech principles

Each script and final screenplay H2 answers from its actual speech and neighboring action. Treatment plans and settings voice descriptions do not establish the quality of an executed exchange.

Statements: [statements/principles/realized.md](../statements/principles/realized.md).

## Do these words belong to these speakers? {#speaker-plausibility}

Does each consequential line fit who says it, to whom and in what situation, and do the speakers differ from one another? A speaker label cannot make an implausible expression plausible.

## Is the relation between action and speech legible? {#action-and-line-order}

Does adjacency communicate whether an action prepares, interrupts, contradicts, completes or follows a line, including simultaneous action and meaningful uncertainty? Written order alone must not imply a decision the scene did not make.
