# Realized speech principles

Each script and final screenplay H2 answers from its actual speech and neighboring action. Treatment plans and settings voice descriptions do not establish the quality of an executed exchange.

Statements: [statements/principles/realized.md](../statements/principles/realized.md).

## Speaker plausibility

Does each consequential line fit who says it, to whom and in what situation, do the speakers differ from one another, and does adjacency show whether an action prepares, interrupts, contradicts, completes or follows a line? A speaker label cannot make an implausible expression plausible, and written order alone must not imply a decision the scene did not make.
