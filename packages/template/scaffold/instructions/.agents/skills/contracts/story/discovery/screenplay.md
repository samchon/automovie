# Final screenplay requirement discovery

The discovery result covers the complete declared audiovisual plan under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership).

## Screenplay requirements

Does the work state testable audiovisual choices? Specify what the audience will see or hear.

Identify work-specific choices in audience access, image and sound, performance, dialogue, rhythm, duration, compression, motifs, transitions, exits, captions, notation and register. State the effect and an observable failure. Adopting a form and realizing it successfully are separate judgments.
