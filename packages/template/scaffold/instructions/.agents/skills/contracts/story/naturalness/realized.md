# Realized speech expression principles

Each script construction H2 answers from its exchanges with their neighboring action. A settings voice description or a treatment plan cannot answer for speech realization.

## Speech yield

In context, does each exchange's wording serve its listener, situation and tactic while delivering the inherited result? Judge voice under [speaker plausibility](../principles/realized.md#speaker-plausibility).

- Check for facts shared speakers would not explain, arguments recited as lists, theme declarations, action repeated in other words, matching metaphors, misplaced status language, neutral exposition and replies whose meaning drifted in editing.
- Let address, honorifics, distance and sentence endings reflect the relationship and the act. Preserve what each speaker wants, knows and says. A marked habit needs a function and consequence. Apply [the named exceptions](common.md#reads-as-written) without prescribing short lines, interruptions, subtext or new conflict.
