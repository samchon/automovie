# Treatment statements

## Decided event {#decided-event}

1. The body settles what happens, who or what participates, why, the alternatives and the consequential choice or process, its costs and result, and the state the next event inherits.
2. It establishes the time, place, exchange or clue when that changes causality.
3. It inherits the meaning of settings and instructions as well as their values, and replaces no inconvenient premise.
4. Goals, beliefs, judgment, institutional roles, knowledge and operating rules are not contradicted while their numbers stay unchanged.
5. Each promised experience is assigned to the event or adopted narrator contribution that carries it, at the scope the instructions require.
6. Tone, humor, perspective and character facets follow that promise, not a compulsory pattern in every unit.
7. Each setup has its intended payoff within the commissioned scope. Exception: an intentionally open promise with its adopted purpose and handoff.
8. Where evidence prevents a payoff, a supported realization is chosen or the setup revised.
9. Recording existing knowledge or state in several H2s is continuity.
10. Reenacting the same recognition has a changed consequence, pressure or meaning.
11. Every repetition develops the event or serves an adopted formal relation.
12. New world facts return to their settings owner.
13. Exact speech, detailed blocking and audiovisual execution are left to scripts. Exception: a specific decision that determines the cause or result.
14. Concise prose, lists and tables may fully decide an event without performing it.

## Information design {#information-design}

1. The people, relationships, time, space and pressure needed for the current choice are placed.
2. A later reveal grows from earlier facts without invalidating them.
3. One event decision has one owner, ordered events stay in order, and reasons stay connected to their choices.
4. No scattered facts, repeated decisions or uniform paragraph template make the handoff ambiguous.
