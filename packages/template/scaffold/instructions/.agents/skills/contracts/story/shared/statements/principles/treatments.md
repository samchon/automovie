# Treatment statements

## Decided event

1. The body settles what happens, who or what participates, why, the alternatives and the consequential choice or process, its costs and result, and the state the next event inherits.
2. It establishes the time, place, exchange or clue where that changes causality.
3. It inherits the meaning of settings and instructions, not only their values: it replaces no inconvenient premise and contradicts no goal, belief, judgment, institutional role, knowledge or operating rule while keeping its numbers.
4. Each promised experience is assigned to the event or adopted narrator contribution that carries it, at the scope the instructions require.
5. Let the promise govern tone, humor, perspective and which character facets recur.
6. Each setup has its intended payoff within the commissioned scope, or, where evidence prevents that payoff, a supported realization or a revised setup. Exception: an intentionally open promise with its adopted purpose and handoff.
7. Every repetition, including a reenacted recognition, develops the event with a changed consequence, pressure or meaning, or serves an adopted formal relation. Exception: recording existing knowledge or state in several H2s is continuity.
8. New world facts return to their settings owner.
9. Exact speech, detailed blocking and audiovisual execution are left to scripts. Exception: a specific decision that determines the cause or result.
10. Concise prose, lists and tables may decide an event fully without performing it.

## Information design

1. The people, relationships, time, space and pressure needed for the current choice are placed.
2. A later reveal grows from earlier facts without invalidating them.
3. Each event decision has one owner, ordered events stay in order and reasons stay with their choices, and no scattered facts or uniform paragraph template make the handoff ambiguous.
