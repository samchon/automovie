## Instructions and backcast

Before bulk brief writing, was each instruction taken apart into what it actually requires, with an owner for every part, and were the planned films read for the decisions about people, places, recurring props and a generated film's look that several scenes would otherwise make up? A requirement left whole is easy to believe kept and hard to check.
