# Brief requirement discovery

The discovery result covers all instructions, adopted promises, references, assets and planned films under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership).

## Were instructions and planned films translated into needed owners and a usable form? {#instructions-and-backcast}

Before bulk brief writing, break every instruction and adopted promise into its consequential elements: premises, people and names, the delivery conditions and representation limits, the audience's capacity for names and information, asset constraints, source boundaries and sensitive subjects. Identify each existing owner or needed decision, and report instruction conflicts rather than silently overwrite either. Check premises against sources only under a declared [factual basis](../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) or where the work claims an external fact.

Read the planned films as a consumer and backcast the rule, relationship, knowledge, place, appearance or behavior they would otherwise invent, leaving events, action and reveal order to the screenplay. An honest negative names the concrete planned risk and its adequate existing owner.
