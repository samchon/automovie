# Treatment principles

Each treatment H2 answers for its event or inseparable progression. Treatments set the event order before scripts choose acts, sequences or episode boundaries.

Statements: [statements/principles/treatments.md](../statements/principles/treatments.md).

## Decided event

Does the body decide who does what, why, at what cost and with what result? Give scripts the resulting state and room to decide execution.

## Information design

Can the next author follow the event's causes, what the audience already knows, what changes here and what remains withheld?
