# Speech expression population statements

Contract: [naturalness/obligations/realized.md](../../../naturalness/obligations/realized.md).

## Voice distribution {#voice-distribution}

1. Each recurring speaker's speech is compared against their changing relationships and situations:
    - vocabulary, address and register;
    - pace, breath and sentence lengths;
    - interruptions, avoidance and silence;
    - argument shape, and openings and endings.
2. The answer finds cross-speaker convergence, repeated explanatory exchanges, common metaphors, uniform courtesy or hostility, and repetitive response tactics that replace a character's choices.
3. A person's deliberate habit or an adopted ritual is preserved through its actual function, and no speaker is assigned a gimmick to differ.
4. All found members are recorded, and retained forms are tested as [common expression obligations](common.md#recurrent-frames) require.
5. The inherited exchange and outcome are preserved. A missing character distinction returns to its owner and prompts no new conflict or fact.
