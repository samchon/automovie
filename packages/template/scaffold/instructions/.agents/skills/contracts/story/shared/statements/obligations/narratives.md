# Narrative population statements

The detailed statements of the [narrative population obligations](../../obligations/narratives.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Continuity and progression {#continuity-and-progression}

1. Every unit is classified by its actual event, understanding, relationship, emotion, pressure, rhythm or formal contribution. Functionless duplication and independently addressable progressions hidden together are identified. Exception: inseparable montage, continuous action, parallel and other owned forms are preserved.
2. Exits and entries connect through their causal or declared formal relation. The order of movable units is tested for what it contributes, without imposing linearity on episodic, nonlinear or nonhuman work.
3. Inherited time, place, knowledge, injury, appearance, clothing, possession, resources, status, relationships and promises are compared across units.
4. Recurring agents' drives, beliefs, interpretations, experiences and actions are followed across units. Unsupported reversals and thresholds crossed without pressure or cost are reported with the earliest contradictory owner and any contradiction derived across facts.
5. An unchanged or unreached threshold has its owned scope or purpose.
6. Where a protagonist's pursuit holds the work together, the answer identifies that desire, its first audience recognition, the actions it drives and how major-unit goals continue or transform it. Exception: an ensemble may sustain competing pursuits and another form another center; local tasks alone do not establish the whole, and no shared goal is invented.

## Promises and distribution {#promises-and-distribution}

Promises:

1. Read without prior settings knowledge, the opening gives a reason to continue and timely understanding of people, relationships, pressure, time and space.
2. The answer traces actual introductions and returns, the declared engine and rewards, every payoff's setup, risk or cost, and the longest gaps without promised satisfaction. It reports spans where the engine fails, rewards are unearned or worn out, or an eventual return cannot support the delay.

Time and variation:

3. Scene, compression, pause, omission, simultaneity, habitual time, flashback, anticipation and retelling are compared against each span's pressure. For each departure from ordinary once-told forward sequence, the answer shows what removing it loses.
4. It compares openings and endings, speech frequency and length, who explains, evades, interrupts or stays silent, the material occupying attention, and recurring image-sound functions. Treatments compare assigned exchanges; scripts and screenplay their realization.
5. Each classification rests on all actual members, not samples or character summaries. Counts locate patterns; reading judges them.
6. An owned uniform form, refrain, silence, narrow world or long frustration realizes its purpose.
7. Central concerns develop through changed events, choices, relationships, places, images, sound and form, and motifs acquire force instead of appending or repeating an answer.
