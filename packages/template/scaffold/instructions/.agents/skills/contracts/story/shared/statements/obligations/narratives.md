# Narrative population statements

Contract: [obligations/narratives.md](../../obligations/narratives.md).

## Continuity and progression {#continuity-and-progression}

1. Every unit is classified by its actual event, understanding, relationship, emotion, pressure, rhythm or formal contribution.
2. Functionless duplication and independently addressable progressions hidden together are identified, while inseparable montage, continuous action, parallel and other owned forms are preserved, in scripts and the screenplay across consecutive scene units of one place and time each.
3. Exits and entries connect through their causal or declared formal relation.
4. The order of movable units is tested for what it contributes, without imposing linearity on episodic, nonlinear or nonhuman work.
5. Inherited time, place, knowledge, injury, appearance, clothing, possession, resources, status, relationships and promises are compared across units.
6. Recurring agents' drives, beliefs, interpretations, experiences and actions are followed across units.
7. Each opposing force, a person, a group or a condition, keeps its agency and its threat until the resolution. Exception: a defeat or withdrawal the story causes and shows.
8. Unsupported reversals and thresholds crossed without pressure or cost are reported with the earliest contradictory owner and any contradiction derived across facts.
9. An unchanged or unreached threshold has its owned scope or purpose.
10. Where a protagonist's pursuit holds the work together, the answer identifies:
    - that desire and its first audience recognition;
    - the actions it drives;
    - how major-unit goals continue or transform it.
11. Where an ensemble sustains competing pursuits, or another form has another center, the answer identifies those in the same way and invents no single protagonist.
12. Local tasks alone do not establish the whole, and no shared goal is invented.

## Promises and distribution {#promises-and-distribution}

Promises:

1. Read without prior settings knowledge, the opening gives a reason to continue and timely understanding of people, relationships, pressure, time and space.
2. The answer traces actual introductions and returns, the declared engine and rewards, every payoff's setup, risk or cost, and the longest gaps without promised satisfaction.
3. The answer reports spans where the engine fails, rewards are unearned or worn out, or an eventual return cannot support the delay.

Time and variation:

4. Scene, compression, pause, omission, simultaneity, habitual time, flashback, anticipation and retelling are compared against each span's pressure.
5. For each departure from ordinary once-told forward sequence, the answer shows what removing it loses.
6. The answer compares:
    - openings and endings;
    - speech frequency and length;
    - who explains, evades, interrupts or stays silent;
    - the material occupying attention;
    - recurring image-sound functions.
7. Treatments compare assigned exchanges; scripts and screenplay compare their realization.
8. Each classification rests on all actual members, not samples or character summaries. Counts locate patterns; reading judges them.
9. An owned uniform form, refrain, silence, narrow world or long frustration realizes its purpose.
10. Central concerns develop through changed events, choices, relationships, places, images, sound and form, and motifs acquire force instead of appending or repeating an answer.
