# Realized speech statements

The detailed statements of the [realized speech principles](../../principles/realized.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Speaker plausibility {#speaker-plausibility}

1. Age, status, situation, knowledge, relationship, vocabulary, address, register, rhythm, dialect, avoidance and silence make each consequential line plausible and distinguish the speakers. Exception: ordinary replies can be shared.
2. The judgment rests on the exchange itself, not on an explanation about the speaker.
3. Authoring jargon and procedural codes appear in speech only when they exist in the world and this speaker would use them.
4. The inherited exchange's purpose and outcome are preserved across naturalistic, theatrical, ritual and other adopted forms.
5. Social and regional signals are chosen deliberately and reduce no community to an unexamined stereotype.

## Action and line order {#action-and-line-order}

1. A repeated ordering fails only when it hides or misstates the required relation, not because it repeats.
2. Delivery notes, speaker cues and neighboring action clarify the exchange and do not explain again what the line has already done.
