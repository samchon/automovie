# Treatment principles

Each treatment H2 answers for its event or inseparable progression. Treatments set the event order before scripts choose acts, sequences or episode boundaries.

## Decided event

Does the body decide who does what, why, at what cost and with what result? Give scripts the resulting state and room to group the events into scenes.

Establish consequential time, place, clue, exchange, participants, alternatives, choice or process, cost and resulting state. Preserve the meaning as well as the values of instructions and settings, including motives, beliefs, roles, knowledge and rules.

Give each promised experience an event or adopted narrator contribution at the required scope. Trace setups to their payoffs, a supported revision or a deliberately open handoff. Let tone, humor, perspective and recurring character facets serve the promise. Each repetition changes pressure, consequence or meaning, or serves the adopted form. Restating inherited state may serve continuity.

Return new world facts to settings. Leave scene partition to scripts and finished speech, blocking and audiovisual execution to the screenplay. Carry any detail that determines a cause or result. Concise prose, lists or tables may settle the event.

## Information design

Can the next author follow the event's causes, what the audience already knows, what changes here and what remains withheld?

Give the current choice its people, relationships, time, space and pressure. Ground later reveals in earlier facts. Keep each decision with its reason and owner, preserving the event sequence so the next author can use it.
