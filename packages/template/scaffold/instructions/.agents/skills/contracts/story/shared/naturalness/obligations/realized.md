# Speech expression population obligation

The completed script construction or revised final screenplay population answers once from all its actual exchanges. Check the item's [statements](../../statements/naturalness/obligations/realized.md) before answering it.

## Do voices and exchange habits remain distinct across the whole? {#voice-distribution}

Compared across the whole, does each recurring speaker keep a voice of their own as relationships and situations change? Local plausible lines do not establish distinct voices across a work.
