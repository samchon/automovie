# Narrative unit statements

Contract: [principles/narratives.md](../../principles/narratives.md).

## Unit progression

1. Entry, operation and result connect through cause and consequence, action and response, question and answer, reversal, or the work's owned formal relation.
2. The unit makes clear how time, place, knowledge, injury, appearance, clothing, possession, resources, status, relationships and promises change. Exception: a functional recurrence or rest.
3. Departures into memory, anticipation, repetition or habitual time, and the return, are marked where needed.
4. The reason to continue arises from this work's question, pursuit, observation, humor, emotion or formal accumulation.
5. Where an actor drives the change, these are intelligible, while an observational or nonhuman process has none of them invented for it:
    - the active desire, belief, fear and value;
    - the limitation, alternative and actual cost;
    - the pressure that makes a change of character credible.
6. New names, rules and relationships that affect the audience's understanding arrive with the context they need.
7. The exit leaves an earned state, question, pressure or formal effect. Exception: a complete unit or an intentional pause need not force a cliffhanger.

## Parent differentiation

Copying:

1. Every downstream paragraph has its actual parent passages identified, with a yes or no test recorded for each of:
    - copied wording or imagery;
    - explanatory labels;
    - narrator or design statements;
    - a borrowed opening or execution sequence.
2. Changed vocabulary or a few search matches do not establish the comparison.
3. Inherited event order, or a concise connection of shared facts or basic action, is not copying unless it imports the parent's wording, imagery or execution sequence.
4. A claim of necessary connection, or of creativity in neighboring paragraphs, does not excuse transcribed directions or action sequences.
5. No paragraph is required to add a new decision, sensation or inward beat.

Inherited values:

6. Each inherited name, form of address, quantity, time, place and consequential state is tested against its actual parent.
7. Needed inherited values are carried without an inert inventory, and an already complete short artifact is preserved without ornamental variation.

Judgment and repair:

8. An unresolved copying judgment stays a candidate to repair on the initial text, and a paragraph rewritten to repair a previous yes fails again only when copying is clear.
9. Where copying fails, the passage is recreated from the parent decisions, not polished.
10. A defective parent is judged apart from copied realization and goes to this layer's upstream item ([treatments](../../upstream/treatments.md#settings-repair), [scripts](../../upstream/scripts.md#script-upstream-repair) or [screenplay](../../upstream/screenplay.md#screenplay-upstream-repair)), which repairs that owner and its affected descendants, when it:
    - consumed the next layer's work;
    - forced a defined failure;
    - blocked an adopted promise;
    - left essential decisions absent.
