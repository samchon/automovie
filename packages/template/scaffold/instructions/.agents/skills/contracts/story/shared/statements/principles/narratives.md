# Narrative unit statements

Contract: [principles/narratives.md](../../principles/narratives.md).

## Unit progression {#unit-progression}

1. Entry, operation and result are connected through cause and consequence, action and response, question and answer, reversal, or the work's owned formal relation.
2. The unit establishes how time, place, knowledge, injury, appearance, clothing, possession, resources, status, relationships and promises change. Exception: a functional recurrence or rest.
3. Departures into memory, anticipation, repetition or habitual time, and the return, are marked where needed.
4. The reason to continue arises from this work's question, pursuit, observation, humor, emotion or formal accumulation.
5. Where an actor drives the change, the active desire, belief, fear, value, limitation, alternative and actual cost are intelligible, with the pressure that makes a change of character credible. Exception: nothing is invented to answer a tag in an observational or nonhuman process.
6. New names, rules and relationships arrive with the context needed when they affect the audience's understanding.
7. The exit leaves an earned state, question, pressure or formal effect. Exception: a complete unit or intentional pause need not force a cliffhanger.

## Parent differentiation {#parent-differentiation}

1. Every downstream paragraph has its actual parent passages identified, with yes or no tests recorded for copied wording or imagery, explanatory labels, narrator or design statements, and a borrowed opening or execution sequence. Inherited event order alone is not copied realization.
2. Each inherited name, form of address, quantity, time, place and consequential state is tested against its actual parent.
3. An unresolved copying judgment stays a candidate to repair on the initial text. A paragraph rewritten to repair a previous yes fails again only when copying is clear, and changed vocabulary or a few search matches do not establish the comparison.
4. Needed inherited values are carried without an inert inventory, and already complete short artifacts are preserved without ornamental variation.
5. Copied realization is judged apart from defective parent design.
6. A parent that consumed the next layer's work, forced a defined failure, blocked an adopted promise or left essential decisions absent goes to this layer's upstream item.
7. That item is [treatments](../../upstream/treatments.md#settings-repair), [scripts](../../upstream/scripts.md#script-upstream-repair) or [screenplay](../../upstream/screenplay.md#screenplay-upstream-repair), and the repair covers that owner and its affected descendants.
8. A concise connection of shared facts or basic action is not copying when it imports neither the parent's wording or imagery nor its execution sequence, and no new decision, sensation or inward beat is demanded in every paragraph.
9. A claim of necessary connection or creativity in neighboring paragraphs does not excuse transcribed directions or action sequences. Where copying fails, the passage is recreated from the parent decisions, not polished.
