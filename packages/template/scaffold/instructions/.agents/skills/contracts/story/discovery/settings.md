# Settings requirement discovery

The discovery result covers all instructions, adopted promises, references, assets and planned consumers under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership).

## Instructions and backcast

Before drafting settings, did discovery assign an owner to each consequential instruction and identify facts later authors would otherwise have to invent?

Locate the instructions governing premises, people and titles, genre rewards, audience information load, asset and source boundaries, sensitive subjects and form. Assign an owner to each. Record open choices, later discoveries and conflicts. Verify external claims under the declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis).

Work back from the planned narrative. Where an author would have to invent authority, causes, resources, knowledge, relationships, spaces, appearance or voice, identify the fact, limit or uncertainty they need. The narrative owns events, outcomes, setups, payoffs and reveals.

Record how the reference identifies owners, orders facts, distinguishes evidence from invention and names downstream subjects.
