# Brief population statements

## Coherent brief

Coherence:

1. The decisions agree with one another, such as a person's body and appearance with the practice level the brief gives them. A disagreement is repaired in the earliest record that owns the decision, and no later record works around it.
2. Where a [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) is declared, the chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities derived from the records agree. Quantities the fiction invents follow that section.

Records:

3. Each record gives its definition, settled content, conditions and open matters in a consistent order.
4. Each independently used subject has one file with its inseparable attributes together, one term per concept and one owner per fact.
5. The brief is prose a screenplay author reads, with no description of code.
6. A decision of a kind the brief holds that a screenplay established and another scene or film must keep is recorded here and cites the beat that established it.
