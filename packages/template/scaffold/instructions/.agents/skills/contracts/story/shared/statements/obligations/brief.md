# Brief population statements

## Coherent brief

Coherence:

1. Check that decisions agree. Repair conflicts at their earliest owner.
2. Where a [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) is declared, the chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities derived from the records agree. Quantities the fiction invents follow that section.

Records:

3. Each record gives its definition, settled content, conditions and open matters in a consistent order.
4. The files cover every subject in the [brief's scope](../../../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds), one file per subject.
5. Each fact has one owner.
6. Each concept has a consistent name.
7. The brief is prose a screenplay author reads, with no description of code.
8. A decision of a kind the brief holds that a screenplay established and another scene or film must keep is recorded here and cites the beat that established it.
