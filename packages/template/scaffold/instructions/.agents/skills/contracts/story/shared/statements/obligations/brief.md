# Brief population statements

Contract: [obligations/brief.md](../../obligations/brief.md).

## Coherent brief {#coherent-brief}

1. The answering file declares the audience, the central experience, the films with their running time and frame, and the adopted representation with what it cannot show.
2. What the commission leaves out of the films is stated once, in this file.
3. Every world rule, person, relationship, place and object a scene will rely on has one owner.
4. No official, partner or crowd a scene shows is left without behavior while it is on screen.
5. The decisions agree with one another:
    - what the world allows with what people do;
    - what each person knows with how they came to know it;
    - each relationship from both sides;
    - each baseline state with what changes it.
6. Where two of the brief's decisions disagree, the disagreement is repaired in the earliest record that owns the decision, and no later record works around it.
7. Where a [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) is declared, the chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities derived from the records agree, and quantities the fiction invents are left to research and code.
