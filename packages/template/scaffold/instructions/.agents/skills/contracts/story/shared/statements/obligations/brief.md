## Coherent brief {#coherent-brief}

1. The answering file declares the audience, the central experience, the films with their running time, frame and delivery, and the adopted representation with what it cannot show.
2. A dimension the commission excludes is excluded once here, not in every record.
3. Every world rule, person, relationship, place and object a scene will rely on has one owner.
4. Every presence a scene shows, such as an official, partner or crowd, has behavior for its whole presence.
5. No two decisions contradict: world rules against what people can do, what each person knows against how they learned it, relationships seen from each side, and baseline states against their change conditions. A contradiction is repaired at its earliest owner.
6. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), derived chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities agree across records. Fiction's own quantities belong to research and code.
