## Coherent brief {#coherent-brief}

1. The answering file declares the audience, the central experience, the films with their running time and frame, and the adopted representation with what it cannot show.
2. A dimension the commission excludes is excluded once here, not in every record.
3. Every world rule, person, relationship, place and object a scene will rely on has one owner.
4. No official, partner or crowd a scene shows is left without behavior while it is on screen.
5. The decisions agree with one another: what the world allows with what people do, what each person knows with how they came to know it, each relationship from both sides, and each baseline state with what changes it.
6. A contradiction is repaired at its earliest owner.
7. Where a [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) is declared, the chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities derived from the records agree. Fiction's own quantities belong to research and code.
