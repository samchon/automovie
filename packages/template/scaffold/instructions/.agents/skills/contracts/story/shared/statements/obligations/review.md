# Review population statements

Contract: [obligations/review.md](../../obligations/review.md).

## Complete current round {#complete-current-round}

Extent:

1. The verdict identifies the exact revision, declared layer and pass, files, H2s, upstream relationships, descendants and observations reviewed.
2. It reconciles the inventory and defined measurements with what was read.
3. The round's extent is the one [narrative rounds](../../../../../review/narrative-rounds.md) requires for its kind, and each carried judgment identifies its source, reader and grounds.
4. Where the pass ran independent classification, stable sentence classifications follow [inspection](../../../../../story/correction/inspection.md#verify-changed-work). They waive no whole reading and carry no unit approval.
5. Closed layers stay closed under the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes), and partial or unavailable inspection is stated.
6. The current review tree holds the complete inventory and verdict, including explicit carries, without relying on missing units in earlier folders.

Classes:

7. Each supported finding class is collected with all discovered members, quotations and locators, the violated obligation, the earliest owner and affected edges.
8. Inspection methods are reproducible, and the revision identity is recorded.
9. Body completeness and settings navigation are checked where relevant.
10. Population classifications and the actual function behind whole-scope negatives, such as silence or refrain, are compared.
11. Similar length, repeated syntax or annotation ratios prompt rereading and establish no defect.
12. A class table introduces no finding unsupported by an inspected unit.

## Round accountability {#round-accountability}

1. The verdict records:
    - the source revision and the review kind;
    - preserved achievements and supported choices left intact;
    - all findings grouped by root cause and contributing factors;
    - the author's decision on each finding with its reason, and the repair of each accepted one, with decisions not yet received marked.
2. The verdict preserves the function of achievements actually read and judged.
3. It distinguishes expressive surplus removed below a duration target from insufficient event material.
4. All accepted findings were repaired by cause with their affected descendants, and the pass closed as its [route](../../../../../story/workflow/SKILL.md) requires.
5. The verdict states the actual open or clean result and any unverified host or output claim.
