# Settings population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering settings file whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned scenes need without requiring unused domains or empty files.

## Is the work's promise and scope a usable basis? {#world-and-scope}

Declare the audience, central experience, the questions or formal effects it develops, its recurring rewards and the title's promise; user-fixed title wording is not a taste question. Establish where delivery begins and ends, its inclusions and exclusions, its relation to a larger work, and its release units with their number and duration ranges. If these are not supplied, derive a provisional plan from the promise, material and production limits, naming its grounds and uncertainty, and revise it when those grounds change. For a series, establish what recurs, closes per release and develops across releases. Scripts chooses release boundaries. Scale serves the audience and available material, not copied size rules or padding. Declare the adopted representation with what it cannot show, such as faceless polygon men, so later layers write only what it carries. A dimension the commission excludes is excluded once here, not in every record.

## Do the world, people and look hold together for the planned scenes? {#people-and-audiovisual-world}

Read the planned narrative as a consumer: does every world rule, power, institution, person, relationship, place and object a scene will rely on have one owner, at a depth that matches its consequence? Central and recurring agents need their situation, drives, values, fears, abilities and limits, knowledge and errors, observable behavior, recognizable appearance and, when heard, voice; nonhuman forces need their inputs, operation and thresholds without invented psychology. Every presence a scene shows, such as an official, partner or crowd, has behavior for its whole presence, because one without it stands idle on screen. Consequential relationships are defined from both sides with their change conditions; treatments owns actual progression. Places and objects carry the identity, layout and use that scenes stage, and the shared material, costume, visual and audible grammar distinguishes factions, regions, eras and classes where they matter.

Do these decisions contradict one another? Compare world rules with what agents can do, what each knows with how they learned it, relationships seen from each side, and baseline states with their change conditions, and explain why an initial threat, mystery or injustice persists: who could end it, and what stops them. Repair a contradiction at its earliest owner. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), also check derived chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities across records and identify every consequential participant the source names; fiction's own quantities belong to research and code.
