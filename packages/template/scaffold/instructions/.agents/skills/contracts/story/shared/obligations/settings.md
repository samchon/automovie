# Settings population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering settings file whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned scenes need without requiring unused domains or empty files.

Statements: [statements/obligations/settings.md](../statements/obligations/settings.md).

## Is the work's promise and scope a usable basis? {#world-and-scope}

Do the declared promise, scope and representation give every later layer one basis to work from? Without them each layer assumes its own audience, scale and limits.

## Do the world, people and look hold together for the planned scenes? {#people-and-audiovisual-world}

Read as a consumer of the planned narrative: does everything a scene will rely on have one owner at a depth that matches its consequence, without contradiction? A presence without behavior stands idle on screen, and a contradiction surfaces only when a scene needs both facts.
