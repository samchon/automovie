# Script principles

Each script H2 is a first draft of one scene for the screenplay writer.

## Scene progression {#executable-scene}

Can the writer follow what happens here, why it matters and what the next scene inherits?

Identify the place, time, participants and starting situation. Draft the consequential action, choice or exchange and its result. Dialogue may establish what a character asks, withholds or reveals and how the reply changes the scene. Judge these lines by their dramatic function. Identify any wording the commission fixes. The screenplay writer decides how the scene plays on screen.
