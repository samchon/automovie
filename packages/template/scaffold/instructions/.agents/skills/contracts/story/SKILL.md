# Story contracts

The [writing workflow](../../story/workflow/SKILL.md) sets the layers and passes each route runs; standalone asset work has no narrative population. A stage the user waived is recorded with its scope and never claimed as passed.

Principles ask each authored H2, obligations ask one document for a complete declared population, discovery asks the inspected scope's discovery result, and review questions ask review records. Do not make every scene testify for an entire film or attach a conditional requirement to unrelated respondents. The evidence configuration owns the selected populations and relationships; the installed evidence package owns its mechanics.

## The short route

A short has no settings, settings index, treatments or scripts, so those rows have no respondent there, and its studies answer no research contract under [evidence configuration](../../evidence/configuration.md#story).

Its event design and scene execution are asked of the screenplay instead: [audiovisual realization](shared/principles/screenplay.md#audiovisual-realization), [beat decisions](shared/principles/screenplay.md#beat-decisions), [unit progression](shared/principles/narratives.md#unit-progression) and [speaker plausibility](shared/principles/realized.md#speaker-plausibility) for each section, and [continuity](shared/obligations/narratives.md#continuity-and-progression), [promises](shared/obligations/narratives.md#promises-and-distribution) and [complete realization](shared/obligations/screenplay.md#complete-realization) for the whole, which cover setup and payoff, the ending and the established space.

## Statements

Each contract's statements sit at the same path under `shared/statements`, such as `shared/statements/principles/screenplay.md` for the screenplay principles, with one section per item anchor. Check them before answering, as the contracts skill's items and statements section describes.

## Discovery, expression and upstream

Use [common discovery](shared/discovery/common.md), then [settings](shared/discovery/settings.md) or [brief](shared/discovery/brief.md), [treatments](shared/discovery/treatments.md), [scripts](shared/discovery/scripts.md), [screenplay](shared/discovery/screenplay.md), and [narrative](shared/discovery/narratives.md) for the selected populations; the story skill's discovery page writes their result.

Scripts construction also answers the expression questions: [common](shared/naturalness/common.md), [realized speech expression](shared/naturalness/realized.md) and [scripts](shared/naturalness/scripts.md) for each unit, and the [common](shared/naturalness/obligations/common.md), [realized speech expression](shared/naturalness/obligations/realized.md) and [script](shared/naturalness/obligations/scripts.md) obligations for the whole population. The screenplay settles its speech in construction under the realized speech principles and has no expression family.

Creation tests its parents through the [treatment](shared/upstream/treatments.md), [script](shared/upstream/scripts.md), and [screenplay](shared/upstream/screenplay.md) upstream questions; repair the earliest semantic owner and its affected descendants together.
