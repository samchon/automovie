# Story contracts

The [writing workflow](../../story/workflow.md#route-and-parents) sets the layers and passes each route runs; standalone asset work has no narrative population. A stage the user waived is recorded with its actual scope and never claimed as passed.

Each checklist H2 asks its named respondent a question. Principles apply to an authored H2, obligations to one document for a complete declared population, discovery to the inspected scope's discovery result, and review questions to review records.

Do not make every scene testify for an entire film or attach a conditional requirement to unrelated hosts. The evidence configuration owns the actual selected populations and relationships; installed Evidence APIs own its mechanics.

## The short route

A short has no treatments, scripts or settings index, so those rows have no respondent there. Its event design and scene execution are asked of the screenplay instead: [audiovisual realization](shared/principles/screenplay.md#audiovisual-realization) and [beat decisions](shared/principles/screenplay.md#beat-decisions) for each scene, and [continuity](shared/obligations/narratives.md#continuity-and-progression), [promises](shared/obligations/narratives.md#promises-and-distribution) and [complete realization](shared/obligations/screenplay.md#complete-realization) for the whole, which cover setup and payoff, the ending and the established space.

## Statements

Each contract's statements sit at the same path under `shared/statements`, such as `shared/statements/principles/screenplay.md` for the screenplay principles, with one section per item anchor. Check them before answering, as [items and statements](../SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes.

## Discovery, expression and upstream

Use [common discovery](shared/discovery/common.md), then [settings](shared/discovery/settings.md) or [brief](shared/discovery/brief.md), [treatments](shared/discovery/treatments.md), [scripts](shared/discovery/scripts.md), [screenplay](shared/discovery/screenplay.md), and [narrative](shared/discovery/narratives.md) for the actual selected populations; [discovery](../../story/planning/discovery.md) writes their result.

Scripts construction and screenplay expression revision use [common](shared/naturalness/common.md) and [actual speech](shared/naturalness/realized.md), plus [scripts](shared/naturalness/scripts.md) or [screenplay](shared/naturalness/screenplay.md). Their full-population comparisons use [common](shared/naturalness/obligations/common.md), [voices](shared/naturalness/obligations/realized.md), and the applicable [script](shared/naturalness/obligations/scripts.md) or [screenplay](shared/naturalness/obligations/screenplay.md) obligations. Fidelity and natural reception remain separate questions.

Creation tests its actual parents through the [treatment](shared/upstream/treatments.md), [script](shared/upstream/scripts.md), and [screenplay](shared/upstream/screenplay.md) upstream audits; repair the earliest semantic owner and its affected descendants together.
