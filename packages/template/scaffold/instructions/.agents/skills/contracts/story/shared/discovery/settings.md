# Settings requirement discovery

The discovery result covers all instructions, adopted promises, references, assets and planned consumers under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership).

## Were instructions and planned use translated into needed owners and a usable form? {#instructions-and-backcast}

Before bulk settings writing, break every instruction and adopted promise into its consequential elements: premises, people and names or titles, genre rewards, the audience's capacity for names and information, asset constraints, source boundaries, sensitive subjects and form dependencies. Identify each existing owner or needed fact, constraint, relationship, ability, uncertainty or creative choice, and report instruction conflicts rather than silently overwrite either. Check premises against sources only under a declared [factual basis](../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) or where the work claims an external fact. Add later discoveries to this inventory and owner.

Read the planned narrative as a consumer and backcast the authority, cause, resource, knowledge boundary, relationship, space, appearance or voice it would otherwise invent, leaving chosen events, choices, results, setups, payoffs and reveal order to the next narrative owner. Identify the observable choices of the reference form: how a record marks its owner, orders facts, separates evidence from invention and names downstream subjects; labels such as rigorous or immersive establish none of them. An honest negative names the concrete planned risk and its adequate existing owner.
