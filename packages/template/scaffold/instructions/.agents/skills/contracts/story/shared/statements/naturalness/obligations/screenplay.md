# Final screenplay expression population statements

Contract: [naturalness/obligations/screenplay.md](../../../naturalness/obligations/screenplay.md).

## Voice and tempo distribution {#voice-and-tempo-distribution}

1. Distance, audience access, image-sound relations, recurring motifs, entrances and exits, acceleration, sustained duration, silence and transitions are compared in order.
2. Different events do not default to the same introduction, commentary, closing image, musical cue, single speed or mechanical alternation.
3. A fixed perspective, deliberate recurrence or sustained voice may function. Counts and formal uniformity alone condemn nothing.
4. Candidates and preservation comparisons follow [common expression obligations](common.md#recurrent-frames).
5. Expression is revised without changing inherited events, reveals or the adopted audience position. Where that position causes the failure, its earliest owner is reported for upstream repair.
