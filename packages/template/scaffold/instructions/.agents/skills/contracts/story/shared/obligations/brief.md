# Brief population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering brief file whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned films need without requiring unused domains or empty files.

## Do the promise, scope and decisions hold together for the planned films? {#coherent-brief}

Declare the audience, central experience, the films with their running time, frame and delivery, and the adopted representation with what it cannot show; a dimension the commission excludes is excluded once here, not in every record. Read the planned films as a consumer: does every world rule, person, relationship, place and object a scene will rely on have one owner, and does every presence a scene shows, such as an official, partner or crowd, have behavior for its whole presence, because one without it stands idle on screen?

Do these decisions contradict one another? Compare world rules with what people can do, what each knows with how they learned it, relationships seen from each side, and baseline states with their change conditions, and repair a contradiction at its earliest owner. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), also check derived chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities across records; fiction's own quantities belong to research and code.
