# Brief population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering brief file, whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned films need without requiring unused domains or empty files.

Statements: [statements/obligations/brief.md](../statements/obligations/brief.md).

## Coherent brief

Read as a consumer of the planned films: does every person, place and recurring prop several scenes will rely on have one findable, current owner, within the commission's scope and representation, without contradiction? A missing owner or a contradiction is otherwise found only when a screenplay needs it, and a decision kept only in one screenplay lets the next film contradict it.
