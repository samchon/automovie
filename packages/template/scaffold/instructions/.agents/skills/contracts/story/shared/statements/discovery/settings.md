## Instructions and backcast {#instructions-and-backcast}

1. Every instruction and adopted promise is broken into its consequential elements: premises, people and names or titles, genre rewards, the audience's capacity for names and information, asset constraints, source boundaries, sensitive subjects and form dependencies.
2. Each element has an existing owner or a needed fact, constraint, relationship, ability, uncertainty or creative choice. Instruction conflicts are reported, and neither side is silently overwritten.
3. Premises are checked against sources only under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) or where the work claims an external fact.
4. Later discoveries are added to this inventory and owner.
5. The planned narrative was read as a consumer, and the authority, cause, resource, knowledge boundary, relationship, space, appearance or voice it would otherwise invent was backcast. Chosen events, choices, results, setups, payoffs and reveal order stay with the next narrative owner.
6. The observable choices of the reference form are identified: how a record marks its owner, orders facts, separates evidence from invention and names downstream subjects. Labels such as rigorous or immersive establish none of them.
7. An honest negative names the concrete planned risk and its adequate existing owner.
