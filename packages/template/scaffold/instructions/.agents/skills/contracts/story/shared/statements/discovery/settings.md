# Settings discovery statements

Contract: [discovery/settings.md](../../discovery/settings.md).

## Instructions and planned use {#instructions-and-backcast}

1. Every instruction and adopted promise is broken into its consequential elements:
    - premises;
    - people and names or titles;
    - genre rewards;
    - the audience's capacity for names and information;
    - asset constraints;
    - source boundaries;
    - sensitive subjects;
    - form dependencies.
2. Each element has an existing owner or a needed fact, constraint, relationship, ability, uncertainty or creative choice. Instruction conflicts are reported, and neither side is silently overwritten.
3. Premises are checked against sources only under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) or where the work claims an external fact.
4. Later discoveries are added to this inventory and owner.
5. The planned narrative was read as a consumer, and what it would otherwise invent was traced back to the decision that must supply it:
    - an authority, cause, resource or knowledge boundary;
    - a relationship, space, appearance or voice.
6. Chosen events, choices, results, setups, payoffs and reveal order stay with the next narrative owner.
7. The observable choices of the reference form are identified: how a record marks its owner, orders facts, separates evidence from invention and names downstream subjects.
8. Labels such as rigorous or immersive establish none of those choices.
9. An honest negative names the concrete planned risk and its adequate existing owner.
