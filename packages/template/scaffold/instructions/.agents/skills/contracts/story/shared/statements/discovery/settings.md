# Settings discovery statements

Contract: [discovery/settings.md](../../discovery/settings.md).

## Instructions and backcast

1. Every instruction and adopted promise is broken into its consequential elements:
    - premises;
    - people and names or titles;
    - genre rewards;
    - the audience's capacity for names and information;
    - asset constraints;
    - source boundaries;
    - sensitive subjects;
    - form dependencies.
2. Each element has an existing owner or a needed fact, constraint, relationship, ability, uncertainty or creative choice. Where two instructions conflict, the conflict is reported and neither silently overrides the other.
3. A premise is checked against a source only where a [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) is declared or the work claims an external fact.
4. An element discovered later joins this inventory with its owner.
5. The planned narrative was read as a consumer, and what it would otherwise invent was traced back to the decision that must supply it:
    - an authority, cause, resource or knowledge boundary;
    - a relationship, space, appearance or voice.
6. Chosen events, choices, results, setups, payoffs and reveal order stay with the next narrative owner.
7. The reference form's observable choices are identified: how a record marks its owner, orders facts, separates evidence from invention and names downstream subjects. A label such as rigorous or immersive establishes none of them.
