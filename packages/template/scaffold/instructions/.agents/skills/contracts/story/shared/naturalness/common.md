# Common expression principles

Each script construction H2 answers from its current body against its parent treatments. Inspect the entire selected body, record every candidate found with its location, and explain its repair or its justified preservation. If none is found, identify the scope actually inspected. A few examples never prove a whole-scope negative.

Statements: [statements/naturalness/common.md](../statements/naturalness/common.md).

## Reads as written

Does each instruction, utterance, image and detail belong to this moment, in language its adopted variety and register would produce on purpose, carrying meaning through an intelligible body, space, object, sound or adopted narrator? Padding, repeated explanation and default patterns make the audience decode the author instead of living the scene, and cutting too hard loses the people, causes and voice the scene needs.

Judge only the named failures of this item's statements, because preference outside them is not a defect, and retain one only under an exception they permit. Simple or flat writing is not inherently a defect.

## Expression fidelity

Would separate downstream authors reading the parent treatments and the script produce the same world, events, people, knowledge, values and degree of certainty? A difference that would cause another fact or scene fails, while a change of wording, emphasis, nuance, sentence shape or punctuation does not fail by itself.

Fidelity and natural reception are separate judgments: a faithful passage can still fail to read as written, and a natural one can still have changed the story.
