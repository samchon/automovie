# Treatment population obligations

The complete ordered population answers each question once, about event design, not the later script partition.

## Whole event design

Read in order, does what the audience knows, expects and receives build from the first event to an ending that follows from it? Clear events can still leave a payoff unsupported, a setup abandoned or a middle that resets.

Map audience knowledge, uncertainty, beliefs and expectations through the events. Check establishment of reveals, reversals and decisive powers or objects. Trace each setup to a payoff, transformation or intentionally open ending. Locate when questions open and close, the longest waits and any span that defers all satisfaction.

Decide the first perception and event. Give choices their needed context. Trace accumulating costs, narrowed options and changed relationships through the middle. Parallel progressions gain from their order, intersections or contrasts. Events establish tone and emotional change, and opponents act from their own priorities. Show the ending's resulting state. Chance may start a crisis. An outside resolution needs an established premise.

## Material and handoff

Can the script author follow the core progression at the declared scale without rebuilding settings? Do the treatments leave execution choices to scripts?

Place canonical facts where the script author needs them. Leave dialogue, blocking and release partition to scripts. Map ordered events and processes to the delivery scale, including turns capable of closing a major unit.

Identify promised intervals with insufficient material, repetition presented as progression and decisions deferred to the script. Concise treatment prose can support extensive action. Repair event material at its treatment owner or return an unsupported promise to its authorized settings owner.
