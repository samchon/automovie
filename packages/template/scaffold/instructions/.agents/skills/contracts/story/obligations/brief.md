# Brief population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering brief file, whose detached construction evidence compares the completed population. Inspect the domains the planned films need, without requiring unused domains or empty files.

## Coherent brief

Does every subject required by the [brief's scope](../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds) have one findable, consistent record?

Compare the brief with the planned films and any drafted scenes. Account for subjects discovered during writing or staging under [brief growth](../../../story/layers/brief.md#growth). Use one record per subject, one owner per fact and consistent names. Present settled decisions, conditions and open questions in reading order. Repair conflicts at the earliest owner.

Under a declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), reconcile derived chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities. Follow its conditions for quantities the fiction invents.
