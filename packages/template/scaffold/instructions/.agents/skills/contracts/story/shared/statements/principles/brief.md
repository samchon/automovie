## Needed decisions

1. The record holds only decisions several scenes must keep true:
    - for a cast member, identity, including the level of any practice the film shows, body and appearance, and wardrobe, with a style or tell only when the story turns on it;
    - for a place, its shape, landmarks, light and crowd;
    - props that recur.
2. It holds no decision the screenplay makes within one scene, and nothing the commission already fixes, such as running time, frame or representation.
3. A measurement appears only when it is itself a story decision. Otherwise its source is in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md) and its value in the code that realizes it.
4. A practice level cites the study it draws on, and an external fact cites its source. A declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) applies its source duties to the claims it covers.
