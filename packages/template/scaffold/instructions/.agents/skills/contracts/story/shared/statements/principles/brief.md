# Brief statements

Contract: [principles/brief.md](../../principles/brief.md).

## Needed decisions {#needed-decisions}

1. The record names its subject and settles a value, its scope and the conditions, limits or exceptions that affect use.
2. The brief records the fixed conditions: each film's running time and frame, and the adopted representation with what it cannot show, such as faceless polygon men or a narrow vertical frame.
3. It records the world and its rules, people's personality, values, motives, abilities and knowledge, relationships, the identity and use of places and objects, and the tone and look.
4. It holds only what the planned films need: no facet nothing uses and no "not applicable".
5. A measurement appears only when it is itself a story decision. Otherwise its source is in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md) and its value in the code that realizes it.
6. A practiced activity records its level and style and cites the study that holds its vocabulary.
7. An external fact the record asserts cites its source, and a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) applies its source duties to the claims it covers.

## Record and currency {#record-and-currency}

1. The definition, settled content, conditions and open matters come in a consistent order.
2. Each independently used subject has one file with its inseparable attributes together, one term per concept and one owner per fact.
3. The brief is prose a screenplay author reads, with no description of code.
4. It holds baseline states and change conditions and leaves events, action and reveal order to the screenplay.
5. A decision a screenplay established that another scene or film must keep is recorded here and cites the unit that established it.
