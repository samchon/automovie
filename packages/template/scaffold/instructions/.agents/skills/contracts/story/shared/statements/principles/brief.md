## Needed decisions

1. The record names its subject and settles a value, its scope and the conditions, limits or exceptions that affect use.
2. It holds only decisions several scenes must keep true: for a cast member, identity, including the level of any practice the film shows, body and look, and wardrobe, with a style or tell only when the story turns on it; for a place, its shape, landmarks, light and crowd; props that recur; and, for a commissioned generated film, the look: lighting style, lens character and grade.
3. It repeats nothing the commission already fixes, such as running time, frame or representation, and holds no decision the screenplay makes within one scene.
4. A measurement appears only when it is itself a story decision. Otherwise its source is in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md) and its value in the code that realizes it.
5. A practice level cites the study it draws on.
6. An external fact the record asserts cites its source, and a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) applies its source duties to the claims it covers.
