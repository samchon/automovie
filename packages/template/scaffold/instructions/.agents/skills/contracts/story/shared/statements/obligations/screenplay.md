# Final screenplay population statements

Contract: [obligations/screenplay.md](../../obligations/screenplay.md).

## Complete realization {#complete-realization}

1. Every long-route script execution is mapped to final units and each final unit to actual parents. A short maps its brief decisions and its screenplay-owned event and execution decisions without invented skipped stages.
2. Identity, events, choices, consequences, states, relationships, knowledge and reveal order are preserved, while new image-sound relations, attention, duration and transitions are judged across the whole.
3. At construction, the answer assesses the complete plan and sufficient material, naming provisional performance, placeholders and future source, sound, edit or output work. Unmade descendants do not block screenplay construction; absent required delivery parts remain gaps.
4. At construction, it reports provisional timing, its basis and the performance and editing measurements still needed.
5. Actual descendants and observations are connected as they exist.
6. At delivery, the actual assembled total duration and release units are measured against the declared duration and format, distinguishing script estimates from recorded speech, performed action, edit timing and the measured file.
7. It reports justified ceilings and the effect of removing only repetitions actually judged redundant. Functional refrains count.
8. A shortfall alone is no defect, while compressed required experience, missing event assignment or poor partition may be. The earliest cause is repaired without padding, new events or stretched sound.

## Published version {#published-version}

1. For an existing public release, the version, changed scope, reason, semantic effect and required notices are identified across the release population.
2. Spelling or metadata, display or rendering, factual, semantic and delivery-required corrections are distinguished.
3. Applicable platform requirements are followed, and settings or brief, treatments, scripts, screenplay, source, media, edit and review are aligned to the new version.
4. Audience reaction or release gaps alone justify no canon change.
5. Where no public version exists, the answer says so. Future editability never excuses skipped pre-delivery review.
