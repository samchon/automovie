# Final screenplay population statements

## Complete realization

1. Every long-route script execution is mapped to final units and each final unit to its parents.
2. A short maps its brief decisions and its screenplay-owned event and execution decisions without invented skipped stages.
3. Identity, events, choices, consequences, states, relationships, knowledge and reveal order are preserved.
4. New image-sound relations, attention, duration and transitions are judged across the whole.
5. Assess the complete construction plan and material. Record provisional timing and its basis, placeholders, pending performance, source, sound, edit and output work, and the measurements still needed.
6. Unmade descendants do not block screenplay construction, while absent required delivery parts remain gaps; descendants and observations are connected as they exist.
7. At delivery, the assembled total duration and release units are measured against the declared duration and format, distinguishing script estimates from recorded speech, performed action, edit timing and the measured file.
8. At delivery, the answer reports justified ceilings and the effect of removing only repetitions judged redundant. Functional refrains count.
9. A shortfall alone is no defect, while compressed required experience, missing event assignment or poor partition may be. The earliest cause is repaired without padding, new events or stretched sound.

## Published version

1. For an existing public release, the version, changed scope, reason, semantic effect and required notices are identified across the release population.
2. Spelling or metadata, display or rendering, factual, semantic and delivery-required corrections are distinguished.
3. Applicable platform requirements are followed, and settings or brief, treatments, scripts, screenplay, source, media, edit and review are aligned to the new version.
4. Audience reaction or release gaps alone justify no canon change.
5. Where no public version exists, the answer says so. Future editability never excuses skipped pre-delivery review.
