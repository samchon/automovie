# Review unit principles

Each review H2 answers for the authored H2 or precise relationship it inspected, identifying its creative reading. These questions assess the review record, not the creative quality of the work reviewed.

## Independent reading

Before parents, contracts or earlier verdicts, did a fresh context read the annotation-free current body as its recipient and lock what it found? A judgment formed after the author's records reads the work as intended, not as received.

- Lock a quotation and locator from the current body for every unit, including a clear one. Headings, annotations, recalled wording and quotations selected after the verdict cannot establish first reading.
- For each finding, locate the passage, obligation, failure, recipient loss and earliest owner. Record the author's reason for declining it and the reviewer's rebuttal or withdrawal. Upstream knowledge, confidence, prior verdicts, cost and taste cannot dismiss an observed loss.
- Where independent classification applies, decide named failures in context before author defenses. Retention needs a proven exception or, for other candidates, the [retention test](../../../story/answers.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate). Respect the item's stated failure boundary.
- Quote achievements at risk and their function, or say none were found. Give repair directions. Reserve counterfactual prose for a comparison.

## Literal and class audit

Did the review find every member of each finding class? A finding repaired alone leaves the rest of its class.

Record the inspected scope, reproducible search or comparison, all members, affected relations and earliest owner of each finding class. Cross-reference members in other units or explain isolation. Match quotes and locators to the revision. Similar wording triggers inspection.

After [independent reading](../../../review/independent-reading.md), compare the body with requirements and each decision with its parent. If classification ran, check coverage of sentences and parent comparisons before author evidence, then recheck revisions and dependencies under [changed work](../../../story/correction/inspection.md#changed-work).
