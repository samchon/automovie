# Review unit principles

Each review H2 answers for the authored H2 or precise relationship it inspected, identifying its creative reading. These questions assess the review record, not the creative quality of the work reviewed.

## Independent reading

Before parents, contracts or earlier verdicts, did a fresh context read the annotation-free current body as its recipient and lock what it found? A judgment formed after the author's records reads the work as intended, not as received.

Lock a quotation and locator from the current body for every unit, including a clear one. Headings, annotations, remembered words and quotations chosen after the verdict cannot establish a first reading. Quote any achievement a repair might put at risk and explain its function, or say none was found.

For each finding, identify the passage, obligation, failure, loss to the recipient and earliest owner. Give repair directions. Reserve counterfactual prose for comparisons. If the author declines a finding, record the grounds and the reviewer's rebuttal or withdrawal. Upstream knowledge, confidence, prior verdicts, cost and taste cannot dismiss an observed loss.

Where independent classification applies, decide named failures in context before the author's defense. Retention requires an applicable exception or, for other candidates, the [retention test](../../../story/answers.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate). Respect the limits the item puts on failure cases.

## Literal and class audit

Did the review find every member of each finding class? A finding repaired alone leaves the rest of its class.

Record the inspected scope, reproducible search or comparison, all members, affected relations and earliest owner of each finding class. Cross-reference members in other units or explain isolation. Match quotes and locators to the revision. Similar wording triggers inspection.

After locking the independent reading, compare decisions with requirements and parents. Where classification applies, verify its coverage for the layer and recheck [changed work](../../../story/correction/inspection.md#changed-work).
