# Common requirement discovery

The discovery result answers for the entire inspected layer population. Authors write it where the story document layout puts it, from findings and inspected negatives; the scaffold supplies no completed negative.

## Requirement ownership

Did discovery find each requirement from the instructions, adopted language and production and give it one accountable owner? Record missing creative decisions before selecting their respondents.

- Inspect instructions, promises, subjects, sources, assets, genre, form and the body. Adopt only a condition that changes what can be made or omitted. Describe its observable choice, failure, audience effect, smallest accountable population and repair owner.
- Assign language to the language contract, form to the work, facts to settings or the [short-route owner](../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds). Move requirements found only in guidance, selectors or status notes to their owner and update consumers under [closed passes](../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes). Research a proposed shared criterion.
- Check regional, period, social and translated usage, medium, register, address, honorifics, names, titles and expressions of numbers, dates, age, color, material, dimensions and units. Name affected speakers, passages, audience cues and owners. Check period claims under the declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) and record departures.
- Inspect settings, research, screenplay, source and available acceptance records for unowned elements, unsupported behavior or staging, missing tools and failures no contract covers. Use installed actions, [shots and cuts](../../../story/layers/shots.md) and studies as applicable.
- Reconcile the whole inspected scope at each [checkpoint](../../../story/planning/discovery.md#checkpoints). Assign local gaps a work contract or folder. Keep scaffold candidates local until corroborated elsewhere. For a negative, name the risk and adequate owner. Leave unread scope open. Record each [user decision](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#asking-the-user) as fixed, delegated or open.
