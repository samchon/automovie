# Common requirement discovery

The discovery result answers for the entire inspected layer population. Authors write it where the story document layout puts it, from findings and inspected negatives; the scaffold supplies no completed negative.

## Requirement ownership

Did discovery find each requirement from the instructions, adopted language and production and give it one accountable owner? Record missing creative decisions before selecting their respondents.

### Finding requirements

Read the instructions, promises, subjects, sources, assets, genre, form and body. Adopt a requirement when it changes what can be made or omitted. Identify the choice, observable failure, audience effect, smallest population that can answer and owner of a repair.

Inspect settings, research, screenplay, source and available acceptance records for unowned elements, unsupported behavior or staging, missing tools and failures no contract covers. Check against installed actions, [shots and cuts](../../../story/layers/shots.md) and relevant studies.

### Assigning an owner

Assign language to the language contract, form to the work, and facts to settings or the [short-route owner](../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds). Move requirements found only in guidance, selectors or status notes to their owner. Update consumers under [closed passes](../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes).

For language requirements, identify the speakers, passages, audience cues and owners affected by the medium, variety, period, social register or translation. Check address, honorifics, names, titles and how people express numbers, dates, ages, colors, materials, dimensions and units. Verify period claims under the declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis) and record departures.

At each discovery checkpoint, account for what the team inspected.

- Assign a local gap to a work contract or folder.
- Research proposed shared criteria. Keep them local pending corroboration.
- For a negative, name the risk and the owner that covers it.

Leave unread scope open. Record each [user decision](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#asking-the-user) as fixed, delegated or open.
