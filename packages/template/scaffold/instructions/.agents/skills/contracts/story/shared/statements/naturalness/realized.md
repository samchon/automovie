# Realized speech expression statements

Contract: [naturalness/realized.md](../../naturalness/realized.md).

## Speech yield

1. The exchange holds none of these:
    - an explanation of facts already shared;
    - an argument recited as a list;
    - a generic declaration of the unit's theme;
    - neighboring action repeated in another register;
    - matching metaphors traded between speakers;
    - inappropriate status or relationship language;
    - uniform neutral exposition;
    - a reply whose meaning shifted while an editor tried to preserve its words.
2. Where the language marks address, honorifics, social distance or sentence endings, those choices realize the established relationship and the act performed here: no level changes without motive, and no common neutral register erases authority or distance.
3. Disguise, irony, deliberate rudeness, quotation, mixed registers, adopted ritual questions, authority exercised through general statements, weaponized proverbs, consequential repetition and speech habits stay when the scene establishes their function and consequence. They do not authorize changing the relationship itself.
4. What each participant says, wants, knows, gains and loses is preserved.
5. Nothing is standardized into short lines, interruptions, hidden meanings or added conflict, and no new demand, fact, victory or outcome is invented.
6. Named failures follow the decision and exception rules of [common expression](common.md#reads-as-written).
