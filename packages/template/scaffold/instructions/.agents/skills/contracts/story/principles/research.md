# Research principles

Each study in `docs/research/` other than the index answers these items under an H1 naming its path. Keep originals in `references/` and index them. Its consumers cite the study.

## Depicted practice

Can an author use the observed sequence, timing and interplay of participants and objects to write or program the practice at the depicted level? Give the laws, limits and sourced ranges the consumer needs. Record deliberate stylization.

Apply each criterion where the subject and a written consumer require it under [when to study](../../../story/planning/research.md#when-to-study).

- Use rules to establish permitted actions and outcomes. Ground claims about frequency, tempo and spatial choices at the depicted level in observed passages or measured studies.
- Establish how replay time relates to event time before measuring duration. For distance, account for camera motion and establish scale in the plane of movement. An uncalibrated view can still show the order of visible actions. Ground claims about intervals hidden by a cut or occlusion separately.
- Trace a continuous exchange at the depicted level. Identify what prompted each choice, the participants' positions and concurrent actions, elapsed time and what produced the outcome. Include consequential waits, recovery, objects and surfaces. A sequence composed entirely of installed motions needs the same observations.
- For a technique beyond the installed vocabulary, describe the law in the performer's frame. Give key poses, travel from the starting distance, phases, timing, reach, contact surfaces and weight transfer. Use the package documentation for mechanics it already supplies.
- Record how the practice is filmed and the measurable targets for picture and sound. Give ranges, method, source and uncertainty at the depicted level. Explain a translation across disciplines and the real analogue and departures of an invented action.

## Traceable sources

Does each observation identify its retained source, locator and observation type so a reader can check it?

Classify each observation as a reported measurement, direct observation, inference or production choice. Give the time range for an observed passage and preserve uncertainty in measured values. When a source establishes only a rule, pose or result, identify the sequence or duration that still needs evidence.
