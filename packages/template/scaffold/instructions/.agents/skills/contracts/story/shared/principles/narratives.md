# Narrative unit principles

Each treatment, script and final screenplay H2 answers for its own progression. Treatments decide it in an event document; scripts and the screenplay realize it. Settings does not answer these questions.

Statements: [statements/principles/narratives.md](../statements/principles/narratives.md).

## Unit progression

Does the unit leave a recognizable change in events, understanding, relationships, emotion, pressure, rhythm or formal effect, and a reason to continue that arises from this work? A unit that repeats the inherited state without a changed force gives the audience nothing to follow.

## Parent differentiation

Does each layer make its own decisions: the treatment choosing events and information order, the script executing them as partitioned action with dialogue, staging and view, and the final screenplay finishing that execution as image, sound, attention, duration and transitions? Changing tense, formatting or volume without those decisions hands the next layer a copy.
