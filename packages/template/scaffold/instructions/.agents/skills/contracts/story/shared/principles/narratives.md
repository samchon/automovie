# Narrative unit principles

Each treatment, script and final screenplay H2 answers for its own progression. Treatments decide it in an event document; scripts and the screenplay realize it. Settings does not answer these questions.

Statements: [statements/principles/narratives.md](../statements/principles/narratives.md).

## Unit progression

Does the unit create a recognizable change in events, understanding, relationships, emotion, pressure, rhythm or form that gives the audience a reason to continue?

## Parent differentiation

Does the treatment decide events and reveal order, the script stage the action, dialogue and view, and the screenplay finish image, sound, timing, emphasis and transitions for its audience?
