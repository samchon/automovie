# Narrative unit principles

Each treatment, script, and final screenplay H2 answers for its own progression. Treatments decide it in an event document; scripts and screenplay realize it in their respective forms. Settings does not answer these questions. Check each item's [statements](../statements/principles/narratives.md) before answering it.

## Does the unit create a legible change and reason to continue? {#unit-progression}

Does the unit leave a recognizable result in events, understanding, relationships, emotion, pressure, rhythm or formal effect, and a reason to continue that arises from this work? A unit that repeats the inherited state without a changed force gives the audience nothing to follow.

## Does it perform this layer's work rather than repeat its parent? {#parent-differentiation}

Does treatment turn facts into chosen events and information order, script turn those events into partitioned executable action with actual dialogue, staging and view, and final screenplay turn that execution into finished image, sound, attention, duration and transitions? Changing tense, formatting or volume without making the layer's decisions hands the next layer a copy.
