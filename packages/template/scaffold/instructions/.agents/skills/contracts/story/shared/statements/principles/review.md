# Review unit statements

## Independent reading

1. The locked observation has at least one exact body quotation and locator, and it is kept for units with no finding too.
2. Headings, annotations, another unit, remembered wording and quotations selected after a verdict do not establish the reading.
3. Every finding identifies its passage, violated obligation and failure condition, recipient loss and earliest repair owner.
4. Each finding the author declined is recorded with the author's reason and the reviewer's rebuttal or withdrawal.
5. Upstream knowledge fills no gap the recipient experienced, and author confidence, repair cost, previous verdicts and taste dismiss no loss.
6. Where the pass ran independent classification, the named failures, such as those the scripts' [decision rule](../../naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written) lists, were classified before author defenses, and the classifier resolved ambiguity in context.
7. A named failure is protected only under a proven contractual exception. Other candidates use the [retention test](../../../../../story/answers.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate).
8. Fragile achievements are recorded with quotations and functions, or the record states that none were identified. Their function is protected, not a defective sentence.
9. The review gives repair directions, not prose for adoption. Counterfactual sentences belong only to the retention comparison.
10. The review judges the applicable question's defined scope: examples leave room for another proven loss, and taste does not widen a named-failure boundary.

## Literal and class audit

1. Each finding class records its inspected scope, a reproducible search or comparison, all discovered members, the affected relations and the earliest owner.
2. It references other review units where they inspected a member, and a genuinely isolated finding states its reason.
3. Quotations and locators match the inspected revision.
4. Where the pass ran independent classification, its inspection record covered every required sentence and parent comparison at the inspected snapshot before author evidence, and handled revised passages and changed dependencies under [changed work](../../../../../story/correction/inspection.md#changed-work) without silently reversing unchanged classifications.
5. Following [independent reading](../../../../../review/independent-reading.md), the body was compared with its requirements, and every decision with its inherited parent.
6. Similar wording prompts inspection, not automatic failure.
