# Final screenplay population obligations

The complete population answers once per question at its current stage: construction judges the plan and provisional material and timing; delivery also judges actual realization. A draft, partial animatic or local shot cannot establish completed delivery.

## Does the complete audiovisual plan realize its parents and delivery promise? {#complete-realization}

Map every long-route script execution to final units and each final unit to actual parents. A short maps its brief decisions and its screenplay-owned event and execution decisions without invented skipped stages. Preserve identity, events, choices, consequences, states, relationships, knowledge and reveal order while judging new image-sound relations, attention, duration and transitions across the whole. At construction, assess complete plan and sufficient material, naming provisional performance, placeholders and future source, sound, edit or output work. Unmade descendants do not block screenplay construction; absent required delivery parts remain gaps.

Connect actual descendants and observations as they exist. Code references and source checks cannot prove output realization. At construction, report provisional timing, its basis and needed performance and editing measurements. At delivery, measure actual assembled total duration and release units against the declared duration and format, distinguishing script estimates from recorded speech, performed action, edit timing and the measured file. Report justified ceilings and the effect of removing only repetitions actually judged redundant; functional refrains count. Shortfall alone is no defect, but compressed required experience, missing event assignment or poor partition may be. Repair the earliest cause without padding, new events or stretched sound.

## Are revisions accountable to the delivered version? {#published-version}

For an existing public release, identify version, changed scope, reason, semantic effect and required notices across the release population. Distinguish spelling or metadata, display or rendering, factual, semantic and delivery-required corrections. Follow applicable platform requirements and align settings or brief, treatments, scripts, screenplay, source, media, edit and review to the new version. Changed goals, relationships or results need accountable handling, not unnoticed replacement. Audience reaction or release gaps alone cannot justify canon changes. If no public version exists, identify that population; future editability never excuses skipped pre-delivery review.
