# Brief principles

Each brief H2 answers as one facet of its subject, owning the anchored H3 decisions beneath it. It does not answer for every film or scene.

Statements: [statements/principles/brief.md](../statements/principles/brief.md).

## Needed decisions

Does it name its subject and settle a value, scope and the conditions, limits or exceptions that affect use, specifically enough that a screenplay author or reviewer can hold the films to it? A screenplay that must invent a decision has no basis to hold to.
