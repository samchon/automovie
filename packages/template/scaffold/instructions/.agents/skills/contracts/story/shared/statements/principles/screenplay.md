# Final screenplay statements

## Audiovisual realization

Realization:

1. Events and execution are inherited from the scripts in a long work, or decided here on the short route.
2. The used time and space, participants or forces, conditions, action, spoken words, response and resulting state are established as far as the chosen form uses them.
3. Settled events, choices, relationships, consequences, world values and reveal order are preserved: realization changes no fact, closes no open relationship, alters no cause or outcome, and takes places, objects, appearance and capacities from the parents. Exception: on a short, the screenplay decides what the [brief leaves to it](../../../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds).
4. Important choices, exchanges and sustained tension, humor or loss play in full and get the time to be felt, instead of becoming synopsis. Exception: concise connection and summary elsewhere, and an already complete short utterance or inserted artifact.
5. Where a parent leaves no room for this layer, its owner is repaired and no length is manufactured.

Performance:

6. Every required observable movement, interaction, reaction, expression the adopted representation can show, and sound is decided through its preparation, development and resulting state, not as an inventory of procedures.
7. Where physical action matters, support, weight transfer, contact or separation, recovery and connecting movement are executable, and no named technique, isolated key pose or emotional label stands in for them.
8. Where a study exists, a specialist performance meets its density and distribution targets for the depicted level, with chained sequences, tempo changes and spatial strategy drawn from it.
9. A recurring motif is varied and interleaved with other exchanges, not repeated as one shape.
10. Authored visible participants and audible events have their performances in the commissioned output. Writing review establishes the plan and delivery review its realization.

Orientation and access:

11. The audience can follow who, when, where, what is happening, what a reference means and what its access permits, and each piece of orientation arrives when a name, relationship, world fact or spatial arrangement first matters, without a lecture that stops the action.
12. Across joins, consequential position, direction, gaze, object and bodily state, and the source and temporal relation of sound are preserved, or their deliberate change is legible.
13. Visible and off-screen speech, audience-only narration, screen text, and simultaneous or successive actions are distinguished where that changes meaning.
14. What the camera shows a character cannot see grants that character no knowledge. Changes of access have legible boundaries, and withholding has a reason within the declared form.
15. Deliberate ambiguity or fragmentation still lets the audience tell its rule from an accidental omission.

Attention and duration:

16. Duration estimates are marked provisional until the performed action and the measured file establish them.
17. Concrete details come from the inherited scene and change action, relationship, atmosphere, understanding or thematic force, and emotion is realized through their context before its effect is demanded.
18. Emphasis, images, motifs and image-sound relations are chosen as the scene needs them, and none of these replaces the audience's experience:
    - a static appearance inventory;
    - a sensory checklist;
    - a generic mood label;
    - an unnecessary factual explanation;
    - an after-the-fact interpretation.
19. Restrained detail, nonemotional atmosphere and deliberate gaps work when their cues let the audience experience their function.
20. Inward meaning reaches the audience through performance, image, sound, or an adopted narration or caption form.

Language:

21. Dialogue, narration and screen text read as idiomatic in the work's adopted language, variety, period and register, with no translated construction nobody would use there.
22. Action lines are judged for precision, who does what, where and with what result, and never for literary style.

## Beat decisions

The scene file:

1. The scene file's first section opens with the standing state, stated once: the place and its light, the cast present and where each starts, any person or object only this scene shows, the camera default and the sound bed.
2. Removed addresses follow [identities](../../../../../story/layers/notation.md#identities).

What each beat decides:

3. The beat names who and what acts by their settings facts; on a short, by brief decisions, or by the standing state for a person or object only this scene shows.
4. Each participant has an intent, and the beat states its outcome and each body's reaction or consequence.
5. Space, camera and sound entries appear only where they change from the standing state as earlier beats left it. Exception: stillness the scene decides is stated where it matters.
6. The beats cover the whole running time with the continuous response of everyone present. No stretch is summarized, as in "they dance".

What the modules can perform:

7. Each action, named in plain words, is one the installed motion vocabulary performs, alone or joined with others, or the one its cited research law describes.
8. Each camera entry states what it changes in the terms the installed camera grammar performs. One that starts a new shot gives that shot's size and subject, opening as [records](../../../../../story/layers/notation.md#records) sets.
9. An outcome of a studied practice, such as whether a strike lands or a lift holds, is decided in the beat and is one the study's recorded limits allow for the written intents and the performers' settings. An outcome those limits forbid is a defect, not dramatic license.
10. The beat asks nothing the adopted representation cannot show, such as a facial expression on a faceless polygon man. Exceptions: a commissioned generated film's beat states the reactions it needs, such as a facial expression, and the generation prompt carries them; a direct 3D film whose representation adopts a face states the expressions that face shows.

Measurements:

11. Every coordinate and offset is left to the code that derives it, from the installed actions or the research law that determines it, and no decision is expressed only as a number.
12. A beat may state its start second within its section as a plan, marked provisional like a duration estimate; the code derives the exact timing.
13. A true anchor is written exactly:
    - a line, lyric or music event the picture syncs to;
    - a count the audience perceives;
    - a key event the edit lands on.
