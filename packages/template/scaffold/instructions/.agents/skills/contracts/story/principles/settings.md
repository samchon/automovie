# Settings principles

Each settings H2 answers as one facet of its subject, owning the anchored H3 facts beneath it. It does not answer for every person or place in the work.

## Usable facts

Does the record name its subject and settle a decision, scope and applicable conditions, costs and limits so later authors can apply it? Include only facets a consumer uses.

Settle only facts the planned scenes or arcs use, including world rules, people, relationships, places, objects, tone and look. For an open decision, give its range, effects, limits and what settles it. Record a practiced activity's level and style and cite its study.

For a place, establish which spaces connect, who can see or reach them and what blocks passage or view. For an independently used object, establish which instance it is, how it can be used and the initial state later scenes inherit.

Put story-defining measurements here and other values in code and tests with sources in [research](../../../story/planning/research.md). Distinguish invention from external claims. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), cite authoritative evidence at the relevant edition, passage, date or page. Record disagreements and limit precision to what the sources establish.
