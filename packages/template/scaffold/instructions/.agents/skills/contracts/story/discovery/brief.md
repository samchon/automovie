# Brief requirement discovery

The discovery result covers all instructions, references, assets and planned films under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership).

## Instructions and backcast

Before bulk writing, did discovery assign each consequential instruction to an owner and identify the cast and other subjects that need brief records?

Assign the premises, people, names, audience information load, delivery and representation limits, assets, source boundaries and sensitive subjects to an owner. Record open decisions and conflicting instructions.

Check source claims under the declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis). Work back from the planned films and assets to find the subjects that need [brief records](../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds). The screenplay decides events, action and reveal order.
