# Brief requirement discovery

The discovery result covers all instructions, references, assets and planned films under the [common discovery boundary](common.md#requirement-ownership).

## Instructions and backcast

Before bulk writing, did discovery assign each consequential instruction to an owner and identify the cast and other subjects that need brief records?

- Assign premises, people, names, delivery and representation limits, audience information load, assets, source boundaries and sensitive subjects to an owner or an open decision. Report conflicting instructions.
- Check source claims under the declared [factual basis](../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis). Work back from the planned films and assets to the subjects that need [brief records](../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds). The screenplay decides events, action and reveal order.
