# Common population statements

The detailed statements of the [common population obligations](../../obligations/common.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Scope and ownership {#scope-and-ownership}

1. Every required beginning, ending, interval, person, event, fact, constraint, relationship and result is mapped to its smallest current owner.
2. The answer reports omissions, incompatible duplicate owners, independently useful content hidden in unrelated units, and files whose removal changes no fact, progression, relationship, experience or formal effect.
3. Independently changeable or refutable facts and progressions are split, dependent attributes and inseparable continuous operations stay together, and purposeless material is removed or reconnected.
4. An omission rests on an authorized scope decision.
5. Central and peripheral development are compared: settings or brief and treatments hold sufficient decisions, scripts and screenplay sufficient execution and experience, and core omissions and disproportionate expansion are identified.
6. Counts, lengths, parent-child ratios and diffs locate passages to read; they are not verdicts.

## Layer boundaries {#layer-boundaries}

1. The semantic owners hold: settings establishes world facts, appearance and baseline constraints; treatments events, event time and place and reveal order; scripts partition, scene execution, staged audiovisual state and meaningful view; the final screenplay finished emphasis, image-sound relations, transitions and temporal experience.
2. A short gives world decisions to its brief and event and execution decisions to its screenplay, never to invented skipped stages.
3. Language, audience, variants, registers, code-switching, access rules and expressive form have one identifiable instruction or work-contract owner, while character voice remains a world property. Declared nonstandard forms and their audience context are preserved.
4. Downstream inventions, duplicated authority and planning language that replaces a layer's output are reported, and the earliest owner and its consumers repaired. Layout and adapter mechanics do not decide these boundaries.
5. Color, material, dimension and unit expressions across the population agree with established values. Where wording would make artists invent incompatible values, the needed reference or conversion is defined at that value's owner.
6. Those values are preserved downstream. Exception: no appearance inventory is required everywhere, and idiomatic perception that changes no fact stays.
