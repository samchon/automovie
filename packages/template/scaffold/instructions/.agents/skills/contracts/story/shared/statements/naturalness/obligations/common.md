# Common expression population statements

The detailed statements of the [common expression population obligation](../../../naturalness/obligations/common.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## Recurrent frames {#recurrent-frames}

1. Sentence and direction molds, closing formulas, gestures, reactions, block shapes and repeated modifiers are compared against the circumstances they serve. Different props or synonyms do not change a repeated operation.
2. Preserved refrains, speech habits, contrasts and declared regularity are compared with a workable population without that mold, and the lost function is shown.
3. The whole discovered cause and its members are repaired before the next full reading. Bulk synonym replacement or another kind of padding is no repair, and frequency locates a pattern without deciding whether it works.
4. The complete population was read in its actual language for recurring translation interference, awkward combinations, unnecessary pronouns, relationship-erasing registers, and sentence or narration endings that impose one rhythm on different material. Local grammatical correctness does not establish natural whole-work reception.
5. Exception: adopted tense, voice, period and institutional language, dialect and purposeful recurrence are protected through their effect. Rotating endings or varying syntax to equalize counts repairs nothing.
