# Settings population statements

The detailed statements of the [settings population obligations](../../obligations/settings.md), one section per item. Check the work against each before answering, as [items and statements](../../../../../contracts/SKILL.md#items-and-statements) describes. This document is not an evidence target.

## World and scope {#world-and-scope}

1. The answering file declares the audience, the central experience, the questions or formal effects it develops, its recurring rewards and the title's promise. User-fixed title wording is not a taste question.
2. It establishes where delivery begins and ends, its inclusions and exclusions, its relation to a larger work, and its release units with their number and duration ranges.
3. Where these are not supplied, a provisional plan is derived from the promise, material and production limits, names its grounds and uncertainty, and is revised when those grounds change.
4. A series establishes what recurs, what closes per release and what develops across releases. Scripts choose the release boundaries.
5. Scale serves the audience and available material, not copied size rules or padding.
6. The adopted representation is declared with what it cannot show, such as faceless polygon men, so later layers write only what it carries.
7. A dimension the commission excludes is excluded once here, not in every record.

## People and audiovisual world {#people-and-audiovisual-world}

Ownership and depth:

1. Every world rule, power, institution, person, relationship, place and object a scene will rely on has one owner, at a depth that matches its consequence.
2. Central and recurring agents have their situation, drives, values, fears, abilities and limits, knowledge and errors, observable behavior, recognizable appearance and, when heard, voice. Nonhuman forces have their inputs, operation and thresholds without invented psychology.
3. Every presence a scene shows, such as an official, partner or crowd, has behavior for its whole presence.
4. Consequential relationships are defined from both sides with their change conditions; treatments own the actual progression.
5. Places and objects carry the identity, layout and use that scenes stage, and the shared material, costume, visual and audible grammar distinguishes factions, regions, eras and classes where they matter.

Coherence:

6. No two decisions contradict: world rules against what agents can do, what each agent knows against how they learned it, relationships seen from each side, and baseline states against their change conditions.
7. The records explain why an initial threat, mystery or injustice persists: who could end it, and what stops them.
8. A contradiction is repaired at its earliest owner.
9. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), derived chronology, ages, distances, travel, money and capacities agree across records, and every consequential participant the source names is identified. Fiction's own quantities belong to research and code.
