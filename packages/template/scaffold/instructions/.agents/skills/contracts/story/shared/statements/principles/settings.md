# Settings statements

## Usable facts

1. The record's decisions are of these kinds:
    - the world and its rules;
    - people's personality, values, motives, abilities and knowledge;
    - relationships;
    - the identity and use of places and objects;
    - the tone and look.
2. It holds only what the planned scenes and arcs need: no facet nothing uses, no inventory of incidental defaults, no "not applicable".
3. Keep an unknown only when its established effects and limits constrain later choices. State the permitted range and what decides an open choice.
4. State measurements only when they are story decisions. Otherwise put their values in code and tests and cite their sources in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md).
5. A practiced activity records its level and style decisions and cites the study that holds its vocabulary.
6. Distinguish adopted invention from factual claims and cite external facts.
7. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), each covered claim has direct primary or authoritative evidence at its edition, passage, date or page, records meaningful disagreement, and reduces its precision where the evidence is weaker.
