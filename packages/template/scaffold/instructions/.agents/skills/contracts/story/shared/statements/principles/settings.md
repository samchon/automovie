# Settings statements

Contract: [principles/settings.md](../../principles/settings.md).

## Usable facts {#usable-facts}

1. The record names its subject and settles a value, its scope and the conditions, limits, costs or exceptions that affect use.
2. It records a decision of these kinds: the world and its rules, people's personality, values, motives, abilities and knowledge, relationships, the identity and use of places and objects, and the tone and look.
3. A bounded unknown stays unknown only when its established effects and limits constrain later solutions. A consequential open decision states its permitted range and decision condition instead of posing as mystery.
4. It holds only what the planned scenes and arcs need: no facet nothing uses, no inventory of incidental defaults, no "not applicable".
5. A measurement such as a dimension, light position or timing appears only when it is itself a story decision. Otherwise its source is in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md) and its value in the code and tests that realize it.
6. A practiced activity records its level and style decisions and cites the study that holds its vocabulary.
7. Adopted invention is kept distinct from any fact claimed as true, and an external fact the record asserts, such as a sport's rule, cites its source. Exception: invented choices need no outside authority.
8. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), each covered claim has direct primary or authoritative evidence at its edition, passage, date or page, with meaningful disagreement, and reduced precision where the evidence is weaker.

## Record and boundary {#record-and-boundary}

1. The definition, settled content, conditions and open matters come in a consistent order for this kind of record.
2. Each independently used subject has one file, with its inseparable attributes together.
3. Each concept has one term and each fact one owner, and a shared fact links to its owner.
4. The record states facts in prose for readers and never describes code. Rendering and generation controls belong to the production's source.
5. It establishes world facts, baseline states and change conditions, and leaves chosen events, causal progression, choices, results, setups, payoffs and reveal order to treatments. Exception: a fixed historical result or time-specific status may constrain treatments without prewriting how the film reaches or reveals it.
6. A lasting state a later layer established, such as a wound or a revealed relationship, is recorded here and cites the unit that established it.
7. The cinematography domain owns the film's look. Editing and audience access belong to production instructions or work contracts, and a character's voice remains a world property.
