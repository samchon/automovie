# Settings statements

## Usable facts

1. The record's decisions are of these kinds:
    - the world and its rules;
    - people's personality, values, motives, abilities and knowledge;
    - relationships;
    - the identity and use of places and objects;
    - the tone and look.
2. It holds only what the planned scenes and arcs need: no facet nothing uses, no inventory of incidental defaults, no "not applicable".
3. An unknown stays unknown only when its established effects and limits constrain later solutions, and a consequential open decision states its permitted range and decision condition instead of posing as mystery.
4. A measurement such as a dimension or a timing appears only when it is itself a story decision. Otherwise its value is in the code and tests that realize it, and any source it draws on in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md).
5. A practiced activity records its level and style decisions and cites the study that holds its vocabulary.
6. Adopted invention is kept distinct from any fact claimed as true, and an external fact the record asserts, such as a sport's rule, cites its source. Exception: invented choices need no outside authority.
7. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), each covered claim has direct primary or authoritative evidence at its edition, passage, date or page, records meaningful disagreement, and reduces its precision where the evidence is weaker.
