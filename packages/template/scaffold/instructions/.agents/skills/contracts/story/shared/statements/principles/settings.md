# Settings statements

Contract: [principles/settings.md](../../principles/settings.md).

## Usable facts

1. The record names its subject and settles a value, its scope and the conditions, limits, costs or exceptions that affect use.
2. It records a decision of these kinds:
    - the world and its rules;
    - people's personality, values, motives, abilities and knowledge;
    - relationships;
    - the identity and use of places and objects;
    - the tone and look.
3. A bounded unknown stays unknown only when its established effects and limits constrain later solutions.
4. A consequential open decision states its permitted range and decision condition instead of posing as mystery.
5. The record holds only what the planned scenes and arcs need: no facet nothing uses, no inventory of incidental defaults, no "not applicable".
6. A measurement such as a dimension, light position or timing appears only when it is itself a story decision. Otherwise its source is in [research](../../../../../story/planning/research.md) and its value in the code and tests that realize it.
7. A practiced activity records its level and style decisions and cites the study that holds its vocabulary.
8. Adopted invention is kept distinct from any fact claimed as true.
9. An external fact the record asserts, such as a sport's rule, cites its source. Exception: invented choices need no outside authority.
10. Under a declared [factual basis](../../../../../story/planning/requirements.md#factual-basis), each covered claim has direct primary or authoritative evidence at its edition, passage, date or page.
11. The claim records meaningful disagreement, and reduces its precision where the evidence is weaker.
