# Entity statements

## Settings realization

1. Every fact the entity realizes is named by its record path and anchor, with how it is realized.
2. Show each cited fact in the entity or identify the consumer that carries it.
3. Hand off facts the adopted form cannot show. Model them only on a commissioned detail option that can show them.
4. Derive body parameters from the cast record for a cast member or the scene's standing state for an incidental person. Without a cast layer, use the commissioned appearance record, the screenplay or the standalone asset's instructions, as applicable.
5. Derive each law, action or archetype parameter from a cited fact and keep it within its owner's range.
6. Source each measurement from its [study](../../../story/planning/research.md#when-to-study) and section, or from an installed package that supplies the measurement and its source.
7. Placements, weights, texture coordinates and measurements derive from the subject's generating definition, so changing the detail option moves none of them.
8. Keep new decisions with the record that owns them. Cite a [short's scene decisions](../../../story/layers/brief.md#what-it-holds) by file and anchor. A directly written screenplay owns its story choices. The instructions own [implementation choices for a fixed screenplay](../../../story/correction/repair.md#concretization) and decisions for a standalone asset.
9. The same prepared body serves the polygon man and its motion.

## Object shape

1. The entity is a class with `readonly` fields, constructed only from its cited facts and immutable configuration.
2. It is composed of small fragments rather than inherited.
3. It imports no other entity.
4. Each capability returns an immutable record of what the subject does and to whom, and never a pose, coordinate, offset or time.
5. Return the installed vocabulary's composition for people and crowds, including [registered extensions](../../../motion/laws/extension.md). Use a commissioned or user-chosen clip only after [retargeting](../../../motion/retargeting.md) it to the person.
6. State such as equipment by story day, fatigue or a learned habit exists only as an explicit snapshot computed at schedule resolution.
7. A habit is a trigger-to-response rule, with the trigger and response its fact describes and the timing its research supports.

## Controls and source

1. The entity exposes the controls its scenes' behaviors and variation use.
2. A commissioned detail option has each deformation its scenes need, and needs no rig complexity they do not use. A coarse form needs neither.
3. Every version of the subject the scenes use is a parameter or the seed of this module, and no copy of the module with edited constants exists.
4. The coarse form is the default, and each commissioned detail builds from the same definition.
5. A subject an installed package already provides follows the production principles. Its actual producer output reaches the drawing and interaction, including its connection frames; an unused import or a parallel handcrafted model does not establish reuse.
6. The entity rebuilds from a fixed source with its usage rights.
7. An adopted or converted asset states which channels and deformation the conversion and the runtime retain.
8. An object a person holds, uses, sits on, rides or fights with declares the affordances the scenes' actions attach to, under [objects people use](../../../development/entities.md#objects).
