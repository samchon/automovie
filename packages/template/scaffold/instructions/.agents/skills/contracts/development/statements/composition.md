# Composition statements

## Screenplay realization

1. Realize the standing state and each cited beat as written, including every change in position, camera and sound. For commissioned generation, carry reactions the representation cannot show in the prompt.
2. Each state continues across cuts until a beat changes it.
3. A film plays every scene file of its folder in the order of their numbers, with the screenplay's transitions, titles and subtitles, and fills its running time.
4. A film takes its raster, frame rate and running time from the delivery profile in [project context](../../../production/project-context.md), and its grade and control channels from the screenplay and that record.
5. The scene's comment opens with its screenplay citation and a summary of what happens.
6. A `Concretization:` paragraph names each decision the code had to make where the screenplay was vague. It never holds an exchange, an outcome or a reaction, which only the screenplay decides.
7. The same change records each concretization in the screenplay, under [concretization](../../../story/correction/repair.md#concretization). Exception: a user's fixed screenplay, whose concretizations go to the instructions.
8. A catalog case composition takes its case parameters and names in its answer's prose the record fact or study section it exhibits, with no evidence tag to it, and the catalog film lists every case the screenplay uses.

## Composition only

1. The declaration only combines entity capabilities, interaction entry points and consequences, through the operators of the installed motion vocabulary and engine.
2. It places absolute time only at the screenplay's anchors.
3. It holds no calculation, constant, coordinate, direct law import, URL parameter, archive load or runtime selection. Each of these is a capability missing from its owner and is added there.
4. A person's or a group's action reaches the scene through its cast or crowd entity's capability, as a composition of the installed motion vocabulary. Exception: a clip the user chose or the commission supplies, [retargeted](../../../motion/retargeting.md) onto the person.
5. A refusal from an installed package is repaired at the axis it names, under [refusals](../../../motion/compose/refusals.md). No declaration catches one and carries on, or widens a value to silence it.
6. The catalog and the film call the same capabilities with the same kind of parameters, and no scene keeps its own variant of a module.
7. The film resolves once, from one seed.
8. A defect a film shows is repaired in its module and accepted again in its catalog case, never in the scene.

## Living scene

1. Every entity performs its role and responds throughout its presence. A hold may retain breathing, gaze and stance sway. Preserve any stillness the screenplay requires.
2. The scene never falls back to isolated single actions or one axis of movement.
3. Each beat's spatial change moves its participants between the set's zones and landmarks.
4. The camera reframes, follows and moves with motivated action. Exception: a fixed view the screenplay decided.
5. Every secondary action is a layer the composition sets under [composing a performance](../../../motion/compose/composition.md#action-axes) or one the screenplay decided.
6. Where a study set measurable targets for what a viewer sees and hears, the composed behaviors aim at them, and delivery review then measures them on the picture.
