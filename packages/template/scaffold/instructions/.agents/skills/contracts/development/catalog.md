# Catalog

Catalog cases in `src/composition/scenes/catalog/<module>/` and the catalog film in `src/composition/films/catalog.ts` answer this contract and [implementation](implementation.md). Review the result under [catalog cases](../../motion/catalog-cases.md).

## Exhibited fact

Does the case exhibit its stated fact through its parameters, and does the catalog film name what each selected case exhibits?

A case traces its behavior or entity parameters to the fact in [source comments](../../development/modules.md#documenting-source). For skilled practice, reproduce the cited exchange from the [performance study](../../story/planning/research.md#performance-study). The film names the fact each case exhibits. Cite an accepted result within its [recorded conditions](../../motion/catalog-cases.md#record).

## Catalog film

Does the catalog film list and play every case adopted for its scope, and resolve once from one seed?

Resolve the film once from one seed. Label each interval with the case it plays. A case answers with the interval the film gives it.

## Composition only

Which [shared capabilities](../../development/modules.md#how-the-parts-work-together) does the declaration compose?

A catalog case names the capability and parameter kinds it shares with the screenplay scene. The catalog film names the cases it composes.
