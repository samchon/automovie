# Contract items

A respondent is the declaration, document or record that answers a contract. Source and test declarations answer in doc comments. Story respondents answer in the locations the narrative workflow assigns.

## Item structure

Each H2 asks one question about an achievement and the loss if it is missed. Put the criteria, failure cases and exceptions in the body of that H2. Give it one answer.

Keep conditions together when the same respondent can establish them with the same evidence. Give a different judgment its own item when it needs separate proof or repair. Links may identify sources and repair procedures.

## Answers

1. Check the work against the complete item.
2. Repair what fails.
3. Write an answer true against all of them, without reciting the list.
4. Name in it each criterion that does not apply, with its reason.

Source reviewers verify source answers. The evidence answerer verifies story answers. Report any proven loss under the question unless the item expressly limits its scope.

Treat enumerations as comparison criteria. Keep research citations in their owning records.

## Outside the graph

Check only what a respondent can establish from its content and references. Review generation requests, prompts, control channels and delivered media outside the contract graph.

Record what each reviewer observed on stills, in continuous playback and by listening in the delivery acceptance record.
