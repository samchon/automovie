# Editing

How to change part of an accepted image and keep the rest.

## Instruction edits

<!-- @reference [FreqEdit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01755) examines detail lost over successive image edits. -->

Edit all findings from one inspection together. State corrections, defining traits and what to preserve. Check the whole result before the next round. Return to frozen canon references when detail drifts.

## Masks and markup

Check which input the endpoint's mask or markup addresses, its mask polarity, and whether the mask is a guide or a hard boundary. Keep any crop, resize and feathering aligned for compositing. Outpainting extends the image beyond its current frame.

## Compositing back

Where surrounding pixels must stay identical, composite the approved change over the original. Check the seam and kept pixels on the resulting file; the mask alone does not establish preservation.
