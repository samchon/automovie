# Turnaround views

Turnaround views serve only a commissioned [detail option](../development/entities.md#detail) or standalone asset, as image-to-3D input or a modeling reference under [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md); a coarse entity needs none. They are a set apart from the canon references, posed as the 3D model asks rather than in the canon's neutral pose, and cut out to transparency afterwards where the image generator gives none.

Pose the subject's coarse form as the model's [request](../3d-modeling/diffusion/request.md) sets, render it from fixed cameras at one framing and even light in the views and order [image hints](../3d-modeling/diffusion/image-hints.md) sets, and restyle each view from the frozen canon references as [shot frames](SKILL.md#shot-frames) are; then check that the views agree.

Generated views carry no camera calibration or scale and disagree with each other at pixel level, so they are never measurements; real dimensions come from the research and the entity's code.
