# Creative evidence

Read the complete selected target, respondent, and necessary parent material. Compare every requirement with every relevant part of that respondent; one correct quotation cannot pass the rest of a unit, file, or population. Use [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) to find questions, and installed `@wrtnlabs/evidence` documentation for annotations and `@automovie/evidence` documentation for production composition and checks.

Before answering, find possible failures across the whole respondent and complete [repair by cause](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#repair-by-cause), including its comparison for a retained expression. Answers diagnose the corrected work or grounds for retaining a candidate; they do not argue missing material into existence. Group a repeated cause and reference its already established grounds rather than reciting them in every answer.

Support assertions with literal quotations and real owner addresses. Verify quotations as exact strings in the current body or parent, and verify claimed counts, sequence, causes, places, and states against the whole relevant scope. Answer what the question asks; unnecessary inventories become stale downstream facts. For an inapplicable question, state inspected scope and precise reason through the mechanism that relationship permits. Whole-population exclusion requires the whole population to be inapplicable.

Positive construction answers live in `docs/evidence/construction/units/<layer>/...md` for H2 respondents and `docs/evidence/construction/files/<layer>/...md` for file respondents. The document's H1 names the exact body file or file-and-anchor address, followed by Evidence headings and supporting prose. Detached documents do not support exclusion or exclusion-review rows: place a permitted exclusion in the actual host unit's HTML comment, or in the designated discovery carrier for discovery negatives. Naturalness answers stay in HTML comments attached to their working unit in `docs/naturalness`; file answers attach before its first heading. The [lifecycle](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md) explains transferring reviewed text without those comments.

After an edit within the submitted pass, reconcile its dependent answers and observations in the same repair, including claims that an inherited value is absent after the body supplies it. Recheck new and revised answers against the complete relevant body before handoff. For upstream changes and expression transfer, follow the lifecycle's [closed-pass boundary](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#upstream-repair-and-scope). Preserve unrelated bytes when the requested operation changes only evidence metadata.

Inspect real selections and full diagnostics with installed Evidence commands, then run unfiltered production evidence and validation. Filtered checks establish only their scope. A mechanical pass establishes connected answers; independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) checks their truth and the recipient's experience. Report diagnostics that the lifecycle leaves closed; preserve failed reports and exit statuses. A stale-review refresh is not new creative judgment, and workflow readiness is not an artistic PASS.
