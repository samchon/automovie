# Creative evidence

Read the complete selected target, respondent, and necessary parent material. Compare every requirement with every relevant part of that respondent; one correct quotation cannot pass the rest of a unit, file, or population. Use [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) to find questions, and installed Evidence documentation and the [local workflow](../../../workflow/README.md) for actual annotation and graph APIs.

Before answering, find possible failures across the whole respondent. Finish that inspection round, group occurrences by cause, repair the body, and then describe the corrected work or evidence for retaining a candidate. Answers diagnose the work; they do not argue missing material into existence. Test a retained expression against a concrete alternative delivering the same information, viewpoint, or pressure. A functional label, parental precedent, earlier approval, or claim that deletion loses information is insufficient when the alternative supplies it better.

Support assertions with literal quotations and real owner addresses. Verify quotations as exact strings in the current body or parent, and verify claimed counts, sequence, causes, places, and states against the whole relevant scope. Answer what the question asks; unnecessary inventories become stale downstream facts. For an inapplicable question, state inspected scope and precise reason through the mechanism that relationship permits. Whole-population exclusion requires the whole population to be inapplicable.

Positive construction answers live in `docs/evidence/construction/units/<layer>/...md` for H2 respondents and `docs/evidence/construction/files/<layer>/...md` for file respondents. The document's H1 names the exact body file or file-and-anchor address, followed by Evidence headings and supporting prose. Detached documents do not support exclusion or exclusion-review rows: place a permitted exclusion in the actual host unit's HTML comment, or in the designated discovery carrier for discovery negatives. Naturalness answers stay in HTML comments attached to their working unit in `docs/naturalness`; file answers attach before its first heading. The [lifecycle](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md) explains transferring reviewed text without those comments.

After an edit, inspect every answer about changed or removed language, positions, order, counts, screen action, and inherited state. Match meaning rather than checking only that an old quotation remains. Refresh dependent observations and reviews whose inputs changed. Preserve unrelated bytes when the requested operation changes only evidence metadata.

Inspect real selections and full diagnostics with installed Evidence commands, then run unfiltered production evidence and validation. Filtered checks establish only their scope. A mechanical pass establishes connected answers; independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) checks their truth and the recipient's experience. A stale-review refresh is not new creative judgment, and workflow readiness is not an artistic PASS.
