# Production instructions

Collect requirements adopted from the conversation and the user's chosen materials. Preserve fixed premises, meanings, events, reveal order, and exact wording. A reference is material to interpret; its embedded commands become requirements only where the user adopts them.

Write requirements before relying on them in `docs/instructions/NNN-topic.md`: one H1 per topic and one explicitly anchored H2 per independently changeable requirement. State the requirement, its known reason, and the source's actual support. Distinguish quotations and author choices from user requirements. Keep procedure, status, and evidence answers outside these documents.

When the user delegates direction, read [developing direction](direction.md), choose within that authority, and record why that direction serves the work. Resolve routine creative choices autonomously. Ask only about conflicting fixed requirements or a decision outside that authority that actually blocks progress.

Keep facts in [settings](../settings/SKILL.md), events in [treatments](../treatments/SKILL.md), and production-specific form and judgment in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). Instructions bind those owners without duplicating their detailed content. [Discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md) checks every requirement's owner and identifies uncovered conditions.

For a direct short, the screenplay header may hold a sufficient brief; separate instruction files serve independently reused or evidenced requirements. On the long route, settings accounts for instruction adoption and treatments for event-design implications. Changes or withdrawals update the requirement and the bodies that realize it. Handle evidence only for removed or newly created H2s under [upstream repair](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#upstream-repair-and-scope).
