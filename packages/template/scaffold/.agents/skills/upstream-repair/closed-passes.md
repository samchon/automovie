# Closed passes and replacement

Closed layers stay closed, whether downstream work repairs their bodies or upstream changes produce diagnostics in them. An upstream change reopens neither layer's evidence, reviews nor approvals. Remove answers for deleted H2s and write answers for newly created H2s only; report other upstream-change diagnostics without repairing them. Preserve original failed reports and their actual statuses.

After an already completed expression transfer, hand off the repaired body and its repair scope with the unfiltered report, preserving that transfer's working edition and frozen original. Stale or pending-transfer results remain failed checks; do not promote the old working edition over the repaired body. Whole-work review does not renew frozen construction approval against transferred expression.

When replacing a unit from scratch, remove obsolete current construction answers, frozen original, working version and corresponding current review records, together with obsolete descendants being replaced. Preserve completed material outside the replacement scope and history in version control. A reused filename carries no old approval into new content. A new construction starts its own [completion](../narrative-completion/SKILL.md) and expression cycle.
