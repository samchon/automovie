# Treatments

Turn reviewed settings into decided events: causes, choices, exchanges, costs, results, and the state and information later events inherit. Use clear explanation, lists, or tables so another author can recreate the same storyline. Complete [discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md), read the settings and instructions relevant to each event, and apply treatments questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md).

Write ordered `NNN-event-title.md` files directly under `docs/body/treatments`, with one H1 per file and explicitly anchored H2s for meaningful decisions. Treatments is the event sequence; [scripts](../scripts/SKILL.md) later determines actual part, episode, sequence, and scene partitions. Do not create release directories here.

Use the production's tensions, beliefs, and systems to design what happens. Establish the first encounter with its promised experience through the relevant agent or process, accumulate consequences through the middle, and decide the ending and aftermath. Preserve the difference between world truth, character knowledge, and what the audience receives. Account for the whole declared work, including setups, payoffs, open threads, and material supporting its promised duration.

Inherit settings by citing owners and carrying facts necessary for execution. Detailed biography, appearance specifications, and institutional explanations remain at their owners rather than becoming lists the next author must recite. Keep material that changes progression or the intended experience. A new fact belongs in settings first; the event changing an established state belongs here.

Decide a detail here when it determines an event's cause or result; leave full dialogue, scene blocking, performance, and expressive audiovisual choices downstream. Where narration has a necessary role, decide its target and added judgment or perspective without prewriting its sentences or restating the scene's result.

Correct explanation and organization in construction; treatments has no separate naturalness pass. Finish [evidence](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md), whole-population comparison, and independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) before scripts. Repair upstream defects at their first owner and renew affected descendants through the [lifecycle](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md).
