# Delivery review

Identify the promised output and inspect that complete output independently, with the unfiltered validation reports. A standalone asset, written screenplay, complete motion hint, direct 3D film and post-diffusion film have different completion claims. Apply only the requested path; respect fixed user choices and record scope through [production decisions](../production/profile.md). Assess the commissioned scope and disclose what remains unproduced; a hint alone establishes no final diffusion quality.

For selected narrative work, first complete required [creative passes](../narrative-completion/SKILL.md). The output reviewer locks the recipient's first response before reading planning documents or author evidence. Then read all current bodies, sources, frozen editions, selected contracts, evidence and configuration. Compare beginning with later consequences and ending, all part boundaries, promises, recurrence, information states and duration distribution. Whole-work review preserves the [closed-pass boundary](../upstream-repair/closed-passes.md); a pilot establishes only its declared slice.

Use [video and conditioning review](video.md) for images, motion hints or finished video, and [asset handoff](assets.md) for models and exports. Review an assembled artifact before diagnosing it against plans or source. Inspect all promised material rather than inferring whole success from tags, local captures or an encoder exit. A screenplay-only commission receives whole-text review without invented playback observations.

Record exact revision, media and output identities, scope, completed checks, actual observations, playback conditions where relevant, and unverified limits under the [delivery questions](../contracts/delivery.md). Repair findings together by [earliest cause](../upstream-repair/SKILL.md), then inspect the complete affected output again. Narrative semantic acceptance follows the [closing-round rule](../review/rounds.md). For a published revision, identify the original edition, changed scope and meaning, reason and required recipient notice, keeping references aligned.
