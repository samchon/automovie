# Video and conditioning review

## Review contiguous segments

During implementation, review each completed short segment in the production's actual moving Viewer before extending it. Use the adopted scene, actors, equipment, view and frame clock, with required sound connected through the actual host. [Motion](../motion/SKILL.md) owns segment scope and continuation; [visual debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md) owns repeatable observation conditions and cost measurement.

Watch the entire segment continuously at normal speed and listen when sound is commissioned. Operate play/pause and the seek/interval controls promised by the host, then replay consequential exchanges, contacts and joins at reduced speed where needed. Gather visible action, reactions, support/contact, camera, sound, console/errors and performance observations in one round before editing. Stills, advancing time logs, sampled poses and unwatched recordings cannot establish continuous playback.

Repair the gathered causes together, then replay the complete affected segment and any accepted material touched by a shared cause under the [review repair loop](SKILL.md). Extend only after a complete segment review has no outstanding or new findings. Continue from its accepted ending and inspect the seam with the next segment; a new camera does not reset continuing action. Use the live Viewer for routine staging and motion debugging; encode when verifying the actual delivery path or final artifact rather than repeating MP4 conversion for each motion correction. Accepted segments still require the whole assembled review below.

## Review the assembled artifact

Watch and, when present, listen to the entire assembled artifact as an audience member who has not read its planning, then lock that response before consulting screenplay or code. Include opening, ending, sustained middle, joins and part transitions. For a separately commissioned still asset, inspect its promised views and shot role; stills alone establish no motion-video result. A whole-video hint must contain the programmed main scenes and complete assembled timeline, including joins and ending.

A diffusion hint must carry the intended staging, actor and object identity, camera, action and timing at its adopted representation level and budget. Stick figures and coarse meshes can fulfill that brief; inspect contacts, support, trajectories, facing, reactions, occlusion and continuity through the complete relevant motion. A description can supply an omitted object only when its visible geometry or interaction is unnecessary for that hint. When a generator is selected, verify its actual input contract instead of assuming it accepts a custom skeleton, depth image or rendered video. [Production generation](../production/generation.md) owns that integration and take adoption. Review the actual accepted inputs and outputs; a representative integration result establishes only its observed compatibility, and unavailable generation leaves that check unverified.

Trace each required screenplay unit into executable shots, the edit's actual inclusion and the resulting output. These relationships can be many-to-many. A function's name or a source reference proves neither invocation nor visible delivery. Use [staging](../motion/staging.md) to check the actual exchange, reactions, view readability and continued state across cuts; a box in the frustum does not prove visible action or a pixel-occlusion threshold. Check source placement, camera and framing against the intended experience, then trace any missing result through accepted media, captions and transitions. Installed packages own capture, import and encoding capabilities; the production supplies its actual host.

For commissioned control channels, inspect the actual [conditioning handoff](../production/conditioning.md): matched frame/camera mapping for time-paired channels, subject coverage, channel meanings and retained palette/sidecars, including converted delivery files. Separate appearance references keep their receiving contract rather than an invented frame pairing. Positional mask colors, omitted instanced skeletons or altered categorical values cannot establish stable identity or a complete pose input. Apply only the channels and compatibility the commission requires.

Use performed and measured timing from actual action, accepted speech, pauses, captions, music and transitions; page or word count cannot establish runtime. Apply [audio acquisition and listening](../audio/acquisition.md) to takes and sound with picture. Changing a voice take can change reactions, subtitles, shot lengths and subsequent timing even with identical words. Return those changes to affected owners and consumers together. A silent motion hint may be complete for its declared timing and action; it cannot establish speech intelligibility or a finished soundtrack.

For commissioned final video, inspect the actual direct 3D or post-diffusion output for preserved action, identity, spatial and temporal continuity, information, emotion, intelligibility of included speech and text, and intended audience effect. Record generator inputs and fixed output bytes separately from the reproducible hint; deterministic replay of adopted bytes does not make external generation deterministic. Judge observed output rather than assuming conditioning guaranteed it. Close the complete assembled artifact through the same [review repair loop](SKILL.md).
