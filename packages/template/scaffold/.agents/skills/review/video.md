# Video and conditioning review

Watch and, when present, listen to the entire assembled artifact as an audience member who has not read its planning, then lock that response before consulting screenplay or code. Include opening, ending, sustained middle, joins and part transitions. For a separately commissioned still asset, inspect its promised views and shot role; stills alone establish no motion-video result. A whole-video hint must contain the programmed main scenes and complete assembled timeline, including joins and ending.

A diffusion hint must carry the intended staging, actor and object identity, camera, action and timing at its chosen representation level. Stick figures and coarse meshes can fulfill that brief; inspect the complete relevant motion for contacts, support, trajectories, facing, reactions, occlusion and continuity through [rendered-use inspection](../3d-modeling/inspection.md). Verify low-cost motion before selectively raising geometric detail through code options. Inspect upgraded objects at their intended use and budget. A description can supply an omitted object only when its visible geometry or interaction is unnecessary for that hint. When a generator is selected, verify its actual input contract instead of assuming it accepts a custom skeleton, depth image or rendered video. [Production generation](../production/generation.md) owns that integration and take adoption. Review the actual accepted inputs and outputs; a representative integration result establishes only its observed compatibility, and unavailable generation leaves that check unverified.

Trace each required screenplay unit into executable shots, the edit's actual inclusion and the resulting output. These relationships can be many-to-many. A function's name or a source reference proves neither invocation nor visible delivery. Use [staging](../motion/staging.md) to check the actual exchange, reactions, view readability and continued state across cuts; a box in the frustum does not prove visible action or a pixel-occlusion threshold. Check source placement, camera and framing against the intended experience, then trace any missing result through accepted media, captions and transitions. Installed packages own capture, import and encoding capabilities; the production supplies its actual host.

Judge the directing through the exchange, not just the presence of its participants. Identify where attention goes during an important statement, reaction, reveal or physical consequence, and whether the actual delivered scale and timing let the audience read it. Repeated wide framing may preserve geography while losing the scene's turn; an intentional shared frame or long take may perform it fully. Support the judgment with the relevant moving sequence and concrete recipient loss, not a required shot count, camera variety or closeup quota. Separate a missing screenplay decision from an authored decision lost in camera, performance or edit implementation, and route the cause to that owner. A clean sampled integration review cannot approve directing it did not observe.

For commissioned control channels, inspect the actual [conditioning handoff](../production/conditioning.md): matched frame/camera mapping for time-paired channels, subject coverage, channel meanings and retained palette/sidecars, including converted delivery files. Separate appearance references keep their receiving contract rather than an invented frame pairing. Positional mask colors, omitted instanced skeletons or altered categorical values cannot establish stable identity or a complete pose input. Apply only the channels and compatibility the commission requires.

Use performed and measured timing from actual action, accepted speech, pauses, captions, music and transitions; page or word count cannot establish runtime. Apply [audio acquisition and listening](../audio/acquisition.md) to takes and sound with picture. Changing a voice take can change reactions, subtitles, shot lengths and subsequent timing even with identical words. Return those changes to affected owners and consumers together. A silent motion hint may be complete for its declared timing and action; it cannot establish speech intelligibility or a finished soundtrack.

For commissioned final video, inspect the actual direct 3D or post-diffusion output for preserved action, identity, spatial and temporal continuity, information, emotion, intelligibility of included speech and text, and intended audience effect. Record generator inputs and fixed output bytes separately from the reproducible hint; deterministic replay of adopted bytes does not make external generation deterministic. Judge observed output rather than assuming conditioning guaranteed it.
