# Review

Read the complete requested scope from the recipient's perspective: the user's request, governing documents, current source and fixed media, selected contracts, and actual outputs. Trace assumptions through their consumers rather than accepting the author's explanation or evidence tags as proof.

For narrative, use [storytelling](../storytelling/SKILL.md) and the [narrative questions](../contracts/narrative.md). For assets and visual changes, use [3d-modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md); for sound, use [audio](../audio/SKILL.md). Check numerical promises through [testing](../testing/SKILL.md). Use the [delivery questions](../contracts/delivery.md) for the requested handoff.

Inspect the whole round before editing. Record all findings with their evidence, cause, affected consumers, and required correction. Fix them together, then repeat the complete affected round until none remain. Recheck claims against the current result after upstream changes.

For video delivery, watch and listen to the assembled artifact, including joins, pacing, speech, titles, captions, and ending; separate shot renders do not establish a finished edit. For an asset delivery, inspect the actual model and promised controls or export. Record the revision, input and output identities, checks and observations, and anything that could not be verified. A self-review is not an independent review; identify which was performed and use another reviewer only when authorized.
