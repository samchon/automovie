# Current units and a whole-pass verdict

Use the configured current review tree, mirroring every authored file and H2 in `docs/review/<draft|final>/<layer>/`. Construction reviews target construction; naturalness reviews target working text. Each body-clean unit carries its native current fingerprint review, including a unit with a separate evidence-statement finding. The fingerprint covers the body, not the truth of its answers. `verdict/index.md` answers whole-pass duties. After [expression transfer](../screenwriting/rewriting/procedure.md#transfer), construction judgments remain with their frozen edition; a fresh body hash or stage flag cannot renew them.

Identify the reviewed revision, inputs, scope, review kind, findings and literal support, causes and occurrences, protected achievements, author responses, unresolved limits and outcome. Follow [independent reading](reading.md) before opening author records. An illustrative broad question can establish a supported recipient loss beyond its examples; a named-failure boundary remains limited to its definitions. Send a recurring uncovered loss to [discovery](../screenwriting/discovery.md).

Complete each creative inspection before correction under [cause rounds](../screenwriting/repair.md#cause-rounds). The author records the repair or a factual objection: a quotation is absent, the passage does not meet the cited definition, a permitted exception is established, or the parent or source contradicts the finding. The reviewer resolves that objection with the actual inputs in the same record. The author chooses how to repair an established creative loss; intention, repair cost, earlier approval and argument about preference cannot dismiss it. Repair all members of each cause and their affected descendants together, reconciling dependent answers.

Use scoped rounds only to verify findings in an open pass. Reread their complete affected dependency closure; carry an unaffected judgment only when its body, inputs and requirements are unchanged, identifying its source, reader and limitations. The current record contains the complete inventory and population verdict, including explicit carries; a carry is not a fresh reading. Keep raw blind reads, sentence tables and comparisons in ignored `.cache/`; keep the current accountable review and use Git for superseded published records under [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership).

A closing creative round rereads every current H2 and makes new unit and population judgments without carrying approval. Reuse stable sentence tests under [inspection](../screenwriting/inspection.md#verify-changed-work), while reading the whole body for effects of repair. Body repair receives a fresh reviewer in the next round. When a complete creative round has no body edits or findings, audit the current evidence once against complete respondents under [evidence writing](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md). Correct and confirm statement findings without another creative round when body, upstream inputs and creative requirements remain unchanged; a newly discovered body defect returns to creative correction. This order adds no graph stage. Close with the actual creative result and audit outcome, not a stage flag.

Preserve [closed-pass diagnostics](../screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes) without renewing old judgments. Mechanical checking locates stale relationships and cannot grant semantic acceptance.

When screenplay naturalness attributes a finding to frozen construction content, record its exact cause and earliest owner as an upstream report in that round's verdict. It does not block that expression-only judgment or recur as the same finding in later naturalness rounds, because the pass cannot change its frozen content. Handle the separate semantic defect under [upstream repair](../screenwriting/repair.md#upstream-defects). Preserve it as an unresolved creative limit; this pass outcome cannot turn it into whole-work or delivery acceptance.
