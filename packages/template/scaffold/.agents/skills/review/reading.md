# Read before auditing

Use a fresh context of the author's model for the same pass and scope, without the author's creative work memory. If an independent context is unavailable, report the missing review. Prepare the reading record from the [independent-reading questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading) before reading the annotation-free body. Lock the first response and every concrete loss of understanding, intent, tension, performance or continuity before opening author evidence. A whole round reads the whole body; a scoped finding-repair round reads the H2s under judgment and statements depending on changed values. Divide a large scope into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling.

After locking that reading, build an inventory from the actual body independently of contract answers: facts, quantities, chronology, names, titles and forms of address, number and date notation, recurring words and constructions, literal quotations, and consequential action, image, sound and continuity values. Then read creative contracts, actual parents, adopted work requirements and evidence. Compare every element with its actual source or parent and adopted language and work requirements. Audit each answer against the entire respondent; contract questions, relationship rows and matching quotations cannot replace this inventory. Account for every first-reading concern as a supported finding or a withdrawal supported by the actual passage.

For settings and treatments, independently compare externally checkable facts and causal claims with the original source's whole relevant sequence and context. A matching word or number cannot establish an inference or comparison. Scripts and screenplay inherit facts through actual parents; report doubtful parent values at their earliest owner instead of silently correcting them downstream.

Read settings as the treatment writer and production designer, treatments as the script writer, scripts as the final screenplay writer and performer, and screenplay as filmmaker and audience. Do not excuse weak final realization because its parent was less expressive. Apply the canonical [retention test](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) independently to contract-named failure candidates; unsupported withdrawal or protection is a finding. Keep reviewer alternatives in comparison records rather than supplying replacement prose for adoption.
