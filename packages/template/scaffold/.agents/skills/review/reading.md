# Read before auditing

Use a fresh context of the author's model for the same pass and scope, without the author's creative work memory. If an independent context is unavailable, report the missing review. Prepare the reading record from the [independent-reading questions](../contracts/story/shared/principles/review.md#independent-reading) before reading the annotation-free body. Lock the first response and every concrete loss of understanding, intent, tension, performance or continuity before opening author evidence. A whole round reads the whole body; a scoped finding-repair round reads the H2s under judgment and statements depending on changed values. Divide a large scope into non-overlapping chunks rather than sampling.

After locking that reading, build an inventory from the actual body independently of contract answers: facts, quantities, chronology, names, titles and forms of address, number and date notation, recurring words and constructions, literal quotations, and consequential action, image, sound and continuity values. Then read creative contracts, actual parents and adopted work requirements. Before author evidence or earlier verdicts, lock the [independent classification](../screenwriting/inspection.md) of named failures and parent differentiation in an actually isolated worker given only the job and the required body, contract and parent file paths. Naming the current context fresh is insufficient; follow the isolation tool and authorization boundary in that guide. Only then open author records, checking their assertions against that reading rather than treating their conclusions as proof. Compare every element with its actual source or parent and adopted language and work requirements. Audit each answer against the entire respondent; contract questions, relationship rows and matching quotations cannot replace this inventory. Account for every first-reading concern as a supported finding or a withdrawal supported by the actual passage.

For settings and treatments, independently compare externally checkable facts and causal claims with the original source's whole relevant sequence and context. A matching word or number cannot establish an inference or comparison. Scripts and screenplay inherit facts through actual parents. For a defective parent value or design, issue a finding identifying the earliest owner and what must become true there; the construction author repairs that owner under [upstream repair](../screenwriting/repair.md#upstream-defects). An expression pass reports it without changing its frozen content. A local downstream workaround cannot correct what the next unit inherits.

Read settings as the treatment writer and production designer, treatments as the script writer, scripts as the final screenplay writer and performer, and screenplay as filmmaker and audience. Do not excuse weak final realization because its parent was less expressive. Apply the selected contract's decision and exception rule to named expression failures; use the [retention test](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) for other candidates. Unsupported withdrawal or protection is a finding. Keep reviewer alternatives in comparison records rather than supplying replacement prose for adoption.

For screenplay expression, lock the voice and continuous experience made by actual speech, performance, image, sound and timing before the detailed audit. Judge [audiovisual voice](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice) across the whole unit after local repairs as well as checking named failures. Fidelity, a function list and negative sentence classifications cannot answer that overall question; cite actual expression and context for the audience loss or the legitimate form it realizes. Screenplay reading judges the specified experience, while [video review](video.md) must observe its actual realization.

Review scripts content and expression together: actual exchanges, executable instructions, adjacent action and replies, and inherited limits all belong to construction. Scene time/place/weather, useful character parentheticals, unnamed-person position markers and first-entry appearance are execution notation, not automatic expression failures. Check treatment prescriptions under its [completion question](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md#decided-event) rather than broadening that question from a review guide.
