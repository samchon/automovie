# Mesh Recovery

Recover a numerical representation from fixed source geometry, then generate a new surface from that representation. Read the installed `@automovie/recovery` public API and README for supported inputs, operators and hosts; package availability establishes neither arbitrary-object coverage nor visual fidelity. Image reconstruction requires its own camera and observation method before this mesh workflow applies.

## Freeze And Register

Commit the generating source and its dependency closure before comparison. Freeze source bytes, observation policy, units, registration, evaluated pose, component selection, cameras and outputs in a hashed manifest. Bind actual loaded code, coefficients, generated geometry and captures to it; a later commit cannot establish a missing generating snapshot. Publish candidates separately and preserve failed runs without replacing a valid publication.

Container inspection is not evaluated geometry. Establish applied transforms, handedness, units and component visibility at the loading boundary; record unknown facts rather than guessing them. Preserve source gaps, cavities, contacting sheets and defects as observations. Do not round coordinates, snap them to a grid or narrow their precision to hide residuals; measure unavoidable format conversions separately.

## Observe And Fit

Extract sections, guides, oblique cuts and feature boundaries algorithmically under a declared policy. Retain cutting frames, arc selection and orientation witnesses so the observations can be reproduced. Locate contour births, deaths, splits, merges, contacts and cavity changes, including incident contours around critical levels; regular spacing and equal loop counts do not establish correspondence.

Choose a supported curve or surface basis from its constraints, corners, curvature and required continuity, following [Lofting](lofting.md) for section families. Fit compact coefficients under physical error and resource budgets, and report unresolved constraints. A cubic per source edge is diagnostic replay, not a compact model; an agent drawing individual source controls is not an extraction algorithm.

Publish parameter widths, orientation, shared boundary identities and explicit continuation with the coefficients. Derive open-arc correspondence and endpoint transport rather than pairing arcs by their order or closing them implicitly. Keep source addresses in observation and evaluation records, outside the reconstructed shape's authority.

## Generate Independently

Follow the package boundaries in the project's governing instructions and installed contracts; extend the owner of a missing general capability. Read the actual consumed operators before attributing a fault to fitting, lofting or rendering.

Reconstruct from saved curves, coefficients and declared boundary relationships alone. Generate new vertices, faces and normals without reading original positions, topology or copied normal fields as a fallback. Derive displayed construction from the same representation, distinguishing controls, interpolation waypoints and evaluated points; record unconsumed families explicitly.

Preserve usable geometry and viewer controls when local qualification fails, with explicit diagnostics and partial status. Keep invalid primitives out of the renderer and distinguish an unavailable region from a retained previous display. Track declared, attempted, generated, written and loaded domains separately; unknown stages remain unknown. Completion of a command or declared coverage does not certify likeness, smoothness or a surface-error bound.

## Compare And Correct

Show the source and independently reconstructed surface side by side with matching camera, framing, lighting, material and resolution. Derive each mesh's normals from its own geometry and disable fine material relief. Copied source shading and lines over the source are separate diagnostics, never proof of reconstructed form. Record loaded artifact hashes, renderer, capture time and report time separately.

Apply [Visual Debugging](visual-debugging.md) to whole objects, affected regions, neighbors and close-ups, showing generating construction as well as surfaces. Compare omitted and added surface, boundaries, orientation, joins, folds, self-intersections and cavities alongside residuals. Separate sample error, curve certificates, full-surface bounds, coverage and visible defects.

Localize a cause through source preparation, observations, fitting, correspondence, surface generation and display. Hold saved observations fixed when changing operators, and hold the operator fixed when changing representations. For an external baseline, inspect its native output independently before comparing the same bytes through this viewer. Collect the full round's findings and correct their common causes under Visual Debugging's correction rounds; increasing precision alone is not a causal explanation.
