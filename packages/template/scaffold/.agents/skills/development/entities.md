# Entity work

Build the subjects the commission's scenes need, each in the entity folder [source layout](layout.md) gives, under the [entities contract](../contracts/development/entities.md). [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md) owns how a body or object is made, rigged and verified.

## Scope

Read [project context](../production/profile.md) first. A single scene can use a ready asset with a few placements; a recurring character needs editable proportions, expressions and motion. A standalone asset defines its views and controls without a screenplay.

## Coarse first {#coarse-first}

Build stick figures, block forms or frames first, keeping identity, dimensions, pivots, attachments and controls independent of surface detail, and finish the complete low-cost performance through [motion](../motion/SKILL.md) before any upgrade. Upgrade only what its visible role needs, keeping the controls and the measured budget. A motion hint needs reliable action and staging; photographic detail only when the output contract asks for it. A 2D image can be appearance reference or a conditioning input, never a substitute for coded motion. Use the installed Human package only for what its public surface provides. A speaking cast member's face fragment moves the mouth from the accepted dialogue take, whose measured timing is the anchor, rather than timing speech itself.

## Inspect and hand off {#inspect-and-hand-off}

Inspect the entity in its commissioned use, with its scene cameras, neighbors and motion, segment by segment under [segment review](../motion/segments.md), and against its settings record's model sheet at every view and state the scenes use. After an upgrade, compare the same segments for changed proportions, pivots, contact, timing and silhouette. The entity names the settings facts it realizes under the [entities contract](../contracts/development/entities.md); settings never link code.
