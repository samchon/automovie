# Expressive rewriting

Recompose the final screenplay's audiovisual expression while preserving its completed construction. Scripts completes expression within [scripts construction](../scripts.md). Use the selected naturalness questions in [story contracts](../../contracts/story/index.md).

Read the annotation-free frozen edition and preceding context as its performer or audience before editing. Record both losses and achieved effects so the rewrite can improve the experience while protecting what works.

Compose from the scene's people or process, purpose, pressure and rhythm. Choose the audience's access, distance and emphasis through actual image, sound, speech, performance, detail, juxtaposition, silence and transitions. Read exchanges with adjacent action as if spoken. The adopted form may use narration, captions, sustained action or restraint; realize its contribution rather than add gestures or cuts as a recipe.

Compare meaning in both directions with the frozen construction under [expression fidelity](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#expression-fidelity). Preserve consequential facts, knowledge and results without compulsory display of every inherited value. Repair lost meaning in the working edition and report a construction defect to its owner.

Reread the complete progression against the initial experience and [audiovisual voice](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice). Then use [inspection](../inspection.md) and [cause rounds](../repair.md#cause-rounds) for the detailed correction cycle; a clean sentence table does not establish the continuous effect.

## Revision cycles

One construction cycle receives one comprehensive expression rewrite. Finish its whole author reading and cause corrections before evidence. Evidence, independent review and subsequent repairs complete that working edition under [procedure](procedure.md).

For a first rewrite, compose in the final medium's own rhythm while retaining passages already doing their work. Changed-sentence counts and distance from the original do not measure improvement.

A requested further expression revision starts from the previous working edition, reviews and decisions, identified through [layout](../../production/layout.md#source-ownership). Compare meaning with the initial frozen construction and expressive gains and losses with the previous edition. Previous acceptance does not answer for changed text.

Keep the initial frozen construction throughout the cycle and preserve work outside the authorized scope. A separate semantic repair follows [upstream repair](../repair.md#upstream-defects); replacing a unit from scratch starts new construction rather than silently replacing this baseline.
