# Expressive rewriting

Preserve reviewed final screenplay construction while writing its audiovisual expression anew. This separate pass does not change established facts or results. Scripts completes expression inside [scripts construction](../scripts.md). Use the selected naturalness questions in [story contracts](../../contracts/story/index.md).

Before editing, read the annotation-free frozen edition and preceding context as its performer or audience. Record unclear action, awkward speech and lost attention; compare the rewrite with those first-response losses.

Rewrite from the scene's actual people, purpose, pressure, and rhythm. Read dialogue with adjacent action and consecutive replies as if spoken, using [realized exchange](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/realized.md#speech-yield) and [common expression](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md) to diagnose it. Repair narration whose actual wording or image-sound relation merely repeats meaning the scene already supplies; preserve a deliberate adopted contribution that the passage realizes. Where a failed summary or copied realization still carries needed meaning, recompose that function through the adopted audiovisual form, including narration or captions when their actual contribution requires them.

Follow the audience's attention through image and sound. Rechoose distance, access and emphasis, then realize them through the actual speech, performance, detail, juxtaposition, silence and transitions. Clearer meaning or smoother wording alone does not establish the intended experience. Do not mechanically convert explanation into gestures or close-ups; select the form that serves the moment within its frozen decisions. A detail's individual function does not excuse a sequence that delays the important action or pressure.

Compare the working version with its frozen original in both directions under [expression fidelity](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#expression-fidelity). Repair lost meaning in the working version and report a construction defect to its owner. Reread the complete progression against the initial response and [audiovisual voice](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/screenplay.md#audiovisual-voice); a clean sentence table cannot establish its continuous effect. After each annotation-free whole reading, use the same layer-specific naturalness questions in [story contracts](../../contracts/story/index.md) as independent review with [independent classification](../inspection.md), then complete [cause-based correction](../repair.md#cause-rounds). Keep the same author through its dry correction cycle; record retained candidates beside the passage under the [retention test](../evidence/writing.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) for independent review.

## Revision cycles

One construction cycle receives one comprehensive expression rewrite. Finish its complete author reading and cause-based corrections before opening evidence. Evidence correction, independent review, author decisions, and repairs then finish that working edition; do not defer known defects to another wholesale rewrite.

For a first rewrite, use the complete construction as material and compose in the layer's own voice and rhythm rather than polish its sentence order. Preserve passages already doing their work. The number of changed sentences, distance from the original, and added detail do not measure improvement.

When a further expression revision is requested, begin from the previous working edition, its reviews, and the author's decisions. Retain those comparison inputs before replacing a working file. Compare meaning in both directions with the initial frozen construction, and compare expressive gains and losses with the previous working edition. The previous edition is not a new construction baseline, and old evidence or acceptance is not an answer for new text.

Keep the initial frozen original throughout repairs to the same construction. Report defects in facts, events, decisions or results during naturalness. Any separate construction repair follows [upstream repair](../repair.md#upstream-defects); keep it distinct from replacing a unit from scratch, and never replace the frozen original during expression work. Preserve work outside the authorized scope.

Use [rewriting procedure](procedure.md) for frozen editions, evidence, review and transfer.
