# Expression procedure

Read the selected body's whole unit and preceding context as its recipient. Freeze reviewed construction in `docs/revision/<scripts|screenplay>` with matching relative paths and work in `docs/naturalness/<scripts|screenplay>`. Preserve file, H1/H2 identities and order. Keep the initial frozen construction for every repair in this cycle; retain a previous working edition and its reviews when another rewrite is commissioned.

Write the complete working edition while its pass is disabled using the selected rewriting method. Inspect the whole result by cause, compare its meaning in both directions with the frozen construction, and finish author corrections before opening evidence. A construction defect returns to its first owner; it is not an expression edit to the frozen baseline.

## Transfer

Open the completed working edition's evidence stage and claims together. Write its selected answers and rewriting notes in comments, then submit the working version and frozen construction to independent review. The [cycle rules](method.md#revision-cycles) preserve the comparisons for initial and further rewrites.

After the [closing round](../../review/rounds.md), transfer every supplied working file to its actual `docs/body` counterpart with HTML comments removed and every other byte preserved. Supplied navigation indexes participate; absent optional indexes need no fabricated copies. Pending initial transfer prevents downstream consumption and completion even when the working edition is ready to reread.

Retain the working edition, its evidence, frozen original, findings and author decisions. Construction evidence, review and approval remain closed against the frozen construction; report construction diagnostics without rewriting answers or renewing its approval against transferred expression. An unchanged working edition keeps its naturalness review.

Exact equality with the comment-free reviewed working edition governs this initial transfer. Subsequent semantic body repairs follow [closed-pass repair](../repair.md#closed-passes); preserve their repair instead of transferring the old working edition over it. A new construction starts a new cycle and receives its own evidence and review.
