# Final video screenplay

Compose the audience's complete viewing and listening experience. The recipient should understand the work without reading its parents. Read [discovery](discovery.md) and the screenplay questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md); this layer has its own construction and subsequent expression pass.

Choose what is seen and heard, how attention moves and when the audience recognizes, waits and connects. Realize the scene through actual speech, action, reactions, sound, detail, duration and transitions in [readable screenplay notation](notation.md). A fixed view, silence, caption or narration can serve the adopted form when its actual contribution earns the attention it receives. [Screenplay principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/screenplay.md) judge the resulting audiovisual experience.

For long work, begin with completed treatments and scripts construction. Extract concise notes of required identities, consequential decisions, states, chronology, spatial conditions and audience knowledge. Use an [isolated drafting context](inspection.md#isolated-context) with those notes, adopted requirements and contracts, and fixed quotations, leaving upstream expression outside it. Then perform the [independent parent and value comparisons](inspection.md) against the actual parents. Preserve decided event order while composing the final realization afresh under [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation). Report an unavailable isolation step rather than claim it complete.

Write a specialist performance from its settings vocabulary and [performance study](../production/research.md#performance-study): chained sequences rather than one isolated action answered by another, changes of tempo between probing, bursts and resets, spatial strategy that moves the participants through the space, and the continuous response of everyone present, including officials and partners. A pattern that repeats one exchange shape, holds a participant still or contradicts the depicted level fails even when each action is executable.

Preserve inherited meanings, constraints and consequential values without reprinting every parent fact or direction. Supply the observable decisions needed to perform the scene and understand its result. An actual consumed shot or choreography companion can carry implementation precision through [execution traceability](../evidence/execution.md); it cannot defer an unresolved creative choice to the implementer. Repair a defective parent at its earliest owner through [upstream repair](repair.md#upstream-defects).

Follow [production layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership). Long work preserves scripts' part and unit identities and cites the realized treatment and script. A direct short composes event design, executable action and final audiovisual realization from its actual brief; a compact short also consumes completed settings and cites the facts it uses. Use one descriptive short file, with a sufficient brief in its header when appropriate. These routes retain all screenplay responsibilities while omitting unused upstream populations.

Before evidence, read the complete progression in viewing order, then use [inspection](inspection.md) and [cause rounds](repair.md#cause-rounds). Preserve meaningful performance and orientation while removing excess. Complete the selected passes through [workflow](workflow.md). A screenplay-only commission ends with its required text and delivery review; commissioned video continues through [production](../production/SKILL.md), with actual performance and playback establishing timing and [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) judging the assembled output.
