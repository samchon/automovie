# Final video screenplay

Make the work the audience will see and hear. For the long route, begin with completed treatments and scripts construction, whose dialogue and directions already answer their expression contracts. Read [discovery](discovery.md) and screenplay questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). This is its own construction, followed by its own expression pass.

Turn the inherited decisions into a fresh audiovisual progression. Preserve events, choices, consequences, knowledge, relationships and fixed world values; compose the viewing and listening experience afresh. Parent dialogue, camera directions, gestures and local action order are creative material, except where the user or an actual causal constraint fixes them. Rewording that execution or adding camera terms around it does not perform this layer's work. [Screenplay principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/screenplay.md) and [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation) judge that contribution and its inherited limits.

Choose audience distance, access and emphasis through the actual image-sound sequence, performance and spoken language. Preserving an event or explaining each detail's function does not establish that the audience experiences the intended pressure or tone. A pause, caption, summary, fixed view or direct statement may serve the chosen form; realize its effect rather than replace every explanation with a gesture or add detail for its own sake.

Own the complete shooting and editing design needed to make the video, in this body and any explicitly linked shot plan that forms part of the canonical screenplay. Decide what the image lets the audience notice, whose speech or reaction holds it, the necessary framing and access, performed action and sound, duration and what carries across each cut or sustained view. A room-wide account of everyone present can leave a decisive exchange uncomposed. Make those choices usable by [motion staging](../motion/staging.md#direct-the-actual-view), which implements them with actual cameras, assets and measured timing. A speaker need not receive a new shot for each line: a listener, shared frame, off-screen voice or deliberate long take may carry the turn. Technical implementation may resolve coordinates and solver values, but it must not silently invent or replace the film's creative decisions.

When making or inspecting the video proves that a creative decision does not work, revise this canonical screenplay and its consumed shot plan, then implement and inspect that revision. Trace a defective event, execution or world value through [upstream repair](repair.md#upstream-defects) to scripts, treatments or settings and repair the earliest cause with its descendants. Keep actual performance and editing discoveries in this loop; a code-only alternative leaves the next renderer or writer with the wrong film. Preserve historical proof at its original edition and verify the changed current work rather than treating the former approval as its acceptance.

For long work:

1. Extract brief notes containing episode/H2 identities, values used in decisions or continuity, who decides what with which result, and the audience's access to character knowledge. Include age, rank, appearance or history only where the scene uses that value. Preserve consequential chronology and conditions that make the result possible. Carry required spatial decisions without copying every production direction or prop-handling step into the next layer's attention plan; individually useful details can collectively bury the important choice or exchange. Leave out interpretations of exchanges, category labels, argument lists, mood instructions, upstream wording and incidental action sequences; these become repeated explanations in the draft.
2. [Launch an isolated drafting context](inspection.md#isolated-context) with only the paths to those notes, adopted requirements and contracts, required identities and explicitly fixed quotations. Keep upstream sentences and unfixed dialogue outside it. Compose the audiovisual progression there. If that context is unavailable, report the missing step rather than claim a construction drafted in the upstream context is complete.
3. Use [independent parent and value comparisons](inspection.md) against the actual treatments and scripts in both directions. Record missing decisions in your own words, close the parents, and realize those notes through fresh image, sound, action and speech. Changing tense or formatting does not transform a copied realization.

A short's entry has no upstream scene expression to isolate and composes from its actual brief, plus reviewed compact settings when that route is adopted. It owns event design, executable action and final audiovisual realization without fabricating skipped treatments or scripts. When construction exposes a defective parent fact or design, repair its earliest owner yourself under [upstream repair](repair.md#upstream-defects), then realize the repaired decisions.

For long work, mirror scripts' part directories, index H1s, episode filenames, H1s, H2 identities, and order in `docs/body/screenplay`. Within each unit, give the complete viewing and listening progression with actual spoken words, important reactions, sounds, and screen text, using the [working notation](notation.md). A shot plan may accompany it; it cannot replace it. On the long route, cite the realized treatment and corresponding script. World facts arrive through those parents; use the repaired handoff rather than direct settings evidence for a missing inherited decision.

For a short, use its screenplay population under `docs/body/screenplay`; a header can hold a sufficient brief. Direct short mode has no settings parent. Compact short mode consumes its completed minimal settings and cites the facts each scene actually uses, retaining their uncertainty and constraints. Optional part indexes need not be invented. No imaginary parents or unused world documents are required. Retain [instructions](brief.md#requirements), discovery, selected screenplay contracts, construction, expressive rewriting, evidence, and independent review. Direct entry omits treatments and scripts, and the plain short route omits settings too; it preserves all screenplay responsibilities.

Before [evidence](evidence/writing.md), read the complete final screenplay in viewing order, then complete [independent classification](inspection.md) and [repair by cause](repair.md#cause-rounds), using the common, speech, and screenplay expression questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) used by independent review, plus parent differentiation. A search for three consecutive shared words locates candidates; it cannot prove or disprove copied realization. Judge the complete parent comparison, preserving required terms, fixed quotations and already complete short utterances without ornamental variation. Remove sentences whose loss changes no information, attention, or pressure; intended duration does not require filling their place. Complete the selected passes under [workflow](workflow.md), then hand the current result to [production](../production/SKILL.md). Keep traceable relationships to actual executable shots and the edit. Estimates remain provisional until performed action, fixed speech and playback establish timing. A screenplay-only commission ends with its required text review; inspect the actual assembled output when video delivery is commissioned.
