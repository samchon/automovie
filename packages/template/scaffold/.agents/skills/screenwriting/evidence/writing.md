## Grounds for retaining a candidate

For a named expression failure, use the selected contract's [decision and exception rule](../../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#reads-as-written); an alternative cannot create an unlisted exception. For another candidate, compare a written alternative conveying the same information, access and pressure without that form with the original passage. Record the actual gain or loss in ignored comparison notes and the supported decision in the current accountable record. Deletion alone, a function label, inherited precedent or earlier protection cannot establish what the expression achieves.

Before review, this comparison guides the author's choice; after an independent finding, it guides repair rather than author acquittal. A factual objection follows [review rounds](../../review/rounds.md), and the independent reviewer resolves the passage against its obligation. Preserve the achieved function by recomposing it when its form fails. A parent carrying the same value does not justify its expression; a defective inherited value or design returns to its earliest owner during construction under [upstream repair](../repair.md#upstream-defects), or is reported during naturalness.
