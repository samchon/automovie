# Writing workflow

Choose the commissioned route from the request, project context, adopted instructions and current bodies.

## Creative ownership

Long work retains settings → treatments → scripts → final screenplay. Each layer makes its own contribution and hands usable decisions to its next recipient.

| Layer | Owns | Hands onward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Settings](settings.md) | World facts, baseline states, characters, relationships, places, objects, knowledge boundaries and sensory conditions | Usable facts with conditions, uncertainty, sources and one semantic owner |
| [Treatments](treatments.md) | Events, causes, choices, exchanges, consequences, setup/payoff and information release | A complete progression that needs no invented decisive event |
| [Scripts](scripts.md) | Partition and fully executed scenes: actual speech, action, reactions, performance, stage, light, sound and view | Playable scenes for final audiovisual composition |
| [Final screenplay](screenplay.md) | The audience's image, sound, access, attention, duration, juxtaposition and transitions | Complete production text traceable into executed shots and the edit |

## Route and parents

Long work follows settings construction → treatments construction → scripts construction → screenplay construction → screenplay naturalness. Scripts completes content and expression together within construction. Settings and treatments correct their documents there too; only final screenplay has a separate expression pass.

A short enters screenplay directly from its brief (`short`) or after compact settings construction (`short-with-settings`). Its screenplay owns events, execution and audiovisual realization, retaining expression, evidence and independent review. Use actual settings dependencies on the compact route; neither route needs empty treatments, scripts or outline populations. Asset-only work has no narrative passes. Only an explicit user waiver omits screenplay naturalness.

Choose form and provisional scale through [direction](direction.md). Complete work is the default; a pilot needs its own commission. Use [rewriting method](rewriting/method.md) and [procedure](rewriting/procedure.md) for expression work and transfer.

## Construction

Read current parent bodies and reviews when starting, resuming or changing a source.

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, material, discovered work requirements and, for a specialist performance, its completed [performance study](../production/research.md#performance-study) |
| Treatments | Completed settings construction |
| Scripts | Completed treatments construction |
| Screenplay in long work | Completed treatments and scripts construction, including scripts expression |
| Screenplay in direct short work | The request and adopted constraints |
| Screenplay in compact short work | Completed compact settings and the actual brief |

Inherit through actual parents. On the long route, repair a missing treatment handoff rather than bypass it with direct settings evidence. [Discovery](discovery.md) checks requirements and owners before bulk settings and at downstream design reviews.

1. Finish the whole declared body while its pass is disabled, guided by the selected goals. Identify its revision through [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership).
2. Complete [inspection and cause repair](repair.md#cause-rounds). Open the corresponding stage and claims through [evidence configuration](../evidence/configuration.md), then write [accountable answers](evidence/writing.md).
3. Submit the complete selected body, boundaries and population to independent [review](../review/SKILL.md), supplying actual inputs rather than a conclusion to confirm. Follow [review rounds](../review/rounds.md) through creative closure and evidence audit.
4. Repair findings at their owners, complete the required closing review and checks, and keep the completed pass in its evidence stage.

Long-route settings completes its finding guide with the records. Population duties require the actual whole population; local answers cannot establish its distribution or complete handoff.

## Completion

Run `pnpm evidence` and read its complete native report. Reevaluate after relevant input changes; passing coverage establishes recorded relationships, while independent review judges creative completion. Use native `list` and `inspect` to examine actual selected respondents; a focused check establishes only its selected scope.

Strict whole-work completion requires the selected passes, current independent closing review, passing unfiltered validation and the production's documented compiler/source checks and required tests. The default `lint` command performs TypeScript compilation with `--noEmit`; run an actual adopted build when the project provides one. [Delivery review](../review/delivery.md) judges the commissioned output, including actual media where required.

Changes touching completed layers follow [closed-pass repair](repair.md#closed-passes), with failed reports and exit statuses retained under [layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership). Continue authorized work against current bodies while reporting mechanical limits. A manuscript with its current closing review and commissioned output checks may be delivered with its exact revision, outcome and named remaining diagnostics. This qualified delivery does not satisfy a request for fully validated completion. A current creative defect, missing parent, pending initial expression transfer, unfinished review or unverified commissioned media still blocks its corresponding completion. Until review finishes, label the result a draft for review.
