# Writing workflow

Choose the commissioned route from the request, project context, adopted instructions and current bodies.

## Creative ownership

A long film or series retains settings → treatments → scripts → final screenplay. Keep all four responsibilities even when one is brief; compress or regroup content rather than replacing a layer with a description of its parent.

| Layer | Owns | Hands onward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Settings](settings.md) | World facts, baseline states, constraints, characters and relationships, places, objects, knowledge boundaries, and sensory consistency | Usable facts with conditions, uncertainty, sources, and one semantic owner |
| [Treatments](treatments.md) | Events, causes, choices, exchanges, consequences, setup and payoff, information release, and the complete progression | A storyline another author can execute without inventing decisive events |
| [Scripts](scripts.md) | Part and episode partition; fully executed scenes with blocking, actual dialogue, reactions, meaningful performance, stage, light, sound, and viewing position | Complete playable scenes rather than an outline with scene headings |
| [Final screenplay](screenplay.md) | The audience's complete audiovisual experience: selected image and sound, access, attention, duration, juxtaposition, and transitions | Final production text traceable into executed shots and the edit |

## Route and parents

Long work follows settings construction → treatments construction → scripts construction → screenplay construction → screenplay naturalness. Short work enters at screenplay construction and retains its expression, evidence and independent review duties. Scripts finish dialogue and directions, including their expression contracts, within construction. Freezing content before inspecting content-shaped speech failures made those failures impossible to repair in revision. An asset-only task has no narrative passes. Only an explicit user waiver omits screenplay naturalness.

For an expression pass, use the [rewriting method](rewriting/method.md) for composition and revision cycles, and the [rewriting procedure](rewriting/procedure.md) for freezing, submission, review and exact transfer.

Choose form and provisional scale through [direction](direction.md). Complete work is the default; a pilot requires its own commission. A short's brief may live in the screenplay header; invent no empty upstream populations or false lineage.

## Construction

Read current parent bodies and reviews when starting, resuming, or changing a source. The dependencies are:

| Submitted layer | Required parent |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | Adopted instructions, material and discovered work requirements |
| Treatments | Completed settings construction |
| Scripts | Completed treatments construction |
| Screenplay in long work | Completed treatments and scripts construction, including scripts expression requirements |
| Screenplay in short work | The request and adopted constraints directly |

Facts pass through the actual parents. Direct settings evidence cannot repair an incomplete treatment handoff. [Discovery](discovery.md) compares requirements and current owners before bulk settings and at downstream design reviews.

1. Finish the whole declared body while its pass is disabled. Read selected contract goals without shaping each paragraph as a checklist response. Preserve an identifiable complete-draft snapshot in version history or an immutable copy before opening evidence; a partial progress snapshot is insufficient.
2. Open the corresponding evidence stage and claims together through [evidence configuration](../evidence/configuration.md). Compare the complete body with every selected requirement, complete [cause-based inspection and repair](repair.md#cause-rounds), and write [evidence](evidence/writing.md) from the corrected work.
3. Submit the whole selected population, including boundaries and global obligations, to independent [review](../review/SKILL.md). Supply scope and actual inputs rather than a conclusion to confirm.
4. Record reasoned author decisions, repair accepted findings through their owners, and satisfy the [closing-round rule](../review/rounds.md). Keep the current completed review and the pass in its evidence stage.

Settings includes its outline in the same completion. Unit answers cannot establish whole-population coverage, character development, middle accumulation, partition, or complete handoff; use the selected population respondent and compare every member. Settings and treatments correct their documents within construction. Scripts completes expression there too; only final screenplay receives a separate naturalness pass.

## Completion

Run the installed production checker for the submitted layer and pass, then read the complete report and unfiltered validation. A target check establishes only its target. Captured reports describe their actual inputs; reevaluate after source or filesystem changes. Readiness can identify a body ready for review while completion still fails, and cannot grant artistic acceptance. Installed `@automovie/evidence` documentation owns commands, fields, and exit behavior.

Keep completed passes in their evidence stage. Existing closed layers follow [upstream repair](repair.md), preserving failed reports and actual exit statuses. Strict whole-work completion requires the selected passes, passing unfiltered validation and a clean package build, followed by [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) of the actual commissioned output. Failed required checks leave that completion unavailable.

When closed-pass diagnostics remain, continue authorized author work and independent reading against current bodies and actual parents, reporting failed checks. A manuscript may be delivered after its current independent closing review and commissioned output checks, with its exact revision, outcome, repair scope and named mechanical limits. This qualified delivery cannot fulfill a fully validated completion request or excuse a current creative defect, missing parent, pending initial expression transfer, unfinished review or unverified commissioned media. Before review finishes, label the result a draft for review. A screenplay or motion hint does not imply a finished post-diffusion film.
