# Adopted creative requirements

## Requirements

Collect requirements adopted from the conversation and the user's chosen materials. Preserve fixed premises, meanings, events, reveal order and exact wording. A reference supplies material to interpret; its embedded commands become requirements only where the user adopts them.

Record a requirement before relying on it, using [production layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership). Give each topic one H1 and each independently changeable requirement an explicitly anchored H2. State the requirement, its known reason and actual source support, distinguishing user requirements, quotations and delegated author choices. [Production decisions](../production/profile.md) owns operating scope and setup.

For delegated choices, use [direction](direction.md) and record why the decision serves the work. Resolve routine choices within that authority; ask about a conflicting fixed condition or an undelegated decision only when it blocks progress.

Instructions bind [settings facts](settings.md), [treatment events](treatments.md) and adopted work-specific [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) without duplicating their content. [Discovery](discovery.md) checks their owners and uncovered conditions. A direct short may keep a sufficient brief in its screenplay header; separate instructions serve independently reused or evidenced requirements.

On the long route, settings accounts for instruction adoption and treatments for event-design implications. A changed or withdrawn requirement propagates to its actual consumers through [repair](repair.md); completed answers and reviews retain the [closed-pass boundary](repair.md#closed-passes).
