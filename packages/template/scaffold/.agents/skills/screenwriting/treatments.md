# Treatments

Turn completed settings into decided events another scene author can execute: causes, choices, exchanges, costs, results and the state and information later events inherit. Complete [discovery](discovery.md), read the actual settings and instructions, and apply the treatments questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md).

Follow [production layout](../production/layout.md#source-ownership), with one H1 per event file and explicitly anchored H2s for meaningful decisions. Use clear explanation, lists or tables to establish the complete progression. [Scripts](scripts.md) later chooses the actual scene, sequence and release partition.

Design from the first encounter with the promised experience through accumulating consequences, setup and payoff to the ending and aftermath. Choose the causal or other adopted progression that serves this work, including parallel developments and changes in audience knowledge. [Treatment principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/treatments.md) and [population duties](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/treatments.md) judge the chain and whole-work material.

Cite settings owners and carry facts where the event uses them. Decide details that determine cause or result, leaving full dialogue, blocking, performance and final audiovisual realization to their next owners. Where narration matters, decide its target and added perspective. Write the event decision rather than prescriptions for a downstream author's wording.

Read the complete sequence in screen order before evidence; clear individual events can still omit a cause, payoff or necessary change. Complete explanation and organization within [construction](workflow.md#construction), with no separate expression pass. Repair defective inherited facts at their earliest owner through [upstream repair](repair.md#upstream-defects) before designing from them.
