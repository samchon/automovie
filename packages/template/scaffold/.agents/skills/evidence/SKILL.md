# Evidence

Connect each required achievement to the work that can establish it. Find the existing obligation before adding another: source declarations answer [common](../contracts/common.md), exported tests answer [testing](../contracts/testing.md), and [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) map creative unit, population and review duties. A scene cannot testify for a whole population or an unobserved output.

When a requirement is missing or changes, use [adoption](adoption.md) to choose its canonical owner, observable difference, and narrowest honest scope. [Instructions](../screenwriting/brief.md#requirements) preserves creative user choices; [project context](../production/profile.md) owns operating scope and choices; [discovery](../screenwriting/discovery.md) investigates what existing owners do not cover. Work and language duties supplement shared questions; never weaken a question or its selected population to conceal a defect.

Use [evidence configuration](configuration.md) to wire populations and relationships while preserving the engineering baseline. It owns graph configuration, not answer prose. Use [source, asset and delivery evidence](answering.md) for those respondents, and [narrative evidence](../screenwriting/evidence/writing.md) for creative construction and expression. Each method compares the complete respondent with its selected requirements and actual observations.

Assets, performance and delivery retain their own [asset questions](../contracts/assets.md), [motion questions](../contracts/motion.md) and [handoff criteria](../contracts/delivery.md). Select evidence for the requested screenplay, generation hint, finished film, or asset; required motion and controls do not imply a particular figure system, reconstruction method, or photorealistic finish. [Delivery review](../review/delivery.md) checks the actual commissioned result.

Answers record actual work, observations and remaining limits; independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) judges their truth and the recipient's experience. [Writing workflow](../screenwriting/workflow.md#completion) owns narrative completion, and [upstream repair](../screenwriting/repair.md) preserves closed proof when requirements or consumers change. Mechanical coverage establishes the recorded relationships, not those judgments.
