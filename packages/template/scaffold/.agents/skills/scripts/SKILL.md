# Scripts

Recreate reviewed treatments as scenes performers and filmmakers can execute. The [script principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/scripts.md) judge the complete execution, staging, and view; [population duties](../contracts/story/shared/obligations/scripts.md) judge partition and handoff.

After [discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md), map treatments to their script consumers and settle part, episode, and meaningful-unit partition before bulk scene writing. Merge thin units and divide heavy ones according to their actual viewing material and release form. Report unresolved parents before fixing that partition.

Use `docs/body/scripts/NNN-part/NNN-episode.md` with one H1 and ordered explicitly anchored H2s. Each part has an `index.md` with its H1 and links to episode files. A continuous film can use one part and a suitable sequence division without pretending it is an episodic release. Hand identities and order to the final screenplay.

Build each scene around what its participants or process do, encounter, and change. Recreate the treatment's pressure through actual exchanges, selecting the material that moves them. Write directions in the order people perform and film them; use block breaks, sustained action, and separated replies to shape the scene's rhythm. The contracts determine which execution decisions must be supplied and when detail matters.

Inherit world facts and uncertainty through treatments, citing every realized parent. Use the [working notation](../../../docs/guides/storytelling.md#working-files). Before evidence, follow the complete script as its performer and filmmaker, reading exchanges with adjacent action as if aloud; use the [execution](../contracts/story/shared/principles/scripts.md#executable-scene) questions and the common, speech, and script expression questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) used by independent review. Collect and judge their whole-scope candidates through [repair by cause](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#repair-by-cause), then remove dialogue and directions whose loss changes no information, access, or pressure.

Complete construction [evidence](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md) and independent [review](../review/SKILL.md), then [rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md) before [screenplay](../screenplay/SKILL.md) construction. Scripts is a complete scene-execution layer; the next layer owns a different, final audiovisual realization. Follow the [lifecycle](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md) for current parents and reviews.
