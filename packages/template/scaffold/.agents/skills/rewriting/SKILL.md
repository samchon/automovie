# Expressive rewriting

Preserve a reviewed construction while writing its expression anew. Scripts becomes a script performers and filmmakers can execute naturally; screenplay becomes the production's final audiovisual experience. Neither rewrites facts or results. Use naturalness questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md), and the [lifecycle](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md) for frozen originals, current reviews, and transfer.

Read the whole unit and preceding context as its recipient before editing. Freeze construction in `docs/revision/<scripts|screenplay>` with matching relative paths, then work in `docs/naturalness/<scripts|screenplay>`. Preserve file and H1/H2 identities and order. Keep the initial frozen original for every repair in this rewriting cycle.

Use [revision cycles](cycles.md) when entering or repeating a rewrite. The initial construction is the meaning baseline; a previous working edition and its reviews are also the comparison for retained expressive achievements. Evidence and review findings receive local repairs within the completed rewrite rather than another wholesale reset.

Rewrite from the scene's actual people, purpose, pressure, and rhythm. Read dialogue with adjacent action and consecutive replies as if spoken, using [realized exchange](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/realized.md#speech-yield) and [common expression](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md) to diagnose it. Delete narration that repeats meaning the scene already shows. Where summary or copied realization still carries needed meaning, recompose it as performed action and sound.

| Layer | Rewriting method |
| --- | --- |
| Scripts | Follow the order of filming and performance. Read ambiguous directions beside the action they govern and exchanges beside their replies; reshape blocks around the changing pressure. |
| Screenplay | Follow the audience's attention through image and sound. Rework selected detail, juxtaposition, reaction, silence, and transitions to realize the moment's intended effect. |

Compare the working version with its frozen original in both directions under [expression fidelity](../contracts/story/shared/naturalness/common.md#expression-fidelity). Repair lost meaning in the working version and report a construction defect to its owner. After each annotation-free whole reading, use the same layer-specific naturalness questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) as independent review to collect and judge whole-scope candidates in the rewriting notes under [cause-based correction](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#repair-by-cause). Repeat until a complete round adopts no further edit; record retained candidates beside the passage under the [retention test](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md#grounds-for-retaining-a-candidate) for independent review.

Write [evidence](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md) in working-version comments, then obtain independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) against the frozen construction. Repair locally until the [closing-round rule](../../../docs/guides/narrative-review.md#current-units-and-a-whole-pass-verdict) is met. Transfer only the comment-free completed body to `docs/body`, retaining working evidence and frozen original. Finished-work requests include these expression passes unless the user explicitly waives one; settings and treatments correct their documents within construction.
