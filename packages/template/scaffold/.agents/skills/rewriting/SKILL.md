# Expressive rewriting

Preserve a reviewed construction while writing its expression anew. Scripts becomes a script performers and filmmakers can execute naturally; screenplay becomes the production's final audiovisual experience. Neither rewrites facts or results. Use naturalness questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md), and the [lifecycle](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md) for frozen originals, current reviews, and transfer.

Read the whole unit and preceding context as its recipient before editing. Freeze construction in `docs/revision/<scripts|screenplay>` with matching relative paths, then work in `docs/naturalness/<scripts|screenplay>`. Preserve file and H1/H2 identities and order. Keep the initial frozen original for every repair in this rewriting cycle.

Rewrite directions, dialogue, and audiovisual flow for this scene's actual people, purpose, pressure, and rhythm. Read dialogue through adjacent action and consecutive replies as if spoken. Keep necessary execution directions and chosen form; replace explanatory labels with playable action and sound. Give images, reactions, pauses, and transitions their intended attention and duration. Remove redundant explanation and habits without replacing them with another uniform gesture, sentence shape, cut, or closing image.

Compare working version and frozen original in both directions for meaning, state, knowledge, uncertainty, and fixed values. Additional expressive detail may realize an established decision; it may not introduce a new event, resource, cause, outcome, or world fact. Repair meaning lost during rewriting in the working version. Report a construction defect to its first owner rather than changing the frozen original to excuse it.

Finish a whole reading round before fixing findings by cause. Sweep the complete scope for repeated problems and verify replacements do not converge into a new habitual form. Test retained suspect language against a concrete alternative supplying the same information or pressure. Preserve functioning restraint, repetition, and characteristic speech on their actual contribution. Stop self-correction after a complete round adopts no further edit.

Write [evidence](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md) in working-version comments, then obtain independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) against the frozen construction. Repair locally until a complete round has no findings or edits. Transfer only the comment-free completed body to `docs/body`, retaining working evidence and frozen original. Reconcile affected construction answers and reviews with transferred text before downstream use. Finished-work requests include these expression passes unless the user explicitly waives one; settings and treatments correct their documents within construction.
