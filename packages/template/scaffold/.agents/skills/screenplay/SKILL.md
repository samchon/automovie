# Final video screenplay

Make the work the audience will see and hear. For the long route, begin with reviewed treatments and scripts after scripts [rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md). Read [discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md) and screenplay questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). This is its own construction, followed by its own expression pass.

Preserve events, choices, exchanges and their meaning, results, relationships, knowledge boundaries, fixed values, uncertainty, and required information order. Recreate their audiovisual expression: audience access and attention, selected visual and sonic detail, juxtaposition, reaction, silence, sustained action, transitions, and the relationship between image and sound. Scripts already supply full executable scenes; this stage does not complete missing story decisions or copy scenes into a new heading format.

First extract decisions and intended effects into your own concise notes rather than copying upstream sentences. Compose the final audiovisual progression from those decisions, then compare with parents in both directions for missing, altered, or invented meaning. Routine coverage, cuts, and sound placement may change within inherited decisions; changing causality, outcome, or required reveal returns to its owner. Express important inner experience through chosen action, sound, performance, or adopted narration and text.

Mirror scripts' part directories, index H1s, episode filenames, H1s, H2 identities, and order in `docs/body/screenplay`. Within each unit, give the complete viewing and listening progression with actual spoken words, important reactions, sounds, and screen text. A shot plan may accompany it; it cannot replace it. Cite the realized treatment and corresponding script. World facts arrive through those parents; missing inherited decisions require upstream repair rather than direct settings evidence.

For a short, enter here directly and use the short mode's screenplay population under `docs/body/screenplay`. Its header can hold a sufficient brief. No imaginary parents or unused world documents are required. Retain [instructions](../instructions/SKILL.md), discovery, selected screenplay contracts, construction, expressive rewriting, evidence, and independent review. Direct entry skips the first three layers, not this layer's responsibilities.

Read the complete final screenplay in viewing order before [evidence](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md). Complete construction [review](../review/SKILL.md), then [rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md), and hand the current result to [production](../production/SKILL.md). Keep traceable relationships to actual executable shots and the edit. Estimates remain provisional until performed action, fixed speech, and playback establish timing; inspect the assembled film before claiming delivery.
