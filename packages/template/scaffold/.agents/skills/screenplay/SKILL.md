# Final video screenplay

Make the work the audience will see and hear. For the long route, begin with reviewed treatments and scripts after scripts [rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md). Read [discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md) and screenplay questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md). This is its own construction, followed by its own expression pass.

Turn the inherited decisions into a fresh audiovisual progression. Select what the audience sees and hears and when it attends, recognizes, waits, and connects images and sounds. Create action and reactions specific to these people and this moment; interchangeable gestures, empty responses, and summaries of what the scene already conveys do not supply that contribution. [Screenplay principles](../contracts/story/shared/principles/screenplay.md) and [parent differentiation](../contracts/story/shared/principles/narratives.md#parent-differentiation) judge that contribution and its inherited limits.

On the long route, extract decisions and intended effects into concise notes in your own words, preserving each needed role, compared value, quantity group, and unresolved limit. Keep upstream wording and incidental within-scene action sequences out of the notes; their details seed the fresh draft. Preserve consequential chronology and required staging as decisions. Give a fresh drafting context only those notes, adopted requirements and contracts, required identities, and explicitly fixed quotations; keep upstream sentences and unfixed dialogue outside it. Compose the final audiovisual progression there, then compare the draft with the actual parents in both directions for missing, altered, or invented meaning. Record missing decisions and exchanges in your own words, close the scripts, and realize those notes through fresh image, sound, action, and speech; changing tense or formatting does not transform copied dialogue, directions, or voiceover. If a fresh context is unavailable, report that missing step rather than write and claim this construction complete in the upstream context.

A short's direct entry has no upstream expression to isolate and composes from its actual brief. Missing inherited decisions return to [upstream repair](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#upstream-repair-and-scope).

Mirror scripts' part directories, index H1s, episode filenames, H1s, H2 identities, and order in `docs/body/screenplay`. Within each unit, give the complete viewing and listening progression with actual spoken words, important reactions, sounds, and screen text, using the [working notation](../../../docs/guides/storytelling.md#working-files). A shot plan may accompany it; it cannot replace it. Cite the realized treatment and corresponding script. World facts arrive through those parents; missing inherited decisions require upstream repair rather than direct settings evidence.

For a short, enter here directly and use the short mode's screenplay population under `docs/body/screenplay`. Its header can hold a sufficient brief. No imaginary parents or unused world documents are required. Retain [instructions](../instructions/SKILL.md), discovery, selected screenplay contracts, construction, expressive rewriting, evidence, and independent review. Direct entry skips the first three layers, not this layer's responsibilities.

Before [evidence](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md), read the complete final screenplay in viewing order, then collect and judge whole-scope candidates under [repair by cause](../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#repair-by-cause), using the common, speech, and screenplay expression questions in [story contracts](../contracts/story/index.md) used by independent review, plus parent differentiation. Search for sentences sharing three or more consecutive words with scripts and recompose copied realizations; preserve required names and terms, explicitly fixed quotations, and already complete short utterances. Remove sentences whose loss changes no information, attention, or pressure; intended duration does not require filling their place. Complete construction [review](../review/SKILL.md), then [rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md), and hand the current result to [production](../production/SKILL.md). Keep traceable relationships to actual executable shots and the edit. Estimates remain provisional until performed action, fixed speech, and playback establish timing; inspect the assembled film before claiming delivery.
