# Acquire generated video

Use this only when the commission calls for generated video; a motion hint or a direct 3D film never silently requires a generator. Read the provider and setup authority in [project context](profile.md) and use the selected service's existing connector, CLI or API.

## Establish the input path

Prepare the motion and its aligned controls through [conditioning](conditioning.md), and verify the generator's actual input contract: dimensions, frame rate, duration, skeleton or coordinate convention, identity controls and output. Before a batch, prove one representative input, generation, download and playback path on a case with the intended performance, contacts and identity continuity, and fix input or integration causes before multiplying them.

## Submit and adopt takes

Bind each request to its exact input bytes, source revision, provider and model, settings, prompt, job identifier and result, without credentials, and record it under [media](media.md). Check the existing job state before retrying, since a retry can bill another job, and keep to the adopted cost and quality limits. A transient URL, seed or finished job is not an accepted take: keep the downloaded original apart from its derivatives. Inspect every candidate before adoption; a take that loses action, identity or causality is repaired at the source, request or selection, never hidden by a cut.

## Hand off

Measure the accepted takes' actual timing and hand them with their source relationships and limits to [finishing](finishing.md).
