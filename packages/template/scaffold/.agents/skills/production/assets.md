# Asset work

Use this guide with [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md) whenever a commission needs a 3D asset. 3D modeling owns how an asset is made, rigged and verified; this guide sets what the commissioned instance, motion hint or direct 3D film needs from it, including required controls and rendering budget.

## Scope The Asset

Read the adopted [production decisions](profile.md); instance-only work does not require a screenplay or whole film. A short scene can use a ready asset with a few placements; a recurring character may need editable proportions, expressions, and motion across many scenes. A standalone modeling task defines its required views and controls without a screenplay. When appearance, form or material needs study, follow [production research](research.md) to inspect original visual evidence, retain local references and derive supported constraints.

## Start With Programmed Control

For newly authored work, build stick figures, coarse block forms or simple frames first. Keep actor/object identity, meaningful dimensions, pivots, attachments and control interfaces independent of surface shape and tessellation, and expose actual project-owned representation/detail options and per-instance overrides where needed. Verify the coarse form and promised controls. For animated work, [motion](../motion/SKILL.md) owns choreography, timing and physical feedback, including the complete low-cost motion sequence before visual upgrades. Retained assets already meeting the task need no rebuild.

After the coarse representation and any commissioned motion work, upgrade only objects whose visible role needs it, retaining the controls and measured host budget. This applies to mixed-detail diffusion hints and requested finished direct 3D films. Important hint objects can remain coarse frames; an unimportant object can remain screenplay description when the chosen conditioning does not need to show it. Record these choices through production decisions.

A generated or supplied 2D image can provide appearance reference or a supported conditioning asset; it does not replace coded main-scene motion and the whole assembled video when that hint is commissioned. Recover editable geometry only where motion, deformation or code ownership needs that control.

## Human

Human is an optional identity and expression representation, not a prerequisite for correct performance. Read the installed Human package's current public surface before using it, and claim only the switches and detail options that surface provides; a planned package feature is not an installed capability. Use a stick figure, a lower detail model or a supported detail option when it fits the task, and present project-authored alternatives as the project's own.

## Inspect The Commissioned Use

The commission sets which uses an inspection covers. An instance-only task inspects that instance and its promised controls; a screenplay-only task requires no render. A static background prop is promised fewer views and motion states than a reusable articulated character, and every promised use includes its shot cameras, visible details and assembled neighbors. A motion hint is judged as its complete commissioned hint artifact, not as an uncommissioned post-diffusion film.

For commissioned video, inspect [contiguous segments](../review/video.md#review-contiguous-segments) as they are implemented, then the complete programmed progression and assembled timeline, including transitions and ending; stills and isolated clips cannot replace it. Inspect the visible consequences of motion's contacts and constraints rather than inferring success from admitted numbers. After a detail upgrade, compare the same affected segments for changed proportions, pivots, attachments, deformation, contact, timing and silhouette, and remeasure cost. A hint needs reliable action and staging; photographic detail is required only by its adopted output contract. A requested direct 3D film receives its commissioned visual finish after low-cost motion works.

## Hand Off And Judge

Hand admitted rigs and controls to [motion](../motion/SKILL.md), which owns performance and [retargeting](../motion/retargeting.md); [motion research](../motion/research.md) informs acquisition for performance and conditioning. Maintain the actual settings-to-asset and asset-to-shot relationships under [execution traceability](../evidence/execution.md). Use the [asset questions](../contracts/assets.md) for review; [contracts](../evidence/SKILL.md) decides whether to select formal evidence and which units answer it. Use [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) for final assembled acceptance or asset handoff; local inspection establishes only its observed use.
