# Stage the screenplay for the camera

Use this branch when a screenplay or standalone performance becomes executable scenes, or when blocking, cameras or shot continuity changes. Read the actual source decisions and [production scope](../production/profile.md). [Asset workflows](../3d-modeling/workflows.md) owns representations, [frame evaluation](timeline.md) owns time sampling, and production owns the final edit. A shot list or an animated object without the required exchange does not realize a scene.

## Decide the observable performance

Read each required source unit in context, including its entry, consequence and audience knowledge. Identify the required action or change, who or what initiates it, any intended perception or reaction, and the resulting state. Include consequential props, support surfaces, entrances and exits. Preserve silence, stillness or an off-screen event when it performs a real source decision; not every beat needs locomotion or a new shot.

Choose executable shot boundaries from that progression. One source unit may need several shots and a shot may realize several units; do not derive shots from paragraph count. Keep the actual parent addresses, required action/result and timing with the shot that executes them under [execution traceability](../evidence/execution.md). An animatic may help plan, but neither it nor a coverage table replaces the complete programmed performance.

## Block one coherent space

Establish meaningful dimensions, local/world frames, participant identities, facing, routes, eye lines and the placement of interactive objects. Use location decisions from the actual settings or direct brief and the adopted asset controls rather than placing unrelated approximations beside their citations. For newly authored representations, start with coarse forms whose silhouettes and articulation distinguish the required action. Retained assets already meeting that use need no rebuild. An action needing a hand/object exchange requires usable contact and attachment controls even at low detail.

Program anticipation, action, reaction, pauses and the resulting state against the shared clock. Use [physical feedback](physics.md) and [retargeting](retargeting.md) where their supported operations govern the performance. Actors and objects retain one transform/control owner; a clip, attachment and procedural driver must not fight for the same state. Resolve scale or reach incompatibilities at their owner rather than changing the intended outcome.

When using the installed shot-definition/compiler route, read its current public contracts for registration, runtime capabilities, required participants, opening/closing states, events and continuity. Feed the actual prior accepted state when continuing a performance, inspect the complete result envelope and consume its compiled artifacts. Choose independent event predicates from the source requirement, not values copied from a failing output. Other supported authoring routes need the same accountable performance; do not resurrect a retired project loader or lint gate. Compilation produces no captured video and cannot certify every visible property.

## Direct the actual view

Choose camera position, projection/lens, framing, movement and cut points for what the audience must perceive at each moment. Preserve the intended spatial relations, screen direction, eye lines, knowledge and reveal timing across views. Deliberate discontinuity or concealment is valid when it serves the adopted experience; conventional coverage is not mandatory.

Translate the canonical screenplay and its linked shot plan into the actual camera and edit, including meaningful turns within a shot. Identify the visible subject or relationship, the screen scale needed to read it, and the performed action, speech or reaction that changes or holds attention. Place those decisions on the shared performance clock; a shot id or source H2 alone supplies no camera direction. Keep a deliberate sustained view when its blocking carries the exchange, and choose other coverage where a distant master loses the decisive action. Do not replace this work with automatic speaker tracking or a cut on every line. If a consequential viewing decision is absent or fails in the actual output, return the creative change to the [screenplay owner](../screenwriting/screenplay.md), propagate any upstream repair, and implement its current revision. The canonical film and the one being rendered must agree.

Inspect required subjects, contact, gesture and reaction at the delivered aspect, crop and screen scale. Check clipping, camera intersections, occlusion and competing background detail through the full relevant action. A subject box intersecting a camera frustum does not establish readable pixels or a measured occlusion ratio. Use the actual camera frames and the supported image observations needed by the chosen requirement; a successful camera solve or an alternate inspection view cannot prove the delivered view works.

## Carry state through the edit

For continuing action, preserve positions, facing, pose, attachments and prop possession across the actual shot boundary, together with relevant velocity, gait/contact phase and sound. A different camera angle must not reset the same performance. A story-time jump can establish a new state deliberately; edit time and story time remain distinct.

Inspect the complete sequence at its adopted representation under [rendered-use inspection](../3d-modeling/inspection.md), including reactions and both sides of joins, before detail upgrades. Keep the executed source, current observations and unresolved limits with their actual consumers. Hand accepted shots to [finishing](../production/finishing.md); [conditioning](../production/conditioning.md) owns any commissioned companion passes. Narrative changes preserve the [closed-proof boundary](../screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes).
