# Programmed motion

Realize the requested performance through code. Read the adopted [production decisions](../production/profile.md), actual brief or final screenplay, current source and installed engine APIs. A single moving instance needs only its promised behavior; a whole-video commission needs its complete scene progression and assembled timeline. Keep user-fixed choices and uncommissioned work explicit.

1. Read [staging for the camera](staging.md) when translating source decisions into executable blocking, views or shot continuity. Establish the scene clock, units, local frames, actor and object identities, attachments, camera and consequential action. Give shared timing and state one owner. Read [frame evaluation and assembly](timeline.md) when choosing sample times, replaying stateful motion, or wiring shots into a render host. Connect required screenplay units to the source that actually executes them.
2. Start new animated instances at the low-cost representation that [asset work](../production/assets.md#start-with-programmed-control) builds first.
3. Build the activity's motion library before staging the film. Give each movement family its own reusable, data-driven module: actions or techniques, locomotion and footwork, responses and reactions, contact and collision, and every secondary participant such as officials or partners. Take phase tables, timing, reach, distances and limits from the [performance study](../production/research.md#performance-study), and transcribe the screenplay's choreography as data the modules execute; the film composes modules rather than hand-tuning shots or growing a film-specific fitting system. Unit-test every module against the study's measured values and inspect it in the moving Viewer with a joint overlay before any scene uses it. Build repeatable behavior as parameterized actions evaluated in local time, with explicit phase boundaries, events and entry/exit state. Distinguish measured preparation, delivery, contact and recovery from authored timing, and keep deterministic variations within verified bounds. Check each action's behavior through [unit tests](../development/testing.md) and the actual moving Viewer before composing its instances, reactions, paths and compatible transitions against the common clock. Use installed engine kinematics, constraints, performance and physical computations where they govern the motion; [physical feedback](physics.md) owns that branch. Actual contact constrains the continuing action and supplies the shared event and state for its counterpart's response and subsequent recovery; the scene consumes those results and diagnostics.
4. Implement one short contiguous segment through the actual scene host, including its invoked assets, performance, adopted view and required sound. Follow the segment duration adopted by the production; otherwise choose a manageable action boundary. Complete [moving Viewer review](../review/video.md#review-contiguous-segments) and cause-based repair before extending from its accepted ending, so defects do not propagate through unobserved motion. Continue through the actual edit plan, its joins and ending; production owns final assembly and encoding. Segment acceptance does not establish the complete film.
5. Once low-cost performance works, upgrade selected objects through code options to the commissioned detail within measured budgets. Preserve identities and intended scene decisions, then recheck affected contacts, deformation, visibility, timing and cost; changed proportions or collision shapes can require motion adjustment. A direct 3D film follows the same sequence and finishes at its commissioned visual and sound quality.

Read [motion adoption and retargeting](retargeting.md) for external clips, changed rigs/proportions and contact preservation. [Primary research](research.md) informs method choices without promising installed features; [motion questions](../contracts/motion.md) judge the actual performance.

Record important representation, physics, provider and budget choices through [production decisions](../production/profile.md), and keep implementation in source. Generated motion or media enters as fixed inputs with provenance; it still needs the same performance inspection. Finish a source change through [development](../development/SKILL.md). Hand scene output and its actual timing to [editing and finishing](../production/finishing.md) for the real host, complete encoded artifact and handoff. [Delivery review](../review/delivery.md) judges only the requested asset, hint or finished video.
