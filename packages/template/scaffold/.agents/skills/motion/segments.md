# Review contiguous segments

Watch each finished short segment in the production's moving Viewer before extending it, with its adopted scene, view, clock and sound, so defects do not spread through unwatched motion. [Visual debugging](../3d-modeling/visual-debugging.md) owns repeatable observation conditions and cost.

Watch the whole segment at normal speed, listening when sound is commissioned, then replay its exchanges, contacts and joins slowly through the host's seek controls. Judge its staging, camera and timing and how its accepted cases join; a case that no longer convinces as the depicted practice returns to [case review](SKILL.md). Numbers, hashes and receipts support that judgment but cannot close it, and stills or sampled poses cannot replace continuous playback. After watching, locate defects with a joint overlay on the [dense contact sheets](../review/delivery.md#measure-and-compare) of the 10-second review segments it spans.

Each 10-second review segment, taken in film order, is one round: capture its sheets, review them at once, repair its findings by cause, recheck that [affected interval](../production/stages.md#iterate-on-the-affected-interval), and only then move to the next segment, checking the seam between them. Accepted segments do not establish the whole film; [delivery review](../review/delivery.md) watches the assembled artifact.
