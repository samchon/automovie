# Contract targets

The five shared contract directories and the selected `docs/language` module form the reusable inventory installed into the project. Treatment, script, and screenplay construction select language discovery plus their construction principles and obligations. Final screenplays separately select shared and language naturalness. Settings, research, design, and brief layers select their common foundations and specialist contracts. The graph declaration and its in-memory binding manifest identify each pass's exact selection.

`docs/discovery` defines open production-specific searches, `docs/naturalness` defines expression-only final-screenplay checklists, `docs/upstream` records what each inheriting construction unit learned by testing its actual parents, and `docs/principles` defines the no-exclusion checklist every selected construction H2, H3, or H4 answers for itself. `docs/obligations` defines roles fulfilled collectively by relevant construction H2 or source-export owners, with optional aggregate accounts for shared conclusions. [Production-specific contract](work-specific.md) owns additional production rules, and [Evidence staging](staging.md) owns their graph and annotation mechanics.

A completed settings, research, or design H2 owns each delivery, repaint, dialogue, or simulation decision. Governed source implements that decision as a typed value; a runtime value is not a Markdown evidence host. Keep semantic ownership at the authored H2 and its configured source relationship, following [Ownership](../../../README.md#ownership) for the project file boundary.

## Discovery

Each authored layer selects its common and specialist discovery files. Treatments, scripts, and screenplays also select `language/discovery/signals.md` from the production's chosen language module.

Each discovery H2 owns an open production-specific search whose result cannot be enumerated from the reusable inventory alone. [Production-specific contract](work-specific.md) owns how the author conducts that search and records a retained rule or truthful no-result; [Evidence staging](staging.md) owns the claim population, activation stage, and annotation placement. A discovery target defines the question and does not become a second copy of either procedure.

## Naturalness

Each final screenplay H2, H3, and H4 answers every selected `naturalness/core/common.md`, `naturalness/story/screenplays.md`, and `language/naturalness/screenplays.md` H2 as a no-exclusion checklist. Apply these checklists to final expression after the construction screenplay completes at evidence and its content is frozen. They never authorize a new content decision.

Construction principles own semantic and structural adequacy from the first draft. [Mechanical audiovisual description](../../../docs/principles/story/screenplays.md#mechanical-audiovisual-description) owns the protected physical clauses; [Screenplay naturalness](../production-lifecycle/naturalness.md) owns the audience-language revision procedure. Population recurrence remains an obligation answered across construction owners; it does not become a second final-unit checklist.

Use [Evidence staging](staging.md#relationship-types) for final-to-construction lineage and the separate annotation populations.

## Upstream revision

[Shared upstream revision duties](upstream.md) owns the unit-local question, parent differentiation boundary, positive repair answer, and concrete negative answer for each inheriting population. Read it after completing an inheriting unit and before staging its other evidence. Settings and research select no upstream target.

## Principles

Each `##` principle owns one semantic concern. Its title names the concern, its body states the binding rule, and its review question asks what the current unit must inspect about itself. Principle references are checklists: every selected authored H2, H3, or H4, and every selected source symbol when a claim governs source, answers every applicable `##`; exclusions are refused. A host that cannot truthfully claim compliance is defective. State an item's applicability limits inside that item rather than turning a missing answer into an exclusion.

## Obligations

Each selected obligation H2 receives positive evidence from its relevant authored H2 owner or owners. A shared conclusion may use the family's declared aggregate account under `docs/accounts/<layer>`. The target's substantive scope decides which contributors must be read and compared. Each active layer covers its own selected obligations.

Source obligations use the same ordinary no-exclusion coverage across their selected public exports. Several exports cite an item when its role requires several contributors.

An unanswered principle calls for reviewing the current unit against that target. An uncovered obligation calls for finding or completing its actual owners, inspecting the required population where the target calls for comparison, and recording the resulting evidence with those owners.
