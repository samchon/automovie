# Storytelling

Make the requested viewing experience through decisions with clear owners. Read [composition](composition.md) before choosing or restructuring the narrative route. Use the [research references](references.md) when interpreting document names or production conventions; they inform the existing contracts rather than add another checklist.

An LLM authors the complete final screenplay. Long work retains [settings](../settings/SKILL.md) → [treatments](../treatments/SKILL.md) → [scripts](../scripts/SKILL.md) → [final screenplay](../screenplay/SKILL.md), each doing its own work. A short may enter directly at final screenplay. Use [instructions](../instructions/SKILL.md) for requirements and delegated choices, and [discovery](../discovery/SKILL.md) for uncovered responsibilities.

[Evidence writing](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md) records accountable answers. [Narrative completion](../narrative-completion/SKILL.md) owns construction, current independent review, and the scripts and screenplay expression handoffs; [upstream repair](../upstream-repair/SKILL.md) owns inherited defects. Use [scene notation](../scripts/notation.md) before drafting seen and heard action.

Hand the screenplay to [production](../production/SKILL.md) for the commissioned output. When video is commissioned, use [motion](../motion/SKILL.md) to program the main scenes' performance and staging and assemble the whole movie hint, even with simple figures and coarse objects. Stills and pilot clips cannot replace that scope. [3D modeling](../3d-modeling/SKILL.md) and [audio](../audio/SKILL.md) supply representation and sound. [Delivery review](../delivery-review/SKILL.md) inspects a separately commissioned screenplay, whole motion hint, or requested direct 3D or post-diffusion film; a correct document or coverage check alone does not establish a rendered experience.
