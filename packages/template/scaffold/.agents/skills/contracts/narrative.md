# Narrative

## Intent and causality

Does this unit fulfill its part of the request through a comprehensible progression? Identify the relevant facts, causes, choices or processes, consequences, and information available to participants and audience. Preserve intentional uncertainty and the chosen form; a demonstration or contemplative film need not invent a character conflict. Name unresolved decisions that block downstream work.

## Screen and sound

Can a filmmaker stage the important action, actual dialogue, meaningful reactions, and transitions without inventing the unit's essential events? Make the intended image, sound, point of attention, and duration legible at this unit's level. Distinguish screen action from internal explanation and leave production choices open where the request leaves them open.

## Continuity and coverage

Does the selected population carry its required events and promises through scene, episode, and film boundaries without unintended gaps, repetition, or contradiction? Trace important state and knowledge changes, setups and payoffs, duration allocations, and source units to their consumers. Identify intentional omissions and the limits of any partial scope; local success does not establish a finished long work.
