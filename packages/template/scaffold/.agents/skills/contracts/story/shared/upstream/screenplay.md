# Upstream repair exposed by final screenplay

Each final screenplay H2 answers about its actual script parents and their treatment and settings causes. A short work authorized to begin at screenplay instead identifies the brief and creative constraints that serve as its actual parents; it does not claim nonexistent upstream stages.

## What did audiovisual realization prove missing or unusable upstream? {#screenplay-upstream-repair}

Identify the exact earliest owner of any missing, contradictory, overdetermined, or unrealizable event, execution, access, relationship, knowledge, staging, world value, or form exposed while creating the final audiovisual experience. Trace a script defect to its treatment or settings cause where applicable, and propagate an authorized repair through all affected existing descendants, evidence, performance and sound, edit, timing, and observed output. Name future verification as pending rather than require unmade output to complete this writing stage. A polished transition cannot conceal unexecuted action or an unsupported result; direct settings evidence cannot replace an absent script decision. If no repair is needed, identify concrete actual parent decisions tested unchanged and how they survived realization. A short work tests its actual brief and screenplay-owned decisions without inventing skipped settings, treatments, or scripts.
