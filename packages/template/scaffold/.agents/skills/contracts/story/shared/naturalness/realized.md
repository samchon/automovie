# Realized exchange revision principles

Each revised script and final screenplay H2 answers from actual exchanges with their neighboring action. A settings voice description or treatment plan cannot answer for speech realization.

## Does a person say this to this person here? {#speech-yield}

Follow each exchange as if reading it aloud, including its surrounding action. Does its language fit the speaker, listener, situation, knowledge, relationship, and immediate tactic, and does the exchange leave an actual recognition, refusal, changed position, broken silence, or other inherited result? Inspect explanation of facts already shared, arguments recited as lists, generic declarations of a unit's theme, neighboring action repeated in another register, matching metaphors traded between speakers, inappropriate status or relationship language, uniform neutral exposition, and replies whose meaning shifted while the editor tried to preserve words.

Preserve what each participant says, wants, knows, gains, and loses. Adopted ritual questions, authority exercised through general statements, weaponized proverbs, consequential repetition, and speech habits may work in context. Do not standardize everything into short lines, interruptions, hidden meanings, or added conflict, or invent new demands, facts, victories, and outcomes. The alternative test and candidate record follow [common expression principles](common.md#reads-as-written).
