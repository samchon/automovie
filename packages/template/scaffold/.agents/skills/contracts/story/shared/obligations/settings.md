# Settings population obligations

Each question has exactly one answering settings file and compares the completed population. Its positive answer lives in that file's detached construction evidence. Inspect all relevant domains without making unused domains or empty files mandatory.

## Is the work's scope and world a coherent, addressable basis? {#world-and-scope}

Declare the audience, central experience, questions or formal effects, concrete recurring rewards and engine or chosen alternative, distinctive variation, and how the work title serves that promise. User-fixed title wording is not subject to a taste judgment. Establish the delivery's actual beginning, ending, inclusions, exclusions, completeness, and relation to a larger work. Declare film, episode, season, or other actual release units, their intended number and duration ranges where applicable, any justified delivery ceiling, and any purchase or access boundary. For a series, establish what recurs, what closes within a release, and what develops across releases; independent, serial, and mixed forms need their own usable rule. Scripts chooses the actual boundaries from treatment material. Explain the audience or delivery reason instead of copying another medium's size rules. Duration is a target for sufficient material, never permission to pad.

Map the work's needed domains to current fact owners: work promise; world, geography, time, environment and species; society, law, economy, culture and language; systems, powers and technology; agents; relationships; places; objects and phenomena; background; knowledge; sensory consistency; and research. For each, distinguish required facts, harmless incidental defaults, justified exclusion, and unresolved matters with their decision conditions. Consider domains without inventing unused content or forcing their names into directories. Consequential reality or genre assumptions need explicit owners even if familiar. Record deviations before descendants depend on them.

Check direct and derived coherence across chronology, ages, distances, travel, units, money, capacities, resources, authority, scale, spatial fit, and routes. Compare quantities implied by different records, not just values written together. Explain why initial threats, mysteries, injustices, absences, or other unresolved conditions have persisted, identifying those who could have ended them and their limits or interests; recent conditions need the actual change. Keep expression and audience access rules with their canonical instructions or work contracts, and provide their location from settings. Repair contradictions at the earliest owner without false precision.

## Can the people, relationships, and audiovisual world be realized consistently? {#people-and-audiovisual-world}

Identify every independently participating or consequentially mentioned person, group, institution, or nonhuman force, including unnamed and one-off participants. A group shares an owner only while acting as one; separate members when individual speech, choice, reaction, consequence, or relationship matters. Scale depth to consequence, not whether the participant has a name. For central and recurring agents, establish situated physical and material life, age or life stage, social and legal position, responsibilities and dependencies; drives, fears, values, biases and coping; abilities, access, limits, costs, alternatives, refusal and thresholds; distinct knowledge, error, attention, memory and interpretation; and observable speech and behavior. For nonhuman agents, define equivalent inputs, priorities, operation, and reaction thresholds without inventing human psychology.

Give all used agents recognizable appearance and, when heard, voice at the needed depth for the adopted abstraction and presentation. Fix attributes that affect identity, causality, spatial feasibility, or continuity; retain references and shared design rules instead of inventing unseen specifications. For central and recurring figures, resolve applicable age impression, height, build, proportions, facial structure, hair, skin, marks, clothing colors and materials, carried items, and voice range, texture, resonance, force, tempo, breath, pronunciation, intonation, and state-dependent changes. Brief figures need fewer concrete distinguishing attributes. Nonhuman bodies, moving groups, and deliberately hidden appearances need their applicable visible and audible boundary. Independent artists or casting directors should reach the same recognizable identity, consistent with retained references, rather than guess from labels such as beautiful or ordinary. Leave incidental design choices to production where their variation cannot change those established facts.

Define consequential relationships from both sides, including shared history, interests, dependency, obligation, refusal, asymmetry, debt, rupture, recovery, and change conditions. Establish credible resistance and irreversible thresholds without prewriting the actual change sequence. Define the world's shared material, architectural, costume, technological, visual, and audible grammar and meaningful distinctions between factions, regions, eras, and classes. Rendering style and shot design remain production choices.

For every used scene location, establish the shape, human-relative dimensions, entrances, windows, levels, paths, orientation, fixed objects, furniture, surfaces, materials, colors, wear, light sources, exterior visibility, and audible surroundings needed for its actual use. A retained plan or reference may resolve these together; unrelated rooms and decorative inventories are unnecessary. Recurring places need applicable variations by time, weather, season, occupancy, and access; outdoors need the used zones, distances, and connecting routes rather than every room or district. Establish recurring, consequential, or focused objects and visible phenomena through size, shape, material, color, markings, and their appearance and sound in operation. Background objects may use the world grammar. Baseline and externally fixed states remain settings facts; treatment events own the occurrence and results of newly chosen damage or occupation changes. References connect those owners without duplicating event progression.
