# Narrative unit principles

Each treatment, script, and final screenplay H2 answers for its own progression. Treatments decide it in an event document; scripts and screenplay realize it in their respective forms. Settings does not answer these questions.

## Does the unit create a legible change and reason to continue? {#unit-progression}

Does the unit leave a recognizable result in events, understanding, relationships, emotion, pressure, rhythm, or formal effect? Connect entry, operation, and result through cause and consequence, action and response, question and answer, reversal, or the work's owned formal relation. Establish how time, place, knowledge, injury, appearance, clothing, possession, resources, status, relationships, and promises change. A recurrence or rest may be functional; simply repeating the inherited state without a changed force is not. Mark departures into memory, anticipation, repetition, or habitual time and the return where needed.

Does the reason to continue arise from this work's question, pursuit, observation, humor, emotion, or formal accumulation? Where an actor drives the change, make the active desire, belief, fear, value, limitation, alternative, and actual cost intelligible, including the pressure or recognition that makes a change of character credible. Do not invent a cost, victim, motive, or conflict to answer a tag in an observational or nonhuman process. New names, rules, and relationships arrive with the context needed when they affect the audience's understanding. An exit leaves an earned state, question, pressure, or formal effect; a complete unit or intentional pause need not force a cliffhanger.

## Does it perform this layer's work rather than repeat its parent? {#parent-differentiation}

Compare the actual parents across the whole unit. Does treatment turn facts into chosen events and information order, script turn those events into partitioned executable action with actual dialogue, staging, and view, and final screenplay turn that execution into finished image, sound, attention, duration, and transitions? Changing tense, formatting, or volume without making the layer's decisions fails. Carry the needed inherited values without an inert inventory, and preserve already complete short artifacts without requiring ornamental variation. If a parent has prematurely consumed the next layer's work or left essential decisions absent, repair that owner and affected descendants together rather than disguising the duplication.
