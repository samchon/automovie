# Common expression revision principles

Each script construction H2 answers from its current body against its parent treatments; each revised final screenplay H2 answers against its frozen construction. Scripts completes expression in construction so content-shaped failures can be repaired there. Settings and treatments receive document editing during construction. Inspect the entire selected body for each named failure, record all found candidates and their locations, and explain repairs or justified preservation. If none are found, identify the actual inspected scope. A few examples never prove a whole-scope negative.

## Does it read as deliberate writing with a concrete world? {#reads-as-written}

Does each instruction, utterance, image, and detail belong to this moment, carrying meaning through an intelligible body, space, object, sound, or adopted narrator? Inspect for repeated explanation of an action's meaning, closing aphorisms or balanced summaries, background that postpones entry, research or later commentary that stops the scene, lists of absent acts or sounds used to manufacture stillness, delivery labels that repeat a line, repeated appearance or rank introductions, habitual gestures and filler, invented misunderstandings to negate, empty links, arbitrary sentence molds, and authoring jargon. Inspect modifiers, qualifications, contrasts, and formatting that carry no information, access, or pressure, including removed padding regenerated in different words.

Speaker identifiers and useful performance parentheticals are execution instructions, not redundant delivery labels; inspect parenthetical excess under [script execution](../principles/scripts.md#executable-scene). First-entry appearance and costume instructions, or a role, relationship or single scene-used identifying trait conveyed through action, address or perception, are not repeated introductions. Removing those cues can make the people impossible to identify.

A character's thought repeats explanation when it merely adds the same conclusion or emotion already shown or about to be shown. A calculation, memory or expectation may instead reveal new present understanding, hope or burden; recalling an earlier event or sharing a later action's goal alone does not make it repetition. Prove that distinction with the passage and neighboring context. Reusing an object's name, a word twice or a script's framing is not by itself a habitual gesture or filler failure.

Does the actual language read and sound idiomatically within its adopted variety, period, register, and relationships? Check word combinations, precise verbs, agreement, word order, recoverable subjects and references, pronouns, nominal chains, clauses, and imported syntax for an immediate understanding of who does what to whom. A correct abstract meaning does not excuse a translated construction or an expression nobody would use here. Preserve functional dialect, quotation, institutional terms, fragments, passive forms, inversion, and deliberate repetition; do not ban grammatical forms or replace all formal words mechanically. Repair language without adding actions, causes, or opposition.

Make viewpoint and movement, object use, and cold, hunger, weight, injury or other bodily conditions concrete enough to understand and feel the current action. Scripts serves executable placement and conditions; final screenplay selects their audience experience and emphasis. Unlocated inventories, unmarked gaze changes, vague impressions in place of required color, material, scale, or position, interchangeable metaphors that erase distinctions, and physical limits reduced to names or numbers fail. Use the inherited values and permitted access without inventing canonical facts. Selective detail and restrained or documentary form may work without describing everything. Even informative details fail this realization when decoding their sequence displaces the present danger, choice or relationship. Judge sufficient specificity and excessive emphasis together in this question rather than maximizing one and undoing it elsewhere.

Does the sentence-level record classify every sentence against every applicable named failure, with exact locations and yes/no results rather than only noticed candidates? Judge only the failures this question names; style preference or a legitimate difference in interpretation outside them is not a failure. Treat an unresolved named failure as yes on the initial text. A passage rewritten to repair a previous yes becomes yes again only when this or another applicable failure is clear, so repeated ambiguous judgments cannot create an endless repair loop. Retain a yes only under an exception permitted by the contract that names it, with the passage proving that exception. Here the exceptions are useful execution directions, meaningful silence, character thought with the new understanding, expectation or burden established above, adopted narration and form, functional lies, refrains, speech habits and formal regularity. Narration must realize its adopted contribution through actual wording, placement or image-sound relations; merely relabeling restated scene meaning does not qualify. A function label such as memory, hope or rhythm, nearby achievement, previous protection, or an alternative judged less effective cannot create another exception. Simple or flat writing is not inherently a defect.

## Does rewriting preserve the storyline and uncertainty? {#expression-fidelity}

Would separate downstream authors reading the draft and revision produce the same world facts, events, causal connections, people and relationships, motives, choices, consequences, knowledge boundaries, chronology, reveal order, values such as names, quantities, dimensions and colors, and degree of certainty? A difference that would cause another fact or scene fails; a change of wording, emphasis, nuance, sentence shape, or punctuation does not fail by itself. Fidelity and natural reception are separate judgments.

For scripts construction, the comparison original is its parent treatments. Preserve realized setup and payoff, motivation-setting speech, promised narrator voice and humor as functions the audience must experience. Removing them can leave the same events with lost causality or tone. If their current expression is a named failure, recompose that function without the failure rather than delete it.

Rewrite fictional dialogue, sentences, paragraphs, and their presentation freely within the established storyline and adopted work form. Preserve meaning rather than the draft's artificial register, negation patterns, source disclaimers, or annotation vocabulary. Clarifying an implied existing actor or developing bodily performance, image, sound, and subtext can realize the scene; adding a dependency, changing a result, resolving an open relationship, or replacing a concrete value with an impression changes it. Report a needed semantic change during naturalness; a separate construction repair returns to its earliest owner under [upstream repair](../../../../screenwriting/repair.md#upstream-defects). Removing surplus may lower duration below its target; never refill it with new facts, events, or replacement padding.
