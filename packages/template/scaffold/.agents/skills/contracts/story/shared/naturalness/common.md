# Common expression revision principles

Each revised script or final screenplay H2 answers from its current body against its construction draft. Settings and treatments receive document editing during construction, not this expression pass. Inspect the entire selected body for each named failure, record all found candidates and their locations, and explain repairs or justified preservation. If none are found, identify the actual inspected scope. A few examples never prove a whole-scope negative.

## Does it read as deliberate writing with a concrete world? {#reads-as-written}

Does each instruction, utterance, image, and detail belong to this moment, carrying meaning through an intelligible body, space, object, sound, or adopted narrator? Inspect for repeated explanation of an action's meaning, closing aphorisms or balanced summaries, background that postpones entry, research or later commentary that stops the scene, lists of absent acts or sounds used to manufacture stillness, delivery labels that repeat a line, repeated appearance or rank introductions, habitual gestures and filler, invented misunderstandings to negate, empty links, arbitrary sentence molds, and authoring jargon. Inspect modifiers, qualifications, contrasts, and formatting that carry no information, access, or pressure, including removed padding regenerated in different words.

Make viewpoint and movement, object position and use, and cold, hunger, weight, injury, or other bodily conditions concrete where they matter. Unlocated inventories, unmarked gaze changes, vague impressions in place of required color, material, scale, or position, interchangeable metaphors that erase distinctions, and physical limits reduced to names or numbers fail. Use the inherited values and permitted access without inventing canonical facts. Selective detail and restrained or documentary form may work without describing everything.

Preserve useful execution directions, adopted narration, functional lies, refrains, speech habits, and formal regularity. A questionable form is justified by comparing it with a workable alternative that conveys the same information, access, and pressure without that form, and showing its additional effect in the actual passage. Deletion alone or an assertion that a function exists is insufficient. Narration may add an adopted judgment, irony, cost, or anticipation unavailable through the scene; it cannot earn protection merely by restating what the scene already gives. Simple or flat writing is not inherently a defect.

## Does rewriting preserve the storyline and uncertainty? {#expression-fidelity}

Would separate downstream authors reading the draft and revision produce the same world facts, events, causal connections, people and relationships, motives, choices, consequences, knowledge boundaries, chronology, reveal order, values such as names, quantities, dimensions and colors, and degree of certainty? A difference that would cause another fact or scene fails; a change of wording, emphasis, nuance, sentence shape, or punctuation does not fail by itself. Fidelity and natural reception are separate judgments.

Rewrite fictional dialogue, sentences, paragraphs, and their presentation freely within the established storyline and adopted work form. Preserve meaning rather than the draft's artificial register, negation patterns, source disclaimers, or annotation vocabulary. Clarifying an implied existing actor or developing bodily performance, image, sound, and subtext can realize the scene; adding a dependency, changing a result, resolving an open relationship, or replacing a concrete value with an impression changes it. A needed semantic change returns to its earliest owner and propagates through descendants. Removing surplus may lower duration below its target; never refill it with new facts, events, or replacement padding.
