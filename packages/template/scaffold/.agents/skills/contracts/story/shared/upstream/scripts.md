# Upstream repair exposed by scripts

Each script H2 answers about its actual treatment parents and, through them, settings causes. Repair follows the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#upstream-repair-and-scope).

## What did scene execution prove missing or unusable upstream? {#script-upstream-repair}

Identify exact treatment decisions about event, cause, knowledge, relationship, time, place, appearance, object, or inherited work constraint that are missing, contradictory, prematurely fixed, or impossible to execute. Trace a settings cause to its exact settings owner rather than bypass a missing treatment with a direct settings acknowledgment. Report the conflict and earliest repair location. If no repair is needed, identify actual parent decisions executed unchanged and the concrete test they survived. Do not invent core events or canonical facts in scripts, and do not call an unexamined parent sound.
