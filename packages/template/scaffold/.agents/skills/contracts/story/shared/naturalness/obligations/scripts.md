# Script expression population obligation

The complete revised script population answers once for execution rhythm across scenes and delivery units.

## Does the arrangement of execution follow changing pressure? {#execution-distribution}

Compare entry, action-response ordering, speaking turns, direction density, block duration, silence, view changes, and exits across the whole. Report habitual beat sequences or mechanically equal blocks that flatten different situations, including a standard gesture before every line or an explanatory conclusion after every scene. Compare units for the same audible closing voiceover, question-and-answer mold or battle progression; changing people or objects can leave the audience experiencing the same scene. Repeated scene headings for time, place and weather, adopted actor-specific performance parentheticals, and positional identifiers for unnamed participants are legitimate execution notation: production sets up each scene independently. These are not repeated dramatic realization. Actual continuous action, a fixed formal view, ritual recurrence, and sustained stillness may justify regularity. Preserve its realized effect, not just its label. Use the candidate record and comparison required by [common expression obligations](common.md#recurrent-frames), repairing all found occurrences together while preserving script-owned partition and executable staging.
