# Common population obligations

Each question compares the complete declared layer population and has exactly one answering body file. Different questions may have different file respondents. Positive construction answers use that file's detached evidence document; individual H2 answers cannot substitute for these comparisons.

## Is the complete scope assigned to useful, unique owners? {#scope-and-ownership}

Enumerate the required beginning, ending, intervals, people, events, facts, constraints, relationships, and results, and map each to its smallest current owner. Report omissions, incompatible duplicate owners, hidden independent content, and files that contribute nothing to facts, progression, relationships, experience, or formal effect. Split independently changeable or refutable facts and progressions; keep inseparable attributes and continuous formal operations together. Remove or reconnect purposeless material rather than maintaining a file to fill a schema. Omissions require an authorized scope decision, not a convenient exclusion.

Compare central and peripheral material for adequate development and disproportionate expansion. Settings and treatments need sufficient decisions; scripts and screenplay need sufficient execution and experience. Counts, text lengths, parent-child ratios, and diffs locate passages to reread but do not establish quality, completeness, or failure.

## Are layer boundaries and work-wide choices consistent? {#layer-boundaries}

Identify the semantic owners of world facts and baseline constraints in settings, events and reveal order in treatments, partition and scene execution in scripts, and finished audiovisual realization in final screenplay. Appearance belongs to settings, event time and place to treatments, staged audiovisual state and meaningful view to scripts, and finished emphasis, image-sound relations, transitions, and temporal experience to screenplay. A short work authorized to begin at screenplay owns the necessary facts and creative choices in its actual brief and screenplay instead of fabricating skipped planning populations. A work's language, audience, adopted variants, registers, code-switching, access rules, and expressive form need one identifiable instruction or work-contract owner; actual character voice remains a world property.

Report decisions invented downstream, duplicated authority, or planning language substituted for a layer's output, and repair the earliest owner with its consumers. Treat declared nonstandard language and form as choices with boundaries and audience context rather than defective standard language. Layout and adapter mechanics do not decide these semantic boundaries.

Collect the actual language's color, material, dimension, and unit expressions across the population and compare them with the established values. A broad color word, approximate material, or traditional unit must not make two artists invent incompatible values; define a needed reference or conversion at the value's owner. Treatments and final realization preserve that value through their wording, while an idiomatic perception or adopted period term may remain when it does not change it. This checks language fidelity rather than requiring new appearance facts everywhere.
