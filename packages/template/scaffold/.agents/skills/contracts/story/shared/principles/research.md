# Research principles

Each performance study in `docs/research/`, apart from its index, answers these items in `docs/evidence/construction/files/research/`, under an H1 naming the study's path. Its originals stay in `references/`, and the index names them. Settings, screenplay and behaviors cite the study they draw from.

## Does the study capture the depicted practice? {#depicted-practice}

Can settings, screenplay and code authors use the study to write and program the practice at the depicted level without inventing it? Compare its laws (each technique's shape prior in the performer's own body frame, phases and timing windows, reach, contact surfaces, support and weight transfer, and legal and illegal actions), its outcome table of which intent combinations can yield which outcome classes, how actions chain through sequences, setups and feints, spatial strategy, tempo across the duration, the conduct of everyone present, officials and crowds included, the markers that separate the depicted level from a beginner, how the genre is shot, and the measurable targets a composed scene must meet, each with its method and uncertainty. An invented action names its closest real analogue and states where it departs. A rank or adjective, a list of technique names without laws, an outcome no table covers or a value with no source leaves the next author to invent the practice. A deliberately stylized departure is recorded as a choice and is not a gap.

## Can each observation be traced to its source? {#traceable-sources}

Does every observation name its retained source with a locator such as a page, figure or timestamp, and say whether it is a reported measurement, a direct observation, an inference or the production's own choice? Uncertain values keep their range, and unavailable sources are recorded as unavailable. A search summary, a portal page or a remembered fact is a route to evidence, not a source.
