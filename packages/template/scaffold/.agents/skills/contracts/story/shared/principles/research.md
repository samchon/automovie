# Research principles

Each performance study in `docs/research/`, apart from its index, answers these items in `docs/evidence/construction/files/research/`, under an H1 naming the study's path. Its originals stay in `references/`, and the index names them. Settings, screenplay and [motion](../../../development/motion.md) cite the study they draw from.

## Does the study capture the depicted practice? {#depicted-practice}

Can settings, screenplay and motion authors use the study to write and program the practice at the depicted level without inventing it? Compare its repertoire (each action's phases, timing, distances, support and weight transfer), how actions chain (sequences, setups, feints and transitions with their intervals), spatial strategy, tempo across the duration, the responses of everyone present, the markers that separate the depicted level from a beginner, and the values motion must meet with their method and uncertainty. An invented action names its closest real analogue and states where it departs. A rank or adjective, a list of technique names without timing, or a value with no source leaves the next author to invent the practice. A deliberately stylized departure is recorded as a choice and is not a gap.

## Can each observation be traced to its source? {#traceable-sources}

Does every observation name its retained source with a locator such as a page, figure or timestamp, and say whether it is a reported measurement, a direct observation, an inference or the production's own choice? Uncertain values keep their range, and unavailable sources are recorded as unavailable. A search summary, a portal page or a remembered fact is a route to evidence, not a source.
