# Settings principles

Each settings H2 answers as one facet of its subject, owning the anchored H3 facts beneath it. It does not answer for every person or place in the work.

## Can a downstream author use this fact and its limits? {#usable-facts}

Does the record identify its subject and question, establish the value and scope, and give the conditions, limits, dependencies, exceptions, authority, resources, or costs that affect its use? Is each H3 fact singular, specific and checkable: one statement a reviewer or later author can hold the work to, such as what triggers a habit, how the body responds and how quickly and reliably it happens, rather than an adjective such as a guard that gets sloppy, which no scene can be held to? Give only applicable attributes, and answer an inapplicable facet `N/A` with its reason. For a person, place, object, or phenomenon, establish recognizable appearance, spatial and material properties, and audible qualities needed for its actual use and consistent realization. Unknown principles may remain unknown, but their established effects, impossibilities, and knowledge boundaries must constrain later solutions. Consequential uncertainty needs its permitted range, investigation route, and decision condition; a missing decision cannot masquerade as mystery.

A practiced activity's performer, official or partner needs its studied vocabulary: repertoire, sequences, spatial and tempo habits, responses and the markers of the depicted level, supported by its research; a rank, adjective or a few technique names leaves the next author to invent the practice.

Distinguish externally supported fact, adopted invention, inference, disputed account, and unresolved matter. Support checkable historical, scientific, cultural, or other factual claims with direct primary or authoritative evidence at the relevant edition, passage, date, or page, including meaningful disagreements and limits. A portal or catalog is a route to evidence, not support for an unstated claim. Reduce precision or retain uncertainty when evidence does not support the value; invented choices need no false outside authority.

## Is the record findable and owned at the right layer? {#record-and-boundary}

Can the next author find the definition, settled content, necessary conditions and limits, references, and consequential unresolved matters in a consistent order for this kind of record? Each independently used subject has one file, with its inseparable attributes together; a group may share a file while it acts as one. Use one term per concept and one owner per fact, linking shared facts to their canonical owner. Keep related explanation together, parallel values in lists or tables, and dependent conditions in connected prose. Empty fields, repeated disclaimers, decorative description, and scattered dependent attributes must not obscure a usable decision. The record states facts in prose for readers and never describes code; rendering and generation controls belong to the production's source.

Does the record establish world facts, baseline states, and change conditions while leaving the chosen events, causal progression, actual choices, results, setups, payoffs, and audience reveal order to treatments on the long route, or to screenplay on the short route? A fixed historical result or time-specific status may constrain treatments without prewriting how the film reaches or reveals it. Baselines may have several times, branches, or worlds. Background belongs here only where it explains the reference state; reference an event's later result without duplicating its progression. The cinematography domain owns the film's look; editing and audience access belong to production instructions or their work contracts, while a character's actual voice remains a world property.
