# Settings principles

Each settings H2 answers as the smallest owner of a fact, constraint, or inseparable group of attributes. It does not answer for every person or place in the work.

## Can a downstream author use this fact and its limits? {#usable-facts}

Does the record identify its subject and question, establish the value and scope, and give the conditions, limits, dependencies, exceptions, authority, resources, or costs that affect its use? Give only applicable attributes. For a person, place, object, or phenomenon, establish the appearance needed for consistent realization. Unknown principles may remain unknown, but their established effects, impossibilities, and knowledge boundaries must constrain later solutions. Consequential uncertainty needs its permitted range, investigation route, and decision condition; a missing decision cannot masquerade as mystery.

Distinguish externally supported fact, adopted invention, inference, disputed account, and unresolved matter. Support checkable historical, scientific, cultural, or other factual claims with direct primary or authoritative evidence at the relevant edition, passage, date, or page, including meaningful disagreements and limits. A portal or catalog is a route to evidence, not support for an unstated claim. Reduce precision or retain uncertainty when evidence does not support the value; invented choices need no false outside authority.

## Is the record findable and owned at the right layer? {#record-and-boundary}

Can the next author find the definition, settled content, necessary conditions and limits, references, and consequential unresolved matters in a consistent order for this kind of record? Use one term per concept and one owner per fact. Keep related explanation together, parallel values in lists or tables, and dependent conditions in connected prose. Empty fields, repeated disclaimers, decorative description, and scattered dependent attributes must not obscure a usable decision.

Does the record establish world facts, baseline states, and change conditions while leaving the chosen events, causal progression, actual choices, results, setups, payoffs, and audience reveal order to treatments on the long route, or to screenplay on the compact short route? A fixed historical result or time-specific status may constrain treatments without prewriting how the film reaches or reveals it. Baselines may have several times, branches, or worlds. Background belongs here only where it explains the reference state; reference an event's later result without duplicating its progression. Camera style, editing, and audience access belong to production instructions or their work contracts, while a character's actual voice remains a world property.
