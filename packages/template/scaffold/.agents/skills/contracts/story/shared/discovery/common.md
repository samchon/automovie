# Common requirement discovery

The discovery result document answers for the inspected layer population. Authors create `work/discovery/results.md` under this story contract tree when doing discovery, recording actual findings and inspected negatives; the scaffold supplies no completed negative or claim that a work has no extra requirements.

## Were requirements found and assigned without inventing duplicate authority? {#shared-work-boundary}

Inspect instructions, promises, subjects, sources, assets, genre, form, and the entire current body for conditions not already owned by active shared, language, work, or settings requirements. A real finding changes what may honestly be created or omitted, has an observable application and failure, and names the smallest appropriate respondent population. Aspirations, genre labels, duplicates, and already answered conditions are not new requirements. Language baselines belong to their language owner, adopted creative form to the work, and world facts to their canonical fact owner: settings in the full pipeline, or the actual brief and screenplay in an authorized short path. A possibly universal candidate remains research until adopted into shared contracts.

For an adopted form, state observable form, scope, audience effect, representative failure, semantic owner, and allowed repair boundary. If no new requirement is needed, identify the concrete risks and positive form actually inspected and the existing owners that cover them. A finding in one part does not establish absence in the rest: account for the remaining inspected scope and its owners, including mixed findings and negatives. Unread or unresolved scope remains open rather than becoming an exclusion.

Inspect the actual language independently of the general discovery result: regional, period, social, medium, translation, register, address and honorific boundaries; names and titles; and number, date, age, color, material, dimension, and unit wording. Identify the speakers and passages affected, audience cues, change conditions, and canonical owners. Check historical language claims against their sources or identify an adopted departure. Choose notation for the actual audience and medium rather than artificial period weight or consistency alone. If existing owners suffice, record the concrete language risks inspected and those owners instead of treating a general discovery negative as language coverage. Unadopted signals and counts remain investigation clues, not universal form bans.

## Does each adopted requirement live at its canonical owner? {#canonical-realization}

Inspect the whole population for a requirement that exists only in guide prose, selector configuration, status notes, or repeated explanations instead of its semantic owner. Identify the destination and every affected population, consolidate authority without changing meaning, migrate evidence and consumers together, and renew review from the literal current body. Neither a selector nor a discovery answer fulfills a missing creative decision. Create work-specific subtrees only for real adopted requirements; do not prefill empty promises or fake applicability.
