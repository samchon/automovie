# Common requirement discovery

The discovery result answers for the entire inspected layer population. Authors create `work/discovery/results.md` under this story contract tree from actual findings and inspected negatives; the scaffold supplies no completed negative.

## Were requirements found and assigned without inventing duplicate authority? {#shared-work-boundary}

Inspect instructions, promises, subjects, sources, assets, genre, form and the current body for conditions not owned by active shared, language, work or settings requirements. A finding changes what may honestly be created or omitted, has observable application and failure, and names the smallest appropriate respondent population. Aspirations, genre labels and existing conditions need no duplicate authority. Language baselines belong to their language owner, creative form to the work, and world facts to settings. A possibly universal candidate remains research until adopted into shared contracts.

For an adopted form, state its observable choices, scope, audience effect, representative failure, semantic owner and allowed repair boundary. Account for findings, already covered choices and the remaining inspected scope together. A negative names the concrete risks and positive form inspected and their adequate existing owners; finding one requirement cannot establish absence elsewhere. Unread or unresolved scope stays open.

Inspect language independently of that general result: regional, period, social, medium, translation, register, address and honorific boundaries; names and titles; number, date, age, color, material, dimension and unit wording. Identify affected speakers and passages, audience cues, change conditions and canonical owners. Check historical claims against sources or identify an adopted departure. Notation serves the actual audience and medium, rather than artificial period weight or consistency alone. When existing owners suffice, record the concrete language risks they cover. Unadopted signals and counts remain investigation clues, not universal bans.

## Does each adopted requirement live at its canonical owner? {#canonical-realization}

Inspect the whole population for adopted requirements left only in guide prose, selectors, status notes or repeated explanations. Name each semantic destination and affected population, consolidate authority without changing meaning, and update affected consumers. Follow the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../story/correction/repair.md#closed-passes) for evidence and reviews. A selector or discovery answer cannot supply a missing creative decision; create work-specific subtrees only for actual adopted requirements.
