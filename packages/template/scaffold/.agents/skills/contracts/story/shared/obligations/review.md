# Review population obligations

One current verdict document answers for the complete declared review surface, identifying its creative reading and evidence audit. Unit notes do not substitute for this population record.

## Did this round inspect the entire current surface and every finding class? {#complete-current-round}

Identify the exact revision, declared layer and pass, files, H2s, upstream relationships, descendants and observations reviewed, reconciling the inventory and defined measurements with what was read. Every layer-closing creative round and whole-work review rereads every selected body afresh without carried unit verdicts. A scoped repair round rereads the complete affected dependency closure and may carry only unaffected judgments with unchanged bodies, inputs and requirements, identifying their source, reader and grounds. Stable sentence classifications follow [inspection](../../../../screenwriting/inspection.md#verify-changed-work); they do not waive whole reading or carry unit approval. Closed layers remain closed under the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../screenwriting/repair.md#closed-passes). State partial or unavailable inspection honestly.

Does the evidence audit follow completed creative correction and compare every required target with its selected hosts and every host with its required targets, including negative scopes? Record a pending audit during creative correction. The current review tree contains the complete inventory and verdict, including explicit carries, without relying on missing units in earlier folders.

Collect each supported finding class with all discovered members, quotations and locators, violated obligation, earliest owner and affected edges. Record reproducible inspection methods and revision identity. Check body completeness and settings navigation where relevant; the evidence audit checks answer completeness and literal relationship support. Compare population classifications and the actual function behind whole-scope negatives such as silence or refrain. Similar length, repeated syntax or annotation ratios prompt rereading rather than establish defects. A class table cannot introduce a finding unsupported by an inspected unit.

## Is the verdict a truthful basis for the next round or delivery? {#round-accountability}

Record the source revision, review kind, preserved achievements, supported choices left intact, all findings grouped by root cause and contributing factors, and the author's actual repairs or factual objections with their resolution. Mark responses not yet received. Preserve the function of achievements actually read and judged; an earlier protection cannot excuse a current failure. Distinguish expressive surplus removed below a duration target from insufficient event material.

Finish the whole inspection before repair, then repair all findings by cause and their affected descendants together, following the [closing-round rule](../../../../review/rounds.md). State the actual open or clean result and any unverified host or output claim. Local passes, earlier clean snapshots, or an exhausted review budget cannot establish a clean current delivery. Do not turn a partial pilot into evidence for unmade episodes.
