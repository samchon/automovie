# Review population obligations

One verdict document answers for the complete declared review surface in the current round. Unit review notes do not substitute for this population record.

## Did this round inspect the entire current surface and every finding class? {#complete-current-round}

Identify the exact snapshot, declared layer and pass, files, H2s, upstream relationships, descendants, and observations reviewed, reconciling the actual inventory and any defined measurements with what was read. Every layer-closing round and whole-work review reread every selected body afresh without carried judgments; old notes or changed files alone cannot establish a fresh full review. A scoped round verifies previous finding repairs within an open pass, identifies every affected dependency, and rereads that complete closure. Only that repair round may carry an unaffected judgment with unchanged reviewed body, inputs, and requirements, its original round and grounds recorded explicitly. Closed layers stay closed after downstream body repairs or upstream-change diagnostics under the [closed-pass boundary](../../../../../../docs/guides/narrative-lifecycle.md#upstream-repair-and-scope). Reconcile the whole current inventory and population obligations in the new round, and distinguish carried judgments from new reading. Audit all required targets against all selected hosts and all hosts against their required targets, including the negative scopes. State partial or unavailable inspection honestly.

Collect each already evidenced finding class with all discovered members, quotations and locators, violated obligation, earliest owner, and affected edges. Record reproducible class membership and inspection methods, including snapshot identity and literal support for relationship defects. Check body completeness, evidence completeness, and settings index coverage where relevant. Independently compare population classifications, their dominant and closest alternative groups, and the actual function behind whole-scope negatives such as silence or refrain. Similar length, repeated syntax, or annotation ratios are rereading clues, not findings by themselves. A class table cannot introduce a finding that no reviewed unit supports.

## Is the verdict a truthful basis for the next round or delivery? {#round-accountability}

Record the round number, snapshot, preserved achievements, considered choices left intact and their reasons, all findings grouped by root cause and contributing factors, and the author's actual acceptance, partial acceptance, or rejection with its basis. Mark responses not yet received instead of inventing them. Carry only achievements actually read and judged, preserving their function through repair. Distinguish expressive surplus removed below a duration target from insufficient event material.

Finish the whole inspection before repair, then repair all findings by cause and their affected descendants together before a fresh complete round. State the actual open or clean result and any unverified host or output claim. Local passes, earlier clean snapshots, or an exhausted review budget cannot establish a clean current delivery. Do not turn a partial pilot into evidence for unmade episodes.
