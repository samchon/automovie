# Delivery

## Complete presentation

Does the actual delivered artifact meet the request's scope, format, duration, and intended audience experience? Compare promised content with the assembled edit, titles, captions, soundtrack, and ending. For an asset delivery, compare the model, controls, and required views instead. Distinguish measured facts, observed playback, and unverified claims.

## Reproducible handoff

Can the recipient locate the fixed inputs, source entry, dependencies, commands, and final outputs needed to reproduce or use the result? Identify the reviewed revision and media identities, external dependencies, rights or attribution requirements, and remaining limitations. A successful intermediate render or encoder exit does not prove the final handoff.
