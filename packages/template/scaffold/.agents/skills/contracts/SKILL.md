# Contracts

This tree holds the questions Evidence asks. Read only the family and contracts the calling task selects. This file is not an evidence target.

| Family | Respondents | Where they answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Development](development/SKILL.md) | Source declarations and tests | Their doc comments |
| [Story](story/SKILL.md) | Research studies, settings, treatments, scripts and screenplay units and populations, discovery results and review records | `docs/evidence/construction/` |

Each H2 is one item, and a separate answer establishes it. An item states what to achieve, why its recipient needs it and where too little and too much begin; an enumeration defines what to compare, not ingredients to add to the respondent. Items carry no source links, so their judgment, reasons and protections stay in the item text.

An item asks only what its authored respondent can establish from its own content and the references it cites. Watching, listening and the judgment of the delivered experience belong to [review](../review/SKILL.md); an answer that records them in place of the work it describes adds paperwork, not evidence.
