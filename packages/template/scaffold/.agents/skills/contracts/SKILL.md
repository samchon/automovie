# Contracts

The default `evidence.config.ts` selects engineering populations. Narrative work adds the production-owned [workflow profile](../../../workflow/README.md#configure-the-production) while retaining that baseline. Exported source declarations answer [common](common.md), and exported test functions answer [testing](testing.md).

The narrative system under [story](story/index.md) preserves unit principles, population obligations, discovery, upstream repair, rewriting, and independent review as distinct families. Long mode applies all four layers; short mode applies final screenplay and its rewriting/review path. Standalone assets need no narrative profile or obligations. Disabled passes draft their bodies before enabling evidence; they are not a way to drop required responsibilities.

Use [evidence writing](../evidence-writing/SKILL.md) for creative answers and [rewriting](../rewriting/SKILL.md) for expression passes. The [contracts guide](../../../docs/guides/contracts.md) owns adoption and technical ownership. Assets and delivery retain their own [questions](assets.md) and [handoff criteria](delivery.md); choose formal respondents where they can establish the facts.

Each selected question needs a declaration-specific answer. Fix the work before claiming compliance and report unresolved limits. Mechanical coverage establishes connections; [review](../review/SKILL.md) establishes their truth. Keep source-stage ownership, selectors, contracts, answers, and affected reviews consistent when requirements change.
