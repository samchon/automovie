## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Optional terminal observation adds source-connectivity and critical-classification work documented by its owner plus the observed event/bracket planes; omitted terminalSeeds performs none of that work.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources
