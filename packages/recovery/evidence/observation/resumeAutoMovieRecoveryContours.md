## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, T triangles and K saved planes, preparation costs O(V+T log T) time and O(V+T) storage. Each indexed cut sorts A intersecting faces in O(A log A), worst-case O(T+A log A), and binding visits samples and incident faces. Retained cuts cost O(sum P) space. For each target with N observations and S at most E spans, the existing adaptive fitter costs O(N log S) for balanced splits and O(N squared) worst case with O(N+S) live storage. Conversion costs O(S); orientations are prepared at most once per sign.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One incidence registry, scalar index and exact cutter serve all planes. Bound cuts supply targets without recutting; only failed targets are fitted and only new fits converted. Original coefficient artifacts incur zero loading, fitting or conversion work.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Source references, raw cuts, orientation closures and maps expire with the synchronous call. Returned coefficients and copied address/publication/policy metadata belong to the caller; no source mesh, original coefficients, listeners, tasks or persistent cache transfer.
