## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, T triangles, K cuts and P observations, preparation costs O(V+T log T), PL links cost O(T) plus local traversals, interval queries prune disjoint face bounds and sort matching face IDs, and cutting/slab graphs traverse only their candidates; fitting owns its adaptive cost. Worst-case all faces remain candidates; memory is O(V+T+P).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Immutable source admission, signed scalar samples and triangle height bounds are prepared once. Each exact plane is cut once; its source supports supply event incidence and optional lineage without recutting. Enabled lineage copies each contour's incident face IDs once; omitted lineage allocates no record or copy.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

maxSections and maxSpans bound generated populations; transient adjacency, raw cut points and source supports expire on return or failure. The observer owns any retained copied IDs and its unbounded callback work; no geometry arrays transfer.
