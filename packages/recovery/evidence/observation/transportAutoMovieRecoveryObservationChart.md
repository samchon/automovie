## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

At least three unique persistent identities determine an ordered cyclic relation. Both traversal directions are checked against incoming anchor order; exact anchor phases and observed-edge arc fractions define the intervening map. No correspondence is invented with insufficient or conflicting anchors.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One pure operation returns the transported row, original-point or PL-interior provenance, direction and virtual seam. Family fitting, boundary ownership and event attachment remain separate owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No nearest-coordinate match, phase epsilon, source topology in a consumer, arbitrary zero-anchor seam or material-correspondence claim supplies this chart. Nonrepresentable intervals retain an explicit refusal.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States physical units, incoming anchor authority, inferred PL interpolation, immutable inputs, tangent directions and independent guide/fidelity qualification.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Identity indexing, cyclic traversal, physical prefix lengths and output construction cost O(N+M). Sorting A persistent anchors and N output requests adds O(A log A+N log N); anchor-to-traversal slots are indexed once rather than searched once per anchor.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The incoming phase is retained when supplied. One physical prefix table supports all anchor intervals, and the same transported entries supply points, tangents, feature indices, schedule and provenance.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Call-owned maps and arrays scale with the two observed rows and expire on return or throw. The caller owns returned arrays and the retained original course on refusal; no handle or cross-call state is retained.
