## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, T faces and K endpoint planes, source incidence and indexing use O(V+T log T) time and O(V+T) storage. Each indexed raw cut visits its A intersecting faces and sorts candidates in O(A log A), with worst-case O(T+A log A); contour binding visits samples and incident faces. The existing slab owner documents source-cell/critical costs. Optional callback cloning is linear in each delivered metadata record; no fitting cost exists.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One immutable incidence registry, normalized scalar vector, face interval index and cutting closure serve all endpoints and slab pairs. Existing coefficients are not loaded or processed; raw exact supports are reused for port publication without a second cut.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Prepared source references, raw cut points and binding maps expire with the synchronous call. Returned records copy source addresses and measured critical positions only. Callback copies are caller-owned; no source arrays, coefficients, listeners, caches or background tasks transfer.
