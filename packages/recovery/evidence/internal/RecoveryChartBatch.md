## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

One physical-key and base-edge insertion network registers all selected chart boundaries before harmonic mapping and constrained physical-area fitting.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

This operation owns the two-pass boundary-to-fit sequence; topology preparation and source-face accounting remain with the caller, and generation consumes only the resulting coefficients.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No coordinate welding, missing-bank invention, source normal publication or source mesh fallback repairs a failed shared network. Independent retained charts remain explicitly unqualified.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States batch scope, private name-based ownership, shared authority, local failures and independent finite retention.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

One network pass processes the bounded selected chart population before sequential fits. A failed network performs at most one independent pass per chart; the network and fit owners state their geometry-dependent costs.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Common arc coefficients and accepted registrations are reused by every incident chart; each chart's private points serve one map and its residual-driven fits.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The caller owns the prepared batch. The generator retains its network clone and at most one active fit until completion or cancellation; no public source topology or process handle escapes.
