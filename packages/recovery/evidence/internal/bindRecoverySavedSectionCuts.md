## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Original contour ordinals, full incidence-set equality, optional exact-set equality and closed/open/degenerate classification bind unchanged symbolic publications to deterministic raw cuts. No geometric proximity or phase matching is used.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One helper owns saved-versus-raw admission and copied lineage; the public observer owns source/frame identity and the existing slab owner owns topology.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Endpoint-star support never substitutes for exact emitted faces; omitted, duplicated, stale and closure-incompatible records refuse instead of inventing a publication or fitting a curve.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States complete contour coverage, comparison semantics, caller-attested curve identity, unsupported-port preservation and original-address ownership.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For C contours, P cut samples and I incident face visits, indexing and address comparisons cost expected O(C+P+I) time and O(C+F) storage for retained face IDs. Prepared edge/vertex incidence avoids a source triangle scan per contour.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The same admitted raw cut and incidence maps supply identity checks and exact port lineage without cutting or fitting a second time.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Input source and saved records remain borrowed. Returned lineage copies address arrays and publication metadata; no points, source geometry, saved coefficients or persistent cache transfer.
