## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, T faces, K saved planes, C contours per target plane, N targets, P target samples and S fitted spans, each call prepares O(V+T) incidence and at most two O(T) orientation closures. Repeated find operations cost O(N(K+C)); selected fits retain the existing balanced O(P log S), worst O(P squared) fitter cost. Per-target point copying, conversion, JSON comparison, sample mapping and endpoint copying add O(P+S); no cut or unselected curve fit runs.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Immutable ordered cuts are reused for every target, incidence is prepared once, and material orientations are reused per actual sign. Duplicate raw addresses are rejected, while differing targets keep their own policies and saved coefficients; no effectful replay is shared across calls or accepted from a matching string alone.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Preparation/orientation maps live for this synchronous call; each target owns temporary copied points, accepted trace and replay/converted spans, which expire after its outcome. Returned maps and saved endpoint jets grow with target samples/spans and transfer to the caller; no aggregate byte ceiling, host handle, task, listener or global cache is added. Per-target errors retain diagnostics, completed milestones and obtained scalar replay-quality metadata but no maps or replacement coefficients; an admission throw releases call-local references.
