## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Boundary lengths add O(BW) integer bit work/storage with W at most 2098 plus ceil(log2 B) bits from binary64 dyadic sums; at most three inserted vertices and three extra triangles are produced.
