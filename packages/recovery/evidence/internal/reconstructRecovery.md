# src/index.ts#reconstructRecovery

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Complete cubics, observed incidence and saved tensor recipes generate independent geometry through their engine owners. A unique group-to-family partition prevents duplicate surface ownership; tensor endpoint coefficients share the admitted curve authority.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Tensor-family admission and realization have one owner; the contour invocation owns the remaining groups. This adapter combines parts and actual construction while preserving separate completion and quality reports.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No source geometry, source normals or source addresses are admitted. Numerical target misses and strip topology/link/orientation warnings never remove returned finite geometry. Actual unresolved incidence remains explicit without fallback or forced closure.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Distinguishes displayability from qualification, work observations, shared curve authority, actual local gaps and source-independent engine generation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Saved section and report gathering are O(K+P), and delegated-group lookup is O(G). Engine owners report tensor and contour realization costs; all retained parts and construction remain in the result.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Each family and retained contour invocation supplies its model, construction and report once; camera changes need no regeneration.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Generated model buffers, actual construction and diagnostics transfer to the caller; the adapter retains no source mesh, cache or handle.
