## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, T triangles, B boundary uses and U requested cuts, initial indexing costs O(V+T+B+U). Current side-chain searches cost O(U*B) in the worst case; triangle incidence updates are constant work per split. Arc traversal is O(B), sorting adds O((B+U) log(B+U)), and owner fitting has its separately declared cost.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One canonical point serves each declared base fraction, edge incidence reuses triangle ownership across splits, and one accepted arc fit supplies every forward or reversed side occurrence.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

All cloned private observations and graph registries are call-local and scale with actual vertices, triangles, cuts and boundary uses. No hidden cap is imposed; caller chart budgets must bound them. Failure releases local state but owner may retain earlier completed fits; returned private arrays belong to the caller.
