## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Lexicographic orientation of the complete incidence word fixes one canonical observation order. One Schneider fit and its accepted Newton parameters define the common coefficient and pointwise phase authority for both chart neighbors.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

A per-observation registry owns boundary fitting; chart mapping and interior fitting consume its oriented result without independently estimating another boundary.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Private incidence, not coordinate proximity or anatomy, establishes sharing. Private source words stay inside the map and only new boundary identities, coefficients and normalized registrations reach chart assembly.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States orientation, normalized pointwise registration, physical units, exact-path sharing and the unsupported partial-overlap case.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Each path of N observations uses O(N) key construction and orientation work; the numerical fitter runs once per unique complete path and retains O(N+S) registration and coefficients for S fitted spans.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Identical forward or reversed private words reuse one fitted curve, accepted parameters and corners under one fixed policy. Changed observations with a reused incidence word fail rather than reuse stale geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The returned owner retains only visited boundary points, words, registrations and coefficient arrays until its observation caller releases it. The caller's chart budget bounds visited boundaries; no source faces, normals, global cache or handle is acquired.
