## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, T selected faces, K planes and per-plane active faces A, points P, incident-face visits I and original-edge witness visits W, admission/indexing costs O(V+T log T); indexed cuts and copied outputs add active-face sorting, P/I traversal, segment face sorting and the cutter's per-point linear witness deduplication, which can reach O(W squared) for many aliases. Complete saved populations are revisited to verify their binding; no whole named curve collection is fitted.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

A local-to-original injection, admitted incidence registry, vertex heights, interval index and cutter closure are reused only for this immutable source/frame/selection. Each distinct plane is cut once after duplicate rejection; persistent reuse is the processor's byte-verified named-stage responsibility, not identity-string caching here.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The call borrows source arrays and owns O(V+T) preparation plus active cut/binding data; its output grows with all requested contour points, witnesses and face addresses. Temporary references expire on return or throw and copied results transfer to the caller. There is no independent aggregate output-byte limit, handle, background task or cancellation listener.
