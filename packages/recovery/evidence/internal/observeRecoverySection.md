# src/internal/observeRecoverySection.ts#observeRecoverySection

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Original source support, material winding, corner policy and Schneider fitting preserve their order. The opt-in callback copies that solve's accepted parameters and explicit raw-order reversal; it does not certify historical trace identity, material phase, source tolerance or transverse surface jets.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

The section owner retains fitting, publication and diagnostics; separate callbacks expose copied lineage and accepted parameters. Delivery in finally lies outside the fit failure catch so observer exceptions escape rather than becoming numerical failure diagnostics.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Default fitting inputs and arithmetic stay unchanged. No source-person branch, correction, nearest-point phase guess, averaged derivative, replacement curve or source-normal fallback is added; a current trace is not presented as proven historical parameters.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Callback members define this-call contour ordinal, signed-area reversal, accepted fit-local span ordinal, inclusive reordered point indices including closed index N, and dimensionless chord/Newton t distinct from raw mm h and saved physical phase. Copied ownership and escaping exceptions are explicit; original reports retain tolerance and open/contact limits.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For P observations, I incident-face visits and S spans, support/preparation retains P/I traversal and the existing balanced O(P log S), worst O(P squared) fit. Opt-in traces copy O(P+S) accepted parameters; default calls allocate no trace array, and observer work remains caller-owned.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One existing fit supplies publication, diagnostics and optional accepted parameters with no second fit, cut or conversion. No persistent cache is added; saved-cut admission and stage receipt owners decide historical equivalence.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Inputs remain borrowed synchronously. Local support, fit and trace data grow with contour points/incidence/spans; returned curves and callback copies transfer to callers, including copies retained by observers. Temporary references expire on completion or throw; no independent metadata byte ceiling, listener, task or cancellation hook is added.
