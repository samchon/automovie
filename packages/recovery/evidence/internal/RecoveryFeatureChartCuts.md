## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Coherent edge incidence and circle/path links admit the source. Original face-pair provenance rejects triangulation diagonals; declared cuts remove incident-face adjacencies, and connected fans receive separate ordinals. Unmaterialized bank pairs receive conforming, correctly rounded dyadic midpoints before fans are rebuilt. Actual components, links and every feature's two boundary banks are revalidated without assuming disk topology.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One private operation owns cut materialization, face ownership, component boundaries and maximal feature-chain banks. Source feature classification, topology-to-UV conversion and fitted publication remain separate owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No threshold reclassification, coordinate welding, guessed closure, anatomical selection, copied reconstruction normal or consumer mesh occurs. Arithmetic collapse, inconsistent provenance and ambiguous bank continuation fail explicitly; midpoint rounding remains measured rather than relabelled exact preservation.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States millimetres, physical versus occurrence identity, source provenance, midpoint rounding, opposite bank directions, non-disk outputs, private lifetime and independent UV, curve and normal qualifications.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V vertices, F faces and K feature edges, bounded incidence and fan passes use O(V+F+K) storage; deterministic key ordering costs O((V+F+K)log(V+F+K)). Each colliding edge adds one point and two faces. Boundary and bank traversal visits each face occurrence and feature segment a bounded number of times; dyadic integer widths are bounded by binary64 encoding.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One admitted incidence registry serves initial bank-collision detection. It is rebuilt only after required conforming subdivisions, then reused for fan labels, actual component boundaries and feature-bank provenance. Each maximal chain has one canonical physical order for both banks.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

All registries and arithmetic buffers expire on return or failure. At most three vertex occurrences per resulting triangle transfer to private chart owners; no global cache, task, handle or source geometry enters a reconstruction consumer.
