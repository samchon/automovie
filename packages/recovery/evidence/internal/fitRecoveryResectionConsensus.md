## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

T proposals over N landmarks use bounded twelve-column DLT plus O(N) scoring. At most L proposals and three final refits receive bounded six-coordinate refinement. Opt-in observation copies at most O(N) already computed evidence per event; observer work and retained history belong to its consumer. Disabled tracing allocates no event or design trace.
