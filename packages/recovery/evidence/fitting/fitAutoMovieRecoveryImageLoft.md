Unweighted two-column linear section equations establish centre and squared-radius initial observations under the explicit aligned-ellipse hypothesis. ## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

This owner coordinates qualified camera support, physical row interpolation and bounded profile candidates; the profile helper owns spline algebra and the engine owns recipe-to-atlas geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No contour-point triangulation, copied mesh, manually placed curve, fixture coefficient or hidden completion supplies a result. Contradictory support, nonpositive radii, inadequate directions and exhausted fit budgets refuse rather than increasing a budget or silently dropping observations.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For V views and R total rows, admission and union sorting cost O(R log R). N common stations cost O(N V), and trying S=1 through maxSpans costs the sum of O(N (S+3) squared) QR work. All loops are bounded by caller-supplied maxViews, maxRows and maxSpans before candidate allocation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Camera trigonometry, two direction QR factorizations and interpolated support are reused across all spline candidates; each candidate factors one basis for all four fields. One measured row disproving the ellipse hypothesis terminates that rejected fit; successful hypotheses still check every row before spline fitting.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Local O(N V) support and O(N (maxSpans+3)) fit scratch expire synchronously. Only compact coefficients, source identities and scalar diagnostics transfer to the caller; no image arrays, file handles or global cache remain.
