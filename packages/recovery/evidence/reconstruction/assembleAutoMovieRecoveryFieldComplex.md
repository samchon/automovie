## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

A conflict excludes its incident charts and records their actual coefficient occurrences while unrelated finite charts remain. No source mesh, coordinate-nearest relation, weld, coefficient averaging, silent first-owner choice or transitive partition supplies geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Native and endpoint occurrences are indexed in one traversal and each chart compiles once. Fixed-degree coefficient comparison and near-linear disjoint-set remapping add O(N+P) work beyond the compilers for N incidences and P controls; connected owner links do not require a quadratic incident clique.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Each named native owner contributes one final boundary authority. Incident patches and endpoints share coefficient indices; the same compiled local controls supply final recipes and source-piece provenance.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Finite chart, native-owner and total-patch budgets bound admitted output. Input occurrences, conflict records, compiler candidates and disjoint-set arrays remain O(N+P) and expire on return or throw. No files, processes or cross-call cache are owned.
