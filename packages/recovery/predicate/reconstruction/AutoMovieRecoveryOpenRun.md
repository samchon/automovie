## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Optional reversal reverses span and within-span order together. Dividing each original span width by the physical total maps its extent onto unit phase. The final interval closes the rounded prefix exactly, and the Engine phase compiler verifies that all bounds remain representable.
