# Head pieces

The head's original view curves own its form before lofting. HeadComposer converts each authored view once into an authoritative metre declaration, evaluates its millimetre source through the engine and constructs its raw loft. HeadTree consumes that same declaration. Declaration replay proves representation consistency; it does not establish anatomical placement, final skin quality or reference agreement.

Table 1, scaled by the person's morphological face height over the 118.6 mm FaceBase women's adult mean. The optional neck display takes the vertical span from side neck point to menton.

The generic globe starts from the axial radius in Bekerman et al. and the 7.8 mm corneal-plane to nasion projection in Wilson et al. ## Frame and measurement sites

The head frame is x forward, y up and z left, with the Frankfort plane horizontal and the median plane at z = 0. Original authoring uses calibration millimetres. Construction declarations use metres. Each side reads its own public dimensions through the same algorithm; independently supplied eye and ear asymmetry is retained.

BodyLaw.headInputs supplies the resolved head identity, stature, neck-course control, infrathyroid tape, sex/fat coordinates, and the neck-base tape with cervicale, side-neck and suprasternale heights. Those three heights do not define a horizontal base ring. The current lower-head source declares a separate axis-normal cut and nonplanar named-height course; retained site/pose assumptions do not identify the palpated measurement landmarks.

## Curve ownership

Each view part supplies four signed original curves, an axis, section-evaluation stations and quarter fullness. A knot list alone does not prove its between-knot spans are correct.

| Owner | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Front positive and negative | Signed supports along the section's u direction. The part's own chart determines whether these are a face profile, lip border, ear chord or another anatomical guide. |
| Side positive and negative | Signed supports along v. They do not repair a wrong front support or an outside axis. |
| Axis | World origin, section plane and source parameter. The parallel-axis declaration preserves its original coordinate curves and fixed basis; it does not rotate a lip plane into a minimal-frame course. |
| Fullness | Interior shape of each quadrant between the four supports. Its conic shoulder and departures must be admitted by the engine without changing independent support dimensions. |
| Evaluation stations | Where the engine evaluates sections. Extra stations can realize a sparse valid three-knot ridge without adding shape knots. |
| Tree joins and trims | Intersections, blending and openings between unchanged original parts. A join cannot translate a part to conceal incompatible anatomical inputs. |

The viewer's front.pos, front.neg, side.pos and side.neg are original-source producer curves. Axis shows the actual source frame course. A displayed top.k is an admitted raw loft section at an original front-positive knot index, not an independent original guide or the final joined skin. Rejected nonpole sections and finite poles must be distinguished from visible rings.

## Neck replacement boundary

HeadNeckCurves is connected before final mass/caliper construction. Its physical-station origin is affine through a finite constant-template band, while its root-parameter lateral scale is constant there. Horizontal supports `[A,L,B,L]`, ELLIPSE quarters and zero departures define the engine's actual native conic/G2 template. `L=G/(2*pi)` is a circle-equivalent lateral scale, not an assertion that this section is circular. The posterior support closes retained C7 X at its public height; a separate finite anterior-support solve closes the actual conic's normal-plane metric. Its selected branch is not claimed globally monotone or unique. The retained mean is an axis-plane centre proxy and the posterior-skin secant is an adopted pose slope. Neither identifies the anatomical infrathyroid point or measures the neck axis. The upper handover retains clinical C and existing face sites.

A separate four-quarter C2 height source passes suprasternale, both declared side heights and C7. Its phase and homothetic conic scale place the course on the same lower mass. Side phase and plateau jets remain generating conventions, without proving the anatomical shoulder junction. The public engine measures the full world derivative, including origin drift, native polar derivative and changing scale. A bounded convex-family minimum observation selects a finite sublevel-to-upper bracket before solving its positive lower scale; computed residual and K/K2 difference have separate meanings and neither certifies quadrature accuracy or taut contact. Equal native/height quarter identities share one canonical parameter operation rather than forming nearly duplicate quadrature boundaries.

NeckSource estimates a circular infrathyroid section from the supplied tape and registers its posterior wall at cervicale. The front, side and back base heights determine the estimated tilt and vertical placement of that section. Its four analytic descriptor rows interpolate the base and normal-section radii with a quintic step. HeadLayout uses the common frame and posterior datum. The mass uses its own shared quintic scalar curves to close the nonplanar base course. [ISO 8559-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/61686.html)

NeckConstruction evaluates the composed mass's lower interval through engine ViewLoft. Source records preserve the original parameter, value and first and second derivatives over the retained interval. An open interval has no pole. The trunk receiver consumes the same original frame and regular boundary data and preserves diagnostics.

The replacement must distinguish the infrathyroid plane normal to the neck axis from the base course through back, side and front neck points. It must state axis, site, AP/aspect and forward-placement assumptions. A common scale can close a declared 3D base course against its tape, but that numerical closure does not locate the infrathyroid landmark or prove taut skin contact.

The trunk receives an internal engine-evaluated cut of the mass, in its original frame and with its regular boundary derivatives. The landmark base course remains the measurement guide.
