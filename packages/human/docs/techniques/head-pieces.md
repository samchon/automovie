# Head pieces

The head's original view curves own its form before lofting. HeadComposer converts each authored view once into an authoritative metre declaration, evaluates its millimetre source through the engine and constructs its raw loft. HeadTree consumes that same declaration. Declaration replay proves representation consistency; it does not establish anatomical placement, final skin quality or reference agreement.

## Frame and measurement sites

The head frame is x forward, y up and z left, with the Frankfort plane horizontal and the median plane at z = 0. Original authoring uses calibration millimetres. Construction declarations use metres. Each side reads its own public dimensions through the same algorithm; independently supplied eye and ear asymmetry is retained.

BodyLaw.headInputs supplies the resolved head identity, stature, neck-course control, infrathyroid tape, sex/fat coordinates, and the neck-base tape with cervicale, side-neck and suprasternale heights. Those three heights do not define a horizontal base ring. The current neck assembly has not yet been replaced by a named-plane/course construction.

| Input or landmark | Meaning consumed by the curves |
| --- | --- |
| n-Gn | FaceBase's 3D chord from nasion to contour gnathion. Nasion is the nasofrontal landmark, not deepest sellion. |
| n-sn, sn-prn and al-al | Independent 3D nasal height, protrusion and breadth. The nose owns its n-sn chord; its maximum forward source point is prn. |
| ls-sto and sto-li | Euclidean vermilion chords, not vertical projections. The normalized own-lip directions and shared contact give their world points. |

## Curve ownership

Each view part supplies four signed original curves, an axis, section-evaluation stations and quarter fullness. A knot list alone does not prove its between-knot spans are correct.

| Owner | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Front positive and negative | Signed supports along the section's u direction. The part's own chart determines whether these are a face profile, lip border, ear chord or another anatomical guide. |
| Side positive and negative | Signed supports along v. They do not repair a wrong front support or an outside axis. |
| Axis | World origin, section plane and source parameter. The parallel-axis declaration preserves its original coordinate curves and fixed basis; it does not rotate a lip plane into a minimal-frame course. |
| Fullness | Interior shape of each quadrant between the four supports. Its conic shoulder and departures must be admitted by the engine without changing independent support dimensions. |
| Evaluation stations | Where the engine evaluates sections. Extra stations can realize a sparse valid three-knot ridge without adding shape knots. |
| Tree joins and trims | Intersections, blending and openings between unchanged original parts. A join cannot translate a part to conceal incompatible anatomical inputs. |

The viewer's front.pos, front.neg, side.pos and side.neg are original-source producer curves. Axis shows the actual source frame course. A displayed top.k is an admitted raw loft section at an original front-positive knot index, not an independent original guide or the final joined skin. Rejected nonpole sections and finite poles must be distinguished from visible rings.

## Current source composition

HeadLayout places the landmark plan from the pure NeckSource record, without constructing a neck chain, the old head pass or radial head skin. HeadComposer creates the mass, orders features and retains each declaration, evaluated part, raw loft and diagnostic. HeadTree.of builds joins from the retained declarations; it does not serialize decoded millimetre parts a second time. This avoids splitting a pole's axis, outline and station across different unit round trips. The selected tragion height and retained t-n chord now resolve X/Y together, instead of replacing Y after an X solve at another height.

The current f1 source has 25 views included in the production head tree: the head mass including its neck, nose, two lips, two orbit recesses, two auricular plates, two helices, two antihelices, four crura, two conchae, lobule, two alae, two nostril recesses, philtrum and mentolabial sulcus. Zero-amplitude features can be absent; absence is not a rendered success. The declaration does not itself prove that each raw loft or final join succeeds.

Four additional independent views, two globes and two fissures, are retained in source.studies. They are visible for original-curve inspection but remain outside the production skin tree. Existing HeadLane.eyes/attachment records still use the legacy orbit-seat convention. Their consistency test does not validate the new studies or establish a realized lid opening.

## Neck replacement boundary

NeckSource retains the four private harmonic authoring rows and their XZ normalization, which is not the nonplanar row's 3D length. Its top mean is explicitly distinct from posterior phase pi, which closes the named C7 height. HeadLayout reads the analytic mean, posterior point and original endpoint secant directly. The standalone head-parts entry uses this same source without whole-person calibration. NeckConstruction still adapts that record through HumanHorizontalChain for the old trunk, so the local assembly has not been removed. The head mass's circle-equivalent tape radius, different back profile and quarter fullness do not together guarantee the supplied tape.

The replacement must distinguish the infrathyroid plane normal to the neck axis from the base course through back, side and front neck points. It must state axis, site, AP/aspect and forward-placement assumptions. A common scale can close a declared 3D base course against its tape, but that numerical closure does not locate the infrathyroid landmark or prove taut skin contact.

Trunk consumers currently read the old bottom ring and its sampled/finite-difference lateral jet. Move them to the new source's actual named bottom frame/supports before deleting the local neck assembly. Source and trunk must share that definition; a completed mesh or sampled old ring is not an independent law. [The head log](../work/log-head.md) tracks this separately from passing declaration replay and viewer recovery.

## Verification

Inspect each signed curve separately, its actual axis and every available top ring over the same reference, then selected neighbour groups and the whole head from front, side, top and necessary oblique directions. Record source hash, scope, curve producer, missing/rejected geometry and frames personally opened. A multicolour whole-head image or correct knot list does not establish original-span quality.

Numerical source tests independently read public Euclidean measurements, prn's maximum, signed halfspaces, whole-span lip contact, own-side eye frames and the mapped primary Bernstein curve. Production-tree declarations and explicit studies are identified separately. After original curves and neighbourhoods pass, use those same declarations to judge the final loft, joins, openings and skin; a surface patch must not compensate for a wrong original guide.
