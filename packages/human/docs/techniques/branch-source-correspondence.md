# Branch correspondence

## Topology before phase

Select a branch component from exact source incidence, coherent boundary cycles and its declared anatomical domain. Equal loop counts, contour ordinals or shared source vertex indices do not establish an unchanged critical point or anatomical correspondence.

A genus-zero three-cycle component has Euler characteristic -1; a four-cycle component has characteristic -2. Multiple births and saddles can lie inside one slab. An apparent three-cycle boundary therefore does not prove a single saddle. Keep contacts, cavities, excluded regions and unresolved events explicit.

Canonical position welding changes adjacency, not measured positions. Its policy and source witnesses must remain explicit. An excluded anatomical domain changes the chart and cycle count; do not reinterpret an extra component as a limb or close it by an average cap.

## Guides and physical phase

A guide plane can anchor a port only when the saved guide actually intersects that port's selected sheet. An intersection computed on the port does not supply a missing guide segment. Native contour publication order is not a longitudinal guide.

For support endpoints A,B, let b=unit(B-A), choose a qualified common forward F, set f=unit(F-b*dot(F,b)) and a=cross(f,b). Two candidate longitudinal planes have normals a and f through A. Degenerate chords or forward projections refuse the chart. Their names describe geometry, not material transport.

Match component incidence and ordered guide intersections before fitting a monotone phase map. Preserve every original physical h, direction and full signed chain-rule Jacobian. A positive local chart Jacobian does not prove global injectivity or boundary bijection.

## Boundary-preserving interior identification

A candidate extension is

```
S(u,v;q) = G(u,v;q) + L(q)*w(u,v)*sum(theta_j(q)*p_j(u,v)).
```

G consumes actual common boundary curves and transverse jets. w and its first derivatives vanish on every port, so the residual cannot alter those jets. L has physical length units; u,v and polynomial basis terms are dimensionless. Separate three-cycle and four-cycle domains rather than changing tessellation to conceal a topology mismatch.

Fit compact common coefficients against complete recovered section and guide observations. Hold out whole arcs or sections, distinguish source-fit uncertainty from common-law error and inspect the connected surface with its neighbors. Chart construction and a low residual alone certify neither anatomy nor embedding.
