# Neutral supports and frame transport

Recovered skin-centre curves and rig support axes are distinct observations. A native joint marker establishes a geometric chart only after its actual transform, units and source binding are known; it is not a clinical landmark by name.

## Coordinates and constraints

Keep the native component transform separate from exported-mesh registration. Apply each declared axis permutation, handedness change and length scale once. Preserve the achieved posture rather than silently straightening the joints.

Define upper-arm and forearm lengths, support chords and measurement courses separately. A course-to-chord relation is an identified approximation unless the measurement definition gives an equality. Solve coupled height, leg and axial constraints together, because independent pelvis-height ratios can contradict them. Refuse an infeasible or nonpositive remaining axial extent.

## Source-local and generated frames

Let a source-local station s map to world station y=a*s+b, with a positive, and let D(s) be an affine centre displacement. Transport the same boundary and centre authority with the same map. Radial amplitudes remain physical lengths; changing station width does not multiply them. [Trunk intervals](trunk-interval-identification.md) gives the derivative rules.

An independent length change moves affected reference planes, curves and neighboring attachment frames together. A fit of support positions alone proves neither skin fidelity nor clinical correspondence. Symmetry is a declared generation assumption, not proof that the original supports were symmetric.
