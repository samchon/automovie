# Reference contour reconstruction

A curve overlay establishes where observations lie, not that a surface reconstructed from them preserves the person. Qualify private reference observations with a consumer that reads the saved sections without reading the original mesh, then compare its output with the fixed source. This diagnostic is distinct from the anatomical parameter family: a dense replay of measured contours does not establish independent controls, population generalization or an acceptable product representation.

The reconstruction experiment must consume repository interface contracts, generate its actual surfaces through the engine and render through the viewer. A standalone mesher can localize a failure, but passing its resulting mesh through a typed wrapper does not establish engine generation. Record the actual curve and surface operators, not only an output model type.

## Orientation and correspondence

Keep the source surface's orientation witness alongside each closed section. Ordering every contour counterclockwise is useful for measurements but removes the distinction between an outer boundary and a cavity boundary. A containment guess based on one contour point is unstable where a folded or self-intersecting source crosses another contour. Recover the sign from the cutting-plane normal, a segment tangent and its supporting source-face normal before discarding source topology. Grade: derived.

[Fuchs, Kedem and Uselton 1977](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~misha/Fall13b/Papers/Fuchs77.pdf), read through the graph formulation, expresses contour tiling as elementary triangles and a minimum-cost path. A fixed-seam dynamic program minimizes the chosen tile cost for that seam; it is not the paper's optimum over all cyclic seams. It does not solve branching or certify injectivity. A coarse cyclic phase search can twist nearly coincident sections even when every observed point is accurate. Grade: derived.

Bracket split and merge events separately from ordinary stations. A temporary connecting slit between disconnected contours is an approximation with a measurable extent; its triangles and caps remain error sources. Preserve internal loops and source defects explicitly rather than treating a closed output mesh as anatomical acceptance.

## Critical levels and source defects

Equal loop counts at two stations do not establish an unchanged section topology: a small component can appear and disappear between them, or an outer contour can exchange with a cavity while the count stays fixed. For a piecewise-linear height function on a triangulated two-manifold, [Bremer, Edelsbrunner, Hamann and Pascucci 2004](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~hamann/BremerEdelsbrunnerHamannPascucci2004TVCG.pdf), sections II.C and III.A, classifies vertices by their lower links and uses a symbolic ordering for tied heights. A regular vertex has a connected lower-link arc; extrema have empty or complete lower links, and saddles have multiple components. This supplies critical physical levels before section correspondence discards source topology. Grade: derived.

Bracket each critical level with actual cuts and preserve the incident signed contours and source defect observations. A symbolic tie-break orders combinatorial events; it does not move the measured surface or separate a physically flat plateau. Record equal-height neighborhoods, non-manifold links and coincident events explicitly. A source can be topologically manifold while intersecting itself in space, so link classification alone does not establish an embedded skin. A finite cavity contour whose correspondence failed must remain unresolved rather than being capped as a disappearing point.

When a branch contains an intermediate island, its first appearance does not establish that both parent branches have joined. Preserve the complete incident event group before selecting a regional boundary; a midline extremum alone is not a connectivity witness. These are geometric observations, not additional anatomical or clinical truth.

Connectivity welding also differs from moving source positions. A compact region can remap canonical adjacency while retaining each original geometric vertex. Replacing those positions with weld representatives changes the source being measured and needs its own displacement record. Grade: derived.

## Between-station interpolation

A fitted contour's residual bound says nothing about interpolation between stations. Closely spaced event sections can produce large Hermite overshoot when derivatives inferred from a distant neighboring station are reused without interval-aware control. The existing [station-channel research](parametric-curve-family.md) describes this distinction. [SciPy's PCHIP documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.interpolate.PchipInterpolator.html), notes read, uses zero derivatives at sign-changing or zero neighboring secants and otherwise an interval-weighted harmonic mean. It preserves scalar monotonicity and first-derivative continuity; second derivatives can jump. The cited original Fritsch-Butland paper has not been read here.

Applying this rule componentwise is a candidate for constraining shared longitudinal derivatives, not proof that a surface is embedded or anatomically smooth. The result depends on the chosen coordinate frame, can flatten extrema and retains alternation already present in observed stations. Inspect station channels, branches, corners and the generated patches together; changing slopes cannot repair a wrong cross-section correspondence. Grade: derived for the scalar properties, inference for the surface application.

## Geometry and display normals

The source's displayed smoothness and its piecewise-planar geometry are different observations. [Three.js BufferGeometry](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/BufferGeometry.html) computes shared vertex normals from adjacent faces. Retriangulating an unchanged planar surface can change these averages and therefore its shaded appearance. Changing anatomical amplitudes to compensate would fit a display operator's loss.

Keep a matched geometric-normal pass and a matched display-normal pass. The latter may replay the source renderer's normal field sampled on private section curves; that field is a shading attribute, not an analytic surface derivative and never product geometry. A reconstructed patch still needs its own geometric tangent and normal contract. Disable fine relief and use the same camera, material and raking lights for both surfaces.

## Qualification

Record every input and consumer hash, units, frame, posture, physical plane, interpolation rule and capture time. Measure both source-to-reconstruction omission and reconstruction-to-source addition with area-weighted surface samples, including reserved sections and oblique views. An observed sample maximum is not a bound over the complete surfaces. Inspect branches, apertures and thin parts separately; aggregate residuals can conceal a failed mouth or finger web. Retain the round's complete findings and correct their causes together before accepting it.

Map every observation family to its fitted spans and the surface operator that consumes them. Observed or fitted curves that do not generate the judged skin remain unsupported construction evidence. Preserve reserved families separately from fitting inputs, and distinguish a measured cubic replay from an anatomical family with independent named dimensions. Bind comparison runtimes and captures to committed source bytes; private tools may use a local source-only commit with private input hashes, provided the loaded runtime bytes are shown to match it.

An independently fitted cubic for almost every source edge is a mesh re-encoding even when each span has a small certified residual. Local tangent directions inherited from neighboring source facets can force this result under a strict full-polygon bound. Couple derivatives and semantic knots across a family, distinguish intentional creases from triangulation artifacts and measure coefficient count, identifiability and independent measurement response. Exact event contours remain a useful diagnostic exception, not an anatomical formula.
