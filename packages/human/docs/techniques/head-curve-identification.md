## Generated physical axes

The generated forward scale is `head.length`, and the lateral scale is `head.length * head.cephalicIndex / 100`. The common normalized curve is transformed by these two scales, then projected into world-radial Fourier degree 12 with 256 quadrature samples. A separate midpoint grid reports this projection's maximum sampled loss. Neither grid is a bound over the continuous curve.

The four achieved forward offsets of the inner-eye/chin chord give the common magnitude `0.07138945147563247 * head.length`. The crown's common forward support offset is `0.06801177631817769 * head.length`. Its zero-radius contour death receives a horizontal tangent-plane condition.
