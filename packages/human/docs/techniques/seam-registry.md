# Seam registry

A seam is exact by construction when one function owns the shared boundary and both sides read its output: the owner publishes the boundary ring with its station jets (value, slope and curvature per millimetre of station) at a declared continuity, and the adopting chain copies that ring unchanged (`AutoMovieHumanSeamStation.copy`, rule J3). A neighbour never computes its own version of a boundary, and a seam is never closed by a fade between two independent estimates. Every seam below gets one test at its declared class: the gap under 1 mm, the normal angle under 0.1 degree for G1, and the normal curvature jump under the visible limit for G2.

Status: `published` the owner publishes and the neighbour copies; `two fits` each side builds its own estimate; `fade` a blend weight closes a mismatch; `undeclared` no owner is named.

## Rule the tests enforce

For each row the test reads both sides at the boundary on the generated skin of every core person: the gap, the normal angle and, for G2, the normal curvature jump, against the declared class. A row whose status is not `published` fails until its owner converts it.
