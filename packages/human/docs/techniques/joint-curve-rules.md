# Elbow and knee support neighborhoods

`HumanJointCurves` constructs small current-pose bands around generated elbow and knee supports. It owns their common regional shape law. The common boundary owner restricts the neighboring shaft authorities, transports their physical phase, and establishes shared positions and transverse jets. The regional band does not weld mesh vertices, average two incompatible boundaries or replace source gaps with an arbitrary middle contour.

## Observation and correspondence

The existing reference-experiments acquisition program supplies three charts for each side and joint. The incoming and outgoing charts use the actual adjacent current native support directions. The bisector chart uses their normalized direction sum, projects common registered forward into that plane to determine roll, and observes planes at signed physical offsets -20, -10, 0, 10 and 20 mm. Incoming and outgoing observations retain their own plane orientations; their shapes are not averaged into the bisector observations. All original components, original-face incidence, reports, Hermite phase intervals and source receipts remain independently retained.

The support chain is upperarm–lowerarm–hand for the elbow and thigh–calf–foot for the knee. Its actual piecewise-linear intersection with the requested plane selects a containing positive shell. It is not replaced by a straight extrapolated joint axis. Selection requires one containing positive shell, no containing cavity and an unambiguous original closed-loop lineage. An approximation-boundary contact, absent or conflicting shell, missing lineage or conflicting positive radial crossing remains unsupported. Surface ownership and between-plane continuation require the common source-incidence analysis; containment alone certifies neither.

Each original cubic Hermite span retains its actual positive phase width `h`. Its exact Bernstein controls are `P0`, `P0+h*D0/3`, `P1-h*D1/3`, `P1`. Adaptive de Casteljau subdivision uses a 0.025 mm control-to-chord bound with depth 20 and 16,000-vertex budgets. These bounds use floating-point arithmetic and are not certified exact predicates. Source points within the chord bound of a flattened boundary remain ambiguous. Original source-fit tolerance failures are recorded separately; numerical refinement of an already fitted source curve does not repair them.

Green polynomial integrals over the original Hermite spans determine the selected loop's signed area and area centroid directly, retaining each actual `h`. Exactly one positive intersection with its adaptively bounded polyline at each of 192 angular rays permits a radial candidate. The common forward projection supplies the physical phase correspondence; it does not establish material correspondence. Right-side U reverses the reflected cross-product sign so both sides retain the same physical angular interpretation. Exact cubic derivative roots, using each original `h`, supply projected width and forward/backward extrema independently of the ray grid. Polynomial integration and root solving still use binary64 arithmetic.

## Common law and physical derivatives

Let `J` be the current generated joint support, `b` its bisector direction, `U,V` its rolled plane axes, `G` the physical Elbow or Knee course in mm, and `lambda` the signed bisector-plane distance from `J`, increasing distally. Source identification uses each reference's independently measured actual `G`. Product generation uses the existing authored `parameters.controls[joint].value`; it neither infers a value from nor fills the independent measurement record. A reference comparison adapter must explicitly supply actual courses as controls when reproducing an observed case. The dimensionless station is `eta=lambda/G`. A shared conditioning scale `a` defines `xi=eta/a`; it changes no physical unit. Two centroid channels and a radial Fourier series are identified jointly across reference people and sides.

The central curve is identified from the actual zero-offset observations. Its common dimensionless intercept is the mean of their normalized channels. A low-order least-squares fit then identifies residual longitudinal powers using the other observed planes while keeping that middle intercept fixed. Increasing angular and longitudinal orders is compared by leaving one entire person out, retaining both correlated sides in the same fold. No coefficient or branch is indexed by reference identity. Additional geometric ratios require independent rank and held-out improvement; the existence of four people alone does not identify muscularity, fat, masculine/feminine or pose responses.

For any fitted channel `f(xi)`, the physical channel is `c=G*f(xi)` and `dc/dlambda=f'(xi)/a`. The physical origin is `O=J+lambda*b+U*c0+V*c1`. With `e=U*cos(phi)+V*sin(phi)` and radial series `R`, the single surface authority is:

```
P             = O + e*R
P_lambda      = b + U*c0_lambda + V*c1_lambda + e*R_lambda
P_phi         = e_phi*R + e*R_phi
P_lambda_phi  = e_phi*R_lambda + e*R_lambda_phi
```

Positions and circumferential derivatives use mm and mm per radian. Longitudinal derivatives use mm per physical station mm. The mixed derivative uses inverse radians. These transverse jets differentiate the fitted common law; source Hermite derivatives around each ring do not directly observe transverse surface jets. `HumanCurveFamily.fromFunction` preserves this exact analytic law and its jets under restriction and recursive subdivision. It does not resample the law into natural cubic intervals or add missing shape by increasing the layer count.

The runtime principal descriptors are the support middle and the two common observed-domain limits. The limits bind neighboring domain ownership; they are not invented anatomical extrema. Observation planes at 10 mm spacing are a measurement grid, not five automatic principal features. Posterior elbow and anterior crease changes, centroid turns and volume extrema must be distinguished from a native support name. In particular, no patella apex or universal joint volume minimum is inferred when the observed band contains only a monotone profile. An extremum bracket among observed profiles does not determine its exact physical location between planes.

The dimensionless supported band is the intersection of the reference ranges `[-20/G,+20/G]`. Runtime scales that intersection by its authored physical input `G`. This coverage limit differs from `a`, which only conditions the least-squares design. Independent changes beyond joint-course scaling, unseen people and other poses remain predictions requiring separate validation. The reference Elbow and Knee courses are nonplanar surface measurements; they are not asserted to equal a generated planar ring's perimeter.

## Integration limits

The common boundary owner alone decides the final cut boundaries and conditioned connecting functions. Shared position, physical phase, longitudinal parameter conversion and mixed jets must all agree. For a shaft surface `F(s,phi)`, cutting it by `lambda=b·(F-J)` requires the actual surface derivative `ds/dlambda=1/(b·F_s)` and implicit phase derivative `ds/dphi=-(b·F_phi)/(b·F_s)`. Dividing only by the angle between the two bone chords describes the support polyline and does not reparameterize the skin. Zero or ambiguous surface-plane intersections remain unresolved.

The regional law supplies no original mesh, original normal, copied curve table, per-person controls, position patch, arbitrary radius floor or clinical certification. It changes neither the common skeleton nor the acquisition program. Final fidelity, neighboring continuity and independent parameter interactions require matched actual renders after common assembly.

## Source features and the third assembled round

The third assembled round removes the previous elbow fold while preserving the analytic shaft laws. Four saved artifacts contain 36,864 inspected triangles each across the eight shafts and eight joint half-bands. No inspected triangle has a reversed face/analytic-normal dot product or zero area. All twelve shaft/joint and joint/middle boundary pairs per person agree in position exactly at saved vertices, and their maximum analytic-normal difference is below `1.4e-15`. These checks cover the emitted sampling; they are not a proof that an unsampled analytic chart is globally injective. They also do not qualify the excluded or missing head, foot, thumb, shoulder, pelvis, wrist or ankle regions.

The visible knee collar and elbow ring shading remain distinct from the removed fold. Reconstructing the emitted cubic log-radius halves gives a middle physical second-derivative jump of 0.0376–0.0585 inverse mm for elbows and 0.0255–0.0329 inverse mm for knees. A shared value, tangent plane and normal therefore do not establish curvature continuity. Removing that jump alone would not recover a patella, posterior fossa, olecranon or shaft muscle profile.

The next common fit must combine source position and shape information with exact boundary constraints. A viable formulation lifts the common owner's exact boundary functions and physical jets unchanged, then solves common interior coefficients in functions that vanish with the required boundary derivatives. This avoids projecting an implicitly reparameterized shaft boundary into a finite Fourier family. Shared value, first derivative and second derivative constraints must refer to the same physical plane station and phase. Positivity can be enforced through log radius; it does not establish correspondence or recover missing features by itself.

An observed guide `gamma(u)` contributes its original physical Hermite derivatives, not a fabricated full surface jet. Where `lambda(u)` is regular, exact chain rules give `gamma_lambda=gamma_u/lambda_u` and `gamma_lambdalambda=(gamma_uu*lambda_u-gamma_u*lambda_uu)/lambda_u^3`. If `phi(lambda)` is the guide's measured polar path and `f=log(radius/G)`, source constraints are `f_lambda+f_phi*phi_lambda=d(log radius)/d lambda` and `f_lambdalambda+2*f_lambdaphi*phi_lambda+f_phiphi*phi_lambda^2+f_phi*phi_lambdalambda=d^2(log radius)/d lambda^2`. These are linear in common coefficients for a fixed correspondence. They constrain only the measured path; they do not independently observe all transverse or mixed derivatives. Source-fit uncertainty must remain separate from common-fit loss and prevents asserting certified derivative accuracy.

A variational quartic correction can satisfy the middle C2 equality while leaving both halves' endpoint values and first derivatives unchanged. Its coefficient is determined by the physical constraint and bending energy, rather than an arbitrary handle scale. That condition belongs in the same constrained source-shape fit: it does not solve shaft-boundary C2, missing centre features or anatomical fidelity on its own. Any new regional law requires the common owner's complete boundary integration and another matched four-person render round before claiming improvement.
