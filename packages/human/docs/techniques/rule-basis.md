## Numerical controls

The quintic port departure bounds come from the infimum of the loop basis divided by the slope basis and from the maximum of the slope basis. Frame, radius and reach come from the active parent's and child's charts. In the atlas where one angular projection crosses grazing, the signed inequalities are evaluated before division.

Fairing measures squared changes in curvature over physical arc length. Multiplication by length cubed cancels its length dimension. Line and ring integrals use the actual station or phase intervals. The correction is bounded by source sample support and preserves the endpoint jets. Acceptance also needs direct evidence that the result removes the visible ridge. [Moreton and Séquin 1992](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sequin/PAPERS/SIGGR92_MVC_MVS.pdf).
