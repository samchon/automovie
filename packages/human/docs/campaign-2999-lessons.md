# Human construction lessons

Start from the existing working figure. Treat its shared skin connections as reusable and concentrate on removing overfitting from the shape construction. Preserve normal proportions, silhouettes, openings and connected anatomy while replacing causes in the existing path. A proposed replacement must earn retirement of that path through actual behavior and viewing; the baseline is a comparison, not evidence of arbitrary-person generality.

## Keep construction, replace fitted authority

Keep generic joins, curve/surface evaluation, topology, frames, groups, appearance, export and viewer consumers. Preserve the anatomical information and parameter meanings carried by the working construction. Separate person-specific or observation-row corrections from reusable rules before removing them; complexity, a coefficient or a saved shape detail is not itself evidence of overfitting.

Construct and expand lofting-curve layers from major anatomical centres, part-local frames, reference directions and dimensions by reusable algorithms, retaining the existing skin-joining extension algorithm. The rejected reconstruction deleted normal shape information and forced the remainder into sparse polynomials or simple primitives, repeating the overfitting mistake. Choose representations to suit the construction and preserve anatomy, parameter meanings and module transforms across the supported parameter domain.

Keep supported behavior and describe its limits. The baseline does not establish completed rigging, pose or expression support. Preserving a consumer does not justify claiming a capability it never implemented.

## Coordinates and derivatives

Give each part a meaningful local origin and axes, with one parent-relative placement. A rig pivot, support-box centre, source-area centroid and generated centre field serve different purposes; none silently replaces another. Convert units and frames once where ownership changes.

Use one common construction and a declared reflection for symmetric sides. Transform positions, boundary maps, derivatives and normal orientation consistently; do not obtain the other side by an independent fit or by reflecting a proper frame into an improper one. One owner supplies each shared neck, wrist or ankle definition to both consumers.

Compute the derivatives the actual consumer needs. A connector differentiating its second-order parent boundary germ through order r needs parent derivatives through r+2; a surface preview that needs first partials must not pay for unrelated order-four work. Keep numerical geometry and needed differentiation available, with exact or whole-domain qualification optional and its unresolved results explicit.

Transport retained loft detail and its attachments through the same map: for `Q = F(P)`, use `Q_i = DF P_i` and, where consumed, `Q_ij = DF P_ij + D²F[P_i,P_j]`. This chain-rule contract requires differentiable coordinates and a valid local map; it does not guarantee global nonintersection or normal anatomy. The existing [BodyWarp](../src/generation/AutoMovieHumanBodyWarp.ts) moves spline controls, which reproduces `F(P)` exactly for an affine map but is an approximation for a nonlinear map; its diagonal Jacobian is not the total derivative of a station-dependent deformation. Reuse the existing curve/axis transport first. The [AxDf publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0010448594901031) describes axis-controlled deformation, and [Harmonic Coordinates section 3](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~misha/ReadingSeminar/Papers/Joshi07.pdf) states affine reproduction and local smoothness for another family. Those scoped sources do not make a cage, Laplace solve, flow optimizer or exactness proof a prerequisite for this campaign.

## Meaning of observations and failures

Differences from Recovery or pre-PR centre and curve coordinates are diagnostics, not targets for person-specific constants, offsets or residual fields. Smoother curves are allowed; exact coordinate reproduction is not the objective. Judge finite, connected, normal anatomy and stable consumers across the supported parameter domain; numerical continuity residuals alone do not establish shape success.

A source-budget refusal, missing clinical qualification or unvisited sample population is not evidence that the anatomy cannot be constructed. Preserve available geometry and distinguish missing inputs, numerical construction, display coverage and optional qualification. An optional appearance feature or display-precision failure must not erase unrelated valid skin.

A registered example succeeding does not establish the changed-input path. Verify actual consumed parameters and separate admission failures from model retirement so a rejected replacement does not remove usable skin. In the restored viewer, retirement preceded rejection; only the saved artifacts established the earlier baseline, not a preserved live model.

Bind cache identity to the restored generator's actual dependencies, including active calibrations. Align viewer adapters, protocols and ordinary development defaults with that working path; retired sparse adapters, excluded calibration hashes and a mandatory heap environment must not silently define the restored generator's behavior.

## Observe and correct by cause

Use the actual viewer from the first usable working figure and throughout development. Compare the same finite model, inputs, camera, material, light, resolution and renderer across whole views, affected parts, neighboring joins and generating curves. Include ordinary input changes and supported motion states.

Finish the complete inspection round before editing: collect every phenomenon, root cause and contributing factor, including interactions across parts and generation/display differences. Immediately stop and reselect a disproven strategy rather than prolonging it with local patches; record the learned causes and applicability here or in the existing campaign documents as work proceeds. Correct all collected causes in one coordinated batch, then inspect the entire affected comparison again.
