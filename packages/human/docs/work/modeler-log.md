At each station the outlines make a box; the section touches the box at the guides with zero angular slope and the guide's own curvature, and its fullness inside each quarter is solved from the tape girth (the taut-tape perimeter runs from `2 sqrt(W^2 + D^2)` to `2 (W + D)`, so one girth gives one fullness; a girth outside that range is an input contradiction, reported at the input). The quintic polar sectors (`lofting/AutoMovieHumanPolarSector.ts`) stay the representation, not raw conics or super-ellipses (C1 only, or flat at the guide). Fairing by ownership: the outlines own the extremes, the girth owns the fullness, the diagonals add observations.
- Reliefs: amplitude as an outline difference (breast projection = chest depth minus underbust depth; gluteal = buttock depth minus waist depth; nose, lips and chin = median profile minus the skull's ellipse, as now), the base's outlines drawn without the protrusion. Valleys (the gluteal cleft, the cleavage, the groin fold) come from the union of two masses, no separate cut. Recesses (orbit, navel, philtrum, nasolabial fold, spinal groove, popliteal hollow) are declared carves. Branches (axilla, finger webs, crotch) and the ear stay branch events and appendage chains.
- Larger parts: one envelope per large part (trunk, each limb, head) replaces the row tables (`AutoMovieHumanLegSectionRows`, `AutoMovieHumanLegGuides`, the `AutoMovieHumanArmRows` and `AutoMovieHumanArmGuides` channels, the trunk's guide rows); `AutoMovieHumanBodyGirth`, `AutoMovieHumanLegGirth` and `AutoMovieHumanArmGirth` become fullness solves inside the box.

### Design

Both operators work on parametric surfaces with exact 2-jets (`AutoMovieHumanLoftJet.at` for a chain, closed forms for the tests); no triangle, ray march or distance field enters.

1. Surface: `IAutoMovieHumanSolidSurface`, a map `(u, v)` to its point and first and second partial derivatives, with the period of `v` where it is periodic. A chain is one with `u` its station and `v` its phase (`AutoMovieHumanSolidChain`).
2. Intersection (`AutoMovieHumanSolidIntersection`): the part `A(s, phi)` against the host `B(y, theta)`. For each part phase phi, `F(s, y, theta) = A(s, phi) - B(y, theta) = 0` is three equations in three unknowns, solved by Newton with Jacobian `[A_s, -B_y, -B_theta]`. The curve's derivatives come from the implicit function theorem: `x' = -J^-1 A_phi`, `x'' = -J^-1 (A_phiphi + 2 s' A_sphi + s'^2 A_ss - y'^2 B_yy - 2 y' theta' B_ytheta - theta'^2 B_thetatheta)`, so the curve's point, tangent and curvature are exact. Tracing is continuation in phi from a seed (a landmark's foot points on both surfaces, `AutoMovieHumanSolidFoot`) with the second-order predictor and a Newton corrector, the step halved where Newton fails to converge; it must close after one turn of phi. Diagnostics: tangential contact where the normals' angle along the loop falls under a bound (J singular); no closure; Newton lost.
3. Rails (`AutoMovieHumanSolidRail`): on the part, the station `s_A(phi)` a chord `w_A(phi)` along the part's station line on its kept side; on the host, the curve's preimage offset by a chord `w_B(phi)` along the direction square to the preimage in the host's first fundamental form (`e = G^-1 R c'`, the same squaring as `AutoMovieHumanSeam.square`), on the kept side. The kept side is read from the other surface's normal: a merge keeps the part outside the host and the host outside the part; a diff keeps the cutter inside the base and the base outside the cutter. The widths are laws of the parts' own sizes passed by the consumer.
4. Ribbon and blend (`AutoMovieHumanSolidRibbon`, `AutoMovieHumanSolidBlend`): a ribbon is a surface's 2-jet along a parameter-domain rail and a parameter-domain cross direction `d`, scaled by `a`: point `S(r)`, cross derivative `a (S_u d_u + S_v d_v)`, second `a^2 (S_uu d_u^2 + 2 S_uv d_u d_v + S_vv d_v^2)`. The blend is the quintic Hermite in its cross parameter `w` between the host ribbon (w = 0, leaving the host past its rail toward the curve) and the part ribbon (w = 1, arriving along the part's station line). Since the blend's w-jet at each edge equals the jet of the surface map `g(phi, w) = S(r(phi) + w a(phi) d(phi))`, its mixed derivative `T_phiw` is the phi derivative of that same jet, so the blend and the surface share their whole parametric 2-jet along the rail: G2 by construction for any smooth rails. `a` is the chord between the rails over the cross derivative's length (as `AutoMovieHumanLimbRootStrip.speed`).
5. Merge and diff (`AutoMovieHumanSolidMerge`, `AutoMovieHumanSolidDiff`): intersection, rails and blend composed; the host's rail is published as its trim loop `(y, theta)` per phase (the `IAppendage.root` loop of `AutoMovieHumanBodyRoots`), the part's as its new first station per phase, so the host, the blend and the part are tessellated from the same rail points. A diff reverses the cutter's kept side; its rim is the same blend, convex.
6. Through-cut (`AutoMovieHumanSolidTrim`): the margin as a closed periodic C2 spline in the skin's own chart through the foot points of its landmarks (endocanthion, palpebrale superius, exocanthion, palpebrale inferius; cheilion and the stomion line), the chart's hole by its inside test; a rolled rim is one more blend from the skin's ribbon at the margin to the inner curve's.
7. Corners: where two blends' host rails would cross (crotch, webs), each width is capped at half the rails' gap; past that, a setback closed by `AutoMovieHumanSeamPatch` over the blends' boundary ribbons. Built after the two-part operators.
