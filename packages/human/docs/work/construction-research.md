## Prior art: who built something like this

Thalmann, Shen and Chauvineau's BodyBuilder (1996) is the closest: a skeleton whose links carry cross-section skin contours, a skin built from those contours, and published plane rules at joints and at boundaries between skin pieces. Our design differs in the source of the contours (published measurements and laws, not artist metaballs). Wang 2005 shows a whole-body feature wireframe with fixed topology and patches, but driven by scans. Anny and MakeHuman are procedural and scan-free but template-and-blendshape based. SCAPE, Seo 2003, SMPL and SKEL are data-driven. Reusable for us: frames and plane rules (ISB, Thalmann), wireframe topology (Wang), calibration sources (Anny); not reusable: any scan, sculpted target or fitted space.

## How the reference is combined (supervisor plan, 22:50)

1. The reference enters generation only as f1's personal measurements taken by published definitions at their sites (ISO 8559-1, ANSUR II, Farkas, ISB landmarks). A GAP row with a published definition (Pg' to the G vertical, n-prn) may be measured on the reference as a new f1 input, with the procedure in comments. No law constant is fitted to it.
2. Diagnostic registration per layer, never fed to generation: L0 estimates the reference's segment frames from its mesh and records each transform (the reference JSON carries no skeleton); L1 compares landmarks per segment in local frames; L2 cuts the reference at the same girth-loop planes and silhouette sections defined in each part's local frame; L3/L4 compare clay and skin by surface distance in local frames once the earlier layers hold.
3. The A-pose difference disappears in local frames. For whole-figure overlays only, the generated skeleton may be displayed at the reference's measured joint rotations as a display transform.
4. Every difference gets one class: input measurement error, law error (replace by a cited published law), GAP (add an input with a published definition), or person-specific variation the law cannot express. No class moves a law constant toward the reference.

## Rejected approaches

- Scan-driven feature extraction and example synthesis (Wang 2005, pp. 2-3): needs a scan database.
- Learned shape and pose spaces (SCAPE, Seo 2003, SMPL, SKEL): fitted to scans.
- Template meshes with sculpted blendshapes (MakeHuman, Anny): not curve-authored; only their documented calibration sources are studied.
- Metaball muscle contours (Thalmann 1996): artist primitives without published measurement.
