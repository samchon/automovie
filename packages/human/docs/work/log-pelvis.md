### Anatomy sources for the fold premise

[Han et al., Clinical Anatomy 2023](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ca.23988) reports distinct upper/lower subcutaneous structures and medial anchoring; its sagittal/lateral lower-buttock sections do not show a continuous deep tether making the whole groove. The specimen group is elderly and supplies structural evidence, not a numerical f1 shape prior. [Stivala et al., Surgical and Radiologic Anatomy 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33462737/) compares MRI in six volunteers with four cadavers and places the fibrous attachment in the medial third of the cutaneous fold. [Babuccu et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18338103/) likewise distinguishes the medial fibrous structure from the lateral crease. These sources support treating a full fold and a local gluteal boundary separately; they justify no new cylinder, clamp value or coefficient.
