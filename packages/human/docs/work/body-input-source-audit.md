## Shoulder joint, measuring pose and source-family alternatives

This inspection precedes a shoulder shape change. The current GH is not identical to acromion in all coordinates: TrunkLandmarks moves it inferiorly by `0.1044 AR`, while retaining the acromion's AP and lateral coordinates. The resulting zero AP/medial offset is an unmeasured placement convention. The neutral AR projection test verifies its longitudinal closure, not its anatomical three-dimensional location. Its current f1 acromial/GH lateral coordinate is 185.9526098098755 mm; public biacromial breadth is 371.905219619751 mm and resolved bideltoid breadth is 418.9147789071624 mm. The remaining lateral distance to half bideltoid is therefore 23.5047796437057 mm. This arithmetic is not an observed generated bideltoid residual or permission to fit the GH to skin width.

The [official ISB recommendation](https://media.isbweb.org/images/documents/standards/isb_jcs_part_ii.pdf), printed p. 983 and Figure 1, was directly opened as an image. It distinguishes GH from acromial/scapular landmarks and treats its location as an estimated rotation center. The retained ANSUR handbook's complete printed pp. 74 and 88 and both figures were also directly opened: bideltoid is a seated, relaxed-upper-arm-down, elbows-90-degree maximum over the deltoid region; the caliper moves vertically, and measurements below the deltoids are excluded. These are source/protocol studies, not current figure-reference renders.
