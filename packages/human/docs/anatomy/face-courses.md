## Nasal, cavity and cheek coupling

The old alar Gaussian was 5.819/7.110/6.559/7.043 mm above zero at SN and vanished below it. Its smooth SN→alar envelope removes that source step. The old underside square-root cutoff also had an infinite endpoint derivative. The replacement meridian uses the endpoint values and zero tip/base slopes to determine a minimal cubic underside; dorsum uses t^p(1+p(1-t)) with p=2.245593. Its four leave-one-reference-out and eight separate normalized native dorsum profiles improve. The maximum of ridge and alar terms can still have separate derivative behavior: the entire nasal surface is not claimed C1.

A positive anterior field cannot describe an inward nostril cavity. Private cavity research reconstructs the spatial ellipse in a shared head-X/SN–PRN frame and scales it by independent nose width/protrusion. The earlier alar-field-derived transverse frame inverted the outward normal and sent the inner wall laterally through the alar collar. Shared axes repair that correspondence, but the surrounding alar cuff/body course remains incomplete and prevents admission. Construction guides use the common frame conversion to model millimetres, matching IRelief; network free nodes separately use model metres. A prototype mistakenly used the latter for the guide, making its construction 1000 times too small while leaving geometry unchanged. Guide-to-source-node identity is therefore checked explicitly. In the private prototype its inward wall depth is the mean first native inward-ray hit over own protrusion (0.388708); it is a geometric wall approximation, not clinical vestibule depth. The private cavity floor is a boundary-preserving rectangular Coons quad grid. Private exact ellipse bounds follow its shared cosine/sine vectors, independent of sampling resolution.

A provisional cavity failed in all four identities (first female-model witness head[-16.04,-20.16,93.44] mm, 0.243 mm overlap). The first causal diagnosis blamed its two-column opening width without verifying physical side correspondence; that diagnosis is withdrawn. The negative chart-column offsets actually address anatomical left/+X in this domain. An array-index sign assumption connected that rim to the opposite −X ellipse. Named left/right rims now bind to their physical shared-frame signs explicitly. A sign-only control preserves all original coefficients, field and two-column opening to separate this primary correspondence defect from the independent enclosing-meridian/bounds contract. The sign-only control still has at least eight strict crossing witnesses in every gate, so it does not identify the side mismatch as the sole cause. Exact ellipse bounds, rather than grid-index width assumptions, still determine the final neighboring source family; they do not justify changing the fitted anatomy to hide the wrong-side connection.

Likewise the new central tip/alar/SN rows were initially applied to every facial column. A female-model eye-bottom row at y≈2.76 mm jumped to a cheek row at −15.29 mm, creating an 18 mm Coons plate; the prior cheek spacing was about 7.6 mm. The correction confines semantic nasal levels to the nasal core and continues independent cheek courses laterally through the shared alar/nasolabial boundary. The lower nasolabial extension is a layout boundary only: a negative Gaussian valley fit was unstable and worsened two held-out-reference comparisons, so it was rejected.
