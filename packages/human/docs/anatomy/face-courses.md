# Facial reference-course fits

These are geometric reconstructions from selected synthetic reference heads, not population measurements or clinical landmark validation. Native head frames, units and connected components distinguish observations. Quad-loop seeds are geometric proxies; their anatomical correspondence remains unverified. Published landmark dimensions remain in [head](head.md), [mouth](mouth.md) and [nose](nose.md).

## Shared frame and dimensions

Anterior quantities act along head-frame +Z while retaining the requested X/Y. Radial displacement on a tilted section changes all three coordinates. Nasal height is the 3D n-sn chord: its sphere, the independently sourced tragion-distance sphere and the sagittal plane constrain subnasale; incompatible inputs have no intersection. Neither a chord nor a tragion distance is a vertical or anterior component by itself.

An oral arch supplies border, meeting and pocket courses before transverse narrowing. Each independent vermilion chord supplies vertical and anterior components by Pythagoras. Pigmentation and construction need the same spatial border and frame. The skeleton's conventional stomion depth and a geometric oral-surface midpoint have no established clinical correspondence.

## Mouth and philtrum

Four selected reference heads supplied normalized outer lip outlines and spatial depth courses. Outline coefficients are bow rise 0.071991 and upper/lower exponents 0.951344/0.561315. The cph-span/mouth-width ratio is 0.26. Spatial coefficients are upper/lower forward fractions 0.482275/0.148011 and arch sag/exponent 0.420557/2.147722. Grade: pooled reference-geometry fit.

Four-reference normalized depth reconstruction RMS was 0.0331 to 0.0629 of each mouth half-width. Eight separate native heads gave 0.0378 to 0.1410, with two negative lower-forward proxies. Original and held-out inputs are separate; these errors do not establish clinical proxy identity, population coverage or generated appearance.

Paired philtral ridge amplitude is 0.12324 upper vermilion heights, fitted to frontmost transverse physical sections after removing a constant/quadratic nuisance background. A trace entering a rear/cavity connected component is excluded by front-surface correspondence. The nuisance surface is not anatomical geometry. Both commissures, both cph crests and the midline are semantic sampling locations; an index grid can miss a crest even when an analytic border includes it.

## Nasal and cavity courses

A dorsum basis t^p(1+p(1-t)), p=2.245593, and a minimal cubic underside use endpoint values and zero tip/base slopes. Four leave-one-reference-out and eight separate normalized native dorsum comparisons improved. This does not prove continuity of the combined ridge/alar maximum or establish a population distribution.

Nostril research uses an SN-to-PRN longitudinal axis and a perpendicular head-X transverse axis, scaled by independent nose width/protrusion. Eight dimensionless ellipse/plane coefficients describe its rim. Mean first native inward-ray hit divided by own protrusion was 0.388708; this is a geometric wall approximation, not clinical vestibule depth. Exact ellipse bounds follow the shared cosine/sine vectors. Physical left/right correspondence must be established in the shared frame rather than inferred from chart-column signs.

A positive anterior field cannot represent an inward cavity. Rim-side correction alone left strict crossings in all four selected heads; enclosing alar cuff/body courses remain unresolved. Nasal semantic row levels also cannot be imposed on lateral cheek rows without checking physical spacing. The alar-to-oral nasolabial course is a layout boundary; a negative Gaussian crease fit worsened two held-out comparisons and supplies no accepted anatomical coefficient. Cavity admission, clinical mapping and rendered facial form remain unverified.
