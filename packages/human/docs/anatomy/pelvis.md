# Pelvis boundary construction

This record preserves the earlier direct-boundary experiment and its rejected feasibility assumptions; it is not the current acceptance ledger. [#2837](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/issues/2837) records the later canonical `a14f19ac` review, remaining paired-mass/roof/groin failures and unadopted source28 research. Shared collar support subsequently changed in `b73ee44d`; the older triangle counts and ring observations below belong to their original experiment and do not describe that later consumer without reproduction.

The pelvis is part of the continuous trunk mass. Its hip/groin boundary must meet the same mid-thigh rings as the legs, through a control-cage junction. The previous extra medial contact section constrained the first hip row to the midsagittal plane while placing that row above lower perineal controls. Its one-row Hermite bridge emitted no interior target. Independently reconstructed production triangles proved strict left/right intersections in all four lean gate identities.

The corrected construction connects each existing nonplanar trunk/groin loop directly to its existing pinned thigh collar with two Hermite rows. One straight support row then reaches the shared mid-thigh ring. The old artificial contact-section function is removed. Every face and control is generated by the named boundary formulas and integer layout; no gap constant or anatomical coefficient changes. All four identities retain the same new topology and ordered chart coordinates. The collar keeps its previous spacing and exact shared controls.

Hip bridge chart coordinates cover longitudinal `u=0..0.65`; the support covers `u=0.65..1`. These are branch chart coordinates, not the independent sweep evaluator parameters. The shared collar receives one identical address from both sides. Shoulder phase and upper-trunk chart definitions remain unchanged.

The committed-source offline combined upper/pelvis experiment reduced independently proven strict triangle overlaps in the original witness's 20 mm local window from 105/28/11/21 to 0/0/6/0, in female-model, male-model, female-muscular, male-muscular order. These are local triangle-pair counts, not a whole-surface injectivity proof. The six female-muscular witnesses span the end of the hip bridge and the beginning of the straight support around the collar. They cannot be described as an endpoint-only failure.

The original shared muscular thigh rings are themselves incompatible with the stance axes and section reaches: their intended regular cubic limits extend approximately 7.991 mm and 3.483 mm beyond the midsagittal plane. Each collar is the same control loop translated upward along its ring axis and therefore has the same intended regular transverse contour. Neighboring inverse-fitted rows can change the actual collar limit, so this input observation does not determine the full displayed penetration. Actual production ring cuts independently retain those intersections. Changing bridge interiors alone cannot establish global injectivity while the shared displayed rings remain fixed. Resolving stance/section feasibility belongs to the common skeleton/ring construction, without an arbitrary flesh gap or hidden clamp.

Four-gate matched physical-plane reference diagnostics also show missing bilateral posterior masses above the crotch. At the authored buttock level, mapped references have midline recesses of 0.098/0.069/0.103/0.078 of their full section depth; the corresponding current production surfaces have 0.007/0.005/0.005/0.004. Reference and Human depths differ, so these normalized shape observations are neither clinical errors nor a basis for replacing measured depth with a reference's depth. A subsequent paired-mass representation must preserve physical posterior extrema and distinguish convex-hull tape from skin perimeter. Merely deepening the old posterior maximum changes the measurement it claims to hold.

The direct-boundary change is one cause-specific construction repair. Muscular ring feasibility, paired gluteal masses, infragluteal transitions, the converter's range, posed states and the whole four-person visual gate remain unpassed. The actual committed consumer must then be inspected in fourteen directions and defect closeups, each with surface, actual construction overlay, construction alone and raking light with fine material normals disabled. A numerical check cannot establish appearance.
