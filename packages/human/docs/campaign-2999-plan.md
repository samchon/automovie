| Incomplete sampling, omitted domains or reconstructed models between camera requests make display faults resemble shape faults. | Inspect one stable model snapshot and its actual generating curves. | World-height grammar, repeated coordinate conversions or independently reconstructed side boundaries hide shared dependencies. | Keep each part in its local frame with one parent-relative placement and one owner of each shared connection. Apply a declared common reflection to symmetric geometry, boundary maps, derivatives and orientation. |

These are the known cause classes to inspect together, not a claim that every existing expression is wrong. Gather the complete affected construction and consumer evidence before selecting the combined replacement. Observation-distance errors explain the result; they do not choose extra sites, offsets, knots, fields or person-specific constants. Increased error is acceptable when unsupported fitting is removed, while normal anatomy and usable joins remain required.
