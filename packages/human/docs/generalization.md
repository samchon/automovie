# Human generalization

The rules the construction follows, by part. A better construction replaces any of them.

Local geometric proofs establish the stated operators' behavior. They do not establish that an assembled person, its posed skin or its appearance passes.

## Whole body

`AutoMovieHumanBodyLaw` generates every chain in world millimetres from the top down: the head's rise sets where the neck ends, the neck carries the shoulders, the column runs to the crotch's height, and the limbs start from the column. `AutoMovieHumanSkeletonLaw` places the skeleton first, and the limbs loft on it.

- Drive dense sections from sparse dimensions: a per-section centre, frame and in-plane affine, interpolated between a dozen anchors, carries most of the difference between people.
- Separate sex from length with people at the ends of height, fat, muscularity and masculine-feminine, and judge a law on people reserved from every fit.
- Fit slopes on every person and levels on the people inside the domain.
- Fit against a control's achieved value.
- Bound the stated domain by tape ratios as well as tapes, so every test person is a whole person.

## Tapes

The [measurement protocol](measurement-protocol.md) separates authored model goals from external clinical observations. The reference body courses retain their achieved native values, but their clinical correspondence is unestablished. The reference wrist extraction used a crease-like minimum because it lacked a reliable styloid prominence. Preserving those inputs does not establish clinical landmark equality.

The candidate model-identity protocol does not replace published anatomical acceptance. A target-free producer address and a separate final-skin reader do not prove that the site is anatomically valid. Keep independently observed anatomy and clinical identity as separate obligations: retain established failures, and mark unestablished landmarks or reference correspondence unavailable with a concrete reason. Neither state becomes a pass because the model-address row closes. In particular, shoulder-contact-centroid to elbow-centre midpoint is not established acromion-to-olecranon midpoint, knee-joint height is not established mid-patella, and the head's `HANDED = 0.7` course is not established least-neck girth. Resolving these correspondences remains open.

- Declare the operation, posture, frame, region and provenance before solving a dimension. Publish target-free geometric addresses; a missing address is an unavailable measurement, not a passing identity check.
- Locate each tape by its declared definition: the model upper-arm section is square to its axis; thigh, knee and calf use horizontal planes. A bone-normal section is not a substitute for the declared measurement plane.
- Close a girth through a shared scale of width and depth, in which a girth is linear.
- Let width follow the tapes at about 0.8 and front depth from about 0.6 at the waist to 0.1 near the neck, as measured over 67 people.
- Solve a tape against the hull of the section it wraps.
- Normalize an elliptical section by its own hull perimeter; an equivalent-area circle is a different tape.
- Close the assembled surface, including root handovers and event joins. A tape closed on a bare section can change when its neighbours are composed.
- Preserve every closure constraint through fairing. In the 2026-11-02 trunk survey, the existing fairing operator retained only 62.6–93.6% of a unit correction at its own tape key and transferred corrections into adjacent keys.
- Preserve positive, monotone progress between tape keys while sharing first and second derivatives. Setting every key's first derivative to zero can introduce a flat band at an intermediate monotone tape.
- Distinguish a planar taut-section hull from a nonplanar surface course. The model neck-base course follows the final skin through four owned contact heights; a planar clipped hull or the contacts' stored radial distances measures a different object.
- Bound a muscle maximum by transitions owned by the geometry, and inspect the full limb profile separately. A private percentage window cannot hide a larger muscle belly. Sampling the complete interval and its exact ends still does not prove a continuous maximum bound.

## Leg

Both legs come from one construction and a reflection in `AutoMovieHumanLegConstruction`, lofted on the femur and tibia.

- Place the leg's axis by a line per station in a constant, leg length, hip tape and the masculine-feminine control.
- Place the crotch by a line in stature, inseam, thigh and the masculine-feminine control, counting the neck and face stretches in the stature's share.
- Take the leg's sections square to its bone, and let only its last interval reach the tilted crotch ring.
- Give the thigh and the trunk one shared direction where they meet.
- Close horizontal tapes on the assembled world-space loft, including its moving frame. A circle closed in the wrong normal plane missed an independent tilted-tube target by more than 9 mm; horizontal triangle-section closure reached 0.01 mm in the fixture.
- Size the trunk-owned root and leg shaft together. In the controlled nine-person survey the parent root hull exceeded the thigh input by 38.19–124.03 mm, while bare flare and private tape height contributed independently.
- Interpolate the complete root-to-shaft coordinates with shared position, tangent and curvature. A zero positional offset does not remove a derivative carrier: the former short carrier reversed direction 5.25–7.25 mm below the root, with rear turning rates of 3.10–7.44 degrees per millimetre.
- Require zero first and second derivatives where pinch rounding meets the copied root. The former `hypot(d,c)-c` has root curvature `1/c`; `d^3/(d^2+c^2)` has zero first and second root derivatives and positive growth. The final assembled guides still require independent curve and render inspection.
- Keep bony knee expansion outside the declared calf muscle domain while retaining it in the full profile health check. A calf input below the knee input does not justify moving a private search window.

## Trunk

- Join a neighbour by a fillet over one radius of the child's layers; an end slope m over a span h stands `4/27 m h` off the chord.

- Let the rear fall under the buttock's crest as the square of the height.
- Hold the front midline, the widest point and the rear midline at fixed phases, and space the rest by arclength.
- Set the crotch fold's softness at 1.25 times the larger of the ring's pitch and the row spacing.
- Close the final interpolated loft at its owned sites, rather than only the analytic outline. In the controlled survey m2's high-hip analytic section was 1001.17 mm, the pre-event loft 997.46 mm and the skin 997.88 mm.
- Preserve the complete root coordinate and its first two transverse derivatives through pelvis and collar handovers. Pure complete-coordinate guide fixtures distinguish this construction from the former reversing displacement carrier; whole-person C2 and appearance remain separate acceptance checks.
- Keep a closed parent half-loop distinct from its reflected whole section. Their circle perimeters are `(pi + 2) r` and `2 pi r`; supplying a half-loop to a whole-section solve produced a 1.222 scale error in an independent semicircle fixture.

## Arm and hand

The arm and digits loft in their own bone frames: sections square to each bone, frames carried by least rotation, each child starting from a round section that clears its arc.

- Size the arm's root from the shoulder ring's area times the cosine of the bone's tilt.
- Hand the forearm's tube to the hand only from the wrist crease to the branch, with its long axis on the hand's breadth, so the least girth sits at the crease.
- Solve the scale across the hand so the section through knuckles II and V spans the breadth input.
- Scale the elbow's turn with the elbow's radius.
- Normalize an oblique wrist tape in its owned crease plane's metric. Independent cylinder cuts at 0, 30 and 60 degrees distinguish this from bone-normal closure; a varying-radius assembled wrist remains an independent consumer check.
- Preserve the parent's position, tangent and curvature through a bounded root collar, and end its correction with zero first and second derivatives. The round-one m2 bare upper-arm tape was 378.00 mm, the shoulder blend changed it to 358.92 mm and the event join to 355.29 mm, against 377.82 mm requested.
- Derive both event arcs from one canonical boundary and reflect them exactly. Use a phase sample set closed under that reflection; midpoint samples avoid exchanging a missing endpoint. On wide, fixing both reduced the reflected shoulder-ring error from 0.412578 mm to 6.83e-13 mm in a controlled comparison.
- Include the parent's skin where a declared plane crosses a shared boundary. In round one, the arm plus `upper` selection reproduced the complete skin's closed cut on all nine people; arm-only opened the m1 and masculine upper-arm cuts.
- Include an exact measured landmark among the candidates when comparing it with a sampled minimum. A 1 mm wrist scan skipped stylion and falsely reported six negative minimum differences of 0.0011–0.0080 mm.
- Map source wrist, web separation and digit tip to their owned longitudinal goals with positive derivative and C2 continuity. Close observed projections, since an along-axis map coordinate need not equal projection onto the measurement axis.
- Solve hand breadth and girth on the same final section. In an independent rectangle fixture, correcting breadth alone left perimeter 240 mm against 260 mm; the coupled solve answered both.
- Distinguish source-control closure, final bind skin and straightened neutral skin. The hand protocol measures neutral projections and actual component separation; bind control points and two web crests do not prove those observations. Compare cached bind and neutral meshes before assigning a residual to geometry or rigging, rather than adding a fitted length multiplier.
- Next: build each web junction's palm side as one blended surface over its four boundaries, sample the palm finely enough for the metacarpal-head grooves, then derive the knuckle grooves from the heads' gap.

## Webs

A web is a saddle: above its crest the two children are one sheet, the parent continued, and below it two walls leave sideways. Mesh it even in `w = sqrt(z)` about its crest, with level rows and a translational chart near the pinches.

- Share physical boundary position, tangent and curvature, not positions alone. Under reparameterization, preserve the reference speed and tangential acceleration while matching the neighbour's normal curvature. Independent quintic, bowed-boundary and preserved-end fixtures test these conditions; generated web health remains open.
- Invert depth on the original curve inside a sampled bracket. Replacing the first saddle cell by a straight chord loses the square-root speed: for `(u,u^2,0)` at depth `1e-8`, the correct transverse coordinate is `1e-4`, while inversion of the first of 256 chords gives `2.56e-6`. The production curve inversion and independent square-root fixture distinguish those cases.

## Neck and head

- Share the neck/head boundary's position, tangent and curvature, and rebuild its dependent placements when a head dimension changes.
- Keep midline rays a millionth of a radian off the midline.
- Solve each head measure against the construction's own surface at its Farkas landmark, with every ratio at its definition.
- Keep a named neck-tube course distinct from a minimum over the entire collar and neck. The m1 inputs request neck base 388.69 mm and neck 411.96 mm; the larger value cannot be the minimum over a domain containing the smaller course. This exposes an unresolved input/domain correspondence; substituting the named course does not discharge the original least-neck acceptance check.
- Give the visible neck and shoulder collar one assembled constraint owner. The old four neck rings were shoulder transitions, with reference bottom hulls 725.76–828.68 mm and top hulls 497.19–643.95 mm, while the least visible neck lay above them in `head.lower`.
- Invert a tilted head section against its actual analytic point rather than a fixed forward coordinate. Recompute neck rise, sockets, ears and jaw placement after every changed head key before observing the composed surface.
- Give the vault separate anterior and posterior extents. A shared posterior radius can keep the backing vault broad after the front shield is narrowed; independent cardinal-intercept and circular-neck fixtures distinguish that constraint.
- Read fissure dimensions on the evaluated eyelid contact margins, not the recessed trim loop. Independently prescribed margins, a wrong 23.3 mm boundary and a rotated local frame distinguish the reader; margin attachment and seam health still require their own checks.

Head landmark provenance remains unresolved. The current reference extraction and candidate reader do not establish all the definitions required by their Farkas labels: deepest nasal root selects a sellion-like point rather than proven nasion; anterior lip relief is not the midline vermilion border; the alar attachment groove is not alare's lateral convex extremum; and a diagonal chin support is not established gnathion. Correct the feature owners, metadata, closure and independent final-skin observations together. A successful surrogate solve cannot establish head identity. The primary-source distinctions and unchanged reference limitations are recorded in the measurement protocol; head circumference and minimum-frontal-width closure also remain open.

## Rig

`AutoMovieHumanRig` gives every part a binding, and a pose moves the prepared skin without generating it again.

- Weight a vertex by its place alone, so every copy of a seam point moves together.
- Pass skin down the skeleton as a tree of cuts: each joint holds a plane and a ramp.
- Take pose zero as anatomical neutral; the generated A-pose stays the skin's bind, and one rotation per bone reaches neutral.
- Blend by dual quaternions, and evaluate the same blend in every consumer of a posed skin.
- Size each ramp for its joint: stretch is about `1 + theta rho |grad w|`, for the joint's turn theta from the bind, the skin's distance rho from the joint centre and the weight gradient, so the ramp widens with the joint's range and the stretching side's radius.
- Let the side of the chest share the arm's raise on the shoulder girdle's bone.

## Ruled out, with the reason

- Changing `AutoMovieHumanBodyGirth` to repair the current figure's tape errors: the 2026-11-02 source survey found zero production callers. The live closures are the fitted station/ratio keys in `ArmConstruction`, `LegConstruction` and `TrunkConstruction`, with separate neck size regressions. The unchanged nine-person baseline reports 55 trunk, 105 arm and 54 leg failures; see `campaign-baseline.md`.
- Treating the baseline leg errors as ellipse-to-circle normalization: the leg already normalizes the convex-hull perimeter with `PolarSection.girth`. Its thigh and knee ratios contribute less than 0.7 and 0.05 mm, respectively, against observed errors of 37.3–118.5 and 22.8–52.3 mm. Private sites and the final handover must be measured instead. The calf ratio explains most of eight people's errors, but removing its contribution still leaves narrow 8.45 mm too large.
- Attributing the leg's crotch handover to the generic event fillet: `TrunkConstruction.apply` declares that event `shared`, so `EventJoin.reconcile` skips it. Its shape must be traced through the leg's own handover and the trunk-owned ring.
- Bypassing the arm's event fillet to fix its residual root crease: on wide the unjoined crease is 44.141/43.873 degrees, the normal join reduces it to 24.932/25.009, and removing the fillet spread worsens it to 32.827/33.418. The collar needs a construction satisfying continuity and the tape together, rather than disabling continuity.
- Blaming a missing parent triangle for every residual biceps error: seven people's arm-only and full-skin cuts agree. Narrow has no parent triangle at that plane and its fixed root lies wholly proximal; its bare cut is 215.545 mm against 215 mm, while the shoulder blend and join produce 228.240 mm. Conversely, masculine needs the parent and changes from 257.871 to 260.928 mm with the complete region.
- Attributing the old leg root shelf to root-position mismatch: the nine-person controlled survey found root station and position agreement at about `1e-11` mm and exact pre/post-event equality. The traced slope reversal was below the root, so coincident seam points alone cannot certify the handover.
- Treating post-closure fairing or final interpolation as tape-preserving: f2's private underbust closure changed from 760.11 to 780.78 mm through fairing against 741.22 mm input, and m2's high-hip loft differed from its analytic section by 3.71 mm. Solve through those operators instead.
- Treating the old global chest and neck-base locators as established clinical landmarks: the chest search hit exactly `0.64 * stature` on m1, m2, wide and masculine, and the neck-base reader placed cervicale 66–92 mm above its selected suprasternale. These observations show protocol ambiguity, not proof of a replacement clinical locator; owned model addresses retain their explicit convention.
- Assigning the four old trunk-test mirror failures to trunk, neck or leg symmetry: their worst points were in `head.lower` near the nose, at 0.6105 mm on f2, 0.6746 mm on m2, 0.5962 mm on tall and 0.5886 mm on masculine.
- Expecting geometry alone to make contradictory synthetic hand goals pass: even with the existing ±1 mm identity allowances, tall and narrow can reach at most breadth/length 0.380107 and 0.381758 against the unchanged population minimum 0.383. Their independent marginal-extreme construction needs a sourced joint-population rule; this does not justify changing reference inputs or relaxing limits.
- A predetermined sparse polynomial or primitive family: boundaries and named measurements agreed while the anatomy was lost, and five keys on the head converged into a cone.
- Full profiles for a local feature: a full section carries no feature location or depth, so the navel disappeared. Carry a local feature by its own location and depth.
- Features as residuals against a coarse surface: a broad difference is not a feature, and the extractor carved deep flank and lumbar grooves.
- Square collocation at arbitrary phases: it amplified foot section spans about 2,800 times. Use a standard periodic interpolant.
- Forcing fine source derivatives across sparse keys: a 509 mm coefficient excursion appeared inside an 82 mm interval. Let a family generate its own derivatives.
- Choosing counts or smoothing by held-out rows of one mesh: neighbouring rows predict each other, so the score improves until the model reproduces the mesh. Hold out whole people.
- Closing a girth through depth alone: a girth barely responds to depth, so a small disagreement became 18 mm of chest depth.
- Carrying the trunk's front and back as depths from the widest point: that point wandered 50 mm between layers, and smoothing one side printed stripes at the same heights on every person.
- Taking a tilted crotch ring's horizontal projection as a thigh section: it threw the thigh 8 mm back and 5 mm wide.
- A web floor as a straight chord across the parent's section: it leaves each pinch 83–88° out of the parent's tangent plane, so the skin meets the parent at a right angle.
- A level jaw cut for the mouth: it puts the jaw's whole turn into a few millimetres round each corner, stretching that skin more than three times under a 20 mm opening.

## Rules to reuse

- Take each frame anchor from a joint's bind affine by polar decomposition, name its axes as the ISB coordinate systems do, and carry in-plane axes by rotation-minimising transport.
- Start a cap where the section's equivalent radius `sqrt(A / pi)` reaches the pole's height over the section's plane.
- Carry only attachment translation into a child's sizing.
- Under a reflection, reverse phase; under an affine change of station, rescale first and second derivatives by its first and second powers.
- Construct positive progress directly in a length law.
- Check what an existing landmark selects before reusing it.
