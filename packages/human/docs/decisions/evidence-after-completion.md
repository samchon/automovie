# Evidence waits until completion

## Decision

No `@evidence` tag is written in Human until the campaign declares completion; then it is applied once.

## Reasons

- Evidence checklists answer for stable code. During the rebuild, part generators, the junction and the rig are rewritten as renders teach, so per-commit answers would be rewritten with them and add review cost without protecting anything.
- In the basis era, evidence tags and issue comments held research that could not be recovered later (#2791). Research now lives in this workspace and in JSDoc, not in evidence answers.

## Consequences

- The build guard and lint run during the campaign; the evidence check for Human is suspended (#2790).
- Applying evidence once is a Mission 3 backlog item on #2788.
