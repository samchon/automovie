# Prahl, optical absorption of hemoglobin

Prahl S. "Optical Absorption of Hemoglobin." Oregon Medical Laser Center, compiled 1999 from several sources. [Overview](https://omlc.org/spectra/hemoglobin/) and [tabulated data](https://omlc.org/spectra/hemoglobin/summary.html).

## Conversion

μa(λ) = 2.303 e(λ) × 150 g/L / 64,500 g/mol = 0.0054 e(λ) cm⁻¹ for whole blood at 150 g Hb per litre, e in cm⁻¹/M.

## What Human takes

The spectral shape that makes blood red: absorption falls by two orders of magnitude from 576 to 650 nm, so red light penetrates and scatters widely while green and blue are absorbed near the surface. A precomputed (melanin, hemoglobin) colour table can be generated from these numbers and the melanin power law without shipping images.
