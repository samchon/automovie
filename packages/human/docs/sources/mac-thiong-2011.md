# Mac-Thiong et al. 2011, sacropelvic morphology in asymptomatic adults

Mac-Thiong J.-M., Roussouly P., Berthonnaud É., Guigui P. "Age- and sex-related variations in sagittal sacropelvic morphology and balance in asymptomatic adults." *European Spine Journal* 20(Suppl 5):572-577, 2011. Read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3175918 (methods and results).

## Licence

Springer copyright; numeric results cited.

## Population and protocol

709 asymptomatic white adults (354 men aged 37.9 +- 14.7, range 18 to 81; 355 women aged 35.7 +- 13.9, range 18 to 76), French and North American centres, 2000 to 2008. Standing lateral radiographs with hips and knees extended. Grade: measured.

## Values

| Parameter | Mean +- SD | Mean +- 2 SD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pelvic incidence (PI) | 52.6 +- 10.4 deg | 31.7 to 73.5 |
| Pelvic tilt (PT) | 13.0 +- 6.8 deg | -0.6 to 26.7 |
| Sacral slope (SS) | 39.6 +- 7.9 deg | 23.7 to 55.4 |
| PT / PI | 0.24 +- 0.11 | |

PI did not change with age (r = 0.03); PT rose slightly (r = 0.16) and SS fell slightly (r = -0.10). No sex difference in PI, PT or SS (mean differences under 0.5 deg). PI = PT + SS holds by geometry.

## What Human takes

The resting pelvic tilt and sacral slope of the standing skeleton and their spread: a fixed pelvic incidence per identity, with tilt as the posture variable that age shifts.
