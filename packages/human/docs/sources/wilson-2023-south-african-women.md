# Wilson 2023, young Black South African woman

Wilson SP, Medapati V, Segwapa K. "Facial Anthropometric Norms of the Young Black South African Woman." *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Global Open* 11(4):e4942, 2023, doi:10.1097/GOX.0000000000004942. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10146544/).

## Population and protocol

156 female students aged 18 to 25 (mean 21) at Sefako Makgatho University, normal occlusion and BMI 18.5 to 25 (mean 21.8), from nine South African groups (Pedi 40 percent, Tsonga 18, Tswana 15, Venda 12, Zulu 9, others). Computer-based photographic analysis in anterior, lateral and basal views. Grade: measured (photogrammetry, so depths are projections).

## What Human takes

Southern African female reference with angles, nostril-type frequencies and lip projection, which the 3D norms lack.

## Limits

Photographic; young women only.
