# Verkouteren 2022, mandibular range of motion in healthy children

Verkouteren DRC, de Sonnaville WFC, Zuithoff NPA, Wulffraat NM, Steenks MH, Rosenberg AJWP, et al. "Growth curves for mandibular range of motion and maximum voluntary bite force in healthy children." *European Journal of Oral Sciences* 130(3):e12869, 2022, doi:10.1111/eos.12869. Read as PMC9321901 (NCBI efetch).

## Population and protocol

169 healthy Dutch schoolchildren aged 6 to 18 (88 boys, 81 girls), Utrecht, followed longitudinally; metal ruler to the nearest millimetre. Opening is interincisal; protrusion adds overjet to the measured incisal distance; laterotrusion is referenced to the dental midlines. Grade: measured, with mixed-model growth curves (population-fitted).

## Values

| Overjet | 3.0 (1.5) | 2.9 (1.4) |

Model estimates: active opening 45.0 mm (boys) and 42.8 mm (girls) at 6, 55.3 and 50.5 mm at 18; laterotrusion 9.2 and 9.1 mm at 6, 10.4 and 10.3 mm at 18. Opening and protrusion flatten with age (quadratic terms).
