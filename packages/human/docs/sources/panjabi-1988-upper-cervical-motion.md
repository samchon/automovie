# Panjabi et al. 1988, upper cervical motion

Panjabi M., Dvorak J., Duranceau J., Yamamoto I., Gerber M., Rauschning W., Bueff H.U. "Three-dimensional movements of the upper cervical spine." *Spine* 13(7):726-730, 1988, doi:10.1097/00007632-198807000-00003. Read: the [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3194778/) only; the full article and White and Panjabi's 1990 handbook were not read.

## Population and protocol

Ten fresh cadaveric occiput-to-C7 specimens; incremental pure moments up to 1.5 Nm, applied separately over three loading cycles. Stereophotogrammetric motion on the third cycle. Grade: measured in vitro.

## Values

| Joint | Flexion | Extension | One-side lateral bending | One-side axial rotation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Occiput-C1 | 3.5 | 21.0 | 5.5 | 7.2 |
| C1-C2 | 11.5 | 10.9 | 6.7 | 38.9 |

All angles are degrees. These load-dependent cadaveric arcs do not establish active clinical limits or a whole-neck residual. The current atlanto-occipital -15 to 10 degree clamp differs from the reported sagittal endpoints.
