# Buryanov and Kotiuk 2010, proportions of hand segments

Buryanov A., Kotiuk V. "Proportions of hand segments." *International Journal of Morphology* 28(3), 2010. Read: the SciELO Chile full text (https://scielo.conicyt.cl/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0717-95022010000300015), its methods and Tables I to III (table images `art15_t1.jpg` to `art15_t3.jpg`).

## Licence

Open access, Creative Commons BY-NC 4.0. Numeric values are facts.

## Population and protocol

Anteroposterior X-rays of both hands of 66 adults aged 19 to 78 without bone pathology or deformity, taken to exclude fractures. A radiopaque ruler on every fifth patient checked scale. Measured: interarticular lengths of the phalanges and metacarpals (joint space to joint space), the soft tissue beyond the distal phalanx, the web heights, and segment lengths from the web, the joints and the tip. Left and right differ within 0.5 %, so right hands are reported. Grade: measured.

## Derived (modelling)

- Phalanx shares of the MCP-to-tip length, with the tip soft tissue counted with the distal phalanx: index 0.486, 0.274, 0.240; middle 0.483, 0.285, 0.231; ring 0.469, 0.291, 0.241; little 0.464, 0.257, 0.279; thumb proximal 0.536, distal with tip 0.464.
- MCP-to-tip lengths over the middle finger's: index 0.886, ring 0.956, little 0.764.
- PIP and DIP from the web, as shares of `de`: index 0.412, 0.722; middle 0.406, 0.737; ring 0.381, 0.718; little 0.341, 0.661.
- Treating interarticular lengths as joint-centre spacing is a modelling inference: each joint centre lies within the proximal bone's head, about one head radius behind the joint space.

## Limits

Radiographic lengths of one population (Ukraine), sexes pooled, ages 19 to 78. The authors attribute the spread of the web heights mainly to the fingers' abduction during the X-ray.
