# Rawal 2014, thyroid cartilage morphometry

Rawal JD, Kariya VB, Doshi BD, Jadav HR. "Morphometric Study of Thyroid Cartilage in Indian Cadavers." *National Journal of Integrated Research in Medicine* 5(3), 2014. Read the article page at https://nicpd.ac.in/ojs-/index.php/njirm/article/view/732 (abstract-level results via the page; the full PDF was not read).

## Licence

Open-access journal page. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

50 larynges from embalmed Indian cadavers, 10 of them female; dissection and caliper morphometry. Grade: measured.

## Values, mean (SD)

| Measure | Male | Female |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal angle between laminae | 74.40 (8.21) degrees | 92.35 (6.63) degrees |
| Lamina height | right 26.56 (2.88), left 26.60 (2.84) mm | 24.20 (3.89) mm |
| Lamina width | right 36.83 (3.60), left 37.07 (3.67) mm | 31.85 (3.69) mm |

## What Human takes

The laryngeal prominence follows from the lamina angle: a narrower male angle projects the anterior notch forward. The angle and lamina size give a sex-dependent prominence on the front of the neck instead of a free "Adam's apple" slider.

## Limits

Ten female larynges; one population; embalmed tissue.
