# Tang 2000, axial alignment of the lower extremity in Chinese adults

Tang W.M., Zhu Y.H., Chiu K.Y. "Axial alignment of the lower extremity in Chinese adults." *Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery, American Volume* 82(11):1603-1608, 2000. Read at second hand (abstract as quoted by search results); the full text was not read.

## Population and protocol

Weight-bearing full-length radiographs of 25 men and 25 women of southern Chinese origin, mean age about 24 and 23 years, compared with the white subjects of Moreland, Bassett and Hanker 1987 (*J Bone Joint Surg Am* 69:745-749) and of Hsu et al.

## Values (measured)

| Measure | Women | Men |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanical axis (hip-knee-ankle), degrees of varus, mean (SD) | 2.2 (2.5) | 2.2 (2.7) |

The Chinese subjects had a larger medial inclination of the knee joint line than Moreland's, and the women more varus alignment.

## Derived band

Mean plus and minus 2.326 SD of the wider sex: 4.1 degrees of valgus to 8.5 degrees of varus, a healthy young adult's hip-knee-ankle angle.

## Limits

Fifty young adults of one ancestry, read at second hand; Moreland's own mean for white adults is not recorded here because it was not read.
