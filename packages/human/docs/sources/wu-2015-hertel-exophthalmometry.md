# Wu 2015, Hertel exophthalmometry in a Chinese Han population

Wu D, Liu X, Wu D, Di X, Guan H, Shan Z, Teng W. "Normal Values of Hertel Exophthalmometry in a Chinese Han Population from Shenyang, Northeast China." *Scientific Reports* 5:8526, 2015, doi:10.1038/srep08526. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4336939/). Read: the abstract, the methods and Table 2 as the open-access page gives them.

## Licence

Open-access article. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

2,010 healthy Han Chinese aged 8 to 87 in Shenyang (1,051 women, 959 men). One physician took every reading with a Keeler Hertel exophthalmometer: the distance, in millimetres, from the lateral orbital rim, where the instrument rests, to the corneal apex. Grade: measured.

## Values, mean (SD) mm, whole cohort

| Eye | Women | Men |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | 14.9 (2.0) | 15.0 (2.0) |
| Right | 15.0 (1.9) | 15.1 (1.9) |

The paper reports no sex difference. The upper limits of normal, mean plus 2 SD over the whole population, are 18.8 mm (left) and 19.0 mm (right). A difference between the two eyes of more than 2 mm is called notable; up to 2 mm is of little clinical significance.

## Use here

A bound on how far the globe's corneal apex may stand in front of the lateral orbital rim, and on the difference between the two eyes. The lateral orbital rim is bone: on the skin it is read at the exocanthion's level, about where the instrument rests, so the bound is a cap, not a landmark.

## Limits

One ancestry; limits for other ancestries differ and were not read here. The reading is a clinical device reading, not a surface landmark distance.
