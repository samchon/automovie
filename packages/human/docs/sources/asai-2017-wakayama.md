# Asai et al. 2017, sagittal spino-pelvic alignment (Wakayama Spine Study)

Asai Y., Tsutsui S., Oka H., Yoshimura N., Hashizume H., Yamada H., Akune T., Muraki S., Matsudaira K., Kawaguchi H., Nakamura K., Tanaka S., Yoshida M. "Sagittal spino-pelvic alignment in adults: The Wakayama Spine Study." *PLoS ONE* 12(6):e0178697, 2017. Read: the PLoS manuscript XML (methods, Tables 1 to 5), https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0178697.

## Population and protocol

1,461 community residents of two Japanese towns (466 men, 995 women; mean age 65.6, height 164.7 cm men and 151.8 cm women). Standing lateral whole-spine radiographs, hips and knees extended, hands on supports at shoulder level. TK: Cobb angle from the upper endplate of T2 to the lower endplate of T12. LL: L1 upper endplate to S1. PT: line from the femoral head axis to the sacral plate midpoint against the vertical. PI. SVA: horizontal distance from the C7 plumb line (centre of the C7 body) to the posterior superior corner of S1. Grade: measured.

SDs are large (SVA 23 to 58 mm). TK larger in men, LL, PT and PI larger in women. Age raised SVA, TK and PI-LL in a multiple regression adjusted for BMI.

## What Human takes

Adult pelvic tilt, sacral slope (PI - PT), thoracic kyphosis and SVA by age and sex. Japanese sample; no European sample with age groups was read.
