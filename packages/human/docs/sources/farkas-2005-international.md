# Farkas 2005, international anthropometric study of facial morphology

Farkas LG, Katic MJ, Forrest CR, et al. "International anthropometric study of facial morphology in various ethnic groups/races." *Journal of Craniofacial Surgery* 16(4):615-646, 2005, doi:10.1097/01.scs.0000171847.58031.9e. Only the abstract was read (Europe PMC REST API). The full text and its tables were not accessible.

## Population and protocol (abstract)

1,470 healthy adults aged 18 to 30 (750 men, 720 women) from Europe (780), the Middle East (180), Asia (300) and African origin (210), measured directly by trained local investigators; 14 measurements, 10 of them those used by Leonardo and Dürer, compared with North American white norms.

## Findings (abstract)

- Forehead height, mouth width and ear height were identical to North American whites in 99.7 percent of comparisons in both sexes.
- The orbital region varied most.
- The nose was "very or extremely significantly wide" in Asian and Black groups of both sexes; Middle Eastern noses matched in width but were taller.
