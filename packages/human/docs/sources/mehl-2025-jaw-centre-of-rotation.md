# Mehl 2025, centres of rotation during jaw movement

Mehl AC, Woodford SC, Robinson DL, Abduo J, Lee PVS, Ackland DC. "Location of centers of rotation and existence of terminal hinge axes during jaw movements - a preliminary in vivo clarification." *Clinical Oral Investigations* 29:530, 2025, doi:10.1007/s00784-025-06619-4. Read as PMC12553608 (NCBI efetch).

## Population and protocol

Eight healthy adults with nearly full dentition and no TMJ history (University of Melbourne). Rigid tracking plates on both dental arches, four-camera Vicon optical motion capture, registered to CT-segmented bone; five cycles each of maximum opening, protrusion and lateral excursions. Centre of rotation by the Reuleaux method (incisal point and chin point). Grade: measured on a small sample.

## Findings

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Centre of rotation for full opening, below the condyle centre | 32.4 (7.4) mm, range 19.8 to 44.8 |
| Same, posterior to the condyle centre | 0.2 (10.8) mm, range -19.0 to 10.9 |
| Theoretical centre from condyle traces | 38.0 (10.1) mm below, 0.7 (11.9) mm posterior |
| Condylar translation per rotation, full opening | 5.51 (1.05) mm per 10 degrees |
| First 10 mm of incisal opening | about 7 degrees of rotation with at least 3 mm of condylar translation |
| Translation of the centre of rotation itself over the full cycle | never above 4 mm |

The relation between condylar rotation and translation is close to linear over the whole opening, with a slight sigmoid near maximum opening in some subjects. No subject showed pure rotation in early opening. Centres of rotation lie near the occlusal plane (coordinates in an occlusal-plane frame with the incisal point at the origin).

## Limits

Eight subjects; no sex or age split.
