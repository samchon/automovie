# Bekerman 2014, eyeball diameters of healthy adults

Bekerman I, Gottlieb P, Vaiman M. "Variations in eyeball diameters of the healthy adults." *Journal of Ophthalmology* 2014:503645, doi:10.1155/2014/503645. Read as PMC4238270 (NCBI efetch).

## Population and protocol

CT scans (Philips Brilliance iCT 256, 3 mm slices) of 250 consecutive adults with healthy eyes at Assaf Harofeh Medical Center, Israel, of several ethnic backgrounds; sagittal, transverse and axial diameters of both globes and orbit size. Grade: measured.

## Values (Table 1, median ± SD, mm, right / left)

| Measure | Right | Left |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transverse, sclera to sclera | 24.16 ± 1.9 | 24.32 ± 1.9 |
| Sagittal (vertical), sclera to sclera | 23.80 ± 1.6 | 23.75 ± 1.7 |
| Axial, myopic | 24.48 ± 1.8 | 24.89 ± 2.2 |
| Axial, emmetropic | 23.42 ± 1.9 | 23.56 ± 1.9 |
| Axial, hypermetropic | 22.31 ± 2.2 | 22.10 ± 1.9 |
| Orbital margin height | 41.08 ± 2.4 | 42.55 ± 2.5 |
| Orbital margin width | 35.33 ± 2.2 | 35.86 ± 2.2 |
| Orbit depth, cornea to optic canal | 47.73 ± 4.6 | 48.40 ± 4.7 |

The abstract concludes "approximately 24.2 mm (transverse) × 23.7 mm (sagittal) × 22.0-24.8 mm (axial)", transverse range 21 to 27 mm, with no significant difference by sex, age or the ethnic backgrounds studied; transverse diameter correlates with orbit width (r = 0.88). Myopia and hypermetropia change only the axial diameter.
