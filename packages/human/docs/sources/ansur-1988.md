## Population and protocol

2,208 women and 1,774 men of the working database, mean age 26.2 and 27.2 (17 to about 50). Millimetres, standing erect, heels together, at the maximum point of quiet respiration. Weight is in tenths of a kilogram.

Natural waist landmark: "Level of the greatest indentation on the right side of the torso, or half the distance between 10th rib and Iliocristale if no single indentation is clear." Waist circumference (natural indentation): horizontal, over the right and left natural waist landmarks. Waist height (natural indentation): standing surface to the right natural waist landmark. Omphalion waist: the level of the centre of the navel, as in ANSUR II.
