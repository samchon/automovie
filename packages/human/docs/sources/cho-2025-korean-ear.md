# Cho 2025, external ear of Korean adults

Cho E, Won JY, Park MH, Oh JH, Lee IW, Choung YH, Cho YS, Han GC, Lee HJ. "Anthropometry of the External Ear in Korean Adults: A Multicenter Study." *Journal of Korean Medical Science* 40(22):e107, 2025, doi:10.3346/jkms.2025.40.e107. Read as PMC12148551 (NCBI efetch).

## Population and protocol

631 Korean adults (331 men, 300 women), 20 to 92 years, roughly 50 per sex per decade, from facial 3D CT taken for other reasons at six hospitals; soft-tissue landmarks on CT reconstructions. Grade: measured.

Ear length by age (left ear): men 63.9 mm at 20-29 to 69.5 mm at 70+; women 60.1 mm to 68.1 mm. All length measures correlate positively with age (strongest for insertion length, R = 0.354).

Lobule types by the angle between the auricle base and the line through the superior and inferior otobasion: tapering (69 degrees or less) 1.5 percent of men and 1.6 percent of women, square (70 to 109) 25.2 and 18.8 percent, pendulate (110 or more) 73.3 and 79.6 percent.

The introduction cites a Korean study in which ear length and width reach 90 percent of adult size at 14 to 15 years, against 9 to 12 years in Western populations.

## Limits

CT patients rather than a random sample; large SDs suggest landmark variability.
