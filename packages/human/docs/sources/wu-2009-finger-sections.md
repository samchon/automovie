# Wu et al. 2009, external finger-section model

Wu J.Z., Dong R.G., McDowell T.W., Welcome D.E. "Modeling the finger joint moments in a hand at the maximal isometric grip: The effects of friction." *Medical Engineering & Physics* 31:1214-1218, doi:10.1016/j.medengphy.2009.07.018. Sections 2.1 and 2.2 and Table 1 in the [CDC author paper](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/187758/cdc_187758_DS1.pdf).

Table 1 gives external cylindrical proxies based on Buchholz's model for an adult unisex hand, breadth 77 mm and length 177 mm. Human uses the proximal breadth/depth ratios as derived model dimensions, not measured shaft stations or subject predictions. The underlying regressions and their population are secondary evidence here. Age and population extrapolation are unvalidated; these proxies supply conditional model proportions, not measured proximal finger sections.
