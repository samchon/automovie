# Leerunyakul 2020, hair density and diameter in Thai adults

Leerunyakul K, Suchonwanit P. "Evaluation of Hair Density and Hair Diameter in the Adult Thai Population Using Quantitative Trichoscopic Analysis." *BioMed Research International* 2020:2476890, doi:10.1155/2020/2476890. Read as PMC7035527 (NCBI efetch).

## Population and protocol

239 healthy Thai adults (79 men, 160 women), Ramathibodi Hospital, Bangkok, 2017 to 2018, quantitative trichoscopy at four scalp sites. Grade: measured. Table 4 quotes Caucasian, African and Hispanic densities from another trichoscopy study (secondary).

## Values, mean (SD)

| Site | Density (hairs/cm²) | Diameter (μm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal | 154.3 (12.8) | 81.1 (6.9) |
| Vertex | 162.9 (15.7) | 80.8 (5.9) |
| Temporoparietal | 133.7 (14.6) | 80.1 (4.5) |
| Occipital | 160.2 (15.3) | 80.3 (5.1) |

No sex difference. Density falls with age (frontal 156.2 in the 20s to 146.7 in the 60s) while diameter stays near 80 μm.

Quoted comparison (hairs/cm²): Caucasian frontal 230 (33), vertex 226 (20), occipital 214 (28); African 160 (27), 149 (23), 148 (25); Hispanic 174 (32), 178 (33), 169 (31).
