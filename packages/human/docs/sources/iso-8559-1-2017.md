# ISO 8559-1:2017, anthropometric definitions for body measurement

ISO 8559-1:2017(E), *Size designation of clothes — Part 1: Anthropometric definitions for body measurement*, first edition 2017-03, ISO/TC 133; it cancels and replaces ISO 3635:1981 and ISO 8559:1989. Read: the publisher's preview (cover, contents and foreword), https://webstore.ansi.org/preview-pages/ISO/preview_ISO+8559-1-2017.pdf. The clause text is sold and was not read; the definitions below come from a measurement vendor's transcription that cites the clause numbers, https://developers.bodygram.com/measurements/definitions/ and, for the trunk and neck, https://docs.bodygram.com/platform/measurement-definitions. ## Definitions (transcribed)

- Thigh girth (5.3.20): "Horizontal circumference of the widest part of the thigh below the gluteal fold, right side."
- Mid-thigh girth (5.3.21): horizontal, midway between the inside leg level and the centre point of the kneecap.
- Knee girth (5.3.22): "Horizontal circumference of the knee at the level of the center point of kneecap (center of patella when the thigh muscles are relaxed), right side."
- Calf girth (5.3.24): "Horizontal circumference of the widest part of the calf, right side."

### Trunk and neck

| Clause | Measurement | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5.3.2 | Neck girth | "Circumference of the neck at a point just below the bulge at the thyroid cartilage (Adam's apple) and measured perpendicular to the longitudinal axis of the neck." |
| 5.3.3 | Neck base girth | "Circumference of the base of the neck measured over back neck point (seventh cervical vertebra) and the front neck point (crossing point of the line connecting medial superior borders of the left and right clavicles and front median line)." |
| 5.3.4 | Bust girth | "Horizontal circumference measured at bust point level (most anterior point of the bust when wearing bra)." |
| 5.3.8 | Under bust girth | "Horizontal circumference of the body at the under bust level (directly below breast when wearing a bra)." |
| 5.3.10 | Waist girth | "Horizontal circumference of the body measured at the waist level (midway between the lowest rib point and the highest point of the hip bone at the side of the body)." |
| 5.3.12 | Top hip girth | "Horizontal circumference of the body measured at the top hip level (midway between the waist level and hip level)." |
| 5.3.13 | Hip girth | "Horizontal circumference of the body measured at the hip level (around the buttocks)"; elsewhere the hip level is the "greatest projection of buttock". |

Every trunk girth is horizontal; the neck girth is perpendicular to the neck's axis, and the neck base girth passes over two median landmarks and the side of the neck, so it is not planar. The waist level is set by bony landmarks (the lowest rib and the iliac crest) rather than by the narrowest girth, which ISO 8559:1989 and ASTM D5219 used.

## Limits

The transcription is not the standard. Section 4.1.4 (posture) and the figures were not read, so the stance (feet apart or together) and how the gluteal fold and the kneecap's centre are found are unverified. All limb girths are horizontal, unlike ANSUR II's thigh circumference, which is perpendicular to the thigh axis at the gluteal furrow. The garment-oriented wording ("when wearing bra") places the bust and under-bust levels on a supported breast; a bare skin's breast sits lower.
