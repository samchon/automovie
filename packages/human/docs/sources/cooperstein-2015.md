# Cooperstein et al. 2015 and 2017, scapular angle and iliac crest levels

Cooperstein R., Haneline M., Young M. "The location of the inferior angle of the scapula in relation to the spine in the upright position: a systematic review of the literature and meta-analysis." *Chiropractic & Manual Therapies*, 2015 (PMC4343265, CC BY). Cooperstein R., Truong F. "Systematic review and meta-analyses of the difference between the spinal level of the palpated and imaged iliac crests," 2017 (PMC5596973). Read: both abstracts, via the Europe PMC API.

## Values

- Pooling five imaging-referenced studies, the upright inferior scapular angle aligns on average with the T8 spinous process, range T4 to T11, not the conventional T7. Grade: measured, meta-analysed.
- Across 31 studies, imaged iliac crests lie nearest the L4-5 interspace in women and the L4 spinous process in men; palpated crests lie 0.7 (men) to 1.0 (women) levels higher, because the examiner's fingers meet the soft tissue above the crest.
