# Lee 2025, averageness and femininity predict attractiveness

Lee P, Li J, Rafiee Y, Jones BC, Shiramizu VKM. "Further evidence that averageness and femininity, rather than symmetry and masculinity, predict facial attractiveness judgments." *Scientific Reports* 15:5498, 2025, doi:10.1038/s41598-025-86974-0. Read as PMC11828934 (NCBI efetch, abstract and introduction).

## Protocol and findings

Shape asymmetry, distinctiveness (the converse of averageness) and sexual dimorphism were measured objectively from natural images of white men and women and entered together in linear mixed models of attractiveness ratings. Attractiveness was predicted by averageness in both sexes and by femininity in female faces; masculinity in male faces and symmetry did not predict it independently. The introduction notes that effects found by manipulating face images shrink or vanish when natural faces are measured, and that earlier studies of Japanese, Vietnamese and ethnically diverse faces agree on averageness but differ on symmetry and dimorphism.
