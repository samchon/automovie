# White et al. 2018, passive thumb CMC motion

White J., Coppola L., Skomurski A., Mitchell-Rekrut E. "Influence of age and gender on normative passive range of motion values of the carpometacarpal joint of the thumb." *Journal of Hand Therapy* 31(3):390-397, 2018, doi:10.1016/j.jht.2017.02.011. Read: the [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28478845/) only.

## Population and protocol

Ninety-six healthy adults, equal gender groups in four age bands: 20-34, 35-49, 50-64 and 65+. Three passive dominant-hand measurements with six-inch universal goniometers. Grade: measured; the abstract does not give the complete positioning and axis protocol.

## Values

| Motion | Mean (SD), degrees |
| --- | --- |
| Flexion | 21.7 (6.8) |
| Extension | 19.5 (5.7) |
| Abduction | 51.1 (5.5) |

These means differ from the current -20 to 15 degree flexion and 0 to 60 degree abduction clamps. They are not individual maxima. Full protocol and frame compatibility need confirmation before replacement; the abstract supplies no opposition angle.
