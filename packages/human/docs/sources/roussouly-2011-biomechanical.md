# Roussouly and Pinheiro-Franco 2011, sagittal parameters of the spine

Roussouly P., Pinheiro-Franco J.L. "Sagittal parameters of the spine: biomechanical approach." *European Spine Journal* 20(Suppl 5):578-585, 2011. Read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3175922.

## Licence

Springer, open access via PMC. Statements and numbers cited.

## Statements used

- Lordosis is split at its apex, "the most anterior point of the lumbar spine", into an inferior arc from S1 to the apex and an upper arc from the apex to the inflexion point; the inferior arc equals the sacral slope.
- The upper lordotic arc is about 20 degrees "whatever the shape of the spine"; lumbar lordosis is about SS + 20 (R = 0.86).
- About two thirds of the lordosis lies from L4 to S1 (Barrey, as cited).
- The inflexion point is classically near T12-L1.
- "The top of thoracic curve on C7 is very stable over the sacrum": spinal tilt 90.8 +- 3.4 degrees and spino-sacral angle 130.4 +- 8.1 in 709 asymptomatic adults (Mac-Thiong et al., as cited).

Grade: established biomechanical account with cited measurements.
