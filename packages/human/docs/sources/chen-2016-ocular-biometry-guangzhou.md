# Chen 2016, axial length and corneal curvature in South China

Chen H, Lin H, Lin Z, Chen J, Chen W. "Distribution of axial length, anterior chamber depth, and corneal curvature in an aged population in South China." *BMC Ophthalmology* 16:47, 2016, doi:10.1186/s12886-016-0221-5. Read as PMC4852406 (NCBI efetch).

## Licence

CC BY 4.0. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

1,015 phakic right eyes of residents of Guangzhou aged 50 or older, IOLMaster partial coherence interferometry and automated keratometry. Grade: measured.

## Values

| Measure | All | Men | Women |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Axial length (mm) | 23.48 | 23.68 | 23.23 |
| Anterior chamber depth (mm) | 3.03 | 3.13 | 2.95 |
| Mean keratometry K (D) | 44.20 | 43.85 | 44.50 |

Corneal radius from keratometric power (derived, standard keratometric index 1.3375): r = 337.5 / K mm, giving 7.64 mm overall, 7.70 mm for men and 7.58 mm for women.

## Limits

Older adults; anterior chamber depth falls with age in men.
