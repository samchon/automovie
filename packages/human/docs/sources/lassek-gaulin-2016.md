# Lassek and Gaulin 2016, waist and hip size in female attractiveness

Lassek W.D., Gaulin S.J.C. "What Makes Jessica Rabbit Sexy? Contrasting Roles of Waist and Hip Size." *Evolutionary Psychology* 14(2), 2016 (PMC10481102). Read in full via the Europe PMC full-text API, including Tables 1 to 6.

## Population and protocol

Three samples: 106 US college women with 3D body measurements and attractiveness ratings (scale 1 to 10); Playboy Playmates of the Month (n = 665 for measures, with BMI, stature, bust, waist and hip as published); 266 images of imaginary women (cartoon, game, comic) chosen as most attractive by students, measured in 2D. Grade: measured (college women, Playmates' published measures) and image-measured (imaginary women).

In college women, attractiveness correlated with waist/stature -0.73, BMI -0.67, hip/stature -0.63, thigh/stature -0.73, arm/stature -0.62, WHR -0.52; with waist/stature in a regression, neither BMI nor WHR added significantly. The authors conclude waist size is the key cue and accounts for the BMI and WHR effects; hip size relative to stature varies little across attractiveness groups. The introduction summarises earlier work: women with WHR 0.60 to 0.70 are most consistently rated highly attractive and above 0.85 less so (citing Singh 1993 and others), and men in well-nourished populations prefer BMI 15 to 20.
