# Wu 2015, Hertel exophthalmometry in Han Chinese

Wu D, Liu X, Wu D, Di X, Guan H, Shan Z, Teng W. "Normal values of Hertel exophthalmometry in a Chinese Han population from Shenyang, Northeast China." *Scientific Reports* 5:8526, 2015, doi:10.1038/srep08526. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4336939/).

## Licence

CC BY 4.0.

## Population and protocol

2,010 healthy Han Chinese (1,051 female, 959 male), 8 to 87 years, Shenyang. Hertel exophthalmometer: distance from the lateral orbital rim to the corneal apex. Grade: measured.

## Values, mean (SD) mm

| Group | n | Age | Exophthalmometric value | Lateral orbital rim distance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Children | 515 | 10.6 | 13.7 (1.6) | 100.2 (5.0) |
| Teenagers | 517 | 16.7 | 15.1 (1.5) | 108.5 (3.9) |
| Adults | 582 | 39.1 | 15.7 (1.8) | 111.0 (4.0) |
| Elderly | 396 | 76.6 | 15.3 (2.2) | 110.8 (4.8) |

No sex difference in this sample. Left-right difference is at most 2 mm. The discussion cites a general pattern: protrusion grows over the first two decades, holds or declines slightly in adulthood and declines from the seventh decade.

## Limits

Northern Chinese only; the paper notes Taiwanese and Hong Kong studies differ.
