# Jung and Yun 2014, total hairline correction in women

Jung JH, Yun IS. "Total Hairline Correction in Female Patient." *Archives of Aesthetic Plastic Surgery* 20(1):44-51, 2014, doi:10.14730/aaps.2014.20.1.44. [Full text](https://e-aaps.org/journal/view.php?number=135).

## Protocol

Hairline correction by follicular unit transplantation in Korean women, with the design rules and per-point corrections measured on the patients. Grade: conventional design rules with measured patient values; whether distances are straight or along the skin is not stated.

## Values

- Forehead height: designed 5 to 7 mm longer than the lower third of the face (subnasale to menton), measured from the mid-brow, a ratio of thirds near 1 : 1 : 0.9. Average Korean women's forehead (mid-brow to the base of a widow's peak): 6.38 cm.
- Temporal point: on a line perpendicular to the outer margin of the lateral orbital rim, at least 2 cm from the brow's lateral end; after Nusbaum, 1 cm from the vertical through the lateral canthus. Distance between the two temporal points: 14.3 cm. Infratemporal apex to lateral canthus: 4.29 cm.
- The temporal recession's apex aligns with the lateral canthus in the frontal view.
- Transition zone: about 1 cm wide, 50 to 60 hairs/cm2 in single-hair units; mid zone 1.5 to 2 cm of two-hair units at 30 to 40 units/cm2; apex zone 20 to 25 units/cm2; sideburns under 20/cm2.
- Exit angle rising from the sideburn to the parting: about 15 to 20 degrees to the temporal point, 20 to 30 to the supratemporal line, 30 to 45 at the lateral frontal line, 45 to 60 at the central frontal line, 60 to 90 near the parting.
- Hair calibre 60 to 80 um.

## Limits

Korean women seeking correction; design targets rather than a population survey.
