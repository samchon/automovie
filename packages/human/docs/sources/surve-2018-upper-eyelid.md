# Surve 2018, upper-lid ultrasound thickness

Surve A, Meel R, Pushker N, Bajaj MS. "Ultrasound biomicroscopy image patterns in normal upper eyelid and congenital ptosis in Indian population." *Indian Journal of Ophthalmology* 66:383-388, 2018. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.4103/ijo.IJO_915_17), [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5859591/). CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Grade: measured, site-specific.

## Population and protocol

The complete article body and Table 1 image were directly read. Of 79 lids, 19 were normal; their mean age was 24.79+/-6.98 years and the male count was 15. The table counts lids, so these are not asserted to be 19 independent people. A single operator repeated 50 MHz UBM scans three times. Subjects lay supine, the imaged lid closed and the opposite eye fixing the ceiling in primary gaze; the vertical scan passed through the corneal centre.

## Definition and limit

Normal full-lid thickness was 1.612+/-0.205 mm immediately above the upper tarsal border. Tarsal thickness, 0.907+/-0.098 mm, was measured midway between its borders. Different measurement sites cannot be summed into a margin thickness. This is a candidate upper transition-section datum for HEAD's eye construction, not a measured free-margin thickness or open-lid globe clearance. Small sample size, scleral-cup placement and tissue ultrasound velocity limit transfer.
