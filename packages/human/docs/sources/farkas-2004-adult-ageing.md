# Farkas 2004, facial framework in adulthood

Farkas LG, Eiben OG, Sivkov S, Tompson B, Katic MJ, Forrest CR. "Anthropometric measurements of the facial framework in adulthood: age-related changes in eight age categories in 600 healthy white North Americans of European ancestry from 16 to 90 years of age." *Journal of Craniofacial Surgery* 15(2):288-298, 2004, doi:10.1097/00001665-200403000-00027. Read: the abstract only.

## Population and protocol (abstract)

261 men and 339 women of European ancestry, 16 to 90, in 10-year categories; four horizontal and one vertical framework measurements on skin and bone, and the soft-tissue thickness between them. Grade: measured.

## Findings (abstract)

- Forehead width increased from 16-20 to early adulthood.
- In late adulthood (71-90) upper and lower jaw measures mostly increased on skin and bone.
- Face width was the most stable measurement and had the thinnest soft-tissue cover.
- Soft tissue was thickest over the midface and thinned moderately with age; over the lower jaw it thickened in early adulthood and thinned in late adulthood.
