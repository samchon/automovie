# Zárate-Tejero et al. 2024, craniovertebral angle reference values

Zárate-Tejero C.A., Rodríguez-Rubio P.R., Brandt L., Krauss J., Hernández-Secorún M., Hidalgo-García C., Lucha-López O. "Measuring Craniovertebral Angle Reference Values in Adults Using Kinovea Software." *Applied Sciences* 14(19):8639, 2024, doi:10.3390/app14198639. Read: the full PDF from the University of Zaragoza repository (https://zaguan.unizar.es/record/145251).

## Population and protocol

122 healthy adults, 61 men and 61 women, aged 18 to 75 (mean 45.16, SD 16.76), about ten per sex per age group (18-25 to 66-75). Standing lateral photographs with a marker on the C7 spinous process; the craniovertebral angle is between the horizontal and the line from C7 to the tragus. Grade: measured.

## Values

Mean 48.76 (SD 6.77); men 47.46 (6.71), women 50.07 (6.63); adjusted sex difference -2.46. Age: -1.92 degrees a decade unadjusted, -1.6 (95% CI 0.93 to 2.27) adjusted for sex and medication.

## What Human takes

The head's carriage in front of the neck by age and sex: the tragus stands where the line from C7 rises at this angle.
