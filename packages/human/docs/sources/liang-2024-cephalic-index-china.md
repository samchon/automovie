# Liang 2024, cephalic index of Chinese children

Liang Q, Shen Z, Sun S, Gong J, Bao N. "Cephalic index and horizontal point of maximum width in children with normal brain development in China." *BMC Pediatrics* 24:685, 2024, doi:10.1186/s12887-024-05173-4. PMC11520368.

## Population and protocol

456 Han Chinese children (257 male, 199 female), 0 to 15 years, with normal brain development, from three hospitals (2015 to 2020); cephalic index (CI = maximum width / maximum length × 100) and the relative height of the point of maximum width (H-PMW) on thin-slice brain CT. Grade: measured.

## Limits

Hospital CT population; bony or soft-tissue CT measurement differs from caliper CI.
