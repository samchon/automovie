## Limits

The standard takes no elbow, wrist, knee or ankle bone breadth and no standing cervicale height; those come from ISAK, NHANES III, ANSUR 1988 and ANSUR II. Numbers of the 1996 edition (ISO 7250:1996, 4.x) differ from these 2017 numbers.
