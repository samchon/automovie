## Population and protocol

NHANES 1999 to 2004 whole-body DXA (Hologic fan-beam scanners), US population sample, ages 8 to 85, by sex and ethnicity (White, Black, Mexican American); LMS reference curves. Grade: measured, population-fitted (LMS). Values below are medians for non-Hispanic White adults.
