# Brito 2020, evidence-based nasal analysis (10-7-5)

Brito ÍM, Avashia Y, Rohrich RJ. "Evidence-based Nasal Analysis for Rhinoplasty: The 10-7-5 Method." *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Global Open* 8(2):e2632, 2020, doi:10.1097/GOX.0000000000002632. Read as PMC7159929 (NCBI efetch).

## Stated ideals

| Item | Ideal as stated |
| --- | --- |
| Nasofrontal angle | Lines tangent to glabella and dorsum meeting at nasion; the radix lies between the superior lash line and the supratarsal crease, nasion about 15 mm anterior to the medial canthus |
| Nasal length | Nasion to tip-defining points equals two-thirds of midface height, or stomion-menton |
| Tip projection | 50 to 60 percent of the tip in front of a vertical line at the upper lip, or 0.67 times the ideal nasal length |
| Tip rotation (nasolabial angle) | about 90 to 95 degrees in men, 95 to 110 in women |
| Tip | Divergence angle 30 degrees, domal arc 4 mm or less, tip-defining points 5 to 6 mm apart; broader and less defined in men |
| Alar base width | about the intercanthal distance, one-fifth of face width, or 70 percent of nasal height |
