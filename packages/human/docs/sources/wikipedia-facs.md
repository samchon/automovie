## Content used

- Origin: based on Hjortsjö's work, published by Ekman and Friesen 1978, revised 2002.
- Intensity letters A (trace) to E (maximum) appended to an AU number.
- AU table with names and muscular basis: 1 to 28 main codes, 29 to 46 gross behaviour and eye codes, 51 to 58 and M-codes for head position, 61 to 69 for eye position, 70 to 74 visibility codes.
- EMFACS combinations: happiness 6+12; sadness 1+4+15; surprise 1+2+5B+26; fear 1+2+4+5+7+20+26; anger 4+5+7+23; disgust 9+15+17; contempt R12A+R14A.

Grade: conventional (a coding standard).
