# Zhang 2026, ideal anterior hairline in Chinese women

Zhang Y, Tang D, Zhang L, Wu J, Cheng F, Cheng H. "Defining ideal anterior hairline contour and hair-bearing proportion via a morphometric analysis in Chinese women." *Frontiers in Surgery* 13:1946150, 2026, doi:10.3389/fsurg.2026.1946150. ## Protocol

90 raters (mean age 28) in Chengdu scored one young woman's photograph edited to five hairline contours and adjusted the hair-bearing region to their preference. Grade: measured preference.

## Contour classes (after Suzan et al.)

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Round | Smooth concave contour, no distinct frontotemporal recession |
| M-shaped | Marked frontotemporal recession, lateral frontal line raised, frontotemporal angle usually under 90 degrees |
| Rectangular | Horizontal frontal margin, mild recession, frontotemporal angle 90 degrees or more |
| Bell-shaped | Normal forehead width, midpoint about 2 cm higher than the population average |
| Triangular | Nearly straight line descending from the central forehead to the temples, with vellus hairs along the edge |

Round, triangular and M-shaped were preferred over rectangular and bell-shaped. Preferred height ratio (hair-bearing height over trichion-glabella forehead height) 0.505 (IQR 0.455 to 0.584); width ratio 0.373 (IQR 0.356 to 0.387).
