# Schectman et al. 2005, maximal angle of duction by age

Schectman D., Shallo-Hoffmann J., Rumsey J., Riordan-Eva P., Hardigan P.C. "Maximal angle of duction during visual fixation as a function of age." *Strabismus* 13(1):21-26, 2005. [NSUWorks abstract](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/hpd_com_faculty_articles/397), abstract-level evidence.

## Population and protocol

204 healthy volunteers with best-corrected acuity; a modified arc perimeter showed letter targets to the dominant eye with the head on a bite bar; targets stepped out in 5 deg then bracketed in 1 deg steps; the threshold was 75% correct. Grade: measured.

## Values

Maximum duction fell with age in all four directions (p < 0.001), with a step decline from the sixth decade. From 14-19 to 80-95 years: abduction -21%, adduction -24%, supraduction -35%, infraduction -26%. Absolute angles are not in the abstract.

## What Human takes

Relative age effects only; without absolute angles, this source does not calibrate the engine's conventional ±30 vertical and ±45 horizontal eye ranges.
