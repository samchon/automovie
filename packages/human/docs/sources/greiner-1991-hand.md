# Greiner 1991, hand anthropometry of US Army personnel

Greiner T.M. *Hand Anthropometry of U.S. Army Personnel*, Technical Report NATICK/TR-92/011, US Army Natick Research, Development and Engineering Center, December 1991 (DTIC ADA244533). Read: the text layer of the DTIC scan (measurement and landmark definitions, summary statistics of digits 1 to 5 and measured hand length).

## Licence

Approved for public release, distribution unlimited; a US Government work.

## Population and protocol

Hands of the 1987-1988 US Army anthropometric survey: 1,304 women and 1,003 men, matched to the working databases. Hand photographs digitized; digit length runs from the fingertip to the centre of the proximal flexion crease at the finger's base (for the thumb, the proximal flexion crease of the metacarpophalangeal joint); hand length measured directly. Grade: measured.
