# Park 2017, eyebrow position and movement with ageing

Park J, Yun S, Son D. "Changes in Eyebrow Position and Movement with Aging." *Archives of Plastic Surgery* 44(1):65-71, 2017, doi:10.5999/aps.2017.44.1.65. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5300927/).

## Population and protocol

Korean women, 20 aged 20 to 30 (mean 24.8) and 20 aged 50 to 70 (mean 55.8), Keimyung University. Photogrammetry scaled by a 10 mm ruler; height of the upper brow margin above the horizontal line through both medial canthi at four verticals: medial canthus (MC), lateral limbus (LL), lateral canthus (LC) and the brow's lateral end point (EP). Six actions. Grade: measured.

## Values, mm above the intercanthal line

| Action | Group | MC | LL | LC | EP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eyes opened smoothly (reference) | young | 26.36 | 31.02 | 28.37 | 21.61 |
| Eyes opened maximally | young | 31.28 | 34.88 | 31.62 | 23.43 |
| Maximal frown | young | 23.81 | 26.20 | 25.07 | 19.89 |
| Eyes closed maximally | young | 21.35 | 24.31 | 23.07 | 18.52 |
| Eyes opened smoothly | older | 24.77 | 26.98 | 24.70 | 18.07 |
| Eyes opened maximally | older | 26.96 | 29.55 | 26.65 | 18.63 |
| Eyes closed maximally | older | 21.20 | 22.85 | 21.64 | 16.47 |
| Range, highest to lowest | young | 9.93 | 10.57 | 8.55 | 4.91 |
| Range, highest to lowest | older | 5.76 | 6.70 | 5.01 | 2.55 |

The brow peak is at the lateral limbus in every action. The lateral end moves about half as far as the rest.

## What Human takes

Brow arch shape (peak over the lateral limbus), brow travel for AU1, AU2 and AU4 by brow point, and the age effect: lower brows and roughly 40 percent less travel at 50 to 70.
