## Allowable errors (Tables 28, 30, 31), mm

Chapter VII: the 1988 ANSUR "set allowable error rates based on the mean of absolute differences", and "Those values have been incorporated into an international standard as well (ISO 20685:2010)". ANSUR II's allowable errors: stature 6, crotch height 10, lateral malleolus height 2, acromion-radiale length 4, radiale-stylion length 6, shoulder-elbow length 6, shoulder length 3, Interscye I 10, Interscye II 13, waist back length 5, waist front length sitting 7, biacromial breadth 8, bideltoid breadth 8, chest breadth 7, chest circumference 14, neck circumference base 8.

## Limits

Army sample, adults 17 to 58. Biacromial breadth is taken sitting, the other lengths standing. The waist back length ends at omphalion, not at ISO 8559-1's waist level. ANSUR II takes no bust point width, across front width, side neck point to bust point or neck length, so those measures have no ANSUR allowable error of their own.
