# Ju et al. 2005, mean value coordinates

Ju T, Schaefer S, Warren J. "Mean Value Coordinates for Closed Triangular Meshes." SIGGRAPH 2005. Read from https://www.cse.wustl.edu/~taoju/research/meanvalue.pdf (abstract and sections 1 to 2).

## Licence

Paper copyright ACM. Read for technique comparison only.

## What it says

- Generalizes mean value coordinates from closed polygons to closed triangle meshes.
- The coordinates are continuous everywhere, smooth inside the mesh, linear on its triangles, and reproduce linear functions inside.
- Applications include volumetric textures and surface deformation by a control mesh.
