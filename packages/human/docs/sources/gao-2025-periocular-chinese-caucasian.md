# Gao 2025, brow and eyelid morphology in young Chinese and Caucasians

Gao T, Guo Y, Rokohl AC, Fan W, Lin M, Ju S, Li X, Ju X, Hou X, Rosenkranz TA, Zhang G, et al. "Racial and sexual differences of eyebrow and eyelid morphology: three-dimensional analysis in young Caucasian and Chinese." *Quantitative Imaging in Medicine and Surgery* 15(1):882-897, 2025, doi:10.21037/qims-24-1113. Read as PMC11744102 (NCBI efetch).

## Population and protocol

147 volunteers aged 18 to 30 at the University of Cologne: 19 Chinese men, 27 Chinese women (Han students from northern and southern China studying in Germany), 42 Caucasian men, 59 Caucasian women. VECTRA M3 3D images, 32 periocular landmarks, one operator. Grade: measured.

The fold measures are distances from the upper lid crease (fold) to the lid margin, so they are the visible pretarsal show, not the crease height on a closed lid.

## What Human takes

Iris diameter, fissure height and width, canthal angles and tilt, fold show and brow position for two ancestries, all from one protocol.

## Limits

Small Chinese groups living abroad; young adults only.
