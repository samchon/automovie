# Rhodes 2006, evolutionary psychology of facial beauty

Rhodes G. "The evolutionary psychology of facial beauty." *Annual Review of Psychology* 57, 2006, doi:10.1146/annurev.psych.57.102904.190208. [Abstract](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.57.102904.190208) only.

## Findings (abstract)

Early-developing and cross-culturally shared preferences argue against arbitrary cultural standards. A critical review and meta-analyses indicate that averageness, symmetry and sexual dimorphism are all attractive in male and female faces and across cultures. Both mate-quality signalling and by-products of perceptual processing are proposed explanations.

Grade: meta-analysis of measured preferences; evidence here is limited to the abstract.
