# Zhu 2017, auricular concha

Zhu Z, Ji X, Gao Z, Hu G. "A Morphometric Study of Auricular Concha in the Population of Young Chinese Adults." *International Journal of Morphology* 35(4):1451-1458, 2017. [Primary article](https://intjmorphol.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/art_43_354.pdf), doi:10.4067/S0717-95022017000401451.

## Population and definition

310 Chinese university students, 141 women and 169 men, aged 18-28; both ear impressions scanned. Grade: measured. P1 is the posterior point of the anterior auricular notch, P3 the deepest intertragic notch and P5 the strongest antihelical curvature. Conchal depth is the perpendicular distance from floor point P6 to plane P1-P3-P5, not scalp protrusion. Table I gives women left 9.65 (SD 0.94), right 9.53 (0.98) mm.

## Reading and use

Abstract, methods, landmark definitions, coordinate frame and Table I were directly read, pp. 1451-1455. Later pages timed out. This supplies a candidate floor datum for the [campaign audit](../work/law-definition-audit.md), not a complete auricle law. Scaling its depth by ear length or using an ellipse between its landmarks would be additional modelling assumptions. No coefficient has been adopted.
