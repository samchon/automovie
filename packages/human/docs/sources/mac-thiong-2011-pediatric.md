# Mac-Thiong, Labelle and Roussouly 2011, pediatric sagittal alignment

Mac-Thiong J.-M., Labelle H., Roussouly P. "Pediatric sagittal alignment." *European Spine Journal* 20(Suppl 5):586-590, 2011. Read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3175924. Also read: Mac-Thiong et al. 2007, "Sagittal spinopelvic balance in normal children and adolescents", Eur Spine J 16(2):227-234 (PMC2200687; 341 subjects aged 3 to 18, standing lateral radiographs, means PI 49.1, PT 7.7, SS 41.4, TK 44.0, LL 48.0; weak age correlations, r < 0.3).
