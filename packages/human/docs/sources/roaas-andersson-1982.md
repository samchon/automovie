# Roaas and Andersson 1982, hip, knee and ankle range of motion in men aged 30 to 40

Roaas A., Andersson G.B.J. "Normal range of motion of the hip, knee and ankle joints in male subjects, 30–40 years of age." *Acta Orthopaedica Scandinavica* 53:205-208, 1982. [Scanned article](https://actaorthop.org/actao/article/download/28654/33535/82106).

## Licence

Copyright Munksgaard 1982 (now Acta Orthopaedica, open archive). Numeric results cited.

## Population and protocol

A random sample of men aged 30 to 40 from the Göteborg population registry, without previous lower-limb injury or disease: 210 hips (105 subjects), 180 knees (90), 192 ankles (96). Passive arcs with an ordinary goniometer by AAOS (1965) technique, to the nearest 5 deg, by one orthopaedic surgeon: hip flexion supine with the opposite hip flexed; hip extension and rotation prone with the knee flexed; abduction and adduction supine, knee extended. Grade: measured.

Ranges observed: hip flexion 90 to 150, knee flexion 115 to 160, abduction 15 to 55 deg. Left and right sides did not differ. The authors attribute the disagreement between references to passive versus active measurement, position (prone versus seated hip rotation) and observer.
