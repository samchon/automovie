# Wahid 2016 thesis, restatement of the Bell, Davis and Harrington hip equations

Wahid A. Engineering PhD thesis, University of Sheffield, 2016, https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/16065/1/Wahid_A_Engineering_PhD_2016.pdf. Pages 79 to 82, equations 29 to 43. Secondary restatements of Bell et al. 1982 and 1990, Davis et al. 1991 and Harrington et al. 2007; the original papers are not independent evidence here. The thesis orders the axes differently from `hara-2016.md`.

## Equations as restated (origin at the ASIS midpoint; metres)

- Bell: RHJC = (0.36 IA, -0.19 IA, -0.30 IA), IA the inter-ASIS distance; components lateral, posterior, inferior.
- Harrington, pelvic measures only: RHJC = (0.33 IA + 0.0073, -0.24 PD - 0.0099, -0.30 IA - 0.0109), PD the mean ASIS-to-PSIS distance.
- Davis (the Plug-in Gait pelvis): C = 0.115 LL - 0.0153; theta = 28.4 deg; beta = 18.0 deg; Xdis = 0.1288 LL - 0.04856 (ASIS to greater trochanter distance); the hip centre combines C, theta, beta, Xdis, half the inter-ASIS distance and the marker radius.

## What Human takes

Cross-checks for `hara-2016.md` only. On Hara's CT data the Harrington errors were close to Hara's own, while Bell and Davis errors were larger (Davis about 10 to 12 mm per axis). Human uses Hara.
