"Reference ranges for body composition indices by dual energy X-ray absorptiometry from the Bone Mineral Density in Childhood Study Cohort." *Am J Clin Nutr* 118(4):792-803, 2023. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10579045/), methods and Table 3.

## Population and protocol

2,011 healthy US children (height and BMI within the 3rd to 97th CDC percentiles at enrolment, 6% with obesity), ages 5 to 19, 9,846 Hologic whole-body scans, LMS curves. Appendicular lean soft tissue index: lean mass of arms and legs excluding bone, over height squared.
