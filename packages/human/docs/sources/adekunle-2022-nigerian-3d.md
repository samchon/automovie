# Adekunle 2022, Nigerian facial anthropometry by stereophotogrammetry

Adekunle AA, Olowo AY, James O, Adamson OO, Alade AA, Agbogidi FO, Oladega AO, Ogunlewe MO, Adeyemo WL, Busch T, et al. "Facial Anthropometry Measurements Using 3D Stereophotogrammetry Analysis Among Nigerians." *Journal of Craniofacial Surgery* 33(4):1178-1181, 2022, doi:10.1097/SCS.0000000000008036. NIH author manuscript PMC8783922; the supplemental tables were not read.

## Licence

Journal article; NIH public-access manuscript. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

452 adults in Lagos (254 men, 198 women), ages 16.6 to 63.8 (mean 29.6), from the three major Nigerian ethnic groups and minor groups; Vectra H1 3D images, 19 landmarks, neutral expression. Grade: measured. Values below pool both sexes because the sex-split means are in the unread supplement.

## Findings

All vertical measures are larger in men; women have slightly larger palpebral fissure width (not significant). BMI predicts interpupillary distance, face width and jaw width; age predicts upper facial height, upper lip height and mouth width. The authors state that Nigerian faces have a longer upper and lower face with a short midface and nose, and do not follow the neoclassical equal thirds.
