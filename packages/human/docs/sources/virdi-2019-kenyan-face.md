# Virdi 2019, Kenyan-African face

Virdi SS, Wertheim D, Naini FB. "Normative anthropometry and proportions of the Kenyan-African face and comparative anthropometry in relation to African Americans and North American Whites." *Maxillofacial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery* 41:9, 2019, doi:10.1186/s40902-019-0191-7. Read in full as PMC6384287 (NCBI efetch).

CC BY 4.0. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

36 male and 36 female students of the University of Nairobi over 18, all four grandparents of Kenyan descent, no facial surgery, "average/normal facial appearance" judged by the lead investigator. Direct digital vernier caliper measurements in natural head position by one examiner, plus standardized frontal and profile photographs for angles. Method error under 0.5 mm linear and 2.4 degrees angular. Comparison data are Farkas's North American white (NAW) and African American (AA) norms as quoted in the paper. Grade: measured.

## Neoclassical canons in this sample

Forehead height exceeded nasal height in every subject; the orbital canon (en-en = ex-en) held in 33 percent of men and 27 percent of women; nasal width exceeded intercanthal width and a quarter of face width in every subject; mouth width equal to 1.5 nasal widths held in 6 and 11 percent.

## What Human takes

East African adult values with the NAW and AA norms side by side, and direct evidence that the equal-thirds and nose-to-eye canons fail as defaults.

## Limits

72 students; NAW and AA values are secondary quotations of Farkas.
