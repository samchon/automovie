# ANSUR II trunk landmark heights and neck girth ratio

Derived from the ANSUR II public working databases recorded in `ansur-ii.md` (Gordon et al. 2014; Paquette and Parham 2017): https://tools.openlab.psu.edu/publicData/ANSUR_II_FEMALE_Public.csv and `..._MALE_Public.csv`, read in full on 2026-11-03.

## Licence

US Government work cleared for unlimited public release (see `ansur-ii.md`).

## Population and protocol

1,986 women and 4,082 men, US Army 2010 to 2012, standing erect, heights measured from the floor to the landmark, right side, millimetres. Each ratio is computed per subject, then its sample mean and SD (n - 1) per sex. Grade: measured.

The 1st and 99th sample percentiles agree with the bands within 0.005 for every height ratio and within 0.008 for the neck ratio, so the bands are not an artefact of a normal assumption.

## Limits

Army sample, adults 17 to 58, fitness standards narrowing the adiposity tails. Heights are to palpated bony landmarks (cervicale, suprasternale, tenth rib, iliocristale) or to surface points (chest point, buttock point, crotch) on a standing subject with arms hanging; a body in an A-pose raises the axilla. The neck circumference is taken at the infrathyroid level perpendicular to the neck's axis (`ansur-ii.md`); the neck base circumference's own wording in Gordon et al. 2014 was not reread for this note.
