# Bohannon et al. 1985, pelvic and thigh motion in hip flexion

Bohannon RW, Gajdosik RL, LeVeau BF. "Relationship of pelvic and thigh motions during unilateral and bilateral hip flexion." *Physical Therapy* 65(10):1501-1504, 1985, doi:10.1093/ptj/65.10.1501. Read from the PubMed abstract, PMID 4048287; the full text was not read.

## Licence

Paper copyright the American Physical Therapy Association. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

17 young subjects, active and passive, unilateral and bilateral hip flexion. Grade: measured.

## Values

- Between one fourth and one third of the hip flexion movement was pelvic rotation.
- Pelvic rotation always began within the first 8 degrees of hip flexion.
- The abstract gives the share for unilateral and bilateral flexion together.

## Use

A clinical hip flexion angle, read between the thigh and the trunk, includes the pelvis's posterior rotation. The midpoint of the reported share, 7/24, is a convention; the abstract gives no mean.
