# Guht 2026, tooth crown dimensions and third molar agenesis

Guht G, Kirschneck C, Daratsianos N, de Araujo CM, Ribeiro Mattos NH, Baratto-Filho F, et al. "Investigating a potential association between agenesis of the third molars and variations in dental crown dimensions." *PLoS One* 21(5):e0348605, 2026, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0348605. Read as PMC13178930 (NCBI efetch, Tables 1 to 3).

## Population and protocol

Digital scans of orthodontic patients (calibration scans from Bonn; author affiliations in Bonn, Regensburg, Frankfurt and Brazil; site mix not re-read); controls 225 patients (113 male, 112 female), mean age 16.7 (12.5 to 41). Mesiodistal crown width measured on digital dental scans between the mesial and distal contact points, at the maximum width of each fully erupted permanent tooth. Grade: measured.

## Mesiodistal crown width, controls, mean (SD) mm, right side

| Tooth | Upper | Lower |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central incisor | 9.05 (0.55) | 5.66 (0.35) |
| Lateral incisor | 7.18 (0.54) | 6.25 (0.37) |
| Canine | 8.21 (0.43) | 7.10 (0.41) |
| First premolar | 7.40 (0.37) | 7.47 (0.37) |
| Second premolar | 7.15 (0.37) | 7.60 (0.43) |
| First molar | 10.87 (0.53) | 11.49 (0.62) |
| Second molar | 10.39 (0.56) | 10.78 (0.52) |

Left-side values differ by under 0.1 mm. Sum of the six upper anterior teeth (derived): 2 × (9.05 + 7.18 + 8.21) = 48.9 mm along the arch.

## Limits

Orthodontic patients; widths only, no crown heights.
