# DGUV form F 4030, the Neutral-0 method

Deutsche Gesetzliche Unfallversicherung, *F 4030 Info Messblatt Neutral-0-Methode: Erläuterungen zur Benutzung der Messblätter und der Messung nach der Neutral-0-Methode*, edition 11/2015, https://www.dguv.de/medien/formtexte/aerzte/f_4030/f4030.pdf. Read in full (one page).

## Licence

German statutory accident insurance form for physicians' reports; a few phrases are quoted for citation.

## Protocol

The German statutory standard for recording joint range of motion (the Neutral-0 method of Debrunner, also the AO's). Grade: clinical convention; no population or measurement study.

## Statements

- Every joint motion is measured from one uniformly defined zero. The Neutral-0 position is the joint position a healthy person takes "im aufrechten Stand mit hängenden Armen und nach vorn gehaltenen Daumen und parallelen Füßen": standing upright, arms hanging, thumbs held forward, feet parallel.
- Each motion is recorded as three numbers with the 0 between the two end positions; a joint that cannot reach 0 has the 0 at the start or end.
- Fingers: the extended position is the zero, from which hyperextension and flexion are measured.
- Thumb: the carpometacarpal (saddle) joint's abduction is measured toward the palmar side (perpendicular to the plane of the hand) and toward the radial side (in the plane of the hand), each from zero, so the zero is the thumb adducted to the hand in both planes.
- Lengths and girths are taken in the neutral position where possible.

## Use

`AutoMovieHumanNeutral` stands each body in this position, so every pose field is a clinical angle from it: the forearm's zero is thumbs forward, where the measured pronation and supination ranges start, rather than the descriptive anatomical position's palms forward ([anatomical position](openstax-2022-anatomical-position.md)). The form names no head posture; the head is held in the Frankfort plane, the convention every head measurement here uses.
