# Rogers et al. 2008, 3D anthropometric hand model

Rogers M.S., Barr A.B., Kasemsontitum B., Rempel D.M. "A three-dimensional anthropometric solid model of the hand based on landmark measurements." *Ergonomics* 51(4):511-526, 2008, doi:10.1080/00140130701710994. [Author manuscript](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/188402), methods and Tables 1 and 3.

## Population and protocol

100 subjects, palmar landmarks digitised in four postures; calliper measurements by the US Army definitions (Greiner 1992, "Hand anthropometry of U.S. Army personnel", NATICK/TR-92/011, cited through Rogers). Grade: measured.

## Values

- Hand breadth: "distance between the radial side of metacarpal D2 (index finger) and ulnar side of metacarpal D5 (small finger)".
- Means (SD), mm: hand length 178.1 (13.5), breadth 84.5 (7.5), wrist breadth 59.1 (5.5), index PIP width 19.4, depth 17.8, DIP width 17.0, depth 14.0.
- Joint breadth and depth of digits 3 to 5 relative to digit 2 (from Greiner): DIP breadth 1.00, 0.93, 0.85; PIP breadth 1.01, 0.94, 0.82; PIP depth 1.03, 0.97, 0.86.
- Phalanx and metacarpal lengths are not given.

## Modeling assumptions and limits

Section 2.5.2 explicitly sets MCP breadth equal to PIP breadth as a modelling assumption. Section 2.5.3 defines the web from adjacent phalangeal sections and the palmar crease, with its width depending on their relative posture. These support a shared section-and-web definition, not a measured uniform finger spacing or a complete calibration target. The 66 digitized positions are model landmarks rather than individual anatomical predictions.
