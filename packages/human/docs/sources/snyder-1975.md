# Snyder et al. 1975, physical characteristics of children (AnthroKids)

Snyder R.G., Spencer M.L., Owings C.L., Schneider L.W. *Physical Characteristics of Children as Related to Death and Injury for Consumer Product Design and Use*, UM-HSRI-BI-75-5, Highway Safety Research Institute, University of Michigan, for the US Consumer Product Safety Commission, May 1975. NIST AnthroKids sources: the measurement list `datatoc75.htm` and the summary tables `data1975/66.txt`, `67.txt` (sitting height, crown-rump, girls and boys) and `70.txt`, `71.txt` (sitting mid-shoulder height, girls and boys), https://math.nist.gov/~SRessler/anthrokids/.

## Licence

A US Government-funded survey published by NIST and the CPSC; numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

US children from birth to 13 years, measured in the early 1970s; summary tables by age group (three- to twelve-month groups) with N, mean, SD and percentiles in centimetres. Sitting mid-shoulder height is measured on children only (from 25 months), sitting height from birth. Grade: measured.

## Values used

| Age (months) | Girls sitting height | Girls mid-shoulder | Boys sitting height | Boys mid-shoulder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25-30 | 52.2 | 31.9 | 51.8 | 31.3 |
| 67-72 | 61.3 | 38.5 | 62.4 | 39.2 |
| 145-156 | 80.8 | 53.6 | 76.8 | 50.2 |

Vertex to the top of the shoulder is therefore 20.3 and 20.5 cm at 25 to 30 months, 22.8 and 23.2 cm at 6 years.
