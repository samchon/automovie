# Banks and Cheney, otoplasty surgical technique

Banks C, Cheney M. "Otoplasty Surgical Technique." In Fagan J (ed.), *Open Access Atlas of Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Operative Surgery*, chapter 2.15. Read on LibreTexts (med.libretexts.org, Atlas of Otolaryngology, 02 Otology, 2.15) on 2026-10-22.

## Values

Normal auricle as a surgical target, stated as conventional ranges without a measured population. Grade: conventional.

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical height | 5 to 6 cm |
| Width | about 55 percent of the vertical length |
| Long-axis inclination | 15 to 20 degrees posterior to vertical |
| Auriculocephalic angle (auricle to scalp) | 25 to 35 degrees |
| Helix-to-scalp protrusion, superior rim | 10 to 12 mm |
| Protrusion, mid-auricle | 16 to 18 mm |
| Protrusion, inferior helix | 20 to 22 mm |

Position: the top of the auricle level with the lateral brow, the bottom of the lobule level with subnasale, and the auricle's height about the distance from the lateral orbital rim to the helical root.

## Limits

Surgical aesthetic norms, not a sample. Protrusion rises from top to bottom; prominence definitions differ between authors (an angle above 40 to 45 degrees is commonly called prominent).
