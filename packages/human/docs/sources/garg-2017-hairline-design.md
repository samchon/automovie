## Protocol

A hair-restoration design method applied to 431 patients (men with hair loss). All distances are taken on the surface of the forehead with a flexible tape. Grade: conventional (a design rule), with measured means.

## Rule ("rule of eight")

| Point | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| A | Glabella |
| E, E' | Lateral canthus points, 8 cm from A |
| B | Midfrontal point, 8 cm above A on the midline (plus or minus 1 cm by baldness grade) |
| C, C' | Frontotemporal points: 8 cm lateral of B and 8 cm above E, at their intersection (plus or minus 1 cm) |
| D, D' | Temporal peaks, on the line from C to E, usually 5 cm from C, slightly more than halfway to E |

Reported means: AB 7.9, AE 8.0, BC 7.9, CD 5.0 cm.

## Limits

A restoration design for receding male hairlines, not a population measurement of unbalded hairlines; the 8 cm distances describe that patient group. Human uses only its proportional relations: the frontotemporal point above the lateral canthus, and the temporal peak at CD/CE = 5/8.
