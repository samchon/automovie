# Du 2008, head-and-face anthropometric survey of Chinese workers

Du L, Zhuang Z, Guan H, Xing J, Tang X, Wang L, Wang Z, Wang H, Liu Y, Su W, Benson S, Gallagher S, Viscusi D, Chen W. "Head-and-Face Anthropometric Survey of Chinese Workers." *Annals of Occupational Hygiene* 52(8):773-782, 2008. Read from the CDC Stacks PDF https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/189638/cdc_189638_DS1.pdf (abstract, Table 2, Table 5).

## Licence

Journal article with NIOSH co-authors. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

3,000 Chinese civilian workers (2,026 men, 974 women), ages 18 to 66, sampled in 2006 by three age strata and two sex strata across regions of China, measured with traditional instruments (calipers, tape, pupillometer) on 15 landmarks. Grade: measured.
