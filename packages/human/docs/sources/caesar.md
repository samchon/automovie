# CAESAR (Civilian American and European Surface Anthropometry Resource)

A 3D scan and traditional anthropometry survey coordinated by SAE International with the US Air Force Research Laboratory in North America and Europe. Read: the WEAR reseller page https://bodysizeshape.com/page-1855750. SAE's own licence terms and the AFRL final report (DTIC ADA408374) were not read.

## Terms as read

The WEAR page states that "SAE owns the name CAESAR", that its 3D data set is "the same data, just organized and packaged" differently, sold for 1,095 EUR (595 EUR for members), and that for commercial use "Once you have it you can do anything you like with it"; it says nothing on redistribution.
