## Definitions and use

The Brannock device aligns to the foot's long axis. Foot breadth, p. 112, is the maximum across support width at the metatarsal heads; its opposite extrema can occur at different along stations. Foot length, p. 113, projects pternion to the longest toe. Ball length, p. 84, uses the first-head pointer; ball girth, p. 83, is a separate tape through heads I/V. Toe splay is excluded from the head landmarks. BODY's independent breadth normalization uses those support extrema, not IBV's oblique ball chord.

Neck girth, p. 139, is infrathyroid and perpendicular to the neck axis. The base tape, p. 140, follows drawn anterior/lateral landmarks; that paragraph does not explicitly name cervicale. Forearm girth, p. 115, is flexed with a clenched fist, so it cannot replace Human's relaxed palm-up definition.

## Hand caliper follow-up, campaign #3062

Section 5.2.36, printed p. 60, defines stylion at the inferior radius and transfers its mark in straight lines to the dorsal and ventral wrist. It does not introduce a separate longitudinal offset for ventral stylion. Hand length, section 6.4.45/p. 121, uses a sliding caliper from stylion to dactylion III with its beam parallel to the arm's long axis. The palm is held on a table, distal phalanges on an 8 mm higher surface, fingers together and digit III aligned with the forearm; flexion and hyperextension are excluded.

Palm length, section 6.4.66/p. 142, uses the ventral stylion and centre of digit III's base crease, choosing the most proximal crease if several exist. The forearm is horizontal, the hand straight and palm up, digit III parallel to the forearm, and the caliper beam parallel to that axis. The endpoint is a skin crease, not a web or MCP centre. Neither protocol defines an already flexed bind's Euclidean distance or bone-chain arclength.

Human's current pure HandBones uses a shared 1.5 mm stylion-to-WJC placement from the forearm, independent de Leva WJC-to-MET3/WJC-to-DAC3 ratios and a separate public crease locus carried onto the proximal-phalanx course in bind. The source's hand-specific Table 2 offsets are instead 2.2/2.4 mm; preserving one common wrist is a retained transfer approximation, not an assertion that the rows agree. MET3-to-MCP-centre and interarticular-length-to-joint-centre transfers remain proxies. No offset, hand shape or measurement value was changed in this follow-up.
