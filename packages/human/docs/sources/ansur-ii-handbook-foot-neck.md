# ANSUR II handbook, foot and neck datums

Hotzman J et al. *Measurer's Handbook: US Army and Marine Corps Anthropometric Surveys, 2010-2011*. NATICK/TR-11/017, 2011. [Primary handbook](https://tools.openlab.psu.edu/publicData/ANSURII-TR11-017.pdf). Approved for public release, unlimited distribution. Grade: measurement protocol.

## Direct reading

Printed pp. 33-34, 83-84, 112-115 and 139-140 were read; rendered figures on pp. 83/84/112/113 were also inspected. PDF page numbers are ten greater. This is not a complete handbook reading.

## Definitions and use

The Brannock device aligns to the foot's long axis. Foot breadth, p. 112, is the maximum across support width at the metatarsal heads; its opposite extrema can occur at different along stations. Foot length, p. 113, projects pternion to the longest toe. Ball length, p. 84, uses the first-head pointer; ball girth, p. 83, is a separate tape through heads I/V. Toe splay is excluded from the head landmarks. BODY's independent breadth normalization uses those support extrema, not IBV's oblique ball chord.

Neck girth, p. 139, is infrathyroid and perpendicular to the neck axis. The base tape, p. 140, follows drawn anterior/lateral landmarks; that paragraph does not explicitly name cervicale. Forearm girth, p. 115, is flexed with a clenched fist, so it cannot replace Human's relaxed palm-up definition.

## Current consumer distinction, campaign #3062

Human's public declarations independently specify the neck girth plane and the neck-base course over back, side and front neck points. The current NeckConstruction instead scales a retained nonplanar harmonic course by its XZ control-polygon perimeter. Its 3D length is a different quantity. HeadMassPart subsequently uses the neck tape's circle-equivalent radius with independent back/fullness profiles; it does not solve that source section's perimeter to the tape. Neither operation alone establishes the published measurement.

HeadLayout's top-ring mean station is also distinct from an actual landmark height. A chosen C7 phase would require its vertical lift to be included in that named point's height; identifying that phase and identifying the public tape plane are separate anatomical decisions. The [law audit](../work/law-definition-audit.md#current-datum-checkpoint-2026-11-05-040146-0900) records the actual consumers and available geometric closure without adding a neck height or forward-shift coefficient.

The current HumanAnatomyNeckSites observer uses the minimum girth along the rig's neck-to-head axis, explicitly without a larynx locator. Its base reading projects sampled concavity landmarks into an estimated plane before taking a convex-hull perimeter, and its C7 is the posterior base concavity. These definitions do not independently establish the public infrathyroid site, 3D base course or cervical spinous-process landmark. The handbook requires the Frankfurt head position and enough tape tension to maintain contact; it does not instruct a minimum-girth search along a rig segment or plane projection of the base course.
