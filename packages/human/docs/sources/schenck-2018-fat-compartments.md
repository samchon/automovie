# Schenck 2018, superficial facial fat compartments

Schenck TL, Koban KC, Schlattau A, Frank K, Sykes JM, Targosinski S, Erlbacher K, Cotofana S. "The Functional Anatomy of the Superficial Fat Compartments of the Face: A Detailed Imaging Study." *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*, 2018, doi:10.1097/PRS.0000000000004364. [Abstract](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000004364) only.

## Population and protocol (abstract)

30 fresh frozen heads of Caucasian body donors (13 male, 17 female, age 78.3 ± 14.2), contrast injected into each superficial compartment, upright CT and MRI, then dissection.

## Findings (abstract)

Distinct subcutaneous compartments exist. Under added volume, the superficial nasolabial, middle cheek and jowl compartments displace downward, while the medial cheek, lateral cheek and superior and inferior temporal compartments gain projection without displacement.
