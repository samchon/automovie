# The anatomical position (OpenStax 2022; LibreTexts, Boundless)

Betts J.G., Young K.A., Wise J.A., et al. *Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, OpenStax, 2022, section 1.6 "Anatomical Terminology", https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/1-6-anatomical-terminology (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Also *Anatomy and Physiology (Boundless)*, Medicine LibreTexts, section 1.4A "Anatomical Position" (CC BY-SA 4.0). Read: the defining paragraphs of both.

## Statements

- OpenStax: "the body standing upright, with the feet at shoulder width and parallel, toes forward. The upper limbs are held out to each side, and the palms of the hands face forward".
- Boundless: the body standing "with feet together, arms to the side, and head, eyes, and palms facing forward", the upper limbs with "the palms facing forward and thumbs pointing away from the body".
- The two textbooks disagree on the feet (together against shoulder width) and agree on upright, arms at the sides, palms forward with the forearms supinated, toes forward. Neither text sets the head beyond facing forward (head measurement holds it in the Frankfort plane, orbitale level with porion, by convention), nor gives a number for the arms' separation from the trunk.

Grade: descriptive convention for naming directions, not a measuring position.

## Use

The descriptive anatomical position differs from the measuring zero of [the Neutral-0 method](dguv-f4030-neutral-zero.md) only in the forearm: palms forward is the forearm fully supinated from thumbs forward, the pose `forearm.pronation` at its supination end (-87.8 degrees, Soucie 2011). The Human's `{}` pose is the measuring zero, so every pose field keeps the clinical units its range was measured in.
