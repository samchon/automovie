# Pallett 2010, new golden ratios for facial beauty

Pallett PM, Link S, Lee K. "New 'golden' ratios for facial beauty." *Vision Research* 50(2):149-154, 2010, doi:10.1016/j.visres.2009.11.003. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2814183/).

## Licence

Copyright Elsevier; author manuscript.

## Protocol

Four experiments of paired attractiveness comparisons of photographs of Caucasian women in which only the eye-to-mouth distance or the interpupillary distance was altered (steps of 10 percent of the original distance), features and contour held fixed. Length ratio: vertical eye-to-mouth distance over hairline-to-chin length. Width ratio: interpupillary distance over face width measured between the inner edges of the ears. Grade: measured preference.

## Findings

- Attractiveness is maximal at a length ratio of about 0.36 (fit 0.37, not different from the 0.36 population average) and a width ratio of about 0.46, both matching the average face.
- The classic golden-ratio construction gives 0.38 for both ratios; the preferred ratios differ significantly from it.
- Tested on female faces only; the authors note male, ethnic and age variation as open.

## What Human takes

The golden ratio is not a target; average spatial relations are. Eye-to-mouth 36 percent of face length and interpupillary 46 percent of face width serve as a check on a generated female face.
