## Eyebrow form and hair direction

| Part | Share of length | Position | Hair direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Head (medial) | 10 to 15 percent, 0.6 to 0.8 cm | Below the orbital margin | One or two staggered rows pointing up (vertical), few short light hairs |
| Body | about 60 percent, 2.5 to 3 cm | Arches upward onto the orbital margin | Upper-border hairs point down and outward, lower-border hairs up and outward, crossing along the axis; middle rows parallel to the axis |
| Tail (lateral) | 20 to 25 percent, 1.2 to 1.7 cm | Above the orbital margin, beyond the lateral canthus | More horizontal and outward, then sloping down |

Men's brows 5 to 6 cm long and 1 to 1.5 cm wide; women's 4.5 to 5 cm long. Density falls laterally. Hairs lie flush to the skin; a hair standing at even a slight angle "grows out like canopies and looks unacceptable". A restoration takes 70 to 120 single-hair grafts.
