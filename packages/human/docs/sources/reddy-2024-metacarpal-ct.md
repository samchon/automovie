# Reddy et al. 2024, CT morphometry of the metacarpals

Reddy M.V., Pai G M.P., Kumar M.M., Bhat A.K., Annapareddy A., Thatte M.R. "Indian Hand Anthropometry: Computer Tomography-Based Morphometric Analysis of Metacarpal." *Indian Journal of Plastic Surgery* 57(4):270-277, 2024, doi:10.1055/s-0044-1787688. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11436343/), population, protocol and Table 4.

## Licence

Open access (Thieme, PMC).

## Population and protocol

100 hands, 50 men (38.6 +- 12.0 years, 19 to 75) and 50 women (43.6 +- 13.6, 18 to 77), retrospective hospital CT in two Indian centres, 2010 to 2021, excluding trauma, deformity, infection, tumour, congenital and inflammatory disease. 128-slice CT at 0.6 mm, measured along each metacarpal's own axis. Grade: measured.

Ratios to MC3 (derived, sexes pooled): MC1 0.711, MC2 1.039, MC4 0.883, MC5 0.797. The second metacarpal is the longest in both sexes. The table's female MC1 articular length (48.4) contradicts its Table 3 (41.6) and is not used.

## Limits

An Indian hospital sample; no children. Holding the ratios in children is an inference.
