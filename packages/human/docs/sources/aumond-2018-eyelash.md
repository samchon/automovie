# Aumond 2018, eyelash follicle review

Aumond S, Bitton E. "The eyelash follicle features and anomalies: A review." *Journal of Optometry* 11(4):211-222, 2018, doi:10.1016/j.optom.2018.05.003. PMC6147748.

## Licence

CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Review; numbers are quoted from cited studies (Thibaut 2010 and others), so the grade is the cited studies' measurement as relayed.

## Values

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Upper lid lashes | 90 to 160, in five to six rows |
| Lower lid lashes | 75 to 80, in three to four rows |
| Growth rate | 0.12 to 0.14 mm per day |
| Anagen | 4 to 10 weeks; full cycle 4 to 11 months |
| Length | rarely exceeds 12 mm; over 12 mm is trichomegaly |

All lashes are curved regardless of ethnicity, the curvature starting at the bulb; the follicle is asymmetric like that of curly scalp hair. A cited study measured lift-up and curl-up angles of upper lashes in Caucasian and Asian women by photograph.
