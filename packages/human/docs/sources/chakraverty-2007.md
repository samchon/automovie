# Chakraverty et al. 2007, spinal levels of the palpated iliac crests and PSIS

Chakraverty R., Pynsent P., Isaacs K. "Which spinal levels are identified by palpation of the iliac crests and the posterior superior iliac spines?" *Journal of Anatomy* 210(2):232-236, 2007. Read: the abstract, via the Europe PMC API. The full text was not read (PMC returned a browser check).

## Population and protocol

75 consecutive adults (49 female) having prone fluoroscopically guided spinal injections for chronic low back pain, Birmingham, 2004; two examiners palpated before imaging. Grade: measured, clinical population.

## Values

- Imaged intercristal line: L4 or L4-5 in 86.7%.
- Palpated intercristal line: L3 or L3-4 in 77.3%, more often in women (85.7% vs 61.5%) and at higher BMI; agreement with imaging poor (kappa 0.05).
- Palpated PSIS line: S2 spinous process in 51%, S1 in 44% (60 patients).

## What Human takes

The bony iliac crest top at the L4 spinous level, and the reason palpation reads a level higher: the surface crest lies above the bone by subcutaneous fat and the oblique roll, more in women and at higher BMI.
