# Hahmann et al. 2008, shape interrogation

Hahmann S., Belyaev A., Busé L., Elber G., Mourrain B., Rössl C. "Shape Interrogation." In *Shape Analysis and Structuring*, Springer, 2008, pp. 1-51. [HAL full text](https://inria.hal.science/inria-00193551/document). Read: sections on reflection lines, highlight lines, isophotes, curvature plots and hedgehog diagrams.

## What it says

- Reflection lines show dents through the pattern of parallel light lines seen from a fixed eye point; highlight lines, where the extended surface normal meets a linear light, do not depend on the eye point. Both reveal irregularities and tangent discontinuities.
- Isophotes are lines of equal light intensity. "If the surface is C^r-continuous, then the isophotes are C^(r-1)-continuous curves"; a curvature discontinuity shows as an isophote tangent break, which an ill-conditioned light direction hides when the light's tangent-plane projection is parallel to the isophote.
- Hedgehog diagrams and curvature plots are established for planar curves and apply to planar sections of a surface; hedgehog diagrams of whole surfaces "are not to be recommended".
