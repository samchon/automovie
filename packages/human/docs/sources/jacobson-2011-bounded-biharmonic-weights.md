# Jacobson et al. 2011, bounded biharmonic weights

Jacobson A, Baran I, Popović J, Sorkine-Hornung O. "Bounded Biharmonic Weights for Real-Time Deformation." SIGGRAPH 2011. Read from the project page https://igl.ethz.ch/projects/bbw/ (abstract only).

## Licence

Paper copyright ACM. Read for technique comparison only.

## What it says

- Linear blending weights for points, bones and cages that give smooth, intuitive deformations.
- The weights minimize the Laplacian energy subject to bound constraints, and spread "in a shape-aware and localized manner".
