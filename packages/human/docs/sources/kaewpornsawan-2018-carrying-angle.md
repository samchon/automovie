# Kaewpornsawan et al. 2018, carrying angle from birth to adolescence

Kaewpornsawan K., Kamegaya M., Udompunturak S., Eamsophana P., Ariyawatkul T. "The Normal Reference Values of Carrying Angle from Birth to Adolescence." *Siriraj Med J* 70(4):284-288, 2018, doi:10.14456/smj.2018.46. Read: the full PDF from https://he02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/sirirajmedj/article/view/153847.

## Population and protocol

921 healthy Thai children (407 boys, 514 girls), one group per year from birth to 16, both elbows. Arm abducted 90 degrees on a table, elbow fully extended and supinated; full-circle goniometer hinged at the centre of the cubital crease, arms along the arm and forearm axes; positive valgus. Inter-observer ICC 0.848. Grade: measured.
