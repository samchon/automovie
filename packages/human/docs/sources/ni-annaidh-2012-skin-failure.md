# Ní Annaidh et al. 2012, failure of excised human skin

Ní Annaidh A, Bruyère K, Destrade M, Gilchrist MD, Otténio M. "Characterization of the anisotropic mechanical properties of excised human skin." *Journal of the Mechanical Behavior of Biomedical Materials* 5(1):139-148, 2012, doi:10.1016/j.jmbbm.2011.08.016. Read from the PubMed abstract, PMID 22100088; the full text was not read.

## Licence

Paper copyright Elsevier. Numbers are facts.

## Population and protocol

56 specimens of excised skin from the back, uniaxial tension at a strain rate of 0.012 per second, strain by digital image correlation. Grade: measured.

## Values

- Failure strain 54 ± 17 percent; ultimate tensile strength 21.6 ± 8.4 MPa; elastic modulus 83.3 ± 34.9 MPa; initial slope 1.18 ± 0.88 MPa; strain energy 3.6 ± 1.6 MJ/m³.
- The properties depend on the specimen's orientation to the Langer lines, and its place on the back changes strength, modulus and energy.

## Use

A principal stretch of 2 lies 2.7 standard deviations above the mean failure stretch of 1.54, so it is a tear for nearly every specimen. The values are for excised back skin in uniaxial tension, not for skin in place, which also slides over fascia and carries a resting tension.
