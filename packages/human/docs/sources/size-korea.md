# Size Korea (Korean national anthropometric survey)

Korean Agency for Technology and Standards (KATS, 국가기술표준원), Size Korea, https://sizekorea.kr/. Read: the home page, its menu and the data-download consent text. No data or report was downloaded (download requires a member login), so no values are taken yet.

## Terms of use for data (the download consent, translated)

1. Any use of Size Korea data must state that it is a result of KATS and the operating institutions, and derivative works using it must state the same.
2. Size Korea data must not be sold to other corporations, organisations or individuals, in Korea or abroad.
3. The de-identified data provided by the site may be used for commercial, non-commercial, research and development purposes, safely; users must cooperate with KATS surveys of use cases and outcomes.

## Contents listed

Survey rounds 1 (1979) to 8 (2020 to 2023); standard body sizes and shapes by sex and age; obesity body types; foot shapes; standardised measurement terms and landmarks; 3D body shape search; product design guidelines (head, trunk, other).

## What Human may take

Published statistics and coefficients fitted to downloaded data, with attribution to KATS in the JSDoc; never the raw data or scans. Size Korea covers ages from childhood to old age in one protocol, which ANSUR II lacks, and an East Asian population for the ancestry converter.
