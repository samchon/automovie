# IBV, Foot Metrics Catalogue

Instituto de Biomecánica de Valencia (IBV), *Feet Metrics Catalog* for its digital measuring tape, last updated 2020-06-10, https://personales.upv.es/alrego/Feet/Feet_DigitalMeasuringTape.html. No authors are named; every metric is attributed to IBV, and some cite the ISO clause they follow. Read: the catalogue page in full. Grade: a 3D foot-scan software vendor's operational definitions.

## Definitions

- Foot axis: it "passes through the projections in the ground plane of the rearmost point of the foot and the midpoint between the 1st and 5th metatarsal heads".
- Foot length: "Distance between the rear- and foremost point of the foot projected into the foot axis"; cited as ISO 7250-1:2017 6.3.7 and ISO 8559-1:2017 5.5.5.
- Ball width: the maximal distance between the extreme points of the ball section, the section through the first and fifth metatarsal heads, projected on the ground plane.
- Ball girth: the perimeter of that section, perpendicular to the ground plane; cited as ISO 8559-1:2017 5.4.7.
- Heel width: the width of the heel at the height of the most prominent lateral point of the heel section, at 16% of foot length (IBV's own, no standard).
- Arch area: the area below the arch on the footprint taken 1 mm above the ground, bounded by a tangent on the medial side (IBV's own).

## Limits

The catalogue does not reproduce the ISO clause text. It cites ISO clauses for foot length, which is read along the axis, but none for the axis itself, so the axis is IBV's definition. It does not say how the metatarsal heads are found on a scan. The ball girth's ISO clause number disagrees with ISO 8559-1's contents, where 5.4 holds distances along the body surface and foot girth is 5.5.7.
