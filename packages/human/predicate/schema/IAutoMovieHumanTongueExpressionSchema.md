## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The signed controls identify their geometric positive and negative directions. Dorsum backing has a separate nonnegative scale.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Typia tags bound elevation, protrusion, lateral tip and dorsum height to [-1, 1]. Backing uses [0, 1]. Each channel is optional. The polygon renderer reports the tongue group as unsupported.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The mouth schema owns the optional tongue block, which retains the Interface's named channels. Evaluation of those channels is a consumer concern.
