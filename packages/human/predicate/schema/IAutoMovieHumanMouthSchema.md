## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The documented mouth width joins the cheilia. The separate upper and lower heights run from their vermilion landmarks to stomion on the closed seam.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Typia bounds required width to [15, 90] millimetres and each vermilion height to [1, 25]. The measurements describe a resting face geometry input.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The face owns this static mouth record. Opening capacity belongs to the head identity and moving jaw and lip channels have their own pose and expression schemas.
