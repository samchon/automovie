## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The schema names the units of the published head dimensions and explains the scaled ratio. Field documentation records the landmark definitions and the units of any angular measurement.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Required lengths and face and ear records carry their existing bounds. Cephalic index admits 60 through 110, and optional offsets can carry either sign where marked. The generated validator checks individual fields.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Human parameters and the detailed identity use the same head contract. Face and single-ear measurements stay within their child schemas and apply to each authored anatomical side.
