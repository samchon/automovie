## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The alias names required trunk and bilateral records and distinguishes standing stature from sitting height. Mechanical hip joint centre breadth has its own definition.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The schema requires 19 shared body-anatomy measures and keeps their existing bounds. Optional hip joint centre breadth is greater than zero and at most 800 millimetres. Other dimensions, posture and both limb pairs carry their own constraints.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The full identity uses this detailed authored record. It reuses the raw anatomy bounds for measurements common to both representations, and it keeps standing posture and limb-specific fields with their owners.
