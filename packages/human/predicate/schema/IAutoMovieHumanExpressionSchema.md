## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

The declaration names the instant, the required list of sided action units and the optional named mouth controls. A caller can supply an empty list.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The list and mouth record reuse their child constraints. Array tags provide no uniqueness check. The polygon face expands bilateral entries into two sides and rejects a repeated action and side across direct entries and named lip fields.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The Interface owns facial intent. The Human alias adds nested admission metadata. The polygon renderer reads supported brow, eye and mouth actions and reports unhandled channels.
