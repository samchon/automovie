## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

This adapter combines formationSlotPosition with formationSlotRecord so compiled and authored callers share the same position and identity laws.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Hero membership comes from the compiled override table, with no actor-specific shortcut; numeric/slot admission remains in the called owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Docs identify compact regeneration, terrain snapshot use and hero identity; the result is one member rather than an expanded formation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

One slot has fixed-size output; H hero overrides are searched and terrain sampling depends on the retained surface polygons, so total lookup is not unconditionally constant time.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Position and record owners are reused by all formation consumers; no result cache survives mutable compiled terrain or hero overrides.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Only one caller-owned member record escapes; the borrowed formation and terrain snapshot are not copied or retained by a registry.
