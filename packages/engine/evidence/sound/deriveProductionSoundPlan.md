## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Event trim joins use the film frame clock and optional adopted cues resolve against those same picture occurrences; subject masses combine count-weighted centroids and parallel-axis variance, approximating formations/instances as uniform axis-aligned boxes. sqrt(memberCount), RMS distance, softened attenuation and clamped pan are declared source-model choices, not measured member distributions.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

The plan owner joins current shot contract/compiled source and camera sampling, while propagation is optional receipt derivation and each caption lowers to its dialogue line through `productionCaptionDialogueLine`; acousticProfile is copied into the plan but does not derive per-event acoustic responses here.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Unknown individual subjects are skipped if another resolves, but zero total resolved mass throws. Missing compiled evidence/camera/events throws, a caption ending before its speech or a speech span past the film throws, and finite assumptions do not establish byte provenance or universal cross-host transcendental identity.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Documents event/cue/dialogue clocks, the speech span of a caption with a sample count and its refusals, spatial approximation and fresh arrays; static resolved camera positions can be borrowed in event.listener, so the plan is not deeply immutable. Production delivery validation consumes this planner to compare expected plans.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For E event samples, each event can scan declared events, subject node/formation/instance lists and object motions; camera/source sampling plus final O(E log E) sort dominate, and event/cue/dialogue arrays use O(E+C+L) storage. Event cue expansion adds one entry per matching cue and retained occurrence.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Current contract/source maps share identity lookup and one camera/source mass serves each event's pan/attenuation/optional propagation; all object motions are still sampled per resolved event, with no cross-event pose cache.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The caller owns fresh plan arrays and copied profiles, while listener positions may retain static camera translations; local mass parts and sampled channels leave scope, and no audio assets or decoder handles are acquired.
