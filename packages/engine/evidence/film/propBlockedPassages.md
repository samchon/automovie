## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Openings use the union of rest-world part boxes, including the shared staged-element-origin fallback for vertexless held models; connectors use axis-aligned route bounds widened by maximal section dimensions. Strict open-interval overlap preserves boundary-only contact as nonblocking.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Delegates invariant passage measurement and strict overlap to createPropPassageBlocker; this one-off API preserves the original environment-and-bounds seam while registry validation shares one preparation across its props.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No opening, connector or prop is special-cased; every unmeasurable passage is skipped by the same stated rule and left to the validator that owns its refusal.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Delegates preparation to createPropPassageBlocker, including opening-part traversal, rest-chain composition and per-route-segment section filtering and repeated section queries. The one-off query then scans all prepared passage boxes; it does not establish a linear bound in route/station counts alone.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The preparation shares rest matrices and element/model indexes across openings for this single query; repeated-prop validation calls createPropPassageBlocker directly to reuse prepared bounds for an unchanged environment during that call, without a global stale-data cache.

## @evidenceExclude contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The lookups and indexes are dropped on return; the caller owns the returned blockages, at most one per opening and connector.
