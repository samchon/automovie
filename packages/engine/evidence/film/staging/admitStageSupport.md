# src/film/staging/admitStageSupport.ts#admitStageSupport

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Documents the staged walkable input, initial time and footprint, later performance checks and output ownership.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Checks a zero start, finite strictly increasing later starts and unique string references to prepared walkable surfaces. A nonempty initial scope must cover the actor's finite staged XZ in world metres with a surface available at shot second zero.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Static shape and reference admission lives at staging. The shot clock and sampled motion belong to performance.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Invalid windows are reported at their stage paths, without assigning a replacement floor or moving the actor. Empty scopes explicitly suspend grounding.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Checks each actor and referenced id once with per-window sets and scans opening footprints only for the initial position.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Reuses the staged surface preparation across actors and original admitted window records in the staging result.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Local validation sets are synchronous. The returned ordered actor entries retain their borrowed windows until staging is discarded.
