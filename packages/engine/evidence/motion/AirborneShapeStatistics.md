## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Counts actual owner events instead of inferring populations from elapsed time, misses or patched methods. A native private numeric record separates observation from physical computation; its dimensionless integer totals are exactly represented within JavaScript's safe range and do not certify physical success.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One factory allocates eight scalars and a snapshot reader, weak registration lends native storage once per history, and each actual owner increments its event counters. Attempts count actual node-construction callbacks; accepted counts a requested instant using its complete cached reduction, and fallback counts an uncached query selecting its stored unavailable stencil. Neighbor-only nodes can remain attempts without either query event. No notification callback participates in physics or clear.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No ambient observer, replaced global, physical-bound change, fixture label or event log is introduced. Physics never calls the supplied read method or a foreign getter, and copying that method or supplying a prepared flag cannot forge weak-map provenance. Refusal and failed attempts are never reclassified as success.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Factory, borrow and snapshot have constant work/storage. A history with no observer allocates no counter record, array or per-query event value. Native observation adds guarded numeric increments only at actual events, and borrow allocates nothing in either branch.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One explicit handle accumulates actual events across its supplied histories and cache clears; snapshots do not reset totals or change cache state. Snapshot mutation cannot change native counters, and measured populations never establish physical eligibility or cached-answer equivalence.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

A caller-owned handle retains eight scalars and its reader, with weak registration retaining no handle. Histories borrow numeric state without the observer/read callback, and clear detaches statistics from issued instants. No body, pose, timestamp history or retained snapshot population is stored.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Defines eight cumulative populations: history queries/memo hits, numeric nodes, SPD/Jacobi calls, and analytic rate attempts/accepted instants/stencil selections. Each rated node attempts rates on first construction, including stencil neighbors; only a requested instant can consume the stored derivative or select its unavailable fallback. Memo hits do neither; callback/reducer throws remain attempts without acceptance or fallback. Generic nodes count per cache epoch and a fixed shape counts its actual construction. Snapshots are independent and foreign handles are refused.
