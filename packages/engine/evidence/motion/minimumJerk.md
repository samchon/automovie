## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

The closed-form Flash and Hogan quintic evaluates its position on the first half and reflects the result above the midpoint. The reflection uses a small, nonnegative remaining share. Derivatives keep the original normalized-time expressions.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One private kernel owns finite admission, endpoints and polynomial expressions. The original function selects a complete profile and share/rate select one actual scalar demand, without a second easing rule or caller-supplied calculation hook.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Nonfinite time throws before clipping. All finite interior times use the same quintic identity, and time at or beyond either endpoint holds that exact position with literal zero derivatives.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

All demands take constant work. Share evaluates just the position. Rate evaluates just the first derivative. Full profiles reuse the squared time on the lower half and allocate one result.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Complete profiles and their share/rate consumers share exact admission, endpoints and selected polynomial expressions. Every current u is evaluated normally; no profile, time or result is cached across requests.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Complete reads return one fresh three-number profile and scalar reads return one number. No demand retains time, caller state or outputs, and no collection, handle, task or global cache is acquired.
