## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Equivalent zero/nonnegative conic expansion feeds an engine-owned Goulart/Chen homogeneous predictor-corrector with sparse LDL and original-KKT iterative refinement. Reversible diagonal equilibration leaves the authored diagonal PSD objective and every finite interval side unchanged; numerical and negative API refusals remain distinct.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Joins conic assembly, equilibration, a synchronous iterate workspace and the interior solver; semantic admission and independent original-row residuals stay at the public adapter.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

No external numerical library, WebAssembly, executable, subprocess, service, fixture dispatch, objective alteration or numerical fallback is used. Initial remains ignored and failure is never relabelled as full convergence.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States input ownership, cold compatibility behavior, all reachable status meanings, unavailable setup diagnostics and the historical inactive transport boundary.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Assembly and scaling use O(n+m+nnz) storage/work outside bounded passes and column sorting; iteration uses a sparse factor with actual fill rather than a dense variable square. The borrowed iterate has n+2m doubles and result copies have original dimensions.

## @evidenceExclude contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Every mutable input receives its own cold state; only one solve's symbolic structure and current factors are reused by the interior owner.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

One synchronous workspace borrow ends on success or exception and result arrays copy out; all numerical arrays and factors are call-owned, with no module, native memory, handle or cache retained across calls.
