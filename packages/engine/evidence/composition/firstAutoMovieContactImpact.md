## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Unknown departure preserves initial penetration/touch reporting. A clearance witness must be finite, strictly later, inside the live search and strictly outside the actual body. No epsilon, shifted query clock, permanent collider exclusion or bounce cap is introduced.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Work is proportional to bodies times the scan steps plus the crossing search's evaluations: superlinear on a smooth gap where a bisection to the same width takes about 23 halvings of a 1/120 s step, and at most the search's limit of 64. The bracket's clear end is the gap the scan read one step before, not read again. Where a weapon and every body it can meet state a bound on the search's grid, a clear gap certifies the grid steps it cannot close within, which are not read (conservative advancement on the grid), with the same bracket and contact; each side's bound is prepared once a search and asked over a window of grid steps doubling from one, the body's first, so neither is read past twice the stretch it certifies and a body that cannot be bound costs the weapon no read. Each bounded body also keeps its own certified second, so where another body cannot be bound and every grid step is read, a bounded body is read again only once its gap could have closed. No measured count is claimed. Each initially contacting body may add its model's certificate cost and one actual witness check; opaque bodies add no certificate work.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Sphere, capsule and tapered-capsule gaps share one reader. Each exact sample produces endpoints once for all bodies at that sample; witnesses also read actual endpoints through the shared primitive. The body supplies model-specific departure knowledge once per initial pair; all source/stop-dependent certificates are rebuilt for each event search, with no retained pose/time cache.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

A lazy call-local map holds at most one scalar release time or no-entry marker per certified body, another one certified clear second per bounded body, and the scan one gap of its last clear step, its weapon's speed reader and one per bounded body it asked, all released with the scan. Returned ids/times retain no callback, body, certificate object or map.
