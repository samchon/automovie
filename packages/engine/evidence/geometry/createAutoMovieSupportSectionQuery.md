## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For P bounded planes, preparation costs and retains O(P squared) pair records and a fixed-normal finite-coordinate bound. Certified offsets compute only demanded coordinates; uncertified offsets retain O(P squared) full preflight. A valid A-corner seed checks O(A P) inequalities plus its exact tight-pair incidences, while a failed seed costs O(P cubed) full enumeration. Query scratch is O(P squared); every demanded coordinate is computed at most once per offset set.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One caller-owned query reuses copied normals, exact nonparallel determinants, their conservative range bound, a numeric plane-pair index matrix and only the previous successful polygon's cyclic edge-pair indices. Current offsets must satisfy the range bound or receive full coordinate preflight, and every reused corner must prove current all-plane feasibility. The bound changes scheduling, not admission or arithmetic. Failure replaces the seed only after a successful full result; returned coordinates/incidence are fresh.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The returned CPU closure retains O(P squared) fixed direction records and at most P active-edge pair indices until released. Each query discards its coordinates/maps and transfers fresh vertices; no offsets, image, file, worker or global input-dependent cache is retained.
