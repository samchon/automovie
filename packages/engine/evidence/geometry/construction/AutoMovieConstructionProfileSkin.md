## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

With no ends, returns in O(1). Otherwise O(B) open records build pair sets. Each end indexes E arcs and scans S cut segments once across its unique faces, then E exact tip-membership predicates retain their reached cycle/source costs, up to O(E(S+L)) for L loops; admitted fan emission is O(E).

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Reuses existing ring IDs and the registered owner. Within one end/strip each face's unchanged cuts are scanned once, while actual phase-specific kept predicates remain separate; bottom/top strips never share that decision. One actual tip point is read per retained end.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Pair sets, each end's local face set and fan arrays are synchronous scratch; cut results are never reused across different station/regular strips. Mesh retains admitted apex/triangle data and source ownership.
