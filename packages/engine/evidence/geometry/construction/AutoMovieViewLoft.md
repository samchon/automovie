Retains original direct scalar sources as final local authority and actual analytic frame jets. A complete regular section owns both uncapped ends. ## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Invalid source/fullness/sections retain their diagnostics. A direct field with a rejected prepared station is not published through surviving compatibility rows, and no fallback section is introduced.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Each source is prepared once. An O(L+K) station-set and declared-knot coverage pass precedes section solves; each station then solves one section or prepares one chain, and coefficient/frame preparation and ordinary field solves are linear in layer rows and component widths.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Reuses prepared scalar sources across station values; SeamStation skips source-owned tables and reuses each ordinary curvature table for its slope solve.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The returned direct chain owns original scalar preparations and O(LN) compatibility bindings alongside its sections; temporary samples are call-local and no cross-part cache is added.
