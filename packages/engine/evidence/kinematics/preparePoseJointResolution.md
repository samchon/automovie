## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

All duplicate, stray and undemanded records remain admitted in original order. A record's identity stands for its coordinates only after that record itself passed admission, under the value contract that admitted records are not edited; no boolean, pose identity, tolerance, editable whitelist or target answer substitutes for a record's first validation. Null never admits an unused frame, failed conversion never replaces a successful row and raw APIs remain separate.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

J records retain constant-size exact checks; a record already admitted costs its name lookup and one identity comparison (a reduced or merged pose shares its source's records). Unchanged selected tuples reuse their components, and so does the tuple before the last, at three more exact comparisons (measured before it on test_human_motion_stagger_recovery_m_average: 1.2 s of 28.5 s profiled inside this commit path, of 3.0 s in the pose reads; unmeasured after); a conversion writes four numbers with no quaternion or tuple record; fixed-demand exclusions validate without creating a joint row, factors or components. Immutable basis/affine work remains lazy, and three actual positive-zero factors/one inner may share across composers. Fixed-source snapshot costs O(B); runtime improvement is unmeasured.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Static axes/frames are owned immutable snapshots. prepareScalarQueries shares fixed scalar/affine prerequisites and lazy exact positive-zero rig-rest factors; negative zero and other values use ordinary factor paths. Every current finite check and first-use per-axis precheck retains original order, while dynamic factors/inner storage and fresh outputs remain operation-owned.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The operation owns O(U+B) successful rows, each with its last tuple and components and the ones before them, and rooted references until release, without raw joint/pose/result history. Each static convention owns one basis/factory, at most three precheck/axis/affine readers, three actual zero factors and one fixed inner until rig release. Snapshots write into the caller's rows and retain no operation reader or source records.
