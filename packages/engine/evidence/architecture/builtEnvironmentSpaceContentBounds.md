## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Pays the linear space subtree walk, full world staging, model indexing and element/population scans. Selected elements add their procedural tessellation and vertex folds; selected populations add placement validation and corner folds, including all stored explicit transforms. These delegated costs are not bounded by population count alone.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One staging pass and one model index are shared by every element in the subtree within the call; nothing is cached across calls because the record is the caller's and may change.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources
