## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

An admitted positive growth span, bounded depth and normalized directions feed deterministic path-seeded growth, Duff's signed perpendicular frame, scalar slab/sphere pruning and rational-circle leaf roll. Binary64 arithmetic and direct squares retain extreme-input limitations; the signed frame is deterministic, not globally continuous.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

One grow owner supplies both quantity/bounds validation and lower installation results; canonical pruning and yaw helpers isolate the same geometric decisions, and emitted stem/leaf measurement supplies the installation's complete canopy envelope.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Zero growth emits no invented seedling, pruned children are omitted and emitted caps refuse overflow rather than silently truncate; degenerate direction/antiparallel fallback choices remain explicit helper policy.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States growth law, recipe-local frame, path seeds, pruning, cap failures and numeric premises without claiming cross-host bit proof from method choice alone.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For B visited branches, C child definitions, L leaves and depth D, emission costs O(B*C+L) scalar work plus O(B*D+L*D) path-id/string storage; final stem support/leaf-card envelope takes O(B+L) time and constant extra live storage.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Child path keys make random draws independent of unrelated emission order; the same parent full length is reused by its children and one leaf alignment is reused for its blades. Changed stages/pruning/recipe values require a fresh derivation without a tree cache.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

All output arrays and complete recipe-local canopy bounds belong to the caller; recursion is bounded by admitted depth, temporary frames and rotated corners are local, and cap failure retains no partially published tree or host task.
