## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Canonical recipe/cluster admission precedes id pairing, then one grow operation and seeded spacing arrangement produce the supplied recipe-local tree and cluster placements. Installation visibility or exact final rendered clearance is not computed here.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Analysis and pairing stay with this join, while growth and arrangement own their independent declared inputs and outputs.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Invalid pairings cannot acquire another record's geometry under a plausible status; counted arrangement rejections remain visible rather than duplicated or squeezed into an authored region.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States pairing, not-run geometry, derivation failures and unwired rendering host.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Validation plus one B-branch/L-leaf derivation and N-member/A-attempt arrangement are charged together; no per-member tree is grown, but actual spacing bucket occupancy determines arrangement comparison work.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One derived plant serves all returned member placements; recipe, cluster and installation mutation do not have a retained cross-call result cache.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Fresh tree/placement arrays transfer with the analysis; admission failure returns no geometry and no renderer/native task is acquired.
