## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Maps resolve environment/domain/cluster/fluid identities; admitted growth supplies the stem-and-leaf envelope, member TRS transforms its eight corners, and room containment checks those corners plus the placement origin. This is not continuous containment proof in nonconvex room unions.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Installation owns the architecture/plant join, support/irrigation kind rules and aquatic free-surface check; recipe and cluster owners retain their own admission laws.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Semantic-only rooms skip geometric claims, unresolved refs produce findings, and invalid recipe/cluster admission skips derivation. Support-id existence does not prove physical load-bearing contact; generation or malformed typed containers may still throw.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Documents reference joining, unique cluster use, geometric versus semantic rooms, per-axis scale and aqueous rooting; exact diagnostic paths are preserved when nested findings are repathed.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For D domains, C clusters and I installations, map construction and validation precede per-installation arrangement/growth; repeated recipe/cluster checks and derivations are still paid, and each of P placements makes at most nine room-point queries plus one fluid-cell query.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Maps share completed identity lookups and each installation derives one canopy for all its members; repeated installation recipe admission is not cached, and changed mutable inputs require a fresh pass.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Call-local maps, arrangements and canopies leave scope after validation; only returned findings retain offending records/points, with no renderer, fluid solver or background work acquired.
