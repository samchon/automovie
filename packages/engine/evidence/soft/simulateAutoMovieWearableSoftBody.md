## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For K frames with N nodes, R actors, A moving anchors, B capsules and J scanned bone rows per query, resolution costs O(K*(N+R+(A+B)*J)); boundary storage O(K*(A+B)) precedes the shared P-particle solver replay cost.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work
