## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Parallel box axes are skipped under the declared zero component; null means no detected cut before the requested length, not proof that arbitrary malformed or nonfinite geometry stayed inside.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

The authored envelope is shared across branch calls but each base/axis/length requires a fresh exit calculation; no clipping cache assumes equivalence of those inputs.
