## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Environment/domain maps and domain admission establish references; softBodyRestConfiguration applies static anchors and the selected state before testing each held particle against a declared room volume. This checks particles, not every cloth face or future trajectory.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Furnishing owns the architecture/cloth join, while domain admission and production-wide domain ownership stay in their respective owners; prevalidated explicitly delegates the latter check to the caller.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Semantic rooms skip geometric checks, absent domains are reported and no volume is invented. Non-null moving bindings are not evaluated here, and invalid domains with matching array length can still reach held-configuration inspection.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Documents typed join, prevalidated ownership prerequisite, named-state fallback after a missing-state finding and point-only initial containment; malformed input shapes or derived numeric failures may throw.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For D domains and F furnishings, indexing plus domain validation precede support scans and O(P) held-configuration/containment checks per furnishing; repeated bindings can rebuild the same configuration and room tests include their architecture cost.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Maps and sets share identity resolution within this pass and held particles reuse the canonical rest owner; no configuration cache is retained across changed domain/state/room records.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Fresh maps, reference sets and held buffers leave scope when the pass ends; the returned violation list alone retains offending inputs/points, without simulation history or viewer handles.
