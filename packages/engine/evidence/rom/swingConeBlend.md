For an outside endpoint, close the bracket from an inside-cone anchor onto the cone's crossing and return the retained feasible lower parameter; largest-boundary meaning requires monotonic swing along that segment. ## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

The width of forty halvings is numerical precision policy; unrestricted multi-turn overrides can violate monotonicity, which current admission does not generally prove, leaving global largest-t behavior unsupported. An anchor that is itself outside the cone is halved toward as before.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Endpoint acceptance reuses its metric and its reading is not taken again; changed angles/anchor/cone require a fresh search without mutable-constraint caching.
