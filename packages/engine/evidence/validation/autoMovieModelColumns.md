## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Repeated ancestry passes resolve axial unit-scale rest chains whose actual yaw matrix has horizontal gain exactly one. Radial polygon apothems bound cylinder/cone cores; the sphere contains a ball of radius R*cos(pi/segments)*cos(pi/(2*rings)), which contains an equal-radius/half-height cylinder after division by sqrt(2). Primitive resolution comes from the tessellator's shared constants.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Formation overlap consumes these rest-axis quantities independently of outer projection radii, so the required no-invented-occupancy meaning must survive any correction.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Unsupported meshes/planes, off-axis or nonunit-scale ancestry, nonisometric axial rest/part rotations, unrepresentable primitive total height/scaled outer envelope and nonfinite output intervals produce no column. Omission loses detections without normalizing authored rotations or changing model admission; capsule cores lie inside the central cylindrical body without certifying the rounded silhouette or repairing extreme producer output.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

For B bones and P parts, repeated settling can take O(B^2) and part lookup O(P), with O(B+P) working/output storage; this is not a single linear parent walk.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One ancestry-height map serves all parts; each core and outer-envelope admission is computed once per part, without a retained cache of mutable model generations.
