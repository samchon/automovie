## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Division for a nonzero linear map, or inversion of a strictly monotone piecewise-linear segment. With a legal domain, compare against its actual forward images first and return a bound only when its forward value equals the request exactly; this round-trips binary64 endpoints without a tolerance or admitting an adjacent value outside the image.
