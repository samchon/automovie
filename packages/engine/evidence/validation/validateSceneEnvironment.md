## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Constant field work and one bounded color check, plus O(L) contextual shadow profiling for L supplied lights; that shared resolver examines casting-light compatibility and builds its caster profile, which is transient here. No per-frame environment revalidation is performed by this function.

## @evidenceExclude contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Owns one collector and a transient shadow profile until return; contextual profile storage is bounded by supplied light count, and only the returned violations transfer to the caller. Module policy sets have fixed size and no resources or tasks are acquired.
