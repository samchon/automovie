# src/validation/detectFreeFall.ts#detectFreeFall

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Finite nonnegative margin and positive finite fall duration precede XZ convex support-hull distance; unsupported declared bodies emit a time-zero event and, absent physicsIntent, warning plus the canonical projectile clip.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Support judgment and analytic trajectory reuse their geometric owners, keeping supplied support/attachment facts separate from future scene derivation or applied motion.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Null body, held/falling bodies and supported centers do not fabricate a fall. PhysicsIntent retains the event but suppresses advice; malformed vector/fps or unallocatable sample populations can still throw in delegated trajectory generation.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Documents world support/center frame, margin .02m, default gravity/velocity, one-second 30fps proposal, node fallback and invalid-input versus warning behavior.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

S support points require O(S log S) hull work and O(S) distance storage; a proposed Q-interval clip adds O(Q) computation/output, while no-fall cases allocate only the result and any gate findings.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One support hull decides the candidate and one canonical trajectory supplies its position/facing tracks; changed support or projectile inputs require a fresh evaluation.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

The caller owns returned clip/findings/events, with the event borrowing the supplied center point; temporary hull storage leaves scope and no simulation cursor or host is acquired.
