## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Admit identities and kinds, simple finite XZ footprint rings, strictly interior disjoint holes, exactly one ground rule and an optional canonical color with opaque alpha.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Polygon and footprint owners handle geometric predicates, height variants own their scalar rules, and the shared color gate admits the optional swatch before the surface checks opacity.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Plan Y is ignored, area/ramp thresholds are absolute policies, and finite admission alone does not guarantee overflow-free extreme arithmetic.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States constant, plane and heightfield shapes, lattice cardinality, walkable references, hole contact policy and opaque color admission.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Ring simplicity/containment/hole-pair predicates can be quadratic; heightfield samples and surface ids require linear scans.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Each admitted ring is built once for its comparisons, sharing canonical polygon/height meanings.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

Ring/ids/hole arrays release after the call; the report owns diagnostics and no terrain mesh or GPU state is created.
