## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

XZ projection produces plan geometry while developed 3D lengths, declared load and directional reach preserve quantities outside that drawing projection.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Shared ownership, length, demand and reach queries supply one report, but its current consumption is test-only rather than production report wiring.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Unknown systems refuse and dangling endpoint ids remain visible; no report success implies the network passed validation or a performance solver.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Distinguishes drawing/physical quantities, system-unit load, unknown-system refusal, dangling endpoint behavior and the current unconsumed product status.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Node/port and segment scans plus route projection are linear in their populations, with reach inheriting its queue-removal worst case; result storage scales with selected points and nodes.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

One owner lookup resolves all selected edges and the projected edge lengths are reused for totalLength; no report survives mutable network equivalence.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources

New drawing rows and point arrays transfer to the caller while temporary ownership/reach maps release without a source-network cache.
