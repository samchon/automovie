## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#principled-implementation

Dispatches on the target discriminator only after the shared record guard, then describes the missing placement or structural context for that supported representation; group faults list authored identities without claiming every member is absent because the caller supplies no placement map here.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#clear-and-simple-structure

Owns the explanatory clause reused by the film gate and target-dependent actor synthesis; shape classification uses the existing isRecord guard, while placement computation remains resolveTargetPoint's responsibility.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#prohibited-implementation-shortcuts

Names the authored node, bone or group rather than rejecting a legal discriminator; no fixture identity changes the wording, and an unknown kind is reported without coercing it to a supported target.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the clause format, identity-based feedback, unknown-input purpose and conversion-failure boundary; the group message reports unresolved membership without guessing which member failed.

## @evidence contracts/development/implementation.md#efficient-algorithms

Field classification is constant; a group visits each authored identity once and joins output-sized text. Conversion is delegated to describeInvalidValue, with no time bound on user-defined hooks, so O(N) traversal excludes that hook work and representation length.

## @evidenceExclude contracts/development/implementation.md#reuse-equivalent-work

Produces one diagnostic from the current target payload and coordinates no requests; the caller owns placement lookup and decides when this explanation is needed.

## @evidenceExclude contracts/development/implementation.md#bound-retention-and-release-resources
