## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Recovery checks the projection tags, finite camera values, positive pitch and distinct physical image limits. It inverts the millimetre row center to v0 − yMm / pitch − 0.5 and both cell edges to columns relative to u0. The downward raster scan and increasing-Y fitting consume the same checker because adjacency uses the absolute row step. The chart fixture checks an interior first row, an immediate neighbor, a fractional column and a two-row gap.
