## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The closed `RELATION_TARGETS` table admits target kinds; the geometry pass shares staged proxy occupancy, probes support through `propSupportGap`, applies subtree corner containment through `propSpaceContainsBounds` and fits a measurable fill's rest AABB. The stated tolerance, sampling and skipped-geometry cases delimit these judgments; they do not certify full mesh contact or whole-box containment in a non-convex union.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

A kind and a target id are the whole claim; geometry stays with the building graph and the cited prop, so the relation never restates a face or a volume.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Citation-only kinds remain explicit consumer limitations even when a record has optional face data. Missing target ids are refused separately from unmeasurable existing geometry, which is skipped rather than invented; declared prop-affordance contact exempts the pair's occupancy overlap but never establishes contact by itself or exempts clearances.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States each kind with the targets it accepts, which kinds are measured and how, why the rest are not, and every refusal made before measuring; members say what each field names.
