## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Framing unions portable rest/proxy member boxes; point verbs take an equal-weight running centroid of every named node. Admission requires every member, but does not reject repeated ids, so duplicates repeat their centroid weight. Group camera following requires a formation cue, updates its base only and retains opening height/radius, which need not enclose later expansion.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

One record names staged nodes and compact formations together, so a captain and his company are one subject without listing slots, and the consuming verb decides whether it needs an extent or a point.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

An unknown node or formation cannot disappear from the target; formation point verbs and group onHit declarations are refused rather than collapsing a crowd to an invented struck actor.
