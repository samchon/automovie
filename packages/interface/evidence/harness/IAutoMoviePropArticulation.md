## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Ordinary graph nodes and the shared profile let `resolveFrame` apply the same driver/constraint machinery as general rigs. The clip gate's critical clock covers direct interpolation under its stated limits; the separate viewer builder requires host wiring, compatible native attach transforms and explicit channel playback, and does not evaluate those drivers.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Three fields split structure, capability and application: the joint nodes, the profile, and the binding that maps the profile's semantic keys onto those nodes, so one profile can serve every prop with the same mechanism.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Missing semantic mappings are refused at forge time. Observed driven-channel limit violations become refusals instead of treating the resolver's clamped result as authored success; undriven rest channels and unsearched nonlinear extrema are outside that refusal guarantee. An empty joint frame is not evidence that a visible part or a production host actually moves.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the purpose, the `null` convention, every forge refusal, the lowered id form, how clips and limits act on the joints, and that drivers are gated but not drawn; members give the parent rule, the part reference that makes a joint visible, and the mapping requirement.
