## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Claims resolve against per-call registries and world AABBs from the staged piece. Only forged, uniquely declared and uniquely staged props with a sound optional footprint enter geometry; upstream staging and building admission remain premises. These rest-placement proxy checks do not measure an articulated or imported mesh at every animation instant.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Relations, the occupied volume and keep-out volumes are the three kinds of claim a prop makes; volumes are model-local so the staging transform is applied once, by the engine.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Registry order does not decide admitted identity. A placementless sound prop remains a clearance obstacle and affordance host, but pairwise occupancy overlap is checked only when both props declare placement; any prop-affordance relation between that pair exempts that overlap. Geometry uses the unique occupied environment or one environment shared by all building citations; no unique environment means no containment or passage measurement.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the validator, the cite-not-copy rule, order independence, the staging requirement, and what an absent placement means; members give the relation limits, the footprint default and when to declare one, and the clearance purpose.
