## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Reference synthesis projects the horizontal away vector onto staged facing/right axes and propagates degree deflections down head, neck, chest and spine with 0.6 per-link falloff. Configured bones use shared ROM clamping; missing chain bones remain authored for downstream refusal. This is a choreographed pose heuristic, not impulse or body-fall dynamics.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

A source, a scalar force and an unbalance flag are the whole verb; the chain, peak gain and clip shape are engine constants, and a launch's recoil is this same verb rather than a second record.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Shot admission refuses an unresolved source, but direct reference synthesis still has a backwards-flinch fallback for null. A horizontal separation below 1e-6 uses the actor's forward vector as the away direction, producing positive flexion; unbalance scales the flinch and produces no fall or dismount. Missing rig/context can yield null synthesis.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States who emits it and when to author it, each refusal, the rig requirement, how direction and magnitude become the flinch, the bone chain and range clamp, the clip timing, the coincident-source rule, the `"auto"` length and the default region; members give the source requirement, the force range and units, and what `unbalance` changes.
