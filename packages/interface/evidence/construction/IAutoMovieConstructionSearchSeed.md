## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The crossing of one curve of the part with the host is a three-equation meeting in the station and the host's chart, solved by Newton from each step's foot, so the seed is a point of the true curve whatever the part's lofted stations turn out to be.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Two face names, one phase, an optional count of lines round the part and two shares are the whole search; the walk's direction is the order of the shares, the lines' order is the phase's then increasing.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The search runs along a finite, declared set of station lines, never over the whole surface or a tessellation; no meeting on any of them is reported.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States why the seed is searched, the walk, the solve, the failure effect and the admission.
