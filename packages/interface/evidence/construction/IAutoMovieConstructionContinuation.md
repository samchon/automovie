## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Equal-order boundaries use the explicit whole-field replacement; different-order native boundaries require original full scalar2/frame2/lift2 equivalence and a sufficient regularity certificate, preserving all rows and intervals without fitting or coarsening. No new section fit or searched rail is implied by these authored parameters.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The band is the split's join strip between each side's own 2-jet across its ring (Hartmann 2001, parametric G^n blending), here with one stretch round the whole ring and no seam, so two faces of any orders meet G2 without either being re-expressed in the other's sections.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

A rail depth, the columns, the rows and how the rail is matched: the strip's existing inputs.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The rail is an authored depth, not searched; a rail outside the part's domain is a diagnostic, never clamped.
