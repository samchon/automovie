## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Each knot is one measurement at its published site, such as a breadth at the waist or a tape at the calf, so the curve reads it back exactly at that station.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

A site, station and value define each measured knot; optional normalized first/second jets add authored tangency or curvature without another curve representation.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The site name is for diagnostics and identity checks; the engine never derives a value from it.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States measurement identity, station units and normalized jet/value units selected by the holding curve.
