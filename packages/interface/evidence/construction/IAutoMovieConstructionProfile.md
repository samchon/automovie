## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Whole original curve intervals and one common physical plane define the sections before surface generation; no polar or convex-envelope representation is imposed. Loop station domains retain metres, piece clocks retain source units and signed traversals preserve original functions.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Closed data references identify functions, physical domains, curve clocks, guides and logical/material/binding regions; runtime callbacks stay in engine. Profile preparation, guide resolution and mesh selections share these members under the family that owns their identities.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Labels do not infer geometry, missing derivatives or shared binding. Parameter intervals retain their original functions rather than sampled refits; topology remains an explicit admission responsibility. No member substitutes output polylines for the original source or treats logical labels as proven geometric continuity.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Distinguishes metre station intervals from original curve clocks, open pieces from closed loops and declared identity from verified geometry. Original contour pieces, guides and ownership regions of a profile family. Member documentation specifies their units, references and absence behavior.
