## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The taut tape is the convex hull's perimeter, monotone in the shoulder, so the girth is solved by a bracketing root finder rather than by scaling a section about its centre.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

One girth curve and an optional set of departures are the whole owner.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Departures need a published source or a derivation; the record carries them as inputs and never fits them, and an unreachable girth is reported rather than forced.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the girth's definition, its range in the box, the solve's uniqueness, the out-of-reach effect and the departures' meaning and null.
