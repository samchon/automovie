## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Three operators applied recursively build every solid the tree can name: a part lofted from its own view outlines in its own frame, the merge of two solids along their intersection curve with a G2 blend, and the diff that cuts one from another; a trim opens a through-cut in a surface. No mesh, ray or distance field enters the recipe.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

One root over one closed node union carries every case, nesting to any depth, so a part, its features and their groups share one representation instead of per-form construction kits.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The record declares geometry only; it names no person, specimen or fitted coefficient, and every value it holds is an input the author states.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation
