## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The gap is the minimum over retained footprint/ring-corner probes. With a finite gap, absolute value within tolerance is resting, positive is floating and otherwise sunk; no probes means not-over-support. Finite, usable height/box arithmetic is a premise: the current classifier does not independently reject NaN gaps, whose failed comparisons fall through to sunk. Thin edge overlap and deeper points between probes can remain unseen.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

One status union plus the numeric gap, the unresolved sides, and the two bases, with each null documented by the case that produces it, so no second result shape exists for suspension or unresolved claims.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

An unresolved side yields `unresolved` with a null gap rather than a guessed default, and the subject basis is narrowed to body bases because only a support can be a surface.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States when each status is chosen, the gap's metre unit, sign, deepest-probe rule and null cases, when `unresolved` is empty, and when each basis is null.
