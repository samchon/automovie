## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation `promoteDesignObservations` maps source points through settled-scale normalized axes and optional TRS while preserving primitive/point order. Admitted finite scale and coordinates can overflow mapping products/sums, and the producer forwards those results without a final finite-output check. This is a gated authored proposal, not verified metric recognition or guaranteed usable finite geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The record carries only the candidate id, its label, and one outline per primitive; it is output of a query and never feeds back into the building source automatically.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Arcs are withheld instead of approximated by chords, disjoint marks are not stitched into one run, and a region is not closed by a repeated point the observer never recorded.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States which mark families can reach the record, that it is a proposal rather than design, the units and frame of the outlines, their ordering, and why they stay separate and unclosed.
