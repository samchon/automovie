## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Opening-operation validation checks unique state identity and total finite in-range panel assignments. The staged gate decomposes/recomposes affected world matrices for named configurations and rejects nonfinite results or linear-basis error above 1e-8 times max(1, largest absolute basis entry); translation does not enlarge that tolerance, and tiny bases retain its absolute floor.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

A state is a name plus one value per panel, so the operation's vocabulary is data and every state, current or not, is applied by the same delta composition.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Missing values are refused at admission rather than silently defaulted to rest. Shared-influence Cartesian combinations are checked without selecting favored state names; their worst-case population can be exponential. The numerical TRS round-trip threshold can admit small residual shear and does not prove continuous intermediate configurations are representable.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States that every state is validated, the coverage and range rules, the shear rule, and which combination of states the shear check covers.
