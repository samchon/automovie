## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

One batch part table shares current-state staging across body resolution. Candidate tops sort descending and a binary-searched ceiling of underside plus tolerance skips higher parts; the first positive-area footprint hit is nearest within that ceiling. `compared` counts non-self candidates inspected after the search, not resolution, sort, search or elapsed cost. Finite ordered proxy bounds remain the numeric premise.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Grounded count, borne count and floating-list length partition measured bodies, with unresolved locators beside them. Empty findings identify only this proxy classification's outcome; they do not prove structural support or absence of real mesh contact defects.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Ground height comes from the caller's argument rather than an assumed environment field, bodies sunk below ground count as grounded as documented, and unresolved bodies are reported rather than skipped.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States that the three counts partition `measured`, where the ground height comes from and its default, what `compared` excludes, the grounded and borne thresholds, and the order of the floating list.
