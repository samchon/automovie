# src/architecture/IAutoMovieResolvedAssembly.ts#IAutoMovieResolvedAssembly

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Accumulates binary64 signed endpoints and unsigned thickness after the producer's validation refuses non-finite derived sums/endpoints. Ordered extent supports either sense; rounding can collapse a sufficiently small layer at a large offset, so this is numerical placement rather than exact-real interval arithmetic.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Keeps the overall dimension, both signed ends, the ordered extent and the placed layers, which is what dimensioning, reveal and junction queries read.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

An invalid build-up is refused before resolution rather than resolved into usable-looking numbers, and the rounding difference between `end` and the last layer's end is stated rather than patched.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the producer and its precondition, the coordinate convention, how `start`, `end` and `extent` relate, and the rounding difference between `end` and the last layer.
