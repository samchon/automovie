## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Admission resolves the driven element under the fill, enforces work-wide unique displacement ownership with carriages, positive finite extents and valid travel. When a usable profile/host face exists, the rest rectangle is transformed, projected to face XY and checked by the shared approximate polygon predicate against the straight arc-envelope hull. This measures the authored rectangle, not all visible mesh vertices or every operating-state collision.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The panel names an element, a leaf size, and one motion; the element hierarchy supplies placement and chaining, so a folding leaf is another panel parented under the first rather than a new record kind.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

A second element driver and an observed rest-rectangle fit failure are refused instead of overwritten or clipped. Fit discards depth and is skipped without a usable profile/face; an arc-envelope hull may contain space outside the authored arc. Sweeping varies only this panel's travel with ancestors at their current state, not every configuration of a folding chain.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the local frame of axis, pivot, and leaf rectangle, the natural door authoring, the rest-pose fit rule against the void hull, the one-driver rule, where the element must sit, and the units of the extents.
