## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

References resolve by declared ids. A stated profile's straight arc-envelope hull undergoes shared approximate face containment and same-boundary overlap tests; usable profiles/faces then constrain each panel's projected rest rectangle. Hull conservatism and binary64 predicate limits apply, circular-arc simplicity is not separately proved, and the record is not itself a rendered wall-cut operation.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Geometry and operation are optional members owned by the opening rather than by the boundary it cuts, so a relation-only door still validates and a boundary stays a plain separation without a list of holes.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

A void on a faceless host is refused rather than placed in an assumed plane, an operation without a fill is refused rather than guessing the moving element, and a leaf smaller than its void is allowed because paired leaves and sashes are ordinary designs.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States that boundary and fill must resolve, what a null fill and an omitted profile or operation mean, what a profile demands of the host face, that an operation needs a fill, and that each panel at rest must fit its void.
