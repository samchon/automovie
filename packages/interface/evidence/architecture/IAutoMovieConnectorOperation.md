## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Admission requires complete finite in-range carriage assignments. Service validation checks each claiming state's carriage origins with other operations current; the separate staged gate checks current, alternative and interacting named-state combinations for finite TRS representability within its 1e-8 scaled basis tolerance. It does not repeat service containment for every cross-operation combination or prove continuous trajectories between states.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Mirrors the opening operation's members-states-current shape so both feed one displacement table, and leaves doors and hardware on openings so one door is never declared in two places.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

An operation with no carriage, no state, or a current state that does not resolve is refused, and alternative states are validated for shear rather than trusting that only the current one is ever staged.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States what the record carries and leaves to openings, that it judges no traversal, that the connector must list its elements, the at-least-one rules, and what lowering and validation do with the current and other states.
