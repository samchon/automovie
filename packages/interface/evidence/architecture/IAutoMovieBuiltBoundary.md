# src/architecture/IAutoMovieBuiltBoundary.ts#IAutoMovieBuiltBoundary

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

A relation record that `validateBuiltEnvironment` checks for one or two resolving, unduplicated spaces, resolving unduplicated elements, a non-blank kind, and, when present, a usable face; only a boundary with a usable face can host an opening's void.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Relation and geometry are separable: the required members state which spaces are separated, and the optional face carries location only when the work needs openings or envelope geometry on it.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

An opening that states a void on a boundary without a face is refused rather than placed on an assumed plane, and the space count is checked instead of padded with an implicit exterior.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States what one and two cited spaces mean, the resolution and uniqueness of both lists, the meaning of an empty element list, and what an absent face leaves out.
