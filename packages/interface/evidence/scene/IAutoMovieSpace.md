## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Surface footprints and height laws supply support/contact height, while an explicit walkable-id population independently admits performer traversal; building lowering contributes this same low-level support representation.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Support geometry is separated from logical rooms, boundaries and connectors owned by the built environment, permitting direct outdoor authoring without duplicating architecture topology.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

A standable patch omitted from walkable can support props but not locomotion; semantic surface kind does not infer traversal permission or replace its actual height law.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation
