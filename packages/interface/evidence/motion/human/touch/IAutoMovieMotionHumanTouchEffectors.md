## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

A touch while standing: one hand, or both, laid on a region of a partner or of the performer's own body and moved on it by a kind of social touch (Jung et al. 2017): a tap, a pat, a stroke, a rub, a press, a squeeze, a hold or a scratch. It is the one law of the affectionate and social touch family (#3284: a pat on the head, a tap on the shoulder, a hand on the arm) and of the self-directed family (#3285: a belly pat, a nape rub, a forearm scratch, a self hug, hands over the ears), each item a row of `(effector, region, kind, timing)`.
