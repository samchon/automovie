## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

# src/motion/human/next/IAutoMovieMotionHumanGesture.ts#IAutoMovieMotionHumanGesture.IParams

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Each field states its meaning, unit, the gestures it applies to and the default an absent value takes.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The kind is gestureStroke's closed union; the target is the shared motion target union, read at each instant so pointing follows a moving target; count, hold and depth are a whole number, seconds and degrees, validated at plan time (positive whole count, non-negative hold, a bow in (0, gesture.DEEPEST_BOW], about 82 degrees) since the types cannot express those ranges.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Owned by this law and read only by its plan; the kind, face region, hand and Effort types are imported from their owners.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The fields that apply to only some kinds (target, region, depth) stay optional on one record rather than a union per kind: a field a kind does not read is ignored, and the one required pairing, `point` with a target, is refused at plan time. A per-kind discriminated union would rule that state out by type.
