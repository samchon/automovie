## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

A face of the piece's turned rest box, a depth out from it and a width along it, both in metres, define one floor rectangle in the room frame. The side is a closed union of the three faces a clearance serves. The producer checks that depth and width are finite and positive and that the rectangle lies in the room.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

The furnishing producer reserves these for each related piece. A front clearance generalizes the counter approach the cafe kit reserved, and side clearances serve the floor beside a bed's long sides, which the room kits need.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The back face is excluded because a wall or another piece stands there. Width defaults to the face length or the aisle, the counter approach's rule, so no clearance is narrower than a walking width unless the author says so. The optional name only labels the rectangle, so a kit's refusal can name the cited clearance it reserves; it changes no geometry.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the side convention as seen from in front of the piece, the label name and its default, the depth and width in metres, the width default and what must stay out of the rectangle.
