# src/human/skeleton/IAutoMovieHumanChainRings.ts#IAutoMovieHumanChainRings

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

States the sampling, units and why the rings of coarser readers are subsets. One loft chain's skin sampled as rings: the fixed record of a body's skin that the binding and the neutral posture read in place of the calibration.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation `chain`: The loft chain's name, such as `trunk` or `leg.left`. `phases`: Phases per ring, evenly spaced over a full turn from phase 0. `stations`: The chain's stations, calibration millimetres of height, bottom first as the chain lists them.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Returned by AutoMovieHumanBindingInputs.rings. Read by AutoMovieHumanNeutral.derive, AutoMovieHumanNeutralHip.derive, AutoMovieHumanNeutralKnuckle.lines. Also types AutoMovieHumanBindingInputs.skin, AutoMovieHumanBindingInputs.neutralRings.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts
