## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

A shot capture cannot satisfy subject coverage merely by containing the subject; stale revision, plan or runtime remains ineligible. The fold requires a nonblank artifact digest without validating its syntax or agreement with actual bytes, which remain caller premises.
