## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Independent seed-and-slot salts sample scale, rotation, palette, visibility and named numeric ranges; ordered finite ranges and representable quaternion arithmetic are caller premises. Equal endpoints remain constant and explicit slot values override their stated channels; the regenerator checks sampled scalar scale, position and procedural traits but not every enhanced or explicit override.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Keeps shared variation laws on the compact set while regenerated slots carry their resolved values; optional axis scale, Euler offsets and visibility extend the basic scale, palette and traits.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Palette entries are actual sRGB swatches decoded before linear shading, not labels or linear triples; omission of enhanced channels preserves the legacy slot shape rather than injecting extra fields.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Defines positive scale ranges, degree Euler order, visibility probability, exact color encoding and named trait bounds with explicit override behavior.
