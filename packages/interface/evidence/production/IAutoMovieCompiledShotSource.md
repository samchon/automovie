## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

The record combines shot source output, resolved models, regenerable populations, formation cues and deterministic effects for Engine measurement and render adapters. The production composer is retired; a host must assemble and explicitly admit these fields, and optional source attribution is declared metadata rather than a surviving graph-selection proof.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Extending shot output preserves its scene and motion representation, while compiler-only resolution and provenance fields stay on the compiled artifact rather than expanding the source authoring API.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Render and measurement consume the supplied artifact without executing acceptance exports. This type supplies no source-owner admission, review/final publication gate or automatic formation-cue validation after production retirement; the host owns those checks.
