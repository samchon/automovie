## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Realization requires an exact finite event sample inside its admitted time window, every declared subject to resolve and every typed predicate to pass against compiled output at that sample.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

A closed event family and explicit subjects describe semantic ownership, while predicates supply independent measurable facts instead of encoding motion in the contract.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Repeating the event id or prose is insufficient; missing, duplicate or out-of-window samples and unresolvable subjects fail rather than being silently dropped.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Defines stable event identity, family, inclusive second window, unique subjects and nonempty machine-checkable obligations.
