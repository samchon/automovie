## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Ordinary member pages preserve total, sorted selected identities and clamped offset; omitted counts all off-page members. Formation descriptions instead retain total designed slots, offset zero and named heroes only, so their items cannot page through every anonymous slot.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Four fields distinguish cardinality, bounded identities, page start and omitted count without a dense response; formation's hero-only sample remains distinguishable by its surrounding subject kind rather than an invented paging capability.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

A bounded page is not the whole population and omitted is not a remaining-page count; formation review may list only named heroes while total still counts every designed slot.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Defines total, sample ordering, ordinary pagination, formation hero-only offset-zero exception and whole-population omission count.
