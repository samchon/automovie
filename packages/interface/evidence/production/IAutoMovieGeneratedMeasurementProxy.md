## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Two closed dimension records separate intended projection envelopes from appearance bytes and collision geometry; actor-root distance and skeletal reach queries keep their own measurement semantics.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The schema supplies no envelope centre or root offset, and the humanoid recipe supplies no depth. Its intended half-diagonal interpretation uses all three box extents or stature and maximum shoulder/hip width; that centred estimate does not certify enclosure of offset visual geometry, and no current producer computes it from this field automatically.
