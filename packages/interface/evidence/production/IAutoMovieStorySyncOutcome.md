## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

evaluateAutoMovieStorySync maps realized local event times through explicit pins and compares maximum minus minimum with the inclusive declared tolerance, reporting points in operand order. Pin validity, finite non-negative tolerance and criterion cardinality are upstream design-admission premises; the direct evaluator permits one point and does not recheck event uniqueness.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Keeps individual measurements, aggregate spread and verdict beside one deterministic diagnostic account, without involving edit positions or image observations.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

Missing pins, missing or non-finite realizations and mapped overflow leave null spread and fail; an empty claim also fails rather than passing a vacuous synchronization assertion.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation
