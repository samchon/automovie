## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

A common shot interval and unique slot ordinals bind designated floor marks to the members that move to them. Drum Corps International documents such individual field coordinates in its dot-book practice (https://www.dci.org/news/do-you-speak-drum-corps/).

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Two local coordinates and an optional facing reuse sparse slot motion without carrying a second runtime movement track.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

No member assignment, speed, foot motion or collision route is inferred from formation labels.
