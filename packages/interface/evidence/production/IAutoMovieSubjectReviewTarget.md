# src/production/IAutoMovieSubjectReviewTarget.ts#IAutoMovieSubjectReviewTarget

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

A shot artifact id and namespaced subject address resolve one exact compiled subject through shared inspection, preventing identically named subjects from another artifact from sharing coverage.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

Two identifiers provide artifact qualification without a frame index or an unrelated film-time target.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

The shot address does not imply that its delivery camera observed this subject; viewpoint authority remains with inspection and missing subjects refuse resolution.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Defines compiled artifact qualification, stable namespaced subject identity and independence from the shot's camera.
