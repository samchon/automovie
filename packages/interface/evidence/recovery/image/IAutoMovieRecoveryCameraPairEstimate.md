## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#principled-representation

Fixed source identities, actual interest-point matches, calibrated pixel residuals, four-branch depth/parallax and competing planar support distinguish measured evidence from a conditional pose. The pose is absent after any qualification refusal, and sampling targets describe only their ideal all-inlier model.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#minimal-and-owned-surface

One JSON-compatible observation record separates completion of computation, explicit camera refusal, retained image evidence and the accepted relative transform; RGB buffers and descriptors remain transient computation data.

## @evidence contracts/development/design.md#prohibited-design-shortcuts

No truth pose, reference mesh, metric depth, unobserved geometry or refused candidate transform is labelled as an accepted camera. Undefined scalar diagnostics remain null rather than invented zero error.

## @evidence contracts/development/common.md#meaningful-documentation

Gives source/assertion ownership, original pixel charts, unit gauge, null meanings, conditional sampling and the distinction between camera evidence and an editable surface asset.
