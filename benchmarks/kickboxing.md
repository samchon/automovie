The root pull request owns general instructions and any required package repairs. The separate examples repository owns the production, its screenplay, source and media.

Picture-only and procedural-effects files remain in the user's workspace. The [delivery record at examples e84f5a62](https://github.com/samchon/automovie-examples/blob/e84f5a62ed4b991612ac2b49cef1db89aa03c141/movies/shorts/kickboxing/docs/acceptance/kickboxing.md) retains actual hashes, measurements, source/encoded inspection and the defects left visible. Completion does not accept exact screenplay realization or elite athletic motion.
