## SDK consumption failures

The first nested SDK unit test exposed a relative lint configuration path inherited into `test/tsconfig.json`: the runtime looked for `test/lint.config.ts`. Removing the redundant `configFile` setting keeps the same plugin and rules and uses its upward configuration discovery. Generated standalone and workspace projects passed their actual install, lint, SDK unit tests, build and Evidence checks with this change.

The first Human/motion browser catalog exposed two browser initialization failures that starter checks had missed. The published CommonJS SDK selected Three.js's Node-only `require` entry, and the production bundle's automatic CommonJS hoisting called a motion module before its exports object was initialized. The scaffold's Vite configuration now selects the resolved Three.js ESM entry and preserves CommonJS initialization order. ## Observation and repair

Each inspection round covers its declared population before repairs are made. Record the affected exchanges and frame times, the visible or execution failure, its cause and contributing factors, and whether an existing instruction already covers it. A missed existing rule needs a repair to its application; an absent or contradictory requirement needs a change at its actual owner.

Keep the failing case and the achievement its repair must preserve. Reproduce the case through the installed production entry point, repair all members of the cause together, and inspect the affected performance again. A compiling program, a passing evidence graph or a larger instruction file does not establish a better film.

The whole performance needs review of support, contacts, recovery, distance, ring geography, opponent responses, referee presence, camera visibility, continuity and the exact closing anchors. Source captures and encoded-artifact captures answer different questions. Playback and sound remain unverified until the user reports watching and listening.

## Scaffold verification

Generalise only what the production demonstrates. A shipped rule belongs to its existing task or contract owner and stays independent of kickboxing names, techniques and production paths. Preserve the short and long routes and legitimate outputs that end at text, a motion hint or another commissioned stage.

For each instruction change, retain the original failure and a contrasting legitimate case, inspect what the scaffold actually distributes, and observe a separate session working from the changed instructions and raw inputs. Record distribution, behaviour, reading cost and media results separately. The draft is ready for final review when the film is complete, each proposed instruction change has such evidence, and remaining observation limits are explicit.
